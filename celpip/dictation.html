Dictation ladder听写阶梯

Hear a sentence, type it, see exactly which words you missed. Each rung is a little harder than the last; 90% on every sentence of a rung means you are ready for the next. Small words you miss (have, the, to) are the ones that also cost you in the test.听一句、打出来，看清漏了哪几个词。每一级比上一级难一点；一级里每句都到 90% 就可以上下一级。你漏掉的小词（have、the、to），正是考试里会丢分的那些。
