Listening trap types听力陷阱类型图鉴

CELPIP 5–6 · ≈B1CELPIP 7–8 · ≈B2CELPIP 9+ · ≈C1

Most wrong answers in the listening test come from a handful of tricks that repeat across the six parts. Learn to recognise each one by ear.听力里的大多数错题，来自在六个部分里反复出现的几种手法。学会用耳朵认出它们。

⚠️ The correction说完又改口

“Let's meet on Tuesday… actually, Wednesday works better for me.”

Write the first detail, then cross it out when you hear actually, sorry, wait, or on second thought. The answer is the last version.先记下第一个细节，听到 actually、sorry、wait、on second thought 就划掉。答案是最后那个版本。

Shows up in:常见于： Part第 1 Part第 2 Part第 3

⚠️ The first idea is dropped第一个方案被放弃

“Why don't you take the bus? Oh, there's no bus near the clinic. I can drive you.”

List every option you hear and circle the one both speakers accept. The option you hear first is often the one on the screen as a wrong choice.把听到的每个方案都列出来，圈出双方最后都同意的。最先听到的方案往往就是选项里的错误答案。

Shows up in:常见于： Part第 1 Part第 2 Part第 5

⚠️ Same meaning, different words同义替换

“You hear: the hotel staff recommended it. The answer says: people at their hotel suggested it.”

Do not hunt for the exact words of the options. Ask what the speaker meant, then find the option that says it another way. An option that repeats a word you heard is often the trap.别在选项里找原词。先想说话人是什么意思，再找换了说法的那个选项。重复了你听到的原词的选项，常常是陷阱。

⚠️ Not, only, hardly否定与限定

“The pool is open every day, but only members can use it before nine.”

Small words change the whole fact. Put a minus sign in your notes for not, never, hardly, and a box around only, except, unless.小词能翻转整个事实。笔记里 not、never、hardly 记一个减号，only、except、unless 画个框。

Shows up in:常见于： Part第 3 Part第 4 Part第 6

⚠️ Numbers that move会变的数字

“It was forty dollars, but with the discount it comes to thirty-two.”

When you hear a number, listen for the next three seconds: was it changed, discounted, or about something else? Write numbers as digits with a word beside them.听到数字后再听三秒：有没有改、有没有打折、是不是在说别的东西？数字用阿拉伯数字记，旁边写一个词说明它是什么。

Shows up in:常见于： Part第 2 Part第 3 Part第 4

⚠️ Who said it是谁说的

“Ana wants Saturday. Ben says most people go away on Saturdays. Cai agrees with Ben.”

Make one column per speaker before the audio starts. Note each opinion under a name, with an arrow when one person agrees with another.音频开始前按说话人分栏。每个观点记在人名下面，有人附和别人就画一个箭头。

Shows up in:常见于： Part第 5 Part第 6

⚠️ Tone and hedging语气与留余地

“In theory, remote work could lower rents, though nobody has shown that yet.”

Hedges (in theory, could, might, some experts) mean the speaker is not sure. Words like however and though mark where the real view begins.in theory、could、might、some experts 说明说话人没有把握；however、though 之后才是真正的看法。

Shows up in:常见于： Part第 5 Part第 6

⚠️ Main idea or detail主旨还是细节

“A fox walked into a coffee shop… the story is really about how the town handled it.”

For main-idea questions, choose the option that covers the whole talk, not the most colourful detail. Your notes from the first and last sentences usually decide it.主旨题要选能盖住整段的选项，而不是最抢眼的细节。第一句和最后一句的笔记通常就能定答案。
