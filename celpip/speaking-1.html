Hi Maria, welcome to your first BC winter! Honestly, it's far milder than people expect, as long as you prepare a little.

First and foremost, invest in a warm, waterproof coat and a pair of insulated boots. Here on the coast it's the rain, not the cold, that wears you down, and nothing ruins a day faster than soaking-wet feet. I picked up my boots in October, when they were on sale, which saved me a fair bit.

Second, make a habit of checking the forecast and the road conditions before you head out. Even a light dusting of snow can turn the roads icy, and buses tend to run behind schedule. A good weather app takes the guesswork out of what to wear.

Finally, if you drive, keep a small emergency kit in the trunk: a blanket, a flashlight, water and a few snacks. Chances are you'll never need it, but it's peace of mind. Last January I was stuck in a snowbank for an hour, and that blanket was a lifesaver.

All in all, it's very manageable. Stay warm, and don't hesitate to call me if anything comes up!

Hi Anna, congratulations on finding your first place! Before you put pen to paper, though, there are a few things worth checking.

First, go through the lease line by line. Pay close attention to the rent, the length of the term and any rules about pets or guests. If a clause is unclear, ask the landlord to explain it; never sign anything under pressure.

Second, inspect the unit itself. Turn on the heater, open and close every window and run the taps. Burnaby winters are cold and damp, so poor insulation can mean a miserable few months. Photograph any existing damage, too, so it can't be taken out of your deposit later.

Third, clarify the extra costs. Is parking included, and are heat and hot water part of the rent? These add up quickly, and some landlords only mention them if you ask.

Strategy策略

  • Speak directly to the person in a helpful tone.直接称呼对方（Hi Sam…），语气友好、愿意帮忙。
  • At least three suggestions, each with a reason or example.至少给三条建议，每条配一个理由或小例子。
  • Signal each new idea with a transition.用 First / Another thing / Finally 把三条分开，听的人才跟得上。

Tips提醒

  • Advice must be "doable": don't say "you must try hard", say "walk for twenty minutes after dinner every day".建议要「做得到」：别说「你要努力」，说「每天晚饭后散步二十分钟」。

  • Don't use long, memorized-sounding sentences. Short sentences with pauses score more steadily than one long complex sentence.不要念稿式的长句；短句 + 停顿比一口气的复杂句得分更稳。
