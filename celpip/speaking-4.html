I imagine the woman will pay for her apples, tuck them away and wander over to the flower stall, since she seems to be in the mood to browse a bit longer.

As for the little girl, she's only loosely holding her balloon, so I suspect it's going to slip out of her hand any second now. She'll probably be on the verge of tears, but the older man, being quite tall, will most likely reach up, grab the string and hand it back to her.

What changes from level to level每升一级换了什么

  • 5–6 → 7–8 Then she will go to the flowers. She wants to buy more things. → Then she will probably walk to the flower table, because she looks like she wants to buy something else. Content / Coherence内容与连贯
    "Because" plus a clue from the picture gives the prediction a reason.because 加上从图里看到的根据，预测就有了理由。
  • 5–6 → 7–8 She will cry. The old man will help her. → She will feel sad and maybe cry, but the older man will probably help her. He is tall, so he can catch the string. Content / Coherence内容与连贯
    Explaining why the man can help (he is tall) makes the prediction believable.说清老人为什么能帮（个子高），预测更可信。
  • 7–8 → 9+ I think it might fly away soon. → I suspect it's going to slip out of her hand any second now Vocabulary词汇
    "I suspect" and "any second now" replace the repeated "I think" and "soon", and grade certainty more finely.I suspect、any second now 换掉了反复的 I think、soon，表达把握程度更细。
  • 7–8 → 9+ He is tall, so he can catch the string. → the older man, being quite tall, will most likely reach up, grab the string and hand it back to her Listenability好懂程度
    The participle phrase "being quite tall" gives the reason, and three verbs in a row keep the pace.插入的分词短语 being quite tall 说明原因，后面三个动词连用，节奏紧凑。
  • 7–8 → 9+ It might eat something from the table, and the seller will laugh → I wouldn't be surprised if it snatched a bite of something, and the vendor, rather than getting annoyed, will laugh Listenability好懂程度
    "I wouldn't be surprised if" is a soft prediction pattern, and "rather than" adds a contrast.I wouldn't be surprised if 是委婉的预测句型，rather than 插入对比。
Notes on this prompt这道题的讲解
  • Each of the three predictions uses a person or animal, and each starts from "what is happening" in the picture, so they are reasonable.三个预测各用一个人或动物，都从图里「正在发生的事」出发，所以合理。
  • Using the pattern "I think ... will probably ..." repeatedly keeps the sentence form steady and sounds smooth.I think … will probably … 这句式反复使用，句型稳定，听感很顺。
  • Common mistake: a prediction unrelated to the picture (for example, sudden heavy rain). Predictions must be based on what is already in the picture.常见错误：预测与图无关（比如突然下大雨）。预测要基于图里已有的东西。

What changes from level to level每升一级换了什么

Notes on this prompt这道题的讲解
  • First predict the nearest event (the car arrives), then go person by person, so the structure is like telling a timeline.先预测最近的事（车来了），再一个一个人说，结构像在讲一条时间线。

  • Common mistake: using the past tense in a prediction. Keep "will / going to" throughout and don't switch to "was" halfway.常见错误：预测里出现过去时。全篇保持 will / going to，不要中途变成 was。

☕ 🧑‍💻 👩 🧍 🥐

Look at the same scene and say what will probably happen next: A busy coffee shop in the morning. A line of five people waits at the counter. A barista makes a drink while another gives a bag to a customer. A man works on a laptop at a table. A woman looks at her watch. A child stares at pastries in the glass case. 早晨很忙的咖啡店。

CELPIP 9+ · ≈C1 134 words词

I expect the woman will pay, bag her vegetables and milk, thank the cashier and head out, all in a couple of minutes.

The child reaching for the candy probably won't get away with it. I can picture his mother spotting him and saying, "Not today," in that firm voice parents use. He'll sulk for a moment, but he'll get over it as soon as something else catches his eye.

The man behind them is clearly in a rush, so he'll likely glance over at lane three, which looks shorter. However, once he notices the "cash only" sign, he'll realize he has no cash on him and reluctantly stay where he is, tapping his foot.

By the time he's done, the cashier will already be greeting the next customer with that same friendly smile.

What changes from level to level每升一级换了什么

  • 5–6 → 7–8 His mother will say no. → I think his mother will see it and say, "Not today." Content / Coherence内容与连贯
    A real line of speech replaces "say no", so the prediction is more concrete.用一句真实的话代替 say no，预测更具体。
  • 5–6 → 7–8 But it is cash only. So he will wait. → But the sign says cash only, and maybe he has no cash. So he will probably stay in the line. Content / Coherence内容与连贯
    Linking "cash only" with "maybe he has no cash" completes the reasoning.把「只收现金」和「他可能没现金」连起来，推理完整。
  • 7–8 → 9+ I think his mother will see it → The child reaching for the candy probably won't get away with it. Vocabulary词汇
    "Get away with it" is a natural phrase.get away with it（得逞、逃过）是地道的短语。
  • 7–8 → 9+ He will probably be upset for a minute, but then he will forget about it. → He'll sulk for a moment, but he'll get over it as soon as something else catches his eye. Listenability好懂程度
    The "as soon as" clause gives the reason he forgets, and "sulk" and "get over it" are more idiomatic.as soon as 从句给出「忘掉」的原因，sulk、get over it 更地道。
  • 7–8 → 9+ So he will probably stay in the line. → he'll realize he has no cash on him and reluctantly stay where he is Vocabulary词汇
    "Reluctantly" adds his feeling, which says more than "stay".reluctantly 加上情绪，比单纯说 stay 更有层次。
Notes on this prompt这道题的讲解
  • "The sign says cash only" is a clever clue for a prediction: the man may not have cash, so he can't switch lines.「牌子写 cash only」是一个很聪明的预测线索：男人也许没带现金，所以换不了队。
  • The turn with "but" makes the prediction show thinking, instead of a mechanical sequence.but 转折把预测变得有思考，而不是机械顺序。
  • Common mistake: switching between "may" and "might" is fine, but don't mix in the present tense.常见错误：用 may 和 might 来回换没问题，但别混着用现在时。

CELPIP 5–6 · ≈B1
  • Uses will to predict, but each prediction is one sentence with no reason.会用 will 预测，但每个预测只有一句，没有理由。
  • Keeps repeating "I think ... will ...".一直重复 I think … will …。
  • Vague references (the boy, the man), so the listener is unsure who is meant.人物指得不清楚（the boy、the man），听者不确定说的是谁。
CELPIP 7–8 · ≈B2
  • Each prediction has a because, based on clues in the picture.每个预测后面有 because，根据来自图里看得到的线索。
  • Mixes will, probably, might and going to.混用 will、probably、might、going to。
  • Identifies the person first (the woman who is looking at her watch), then predicts.先说清是哪个人（the woman who is looking at her watch），再预测。
CELPIP 9+ · ≈C1
  • Predictions are graded by certainty: I suspect, is bound to, I wouldn't be surprised if.预测有把握程度的层次：I suspect、is bound to、I wouldn't be surprised if。
  • Predictions form a storyline with people reacting to each other, as the official Level 9 sample guessed how the parent would respond.预测连成一条故事线，人物之间有互动和反应，像官方 9 级样本还猜到了家长怎么回应。
  • As soon as, until and once make the timing clear.用 as soon as、until、once 把时间顺序说清楚。

Why people get stuck at 7为什么常卡在 7 级

  • Describes the present (Task 3 habits) instead of predicting what comes next.在描述现在（任务 3 的做法），而不是预测接下来。
  • Every prediction uses will, with no difference between likely and certain.所有预测都是 will，没有「可能」「肯定」的区别。
  • Predictions are random, not linked to clues in the picture.预测天马行空，和图里的线索没关系。

Three moves to 9+冲 9 级以上的三步

  • Give each prediction a certainty word: probably, I suspect, is bound to.每个预测用一个「把握词」：probably / I suspect / is bound to。
  • Make at least one prediction a chain: A does something, B reacts.至少一个预测写成「A 做了什么→B 会怎么反应」。
  • End with how the whole scene will wrap up.结尾说这一幕最后会怎样收场。

📘 Template and phrases模板与常用句

Strategy策略

  • Be imaginative but realistic.预测要现实，和图里人物正在做的事有关。
  • What will they do, and how will they feel?想两件事：他们接下来会做什么？会有什么感受？
  • Use future forms and probability words.多用 will / is going to / probably / might。

Tips提醒

  • Predictions should relate to what the people in the picture are doing. Don't jump to another story.预测要和图里人物正在做的事有关，别突然跳到别的故事。
  • Give each prediction a "why" or a "feeling": because ... / he will feel ...每个预测配「为什么」或「感受」：because … / he will feel …。
  • For tense, use will / is going to. Don't mix in the past tense.时态用 will / is going to，别混成过去时。

  • is going to … 将要……
    The boy is going to get very wet.
  • might … 可能会……

  • Soon, … 很快……
    Soon, the rain will stop.
  • After that, … 之后……
    After that, they will go to work.

    As a result, her coffee will get cold.

    He will feel relieved.

    In a few minutes, the street will be quiet.
  • I guess … 我猜……
    I guess they will go home early.
