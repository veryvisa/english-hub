Notes on this prompt这道题的讲解
  • First affirm the other person's plan (cheap, near the subway), then compare with the numbers in the prompt: 900 vs 2,400. This is the most important move in Part 2.先肯定对方的方案（便宜、近地铁），再用题目里的数字对比：900 vs 2,400。这是 Part 2 最关键的动作。
  • Your reasons should answer the weaknesses of the other plan (shared kitchen, no parking), not just talk about your own strengths.理由要对应对方的方案缺点（shared kitchen, no parking），不是只讲自己的好处。
  • Common mistake: using the wrong numbers. 1,500 is 2,400 minus 900, so don't just say any number. If you can't calculate it, say "a lot more".常见错误：数字用错。1,500 是 2,400 减 900，别随口乱报。算不清就说 a lot more。

Why people get stuck at 7为什么常卡在 7 级

  • Compares only your choice with the original other option and forgets the new suggestion.只比较自己选的方案和原来的另一个选项，忘了对方提的新方案。
