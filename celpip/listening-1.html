Part 1 · Problem Solving 第 1 部分 · 解决问题

CELPIP 5–6 · ≈B1CELPIP 7–8 · ≈B2CELPIP 9+ · ≈C18 questions题 · about 8 min

🎧 What it tests考什么
A short conversation in which one person has a problem and the other helps. Two speakers (one man, one woman) who do not know each other, polite and fairly informal. The conversation comes in 3 audio sections of about 1 to 1.5 minutes, with 2 to 3 questions after each.一个人遇到问题，另一个人帮忙。两位说话人（一男一女）互不认识，礼貌、偏口语。对话分成 3 段音频，每段约 1–1.5 分钟，每段后有 2–3 道题。典型场景：同事谈家里的事、顾客找收银员退货或买东西。

Multiple choice. You hear each question and read 4 answer choices. Questions appear one by one, in order, and you have 30 seconds to hear and answer each.单选题。你会听到问题并看到 4 个选项；题目一道一道出现、按顺序做，每题 30 秒内听题并作答。

⚠️ Traps in this part这一部分的陷阱

  • The first solution is not the final one第一个方案不一定是最后选的
    Speakers often try one idea and drop it. Write every option, then circle the one they agree on.说话人常先提一个办法又放弃。把每个方案都记下来，再圈出最后同意的那个。
  • Details get corrected细节会被更正
    “Let's say Tuesday … actually, Wednesday is better.” Note the later one. Numbers and days change mid-conversation.「周二吧……其实周三更好。」以后面那个为准。数字和日期会在对话中途改。
  • Repeated words in the wrong answer错误选项里重复了原话
    A choice that repeats a word from the audio is often a trap. The right answer usually paraphrases.选项里逐字重复了音频里的词，常常是陷阱；正确答案多半是换种说法。
  • Who decides?谁做决定？
    The helper suggests; the person with the problem decides. Questions often ask what the person with the problem will do.帮忙的人提建议，有问题的人做决定。题目常问「有问题的人接下来会怎么做」。

✍️ Text drills文字练习

Read each short exchange as if you heard it once, then answer. Each drill sits on a topic you can study on this site.把每段短对话当作只听一遍来读，再作答。每道题都挂在本站的一个主题上。

  1. Man: “I booked the 3 p.m. appointment, but my car is in the shop.” Woman: “Why don't you take the bus? Oh, wait, there's no bus near the clinic. I can drive you, if it's okay.” Man: “That would be great, thanks.”
    What will the man do? Seeing a doctor看病就医
    Answer答案: Ask the woman to drive him
    The woman first suggests the bus, then corrects herself and offers a ride. He accepts the ride.女士先建议坐公交，马上又自我否定，改为开车送他。他接受了这个。
  2. Customer: “I'd like to return this lamp. It doesn't turn on.” Clerk: “Do you have the receipt?” Customer: “It was a gift, so no.” Clerk: “Then I can only give you store credit.”
    What does the clerk offer? Complaints and consumer rights投诉与消费者权益
    Answer答案: Store credit
    Without a receipt the clerk can give only store credit; the other options are not mentioned as offers.没有收据，店员只能给店内credit。其他选项没有被提出。
  3. Woman: “My internet has been down since Monday.” Man: “Have you tried restarting the router?” Woman: “Twice. And I called the company, but they can only come next Friday.” Man: “That's too long. My cousin fixes these things; I'll give you his number.”
    What is the man's solution? Complaints and consumer rights投诉与消费者权益
    Answer答案: Calling his cousin
    Router and company were already tried or too slow. The man's own solution is his cousin.路由器和公司都试过或太慢。男士自己提出的办法是他的表哥。
  4. Man: “I can't come to the meeting on Thursday.” Woman: “How about Friday morning?” Man: “I have to take my daughter to the dentist. Friday afternoon works, though.”
    When will they meet? Talking at work职场沟通
    Answer答案: Friday afternoon
    Thursday is impossible, Friday morning is blocked, and only Friday afternoon works.周四不行，周五上午也不行，只有周五下午可以。
  5. Woman: “I'm worried about the rent. It went up again.” Man: “Have you talked to your landlord?” Woman: “Not yet. I'm a bit scared he'll say no.” Man: “I think you should at least ask.”
    How does the woman feel about talking to her landlord? Finding a home找房与安家
    Answer答案: Nervous
    “A bit scared he'll say no” means she is nervous. This is an inference question about feeling.「有点怕他拒绝」说明她紧张。这是考感受的推理题。

📏 Strategy for every part每个部分通用的做法

Before the audio音频开始前

  • Set up your notes before the audio starts.音频开始前先把笔记页分好格。
  • Read the introductory statement for context clues.先读一遍屏幕上的开场说明，里面有线索。

While you listen听的时候

  • Identify the topic. Is there a problem? What is the solution?先抓话题。有没有问题？解决办法是什么？
  • Use tone of voice for attitudes and opinions.用语气判断态度和观点。
  • Focus on what you understand, not on what you missed.盯着听懂的部分，别纠结漏掉的词。

After you listen听完之后

  • Match key words in the question to your notes.用题目里的关键词对笔记。
  • Answer every question. Wrong answers are not penalised.每题都要答，答错不扣分。
