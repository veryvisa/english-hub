Part 3 · Listening for Information 第 3 部分 · 听取信息

CELPIP 5–6 · ≈B1CELPIP 7–8 · ≈B2CELPIP 9+ · ≈C16 questions题 · about 6 min

🎧 What it tests考什么
One longer clip of about 2 to 2.5 minutes in which one speaker has information the other wants: a customer buying a car from a salesperson, an experienced employee explaining a procedure to a new coworker. Polite, informal to somewhat formal.一段约 2–2.5 分钟的音频，一方掌握另一方想要的信息：比如顾客向销售了解买车、老员工向新同事讲解流程。礼貌，口语到较正式。

Multiple choice again: 30 seconds per question, in order, 4 choices. About 6 questions.还是单选：每题 30 秒，按顺序，4 选项，约 6 题。

⚠️ Traps in this part这一部分的陷阱

  • Many numbers一堆数字
    Prices, dates, quantities. Write a number with its label every time: “$300 deposit”.价格、日期、数量。每个数字都和它的标签一起记：「押金 300」。
  • Conditions条件句
    “If you sign by Friday, the price stays.” Questions test the condition, not just the fact.「周五前签约价格不变」——题目会考条件，不只是事实。
  • Not / except不是 / 除了
    A question asking what is not included needs every option checked against your notes.问「哪项不包括」时，要把每个选项都对照笔记。
  • What the listener finally chooses听的人最后选了什么
    The listener compares options and decides at the end. Wait for the final decision.听的人会比较几个选项，最后才决定。等最后的决定。

✍️ Text drills文字练习

Read each short exchange as if you heard it once, then answer. Each drill sits on a topic you can study on this site.把每段短对话当作只听一遍来读，再作答。每道题都挂在本站的一个主题上。

  1. Salesperson: “The standard plan is $40 a month. The premium plan is $60 and includes free repairs, but only if you sign a two-year contract.”
    What is required for free repairs? Complaints and consumer rights投诉与消费者权益
    Answer答案: A two-year contract
    The condition is “only if you sign a two-year contract”. $40 is the standard plan.条件是「只有签两年合同」。$40 是标准套餐的价格。
  2. Employee: “To book a meeting room, first log in, then choose the floor. Rooms on the third floor are free; on the fourth floor, you pay ten dollars an hour.”
    Which rooms are free? Talking at work职场沟通
    Answer答案: Rooms on the third floor
    Third floor free; fourth floor costs $10 per hour.三楼免费；四楼每小时 10 元。
  3. Agent: “The rent is $1,800. It includes heat, water and one underground parking spot, but electricity is not included.”
    Which is NOT included in the rent? Finding a home找房与安家
    Answer答案: Electricity
    “Electricity is not included.” Heat, water and parking are all named as included.「电费不含」。暖气、水和车位都明确说了包含在租金里。
  4. Customer: “Which car is cheaper to run?” Salesperson: “The hybrid is about $2,000 more, but you'll save on gas.” Customer: “I'd rather pay less now. I'll take the regular one.”
    Which car will the customer buy? Driving and getting around开车与出行
    Answer答案: The regular car
    The final decision is the regular one, even though the hybrid saves gas.最后的决定是普通款，虽然混动省油。
  5. Coworker: “The training starts at 9 a.m., but the doors open at 8:30. You'll need your badge, and lunch is provided.”
    What do you need to bring? Talking at work职场沟通
    Answer答案: A badge
    You need your badge; lunch is provided, so do not bring it.需要带工牌；午餐提供，不用自己带。

  1. Does Riley need glasses?
    Answer答案: No, he has 20/20 vision
    Evidence依据: riley has 20 20 vision
    Vision is 20/20. The problem is the functional movement of the eyes.视力 20/20，问题在眼睛的功能性运动。
  2. Which way of learning the exercises costs the least?
    Answer答案: The loaned computer program
    Evidence依据: the computer program is the most affordable
    The doctor says the computer program is the cheapest.医生说电脑程序最便宜。
  3. How long must Riley do the exercises at home?
    Answer答案: 30 minutes a day for 12 weeks
    Evidence依据: 30 minutes a day for 12 weeks
    30 minutes a day, for 12 weeks in total.每天 30 分钟，共 12 周。

    Answer答案: Some private insurance plans
    Evidence依据: some private insurance plans

📏 Strategy for every part每个部分通用的做法

Before the audio音频开始前

  • Set up your notes before the audio starts.音频开始前先把笔记页分好格。
  • Read the introductory statement for context clues.先读一遍屏幕上的开场说明，里面有线索。

While you listen听的时候

  • Identify the topic. Is there a problem? What is the solution?先抓话题。有没有问题？解决办法是什么？
  • Use tone of voice for attitudes and opinions.用语气判断态度和观点。
  • Focus on what you understand, not on what you missed.盯着听懂的部分，别纠结漏掉的词。

After you listen听完之后

  • Match key words in the question to your notes.用题目里的关键词对笔记。
  • Answer every question. Wrong answers are not penalised.每题都要答，答错不扣分。
