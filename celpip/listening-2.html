Part 2 · Daily Life Conversation 第 2 部分 · 日常对话

CELPIP 5–6 · ≈B1CELPIP 7–8 · ≈B2CELPIP 9+ · ≈C15 questions题 · about 5 min

🎧 What it tests考什么
One conversation of about 1.5 to 2 minutes between two people who know each other, such as coworkers on a project, team members before a game, or a couple making weekend plans. Conversational, polite, not too casual or personal.一段约 1.5–2 分钟的对话，两个人互相认识：比如同事谈项目、队友赛前准备、夫妻商量周末。口语、礼貌，不太私人。

Multiple choice, same as Part 1: hear the question, read 4 choices, 30 seconds each, answer in order.和第 1 部分一样：听问题、看 4 个选项，每题 30 秒，按顺序做。

⚠️ Traps in this part这一部分的陷阱

  • Who does what谁负责什么
    Plans are split between two people. Write A: ... and B: ... so you do not swap them.计划会分给两个人。笔记写成「A：……、B：……」，免得混淆。
  • Time, place, and changes时间、地点和更改
    Plans move. Keep a mini calendar: day, time, place, and cross out old ones.计划会变。记一个小日历：日期、时间、地点，旧的划掉。
  • Agreement vs. suggestion同意还是只是建议
    “That might work” is not a yes. “Sure, let's do it” is. Listen for the final agreement.「可能行」不是同意；「好，就这么办」才是。听最后的确认。
  • Casual speech口语缩读
    gonna, wanna, I'll have to, d'you. Practise hearing reduced sounds, not just the written forms.gonna、wanna、d'you 这类缩读要练习去「听」，不能只认书面形式。

✍️ Text drills文字练习

Read each short exchange as if you heard it once, then answer. Each drill sits on a topic you can study on this site.把每段短对话当作只听一遍来读，再作答。每道题都挂在本站的一个主题上。

  1. Woman: “So, who's bringing the snacks on Saturday?” Man: “I can bring drinks. Can you get the snacks?” Woman: “Sure, I'll get chips and fruit.”
    What will the man bring? Neighbours and community邻里与社区
    Answer答案: Drinks
    The man offers drinks; the woman takes snacks. Do not swap A and B.男士带饮料，女士带零食。不要把两个人弄反。
  2. Man: “Do you want to go to the movie at seven?” Woman: “Seven is a bit early; I finish work at six thirty. Eight might be better.” Man: “Okay, eight it is.”
    What time will they go? Neighbours and community邻里与社区
    Answer答案: 8:00
    Seven is rejected and eight is agreed with “eight it is”. 6:30 is just when she finishes work.七点被否掉，「八点」被确认；6:30 只是她下班时间，是干扰项。
  3. Woman: “Could you finish the report by Monday?” Man: “I'll try, but I have the training on Friday.” Woman: “If it's too much, I can ask Sam to help.” Man: “Let's see how Thursday goes.”
    Does the man agree to finish the report by Monday? Talking at work职场沟通
    Answer答案: Not yet; he is unsure
    “I'll try” and “let's see” are not a firm yes. He is unsure.「我试试」「再看看」都不是肯定回答。他还没有把握。
  4. Man: “D'you wanna grab lunch?” Woman: “I'd love to, but I've gotta finish this first. Give me twenty minutes.”
    What does the woman say? Talking at work职场沟通
    Answer答案: She will go to lunch after finishing
    “I'd love to, but … give me twenty minutes” means yes, a bit later.「很想去，但……给我二十分钟」意思是晚一点去。
  5. Woman: “Our team is playing the final on Sunday.” Man: “Great! Is Ben coming back from his injury?” Woman: “The coach says maybe, but he doesn't look ready.”
    What will probably happen on Sunday? Neighbours and community邻里与社区
    Answer答案: Ben will probably not play
    Inference: “maybe” plus “doesn't look ready” points to Ben probably not playing.推理：「也许」加「看起来还没准备好」，说明 Ben 大概不会上场。

📏 Strategy for every part每个部分通用的做法

Before the audio音频开始前

  • Set up your notes before the audio starts.音频开始前先把笔记页分好格。
  • Read the introductory statement for context clues.先读一遍屏幕上的开场说明，里面有线索。

While you listen听的时候

  • Identify the topic. Is there a problem? What is the solution?先抓话题。有没有问题？解决办法是什么？
  • Use tone of voice for attitudes and opinions.用语气判断态度和观点。
  • Focus on what you understand, not on what you missed.盯着听懂的部分，别纠结漏掉的词。

After you listen听完之后

  • Match key words in the question to your notes.用题目里的关键词对笔记。
  • Answer every question. Wrong answers are not penalised.每题都要答，答错不扣分。
