What changes from level to level每升一级换了什么

  • 5–6 → 7–8 He has a big green sofa on his bike. The sofa is very big. → he has a huge green sofa on the back. It's tied to the bike with a rope, and it's almost as big as the bike! Content / Coherence内容与连贯
    Saying how it is tied on and how big it is next to the bike lets a listener who cannot see it picture it.说清怎么固定的、和车比有多大，对方看不到也能想象。
  • 5–6 → 7–8 The sofa is wet. The man is riding. → The sofa is getting wet, but he doesn't seem to care. Content / Coherence内容与连贯
    "But he doesn't seem to care" points to what is strange: his reaction.but he doesn't seem to care 点出了「怪」在哪里——人的反应。
  • 7–8 → 9+ There's a man riding a bicycle on a rainy street → There's a guy cycling down a rain-soaked street Vocabulary词汇
    "Cycling down" and "rain-soaked" are tighter and livelier.cycling down、rain-soaked 比 riding a bicycle on a rainy street 更紧凑生动。
  • 7–8 → 9+ The sofa is getting wet, but he doesn't seem to care. → The sofa is getting absolutely drenched, but he couldn't care less. Vocabulary词汇
    "Drenched" and "couldn't care less" are idiomatic intensifiers.drenched（湿透）、couldn't care less（毫不在乎）是地道强调说法。
  • 7–8 → 9+ The strangest part is the people. → What makes it even funnier is the reaction. Listenability好懂程度
    The "what" clause puts the focus at the end and leads into the next part.what 主语从句把重点放在句尾，引出下一个画面。
Notes on this prompt这道题的讲解
  • Start as if you are really on the phone. The other person can't see the picture, so say "what it is, what color, how it's tied".开头像真的打电话，对方看不见图，所以要说清「是什么、什么颜色、怎么绑的」。
  • "Almost as big as the bike" is a simple comparison that helps the other person "see" the picture in their mind.almost as big as the bike 是一个简单的比较，帮助对方在脑子里「看到」画面。
  • Common mistake: no ending. Task 8 is a phone scenario, so end with "I have to go" or "Talk to you later".常见错误：没有收尾。Task 8 是电话情景，最后要有 I have to go / Talk to you later。

📘 Template and phrases模板与常用句

Strategy策略

  • Set up the role-play first.先「搭场」：Hi Jill, you won't believe what I'm looking at…
  • Describe the unique parts you can describe well.描述最特别的部分，说你能说得好的。
  • Use common words if you lack a word; end the call at the close.缺词就用简单词拼：a big thing with wheels。结尾记得结束通话。

Tips提醒

  • Start by "making the call": Hi ..., it's me. End by hanging up: Talk to you later. The official strategy has both.开头要「打电话」：Hi …, it's me。结尾要挂电话：Talk to you later。官方策略里都有。
  • Don't get stuck on words you're not sure of: go around them with "a big round thing" or "something like a ...".不确定的词不要卡住：用 a big round thing / something like a … 绕过去。
  • Say the overall picture first, then the strangest part. Don't put equal effort everywhere.先说整体，再说最奇怪的部分，别平均用力。
