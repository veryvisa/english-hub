What changes from level to level每升一级换了什么

  • 5–6 → 7–8 A woman is buying apples. The seller is smiling. → a woman is buying apples from a seller. The seller is smiling, so they probably know each other. Content / Coherence内容与连贯
    Going one step past the smile (they probably know each other) adds interpretation.看到微笑后再推一步（可能认识），描述就有了「解读」。
  • 5–6 → 7–8 This picture is a farmers' market. It is sunny. → This picture shows a farmers' market on a sunny Saturday morning. In the front Content / Coherence内容与连贯
    One opening sentence gives place and time, then "In the front" marks location so the listener can follow.开头一句交代地点和时间，再用 In the front 标位置，听者能跟上。
  • 7–8 → 9+ He is trying a piece of cheese, and he looks like he likes it. → is sampling a piece of cheese, and he looks rather impressed Vocabulary词汇
    "Sampling" and "rather impressed" are more precise than "trying" and "likes it".sample（试吃）、rather impressed 比 trying、likes it 更精准。
  • 7–8 → 9+ She seems really happy. → beaming from ear to ear Vocabulary词汇
    "Beaming from ear to ear" is a vivid set phrase.beam from ear to ear（笑得合不拢嘴）是生动的固定说法。
  • 7–8 → 9+ Next to a table of flowers, a dog is waiting quietly. Maybe he is waiting for his owner. → while a dog waits patiently next to a table piled high with flowers, presumably for its owner to finish shopping Listenability好懂程度
    "While" links two subjects in one sentence; "piled high with" and "presumably" sharpen the detail and the guess.while 把两个人物连成一句，piled high with、presumably 让细节和推测都更精确。
Notes on this prompt这道题的讲解
  • Start with one overall sentence (place and time), then foreground, nearby, right side and background. The position words keep the order clear.先一句总体（地点+时间），再前景、旁边、右边、背景，位置词把顺序排得很清楚。
  • "The seller is smiling, so they probably know each other" is a reasonable small guess. It earns points and is not risky.the seller is smiling, so they probably know each other 是合理的小推测，加分又不危险。
  • Common mistake: repeating "there is / there are" all the time. Switch to continuous forms like "is holding / is buying" to sound more like you're describing a picture.常见错误：一直重复 there is / there are。换成 is holding / is buying 等进行时，更像在描述画面。

What changes from level to level每升一级换了什么

  • 5–6 → 7–8 She has a coffee and a phone. → She is holding a coffee and looking at her phone. Maybe she is checking when the bus will come. Content / Coherence内容与连贯
    Saying what she is doing (present continuous) and guessing why says more than listing objects.用进行时说她在做什么，再猜原因，比列物品有内容。
  • 5–6 → 7–8 His mother is holding his hand. → His mother is holding his hand, and she looks a bit tired. Content / Coherence内容与连贯
    Adding the mother's expression completes the picture.给人物加一个表情，画面更完整。
  • 7–8 → 9+ an older man is standing under a black umbrella. He looks a little cold → an older man is sheltering under a large black umbrella, his shoulders hunched against the cold Listenability好懂程度
    "His shoulders hunched" is an absolute phrase that shows cold through posture.his shoulders hunched 是独立主格结构，用一个姿势表现「冷」。
  • 7–8 → 9+ a boy is jumping in a puddle → a little boy in red rubber boots who is gleefully stomping in a puddle Vocabulary词汇
    "Stomping" and "gleefully" give the action sound and feeling.stomp（用力踩）、gleefully 让动作有声音和情绪。
  • 7–8 → 9+ she looks a bit tired → looks somewhere between amused and exhausted Vocabulary词汇
    "Somewhere between amused and exhausted" captures a mixed expression: a subtle high-level move.somewhere between A and B 描写复杂表情，是高分段的细腻用法。
Notes on this prompt这道题的讲解
  • Clothing color is the safest detail (yellow raincoat, red boots). For each person, say what they wear, what they do and their expression.衣服颜色是最安全的细节（yellow raincoat, red boots），每个人说「穿什么、做什么、什么表情」三样就够了。
  • "Maybe she is ..." and "He looks ..." are common uncertain phrases for describing pictures.Maybe she is … / He looks … 都是描述图片常用的不确定语气。
  • Common mistake: saying "she wears" (habit). Use "she is wearing" (right now).常见错误：说 she wears（习惯），应该用 she is wearing（此刻）。

    "Groaning under the weight" is a playful figure of speech for lots of food.groaning under the weight（被压得吱吱响）是夸张的比喻，形容食物多。
  • 7–8 → 9+ They seem to be having a great time. → clearly having the time of their lives Vocabulary词汇
    "Having the time of their lives" is the idiom for great fun.have the time of their lives 是「玩得特别开心」的习语。
  • 7–8 → 9+ In the background, there are lights hanging between the houses. → strings of lights are draped between the houses, giving the whole street a warm, festive glow Listenability好懂程度
    The "giving ..." participle phrase adds the effect, so one sentence holds both object and mood.giving … 分词短语说明效果，一句话里有事物也有气氛。
Notes on this prompt这道题的讲解
  • The mood of the party picture is "happy and lively". Say it at both the start and the end (warm and friendly).聚会图的情绪是「开心、热闹」，开头和结尾都点明（warm and friendly）。
  • The "long table" is the object that ties the whole scene together. Talk about people and food around it, and you won't get lost.long table 是把全场串起来的物件，围绕它说人和食物，不容易乱。
  • Common mistake: mispronouncing "barbecue". If you can't say it, say "grill" or "cooking outside".常见错误：barbecue 读错。不会读时说 grill 或 cooking outside。

What changes from level to level每升一级换了什么

  • 5–6 → 7–8 He is looking at his watch. He is in a hurry. → He keeps looking at his watch, so he is probably in a hurry. Listenability好懂程度
    "Keeps" shows repetition, and "so" links the action to the conclusion.keeps 表示反复，so 把动作和推论连起来。
  • 5–6 → 7–8 A child is taking a candy. → a child is grabbing a candy from the rack, and I think his father didn't see it Content / Coherence内容与连贯
    "Grabbing" is livelier than "taking", and one more plot point (dad didn't see) is added.grab 比 take 生动，再补一个情节（爸爸没看见）。
  • 7–8 → 9+ a man has a full cart → a man with an overflowing cart keeps checking his watch and shifting from foot to foot Vocabulary词汇
    "Overflowing" and "shifting from foot to foot" show impatience through detail.overflowing、shifting from foot to foot 用细节表现「急」。
  • 7–8 → 9+ a child is grabbing a candy from the rack → a young child has seized the moment and is sneakily reaching for a candy bar Vocabulary词汇
    "Seized the moment" and "sneakily" add motive and attitude to the action.seize the moment（趁机）、sneakily 给动作加了动机和态度。
  • 7–8 → 9+ On the sign in lane three, it says cash only. → a sign over lane three reads "cash only", which could explain why that lane looks empty Content / Coherence内容与连贯
    The "which" clause links the sign to the rest of the scene and makes a reasonable inference.which 从句把一个细节和画面里的其他信息联系起来，做出合理推断。
Notes on this prompt这道题的讲解
  • The words on the sign (cash only) are also information in the picture. Reading them out shows you noticed the details.牌子上的字（cash only）也是图里的信息，读出来说明你看到了细节。
  • "He might be thinking, '...'" uses imagined words to describe feelings. It is a very vivid spoken technique.He might be thinking, "…" 用想象的话来描述情绪，是很生动的口语技巧。

CELPIP 5–6 · ≈B1
  • Lists people and things (There is..., A man is...); the listener knows what is there but cannot picture where or the mood.一个个列出人和物（There is…, A man is…），听者知道有什么，但很难想象位置和气氛。
  • Few location words, almost no guessing.很少用方位词，几乎没有推测。
  • Ends with "It is nice".结尾一句 It is nice。
CELPIP 7–8 · ≈B2
  • Sets the scene, then guides the listener: in the front, next to her, in the background.先交代场景，再用 In the front / Next to her / In the background 带着听者看。
  • Each person has an action and an expression, with some guesses (probably, maybe).每个人物有动作＋表情，偶尔推测（probably、maybe）。
  • Ends by summing up the mood.结尾概括全图气氛。
CELPIP 9+ · ≈C1
  • Precise verbs (scramble, sample, clutch) and a detail instead of an adjective (nose pressed to the glass = wanting).动作用精准动词（scramble、sample、clutch），用一个细节代替形容词（鼻子贴着玻璃＝很想要）。
  • While, who and which clauses join two people or details in one sentence.用 while、who、which 从句把两个人或细节连成一句。
  • Names the focus or point of tension, and, like the official Level 9 sample, counts and locates before describing.点出全图的「焦点」或「紧张点」，像官方 9 级样本那样先数、先定位，再描述。

Why people get stuck at 7为什么常卡在 7 级

  • Tries to mention everything, half a sentence each, so nothing is clear.想描述所有东西，每样只说半句，结果哪样都不清楚。
  • Only says what is seen, never what it suggests.只说看到的，不说「这说明什么」。
  • Always "is + -ing"; no variety.一直用 is + -ing，句型没有变化。

Three moves to 9+冲 9 级以上的三步

  • In the 30 seconds of prep, pick four focus points and order them by location.准备的 30 秒里选 4 个重点，按位置排好顺序。
  • For each person give action, one detail, one guess.每个人物说「动作＋一个细节＋一个推测」。
  • Use while or who at least once to join two parts of the picture.至少用一次 while 或 who 把两个画面连起来。

📘 Template and phrases模板与常用句

Strategy策略

  • Start with a general statement, then pick some details.先说总体（这是一个……的场景），再挑几个细节。
  • Use positions to stay organized.按位置说：in the front / on the left / in the background。
  • Appearance, actions and feelings; use simple words if you lack a word.人物的外貌、动作、感受都可以说；不会的词用简单词绕开。

Tips提醒

  • You don't need to describe everything: pick 4 to 5 details you can say well. Saying them clearly matters more than saying them all.不用说完所有东西：挑 4–5 个你能说好的细节，说清楚比说全更重要。
  • Walk around by position (foreground, left and right, background), so you won't miss anything or get messy.按位置走一圈（前景 → 左右 → 背景），不会漏也不会乱。
  • For objects you don't know, go around with simple words: "a thing you use to carry food".看不懂的物品用简单词绕：a thing you use to carry food。
