Part 4 · News Item 第 4 部分 · 新闻播报

CELPIP 5–6 · ≈B1CELPIP 7–8 · ≈B2CELPIP 9+ · ≈C15 questions题 · about 5 min

🎧 What it tests考什么
One speaker, about 1.5 minutes, in the style of a radio or TV news report about an everyday situation: an unexpected local event, new technology that helps people, or an interesting animal encounter. Formal, factual, descriptive.一位播音员，约 1.5 分钟，像电台或电视新闻，讲日常相关的事：本地的意外事件、帮到人的新技术、有趣的动物故事。正式、客观、描述性。

Sentence completion. All questions appear on the same screen and you have a set amount of time for all of them. You can answer in any order, so watch the clock and do the sure ones first.句子补全题。所有题目同一屏出现，一共给一段固定时间，可以任意顺序作答。所以要看时间，先做有把握的。

⚠️ Traps in this part这一部分的陷阱

  • Full name, then last name only先报全名，后只说姓
    A person is introduced as “Maria Lopez”, then called “Lopez”. Write the last name so you can follow the story.新闻里先说「Maria Lopez」，之后只说「Lopez」。记姓，才能跟上故事。
  • 5 W's五个 W
    Who, what, when, where, why. Write one line for each as you hear it.who、what、when、where、why：边听边各记一行。
  • Numbers and times数字和时间
    News is full of dates and amounts. Note them with their meaning.新闻里日期和数字很多，连同含义一起记。
  • Time management时间分配
    All questions share one clock. Answer the easy ones first and guess the rest. Never leave blanks.所有题共用一个计时。先做容易的，其余猜。不要留空。

✍️ Text drills文字练习

Read each short exchange as if you heard it once, then answer. Each drill sits on a topic you can study on this site.把每段短对话当作只听一遍来读，再作答。每道题都挂在本站的一个主题上。

  1. Reporter: “A rescue team in Kelowna saved a dog that had been stuck on an ice patch for six hours. Firefighter Dana Reyes said the dog was cold but healthy. Reyes added that the owner had been looking for it since morning.”
    Who said the dog was healthy? Neighbours and community邻里与社区
    Answer答案: Dana Reyes
    A name introduced in full and then used as “Reyes”: Firefighter Dana Reyes said it.全名 Dana Reyes 后面简称 Reyes，是消防员说的。
  2. Reporter: “The city will open a new bike lane on Main Street next Monday. The lane, which cost about two million dollars, will run for three kilometres.”
    The bike lane will be … Cities and the cost of housing城市与住房成本
    Answer答案: open next Monday
    Open next Monday. Cost is two million and length three kilometres.下周一开放。造价约两百万，长三公里。
  3. Reporter: “A new app helps seniors find free community events near their homes. Developer Ken Wu said that more than 5,000 people had already downloaded it in the first week.”
    What is the app for? Neighbours and community邻里与社区
    Answer答案: Finding free community events
    General meaning: the app helps seniors find free events.大意题：App 帮老年人找免费社区活动。
  4. Reporter: “On Tuesday, a fox walked into a coffee shop in Victoria and sat near the counter for ten minutes before leaving. Staff say nobody was hurt.”
    What happened on Tuesday? Neighbours and community邻里与社区
    Answer答案: A fox entered a coffee shop
    Specific information: where and what happened.细节题：地点和发生的事。
  5. Reporter: “Organizers expected about 200 runners, but nearly 500 showed up for the Sunday race, which surprised everyone.”
    The organizers were ___. Exercise and ageing well运动与健康老去
    Answer答案: surprised
    “Which surprised everyone”. The reporter states the feeling directly.原话「让大家都很惊讶」，直接给出了感受。

📏 Strategy for every part每个部分通用的做法

Before the audio音频开始前

  • Set up your notes before the audio starts.音频开始前先把笔记页分好格。
  • Read the introductory statement for context clues.先读一遍屏幕上的开场说明，里面有线索。

While you listen听的时候

  • Identify the topic. Is there a problem? What is the solution?先抓话题。有没有问题？解决办法是什么？
  • Use tone of voice for attitudes and opinions.用语气判断态度和观点。
  • Focus on what you understand, not on what you missed.盯着听懂的部分，别纠结漏掉的词。

After you listen听完之后

  • Match key words in the question to your notes.用题目里的关键词对笔记。
  • Answer every question. Wrong answers are not penalised.每题都要答，答错不扣分。
