Part 5 · Discussion 第 5 部分 · 三人讨论（视频）

CELPIP 5–6 · ≈B1CELPIP 7–8 · ≈B2CELPIP 9+ · ≈C18 questions题 · about 9 min

🎧 What it tests考什么
A video of about 1.5 to 2 minutes with 3 speakers having a relatively informal discussion: teammates deciding where and when to hold a tournament, neighbours planning a block party, classmates dividing a project. It includes facts, opinions and emotions, and the speakers sometimes disagree.一段约 1.5–2 分钟的视频，3 个人比较随意地讨论：队友定比赛时间地点、邻居筹备街区派对、同学分工做项目。有事实、观点和情绪，说话人有时意见不同。

Sentence completion; about 8 questions on one screen with a set time for all. A good pace is roughly 30 seconds per question. Faces and body language help you judge feelings, but you are not tested on irrelevant visual details such as shirt colour.句子补全；约 8 题同屏，共用一段时间，大约每题 30 秒。表情和肢体语言能帮你判断感受，但不会考无关的画面细节（比如衣服颜色）。

⚠️ Traps in this part这一部分的陷阱

  • Label three speakers给三个说话人标记
    Write their positions on screen (left, middle, right) when they are introduced. Then note one opinion for each.他们出场时，按画面位置（左、中、右）标好，再各记一条观点。
  • Who agrees with whom谁同意谁
    Questions ask what two speakers agree about, or who disagrees. Use arrows: A → B (agree) / A ≠ C.题目会问两个人同意什么、谁不同意。用箭头记：A→B（同意）、A≠C。
  • Opinion changes观点会变
    A speaker may start against an idea and finish for it. Mark changes with “→”.有人一开始反对，后来被说服。用「→」记下变化。
  • Pacing控制节奏
    About 30 seconds per question. Skip a hard one and return; you can answer in any order.每题大约 30 秒。难题先跳过，回头再做；可以任意顺序。

✍️ Text drills文字练习

Read each short exchange as if you heard it once, then answer. Each drill sits on a topic you can study on this site.把每段短对话当作只听一遍来读，再作答。每道题都挂在本站的一个主题上。

  1. Ana: “I think we should hold the party on Saturday.” Ben: “Saturday is when most people go shopping. Sunday is better.” Carl: “I agree with Ben. And Sunday gives us time to prepare.”
    Who agrees with Ben? Neighbours and community邻里与社区
    Answer答案: Carl
    Carl says “I agree with Ben”. Ana proposed Saturday.Carl 说「我同意 Ben」。Ana 提的是周六。
  2. Dee: “I don't want to do the budget; numbers stress me out.” Eli: “I can do it, but then you write the report.” Dee: “Deal.”
    What will Dee do? Talking at work职场沟通
    Answer答案: The report
    They exchange tasks: Eli budget, Dee report.两人互换任务：Eli 做预算，Dee 写报告。
  3. Fay: “A tournament in May is too early; the field is still wet.” Gus: “It's usually dry by then.” Fay: “Hmm, you may be right. Let's check the forecast.”
    How does Fay's opinion change? Neighbours and community邻里与社区
    Answer答案: She becomes open to May
    From “too early” to “you may be right”: she is open to it.从「太早」到「你可能是对的」：她松口了。
  4. Hana: “We need volunteers for the food table.” Ian: “I'm busy that day.” Jo: “Same here, but I could come for an hour.”
    Which statement is true? Neighbours and community邻里与社区
    Answer答案: Jo might volunteer for a short time
    Jo says “I could come for an hour”. Ian is busy.Jo 说「可以来一小时」。Ian 有事。
  5. Kim: “We can't afford a band.” Lee: “What about a playlist?” Max: “That's actually a great idea. It's cheaper and everyone gets to choose a song.”
    What do Lee and Max suggest? Neighbours and community邻里与社区
    Answer答案: Using a playlist
    General meaning: they choose a playlist instead of a band.大意：用歌单代替乐队。

📏 Strategy for every part每个部分通用的做法

Before the audio音频开始前

  • Set up your notes before the audio starts.音频开始前先把笔记页分好格。
  • Read the introductory statement for context clues.先读一遍屏幕上的开场说明，里面有线索。

While you listen听的时候

  • Identify the topic. Is there a problem? What is the solution?先抓话题。有没有问题？解决办法是什么？
  • Use tone of voice for attitudes and opinions.用语气判断态度和观点。
  • Focus on what you understand, not on what you missed.盯着听懂的部分，别纠结漏掉的词。

After you listen听完之后

  • Match key words in the question to your notes.用题目里的关键词对笔记。
  • Answer every question. Wrong answers are not penalised.每题都要答，答错不扣分。
