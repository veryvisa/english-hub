CELPIP: the ruler for what you learn here思培：检验你学到了什么的尺子

You can read a topic text and know every chunk in it, and still freeze when a timer starts and you have sixty seconds to give advice. The CELPIP speaking and listening tasks are that moment, in a form you can repeat. So at the end of each week, take the same-topic task: the words you studied should come out in your answer, and the listening questions should feel easier.一篇主题文章你能读懂、里面的词块都认得，可计时器一响、要你六十秒内给出建议，还是会卡住。思培的口语和听力任务就是那个时刻，而且可以反复练。所以每周收口时，做同主题的任务：这周学的词应该出现在你的回答里，听力题也应该变得更容易。
