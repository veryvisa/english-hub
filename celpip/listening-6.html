Part 6 · Listening for Viewpoints 第 6 部分 · 听取观点

CELPIP 5–6 · ≈B1CELPIP 7–8 · ≈B2CELPIP 9+ · ≈C16 questions题 · about 8 min

🎧 What it tests考什么
One speaker for about 3 minutes giving a formal, prepared talk: a speech about climate change, the effects of a technology, or a community issue with several proposed solutions. It includes concepts, possibilities and analysis, and uses less common, more specialised language.一位说话人，约 3 分钟，正式、事先准备好的发言：比如气候变化演讲、某项技术的影响、一个社区问题和几个解决方案。有概念、可能性和分析，用词较少见、较专业。

Sentence completion; about 6 questions on one screen with a set time. The last part of the Listening test, and the hardest. Answer every question; guess if you have to.句子补全；约 6 题同屏共用一段时间。这是听力最后一部分，也最难。每题都要答，不会就猜。

⚠️ Traps in this part这一部分的陷阱

  • Whose opinion is it?这是谁的观点？
    The speaker reports other people's opinions. Write each name with a one-line summary of their view and reason.说话人会转述别人的观点。每个人名后面写一句话观点和理由。
  • Hedging language含蓄措辞
    “Some argue”, “it may be”, “in theory”. These are not facts. Questions test the exact strength.「有人认为」「可能」「理论上」不是事实。题目会考语气强弱。
  • Hard words生词
    You cannot look them up. Decide if the word is positive or negative from context, and move on.不能查词典。用上下文判断褒贬，然后继续听。
  • Summary at the end结尾的总结
    The speaker often repeats the main point at the end. Do not stop listening.说话人常在结尾再说一次要点，别提前松懈。

✍️ Text drills文字练习

Read each short exchange as if you heard it once, then answer. Each drill sits on a topic you can study on this site.把每段短对话当作只听一遍来读，再作答。每道题都挂在本站的一个主题上。

  1. Speaker: “Councillor Mehta believes the new parking fee will reduce traffic. However, the business owner Mr. Tran argues that it will hurt small shops.”
    Who is worried about small shops? Cities and the cost of housing城市与住房成本
    Answer答案: Mr. Tran
    Mr. Tran argues it will hurt small shops. Mehta thinks it reduces traffic.Tran 认为会伤害小商店；Mehta 认为减少交通。
  2. Speaker: “Some experts suggest that remote work could, in theory, lower city rents, though the evidence is still limited.”
    Which statement is closest? Cities and the cost of housing城市与住房成本
    Answer答案: Remote work might lower rents, but proof is limited.
    “Some experts suggest”, “could”, “in theory”, and “evidence is limited” all weaken the claim.「有专家认为」「可能」「理论上」「证据有限」都在削弱结论。
  3. Speaker: “The mayor's plan is ambitious, but its costs are staggering.”
    “Staggering” most likely means ___. Cities and the cost of housing城市与住房成本
    Answer答案: very large
    Context: “but” signals a negative. Costs that are “staggering” are huge.语境：「but」引出负面，「staggering」说明费用很大。
  4. Speaker: “Dr. Ibarra proposes planting more trees. Dr. Cole prefers building shaded shelters. In the end, I think both are needed.”
    What does the speaker conclude? Environment and climate环境与气候
    Answer答案: Both ideas are needed
    The conclusion, at the end: “both are needed”.结论在最后：「两者都需要」。
  5. Speaker: “A small group of residents oppose the plan. However, a recent survey found that most people support it.”
    What does the survey show? Cities and the cost of housing城市与住房成本
    Answer答案: Most people support it
    “However” brings the opposite of the small group: most support.「however」转折：多数人支持，与那小群人相反。

📏 Strategy for every part每个部分通用的做法

Before the audio音频开始前

  • Set up your notes before the audio starts.音频开始前先把笔记页分好格。
  • Read the introductory statement for context clues.先读一遍屏幕上的开场说明，里面有线索。

While you listen听的时候

  • Identify the topic. Is there a problem? What is the solution?先抓话题。有没有问题？解决办法是什么？
  • Use tone of voice for attitudes and opinions.用语气判断态度和观点。
  • Focus on what you understand, not on what you missed.盯着听懂的部分，别纠结漏掉的词。

After you listen听完之后

  • Match key words in the question to your notes.用题目里的关键词对笔记。
  • Answer every question. Wrong answers are not penalised.每题都要答，答错不扣分。
