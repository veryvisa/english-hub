About this site关于本站

You follow the meeting until three people talk at once. Your email has no mistakes in it, and the reply still sounds a little cold. The distance between B2 and C1 is made of moments like these.

一个人说话时听得懂，三个人一起说就跟丢了；邮件里没有语法错误，对方的回复却有些冷淡。从 B2 到 C1 的距离，就是由这样的时刻组成的。

Who writes it谁写的

VeryVisa writes and maintains these pages for Chinese-speaking adults living and working abroad. The explanations, example sentences, articles and exercises are original. Open word lists are credited on the Books page.

VeryVisa（微出国）为在海外生活和工作的华人撰写并维护本站。讲解、例句、文章和练习都是原创；用到的开放词表在教材对照页署名。

Who it suits适合谁

Adults who already read everyday English and can hold a conversation, roughly B2 or CELPIP 7–8, and want more words, better control of grammar and clearer speech. Beginners will find it too dense.

日常英语读得懂、能对话的成年人，大致在 B2 或思培 7–8 分，想要更多的词块、更有把握的语法和更清楚的发音。零基础的读者会觉得太密。

How to use it怎么用

Set aside forty minutes and stay with one topic: review what is due, read the main article, learn one point from it, read a second article, then speak on the same subject. A topic lasts about a week. Your record stays in this browser; export a backup before changing devices.

每天留出四十分钟，守住一个主题：先复习到期的内容，读主文，学文中的一个知识点，再读一篇同主题文章，最后就这个话题开口说。一个主题大约走一周。学习记录只存在这个浏览器里，换设备前请先导出备份。
