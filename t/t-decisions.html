🎙 Speak on this topic同主题口语任务 Task 1 · Giving advice · 90 s

A friend has two job offers. One pays more but means a long commute; the other is closer to home and more interesting but less secure. She has been going back and forth for a week. Give her advice on how to make the decision, not just which job to take.朋友手上有两个工作机会：一个钱多但通勤远，另一个离家近、更有意思，但不太稳定。她已经反复纠结了一周。给她建议：怎么做这个决定，而不只是选哪个。
