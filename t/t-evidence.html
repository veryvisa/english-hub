🎙 Speak on this topic同主题口语任务 Task 3 · Describing a community health fair · 60 s

Imagine you are walking through a community health fair in a school gym. Some tables show posters about vaccines and hand-washing, a nurse is answering questions, and a small group is reading a printed summary of a study. Describe the scene in detail: what you see, what people are doing, and how the fair helps visitors judge which health information they can trust.想象你走进学校体育馆里的一场社区健康展。有些桌子上摆着关于疫苗和洗手的海报，一位护士在回答问题，一小群人正在阅读一份研究摘要。请详细描述这个场景：你看到了什么，人们在做什么，以及这场展览如何帮助参观者判断哪些健康信息值得信赖。
