🎙 Speak on this topic同主题口语任务 Task 4 · Choosing between two homes · 90 s

You are choosing between two places to live: a small apartment close to your workplace in the city centre, and a larger townhouse in a quiet suburb with a longer commute. Explain which home you would choose and why. Compare the two options, mention at least two things that matter to you (for example cost, space, transport or neighbours), and give reasons to support your decision.你正在两处住所之间做选择：一处是靠近市中心工作地点的小公寓，另一处是安静郊区里更大的联排屋，但通勤更远。请说明你会选择哪一处，并解释原因。请比较两种选择，提到至少两个对你重要的因素（例如费用、空间、交通或邻居），并给出支持你决定的理由。
