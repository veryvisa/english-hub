Some people say ordinary people should be able to settle small disputes, such as a broken fence or an unpaid repair bill, at a simple local office without a lawyer. Others say even small cases need a professional. What is your opinion? Give reasons and an example.有人认为，像篱笆坏了、修理费没付这样的小纠纷，普通人应该能去一个简单的本地机构自己解决，不用请律师；也有人认为再小的案子也需要专业人士。你怎么看？给出理由和一个例子。
