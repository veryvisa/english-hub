🎙 Speak on this topic同主题口语任务 Task 1 · Advising on a conflict · 90 s

A friend of yours is having a conflict with a roommate about shared chores and is about to have a serious talk with them. Give your friend advice on how to handle the conversation so that both sides feel respected and the problem gets solved. Explain your suggestions and give reasons for each one.你的一位朋友和室友因为分担家务的事产生了矛盾，马上要和对方认真谈一谈。请给朋友一些建议，说明怎样处理这次谈话，才能让双方都感到被尊重并解决问题。请解释你的每条建议并给出理由。
