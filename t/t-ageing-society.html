Some people think the government should pay for most of the care that older people need. Others believe families should take on this responsibility. Which view do you agree with more? Explain your opinion and give at least one example or reason to support it.有人认为政府应该承担老年人所需的大部分照护费用，也有人认为这应该是家庭的责任。你更同意哪种观点？请说明你的看法，并至少给出一个例子或理由。
