A friend is choosing between a small apartment close to the city centre and a larger home in a quiet suburb with a long commute. Which option would you recommend, and why? Give clear reasons and at least one example from your own life or from people you know.你的朋友正在两个住处之间做选择：一个是靠近市中心的小公寓，另一个是安静郊区里更大但通勤很远的房子。你会推荐哪一个？为什么？请给出清楚的理由，并至少举一个你自己或身边人的例子。
