🎙 Speak on this topic同主题口语任务 Task 3 · Describing an artistic experience · 60 s

Describe a piece of art that caught your attention. It could be a painting, sculpture, photograph, or another creative work. Explain what it looked like, what you found interesting about it, and how it made you feel. You should also explain why different people might have different opinions about the same artwork.描述一件吸引你注意的艺术作品。它可以是一幅画、一件雕塑、一张照片或其他创意作品。介绍它的外观、你觉得它有趣的地方，以及它带给你的感受。你还应该解释为什么不同的人可能会对同一件艺术作品有不同的看法。
