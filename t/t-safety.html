🎙 Speak on this topic同主题口语任务 Task 6 · Dealing with a difficult situation · 60 s

Your elderly father tells you that yesterday he read a code from a text message to a caller who said he was from the bank. He feels embarrassed and asks you not to make a fuss. Choose one: insist that you both call the bank right now, or first sit down with him and calmly explain how this kind of trick works. Talk to him.年迈的父亲告诉你，他昨天把短信里的验证码念给了一个自称银行的来电者。他觉得丢脸，叫你别大惊小怪。二选一：坚持现在就和他一起给银行打电话，或者先坐下来，平静地给他讲清这种骗局怎么运作。对父亲说。
