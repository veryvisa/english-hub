Your teenage cousin posts almost everything online: her school, where she is right now, photos of her house and her friends. Her parents asked you to talk to her. Give her advice about what to share and what to keep private, without sounding like a lecture.你十几岁的表妹几乎什么都往网上发：学校、此刻在哪、家里和朋友的照片。她父母请你跟她谈谈。给她建议：哪些可以分享、哪些该留着，别说成训话。
