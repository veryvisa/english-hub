🎙 Speak on this topic同主题口语任务 Task 4 · Explaining a cultural misunderstanding · 90 s

Describe a situation in which two people interpreted the same words differently because of their cultural backgrounds. Explain what caused the misunderstanding, how the people responded, and what you think we can learn from such experiences. You may use a personal experience, something you observed, or an imagined situation. Try to explain how language, tone, and cultural expectations can influence communication.描述一个情境：由于文化背景不同，两个人对同样的话产生了不同的理解。解释误解产生的原因、当事人如何应对，以及你认为我们可以从这类经历中学到什么。你可以讲述自己的经历、观察到的事情，也可以描述一个假设情境。尽量说明语言、语气和文化预期如何影响交流。
