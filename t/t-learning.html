🎙 Speak on this topic同主题口语任务 Task 3 · Explaining a learning experience · 90 s

Think about a study habit that has helped you become a better learner. Describe the habit, explain how you developed it, and give an example of how it has helped you at school or in your daily life. You should also explain why you would recommend this habit to another student.想一想一种帮助你成为更优秀学习者的学习习惯。描述这种习惯，解释你是如何养成它的，并举例说明它如何帮助你在学校或日常生活中取得进步。你还应该解释为什么会向其他学生推荐这种习惯。
