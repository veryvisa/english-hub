🎙 Speak on this topic同主题口语任务 Task 5 · Weighing both sides · 90 s

A friend has received a fine that he believes is unfair, and he is thinking about disputing it formally. He asks for your opinion. Explain whether you think he should challenge the decision or accept it. Give at least two reasons and describe what could go well or badly.你的朋友收到一张他认为不公平的罚单，正在考虑是否正式提出异议，并来征求你的意见。请说明你认为他应该申诉还是接受处罚，至少给出两个理由，并说明可能出现的好结果与坏结果。
