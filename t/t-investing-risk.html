🎙 Speak on this topic同主题口语任务 Task 5 · Choosing between two options · 90 s

Imagine you have some extra savings. You can keep them in a safe account that grows slowly, or put them into a riskier investment that could grow faster but could also lose value. Explain which option you would choose and give clear reasons for your choice.假设你有一笔额外的积蓄。你可以把钱存进增长缓慢但很安全的账户，也可以投入风险更高的投资，后者可能增长更快，但也可能亏损。请说明你会选择哪一种，并给出清楚的理由。
