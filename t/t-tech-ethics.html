A close friend is thinking about buying a smart speaker that is always listening for voice commands. They are excited about the convenience but nervous about privacy. Give your friend advice. Explain what you would suggest they check before buying, what risks they should keep in mind, and how they can enjoy the technology responsibly.你的一位好朋友打算买一个会一直监听语音指令的智能音箱。他既期待它带来的便利，又担心隐私问题。请给朋友一些建议：购买前应该查看什么，需要留意哪些风险，以及如何负责任地使用这项科技。
