🎙 Speak on this topic同主题口语任务 Task 8 · Describing an unusual situation · 60 s

In a science museum gift shop you find an unusual object: a glass ball the size of an orange, filled with clear liquid, with small coloured weights floating at different heights. A card says it tells the temperature. Call a friend who collects science gadgets and describe it in detail.你在科学博物馆的礼品店看到一样怪东西：橙子大小的玻璃球，装满透明液体，里面几个彩色小坠子浮在不同高度，卡片上说它能显示温度。给收集科学小玩意的朋友打电话，把它仔细描述一遍。
