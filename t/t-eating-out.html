🎙 Speak on this topic同主题口语任务 Task 3 · Describing a scene · 60 s

Imagine you are standing at the entrance of a busy restaurant at dinner time. Describe what you see: the people, the food, the staff and the atmosphere. Say what you like most about the place and whether you would choose to eat there.想象你正站在一家晚餐时段很热闹的餐厅门口。请描述你看到的情景：客人、食物、服务员和整体氛围。说说你最喜欢这个地方的什么，以及你是否愿意在这里用餐。
