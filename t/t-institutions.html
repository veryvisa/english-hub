🎙 Speak on this topic同主题口语任务 Task 3 · Explaining how an institution works · 90 s

Think about an institution you have interacted with, such as a school, library, workplace, or community organization. Describe how it handles requests or solves problems. Explain what makes its procedures effective or ineffective, and suggest one way it could build greater trust among the people it serves.想一想你曾经接触过的一个机构，例如学校、图书馆、工作单位或社区组织。描述它如何处理请求或解决问题。解释哪些因素使它的办事程序有效或无效，并提出一个能够帮助它赢得服务对象更多信任的改进建议。
