🎙 Speak on this topic同主题口语任务 Task 1 · Giving directions and travel advice · 90 s

Imagine your friend is visiting an unfamiliar city and needs to take a bus to a local attraction. Explain how they can plan their journey, find the correct bus stop, and ask for directions if they get lost. Give your friend some practical advice to make the trip easier and less stressful.假设你的朋友要去一座陌生的城市旅游，需要乘坐公交车前往一个当地景点。请向朋友解释如何规划行程、找到正确的公交车站，以及在迷路时如何向别人问路。给朋友一些实用建议，让这次出行更加轻松，减少不必要的压力。
