🎙 Speak on this topic同主题口语任务 Task 3 · Describing a leadership situation · 90 s

Imagine you are leading a team at work or in a community organisation. Your team members have different opinions about how to complete an important project. Some people are very confident and speak frequently, while others rarely express their ideas. Explain how you would encourage everyone to participate, manage disagreements, and help the team reach a shared goal. Describe the leadership qualities you would use and explain why they are important.假设你正在领导一个工作团队或社区组织。团队成员对于如何完成一项重要项目有不同意见。有些人非常自信，经常发表意见，而另一些人很少表达自己的想法。请说明你会如何鼓励每个人参与、处理意见分歧，并帮助团队实现共同目标。描述你会运用哪些领导能力，并解释为什么这些能力很重要。
