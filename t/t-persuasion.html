🎙 Speak on this topic同主题口语任务 Task 4 · Weighing a media message · 90 s

You see an online video in which a famous-looking presenter strongly recommends a product, but you suspect the video is more about selling than informing. Describe what you notice in the video, explain which techniques it uses to persuade viewers, and say whether you would trust it.你在网上看到一段视频，一位看起来很有名的主持人极力推荐某款产品，但你怀疑视频更多是在推销而不是提供信息。请描述你在视频中注意到的内容，解释它使用了哪些说服手段，并说明你是否会相信它。
