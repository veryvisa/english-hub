repertoire noun

US /ˈrɛpɚtwɑr/ UK /ˈrɛpətwɑː/ NAWL · band 1 · #1

all the pieces, skills or ways of behaving that a person or group has ready to use 全部曲目、剧目；（某人掌握的）全部技能、手法

Forms词形: repertoires

How it is used怎么用

The first meaning belongs to music and theatre, but in reports and lectures the word usually means the range of things someone is able to do: a repertoire of skills, strategies or responses.本义是音乐、戏剧里的「保留曲目、剧目」；在报告和讲座里更常指一个人会的「全套本事」：a repertoire of skills / strategies / responses。

You have something in your repertoire, and you add to, extend or broaden it.说某样本事「在」你的 repertoire 里用 in；要扩充它，用 add to、extend、broaden。

Test yourself先自测

After a year abroad she had added several polite ways of refusing to her _____.

在国外待了一年，她的「本领库」里多了好几种礼貌拒绝的说法。

After a year abroad she had added several polite ways of refusing to her repertoire.

Collocations搭配

  • a wide repertoire 广泛的曲目；多样的本领
  • a repertoire of skills 一整套技能
  • add to your repertoire 给自己的本领库添一样
  • broaden your repertoire 拓宽自己的曲目或本领
  • part of the repertoire 属于常备的那一套

Word family词族

  • repertory noun 轮演剧目（制）；库存

Examples例句 8

  • After a year abroad she had added several polite ways of refusing to her repertoire. 在国外待了一年，她的「本领库」里多了好几种礼貌拒绝的说法。
  • The choir has a wide repertoire, from folk songs to modern film music. 这个合唱团曲目很广，从民歌到现代电影配乐都唱。
  • Good teachers draw on a repertoire of techniques rather than a single method. 好老师用的是一整套手法，而不是单一的方法。
  • Small talk about the weather is part of the repertoire of anyone who works with customers. 聊天气这种寒暄，是每个跟客户打交道的人必备的一招。
  • Reading widely is the quickest way to broaden your repertoire of sentence patterns. 大量阅读是拓宽句型储备最快的办法。
  • Young children build up their repertoire of sounds long before they say a real word. 小孩在说出第一个真正的词之前，早就在积累自己会发的各种音了。

Where it comes from词源与派生

Borrowed from French répertoire, from Middle French repertoire, from Late Latin repertōrium (“an inventory, list, repertory”). Doublet of repertory.

Derived words派生词: subrepertoire, immunorepertoire, repertoreme
