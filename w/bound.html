bound adjective, verb, noun

US /baʊnd/ UK same相同 NAWL · band 1 · #35

certain to happen; forced by a rule or duty; travelling towards a place; as a plural noun, limits （be bound to）必定；受……约束的；开往……的；（复数名词）界限

Forms词形: bounds, bounded, bounding

How it is used怎么用

Four patterns do nearly all the work. Be bound to do something: certain to. Be bound by a rule or contract: required to follow it. Be bound for a place: on the way there. And the noun in the plural: out of bounds, within the bounds of reason, know no bounds.四个句型几乎包办了全部用法。be bound to do：必定。be bound by a rule / contract：受其约束。be bound for＋地点：正前往。名词用复数：out of bounds（禁止入内）、within the bounds of reason（在情理之内）、know no bounds（没有止境）。

Bound to is stronger than likely to and warmer than will certainly: you are bound to make mistakes at first.bound to 比 likely to 语气强，又比 will certainly 口气亲切：you are bound to make mistakes at first。

Examples例句 12

  • You are bound to feel tired in the first week of a new job. 新工作的头一个星期，你肯定会觉得累。
  • Both sides are bound by the terms of the agreement they signed. 双方都受自己所签协议条款的约束。
  • The train bound for the coast leaves from platform four. 开往海边的那趟火车从四号站台发车。
  • The kitchen is out of bounds to customers at all times. 厨房任何时候都不许顾客进入。
  • With that many people in one room, someone was bound to disagree. 一屋子那么多人，总会有人不同意。
  • Her patience with small children seems to know no bounds. 她对小孩子的耐心好像没有尽头。

Where it comes from词源与派生

From Middle English bound, bund and bounden, bunden, ibunden, ȝebunden, from Old English bund- and bunden, ġebunden respectively.

Derived words派生词: bounder, rebound, unbound, boundary, disbound, bound for, boundable, boundless, boundling, overbound, unbounded, boundsgoer, underbound, bound ahead
