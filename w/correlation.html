correlation noun

US /ˌkɔrəˈleɪʃən/ UK /ˌkɒrəˈleɪʃən/ NAWL · band 1 · #19

a connection between two things in which one tends to change as the other changes 相关性，相互关联

Forms词形: correlations

How it is used怎么用

The patterns are a correlation between A and B, and a correlation of A with B. A correlation is strong, weak, positive or negative; it is found or shown, never made.句型是 a correlation between A and B，或 a correlation of A with B。相关性可以 strong、weak、positive、negative；动词用 find、show，不用 make。

The sentence every reader of health news needs is correlation does not imply causation: two things can move together without one causing the other.读健康新闻的人都该记住 correlation does not imply causation：两件事一起变化，不等于一个导致另一个。

Collocations搭配

  • a correlation between ……之间的相关性
  • a strong correlation 很强的相关性
  • a positive correlation 正相关
  • show a correlation 显示出相关性
  • correlation does not imply causation 相关不等于因果

Word family词族

  • correlate verb 相关，有关联
  • correlated adjective 相关的
  • causation noun （对照）因果关系

Examples例句 8

  • There is a strong correlation between how much children read and how well they write. 孩子的阅读量和写作水平之间有很强的相关性。
  • The study found a correlation between long commutes and poor sleep. 这项研究发现通勤时间长和睡眠差之间存在关联。
  • A correlation does not prove that coffee causes the headaches. 有相关性并不能证明是咖啡引起了头痛。
  • The school survey found only a weak correlation between hours of homework and exam results. 学校的调查发现，作业时间和考试成绩之间只有微弱的相关性。
  • Ice cream sales and sunburn show a positive correlation because both rise in hot weather. 冰淇淋销量和晒伤呈正相关，因为两者都在天热时上升。
  • Before you share the headline, ask whether it describes a correlation or a cause. 转发这条标题之前，先问一句：它说的是相关还是因果？

Where it comes from词源与派生

From Middle French corrélation. Morphologically correlate + -ion.
