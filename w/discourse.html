discourse noun

US /ˈdɪskɔrs/ UK /ˈdɪskɔːs/ NAWL · band 1 · #25

serious talk or writing on a subject; language as it is used in whole conversations and texts 论述，话语；（公共）讨论；语篇

Forms词形: discourses, discoursed, discoursing

How it is used怎么用

Discourse is the formal word for the way a society talks about something: public discourse, political discourse, the discourse around immigration. It is uncountable in this sense.discourse 是个正式的词，指一个社会谈论某件事的方式：public discourse、the discourse around…。这个意思不可数。

In language study a discourse marker is a word such as well, anyway or however that shows how one part of what you say connects to the next.语言学里 discourse marker（话语标记）指 well、anyway、however 这类词，标明前后两段话怎么衔接。

Examples例句 11

  • Social media has changed the tone of public discourse. 社交媒体改变了公共讨论的语气。
  • Words like anyway and so are discourse markers that tell the listener where you are going. anyway、so 这类词是话语标记，告诉听的人你接下来要往哪儿说。
  • The discourse around ageing tends to focus on cost rather than on experience. 围绕老龄化的讨论，往往盯着成本而不是老年人的阅历。
  • New students have to learn the rules of academic discourse as well as the subject itself. 新生除了学专业本身，还得学会学术话语的规矩。
  • Insults lower the level of discourse and rarely change anyone's mind. 骂人只会拉低讨论的水平，也很少能改变谁的想法。
  • Her book examines how medical discourse describes pain. 她的书考察了医学话语是怎么描述疼痛的。

Where it comes from词源与派生

From Middle English discours, borrowed from Middle French discours (“conversation, speech”), from Latin discursus (“the act of running about”), from Latin discurrō (“run about”), from dis- (“apart”) + currō (“run”). Spelling modified by influence of Middle French cours (“course”).

Derived words派生词: discourser, discourseless, metadiscourse, cyberdiscourse, macrodiscourse, microdiscourse, counterdiscourse, direct discourse, discourse marker, discourse analyst, mode of discourse, discourse analysis, indirect discourse, sandwich discourse
