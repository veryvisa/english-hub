linear adjective

US /ˈlɪniɚ/ UK /ˈlɪniə/ NAWL · band 1 · #16

going in a straight line, or moving from one stage to the next in a fixed order 线性的；直线的；按部就班、一步接一步的

Forms词形: linears

How it is used怎么用

Outside mathematics the word is mostly used in the negative: progress is not linear, careers are no longer linear. It tells the listener to expect steps back as well as forward.数学之外，这个词多用在否定里：progress is not linear、careers are no longer linear，提醒对方「有进也会有退」。

The opposite is non-linear, written with a hyphen in British English and usually without one in American English.反义词是 non-linear，英式带连字符，美式通常写成 nonlinear。

Examples例句 8

  • Progress in a language is rarely linear; most learners hit a flat stretch in the middle. 学语言的进步很少是直线上升的，多数人到中段都会遇到一段平台期。
  • There is a roughly linear relationship between hours of practice and typing speed. 练习时数和打字速度之间大致是线性关系。
  • Few people now expect a linear career with one employer. 如今很少有人指望在一家单位一条道走到底。
  • The novel does not follow a linear plot, so the ending appears in the first chapter. 这部小说不是按时间顺序写的，所以结局出现在第一章。
  • Recovery after surgery is not linear, and a bad day does not mean you are getting worse. 术后恢复不是一条直线，某一天状态差并不代表情况在恶化。
  • Costs rise in a linear way at first and then far more steeply. 成本起初呈线性上升，之后涨得陡得多。
  • Writing an essay is not a linear process; you plan, draft and plan again. 写文章不是一步接一步的过程：你会先计划、起草，再回头重新计划。

Where it comes from词源与派生

Borrowed from Latin līneāris, from līnea (“line”) + -āris (adjectival suffix), equivalent to line + -ar. Doublet of lineal.

Derived words派生词: bilinear, colinear, linearly, collinear, isolinear, linear TV, linearise, linearism, linearity, linearize, linearoid, sublinear, trilinear, unilinear
