fluid noun, adjective

US /ˈfluɪd/ UK same相同 NAWL · band 1 · #33

a liquid; as an adjective, moving smoothly, or likely to change 液体，流体；（形容词）流畅的；不固定、易变的

Forms词形: fluids

How it is used怎么用

Doctors say fluids where everyone else says drinks: drink plenty of fluids. In that sense it is usually plural.别人说 drinks 的地方，医生说 fluids：drink plenty of fluids。这个意思通常用复数。

The adjective has two uses worth copying. A fluid movement is smooth and graceful. A fluid situation is one that could still change, a polite way of saying nobody knows yet.形容词有两个值得学的用法。a fluid movement 是流畅优美的动作；a fluid situation 是「还有变数」的局面，是「现在谁也说不准」的客气说法。

Examples例句 11

  • Drink plenty of fluids and rest for a couple of days. 多喝水，休息两天。
  • The situation is still fluid, so we will not confirm the date until Friday. 情况还有变数，所以要到周五我们才会确认日期。
  • The dancer's movements were so fluid that they looked effortless. 这位舞者的动作十分流畅，看上去毫不费力。
  • Check the brake fluid before a long drive in the mountains. 开长途山路之前，检查一下刹车油。
  • Plans for the merger remain fluid and may change again next week. 合并计划仍未定型，下周可能还会变。
  • Older people lose fluid quickly in hot weather and may not feel thirsty. 天热时老年人体内水分流失得快，而且可能并不觉得渴。

Where it comes from词源与派生

From Middle English fluid, from Latin fluidus (“flowing; fluid”), from Latin fluō (“to flow”), from Proto-Indo-European *bʰleh₁- (“to swell; surge; overflow; run”). Akin to Ancient Greek φλύειν (“to swell; overflow”).

Derived words派生词: biofluid, fluidism, fluidist, fluidram, geofluid, nonfluid, subfluid, cryofluid, fluidless, fluidlike, fog fluid, gyrofluid, nanofluid, serofluid
