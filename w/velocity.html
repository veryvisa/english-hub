velocity noun

US /vəˈlɑsəti/ UK /vəˈlɒsəti/ NAWL · band 1 · #23

the speed of something in a particular direction; great speed (formal) 速度（物理学中指有方向的速度）；高速

Forms词形: velocities

How it is used怎么用

In physics, speed says how fast and velocity says how fast and in which direction. In ordinary writing velocity is simply a formal, technical word for speed: wind velocity, at high velocity.物理学里 speed 只说快慢，velocity 既说快慢也说方向。普通文章里 velocity 就是 speed 的正式、技术性说法：wind velocity、at high velocity。

Do not use it for people or traffic in conversation. A car travels at high speed; a bullet travels at high velocity.口语里说人或车流不用这个词。汽车 at high speed，子弹 at high velocity。

Examples例句 9

  • The wind velocity on the bridge was high enough to close it to lorries. 桥上的风速高到必须禁止货车通行。
  • An object falling through the air soon reaches a constant velocity. 在空气中下落的物体很快就会达到匀速。
  • The velocity of the water doubles where the river narrows. 河道变窄的地方，水流速度翻了一倍。
  • Small stones thrown up at high velocity can crack a windscreen. 高速弹起的小石子能把挡风玻璃打裂。
  • The ball loses velocity quickly on wet grass. 球在湿草地上速度掉得很快。
  • Rumours gain velocity when nobody in charge says anything. 管事的人都不吭声的时候，谣言传得更快。

Where it comes from词源与派生

From Middle French vélocité, from Latin vēlōcitās (“speed”), from vēlōx (“fast”), thus a doublet of veloce.

Derived words派生词: isovelocity, subvelocity, equivelocity, low-velocity, four-velocity, high-velocity, pseudovelocity, quadrivelocity, velocity curve, velocity potential, velocity dispersion, intermediate-velocity, velocitize, velocimeter
