impact noun, verb

US /ˈɪmpækt/ UK same相同 NAWL · band 1 · #7

the strong effect that one thing has on another; also the force of one thing hitting another 影响，冲击；撞击（动词：对……产生影响）

Forms词形: impacts, impacted, impacting

How it is used怎么用

The noun takes on: have an impact on something. Writing impact to or impact for is one of the commonest slips in essays by Chinese speakers.名词后面接 on：have an impact on something。写成 impact to、impact for 是华人作文里最常见的错误之一。

The verb is stressed on the second syllable and takes a direct object in American English (the strike impacted services); many British writers still prefer affect or have an impact on.作动词时重音在第二个音节；美式用法直接接宾语（the strike impacted services），不少英国作者仍偏好 affect 或 have an impact on。

Test yourself先自测

Lack of sleep has a direct _____ on how well you remember new words.

睡眠不足会直接影响你记新词的效果。

Lack of sleep has a direct impact on how well you remember new words.

Collocations搭配

  • have an impact on 对……产生影响
  • a significant impact 重大影响
  • the environmental impact 对环境的影响
  • a lasting impact 持久的影响
  • reduce the impact of 减轻……的影响

Word family词族

  • impactful adjective 有影响力的（较新，正式文体慎用）

Examples例句 12

  • Lack of sleep has a direct impact on how well you remember new words. 睡眠不足会直接影响你记新词的效果。
  • The new bus route had little impact on traffic in the city centre. 新公交线路对市中心的交通几乎没什么影响。
  • We tried to reduce the impact of the noise by closing the windows. 我们把窗户关上，想减轻噪音的影响。
  • Her first teacher made a lasting impact on the way she reads. 她的启蒙老师对她的阅读方式产生了持久的影响。
  • The report measures the environmental impact of cheap flights. 这份报告评估了廉价航班对环境的影响。
  • Rising rents will impact students more than any other group. 房租上涨对学生的冲击会比对其他任何群体都大。
