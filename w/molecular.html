molecular adjective

US /məˈlɛkjəlɚ/ UK /məˈlɛkjʊlə/ NAWL · band 1 · #36

connected with molecules, the smallest units of a chemical substance 分子的

Forms词形: moleculars

How it is used怎么用

The word comes before a noun and names a level of detail: at the molecular level means looking at what the smallest parts of a substance are doing.这个词放在名词前，说的是观察的精细程度：at the molecular level 就是看一种物质最小的组成部分在干什么。

Stress shifts from the first syllable of molecule to the second syllable of molecular.molecule 的重音在第一个音节，到了 molecular 挪到第二个音节。

Examples例句 8

  • Cooking changes food at the molecular level, which is why a boiled egg cannot be unboiled. 烹饪在分子层面改变了食物，所以煮熟的鸡蛋变不回生的。
  • She works in a molecular biology laboratory at the university. 她在大学的一间分子生物学实验室工作。
  • The two sugars have the same atoms but a different molecular structure. 这两种糖的原子相同，分子结构却不一样。
  • A molecular test can detect the virus before any symptoms appear. 分子检测能在出现任何症状之前查出这种病毒。
  • Scientists are beginning to understand ageing at the molecular level. 科学家开始在分子层面理解衰老。
  • Water has a low molecular weight, so it evaporates easily. 水的分子量小，所以容易蒸发。
  • The label uses the phrase molecular formula to make an ordinary face cream sound advanced. 标签上用「分子配方」这个说法，让一瓶普通面霜听起来很高级。
