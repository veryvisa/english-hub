developmental adjective

US /dɪˌvɛləpˈmɛntəl/ UK same相同 NAWL · band 1 · #34

connected with the way a child, a skill or a system grows and changes over time 发育的；发展的，成长阶段的

Forms词形: developmentals

How it is used怎么用

Parents meet this word at the clinic and at school: a developmental stage, a developmental milestone, a developmental delay. It describes the normal order in which children gain skills, not how clever they are.家长在诊所和学校会碰到这个词：a developmental stage、a developmental milestone、a developmental delay。它说的是孩子获得各项能力的正常先后，不是聪明不聪明。

The main stress moves forward as the family grows: develop, development, developmental.这一族词越长，主重音越往后挪：develop、development、developmental。

Examples例句 8

  • Walking is a developmental milestone that most children reach at around one year. 走路是一个发育里程碑，多数孩子一岁左右就会走。
  • Saying no to everything is a normal developmental stage for a two-year-old. 什么都说「不」，是两岁孩子正常的发育阶段。
  • The nurse asked a few questions to check for any developmental delay. 护士问了几个问题，看看有没有发育迟缓的情况。
  • Good toys match a child's developmental needs rather than the age printed on the box. 好玩具对得上孩子的发展需要，而不是盒子上印的年龄。
  • She studied developmental psychology before she trained as a teacher. 她在接受教师培训之前学过发展心理学。
  • Errors such as goed are developmental; they show that the child has found a rule. 像 goed 这样的错误是发展过程中出现的，说明孩子已经摸到了一条规则。
  • Teenagers sleep late for developmental reasons, not simply because they are lazy. 青少年睡得晚有发育上的原因，并不只是因为懒。
