finite adjective

US /ˈfaɪnaɪt/ UK same相同 NAWL · band 1 · #26

having a limit or an end; in grammar, showing tense and subject 有限的；（语法）限定的

How it is used怎么用

Finite is the precise word for things that will run out: a finite resource, a finite number, finite time. Limited is the everyday word; finite says there is an end even if it is far away.finite 是说「会用完」的精确用词：a finite resource、a finite number、finite time。日常用 limited；finite 强调尽头是存在的，哪怕还很远。

The vowel changes in the opposite: finite has the sound of fine, but infinite is stressed on in and its last two vowels are weak.反义词的元音变了：finite 读如 fine，infinite 重音却在 in 上，后两个元音都弱读。

Examples例句 10

  • Attention is a finite resource, and every notification spends a little of it. 注意力是有限的资源，每一条通知都会花掉一点。
  • There is a finite number of seats, so book early. 座位数量有限，请尽早预订。
  • Oil is finite, which is one reason prices swing so sharply. 石油是有限的，这是油价大起大落的原因之一。
  • Accepting that your time is finite makes it easier to say no. 接受「时间有限」这件事，拒绝别人就容易多了。
  • Every English sentence needs at least one finite verb. 每个英语句子至少要有一个限定动词。
  • A household has a finite budget, however generous the offer looks. 不管优惠看上去多诱人，一个家庭的预算终归是有限的。

Where it comes from词源与派生

The adjective is derived from Middle English fynyte, finit, from Latin fīnītus, perfect passive participle of fīniō (“to finish; to terminate”), from fīnis (“boundary”). The word displaced Old English ġeendodlīċ.

Derived words派生词: cofinite, finitely, unfinite, nonfinite, profinite, finite set, finiteless, finiteness, metafinite, non-finite, semifinite, hyperfinite, quasifinite, superfinite
