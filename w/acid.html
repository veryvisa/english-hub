acid noun, adjective

US /ˈæsɪd/ UK same相同 NAWL · band 1 · #21

a chemical substance with a sour taste that can damage what it touches; as an adjective, sour or sharp 酸；（形容词）酸的，酸性的；尖刻的

Forms词形: acids

How it is used怎么用

In everyday reading the word turns up in food and health: stomach acid, fatty acids, amino acids, acid reflux. The adjective for things that contain acid is acidic.日常阅读里这个词多出现在饮食和健康话题：stomach acid、fatty acids、amino acids、acid reflux。「含酸的、酸性的」用形容词 acidic。

An acid test is a figurative phrase: the one trial that shows whether something really works.acid test 是比喻说法：能看出某样东西到底行不行的那一次决定性检验。

Examples例句 12

  • Too much coffee on an empty stomach can increase stomach acid. 空腹喝太多咖啡会让胃酸增多。
  • Lemon juice is acid enough to stop sliced apples going brown. 柠檬汁的酸度足以防止切开的苹果变色。
  • The acid in fizzy drinks slowly wears away the surface of your teeth. 汽水里的酸会慢慢侵蚀牙齿表面。
  • Oily fish is a good source of the fatty acids the body cannot make. 多脂鱼富含人体无法自行合成的脂肪酸。
  • The acid test of a language course is whether you can order a meal afterwards. 检验一门语言课的硬标准，是学完以后你能不能点一顿饭。
  • Acid rain damaged forests and stone buildings across the region for decades. 几十年间，酸雨损害了这一地区的森林和石头建筑。

Where it comes from词源与派生

From French acide, from Latin acidus (“sour, acid”), from aceō (“to be sour”). Doublet of agita.

Derived words派生词: acidic, acidly, acidify, acidity, acidhead, acidlike, acidosis, acid jazz, acid rain, acid test, acid-fast, acidulate, acidulous, drop acid
