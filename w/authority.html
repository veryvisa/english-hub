authority noun

US /əˈθɔrəti/ UK /ɔːˈθɒrəti/ NAWL · band 1 · #11

the power to give orders and make decisions; an official body; a person who knows a subject very well 权力，职权；（常用复数）当局，官方机构；权威人士

Forms词形: authorities

How it is used怎么用

Three meanings, three grammars. Uncountable for power: she has the authority to sign. Plural with the for officials: report it to the authorities. Countable with on for an expert: an authority on tropical plants.三个意思，三种语法。指「权力」不可数：she has the authority to sign；指「当局」用复数加 the：report it to the authorities；指「专家」可数，接 on：an authority on tropical plants。

A local authority is the British term for the council that runs a town or district.local authority 是英式说法，指管理一个城镇或地区的地方政府。

Examples例句 11

  • Only the head teacher has the authority to close the school for the day. 只有校长有权决定停课一天。
  • If the landlord refuses to repair the heating, you can complain to the local authority. 如果房东不肯修暖气，你可以向地方政府投诉。
  • The health authorities advised people to stay indoors during the smoke. 卫生部门建议大家在烟雾期间待在室内。
  • She is a leading authority on how children learn to read. 她是儿童阅读启蒙领域的顶尖权威。
  • He speaks with such authority that nobody thinks to check his facts. 他说话太有权威感，没有人想到要去核对他说的事实。
  • People in a position of authority should expect their decisions to be questioned. 身居要职的人，就该料到自己的决定会受到质疑。

Where it comes from词源与派生

From Middle English auctorite, autorite (“authority, book or quotation that settles an argument”), from Old French auctorité, from Latin stem of auctōritās (“invention, advice, opinion, influence, command”), from auctor (“master, leader, author”). For the presence of the h, compare the etymology of author.

Derived words派生词: coauthority, antiauthority, authorityless, authoritywise, tax authority, multiauthority, port authority, local authority, moral authority, authority figure, authority issues, postal authority, public authority, rudder authority
