ion noun

US /ˈaɪən/ UK same相同 NAWL · band 1 · #14

an atom or a group of atoms that carries an electric charge 离子（带电荷的原子或原子团）

Forms词形: ions

How it is used怎么用

Most people meet this word on a battery, not in a laboratory: lithium-ion is written with a hyphen when it stands before a noun.多数人是在电池上而不是在实验室里见到这个词的：lithium-ion 放在名词前要加连字符。

The word has two syllables. Keep it apart from iron, which is said like I-ern.这个词是两个音节，别和 iron（读作 /ˈaɪɚn/）混了。

Charging a phone moves lithium _____ from one side of the battery to the other.

给手机充电，就是把锂离子从电池的一侧移到另一侧。

Charging a phone moves lithium ions from one side of the battery to the other.

Examples例句 8

  • Charging a phone moves lithium ions from one side of the battery to the other. 给手机充电，就是把锂离子从电池的一侧移到另一侧。
  • When salt dissolves in water it breaks up into ions. 盐溶于水时会分解成离子。
  • An atom that loses an electron becomes a positive ion. 失去一个电子的原子会变成正离子。
  • The filter removes metal ions from the water by ion exchange. 这种过滤器通过离子交换去掉水里的金属离子。
  • Sports drinks replace some of the ions the body loses in sweat. 运动饮料能补充一部分身体随汗水流失的离子。
  • The label claims the device fills the room with negative ions, but offers no evidence that this helps. 标签上说这台设备能让房间充满负离子，却拿不出这样有好处的证据。
