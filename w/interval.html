interval noun

US /ˈɪntɚvəl/ UK /ˈɪntəvəl/ NAWL · band 1 · #24

a period of time or a space between two events or things; a short break in a play or concert 间隔，间隙；（戏剧、音乐会的）幕间休息；区间

Forms词形: intervals

How it is used怎么用

The phrase to own is at intervals: at regular intervals, at five-minute intervals, at intervals of ten metres. The preposition is at, and interval is plural.最该掌握的是 at intervals：at regular intervals、at five-minute intervals、at intervals of ten metres。介词用 at，interval 用复数。

In British English the break in the middle of a play is the interval; Americans call it the intermission.英式英语把演出中场休息叫 interval，美式叫 intermission。

Examples例句 11

  • Buses leave the station at ten-minute intervals during the day. 白天车站每隔十分钟发一班车。
  • Reviewing a word at longer and longer intervals fixes it in memory. 以越来越长的间隔复习一个词，能把它牢牢记住。
  • After an interval of five years, the two friends met again at a wedding. 时隔五年，两位朋友在一场婚礼上重逢。
  • We bought ice cream during the interval and missed the start of the second half. 我们在幕间休息时买了冰淇淋，结果错过了下半场的开头。
  • Trees were planted along the road at regular intervals. 路边每隔一段距离种了一棵树。
  • Interval training mixes short bursts of hard effort with periods of rest. 间歇训练是把短时间的高强度发力和休息穿插起来。

Where it comes from词源与派生

From Middle English interval, intervalle, from Old French intervalle, entreval, from Latin intervallum (“space between, interval, distance, interval of time, pause, difference; literally, space between two palisades or walls”), from inter (“between”) + vallum (“palisade, wall”).

Derived words派生词: intervaled, intervally, intervalize, intervallic, midinterval, QT interval, bit interval, even-interval, hyperinterval, interval time, time interval, unit interval, wolf interval, interval class
