distribution noun

US /ˌdɪstrəˈbjuʃən/ UK /ˌdɪstrɪˈbjuːʃən/ NAWL · band 1 · #3

the way something is shared out or spread over an area or a group; the act of delivering it 分布；分配；分发，配送

Forms词形: distributions

How it is used怎么用

Two ideas share this word: how something is spread (the distribution of wealth, of rainfall, of ages in a class) and the business of getting goods to people (a distribution centre).这个词有两层意思：一是「怎么分布」（财富、降雨、班里年龄的分布），二是把货送到人手里的「配送」（distribution centre 配送中心）。

The usual pattern is the distribution of A among, across or between B. Even, uneven, equal and fair are the adjectives that go with it.常见搭配是 the distribution of A among / across / between B；修饰它的形容词常是 even、uneven、equal、fair。

Examples例句 10

  • The map shows the distribution of rainfall across the province. 这张图显示了全省降雨的分布。
  • An uneven distribution of housework is a common cause of arguments at home. 家务分配不均是家里吵架的常见原因。
  • The charity handles the distribution of food parcels to families in need. 这家慈善机构负责把食品包分发给有需要的家庭。

  • Most of the delay happened at the distribution centre, not on the road. 耽搁主要出在配送中心，而不是在路上。
  • A fair distribution of tasks keeps a small team from burning out. 任务分得公平，小团队才不会累垮。

Where it comes from词源与派生

From Old French distribution, from Latin distributio, from distribuere (“to distribute”), itself from dis- (“apart”) + tribuere (“to assign, allot”) (from tribus). By surface analysis, distribute + -ion.

Derived words派生词: codistribution, distributional, e-distribution, F distribution, redistribution, t distribution, t-distribution, biodistribution, distributionism, distributionist, geodistribution, maldistribution, misdistribution, nondistribution
