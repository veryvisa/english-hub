stimulus noun

US /ˈstɪmjələs/ UK same相同 NAWL · band 1 · #27

something that causes a reaction or encourages activity and growth 刺激，刺激物；促进因素

Forms词形: stimuli

How it is used怎么用

The plural is stimuli, with the last syllable said like lie.复数是 stimuli，最后一个音节读如 lie。

In psychology a stimulus is anything the senses pick up: a visual stimulus, respond to a stimulus. In economics it is public spending meant to restart growth: a stimulus package. Elsewhere it means an encouragement, followed by to or for.心理学里 stimulus 是感官接收到的任何东西：a visual stimulus、respond to a stimulus。经济学里指为重启增长而投入的公共开支：a stimulus package。其他场合是「推动力」，后接 to 或 for。

Test yourself先自测

Babies respond to a visual _____, such as a face, within days of birth.

婴儿出生没几天，就会对人脸这样的视觉刺激有反应。

Babies respond to a visual stimulus, such as a face, within days of birth.

Collocations搭配

  • a stimulus package 经济刺激方案
  • respond to a stimulus 对刺激作出反应
  • a visual stimulus 视觉刺激
  • a stimulus to growth 对增长的推动
  • provide a stimulus for 为……提供推动力

Word family词族

  • stimuli noun （复数）刺激
  • stimulate verb 刺激，促进
  • stimulating adjective 启发人的，让人有劲的

Examples例句 9

  • Babies respond to a visual stimulus, such as a face, within days of birth. 婴儿出生没几天，就会对人脸这样的视觉刺激有反应。
  • The new railway was a stimulus to trade in the smaller towns. 新铁路推动了小城镇的贸易。
  • A deadline is the only stimulus some writers need. 对有些写作者来说，截稿日期就是唯一需要的动力。
  • The government announced a stimulus package to protect jobs. 政府宣布了一项保就业的经济刺激方案。
  • Our brains filter out most of the stimuli that reach them. 抵达大脑的刺激，大部分都被它过滤掉了。
  • Moving to a new city gave her the stimulus to start painting again. 搬到一座新城市，给了她重新拿起画笔的动力。
