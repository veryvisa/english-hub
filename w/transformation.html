transformation noun

US /ˌtrænsfɚˈmeɪʃən/ UK /ˌtrænsfəˈmeɪʃən/ NAWL · band 1 · #37

a complete change in the form, appearance or character of someone or something （彻底的）转变，变革；转化

Forms词形: transformations

How it is used怎么用

Change can be small; a transformation is complete. Save the word for cases where the thing afterwards is hardly recognisable.change 可大可小，transformation 则是彻底的。只有事后几乎认不出原样时，才用这个词。

It undergoes or goes through a transformation, and the result follows into: the transformation of an old factory into flats. Business writing overuses digital transformation; say what actually changed.动词用 undergo 或 go through；变成什么用 into：the transformation of an old factory into flats。商务文章里 digital transformation 用滥了，不如直接说到底变了什么。

Examples例句 9

  • The old port has undergone a complete transformation in the last ten years. 过去十年里，这个老港口已经彻底变了样。
  • Becoming a parent brought about a transformation in the way he spent his evenings. 当了父亲以后，他晚上的过法彻底变了。
  • The transformation of the factory into flats took three years. 把那座工厂改造成公寓花了三年。
  • A new coat of paint is an improvement, not a transformation. 重新刷一遍漆是改善，算不上脱胎换骨。
  • The internet caused a dramatic transformation in how people look for work. 互联网使人们找工作的方式发生了戏剧性的变化。
  • Real transformation in a company starts with what managers do, not what they announce. 一家公司真正的转变，始于管理者做了什么，而不是宣布了什么。

Where it comes from词源与派生

Inherited from Middle English transformacioun, from Middle French, from Ecclesiastical Latin trānsfōrmātiō. Morphologically transform + -ation.

Derived words派生词: cotransformation, detransformation, retransformation, transformational, biotransformation, mistransformation, transformationism, transformationist, autotransformation, backtransformation, cryotransformation, agro-transformation, intertransformation, neurotransformation
