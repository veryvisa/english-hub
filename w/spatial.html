spatial adjective

US /ˈspeɪʃəl/ UK same相同 NAWL · band 1 · #38

connected with space and the position, size and shape of things in it 空间的，与空间位置、大小、形状有关的

Forms词形: spatials

How it is used怎么用

Spatial is the adjective of space, though the spelling hides it: the c becomes t. It is pronounced like special with the vowel of day.spatial 是 space 的形容词，只是拼写把关系藏起来了：c 变成了 t。读音像 special，但元音是 day 里的那个。

It mostly appears in two phrases: spatial awareness, the sense of where your body and other objects are, and spatial reasoning, the ability to picture shapes turning in your head.它主要出现在两个短语里：spatial awareness（空间感，知道自己的身体和周围物体在哪儿）和 spatial reasoning（空间推理，在脑子里转动图形的能力）。

Examples例句 8

  • Parking in a tight space is a real test of spatial awareness. 在窄车位里停车，真是对空间感的考验。
  • Building with blocks helps young children develop spatial reasoning. 搭积木有助于幼儿发展空间推理能力。
  • Taxi drivers who learn a city by heart tend to have excellent spatial memory. 把一座城市的路记在心里的出租车司机，空间记忆往往极好。
  • The map makes the spatial relationship between the three villages clear at once. 这张地图让三个村子的空间关系一目了然。
  • Good spatial planning puts schools and shops within walking distance of homes. 好的空间规划会让学校和商店都在住家的步行范围之内。
  • Some people give directions in spatial terms, such as north, and others use landmarks. 有些人指路用空间方位，比如「往北」，另一些人用地标。
  • The game is designed to train spatial skills rather than speed. 这个游戏是用来训练空间能力的，不是比速度。
