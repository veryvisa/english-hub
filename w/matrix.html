matrix noun

US /ˈmeɪtrɪks/ UK same相同 NAWL · band 1 · #15

an arrangement of numbers or items in rows and columns; the conditions in which something develops 矩阵；（由行和列组成的）表格；（事物生长、形成的）环境，基质

Forms词形: matrices, matrixes

How it is used怎么用

The plural is matrices in mathematics and usually matrixes elsewhere.复数在数学里用 matrices，其他场合多用 matrixes。

At work a matrix is simply a grid for comparing choices: a decision matrix lists the options down the side and the criteria across the top.职场里的 matrix 就是用来比较选项的表格：decision matrix 竖着列选项，横着列标准。

Examples例句 8

  • We drew up a decision matrix to compare the three flats we had seen. 我们画了一张决策矩阵，来比较看过的那三套公寓。
  • Each row of the matrix stands for a student and each column for a test. 矩阵的每一行代表一个学生，每一列代表一次测验。
  • The manager keeps a skills matrix showing who can cover for whom. 经理手里有一张技能矩阵，标明谁能顶替谁。
  • A risk matrix sorts problems by how likely and how serious they are. 风险矩阵按可能性和严重程度给问题分类。
  • Language grows inside a matrix of family, school and friendships. 语言是在家庭、学校和友谊交织成的环境里长出来的。
  • Multiplying two matrices by hand takes patience and a large sheet of paper. 手算两个矩阵相乘，需要耐心和一大张纸。

Where it comes from词源与派生

From Middle English matris, matrice, matrix, from Old French matrice (“pregnant animal”), or from Latin mātrīx (“dam, womb”), both ultimately from māter (“mother”). Doublet of mother from Indo-European ancestor.

Derived words派生词: matrixed, matrixial, Matrixism, dot-matrix, matrixlike, matrixwise, matrix group, matrix mixer, matrixectomy, matrix clause, matrix algebra, matrix diagram, matrix protein, matrix language
