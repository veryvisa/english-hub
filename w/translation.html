translation noun

US /trænzˈleɪʃən/ UK same相同 NAWL · band 1 · #40

the process of changing words into another language, or a text that has been changed in this way 翻译；译文，译本

Forms词形: translations

How it is used怎么用

Uncountable for the activity (translation is hard work), countable for a finished text (a new translation of the novel). You read a book in translation, and a meaning can be lost in translation.指翻译这项活动不可数（translation is hard work），指一份译文可数（a new translation of the novel）。读译本说 read a book in translation；意思在翻译中走样说 lost in translation。

Translation is written; interpreting is spoken. The person at a hospital appointment is an interpreter, not a translator.translation 是笔译，interpreting 是口译。陪你看病的是 interpreter，不是 translator。

Examples例句 12

  • A literal translation of the menu produced some very strange dishes. 把菜单逐字直译，结果冒出了一些非常古怪的菜名。
  • Much of the humour was lost in translation. 大部分幽默在翻译中都丢了。
  • I first read the novel in translation and only later in the original. 这部小说我先读的是译本，后来才读原文。
  • Machine translation is good enough for a timetable but risky for a contract. 机器翻译看个时刻表够用，拿来译合同就有风险。
  • Can you give me a rough translation of what the letter says? 你能把这封信的大意给我翻一下吗？
  • The clinic provides a written translation of the form on request. 这家诊所可应要求提供表格的书面译文。

Where it comes from词源与派生

From Middle English translacioun (“transfer, translation”), from Anglo-Norman translacioun, from Latin trānslātiō, from trānsferō (“to transfer, transport, transform, translate”). By surface analysis, translate + -ion.
