domain noun

US /doʊˈmeɪn/ UK /dəˈmeɪn/ NAWL · band 1 · #8

an area of knowledge or activity; also the name of a website on the internet 领域，范围；（互联网）域名

Forms词形: domains

How it is used怎么用

In academic writing domain means a field that someone knows or controls: the domain of medicine, outside my domain.学术文章里 domain 指某人熟悉或掌管的「领域」：the domain of medicine、outside my domain。

Two fixed phrases are worth learning whole: in the public domain (free for anyone to use, or known to everyone) and a domain name.两个固定说法值得整块记：in the public domain（任何人可用；已为公众所知）和 a domain name（域名）。

Examples例句 12

  • Questions about tax are outside my domain, so please ask an accountant. 税务问题不在我的专业范围内，请去问会计。
  • The photographs are in the public domain, so anyone may print them. 这些照片属于公有领域，谁都可以印。
  • She registered a domain name for the shop before she had sold a single cake. 一块蛋糕还没卖出去，她就先给小店注册了域名。
  • Cooking used to be seen as a private domain rather than a subject for study. 做饭从前被看作私人领域的事，而不是一门学问。
  • Good translators need domain knowledge as well as two languages. 好译者除了懂两门语言，还得有行业知识。
  • Once the figures are in the public domain, the company will have to explain them. 这些数字一旦公开，公司就得出面解释。

Where it comes from词源与派生

From Middle English demayne, demain (“rule”), from Old French demeine, demaine, demeigne, domaine (“power”), (French domaine), from Latin dominium (“property, right of ownership”), from dominus (“master, proprietor, owner”). Doublet of demesne and dominium, and closely related to dominion and domino.

Derived words派生词: bidomain, didomain, domainal, domained, domainer, biodomain, domaining, domainome, ecodomain, exodomain, nondomain, prodomain, antidomain, cytodomain
