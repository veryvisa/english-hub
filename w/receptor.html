receptor noun

US /rɪˈsɛptɚ/ UK /rɪˈsɛptə/ NAWL · band 1 · #30

a part of a cell or a nerve ending that reacts to a particular substance or signal 受体；感受器

Forms词形: receptors

How it is used怎么用

Health articles use this word to explain why a substance works: caffeine blocks certain receptors, a drug binds to a receptor. The verbs to learn with it are bind to, block and activate.健康类文章用这个词来解释某种物质为什么起作用：咖啡因 blocks 某些受体，药物 binds to 受体。要一起记的动词是 bind to、block、activate。

It belongs to a family you already know: receive, reception, receptive. A receptor is literally a receiver.它属于你早就认识的一族：receive、reception、receptive。receptor 字面上就是「接收者」。

Caffeine keeps you awake by blocking the _____ that signal tiredness.

Examples例句 8

  • Caffeine keeps you awake by blocking the receptors that signal tiredness. 咖啡因让人清醒，靠的是阻断传递疲倦信号的受体。
  • The skin on your fingertips is packed with touch receptors. 指尖的皮肤上密布着触觉感受器。
  • The drug binds to a receptor on the surface of the cell. 这种药与细胞表面的一种受体结合。
  • Taste receptors for bitterness warn us off food that might be harmful. 感受苦味的味觉受体提醒我们避开可能有害的食物。
  • Chilli feels hot because it activates the same receptors as real heat. 辣椒让人觉得「烫」，是因为它激活的受体和真正的热激活的是同一批。
  • As we age, some receptors in the ear stop working and are not replaced. 随着年龄增长，耳朵里的一些感受器会失灵，而且不会再生。

Where it comes from词源与派生

From Middle English receptour, from Old French receptour or Latin receptōrius, from recipiō (“receive”), from re- (“back”) + capiō (“to hold”).

Derived words派生词: coreceptor, receptoral, aporeceptor, bioreceptor, isoreceptor, nonreceptor, receptorome, autoreceptor, gasoreceptor, holoreceptor, minireceptor, osmoreceptor, perireceptor, postreceptor
