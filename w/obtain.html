obtain verb

US /əbˈteɪn/ UK same相同 NAWL · band 1 · #2

to get something, especially by asking, working or going through a process (formal) 获得，取得（比 get 正式，多指经过申请、努力或手续才拿到）

Forms词形: obtains, obtained, obtaining

How it is used怎么用

Obtain is the formal partner of get. Use it for things that take a request or some effort: permission, a licence, information, a result. Nobody obtains a cup of coffee.obtain 是 get 的正式说法，用在要申请或费点功夫才拿得到的东西上：许可、执照、信息、结果。没有人会说 obtain a cup of coffee。

It is very often passive in forms and reports: the data were obtained from, further details can be obtained from.表格和报告里常用被动：the data were obtained from…、further details can be obtained from…。

Test yourself先自测

You must _____ written permission from the landlord before you paint the walls.

刷墙之前，你必须拿到房东的书面许可。

You must obtain written permission from the landlord before you paint the walls.

Collocations搭配

  • obtain permission 获得许可
  • obtain a licence 取得执照
  • obtain information from 从……获取信息
  • obtain a result 得出结果
  • easily obtained 容易拿到的

Word family词族

  • obtainable adjective 可获得的，买得到的
  • unobtainable adjective 无法得到的

Examples例句 12

  • You must obtain written permission from the landlord before you paint the walls. 刷墙之前，你必须拿到房东的书面许可。
  • Further details can be obtained from the front desk. 更多详情可向前台索取。
  • The figures were obtained from a survey of two hundred households. 这些数字来自对两百户家庭的调查。
  • It took her three months to obtain a copy of her birth certificate. 她花了三个月才拿到一份出生证明的副本。
  • Fresh fruit was hard to obtain in the village during winter. 冬天在那个村子里很难弄到新鲜水果。
  • Researchers obtained similar results when they repeated the test with older adults. 研究人员在老年人身上重做测试，得到了相似的结果。

Where it comes from词源与派生

From Middle English obteinen, from Anglo-Norman obtenir, optiner et al., and Middle French obtenir, from Latin obtinēre (“to gain, achieve, succeed, possess”), from ob- + tenēre (“to hold”).

Derived words派生词: obtainer, reobtain, preobtain, obtainable, obtainance, obtainment, unobtained, obtainability, unobtainability
