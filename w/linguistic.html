linguistic adjective

US /lɪŋˈɡwɪstɪk/ UK same相同 NAWL · band 1 · #20

connected with language or with the scientific study of language 语言的；语言学的

How it is used怎么用

Linguistic is formal. In everyday speech people say language skills, not linguistic skills; in a report or an essay the adjective is the natural choice: linguistic diversity, a linguistic barrier.linguistic 比较正式。日常说 language skills，不说 linguistic skills；写报告、文章时用这个形容词才自然：linguistic diversity、a linguistic barrier。

A linguist studies how languages work. The word does not mean someone who speaks many languages; that person is a polyglot.linguist 是研究语言怎么运作的人，不是「会很多门语言的人」，后者叫 polyglot。

Examples例句 11

  • The city is proud of its linguistic diversity; you can hear a dozen languages on one bus. 这座城市以语言多样为荣，一辆公交车上就能听到十几种语言。
  • Linguistic barriers make even a simple visit to the doctor stressful. 语言障碍让看个病这样简单的事都叫人紧张。
  • Children who grow up with two languages often show strong linguistic awareness. 在双语环境中长大的孩子，往往有很强的语言意识。
  • Her research looks at the linguistic habits of teenagers online. 她研究的是青少年在网上的语言习惯。
  • The misunderstanding was cultural rather than linguistic; every word had been translated correctly. 这次误会是文化上的，不是语言上的：每个词都翻译对了。
  • Good interpreters need more than linguistic ability; they need nerve and a fast memory. 好的口译员光有语言能力不够，还得有胆量和过硬的短时记忆。

Where it comes from词源与派生

Borrowed from German linguistisch, equivalent to linguist + -ic. Ultimately from Latin lingua (“tongue, language”).

Derived words派生词: linguistics, linguistical, linguisticky, linguistician, linguisticize, linguistic map, linguistically, linguistic turn, linguistic unit, cross-linguistic, linguistic atlas, neuro-linguistic, linguisticization, logico-linguistic
