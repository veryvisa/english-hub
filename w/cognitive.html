cognitive adjective

US /ˈkɑɡnətɪv/ UK /ˈkɒɡnətɪv/ NAWL · band 1 · #17

connected with thinking, understanding, learning and remembering 认知的，与思考、理解、记忆有关的

How it is used怎么用

Cognitive always comes before a noun: cognitive skills, cognitive decline, cognitive load. It is not used after be.cognitive 总是放在名词前：cognitive skills、cognitive decline、cognitive load，不放在 be 动词后面。

Cognitive load is a useful phrase for learners: it is the amount your working memory is holding at one moment.cognitive load 对学习者很有用，指工作记忆在同一时刻要扛的量。

Test yourself先自测

Regular walking seems to slow _____ decline in older adults.

经常散步似乎能延缓老年人的认知衰退。

Regular walking seems to slow cognitive decline in older adults.

Examples例句 12

  • Regular walking seems to slow cognitive decline in older adults. 经常散步似乎能延缓老年人的认知衰退。
  • Reading subtitles while listening adds to your cognitive load. 边听边看字幕会增加你的认知负荷。
  • Play is central to a young child's cognitive development. 玩耍对幼儿的认知发展至关重要。
  • Trusting the first number you hear is a well-known cognitive bias. 轻信听到的第一个数字，是一种人所共知的认知偏差。
  • Even one night of poor sleep affects cognitive performance the next day. 哪怕只有一晚没睡好，第二天的认知表现也会受影响。
  • The test measures cognitive skills such as memory and attention, not knowledge. 这项测试测的是记忆、注意力这类认知能力，而不是知识。

Where it comes from词源与派生

From Medieval Latin cognitīvus, from Latin cognitus, perfect passive participle of cognōscō (“to get to know”) + -īvus.
