aspect noun

US /ˈæspɛkt/ UK same相同 NAWL · band 1 · #5

one part or side of a situation, problem or subject 方面；（问题、情况的）一个侧面

Forms词形: aspects

How it is used怎么用

Aspect is the tidy word for dividing a topic: one aspect of the problem, every aspect of life. It is followed by of, not about.要把一个话题分块来讲，aspect 是最顺手的词：one aspect of the problem、every aspect of life。后面接 of，不接 about。

Chinese speakers tend to overuse in the aspect of. Native writers say in terms of cost, or simply name the aspect: the financial aspect.华人爱写 in the aspect of；母语者说 in terms of cost，或者直接点名：the financial aspect。

Test yourself先自测

The hardest _____ of moving abroad was leaving her parents behind.

出国最难的一面，是把父母留在国内。

The hardest aspect of moving abroad was leaving her parents behind.

Examples例句 12

  • The hardest aspect of moving abroad was leaving her parents behind. 出国最难的一面，是把父母留在国内。
  • Cost is only one aspect of the decision; time matters just as much. 费用只是这个决定的一个方面，时间同样重要。
  • The course covers every aspect of running a small business. 这门课涵盖了经营小生意的方方面面。
  • We need to look at the legal aspects before we sign anything. 签任何东西之前，我们得先看看法律方面的问题。
  • One aspect of the job that surprised him was how much writing it involved. 这份工作有一点让他意外：要写的东西特别多。
  • Technology now touches almost every aspect of how children learn. 如今科技几乎影响到孩子学习的每一个方面。

Where it comes from词源与派生

From Middle English aspect, from Latin aspectus (“look, sight; appearance”), from aspiciō (“see; catch sight of; inspect”), from ad- (“to, towards, at”) + speciō (“look, look at, behold; observe”).
