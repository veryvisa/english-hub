semantic adjective

US /səˈmæntɪk/ UK /sɪˈmæntɪk/ NAWL · band 1 · #12

connected with the meaning of words and sentences 语义的，与词句意义有关的

Forms词形: semantics

How it is used怎么用

Semantic is the adjective; semantics is the noun and takes a singular verb when it names the field of study.semantic 是形容词；semantics 是名词，指这门学科时谓语用单数。

Outside linguistics, saying that a disagreement is just semantics means the two sides are arguing about wording, not about facts.在语言学之外，说一场争论 is just semantics，意思是双方争的只是措辞，不是事实。

Test yourself先自测

There is a small _____ difference between cheap and inexpensive.

cheap 和 inexpensive 在语义上有细微差别。

There is a small semantic difference between cheap and inexpensive.

Word family词族

Examples例句 11

  • There is a small semantic difference between cheap and inexpensive. cheap 和 inexpensive 在语义上有细微差别。
  • Whether you call it a fee or a charge is a matter of semantics; you still have to pay. 叫 fee 还是叫 charge 只是说法不同，钱照样得付。
  • Words like nice have gone through a great deal of semantic change over the centuries. 像 nice 这样的词，几百年来词义变化很大。
  • Learning words in semantic groups, such as kitchen tools, helps some students and confuses others. 按语义分组学词（比如厨房用具），对有些学生有帮助，对另一些反而容易混。
  • The two sentences have the same semantic content, but one sounds far more polite. 这两句话的语义内容相同，但其中一句听起来礼貌得多。
  • Search engines now try to work out the semantic link between your words instead of matching them one by one. 如今的搜索引擎会设法弄清你输入的词之间的语义联系，而不是逐个匹配。
