denote verb

US /dɪˈnoʊt/ UK /dɪˈnəʊt/ NAWL · band 1 · #10

to be a sign of something; to stand for or mean something (formal) 表示，标志；（符号、词语）指代，意思是

Forms词形: denotes, denoted, denoting

How it is used怎么用

Denote is what symbols, colours and technical terms do: the letter n denotes the number of people, a red flag denotes danger. In conversation people say stands for, means or shows.denote 是符号、颜色、术语干的事：the letter n denotes the number of people、a red flag denotes danger。口语里说 stands for、means、shows。

Its partner is connote. A word denotes its plain meaning and connotes the feelings that come with it.和它成对的是 connote：一个词 denote 的是字面意思，connote 的是附带的感情色彩。

  • the term denotes 这个术语指的是
  • a symbol denoting 表示……的符号

Word family词族

  • denotation noun 字面意义，本义

Examples例句 8

  • On this map a blue line denotes a river and a red line denotes a road. 这张地图上，蓝线表示河流，红线表示公路。
  • In the formula, the letter t denotes time in seconds. 公式里的字母 t 表示以秒计的时间。
  • A star next to a name denotes a member who joined this year. 名字旁边的星号表示今年新加入的成员。
  • The word home denotes a building but suggests warmth and safety as well. home 这个词指的是一座建筑，却也让人想到温暖和安全。
  • Dark circles under the eyes do not always denote a lack of sleep. 黑眼圈并不总是表示睡眠不足。
  • The amount of water used is denoted by the shaded area of the chart. 用水量由图中的阴影部分表示。
  • In some cultures white clothing denotes mourning rather than celebration. 在一些文化里，白色衣服表示哀悼而不是庆祝。

Where it comes from词源与派生

From Middle French denoter, from Latin denotare, from de- (“complete”) and notare (“to mark out”).

Derived words派生词: denotee, redenote, undenoted, denotement, denotive, denotable, denotation, denotative, denotatory, denotability
