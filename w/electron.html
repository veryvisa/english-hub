electron noun

US /ɪˈlɛktrɑn/ UK /ɪˈlɛktrɒn/ NAWL · band 1 · #29

a very small particle with a negative electric charge that moves around the centre of an atom 电子

Forms词形: electrons

How it is used怎么用

The stress falls on the second syllable in electron, electric and electrical, but moves in electronic and electricity. Getting this right matters more for being understood than any single sound.electron、electric、electrical 的重音都在第二个音节，到了 electronic、electricity 重音就挪了。要让人听懂，重音放对比哪个音发得准都要紧。

An electron microscope uses a beam of electrons instead of light, which is why it can show things far too small for an ordinary microscope.电子显微镜用电子束代替光线，所以能看到普通显微镜远远看不清的东西。

Examples例句 11

  • An electric current is simply a flow of electrons through a wire. 电流不过是电子在导线里的流动。
  • An electron is far lighter than the centre of the atom it circles. 电子比它所环绕的原子核轻得多。
  • Under an electron microscope the surface of a leaf looks like a landscape. 在电子显微镜下，叶子的表面看上去像一片地貌。
  • When an atom gains an extra electron it carries a negative charge. 原子多得到一个电子，就带上了负电荷。
  • Metals conduct electricity well because some of their electrons are free to move. 金属导电好，是因为其中一部分电子可以自由移动。
  • The discovery of the electron showed that atoms were not the smallest things after all. 电子的发现表明，原子终究不是最小的东西。
