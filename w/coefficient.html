coefficient noun

US /ˌkoʊəˈfɪʃənt/ UK /ˌkəʊɪˈfɪʃənt/ NAWL · band 1 · #39

a number that measures a particular property, or that multiplies a letter in a formula 系数

Forms词形: coefficients

How it is used怎么用

You do not need the mathematics to read the word in a report. A coefficient is one number that sums up a relationship or a property, and it nearly always comes with a name in front: a correlation coefficient, a coefficient of friction.读报告时遇到这个词，不必懂背后的数学。coefficient 就是用一个数概括一种关系或性质，前面几乎总带着名字：a correlation coefficient、a coefficient of friction。

The stress is on fi, the third syllable. The first two letters are said as two syllables: co-e.重音在第三个音节 fi 上。开头的 co 和 e 分属两个音节。

Word family词族

Examples例句 8

  • A correlation coefficient close to one means the two measures rise together. 相关系数接近一，说明两项指标同步上升。
  • In the expression three x, the coefficient of x is three. 在 three x 这个式子里，x 的系数是三。
  • Ice has a very low coefficient of friction, which is why you slip on it. 冰的摩擦系数很低，所以人在上面会打滑。
  • The report gives a single coefficient to describe how unequal incomes are. 这份报告用一个系数来描述收入的不平等程度。
  • You can calculate the coefficient in a spreadsheet in a few seconds. 在电子表格里几秒钟就能算出这个系数。
  • A coefficient near zero tells you there is no clear pattern in the data. 系数接近零，说明数据里看不出明显的规律。

Where it comes from词源与派生

From French coefficient, or influenced by New Latin coefficient-, the stem of coefficiens, from co- and efficio.

Derived words派生词: coefficiently, eigencoefficient, piezocoefficient, activity coefficient, Coriolis coefficient
