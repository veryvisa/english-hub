dynamic adjective, noun

US /daɪˈnæmɪk/ UK same相同 NAWL · band 1 · #6

full of energy and always changing; as a noun, the way people or forces act on each other 有活力的；动态的；（名词）相互作用的方式，互动关系

Forms词形: dynamics

How it is used怎么用

As an adjective it praises a person or an economy for energy and change. As a noun, often plural, it describes how the people in a group affect each other: family dynamics, the dynamics of a meeting.作形容词是夸人或经济「有冲劲、在变化」。作名词（常用复数）指一群人之间怎么互相影响：family dynamics、the dynamics of a meeting。

The opposite of the adjective is static.形容词的反义词是 static（静止的）。

Examples例句 12

  • The arrival of a new baby changes the dynamic of the whole family. 新生儿的到来会改变整个家庭的相处方式。
  • She is a dynamic manager who never keeps a meeting longer than it needs to be. 她是个雷厉风行的经理，开会从不拖时间。
  • Group dynamics matter more than talent when a team is under pressure. 团队承压的时候，成员之间的互动比才华更要紧。
  • Prices on the site are dynamic and can rise within minutes. 这个网站上的价格是动态的，几分钟之内就可能上涨。
  • It took me months to understand the dynamics of the office. 我花了好几个月才摸清办公室里的人际关系。
  • A dynamic city attracts young workers, but it also pushes rents up. 有活力的城市吸引年轻劳动者，也把房租推高。

Where it comes from词源与派生

From French dynamique, from Ancient Greek δυναμικός (“powerful”), from δύναμις (“power”), from δύναμαι (“I am able”).
