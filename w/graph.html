graph noun

US /ɡræf/ UK /ɡrɑːf/ NAWL · band 1 · #18

a drawing that uses a line or bars to show how two sets of numbers are related 图表（尤指曲线图、柱状图）

Forms词形: graphs, graphed, graphing

How it is used怎么用

A graph shows, illustrates or indicates something; a line on it rises, falls, levels off or peaks. These verbs are what examiners listen for when you describe a chart.图表 shows / illustrates / indicates 某事；图上的线 rises、falls、levels off、peaks。描述图表时，考官听的就是这些动词。

Things are shown on a graph or in a graph. A chart is the wider word; a table has rows and columns and no line.说 on a graph 或 in a graph 都可以。chart 是更宽的词；table 是有行有列、没有线的表格。

Test yourself先自测

The _____ shows a sharp rise in rents over the last five years.

图表显示过去五年房租急剧上涨。

The graph shows a sharp rise in rents over the last five years.

Collocations搭配

  • a line graph 折线图
  • a bar graph 柱状图
  • the graph shows 图表显示
  • plot a graph 绘制图表
  • as the graph illustrates 如图所示

Word family词族

  • graphic adjective 图表的；生动的
  • graphical adjective 用图形表示的
  • paragraph noun （同词根）段落

Examples例句 12

  • The graph shows a sharp rise in rents over the last five years. 图表显示过去五年房租急剧上涨。
  • Always read the scale on the side of a graph before you trust its shape. 相信一张图的走势之前，先看清旁边的刻度。
  • She plotted her weekly running distance on a graph taped to the fridge. 她把每周的跑步里程画成图，贴在冰箱上。
  • A bar graph is better than a table when you want people to compare at a glance. 想让人一眼看出差别，柱状图比表格好用。
  • As the graph illustrates, sales peak in December and fall away in January. 如图所示，销售额在十二月达到顶峰，一月回落。
  • The line on the graph levels off after the third week. 图上的曲线在第三周之后趋于平稳。

Where it comes from词源与派生

Clipping of graphic formula. From 1878; verb from 1889.
