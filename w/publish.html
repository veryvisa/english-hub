publish verb

US /ˈpʌblɪʃ/ UK same相同 NAWL · band 1 · #9

to make a book, an article or information available to the public 出版；发表；公布

Forms词形: publishes, published, publishing

How it is used怎么用

A publisher publishes a book; a writer or researcher also publishes work, in a journal or on a site. Results, figures and reports are published too, which simply means made public.出版社 publish 书；作者、研究者也 publish 自己的作品（in a journal、on a site）。结果、数字、报告被 publish，就是「对外公布」。

In science the passive is the normal form: the study was published in, the findings were published last year.科研语境里常用被动：the study was published in…、the findings were published last year。

Test yourself先自测

The council will _____ the results of the survey next week.

市议会下周将公布调查结果。

Collocations搭配

  • publish the results 公布结果
  • be published in a journal 发表在期刊上
  • publish online 在网上发布
  • recently published 最近出版的，最近发表的

Word family词族

  • publisher noun 出版社；出版人
  • publication noun 出版；出版物；公布
  • unpublished adjective 未发表的

Examples例句 12

  • The council will publish the results of the survey next week. 市议会下周将公布调查结果。
  • Her first novel was published when she was nearly sixty. 她的第一部小说出版时，她已年近六十。
  • The study was published in a journal that few people outside the field read. 这项研究发表在一本圈外人很少读的期刊上。
  • Think twice before you publish a photograph of someone else's child online. 把别人孩子的照片发到网上之前，要三思。
  • The school publishes its exam results every August. 这所学校每年八月公布考试成绩。
  • Most of what he wrote in those years was never published. 他那些年写的东西大多从未发表。

Where it comes from词源与派生

From Middle English publicen, from Old French publier, from Latin publicare (“to make public”), from publicus (“pertaining to the people, public”).

Derived words派生词: copublish, depublish, e-publish, publishee, publisher, republish, unpublish, mispublish, outpublish, prepublish, overpublish, publishable, publishment, telepublish
