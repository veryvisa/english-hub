algorithm noun

US /ˈælɡəˌrɪðəm/ UK /ˈælɡərɪðəm/ NAWL · band 1 · #32

a set of steps that a computer follows to solve a problem or to decide what to show you 算法

Forms词形: algorithms

How it is used怎么用

Outside computer science the word now means the hidden rules that choose what appears on your screen: the algorithm decides, the algorithm recommends, the algorithm favours. People speak of it almost as a person.在计算机科学之外，这个词现在指决定你屏幕上出现什么的那套看不见的规则：the algorithm decides / recommends / favours。大家说起它，几乎像在说一个人。

The ending is spelled -rithm and sounds like rhythm; it is not -rism.词尾拼作 -rithm，读音同 rhythm，不是 -rism。

Examples例句 12

  • The algorithm decides which posts you see first, not the order they were written in. 你先看到哪些帖子由算法决定，而不是按发布时间排列。
  • A recommendation algorithm learns from every video you finish. 你每看完一个视频，推荐算法都会从中学习。
  • The bank uses an algorithm to flag payments that look unusual. 银行用算法标出看起来不寻常的付款。
  • Nobody at the company could fully explain why the algorithm had rejected her application. 公司里没有人能说清楚，算法为什么拒了她的申请。
  • A recipe is a kind of algorithm: follow the steps and you get the same cake. 菜谱就是一种算法：照着步骤做，就能做出一样的蛋糕。
  • An algorithm designed to keep you watching is not designed to keep you informed. 为了让你一直看下去而设计的算法，并不是为了让你了解情况而设计的。

Where it comes from词源与派生

From Middle English algorisme, augrym, from Anglo-Norman algorisme, augrim, from Medieval Latin algorismus, from Arabic الخَوَارِزْمِيّ, the nisba of Persian mathematician Muḥammad ibn Mūsā al-Khwārizmī and a toponymic name meaning "the Chorasmian".

Derived words派生词: algorithmist, subalgorithm, autoalgorithm, polyalgorithm, multialgorithm, superalgorithm, algorithmocracy, algocracy, algorethics
