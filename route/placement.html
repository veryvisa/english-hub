🧭 Find your starting point找到你的起点

12 questions, about six minutes; they get harder when you are right and easier when you are not. A short piece of writing is optional (about four minutes more). You get a starting station, not a grade. Stop at any time.12 道题，约六分钟；答对就变难、答错就变易。短写可选（再加约四分钟）。得到的是起点，不是分数；随时可以停。

ℹ️ How the result is worked out, and what it cannot tell you结果怎么算的，以及它说明不了什么

Meanings: up to 7 sentences with words you know; you choose which meaning is used. They start at the second meaning of a word, move to a less common meaning when you are right and back to the first meaning when you are not. Sentences: up to 5 grammar questions from this site, starting at B2 and moving to C1 when you are right. Writing (optional): the browser counts sentence shapes and linking in your text; it does not judge your ideas or your accuracy.词义：最多 7 个句子，里面的词你都认识，选这句里用的是哪个意思；从一个词的第二个意思开始，答对换不太常用的意思，答错退回第一个意思。句式：最多 5 道本站语法题，从 B2 开始，答对换 C1。短写（可选）：浏览器数你这段话里的句子形状和衔接，不判断你的想法，也不判断对错。

Only meanings and sentences decide the starting station. The writing result is for reference only: it has been checked only against sample answers at three levels, not real learners, so it never moves your station. The cut-off points were set by design, not from learner data. Treat the result as a suggestion for where to start. It is not a test score and does not predict CELPIP or IELTS.起点只由词义和句式定。短写结果只供参考：它只拿三档示范答案标定过，没用真实学习者的稿子标定，判不稳，所以不会改变你的起点。分界线是设计值，没有学习者数据支持。请把结果当作起点建议：它不是考试成绩，也不预测思培或雅思。
