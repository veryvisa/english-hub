👂 Thursday: listen first, then read周四：先听懂，再看文字

“Tai Chi or Weights?” is a text you have not read yet.「太极还是举铁？」：这一篇你还没读过。 Three steps: listen without the text, answer, say the main points. Then open the text to check and shadow it.三步：不看文字听，答题，说出要点；然后打开原文核对并跟读。

1 · Answer without looking不看文字答题

1 Why does Patrick go to the gym as well as doing balance work?

I also need to load the muscles, especially the legs.要给肌肉加负荷。

2 What does Patrick suggest for Mr Ho?

keep your tai chi in the morning and do the class twice a week.保留太极，加上老年班。

2 · Say the main points说出要点

3 · Now look at the text再看原文

📄 Show the text显示原文

- Mr Ho: Morning, Patrick. You're late today. Patrick: I've been at the gym. My doctor suggested strength training twice a week, so I finally made a decision and joined. Mr Ho: At seventy? You'll hurt yourself. Tai chi is enough for me. It's gentle on the joints and good for balance. - Patrick: Tai chi is great for balance, I agree. Some studies even say it lowers the risk of falls. But my doctor said I also need to load the muscles, especially the legs. She took my bad knee into account and gave me a plan with light weights and machines. Mr Ho: And the young people there don't laugh at you? - Patrick: The opposite. The trainer told me to warm up for ten minutes first, start light, and take a break between sets. She said the most common mistake is doing too much in the first week and quitting in the second. "Pace yourself," she keeps saying. I'm making progress slowly. Three months ago I couldn't do ten squats. Now I can do twenty. - Mr Ho: Hm. My daughter keeps telling me to do more. Maybe I should make an effort. But I don't want to go to a gym full of young people. Patrick: Then don't. The community centre has a class for over-sixties on Tuesdays. Same idea, slower pace. You could keep your tai chi in the morning and do the class twice a week. - Mr Ho: You've thought about this a lot. Patrick: I had to. When my brother had a bad fall last year, it had a huge impact on his whole family. I'd rather put in an hour now than spend a year recovering later. Mr Ho: Fine. Tuesday. But if I'm sore on Wednesday, you're buying the dim sum. Patrick: Deal.

🆚 After your first draft: the same content written three ways写完第一稿再看：同一个内容的三种写法

Plain平实版

In my opinion, the community centre should build the studio, but it must keep a quiet space for talking too. Many older residents walk every day, and walking is good for the heart. But it does not build strong muscles. Older people need strength, because daily tasks like carrying groceries and climbing stairs get harder. Gentle training also prevents falls, so they can live on their own for longer. At the same time, the reading room is important. Some visitors just want to rest and chat with friends, and they will feel left out without a quiet room. So the centre should open the studio and put comfortable chairs in another room.

B2B2 版

I support the centre's plan to turn its ground-floor reading room into a strength-training studio, as long as it provides another quiet space for residents to socialise. While a daily walking routine is helpful for heart health, it does not stop the gradual muscle loss that comes with age. Gentle strength training directly targets this problem: stronger legs and core muscles improve balance, which makes everyday tasks like climbing stairs and carrying groceries safer and helps older adults remain independent for longer. However, removing the only quiet room without offering an alternative brings a real downside. Less active visitors who come mainly to read or chat with friends could feel excluded and lose an important source of company. Therefore, the centre should go ahead with the studio while setting aside comfortable seating in another room so nobody is pushed out.

C1C1 版

On balance, converting the ground-floor reading room into a strength-training studio is worthwhile, provided that the centre reserves another quiet space for conversation. Valuable as brisk walking is for cardiovascular fitness, it tends to leave age-related muscle loss unchecked. Progressive resistance training addresses that gap by rebuilding the leg and core stability needed for routine tasks such as climbing stairs and carrying groceries, thereby extending independent living and lowering the risk of falls. Admittedly, eliminating the building's main quiet refuge could alienate less active regulars who simply value unhurried company over structured exercise. Yet these two priorities need not conflict: relocating comfortable seating to an adjacent room would enable the centre to foster physical resilience among older visitors without eroding its role as a welcoming social hub.

What changes from one to the next每升一档换了什么

  • The 'and' clause with a flat claim ('will feel left out') is upgraded to a relative clause ('who come mainly to read or chat with friends') plus the cautious modal 'could feel excluded' and a specific noun phrase ('an important source of company').把带有绝对判断（will feel left out）的 and 并列句，改写为带有 who 定语从句的句子，并用更谨慎的 could feel excluded 搭配具体名词短语 an important source of company。
  • The 'While' clause and 'heart health' are replaced by a fronted concessive structure ('Valuable as brisk walking is') with 'cardiovascular fitness', while 'does not stop the gradual muscle loss that comes with age' is hedged with 'tends to' and condensed into 'leave age-related muscle loss unchecked'.用前置让步结构（Valuable as brisk walking is）和更专业的 cardiovascular fitness 替代了 While 从句与 heart health；同时用 tends to 对绝对否定进行缓和，并将 that comes with age 定语从句压缩为前置定语 age-related，搭配 leave ... unchecked。
