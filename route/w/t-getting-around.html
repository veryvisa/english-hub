👂 Thursday: listen first, then read周四：先听懂，再看文字

“Planning a Journey Across Town” is a text you have not read yet.「规划一次穿越城市的旅程」：这一篇你还没读过。 Three steps: listen without the text, answer, say the main points. Then open the text to check and shadow it.三步：不看文字听，答题，说出要点；然后打开原文核对并跟读。

1 · Answer without looking不看文字答题

1 Why does Ben say the underground might be a good choice?

Ben explains that the underground is faster, although they need to walk to the station first.本解释说地铁更快，不过他们需要先步行到车站。

2 Where is the nearest underground station?

The local resident gives directions using two blocks, traffic lights and a supermarket.当地居民通过两个街区、红绿灯和一家超市来描述路线。

2 · Say the main points说出要点

3 · Now look at the text再看原文

📄 Show the text显示原文

- Anna: We need to get to the city museum this afternoon. Do you know how far it is from here? Ben: I checked the map earlier. It is not very far, but walking would take quite a while. We could take a bus or the underground. Anna: Which option would be easier? I do not know this area very well. - Ben: The bus stop is just across the street. We can take the number shown on the route information board, but we should check that it goes towards the museum. Anna: Is there a direct route? Ben: Yes, although the bus may be slower during the afternoon rush hour. The underground is faster, but we would need to walk to the station first. - Anna: Perhaps we should ask someone nearby. Excuse me, could you tell us where the nearest underground station is? Local resident: Certainly. Go straight ahead for two blocks, then turn left at the traffic lights. You will see the station entrance next to a large supermarket. Anna: Thank you very much. That sounds easy enough. - Ben: Great. Let's take the underground, then. We should also check the return journey before we leave the museum. Anna: Good idea. I will save the station name on my phone. Ben: We can use the map as a guide, but we should also pay attention to signs inside the station. Anna: Agreed. A little planning will make the whole trip much more comfortable.

🆚 After your first draft: the same content written three ways写完第一稿再看：同一个内容的三种写法

Plain平实版

I think Emma should ask someone in a nearby shop for directions. Her phone map may not work well if the internet is slow. A local person can tell her which street to take and where to turn. She can also ask how long it takes to walk to the museum. This is a good way to learn about the town. Of course, she can still use her phone to check the route. I think asking people is helpful because it is easy and she may get more useful information.

B2B2 版

I would advise Emma to ask someone at a nearby shop for directions rather than rely only on her phone. Since she is unfamiliar with the seaside town, a local person may know the easiest walking route and can explain which streets are worth avoiding. She could also ask how long the journey should take, making it easier to recognise whether she is going in the right direction. However, she should keep her map open as a backup in case the explanation is unclear. Combining local advice with digital navigation is likely to make her journey simpler and less stressful.

C1C1 版

I would suggest that Emma ask a shopkeeper for directions, while keeping her phone map available as a backup. In an unfamiliar seaside town, local knowledge can be more useful than a digital route alone: a shopkeeper might point out a convenient shortcut, explain an awkward junction or warn her about a street that is difficult to navigate on foot. Asking about the estimated walking time would also give her a practical reference against which to check her progress. Although phone maps are convenient, they do not always reflect the experience of navigating a particular neighbourhood. On balance, combining human guidance with digital navigation offers the most reliable approach.

What changes from one to the next每升一档换了什么

  • Plain only says a local can 'tell her which street to take and where to turn', while B2 grounds the same advice in the specific fact that Emma is 'unfamiliar with the seaside town'.基础版只说当地人能'tell her which street to take and where to turn'（告诉她该走哪条街、在哪转弯），B2 版把这个建议落到具体情境：Emma 正'unfamiliar with the seaside town'（对这个海滨小镇不熟悉）。
  • B2 states generally that a local can 'explain which streets are worth avoiding', while C1 unpacks this into parallel, specific actions such as to 'warn her about a street that is difficult to navigate on foot'.B2 只是笼统地说当地人能'explain which streets are worth avoiding'（解释哪些街道该避开），C1 把它展开为并列的具体动作，例如'warn her about a street that is difficult to navigate on foot'（提醒她哪条街步行难走）。
