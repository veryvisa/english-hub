👂 Thursday: listen first, then read周四：先听懂，再看文字

“The Evidence Behind a Public Health Alert” is a text you have not read yet.「公共卫生警报背后的证据」：这一篇你还没读过。 Three steps: listen without the text, answer, say the main points. Then open the text to check and shadow it.三步：不看文字听，答题，说出要点；然后打开原文核对并跟读。

1 · Answer without looking不看文字答题

1 What is the main purpose of public health surveillance?

Surveillance examines patterns and helps identify possible emerging threats.监测通过分析变化模式，帮助识别可能出现的健康威胁。

2 Why might an apparent increase in illness be misleading?

Different reporting and testing practices can influence observed trends.不同的报告和检测方式可能影响观察到的趋势。

2 · Say the main points说出要点

3 · Now look at the text再看原文

📄 Show the text显示原文

- A regional health authority has begun reviewing reports of an unusual increase in respiratory illness after several local clinics noticed a similar pattern. Officials say that the reports deserve attention, but they have not yet established whether the increase represents a genuine outbreak. The investigation combines clinical observations, laboratory findings and information from community healthcare providers. - Public health surveillance plays an essential role in identifying possible threats. Researchers examine patterns over time and compare current observations with historical records. A sudden increase may indicate a developing problem, although seasonal changes, improved reporting or changes in testing practices could also explain it. Consequently, an apparent trend should not automatically be treated as proof of a new health threat. - Investigators are also assessing the quality of the available data. Some patients may never seek medical attention, while others may receive different diagnoses despite experiencing similar symptoms. These limitations can distort estimates of disease prevalence. On the whole, combining several independent sources tends to produce a more reliable assessment than relying on a single reporting system. - The authority plans to publish an updated assessment when additional laboratory results become available. In the meantime, residents are being encouraged to follow established public health advice and consult healthcare professionals when necessary. Transparent communication matters because uncertainty should be explained rather than concealed. An early warning is useful not because it provides every answer, but because it allows communities to prepare responsibly.

🆚 After your first draft: the same content written three ways写完第一稿再看：同一个内容的三种写法

Plain平实版

I want the council to keep the van. The van goes to the seniors' building every month. Many old people cannot go to a clinic, so the van helps them. The councillor wants proof. Proof is important, I agree. But the van is new and there is not much information yet. The nurses can write down simple facts. Then the council can look again next year. Maybe the facts are bad. Then the council can stop the van. But stopping it now is too early.

B2B2 版

I would keep the vaccination van, provided the council also asks the nurses to record basic results from now on. The van visits the seniors' building every month, and many residents find it hard to reach a clinic on their own. That is a real advantage, even if nobody has yet proved that the van reduces hospital visits. The councillor is right that public money should be spent on things that work. However, the programme is quite new, so there is simply not enough information to judge it fairly today. Cutting it now would mean making a decision without evidence, which is exactly what the councillor wants to avoid. A better plan is to keep it for one more year and collect simple records, such as how many residents were vaccinated and how many later needed treatment. Although this will cost some staff time, it will give the council something solid to discuss. The main risk is that the council keeps paying for a service that turns out to be ineffective, so it should agree in advance what result would justify ending it.

C1C1 版

On balance, I would keep the van, provided that the nurses begin keeping basic records now. It calls monthly at the seniors' building, where many residents struggle to reach a clinic unaided, and that access matters even though no one has yet shown that it cuts hospital visits. The councillor is right that public money should go to services that work; the difficulty is that the programme is too young to be judged fairly, so withdrawing it today would mean deciding without the very evidence being demanded. A sounder course is to fund it for one further year and track simple measures, such as how many residents were vaccinated and how many later needed treatment. This will absorb some staff time, which is a genuine cost, but it would give the council something solid to weigh. The residual risk is that money keeps flowing to a service that proves ineffective, and the safeguard against that is to agree now what result would justify ending it.

What changes from one to the next每升一档换了什么

  • The plain answer only states that stopping is too early; the B2 answer explains why, using the noun phrase 'a decision without evidence' to link the cut to the councillor's own concern.简单版只说现在停掉太早；B2 版用“a decision without evidence”（没有证据的决定）解释了原因，并把削减与议员自己关心的问题联系起来。
  • The 'although' clause becomes a main clause with a non-restrictive 'which' clause, and 'cost' (verb) is replaced by the more precise 'absorb' plus the noun 'a genuine cost'.B2 的 although 从句在 C1 中变成主句加非限定性 which 从句，动词 cost 换成更精确的 absorb，并用名词短语 a genuine cost 点明代价。
