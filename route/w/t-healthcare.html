👂 Thursday: listen first, then read周四：先听懂，再看文字

“How to Describe Pain So a Doctor Can Use It” is a text you have not read yet.「怎样描述疼痛，医生才用得上」：这一篇你还没读过。 Three steps: listen without the text, answer, say the main points. Then open the text to check and shadow it.三步：不看文字听，答题，说出要点；然后打开原文核对并跟读。

1 · Answer without looking不看文字答题

1 Why is the zero-to-ten number useful?

reliable for you over time.对你自己前后对比是可靠的。

2 Which description helps the doctor most?

Where, what kind and when.说了在哪里、什么样、什么时候。

2 · Say the main points说出要点

3 · Now look at the text再看原文

📄 Show the text显示原文

- "It hurts here." For many patients this is the whole description, and in a second language it is often all we can manage. The doctor then has to guess, and a guess in a ten-minute appointment can lead to a misunderstanding that costs you another visit. A few simple tools make your description far more useful. - First, say where it is and what it feels like. Doctors listen for a small set of words: sharp or dull, constant or on and off, burning, aching, throbbing. "A dull ache in my lower back that is worse in the morning" tells a doctor more than five minutes of "very painful". Many of us reach for one all-purpose word; choosing a precise one is the quickest improvement you can make. - Second, give a number. Many clinics use a scale from zero to ten, where ten is the worst pain you can imagine. The number is not reliable when you compare two people, but it is reliable for you over time. If your pain was a seven last week and is a four today, the doctor can see the change at once. - Third, describe what the pain stops you from doing. "I can't sleep on my left side" or "I had to stop driving" is a fact the doctor can use. It also raises their awareness of how serious the problem is for you, which a polite smile can easily hide. - Finally, check your expectations. A short visit does not mean the doctor is not interested. Write your symptoms down before you go, and you will walk in with more confidence and walk out with a clearer plan.

🆚 After your first draft: the same content written three ways写完第一稿再看：同一个内容的三种写法

Plain平实版

In my opinion, a video call with a doctor is a good first step for small health problems. I like this idea because it saves time and energy. You stay at home, so you do not travel far or sit in a crowded waiting room with sick people. The doctor listens to your problem and gives clear advice. But I take one risk seriously. A doctor cannot examine your body through a screen, so a video call misses important signs. Patients still need a real clinic visit for strong or lasting pain.

B2B2 版

Starting with a video consultation is a sensible first step when seeing a doctor, as long as your symptoms feel relatively mild. The main advantage is that it saves both time and energy while you are already unwell. Instead of travelling across town and sitting in a crowded waiting room where you might catch another illness, you can speak to a doctor from home and get clear advice on whether a clinic visit is needed. As a result, patients avoid unnecessary stress while clinics keep space open for urgent cases. However, remote care carries a real downside because a doctor cannot examine your body physically through a screen, so important warning signs could easily be missed. For this reason, I take clinical safety seriously and believe video calls should guide early decisions rather than replace a proper checkup when pain continues.

C1C1 版

On balance, booking a video consultation is a practical first step when seeking medical advice, provided that the initial complaint appears minor. Much as convenience appeals to busy patients, the deeper value lies in efficient triage: avoiding an unnecessary journey spares unwell individuals a crowded waiting room, where secondary infections spread easily, while enabling clinicians to screen routine cases and reserve clinic appointments for urgent needs. That said, the diagnostic limitations of remote care are something I take seriously. In the absence of a physical examination, subtle warning signs tend to go unnoticed, and excessive reliance on virtual visits risks delaying timely treatment. Consequently, online consultations serve best as an initial filter rather than a substitute for direct clinical assessment, which remains indispensable whenever symptoms worsen or fail to settle within a reasonable time.

What changes from one to the next每升一档换了什么

  • The B2 version adds a limiting condition ('as long as your symptoms feel relatively mild'), expands the short 'because' and 'so' sentences into explained reasons with 'Instead of...' and a 'where' clause, and introduces B2 transitions like 'As a result', 'However', and 'For this reason'.B2版本加入了限定条件（‘as long as your symptoms feel relatively mild’），用‘Instead of...’和‘where’从句将原本简短的‘because’和‘so’句扩展为有解释的理由，并引入了‘As a result’、‘However’和‘For this reason’等B2过渡词。
  • The C1 version shifts 'screen' from the physical noun ('through a screen') to the clinical verb ('screen routine cases'), hedges claims with 'On balance', 'provided that', and 'tend to', and packs ideas more densely through a fronted 'Much as...' clause, nominalisations ('absence', 'reliance'), and a non-restrictive ', which' clause.C1版本将‘screen’从表示实物的名词（‘through a screen’）转为医疗筛选动词（‘screen routine cases’），用‘On balance’、‘provided that’和‘tend to’使表达更审慎，并通过前置的‘Much as...’从句、名词化结构（‘absence’、‘reliance’）以及非限制性‘, which’从句提升了信息密度。
