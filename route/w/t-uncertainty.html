👂 Thursday: listen first, then read周四：先听懂，再看文字

“Ice Study Comes with a Warning Label” is a text you have not read yet.「冰层研究附带“警示标签”」：这一篇你还没读过。 Three steps: listen without the text, answer, say the main points. Then open the text to check and shadow it.三步：不看文字听，答题，说出要点；然后打开原文核对并跟读。

1 · Answer without looking不看文字答题

1 What does ‘provisional’ suggest about the findings?

Provisional means temporary or open to change, which is why the team wanted more studies.provisional 意为暂定、可能改变，所以团队希望做更多研究。

2 In ‘If the results hold up’, what does ‘hold up’ mean?

Here ‘hold up’ is a phrasal verb meaning to stay true or convincing when checked.这里 hold up 是短语动词，意为经检验仍然成立。

2 · Say the main points说出要点

3 · Now look at the text再看原文

📄 Show the text显示原文

- A team of researchers studying polar ice announced on Tuesday that the ice sheet is thinning faster than earlier models suggested, although they were careful to describe their findings as provisional. At a briefing for journalists, the lead scientist said the measurements came from a limited number of sites and would need to be confirmed by further work. - The headline figure came with a wide margin of error, and the team spent much of the briefing explaining what that means. A number such as this one is not a single point, they said, but a range within which the true value is likely to lie. On the whole, the range was narrower than it had been a decade ago, which the scientists described as a sign of progress rather than a final answer. - Not everyone was reassured. One independent expert, who had not been involved in the work, warned that dramatic headlines tend to travel faster than careful caveats. While she welcomed the new data, she argued that the public should not be asked to treat every revision as a crisis or, equally, as proof that scientists cannot be trusted. - The research team plans to repeat its measurements next season with additional instruments. If the results hold up, the estimate will be published in a peer-reviewed journal. If they do not, the scientists said, they will say so openly, because correcting the record is part of how the process is meant to work. - For now, the findings are being treated as a warning rather than a verdict. Local planners have been asked to consider several possible outcomes instead of relying on one prediction, a request the team says reflects good practice in any field where the evidence is still developing.

🆚 After your first draft: the same content written three ways写完第一稿再看：同一个内容的三种写法

Plain平实版

I think the council should not close Green Lake beach for the whole summer. One water sample is strange, but it is not enough to know the lake is unsafe. The council should put up a warning and stop swimming for a short time. It should test the water again. The first test may show a real problem, or it may be a small problem in one place. People need to be safe, so the council should not do nothing. But closing the beach all summer is too much. The council should tell people the facts and check the water again soon. Then it can decide what to do next. This is fair to visitors and gives the council time to learn more.

B2B2 版

I would not support closing Green Lake beach for the whole summer on the basis of one unusual water sample. I would, however, support a temporary warning and a pause in swimming until further tests are available. One result is important evidence, but it does not tell the whole story. The sample may point to a real problem, or it may reflect a local or short-lived change. More testing can help the council understand which explanation is more likely. There is a risk in waiting: if the water is unsafe, people could be exposed before the results arrive. For that reason, the council should explain the concern clearly, arrange prompt independent tests and review the warning when the new evidence comes in. This approach protects visitors without treating an uncertain result as a final verdict, while making clear to residents that the warning will be lifted as soon as the independent tests allow.

C1C1 版

Green Lake’s beach should not be closed for the entire summer on the strength of a single unusual water sample. The council should issue a temporary warning and suspend swimming until prompt, independent testing provides a clearer picture. A lone result is meaningful evidence, but it is not, by itself, a complete account of conditions across the lake. The sample could indicate a genuine hazard, or it could equally reflect a localised or short-lived change that says little about the wider swimming area. That uncertainty does not justify inaction. If the water is unsafe, delaying precautions could expose visitors before further results are available. The proportionate response is therefore to communicate the concern plainly, commission repeat tests and set a clear point for reviewing the warning. Such a policy acknowledges the limits of the current evidence while keeping the decision open to revision. It avoids both complacency and the false certainty of a season-long closure.

What changes from one to the next每升一档换了什么

  • The B2 version replaces the basic description 'strange' with the more precise adjective 'unusual' and incorporates it into a more formal description of the evidence.B2版本用更准确的形容词“unusual”替换了基础表达“strange”，并将其融入对检测证据更正式的描述中。
  • The C1 version replaces the noun phrase 'a pause in swimming' with the more concise and formal verb phrase 'suspend swimming', making the proposed action more direct.C1版本用更简洁、正式的动词短语“suspend swimming”替换名词短语“a pause in swimming”，使所提出的行动更加直接明确。
