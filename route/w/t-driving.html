📄 Show the text显示原文

- It was a Saturday afternoon at a busy shopping centre. Jun was backing out of a parking space when he felt a soft bump. He had checked his mirror, but the other car had been in his blind spot. The other driver, a woman in her sixties, was already getting out. Here is how the next five minutes went. - "Are you okay?" "I'm fine. Are you?" "Yes. I'm sorry, I didn't see you." "Well, I didn't see you either. Let's have a look." The damage was small: a crack in his rear light and a dent in her bumper. "I think we should exchange details and let the insurance companies sort it out," she said. "Good idea. Could I take a photo of your licence plate and your insurance card?" "Of course. I'll do the same." - While they were writing down names and numbers, a man walking past pointed out a security camera above the parking lot. "Might help you figure out who backed into whom," he said. They both laughed, a little nervously. - Jun had read that it is usually better not to argue about fault at the scene, because insurers decide that later, and that the rules on reporting a collision differ from place to place. So he did not argue. He stayed polite, took photos of both cars from several angles and wrote down the time and place. - On Monday he called his insurer. It took about twenty minutes to deal with, and the agent asked him to follow up by email with the photos. The whole thing was annoying but not frightening, mostly because neither driver turned a small bump into a fight.

🆚 After your first draft: the same content written three ways写完第一稿再看：同一个内容的三种写法

Plain平实版

I think learning to drive is useful. It helps me reach work and care for my family. It also helps me visit places with no good buses or trains. But driving is serious. It can cost a lot of money. It can also be dangerous. I would choose it for real convenience. I would take safety seriously.

B2B2 版

I believe learning to drive is worthwhile when a person already has a safe place to practise and can afford regular training, because independent travel can make daily life much more manageable. It is particularly valuable for reaching work, caring for family, and visiting places that buses or trains do not serve reliably. In less crowded areas, this freedom can save time and reduce dependence on other people. However, I would take responsibility seriously: driving brings heavy responsibility for passengers and other road users, so rushing lessons or ignoring local rules would be dangerous. The costs of fuel, insurance, maintenance, and parking should also be considered. In practice, I would choose driving only if its real convenience outweighed that financial and safety burden.

C1C1 版

I consider learning to drive worthwhile provided that a person has a safe place to practise and can afford regular training, since independent travel can make daily life considerably more manageable. Its practical value, which includes reaching work, caring for family, and visiting places that public transport serves poorly, goes beyond simple convenience. In areas with limited transport, this freedom may save time and reduce dependence on others. Responsibility, however, remains the deciding concern: driving places a heavy burden on the driver because passengers and every other road user depend on sound judgement. The costs of fuel, insurance, maintenance, and parking must also temper that view. On balance, I would choose driving only when its real convenience outweighed the financial and safety burden it creates.

What changes from one to the next每升一档换了什么

  • The plain version gives simple reasons and a safety warning, while B2 adds the conditions of safe practice and affordable training, explains the benefits, introduces a downside with “however,” and adds costs.简单版直接给出理由和安全提醒，而B2版补充了安全练车和负担得起训练的条件，解释了好处，用“however”引入缺点，并加入了费用。
  • From B2 to C1, “provided that” and “on balance” make the claim more qualified, “practical value” gives “valuable” a more specific meaning, and a non-restrictive “which” clause adds density.从B2到C1，“provided that”和“on balance”使观点更有限制；“practical value”让“valuable”具有更具体的含义；而“which”非限制性从句使表达更紧凑。
