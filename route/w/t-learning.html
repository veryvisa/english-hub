👂 Thursday: listen first, then read周四：先听懂，再看文字

“Dear Teacher: I Cannot Stay Focused” is a text you have not read yet.「亲爱的老师：我无法集中注意力」：这一篇你还没读过。 Three steps: listen without the text, answer, say the main points. Then open the text to check and shadow it.三步：不看文字听，答题，说出要点；然后打开原文核对并跟读。

1 · Answer without looking不看文字答题

1 What is the reader’s main problem?

The reader picks up a phone after ten minutes and loses an hour.读者十分钟后就拿起手机，结果一个小时就没了。

2 According to the teacher, what is usually better?

The teacher says thirty minutes of focused study is usually more useful than two tired hours.老师说三十分钟的专注学习通常比疲惫的两小时更有用。

2 · Say the main points说出要点

3 · Now look at the text再看原文

📄 Show the text显示原文

- Dear Teacher, I take evening classes after work, and I have a problem with revision. Every time I sit down to study for a test, I pick up my phone after ten minutes. Then an hour disappears and I feel bad. My marks are getting worse, so I am starting to lose confidence. What should I do? - Thank you for your honest letter. You are not alone: almost everyone finds it hard to pay attention when a phone is nearby. The first step is simple. Put your phone in another room before you start, because it is much harder to ignore something you can see. - Next, make your sessions shorter. Many people believe that longer is better, but thirty minutes of focused study is usually more useful than two tired hours. Take a five-minute break, walk around, and then begin again. When you revise for a test, give each session one clear goal, such as learning ten new words or finishing one exercise. - Finally, keep a simple record. Write down what you finished each evening. When you see a list of small successes, you will see that you can make progress, and you will be less tempted to give up. Do not expect a perfect week at once. Even two good evenings are a step forward. - Please write to me again in a month and tell me how it goes. Good luck, and remember that being tired and busy is normal. What matters is that you keep trying.

🆚 After your first draft: the same content written three ways写完第一稿再看：同一个内容的三种写法

Plain平实版

I think the school should start one hour later. Many students are tired in the morning. They stay up late with phones and homework. So they cannot listen in class. A later start gives them more sleep. Some parents work early, and this is a problem for them. The bus time may change too. But I think students' health is more important. The school can open the gate early, and teachers can watch the children there. I agree with the new plan.

B2B2 版

I support the change, provided that the school helps families who cannot adjust easily. Teenagers who stay up late with homework and phones are often exhausted at eight o'clock, so they absorb very little in the first lesson. An extra hour of sleep could therefore improve both their concentration and their mood. However, I understand why some parents are worried. Those who start work early may have no one to take their children to school, and bus timetables would have to change as well. Although these problems are real, they can be solved. If the school opened its gate early and let teachers supervise students there, working parents would be much less affected.

C1C1 版

On balance, I favour the later start, provided that the school cushions the impact on working families. Teenagers tend to stay up late with homework and phones, which leaves them too drained at eight to absorb much of the first lesson. An extra hour of sleep would probably sharpen their concentration and lift their mood. Admittedly, the parents' concern is legitimate: those who leave for work early may have nobody to take their children in, and bus timetables would need rearranging. Yet these difficulties are manageable. Were the school to open its gate early and have teachers supervise arriving students, the disruption to working households would be considerably reduced.

What changes from one to the next每升一档换了什么

  • The plain answer simply agrees, while the B2 answer states support with a limiting condition introduced by 'provided that'.基础版只是简单表示同意，B2 版则用 'provided that' 加上了一个限制条件来表达支持。
  • The B2 'If... opened' conditional becomes a fronted inversion ('Were the school to open'), and 'let teachers supervise students there' becomes the tighter 'have teachers supervise arriving students'.B2 版的 'If... opened' 条件句在 C1 版变成了倒装句 'Were the school to open'，同时 'let teachers supervise students there' 也被更紧凑的 'have teachers supervise arriving students' 取代。
