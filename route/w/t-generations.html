👂 Thursday: listen first, then read周四：先听懂，再看文字

“A Lunch-Break Chat” is a text you have not read yet.「午休闲聊」：这一篇你还没读过。 Three steps: listen without the text, answer, say the main points. Then open the text to check and shadow it.三步：不看文字听，答题，说出要点；然后打开原文核对并跟读。

1 · Answer without looking不看文字答题

1 Why is Ray worried?

Ray says he feels like he is being left behind.雷说他觉得自己被落下了。

2 What does Dana suggest?

Dana tells Ray to ask the interns for help and to teach them something in return.黛娜建议雷请实习生帮忙，并以教他们作为回报。

2 · Say the main points说出要点

3 · Now look at the text再看原文

📄 Show the text显示原文

- Dana: You look tired, Ray. Did the new software cause trouble again? Ray: Yes, it did. I have been using the old system for twenty years, and now everything has changed. I feel like I am being left behind, and the interns seem to understand it without even trying. - Dana: I know what you mean, but you are not alone. When I started here, my manager used to say that young people did not know how to work hard. Now I am the one who hears that I am too old-fashioned. Ray: Really? You? Dana: Yes. It seems that every generation looks down on the next, and then gets looked down on in turn. - Ray: So what do I do? Dana: Ask the interns for help. They enjoy it, and you can teach them something in return. Last week one of them showed me a shortcut that saved me an hour, and I explained how to calm an angry client. Ray: That sounds like a fair deal, if they have the patience. - Ray: Thanks, Dana. I was afraid you would laugh at me. Dana: Never. Nobody is born knowing everything, whatever their age. If we can find some common ground, work gets easier for everyone. Ray: All right, I will try. Maybe you could sit with me this afternoon? Dana: Of course. Bring your coffee, and we will start after lunch.
