From intermediate to advanced, one week at a time从中级到高级：一周一个主题，一步一步走

Start here从这里开始

Ten minutes, three short parts. You get a starting station and a first week, not a grade.十分钟，三小段。得到的是起点和第一周的安排，不是一个分数。

🧭 Find my starting point找到我的起点 I know my level: choose a station我知道自己的水平，直接选一站

🛤 The route: three stations路线：三站

Each station is about three months at 40 minutes a day. You move on when you can do the exit task, not when you have finished a book. To choose, press “Start at this station” on one of the three.每站大约三个月（每天 40 分钟）。做得到出站任务就往下走，不是学完哪本书才算。直接选：在三站里点「从这一站开始」。

📈 How you will know it is working怎么知道自己在进步

  • Meanings you can tell apart分得清的意思–Counted: meanings you got right twice or more in word study, per meaning, not words ticked off.口径：单词学习里答对过两次以上的意思，按意思数，不是点过的词数。Starts from your first word-study session.从第一次单词学习开始数。
  • Drafts kept留下的稿子–Counted: drafts you kept on a Saturday; the same question weeks apart is the evidence.口径：周六留下的稿子；同一道题隔几周并排看，就是证据。Starts from your first Saturday.从第一个周六开始数。
  • Sentence shapes in your writing你写出来的句子形状–Counted: kinds of sentence shape, first draft against latest draft.口径：句子形状的种类，第一稿对最近一稿。Needs two kept drafts.要有两稿才有对比。
  • Weeks finished走完的周–Counted: weeks with every core task ticked; no streaks.口径：核心任务全勾上的周；不计连续天数。Starts from your first week.从第一周开始数。
