How to speak so that people want to listen

🧩① Before you listen: chunks听前：词块 9

All example sentences are ours. Say each one aloud, then add them to review: the card shows the sentence with the chunk blanked out.例句都是本站自写的。每句读出声，再加入复习：卡片正面是挖掉词块的这句话。

fall into a habit
养成（坏）习惯
It's easy to fall into the habit of checking your phone during meetings.
开会时看手机，这个习惯很容易就养成了。
speak ill of
说……的坏话
In a small office, never speak ill of a colleague who isn't there.
小办公室里，千万别说不在场同事的坏话。
take responsibility for
为……负责
A good manager takes responsibility for the team's mistakes.
好经理会为团队的错误负责。
be straight with
对……直说
Be straight with your landlord about when you can pay.
什么时候能交房租，跟房东直说。
stand in your own truth
做真实的自己（坚持自我）
At the interview she stood in her own truth and talked about the gap in her career.
面试时她坦然做自己，谈了职业生涯中的空档。
put something in context
放进背景里看
Let me put that number in context: it's half of last year's figure.
我把这个数字放到背景里讲：它只有去年的一半。
warm up
热身
Warm up your voice before a phone interview, even if nobody can see you.
电话面试前也要给嗓子热身，哪怕没人看得见你。
on one note
一个调子到底
He read the report on one note, and half the room stopped listening.
他用一个调子念完报告，半屋子人都不听了。
fit for purpose
适合用途的
The old meeting room is not fit for purpose: it echoes.
那间旧会议室不合用：有回声。

🔁⑤ Shadowing lines跟读句

Our own sentences that sum up the talk, read by your device voice. Listen, record yourself, compare. Then shadow a minute of the real talk in the same way.本站自写的、概括演讲观点的句子，用设备语音朗读。听、录、对比。然后用同样方法跟读原演讲的一分钟。

  • It's hard to listen to someone who complains about everything. 一个人什么都抱怨，听他说话很累。
  • Don't present your opinions as if they were facts. 别把你的观点当成事实来讲。
  • Be honest, but wish people well while you're doing it. 要诚实，但同时要怀着善意。
  • Slow down when you want people to notice something. 想让人注意某件事时，放慢速度。
  • A short pause can say more than a long sentence. 一个短暂的停顿，有时胜过一长句话。
  • Before any important talk, take two minutes to warm up your voice. 任何重要的谈话前，花两分钟热热嗓子。

🎙⑥ Speak: CELPIP-style tasks开口：仿思培口语题

Tasks written for this site in the style of the CELPIP speaking test, on the talk's theme. Reuse at least four chunks from section ①. Recordings stay on this device.本站按思培口语形式自写的题，主题来自这场演讲。至少用上第①节的四个词块。录音只留在本机。

Task任务 1 · Giving Advice给出建议 prepare准备 30 s · speak作答 90 s

Your friend has to give a short speech at her brother's wedding and is afraid people will not listen. Give her advice on how to use her voice.朋友要在弟弟婚礼上讲几句话，怕没人听。给她建议，怎样用好声音。

Task任务 6 · Dealing with a Difficult Situation处理棘手情况 prepare准备 60 s · speak作答 60 s

A colleague often complains loudly about the company in the open office, and it is affecting the team. Talk to him about it.一位同事常在开放办公区大声抱怨公司，影响了整个团队。和他谈谈这件事。

Task任务 3 · Describing a Scene描述场景 prepare准备 30 s · speak作答 60 s

Describe a busy open-plan office during a Monday morning meeting: who is speaking, who is listening and who is not.描述周一早会时一间忙碌的开放式办公室：谁在讲、谁在听、谁没在听。
