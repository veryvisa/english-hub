🧩① Before you listen: chunks听前：词块 9

All example sentences are ours. Say each one aloud, then add them to review: the card shows the sentence with the chunk blanked out.例句都是本站自写的。每句读出声，再加入复习：卡片正面是挖掉词块的这句话。

move from place to place
四处搬家
As students we moved from place to place every September.
读书时我们每年九月都要搬一次家。
plenty of room
足够的空间
The new kitchen has plenty of room for two cooks.
新厨房两个人做饭都绰绰有余。
credit card debt
信用卡债
Holiday shopping left many families with credit card debt.
节日购物让不少家庭背上信用卡债。
not coincidentally
并非巧合地
Rents went up, and not coincidentally, more young people moved back home.
房租涨了，不是巧合，更多年轻人搬回了父母家。
let it go
放手、舍弃
I hadn't used the bread maker in three years, so I let it go.
面包机三年没用了，我就舍了。
think before you buy
买之前想一想
Think before you buy: will you still use it next year?
买之前想一想：明年你还会用吗？
by all means
当然可以、尽管
By all means buy good shoes; just don't buy six pairs.
好鞋尽管买，别一买六双就行。
fold away
折叠收起
The desk folds away into the wall when guests come.
来客人时，书桌可以折起收进墙里。
make room for
为……腾出空间
We sold the old sofa to make room for a bookshelf.
我们卖掉旧沙发，给书架腾地方。

🔁⑤ Shadowing lines跟读句

Our own sentences that sum up the talk, read by your device voice. Listen, record yourself, compare. Then shadow a minute of the real talk in the same way.本站自写的、概括演讲观点的句子，用设备语音朗读。听、录、对比。然后用同样方法跟读原演讲的一分钟。

  • We have more space than ever, and we still run out of room. 我们的空间比以前多，却仍然不够放东西。
  • Owning less can give you more time and more freedom. 东西少了，时间和自由就多了。
  • If you haven't used it in years, it may be time to let it go. 好几年没用的东西，也许该舍了。
  • Buy things you'll love for years, not just more things. 买能喜欢很多年的东西，而不是更多东西。
  • A smaller home can mean lower bills and less cleaning. 房子小一点，账单少一点，打扫也少一点。
  • You don't have to live in a tiny flat; just try a little less. 不一定要住小房子，试着少一点就行。

🎙⑥ Speak: CELPIP-style tasks开口：仿思培口语题

Tasks written for this site in the style of the CELPIP speaking test, on the talk's theme. Reuse at least four chunks from section ①. Recordings stay on this device.本站按思培口语形式自写的题，主题来自这场演讲。至少用上第①节的四个词块。录音只留在本机。

Task任务 5 · Comparing and Persuading比较与说服 prepare准备 60 s · speak作答 60 s

Your family is choosing between a large house far from the city and a small condo downtown. Choose one and persuade your partner.你家要在城外大房子和市中心小公寓之间选。选一个并说服你的伴侣。

Task任务 1 · Giving Advice给出建议 prepare准备 30 s · speak作答 90 s

Your friend is moving from a three-bedroom house into a one-bedroom apartment. Give her advice on what to keep and what to let go.朋友要从三居室的房子搬进一居室公寓。给她建议：留什么、舍什么。

Task任务 4 · Making Predictions做出预测 prepare准备 30 s · speak作答 60 s

Rents in your city keep rising. Predict how this will change the way young people live over the next ten years.你所在城市的房租持续上涨。预测这会怎样改变年轻人未来十年的生活方式。

Task任务 8 · Describing an Unusual Situation描述不寻常的情况 prepare准备 30 s · speak作答 60 s

You are visiting a friend's very small apartment where the bed folds down from the wall and the coffee table rises to become a dining table. Call your sister, who has never seen anything like it, and describe the apartment so she can picture it.你去朋友的小公寓，床从墙里翻下来，茶几能升高变成餐桌。给从没见过这种东西的妹妹打电话，描述这间公寓，让她能想象出来。
