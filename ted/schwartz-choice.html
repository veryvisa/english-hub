The paradox of choice

🧩① Before you listen: chunks听前：词块 9

All example sentences are ours. Say each one aloud, then add them to review: the card shows the sentence with the chunk blanked out.例句都是本站自写的。每句读出声，再加入复习：卡片正面是挖掉词块的这句话。

on offer
可供选择的
There are twelve phone plans on offer, and I can't tell them apart.
有十二种话费套餐可选，我分不清有什么区别。
a matter of choice
一个选择问题
Where you work is now a matter of choice for many office staff.
对很多文员来说，在哪儿办公现在是可以选的。
shift the burden
转移负担
The new system shifts the burden of checking errors onto customers.
新系统把查错的负担转到了顾客身上。
up for grabs
谁都可以争取的、悬而未决的
With the manager leaving, her job is up for grabs.
经理要走了，她的职位谁都可以争。
put it off
推迟
The dentist? I kept putting it off until the pain was too bad.
看牙？我一直拖着，直到疼得受不了。
pass up
错过、放弃（机会）
Don't pass up free money from your employer's pension matching.
别错过雇主配比养老金这笔白给的钱。
opportunity cost
机会成本
The opportunity cost of a long commute is the time you could spend with your kids.
长通勤的机会成本，是本可以陪孩子的时间。
go through the roof
飙升
Prices for rentals near the university have gone through the roof.
大学附近的租房价格飙升。
settle for
将就接受
We couldn't find the perfect house, so we settled for a good one.
找不到完美的房子，我们就接受了一套不错的。

🔁⑤ Shadowing lines跟读句

Our own sentences that sum up the talk, read by your device voice. Listen, record yourself, compare. Then shadow a minute of the real talk in the same way.本站自写的、概括演讲观点的句子，用设备语音朗读。听、录、对比。然后用同样方法跟读原演讲的一分钟。

  • More choice doesn't always make people happier. 选择更多不一定让人更快乐。
  • With too many options, many people end up choosing nothing. 选项太多，很多人最后什么都没选。
  • When you can imagine a better option, you start to regret your choice. 一想到可能有更好的，你就开始后悔自己的选择。
  • High expectations make good results feel disappointing. 期望太高，好结果也会让人失望。
  • Some choice is better than none, but more is not always better. 有选择比没有好，但越多不一定越好。
  • Sometimes settling for good enough is the smart move. 有时候接受「够好」才是聪明的做法。

Task任务 5 · Comparing and Persuading比较与说服 prepare准备 60 s · speak作答 60 s

Your friend is choosing a phone plan. One company offers 30 plans; the other offers 3 simple ones at a similar price. Recommend one and persuade him.朋友在选话费套餐：一家有 30 种，另一家只有 3 种简单的，价格差不多。推荐一家并说服他。

Task任务 7 · Expressing Opinions表达观点 prepare准备 30 s · speak作答 90 s

Do people in rich countries have too many choices? Give your opinion with examples from daily life in Canada.富裕国家的人是否选择太多？结合加拿大日常生活举例说出你的看法。

Task任务 6 · Dealing with a Difficult Situation处理棘手情况 prepare准备 60 s · speak作答 60 s
