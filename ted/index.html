TED talk lessonsTED 演讲课

New Concept English works because one good text is squeezed dry: words, grammar, retelling, summary. These lessons do the same with real talks, for listening and speaking. Each lesson takes about an hour across two days.新概念好在一篇好课文榨干：词、语法、复述、摘要。这里用真实演讲做同样的事，目标是听和说。每课约一小时，分两天完成。

  1. Chunks before listening听前学词块
  2. Listen for gist, then section by section先听大意，再分段听
  3. Answer 10+ questions答 10 道以上的题
  4. Fill the note frame from memory凭记忆填笔记框
  5. Shadow and record跟读并录音
  6. Speak on a CELPIP-style task and rate yourself做仿思培口语题并自评
