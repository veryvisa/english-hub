🧩① Before you listen: chunks听前：词块 8

All example sentences are ours. Say each one aloud, then add them to review: the card shows the sentence with the chunk blanked out.例句都是本站自写的。每句读出声，再加入复习：卡片正面是挖掉词块的这句话。

stuck in a rut
陷入一成不变
After five years in the same job, I felt stuck in a rut.
同一份工作做了五年，我觉得生活一成不变。
follow in the footsteps of
追随……的脚步
She followed in the footsteps of her mother and became a nurse.
她追随母亲的脚步当了护士。
add a habit
养成一个习惯
This month I'm adding one habit: a ten-minute walk after dinner.
这个月我加一个习惯：晚饭后散步十分钟。
from scratch
从零开始
We built the garden from scratch in our first spring here.
来这儿的第一个春天，我们从零开始建了菜园。
sleep-deprived
睡眠不足的
New parents are often sleep-deprived for months.
新手父母常常连着几个月睡眠不足。
stick
（习惯）坚持下来
Small changes are more likely to stick.
小改变更容易坚持下来。
a ton of fun
非常好玩
The winter festival was a ton of fun for the kids.
冬季节庆让孩子们玩得非常开心。
give it a shot
试一试
I've never skated before, but I'll give it a shot.
我从没滑过冰，但我愿意试试。

🔁⑤ Shadowing lines跟读句

Our own sentences that sum up the talk, read by your device voice. Listen, record yourself, compare. Then shadow a minute of the real talk in the same way.本站自写的、概括演讲观点的句子，用设备语音朗读。听、录、对比。然后用同样方法跟读原演讲的一分钟。

  • Thirty days is about the right amount of time to start a new habit. 三十天差不多够养成一个新习惯。
  • Doing something new every day made the month easy to remember. 每天做点新事，这个月就很好记。
  • The more challenges I finished, the more confident I became. 完成的挑战越多，我就越自信。
  • You can do almost anything for thirty days. 三十天里，几乎什么都能坚持。
  • Small changes are easier to keep than big ones. 小改变比大改变更容易坚持。
  • The next thirty days will pass anyway, so why not try something? 反正接下来的三十天也会过去，为什么不试点什么？
