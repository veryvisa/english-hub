🧩① Before you listen: chunks听前：词块 9

All example sentences are ours. Say each one aloud, then add them to review: the card shows the sentence with the chunk blanked out.例句都是本站自写的。每句读出声，再加入复习：卡片正面是挖掉词块的这句话。

spread the work out
把工作分摊开
If you spread the work out over two weeks, the move is much less stressful.
把搬家的活分摊到两周，压力小得多。
a staircase plan
循序渐进的计划
I made a staircase plan for my savings: a little more every three months.
我给储蓄定了个阶梯式计划：每三个月多存一点。
take the wheel
掌控局面
When the project leader got sick, Maria took the wheel.
项目负责人病倒后，玛丽亚接了手。
instant gratification
即时满足
Online shopping is built on instant gratification.
网购建立在即时满足之上。
make sense
合理、讲得通
It makes sense to book flights early for the holidays.
节假日的机票早点订才合理。
wake up
醒来、警觉起来
The team only woke up when the client called to complain.
客户打电话投诉，团队才警觉起来。
a self-starter
主动做事的人
The job ad asks for a self-starter who can work without supervision.
招聘启事要找一个不用人盯也会主动做事的人。
a spectator in your own life
自己人生的旁观者
After ten years in the same job, he felt like a spectator in his own life.
同一份工作做了十年，他觉得自己成了人生的旁观者。
take a long, hard look at
认真审视
It's time to take a long, hard look at our monthly spending.
是时候认真看看我们每个月的开销了。

🔁⑤ Shadowing lines跟读句

Our own sentences that sum up the talk, read by your device voice. Listen, record yourself, compare. Then shadow a minute of the real talk in the same way.本站自写的、概括演讲观点的句子，用设备语音朗读。听、录、对比。然后用同样方法跟读原演讲的一分钟。

  • I always planned to spread the work out, but I never did. 我总打算把工作分摊开，可从来没做到。
  • The Monkey only cares about what's easy and fun right now. 猴子只在乎眼下什么轻松好玩。
  • Deadlines work because they wake up the panic. 截止日期有用，因为它会唤醒恐慌。
  • The real problem is the things that have no deadline at all. 真正的问题是那些根本没有截止日期的事。
  • Seeing life as weeks makes it feel much shorter. 把人生按周来看，会觉得短得多。
  • Maybe not today, but soon. 也许不是今天，但要快。

🎙⑥ Speak: CELPIP-style tasks开口：仿思培口语题

Tasks written for this site in the style of the CELPIP speaking test, on the talk's theme. Reuse at least four chunks from section ①. Recordings stay on this device.本站按思培口语形式自写的题，主题来自这场演讲。至少用上第①节的四个词块。录音只留在本机。

Task任务 1 · Giving Advice给出建议 prepare准备 30 s · speak作答 90 s

Your friend wants to start her own business but keeps putting it off. Give her advice on how to get started.朋友想创业，却一直拖着。给她建议，怎样迈出第一步。

Task任务 2 · Talking about a Personal Experience谈个人经历 prepare准备 30 s · speak作答 60 s

Talk about something important you kept putting off. Why did you delay, and what finally happened?讲一件你一拖再拖的重要事情：为什么拖，最后怎样了？

Task任务 4 · Making Predictions做出预测 prepare准备 30 s · speak作答 60 s

Imagine a student who has three days left to write a long paper and has not started. Predict what will happen in the next three days.想象一个学生离交长论文还剩三天，一个字没写。预测这三天会发生什么。
