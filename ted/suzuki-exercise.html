🧩① Before you listen: chunks听前：词块 9

All example sentences are ours. Say each one aloud, then add them to review: the card shows the sentence with the chunk blanked out.例句都是本站自写的。每句读出声，再加入复习：卡片正面是挖掉词块的这句话。

have a benefit for
对……有好处
Walking to work has a real benefit for your mood.
走路上班对心情真有好处。
switch
转换（方向）
Mid-career, she switched from law to teaching.
职业中期她从法律转行当老师。
gain weight
长胖
I gained weight in my first winter here: five kilos.
来这里的第一个冬天我长胖了，足足五公斤。
sit up and take notice
开始重视
The rise in rent made the city council sit up and take notice.
房租上涨让市议会开始重视。
put it together
想明白、联系起来
When my headaches stopped on holiday, I finally put it together: it was the screen.
度假时头疼没了，我终于想明白：是屏幕的问题。
last for
持续
The effect of one workout lasts for at least two hours.
一次锻炼的效果至少持续两小时。
be susceptible to
容易受……影响
In icy weather, older adults are susceptible to falls.
结冰天气里，老人容易摔倒。
a rule of thumb
经验法则
A rule of thumb: if you can talk but not sing, you're exercising hard enough.
经验法则：能说话但唱不了歌，运动强度就够了。
get your heart rate up
让心率升上去
Take the stairs if you want to get your heart rate up without a gym.
不去健身房也想提心率，就走楼梯。

🔁⑤ Shadowing lines跟读句

Our own sentences that sum up the talk, read by your device voice. Listen, record yourself, compare. Then shadow a minute of the real talk in the same way.本站自写的、概括演讲观点的句子，用设备语音朗读。听、录、对比。然后用同样方法跟读原演讲的一分钟。

  • Moving your body has an immediate effect on your mood. 动起来，心情马上就不一样。
  • One workout can help you focus for the next two hours. 一次锻炼能让你接下来两小时更专注。
  • Regular exercise helps the brain grow new cells. 规律运动帮助大脑长出新细胞。
  • It won't cure dementia, but it can delay the damage. 它治不了痴呆，但能推迟损伤。
  • Three or four times a week, half an hour each time, is enough to start. 每周三四次、每次半小时，就足够起步。
  • You don't need a gym; take the stairs. 不需要健身房，走楼梯就行。
