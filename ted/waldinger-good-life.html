🧩① Before you listen: chunks听前：词块 9

All example sentences are ours. Say each one aloud, then add them to review: the card shows the sentence with the chunk blanked out.例句都是本站自写的。每句读出声，再加入复习：卡片正面是挖掉词块的这句话。

lean in to
全身心投入
After the move, we decided to lean in to the new neighbourhood instead of complaining about it.
搬家后我们决定投入新社区，而不是抱怨它。
fall apart
散架、失败
Most book clubs fall apart within a year because people stop showing up.
多数读书会一年内就散了，因为大家不再来。
dry up
枯竭
The project ended when the funding dried up.
经费断了，项目也就停了。
from all walks of life
各行各业、各种背景的人
The volunteers came from all walks of life, from students to retired judges.
志愿者来自各行各业，从学生到退休法官。
it turns out that
结果发现
It turns out that the quiet neighbour downstairs used to be a chef.
结果发现楼下那位安静的邻居以前是厨师。
take a toll on
造成损害
Night shifts started to take a toll on his health.
夜班开始损害他的健康。
count on
依靠、指望
You can count on her to show up when you're moving house.
你搬家时可以指望她来帮忙。
a quick fix
速效办法
There is no quick fix for loneliness; it takes regular contact.
孤独没有速效药，靠的是经常来往。
hold a grudge
记仇
My uncle held a grudge against his brother for twenty years over a small loan.
我叔叔为一笔小钱记恨了他哥哥二十年。

🔁⑤ Shadowing lines跟读句

Our own sentences that sum up the talk, read by your device voice. Listen, record yourself, compare. Then shadow a minute of the real talk in the same way.本站自写的、概括演讲观点的句子，用设备语音朗读。听、录、对比。然后用同样方法跟读原演讲的一分钟。

  • The study followed the same men from their teens into old age. 这项研究从少年时期一直跟踪同一批人到老年。
  • It isn't the number of friends that matters, but how close they are. 重要的不是朋友多少，而是关系多近。
  • Loneliness turns out to be bad for both body and mind. 结果发现，孤独对身体和心理都有害。
  • People who could count on their partner kept their memory sharper for longer. 能依靠伴侣的人，记忆力保持得更久。
  • There's no quick fix; relationships need attention for a lifetime. 没有速效办法，关系需要用一辈子去经营。
  • Maybe it's time to call that relative you haven't spoken to in years. 也许是时候给那位多年没联系的亲戚打个电话了。

🎙⑥ Speak: CELPIP-style tasks开口：仿思培口语题

Tasks written for this site in the style of the CELPIP speaking test, on the talk's theme. Reuse at least four chunks from section ①. Recordings stay on this device.本站按思培口语形式自写的题，主题来自这场演讲。至少用上第①节的四个词块。录音只留在本机。

Task任务 1 · Giving Advice给出建议 prepare准备 30 s · speak作答 90 s

Your neighbour retired last month and says he feels bored and lonely. Give him advice on how to build a new social life.你的邻居上个月退休了，说觉得无聊又孤单。给他建议，怎样建立新的社交生活。

Task任务 5 · Comparing and Persuading比较与说服 prepare准备 60 s · speak作答 60 s

Your friend has two job offers: one pays more but means moving far from family; the other pays less and is close to home. Choose one and persuade her.朋友手上有两份工作：一份钱多但要远离家人，一份钱少但离家近。选一份并说服她。

Task任务 7 · Expressing Opinions表达观点 prepare准备 30 s · speak作答 90 s

Do you think people today are lonelier than people 30 years ago? Explain why.你认为今天的人比 30 年前的人更孤独吗？说明理由。
