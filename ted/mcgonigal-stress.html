How to make stress your friend

Kelly McGonigal · TEDGlobal 2013

B2 · CELPIP 7–8⏱ 14:15Topic:主题： Sleep, stress and mental health睡眠、压力与心理健康Speaking口语 1Speaking口语 2Speaking口语 7

🎧 Why this talk为什么选这一讲
A clear research talk built on two studies with numbers. It trains the CELPIP skill of catching a claim, the evidence and the twist ("but that was only true for...").一场靠两项研究和几组数字撑起来的讲座。练的正是思培听力要的：抓住主张、证据，以及「但这只对……成立」这种转折。

🧩① Before you listen: chunks听前：词块 9

All example sentences are ours. Say each one aloud, then add them to review: the card shows the sentence with the chunk blanked out.例句都是本站自写的。每句读出声，再加入复习：卡片正面是挖掉词块的这句话。

change my mind about
改变对……的看法
I used to hate public transit, but living downtown changed my mind about it.
我以前讨厌公交，但住到市中心后改变了看法。
do more harm than good
弊大于利
Checking your work email at midnight probably does more harm than good.
半夜查工作邮件大概弊大于利。
be associated with
与……有关联
In our staff survey, long commutes were associated with lower job satisfaction.
在我们的员工调查里，通勤时间长与工作满意度低有关联。
rise to the challenge
迎接挑战、顶上去
Nobody expected the new hire to rise to the challenge so quickly.
谁都没想到新同事这么快就顶上去了。
reach out to
主动联系、求助
When my car broke down, I reached out to a neighbour I had barely spoken to.
车坏了的时候，我去找了一位几乎没说过话的邻居帮忙。
bottle it up
憋在心里
If something at work is bothering you, don't bottle it up for months.
工作上有事让你烦，别憋几个月。
no more likely to
并不比……更可能
In our team, people who work from home are no more likely to miss deadlines than people in the office.
在我们组，在家办公的人并不比在办公室的人更容易误期。
a built-in mechanism
内置的机制
The app has a built-in mechanism that stops you from booking two rooms at once.
这个应用有个内置机制，防止你同时订两间房。
chase meaning
追求意义
She left a well-paid job to chase meaning in community work.
她辞掉高薪工作，去社区工作里找意义。

🎧② Listening plan听力计划

Listen once straight through for the gist. Then listen section by section, pausing at each time stamp to answer the questions.先完整听一遍抓大意；再按段听，每到一个时间点停下来答题。

  • 0:28-2:57 The speaker's confession and the first study: who took part, for how long, and the result with its condition.讲者的「坦白」和第一项研究：谁参加、多长时间、结果和它的前提。
  • 3:10-5:01 The social stress test: what participants had to do.社会压力测试：参加者要做什么。
  • 5:01-6:41 The Harvard study: what people were taught, and what happened to their blood vessels.哈佛研究：参加者被教了什么，血管有什么变化。
  • 7:18-10:39 Oxytocin: why she calls stress social.催产素：她为什么说压力让人更想与人连接。
  • 10:39-11:48 The last study: helping others and the risk of dying.最后一项研究：帮助别人与死亡风险。
  • 13:20-14:03 The question and answer: how to choose between a stressful and a calm job.问答：在高压工作和轻松工作之间怎么选。

🔁⑤ Shadowing lines跟读句

Our own sentences that sum up the talk, read by your device voice. Listen, record yourself, compare. Then shadow a minute of the real talk in the same way.本站自写的、概括演讲观点的句子，用设备语音朗读。听、录、对比。然后用同样方法跟读原演讲的一分钟。

  • How you think about stress can change how your body reacts to it. 你怎么看待压力，会改变身体对压力的反应。
  • A pounding heart can be a sign that your body is getting ready to act. 心跳加速可能说明身体正在为行动做准备。
  • The risk only went up for people who believed stress was harmful. 风险只在相信压力有害的人身上升高。
  • When you're under pressure, it's natural to want to reach out to someone. 压力大时想找人说说，是很自然的。
  • Helping other people seems to protect us from the effects of stress. 帮助别人似乎能保护我们不受压力的伤害。
  • Go after what gives your life meaning, and trust yourself to cope. 去追求让生活有意义的事，并相信自己应付得来。

🎙⑥ Speak: CELPIP-style tasks开口：仿思培口语题

Tasks written for this site in the style of the CELPIP speaking test, on the talk's theme. Reuse at least four chunks from section ①. Recordings stay on this device.本站按思培口语形式自写的题，主题来自这场演讲。至少用上第①节的四个词块。录音只留在本机。

Task任务 1 · Giving Advice给出建议 prepare准备 30 s · speak作答 90 s

A coworker is very nervous about giving a presentation to senior managers next week. Give her advice on how to prepare and how to think about her nerves.一位同事下周要给高层做汇报，非常紧张。给她建议：怎么准备，以及怎么看待自己的紧张。

Task任务 2 · Talking about a Personal Experience谈个人经历 prepare准备 30 s · speak作答 60 s

Talk about a time you were under a lot of pressure. What was happening, how did you handle it, and what did you learn?讲一次你压力很大的经历：发生了什么、你怎么应对的、学到了什么。

Task任务 7 · Expressing Opinions表达观点 prepare准备 30 s · speak作答 90 s

Some people say a stressful job with meaning is better than an easy job without it. Do you agree? Give reasons and examples.有人说有意义但压力大的工作，胜过轻松但没意义的工作。你同意吗？给出理由和例子。
