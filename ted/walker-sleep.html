🧩① Before you listen: chunks听前：词块 9

All example sentences are ours. Say each one aloud, then add them to review: the card shows the sentence with the chunk blanked out.例句都是本站自写的。每句读出声，再加入复习：卡片正面是挖掉词块的这句话。

pull an all-nighter
熬通宵
I pulled an all-nighter to finish the tax forms, and I made three mistakes.
我熬通宵填完报税表，结果错了三处。
hit the save button
存档、记牢
A short review before bed is like hitting the save button on what you studied.
睡前快速复习一遍，就像给学过的内容按下保存键。
soak up
吸收
Kids soak up a new language from the playground faster than from textbooks.
孩子在操场上吸收新语言，比从课本里快。
head to head
正面比较
When you put the two phone plans head to head, the cheaper one has more data.
两个话费套餐正面一比，便宜的那个流量反而多。
a silver lining
坏事中的好处
Losing the job had a silver lining: I finally finished my certificate.
丢了工作也有好处：我终于把证书读完了。
catch up on
补上（落下的事）
I spent the weekend catching up on emails I had ignored.
我花了整个周末补回那些没处理的邮件。
non-negotiable
没得商量的
For our family, dinner together on Sunday is non-negotiable.
在我们家，周日一起吃晚饭是雷打不动的。
get away unscathed
毫发无损
Few small businesses got away unscathed when the road was closed for a year.
那条路封了一年，小店很少有毫发无损的。
break the association
打破联想
If you work in bed, you need to break the association between bed and work.
如果你在床上办公，就要打破「床＝工作」的联想。

🔁⑤ Shadowing lines跟读句

Our own sentences that sum up the talk, read by your device voice. Listen, record yourself, compare. Then shadow a minute of the real talk in the same way.本站自写的、概括演讲观点的句子，用设备语音朗读。听、录、对比。然后用同样方法跟读原演讲的一分钟。

  • Sleep before learning gets your brain ready to take in new information. 学习前睡好，大脑才准备好接收新信息。
  • Without enough sleep, new memories don't stick. 睡眠不够，新记忆就留不住。
  • Even one short night can weaken your immune system. 哪怕只短睡一晚，也会削弱免疫系统。
  • Going to bed and getting up at the same time is the first rule. 同一时间睡、同一时间起，是第一条规则。
  • A cool bedroom makes it easier to fall asleep. 卧室凉一点更容易入睡。
  • If you can't sleep, get up and come back when you feel sleepy. 睡不着就起来，困了再回床上。

Task任务 1 · Giving Advice给出建议 prepare准备 30 s · speak作答 90 s

Your cousin is a university student who often studies all night before exams. Give him advice about sleep and studying.你的表弟是大学生，考试前常通宵复习。就睡眠和学习给他建议。

Task任务 4 · Making Predictions做出预测 prepare准备 30 s · speak作答 60 s

Many workplaces in your city are moving to four-day weeks with longer days. Predict how this will affect workers' sleep and health.你所在城市很多单位改成每周四天、每天更长。预测这会怎样影响员工的睡眠与健康。

Task任务 6 · Dealing with a Difficult Situation处理棘手情况 prepare准备 60 s · speak作答 60 s

Your manager wants you to work night shifts for two months. You are worried about your health. Talk to your manager and suggest another arrangement.经理要你连上两个月夜班，你担心健康。和经理谈，提出别的安排。

Task任务 7 · Expressing Opinions表达观点 prepare准备 30 s · speak作答 90 s

Should schools start later in the morning so teenagers can sleep more? Give your opinion.学校该不该推迟上课时间，让青少年多睡一点？说说你的观点。
