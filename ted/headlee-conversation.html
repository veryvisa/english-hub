🧩① Before you listen: chunks听前：词块 9

All example sentences are ours. Say each one aloud, then add them to review: the card shows the sentence with the chunk blanked out.例句都是本站自写的。每句读出声，再加入复习：卡片正面是挖掉词块的这句话。

be present
用心在场
Put the phone away and be present when your kids tell you about their day.
孩子讲他们这一天时，把手机放下，用心听。
state your opinion
表明观点
At the meeting, everyone got two minutes to state their opinion.
会上每人有两分钟表明观点。
take a cue from
学……的做法
Take a cue from good nurses: ask one clear question at a time.
学学好护士的做法：一次只问一个清楚的问题。
go with the flow
顺其自然
The plan changed three times, so we just went with the flow.
计划改了三次，我们就顺其自然了。
err on the side of caution
宁可谨慎
If you're not sure the meat is cooked, err on the side of caution.
不确定肉熟没熟，就宁可谨慎一点。
equate A with B
把 A 等同于 B
Don't equate a quiet student with a weak student.
别把安静的学生等同于差学生。
stay out of the weeds
别陷入细节
In a two-minute update, stay out of the weeds and give the result first.
两分钟的汇报里别陷进细节，先说结果。
with the intent to
抱着……的目的
He asked the question with the intent to embarrass me.
他问那个问题，是存心让我难堪。
be prepared to be amazed
准备好被惊艳
Talk to the older people in your building, and be prepared to be amazed.
和你楼里的老人聊聊，准备好被惊艳吧。

🔁⑤ Shadowing lines跟读句

Our own sentences that sum up the talk, read by your device voice. Listen, record yourself, compare. Then shadow a minute of the real talk in the same way.本站自写的、概括演讲观点的句子，用设备语音朗读。听、录、对比。然后用同样方法跟读原演讲的一分钟。

  • If you want to leave the conversation, leave it; don't be half in it. 想离开对话就离开，别人在心不在。
  • Everyone you meet knows something you don't. 你遇到的每个人都懂一些你不懂的东西。
  • Ask "What was that like?" and let them describe it. 问一句「那是什么感觉？」，让对方自己说。
  • If you don't know, just say you don't know. 不知道就直说不知道。
  • It's not about you, so don't turn their story into yours. 这不是关于你的，别把对方的故事变成你的。
  • Keep your mouth shut and your mind open. 少开口，多开放心态。

Tasks written for this site in the style of the CELPIP speaking test, on the talk's theme. Reuse at least four chunks from section ①. Recordings stay on this device.本站按思培口语形式自写的题，主题来自这场演讲。至少用上第①节的四个词块。录音只留在本机。

Task任务 1 · Giving Advice给出建议 prepare准备 30 s · speak作答 90 s

A friend says people at her new job think she is unfriendly because she is quiet at lunch. Give her advice on starting and keeping conversations.朋友说新同事觉得她不友好，因为午饭时她不说话。给她建议，怎样开启并维持聊天。

Task任务 6 · Dealing with a Difficult Situation处理棘手情况 prepare准备 60 s · speak作答 60 s

Your neighbour always talks about himself and never lets you finish a sentence. He has invited you to a long dinner. Decide whether to go, and explain your decision to him politely.你的邻居总是只讲自己、从不让你把话说完。他请你去吃一顿很长的晚饭。决定去不去，并礼貌地跟他说明。

Task任务 7 · Expressing Opinions表达观点 prepare准备 30 s · speak作答 90 s

Do smartphones make people worse at face-to-face conversation? Give your opinion with examples.智能手机是否让人们更不会当面聊天？举例说出你的看法。
