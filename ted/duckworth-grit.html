🧩① Before you listen: chunks听前：词块 9

All example sentences are ours. Say each one aloud, then add them to review: the card shows the sentence with the chunk blanked out.例句都是本站自写的。每句读出声，再加入复习：卡片正面是挖掉词块的这句话。

a demanding job
要求很高的工作
Nursing is a demanding job, especially on night shifts.
护士是很辛苦的工作，夜班尤其如此。
drop out
退学、中途退出
Two people dropped out of the training course after the first week.
培训课第一周后有两人退出了。
a predictor of
……的预测指标
Attendance is a strong predictor of who passes the course.
出勤率是谁能通过课程的有力预测指标。
stick with
坚持（做某事）
If you stick with the piano for a year, it gets easier.
钢琴坚持学一年，就会变容易。
a marathon, not a sprint
是马拉松，不是短跑
Learning English as an adult is a marathon, not a sprint.
成年人学英语是马拉松，不是短跑。
follow through on
把……贯彻到底
He makes big promises but rarely follows through on them.
他许下大承诺，却很少兑现。
a growth mindset
成长型思维
With a growth mindset, a bad test score is information, not a verdict.
有了成长型思维，考砸是信息，不是判决。
be willing to
愿意
You have to be willing to sound silly when you practise speaking.
练口语时你得愿意听起来傻一点。
start over
从头再来
The file was lost, so we had to start over.
文件丢了，我们只好从头再来。

🎧② Listening plan听力计划

Listen once straight through for the gist. Then listen section by section, pausing at each time stamp to answer the questions.先完整听一遍抓大意；再按段听，每到一个时间点停下来答题。

  • 0:00-1:36 Her teaching job and what she noticed about IQ.她的教书经历，以及她对智商的观察。
  • 1:36-2:49 The four settings she studied, and the one predictor.她研究的四种场景，以及那一个预测因素。
  • 2:49-3:57 Her definition of grit and the Chicago study.她对坚毅的定义，以及芝加哥研究。
  • 3:57-4:40 What science does not know, and how talent relates to grit.科学还不知道什么，天赋与坚毅的关系。
  • 4:40-5:50 Growth mindset, and her call to test ideas.成长型思维，以及她呼吁检验想法。

🔁⑤ Shadowing lines跟读句

Our own sentences that sum up the talk, read by your device voice. Listen, record yourself, compare. Then shadow a minute of the real talk in the same way.本站自写的、概括演讲观点的句子，用设备语音朗读。听、录、对比。然后用同样方法跟读原演讲的一分钟。

  • Being smart is not the same as doing well. 聪明不等于做得好。
  • The students who kept going were the ones who graduated. 坚持下去的学生才是毕业的那批人。
  • Grit means sticking with a goal for years, not weeks. 坚毅是坚持一个目标好几年，而不是几周。
  • Talented people don't always follow through. 有天赋的人不一定能坚持到底。
  • If you believe ability can grow, failure feels less permanent. 如果你相信能力可以成长，失败就不那么像定局。
  • We have to test our ideas and be ready to start over. 我们要检验自己的想法，并准备好从头再来。

Task任务 1 · Giving Advice给出建议 prepare准备 30 s · speak作答 90 s

Your nephew wants to quit his piano lessons after three months because he is not improving. Give him advice.你的侄子学钢琴三个月没进步，想放弃。给他建议。

Task任务 7 · Expressing Opinions表达观点 prepare准备 30 s · speak作答 90 s

Is hard work more important than talent for success in a new country? Give your opinion.在一个新国家，成功靠勤奋还是靠天赋更多？说说你的观点。

Task任务 2 · Talking about a Personal Experience谈个人经历 prepare准备 30 s · speak作答 60 s
