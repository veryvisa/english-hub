🎧 Listen by topic窄听：按主题连着听

One new topic a day means one set of new words a day, met once and lost. Stay with a topic for a week and the same chunks come back in the second text, the third and the fifth. By then you hear them without trying.每天换一个主题，等于每天见一批新词，见一次就忘。同一个主题连着听一周，同样的词块在第二篇、第三篇、第五篇里会再出现，到那时不费力就听得出来。

First time: listen without the text. Second time: open the text and follow along. After that: dictation for the lines you missed, then shadowing. The readings are AI voices in British English.第一遍不看文字；第二遍打开文章跟着看；之后把没听出来的句子拿来听写，再跟读。朗读是英式 AI 合成音。
