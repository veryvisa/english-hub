🏋️ Sentence gym句式健身房

An accountant writes in a report, “The office closed and we lost a week of work.” Nothing is wrong with it, and nothing in it tells the reader what mattered. Rebuilt, it can point anywhere she likes: “It was the office closure that cost us a week,” or “The closure of the office cost us a week of work.” Advanced writers and speakers do not know more grammar than she does; they choose between structures like these on purpose. Each section below takes one structure, shows what it adds and when it backfires, and gives you plain sentences to rebuild.一位会计在报告里写：「The office closed and we lost a week of work.」这句没毛病，可读者看不出哪一点最要紧。换个结构，焦点就能放到她想放的地方：「It was the office closure that cost us a week」，或者「The closure of the office cost us a week of work」。高水平的人懂的语法不一定比她多，他们是有意在这些结构之间做选择。下面每一节讲一种结构：它多了什么、什么时候用了反而坏事，再给你一组平淡句来改写。

Levels: CEFR with the CELPIP / CLB band beside it; they correspond only roughly. Get one rewrite right in a structure and it becomes a review card, counted as one of today’s new points.级别：CEFR 旁边标思培／CLB 分级，只是大致对应。一种结构里做对一道改写，它就变成一张复习卡，算作今天的一个新知识点。

Inversion after negative adverbs 否定副词后的倒装

C1 · CELPIP 9+ formal正式 📏 Inversion after negative adverbials否定副词后的倒装

🎯 What it does它传达什么
Moving never, rarely, not only or only when to the front, with the auxiliary before the subject, puts a spotlight on how unusual or how strong something is; it sounds formal and a little dramatic.把 never、rarely、not only、only when 提到句首、助动词放到主语前，是给「多么少见、多么强烈」打聚光灯；听起来正式，带一点戏剧感。

⚠️ When not to use it什么场合不该用 In everyday chat, a text message or a quick reply at work it sounds like a speech. Use it once in a paragraph, where the surprise really is, and never twice in a row.日常聊天、短信、工作上的随口回复里用，像在演讲。一段里只用一次，用在真正出乎意料的地方，别连着用。

⚖️ Same idea, three ways同一个意思，三组对比

  • ⚪ I have never seen the clinic this busy. 我从没见过诊所这么忙。
    ↳ A plain report of experience.平平地讲经历。
  • 🔵 Never have I seen the clinic this busy. 我从没见过诊所这么忙。
    ↳ The front position makes "never" the news: this is a striking first.句首的 never 成了重点：头一回见到，很惊人。
  • ⚪ We only understood the letter when a neighbour translated it. 直到邻居帮我们翻译，我们才看懂那封信。
    ↳ Neutral, the time clause is just background.中性，时间从句只是背景。
  • 🔵 Only when a neighbour translated it did we understand the letter. 直到邻居帮我们翻译，我们才看懂那封信。
    ↳ The delay is the point; it suggests how long we were confused.重点在「迟到」：暗示我们糊涂了很久。
  • ⚪ The new contract cuts our hours and also lowers the pay. 新合同既减了我们的工时，又降了工资。
    ↳ Two facts, side by side.两件事并列摆出来。
  • 🔵 Not only does the new contract cut our hours, but it also lowers the pay. 新合同不仅减了我们的工时，还降了工资。
    ↳ Builds up: the second point lands harder than the first.层层加码：第二点比第一点更重。

Conditionals without if: Had I known, Should you need, Were it not for 省略 if 的倒装条件句

C1 · CELPIP 9+ formal正式 📏 Had I known: conditionals without if省略 if 的倒装条件句

🎯 What it does它传达什么
Dropping if and putting had, should or were first makes a condition sound formal and compact; Should you is the polite voice of letters and notices, Had I known the voice of a careful regret.去掉 if、把 had、should、were 放到句首，条件句显得正式、紧凑；Should you 是信函和告示里客气的口吻，Had I known 是郑重的追悔。

⚠️ When not to use it什么场合不该用 With friends it can sound stiff or even mocking. Only had, should and were invert; you cannot say "Did I know" or "Would you need". Negatives stay uncontracted: Had she not, never Hadn't she.跟朋友说话会显得生硬，甚至像在打趣。只有 had、should、were 能这样倒装，不能说 Did I know、Would you need。否定不缩写：Had she not，不说 Hadn't she。

⚖️ Same idea, three ways同一个意思，三组对比

  • ⚪ If you need help with the form, call the front desk. 填表如需帮助，请致电前台。
    ↳ Ordinary, friendly instructions.普通、友好的说明。
  • 🔵 Should you need help with the form, call the front desk. 填表如需帮助，请致电前台。
    ↳ The tone of an official notice: polite and a bit distant.官方告示的口吻：客气，略带距离。
  • ⚪ If I had known about the strike, I would have driven. 要是我知道罢工，我就开车了。
    ↳ A normal past regret.一般的过去追悔。
  • 🔵 Had I known about the strike, I would have driven. 早知道罢工，我就开车了。
    ↳ Tighter and more formal; it suits writing or a measured complaint.更紧凑、更正式，适合书面或有分寸的投诉。
  • ⚪ If the community centre did not exist, many seniors would be alone all week. 要是没有社区中心，很多老人一整周都没人说话。
    ↳ Plain hypothetical.平常的假设。
  • 🔵 Were it not for the community centre, many seniors would be alone all week. 若不是有社区中心，很多老人一整周都没人说话。
    ↳ Puts weight on how much the centre matters.强调社区中心有多重要。

It-cleft: It was the price that... It 分裂句：It was ... that

C1 · CELPIP 9+ neutral中性 📏 Cleft sentences: It was…, What I need is…分裂句

🎯 What it does它传达什么
It is X that... picks one part of the sentence and says "this, and not something else"; it is the natural way to correct someone or to name the real cause, in speech as well as in writing.It is X that... 挑出句子的一部分说「是这个，不是别的」；纠正别人、点出真正原因时最自然，口语书面都用。

⚠️ When not to use it什么场合不该用 If nothing is being contrasted or corrected, it just makes the sentence longer. Two or three in one paragraph sound like an argument.没有在对比或纠正什么的时候，它只会把句子拉长。一段里出现两三个，听着像在吵架。

⚖️ Same idea, three ways同一个意思，三组对比

  • ⚪ The night shift changed my sleep. 夜班改变了我的睡眠。
    ↳ A statement with no particular focus.一句陈述，没有特别的焦点。
  • 🔵 It was the night shift that changed my sleep, not the commute. 改变我睡眠的是夜班，不是通勤。
    ↳ Names the real cause and rules out the other one.点出真正原因，并排除另一个。
  • ⚪ My sister found the mistake. 我姐姐发现了那个错误。
    ↳ Just news.只是告诉你这件事。
  • 🔵 It was my sister who found the mistake. 是我姐姐发现的错误。
    ↳ Answers "who?" and gives her the credit.回答「是谁」，把功劳给她。
  • ⚪ I didn't feel at home until I joined the choir. 直到加入合唱团，我才有了家的感觉。
    ↳ The time is part of the story.时间只是叙述的一部分。
  • 🔵 It was not until I joined the choir that I felt at home. 直到加入合唱团，我才真正有了家的感觉。
    ↳ The moment becomes the point: everything before it was not home.这个时刻成了重点：在此之前都不算家。

What-cleft: What we need is... What 分裂句：What we need is ...

C1 · CELPIP 9+ neutral中性 📏 Cleft sentences: It was…, What I need is…分裂句

🎯 What it does它传达什么
What we need is... builds a little suspense and then delivers the key information at the end, where English puts its weight; it sounds natural in speech, meetings and opinion writing.What we need is... 先吊一下胃口，再把关键信息放到句末，而英语的重心本来就在句末；口语、开会、观点文章里都很自然。

⚠️ When not to use it什么场合不该用 Not for simple facts nobody is waiting to hear ("What I ate was rice"). In formal reports, one per section is plenty. Keep is or was singular even before a plural: What we need is more nurses.别用在没人在等答案的小事上（What I ate was rice）。正式报告里一节用一次就够。后面即使是复数也用单数 is/was：What we need is more nurses。

⚖️ Same idea, three ways同一个意思，三组对比

  • ⚪ We need a clear timetable. 我们需要一张清楚的时间表。
    ↳ A plain request.一句普通的要求。
  • 🔵 What we need is a clear timetable. 我们需要的是一张清楚的时间表。
    ↳ Sets up the point, then lands it at the end: this, above everything else.先铺垫，再在句末落下：要的就是这个，别的都次要。
  • ⚪ The delay annoyed me. 那次延误让我恼火。
    ↳ Neutral report of a feeling.中性地讲一种感受。
  • 🔵 What annoyed me was the delay, not the cost. 让我恼火的是延误，不是花费。
    ↳ Separates the real complaint from the one people assume.把真正的不满和别人以为的不满分开。
  • ⚪ She only wanted a straight answer. 她只想要一个直截了当的回答。
    ↳ Fine, but flat.没错，只是平。
  • 🔵 All she wanted was a straight answer. 她要的不过是一个直截了当的回答。
    ↳ Stresses how small and fair the request was, often with frustration.强调这个要求多么小、多么合理，常带着不满。

Participle clauses: Having finished..., Not knowing..., Built in... 分词从句：Having finished...、Not knowing...、Built in...

C1 · CELPIP 9+ mostly written多见于书面 📏 Participle clauses分词从句

🎯 What it does它传达什么
A participle clause folds a reason, a time or a result into a few words, so the main clause carries the news; it is the tight, smooth sound of good written English and prepared speech.分词从句把原因、时间或结果压成几个词，主句专心讲新信息；这是好的书面英语和准备过的讲话那种紧凑顺滑的声音。

⚠️ When not to use it什么场合不该用 The subject of the participle must be the subject of the main clause: "Walking home, the rain started" says the rain was walking. In casual talk most people use a full clause instead.分词的逻辑主语必须是主句主语：Walking home, the rain started 等于说雨在走路。随意聊天时多数人用完整从句。

⚖️ Same idea, three ways同一个意思，三组对比

  • ⚪ Because I didn't know the area, I booked a hotel near the station. 因为不熟悉那一带，我订了车站附近的酒店。
    ↳ Full reason clause: clear, a little heavy.完整的原因从句：清楚，略显笨重。
  • 🔵 Not knowing the area, I booked a hotel near the station. 不熟悉那一带，我就订了车站附近的酒店。
    ↳ The reason is folded in; the decision is the news.原因被压缩进去，决定才是重点。
  • ⚪ After she had finished her shift, she drove straight to the school. 她下了班就直接开车去学校。
    ↳ Two clauses with the same subject.两个从句，主语相同。
  • 🔵 Having finished her shift, she drove straight to the school. 下了班，她直接开车去了学校。
    ↳ One smooth sentence; having + past participle shows the first action came first.一气呵成；having + 过去分词表示前一个动作先完成。
  • ⚪ The storm knocked out power across the region, and it left thousands without heat. 暴风雨让整个地区停电，成千上万人没有暖气。
    ↳ Two equal events.两件并列的事。
  • 🔵 The storm knocked out power across the region, leaving thousands without heat. 暴风雨让整个地区停电，成千上万人因此没有暖气。
    ↳ The -ing clause at the end reads as the result.句末的 -ing 从句读作结果。
