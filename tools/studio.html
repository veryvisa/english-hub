🎙 Shadowing studio跟读录音室

You can read “Could we push the viewing back to Thursday?” without a pause, yet on the phone it comes out word by word, every word stressed the same. The fix is unglamorous: hear one sentence, say it straight after, hear the two side by side, and do it again until the rhythm stops sounding like yours. Then use the same chunks against the clock.「Could we push the viewing back to Thursday?」读的时候一口气就过去了，打电话时却一个词一个词往外蹦，每个词都一样重。办法并不花哨：听一句，紧跟着说一句，两段放在一起听，反复到节奏不再像你原来的样子。然后在限时作答里把这些词块用出来。
