🎯 Mistake radar华人错误雷达

“I have seen him yesterday.” “Could you tell me where is the pharmacy?” Mistakes like these are not careless: they come straight from Chinese, which is why they survive years of study. Find them in a sentence, rewrite them, hear them, and watch the chart fill out.「I have seen him yesterday.」「Could you tell me where is the pharmacy?」这类错不是粗心，而是从中文直接带过来的，所以学了多年还在。在句子里把它找出来、改过来、听出来，看着雷达图一点点撑满。

📡 Your radar你的雷达

Each spoke is one hard spot; the further out, the more you got right. Grey labels: not tried yet. Tap a label to practise it.每根轴是一条难点，越靠外答对得越多；灰色＝还没练过。点轴上的编号去练。
