Squeeze a text课文榨干

The New Concept English method, for any text you own: read it line by line, pull out the words you do not know yet, retell it from an outline, summarise it in your own words, write it from dictation and shadow it until it is yours.新概念的做法，用在你手上的任何一篇：逐句读懂，挑出还不会的词，按提纲复述，用自己的话写摘要，听写，再跟读到能脱口而出。
