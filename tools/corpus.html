My corpus个人语料

A chunk you have met once in a list is not yours yet. One you have met ten times in real sentences, and recalled twice in a row in review, mostly is. This page keeps that count for you, from the texts you have finished here and from anything you paste in yourself.在词表里见过一次的词块还不是你的。在真实句子里见过十次、复习时又连续两次想得起来的，基本就是了。这一页替你记这个数：来源是你在本站读完的文章，加上你自己贴进来的任何英文。

🔒 Everything here is counted in this browser and saved on this device only.这里的一切都在这个浏览器里统计，只存在这台设备上。 Mastered = met 10 or more times and its review card passed 2 times in a row.掌握＝见面 10 次以上，且它的复习卡连续通过 2 次。

Add your own text加入你自己的文本

Paste any English you have read or written — an email, a report, a chapter, a transcript — or drop a text file. It is counted against the chunk list of this site and joins your corpus. The text itself is not kept; only the counts and up to two sentences per chunk are saved.把你读过或写过的任何英文贴进来——邮件、报告、书的一章、文字稿——或者拖入一个文本文件。它会按本站的词块表统计并进入你的语料。原文不保存，只存次数和每个词块至多两句原句。

Seven-day check七天抽测

Every 7 days, up to 20 chunks are drawn from the ones counted as mastered and shown as gaps in sentences you have read. A chunk you cannot recall goes back into review and leaves the mastered count until it passes twice again.每 7 天，从算作「已掌握」的词块里抽至多 20 个，放回你读过的句子里挖空。想不起来的词块回到复习队列，并从掌握数里退出，直到再连续通过两次。
