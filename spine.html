Text spine: which coursebooks, in what order课文主线：用哪几套、怎么配

New Concept English works because each lesson is one good text that you squeeze dry, and each lesson is a little harder than the last. Its weak side today: old, written, British texts and almost no real spoken English. This page keeps the method and changes the material.新概念好在：每课一篇好课文、把它榨干，并且一课比一课难。它今天的短板：课文旧、偏书面、偏英式，几乎没有真实口语。这一页保留方法，换掉材料。

Exam scope: CELPIP listening and speaking only.考试范围：只做思培听力与口语。 ✓ checked on a publisher page or a published review · ≈ second-hand · ? not checked.✓ 出版社页面或已发表书评核过 · ≈ 二手来源 · ? 未核。

🧭 The plan配法

  1. Backbone主干 · English File 5th edition, Upper-intermediate → Advanced. One unit a week: grammar, vocabulary, pronunciation, everyday listening.每周一个单元：语法、词汇、发音、日常听力。
  2. Listening and speaking spine听说主线 · Pathways Listening, Speaking and Critical Thinking 3 → 4. One unit every two weeks: lecture and discussion listening with notes, then an argued answer. This is what CELPIP Listening Parts 5 and 6 and Speaking Tasks 5 and 7 need, and English File does least of.每两周一个单元：讲座与讨论式听力＋笔记，再有论证地作答。这正是思培听力第 5、6 部分和口语任务 5、7 要的，也是 English File 做得最少的。
  3. Reading spine阅读主线 · Reading Explorer 4 → 5 (CLB 7 → 8–10). One real text a week, squeezed dry with the text tool: this is the New Concept habit with current, factual texts, and the main source of mid-frequency words.每周一篇真实文章，用「课文榨干」工具榨干：这是新概念的习惯，换成当代纪实文章，也是中频词的主要来源。
  4. Authentic listening真实听力 · TED talk lessonsTED 演讲课. One talk a week. Coursebook audio is scripted; real talks fill that gap. Free.每周一讲。教材音频是脚本录的，真实演讲补这个缺口。免费。
  5. One tool for everything一个工具管所有材料 · Squeeze a text课文榨干. Any text or transcript from any book: close reading, new words by band, retell, summary, dictation, shadowing.任何书的任何课文或转写：精读、按频段找生词、复述、摘要、听写、跟读。

Why not more books: the general courses (Headway, Life, Speakout, Keynote, Cutting Edge) share one B2–C1 syllabus. Two at once means meeting the same grammar twice and nothing new. The two spines were chosen because they add what the backbone lacks: lecture listening with notes, and long real texts.为什么不多学几套：综合教程（Headway、Life、Speakout、Keynote、Cutting Edge）共用同一份 B2–C1 大纲，同时学两套就是同一批语法见两遍、没有新东西。两条主线入选，是因为它们补的正是主干缺的：讲座听力加笔记，以及长篇真实文章。

No coursebooks beyond English File?除了 English File 不想再买书？

Use the free path: TED lessons for the listening spine, VOA Learning English articles squeezed with the text tool for the reading spine. You lose the graded lecture practice of Pathways; everything else is covered.走免费路线：听说主线用 TED 演讲课，阅读主线用 VOA Learning English 的文章配「课文榨干」。少了 Pathways 那种分级讲座练习，其余都覆盖到。
