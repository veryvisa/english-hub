Linking words together 连读

📘 In your coursebook: English File 5e · Upper-Intermediate · 4B “At your own risk” · English File 5e · Advanced · 5A “Live in the moment” · English File 5e · Advanced · 7A “Is that a fact?”. Use the texts and recordings with your coursebook; this page adds new words, grammar and practice.教材定位：English File 5e · Upper-Intermediate · 4B《At your own risk》；English File 5e · Advanced · 5A《Live in the moment》；English File 5e · Advanced · 7A《Is that a fact?》。课文与录音请配合教材学习，本页补充新词、语法与练习。

Join words the way fluent speakers do, so that your speech flows and fast speech stops sounding like one long unknown word. 像流利的人那样把词连起来，让自己说得顺，也让快语速不再像一长串听不懂的音。

B2 · CELPIP 7–8⏱ 25 minP09 Words are not linked; each one is said alone不连读，一词一顿

📏 The rule规则

📏 The point in one line一句话要领
In a thought group there are no gaps between words: a final consonant moves onto the next vowel, and two vowels are joined by a small /j/, /w/ or /r/.一个意群内部词与词之间没有停顿：词尾辅音挪到下一个元音上，两个元音之间用轻微的 /j/、/w/ 或 /r/ 接起来。

Linking and weak forms together. 连读加弱读。

Sounds like one word. 听起来像一个词。
I agree. /aɪ jəˈɡriː/

📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Open the full explanation: 5 points展开细讲：5 段讲解

Chinese is written and spoken syllable by syllable, each one a clear unit. Speaking English that way, with a tiny stop after every word, is the main reason learners are told they sound "choppy", and the main reason natural speech is hard to follow.

汉语一字一音节，界限分明。用这种方式说英语、每个词后面顿一下，是被说「一顿一顿」的主因，也是听不懂自然语速的主因。

Consonant to vowel: the consonant jumps forward. turn it off sounds like tur-ni-toff; an hour and a half like a-nou-ran-da-half. This is the most frequent kind of linking and the first to practise.

辅音接元音：辅音跳到后面去。这是最常见的连读，先练它。

Vowel to vowel: a glide appears. After /iː/, /eɪ/ or /aɪ/ you hear a small /j/: I agree → I-yagree. After /uː/ or /əʊ/ you hear a small /w/: go out → go-wout. In British English a silent r in the spelling comes back before a vowel: far away. North Americans pronounce that r everywhere.

元音接元音：中间冒出一个滑音。前元音后是 /j/，圆唇元音后是 /w/；英式英语里平时不读的词尾 r 在元音前要读出来；北美则处处都读 r。

Same or similar consonants are said once and held: black coffee, next time, good day. And a /t/ or /d/ between consonants often disappears: nex(t) week, mos(t) people, han(d)bag.

相同或相近的辅音只发一次、稍稍拉长；夹在辅音之间的 /t/、/d/ 常常脱落。

Linking happens inside a thought group, not across a pause. Pause where the meaning breaks, and link everything in between.

连读发生在意群内部，不跨停顿。意思断开的地方停，其间全部连起来。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ turn / it / off (three separate words)
✓ tur-ni-toff
Gaps between words sound choppy and slow the listener down.
词间停顿显得断断续续，也拖慢听者的理解。
✗ nex-tuh week (adding a vowel after t)
✓ nex' week
Do not add a vowel to keep the /t/; let it go.
不要为了保住 /t/ 而加元音，让它脱落。
✗ I / agree (a stop between the vowels)
✓ I-yagree
A glottal stop between vowels breaks the flow; glide instead.
两个元音之间别卡住，用滑音过渡。
