A Letter About Two Job Offers 一封谈两份工作邀约的信

🧠 Learn the 3 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 3 个新词

Read a friendly letter about choosing between two jobs and see how unreal conditionals help people imagine other choices. 读一封朋友间谈两份工作取舍的信，看虚拟条件句怎样帮人设想另一种选择。

📖 Key card知识卡
A list of pros and cons shows what each option costs; imagining your future regret shows what you care about.利弊清单告诉你每个选项的代价；想象将来的后悔，告诉你自己在乎什么。

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Dear Wei, You asked how I finally chose between the two job offers, so here is the long answer. As you know, one was in Toronto with a bigger salary, and the other was here in Victoria, at a smaller firm where I would lead my own team. On paper, Toronto won easily.

亲爱的伟：你问我最后是怎么在两份工作之间做出选择的，那就给你一个长答案。你知道，一份在多伦多，薪水更高；另一份就在维多利亚，一家小一些的公司，我可以带自己的团队。纸面上看，多伦多轻松胜出。

For a week I made lists of pros and cons. Every time Toronto came out ahead, I had a heavy feeling in my stomach. My sister told me to stop overthinking and go with my gut feeling. I did not trust that advice at first. If I followed my feelings every time, I would still be eating instant noodles in a basement.

那一周我列了好几张利弊清单。每次多伦多胜出，我胃里就沉甸甸的。我姐叫我别想太多，跟着直觉走。一开始我不信这个建议。要是我每次都跟着感觉走，我现在还在地下室吃泡面呢。

So I tried something else. I imagined it was a year later, and I had chosen Toronto. What would I regret? The answer came quickly: I would miss the ocean, and I would miss the chance to build something of my own. Then I imagined I had stayed. I would wonder about the money, but I would not regret the choice. The trade-off was suddenly clear.

于是我换了个办法。我想象已经过去了一年，而我选了多伦多。我会后悔什么？答案来得很快：我会想念海，会想念亲手做点什么的机会。然后我想象自己留了下来。我会琢磨那笔钱，但不会后悔这个选择。取舍一下子清楚了。

I said yes to Victoria, and I have not looked back, although I still check Toronto salaries now and then. If I had only counted the points on my list, I would have moved across the country for a job I did not really want. The list was not useless, though. It showed me what each option cost. It just could not tell me what I cared about.

我答应了维多利亚，再没回头，虽然现在偶尔还是会看看多伦多的薪水。如果我只数清单上的分数，就会为一份自己并不真想要的工作搬到全国另一头。不过清单也不是没用，它让我看清了每个选项的代价，只是没法告诉我自己在乎什么。

So my advice, since you are deciding too: weigh up the facts first, then sleep on it and listen to how you feel in the morning. Do not judge a job by its salary alone. And call me before you sign anything. Love, Mei

所以，既然你也在做决定，我的建议是：先把事实权衡清楚，然后睡一觉，听听早上醒来自己是什么感觉。别只凭薪水评判一份工作。签任何东西之前给我打电话。爱你的，梅

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

If I followed my feelings every time, I would still be eating instant noodles in a basement.
Second conditional about the present: if + past for a habit she does not have, would still be doing for the imagined situation now. Both halves are unreal now, so it is not a mixed conditional.
第二类条件句，与现在事实相反：if + 过去式表示她并没有的习惯，would still be doing 表示设想中的现在状态。两半都指现在，所以不是混合条件句。
If I had only counted the points on my list, I would have moved across the country for a job I did not really want.
Third conditional: had counted … would have moved. In real life she did not move. only before the verb limits what she did: counting and nothing else.
第三类条件句：had counted … would have moved，现实中她没搬。only 放在动词前，限定「只数分数、不做别的」。
