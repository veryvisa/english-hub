"Can I Use It for the Report?" 「报告能用它写吗？」

🧠 Learn the 4 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 4 个新词

Read a conversation between an employee and her manager about using AI for a report, and practise work phrasal verbs in real talk. 读一段员工和经理关于用 AI 写报告的对话，在真实对话里练职场短语动词。

B2 · CELPIP 7–8⏱ 20 minV04 Phrasal verbs avoided; the formal verb sounds stiff回避短语动词，说话太书面V06 Register: too formal, too casual, or too blunt语域不合：太正式、太随意或太冲

📖 Key card知识卡

Read and listen读与听

265 words
Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Priya: Have you got a minute? I wanted to talk about the monthly report. I used the AI tool to put it together this time, and I'd like to know whether that's all right. Tom: Sure. Did it help?

普丽娅：您有空吗？我想聊聊月报。这次我用 AI 工具把它拼起来的，想问问这样行不行。汤姆：当然。有帮助吗？

Priya: A lot. It summarised three pages of notes in a few seconds, and very clearly. But I'm not sure about one thing. It pointed out a "trend" in the sales figures, and when I went back to the data, I couldn't find it. I think it made things up. Tom: Good catch. What did you do?

普丽娅：帮了很多。几秒钟就把三页笔记总结好了，还很清楚。但有一点我拿不准：它指出销售数字里有个「趋势」，我回头查数据却找不到。我觉得是它编的。汤姆：发现得好。你怎么处理的？

Priya: I took the paragraph out and wrote my own. I checked every number against the spreadsheet. Tom: That's exactly the right instinct. Here's how I'd like us to deal with it as a team: the tool can draft, but a person has to own the final version. If your name is on it, you're the one who answers for it.

普丽娅：我把那段删了，自己重写，每个数字都对照表格核了一遍。汤姆：这个直觉完全对。我希望团队这样处理：工具可以起草，但定稿必须有人负责。署了你的名字，就由你来担。

Priya: That makes sense. Can I paste client information into it? Tom: No. Nothing with clients' personal details. If you need to describe a situation, take the names out first. I'll send round some guidelines so we're all on the same page.

普丽娅：明白。客户信息能贴进去吗？汤姆：不行，凡是带客户个人信息的都不行。要描述情况，先把名字去掉。我会发一份指引，让大家想法一致。

Priya: Thanks. One more thing: should I mention that I used it? Tom: In the report, there's no need. But bring it up at the team meeting. People are trying to figure out how to use these tools, and an honest example of what went wrong is worth more than a polished one where everything went right. Could you follow up with a short note afterwards?

普丽娅：谢谢。还有一件事：我要说明用了它吗？汤姆：报告里不用。不过在团队会上提一下。大家都在摸索怎么用这些工具，一个出了错的真实例子，比一个处处顺利的漂亮例子更有价值。会后你能再补一份简短的说明吗？

/pʊt təˈɡeðə/ phr. v.

put together a report; put together a team
She put together a plan in one afternoon. 她一个下午就拿出了一份计划。
/ɡʊd kætʃ/ phr.
发现得好（口语）
That was a good catch.
"The date is wrong." "Good catch, thanks." 「日期错了。」「眼尖，谢谢。」
/ˈɪnstɪŋkt/ n.

/ˈɑːnsə fɔː/ v.

Someone will have to answer for the delay. 总得有人为这次拖延负责。
/ɒn ðə seɪm peɪdʒ/ phr.

get everyone on the same page
Let's meet briefly so we are all on the same page. 我们简短碰个头，统一一下想法。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

It pointed out a "trend" in the sales figures, and when I went back to the data, I couldn't find it.
point out = show or mention something others may not have noticed; the quotation marks show she doubts it is a real trend.
point out＝指出别人可能没注意的东西；引号表示她怀疑这不是真的趋势。
People are trying to figure out how to use these tools, and an honest example of what went wrong is worth more than a polished one where everything went right.
figure out + a how to phrase; one replaces example to avoid repeating it.
figure out 后接 how to 结构；one 代替 example，避免重复。
