Tai Chi or Weights? 太极还是举铁？

🧠 Learn the 4 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 4 个新词

Follow two retired neighbours debating tai chi and weights, and pick up the make/take/have collocations of everyday persuasion. 跟着两位退休邻居争论太极和举铁，学日常劝说里 make、take、have 的搭配。

B2 · CELPIP 7–8⏱ 20 minV01 Wrong collocation built word by word逐词直译的错搭配V06 Register: too formal, too casual, or too blunt语域不合：太正式、太随意或太冲

📖 Key card知识卡
Balance work and strength work do different jobs; most older adults need both.平衡练习和力量练习各管一摊，大多数老人两样都需要。

Read and listen读与听

298 words
Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Mr Ho: Morning, Patrick. You're late today. Patrick: I've been at the gym. My doctor suggested strength training twice a week, so I finally made a decision and joined. Mr Ho: At seventy? You'll hurt yourself. Tai chi is enough for me. It's gentle on the joints and good for balance.

何先生：早啊，帕特里克，今天来晚了。帕特里克：我去健身房了。医生建议我每周做两次力量训练，我终于下决心办了卡。何先生：七十了还去？会伤着的。我打太极就够了，对关节温和，对平衡也好。

Patrick: Tai chi is great for balance, I agree. Some studies even say it lowers the risk of falls. But my doctor said I also need to load the muscles, especially the legs. She took my bad knee into account and gave me a plan with light weights and machines. Mr Ho: And the young people there don't laugh at you?

帕特里克：太极对平衡很好，这我同意，有些研究甚至说它能降低摔跤的风险。可医生说我还得给肌肉加负荷，尤其是腿。她把我膝盖不好考虑进去了，给了我一个用轻重量和器械的计划。何先生：那儿的年轻人不笑话你？

Patrick: The opposite. The trainer told me to warm up for ten minutes first, start light, and take a break between sets. She said the most common mistake is doing too much in the first week and quitting in the second. "Pace yourself," she keeps saying. I'm making progress slowly. Three months ago I couldn't do ten squats. Now I can do twenty.

帕特里克：正相反。教练让我先热身十分钟，从轻的开始，组间休息。她说最常见的错误是第一周练太猛、第二周就放弃。她老说「别急，悠着点」。我在慢慢进步。三个月前我深蹲做不了十个，现在能做二十个。

Mr Ho: Hm. My daughter keeps telling me to do more. Maybe I should make an effort. But I don't want to go to a gym full of young people. Patrick: Then don't. The community centre has a class for over-sixties on Tuesdays. Same idea, slower pace. You could keep your tai chi in the morning and do the class twice a week.

何先生：嗯，我女儿也老让我多练练。也许我该下点功夫。可我不想去满是年轻人的健身房。帕特里克：那就不去。社区中心周二有个六十岁以上的班，道理一样，节奏慢些。你早上照样打太极，一周去上两次课。

Mr Ho: You've thought about this a lot. Patrick: I had to. When my brother had a bad fall last year, it had a huge impact on his whole family. I'd rather put in an hour now than spend a year recovering later. Mr Ho: Fine. Tuesday. But if I'm sore on Wednesday, you're buying the dim sum. Patrick: Deal.

何先生：你想得挺多。帕特里克：不得不想。去年我哥哥狠狠摔了一跤，对他全家影响都很大。我宁愿现在花一个小时，也不想以后花一年养伤。何先生：行，周二。但要是周三我浑身酸痛，点心你请。帕特里克：成交。

Words词

/wɔːm ʌp/ phr. v.
热身
warm up before exercise; a proper warm-up
Always warm up before you lift. 举重前一定要热身。
/set/ n.
（训练的）一组
three sets of ten; rest between sets
Do two sets of ten squats. 深蹲做两组，每组十个。
/peɪs/ phr.
量力而行；控制节奏
pace yourself; at your own pace
It's a long walk, so pace yourself. 路很长，悠着点走。
/sɔː/ adj.
（肌肉）酸痛的
sore muscles; feel sore; sore legs
My legs were sore for two days. 我腿酸了两天。
/pʊt ɪn/ phr. v.
投入（时间、精力）
put in an hour; put in the effort; put in the work
If you put in the time, you will see results. 肯花时间，就会见效果。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

She took my bad knee into account and gave me a plan with light weights and machines.
take ... into account wraps around its object; with a short object it usually splits like this.
take ... into account 夹着宾语；宾语短时通常这样拆开。
I'd rather put in an hour now than spend a year recovering later.
would rather A than B with bare infinitives; spend + time + -ing.
would rather A than B，两边都用原形；spend + 时间 + -ing。
