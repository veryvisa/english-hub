Why Changing Our Minds Is Science Working 改变主意，恰是科学在运转

🧠 Learn the 3 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 3 个新词

Revision and doubt are not signs that science has failed but evidence that its method of reducing error is working. 修订与怀疑并非科学失败的标志，而是其减少错误的方法在起作用的证据。

Read and listen读与听

292 words
Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

It has become fashionable to complain that scientists keep changing their minds. One month a food is harmful, the next it is harmless, and the public is left wondering whom to believe. That frustration is understandable, but it rests on a misunderstanding of what science is for.

抱怨科学家总是改变主意，已经成了一种时髦。这个月某种食物有害，下个月又无害，公众不知该相信谁。这种沮丧可以理解，但它源于对科学用途的误解。

Science is not a collection of final truths. It is a method for reducing error, and by and large it works because it admits that error is possible. A claim that cannot be questioned is not strong; it is merely protected. The findings we trust most are those that have survived repeated attempts to break them, and they are robust precisely because so many people have tried.

科学不是一堆终极真理的集合，而是一种减少错误的方法；总体来说，它之所以有效，正是因为承认错误是可能的。无法被质疑的主张并不强大，只是被保护起来而已。我们最信任的发现，是那些经受住反复推翻尝试的发现，它们之所以稳固，恰恰是因为有那么多人试过。

That said, I do not want to excuse sloppy work. Uncertainty is not a licence to say anything at all. There is a world of difference between an honest range of estimates and a vague answer designed to avoid criticism. Good scientists tell us how sure they are, and why, whereas poor ones either claim certainty they lack or hide behind doubt they do not feel.

话虽如此，我并不想为草率的工作开脱。不确定性不是可以随便说话的许可证。诚实的估计区间与为回避批评而给出的含糊回答之间，有天壤之别。优秀的科学家会告诉我们他们有多确定以及为什么；而糟糕的科学家要么宣称自己并没有的确定性，要么躲在自己并不真有的怀疑背后。

The burden also falls on the rest of us. We should learn to read a headline the way we read a weather forecast: as a judgement about likelihood, not a promise. Journalists, for their part, are likely to serve readers better if they report the size of the margin of error alongside the dramatic number.

责任也落在我们其他人身上。我们应当学会像读天气预报那样读新闻标题：把它当作对可能性的判断，而不是承诺。至于记者，如果在报道惊人数字的同时说明误差范围的大小，很可能更好地服务读者。

So the next time a study overturns an earlier one, resist the urge to sneer. Revision is not a sign that the system has failed. On the whole, it is the clearest evidence that the system is doing its job, and a society that cannot tolerate that kind of honesty will end up believing something far worse.

所以，下次有研究推翻了早先的研究时，请克制讥讽的冲动。修订并不意味着体系失灵。总的来说，它恰恰是体系在发挥作用的最清楚证据；而一个无法容忍这种诚实的社会，最终会相信某种糟糕得多的东西。

Words词

/rəʊˈbʌst/ adj.
稳固的，经得起检验的
a robust finding; robust evidence
The conclusion is robust because several independent teams reached it. 这一结论很稳固，因为多个独立团队都得出了它。
/baɪ ənd lɑːdʒ/ phr.
总体上，大体而言
by and large accurate; by and large true
By and large, the forecasts have been reliable this year. 总体而言，今年的预报一直比较可靠。
/ðæt sed/ phr.
话虽如此
That said, however; That said, it is worth noting
The plan is sound. That said, it will need careful monitoring. 这个计划是合理的。话虽如此，仍需要仔细监督。
/rɪˈvɪʒən/ n.
修订，修正
a major revision; subject to revision
Each revision of the estimate brought it closer to the truth. 每一次修订都使这个估计更接近真相。
/ˈlaɪsəns/ n.
许可，放任的借口
a licence to do something; poetic licence
Doubt should not become a licence to ignore all evidence. 怀疑不应成为无视一切证据的借口。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

A claim that cannot be questioned is not strong; it is merely protected.
The semicolon joins two balanced statements, and the second reverses the expected meaning by redefining ‘strong’ as ‘merely protected’.
分号连接两个对称的陈述，第二句把“强”重新定义为“只是被保护”，形成反转。
Good scientists tell us how sure they are, and why, whereas poor ones either claim certainty they lack or hide behind doubt they do not feel.
’Whereas’ sets up a contrast between two groups, ‘poor ones’ substitutes for ‘poor scientists’, and ‘certainty they lack’ is a relative clause with the relative pronoun omitted.
whereas 引出两类人的对比，'poor ones' 替代 poor scientists，'certainty they lack' 是省略了关系代词的定语从句。
