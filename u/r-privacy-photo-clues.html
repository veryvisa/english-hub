What a Family Photo Can Tell a Stranger 一张家庭照能告诉陌生人什么

🧠 Learn the 3 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 3 个新词

Read an explainer on what ordinary photos reveal to strangers and learn the word families that carry the argument. 读一篇科普，看普通照片会向陌生人泄露什么，学会撑起论证的词族。

A family photo on a front step, a new car in the driveway, a child in a school uniform: to the person who posts it, each one is a happy moment. To a stranger, it may also be a set of clues. People who study online privacy often point out that the risk of a single post is small, but the risk of many posts added together is not.

门前台阶上的全家福、车道上的新车、穿校服的孩子：对发帖的人来说，每一张都是开心的瞬间；对陌生人来说，它们也可能是一串线索。研究网络隐私的人常指出，单条帖子的风险很小，许多条加在一起就不小了。

Think about what a stranger can learn from an ordinary public account. The house number in the background shows where you live. The school logo shows where your children spend the day. A photo of a boarding pass, even a blurred one, can reveal a name and a booking code. Holiday pictures posted in real time announce that the house is empty. None of these details is dangerous alone; together they form a surprisingly reliable picture of a life.

想想陌生人能从一个普通的公开账号里知道些什么。背景里的门牌号说明你住哪儿，校徽说明你的孩子白天在哪儿。一张登机牌的照片，哪怕打了码，也可能泄露姓名和订票码。实时发出的度假照等于宣布家里没人。这些细节单独看都不危险，放在一起，却能拼出一幅准得出奇的生活图景。

Many people also do not realise that photos can carry hidden information. Depending on the phone and the app, a picture may include the time and the place where it was taken. Some platforms remove this data when you upload; others do not. A basic awareness of this is more useful than any single setting.

很多人还没意识到照片里可能带着隐藏信息。取决于手机和应用，一张照片可能记录了拍摄的时间和地点。有些平台在上传时会删掉这些数据，有些不会。对这一点有基本的意识，比任何单个设置都有用。

A common misunderstanding is that privacy settings solve the problem. They help, but they are not a complete fix: a "friends only" post can still be copied, saved as a screenshot and shared. The safest expectation is that anything you post may one day be seen by someone you did not choose.

一个常见的误解是：调好隐私设置就万事大吉。设置有帮助，但不是彻底的解决办法：「仅好友可见」的帖子照样能被复制、截图、转发。最稳妥的预期是：你发的任何东西，总有一天可能被你没选的人看到。

This is not an argument for leaving social media. It is an argument for a small habit: before you post, look at the picture as a stranger would. Crop out the house number, wait until you are home to share the holiday, and leave your children's school out of the frame.

这不是在劝你退出社交媒体，而是在劝你养成一个小习惯：发之前，用陌生人的眼光看一遍照片。把门牌号裁掉，回到家再分享假期，别让孩子的学校出现在画面里。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

None of these details is dangerous alone; together they form a surprisingly reliable picture of a life.
None of + plural noun can take is (careful style) or are (everyday style). The semicolon sets alone against together.
None of 加复数名词，谓语可用 is（较正式）或 are（日常）。分号把 alone 和 together 对照起来。
The safest expectation is that anything you post may one day be seen by someone you did not choose.
expect → expectation; the passive may… be seen keeps the focus on what you post, not on who sees it.
expect 变名词 expectation；被动语态 may… be seen 把焦点放在你发的东西上，而不是谁在看。
