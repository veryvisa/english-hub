A Call to the Landlord 给房东打的一通电话

🧠 Learn the 3 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 3 个新词

Follow a phone call about a broken heater and learn the six-point order that makes such calls work. 读一通报修暖气的电话，学会让这类电话有效的六点顺序。

B2 · CELPIP 7–8⏱ 20 minV06 Register: too formal, too casual, or too blunt语域不合：太正式、太随意或太冲V04 Phrasal verbs avoided; the formal verb sounds stiff回避短语动词，说话太书面

📖 Key card知识卡
A problem call works in this order: who I am, what is wrong, since when, what I have tried, what I need, by when.报修电话按这个顺序说：我是谁、哪里坏了、从什么时候起、我试过什么、我需要什么、什么时候之前。

Read and listen读与听

260 words
Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

The heating in Mei's apartment stopped working on a Sunday in January. She had put off calling the landlord twice before, about smaller things, because she was nervous about her English. This time she could not wait. Here is the call, more or less as it happened.

一月的一个周日，阿美公寓的暖气坏了。此前有两次小问题，她因为对自己的英语没底，都拖着没给房东打电话。这一次等不了了。下面是那通电话，大致就是这样。

"Hello, Mr Davies? It's Mei from unit 4. Sorry to bother you on a Sunday. I'm calling because we have no heat." "Oh. Since when?" "Since this morning. I've checked the thermostat, and it's set to twenty-one, but the radiators are cold." "Right. Have you tried turning it off and on again?" "Yes, twice. Nothing happens."

「您好，戴维斯先生吗？我是 4 号的阿美。周日打扰您，不好意思。我打电话是因为家里没暖气了。」「哦，什么时候开始的？」「今天早上开始的。我检查过温控器，设的是二十一度，但暖气片是凉的。」「好。你试过关掉再打开吗？」「试过两次，没反应。」

"Okay. I can't come today, but I'll have a word with my plumber." "Thank you. Could you tell me roughly when he might come? It's minus ten tonight, and I have a small child." "I'll find out and call you back within the hour." "That would be great. And if he can't come today, could we sort out a heater for tonight?" "Fair enough. I'll bring one over."

「好。我今天过不去，不过我会跟我的水暖工说一声。」「谢谢。您能告诉我他大概什么时候来吗？今晚零下十度，我家有小孩。」「我问一下，一小时内给你回电话。」「那太好了。如果他今天来不了，今晚能不能先解决一台取暖器？」「也是。我送一台过去。」

After she hung up, Mei did one more thing: she followed up with a text message that repeated the time, the problem and what he had promised. Nothing in the call was complicated. What made it work was the order: who I am, what is wrong, since when, what I have already tried, what I need, and by when. She had made an effort to prepare those six points on paper first. The plumber came at four. The landlord, she has noticed, has taken her calls more seriously ever since.

挂了电话，阿美又做了一件事：发了一条短信跟进，把时间、问题和他答应的事重复了一遍。这通电话里没有一句复杂的话。让它管用的是顺序：我是谁、哪里坏了、从什么时候起、我已经试过什么、我需要什么、什么时候之前。她事先下了功夫，把这六点写在了纸上。水暖工四点到了。阿美发现，从那以后房东对她的电话认真多了。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

She had put off calling the landlord twice before, about smaller things, because she was nervous about her English.
Past perfect for what happened before that Sunday. put off takes -ing.
过去完成时表示那个周日之前发生的事；put off 后接 -ing。
What made it work was the order: who I am, what is wrong, since when, what I have already tried, what I need, and by when.
What made it work is the subject; the colon lists the order. make + object + bare infinitive.
What made it work 作主语，冒号后列出顺序；make 后接宾语加不带 to 的不定式。
