What I Would Tell Myself in Year One 我想对第一年的自己说的话

Read and listen读与听

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Good evening, everyone. I was asked to talk for five minutes about my first year here, and I said yes before I could change my mind. Eleven years ago I was sitting where you are now, holding a paper cup of coffee and understanding about half of what the speaker said. So I will keep it simple. Here is what I would tell myself in year one.

大家晚上好。主办方请我讲五分钟我在这里的第一年，我没等自己反悔就答应了。十一年前，我就坐在你们现在坐的地方，端着一纸杯咖啡，台上讲的只听懂一半。所以我讲得简单些：这是我想对第一年的自己说的话。

First, if I were you, I would say yes to every invitation, even the boring ones: the barbecue where you know nobody, the school fundraiser, a colleague's birthday drinks. I said no to most of them, because my English felt too slow. If I had gone, I would have found friends a year earlier. Belonging is built in small, awkward rooms.

第一，如果我是你，我会接受每一个邀请，哪怕是无聊的：谁都不认识的烧烤、学校的筹款会、同事的生日小酌。那时我大多拒绝了，因为觉得自己英语太慢。要是我去了，我会早一年交到朋友。归属感就是在一个个小小的、尴尬的房间里建起来的。

Second, you will feel homesick, and it will surprise you when it comes. For me it was the smell of a bakery in my second winter. I wish someone had told me that this is normal and that it passes. Call home, cook the food you miss, and then go back out.

第二，你会想家，而且它来的时候会让你吃一惊。对我来说，是第二个冬天里面包店飘出的一阵香味。真希望当时有人告诉我，这很正常，会过去的。给家里打个电话，做一顿你想念的饭，然后再出门去。

Third, do not wait until your English is perfect. Mine is still not perfect. People here are far more patient with a slow sentence than with silence. If I could go back, I would speak up more and apologise less.

第三，别等英语完美了再开口。我的到现在也不完美。这里的人对一句说得慢的话，比对沉默要耐心得多。如果能回到过去，我会多开口，少道歉。

Finally, find one thing to give, not just to receive. Coach a team, help at a food bank, or teach a neighbour to make dumplings. If you are not sure where to start, just turn up at the next community event. The day you are useful to someone here is the day this place starts to feel like yours. Thank you, and welcome.

最后，找一件可以付出的事，而不只是接受。去当球队教练，去食物银行帮忙，或者教邻居包饺子。不知道从哪儿开始，就直接去参加下一次社区活动。你对这里的某个人有用的那一天，就是这个地方开始让你觉得属于你的那一天。谢谢大家，欢迎你们。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

If I had gone, I would have found friends a year earlier.
Third conditional: had gone and would have found are both contrary to past fact. The speaker did not go.
第三类条件句：had gone 和 would have found 都与过去事实相反。讲者当时没去。
People here are far more patient with a slow sentence than with silence.
far more ... than makes a strong comparison; patient with is the fixed preposition.
far more ... than 加强比较；patient with 是固定介词搭配。
