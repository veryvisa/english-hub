Let Our Street Grow a Little 让我们这条街长大一点

🧠 Learn the 3 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 3 个新词

Read an opinion column for more homes on a quiet street, and notice the nouns and adjectives built with suffixes that carry a careful argument. 读一篇主张在安静街区多建住房的评论，留意那些加了后缀、撑起审慎论证的名词和形容词。

Read and listen读与听

291 words
Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

On my street of detached houses, almost every proposal for something denser meets the same response at council meetings: it will ruin the character of the neighbourhood. I used to agree. I have changed my mind, and I want to explain why without calling anyone unreasonable.

我住的这条街全是独立屋。几乎每一个想建得更密一点的提案，在市议会会议上都会碰到同一种回应：这会毁了社区的特色。我以前也这么想。现在我改变了看法，想解释一下原因，而不去说谁不讲道理。

The character of a neighbourhood is not only its buildings. It is also who can afford to live there. On our street, the teachers, nurses and young families who gave it life twenty years ago have mostly been priced out. Their children cannot rent here, let alone buy. A street of beautiful houses where nobody under fifty can afford to live is not preserving its character; it is slowly losing it.

一个社区的特色不只是它的房子，也是谁住得起这里。二十年前给这条街带来生气的老师、护士和年轻家庭，如今大多已被房价挤走。他们的孩子在这里连房都租不起，更别说买了。一条全是漂亮房子、五十岁以下的人却住不起的街，不是在保留特色，而是在慢慢失去它。

I am not arguing for towers on every corner. I am arguing for gentle density: a townhouse here, a fourplex there, basement suites that are legal and safe. These buildings look like houses, and they would let a family stay in the neighbourhood when its needs change. My expectation is not that this would make housing cheap, but that it would add a little supply, make homes a little more affordable and give families a little more flexibility.

我并不是主张每个街角都盖高楼，我主张的是「温和加密」：这里一栋联排屋，那里一栋四户楼，再加上合法、安全的地下室套间。这些房子看上去就像普通住宅，却能让一个家庭在需要变化时还留在社区里。我的预期不是房子会因此变便宜，而是能多一点供应，让房子稍微好负担一点，也让家庭多一点灵活的余地。

Some neighbours worry about parking, shade and noise, and those worries deserve serious answers, not slogans. But I would ask them to show the same willingness to imagine a different street that we expect from newcomers. Most of us live in houses that someone once objected to.

有些邻居担心停车、遮光和噪音，这些担心值得认真回应，而不是喊口号。但我想请他们拿出我们期待新来者拥有的那份意愿，去想象一条不一样的街。我们大多数人住的房子，当年也有人反对过。

There is no perfectly reliable way to predict what a few more homes will do to one street. That is fine. Cities have always changed; the question is who gets to be part of the change. I would like the answer to include the people who teach our children.

没有哪种方法能完全可靠地预测多建几栋房子会给一条街带来什么。这没关系。城市一直在变，问题是谁能参与这种变化。我希望答案里包括那些教我们孩子的人。

Words词

/dɪˈtætʃt haʊs/ n.
独立屋
a detached house with a garden; a street of detached houses
Most of the street is detached houses. 这条街大多是独立屋。
/ˈdʒentl ˈdensəti/ n.
温和加密（在低密度街区适度增加住房）
allow gentle density; a gentle density plan
Gentle density adds homes without building towers. 温和加密不建高楼也能增加住房。
/let əˈləʊn/ phr.
更不用说
can't…, let alone…
I can't afford the rent, let alone a house. 我连房租都付不起，更别说买房了。
/əbˈdʒekt tə/ v. phr.
反对
object to a plan; object to doing sth
Several neighbours objected to the new building. 好几位邻居反对那栋新楼。
/ˈsləʊɡən/ n.
口号
shout a slogan; a campaign slogan
A slogan is not an answer. 口号不是答案。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

Their children cannot rent here, let alone buy.
let alone follows a negative and adds something even less likely: if they cannot rent, buying is even further away.
let alone 跟在否定之后，补充一件更不可能的事：连租都租不起，买就更远了。
But I would ask them to show the same willingness to imagine a different street that we expect from newcomers.
would ask makes the request polite. the same … that we expect compares two groups; willingness is the noun of willing.
would ask 让请求更客气。the same … that we expect 比较两群人；willingness 是 willing 的名词形式。
