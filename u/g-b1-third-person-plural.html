Verb and noun endings you cannot skip 不能省的动词与名词词尾

The learner will be able to use third-person singular verb endings and plural noun endings accurately, especially when speaking or writing quickly. 学习者能够准确使用第三人称单数动词词尾和名词复数词尾，尤其是在快速表达和写作时避免遗漏。

My brother works in Vancouver. 我哥哥在温哥华工作。
A singular subject requires the third-person singular verb ending.单数主语要求动词使用第三人称单数形式。
My brothers work in Vancouver. 我的几个哥哥在温哥华工作。
A plural subject takes the base form of the verb.复数主语搭配动词原形。
My brother does not work in Vancouver. 我哥哥不在温哥华工作。
The auxiliary does carries the singular marking, so the main verb stays in its base form.助动词 does 承担单数标记，因此主要动词使用原形。
My brothers do not work in Vancouver. 我的几个哥哥不在温哥华工作。
The plural subject takes do, followed by the base form of the main verb.复数主语搭配 do，后面的主要动词使用原形。

📏 The rule规则

📏 The rule in one line规则一句话
English uses grammatical endings to show who performs an action and whether a noun refers to one or more things, even when the meaning seems obvious from context.英语通过词尾表达动作执行者的人称以及名词的单复数，即使上下文已经能够说明这些信息，词尾通常也不能省略。

When the subject is he, she, it, or a singular noun, use the third-person singular verb form in the simple present. Do not let the fact that the subject is already clear make you drop the ending. For example, a sentence such as 'My brother works here' needs the final s even though the subject is explicitly named.

主语是 he、she、it 或单数名词时，一般现在时的实义动词通常需要加 -s 或 -es。不能因为主语已经很明确，就省略动词词尾。例如，My brother works here 中，虽然已经明确说出主语是哥哥，works 仍然必须保留词尾。

When the subject is plural, do not add the third-person singular ending to the verb. Compare 'The student works' with 'The students work'. The important choice depends on the grammatical subject, not on whether the sentence sounds more formal or describes a regular activity.

主语是复数时，动词不能再加第三人称单数词尾。比较 The student works 和 The students work。关键在于语法主语的单复数，而不是句子是否正式，也不是动作是否经常发生。

Use plural noun endings when you mean more than one countable item. A number, a plural determiner, or a quantity expression often makes the plural meaning clear, but the noun still needs its ending. For example, 'three books' is correct, not 'three book'.

表示多个可数事物时，名词需要使用复数形式。数字、复数限定词或数量表达通常已经清楚地说明数量，但名词本身仍然需要词尾。例如，应该说 three books，而不是 three book。

Pay attention to the difference between a singular subject and a plural noun inside a longer subject phrase. In 'The price of these products changes', the head noun is 'price', so the verb takes s. The nearby plural noun 'products' does not control the verb.

较长的主语短语中，要区分中心名词和附近的复数名词。在 The price of these products changes 中，主语中心词是单数 price，因此动词使用 changes。附近的 products 虽然是复数，却不决定动词形式。

Do not add s to the main verb after does, doesn't, or another auxiliary that already carries the third-person singular marking. The correct form is 'She doesn't work', not 'She doesn't works'. This is especially important in questions and negative sentences.

does、doesn't 等助动词已经承担了第三人称单数标记，后面的主要动词要使用原形。应该说 She doesn't work，而不是 She doesn't works。这一点在疑问句和否定句中尤其容易出错。

Remember that be and have have special third-person singular forms. Use 'is' rather than 'are' with a singular subject, and use 'has' rather than 'have'. These forms do not follow the ordinary rule of simply adding s to the base form.

be 和 have 的第三人称单数形式比较特殊。单数主语搭配 is，而不是 are；搭配 has，而不是 have。这些形式不能简单地按照普通动词直接添加 s 的规则处理。

More pairs: same idea, different choice更多对比句组

She owns a car. 她有一辆汽车。
The singular noun car refers to one vehicle.单数名词 car 表示一辆汽车。
She owns two cars. 她有两辆汽车。
The plural noun cars shows that there is more than one vehicle.复数名词 cars 表示不止一辆汽车。
She owns several cars. 她拥有几辆汽车。
The plural determiner several requires a plural countable noun.复数限定词 several 要求后接可数名词复数。
She owns a used car. 她有一辆二手汽车。
The singular article a indicates one countable vehicle.单数冠词 a 表示一辆可数汽车。
The student studies at home. 这名学生在家学习。
The singular head noun student controls the verb, which takes s.单数中心名词 student 决定动词使用带 s 的形式。
The students study at home. 这些学生在家学习。
The plural subject students takes the base form study.复数主语 students 搭配动词原形 study。
Each student studies at home. 每名学生都在家学习。
Each makes the subject singular even when it refers to members of a group.Each 强调群体中的每一个人，因此语法上仍使用单数动词。
Many students study at home. 许多学生在家学习。
Many requires a plural noun, and the plural subject takes the base verb.Many 后接复数名词，复数主语搭配动词原形。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ He work in a bank.
✓ He works in a bank.
The subject he is third-person singular. In the simple present, the main verb needs the s ending. Chinese does not normally change verb forms for grammatical person, so learners may overlook this English requirement.
主语 he 是第三人称单数，一般现在时的主要动词需要加 s。汉语通常不根据人称改变动词形式，因此学习者容易忽略英语中的这一要求。
✗ She go to work by bus.
✓ She goes to work by bus.
Verbs ending in certain sounds or spelling patterns require es rather than just s. Go becomes goes in the third-person singular simple present.
部分动词需要加 es，而不是直接加 s。go 的第三人称单数形式是 goes。
✗ My father doesn't likes coffee.
✓ My father doesn't like coffee.
Doesn't already carries the third-person singular marking. The main verb must remain in its base form, so adding another s is incorrect.
Doesn't 已经承担了第三人称单数标记，主要动词必须使用原形，因此不能再添加 s。
✗ There are three student in the room.
✓ There are three students in the room.
The number three requires a plural countable noun. The noun must show plurality even though the quantity is already stated.
数字 three 后面需要接可数名词复数。即使数量已经由数字表达，名词本身仍然需要使用复数形式。
✗ My sister have two children.
✓ My sister has two children.
Have has the irregular third-person singular form has. The noun children is already the irregular plural of child and should not be changed to childrens.
Have 的第三人称单数特殊形式是 has。Children 已经是 child 的不规则复数形式，不能再写成 childrens。
✗ These informations are useful.
✓ This information is useful.
Information is normally an uncountable noun in English and does not take a plural s. Use some information or pieces of information when referring to an amount or separate items.
Information 在英语中通常是不可数名词，不能直接加 s。表示一定数量的信息可以使用 some information，表示不同的信息条目可以使用 pieces of information。
