📏 The rule规则

🎯 The point in one line一句话要领
In English the politeness is in the grammar: past tense, questions, could and might make a request softer. A direct I want sounds like an order, even with please.英语的礼貌藏在语法里：过去时、疑问句、could 和 might 让请求变软。直接说 I want，加了 please 也像命令。

📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Open the full explanation: 5 points展开细讲：5 段讲解

Chinese can be direct and still polite because the tone and qing do the work. English uses distance instead: the further a request is from a plain order, the more polite it feels. Send me the file → Can you send me the file? → Could you send me the file? → I was wondering if you could send me the file.

中文可以说得直接又不失礼，因为语气和「请」字在起作用。英语靠的是「距离」：请求离直白命令越远，越显得客气。Send me the file → Can you… → Could you… → I was wondering if you could…。

Choose the level by cost, not by status. A small favour from a colleague: Can you...?. A bigger one, or someone you do not know: Could you possibly...? or Would you mind...?. Something that really costs them time: I was wondering if you might be able to.... Using the top level for a tiny request sounds sarcastic.

按对方要付出的代价选档位，不按地位选。同事帮个小忙：Can you…?；忙大一点或对方不熟：Could you possibly…? 或 Would you mind…?；真要占用对方时间的：I was wondering if you might be able to…。小事用最高档反而像在讽刺。

Would you mind is followed by -ing, and the polite answer to it is No, not at all, because you are saying you do not mind. Answering Yes means "Yes, I mind", which is a refusal.

Would you mind 后接 -ing；礼貌的答应是 No, not at all，因为你是在说「不介意」。回答 Yes 等于「我介意」，就是拒绝了。

Refusals have three parts: a softener (I'm afraid, Unfortunately), a reason or a limit (I'm not sure I can this week), and often an alternative (Maybe another time, Let me get back to you). A bare No or I can't is not rude in itself, but without the other parts it feels like a closed door.

拒绝通常有三步：先垫一句（I'm afraid、Unfortunately），再说理由或限度（I'm not sure I can this week），常常还给个替代（Maybe another time、Let me get back to you）。光说 No 或 I can't 本身不算无礼，但缺了另两步就像把门关上了。

Small words soften too: just (Can I just ask...), a bit (It's a bit difficult), really in the negative (I'm not really free). In writing, when you have a moment or when you get a chance turns a demand into a request.

小词也能起缓和作用：just（Can I just ask…）、a bit（It's a bit difficult）、否定里的 really（I'm not really free）。书面上加一句 when you have a moment 或 when you get a chance，要求就变成了请求。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ I want you to send me the report today.
✓ Could you send me the report today?
I want you to sounds like an order to a colleague.

✗ Would you mind to open the door?
✓ Would you mind opening the door?
mind takes -ing.
mind 后接 -ing。
✗ "Would you mind waiting?" "Yes, of course." (meaning you agree)
✓ "Would you mind waiting?" "No, not at all."
You are saying you do not mind.
你要表达的是「不介意」。
✗ No, I have no time.
✓ I'm afraid I'm a bit busy this week. Maybe next week?
Softener, limit, alternative.
先垫一句，再说限度，再给替代。
✗ I was wondering can you help me.
✓ I was wondering if you could help me.
