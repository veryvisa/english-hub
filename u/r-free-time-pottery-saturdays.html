My Saturday Pottery Class 我的周六陶艺课

For years, my weekends followed the same pattern. I slept late, watched videos on my phone, and felt tired by Sunday evening. I had plenty of free time, but I never felt rested. One day a colleague asked me what I did to unwind, and I could not give her an answer.

多年来，我的周末总是一个模样。我睡到很晚，在手机上看视频，到周日晚上又觉得很累。我有大把的空闲时间，却从来没有真正休息过。有一天，一位同事问我平时怎么放松，我竟答不上来。

That question stayed in my mind, so I decided to take up something new. A friend told me about a pottery class near my flat. I was nervous at first because I had never made anything with my hands. However, the teacher was kind, and the other students were beginners too.

这个问题一直留在我心里，于是我决定尝试点新东西。一位朋友告诉我，我家附近有个陶艺班。起初我有点紧张，因为我从没亲手做过任何东西。不过老师很和善，其他学员也都是初学者。

My first bowl was a disaster. It was uneven, and one side was much thicker than the other. Everyone laughed, including me. Slowly, though, I started to get better at it. By the fourth week, I could make a small cup that actually looked like a cup.

我做的第一个碗简直是场灾难。它歪歪扭扭，一边比另一边厚得多。大家都笑了，包括我自己。不过慢慢地，我开始越做越好。到第四周，我已经能做出一个真正像杯子的小杯子了。

Now I look forward to every Saturday morning. For two hours, I do not think about work or my phone. My hands are busy, my mind is quiet, and I go home feeling happy instead of tired. I even gave my mother a plate last month, and she still uses it.

现在我每个星期六早上都满心期待。整整两个小时里，我不去想工作，也不看手机。我的双手忙着，心里却很安静，回家时感到的是快乐而不是疲惫。上个月我甚至送了妈妈一个盘子，她到现在还在用。

I think a hobby does not have to be impressive. It only has to make you feel like yourself again. If you are tired of doing nothing in your free time, find something small and try it for a month.

我认为爱好不必给人留下深刻印象，它只需要让你重新找回自己。如果你厌倦了在空闲时间什么也不做，就找点小事试一试，坚持一个月吧。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

That question stayed in my mind, so I decided to take up something new.
The conjunction ‘so’ introduces a result, and ‘decided to’ is followed by the infinitive ‘take up’.
连词 so 引出结果，decided to 后面接动词不定式 take up。
If you are tired of doing nothing in your free time, find something small and try it for a month.
An if-clause in the present tense is followed by an imperative, and ‘tired of’ is followed by a gerund.
现在时的 if 从句后接祈使句；tired of 后面要接动名词。
