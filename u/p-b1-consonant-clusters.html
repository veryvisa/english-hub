Consonant clusters 辅音连缀

📘 In your coursebook: English File 5e · Advanced · 4B “Sound effects”. Use the texts and recordings with your coursebook; this page adds new words, grammar and practice.教材定位：English File 5e · Advanced · 4B《Sound effects》。课文与录音请配合教材学习，本页补充新词、语法与练习。

Say groups of two or three consonants without breaking them with a vowel or dropping one, at the start of words (street, spring) and at the end (texts, asked). 读出两三个辅音连在一起的组合，不插元音、不丢辅音，词首（street、spring）和词尾（texts、asked）都一样。

📏 The rule规则

📏 The point in one line一句话要领
A cluster is said in one movement, with the tongue already moving to the next consonant before the first one ends; nothing is inserted between them.辅音连缀是一个连贯动作：前一个辅音还没结束，舌头已经在往下一个辅音走，中间什么都不插。

📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Open the full explanation: 5 points展开细讲：5 段讲解

A Mandarin syllable has at most one consonant before the vowel, so a word like street has nowhere to go. The mouth solves it by adding vowels: su-tu-reet, or by dropping a consonant: seet, treet. The first version adds two syllables; the second changes the word. Listeners usually recover street in context, but in less predictable words, such as sprint, scrap or strict, they lose it.

普通话音节元音前最多一个辅音，street 这样的词就没处放。嘴的办法是插元音（su-tu-reet）或丢辅音（seet、treet）。前者多出两个音节，后者换了词。常见词靠上下文还能猜，sprint、scrap、strict 这类不好猜的词就丢了。

Start clusters with s by holding the /s/: make a long hiss, then add the next consonant without stopping the air: sssss-treet. Once the movement is clear, shorten the hiss. The /p t k/ after /s/ have no puff of air, which helps: spin sounds softer than pin.

以 s 开头的连缀，先拉长 /s/：嘶嘶不停，再接下一个辅音，气流不断。动作清楚后再缩短嘶声。/s/ 后的 /p t k/ 不送气，这反而有帮助：spin 比 pin 听着软。

For clusters with r and l (train, bread, please, class), prepare the second sound early: put the lips and tongue in the /r/ or /l/ shape while you say the /t/ or /p/. Learners who say te-rain or pu-lease are making the two consonants one after the other; native speakers make them at the same time.

带 r、l 的连缀（train、bread、please、class）要提前准备第二个音：发 /t/、/p/ 时口型已经摆成 /r/ 或 /l/。读成 te-rain、pu-lease 是一个接一个发，母语者是几乎同时发。

Final clusters carry grammar: asked, helped, texts, months, sixths. Here native speakers simplify too, but in a fixed way: they drop a middle /t/ or /d/ (tex's, ask't said as ast, mos' people) and keep the last sound. Learners usually do the opposite: they keep the first consonant and drop the last one, and the last one is the -s or -ed that the listener needed.

词尾连缀带语法意思：asked、helped、texts、months。母语者也会简化，但简化方式固定：丢中间的 /t/、/d/，保住最后一个音。学习者往往反过来：留第一个辅音、丢最后一个，而最后一个正是听者需要的 -s 或 -ed。

A learner's cluster check: count syllables. street is one, strengths is one, instructions is three. Clap as you say the word; if you clap more than the dictionary says, a vowel has crept in.

自检法：数音节。street 一个，strengths 一个，instructions 三个。边说边拍手，拍的比词典多，就说明混进了元音。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ su-tu-reet, su-pring
✓ street, spring (one syllable each)
Inserted vowels add syllables; the listener has to rebuild the word.
插入的元音增加了音节，听者得重新拼出这个词。
✗ I tex him yesterday.
✓ I texted him yesterday. /ˈtekstɪd/
Dropping the end of a cluster drops the tense.
丢掉连缀的末尾，也丢掉了时态。
✗ I sent you two tex.
✓ I sent you two texts. (/teks/ with a clear final /s/ is fine)
Native speakers drop the middle /t/, not the final /s/.
母语者丢的是中间的 /t/，不是最后的 /s/。
✗ Pu-lease sit down.
✓ Please sit down.
/pl/ is one movement; an inserted vowel can turn please into police.
/pl/ 是一个动作；插元音会把 please 说成 police。
