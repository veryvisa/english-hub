Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Dear Coach: I am thirty-four and worried that my job could be automated within a few years. My manager says not to panic, but I see colleagues quietly taking online courses and I feel left behind. Should I retrain, or is that an overreaction? Anxious in the Office

亲爱的教练：我三十四岁，担心自己的工作几年内就可能被自动化。经理叫我不要慌，但我看到同事们悄悄在上网课，我觉得自己落后了。我应该再培训，还是这样做反应过度？——办公室里的焦虑者

Dear Anxious: Your instinct deserves respect, but not panic. While it is true that some tasks are likely to be automated, most jobs change gradually rather than vanish overnight. The sensible question is not whether to retrain completely, but which parts of your skill set need refreshing so that you can adapt to what is coming.

亲爱的焦虑者：你的直觉值得尊重，但不必恐慌。虽然确实有些任务很可能被自动化，但大多数工作是逐渐变化而不是一夜消失。明智的问题不是要不要彻底再培训，而是你的技能组合中哪些部分需要更新，好让你能适应即将到来的变化。

Start with a simple audit. List the tasks you perform each week and mark those that are repetitive, those that need judgement, and those that involve people. On the whole, the second and third groups are safer. Then look for small ways to grow in them, such as leading a meeting, mentoring a newcomer or learning how your team uses new tools.

先做一次简单的盘点。列出你每周要做的任务，标出哪些是重复性的，哪些需要判断力，哪些涉及与人打交道。总体而言，第二类和第三类更安全。然后寻找在这些方面成长的小办法，比如主持一次会议、指导一位新人，或了解团队如何使用新工具。

Next, talk to your manager again, but ask a sharper question: which skills will the team value most in two years? That said, do not rely on one person’s answer. Read job advertisements in your field, speak to people in neighbouring roles, and notice which abilities appear again and again.

接下来，再和你的经理谈一次，但要提一个更尖锐的问题：两年后团队最看重哪些技能？话虽如此，不要只依赖一个人的回答。阅读你所在领域的招聘广告，与相邻岗位的人交谈，留意哪些能力反复出现。

Finally, be kind to yourself. People who feel they might lose out often make rushed decisions, enrolling in expensive courses they never finish. Choose one modest goal for the next three months and stick to it. Steady progress, rather than dramatic reinvention, tends to be what keeps careers resilient.

最后，对自己宽容一些。觉得自己可能吃亏的人常常做出仓促的决定，报名参加昂贵却从未读完的课程。为未来三个月选一个适度的目标并坚持下去。稳步的进步，而不是戏剧性的自我重塑，往往才是让职业生涯保持韧性的东西。

/ˈɔːdɪt/ n.

carry out an audit; a skills audit
A quick audit of her weekly tasks showed where her time went. 对她每周任务的一次快速盘点显示了时间花在了哪里。

/əˈdæpt tuː/ phr. v.
适应

Good teams adapt to new demands without losing focus. 优秀的团队能适应新的要求而不失去重点。
/luːz aʊt/ phr. v.

lose out on a job; lose out to others
Candidates who skip interviews may lose out on good roles. 不参加面试的候选人可能会错失好职位。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

While it is true that some tasks are likely to be automated, most jobs change gradually rather than vanish overnight.
The ‘While it is true that...’ clause concedes a point, and ‘rather than’ contrasts two verbs in parallel: ‘change gradually’ and ‘vanish overnight’.
While it is true that... 从句做出让步，rather than 则对两个并列动词短语 change gradually 与 vanish overnight 进行对比。
Steady progress, rather than dramatic reinvention, tends to be what keeps careers resilient.
The subject is ‘steady progress’ with an inserted contrast, and ‘tends to be what...’ uses a noun clause as the complement to hedge the claim.
主语是 steady progress，中间插入了对比成分，tends to be what... 用名词性从句作表语，并以 tends to 缓和语气。
