Modal nuance: will for habit, would for distance 情态的细微差别

📘 In your coursebook: English File 5e · Advanced · 4B “Sound effects”. Use the texts and recordings with your coursebook; this page adds new words, grammar and practice.教材定位：English File 5e · Advanced · 4B《Sound effects》。课文与录音请配合教材学习，本页补充新词、语法与练习。

Use the quieter jobs of modals: will and would for habits and typical behaviour, would to put polite distance into opinions and requests, will for confident assumptions, and can, may and might to hedge a claim. 用好情态动词的「隐性功能」：will 和 would 表示习惯与典型行为；would 给观点和请求加一点客气的距离；will 表示有把握的推断；can、may、might 给论断留余地。

📏 The rule规则

📏 The rule in one line规则一句话
Modals do more than talk about the future and possibility. Will describes typical behaviour (He'll sit there for hours), would describes past habits (My grandmother would cook all day), would softens an opinion or a request (I'd say it's too early), will makes a confident guess about now (That'll be the delivery), and can, may, might keep a claim from sounding absolute (This can take weeks).情态动词不只讲将来和可能。will 描述典型行为；would 描述过去的习惯；would 让观点或请求更委婉；will 对眼下情况作有把握的推断；can、may、might 让论断不那么绝对。
  • Easy to miss易漏 ✗ When I was a child, we would live in a small town. → ✓ When I was a child, we used to live in a small town.

Will for typical behaviour sounds like describing a character: If you leave food out, the raccoons will find it. My son will play video games for hours if you let him. It is stronger than the present simple and often carries a mild comment. With stress on will, it is a complaint: He WILL leave his shoes in the hall.

will 表典型行为，像在刻画一个人或一种动物的性格，比一般现在时更有画面感，常带一点评论。重读 will 就成了抱怨：他偏要把鞋放在走廊。

Would for past habits tells a story of how things used to be: Every Sunday my father would take us to the market. It works only for repeated actions, not states: ✗ We would live in Harbin → We used to live in Harbin. A story usually starts with used to to set the scene and continues with would.

would 表过去习惯，用来讲「以前总是怎样」。它只用于反复的动作，不用于状态；状态要用 used to。讲故事时常先用 used to 铺垫，再用 would 接着讲。

Would for distance makes you sound measured, not unsure. I'd say, I would think, I'd suggest, I would argue turn a flat statement into a considered opinion. Would you mind, I'd be grateful if, Would it be possible make requests polite. Chinese speakers often sound too blunt in English because these small words are missing, not because the vocabulary is wrong.

would 带来的距离感让你显得审慎，而不是没把握。I'd say、I would think 把直白的陈述变成经过考虑的观点；Would you mind 之类让请求更客气。华人英语常显得太冲，往往不是用词错，而是少了这些小词。

Will and must make guesses about the present. The doorbell rings: That'll be the courier (I expect so, based on what I know). She must be tired (I conclude from evidence). He'll be at work now means "I am sure, given his routine". This will is about now, not the future.

will 和 must 可以对现在作推测。门铃响了：那应该是快递员。will 依据已知常识，must 依据眼前证据。这个 will 讲的是现在，不是将来。

Hedging belongs to careful claims. Can states what is generally possible (Winters here can be very dark); may and might say it is possible in this case (This may take longer than expected); could suggests an option or risk (This could cause delays). Add tend to for general patterns (Prices tend to rise in spring). In opinion writing and at work, this is the difference between sounding arrogant and sounding expert.

留余地属于严谨的表达。can 说一般情况下可能；may 和 might 说这次可能；could 提出一种选项或风险；tend to 讲一般规律。在评论写作和职场里，这就是显得自大与显得专业的区别。

Examples例句

  • If you leave the gate open, the dog will run straight into the road. 你要是不关门，狗会直接冲到马路上。
    Typical behaviour.典型行为。
  • My son will happily read for hours, but he won't touch a vegetable. 我儿子能高高兴兴读几个小时书，可蔬菜碰都不碰。
    Character description with will / won't.用 will / won't 刻画性格。
  • Every winter my grandfather would make dumplings for the whole street. 每年冬天我爷爷都会给整条街的人包饺子。
    Past habit.过去的习惯。
  • We used to live above a noodle shop, and we would fall asleep to the noise of the kitchen. 我们以前住在一家面馆楼上，常常伴着厨房的声音入睡。
    used to for the state, would for the habit.used to 讲状态，would 讲习惯。
  • I'd say it's a bit early to make a decision. 我觉得现在做决定有点早。
    Softened opinion.委婉的观点。
  • I would think most people would rather pay a little more for a shorter wait. 我想大多数人宁愿多花点钱少等一会儿。
    Measured, not unsure.审慎，而不是没把握。
  • I'd be grateful if you could send the form again. 如能再寄一次表格，我将不胜感激。
    Formal request.正式请求。
  • Would you mind waiting a few minutes? 您介意等几分钟吗？
    Polite request.客气的请求。
  • That'll be the plumber; he said he'd come before noon. 那应该是水管工，他说中午前来。
    Confident guess about now.对现在的有把握推测。
  • It's nine o'clock, so she'll be on the train by now. 现在九点了，她这会儿应该在火车上了。
    will about the present.will 讲现在。
  • Winters here can be very dark, so plan some indoor activities. 这里的冬天可能会很阴暗，所以安排些室内活动。
    General possibility.一般情况下的可能。
  • The application may take longer than eight weeks. 申请可能要超过八周。
    Possible in this case.这次可能会。
  • A late payment could affect your credit score. 逾期付款可能会影响你的信用分。
    A risk.一种风险。
  • Prices tend to rise in the spring, when more people move. 春天搬家的人多，价格往往会涨。
    General tendency.一般规律。

More pairs: same idea, different choice更多对比句组

Send me the file. 把文件发给我。
Blunt order.生硬的命令。
Could you send me the file? 能把文件发给我吗？
Polite, everyday.客气，日常。
I'd be grateful if you could send me the file when you have a moment. 您方便时把文件发给我，不胜感激。
Formal and very polite.正式而非常客气。
My grandmother cooked every Sunday. 我奶奶每个星期天做饭。
Plain past fact.简单的过去事实。
My grandmother used to cook every Sunday. 我奶奶以前每个星期天都做饭。
A past habit that has stopped.已经不再的过去习惯。
My grandmother would cook all day every Sunday, and the whole family would come. 我奶奶每个星期天会做一整天饭，全家人都会来。
Storytelling: a warm picture of how things were.讲故事：温暖地描绘过去的样子。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ When I was a child, we would live in a small town.
✓ When I was a child, we used to live in a small town.
would is for repeated actions, not states.
would 只用于反复的动作，不用于状态。
✗ I think this is the best way. (in a meeting, to senior colleagues)
✓ I'd say this might be the best way. / I would suggest this as the best way.
Too flat in a meeting; add would for distance.
会议上太直白，加 would 拉开距离。
✗ This medicine will cure your cold.
✓ This medicine can help with cold symptoms.
Absolute claims sound false; hedge with can or may.
绝对的说法显得不可信，用 can 或 may 留余地。
✗ Someone is at the door. It must to be the courier.
✓ Someone's at the door. That'll be the courier. / It must be the courier.
No to after a modal.
情态动词后不加 to。
✗ I would like that you send me the report.
✓ I would like you to send me the report. / I'd be grateful if you could send me the report.
would like + person + to.
would like 加人再加 to。
