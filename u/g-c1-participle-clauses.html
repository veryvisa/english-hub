Participle clauses 分词从句

Replace a full adverbial clause with an -ing, -ed or having + participle clause to say time, reason or result more tightly, and keep the hidden subject the same as the main one. 用 -ing、-ed 或 having + 过去分词从句替代完整的状语从句，更紧凑地表达时间、原因或结果；并保证隐含主语与主句主语一致。

Because I didn't know the rules, I parked in the wrong place. 因为不知道规定，我停错了地方。
Full clause: clear, neutral.完整从句：清楚，中性。
Not knowing the rules, I parked in the wrong place. 不了解规定，我停错了地方。
Participle: tighter, more written.分词：更紧凑，更书面。
I didn't know the rules, so I parked in the wrong place. 我不知道规定，所以停错了地方。
Two clauses with so: everyday speech.两个分句加 so：日常口语。

📏 The rule规则

📏 The rule in one line规则一句话
A participle clause is an adverbial clause with the subject and the conjunction removed: Walking home, I saw the fire = While I was walking home…. It sounds tighter and more written, but the hidden subject must be the same as the subject of the main clause.分词从句就是去掉主语和连词的状语从句。它更紧凑、更书面，但隐含主语必须和主句的主语一致。
  • Easy to miss易漏 ✗ Arriving at the airport, my phone died. → ✓ Arriving at the airport, I found my phone had died. / When I arrived at the airport, my phone died.

Three forms carry three relations in time. The -ing form is at the same time or just before: Opening the letter, she saw the date. Having + participle is clearly before, often with a cause: Having lost the receipt, I couldn't return it. The -ed form is passive: Asked about the delay, the manager apologised (= when he was asked).

三种形式表示三种时间关系。-ing 表示同时或紧接着之前；having + 过去分词明确表示在先，常带原因；-ed 是被动意义。

The hidden subject is the main subject. Walking into the office, the phone rang says the phone walked. Chinese can drop subjects freely because context supplies them; in English the participle borrows its subject from the main clause, so check the first noun after the comma every time. Repair: As I walked into the office, the phone rang, or Walking into the office, I heard the phone ring.

隐含主语就是主句主语。中文主语可以随便省，语境会补上；英语的分词借用主句的主语，所以每次都要检查逗号后的第一个名词是不是做这个动作的人。

A participle clause leaves the exact relation to the reader: time, reason or condition. Living near the school, we never drive can mean "because" or "while". When the relation matters, keep the conjunction: After moving, While waiting, Since leaving, When asked. These are halfway between a full clause and a bare participle, and very common in reports and instructions.

分词从句把具体关系（时间、原因、条件）交给读者去推。关系要紧时保留连词：After moving、While waiting、When asked。这是介于完整从句和光秃分词之间的写法，报告和说明里很常见。

A participle after the main clause often states a result: The pipe burst, flooding the basement. Rents rose by a third, forcing many families to move. This is how news and reports join a cause and its effect in one sentence. Keep the -ing clause about the whole event, not about the last noun.

主句之后的分词常表示结果：水管爆了，淹了地下室。新闻和报告就是这样把因与果放进一句话。-ing 分句说的是整件事，不是最后那个名词。

In speech, a short participle clause sounds natural (Thinking about it, I'd say no), but long chains sound like a report being read aloud. In writing, one or two per paragraph tightens the prose; five makes it hard to follow.

口语里短的分词从句很自然，但一长串会像在念报告。写作时一段一两个能让文字更紧，五个就难读了。

-ed (front)passiveWhen he was asked, he…Asked, he…

Examples例句

  • Walking home from the station, I noticed the smoke. 从车站走回家的路上，我看到了烟。
    Same time; the subject is I.同时发生；主语是 I。
  • Having lost the receipt, I couldn't return the jacket. 收据丢了，我没法退那件外套。
    Earlier action and cause.在先的动作，同时是原因。
  • Not knowing anyone in the city, she joined a hiking group. 她在这座城市谁也不认识，就加入了一个徒步群。
    Negative reason: Not + -ing.否定的原因：not 加 -ing。
  • Asked about the delay, the spokesperson blamed the weather. 被问到延误时，发言人归咎于天气。
    Passive: he was asked.被动：他被问到。
  • Built in 1912, the school is now too small for the neighbourhood. 这所学校建于 1912 年，现在已经容不下这个社区的孩子。
    Passive background fact.被动的背景事实。
  • After moving to Surrey, we finally had a garden. 搬到素里之后，我们终于有了院子。
    Conjunction kept: the time relation is clear.保留连词：时间关系清楚。
  • While waiting for the bus, I listen to a podcast. 等公交时我会听播客。
    While + -ing: a habit.while 加 -ing：习惯。
  • When asked for ID, show your card and your letter. 被要求出示证件时，出示卡和信。
    Instructions: When asked.说明文：when asked。
  • The pipe burst overnight, flooding the whole basement. 水管夜里爆了，淹了整个地下室。
    Result clause at the end.句末的结果从句。
  • Rents rose by a third, forcing many young families to move out. 房租涨了三分之一，迫使许多年轻家庭搬走。
    Result of the whole event.整件事的结果。
  • Feeling tired, he left the party early. 他觉得累，就早早离开了聚会。
    Reason.原因。
  • Having worked nights for ten years, my mother sleeps badly even now. 我妈妈上了十年夜班，到现在都睡不好。
    Long past action explains the present.长期的过去行为解释现在。
  • Compared with last winter, this one has been mild. 和去年冬天比，今年的冬天算暖和。
    Fixed -ed opener.固定的 -ed 开头。
  • Generally speaking, the bus is faster than driving at rush hour. 一般来说，高峰时坐公交比开车快。
    Fixed expressions have no subject rule.固定说法不受主语规则约束。

More pairs: same idea, different choice更多对比句组

Walking into the office, the phone rang. 走进办公室，电话响了。
Wrong: the phone did not walk.错误：电话不会走路。
As I walked into the office, the phone rang. 我走进办公室时，电话响了。
Repair 1: full clause with its own subject.修法一：完整从句，有自己的主语。
Walking into the office, I heard the phone ring. 走进办公室时，我听到电话响了。
Repair 2: make I the main subject.修法二：让 I 当主句主语。
The river flooded, and this closed the highway. 河水泛滥了，这导致高速公路封闭。
Two clauses, plain.两个分句，平实。
The river flooded, which closed the highway. 河水泛滥了，结果高速公路封闭了。
which for the whole event.which 指整件事。
The river flooded, closing the highway for two days. 河水泛滥，高速公路封闭了两天。
Participle result: news style.分词表结果：新闻体。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ Arriving at the airport, my phone died.
✓ Arriving at the airport, I found my phone had died. / When I arrived at the airport, my phone died.
The hidden subject must be the main subject.
隐含主语必须是主句主语。
✗ Having finish the form, I sent it.
✓ Having finished the form, I sent it.
Having + past participle.
having 后接过去分词。
✗ Knowing not the answer, I stayed quiet.
✓ Not knowing the answer, I stayed quiet.
Not goes before the participle.
not 放在分词前面。
✗ Interviewing for the job, the questions were hard.
✓ When I was interviewed for the job, the questions were hard. / Interviewing for the job, I found the questions hard.
The questions did not interview; give the clause its own subject.
问题不会去面试，给从句补上主语。
✗ Being a rainy day, we stayed home.
✓ It being a rainy day, we stayed home. / As it was a rainy day, we stayed home.
The weather is not we; keep a subject or use as.
下雨的不是我们；保留主语或用 as。
