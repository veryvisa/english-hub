Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Dear Manager, I am writing about a lunch I had at your restaurant last Thursday with three colleagues. We had called earlier to book a table for twelve thirty, and we arrived on time. However, we waited fifteen minutes before anyone showed us to our seats, and nobody told us why.

尊敬的经理：我写信是想说说上周四我和三位同事在贵餐厅的一次午餐。我们提前打电话订了十二点半的位子，也准时到了。然而，我们等了十五分钟才有人带我们入座，也没人告诉我们原因。

The service was slow for the rest of the meal. My soup arrived cold, and one of my colleagues received a dish she had not ordered. When we asked the waiter about it, he looked tired and apologised several times. He then offered to bring a fresh bowl of soup, but by then we had very little time left, so I decided to skip it.

接下来整顿饭的服务都很慢。我的汤上来时是凉的，一位同事还收到了一道她没点的菜。我们向服务员询问时，他看起来很疲惫，连连道歉。他提出给我换一碗新鲜的汤，但那时我们剩下的时间已经很少，所以我决定不要了。

I want to be fair, though. The waiter was polite and honest, and the grilled fish that my other colleague ordered was excellent. I also noticed that the restaurant was very crowded that day, so I understand that the kitchen was under pressure. Perhaps you did not have enough staff, or perhaps there were too many dishes on the menu for such a small kitchen.

不过我想公平地说，服务员礼貌又坦诚，另一位同事点的烤鱼也非常棒。我还注意到那天餐厅非常拥挤，所以我理解厨房压力很大。也许是你们人手不够，也许是对这么小的厨房来说，菜单上的菜太多了。

I would be grateful if you could look into this. I do not expect anything special, but a short reply would show that you care about your customers. I would also like to know how we can book a table for a larger group next month. We liked the grilled fish, and we would like to come back. It would also help if the staff could split the bill into separate payments, because that is easier for a big group.

如果您能调查一下这件事，我将不胜感激。我并不期待任何特别的补偿，但一封简短的回复会表明你们在乎顾客。我还想了解，下个月我们怎样为一个较大的团体订位。我们很喜欢那道烤鱼，也希望再来。如果店员能把账单分成几笔单独付款，也会有帮助，因为这对大团体更方便。

Thank you for your time. I look forward to hearing from you. Yours faithfully, A hungry customer

感谢您抽出时间。期待您的回复。此致，一位饥肠辘辘的顾客

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

I would be grateful if you could look into this.
A polite request uses ‘would’ in the main clause and ‘could’ after ‘if’, which makes the request softer and less direct.
礼貌请求在主句用 would、在 if 后用 could，使语气更委婉、不那么直接。
When we asked the waiter about it, he looked tired and apologised several times.
A ‘when’ clause with the past simple gives the time, and the main clause joins ‘looked tired’ and ‘apologised’ with the word and.
带过去式的 when 从句交代时间，主句用 and 连接 looked tired 和 apologised 两个谓语。
