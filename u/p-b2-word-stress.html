Word stress in long words 多音节词的重音

Use the texts and recordings with your coursebook; this page adds new words, grammar and practice.教材定位：English File 5e · Upper-Intermediate · 3B《A twist in the tale》；English File 5e · Upper-Intermediate · 5A《Get me out of here!》；English File 5e · Upper-Intermediate · 8B《Faking it》；English File 5e · Advanced · 6A《A helping hand》。课文与录音请配合教材学习，本页补充新词、语法与练习。

Put the stress on the right syllable of long words, so that people recognise the word the first time you say it. 把长词的重音放在对的音节上，让对方第一次就听出你说的是哪个词。

B2 · CELPIP 7–8⏱ 25 minP08 Word stress in the wrong place词重音放错

📏 The rule规则

📏 The point in one line一句话要领
A long English word has one strong syllable, louder, longer and higher; the others shrink, and many of them become a schwa. Get the strong syllable wrong and the word may not be recognised at all.英语长词里只有一个强音节，更响、更长、更高；其余音节缩短，很多变成 /ə/。强音节放错，对方可能根本认不出这个词。

comfortable /ˈkʌmftəbəl/
Three syllables, not four: COMF-tuh-bul. 三个音节，不是四个。
vegetable /ˈvedʒtəbəl/
First syllable strong; the second e disappears. 重读第一节，第二个 e 不发音。
chocolate /ˈtʃɒklət/
Two syllables in normal speech. 正常语速只有两个音节。
calendar /ˈkæləndə/
CAL, not len. 重读 CAL，不是 len。
develop /dɪˈveləp/
de-VEL-op; the first syllable is a weak /dɪ/. 第一节是弱的 /dɪ/。
photographer /fəˈtɒɡrəfə/
The first syllable shrinks to /fə/. 第一节缩成 /fə/。
economy /ɪˈkɒnəmi/
e-CON-o-my; three weak syllables around one strong one. 一强三弱。
economic /ˌiːkəˈnɒmɪk/
-ic pulls the stress to the syllable before it. -ic 把重音拉到前一节。
information /ˌɪnfəˈmeɪʃən/
-tion: stress the syllable just before. -tion 结尾重读前一节。
ability /əˈbɪləti/
-ity: stress two syllables before the end. -ity 结尾重读倒数第三节。
interesting /ˈɪntrəstɪŋ/
IN-tres-ting, three syllables, stress on the first. 三个音节，重音在第一节。
appointment /əˈpɔɪntmənt/
a-POINT-ment; both a and -ment are weak. 首尾都弱。
I'd like to make an appointment for Thursday. /aɪd ˈlaɪk tə ˈmeɪk ən əˈpɔɪntmənt fə ˈθɜːzdeɪ/
Keep POINT strong inside the sentence. 在句子里也要让 POINT 突出。
The economy affects the economic data. /ði ɪˈkɒnəmi əˈfekts ði ˌiːkəˈnɒmɪk ˈdeɪtə/
CON in the noun, NOM in the adjective. 名词重读 CON，形容词重读 NOM。
It's a comfortable hotel with a good restaurant. /ɪts ə ˈkʌmftəbəl həʊˈtel wɪð ə ɡʊd ˈrestrɒnt/
ho-TEL is stressed on the second syllable. hotel 重音在第二节。
We need more information about your ability to pay. /wi ˈniːd ˈmɔːr ɪnfəˈmeɪʃən əˌbaʊt jər əˈbɪləti tə ˈpeɪ/
MA and BI carry the long words. 两个长词分别靠 MA 和 BI 撑起来。

📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Open the full explanation: 5 points展开细讲：5 段讲解

A colleague says she will "de-VE-lop the AL-go-rithm" correctly, but asks about the "ca-LEN-dar" and the "pho-to-GRAPH". Nobody laughs; they just look blank for a second. English listeners store words by their stress pattern, so a wrong stress is not a small accent, it sends them looking for a different word. A wrong vowel inside the right pattern is understood far more often than right vowels inside the wrong pattern.

同事说 calendar、photograph 时把重音放错，没人笑，只是愣了一秒。英语听者按重音模式记词，重音错不是小口音，而是让对方去找另一个词。模式对、元音偏一点，往往能懂；元音都对、模式错了，常常听不懂。

Making a stressed syllable takes three things at once: say it a little louder, hold the vowel longer, and let the pitch jump up on it. Then do the opposite to the rest: make them short and quiet, and let their vowels relax into /ə/ or /ɪ/. In chocolate you hear CHOC and almost nothing else: /ˈtʃɒklət/. The contrast between strong and weak is the point; a stressed syllable surrounded by full vowels does not sound stressed.

重读音节要同时做三件事：稍响一点、元音拉长、音高往上跳。其余音节反过来：短、轻、元音放松成 /ə/ 或 /ɪ/。关键是强弱对比；周围音节都读得饱满，重音就显不出来。

Mandarin gives every syllable a full vowel and its own tone, so Chinese speakers tend to say com-for-ta-ble as four equal beats, or to stress the syllable that looks important in the spelling. Spelling does not help: photograph, photographer and photographic share their letters but not their stress. Learn every new long word with its pattern, the way you learn its meaning.

普通话每个音节都有完整的元音和声调，所以华人常把 comfortable 读成四个一样重的音节，或者重读拼写上看着重要的那一节。拼写帮不了忙：同样的字母，重音位置可以不同。学长词要连重音一起记，就像记词义一样。

Some patterns are reliable enough to use. Most two-syllable nouns and adjectives stress the first syllable (PROgress, HAPpy); many two-syllable verbs stress the second (deCIDE, reLAX). Words ending in -tion, -sion, -ic and -ical stress the syllable just before the ending (inforMAtion, ecoNOmic). Words ending in -ity, -ify and -graphy stress the syllable two before the end (aBIlity, phoTOgraphy).

有些规律足够可靠。双音节名词、形容词多重读第一节，双音节动词多重读第二节；-tion、-sion、-ic 结尾重读结尾前一节；-ity、-ify、-graphy 结尾重读倒数第三节。

The listening side matters just as much. In a fast meeting you rarely catch every syllable of responsibility; you catch a weak run and then BI. If you know where the stress of a word lives, you can find the word from that one strong syllable. Train your ear by humming the pattern of a word before you say it: da-da-DA-da for photographic.

听力上同样重要。语速快时你很少听全 responsibility 的每个音节，只听到一串轻音再加一个 BI。知道一个词的重音在哪，就能凭那一个强音节把词认出来。练法：说词之前先用「哒」哼出它的节奏型。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ com-FOR-ta-ble (four syllables, stress on FOR)
✓ COMF-tuh-bul
The spelling shows four syllables; the spoken word has three, stressed on the first.
拼写上四节，口语里三节，重读第一节。
✗ de-ve-LOP / DE-ve-lop
✓ de-VEL-op
Either wrong pattern makes listeners search for another word.
两种错误模式都会让听者去找别的词。
✗ PHO-TO-GRA-PHER (every syllable full)
✓ fuh-TOG-ruh-fuh
A stressed syllable only stands out if the others are weak.
只有其他音节弱下去，重音才显得出来。
✗ ho-tel stressed like a Chinese two-syllable word (HO-tel)
✓ ho-TEL
Some common loanwords are stressed on the last syllable: hotel, guitar, cartoon.
一些常见外来词重读最后一节。
