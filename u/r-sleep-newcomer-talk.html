Starting Over Is Stressful, and That Is Normal 从头再来本来就累，这很正常

Read and listen读与听

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Good evening, everyone, and thank you for coming out on a cold Tuesday. I was asked to talk about stress, and I want to start with a number that is not a statistic: three. In my first year here I moved three times, changed jobs three times, and lay awake most nights wondering whether I had made a terrible mistake.

大家晚上好，谢谢各位在这么冷的周二出门来。我受邀来讲压力，想先从一个不是统计数字的数字说起：三。来这里第一年，我搬了三次家，换了三次工作，大多数夜里睁着眼躺着，想自己是不是犯了个可怕的错误。

I tell you this because many newcomers believe they are the only ones struggling. You are not. Moving to a new country means losing your language, your network and often your professional identity, all at once. Feeling overwhelmed by that is not a weakness. It is a reasonable response to an unreasonable amount of change.

我说这些，是因为很多新移民以为只有自己在挣扎。不是的。移居新国家，意味着同时失去你的语言、你的人脉，往往还有你的职业身份。为此觉得招架不住不是软弱，而是对过量变化的合理反应。

What helps? The first step is awareness. Learn your own warning signs: for some people it is poor sleep, for others it is anger or low mood, or a body clock that has gone completely out of order. Notice them early, before they become a crisis. The second step is flexibility about your expectations. Your first job here may not match the one you left. That is not the end of the story, only the first chapter.

什么有帮助？第一步是觉察。了解你自己的警示信号：有人是睡不好，有人是易怒或情绪低落，有人是生物钟彻底乱了。早点注意到，别等它变成危机。第二步是对期望保持弹性。你在这里的第一份工作也许比不上你离开的那份，但那不是故事的结局，只是第一章。

The third step is connection. Find one place where people know you by name, such as a class, a team or a volunteer shift, and go every week. Confidence comes back slowly, from small things done regularly, not from one big success. And learn to switch off: a walk, a phone call home, an evening with no news.

第三步是联结。找一个有人叫得出你名字的地方，一门课、一支球队、一个义工班次都行，每周都去。自信是慢慢回来的，来自规律地做小事，而不是一次大成功。还要学会放空：散个步，给家里打个电话，过一个不看新闻的晚上。

Finally, if you are not coping, please tell someone: a doctor, a counsellor, or a friend in this room. There is no prize for suffering in silence. Any improvement, even a small one, is worth noticing. Thank you.

最后，如果你撑不住了，请告诉别人：医生、咨询师，或者这屋里的一个朋友。默默硬扛没有奖。任何一点改善，哪怕很小，都值得留意。谢谢大家。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

It is a reasonable response to an unreasonable amount of change.
The same root twice: reasonable and un- + reasonable. The contrast carries the whole point.
同一词根用两次：reasonable 与 un- + reasonable，整句的意思就在这个对照里。
Confidence comes back slowly, from small things done regularly, not from one big success.
done regularly is a past participle phrase after the noun (= things that are done regularly).
done regularly 是后置的过去分词短语（＝that are done regularly）。
