📏 The rule规则

🎯 The point in one line一句话要领
Prices rose sharply, so families bought less becomes The sharp rise in prices led to a reduction in spending. The nouns turn events into objects, and objects can be measured, blamed and compared.「价格猛涨，所以家庭买得少了」变成「价格的急剧上涨导致了支出的减少」。名词把事件变成物件，物件就能被衡量、追责和比较。
  • Easy to miss易漏 ✗ There was an increase of prices last year. → ✓ There was an increase in prices last year.

📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Open the full explanation: 4 points展开细讲：4 段讲解

Spoken English tells stories with verbs: people moved, prices went up, the council reacted. Written argument packs those verbs into nouns: the move, the increase, the council's response. This is called nominalisation, and it is the single biggest difference between a B2 essay and a C1 report.

口语用动词讲故事；书面论证把动词装进名词。这就是名词化，也是 B2 作文和 C1 报告之间最大的差别。

Once an event is a noun, you can add information around it: a sudden increase, the increase in rents, the increase since 2020, the increase that worried tenants. You can also make it the subject of a cause verb: the closure led to delays, the decline resulted from, the shift reflects.

事件一旦成为名词，就可以在它周围加信息（形容词、介词短语、从句），也能让它当因果动词的主语：lead to、result from、reflect。

Each noun has a fixed preposition, and this is where most learners slip: an increase in (not of) prices, a decline in numbers, a reduction in costs, demand for housing, the impact on families, the reason for the change.

每个名词带固定介词，这是最常错的地方：increase in、decline in、reduction in、demand for、impact on、reason for。

Do not nominalise everything. A paragraph made only of nouns (the implementation of the reduction of the duplication of…) is hard to read. Use a noun when you want to say something about the event; keep the verb when you just want to tell what happened.

别什么都名词化。满段名词堆叠很难读。想对事件评说时用名词，只是叙述发生了什么时保留动词。

✓ There was an increase in prices last year.
increase in the thing that rises; of is for the amount: an increase of 5%.
增加的对象用 in；of 后面接幅度。
✗ The closure had a big impact to local shops.
✓ The closure had a big impact on local shops.
impact on.
impact on。
✗ The demand of housing is growing.
✓ The demand for housing is growing.
demand for.
demand for。
✗ The lack of sleep leads to decrease concentration.
✓ A lack of sleep leads to a decrease in concentration.
After lead to, use a noun phrase.
lead to 后接名词短语。
