Negative questions and questions with that-clauses 否定疑问句与带 that 从句的疑问句

📘 In your coursebook: English File 5e · Upper-Intermediate · 1A “A tricky question?”. Use the texts and recordings with your coursebook; this page adds new words, grammar and practice.教材定位：English File 5e · Upper-Intermediate · 1A《A tricky question?》。课文与录音请配合教材学习，本页补充新词、语法与练习。

Choose accurately between positive and negative yes/no questions, and build questions that contain that-clauses, so that your expectation, surprise, or criticism comes across as intended. 准确选择肯定与否定一般疑问句，并正确构造带 that 从句的疑问句，使自己的预期、惊讶或批评如实传达。

Is he coming to the party? 他来参加聚会吗？
A neutral question; the speaker has no particular expectation.中性提问；说话人没有特别的预期。
Isn't he coming to the party? 他不来参加聚会吗？
The speaker expected him to come and is surprised or disappointed by new evidence.说话人原本预期他会来，因新的迹象而感到惊讶或失望。
He's coming to the party, isn't he? 他会来参加聚会的，对吧？
A tag question; the speaker is seeking confirmation of what they already believe.附加疑问句；说话人在求证自己已有的看法。

📏 The rule规则

📏 The rule in one line规则一句话
A negative question is not simply a negated question: it carries a bias, so the speaker is usually checking, expressing surprise, suggesting, or complaining rather than asking neutrally.否定疑问句不只是把问句变成否定形式：它带有说话人的倾向，通常是在求证、表示惊讶、提出建议或表达不满，而不是中性提问。

Use a positive question when you genuinely have no expectation, and a negative question when you expected one answer and the evidence points the other way, or when you want the listener to confirm what you already believe. Compare 'Is she coming?' with 'Isn't she coming?': the second signals that you assumed she would come and are now surprised or worried. Choosing the wrong form makes you sound either oddly suspicious or oddly leading.

如果你确实没有预期，就用肯定疑问句；如果你原本预期是某个答案、现在证据却相反，或你想让对方确认你已有的看法，就用否定疑问句。比较 Is she coming? 和 Isn't she coming?：后者表明你本以为她会来，现在感到意外或担心。用错形式，会让你听起来要么莫名多疑，要么在诱导对方。

Negative questions can serve as polite suggestions, invitations, or mild criticism, which is why they feel softer than imperatives in many contexts. 'Wouldn't it be better to wait?' suggests waiting without issuing an order, while 'Why don't you wait?' is a casual suggestion and 'Why didn't you wait?' is a complaint about the past. The tense and the wh-word decide whether the same negative shape is advice or blame.

否定疑问句可以充当委婉的建议、邀请或轻微的批评，所以在很多场合比祈使句更柔和。Wouldn't it be better to wait? 是不下命令的建议；Why don't you wait? 是随意的提议；Why didn't you wait? 则是对过去的责备。同样的否定形式是建议还是指责，取决于时态和疑问词。

Answering a negative question is a trap even at an advanced level, because the answer words refer to the facts, not to the question's polarity. If someone asks 'Don't you like jazz?', you say 'Yes, I do' if you like it and 'No, I don't' if you do not; the answer must be followed by a full short form to avoid ambiguity. When the situation matters, such as a medical or legal exchange, add a clarifying clause instead of relying on yes or no alone.

即便到了高级阶段，回答否定疑问句仍是个陷阱，因为 yes 和 no 对应的是事实，而不是问句的肯定或否定形式。有人问 Don't you like jazz?，喜欢就答 Yes, I do，不喜欢就答 No, I don't，并且要带上完整的简短形式以避免歧义。在医疗或法律等重要场合，最好再补一句话说明，而不是只靠 yes 或 no。

Questions that contain a that-clause, such as 'Do you think that he will resign?', behave differently from simple yes/no questions because the question word or auxiliary belongs to the main verb, while the content of the that-clause is the thing being asked about. With wh-questions, a famous choice appears: 'What do you think he said?' with the wh-word fronted, versus 'Do you know what he said?' with embedded word order. Using verbs like think, suppose, believe and imagine produces fronted wh-words; verbs like know, wonder and ask do not.

带 that 从句的疑问句（如 Do you think that he will resign?）与简单的是非问句不同：助动词属于主句动词，而从句的内容才是被询问的对象。在特殊疑问句中会出现一个著名的选择：What do you think he said? 把疑问词提前，而 Do you know what he said? 则用陈述语序的嵌入从句。think、suppose、believe、imagine 这类动词会让疑问词前置，know、wonder、ask 则不会。

Negative raising and negative questions interact in a way that confuses many learners: 'Don't you think that he should apologize?' is a leading question that invites agreement, whereas 'Do you think that he shouldn't apologize?' is a neutral but awkward request for the listener's view of a negative proposition. Native speakers prefer to put the negative in the main clause when asking for agreement, because it sounds more natural and conveys the same meaning more gently.

否定转移与否定疑问句会相互作用，让许多学习者困惑：Don't you think that he should apologize? 是邀请对方同意的引导性问句，而 Do you think that he shouldn't apologize? 虽然中性，却显得生硬。母语者想征求同意时，更倾向于把否定放在主句里，因为这样听起来更自然、语气也更温和。

More pairs: same idea, different choice更多对比句组

Why don't you ask your manager for a raise? 你为什么不向经理要求加薪呢？
A casual, friendly suggestion about the present or future.对现在或将来的随意、友好的建议。
Why didn't you ask your manager for a raise? 你当时为什么没向经理要求加薪呢？
A criticism or a request for an explanation about a past omission.对过去未做之事的批评或解释请求。
Why do you ask your manager for a raise every year? 你为什么每年都向经理要求加薪？
A neutral question about a habit, with no suggestion or complaint.对习惯的中性提问，没有建议或抱怨。
What do you think he wants? 你认为他想要什么？
The wh-word is fronted because the main verb is a opinion verb; the speaker asks for a guess.主句动词是表示看法的动词，所以疑问词前置；说话人请对方猜测。
Do you know what he wants? 你知道他想要什么吗？
The wh-clause stays embedded; the speaker asks whether the listener has the information.wh 从句保持嵌入；说话人在问对方是否掌握这个信息。
Can you guess what he wants? 你能猜到他想要什么吗？
The embedded form again, with the guessing itself being the yes/no question.同样是嵌入形式，猜测本身是被询问的是非问题。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ A: Don't you like coffee? B: Yes, I don't.
✓ A: Don't you like coffee? B: No, I don't. / Yes, I do.
The answer word follows the facts, not the form of the question: agreement with a negative fact takes no, and disagreement takes yes. Learners often copy the pattern of their first language, where the answer word agrees with the question's polarity.
回答词要对应事实，而不是问句形式：与否定的事实一致用 no，相反则用 yes。学习者常照搬母语的习惯，即回答词与问句的肯定否定保持一致。
✗
✓ Why didn't you tell me about the problem?
In a negative wh-question the negated auxiliary moves in front of the subject. Learners keep statement word order and place the negative after the subject.
在否定的特殊疑问句中，否定的助动词要移到主语之前。学习者常保留陈述语序，把否定词放在主语后面。
✗
✓ What do you think he said? / Do you know what he said?
With think, believe and suppose the wh-word is fronted and appears only once; with know the wh-word stays in the embedded clause. Learners use the wh-word twice or mix the two patterns.
think、believe、suppose 之后疑问词前置且只出现一次；know 之后疑问词留在嵌入从句中。学习者常把疑问词用两次，或把两种结构混在一起。
✗
✓ Do you know where the station is?
An embedded question keeps statement word order, with no inversion of the verb. Learners carry over the word order of a direct question into the embedded clause.
嵌入疑问从句要保持陈述语序，不倒装动词。学习者常把直接疑问句的语序带进嵌入从句。
✗
✓ Don't you think that he loves her? / Do you think he loves her or not?
When the speaker wants to ask for agreement, the negative normally goes in the main clause, which sounds natural; pushing the negative into the that-clause feels awkward and shifts the meaning. Learners put the negative where their first language puts it.
想征求对方同意时，否定通常放在主句，听起来才自然；把否定塞进 that 从句既生硬又会改变语气。学习者常按母语把否定放在从句里。
