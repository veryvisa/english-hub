📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Open the full explanation: 6 points展开细讲：6 段讲解

Idioms usually communicate a meaning that cannot be understood by translating each word separately. For example, 'break the ice' means to make people feel more comfortable in an unfamiliar social situation, not to break actual ice. Learn the whole expression together with its typical context.

习语通常表达无法通过逐字翻译理解的意思。例如，break the ice 指打破陌生场合中的拘谨、让人放松，而不是真的打碎冰块。学习时应把整个表达和它常见的使用场景结合起来。

Register describes how formal, informal, or neutral a particular expression sounds. 'Ask for help' is neutral, while 'give me a hand' is more conversational. Both can communicate a similar idea, but they do not always suit the same audience or situation.

语域指某种表达听起来有多正式、随意或中性。ask for help 比较中性，而 give me a hand 更偏口语。两者意思相近，但并不一定适合相同的听众和场合。

Some idioms are common in everyday conversation but sound too casual in formal reports, academic writing, or official correspondence. For instance, 'a long shot' is useful when discussing uncertain possibilities, but 'an unlikely possibility' may be more appropriate in a formal analysis.

有些习语在日常对话中很常见，但放在正式报告、学术写作或官方通信中可能显得过于随意。例如，a long shot 可以用来谈论成功机会不大的事情，但正式分析中使用 an unlikely possibility 可能更合适。

An idiom can be perfectly grammatical but still sound unnatural if it is used with the wrong collocation or in the wrong situation. 'Get the ball rolling' commonly refers to starting a process or activity. It is not normally used to describe physically moving an object in a literal context.

一个习语即使语法正确，如果搭配不当或使用场景不合适，仍然会显得不自然。get the ball rolling 通常指启动一个流程或活动，而不是描述真的把一个球滚动起来。

Professional communication does not require avoiding all idioms. Expressions such as 'on the same page' and 'in the long run' are widely understood in workplace discussions. The important distinction is between familiar, widely accepted expressions and slang that may sound too relaxed or unclear.

职场交流并不意味着必须完全避免习语。on the same page 和 in the long run 等表达在工作讨论中很常见，也容易理解。关键是区分广泛接受的习语与可能显得过于随意或含义不清的俚语。

Some idioms have a regional spelling variant that does not change the meaning. This unit's IPA follows British pronunciation, and 'beat around the bush' is the American form; British English usually says 'beat about the bush'. Either is understood, but it helps to recognise both.

有些习语存在不影响含义的地区拼写差异。本单元音标按英式标注，而 beat around the bush 是美式说法；英式英语通常说 beat about the bush。两种说法都能被理解，但最好都能认出来。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ Let's break the ice between the two companies by signing the contract.
✓ Let's break the ice between the two teams by starting with a friendly conversation.
Breaking the ice means reducing social awkwardness or tension, usually when people first meet. Signing a contract is a business action, not an ice-breaking activity.
break the ice 指通过交流缓解初次见面时的拘谨或紧张。签合同本身是一项商业行为，并不是打破社交僵局的活动。
✗ I am writing to cut to the chase: the invoice is overdue.
✓ I am writing to raise an urgent matter: the invoice is overdue.
'Cut to the chase' is conversational, so it clashes with the formal opening of a written request. 'Raise an urgent matter' keeps the same directness but at a register that suits a business email.
cut to the chase 偏口语，用在正式邮件开头与语域不符。raise an urgent matter 保留了同样的直接，但语域更适合商务信函。
✗ My manager hit the nail at the head when she identified the problem.
✓ My manager hit the nail on the head when she identified the problem.
The idiom has a fixed preposition: 'on the head', not 'at the head'. Idioms should generally be learned as complete expressions rather than assembled from individually translated words.
这个习语有固定介词搭配，必须说 on the head，而不是 at the head。习语应作为完整表达记忆，不能根据单词的中文意思随意组合。
✗ Please keep me in the loop about of any changes.
✓ Please keep me in the loop about any changes.
The preposition 'about' already introduces the information, so adding 'of' creates an unnecessary double preposition. You can also say 'keep me informed about any changes'.
about 已经引出相关信息，后面不能再额外加 of。也可以使用更中性的 keep me informed about any changes，表示及时告知变化。
✗ With all due respect, your idea is stupid.
✓ With all due respect, I don't think your proposal is practical.
'With all due respect' introduces disagreement, but it does not automatically make insulting language polite. In professional communication, express the specific concern rather than attacking the person or their intelligence.
with all due respect 虽然用于引出不同意见，却不能自动让侮辱性语言变得礼貌。在职场交流中，应指出具体问题，而不是攻击对方或贬低对方的能力。
✗ It has been raining all week, so the whole team feels a bit under the weather about the outdoor event.
✓ I'm feeling a bit under the weather today, so I might work from home.
'Under the weather' always describes a person feeling slightly ill, never bad weather itself or a mood caused by it. Do not use it to mean 'affected by the weather'.
under the weather 永远指某人身体有点不舒服，而不是天气本身不好，也不是因天气而产生的心情。不能用来表示「受天气影响」。
