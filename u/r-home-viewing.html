Twelve Viewings and One Lesson 看了十二套房，学到一件事

🧠 Learn the 5 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 5 个新词

Read a story about apartment hunting and watch the tenses move between then and now. 读一个找房的故事，看时态怎样在「那时」和「现在」之间切换。

B2 · CELPIP 7–8⏱ 20 minG02 Present perfect and past simple confused现在完成时与一般过去时混用G08 Tense not kept consistent across a story叙事时态前后不一

We have lived in this apartment for two years now, and I have almost forgotten how hard it was to find. In the spring of our first year here, my wife and I saw twelve places in three weeks.

我们在这套公寓住了两年了，我几乎忘了当初找它有多难。来这里第一年的春天，我和妻子三周里看了十二套房。

The first viewing was a basement with one small window. The agent said it was "cosy". The second had a beautiful kitchen and a bedroom that faced a six-lane road. By the fifth, we had stopped believing the photos. One listing showed a bright living room; when we arrived, we discovered that the picture had been taken from the neighbour's unit.

第一套是只有一扇小窗的地下室，中介说它「温馨」。第二套厨房很漂亮，卧室却正对着六车道的马路。看到第五套，我们已经不再相信照片了。有一条房源展示了明亮的客厅；到了才发现，照片是在隔壁那套里拍的。

The lesson came at number nine. It was perfect: quiet street, big windows, fair rent. We said we would think about it overnight. By the next morning, someone else had signed. We had never lost anything so quickly. After that we brought every document with us, and when number twelve felt right, we applied on the spot.

教训出在第九套。它无可挑剔：街道安静，窗户大，租金公道。我们说回去考虑一晚。第二天早上，别人已经签了。我们从没这么快地错失过什么。此后我们每次都把材料带齐，等第十二套感觉对了，就当场申请。

I have told this story to every newcomer I have met since. You cannot control the market, but you can be ready. Have you got your references? Have you checked your credit report? If not, do it before the first viewing, not after the ninth. We were lucky in the end. We have never regretted the choice, though I still walk past number nine sometimes and look up at those windows.

此后遇到的每个新来的人，我都讲过这个故事。市场你左右不了，但你可以做好准备。推荐信拿到了吗？信用报告查过了吗？如果没有，在第一次看房之前办好，别等到第九次之后。我们最后算是走运。我们从没后悔过这个选择，不过我有时还是会路过第九套，抬头看看那几扇窗。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

One listing showed a bright living room; when we arrived, we discovered that the picture had been taken from the neighbour's unit.
Past perfect passive had been taken: the photo was taken before the visit.
过去完成时的被动 had been taken：拍照发生在看房之前。
I have told this story to every newcomer I have met since.
Two present perfects: both the telling and the meeting run from then up to now. since at the end means "since that time".
两个现在完成时：讲和遇见都从那时延续到现在。句末的 since 意为「从那以后」。
