Why Small Talk Is Not Small 寒暄为什么不是小事

🧠 Learn the 5 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 5 个新词

Follow an argument about why small talk matters at work, and notice how a, the and no article are used along the way. 读懂一篇讲职场寒暄为什么重要的评论，顺便留意冠词的用法。

B2 · CELPIP 7–8⏱ 20 minV06 Register: too formal, too casual, or too blunt语域不合：太正式、太随意或太冲G01 Articles left out or over-used冠词漏用、滥用

📖 Key card知识卡
Small talk carries no information; it carries a signal: I see you, and I am not a threat.寒暄不传递信息，它传递一个信号：我看见你了，我没有恶意。

Read and listen读与听

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

When I moved from Shanghai to Vancouver, I thought small talk was a waste of time. At work I said good morning and went straight to my desk. I was there to do research and write reports, not to discuss the weather. After three months my reviews were fine, yet nobody invited me to anything.

我从上海搬到温哥华时，觉得寒暄是浪费时间。上班时我说声早上好就直奔座位。我是来做研究、写报告的，不是来聊天气的。三个月后，我的考评不错，却没有人邀请我参加任何活动。

A colleague finally explained it over lunch. "You are good at your job," she said, "but people do not know you. They cannot tell if you are friendly or angry." The advice she gave me was simple: every morning, ask one person one question that is not about work.

一位同事终于在午饭时点破了。她说：「你工作做得好，但大家不了解你，看不出你是友善还是在生气。」她给我的建议很简单：每天早上，问一个人一个与工作无关的问题。

So I made an effort. I asked about the hockey game, the dog in the photo on someone's desk, the long weekend. The questions felt empty at first. Then I noticed what they were doing. Small talk is not really about information. It is a signal that says: I see you, and I am not a threat. Once people have that signal, they give feedback more honestly, share news earlier, and forgive mistakes faster.

于是我下了功夫。我问冰球比赛，问某人桌上照片里的狗，问长周末。起初这些问题显得空洞。后来我看出了它们的作用。寒暄其实与信息无关，它是一个信号：我看见你了，我没有恶意。有了这个信号，人们给反馈更坦诚，分享消息更早，原谅错误也更快。

Small talk also has rules, and they differ from the rules at home. Safe topics are the weather, food, travel, sport and weekend plans. Salary, age, weight and politics are not. And the answer to "How are you?" is never a medical report. It took me a year to stop finding this strange. Now, when a new colleague walks past everyone in silence, I recognise the person I was, and I make a point of asking the first question myself.

寒暄也有规矩，而且和老家的规矩不同。安全的话题是天气、吃的、旅行、运动和周末计划；薪水、年龄、体重和政治不是。「你好吗？」的回答永远不是一份体检报告。我花了一年才不再觉得这奇怪。现在，看到新同事一言不发地从大家身边走过，我认出了从前的自己，于是特意由我先开口问第一个问题。

/weɪst/ phr.
浪费时间

/ˈsɪɡnəl/ n.

A smile sends a signal that you are open to talk. 微笑传递出你愿意交谈的信号。
/θret/ n.
威胁
a threat to sb; pose a threat
He did not see the new hire as a threat. 他没把新来的人当成威胁。
/fəˈɡɪv/ v.
原谅

She forgave me for forgetting her name. 她原谅了我忘记她的名字。
/pɔɪnt/ phr.

I make a point of learning everyone's name. 我特意记住每个人的名字。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

Once people have that signal, they give feedback more honestly, share news earlier, and forgive mistakes faster.
Once means "as soon as, after". Three parallel verb phrases share the subject they, each ending in a comparative adverb.
Once 意为「一旦」。三个并列的动词短语共用主语 they，各以一个比较级副词收尾。
Now, when a new colleague walks past everyone in silence, I recognise the person I was, and I make a point of asking the first question myself.
the person I was is "the person that I used to be"; the relative pronoun is dropped.
the person I was 即「从前的我」，关系代词被省略。
