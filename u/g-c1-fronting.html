Fronting and word order for focus 前置与焦点语序

Move an object, a complement or a place phrase to the front to link back to what was just said or to set up a contrast, without falling into the Chinese topic-comment pattern. 把宾语、表语或地点短语提到句首，用来承接上文或制造对比，同时避开中文「话题—评论」式的错句。

I found this book useful. 我觉得这本书有用。
Plain order: the default.普通语序：默认说法。
This book I found useful; the other one less so. 这本我觉得有用，另一本没那么有用。
Fronted for contrast with another book.前置是为了和另一本书对比。
This book, I think it is useful. 这本书，我觉得它有用。
Wrong in writing: Chinese topic-comment with a doubled object.书面上是错的：中文话题评论式，宾语重复了。

📏 The rule规则

📏 The rule in one line规则一句话
English puts old information first and new information last. Fronting moves a phrase to the start because it links to what you just said (This I understand) or contrasts with it (The food was fine. The service we could have done without.). The subject and verb stay in normal order, and there is no second pronoun.英语习惯旧信息在前、新信息在后。前置是把一个短语挪到句首，因为它承接上文（This I understand），或与上文形成对比。主语和谓语保持正常语序，也不再多加一个代词。

Chinese often starts with a topic and comments on it: this book, I think it is useful. English allows a fronted object, but only when the subject and verb stay in place and the fronted thing is not repeated: This book I found useful. (correct, emphatic) vs ✗ This book I found it useful. Most of the time the plain order is better: I found this book useful.

中文常先说话题再评论。英语也允许宾语前置，但主谓语序不变，前置的东西也不能再用代词重复一遍。大多数时候普通语序更好。

The real reason to front is linking. If the last sentence ended with the paperwork, starting the next one with it makes the paragraph flow: We were asked for three documents. Two of them we had; the third we had never heard of. The fronted part carries old information; the end of the sentence carries the news.

前置真正的理由是衔接。上一句以文件结尾，下一句就从文件说起，段落就顺了。前置的部分承载旧信息，句末才是新消息。

Fronting for contrast works in pairs: Some of the advice was useful. The rest I ignored. Money we can find. Time we can't. In speech the fronted word takes strong stress. Without a contrast nearby, a fronted object can sound strange or poetic.

为了对比的前置通常成对出现。口语里前置词要重读。附近没有对比时，宾语前置会显得奇怪或像诗歌。

Fronted place and movement phrases bring a full inversion of a simple verb: Down came the rain. Here comes the bus. On the wall hung a map of the city. This is storytelling and description, often for something arriving or being revealed. With a pronoun subject, do not invert: Here it comes, not ✗ Here comes it.

地点或方向短语前置时，简单动词和主语会整体倒装，用于讲故事和描写，常用在某物到来或被看到的时刻。主语是代词时不倒装：Here it comes。

Fronting a complement adds weight and is mainly written: Gone are the days when one income could buy a house. So serious was the damage that the road closed for a month. Such was the demand that the shop ran out by noon. These are good in a column or a speech; once per text is enough.

表语前置增加分量，主要用于书面：一份收入就能买房的日子一去不复返了；损坏严重到道路封了一个月。这些适合专栏或演讲，一篇用一次就够了。

Examples例句

  • This I can accept; what I can't accept is the silence. 这一点我能接受，我不能接受的是不回应。
    Fronted object links to the previous point.前置宾语承接上一点。
  • Two of the documents we had. The third we had never heard of. 两份文件我们有，第三份我们听都没听过。
    Contrast between two parts.两部分之间的对比。
  • Some of the advice was useful. The rest I quietly ignored. 有些建议有用，其余的我悄悄忽略了。
    The rest = old information placed first.the rest 是旧信息，放在前面。
  • Money we can always find. Time we can't. 钱总能找到，时间找不到。
    Short contrastive pair; stress the fronted words.简短对比，重读前置词。
  • That kind of mistake I make only once. 那种错误我只犯一次。
    Emphatic, slightly informal.带强调，略口语。
  • Here comes the bus, finally. 公交车终于来了。
    Noun subject: verb before subject.名词主语：动词在主语前。
  • Here it comes. 来了。
    Pronoun subject: normal order.代词主语：正常语序。
  • Down came the rain, just as we reached the car. 我们刚走到车边，雨就下来了。
    Movement phrase fronted in a story.讲故事时把方向短语前置。
  • On the kitchen wall hung a calendar full of appointments. 厨房墙上挂着一本记满约会的日历。
    Description: the new thing comes last.描写：新事物放在最后。
  • At the end of the street stands the old fire hall. 街道尽头矗立着老消防站。
    Place first, building last.先说地点，最后说建筑。
  • Gone are the days when one income could pay for a house. 一份收入就能买房的日子一去不复返了。
    Fixed, written frame for change over time.讲时代变化的固定书面框架。
  • So strong was the wind that the ferry stayed in port. 风大到渡轮都没出港。
    So + adjective fronted: formal storytelling.so 加形容词前置：正式叙事。
  • Such was the demand that the store ran out of heaters by noon. 需求大到商店中午就卖光了取暖器。
    Such was … that: written emphasis.such was … that：书面强调。
  • Most of this you already know. 这些大部分你已经知道了。
    Spoken opening that links to shared knowledge.口语开场，承接双方已知的内容。

More pairs: same idea, different choice更多对比句组

The days when one income could buy a house are gone. 一份收入能买房的日子已经过去了。
Plain, neutral.普通，中性。
Gone are the days when one income could buy a house. 一份收入就能买房的日子一去不复返了。
Fronted complement: nostalgic, written.表语前置：带感慨，书面。
You can't buy a house on one income any more. 现在靠一份收入买不起房了。
Everyday speech.日常口语。
The bus is coming. 公交车来了。
Neutral report.中性陈述。
Here comes the bus. 车来了！
Spoken, pointing at it as it arrives.口语，看着车开过来时说。
Here it comes. 来了！
Pronoun: no inversion.代词：不倒装。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ This problem, we must solve it soon.
✓ We must solve this problem soon. / This problem we must solve soon.
Do not repeat the fronted object with it.
不要用 it 重复前置的宾语。
✗ My English teacher, she is very strict.
✓ My English teacher is very strict.
A doubled subject is a Chinese topic-comment habit; in English the noun is the subject.
主语重复是中文话题评论的习惯，英语里名词本身就是主语。
✗ Here comes it.
✓ Here it comes.
No inversion with a pronoun subject.
代词主语不倒装。
✗ On the table were lying my keys.
✓ My keys were on the table. / On the table lay my keys.
Fronted place + simple verb, not a continuous form; usually the plain order is best.
地点前置配简单动词，不用进行时；通常普通语序最好。
✗ So tired I was that I fell asleep.
✓ So tired was I that I fell asleep. / I was so tired that I fell asleep.
After fronted so + adjective, invert be and the subject.
so 加形容词前置后，be 与主语倒装。
