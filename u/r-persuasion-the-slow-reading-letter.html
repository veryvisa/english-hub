Dear Alex, I have been thinking about how much time we spend consuming media every day. News updates, short videos, podcasts and online discussions compete for our attention from morning until night. Although access to information has become remarkably convenient, the abundance of content does not automatically produce better understanding. Sometimes, the more we read, the less clearly we think.

亲爱的亚历克斯：我一直在思考我们每天花多少时间接触媒体。从早晨到晚上，新闻更新、短视频、播客和网络讨论都在争夺我们的注意力。尽管获取信息已经变得非常方便，但内容数量的增加并不会自动带来更好的理解。有时候，我们读得越多，思考反而越不清晰。

I have therefore started to change my reading habits. Instead of checking every notification immediately, I choose particular times to review important developments. I also try to read complete articles rather than relying on headlines or short extracts. This approach takes longer, but it gives me a better opportunity to understand context, recognise uncertainty and evaluate the evidence behind an argument.

因此，我开始改变自己的阅读习惯。我不再立即查看每一条通知，而是选择特定时间了解重要进展。我也尽量阅读完整文章，而不是依赖标题或简短摘录。这种方法需要更多时间，但能让我更好地理解背景、识别不确定性，并评估论点背后的证据。

Another useful habit is to notice my emotional reactions. If a story makes me unusually angry or frightened, I now pause before sharing it. That reaction may be understandable, but it does not necessarily mean that the report is accurate. Strong emotions can encourage immediate judgement, while careful investigation tends to produce more reliable conclusions. I have also learned to distinguish between disagreement and deliberate misinformation.

另一个有用的习惯是留意自己的情绪反应。如果一则新闻让我异常愤怒或害怕，我现在会在分享之前暂停一下。这种反应可能可以理解，但并不一定意味着报道准确。强烈的情绪可能促使人们立即作出判断，而仔细调查往往会产生更加可靠的结论。我也学会了区分意见分歧与蓄意传播的错误信息。

I am not suggesting that we should distrust everything we read. That would be exhausting and unrealistic. Rather, I believe we should match our level of scrutiny to the importance of the claim. For a casual entertainment recommendation, a quick check may be enough. For information that could affect our health, finances or relationships, a more careful review is appropriate. By and large, independent thinking begins with the willingness to slow down. Best wishes, Sam.

我并不是建议我们不信任读到的一切。那样既令人疲惫，也不现实。相反，我认为我们应该根据主张的重要程度来调整审查的力度。对于普通的娱乐推荐，简单核实可能就足够了。对于可能影响健康、财务或人际关系的信息，则应该进行更加仔细的审查。总体而言，独立思考始于愿意放慢脚步。祝好，萨姆。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

Although introduces a concession, and the present perfect has become describes a development that began in the past and remains relevant now.
Although 引导让步状语从句，现在完成时 has become 描述从过去开始并持续影响现在的发展。
If a story makes me unusually angry or frightened, I now pause before sharing it.
