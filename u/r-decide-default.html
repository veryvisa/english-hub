One Line on a Form Doubles Sign-ups 表格上改一行，参与翻一倍

🧠 Learn the 5 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 5 个新词

Read a local news story about how a default choice changed behaviour, and use the noun and adjective forms that carry news English. 读一则关于默认选项改变行为的本地新闻，学会新闻英语里常用的名词与形容词形式。

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

When a mid-sized city in Ontario changed one line on a form, the number of households using its food-waste program more than doubled. Residents were not offered money or prizes. The city simply made the program the default: instead of asking people to sign up, it sent every home a green bin and allowed people to opt out.

安大略省一座中型城市在一张表格上改了一行字，使用厨余垃圾项目的家庭数量就翻了一倍多。居民并没有拿到钱或奖品。市政府只是把这个项目设成了默认选项：不再请大家报名，而是给每户都送了一个绿桶，允许不想参加的人退出。

City staff say the results were well above their expectations. Under the old system, about a third of households had joined after three years of advertising. Within a year of the change, most households were using the bins, and only a small number had asked to opt out. "We did not change people's opinions," one manager said. "We changed which option needed effort."

市政工作人员说，结果远超他们的预期。旧制度下，宣传三年后约三分之一的家庭加入了。改动后一年之内，大多数家庭都在用这个桶，只有少数人要求退出。「我们并没有改变人们的看法，」一位负责人说，「我们改变的是哪个选项需要花力气。」

Researchers who study decision-making are not surprised. Defaults work because doing nothing is the easiest choice, and because many people assume the default is what experts recommend. The same pattern has been found with workplace savings plans and in other areas where the sensible choice is clear but easy to put off.

研究决策的学者并不意外。默认选项之所以管用，是因为什么都不做最省事，也因为很多人以为默认的就是专家推荐的。同样的规律也在职场储蓄计划等领域被发现过——那些地方明智的选择很清楚，却很容易被拖延。

Not everyone is pleased. Some residents complained that the city had made the decision for them, and one local councillor called the approach "unreasonable pressure". Supporters reply that every form has a default anyway; the only question is whether it is chosen with care. The city says flexibility matters, and anyone can return the bin with one phone call.

不是每个人都满意。有居民抱怨市政府替他们做了决定，一位本地市议员称这种做法是「不合理的施压」。支持者回应说，每张表格反正都有默认选项，唯一的问题是它是不是用心选的。市政府表示灵活性很重要，任何人打一个电话就能退还垃圾桶。

Experts say the lesson goes beyond garbage. Awareness of defaults helps in everyday life: check which plan your phone company puts you on, what happens when a free trial ends, and which box is already ticked on a form. Often the most important decision is the one you did not know you were making.

专家说，这个教训不止适用于垃圾。留意默认选项在日常生活里也有帮助：看看电话公司给你安排的是哪个套餐，免费试用结束后会发生什么，表格上哪个框已经被勾上了。最重要的决定，往往是那个你不知道自己正在做的决定。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

Under the old system, about a third of households had joined after three years of advertising.
Past perfect had joined: a result already reached before the change. a third of + plural noun takes a plural verb.
过去完成时 had joined：在改动之前就已达到的结果。a third of 后接复数名词，谓语用复数。
Awareness of defaults helps in everyday life: check which plan your phone company puts you on, what happens when a free trial ends, and which box is already ticked on a form.
aware of → awareness of: the noun keeps the preposition. The three which / what clauses are indirect questions, so normal word order (your phone company puts).
aware of 变 awareness of，介词保留。三个 which/what 从句是间接疑问，用陈述语序。
