A Shovel and a Neighbour 一把雪铲和一位邻居

🧠 Learn the 4 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 4 个新词

Read how one snowy morning turned a stranger next door into a neighbour, and notice how the story keeps its past tenses straight. 读一个雪天早晨怎样把隔壁的陌生人变成邻居，留意故事里的过去时态怎样一路保持一致。

The first winter after we moved into our house, I kept to myself. I barely knew a single name on the street, and I told myself that this was normal in a Canadian city. People waved, but everyone drove straight into their garage, and the door came down behind them.

搬进新房子后的第一个冬天，我一直独来独往。整条街的人名我几乎一个都叫不上来，还安慰自己说，在加拿大的城市里这很正常。大家会挥挥手，但每个人都把车直接开进车库，车库门随后就落了下来。

Then came a storm that left thirty centimetres of snow overnight. While I was clearing our sidewalk, I noticed that the house next door had not been touched. An old man lived there alone; I had seen him once, carrying groceries very slowly. I took a short break, looked at his front steps and decided to do the whole thing. It took forty minutes, and my back hurt for two days.

后来来了一场暴风雪，一夜积了三十厘米。我在清自家门前的人行道时，发现隔壁那家一点都没动。那里独自住着一位老人，我只见过他一次，拎着一袋菜走得很慢。我歇了一会儿，看看他家门前的台阶，决定全部铲完。花了四十分钟，我的腰疼了两天。

The next evening he knocked on our door with a box of chocolates. "You did me a favour," he said. "My son usually comes, but he couldn't get here." His name was Gordon. He had lived on the street for forty years, and in ten minutes he told me more about the neighbourhood than I had learned in six months: who looks out for whose kids, which family runs the summer barbecue and who to call when the power goes out.

第二天晚上他拿着一盒巧克力来敲我们家的门。「你帮了我一个忙，」他说，「平时我儿子会来，可这次他过不来。」他叫戈登，在这条街上住了四十年。十分钟里，他告诉我的街坊事比我半年里知道的还多：谁会帮着照看谁家的孩子，哪家张罗夏天的烧烤，停电了该找谁。

I do not want to make this sound bigger than it was. We are not best friends. But I now make an effort to have a word with him whenever I see him, he drops by with the local paper on Saturdays, and he keeps an eye on our house when we travel. Last month he asked me to help him get to know the new family across the road, because, he said, I knew what it was like to be a newcomer.

我不想把这件事说得太大，我们算不上好朋友。但现在我每次见到他都会特意跟他聊两句，他周六会顺路给我们捎来本地报纸，我们出门旅行时他帮着照看房子。上个月他请我帮他去认识对面新搬来的一家，因为他说，我知道当新来的是什么滋味。

What I learned is simple. On a street where people keep to themselves, nobody is waiting for an invitation. Someone has to go first, and a snow shovel is a very good excuse.

我学到的道理很简单：在人人各过各的街上，没有人在等邀请。总得有人先迈出一步，而一把雪铲就是很好的借口。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

While I was clearing our sidewalk, I noticed that the house next door had not been touched.
Past continuous for the background (was clearing), past simple for the moment (noticed), past perfect passive for what had not happened before it (had not been touched).
过去进行时铺背景，一般过去时写那一刻，过去完成时被动态写在那之前没发生的事。
Last month he asked me to help him get to know the new family across the road, because, he said, I knew what it was like to be a newcomer.
Reported speech inside a story: I knew, not you know, because the reporting verb said is past. help sb get to know: help + object + bare infinitive.
故事里的转述：因为 said 是过去时，所以写 I knew 而不是 you know。help 后接宾语加不带 to 的不定式。
