Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Dear Column, my mother is a wonderful grandmother, and she adores our two children. She also posts photos of them almost every day: in the bath, at school, crying after a fall. Her profile is public, and she tags me in every picture. I have asked her to stop, and she was deeply hurt. She says she is proud of them and I am being paranoid. How do I protect my kids without losing my mother? — Torn

专栏你好：我妈是个很好的长辈，疼我们的两个孩子疼得不得了。可她也几乎天天发孩子的照片：洗澡的、上学的、摔跤后哭的。她的主页是公开的，每张照片都标记我。我请她别发了，她很受伤，说她是为孩子们骄傲，是我疑神疑鬼。我怎样才能既保护孩子，又不伤了和我妈的感情？——「左右为难」

Dear Torn, you are not being paranoid. Your children cannot give permission yet, and pictures posted today may still be searchable when they are teenagers. A bath photo that seems sweet to a grandmother may be humiliating to a fourteen-year-old who finds it. Still, your mother is not a threat; she is a proud grandparent who has not thought about the risks.

「左右为难」你好：你不是疑神疑鬼。孩子现在还没法表示同意，今天发的照片，到他们十几岁时可能还搜得到。一张祖辈觉得可爱的洗澡照，被十四岁的孩子自己翻到，可能让他无地自容。话说回来，你妈不是威胁，她只是一个骄傲、还没想到风险的祖辈。

So have a word with her in private, and start with her feelings, not the rules. Tell her you know how much she loves them. Then make a specific request rather than a general complaint: no bath or bedtime photos, no school uniforms, no tags, and a private family group instead of a public profile. Specific rules are easier to follow than "post less".

所以，私下和她谈谈，先谈她的感受，再谈规矩。告诉她你知道她有多爱孩子。然后提具体的请求，而不是笼统地抱怨：不发洗澡和睡前的照片，不拍校服，不标记，用一个私密的家庭群代替公开主页。具体的规矩比「少发点」容易做到。

It may help to take her generation into account. Many grandparents learned social media as a way to stay close to family far away, not as a public stage. Offer to check her privacy settings with her, and make the effort to send her new photos privately, so she does not feel shut out.

把她那一代人的情况考虑进去也许有帮助。很多祖辈学社交媒体，是为了和远方的家人保持亲近，而不是把它当公开舞台。主动陪她一起检查隐私设置，并且花点心思私下给她发新照片，免得她觉得被关在门外。

Finally, decide what you will do if she forgets. A gentle screenshot and "Could you take this one down?" is better than another argument. You will both make progress faster if she feels she is part of the solution.

最后，先想好她忘了的时候怎么办。轻轻发张截图，问一句「这张能删掉吗？」，比再吵一架好。让她觉得自己是解决办法的一部分，你们俩都会更快见效。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

Then make a specific request rather than a general complaint: no bath or bedtime photos, no school uniforms, no tags, and a private family group instead of a public profile.
make a request and make a complaint share the verb make; rather than sets the two side by side. The colon introduces the list of rules.
make a request 和 make a complaint 共用动词 make；rather than 把两者并列对照。冒号后列出具体规矩。
It may help to take her generation into account.
It is a dummy subject for the to phrase; take sth into account = consider it when you decide.
It 是形式主语，真正的主语是后面的 to 短语；take sth into account＝决定时把它考虑进去。
