Reported speech and reporting verbs 间接引语与转述动词

📘 In your coursebook: English File 5e · Upper-Intermediate · 8B “Faking it”. Use the texts and recordings with your coursebook; this page adds new words, grammar and practice.教材定位：English File 5e · Upper-Intermediate · 8B《Faking it》。课文与录音请配合教材学习，本页补充新词、语法与练习。

Report what people said without repeating it word for word, choose a reporting verb that carries their attitude, and decide when the tense really has to move back. 转述别人的话时不照搬原话，选一个能带出对方态度的转述动词，并判断时态什么时候真的需要往后退。

"I'll fix it tomorrow," he said. 「我明天修，」他说。
The exact words.原话。
He said he would fix it the next day. 他说他第二天修。
Neutral report, told later.事后中性转述。
He promised to fix it the next day. 他答应第二天修。
A commitment: you can hold him to it.是承诺，可以拿来追责。
He claimed he would fix it the next day. 他声称第二天会修。
You doubt it.你不太信。

📏 The rule规则

📏 The rule in one line规则一句话
Reporting is summarising, not quoting. Pick a verb that shows what the speaker was doing (warning, admitting, refusing), then move the tense back only if the situation has changed or you want to sound distant from it.转述是概括，不是引用。先选一个能说明对方在「做什么」的动词（警告、承认、拒绝），再决定时态要不要后退：只有情况已经变了，或你想表示和那句话保持距离时才退。

Many learners report everything with said that and copy the words. Fluent speakers use the reporting verb to do the work. Instead of He said, "Sorry, I was wrong", they say He admitted he was wrong or He apologised for the mistake. One verb replaces a whole sentence and tells the listener the attitude.

很多学习者转述一律用 said that 再照搬原话。说得好的人让转述动词来干活：不说 He said, "Sorry, I was wrong"，而说 He admitted he was wrong 或 He apologised for the mistake。一个动词顶一整句，还交代了态度。

Reporting verbs come with fixed patterns, and this is where most errors happen. + that: say, explain, admit, claim, insist, complain. + to: agree, refuse, promise, offer, threaten. + object + to: tell, ask, warn, remind, advise. + -ing: deny, admit, suggest, recommend. + preposition + -ing: apologise for, insist on, accuse someone of, blame someone for.

转述动词各有固定句型，大多数错误出在这里。接 that：say、explain、admit、claim、insist、complain。接 to：agree、refuse、promise、offer、threaten。接宾语加 to：tell、ask、warn、remind、advise。接 -ing：deny、admit、suggest、recommend。接介词加 -ing：apologise for、insist on、accuse sb of、blame sb for。

The tense shift is not automatic. If the statement is still true, keep the present: The landlord said the rent includes heat. Shift back when the situation has changed, when you are telling a story in the past, or when you want to suggest it may not be true: He told me the car had never been in an accident. The backshift here quietly adds doubt.

时态后退不是自动的。如果那句话现在仍然成立，就保留现在时：The landlord said the rent includes heat。情况已经变了、你在讲过去的故事、或者想暗示那句话未必可信时才后退：He told me the car had never been in an accident。这里的后退悄悄带出了怀疑。

Reported questions use statement word order and no question mark. "Where do you live?" becomes She asked where I lived, not where did I live. Yes/no questions use if or whether: He asked whether we had a parking spot. whether sounds a little more formal and is better when there are two real options.

转述问句用陈述语序，不加问号。"Where do you live?" 变成 She asked where I lived，不说 where did I live。一般疑问句用 if 或 whether：He asked whether we had a parking spot。whether 稍正式，两个选项都实在时更合适。

In news and workplace English, the choice of verb is also a judgement. claim keeps distance: the reporter does not vouch for it. point out and explain treat it as true. admit means it was against the speaker's interest. insist means others doubted it. Choosing between these is how you sound neutral, or fair, in a report.

在新闻和职场英语里，选哪个动词本身就是一种判断。claim 保持距离，记者不为它背书；point out、explain 把内容当真；admit 表示这话对说话人不利；insist 表示别人有怀疑。在报告里显得中立、公平，靠的就是在这几个词之间选。

PatternVerbsExample
verb + thatsay, explain, admit, claim, insist, complain, point outShe explained that the office closes early on Fridays.
verb + to + verbagree, refuse, promise, offer, threatenThe contractor promised to finish by Monday.
verb + object + to + verbtell, ask, warn, remind, advise, urgeThe officer warned us not to leave valuables in the car.
verb + -ingdeny, admit, suggest, recommendHe denied taking the money.
verb + preposition + -ingapologise for, insist on, accuse of, blame forShe apologised for missing the call.
verb + if / whether / wh-ask, wonder, want to knowThey asked whether we needed a ride.

Examples例句

  • The landlord said the rent includes hot water. 房东说房租包热水。
    Still true, so present tense.现在仍成立，保留现在时。
  • The seller told us the car had never been in an accident. 卖家告诉我们这辆车从没出过事故。
    The backshift adds a little doubt.后退时态带出一点怀疑。
  • My colleague admitted that she hadn't read the report. 同事承认她没看那份报告。
    Against her interest.对她不利的话。
  • He denied taking the last parking spot on purpose. 他否认故意抢了最后一个车位。
    deny + -ing.deny 接 -ing。
  • The contractor promised to call back before noon. 承包商答应中午之前回电话。
    promise + to.promise 接 to。
  • She refused to sign the form until someone explained the fee. 在有人解释那笔费用之前，她拒绝签表。
    refuse + to.refuse 接 to。
  • The officer warned us not to leave bags on the back seat. 警察提醒我们别把包放在后座上。
    warn + object + not to.warn 加宾语加 not to。
  • The doctor asked whether I had any allergies. 医生问我有没有过敏。
    Reported yes/no question.转述一般疑问句。
  • The interviewer asked me why I had left my last job. 面试官问我为什么离开上一份工作。
    Statement word order.陈述语序。
  • A neighbour suggested asking the city about the tree. 一位邻居建议去问问市政府那棵树的事。
    suggest + -ing.suggest 接 -ing。
  • The airline apologised for the delay and offered us meal vouchers. 航空公司为延误道歉，还给了我们餐券。
    Two verbs, two patterns.两个动词，两种句型。
  • The company claims its app can cut your bills in half. 这家公司声称它的应用能让账单减半。
    claim keeps distance.claim 保持距离。
  • He insisted on paying for dinner, even though I had invited him. 虽然是我请他的，他坚持要付晚饭钱。
    insist on + -ing.insist on 接 -ing。
  • My manager reminded everyone to submit their hours by Friday. 经理提醒大家周五前提交工时。
    remind + object + to.remind 加宾语加 to。

More pairs: same idea, different choice更多对比句组

She said she works from home on Mondays. 她说她周一在家上班。
Still true.现在仍然如此。
She said she worked from home on Mondays. 她说她周一在家上班（那时候）。
Maybe no longer true, or simply told as a story.可能已经不是了，或只是按故事讲。
She said she had worked from home on Mondays. 她说她以前周一在家上班。
Clearly before that conversation.明确是在那次谈话之前。
The report says the plan will save money. 报告说这个方案能省钱。
Neutral.中性。
The report claims the plan will save money. 报告声称这个方案能省钱。
The writer is not convinced.作者并不信服。
The report shows that the plan will save money. 报告表明这个方案能省钱。
The writer accepts it as fact.作者认可这是事实。
