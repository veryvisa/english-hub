Designing a Meaningful Life 设计有意义的人生

🧠 Learn the 5 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 5 个新词

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Good afternoon, everyone. Let me begin with a question: if you could design your ideal life, what would you include? Perhaps you would choose meaningful work, close relationships, financial security, good health, or the freedom to explore the world. These are reasonable aspirations, but they raise a deeper question. How do we decide which possibilities deserve our time, attention, and commitment?

大家下午好。让我先提出一个问题：如果你能够设计自己理想中的生活，你会把什么包含在其中？也许你会选择有意义的工作、亲密的关系、经济保障、良好的健康，或者探索世界的自由。这些都是合理的愿望，但它们引出了一个更深层的问题：我们如何决定哪些可能性值得投入时间、关注和承诺？

I would suggest beginning with values rather than achievements. Achievements describe what we have accomplished, whereas values explain why those accomplishments matter. Someone may value creativity, another person may prioritize family, and someone else may find meaning in contributing to a community. There is no universally correct combination. A sense of purpose tends to develop when our choices reflect principles that we genuinely believe in.

我建议从价值观而不是成就开始思考。成就描述我们完成了什么，而价值观解释这些成就为什么重要。有人可能重视创造力，另一个人可能优先考虑家庭，还有人可能从为社区作贡献中找到意义。不存在放之四海而皆准的正确组合。当我们的选择反映出自己真正相信的原则时，目标感往往就会逐渐形成。

Of course, knowing our values does not make life predictable. Careers change, relationships evolve, and unexpected events can disrupt carefully prepared plans. While planning provides direction, excessive attachment to a particular outcome can make us less adaptable. A fulfilling life therefore requires both commitment and flexibility. We need goals that motivate us, but we also need enough room to breathe when circumstances change.

当然，了解自己的价值观并不会让生活变得可以预测。职业会发生变化，人际关系会不断发展，意外事件也可能打乱精心制定的计划。虽然计划能够提供方向，但过度执着于某个特定结果可能使我们变得不够灵活。因此，充实的生活既需要坚定的投入，也需要灵活性。我们需要能够激励自己的目标，但当环境发生变化时，也需要足够的喘息空间。

I would also encourage you to think about the ordinary structure of your days. A meaningful life is not built entirely through major decisions. It is shaped by how we spend our mornings, how we treat other people, what we choose to learn, and whether we make time for rest. By and large, our habits reveal our priorities more honestly than our stated ambitions. The question is not simply what kind of person we hope to become, but what kind of life our repeated actions are already creating.

我还想鼓励大家思考日常生活的基本结构。有意义的人生并不完全通过重大决定建立起来。它取决于我们如何度过早晨、如何对待他人、选择学习什么，以及是否为休息留出时间。总的来说，我们的习惯比我们口头表达的抱负更诚实地反映出我们的优先事项。问题不仅仅在于我们希望成为什么样的人，还在于我们重复的行动正在创造什么样的生活。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

A sense of purpose tends to develop when our choices reflect principles that we genuinely believe in.
The sentence contains a main clause with the hedging expression tends to and a when-clause, followed by a relative clause modifying principles.
句子主句使用限定表达 tends to，后接 when 时间状语从句，而 that 引导的关系从句修饰 principles。
The question is not simply what kind of person we hope to become, but what kind of life our repeated actions are already creating.
The cleft-like pattern is not simply ... but ... balances two indirect questions, and both are introduced by what.
句子使用 not simply ... but ... 结构平衡两个间接疑问从句，两个从句都由 what 引导。
