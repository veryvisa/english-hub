My Second Career at Fifty 五十岁的第二份职业

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Three years ago, the factory where I had worked for nineteen years began to reduce its night shifts. I was fifty, I had never written a professional email, and I assumed my working life was nearly over. My daughter, who was home from university, simply asked me what I enjoyed doing. It was a question nobody had put to me in decades.

三年前，我工作了十九年的工厂开始削减夜班。我当时五十岁，从没写过一封正式的电子邮件，还以为自己的职业生涯快要结束了。当时从大学回家的女儿只是问我喜欢做什么。这是几十年来没人问过我的问题。

The honest answer was that I liked fixing things and explaining them to younger colleagues. That gave me an idea. I enrolled in an evening course on maintenance planning and, although I struggled at first with the software, I gradually learned to adapt to it. Some classmates were half my age, which was humbling, but they were kind.

诚实的回答是，我喜欢修东西，也喜欢向年轻同事讲解。这给了我一个想法。我报名参加了一门维护规划的夜校课程，虽然起初我在软件上很吃力，但慢慢学会了适应它。有些同学的年纪只有我的一半，这让我感到谦卑，但他们都很友善。

What surprised me was how much of my old skill set turned out to be valuable. Patience, safety awareness and the habit of checking twice were not on any certificate, yet employers recognised them immediately. On the whole, people who change careers late tend to underestimate what they already know, and I was no exception.

让我吃惊的是，我过去的技能组合中有那么多最终被证明很有价值。耐心、安全意识和检查两遍的习惯并不写在任何证书上，雇主们却立刻认可了它们。总体而言，大龄转行的人往往低估自己已经掌握的东西，我也不例外。

I now work three days a week from home, planning repairs for a group of care homes, and spend the other days training apprentices. The pay is lower than before, and I will not pretend that is unimportant. Even so, the work is calmer, and I see my family far more often than I used to.

我现在每周在家工作三天，为一批养老院规划维修，其余时间则培训学徒。收入比以前低，我也不会假装这无关紧要。即便如此，工作更平静了，我见到家人的次数也比过去多得多。

If I could speak to my younger self, I would say that a job is not an identity. Companies change, machines arrive, and sometimes you lose out through no fault of your own. But the ability to keep learning is something nobody can take away, and it has turned out to be worth far more than I imagined.

如果我能和年轻时的自己说话，我会说，工作不等于身份。公司会变，机器会来，有时你会因为并非自己的过错而吃亏。但持续学习的能力是谁也拿不走的，它最终比我想象的要宝贵得多。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

What surprised me was how much of my old skill set turned out to be valuable.
This is a pseudo-cleft sentence: ‘What surprised me’ is the subject, and the ‘how much...’ clause after ‘was’ carries the new information.
这是一个伪分裂句：What surprised me 作主语，was 之后的 how much... 从句承载新信息。
If I could speak to my younger self, I would say that a job is not an identity.
This is a second conditional, using past ‘could’ in the if-clause and ‘would’ in the main clause to describe an imaginary situation.
这是第二类条件句，if 从句用过去式 could，主句用 would，描述一个想象中的情形。
