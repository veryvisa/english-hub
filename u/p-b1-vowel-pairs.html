Vowel pairs that change the word 会改变词义的元音对

📏 The rule规则

📏 The point in one line一句话要领
The difference is the shape of the mouth, not only the length: /ɪ/ is relaxed and lower than /iː/; it is not a short /iː/.区别在口型，不只在长短：/ɪ/ 更松、舌位更低，它不是「短的 /iː/」。

ship, sheep /ʃɪp/ /ʃiːp/
Relaxed, then spread. 先放松，再咧嘴。
live, leave /lɪv/ /liːv/
The final /v/ is voiced in both. 两个词尾的 /v/ 都要浊。
fill, feel /fɪl/ /fiːl/
Keep the dark l at the end. 保留词尾的 l。
full, fool /fʊl/ /fuːl/
Relaxed lips, then tightly rounded. 先松唇，再收圆。
pull, pool /pʊl/ /puːl/
Same contrast as full, fool. 与 full、fool 同一组对立。
bed, bad /bed/ /bæd/
Half open, then wide open. 半开，再全开。
men, man /men/ /mæn/
The plural has the smaller mouth. 复数的口型更小。
cup, cap /kʌp/ /kæp/
Central and relaxed, then wide. 先放松居中，再张大。
I'd like to live here, not leave here. /lɪv/ /liːv/
Stress the two verbs and make them different. 重读两个动词，把它们发得不一样。
The man said the men were late. /mæn/ /men/
Wide mouth for one man. 单数张大嘴。
This seat is a bit too low to sit on. /siːt/ /sɪt/
seat long and tense, sit short and relaxed. seat 长而紧，sit 短而松。
Is the pool full? /puːl/ /fʊl/
Round, then relax. 先圆后松。
luck, lock /lʌk/ /lɒk/
Half open, then wide; in North America lock is /lɑːk/. 先半开，再大开；北美 lock 读 /lɑːk/。

📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Open the full explanation: 5 points展开细讲：5 段讲解

Mandarin has one vowel in the area where English has two, so ship and sheep, full and fool, bed and bad tend to merge. Listeners then hear a different word: I want to live here becomes I want to leave here.

普通话在英语有两个元音的区域只有一个元音，所以 ship 和 sheep、full 和 fool、bed 和 bad 容易合并，对方听到的是另一个词：「我想住在这儿」变成「我想离开这儿」。

/ɪ/ versus /iː/: for /iː/ spread the lips and push the tongue high and forward. For /ɪ/ relax everything and let the jaw drop a little; the result sits between the vowels of Mandarin yi and ye.

/iː/ 咧嘴、舌位高而靠前，像「衣」；/ɪ/ 全部放松、下巴略降，舌位落在「衣」和「耶」的元音之间。

/ʊ/ versus /uː/: for /uː/ round the lips tightly. For /ʊ/ relax the lips; good, look, full and put all have this relaxed sound.

/uː/ 双唇收圆，像「乌」；/ʊ/ 嘴唇放松，good、look、full、put 都是这个音。

/e/ versus /æ/: for /æ/ open the jaw wide and spread the lips, almost a smile with the mouth open. bed needs a half-open mouth; bad needs a fully open one.

/æ/ 下巴张大、嘴角向两边拉；bed 半开口，bad 全开口。

/ɒ/ or /ɑː/ versus /ʌ/: cup, luck and come have a short central sound made with a relaxed, half-open mouth. Do not round the lips. cop and lock open the mouth much wider; British speakers also round the lips a little, North Americans do not.

cup、luck、come 是口腔半开、放松的短中央元音，不要圆唇。cop、lock 嘴张得大得多；英式发音略圆唇，北美不圆唇。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ Please take a sit.
✓ Please take a seat.
The noun is seat /siːt/; sit is the verb.
名词是 seat，sit 是动词。
✗ I am very tied today.
✓ I am very tired today.
tired /ˈtaɪəd/ has a full glide; do not flatten the diphthong.
tired 的双元音要滑动到位，别读扁。
✗ We had a great bitch holiday.
✓ We had a great beach holiday.
beach /biːtʃ/ needs the tense, spread vowel.
beach 要用紧而咧嘴的 /iː/。
