Who Watches the Decision-Makers? 谁来监督决策者？

Every institution needs some form of authority. A hospital must decide how to organise its services, a university must determine academic standards, and a public agency must allocate limited resources. Yet authority creates an important question: who checks whether those decisions are reasonable? The answer reveals much about an institution's character. An organisation that can make decisions but cannot explain them may possess power without earning public confidence.

每个制度都需要某种形式的权力。医院必须决定如何组织服务，大学必须确定学术标准，公共机构则必须分配有限资源。然而，权力也带来了一个重要问题：谁来检查这些决定是否合理？答案能够揭示一个制度的许多特征。一个能够作出决定却无法解释决定的组织，可能拥有权力，却无法赢得公众信任。

Oversight provides one answer. Independent reviews, transparent records, professional standards, and complaint procedures can all help identify errors or misuse of authority. These mechanisms do not necessarily prevent every failure. Their purpose is to make decisions open to examination and correction. While oversight may slow down administrative work, it tends to improve the quality of decisions when reviewers have genuine independence and access to relevant information.

监督提供了一种解决方案。独立审查、透明记录、职业标准和投诉程序都有助于发现错误或权力滥用。这些机制不一定能够防止所有失误，其目的是让决策接受检查和纠正。虽然监督可能减慢行政工作的速度，但当审查者真正独立并能够获得相关信息时，监督通常有助于提高决策质量。

However, accountability should not be confused with the search for someone to blame. Complex decisions often involve several departments, competing objectives, and incomplete evidence. If every mistake produces a defensive reaction, employees may conceal problems rather than report them. A healthier system distinguishes between reasonable errors, poor judgement, and deliberate misconduct. It also asks whether institutional procedures contributed to the problem.

然而，问责不应与寻找替罪羊混为一谈。复杂决策往往涉及多个部门、相互竞争的目标和不完整的证据。如果每一次错误都会引发防御性反应，员工可能会选择隐瞒问题，而不是报告问题。更健康的制度会区分合理失误、判断不当和故意违规，也会追问制度程序是否促成了问题的发生。

Public confidence is therefore not simply a matter of popularity. People may dislike a particular outcome but still accept it if the process was fair, understandable, and open to review. By and large, institutions earn legitimacy through repeated demonstrations of competence and restraint. Their credibility is likely to weaken when explanations are inconsistent or oversight exists only on paper. The real test is whether accountability changes behaviour, not whether an organisation publishes impressive statements about it.

因此，公众信任并不只是受欢迎程度的问题。即使人们不喜欢某个结果，只要程序公平、容易理解并且可以接受审查，他们仍可能接受这个结果。总体而言，制度通过反复展示能力和克制来赢得正当性。如果解释前后不一致，或者监督只停留在纸面上，制度的可信度就可能下降。真正的检验标准是问责是否改变了行为，而不是组织是否发布了关于问责的漂亮声明。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

While oversight may slow down administrative work, it tends to improve the quality of decisions when reviewers have genuine independence and access to relevant information.
The sentence combines a concessive while-clause with the hedging expression tends to, followed by a when-clause specifying the relevant condition.
该句结合了 while 引导的让步从句和 tend to 这一限定表达，并通过 when 从句说明相关条件。
The real test is whether accountability changes behaviour, not whether an organisation publishes impressive statements about it.
The cleft-like structure The real test is highlights the central criterion, while parallel whether-clauses establish a contrast.
The real test is 这一强调结构突出核心判断标准，两个并列的 whether 从句则形成对比。
