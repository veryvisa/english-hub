How to Describe Pain So a Doctor Can Use It 怎样描述疼痛，医生才用得上

🧠 Learn the 5 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 5 个新词

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

"It hurts here." For many patients this is the whole description, and in a second language it is often all we can manage. The doctor then has to guess, and a guess in a ten-minute appointment can lead to a misunderstanding that costs you another visit. A few simple tools make your description far more useful.

「这里痛。」对许多病人来说，这就是全部描述；用第二语言时，我们往往也只说得出这一句。医生只好去猜，而在十分钟的门诊里猜错，可能造成一个误会，让你再跑一趟。几个简单的工具能让你的描述有用得多。

First, say where it is and what it feels like. Doctors listen for a small set of words: sharp or dull, constant or on and off, burning, aching, throbbing. "A dull ache in my lower back that is worse in the morning" tells a doctor more than five minutes of "very painful". Many of us reach for one all-purpose word; choosing a precise one is the quickest improvement you can make.

第一，说清在哪里、是什么感觉。医生要听的是一小组词：刺痛还是钝痛，持续还是时有时无，灼痛、酸痛、跳痛。「腰下部钝痛，早上更厉害」比说五分钟「非常痛」告诉医生的更多。我们很多人习惯用一个万能词；换成一个精确的词，是你能做的最快的改进。

Second, give a number. Many clinics use a scale from zero to ten, where ten is the worst pain you can imagine. The number is not reliable when you compare two people, but it is reliable for you over time. If your pain was a seven last week and is a four today, the doctor can see the change at once.

第二，给一个数字。许多诊所用零到十的量表，十是你能想象的最痛。这个数字拿两个人来比并不可靠，但对你自己前后对比是可靠的。上周七分，今天四分，医生一眼就能看出变化。

Third, describe what the pain stops you from doing. "I can't sleep on my left side" or "I had to stop driving" is a fact the doctor can use. It also raises their awareness of how serious the problem is for you, which a polite smile can easily hide.

第三，说出这种痛让你做不了什么。「我没法朝左侧睡」「我只好不开车了」，都是医生用得上的事实。这也让医生意识到这个问题对你有多严重，而一个礼貌的微笑很容易把它掩盖掉。

Finally, check your expectations. A short visit does not mean the doctor is not interested. Write your symptoms down before you go, and you will walk in with more confidence and walk out with a clearer plan.

最后，调整你的预期。看得快不等于医生不上心。去之前把症状写下来，你进门时会更有底气，出门时也会带着更清楚的方案。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

The doctor then has to guess, and a guess in a ten-minute appointment can lead to a misunderstanding that costs you another visit.
ten-minute is an adjective, so no plural s. lead to + noun; that refers back to misunderstanding.
ten-minute 作形容词，不加复数 s。lead to 后接名词；that 指代 misunderstanding。
It also raises their awareness of how serious the problem is for you, which a polite smile can easily hide.
raise awareness of is a fixed chunk; how serious the problem is keeps statement word order; which refers to the whole idea before it.
raise awareness of 是固定词块；how serious the problem is 用陈述语序；which 指代前面整件事。
