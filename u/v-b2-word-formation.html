📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Open the full explanation: 5 points, a table展开细讲：5 段讲解、一张表

Chinese words do not change shape, so a learner tends to know one form of a word and use it everywhere: I am very confidence, it is a success project. In English the ending tells the listener what job the word is doing.

中文的词不变形，所以学习者往往只会一个词形到处用。在英语里，词尾告诉对方这个词在句中干什么。

Four noun endings do most of the work at this level. -ness turns almost any adjective into a quality: aware → awareness, willing → willingness. -ity does the same for Latin adjectives, often moving the stress: FLEXible → flexiBILity, reLIable → reliaBILity. -ment and -tion turn verbs into results: improve → improvement, expect → expectation.

这个阶段四个名词词尾最常用：-ness、-ity（常移动重音）、-ment、-tion。

Adjectives come from -able, -ive, -ful and -al: rely → reliable, decide → decisive, compete → competitive. Negatives take un-, in-/im- or mis-: unreasonable, inflexible, misunderstanding. Which prefix a word takes must be learned with the word.

形容词常由 -able、-ive、-ful、-al 构成；否定用 un-、in-/im-、mis-，具体用哪个要跟词一起记。

Why it matters for reaching C1: a noun can take adjectives and sit as the subject. Her reliability impressed the client packs a whole clause into two words and lets the sentence move on. Formal writing does this constantly.

为什么这是通往 C1 的关键：名词可以加修饰、可以作主语，把一个从句压成两个词。正式写作一直在这么做。

Learn new words as a family of four, and say the family aloud to hear the stress move: analyse, analysis, analytical, analytically.

新词按四个一族来记，并读出声，听重音怎么移动。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ I am very confidence about the interview.
✓ I am very confident about the interview.
After be very you need the adjective.
be very 后面要形容词。
✗ It was a success project.
✓ It was a successful project.
Nouns do modify nouns (a success story), but to say what something was like you need the adjective.
名词可以修饰名词（a success story），但说事情「怎么样」要用形容词。
✗ Thank you for your flexible.
✓ Thank you for your flexibility.
After your you need a noun.
your 后面要名词。
✗ He is not reliable, he has many unresponsible.
✓ He is unreliable and irresponsible.
responsible takes ir-, not un-.
responsible 的否定是 ir-，不是 un-。
