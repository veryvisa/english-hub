Talks Resume After Week-Long Standoff 僵持一周后谈判恢复

Negotiators for a regional transit union and the city’s transport authority returned to the table on Thursday after a week-long standoff that had left thousands of commuters stranded. Both sides described the atmosphere as cautious, although neither would say whether a deal was close. Talks had collapsed earlier when each side accused the other of refusing to back down. The mediator, a retired judge, urged both delegations to look for common ground rather than trade accusations.

周四，一家地区交通工会的谈判代表与市交通管理部门回到谈判桌前，此前双方僵持了一周，导致数千名通勤者滞留。双方都形容气氛谨慎，但都不愿透露是否已接近达成协议。此前谈判破裂时，各方都指责对方拒不让步。调解人是一位退休法官，他敦促两个代表团寻找共同点，而不是互相指责。

The union says its members want a predictable schedule and a firm commitment on safety. The authority, for its part, argues that its budget leaves little room to manoeuvre. That said, officials signalled on Wednesday that they were prepared to meet halfway on rostering, which many observers see as the most promising opening so far. Union leaders, however, insist that any concession must be matched by a concrete offer in return.

工会称，会员想要可预测的排班和对安全问题的明确承诺。管理部门则表示，预算几乎没有回旋余地。不过，官员们周三暗示，他们愿意在排班问题上各退一步，许多观察人士认为这是迄今最有希望的突破口。然而工会领导人坚持认为，任何让步都必须换来具体的回应。

Analysts caution that the timing matters. Disputes of this kind tend to harden the longer they last, because each side becomes invested in not appearing weak. Public sympathy, which initially favoured the drivers, is likely to fade if disruption continues into the holiday period, and that gives the authority extra leverage. Even so, a rushed agreement could simply postpone the conflict rather than resolve it.

分析人士提醒，时机很重要。这类纠纷往往拖得越久越僵化，因为双方都不愿显得软弱。起初倾向于司机的公众同情，如果干扰持续到假期，很可能会消退，这会让管理部门多一分筹码。即便如此，仓促达成的协议也可能只是推迟冲突，而不是解决冲突。

Talks are scheduled to continue through the weekend. Commuters have been advised to check service updates each morning, and both sides have promised to keep the public informed of any progress. One union spokesperson said only that the mood was better than it had been, which, by the standards of this dispute, counts as good news. Neither side expects a quick fix.

谈判计划持续到周末。通勤者被建议每天早晨查看服务更新，双方都承诺会向公众通报任何进展。一位工会发言人只说气氛比之前好了，按这场纠纷的标准，这已算是好消息。双方都不指望能很快解决。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

Disputes of this kind tend to harden the longer they last, because each side becomes invested in not appearing weak.
‘Tend to’ hedges the claim, and ‘the longer they last’ works as an adverbial clause meaning as time goes on.
「tend to」对断言作了限定，「the longer they last」是状语从句，意为「随着时间推移」。
That said, officials signalled on Wednesday that they were prepared to meet halfway on rostering, which many observers see as the most promising opening so far.
‘That said’ introduces a concession against the earlier hard line, and the non-defining clause beginning ‘which’ comments on the whole preceding statement.
「That said」引出对前面强硬立场的让步，以「which」开头的非限定性从句是对整个前面内容的评论。
