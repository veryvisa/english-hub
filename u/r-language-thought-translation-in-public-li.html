Translation Is More Than Replacing Words 翻译不只是替换词语

🧠 Learn the 5 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 5 个新词

Public translation requires cultural interpretation and contextual judgement as well as linguistic accuracy. 公共领域的翻译除了语言准确性，还需要文化解释和语境判断。

Public institutions increasingly communicate with audiences who speak different languages and bring different cultural expectations. Notices, educational materials and public announcements must therefore do more than reproduce individual words. A technically accurate translation may still confuse readers if its examples, tone or underlying assumptions are unfamiliar. Communication specialists are consequently paying greater attention to accessibility, audience research and the cultural implications of wording.

公共机构越来越多地需要与使用不同语言、具有不同文化预期的受众交流。因此，通知、教育材料和公共公告不能仅仅重复单个词语。即使翻译在技术上准确，如果其中的例子、语气或潜在假设不熟悉，仍然可能使读者感到困惑。因此，传播专业人士越来越重视信息的易理解性、受众研究以及措辞的文化影响。

Consider a public notice explaining how to access a community service. In one language, a brief instruction may be perfectly clear because readers are familiar with the relevant institutional procedures. In another, the same instruction may raise questions about where to go, whom to contact or what action is expected. While literal translation can preserve the original wording, it may fail to preserve the practical meaning. Translators therefore need to identify what readers must understand and do.

以一份说明如何使用社区服务的公共通知为例。在一种语言中，简短的指示可能非常清楚，因为读者熟悉相关机构的办事程序。在另一种语言中，相同的指示却可能引发疑问，例如应该去哪里、联系谁，或者需要采取什么行动。虽然直译能够保留原文措辞，却可能无法保留实际含义。因此，译者需要确定读者必须理解什么以及应该采取什么行动。

This process often involves consultation rather than translation alone. Community members can identify expressions that sound unnatural, carry unintended associations or overlook important local knowledge. Editors may then revise sentence structures, replace unfamiliar examples or provide additional explanations. Such decisions are not merely stylistic. They influence whether information can be understood and whether people feel that the communication addresses their circumstances. By and large, effective public translation combines linguistic precision with sensitivity to the audience.

这一过程通常需要咨询，而不仅仅是翻译。社区成员能够发现听起来不自然、带有意外联想或忽略重要本地知识的表达。编辑随后可以调整句子结构、替换陌生的例子，或者提供额外说明。这些决定不仅仅涉及文体。它们会影响信息是否能够被理解，以及人们是否觉得这些信息考虑到了自己的实际情况。总体而言，有效的公共翻译需要将语言准确性与对受众的敏感度结合起来。

Technology is also changing this work. Automated translation tools can produce rapid drafts and help organisations manage large volumes of routine material. However, speed does not guarantee cultural appropriateness or factual clarity. Human review remains valuable when messages involve uncertainty, sensitive relationships or unfamiliar institutional concepts. The most promising approach is likely to combine technological assistance with professional judgement and feedback from intended readers. In this way, translation becomes an ongoing process of negotiation between language, context and public understanding.

技术也正在改变这项工作。自动翻译工具能够快速生成初稿，并帮助机构处理大量日常材料。然而，速度并不能保证文化上的恰当性或事实表达的清晰度。当信息涉及不确定性、敏感的人际关系或陌生的机构概念时，人工审核仍然具有价值。最有前景的方法可能是将技术辅助与专业判断以及目标读者的反馈结合起来。这样一来，翻译就成为语言、语境和公众理解之间持续协商的过程。
