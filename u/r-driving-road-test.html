I Failed My Road Test Twice 路考我挂了两次

🧠 Learn the 5 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 5 个新词

Read an experienced driver's story of failing a road test and see how unreal conditionals carry regret and advice. 读一位老司机路考失败的故事，看非真实条件句怎样承载遗憾和建议。

I failed my road test twice before I passed. The first time, I had been driving for fifteen years in another country, and I walked in thinking the test was a formality. Twenty minutes later the examiner handed me a sheet with four red marks on it.

我路考挂了两次才过。第一次考的时候，我已经在另一个国家开了十五年车，走进考场时觉得这不过是走个过场。二十分钟后，考官递给我一张纸，上面有四个红色扣分。

The biggest one was the blind spot. Back home I had always used my mirrors, but nobody had taught me to turn my head before changing lanes. In the test I tried to merge onto a busy road without a shoulder check, and a car I had not seen sounded its horn. If that driver had not braked, we would have hit each other. I still think about that horn.

最大的一个扣分是盲区。在国内我一直靠后视镜，可从没有人教过我变道前要回头看。考试时我没回头确认就想并入一条车很多的路，一辆我没看见的车按了喇叭。要是那个司机没踩刹车，我们就撞上了。那声喇叭我到现在还会想起。

The second time, I failed for something smaller: I rolled through a stop sign at walking speed. In my old city, nobody stopped completely unless there was traffic to deal with. Here a stop means the wheels stop. If I had known how seriously examiners take that, I would have practised it a hundred times.

第二次挂在一件更小的事上：我在停车标志前用步行的速度溜了过去。在我原来住的城市，除非有车要应付，没人会完全停下。在这里，停车就是车轮必须停住。要是我早知道考官对这一点有多较真，我会练上一百遍。

Before the third test, I signed up for two lessons with a local instructor. She did not teach me how to drive; she pointed out how people drive here and helped me figure out what I kept getting wrong. Keep your distance. Give way to people at a crosswalk before they even step off the sidewalk. Pull over completely for an ambulance. None of it was difficult. It was just different, and I had assumed my experience would carry over.

第三次考试前，我报了一位本地教练的两节课。她没教我怎么开车，而是指出了这里的人怎么开车，还帮我弄清了自己总在哪儿出错：保持车距；人行横道上的行人还没走下人行道就要先让；救护车来了要完全靠边停下。这些都不难，只是不一样，而我原以为自己的经验可以直接拿过来用。

I passed with one small mark. If I were giving advice to a newcomer, it would be this: forget how long you have been driving, and learn the local habits as if you were sixteen.

我只扣了一个小分就过了。如果要给新来的人一句建议，那就是：忘掉你开了多少年，像十六岁那样从头学本地的习惯。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

If that driver had not braked, we would have hit each other.
Third conditional: an imagined past. Both halves are past and unreal: the driver did brake, and they did not hit each other.
第三类条件句，设想的过去。两半都与事实相反：司机踩了刹车，他们没撞上。
If I were giving advice to a newcomer, it would be this: forget how long you have been driving
Second conditional with a continuous form: were giving … would be, about now. After the colon, the advice is an imperative.
第二类条件句用进行式：were giving … would be，说的是现在。冒号后是祈使句给建议。
