Giving reasons: as, since, due to, with 表原因：as、since、due to、with

Choose accurately between as, since, due to, and with when giving a reason, so that the clause or phrase fits both the information structure and the register. 在说明原因时,能根据信息结构和语体,准确选用 as、since、due to 和 with。

👀 Look first: what changes?先看对比：差在哪里

Read the sentences and decide what each one tells you. Then open the notes.先读这几句，自己判断各自传达了什么，再点开看注解。

Because the train was late, I missed the meeting. 因为火车晚点,我错过了会议。
The reason is the new or focal information; it would answer 'Why did you miss the meeting?'理由是新信息或焦点,可回答'你为什么错过会议'。
As the train was late, I missed the meeting. 由于火车晚点,我错过了会议。
The delay is treated as background; the speaker foregrounds the result.晚点被当作背景,说话人强调的是结果。
Since the train was late, I missed the meeting. 既然火车晚点了,我就错过了会议。
Sounds slightly formal and assumes the listener already knows or accepts the delay.语气略正式,默认听者已知道或接受晚点这件事。
Due to the train's delay, I missed the meeting. 由于火车的晚点,我错过了会议。
A noun-phrase cause in a formal tone, common in notices and reports.名词性原因,语气正式,常见于通知和报告。

📏 The rule in one line规则一句话

📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Open the full explanation: 6 points, 24 examples展开细讲：6 段讲解、24 个例句

Reach for as or since when the reason is background information the reader can already accept, and the real news is the result. Because puts the reason in focus and answers a why-question, so it fits when the reason is the new information. If someone asks 'Why were you late?', 'Because the bus broke down' works, while 'As the bus broke down' sounds like an odd reply.

当理由是读者已经知道或容易接受的背景、真正的新信息是结果时,用 as 或 since。because 突出理由本身,适合回答 why 的问题。有人问 Why were you late?,回答 Because the bus broke down 自然,而 As the bus broke down 就很别扭。

Position matters. As and since clauses usually come first, which sets the reason as the frame for what follows: 'Since the deadline has passed, we cannot accept new entries.' If you put them at the end, as in 'We cannot accept new entries since the deadline has passed', it reads as an afterthought and, with as, may be mistaken for a time clause. Because has no such restriction.

位置很重要。as 和 since 从句通常放句首,先搭好理由的框架:Since the deadline has passed, we cannot accept new entries.放句尾则像事后补充,as 放句尾还可能被误读为时间状语。because 没有这个限制。

Watch for ambiguity with since and as. Since can mean 'from a point in time' and as can mean 'while', so 'Since he left, the office has been quiet' is a time reading. Cause is clear when the verb is a state or the context excludes time: 'Since you are here, you may as well help.' If ambiguity is a risk, switch to because or given that.

注意歧义:since 可表示'自从',as 可表示'当……时'。Since he left, the office has been quiet 读作时间。当谓语是状态动词或语境排除了时间义时,原因义才清楚,比如 Since you are here, you may as well help。有歧义风险时改用 because 或 given that。

Due to is followed by a noun phrase and traditionally acts like an adjective after be: 'The delay was due to fog.' Many careful writers still prefer because of or owing to when the phrase modifies a verb, as in 'We were delayed because of fog'. In formal writing, due to after a noun or be is safest, and a full clause after it is a mistake: not 'due to the weather was bad'.

due to 后接名词性成分,传统上在 be 之后作表语:The delay was due to fog。当它修饰动词时,讲究的写作者更偏好 because of 或 owing to,如 We were delayed because of fog。正式写作中,due to 放在 be 或名词之后最稳妥,后面接完整从句是错误的:不能说 due to the weather was bad。

With builds a compact absolute construction: noun plus a participle, adjective, or prepositional phrase, such as 'With prices rising, many families cut spending.' It gives a reason as one of several circumstances rather than a strict cause, which makes it fit news writing and summaries. Because it has no tense and no subject-verb agreement, avoid it when you need to state a precise or disputed cause.

with 构成紧凑的独立结构:名词加分词、形容词或介词短语,如 With prices rising, many families cut spending。它把原因当作若干背景情况之一,不强调严格因果,适合新闻和概述。因为它没有时态,也没有主谓一致,需要明确或有争议的因果时不要用。

Match the register to the situation. Since and as clauses suit written and formal speech, while in casual conversation people say 'because' or 'so'. In academic prose, 'owing to' and 'due to' appear in cause-and-effect sentences, and 'given that' offers a more explicit way to mark an accepted premise. Choosing a connector is therefore part of choosing a tone.

语体要匹配场合。since 和 as 从句适合书面和正式口语,日常对话更常说 because 或 so。学术文体中 owing to 和 due to 出现在因果句里,given that 则更明确地标示已被接受的前提。选连接词也是在选语气。

Examples例句

  • As it was raining, we moved the party indoors. 因为下雨,我们把聚会挪到了室内。
  • Since you have already paid, you can collect the tickets at the desk. 既然你已经付了款,就可以到前台取票。
  • Since nobody objected, the proposal went ahead. 由于没人反对,提案继续推进了。
  • As she was the only one who spoke French, she led the negotiation. 因为她是唯一会说法语的人,所以由她主导谈判。
  • The match was cancelled due to heavy snow. 比赛因大雪被取消了。
  • The shortage was due to a delay at the port. 短缺是港口延误造成的。
  • Due to staff illness, the clinic will open late today. 由于员工生病,诊所今天会晚开门。
  • With so many people out sick, we couldn't finish the project on time. 这么多人请病假,我们没能按时完成项目。
  • With interest rates climbing, fewer people are buying homes. 随着利率攀升,买房的人变少了。
  • With the deadline looming, the whole team worked through the weekend. 截止日期临近,整个团队周末也在加班。
  • Since the road is closed, you will have to take the long way round. 既然路封了,你就得绕远路了。
  • As the manager was away, the decision was postponed. 由于经理不在,决定被推迟了。
  • Owing to a technical fault, the flight was delayed by two hours. 由于技术故障,航班延误了两个小时。
  • The company's losses were largely due to poor planning. 公司的亏损主要是规划不当造成的。
  • With her parents working abroad, she grew up mostly with her grandmother. 因为父母在国外工作,她主要是和祖母一起长大的。
  • Since it is your first offence, the judge decided to be lenient. 因为这是你的初犯,法官决定从轻处理。
  • As we had no map, we asked a local for directions. 因为没有地图,我们向当地人问路。
  • With the economy slowing down, the government introduced new support measures. 随着经济放缓,政府推出了新的扶持措施。
  • His absence was due to a family emergency. 他缺席是因为家里有急事。
  • Since the rules have changed, applicants must submit a new form. 由于规定变了,申请人必须提交新表格。
  • As prices have risen sharply, many shops are offering discounts to attract customers. 由于价格大幅上涨,许多商店推出折扣来吸引顾客。
  • With nothing left to lose, he decided to take the risk. 既然已经没什么可失去的了,他决定冒险一试。
  • Due to the high demand, tickets sold out within minutes. 由于需求旺盛,门票几分钟内就售罄了。
  • Since many of the staff are new, we are running extra training sessions. 因为许多员工是新人,我们加开了培训课程。

More pairs: same idea, different choice更多对比句组

The match was postponed because of the storm. 比赛因风暴推迟了。
A neutral phrase that attaches the cause directly to the verb 'postponed'.中性表达,把原因直接挂在动词 postponed 上。
The postponement was due to the storm. 推迟是风暴造成的。
Due to follows the noun 'postponement' and states the cause as a fact about it.due to 跟在名词 postponement 之后,把原因陈述为关于它的事实。
With the storm approaching, the match was postponed. 风暴逼近,比赛被推迟了。
The storm is one circumstance among others, and the link is looser and more narrative.风暴只是背景情况之一,因果关系更松散,带叙述色彩。
Since a storm was approaching, the match was postponed. 既然风暴正在逼近,比赛就推迟了。
Presents the storm as an accepted premise that justifies the decision.把风暴视为已被接受的前提,为决定提供依据。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ Due to the weather was bad, the flight was cancelled.
✓ Due to the bad weather, the flight was cancelled. / Because the weather was bad, the flight was cancelled.
Due to takes a noun phrase, not a full clause. Use because or since if you want a clause.
due to 后面接名词短语,不能接完整从句。要用从句就换成 because 或 since。
✗ Since I was a child, I was afraid of dogs.
✓ Since I was a child, I have been afraid of dogs. / Because I was bitten as a child, I was afraid of dogs.
In a time reading, since needs a present perfect main clause; with a past tense it is read as a reason, which makes no sense here.
表示'自从'时,主句要用现在完成时;主句用一般过去时则会被读成原因,在此语义上不通。
✗ Because he was ill, so he stayed at home.
✓ Because he was ill, he stayed at home. / He was ill, so he stayed at home.
Because and so do not go together in English; use one connector per cause-effect link.
英语里 because 和 so 不能同时出现,一组因果只用一个连接词。
✗ We cancelled the picnic, since of the rain.
✓ We cancelled the picnic because of the rain. / We cancelled the picnic since it was raining.
Since is a conjunction and cannot be followed by 'of'; use because of before a noun, or since before a clause.
since 是连词,后面不能接 of;名词前用 because of,从句前用 since。
✗ With the prices are rising, people buy less.
✓ With prices rising, people buy less.
With forms a phrase, not a clause, so the verb must be an -ing participle without a finite 'are'.
with 构成的是短语而非从句,动词要用 -ing 分词,不能带限定动词 are。
✗ As the reason that the shop was closed, we went home.
✓ As the shop was closed, we went home. / The reason we went home was that the shop was closed.
As introduces the reason by itself; adding 'the reason' duplicates the meaning and breaks the structure.
as 本身就引出原因,再加 the reason 属于语义重复并破坏结构。
