A Credit Score, Explained 信用分是怎么回事

🧠 Learn the 3 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 3 个新词

Understand what a credit score measures and why a newcomer with savings can still start near zero; practise word families like reliable, reliability. 弄懂信用分量的是什么、为什么有存款的新移民也可能从零起步；练 reliable、reliability 这样的词族。

B2 · CELPIP 7–8⏱ 20 minV03 Word family: the wrong part of speech词性用错G01 Articles left out or over-used冠词漏用、滥用

📖 Key card知识卡
A credit score measures how reliably you have repaid lenders in this country, not how much money you have.信用分衡量的是你在这个国家向贷方还款有多可靠，而不是你有多少钱。

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Wei had a good salary, savings in the bank and no debt at all, and he was still refused a phone contract in his first month. The reason surprised him. He had no credit history in this country, so as far as lenders were concerned, he did not exist.

阿伟工资不错，银行里有存款，一分钱债都没有，可落地第一个月照样办不下一份手机合约。原因让他意外：他在这个国家没有信用记录，所以在贷方眼里，他这个人不存在。

A credit score is a number that tries to answer one question: if we lend this person money, will we get it back on time? Credit bureaus build it from your record with lenders here, such as credit cards, loans and phone contracts. Savings do not count, and neither does a perfect record in another country. That is why a newcomer with money can have a weaker score than a student with one small card.

信用分是一个数字，它想回答一个问题：把钱借给这个人，能不能按时收回来？征信机构根据你在本地和贷方打交道的记录来算，比如信用卡、贷款和手机合约。存款不算，在别的国家记录再完美也不算。所以一个有钱的新移民，信用分可能还不如一个只有一张小额卡的学生。

Several things shape the score. The most important is reliability: paying every bill on time. The second is how much of your credit limit you use. A balance close to the limit every month suggests that you depend on credit, even if you pay it off. The third is time, because a long history builds confidence. Applying for many cards in a short period can lower the score, since each application leaves a mark.

影响信用分的有几样东西。最重要的是可靠性：每笔账都按时还。第二是你用掉了多少信用额度。每个月余额都接近额度，说明你依赖信贷，哪怕你都还清了。第三是时间，记录越长，越让人有信心。短时间里申请很多张卡会拉低分数，因为每次申请都会留下记录。

The practical lesson is boring, which is good news. Get one card early, use it for a few regular bills, keep the balance well below the limit, and pay in full by the due date. Do not expect a quick improvement. Check your report once a year for errors and for accounts you never opened. Wei did exactly that. A year later, he signed the same kind of phone contract without a problem.

实用的结论很枯燥，而这恰恰是好消息。早点办一张卡，用来付几笔固定账单，余额远低于额度，到期日前全额还清。别指望很快见效。每年查一次信用报告，看有没有错误、有没有你从没开过的账户。阿伟就是这么做的。一年后，他毫无障碍地签下了同一类手机合约。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

That is why a newcomer with money can have a weaker score than a student with one small card.
a newcomer / a student: any one of many, so a. That is why points back to the reason just given.
a newcomer、a student 指同类中的任何一个，用 a。That is why 回指刚说的原因。
A balance close to the limit every month suggests that you depend on credit, even if you pay it off.
The subject is A balance close to the limit every month; the verb is suggests.
主语是 A balance close to the limit every month，谓语是 suggests。
