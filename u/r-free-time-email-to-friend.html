Dear Lily, thank you for your last email. It was lovely to hear that you have started learning the guitar! You asked what I do in my free time these days, so I thought I would give you a proper answer.

亲爱的莉莉，谢谢你上一封邮件。听说你开始学吉他了，真让人高兴！你问我现在空闲时间都做什么，所以我想好好回答你一下。

Honestly, I used to spend most evenings online. Last year, however, I noticed that I was always tired and never really enjoyed it. So I made a rule: no screens after nine o’clock. At first it felt strange, but now I read, cook, or call my parents instead.

说实话，我以前大部分晚上都泡在网上。不过去年我发现自己总是很累，也并没有真正享受其中。于是我定了个规矩：晚上九点以后不看屏幕。起初觉得很奇怪，但现在我改为读书、做饭，或者给父母打电话。

My biggest surprise has been baking. I took up baking in the winter, and my first loaf was as hard as a stone! My flatmates still joke about it. But I have improved a lot, and last weekend I baked bread for the whole building. People said it was delicious, which made me very proud.

最大的惊喜是烘焙。我是在冬天开始学烘焙的，第一个面包硬得像石头！我的室友们到现在还拿它开玩笑。不过我进步了很多，上周末我给整栋楼的人烤了面包。大家都说很好吃，这让我非常自豪。

Another thing I have learned is that a hobby is easier to keep up when you share it. I now meet two friends every Sunday to bake or go for a walk, and I look forward to it all week. Without them, I would probably give up after a few days.

我学到的另一件事是，爱好与别人分享时更容易坚持下去。我现在每个星期天和两个朋友见面，一起烘焙或散步，整个星期我都在期待。没有他们的话，我大概几天后就放弃了。

So please keep playing the guitar, even if your fingers hurt at the beginning! Send me a short video when you can play one song. Maybe next summer we can meet and make some music together, and I will bring the bread. Write soon, and tell me everything. Love, Mei

所以请继续弹吉他，哪怕一开始手指会疼！等你能弹完一首歌时，给我发一段短视频。也许明年夏天我们可以见面一起玩音乐，我会带上面包。早点来信，把一切都告诉我。爱你的，梅

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

Another thing I have learned is that a hobby is easier to keep up when you share it.
The subject ‘another thing (that) I have learned’ is a noun phrase with an omitted relative pronoun before ‘I have learned’; the second ‘that’, in ‘is that a hobby…’, is a different word introducing what the thing actually is. ‘Easier to keep up’ is a comparative adjective followed by an infinitive.
主语 another thing (that) I have learned 是省略了关系代词 that 的名词短语，省略处在 thing 和 I have learned 之间；第二个 that（出现在 is that a hobby… 中）是另一个词，用来引出这件事具体是什么。easier to keep up 是比较级形容词加不定式。
Without them, I would probably give up after a few days.
’Without them’ replaces an ‘if’ clause, and ‘would probably’ gives an imagined result.
Without them 代替了 if 从句，would probably 表示想象中的结果。
