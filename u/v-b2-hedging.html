📏 The rule规则

🎯 The point in one line一句话要领
A hedge is not weakness. It tells the reader exactly how sure you are, and that makes the rest of what you say easier to trust.留余地不是软弱。它告诉读者你到底有几分把握，你其余的话因此更可信。

📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Open the full explanation: 5 points展开细讲：5 段讲解

Learners often write in absolutes: This proves that..., Everyone knows..., Children always.... In English, especially at work, in reports and in exams, a claim stronger than the evidence makes the reader doubt you. This suggests that... and Children tend to... say what you can defend.

学习者常把话说满：This proves that…、Everyone knows…、Children always…。英语里，尤其是在工作、报告和考试中，判断比证据强，读者反而会怀疑你。This suggests that…、Children tend to… 说的是你守得住的那部分。

Hedges come in grades. From strong to weak: will, is likely to, may well, may, might, could possibly. Choose one deliberately. Prices are likely to rise is a forecast; prices might rise is barely more than a guess.

留余地的说法有档位，由强到弱：will、is likely to、may well、may、might、could possibly。要有意识地选：Prices are likely to rise 是预测；prices might rise 只比猜测强一点。

Verbs carry hedges too. Seem to and appear to report how something looks: The new rule seems to be working. Tend to describes a general pattern with exceptions: Older tenants tend to stay longer. Suggest that links evidence to a conclusion without claiming proof.

动词也能留余地：seem to、appear to 说的是「看上去如此」，The new rule seems to be working；tend to 讲的是有例外的一般规律，Older tenants tend to stay longer；suggest that 把证据和结论连起来，但不声称已证明。

Limit the scope instead of the verb: to some extent, in most cases, broadly speaking, as far as I know. These let you keep a strong verb while being honest about where it applies: In most cases, the bank will refund the money.

也可以限定范围而不弱化动词：to some extent、in most cases、broadly speaking、as far as I know。这样动词可以保持有力，同时诚实交代适用范围：In most cases, the bank will refund the money。

Do not stack them. It may possibly perhaps seem to suggest is not careful, it is unreadable. One hedge per claim is usually enough, and in speech I would say or not necessarily often does the whole job.

别叠着用。It may possibly perhaps seem to suggest 不叫严谨，叫读不下去。一个判断通常一处留余地就够；口语里一句 I would say 或 not necessarily 往往就够了。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ This proves that remote work is better.
✓ This suggests that remote work may suit some teams better.
One study rarely proves anything: suggest, plus a limited scope.
一项研究很少能「证明」什么：用 suggest，再收窄范围。
✗ All Chinese parents care about grades.
✓ Many parents, broadly speaking, tend to care a lot about grades.
Generalisations about groups need a hedge, or they become stereotypes.
对群体的概括要留余地，否则就成了刻板印象。
✗ It may possibly perhaps be true.
✓ It may well be true.
One hedge, chosen at the right strength.
一处留余地，选对档位。
✗ He seems that he is tired.
✓ He seems to be tired. / It seems that he is tired.
seem takes to + verb, or It seems that.
seem 接 to do，或用 It seems that。
✗ As I know, the office opens at nine.
✓ As far as I know, the office opens at nine.
The chunk is as far as I know.
固定说法是 as far as I know。
