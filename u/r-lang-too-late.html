Q: I am 47 and moved to Canada four years ago. My English is good enough to get by at work, but my teenage children speak like native speakers and laugh at my mistakes. Everyone says adults cannot really learn a language. If I had come here at twenty, would I be fluent by now? Is it too late to get much better? — Late Starter in Burnaby

问：我 47 岁，四年前搬来加拿大。我的英语够在工作中应付，可我十几岁的孩子说起来像母语者，还笑我的错误。大家都说成年人学不好语言。如果我二十岁就来了，现在会说得流利吗？想再大幅提高，是不是太晚了？——本拿比的晚起步者

A: It is not too late, and the research is more encouraging than the popular belief. Children do have an advantage with accent: people who start young are much more likely to end up sounding native. But adults usually learn vocabulary and grammar faster at the beginning, because they can read, compare and plan. For most adult goals, such as working, reading the news or dealing with a landlord, sounding native is not the requirement. Being clear is.

答：不晚，而且研究结果比流行看法更让人振奋。孩子在口音上确实有优势：从小开始学的人更可能最后听起来像母语者。可成年人通常一开始学词汇和语法更快，因为他们会读、会比较、会规划。对大多数成年人的目标来说，比如工作、读新闻、跟房东打交道，要求不是听起来像母语者，而是说清楚。

As for your question: if you had arrived at twenty, you would probably have a lighter accent. You would not necessarily have a bigger vocabulary or more confidence in meetings. Those depend far more on how much English you read and hear, and that is something you can still change.

至于你的问题：如果你二十岁就来了，口音大概会轻一些，但词汇量不一定更大，开会时也不一定更有底气。那些更多取决于你读多少、听多少英语，而这是你现在仍然能改变的。

Adults who keep improving after the intermediate plateau tend to share three habits. They get a large amount of input on topics they care about. They collect chunks, not single words, often by shadowing short recordings. And they accept that mistakes are part of the process; their willingness to sound imperfect is what keeps them talking. Your expectations matter too. Measure yourself against last year's version of you, not against your children.

过了中级平台期还在进步的成年人往往有三个共同习惯：在自己关心的话题上大量输入；收集词块而不是单个单词，常用的办法是跟读短录音；接受犯错是过程的一部分，愿意说得不完美，才让他们一直开口。你的预期也很重要。拿去年的自己来比，别拿孩子来比。

Finally, about the laughter at home. Try turning it into an advantage: ask your children to correct one thing a day, and pay them back by teaching them something in your first language. I wish more families did this. Everyone learns, and nobody is the only beginner in the house.

最后说说家里的笑声。试着把它变成优势：请孩子们每天纠正你一处，作为回报，你用母语教他们一点东西。我真希望有更多家庭这样做。大家都在学，家里就没有谁是唯一的初学者。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

If I had come here at twenty, would I be fluent by now?
Mixed conditional: an unreal past cause (had come) with an imagined present result (would be … now).
混合条件句：与过去事实相反的原因（had come），设想的现在结果（would be … now）。
And they accept that mistakes are part of the process; their willingness to sound imperfect is what keeps them talking.
willing → willingness, and the noun keeps to + verb. keep sb doing: make sb continue.
willing 变 willingness，名词保留 to + 动词。keep sb doing：让人一直做下去。
