Reading a Job Posting Between the Lines 读懂招聘启事的言外之意

🧠 Learn the 5 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 5 个新词

Learn to read a job posting as an employer's list of priorities, and answer it with evidence instead of adjectives. 学会把招聘启事当成雇主的优先级清单来读，并用证据而不是形容词去回应它。

B2 · CELPIP 7–8⏱ 20 minV03 Word family: the wrong part of speech词性用错G01 Articles left out or over-used冠词漏用、滥用

📖 Key card知识卡
A job posting tells you what the employer cares about most; answer each key noun with one concrete example, not with the same adjective.招聘启事告诉你雇主最在乎什么；对每个关键名词，用一个具体例子回应，而不是重复同一个形容词。

Read and listen读与听

278 words
Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Li Na had sent out forty applications in two months and heard back from three. When a career adviser finally looked at them, the problem was not her experience. It was that she had answered every job posting with the same résumé and the same cover letter, without really reading what each employer had asked for.

李娜两个月投了四十份申请，只收到三份回音。等到一位职业顾问终于看了她的材料，问题并不在她的经验，而在于她用同一份简历、同一封求职信回应了每一则招聘启事，根本没认真读每个雇主到底要什么。

A job posting is a short document, but every line has a job to do. The first section usually describes the role; the second lists the requirements. Read the requirements as two groups. "Must have" items are real filters: if you do not meet them, a recruiter will rarely put you on the shortlist. "Nice to have" items are a wish list, and most people who get the job meet only some of them.

招聘启事篇幅不长，但每一行都有用处。第一部分通常描述职位，第二部分列出要求。把要求分成两组来读。「必须具备」是真正的筛子：不符合，招聘人员很少会把你放进候选名单。「具备更好」是愿望清单，最后拿到工作的人大多只符合其中几条。

Then look at the nouns the employer repeats. If "reliability" appears twice and "flexibility" once, that is the order in which they care. Turn those nouns into evidence: not "I am reliable", but one sentence about a time you kept a project on schedule when others were late. Word families matter here. A posting that asks for "willingness to learn" is answered by a line about something you learned quickly, not by the word "willing".

再看雇主反复用的名词。如果「可靠性」出现两次、「灵活性」出现一次，那就是他们在乎的先后顺序。把这些名词变成证据：不是写「我很可靠」，而是用一句话讲一次别人都拖了、你却让项目按时完成的经历。这里词族很要紧。启事要的是「学习意愿」，回应它的是一句讲你很快学会了什么的话，而不是「愿意」这个词。

Finally, notice what is missing. A posting that never mentions a salary range, or says "competitive pay" and nothing else, leaves the money for a later conversation. A posting full of "fast-paced" and "wear many hats" may be telling you about long hours. After Li Na rewrote the first paragraph of her letter for each posting, she got four interviews in three weeks. Her experience was the same; her reading was different.

最后，留意启事里没写的东西。一则启事从不提薪资范围，或者只写「薪酬有竞争力」，说明钱要留到以后再谈。满篇「节奏快」「一人多岗」的启事，可能是在告诉你工时很长。李娜针对每一则启事重写了求职信的第一段，三周里拿到了四次面试。经验还是那些经验，读法不一样了。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

It was that she had answered every job posting with the same résumé and the same cover letter, without really reading what each employer had asked for.
It was that… points back to "the problem". Past perfect had answered and had asked for go back before the adviser's visit.
It was that… 回指前面的「问题」。过去完成时 had answered、had asked for 都发生在顾问看材料之前。
A posting that asks for "willingness to learn" is answered by a line about something you learned quickly, not by the word "willing".
The subject is long (A posting that asks for…); the verb is passive is answered by; not by contrasts the wrong answer.
主语很长（A posting that asks for…），谓语是被动的 is answered by，not by 引出错误的回应方式作对比。
