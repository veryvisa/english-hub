Hobby Fair Draws a Big Crowd 爱好博览会人气爆棚

🧠 Learn the 4 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 4 个新词

A free hobby fair at a town library attracted far more visitors than expected, many of them looking for ways to use their free time. 一座小镇图书馆举办的免费爱好博览会吸引了远超预期的访客，许多人想找到利用空闲时间的方式。

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

A hobby fair held at a town library on Saturday attracted more than 600 visitors, nearly twice as many as last year. The event was free to enter. Organisers said they were surprised by the crowd. Visitors tried activities such as knitting, chess, painting and bird watching.

上周六，一座小镇图书馆举办的爱好博览会吸引了600多名访客，几乎是去年的两倍。活动免费入场。组织者说，他们对人流之多感到惊讶。参观者尝试了编织、国际象棋、绘画和观鸟等活动。

According to the organisers, many people came because they wanted to take up a new activity but did not know where to start. ‘Most visitors told us they have free time at the weekend, but they are not sure how to use it,’ one volunteer explained.

据组织者介绍，许多人前来是因为想开始一项新活动，却不知从何下手。一位志愿者解释说：“大多数访客告诉我们，他们周末有空闲时间，但不确定该怎么用。”

Several older visitors said they were looking forward to joining weekly groups. A retired teacher, who tried chess for the first time, said the game was harder than she had expected. ‘I lost three times in a row, but I will come back,’ she said.

几位年长的访客说，他们期待加入每周的小组活动。一位退休教师第一次尝试了国际象棋，她说这个游戏比她预想的要难。她说：“我连输了三次，但我还会再来的。”

Younger people were interested in different things. Many of them wanted to unwind after busy weeks at work or school, and they preferred quiet activities that did not need much money. Some students said they spend too much time on their phones and want a change.

年轻人的兴趣则有所不同。他们中许多人想在忙碌的工作周或学习周之后放松一下，更喜欢不太花钱的安静活动。有些学生说，他们花在手机上的时间太多，想换换花样。

The library plans to hold another fair in the autumn. It is likely to be even bigger. Organisers hope to include more sports and music, and they are asking local people to suggest ideas. Anyone who wants to help can leave a note at the front desk.

图书馆计划在秋天再办一次博览会，规模很可能会更大。组织者希望加入更多体育和音乐项目，并请当地居民提出建议。想帮忙的人可以在前台留一张便条。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

A retired teacher, who tried chess for the first time, said the game was harder than she had expected.
The non-defining clause ‘who tried chess for the first time’ adds extra information, and the past perfect ‘had expected’ shows she expected it before she played.
非限制性定语从句 who tried chess for the first time 补充额外信息；过去完成时 had expected 表示她在玩之前就有的预期。
Many of them wanted to unwind after busy weeks at work or school, and they preferred quiet activities that did not need much money.
The defining relative clause ‘that did not need much money’ tells us which activities they preferred.
限制性定语从句 that did not need much money 说明他们喜欢的是哪类活动。
