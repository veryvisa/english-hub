📏 The rule规则

📏 The rule in one line规则一句话
When you want another person to act, English puts that person right after the verb and the action after them: want him to go, ask her to wait. Only make, let and have (and often help) take the bare verb: let him go.想让别人做某事时，英语把那个人紧跟在动词后面，再接他要做的动作：want him to go、ask her to wait。只有 make、let、have（help 常常也是）接不带 to 的原形：let him go。

Chinese often uses a full clause here, and with hope English can too: I hope that he comes tomorrow. But want cannot take a that clause in English. You must say I want him to come tomorrow. The same is true of would like, expect in the sense of requirement, and need: We need you to sign here.

中文常用一个小句：我希望他明天来。用 hope 可以说 I hope that he comes tomorrow，但 want 后面不能接 that 从句，必须说 I want him to come tomorrow。would like、表「要求」的 expect，以及 need 也一样：We need you to sign here。

The choice of verb carries the force of the request. ask and invite leave the other person free. tell and remind assume they will do it. persuade and convince mean they did not want to at first. force and require mean they had no choice. In an email to your landlord, I'd like you to fix the heater is firm and polite; I need you to fix the heater by Friday is firmer.

动词本身就带着请求的力度。ask、invite 给对方留了余地；tell、remind 默认对方会做；persuade、convince 说明对方一开始不想；force、require 说明对方没得选。给房东写信，I'd like you to fix the heater 坚定又礼貌；I need you to fix the heater by Friday 更硬。

make, let and have take the object and a bare verb. make is pressure: The cold made us leave early. let is permission: My manager let me work from home. have is arranging for a professional or a staff member to do something, common in service English: I'll have someone call you back. In the passive, make gets its to back: We were made to wait.

make、let、have 后接宾语和原形动词。make 是迫使；let 是允许；have 是安排专人去做，在服务场合很常见：I'll have someone call you back。改成被动时 make 要加回 to：We were made to wait。

Negatives go before to, not before the main verb: The doctor told me not to lift anything heavy. This changes the meaning. She didn't tell me to wait means there was no instruction; She told me not to wait means there was an instruction, and it was to leave.

否定放在 to 前面，不放在主动词前面，意思不同：She didn't tell me to wait 是她没给指示；She told me not to wait 是她给了指示，叫我别等。

Some common verbs do not take this pattern at all. suggest, recommend and insist use a that clause or -ing: The pharmacist suggested that I take it with food, not suggested me to take. explain and say take to + person before a clause: She explained to us how it works. These are the errors most often heard from fluent but unpolished speakers.

有些常用动词根本不用这个句型。suggest、recommend、insist 用 that 从句或 -ing：The pharmacist suggested that I take it with food，不说 suggested me to take。explain、say 要先接 to 再接人：She explained to us how it works。说得流利但不够地道的人，最常错的就是这几个。

PatternVerbsExample
verb + object + to + verbwant, ask, tell, expect, allow, remind, persuade, advise, warn, encourage, needThey asked us to leave our shoes at the door.
verb + object + bare verbmake, let, haveThe landlord let us paint the bedroom.
help + object + (to) verbhelpA friend helped me (to) fill in the form.
verb + that + clausesuggest, recommend, insist, proposeThe doctor recommended that he rest for a week.
verb + to + person + clauseexplain, say, describe, suggestCould you explain to me what this fee is for?

Examples例句

  • My manager wants me to lead the next team meeting. 经理想让我主持下一次团队会议。
    Person, then the action.先说人，再说动作。
  • The school asked parents to label every water bottle. 学校要求家长给每个水瓶贴上名字。
    A polite request.客气的请求。
  • I'd like you to read this before we sign anything. 签任何东西之前，我想请你先看看这个。
    Firm but polite.坚定但礼貌。
  • The pharmacist reminded me to take the tablets with food. 药剂师提醒我随餐服药。
    The person already planned to do it.对方本来就该做。
  • We finally persuaded my father to see a doctor about his knee. 我们终于说服父亲去看看膝盖。
    He did not want to at first.他起初不想去。
  • The new policy doesn't allow staff to use personal phones on the floor. 新规定不允许员工在营业区用私人手机。
    Permission, formal.正式的许可。
  • The doctor told him not to drive for twenty-four hours. 医生叫他二十四小时内别开车。
    not before to.not 放在 to 前面。
  • The long wait made everyone feel tired and annoyed. 漫长的等待让每个人都又累又烦。
    make + bare verb.make 接原形。
  • Our landlord let us keep the cat after all. 房东最后还是让我们留下了猫。
    let = permission.let＝允许。
  • I'll have the manager call you back this afternoon. 我请经理今天下午给您回电话。
    have = arrange for someone to do it.have＝安排人去做。
  • A neighbour helped us carry the sofa up the stairs. 一位邻居帮我们把沙发抬上了楼。
    help often drops to.help 后常省 to。
  • Passengers were made to wait on the plane for two hours. 乘客被迫在飞机上等了两个小时。
    Passive make takes to.make 的被动要加 to。
  • The bank expects customers to report a lost card immediately. 银行要求客户立即挂失丢失的卡。
    expect = require.expect＝要求。
  • The counsellor encouraged her to apply for the evening program. 辅导员鼓励她申请夜校项目。
    Supportive pressure.鼓励性的推动。

More pairs: same idea, different choice更多对比句组

She didn't tell me to wait. 她没说让我等。
No instruction was given.根本没有指示。
She told me not to wait. 她叫我别等。
An instruction to leave.有指示：别等了。
She made me wait. 她让我等着。
I had no choice.我没得选。
My parents let me choose my own university. 我父母让我自己选大学。
Permission.允许。
My parents made me choose a practical major. 我父母逼我选一个实用的专业。
Pressure.迫使。
My parents encouraged me to choose what I loved. 我父母鼓励我选自己喜欢的。
Support.支持。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ I want that you come to the party.
✓ I want you to come to the party.
want never takes a that clause.
want 后面不能接 that 从句。
✗ The doctor suggested me to rest.
✓ The doctor suggested that I rest. / The doctor suggested resting.
suggest does not take object + to.
suggest 不用「宾语＋to」。
✗ My boss let me to leave early.
✓ My boss let me leave early.
let takes a bare verb.
let 接原形。
✗ He told me don't park there.
✓ He told me not to park there.
Reported orders use not to, not don't.
转述命令用 not to，不用 don't。
✗ Can you explain me this bill?
✓ Can you explain this bill to me?
explain needs to before the person.
explain 要先接 to 再接人。
