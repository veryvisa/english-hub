Institutions for a Changing World 面向变化世界的制度

Read and listen读与听

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Ladies and gentlemen, institutions are often judged by their ability to preserve stability. We expect schools to educate, courts to resolve disputes, and public agencies to provide reliable services. Yet stability alone is not enough. The conditions under which institutions operate are constantly changing. Technology alters communication, economic pressures reshape priorities, and public expectations evolve. An institution that cannot adapt may eventually protect outdated practices rather than serve the people who depend on it.

女士们、先生们，人们往往根据制度维护稳定的能力来评价它们。我们期待学校承担教育职责、法院解决争议、公共机构提供可靠服务。然而，仅有稳定还不够。制度运作所处的环境一直在变化。技术改变了沟通方式，经济压力重塑了优先事项，公众期望也在不断演变。一个无法适应变化的制度，最终可能会保护过时的做法，而不是服务于依赖它的人们。

Consider the growth of digital administration. Online systems can make services more accessible, reduce repetitive work, and improve record management. However, technological efficiency should not become the only measure of success. Some people may have limited digital skills, while others may be concerned about privacy or automated decisions. Although digital tools can strengthen institutional capacity, they tend to deliver better results when supported by accessible alternatives, clear explanations, and meaningful human review.

以数字化行政的发展为例。在线系统能够使服务更容易获得、减少重复工作并改善记录管理。然而，技术效率不应成为衡量成功的唯一标准。有些人的数字技能可能有限，另一些人则可能担心隐私或自动化决策。尽管数字工具能够增强制度能力，但如果同时提供便捷的替代方式、清晰的解释和有意义的人工审查，它们通常能够取得更好的结果。

The challenge is to distinguish principles from procedures. Principles such as fairness, transparency, and accountability should remain stable enough to guide decisions. Procedures, by contrast, may need regular adjustment as circumstances change. This distinction allows institutions to innovate without abandoning their fundamental responsibilities. It also creates a basis for evaluating reform: does a new arrangement preserve important protections while making the institution more responsive and effective?

关键在于区分原则和程序。公平、透明和问责等原则应该保持足够的稳定性，以便指导决策。相比之下，程序可能需要随着环境变化而定期调整。这种区分使制度能够创新，而不必放弃基本责任，也为评估改革提供了依据：新的安排是否在保留重要保障的同时，使制度更具回应能力和效率？

Looking ahead, resilient institutions are likely to be those that learn systematically. They collect evidence, listen to affected communities, review unintended consequences, and explain why changes are being introduced. By and large, institutional adaptability depends less on constant restructuring than on the willingness to identify problems and correct them. What matters most is not preserving every existing procedure, but maintaining the principles that make institutional authority worthy of public confidence. Stability and change are not opposites; when carefully balanced, they reinforce one another.

展望未来，具有韧性的制度很可能是那些能够系统性学习的制度。它们收集证据、倾听受影响群体的意见、审查意外后果，并解释引入改革的原因。总体而言，制度的适应能力与其说依赖不断重组，不如说取决于识别问题并加以纠正的意愿。最重要的不是保留每一项现有程序，而是维护那些使制度权力值得公众信任的原则。稳定与变化并非对立面；如果两者得到谨慎平衡，就能够相互促进。

Words词

/rɪˈzɪliənt/ adj.
有韧性的；能够适应冲击的
resilient institution; build resilience
A resilient organisation can continue operating during unexpected disruptions. 有韧性的组织能够在意外中断期间继续运作。
/əˌdæptəˈbɪləti/ n.
适应能力
institutional adaptability; improve adaptability
Adaptability is an important quality in a rapidly changing environment. 在快速变化的环境中，适应能力是一项重要品质。
/kəˈpæsəti/ n.
能力；承载能力
institutional capacity; build capacity
The programme aims to strengthen the institution's research capacity. 该项目旨在增强这个机构的研究能力。
/rɪˈspɒnsɪv/ adj.
能够及时回应的；反应灵敏的
responsive institution; highly responsive
A responsive administration listens carefully to public concerns. 反应灵敏的行政机构会认真倾听公众关切。
/ˌʌnɪnˈtendɪd/ adj.
非预期的；意料之外的
unintended consequences; unintended effects
The new policy produced several unintended consequences. 这项新政策产生了若干意料之外的后果。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

Although digital tools can strengthen institutional capacity, they tend to deliver better results when supported by accessible alternatives, clear explanations, and meaningful human review.
The sentence combines concession with hedging, then uses a reduced passive phrase beginning with supported to describe the conditions for better results.
该句结合了让步和限定表达，随后使用以 supported 开头的简化被动结构来说明取得更好结果的条件。
Stability and change are not opposites; when carefully balanced, they reinforce one another.
The semicolon joins two closely related independent clauses, while when carefully balanced is a reduced passive conditional clause.
分号连接两个密切相关的独立分句，而 when carefully balanced 是一个简化的被动条件从句。
