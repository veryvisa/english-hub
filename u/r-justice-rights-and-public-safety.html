Freedom, Security, and the Law 自由、安全与法律

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Ladies and gentlemen, we often discuss freedom and security as if they were opposing forces. When a serious threat emerges, people understandably expect public institutions to respond quickly. Governments may introduce new investigative powers, increase surveillance, or restrict certain activities. Such measures can sometimes protect the public. However, the existence of a threat does not automatically justify every proposed restriction. The central challenge is to protect society without weakening the principles that give public authority its legitimacy.

女士们、先生们，我们经常把自由和安全视为彼此对立的力量。当严重威胁出现时，人们自然希望公共机构迅速作出回应。政府可能引入新的调查权力、加强监控，或者限制某些活动。这些措施有时能够保护公众。然而，威胁的存在并不意味着所有拟议中的限制都自动具有正当性。核心挑战是在保护社会的同时，不削弱赋予公共权力合法性的原则。

The rule of law offers an essential starting point. It requires public authorities to act within established legal limits rather than relying on personal preference or political convenience. Laws should be publicly accessible, reasonably predictable, and applied consistently. While emergencies may require exceptional measures, those measures should remain subject to meaningful oversight. Otherwise, temporary restrictions can gradually become permanent features of government, even after the original danger has diminished.

法治原则提供了一个重要的出发点。它要求公共权力机构在既定法律界限内行事，而不是依赖个人偏好或政治便利。法律应该公开可查、具有合理的可预测性，并得到一致执行。尽管紧急情况可能需要采取特殊措施，但这些措施仍然应该接受有意义的监督。否则，即使最初的危险已经减弱，临时限制也可能逐渐成为政府的永久性特征。

Proportionality is another critical principle. A restriction should be connected to a legitimate objective, and its likely benefits should justify the interference with individual rights. For example, surveillance powers may help investigate serious offences, but indiscriminate monitoring can threaten privacy and discourage lawful expression. The effectiveness of such measures should be assessed rather than simply assumed. Independent courts, legislative scrutiny, and transparent reporting can help identify excessive uses of authority.

相称性是另一项关键原则。限制措施应该与正当目标相关联，而且其可能带来的好处应该足以证明对个人权利进行干预是合理的。例如，监控权力可能有助于调查严重犯罪，但不加区分的监控可能威胁隐私，并抑制合法表达。此类措施的有效性应该经过评估，而不是被简单地假定。独立法院、立法审查和透明报告能够帮助识别权力的过度使用。

Ladies and gentlemen, neither absolute freedom nor unlimited state power provides a satisfactory foundation for a just society. Security is indispensable, but it must be pursued through lawful and accountable institutions. On balance, societies are more likely to preserve public confidence when restrictions are clearly justified, regularly reviewed, and open to challenge. The rule of law is not merely a technical framework for officials. It is a public commitment that power must have limits, even when circumstances are difficult.

女士们、先生们，绝对自由和不受限制的国家权力，都无法为公正社会提供令人满意的基础。安全不可或缺，但必须通过合法且负责任的机构来实现。总体而言，当限制措施有明确的正当理由、定期接受审查并且可以受到质疑时，社会更有可能保持公众信心。法治不仅仅是供官员使用的技术框架。它是一项公共承诺，即使在困难的环境下，权力也必须受到限制。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

The central challenge is to protect society without weakening the principles that give public authority its legitimacy.
The infinitive phrase to protect society functions as a subject complement, while without is followed by a gerund to express a condition that must be avoided.
不定式短语 to protect society 作表语补足成分，而 without 后接动名词，表示需要避免的情况。
On balance, societies are more likely to preserve public confidence when restrictions are clearly justified, regularly reviewed, and open to challenge.
On balance introduces an overall judgment, and the comparative expression more likely to indicates a qualified assessment of probability.
On balance 引出总体判断，而 more likely to 表示带有限定的可能性评估。
