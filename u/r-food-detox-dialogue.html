The Juice Cleanse Conversation 关于果汁排毒的一次对话

🧠 Learn the 5 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 5 个新词

Follow two friends arguing about a juice cleanse and pick up the verb–noun collocations that carry everyday persuasion. 跟着两个朋友争论果汁排毒，学日常劝说里最常用的动名搭配。

📖 Key card知识卡
If a product promises big results in five days, ask who gets the benefit: your body or the seller.一样东西承诺五天见大效，先问好处归谁：你的身体还是卖家。

Read and listen读与听

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Anna: I've made a decision. Starting Monday, I'm doing a five-day juice cleanse. No food, just juice. Tom: Five days? What's it supposed to do? Anna: Detox. Get rid of all the processed stuff I've been eating. The label says it flushes out toxins.

安娜：我决定了。从周一开始，我要做五天果汁排毒。不吃饭，只喝果汁。汤姆：五天？它是干什么用的？安娜：排毒啊，把我吃进去的那些加工食品都清掉。标签上说能把毒素冲出去。

Tom: Which toxins, though? I'm not trying to be difficult. I just want to know what it actually removes. Anna: You know, toxins. From sugar and stress. Tom: Your liver and kidneys already do that job, all day, for free. If a juice could do it better, every doctor would be prescribing it.

汤姆：可到底是哪些毒素？我不是故意抬杠，只是想知道它到底清掉了什么。安娜：你懂的，毒素嘛，糖和压力带来的。汤姆：你的肝和肾整天都在免费干这活。要是果汁能干得更好，每个医生都会给人开了。

Anna: So you think it's a waste of money. Tom: I think you should take the claim with a pinch of salt. You'll probably lose a couple of kilos, but most of it will be water, and it'll come back the first week you eat normally. A friend of mine tried one last year. She felt terrible by day three and spent the weekend eating crisps.

安娜：所以你觉得这是浪费钱。汤姆：我觉得这个说法你别全信。你大概会掉一两公斤，但大多是水分，恢复正常吃饭的第一周就会回来。我一个朋友去年试过，第三天就难受得不行，整个周末都在吃薯片。

Anna: Okay, but I really want to make an effort this year. I feel like I've been eating badly since Christmas. Tom: Then that's the part worth keeping. Forget the cleanse and choose one change you can live with. Cook at home three nights a week. Take a break from the snack machine at work. It's less exciting, but you'll actually make progress, and you won't need to cut out food completely.

安娜：好吧，可我今年真的想下点功夫。我觉得从圣诞节起我就一直吃得很差。汤姆：那这份心思值得留着。别管排毒了，挑一个你能长期坚持的改变：一周在家做三晚饭，上班时别老往零食机那儿跑。这没那么刺激，但你会真的有进步，也不用把饭完全戒掉。

Anna: You sound like my mother. Tom: Your mother sounds sensible. Look, here's a rule of thumb. If something promises big results in five days, the benefit usually goes to the seller, not to your health. It's the same with half the supplements online. Anna: Fine. I'll give the cleanse a miss. But you're cooking on Wednesday. Tom: Deal.

安娜：你说话像我妈。汤姆：你妈挺明智的。听着，有条经验法则：一样东西承诺五天见大效，好处通常落在卖家那儿，不在你的健康上。网上一半的保健品也是这样。安娜：行，排毒我就不做了。但周三你做饭。汤姆：成交。

Words词

/ˈdiːtɒks/ n. / v.
排毒（多为营销用语）
a detox diet; a juice detox; detox from sth
Your body does its own detox. 身体自己就会排毒。
/pɪntʃ/ idiom
对……别全信
take a claim with a pinch of salt; take the headline with a pinch of salt
Take online reviews with a pinch of salt. 网上的评价别全信。
/θʌm/ n.
经验法则
a good rule of thumb; as a rule of thumb
As a rule of thumb, fill half your plate with vegetables. 经验法则是半盘装蔬菜。
/flʌʃ aʊt/ phr. v.
冲出；清除
flush out toxins; flush sth out of the body
Drinking water helps flush out salt. 喝水有助于把盐分排出去。
/mɪs/ phr.
不去做；放弃
give it a miss; give the party a miss
I think I'll give dessert a miss tonight. 今晚甜点我就不吃了。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

If a juice could do it better, every doctor would be prescribing it.
Unreal present: could in the if-clause, would be + -ing for what would be happening now.
与现在事实相反：if 从句用 could，结果用 would be + -ing 表示此刻本会发生的事。
If something promises big results in five days, the benefit usually goes to the seller, not to your health.
A real (zero) conditional: a general rule, present in both halves. Compare it with the unreal one above.
真实条件句：说一般规律，前后都用现在时。和上一句的虚拟条件对比。
