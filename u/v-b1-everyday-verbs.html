📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Open the full explanation: 5 points展开细讲：5 段讲解

English often uses different verbs for actions that Chinese speakers may express with one general verb. For example, attend a meeting, join a discussion, and participate in an activity describe different ways of being involved. Choosing the precise verb makes everyday communication clearer.

英语经常使用不同的动词来表达中文中可能由同一个笼统动词表示的动作。例如，attend a meeting、join a discussion 和 participate in an activity 分别描述不同的参与方式。选择准确的动词能让日常交流更加清楚。

Pay attention to the nouns that commonly follow each verb. We usually say make a decision, take a break, give advice, and spend time. These combinations are natural collocations, so learning them as complete chunks is more effective than memorising individual words.

注意每个动词后面经常搭配的名词。英语通常说 make a decision、take a break、give advice 和 spend time。这些都是自然的固定搭配，把它们作为完整词块学习，比单独记忆单词更有效。

Some everyday verbs describe a result, while others describe an activity or process. For example, borrow means receiving something temporarily, whereas lend means giving something temporarily. Similarly, choose focuses on making a selection, while decide focuses on reaching a decision.

有些日常动词强调结果，另一些则强调活动或过程。例如，borrow 表示暂时从别人那里借入某物，而 lend 表示暂时把某物借给别人。同样，choose 强调选择某个选项，而 decide 强调作出决定。

Several common verbs require particular prepositions or grammatical patterns. We say explain something to someone, suggest doing something, and depend on someone or something. Learners should memorise the complete patterns because translating prepositions directly from Chinese often produces unnatural English.

一些常见动词需要特定的介词或语法结构。我们说 explain something to someone、suggest doing something 和 depend on someone or something。学习者应该记住完整结构，因为直接按照中文翻译介词往往会产生不自然的英语。

Register also matters when choosing everyday verbs. Help is common in ordinary conversation, whereas assist sounds more formal. Similarly, buy is more conversational than purchase. The best choice depends on the situation, the audience, and the level of formality.

选择日常动词时还要注意语体。help 常用于普通对话，而 assist 听起来更加正式。同样，buy 比 purchase 更口语化。最合适的选择取决于场合、交流对象以及正式程度。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ I attended to the meeting yesterday.
✓ I attended the meeting yesterday.
Attend is normally a transitive verb in this meaning and takes a direct object without a preposition. Do not add to after attend when describing participation in a meeting or class.
Attend 在表示参加会议或课程时通常是及物动词，后面直接接宾语，不需要介词。描述参加会议或课程时，不要在 attend 后面添加 to。
✗ I suggested him to take a break.
✓ I suggested that he take a break.
Suggest is not normally followed by an object plus an infinitive. Use suggest doing something or suggest that someone do something.
Suggest 通常不能直接使用宾语加不定式的结构。应该使用 suggest doing something，或者 suggest that someone do something。
✗ Can you explain me this problem?
✓ Can you explain this problem to me?
Explain takes the thing being explained as its direct object. When mentioning the person receiving the explanation, use to before that person.
Explain 后面直接接被解释的事情。如果还要说明向谁解释，需要在人物前使用 to。
✗ I borrowed him my car for the weekend.
✓ I lent him my car for the weekend.
Borrow describes receiving something temporarily, while lend describes giving something temporarily. The direction of the action determines which verb is correct.
Borrow 表示暂时从别人那里借入东西，而 lend 表示暂时把东西借给别人。动作的方向决定应该使用哪个动词。
✗ I decided going home early.
✓ I decided to go home early.
When decide is followed by an action, it normally takes a to-infinitive. Remember the pattern decide to do something.
Decide 后面表示决定做某个动作时，通常接带 to 的不定式。记住 decide to do something 这个结构。
✗ It depends of the weather.
✓ It depends on the weather.
The standard expression is depend on, not depend of. The preposition on is part of the complete verb pattern.
标准表达是 depend on，而不是 depend of。介词 on 是这个动词短语不可缺少的一部分。
