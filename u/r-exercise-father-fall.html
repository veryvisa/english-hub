The Fall That Changed My Father's Mornings 改变父亲早晨的那一跤

🧠 Learn the 5 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 5 个新词

Read a story about a father's fall and recovery, and watch the tenses move from "two winters ago" to "since then". 读一个父亲跌倒又恢复的故事，看时态怎样从「两个冬天前」走到「从那以后」。

B2 · CELPIP 7–8⏱ 20 minG02 Present perfect and past simple confused现在完成时与一般过去时混用G08 Tense not kept consistent across a story叙事时态前后不一

📖 Key card知识卡
After a fall, sitting still feels safe but is the risky choice; balance and strength can be rebuilt at any age.跌倒之后，坐着不动感觉安全，其实才是冒险；平衡和力量在任何年龄都能练回来。

Read and listen读与听

310 words
Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Two winters ago my father slipped on the ice outside his building. He was seventy-four. He did not break anything, but he lay on the pavement for ten minutes before a neighbour helped him up, and something in him changed. For months afterwards he hardly went out. He said the ice was dangerous. I think he had lost trust in his own legs.

两个冬天前，我父亲在楼外的冰上滑倒了。那年他七十四岁。他没骨折，但在人行道上躺了十分钟才有邻居把他扶起来，他身上有什么东西从此变了。之后好几个月他几乎不出门。他说冰太危险，我觉得是他不再信任自己的腿了。

His doctor said something that surprised us both. The fall itself was bad luck, but the weakness that followed was not. When older people stop moving out of fear, they lose muscle and balance quickly, and that makes the next fall more likely. She suggested a balance and strength class at the community centre, twice a week.

医生的话让我们俩都很意外：跌倒本身是运气不好，但随后的虚弱不是。老人因为害怕而不再活动，肌肉和平衡就会很快流失，下一次跌倒反而更可能发生。她建议他去社区中心上平衡与力量课，一周两次。

My father has never liked classes. He went the first time only because I drove him there and waited in the car park. The exercises looked almost too simple: standing on one leg while holding a chair, rising from a chair without using his hands, walking heel to toe along a line. He came out red in the face and said nothing all the way home.

我父亲一向不喜欢上课。第一次去，只是因为我开车送他、在停车场等着。那些动作看着简单得过分：扶着椅子单腿站，不用手从椅子上站起来，脚跟贴脚尖沿一条线走。他出来时满脸通红，回家一路没说话。

That was eighteen months ago. Since then he has missed perhaps five sessions, and he has kept up the walking on the other days. He has made two friends there, a retired teacher and a man who used to build boats. Last month he climbed the stairs at my house without touching the rail. He has not become fearless, but he has stopped being afraid.

那是十八个月前的事。从那以后他大概只缺过五次课，其余日子也一直坚持散步。他在那里交了两个朋友，一位退休老师，一位以前造船的。上个月他上我家楼梯，没碰扶手。他没有变得天不怕地不怕，但已经不再害怕了。

I have thought a lot about the months when he stayed at home. Nobody told us then that sitting still was the risky choice. If your parents have had a fall, or have started to avoid stairs and kerbs, ask their doctor about balance training early. It is much easier to build up strength than to win it back.

我常常想起他待在家里的那几个月。那时没人告诉我们，坐着不动才是冒险的选择。如果你的父母摔过跤，或者开始躲着楼梯和路沿走，早点问问医生平衡训练的事。力量攒起来，比丢了再找回来容易得多。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

That was eighteen months ago. Since then he has missed perhaps five sessions, and he has kept up the walking on the other days.
ago fixes a finished time → past simple; since then opens a period up to now → present perfect.
ago 锁定过去的时间点用一般过去时；since then 开启一段延续到现在的时间用现在完成时。
When older people stop moving out of fear, they lose muscle and balance quickly, and that makes the next fall more likely.
stop moving (stop an activity) vs stop to move (stop in order to move). out of fear: because of fear.
stop moving 是停止活动，stop to move 是停下来去动。out of fear：出于害怕。
