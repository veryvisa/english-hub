Comparing: as…as, far more, the more…the more 比较结构

📘 In your coursebook: English File 5e · Upper-Intermediate · 1B “We may never know”. Use the texts and recordings with your coursebook; this page adds new words, grammar and practice.教材定位：English File 5e · Upper-Intermediate · 1B《We may never know》。课文与录音请配合教材学习，本页补充新词、语法与练习。

Choose and use as...as, far more, and the more...the more accurately to express different kinds of comparisons and relationships between changes. 能够准确选择并使用 as...as、far more 和 the more...the more，表达不同类型的比较以及两个变化之间的关联。

The first laptop has greater power.第一台笔记本电脑的性能更强。

The difference in power is substantial.两台电脑的性能存在明显差距。

📏 The rule规则

📏 The rule in one line规则一句话
Use as...as to compare equal degrees, far more to emphasize a substantial difference, and the more...the more to show that two changes are connected.用 as...as 表示程度相同，用 far more 强调明显差距，用 the more...the more 表示两个变化之间存在关联。

📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Open the full explanation: 6 points, 24 examples展开细讲：6 段讲解、24 个例句

Choose as...as when the important point is that two people, things, or situations reach the same degree. Unlike a comparative with -er or more, this structure does not claim that one side is greater. In negative sentences, not as...as is usually more natural than not so...as in everyday modern English.

当重点是两个人、事物或情况的程度相同时，选择 as...as，而不是普通比较级。它不表示一方超过另一方。在现代日常英语中，否定句通常使用 not as...as，而不是 not so...as。

Use far more when a normal comparative is not strong enough to communicate the size of a difference. Far emphasizes a substantial gap, whereas slightly suggests a small one and much also intensifies the comparison. Do not use far directly before an adjective in its positive form.

当普通比较级不足以表达差距的大小时，使用 far more。far 强调明显差距，而 slightly 表示差距较小，much 也可以加强比较程度。不要将 far 直接放在形容词原级前面。

Choose the more...the more when one changing condition is associated with another changing result. It often describes a general tendency, a predictable relationship, or an observation about behavior. The two parts do not have to describe a strict cause-and-effect relationship, but they must express a meaningful connection.

当一个变化的条件与另一个变化的结果相关联时，使用 the more...the more。它常用于描述普遍趋势、可预测的关系或行为观察。两个部分不一定构成严格的因果关系，但必须表达有意义的关联。

Pay attention to the difference between as much as and as many as. Use much with uncountable nouns and many with plural countable nouns. When comparing actions, place an adverb between the two instances of as, rather than using an adjective.

注意 as much as 和 as many as 的区别。much 用于不可数名词，many 用于可数名词复数。比较动作时，两个 as 之间应使用副词，而不是形容词。

In the more...the more construction, each clause normally has its own comparative expression. Do not replace the second the with than, because than introduces a different type of comparison. The verb tense depends on the time and meaning of the situation, not simply on the word the.

在 the more...the more 结构中，两个分句通常各自包含一个比较表达。不要把第二个 the 换成 than，因为 than 引出的是另一种比较关系。动词时态应根据实际时间和语境决定，而不是由 the 决定。

The as...as pattern can also show an exact ratio by adding a multiplier before the first as, such as twice as, three times as or half as: 'The journey took twice as long as we had expected' doubles the degree, and 'this model uses half as much power' halves it. Far more, by contrast, only signals that a gap is large; it never gives a precise ratio, so use a multiplier with as...as when the exact proportion matters.

as...as 结构还可以在第一个 as 前加倍数词表示精确的比例关系，如 twice as、three times as、half as：The journey took twice as long as we had expected 表示翻倍，this model uses half as much power 表示减半。相比之下，far more 只表示差距很大，不给出精确比例，所以需要精确比例时要用带倍数词的 as...as。

Examples例句

  • This apartment is as spacious as the one we rented last year. 这套公寓和我们去年租的那套一样宽敞。
  • The new system is not as complicated as the old one. 新系统没有旧系统那么复杂。
  • She speaks English as fluently as her colleagues. 她说英语和她的同事一样流利。
  • The second proposal is almost as expensive as the first. 第二份提案几乎和第一份一样昂贵。
  • I do not earn as much as my older brother. 我的收入没有哥哥那么高。
  • The company employs as many people as it did five years ago. 这家公司雇用的人数和五年前一样多。
  • You should prepare as carefully as possible before the interview. 面试前你应该尽可能认真地做好准备。
  • The journey took twice as long as we had expected. 这段旅程花费的时间是我们预期的两倍。
  • Her explanation was not nearly as convincing as his. 她的解释远没有他的那么有说服力。
  • The local library is just as important as the university library. 当地公共图书馆和大学图书馆同样重要。
  • Housing is far more expensive in the city center. 市中心的住房贵得多。
  • Online courses are far more accessible than they were a decade ago. 如今在线课程比十年前容易获得得多。
  • The second candidate has far more relevant experience. 第二位候选人拥有多得多的相关经验。
  • Public transportation is much more reliable in this city. 这座城市的公共交通可靠得多。
  • The actual cost was far higher than the original estimate. 实际成本远高于最初的估算。
  • This approach is considerably more effective for beginners. 这种方法对初学者有效得多。
  • The new model uses far less electricity than the previous one. 新型号使用的电量比上一款少得多。
  • Her latest article attracted far more attention than expected. 她最近发表的文章获得了远超预期的关注。
  • The repair turned out to be much more complicated than we thought. 结果发现维修工作比我们想象的复杂得多。
  • There are far fewer affordable homes available this year. 今年可供选择的经济适用住房少得多。
  • The more you practice, the more confident you become. 你练习得越多，就会变得越自信。
  • The more people work remotely, the more important digital communication becomes. 远程办公的人越多，数字沟通就变得越重要。
  • The longer we wait, the more difficult the decision becomes. 我们等待的时间越长，做出决定就越困难。
  • The less you spend, the more money you can save. 你花得越少，就能存下越多的钱。

More pairs: same idea, different choice更多对比句组

The more time you spend reading, the more vocabulary you learn. 你花在阅读上的时间越多，学到的词汇就越多。
Both reading time and vocabulary acquisition increase together.阅读时间和词汇积累呈同步增长关系。
The more time you spend reading, the better your writing becomes. 你花在阅读上的时间越多，写作能力就越好。
More reading is associated with improved writing quality.更多阅读与写作质量的提升相关。
The more time you spend reading, the less time you have for television. 你花在阅读上的时间越多，看电视的时间就越少。
An increase in one activity is associated with a decrease in another.一种活动增加与另一种活动减少相关。
The course is as useful as I expected. 这门课程和我预期的一样有用。
The actual usefulness matches the expectation.实际效果与预期相符。
The course is more useful than I expected. 这门课程比我预期的更有用。
The actual usefulness exceeds the expectation.实际效果超过预期。
The course is far more useful than I expected. 这门课程比我预期的有用得多。
The actual usefulness greatly exceeds the expectation.实际效果远远超过预期。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ This room is as bigger as the other one.
✓ This room is as big as the other one.
The as...as pattern requires the positive form of the adjective, not its comparative form. Do not combine as with an additional comparative ending.
as...as 结构中应使用形容词原级，而不是比较级。不要在 as 结构中再添加比较级词尾。
✗ My salary is not as higher as yours.
✓ My salary is not as high as yours.
The adjective between the two instances of as must stay in its positive form whether the sentence is affirmative or negative. Adding -er on top of as...as creates an ungrammatical double comparison.
两个 as 之间的形容词无论肯定句还是否定句都必须用原级。在 as...as 结构上再加 -er 会构成错误的双重比较。
✗ This solution is far easier as the previous one.
✓ This solution is far easier than the previous one.
Far can intensify a comparative adjective, but the comparison normally takes than, not as. Use as...as only when expressing equality.
far 可以加强比较级，但比较对象通常由 than 引出，而不是 as。只有表达程度相同时才使用 as...as。
✗ This option is very more affordable than the other one.
✓ This option is far more affordable than the other one.
Very does not normally modify a comparative form such as more affordable. Use far, much, or a lot to intensify the difference.
very 通常不能直接修饰 more affordable 这样的比较结构。可以使用 far、much 或 a lot 来加强差距。
✗ The more you study, than the better your results become.
✓ The more you study, the better your results become.
This paired construction requires the before both comparative expressions. Than belongs to a different comparative pattern and cannot replace the second the.
这种对应结构要求两个比较表达前都使用 the。than 属于另一种比较句型，不能代替第二个 the。
✗ The more carefully you drive, the more safer you are.
✓ The more carefully you drive, the safer you are.
Safer is already a comparative adjective. Adding more creates an incorrect double comparative. Use more with longer adjectives that form their comparative with more.
safer 本身已经是比较级，再加 more 就构成了错误的双重比较级。只有需要用 more 构成比较级的较长形容词才使用 more。
