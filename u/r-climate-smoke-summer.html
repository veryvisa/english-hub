The Summer We Kept the Windows Shut 那个关着窗的夏天

🧠 Learn the 5 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 5 个新词

Read a newcomer's story of a smoky summer, and watch the present perfect pull four years of change into the present. 读一位新移民讲述的烟雾之夏，看现在完成时怎样把四年的变化拉到眼前。

Extreme weather stops being news once it happens on your own street; then it shows up in small weekly decisions.极端天气一旦落到自家街上就不再是新闻，而是出现在每周的小决定里。

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

On the first smoky day of our first summer here, I thought someone nearby was burning leaves. By noon the sky was orange, the sun was a pale pink circle, and my phone showed an air quality warning I did not know how to read. The wildfires were hundreds of kilometres away. The smoke had come to us anyway.

我们来这里的第一个夏天，第一个烟雾天，我还以为是附近有人在烧落叶。到中午，天成了橙色，太阳成了一个淡粉色的圆圈，手机上弹出一条空气质量警报，我都不知道该怎么看。山火在几百公里之外，烟却照样飘到了我们这儿。

We kept the windows shut for most of August. My son, who had looked forward to his first Canadian summer of camps and lakes, spent it indoors. A neighbour lent us an air purifier and showed me the website she checks every morning. I have checked it every morning since, even in winter, out of habit.

那年八月我们大部分时间都关着窗。儿子一直盼着在加拿大过第一个有夏令营、有湖的夏天，结果整个夏天都待在屋里。一位邻居借给我们一台空气净化器，还给我看了她每天早上都查的网站。从那以后我每天早上都查，连冬天也是，成了习惯。

Four summers have passed since then, and smoke has become part of the season, like mosquitoes. We have bought a second purifier, I have learned which masks actually help, and we have made a family rule: when the index goes above a certain level, outdoor sport moves inside. My son, now eleven, has stopped complaining. He checks the number before I do.

从那以后过了四个夏天，烟雾已经成了这个季节的一部分，就像蚊子一样。我们又买了一台净化器，我弄清了哪种口罩真的有用，家里还定了一条规矩：指数超过某个水平，户外运动就改到室内。儿子现在十一岁了，已经不再抱怨。他比我还先看那个数字。

What I did not expect was the effect on how we think about where we live. When we moved, we compared schools, rents and commutes. Nobody told us to ask about smoke or heat. Last spring, when friends were choosing between two towns, the first thing I asked was how bad the summers had been.

我没想到的是，这件事改变了我们看待住处的方式。搬来时，我们比较了学校、房租和通勤，没有人提醒我们问问烟雾和高温。去年春天，朋友在两个小镇之间做选择，我问的第一件事，就是那里前几年的夏天有多糟。

I still love it here. But I have stopped thinking of extreme weather as something that happens on the news, to other people. It happens on our street, in our son's lungs, and in the small decisions we make every week.

我还是很喜欢这里。但我已经不再把极端天气当成新闻里、发生在别人身上的事。它就发生在我们街上、在儿子的肺里、在我们每周做的那些小决定里。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

The smoke had come to us anyway.
Past perfect: the smoke arrived before the moment the writer is describing. anyway = in spite of the distance.
过去完成时：烟在作者描述的那一刻之前就已经飘到了。anyway＝尽管离得那么远。
I have checked it every morning since, even in winter, out of habit.
Present perfect with since at the end: from that August until now, and still going on.
句末的 since 配现在完成时：从那年八月到现在，而且还在继续。
