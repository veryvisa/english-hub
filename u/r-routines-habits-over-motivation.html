Habits Matter More Than Motivation 习惯比动力更重要

🧠 Learn the 2 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 2 个新词

Building reliable habits is often more effective than depending on motivation to achieve long-term goals. 培养可靠的习惯通常比依赖一时的动力更有助于实现长期目标。

Many people start a new activity with great enthusiasm. They buy running shoes, choose a language course, or plan to read every evening. For the first few days, everything seems exciting. Then work becomes busy, the weather changes, or they simply feel tired. Their original motivation begins to disappear, and the new activity slowly becomes less important.

许多人带着极大的热情开始一项新活动。他们购买跑鞋、选择语言课程，或者计划每天晚上阅读。在最初的几天里，一切似乎都令人兴奋。后来，工作变得忙碌、天气发生变化，或者他们只是感到疲倦。最初的动力开始消失，新活动也逐渐变得不那么重要。

This is why habits deserve more attention than motivation. Motivation is a feeling, and feelings naturally change. A habit, by contrast, is an action that becomes familiar through repetition. If someone decides to read for ten minutes after dinner every day, the activity may eventually require less effort. The person no longer needs to make the same decision each evening.

这就是为什么习惯比动力更值得关注。动力是一种感觉，而感觉自然会发生变化。相比之下，习惯是通过重复而变得熟悉的行动。如果有人决定每天晚饭后阅读十分钟，这项活动最终可能不再需要那么多努力。这个人也不再需要每晚重新做出同样的决定。

Of course, motivation still has value. It can help people begin, especially when a goal feels difficult. However, depending on motivation alone tends to create an unstable pattern. A more practical strategy is to make the desired action easy to start. Put a book beside the bed, prepare exercise clothes in advance, or set a regular time for study. Small arrangements can make a big difference.

当然，动力仍然有价值。尤其是在目标显得困难时，它能够帮助人们迈出第一步。然而，仅仅依赖动力往往会造成不稳定的模式。更实际的策略是让想要进行的行动更容易开始。把书放在床边、提前准备好运动服，或者为学习设定固定时间。小小的安排也可能带来很大的变化。

Another important point is that missing one day does not mean failure. People sometimes become too strict with themselves and give up after making a small mistake. While consistency matters, perfection is unnecessary. If a person misses an exercise session, they can simply return to the routine the next day. Long-term progress usually comes from repeated effort rather than a perfect record.

另一个重要的观点是，漏掉一天并不意味着失败。有些人对自己过于严格，一旦犯了小错误就选择放弃。虽然坚持很重要，但完美并非必要。如果一个人错过了一次锻炼，可以在第二天简单地重新开始。长期进步通常来自反复努力，而不是毫无缺漏的记录。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

A habit, by contrast, is an action that becomes familiar through repetition.
By contrast is a linking expression that introduces an opposing idea, while that introduces a defining relative clause describing action.
By contrast是表示对比的连接表达；that引导限定性定语从句，修饰action并进一步解释习惯的含义。
Long-term progress usually comes from repeated effort rather than a perfect record.
Rather than compares two possible sources of progress and emphasizes repeated effort; repeated is a past participle used as an adjective.
Rather than用于比较两种可能的进步来源，并强调反复努力；repeated是过去分词，在句中作形容词修饰effort。
