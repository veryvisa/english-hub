📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Open the full explanation: 5 points展开细讲：5 段讲解

The verbs look, sound, feel, smell, and taste can function as linking verbs when they describe perception. They are usually followed by adjectives rather than adverbs: The fabric feels soft, not softly. This is an important pattern because Chinese learners may assume that every verb describing an action needs an adverb.

look、sound、feel、smell 和 taste 在描述感官印象时可以作连系动词，后面通常接形容词，而不是副词。例如：The fabric feels soft（这种布料摸起来很柔软），而不是 softly。中文学习者容易认为动词后面都应该使用副词，因此需要特别注意这一结构。

The five senses are not always used literally. Look and sound often describe impressions or opinions, while feel can describe both physical sensations and emotions. For example, That sounds reasonable expresses an evaluation rather than an actual hearing experience.

五种感官动词并不总是用于字面意义。look 和 sound 经常用来描述印象或看法，而 feel 既可以描述身体感觉，也可以表达情绪。例如，That sounds reasonable 表示某个想法听起来合理，并不是真的在描述听觉体验。

Notice the difference between feel and touch. Feel often describes the sensation someone experiences, while touch emphasizes the physical action of making contact. You can say The surface feels rough and Please touch the surface, but these sentences focus on different things.

注意 feel 和 touch 的区别。feel 通常描述某人感受到的触觉，而 touch 更强调用身体接触某物的动作。The surface feels rough（表面摸起来很粗糙）描述感受，Please touch the surface（请触摸这个表面）则强调动作。

Some adjectives describe the quality of a sensation, while others describe its intensity. Words such as smooth, rough, bitter, and sweet identify qualities; extremely, slightly, and surprisingly help express degree or unexpected impressions. Combining them makes descriptions more precise.

有些形容词描述感觉的性质，有些词则描述感觉的程度。smooth、rough、bitter 和 sweet 用来说明具体性质；extremely、slightly 和 surprisingly 等词可以表达程度或出乎意料的印象。将它们组合起来能够使描述更加准确。

Be careful with the difference between seem and look. Look usually refers to visual appearance, while seem expresses a broader impression based on available information. A person may look tired because of their appearance, but they may seem worried because of their words or behaviour.

注意 seem 和 look 的区别。look 通常指视觉上呈现的样子，而 seem 表达根据现有信息形成的整体印象。一个人可能因为外表而看起来疲惫（look tired），也可能因为言语或行为而显得担忧（seem worried）。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ The soup tastes deliciously.
✓ The soup tastes delicious.
Taste is a linking verb in this sentence, so it takes an adjective describing the soup rather than an adverb describing an action.
这里的 taste 是连系动词，后面需要用形容词 delicious 描述汤的味道，而不是用副词 deliciously 修饰动作。
✗ The fabric feels softly.
✓ The fabric feels soft.
Feel describes the physical quality experienced through touch. Use the adjective soft after this linking verb.
feel 在这里描述通过触觉感受到的性质，是连系动词，因此应该使用形容词 soft，而不是副词 softly。
✗ This perfume smells strongly.
✓ This perfume smells strong.
When smell describes the perfume's characteristic, it is a linking verb followed by an adjective. Smells strongly would describe the manner of smelling in a different grammatical structure.
当 smell 描述香水本身的气味特征时，它是连系动词，后面应接形容词 strong。smells strongly 在其他结构中可以表示以强烈的方式闻某物，但不是这里想表达的意思。
✗ You look beautifully in that dress.
✓ You look beautiful in that dress.
Look means appear in this sentence, so it needs the adjective beautiful. Look at something is a different structure in which look describes an action.
这里的 look 表示看起来，属于连系动词，后面应该接形容词 beautiful。look at something 则是另一种结构，表示看某物。
✗ Your idea sounds interestingly.
✓ Your idea sounds interesting.
Sound describes the impression created by an idea, so an adjective is required. Interesting describes the idea, while interestingly describes the manner in which someone does something.
sound 在这里表示某个想法给人的听觉印象或整体印象，因此需要形容词 interesting。interesting 描述想法本身，interestingly 则表示以有趣的方式。
✗ I touched soft the surface.
✓ I touched the soft surface.
Touch is an action verb here, so the adjective soft must describe the noun surface. Alternatively, you can say The surface feels soft.
这里的 touch 是表示触摸动作的实义动词，形容词 soft 应放在名词 surface 前面。也可以说 The surface feels soft，使用 feel 描述触感。
