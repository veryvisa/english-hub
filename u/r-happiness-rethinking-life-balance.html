Is Balance Possible? 生活真的能平衡吗？

🧠 Learn the 3 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 3 个新词

A fulfilling life requires flexible priorities rather than a perfect and permanent division between work, family, leisure, and personal development. 充实的人生需要灵活调整优先事项，而不是在工作、家庭、休闲和个人成长之间追求完美且永久不变的分配。

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Maya: I have been thinking about what people mean when they talk about work-life balance. The phrase sounds attractive, but I wonder whether it creates unrealistic expectations. Most people have periods when work demands more attention and other periods when family or personal needs become more important. Perhaps the real challenge is learning how to adjust without losing sight of what makes life meaningful.

玛雅：我一直在思考人们谈论工作与生活平衡时究竟是什么意思。这个说法听起来很吸引人，但我怀疑它是否制造了不切实际的期待。大多数人都会经历一些工作需要更多关注的时期，也会经历家庭或个人需求变得更加重要的时期。也许真正的挑战在于学会调整，同时不忽视让生活变得有意义的东西。

Daniel: I agree to some extent. However, I think the problem is not the idea of balance itself but the assumption that balance means giving everything equal attention. That is rarely realistic. When someone has a demanding project, work may temporarily take priority. The important thing is whether that arrangement remains sustainable and whether the person can eventually make room for rest, relationships, and personal interests.

丹尼尔：在某种程度上我同意。然而，我认为问题不在于平衡这个概念本身，而在于人们假设平衡意味着给予所有事情同等的关注。这很少符合现实。当一个人有一项要求很高的项目时，工作可能暂时成为优先事项。重要的是这种安排是否能够持续，以及这个人最终能否为休息、人际关系和个人兴趣留出空间。

Maya: That makes sense. I also think people underestimate the value of small boundaries. For example, deciding not to check work messages during dinner can protect a meaningful part of the day. It may seem insignificant, but repeated choices gradually shape our routines. While flexible working arrangements can help, they do not automatically create a healthier lifestyle. We still need to decide when to stop.

玛雅：这很有道理。我还认为人们低估了小小的界限所具有的价值。例如，决定吃晚饭时不查看工作信息，可以保护一天中一段有意义的时间。这看起来可能微不足道，但反复做出的选择会逐渐塑造我们的日常习惯。虽然灵活的工作安排有所帮助，但它们不会自动创造更健康的生活方式。我们仍然需要决定什么时候停止工作。

Daniel: Exactly. I would add that personal development matters too. Reading, exercise, learning new skills, and creative activities can give people a sense of purpose beyond their professional identity. Of course, nobody can maintain an ideal routine every day. On the whole, a fulfilling life is more likely to emerge from thoughtful adjustments than from following a perfect timetable. Balance should be a direction, not a strict formula.

丹尼尔：正是如此。我还想补充一点，个人成长同样重要。阅读、锻炼、学习新技能和创造性活动，都能让人们在职业身份之外找到目标感。当然，没有人能够每天维持理想的日常安排。总的来说，充实的生活更可能来自深思熟虑的调整，而不是遵循一张完美的时间表。平衡应该是一种方向，而不是一套严格的公式。

/səˈsteɪnəbəl/ adj.
可持续的；能够长期维持的
a sustainable routine; a sustainable lifestyle
Walking to work every day became a sustainable habit for him. 每天步行上班逐渐成为他能够长期坚持的习惯。
/teɪk praɪˈɒrəti/ phr. v.
优先考虑；成为首要事项
health takes priority; family takes priority
During the emergency, everyone's safety had to take priority. 紧急情况下，每个人的安全都必须放在首位。
/ˈfleksəbəl/ adj.
灵活的；可调整的

A flexible approach allows students to learn at their own pace. 灵活的方法让学生能够按照自己的节奏学习。
/əˈdʒʌstmənt/ n.
调整；适应
make an adjustment; gradual adjustment
A small adjustment to her schedule improved her daily routine. 对日程做出的小调整改善了她的日常生活。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

The important thing is whether that arrangement remains sustainable and whether the person can eventually make room for rest, relationships, and personal interests.
The linking word whether introduces two coordinated indirect questions functioning as the complement of is.
连接词 whether 引导两个并列的间接疑问从句，共同充当 is 后面的补语。
On the whole, a fulfilling life is more likely to emerge from thoughtful adjustments than from following a perfect timetable.
The expression more likely to ... than establishes a comparison, while the preposition from is followed by the gerund forms adjustments and following.
more likely to ... than 构成比较结构，而介词 from 后面分别接名词 adjustments 和动名词 following。
