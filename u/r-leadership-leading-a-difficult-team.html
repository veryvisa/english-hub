Dear Reader: How Do I Lead a Team That Resists Me? 亲爱的读者：如何带领一个抗拒我的团队？

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

A reader writes that she has recently taken charge of a team whose members seem indifferent to her ideas. Meetings are quiet, deadlines slip, and she is beginning to suspect that people are quietly working against her. She asks whether she should be firmer. My answer is that firmness may help eventually, but it is rarely the right first move.

一位读者来信说，她最近接手了一个团队，成员们似乎对她的想法漠不关心。会议安静，截止日期一再拖延，她开始怀疑大家在暗中与她作对。她问自己是否应该更强硬。我的回答是，强硬或许最终有帮助，但很少是正确的第一步。

Resistance usually has a reason, and the reason is often not about you. Perhaps the team has seen several leaders come and go, or perhaps earlier changes were announced and then abandoned. While it is tempting to read silence as hostility, it is just as likely to signal caution. Start by asking each person, privately, what they would change if they could, and then listen without defending anything.

抗拒通常有其原因，而这个原因往往与你无关。也许团队见过几任领导来来去去，也许早先的变革被宣布后又被放弃。虽然把沉默解读为敌意很诱人，但它同样可能意味着谨慎。先私下问每个人，如果可以的话他们想改变什么，然后不为任何事辩解地倾听。

Next, look for a small, visible win. Choose a problem the team cares about and has the power to fix, and make sure they get the credit when it is solved. Nothing builds trust faster than evidence that you do what you say. On the whole, people are more willing to follow a leader who has proved useful than one who has simply asked to be followed.

接下来，寻找一个小而可见的胜利。选择一个团队关心且有能力解决的问题，并确保问题解决后功劳归他们。没有什么比你言出必行的证据更能迅速建立信任。总体而言，人们更愿意追随一位已被证明有用的领导者，而非一位仅仅要求别人追随的领导者。

Finally, be patient with yourself. Turning around a sceptical team can take months, and setbacks are almost certain along the way. That said, if after a sincere effort nothing changes, it may be time to seek advice from someone senior. Asking for help is not an admission of failure; it is, by and large, what capable leaders do.

最后，对自己要有耐心。扭转一个持怀疑态度的团队可能需要数月，期间几乎必然会有挫折。话虽如此，如果真诚努力之后仍无改变，也许是时候向资深人士寻求建议了。求助并非承认失败；总的来说，这正是能干的领导者会做的事。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

While it is tempting to read silence as hostility, it is just as likely to signal caution.
’While’ introduces a concession with the dummy subject ‘it’ and an infinitive, and ‘just as likely to’ is a hedged comparison.
’While’ 引出让步，使用形式主语 ‘it’ 加不定式，'just as likely to' 是限定性比较。
Nothing builds trust faster than evidence that you do what you say.
A negative subject ‘nothing’ is followed by a comparative, and ‘that you do what you say’ is a noun clause in apposition to ‘evidence’.
否定主语 ‘nothing’ 后接比较级，'that you do what you say' 是 ‘evidence’ 的同位语从句。
