A Chat About Free Time 一场关于空闲时间的聊天

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Anna: Hi, Ben! You look cheerful today. Did something good happen? Ben: Yes, actually. I joined a running club last month, and this morning I finished my first five-kilometre run. I did not stop once!

安娜：嗨，本！你今天看起来很开心。有什么好事吗？本：是的，确实有。上个月我加入了一个跑步俱乐部，今天早上我跑完了人生第一个五公里。我一次都没停！

Anna: Wow, well done! I thought you hated sport. Ben: I did, to be honest. But I wanted to take up something that would get me out of the flat after work. Now I look forward to Tuesday and Thursday evenings. Anna: That sounds like a healthy habit.

安娜：哇，太棒了！我还以为你讨厌运动呢。本：说实话，我以前确实讨厌。但我想找件事做，让自己下班后能走出家门。现在我很期待每周二和周四的晚上。安娜：听起来是个健康的好习惯。

Anna: Is it difficult? I tried running once, but I gave up after ten minutes. Ben: It was hard at first. I could hardly finish one kilometre. But the group is friendly, and nobody minds if you are slow. After a few weeks, I started to get better at it. We even stop for tea at the end. Anna: That is encouraging.

安娜：难吗？我试过一次跑步，跑了十分钟就放弃了。本：一开始很难，我连一公里都跑不完。但小组里的人很友好，没人在意你跑得慢。几周之后，我开始越跑越好。我们结束时还会一起去喝茶。安娜：这很鼓舞人。

Ben: What about you? What do you do in your free time? Anna: Mostly I watch series and scroll on my phone, and I never feel relaxed afterwards. I would like to try painting, but I have no idea where to start. Ben: I know that feeling. Screens are easy, but they rarely make us feel better.

本：说说你吧？你空闲时间都做些什么？安娜：大多是看剧、刷手机，之后总觉得并不放松。我想试试画画，可是完全不知道从哪儿开始。本：我懂那种感觉。屏幕让人轻松，却很少让我们感觉更好。

Ben: Listen, my neighbour teaches a beginners’ painting class at the community centre on Saturdays. Why don’t you come along? Anna: Really? That sounds perfect. It would help me unwind after a long week. Ben: Great. I will send you the address tonight, and afterwards we can have coffee. Anna: It is a deal!

本：听着，我邻居每周六在社区中心教一个初学者绘画班。你要不要一起去？安娜：真的吗？听起来太棒了。这能帮我在漫长的一周之后放松下来。本：太好了。我今晚把地址发给你，之后我们可以一起喝杯咖啡。安娜：一言为定！

Words词

/ˈtʃɪəfʊl/ adj.
开朗的，高兴的
a cheerful smile; look cheerful
The waiter was cheerful even on a busy night. 即使在忙碌的夜晚，服务员也很开朗。
/ˈhɑːdli/ adv.
几乎不
can hardly wait; hardly ever
I could hardly hear the teacher at the back of the room. 我坐在教室后面，几乎听不见老师说话。
/ɡeɪv ʌp/ phr. v.
放弃（give up 的过去式）
give up easily; give up after a few tries
She gave up the piano when she moved abroad. 她搬到国外时放弃了钢琴。
/lʊk ˈfɔːwəd tuː/ phr.
期待，盼望
look forward to the weekend; look forward to seeing you
I look forward to Friday every week. 我每周都盼着周五。
/ʌnˈwaɪnd/ v.
放松
unwind at home; unwind with friends
We often unwind by cooking together. 我们常常一起做饭来放松。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

But I wanted to take up something that would get me out of the flat after work.
’That would get me out of the flat’ is a defining relative clause describing ‘something’, and ‘would’ shows the result he hoped for.
that would get me out of the flat 是修饰 something 的限制性定语从句，would 表示他所期望的结果。
I could hardly finish one kilometre.
’Hardly’ means almost not, so the sentence is already negative and does not need ‘not’.
hardly 的意思是“几乎不”，句子本身已是否定含义，不需要再加 not。
