The First Reader Is Not a Person 第一个读你简历的不是人

🧠 Learn the 5 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 5 个新词

Read a news report on screening software and see how the present perfect and the past simple share the work in news writing. 读一篇关于简历筛选软件的新闻，看新闻写作里现在完成时和一般过去时怎样分工。

📖 Key card知识卡
Many applications are first read by software that looks for a match with the posting, so write for two readers: the machine first, then the person.很多申请先由软件读，它找的是和启事的匹配度；所以要写给两个读者：先是机器，再是人。

Read and listen读与听

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Job seekers who have sent dozens of applications without a single reply may be right to suspect that no person has read them. A growing number of employers now use software, often called an applicant tracking system, to screen applications before a recruiter sees them, according to career advisers and hiring staff interviewed for this report.

投了几十份申请却一封回音都没有的求职者，怀疑根本没人读过自己的材料，可能是对的。据接受本报采访的职业顾问和招聘人员说，越来越多的雇主用一种通常叫「求职者追踪系统」的软件，在招聘人员看到申请之前先做筛选。

The software reads the résumé and the cover letter, compares them with the job posting, and ranks each applicant. Those near the top reach a human; many of the rest are never opened. "The machine is not looking for talent. It is looking for a match," said a recruiter at a mid-sized firm.

软件读简历和求职信，拿它们和招聘启事比对，再给每位申请人排名。排在前面的才到人手里，其余的很多根本没被打开。「机器找的不是人才，是匹配。」一家中型公司的招聘人员说。

Advisers say the most common problems are simple. Tables, columns and images can confuse the software, so a plain document is safer, and applicants should attach the file type the posting asks for. Wording matters too: if a posting asks for "project management", "led projects" may not count as a match. Advisers recommend using the employer's exact nouns where they are true, such as "reliability" or "flexibility", and showing each improvement you made with a real number.

顾问们说，最常见的问题其实很简单。表格、分栏和图片会把软件弄糊涂，所以朴素的文档更稳妥；启事要什么文件格式，申请人就附什么格式。措辞也要紧：启事写的是「项目管理」，你写「带过项目」可能就不算匹配。顾问建议，在属实的前提下直接用雇主原话里的名词，比如「可靠性」「灵活性」，并用真实数字说明你带来的每一项改进。

Newcomers are hit hardest, advisers say, because the software has no awareness of foreign job titles or credentials. One adviser who has worked with newcomers for a decade said her clients had started to list volunteer roles and local courses near the top of the résumé, so that the software would find some local experience in the first lines. Some employers say they have accepted the criticism and now ask a person to check a sample of rejected applications every month.

顾问们说，新移民受的冲击最大，因为软件不认识外国的职位名称和资质。一位和新移民打了十年交道的顾问说，她的客户已经开始把义工经历和本地课程列在简历靠前的位置，好让软件在头几行就找到一点本地经验。一些雇主表示，他们已经接受了这些批评，现在每月让专人抽查一部分被拒的申请。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

A growing number of employers now use software, often called an applicant tracking system, to screen applications before a recruiter sees them, according to career advisers and hiring staff interviewed for this report.
The core is employers use software to screen applications. often called… is a short passive phrase; interviewed for this report describes the staff.
主干是 employers use software to screen applications。often called… 是插入的被动短语，interviewed for this report 修饰前面的人员。
One adviser who has worked with newcomers for a decade said her clients had started to list volunteer roles and local courses near the top of the résumé, so that the software would find some local experience in the first lines.
has worked (up to now) inside a past-tense report; had started is reported speech moved one step back.
在过去时的转述里，has worked 表示一直到现在；had started 是间接引语往前推一步。
