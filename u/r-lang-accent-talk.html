Being Understood Is Enough 被听懂就够了

🧠 Learn the 5 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 5 个新词

Read a short graduation talk about living with an accent, and hear the present perfect carry nine years of experience into one evening. 读一篇毕业典礼上关于口音的短讲稿，听现在完成时怎样把九年的经历带进一个晚上。

B2 · CELPIP 7–8⏱ 20 minG02 Present perfect and past simple confused现在完成时与一般过去时混用P10 Flat or wrong intonation; the important word is not stressed语调平，重点词不突出

📖 Key card知识卡
Being understood matters more than sounding native: stress the key words, slow down and link your words.被听懂比听起来像母语者更重要：重读关键词、放慢速度、把词连起来。

Read and listen读与听

275 words
Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Good evening, everyone. When my teacher asked me to speak tonight, my first thought was, "Not with this accent." I have lived in Canada for nine years, and for most of that time I have been embarrassed every time I opened my mouth. So I thought I would talk about that.

大家晚上好。老师请我今晚发言时，我的第一反应是：「就我这口音？算了吧。」我在加拿大住了九年，大部分时间里，我每次开口都觉得难为情。所以我想就讲讲这个。

When I arrived, I spent hours trying to sound like a native speaker. I watched videos about the "th" sound and recorded myself again and again. It helped a little. But I also became so self-conscious that I stopped speaking in meetings. I was so busy listening to my own accent that I forgot what I wanted to say.

刚来的时候，我花了好几个小时想让自己听起来像母语者。我看讲 th 发音的视频，一遍又一遍给自己录音。有一点帮助。可我也变得特别在意自己的样子，以至于在会上不再发言。我忙着听自己的口音，忘了自己想说什么。

Three years ago, a colleague told me something I have never forgotten. She said, "I don't care about your accent. I care when you speak too fast and I lose the important word." That changed my goal. I stopped trying to lose my accent and started working on being clear: stressing the key words, slowing down, linking words together. People understood me better within a few months.

三年前，一位同事跟我说了一句话，我至今没忘。她说：「我不在乎你的口音。我在乎的是你说得太快、我听丢了关键的那个词。」这改变了我的目标。我不再想去掉口音，开始练习说清楚：重读关键词、放慢速度、把词连起来。几个月之内，大家就更听得懂我了。

Since then I have given presentations, chaired meetings and once even made a complaint to a landlord on the phone, which, as some of you know, is the real test. My accent has not disappeared. It has simply stopped getting in the way. My English is still imperfect, and that is fine.

从那以后，我做过演讲，主持过会议，有一次甚至在电话里向房东投诉——在座有些人知道，那才是真正的考验。我的口音没有消失，它只是不再碍事了。我的英语仍然不完美，这也没关系。

So if you are sitting there worrying about how you sound, here is what I have learned. Being understood matters more than sounding native. Your accent tells people where you have been. It is not something to hide; it is part of your story. Thank you.

所以，如果你正坐在那儿担心自己的发音，这是我学到的：被听懂比听起来像母语者更重要。你的口音告诉别人你从哪里走来。它不是需要藏起来的东西，它是你故事的一部分。谢谢大家。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

I have lived in Canada for nine years, and for most of that time I have been embarrassed every time I opened my mouth.
have lived … for nine years: started in the past, still true now. every time I opened stays past because each time is a finished event inside that period.
have lived … for nine years：从过去开始、现在仍然如此。every time I opened 用过去式，因为每一次都是这段时间里已完成的事件。
Three years ago, a colleague told me something I have never forgotten.
Two time frames in one sentence: three years ago … told (finished time) and have never forgotten (from then until now).
一句话里两个时间框：three years ago … told 是已结束的时间；have never forgotten 是从那时到现在。

Prepare a 60-second talk: "How my English has changed since I arrived". Use at least three present perfect sentences (have lived, have learned, have never…) and two past simple sentences with a time (three years ago, when I arrived). Record it once and listen for your key-word stress.
准备一段 60 秒的发言：「我来了以后英语有什么变化」。至少用三句现在完成时（have lived、have learned、have never…）和两句带时间的一般过去时（three years ago、when I arrived）。录一遍，听自己有没有重读关键词。
