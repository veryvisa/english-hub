Five Things I Tell People at the Legal Clinic 我在法律诊所常对人说的五件事

🧠 Learn the 3 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 3 个新词

Read a volunteer's talk at a free legal clinic, and learn phrasal verbs that keep a serious talk plain and friendly, plus present perfect for experience. 读一位免费法律诊所志愿者的讲话，学会让严肃话题讲得平实亲切的短语动词，以及表达经验的现在完成时。

B2 · CELPIP 7–8⏱ 20 minV06 Register: too formal, too casual, or too blunt语域不合：太正式、太随意或太冲V12 Hedging is missing; claims sound absolute不会留余地，话说得太满

📖 Key card知识卡
Deadlines, records and reading before you sign matter more than knowing the law; ask for help early, including help with language.期限、记录和签字前先读，比懂法律更重要；早点求助，包括语言上的帮助。

Read and listen读与听

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Good evening. I have volunteered at our free legal clinic for six years. We cannot take on every case, but we have met thousands of people, and the same few problems come up again and again. Here are five things I wish everyone knew before they walked through our door.

晚上好。我在我们的免费法律诊所做志愿者六年了。我们没法接下每一个案子，但见过成千上万的人，同样的几个问题一再出现。下面五件事，我希望每个人在走进我们门之前就知道。

One: deadlines matter more than almost anything. Almost every legal process has a limitation period, and people lose good cases because they put off dealing with a letter they did not understand. If you get a letter, a ticket or a notice, read it the day it arrives and write the deadline on your calendar.

第一：期限比几乎任何事都重要。几乎每个法律程序都有时效，有人因为把一封看不懂的信一拖再拖，输掉了本来能赢的案子。收到信、罚单或通知，到的当天就读，把期限写在日历上。

Two: keep records. Dates, names, copies of letters, photos. When we help someone sort out a dispute between a tenant and a landlord, or a worker and an employer, the side with better notes usually has the stronger position.

第二：留记录。日期、名字、信件副本、照片。我们帮人解决房客和房东、员工和雇主之间的纠纷时，记录更好的一方通常处于更有利的位置。

Three: a signature is binding. Do not sign anything you have not read and understood, and remember that you can usually take a document home and read it first. Four: you can ask for help with language. Many courts and tribunals provide interpreters, so ask if you need one, and bring up the question early, not on the morning of the hearing.

第三：签了字就有约束力。没读过、没读懂的东西别签；记住，你通常可以把文件带回家先读。第四：你可以要求语言上的帮助。很多法院和审裁处提供口译，需要就提出来，而且要早提，别等到开庭那天早上。

Five: using the rules does not make you a troublemaker. Being a witness, disputing a fine or asking for your deposit back is what the rules are for. None of this replaces advice about your own situation; that is what the clinic is for. Come early, bring your papers, and we will deal with it together.

第五：按规则办事不会让你变成麻烦制造者。出面作证、对罚款提出异议、要回押金，规则本来就是为这些设的。这些都代替不了针对你个人情况的建议，那正是诊所要做的。早点来，带上你的文件，我们一起处理。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

Almost every legal process has a limitation period, and people lose good cases because they put off dealing with a letter they did not understand.
put off + -ing (put off dealing), not put off to deal. a letter they did not understand is a relative clause without that.
put off 后接 -ing（put off dealing），不说 put off to deal。a letter they did not understand 是省略 that 的定语从句。
We cannot take on every case, but we have met thousands of people, and the same few problems come up again and again.
take on: accept work or responsibility. have met (present perfect) for experience up to now; come up (present simple) for something that keeps happening.
take on：接下工作或责任。have met（现在完成时）表示到现在的经验；come up（一般现在时）表示反复发生的事。

Give a two-minute talk to new arrivals called "Three things I wish I had known about letters and forms". Use the present perfect once for your own experience and at least three of put off, sort out, bring up, deal with, take on. Keep it general: no advice on anyone's own case.
给新移民做两分钟讲话，题目是「关于信件和表格，我希望早知道的三件事」。用一次现在完成时讲自己的经验，并用上 put off、sort out、bring up、deal with、take on 中至少三个。只讲一般做法，不给任何个案建议。
