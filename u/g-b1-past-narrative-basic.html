Past simple and past continuous in stories 叙事里的一般过去与过去进行

Choose between past simple and past continuous so that a story clearly separates the events that move it forward from the background and interrupted actions. 能在叙事中正确选用一般过去和过去进行时，区分推动情节的事件、背景场景和被打断的动作。

B1 · CELPIP 5–6⏱ 25 minG08 Tense not kept consistent across a story叙事时态前后不一

👀 Look first: what changes?先看对比：差在哪里

Read the sentences and decide what each one tells you. Then open the notes.先读这几句，自己判断各自传达了什么，再点开看注解。

When I opened the door, the dog ran out. 我一开门，狗就跑了出去。
Two events in sequence: the dog ran out because the door opened.两个事件按先后顺序发生：门开了，狗才跑出去。
When I was opening the door, the dog ran out. 我正在开门的时候，狗跑了出去。
The opening was still in progress; the dog ran out in the middle of it, maybe before the door was fully open.开门这个动作还在进行中，狗在途中跑了出去，门也许还没完全打开。
When I had opened the door, the dog ran out. 我把门打开之后，狗跑了出去。
The opening was completed first; the focus is on the finished action before the next event.开门先完成，重点在于这个动作结束之后才发生下一件事。

📏 The rule规则

📏 The rule in one line规则一句话
Past simple moves the story forward one finished event at a time, while past continuous holds a moment still to show the scene or the action that was in progress when something happened.一般过去时让故事一件接一件地向前推进，过去进行时则把某一刻“定格”，用来交代场景或事件发生时正在进行的动作。

📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Open the full explanation: 6 points, 24 examples展开细讲：6 段讲解、24 个例句

Think of the past simple as the spine of the story and the past continuous as the scenery. 'He opened the door, saw the mess and called the police' is a chain: each verb happens after the previous one. 'It was raining and a dog was barking' does not move time forward; it paints the moment. If you can ask 'and then what happened?', you need the past simple.

可以把一般过去看作故事的主线，把过去进行看作布景。“He opened the door, saw the mess and called the police”是一条链：每个动词都发生在前一个之后。“It was raining and a dog was barking”并不推动时间，只是描绘那一刻。如果能追问“然后呢？”，就用一般过去。

The classic pattern is a long background action interrupted by a short event: 'I was walking home when I saw an accident.' The continuous verb says the action had already started and was still going on; the simple verb is the new event that cuts in. Reverse them and the meaning changes completely: 'I walked home when I saw an accident' sounds like a strange sequence, as if the accident made you start walking.

经典结构是一个较长的背景动作被一个短暂事件打断：“I was walking home when I saw an accident.”进行时说明动作已经开始且仍在继续，简单过去时则是突然插入的新事件。如果把两者对调，意思就全变了：“I walked home when I saw an accident”听起来像是看到事故之后才开始走路，顺序很奇怪。

With 'when', the tense of the clause tells the listener the order. 'When I arrived, she cooked' suggests she began cooking after I came; 'When I arrived, she was cooking' means the cooking was already under way. Learners who only think about 'it happened in the past' miss this signal, so choose the tense according to whether the second action began before or after the point you describe.

用 when 引导时，从句的时态会告诉听者先后顺序。“When I arrived, she cooked”暗示我到了之后她才开始做饭；“When I arrived, she was cooking”则表示她早就在做了。只想着“这是过去的事”的学习者会漏掉这个信号，所以要看第二个动作是在所描述的时间点之前还是之后开始的。

The past continuous is about duration around a moment, not about how long something took in total. A completed action with a result needs the past simple even if it lasted hours: 'I wrote the report last night' means it is done, while 'I was writing the report last night' only says what I was busy with and leaves open whether I finished. Use the continuous when the unfinished, in-progress picture matters.

过去进行时强调的是围绕某一时刻的“进行中”，而不是整个动作花了多长时间。已经完成并有结果的动作，即使持续了几个小时，也要用一般过去：“I wrote the report last night”表示写完了，“I was writing the report last night”只说明当时在忙这件事，并没有说完成与否。需要强调未完成的进行画面时才用进行时。

Two or more continuous verbs joined by 'while' show actions running in parallel, usually as scene-setting: 'While I was cooking, the kids were doing their homework.' Do not use the continuous for every verb in a story, though. Short single events, repeated habits and state verbs such as 'know', 'want', 'seem' or 'be' normally stay in the simple form, so 'I knew the answer' is natural and 'I was knowing the answer' is not.

用 while 连接两个进行时，表示两个动作同时进行，常用来铺陈场景：“While I was cooking, the kids were doing their homework.”但不要把故事里每个动词都用进行时。短暂的单一事件、重复的习惯，以及 know、want、seem、be 这类状态动词通常保持简单形式，所以“I knew the answer”自然，“I was knowing the answer”则不对。

Chinese speakers often rely on aspect particles and time words to show whether an action was ongoing or finished, but English forces you to mark it on the verb every time. That is why storytelling can feel flat or confusing when every verb is in the past simple: the listener cannot tell what was background and what was foreground. Before you write a verb, ask yourself whether it is a step in the plot or a picture of the situation.

汉语习惯靠体标记和时间词来表示动作是在进行还是已完成，而英语每次都必须在动词上标出来。所以如果讲故事时每个动词都用一般过去，听者就分不清哪些是背景、哪些是前景，叙述会显得平淡甚至混乱。落笔写动词之前，先问自己：这是情节中的一步，还是对当时情境的一幅画面？

Examples例句

  • I was walking home when it started to rain. 我走路回家的时候开始下雨了。
  • She was talking on the phone when the doorbell rang. 她正在打电话，这时门铃响了。
  • While we were waiting for the bus, a man asked us for directions. 我们等公交车的时候，有个人向我们问路。
  • The sun was shining and the children were playing in the garden when the storm hit. 太阳照耀着，孩子们在花园里玩耍，这时暴风雨来了。
  • He opened the window, looked outside and shouted for help. 他打开窗户，朝外看了看，大声呼救。
  • When I got to the station, the train was leaving. 我到车站的时候，火车正要开走。
  • When I got to the station, the train left. 我到车站之后，火车就开走了。
  • I was reading in bed when I heard a strange noise downstairs. 我正躺在床上看书，忽然听到楼下有奇怪的声音。
  • They were having dinner when the power went out. 他们正在吃晚饭时停电了。
  • At eight o'clock last night, I was watching a documentary. 昨晚八点，我正在看一部纪录片。
  • My sister was studying while I was cooking dinner. 我做晚饭的时候，我姐姐在学习。
  • As she was crossing the street, a cyclist almost hit her. 她过马路时，一个骑自行车的人差点撞到她。
  • I dropped my phone while I was getting off the bus. 我下公交车的时候把手机摔了。
  • He was driving too fast, so he couldn't stop in time. 他当时开得太快，所以没能及时刹住车。
  • We were all laughing when the teacher walked in. 老师走进来的时候，我们都在笑。
  • She knew something was wrong because nobody was answering the door. 她知道出事了，因为没人来开门。
  • I lost my keys yesterday, so I called my landlord and waited outside. 我昨天丢了钥匙，所以给房东打了电话，然后在外面等着。
  • The shop was closing when we finally found it. 我们终于找到那家店的时候，它正要关门。
  • While he was working abroad, his parents sold the old house. 他在国外工作期间，他父母把老房子卖了。
  • It was getting dark, and the wind was getting stronger, so we decided to turn back. 天越来越黑，风也越来越大，所以我们决定折返。

  • I was just leaving the office when my boss called me back. 我刚要离开办公室，老板就把我叫了回去。
  • When the lights went out, everyone stopped talking. 灯一灭，所有人都不说话了。
  • He fell asleep while he was watching the match. 他看比赛的时候睡着了。

More pairs: same idea, different choice更多对比句组

I wrote the email while the kids slept. 孩子们睡觉的时候，我把邮件写完了。
A completed email; the sleeping is treated as a single block of time.邮件已经写完，孩子睡觉被看作一整段时间。
I was writing the email while the kids were sleeping. 我在写邮件，孩子们在睡觉。
Two background actions in progress together; scene-setting, with no promise that the email was finished.两个背景动作同时进行，是在铺陈场景，并不保证邮件写完了。
I was writing the email when the kids woke up. 我正在写邮件时孩子们醒了。
My writing was interrupted by the kids waking; the waking is the new event.我的写作被孩子们醒来打断，醒来是新发生的事件。
At six o'clock I had dinner. 六点的时候我吃了晚饭。
Dinner started at six, so the time marks the beginning of the event.晚饭是六点开始吃的，时间点标志着事件的开始。
At six o'clock I was having dinner. 六点的时候我正在吃晚饭。
Dinner was already in progress at six; the time is a snapshot in the middle of it.六点时晚饭已经在吃了，这个时间点是进行过程中的一个截面。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ I walked home when I saw an accident.
✓ I was walking home when I saw an accident.
The walking was already in progress and was interrupted by a short event, so it needs the past continuous. With the past simple, the sentence suggests you started walking after, or because of, seeing the accident.
走路这个动作在看到事故之前已经在进行，被一个短暂事件打断，所以要用过去进行时。用一般过去的话，句子会让人理解成看到事故之后（或因为看到事故）才开始走路。
✗ I was knowing the answer, but I didn't say anything.
✓ I knew the answer, but I didn't say anything.
State verbs like know, want, like and believe describe a condition, not an action in progress, so they normally stay in the simple form even in a scene-setting sentence.
know、want、like、believe 这类状态动词描述的是一种状态而不是进行中的动作，即使在铺陈场景的句子里通常也用简单形式。
✗ While I did my homework, my brother was playing games.
✓ While I was doing my homework, my brother was playing games.
When 'while' introduces two actions running in parallel with no special context, both parts normally use the continuous to keep the picture balanced. Putting the simple form on one side can sound like that action finished before the other one started.
在没有特殊语境时，while 引出两个同时进行的动作，两部分通常都用进行时，让画面保持平衡。一边用简单式会让人以为那个动作在另一个开始之前就结束了。
✗ I was living in Vancouver for five years.
✓ I lived in Vancouver for five years.
When you simply report a finished period with its total length and no further context, the past simple is the natural choice, because it presents the five years as one completed block. The continuous belongs inside that period, pointing at one moment, such as 'I was living in Vancouver when the pandemic started'.
单纯报告一段已经结束、带明确总时长的时期而没有其他语境时，自然的选择是一般过去，因为它把这五年当作一个已完成的整体。进行时用于这段时期中的某个时刻，例如“I was living in Vancouver when the pandemic started”。
✗ She was walk to the shop when it began to rain.
✓ She was walking to the shop when it began to rain.
Learners often forget the -ing ending or the auxiliary, because the Chinese verb does not change form. Past continuous always needs was or were plus the -ing form.
汉语动词没有形态变化，所以学习者常常漏掉 -ing 或助动词。过去进行时必须是 was/were 加动词的 -ing 形式。
✗ Yesterday it was raining, so I was staying home, was cooking and was watching a film.
✓ Yesterday it was raining, so I stayed home, cooked and watched a film.
Actions that follow one another are plot events and take the past simple. Putting every verb in the continuous makes the story sound like a slow description of a single moment instead of a sequence.
依次发生的动作是情节事件，要用一般过去。把每个动词都用进行时，故事就会像对某一瞬间的缓慢描写，而不是一连串事件。
