The Photo of Our New Keys 新钥匙的那张照片

🧠 Learn the 3 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 3 个新词

Read a story about a proud post that shared too much, and see unreal conditionals look back at a choice and forward to a better one. 读一个因得意发帖而分享过头的故事，看非真实条件句怎样回看一个选择、又指向更好的做法。

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

The day we got the keys to our first apartment, I took a photo of them in my hand, with the front door and the building number behind them, and posted it with the caption "Finally home!" Within an hour it had forty likes. I felt wonderful.

拿到第一套公寓钥匙那天，我拍了张钥匙握在手里的照片，背后是大门和楼号，配上一句「终于有家了！」发了出去。一个小时就有四十个赞。我心里美极了。

A week later, a woman I had never met sent me a friendly message. She said she lived nearby, had seen my post and wanted to welcome me to the neighbourhood. She knew my building, my floor and the name of my son's daycare, which I had mentioned in another post. She was probably harmless. I still did not sleep well that night.

一周后，一个从没见过的女人给我发来一条友好的消息。她说她住在附近，看到了我的帖子，想欢迎我搬来。她知道我住哪栋楼、哪一层，还知道我儿子日托所的名字——那是我在另一条帖子里提过的。她多半没有恶意，可那晚我还是没睡好。

When I told a colleague, she did not laugh at me. She opened my profile and showed me how much anyone could see. My account was public; I had never changed the privacy settings since I created it as a student. If I had taken ten minutes to check them, none of those posts would have reached a stranger. If I had cropped the building number out, the photo would have been just a nice picture of keys.

我跟一位同事说了，她没笑话我。她点开我的主页，让我看任何人都能看到多少东西。我的账号是公开的，从学生时代注册以来就没改过隐私设置。要是我花十分钟查一下，那些帖子一条都到不了陌生人那儿。要是我把楼号裁掉，那张照片就只是一张好看的钥匙照。

That evening I went through five years of posts. I deleted some, made others visible to friends only and turned off location tags. It was embarrassing to see how much I had shared without thinking.

那天晚上我把五年的帖子翻了一遍，删了一些，把另一些设成仅好友可见，又关掉了位置标记。看到自己不假思索地分享了那么多，真让人难为情。

I still post. I like sharing good news with people I love. But I post later, and I post less. If I were moving again tomorrow, I would celebrate the keys with a photo of a cake instead.

我现在还发帖。我喜欢和爱的人分享好消息。但我晚点发，也少发。要是明天再搬一次家，我会改发一张蛋糕的照片来庆祝拿到钥匙。

Words词

/ˈkæpʃn/ n.
（图片的）说明文字；配文
write a caption; post sth with a caption
The caption gave away the name of the street. 配文把街名暴露了。
/ˈhɑːmləs/ adj.
无害的；没有恶意的
probably harmless; a harmless joke
Most messages from strangers are harmless, but not all. 陌生人发来的消息大多无害，但不是全部。
/ˈprəʊfaɪl/ n.
（社交网站的）个人主页；资料
a public profile; update your profile
Anyone can see her profile picture. 任何人都能看到她的头像。
/tæɡ/ n./v.
标签；标记（某人或地点）
location tags; tag sb in a photo
Please do not tag me in photos of the party. 聚会照片请别标记我。
/ɡəʊ θruː/ phr. v.
逐一查看；仔细检查
go through old posts; go through your emails
I went through every photo before I sent the album. 发相册之前，我把每张照片都过了一遍。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

If I had taken ten minutes to check them, none of those posts would have reached a stranger.
Third conditional: had taken + would have reached. She did not check, and the posts did reach a stranger.
第三类条件句：had taken + would have reached。她没查，帖子确实到了陌生人那里。
She knew my building, my floor and the name of my son's daycare, which I had mentioned in another post.
Past perfect had mentioned: the earlier post came before the message. which refers to the daycare's name.
过去完成时 had mentioned：那条帖子发生在消息之前。which 指日托所的名字。
