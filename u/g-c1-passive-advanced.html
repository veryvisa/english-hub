Advanced passives: get, need doing, being done 高级被动

Choose accurately between be-passives, get-passives, need + -ing, and being + past participle to control stance, agency, and register. 能够准确选择 be 被动、get 被动、need + -ing 以及 being + 过去分词，以控制语气、施动者和语体。

The report was finished on Friday. 报告在周五完成了。
Neutral, formal reporting of a completed event; the agent is unimportant.中性、正式地陈述一个已完成的事件，施动者不重要。
The report got finished on Friday. 报告在周五总算完成了。
Informal; implies some effort, difficulty, or a sense of relief that it happened.非正式；暗示费了些力气、遇到过困难，或有“总算完成”的释然感。
The report is being finished as we speak. 报告此刻正在完成中。
The action is in progress right now, so it is not done yet.动作正在进行，所以还没完成。
The report needs finishing by Friday. 报告需要在周五前完成。
Names the Friday deadline but not who is imposing it; the focus stays on the report's unfinished state, with no agent named.点出了周五这个期限，却没有说是谁要求的；重点仍是报告尚未完成的状态，没有施动者。

📏 The rule规则

📏 The rule in one line规则一句话
Get-passives stress change, events, or personal impact; need/want/require + -ing expresses a passive meaning without naming an agent; being + past participle stresses that an action is in progress or ongoing.get 被动强调变化、事件或对当事人的影响；need/want/require + -ing 在不点明施动者的情况下表达被动含义；being + 过去分词强调动作正在进行或持续。

📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Open the full explanation: 5 points, 24 examples展开细讲：5 段讲解、24 个例句

Choose get over be when you want to stress an event happening to someone, often something unexpected, unwelcome, or the result of a process: 'He got promoted' feels like news about what happened to him, while 'He was promoted' is neutral reporting. Get-passives are common in speech and informal writing, and they often carry a sense that the subject was affected, so they are less natural in formal reports, academic prose, or legal text where be-passives dominate.

想强调“某事发生在某人身上”，尤其是意外、不愉快或经过某个过程之后的结果时，选 get 而不是 be：He got promoted 像是在讲他身上发生了什么，He was promoted 则是中性陈述。get 被动在口语和非正式写作里很常见，往往带有“主语受到影响”的意味，所以在正式报告、学术文章和法律文本中不如 be 被动自然。

Get-passives work best with dynamic verbs and often with adjective-like participles describing a change of state: get married, get lost, get stuck, get dressed. They are awkward with stative verbs, so 'The mistake got known by everyone' sounds wrong, while 'The mistake was known by everyone' is fine. A useful test is whether the verb describes something that can happen to a subject at a point in time; if it describes a lasting state, stay with be.

get 被动最适合动态动词，也常和类似形容词的分词搭配，表示状态的变化：get married、get lost、get stuck、get dressed。它和状态动词搭配会别扭，所以 The mistake got known by everyone 不自然，而 was known by everyone 没问题。可以用一个小测试：这个动词是否表示可以在某个时间点发生在主语身上的事？如果表示持续状态，就用 be。

Use need/want/require + -ing when the focus is on the condition of a thing and the agent is irrelevant or obvious: 'The car needs washing' and 'The car needs to be washed' mean nearly the same, but the -ing form is shorter and more idiomatic in everyday English. It is limited to a small set of verbs, mainly need, want, require, deserve, and bear, and it works for things or situations, so 'The report needs checking' is natural but 'She needs checking' would be understood as a different, odd idea.

当重点是事物的状态、施动者无关紧要或显而易见时，用 need/want/require + -ing：The car needs washing 和 The car needs to be washed 意思几乎相同，但 -ing 形式更简短、更地道。这个结构只适用于少数动词，主要是 need、want、require、deserve 和 bear，并且适用于事物或情形，所以 The report needs checking 很自然，而 She needs checking 会让人觉得怪异。

Choose being + past participle when you want to show that the action is happening right now or over a period of time, or when the action itself is the topic: 'The bridge is being repaired' tells us the work is in progress, while 'The bridge is repaired' tells us it is finished. In non-finite clauses it also gives a reason or background: 'Being asked to leave, she packed her things' foregrounds the passive event, and 'Having been asked to leave' would stress that it came before the next action.

想表明动作正在发生或持续一段时间，或者动作本身就是话题时，用 being + 过去分词：The bridge is being repaired 说明工程正在进行，The bridge is repaired 则说明已经完成。在非谓语结构中它还能表示原因或背景：Being asked to leave, she packed her things 突出被动事件本身，而 Having been asked to leave 则强调这件事发生在后一个动作之前。

Pay attention to who is responsible and how much you want to say. Get-passives rarely take a by-agent, and when they do the sentence often sounds odd or blames someone: 'He got fired by the manager' is acceptable but marked. Be-passives take a by-agent naturally when the agent is new or important information. When you want to hide or skip the agent entirely, all three forms work, but need + -ing is the most agent-free, because it does not even imply that someone has acted yet.

要留意谁该负责以及你想说多少。get 被动很少带 by 施动者，一旦带上，句子往往显得别扭或带有指责意味：He got fired by the manager 可以接受但有标记性。当施动者是新信息或重要信息时，be 被动能自然地带 by 短语。如果想隐去或略去施动者，三种形式都行，但 need + -ing 最不涉及施动者，因为它甚至不暗示已经有人做了什么。

More pairs: same idea, different choice更多对比句组

The kitchen needs to be cleaned. 厨房需要被打扫。
Neutral, slightly more formal; a general statement of necessity.中性，略偏正式；对必要性的一般性陈述。
The kitchen needs cleaning. 厨房需要打扫。
Idiomatic and compact; the usual choice in everyday speech.地道简洁；日常口语中的常规选择。
The kitchen is being cleaned. 厨房正在被打扫。
Someone is cleaning it now, so the need is already being met.此刻有人在打扫，需求已经在被满足。
The kitchen got cleaned. 厨房被打扫干净了。
Reports a completed event informally, often after a fuss or delay.非正式地报告一个已完成的事件，常带有折腾或拖延之后的意味。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ The window got broken by the wind, so we have to repair it urgently.
✓ The window was broken by the wind, so we have to repair it urgently.
With a natural force as the agent and a formal, explanatory tone, a be-passive is more natural; get-passives rarely take a by-agent and sound casual or accusing.
当施动者是自然力量且语气偏正式、偏说明时，be 被动更自然；get 被动很少带 by 施动者，听起来随意或带有指责意味。
✗
✓ The truth became known to everyone in the company.
Know is a stative verb, so it does not combine naturally with get; use become known or be known instead.
know 是状态动词，不能自然地与 get 搭配；应使用 became known 或 was known。
✗
✓ My car needs washing. / My car needs to be washed.
After need, a passive meaning requires either -ing or to be + past participle; the bare infinitive gives an active meaning and is wrong here.
need 之后要表达被动含义，必须用 -ing 或 to be + 过去分词；用不带被动的不定式会变成主动含义，在这里是错的。
✗
✓ The new building is being built at the moment.
A progressive passive needs being + past participle; learners often drop the passive and use the active progressive by mistake.
进行时被动需要 being + 过去分词；学习者常常漏掉被动，错用主动进行时。
✗
✓ She arrived late and missed the meeting.
Arrive is intransitive and cannot be passive, so no get-passive is possible; learners often add get after verbs with no object.
arrive 是不及物动词，不能变被动，因此无法构成 get 被动；学习者常在没有宾语的动词后误加 get。
✗
✓ He hates being told what to do.
After a verb like hate that takes a gerund, use being + past participle directly; do not add an extra to-infinitive.
hate 这类后接动名词的动词，直接用 being + 过去分词；不要再多加一个 to 不定式。
