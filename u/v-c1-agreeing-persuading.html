📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Open the full explanation: 5 points展开细讲：5 段讲解

Chinese speakers often agree or disagree very directly, for example 'I disagree' or 'You are wrong'. In English, especially in professional settings, direct disagreement sounds blunt. Chunks such as 'I see where you're coming from, but...' or 'I'm not entirely convinced' acknowledge the other person first and then present your view, which keeps the relationship safe.

中文母语者常常直接表示同意或反对,比如“我不同意”“你错了”。在英语中,尤其是职场场合,这样直接反对会显得生硬。像 'I see where you're coming from, but...' 或 'I'm not entirely convinced' 这样的表达先承认对方,再提出自己的看法,更能保护关系。

Agreement has degrees. 'I couldn't agree more' and 'I'm with you on that' show strong, warm agreement, while 'I suppose so' or 'I'll go along with that' show weak or reluctant agreement. Learners who use 'I agree' every time miss this range and may sound flat or accidentally insincere.

同意是有程度之分的。'I couldn't agree more' 和 'I'm with you on that' 表示强烈而热情的同意,而 'I suppose so' 或 'I'll go along with that' 表示勉强或不太情愿的同意。总是用 'I agree' 的学习者体现不出这种层次,听起来可能平淡甚至显得不真诚。

Complaints also work on a scale of formality. 'I'd like to make a complaint about...' and 'I'm writing to express my dissatisfaction with...' are formal and suit emails and customer service. 'I have a bone to pick with you' or 'I can't help grumbling about...' are informal and suit friends. A common mistake is to translate 'I complain you' or 'I want to complain the service' directly from Chinese; the correct patterns are 'complain about something' and 'complain to someone'.

投诉同样有正式程度之分。'I'd like to make a complaint about...' 和 'I'm writing to express my dissatisfaction with...' 很正式,适用于邮件和客服场景;'I have a bone to pick with you' 或 'I can't help grumbling about...' 则很口语,适合朋友之间。常见错误是把中文直译成 'I complain you' 或 'I want to complain the service';正确搭配是 complain about something 和 complain to someone。

Apologies differ in weight too. 'Sorry about that' is casual and quick, whereas 'I owe you an apology' and 'Please accept my sincere apologies' are serious and formal. 'I take full responsibility' goes further, because it accepts blame rather than just expressing regret. Chinese learners often say 'I'm very sorry' repeatedly, but a native-sounding apology usually names what went wrong and offers a remedy.

道歉也有轻重之别。'Sorry about that' 随意而简短,而 'I owe you an apology' 和 'Please accept my sincere apologies' 则严肃正式。'I take full responsibility' 更进一步,因为它承认过错,而不只是表达遗憾。中国学习者常常反复说 'I'm very sorry',而地道的道歉通常会说明哪里出了问题,并提出补救办法。

Many of these chunks take fixed prepositions and structures: 'agree with someone', 'agree on a plan', 'agree to a proposal', 'apologise to someone for something', 'object to doing something', 'take issue with something'. Note that 'object to' and 'be opposed to' are followed by a noun or -ing form, not by an infinitive, which is a very common source of errors.

这些词块大多带有固定的介词和结构:agree with someone、agree on a plan、agree to a proposal、apologise to someone for something、object to doing something、take issue with something。注意 'object to' 和 'be opposed to' 后面接名词或 -ing 形式,而不是不定式,这是非常常见的错误来源。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ I am agree with you.
✓ I agree with you.
'Agree' is a verb, so it does not take 'am/is/are'. The adjective forms are different, such as 'be in agreement with'.
agree 是动词,前面不能加 am/is/are。形容词形式是另一种结构,如 be in agreement with。
✗ I object to share the cost.
✓ I object to sharing the cost.
In 'object to', the word 'to' is a preposition, so it must be followed by a noun or an -ing form, not a bare infinitive.
object to 中的 to 是介词,后面必须接名词或 -ing 形式,不能接动词原形。
✗ I want to complain the hotel service.
✓ I want to complain about the hotel service.
'Complain' needs 'about' before the thing you are unhappy with, and 'to' before the person you tell.
complain 后面要用 about 引出不满的对象,用 to 引出听你抱怨的人。
✗ I apologise you for being late.
✓ I apologise to you for being late.
'Apologise' is intransitive here: use 'apologise to someone for something'. You can also say 'Sorry for being late' or 'Please accept my apologies'.
apologise 在这里是不及物动词,要说 apologise to someone for something。也可以说 Sorry for being late 或 Please accept my apologies。
✗ You are wrong. I totally disagree.
✓ I see where you're coming from, but I'm not entirely convinced.
The first version is blunt and can sound rude at work. Acknowledging the other view first and then softening the disagreement keeps the tone professional.
前一种说法很生硬,在工作中可能显得无礼。先承认对方的观点,再委婉地表示不同意,语气更专业。
✗ We have different opinions, so we should agree to disagree with each other's opinion.
✓ We have different opinions, so we should agree to disagree.
'Agree to disagree' is a complete fixed phrase and does not take an object. Adding extra words makes it unnatural.
agree to disagree 是完整的固定短语,后面不接宾语。多加成分会显得不自然。
