Read and listen读与听

275 words
Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Three years ago I fell out with a colleague over a project that neither of us wanted to lose. We had each done a fair share of the work, but the presentation would carry only one name, and we both believed it should be ours. For a week we exchanged polite, icy emails, and each of us waited for the other to back down.

三年前，我和一位同事因为一个谁都不想放手的项目闹翻了。我们各自做了相当一部分工作，但演示文稿上只能署一个名字，而我们都认为应该是自己的。那一周里，我们互发礼貌而冰冷的邮件，各自等着对方让步。

I remember treating my version of events as a bargaining chip. I had saved every message, and I was ready to produce them like evidence in court. Looking back, that was exactly the wrong instinct. The more proof I gathered, the less I listened, and the more she hardened.

我记得自己把我这一版本的事实当作谈判筹码。我保存了每一条消息，准备像法庭上的证据那样拿出来。回头看，那恰恰是错误的本能。我收集的证据越多，听得越少，她也就越强硬。

The turning point came by accident. We ended up sharing a lift, and in the awkward silence she said, ‘I don’t actually care about the name. I care that nobody thinks I did less.’ It had never occurred to me that her real concern was recognition, not ownership. Had she said so earlier, we would have saved ourselves a week of misery.

转折点出于偶然。我们碰巧同乘一部电梯，在尴尬的沉默中她说：「我其实并不在乎署名。我在乎的是别人不要以为我做得少。」我从没想到，她真正在意的是认可，而不是归属。如果她早点说出来，我们就能省掉一周的痛苦。

So we found common ground. I conceded that her contribution deserved to be named on the first slide, and she agreed that I would present, since I had drafted most of the text. It was not a perfect deal, but it was one we could both defend, and on the whole it held up well.

于是我们找到了共同点。我承认她的贡献应该写在第一页幻灯片上，她则同意由我来做演讲，因为大部分文字是我起草的。这不是一个完美的协议，但我们都能为之辩护，总体而言它执行得很好。

What I took from the episode is simple. Most quarrels are not about what they appear to be about. Although it is tempting to defend your position, a single honest question, asked early enough, tends to achieve more than a folder full of evidence.

我从这件事中得到的体会很简单：大多数争吵并不真的关乎表面上的事。虽然捍卫自己的立场很诱人，但一个足够早提出的坦诚问题，往往比满满一文件夹证据更有效。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

Had she said so earlier, we would have saved ourselves a week of misery.
This is an inverted third conditional: ‘Had she said’ replaces ‘If she had said’, and ‘would have saved’ describes an imagined past result.
这是倒装的第三类条件句：「Had she said」替代「If she had said」，「would have saved」描述想象中的过去结果。
The more proof I gathered, the less I listened, and the more she hardened.
The double comparative ‘the more..., the less...’ shows two changes moving together, and the final ‘the more she hardened’ adds a third parallel change.
双重比较结构「the more…, the less…」表示两种变化同步发展，最后的「the more she hardened」又加入第三个并列的变化。
