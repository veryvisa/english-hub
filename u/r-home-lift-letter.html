A Letter About the Lift 一封关于电梯的信

🧠 Learn the 5 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 5 个新词

Read a firm, polite letter from tenants to a property manager and see how inversion, formal conditionals and the subjunctive make a complaint sound serious without sounding angry. 读一封租户写给物业经理的信，坚定而客气；看倒装、正式条件句和虚拟式怎样让投诉显得郑重而不失态。

C1 · CELPIP 9+⏱ 20 minG05 Topic-comment sentences and double subjects主题句与双主语G07 Unreal conditionals and wish虚拟条件句与 wish

📖 Key card知识卡
A complaint letter works when it gives dates, shows the cost to real people, and asks for specific actions by a specific date. Formal grammar adds weight; the facts do the persuading.投诉信要奏效，得给出日期、讲清对真实住户的影响，并要求在具体日期前采取具体行动。正式语法增加分量，真正说服人的是事实。

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Mrs Okafor lives on the eleventh floor of a rental building with one lift. When it broke down for the third time in two months, she and four neighbours sat at her kitchen table and wrote the letter below. Her daughter, who works in an office, checked the grammar. "Be polite," she said, "but don't be soft."

奥卡福太太住在一栋只有一部电梯的出租楼的十一层。两个月里电梯第三次坏掉时，她和四位邻居围坐在她家厨房的桌边，写了下面这封信。她在写字楼上班的女儿帮忙检查了语法。「要客气，」女儿说，「但不能软。」

"Dear Ms Grant, we are writing on behalf of the residents of floors eight to fourteen. The lift has been out of service since Monday, 3 March. Not only is this the third breakdown since January, but on each occasion we were told nothing until the repair was already under way. Two of us use walkers. One resident, who is ninety-one, has not left her apartment for six days."

「尊敬的格兰特女士：我们代表八至十四层的住户写信给您。电梯自三月三日周一起停运。这不仅是一月以来的第三次故障，而且每一次我们都是在维修已经开始后才得到通知。我们中有两人要用助行器。一位九十一岁的住户已经六天没出过家门了。」

"Had we been told on the first day, families could have arranged help. Never before have we had to carry groceries up eleven floors for a neighbour. This we were glad to do. What we cannot accept is the silence."

「如果第一天就通知我们，各家本可以安排人手帮忙。我们从来没有为邻居扛着食品爬过十一层楼。这件事我们很乐意做。我们不能接受的是一言不发。」

"We therefore request that the lift be inspected by an independent technician, and that residents be informed in writing within 24 hours of any future breakdown. It is essential that a plan for residents with limited mobility be in place before the next one. Should you wish to discuss this, we would be happy to meet in the lobby on any weekday evening. We look forward to your reply by 20 March. Yours sincerely, the residents' group."

「因此，我们要求请独立技术员检查电梯，并在今后任何一次故障后 24 小时内书面通知住户。在下一次故障之前，必须为行动不便的住户制定好方案。如您希望商谈此事，我们乐意在任何一个工作日晚上于大堂面谈。期待您在三月二十日前回复。住户小组 敬上。」

The reply came in nine days. The manager apologised, booked the inspection and set up a text alert for the building. Mrs Okafor framed neither the letter nor the reply. She kept both in a folder marked "Lift", just in case.

九天后回信到了。经理道了歉，预约了检查，并为整栋楼设了短信提醒。奥卡福太太没有把信或回信装裱起来，而是都收进一个标着「电梯」的文件夹，以防万一。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

Not only is this the third breakdown since January, but on each occasion we were told nothing until the repair was already under way.
Not only at the front inverts the first half (is this); the second half after but keeps normal order.
句首 not only 让前半句倒装（is this），but 之后的后半句保持正常语序。
Had we been told on the first day, families could have arranged help.
= If we had been told. The inverted form sounds measured, which suits a complaint.
相当于 If we had been told。倒装形式显得克制，适合投诉信。
We therefore request that the lift be inspected by an independent technician, and that residents be informed in writing within 24 hours of any future breakdown.
request that + bare be + past participle: the passive subjunctive, used twice.
request that 加原形 be 再加过去分词：被动虚拟式，用了两次。
This we were glad to do. What we cannot accept is the silence.
Fronted This links to the groceries; then a cleft (What … is) puts the silence at the end, where the stress falls.
前置的 This 承接扛食品这件事；接着用分裂句把 the silence 放到句末重音处。
