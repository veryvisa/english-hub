My Children Feel at Home and I Do Not 孩子们有家的感觉，我却没有

Read an advice column about parents who feel left behind by their children, and collect collocations with make, have and take. 读一篇讲「父母觉得被孩子落下」的问答专栏，收集 make、have、take 的搭配。

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Dear Ruth, we moved here five years ago. My children, now eleven and fourteen, speak English to each other, laugh at jokes I do not get, and call this place home. I am proud of them. I also feel left behind, as if they have joined a club I cannot join. Is it wrong to feel this way? Left Behind

露丝您好：我们五年前搬来这里。我的两个孩子，现在一个十一岁、一个十四岁，彼此说英语，听得懂我不懂的笑话，把这里叫作家。我为他们骄傲，也觉得自己被落下了，好像他们加入了一个我进不去的俱乐部。我这样想不对吗？被落下的人

Dear Left Behind, it is not wrong, and you are far from alone. Children usually adapt faster than adults. School gives them a group, a routine and a language every day, while many parents spend their days in jobs where nobody asks how they feel. The gap you see is common. It is not proof that you have failed.

被落下的人您好：这没有不对，而且远不止你一个人这样。孩子通常比大人适应得快。学校每天给他们一个群体、一套日常和一门语言，而许多父母整天待在没人问他们感受的工作里。你看到的这道差距很常见，它不能证明你失败了。

Still, the feeling deserves attention, because it can have an impact on the whole family. Parents who feel shut out sometimes push harder on rules, or go quiet. Children often read that as disapproval. It may help to have a word with them, calmly, about how you feel. The aim is not to make them feel guilty, but to help them see that your silence is not anger.

不过，这种感受值得重视，因为它可能影响整个家庭。觉得被关在门外的父母，有时会在规矩上逼得更紧，或者变得沉默。孩子常把这理解为不认可。不妨心平气和地和他们谈谈你的感受。目的不是让他们内疚，而是帮他们明白，你的沉默不是生气。

Then make an effort to enter one part of their world. Ask your fourteen-year-old to explain a joke, even if it takes three tries. Watch the show your eleven-year-old loves. You do not have to like it; you only have to be curious. On days when you do not feel like trying, rest, and try again next week.

然后，努力走进他们世界的一角。让十四岁的那个给你解释一个笑话，哪怕要讲三遍。看一看十一岁的那个喜欢的节目。你不必喜欢它，只需要好奇。哪天不想努力了，就歇一歇，下周再试。

And keep a door open in the other direction. Teach them a recipe, a song or a story from your childhood. Belonging in a family does not mean everyone shares one culture. It can mean everyone takes responsibility for building a shared one. Ruth

同时，把另一个方向的门也开着。教他们一道菜、一首歌，或者一个你小时候的故事。在一个家里有归属感，不等于每个人都属于同一种文化。它可以意味着，每个人都为共同建一种文化出一份力。露丝

Words词

/left bɪˈhaɪnd/ adj. phr.
被落下的；被抛在后面的
feel left behind; be left behind
When my son started speaking only English, I felt left behind. 儿子开始只说英语时，我觉得被落下了。
/əˈdæpt/ v.
适应
adapt to a new country; adapt quickly
Children often adapt faster than their parents. 孩子往往比父母适应得快。
/ruːˈtiːn/ n.
日常惯例；固定安排
a daily routine; get into a routine
A weekly routine helped me through the first winter. 每周的固定安排帮我熬过了第一个冬天。
/ˌdɪsəˈpruːvəl/ n.
不赞成；不认可
show disapproval; a look of disapproval
Children can read silence as disapproval. 孩子可能把沉默理解为不认可。
/ˈɡɪlti/ adj.
内疚的
feel guilty about sth; make sb feel guilty
Do not make them feel guilty for fitting in. 别因为他们融入了就让他们内疚。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

Still, the feeling deserves attention, because it can have an impact on the whole family.
Still at the start = however. have an impact on is a fixed chunk.
句首的 Still＝不过。have an impact on 是固定词块。
The aim is not to make them feel guilty, but to help them see that your silence is not anger.
not ... but ... sets two aims side by side; make / help + object + bare infinitive.
not ... but ... 把两个目的并列；make、help 后接宾语加不带 to 的不定式。
