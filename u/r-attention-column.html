Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Dear Column, my wife says I am addicted to my phone. She wants me to delete every social media app. The trouble is that I run a small renovation business, and most of my customers find me online. If I disappear, so does my work. How do I take her complaint seriously without damaging the business? — Always Online

专栏你好：我妻子说我手机上瘾，要我把社交软件全删掉。麻烦在于，我做一家小装修公司，大部分客户是在网上找到我的。我一消失，生意也跟着没了。我怎样才能认真对待她的抱怨，又不伤到生意？——「永远在线」

Dear Always Online, your wife is probably not asking you to close the business. She is asking you to look up. Before you make a decision about deleting anything, ask her what exactly bothers her. Is it the phone at dinner, in bed, or while she is talking to you? The answer will tell you where to start.

「永远在线」你好：你妻子多半不是要你关掉生意，她是要你抬起头来。在决定删任何东西之前，先问她到底什么让她烦：是饭桌上的手机、床上的手机，还是她跟你说话时你看手机？答案会告诉你从哪儿下手。

Next, separate the work from the scrolling. Much of what we call "work" on our phones is not work. Set two fixed times a day to answer customers, perhaps late morning and early evening, and take responsibility for keeping to them. Outside those times, mute your notifications. Customers will wait an hour; very few expect an instant answer from a builder.

其次，把工作和刷手机分开。我们在手机上所谓的「工作」，很多其实不是工作。每天定两个固定时间回复客户，比如上午晚些和傍晚，并且自己负责守住。其余时间把推送静音。客户等一个小时没问题，很少有人指望装修师傅秒回。

It also helps to take a proper break from the screen at the same time every day, together. A walk after dinner without phones has more impact on a marriage than any promise to "use it less". Promises are vague; habits are visible.

每天同一时间两人一起真正离开屏幕歇一会儿，也有帮助。饭后不带手机散个步，对婚姻的作用比任何「少用点」的承诺都大。承诺是含糊的，习惯是看得见的。

Finally, take her feelings into account even when you disagree. You may feel that you are only glancing at messages, but she feels the distraction every time you reach for your pocket. If you make a real effort for a month, you will both see whether the problem was the apps or the way you used them.

最后，即使你不同意，也要把她的感受考虑进去。你可能觉得自己只是瞄几眼消息，可你每次伸手掏口袋，她都感受到你分了心。你认真试一个月，你们俩就都能看清，问题出在软件本身，还是出在你用它的方式。

Words词

/əˈdɪktɪd/ adj.
上瘾的
be addicted to sth; feel addicted
He says he is not addicted; he just likes the news. 他说自己没上瘾，只是爱看新闻。
/ˈsepəreɪt/ v.
把……分开
separate A from B; separate work from home life
Try to separate work calls from family time. 尽量把工作电话和家庭时间分开。
/kiːp tuː/ v.
遵守；坚持
keep to a plan; keep to the rules; keep to a schedule
It is easy to make a schedule and hard to keep to it. 定时间表容易，守住难。
/ˈɪnstənt/ adj.
即时的；立刻的
an instant answer; instant messaging
Nobody expects an instant reply on a Sunday. 周日没人指望你秒回。
/veɪɡ/ adj.
含糊的；不具体的
a vague promise; a vague idea
"Soon" is too vague; give me a date. 「很快」太含糊了，给我个日期。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

Much of what we call "work" on our phones is not work.
what we call "work" is a noun clause acting as the subject after Much of; the main verb is the second is.
what we call "work" 是名词性从句，接在 Much of 后面作主语；谓语是后面那个 is。
A walk after dinner without phones has more impact on a marriage than any promise to "use it less".
Collocation have an impact on, here made comparative: have more impact on… than…
搭配 have an impact on 的比较形式：have more impact on… than…。
