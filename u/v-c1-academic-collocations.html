📏 The rule规则

🎯 The point in one line一句话要领
In academic English the noun decides the verb: you conduct research, raise a question, draw a conclusion. Get the pair right and the sentence sounds expert even if the idea is simple.学术英语里名词决定动词：conduct research、raise a question、draw a conclusion。搭配对了，想法再简单句子也显得专业。

📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Open the full explanation: 5 points展开细讲：5 段讲解

Chinese writers often pick the verb from the Chinese sentence: zuo yanjiu becomes do a research, de chu jielun becomes get a conclusion. Neither is wrong enough to block meaning, but both mark the writer as a learner at once. Academic readers notice collocations before they notice grammar.

华人常按中文选动词：做研究成了 do a research，得出结论成了 get a conclusion。意思不至于读不懂，却一眼看出是学习者。学术读者先注意到的是搭配，然后才是语法。

A small set of verbs does most of the work: conduct and carry out (research, a survey), raise and address (a question, an issue), draw and reach (a conclusion), play (a role), pose (a risk, a threat), provide (evidence, support). Learn each with two or three nouns.

少数几个动词干了大部分活：conduct/carry out 研究与调查，raise/address 问题，draw/reach 结论，play 角色，pose 风险，provide 证据。每个动词配两三个名词一起记。

Adjectives work the same way. Evidence is strong or compelling, not big; a difference is significant or marked; a factor is key or major; a claim is widely held. These pairs let you grade how sure you are without adding very.

形容词同理：证据用 strong/compelling，不用 big；差异用 significant/marked；因素用 key/major；观点用 widely held。这些搭配让你分出把握的程度，不必加 very。

Significant is a trap word. In a study it means "unlikely to be chance", not just "big". Use considerable or substantial when you mean "large", and keep significant for results that were tested.

significant 是陷阱词：在研究里指「不太可能是偶然」，不只是「大」。表示大用 considerable 或 substantial，significant 留给检验过的结果。

Many of these chunks also appear in good journalism and workplace reports, so they repay study even if you never write a paper: the findings suggest, a growing body of evidence, play a key role.

这些搭配在好的新闻和职场报告里同样常见，所以即使不写论文也值得学。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ We did a research on public transport.
✓ We conducted research on public transport. / We carried out a study of public transport.
research is uncountable and pairs with conduct or carry out.
research 不可数，配 conduct 或 carry out。
✗ We got a conclusion from the data.
✓ We drew a conclusion from the data. / We reached a conclusion.
The verb for a conclusion is draw or reach.
结论配 draw 或 reach。
✗ There is big evidence that sugar harms teeth.
✓ There is strong evidence that sugar harms teeth.
Evidence is strong or compelling.
证据用 strong 或 compelling。
✗ Prices rose significantly, so the result is statistically important.
✓ Prices rose considerably. / The difference was statistically significant.
Keep significant for tested results; use considerable for size.
significant 留给检验过的结果，表大小用 considerable。
✗ Sleep plays an important role for memory.
✓ Sleep plays an important role in memory.
play a role in.
play a role in。
