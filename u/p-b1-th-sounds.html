The two th sounds 两个 th 音

Make both th sounds with the tongue in the right place, and stop think turning into sink or this into dis in everyday words. 舌头放对位置发出两个 th 音，不再让 think 变成 sink、this 变成 dis。

B1 · CELPIP 5–6⏱ 20 minP02 /θ/ /ð/ become /s/ /z/ or /d/th 读成 s、z 或 d

👂 Hear the difference first先听辨

Minimal pairs: the two words differ in one sound only. Get your ear in before you read the rule.最小对立对：两个词只差一个音。先把耳朵调准，再看规则。

📏 The rule规则

📏 The point in one line一句话要领
For both th sounds the tongue tip touches the back of the top teeth and air keeps flowing; /θ/ is voiceless, /ð/ is the same shape with the voice on.两个 th 音都是舌尖轻贴上齿背、气流不断；/θ/ 不振声带，/ð/ 口型一样、振声带。

📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Open the full explanation: 5 points展开细讲：5 段讲解

Mandarin has no sound made with the tongue against the teeth, so the brain swaps in the nearest familiar one: /θ/ becomes /s/ or /f/ (I sink so, free for three), and /ð/ becomes /z/ or /d/ (zis, dat). Most listeners can still follow you in context, but in short answers and numbers the swap changes the word: three and free, thick and sick, thank and sank.

普通话没有舌齿音，大脑就换成最近的熟悉音：/θ/ 变 /s/ 或 /f/，/ð/ 变 /z/ 或 /d/。有上下文时多半还听得懂，但在简短回答和数字里，替换会直接变成另一个词。

Place the tongue first, then let the air through. Put the tongue tip lightly against the back of the upper front teeth (or just between the teeth while you are practising), keep it relaxed, and blow. If you hear a hiss like /s/, the tongue has slid back behind the teeth ridge. If you hear a pop like /t/ or /d/, you pressed too hard and stopped the air.

先放舌头，再送气。舌尖轻贴上门齿背（练习时可以略伸到齿间），放松、送气。听到 /s/ 的嘶声说明舌头退到齿龈后面了；听到 /t/、/d/ 的爆破说明压太紧、把气堵住了。

/ð/ lives in the small, frequent words: the, this, that, they, there, then, with, other, mother. Because these are often unstressed, the tongue touch is very short, but it is still a touch. /θ/ lives in content words and numbers: think, three, thousand, month, health, both, fifth.

/ð/ 集中在高频小词里，这些词常弱读，舌头只碰一下，但仍要碰到。/θ/ 集中在实词和数字里。

In fast speech a /ð/ after /n/, /l/ or /s/ often blends: in the sounds like in-ne with the tongue already on the teeth, all the like all-le. This is normal and native, not an error. What matters for you as a listener is that the /ð/ of the can almost vanish, so do not wait for a clear /ð/ before you recognise the word.

快语速里，/n/、/l/、/s/ 后面的 /ð/ 常被同化：in the 听起来像 in-ne。这是母语者的正常读法。作为听者要知道 the 的 /ð/ 可能几乎听不到，别等清楚的 /ð/ 才认出这个词。

The hardest place is a th next to /s/ or another consonant: months, clothes, fifths, sixth. Slow these down at first and make each movement; a native-like months is acceptable as /mʌns/, and clothes is very often said /kləʊz/ by native speakers too.

最难的是 th 和 /s/ 或别的辅音挨着：months、clothes、fifths、sixth。先放慢、每个动作做到位。母语者自己也常把 months 读成 /mʌns/、clothes 读成 /kləʊz/。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ I sink so.
✓ I think so.
/s/ for /θ/ turns a verb of opinion into sink; keep the tongue on the teeth.
用 /s/ 代 /θ/ 会把「认为」说成「下沉」，舌头要贴齿。
✗ It costs free hundred.
✓ It costs three hundred.
/f/ for /θ/ in numbers can mean "no money" to the listener.
数字里用 /f/ 代 /θ/，对方可能听成「免费」。
✗ Dis is my brudder.
✓ This is my brother.
/d/ for /ð/ is understood but heard as a strong accent; the tongue touch should be light, not a stop.
用 /d/ 代 /ð/ 能听懂但口音很重；舌头轻触，不要堵住气流。
✗ month-uh, mons-uh
✓ month /mʌnθ/
Do not add a vowel to hold the th; end with the air.
不要为了保住 th 加元音，以气流收尾。
