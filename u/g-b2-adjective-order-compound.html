Adjective order and compound adjectives 形容词顺序与复合形容词

📘 In your coursebook: English File 5e · Upper-Intermediate · 2B “You’re never too old…”. Use the texts and recordings with your coursebook; this page adds new words, grammar and practice.教材定位：English File 5e · Upper-Intermediate · 2B《You’re never too old…》。课文与录音请配合教材学习，本页补充新词、语法与练习。

Stack two or three adjectives in the order native speakers expect, and build compound adjectives like a ten-minute walk and a well-paid job instead of long of phrases. 按母语者习惯的顺序叠两三个形容词，并用 a ten-minute walk、a well-paid job 这样的复合形容词，代替冗长的 of 短语。

📏 The rule规则

📏 The rule in one line规则一句话
Opinion comes before fact, and the facts go from size and age to colour, origin and material, with the noun's own purpose closest to it: a lovely old wooden kitchen table. A compound adjective joins words with hyphens and keeps the noun singular: a five-year plan, not a five-years plan.观点在前、事实在后；事实一般按大小、新旧、颜色、产地、材料排列，用途最贴近名词：a lovely old wooden kitchen table。复合形容词用连字符把几个词连起来，里面的名词用单数：a five-year plan，不说 a five-years plan。

Native speakers rarely think about adjective order, but they hear a wrong order at once. a wooden old table sounds broken even though every word is right. The useful version of the rule is short: opinion, size, age, shape, colour, origin, material, purpose, then the noun. You almost never need more than three, and two or three is the natural maximum in speech.

母语者很少想形容词顺序，但顺序错了一听就出来。a wooden old table 每个词都对，听起来却是坏的。好用的规则很短：观点、大小、新旧、形状、颜色、产地、材料、用途，然后是名词。实际很少超过三个，口语里两三个就是自然的上限。

The closer an adjective is to the noun, the more it belongs to the thing itself. a Chinese cooking class is a class about Chinese cooking. a cooking class in Chinese is a different thing. Purpose words like kitchen, running or winter are often nouns used as adjectives, and they stay last: new winter tyres, comfortable running shoes.

越靠近名词的形容词，越是这个东西本身的属性。a Chinese cooking class 是讲中国菜的烹饪课，a cooking class in Chinese 是另一回事。kitchen、running、winter 这类表用途的词常常是名词当形容词用，放在最后：new winter tyres、comfortable running shoes。

Compound adjectives pack a whole phrase before the noun. Number + noun: a two-bedroom apartment, a thirty-minute drive, a six-year-old boy. Notice the singular noun and the hyphens. After the noun, the normal plural returns: The drive is thirty minutes. The boy is six years old.

复合形容词把一整个短语压到名词前面。数字加名词：a two-bedroom apartment、a thirty-minute drive、a six-year-old boy。注意名词用单数，带连字符。放到名词后面就恢复正常的复数：The drive is thirty minutes；The boy is six years old。

Other common compounds: adverb + participle (well-paid, badly-lit, well-known), adjective + noun-ed (open-minded, short-sighted, left-handed), noun + participle (family-run, home-made, Toronto-based), and noun + adjective (child-friendly, sugar-free, time-consuming). They sound educated and save words: a job that pays well becomes a well-paid job.

其他常见复合词：副词加分词（well-paid、badly-lit、well-known），形容词加名词-ed（open-minded、short-sighted、left-handed），名词加分词（family-run、home-made、Toronto-based），名词加形容词（child-friendly、sugar-free、time-consuming）。它们显得有文化，又省字。

Use hyphens before the noun, and usually not after it: a well-known writer, but The writer is well known. Two adjectives of the same type, such as two opinions, are separated by a comma or and: a long, boring meeting. Adjectives of different types are not: a boring weekly meeting.

复合形容词在名词前加连字符，放在名词后一般不加。同一类的两个形容词（比如两个观点）之间用逗号或 and；不同类的不用。

Examples例句

  • We bought a small old wooden table at a garage sale. 我们在一次车库甩卖上买了一张小旧木桌。
    Size, age, material.大小、新旧、材料。
  • She wore a beautiful long red dress to the wedding. 她穿了一条漂亮的红色长裙去参加婚礼。
    Opinion, size, colour.观点、大小、颜色。
  • I need some warm winter boots before December. 我得在十二月前买双暖和的冬靴。
    Purpose word last.用途词放最后。
  • They live in a quiet three-bedroom house near the park. 他们住在公园附近一栋安静的三居室房子里。
    Opinion, then compound.先观点，后复合词。
  • It's a twenty-minute walk from the station. 从车站走路二十分钟。
    Number + singular noun.数字加单数名词。
  • The walk from the station is twenty minutes. 从车站走过来要二十分钟。
    After the noun: plural.放后面用复数。
  • Their eight-year-old daughter already reads chapter books. 他们八岁的女儿已经在读章节小说了。
    year-old before the noun.名词前的 year-old。
  • Nursing can be a well-paid job, but the shifts are hard. 护士可以是一份收入不错的工作，但轮班很辛苦。
    Adverb + participle.副词加分词。
  • We ate at a small family-run restaurant in Chinatown. 我们在唐人街一家家庭经营的小餐馆吃饭。
    Noun + participle.名词加分词。
  • Filling in the form was more time-consuming than we expected. 填这张表比我们预想的费时间。
    Noun + adjective.名词加形容词。
  • My manager is very open-minded about flexible hours. 我经理对弹性工作时间很开明。
    Adjective + noun-ed.形容词加名词-ed。
  • The hotel has a child-friendly pool and a sugar-free menu for kids. 酒店有适合儿童的泳池，还有给孩子的无糖菜单。
    Two noun + adjective compounds.两个名词加形容词的复合词。
  • It was a long, tiring day. 这是漫长又累人的一天。
    Two opinions: comma.两个观点：用逗号。
  • He's a well-known local artist. 他是一位有名的本地艺术家。
    Compound, then origin.复合词，再接来源。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ It's a ten-minutes drive.
✓ It's a ten-minute drive.
In a compound adjective the noun stays singular.
复合形容词里的名词用单数。
✗ We have a five years old son.
✓ We have a five-year-old son. / Our son is five years old.
Before the noun: singular and hyphens; after: plural.
名词前用单数加连字符；放后面用复数。
✗ I bought a leather black new jacket.
✓ I bought a new black leather jacket.
Age, colour, material.
新旧、颜色、材料。
✗ She has a high paid job.
✓ She has a well-paid job. / She has a highly paid job.
Use well or highly before the participle, not high.
分词前用 well 或 highly，不用 high。
✗ It is a very time consume task.
✓ It is a very time-consuming task.
Noun + -ing adjective.
名词加 -ing 形容词。
