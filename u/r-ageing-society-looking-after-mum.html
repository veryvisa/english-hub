Three years ago my mother, who had always been fiercely independent, fell in the kitchen and broke her wrist. Nothing dramatic followed, but I noticed how long it took her to recover, and for the first time I understood that I was no longer the child in our relationship. She would never have asked for help. I live two hours away, so every visit became a small logistical project.

三年前，一向十分独立的母亲在厨房里摔倒，摔断了手腕。后来并没有发生什么严重的事，但我注意到她恢复得有多慢，也第一次明白，在我们的关系中，我不再是那个孩子了。她从来不会开口求助。我住在两小时车程之外，所以每次探望都成了一个小小的后勤项目。

Like many families, we drifted into caring without ever deciding to. First it was shopping, then appointments, then paperwork that she could no longer follow. Although I was happy to help, the work began to take its toll on my own job and my sleep. My brother, who lives abroad, sent money and advice, which was kind but not the same as being there.

和许多家庭一样，我们不知不觉就走进了照护的角色，从未真正做过决定。起初是购物，然后是陪诊，再后来是她已无法看懂的文书工作。虽然我乐于帮忙，这些事还是开始损耗我的工作和睡眠。我哥哥住在国外，寄来钱和建议，这很体贴，但和人在身边不一样。

What helped most was a day centre near her home. She went twice a week, played cards, ate a proper lunch and came back talking about people I had never heard of. Her friends there were, by and large, in the same position, and their company did more for her mood than any amount of my worrying. Services like that are fragile, though, and I know how lucky we were.

帮助最大的是她家附近的一家日间照料中心。她每周去两次，打牌，吃一顿像样的午饭，回来时谈论的都是我从没听说过的人。那里的朋友们大体上境况相同，他们的陪伴对她情绪的帮助，比我再多的担忧都大。不过这类服务很脆弱，我知道我们有多幸运。

I have learnt that an ageing population is not an abstract statistic. It is a phone call at midnight, a form that needs signing, a conversation about what she wants when she can no longer decide. Neither of us enjoys that conversation, yet it is the one we most needed to have. These days, I try to remember that shouldering the burden together makes us closer rather than weaker.

我了解到，人口老龄化不是抽象的统计数字。它是半夜的一通电话，是一张需要签字的表格，是一场关于她在无法再自己做决定时想要什么的谈话。我们俩都不喜欢这场谈话，但它恰恰是我们最需要进行的。如今，我努力提醒自己：一起承担这份重担，会让我们更亲近，而不是更疏远。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

Although I was happy to help, the work began to take its toll on my own job and my sleep.
The ‘Although’ clause concedes the writer’s willingness, and the main clause uses ‘began to take its toll’ to describe a gradual negative effect.
Although 从句承认作者乐于帮忙，主句用 began to take its toll 描述逐渐显现的负面影响。
Neither of us enjoys that conversation, yet it is the one we most needed to have.
’Neither of us’ takes a singular verb, and ‘the one we most needed to have’ is a relative structure with the relative pronoun omitted.
Neither of us 后接单数动词；the one we most needed to have 是省略了关系代词的关系从句结构。
