Justice Should Not Depend on Wealth 正义不应取决于财富

🧠 Learn the 5 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 5 个新词

A fair legal system must make meaningful access to justice possible regardless of a person's financial circumstances. 公平的法律制度必须确保人们无论经济状况如何，都能够真正获得司法救济。

A legal right may look impressive on paper, yet remain practically useless to someone who cannot afford to enforce it. Imagine a tenant facing an unlawful eviction, an employee experiencing discrimination, or a family disputing an inheritance. Each may have a legitimate claim, but pursuing it requires time, information, and often professional assistance. When these resources are distributed unequally, the promise of equal treatment becomes difficult to fulfil.

一项法律权利在纸面上可能看起来十分完善，但对于无力维护这项权利的人而言，它在实际中可能毫无用处。设想一名面临非法驱逐的租客、一名遭受歧视的员工，或一个因遗产产生纠纷的家庭。他们都可能拥有正当的主张，但维护这些权利需要时间、信息，而且往往需要专业协助。当这些资源分配不均时，平等对待的承诺就很难实现。

Access to justice involves much more than entering a courtroom. It includes understanding legal rights, obtaining reliable advice, finding affordable representation, and having disputes resolved without unreasonable delay. While public legal assistance can reduce financial barriers, its availability and scope vary across jurisdictions. A system may formally offer legal protection while leaving substantial groups unable to use it. That said, public funding alone is unlikely to solve every structural problem.

获得司法救济远不只是进入法庭。它还包括了解法律权利、获得可靠建议、找到负担得起的法律代理，以及在没有不合理拖延的情况下解决争议。尽管公共法律援助能够降低经济障碍，但其可获得性和覆盖范围因司法管辖区而异。一个制度可能在形式上提供法律保护，却让相当一部分群体无法真正使用这些保护。话虽如此，仅靠公共资金也不太可能解决所有结构性问题。

Legal literacy is another essential element. People who understand basic procedures are more likely to recognize when their rights have been violated and where to seek help. Community organizations, public information services, and accessible legal documents can narrow the knowledge gap. Digital platforms may simplify applications and provide guidance, although they can also exclude people with limited internet access or insufficient digital skills. Effective reform therefore needs both technological innovation and human support.

法律素养是另一个重要因素。了解基本程序的人更有可能意识到自己的权利何时受到侵犯，以及应该在哪里寻求帮助。社区组织、公共信息服务和易于理解的法律文件能够缩小知识差距。数字平台可能简化申请流程并提供指导，不过它们也可能排斥互联网使用受限或数字技能不足的人。因此，有效的改革既需要技术创新，也需要人工支持。

Ultimately, the quality of a justice system should be judged partly by the people it fails to reach. Wealthier individuals may purchase specialized advice, whereas others may abandon valid claims because the process appears overwhelming. This imbalance tends to weaken public confidence and may deepen existing social divisions. Justice cannot guarantee identical outcomes, but it should offer a meaningful opportunity to present a case. A right that cannot realistically be exercised is an incomplete promise.

归根结底，司法制度的质量在一定程度上应该根据它未能帮助到的人来评判。富裕人士可能购买专业法律建议，而其他人则可能因为诉讼程序看起来难以应对而放弃正当主张。这种不平衡往往会削弱公众信心，并可能加深现有的社会分化。正义无法保证每个人获得相同结果，但应该提供真正陈述诉求的机会。一项在现实中无法行使的权利，只是一项不完整的承诺。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

While public legal assistance can reduce financial barriers, its availability and scope vary across jurisdictions.
The while-clause expresses concession, acknowledging a benefit before introducing a qualification in the main clause.
while 引导让步状语从句，先承认某种好处，再通过主句对这一观点作出限定。
A system may formally offer legal protection while leaving substantial groups unable to use it.
The modal may indicates possibility, while the -ing clause describes a simultaneous consequence or accompanying situation.
情态动词 may 表示可能性，而 while leaving 引导的现在分词结构描述同时存在的结果或情况。
