An Email to a Tutor About Articles 给老师的一封问冠词的邮件

🧠 Learn the 5 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 5 个新词

Read a learner's email to a tutor about article mistakes, and see where English uses a, the, or no article at all. 读一封学习者写给老师的邮件，讲冠词错误，看英语在哪里用 a、the 或干脆不用冠词。

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Hi Ms Patel, Thank you for the feedback on my last two essays. You were right that most of my mistakes are with articles. I counted them: in the essay about my hometown, I made eleven article mistakes in a page and a half. I would like to ask for your help with a plan.

帕特尔老师您好：谢谢您对我上两篇作文的反馈。您说得对，我的错误大多出在冠词上。我数了一下：在写家乡的那篇里，一页半我犯了十一个冠词错误。我想请您帮我定个计划。

Here is what I understand so far. My first language has no articles, so when I write quickly, I simply leave them out and write "I went to bank" instead of "I went to the bank". When I slow down and think, I make the opposite mistake and put "the" everywhere, as in "The life in the big cities is hard." My brain seems to have only two settings: never and always.

我目前的理解是这样的：我的母语没有冠词，所以写得快的时候我就直接漏掉，写成 I went to bank，而不是 I went to the bank。慢下来想的时候，我又犯相反的错，到处加 the，比如 The life in the big cities is hard。我的大脑好像只有两个档：从不和总是。

I have two questions. First, is there a short list of situations where English uses no article at all, such as "at home", "by bus" or "go to work"? I would like to learn those as chunks rather than rules. Second, do you think it is useful to read a text aloud and pay attention to the articles only? A friend from my conversation circle says it helped her, but I am not sure it works for writing.

我有两个问题。第一，有没有一份简短的清单，列出英语里完全不用冠词的情况，比如 at home、by bus、go to work？我想把这些当作词块来学，而不是当规则。第二，您觉得把一篇文章大声读出来、只注意冠词，这样有用吗？我在会话小组的一个朋友说这对她有帮助，可我不确定对写作管不管用。

I also wonder whether I should slow down. When I write emails at work, I do not have the time to check every noun. If you have any advice on which mistakes matter most to readers, I would be grateful. I would rather fix the errors that cause a misunderstanding first and leave the small ones for later.

我也在想是不是应该写慢一点。在工作中写邮件时，我没时间检查每个名词。如果您对「哪些错误对读者影响最大」有什么建议，我会很感激。我宁愿先改会造成误解的错误，小错留到以后。

I have attached the essay with my own corrections in red, so you can see what I noticed and what I missed. Would the same time next Thursday work for our lesson? Thank you again for your patience. Best regards, Hana

我把自己用红色改过的那篇作文附上了，您可以看到我注意到了什么、漏掉了什么。下周四同一时间上课可以吗？再次感谢您的耐心。顺祝安好，花

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

When I slow down and think, I make the opposite mistake and put "the" everywhere, as in "The life in the big cities is hard."
The quoted sentence is the mistake. General ideas take no article: Life in big cities is hard. Use the only for a specific one: the life she had in Shanghai.
引号里是错句。泛指不加冠词：Life in big cities is hard。特指才加 the：the life she had in Shanghai。
Would the same time next Thursday work for our lesson?
the same always takes the. next Thursday takes no article and no on.
the same 前一定有 the。next Thursday 前不加冠词，也不加 on。
