Answering the Front Desk 怎么回答前台的问题

🧠 Learn the 6 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 6 个新词

Follow a patient at a clinic front desk and learn to answer negative tags by the facts, not by the grammar. 跟着一位病人在诊所前台走一遍，学会回答否定附加疑问时按事实答，而不是按语法答。

B2 · CELPIP 7–8⏱ 20 minG13 Word order in indirect questions间接疑问句语序P10 Flat or wrong intonation; the important word is not stressed语调平，重点词不突出

📖 Key card知识卡
When someone asks You haven't been here before, have you?, answer the facts: if you have not been, say No, I haven't.别人问 You haven't been here before, have you? 时，按事实回答：没来过就说 No, I haven't。

Read and listen读与听

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Hao had booked his first appointment at a family practice near his new apartment. He had practised his date of birth, his address and how to describe his sore shoulder. What he had not practised was the way the receptionist checked every detail with short questions that sounded like statements.

阿浩在新公寓附近的一家家庭诊所约了第一次门诊。他事先练过怎么说出生日期、地址，以及怎么描述肩膀疼。他没练过的，是前台用一句句听起来像陈述的短问句来核对每个细节。

"You haven't been here before, have you?" she asked, looking at her screen. Hao hesitated. Did she want a yes or a no? "No, I haven't," he said, and she nodded. With a negative tag, he realised, you answer the facts, not the grammar. If you have not been there, the answer is "No", whatever the question looks like.

「您以前没来过，对吧？」她看着屏幕问。阿浩犹豫了：她要的是 yes 还是 no？「没来过，」他说，她点了点头。他这才明白，遇到否定的附加疑问，要按事实回答，不按语法。没去过就答 No，不管问句长什么样。

"And this is still your current address, isn't it?" she asked, turning the screen toward him. "Actually, no. We moved last month." "Did you? Then I'll need the new one." She typed it in. "You've brought your health card, haven't you?" "Yes, I have." "Great. The doctor's running a bit late today, I'm afraid." "Is she? How long, do you think?" "Twenty minutes or so."

「这还是您现在的地址，对吧？」她把屏幕转向他。「其实不是，我们上个月搬家了。」「是吗？那我得要新地址。」她把地址打了进去。「您带了健康卡，对吧？」「带了。」「好的。恐怕医生今天要晚一点。」「是吗？大概多久？」「二十分钟左右。」

Sitting in the waiting room, Hao thought about how much the receptionist had done with those tags. Each one let her confirm a detail quickly and politely, without sounding like an officer filling in a form. And his own echo, "Is she?", had come out naturally, a small sign that he was following.

坐在候诊室里，阿浩想着前台靠这些附加疑问做了多少事。每一句都让她又快又礼貌地确认了一个细节，听起来不像官员在填表。而他自己那句回声问句「Is she?」脱口而出，说明他跟上了。

Before he left, the receptionist said, "You'll call us if anything changes, won't you?" "I will," he said. It was a two-word answer, but it was exactly the right one.

他离开前，前台说：「有什么变化您会打电话告诉我们的，对吧？」「会的，」他说。只有两个词，却恰到好处。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

"You haven't been here before, have you?" she asked, looking at her screen.
Negative statement, positive tag. Answer the facts: No, I haven't means "correct, I have not been here".
否定句配肯定附加疑问。按事实答：No, I haven't 表示「对，我没来过」。
The doctor's running a bit late today, I'm afraid." "Is she? How long, do you think?
Is she? is an echo of The doctor's (is) running: it shows surprise and interest. How long, do you think? is a softer way to ask How long will it be?
Is she? 是对 The doctor's running 的回应，表示意外和关心。How long, do you think? 是更委婉的问法。
"You'll call us if anything changes, won't you?"
will → won't you? With a falling voice, this is a polite request disguised as a question.
will 变 won't you?。降调时，这是披着问句外衣的礼貌请求。
