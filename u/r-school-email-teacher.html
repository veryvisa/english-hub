An Email to Ms Patel 写给帕特尔老师的一封邮件

🧠 Learn the 2 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 2 个新词

Study a parent's email to a teacher: an update, a request and an offer, each with the right collocation and a polite tone. 学一封家长写给老师的邮件：一个进展、一个请求、一个主动帮忙，每一处都有对的搭配和礼貌的语气。

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Subject: Leo's reading, a quick update. Dear Ms Patel, thank you again for meeting me last month and for the reading list you sent home. I wanted to give you a short update and ask one question.

主题：利奥的阅读，简短近况。帕特尔老师您好：再次感谢您上个月和我面谈，也感谢您带回家的书单。我想跟您简单说说近况，再问一个问题。

Leo has made progress faster than we hoped. He has read three books from the list, and he now asks for "twenty more minutes" at bedtime, which has never happened before. He also says he has started asking you questions after class. Thank you for making that feel normal for him; I think it has done more for his confidence than any workbook.

利奥的进步比我们预想的快。他读完了书单上的三本书，现在睡前还会要求「再读二十分钟」，这是从来没有过的。他还说自己开始在下课后问您问题了。谢谢您让他觉得这很正常；我觉得这对他信心的帮助比任何练习册都大。

My question is about the field trip next month. Leo has a mild allergy to nuts, and I am not sure whether the school already has this on file. I would be grateful if you could take it into account when lunches are planned, and let me know if I need to fill in a form. I am also happy to volunteer as a helper that day if you need one.

我的问题是关于下个月的校外考察。利奥对坚果轻度过敏，我不确定学校是否已经有这条记录。如果安排午餐时能考虑到这一点，我会很感激；如需填表也请告诉我。如果那天需要帮手，我也很乐意去做志愿者。

Finally, if you could give feedback on how we can support his writing at home, I would welcome it. His spelling still needs work, and we are making an effort with ten minutes of practice a day. If you think another approach would suit him better, please let me know.

最后，如果您能就我们在家怎样帮他提高写作给些反馈，我会很欢迎。他的拼写还需要下功夫，我们每天练十分钟，在努力。如果您觉得别的方法更适合他，请告诉我。

Best regards, Wei Zhao, Leo's father

此致，赵伟（利奥的父亲）

/ˈprəʊɡres/ v. phr.
取得进步
make real progress; make progress in reading
She has made great progress since September. 从九月以来她进步很大。
/ˈælədʒi/ n.
过敏
a nut allergy; an allergy to sth
Tell the school about any allergy. 有任何过敏都要告诉学校。
/ˈɡreɪtfəl/ adj.
感激的
I would be grateful if...; grateful for sth
I would be grateful if you could call me back. 如能回电，不胜感激。
/əˈprəʊtʃ/ n.
方法；处理方式
a different approach; approach to reading
The teacher suggested a new approach to spelling. 老师建议换一种练拼写的方法。
/ɒn faɪl/ phr.
存档的；有记录的
keep sth on file; have sth on file
The school has her medical details on file. 学校存有她的健康资料。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

I would be grateful if you could take it into account when lunches are planned, and let me know if I need to fill in a form.
I would be grateful if you could is a polite request frame; take it into account splits around it.
I would be grateful if you could 是礼貌的请求句框；take it into account 中间夹着 it。
Thank you for making that feel normal for him; I think it has done more for his confidence than any workbook.
make + object + bare infinitive (feel); do more for = help more.
make 加宾语加不带 to 的不定式 feel；do more for＝更有帮助。
