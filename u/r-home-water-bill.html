The Water Bill That Didn't Add Up 对不上的水费单

🧠 Learn the 5 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 5 个新词

Read a short column about a strange water bill and follow how the writer moves from guesses to a conclusion. 读一篇讲奇怪水费单的短专栏，跟着作者从推测一步步走到结论。

📖 Key card知识卡
When a number on a bill looks wrong, the cheapest step is to find out what it is measuring before you argue with it.账单上的数字看着不对时，最省钱的一步是先弄清它量的是什么，再去争。

Read and listen读与听

247 words
Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

When our water bill arrived in October, it was almost three times the usual amount. My first thought was that the city must have made a mistake. Nobody had moved in with us, we had not filled a pool, and our habits had not changed. A billing error seemed the only explanation.

十月的水费单寄来时，金额几乎是平时的三倍。我第一个念头是市里肯定弄错了。没人搬来和我们住，我们没给泳池灌水，生活习惯也没变。看起来只能是计费出了错。

Before calling, I read the bill again. The meter readings were real, not estimated, so the numbers could not have been invented in an office. The water must have gone somewhere. My husband thought it might have been the garden hose, which the kids sometimes leave dripping, but it had been switched off at the wall since the summer.

打电话之前，我又读了一遍账单。水表读数是实抄的，不是估算的，所以这些数字不可能是办公室里编出来的。水总得流到什么地方去了。我丈夫觉得可能是孩子们有时没关紧的花园水管，可那根水管从夏天起就在墙上的总阀关掉了。

So we had the meter checked, and a plumber came the next day. The problem was the toilet upstairs. A small part inside the tank had begun to wear out, and water had been running into the bowl, quietly, day and night, for weeks. It is said that a leak like this can waste more water than a family uses for cooking and washing, and after seeing our bill, I believe it.

于是我们请人查了水表，第二天来了一位水管工。问题出在楼上的马桶。水箱里一个小零件开始磨损了，水几个星期来不分昼夜地悄悄流进马桶里。据说这样的漏水浪费的水，可能比一家人做饭洗衣用的还多，看了我们的账单，我信了。

We got the part replaced for less than the cost of one bad month. The city, it turned out, offers a one-time adjustment for leaks that are fixed quickly, so we sent in the plumber's invoice. By the next billing period, the repair will have paid for itself.

我们花了不到一个月冤枉水费的钱把零件换了。原来市里对及时修好的漏水有一次性的费用调整，于是我们把水管工的发票寄了过去。到下一个账期，这次修理就回本了。

The lesson is simple. A strange number is usually telling you something. Listen before you argue.

道理很简单：一个奇怪的数字通常是在告诉你什么。先听，再争。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

the numbers could not have been invented in an office
couldn't have been done: almost sure something did not happen in the past, here with a passive verb.
couldn't have been done：几乎肯定过去没有发生，这里是被动形式。
So we had the meter checked, and a plumber came the next day.
had + object + past participle: someone checked the meter for us.
had 加宾语加过去分词：别人替我们检查了水表。
water had been running into the bowl, quietly, day and night, for weeks
Past perfect continuous: an activity that had been going on for some time before the moment of discovery.
过去完成进行时：发现之前已经持续了一段时间的活动。
