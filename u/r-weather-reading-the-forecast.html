A Week of Changing Weather 一周天气变化

🧠 Learn the 2 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 2 个新词

Changing weather conditions are affecting daily activities, and residents are being advised to check forecasts and prepare for different conditions. 天气状况的变化正在影响人们的日常活动，当地居民被建议查看天气预报并为不同的天气情况做好准备。

Residents across the region are preparing for a week of changing weather. Monday began with sunshine and mild temperatures, but heavy rain arrived in the afternoon. Local weather services expect a mixture of sunshine, clouds, and showers over the next several days. Temperatures may also change quite quickly, making it difficult to choose suitable clothes for the entire day.

该地区的居民正在为一周内多变的天气做准备。星期一以阳光和温和的气温开始，但下午迎来了大雨。当地气象服务部门预计，未来几天将出现阳光、云层和阵雨交替的天气。气温也可能迅速变化，因此很难为一整天选择合适的衣服。

Weather experts advise people to check the forecast before leaving home. A raincoat and a small umbrella may be useful even when the morning looks bright. Drivers should also allow extra time because wet roads can become slippery. People who cycle or walk to work are being encouraged to wear visible clothing when the sky becomes dark.

天气专家建议人们出门前查看天气预报。即使早晨看起来阳光明媚，雨衣和小雨伞也可能派上用场。驾驶员还应该预留更多时间，因为潮湿的道路可能变得很滑。骑自行车或步行上班的人，在天色变暗时应穿着醒目的衣服。

The changing conditions are also affecting outdoor events. Several sports clubs have moved their training sessions indoors, while some community groups are considering alternative dates for weekend activities. Organisers say that safety is their main concern. Although rain may interrupt outdoor plans, it can also improve local water supplies after a relatively dry period.

天气变化也影响着户外活动。一些体育俱乐部已经将训练安排转移到室内，而部分社区团体正在考虑为周末活动安排其他日期。组织者表示，安全是他们最关心的问题。虽然降雨可能打乱户外计划，但在经历了相对干燥的时期后，降雨也有助于改善当地的水资源供应。

Forecasters expect conditions to become more stable towards the end of the week, although this prediction may change. Residents are reminded that a forecast describes likely conditions rather than guaranteeing exactly what will happen. Checking updated information, dressing in layers, and planning ahead can make everyday life easier when the weather is unpredictable.

气象预报员预计，到本周末天气状况将趋于稳定，不过这一预测仍可能发生变化。居民们被提醒，天气预报描述的是可能出现的情况，并不能保证实际天气一定如此。天气难以预测时，查看最新信息、分层穿衣和提前安排计划，都能让日常生活更加轻松。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

Although rain may interrupt outdoor plans, it can also improve local water supplies after a relatively dry period.
Although introduces a concessive clause that presents an opposing fact before the main clause gives a different perspective.
Although 引导让步状语从句，先提出一个相反的事实，再由主句给出不同的观点。
Checking updated information, dressing in layers, and planning ahead can make everyday life easier when the weather is unpredictable.
The three gerund phrases form a parallel subject, followed by the modal verb can and the base form make.
三个动名词短语构成并列主语，后面接情态动词 can 和动词原形 make。
