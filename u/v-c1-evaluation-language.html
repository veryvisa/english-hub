📏 The rule规则

🎯 The point in one line一句话要领
A strong evaluation is never just "good" or "bad". It says what works, what does not, for whom, and what it costs: The main strength is…, but the drawback is…, so on balance…好的评价从不只是「好」或「不好」：它说清什么管用、什么不管用、对谁而言、代价是什么。
  • Easy to miss易漏 ✗ The plan is very good but also not good. → ✓ The plan has real strengths, but it also has one serious drawback.

📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Open the full explanation: 5 points展开细讲：5 段讲解

Learners at B2 often evaluate with one adjective, usually very good or not good (V05 on the pains list). At C1 the evaluation is a structure: a named strength, a named weakness, and a judgement that weighs them. The words in this unit fill each slot.

B2 学习者评价常只有一个形容词。C1 的评价是一个结构：点名一个优点、点名一个缺点、再给一个权衡后的判断。本单元的词分别填这几个空位。

Strength words: a strength, an advantage, a merit, a selling point, it works well for. Weakness words come in grades: a drawback or downside is a cost you can live with; a flaw is a fault in the design; a fatal flaw or a serious shortcoming means it fails. Choosing the grade is the judgement.

优点词：strength、advantage、merit、selling point。缺点词分档：drawback/downside 是可以接受的代价；flaw 是设计上的毛病；fatal flaw、serious shortcoming 意味着不行。选哪一档本身就是判断。

Balance comes from frames: On the one hand… on the other, While X is true, Y…, It comes at a cost, The trade-off is…, On balance…. These make you sound fair even when you reach a firm conclusion.

平衡靠句框架：on the one hand… on the other、while…、it comes at a cost、the trade-off is、on balance。用了它们，即使结论很明确也显得公允。

Hedge the judgement to the strength of the evidence (V12). It is arguably the best option, It tends to work better for small teams, It may not suit everyone leave room for the reader. Claims without hedges sound like adverts.

判断要按证据强度留余地：arguably、tends to、may not suit everyone。不留余地的话听着像广告。

Evaluation words are common in reviews, reports and the CELPIP speaking tasks that ask you to compare two options. Practise them aloud as well as in writing.

评价词常见于评测、报告，也常见于思培口语里的二选一比较题，所以要说也要写。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ The plan is very good but also not good.
✓ The plan has real strengths, but it also has one serious drawback.
Name the strength and the weakness.
点名优点和缺点。
✗ The advantages are outweighed than the disadvantages.
✓ The advantages outweigh the disadvantages. / The disadvantages are outweighed by the advantages.
outweigh is a transitive verb; no than.
outweigh 是及物动词，不用 than。
✗ There is a trade-off of price and quality.
✓ There is a trade-off between price and quality.
a trade-off between A and B.
trade-off between A and B。
✗ This is the best method in the world. (about a study tip)
✓ This is arguably one of the most effective methods.
Hedge to the evidence; absolute claims sound like adverts.
按证据留余地，话太满像广告。
✗ On one hand… on the other hand… (in formal writing)
✓ On the one hand… on the other (hand)…
Careful writing usually keeps the in the first half; on one hand is common in speech.
规范写作前半句一般带 the；不带 the 多见于口语。
