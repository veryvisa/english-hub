The Evening I Changed My Habits 改变习惯的那个晚上

For years, I ended my working day in almost the same way. I arrived home, prepared something quick to eat, and sat down with my phone. I often planned to read a book or practise English, but I usually spent the evening watching short videos. Before I noticed it, the clock showed a late hour, and I went to bed feeling that I had wasted my free time.

多年来，我几乎总是以同样的方式结束工作日。我回到家，随便准备点吃的，然后坐下来玩手机。我经常计划读书或练习英语，但通常整个晚上都在看短视频。等我注意到时间时，已经很晚了，我带着浪费了空闲时间的感觉上床睡觉。

One Monday, I decided to make a small change. Instead of trying to improve my entire life, I chose three simple activities: prepare dinner, spend fifteen minutes studying, and read a few pages before bed. I also put my phone in another room while studying. At first, the new routine felt strange, but it was easier to follow than my ambitious plans had been.

一个星期一，我决定做出一个小小的改变。我没有试图改善整个生活，而是选择了三项简单的活动：准备晚餐、花十五分钟学习，以及睡前阅读几页书。我还在学习时把手机放到了另一个房间。起初，新的作息让我觉得有些不习惯，但它比我之前那些雄心勃勃的计划更容易坚持。

After a few weeks, I began to notice a difference. I was not suddenly more productive, and I still had evenings when I felt tired. However, I spent less time scrolling through my phone and more time doing things I genuinely enjoyed. Preparing dinner in advance also made my evenings feel calmer. The routine did not solve every problem, but it gave my free time a clearer structure.

几周之后，我开始注意到一些变化。我并没有突然变得更有效率，而且仍然会有感到疲惫的晚上。然而，我花在刷手机上的时间减少了，花在真正喜欢的事情上的时间增加了。提前准备晚餐也让我的晚上变得更加平静。这套作息并没有解决所有问题，但它让我的空闲时间有了更清晰的安排。

I sometimes miss a study session or stay up later than planned. I no longer treat these moments as reasons to abandon the routine. Instead, I return to it the next evening. I have learned that a useful habit should support real life, not become another source of pressure. My evenings are still busy, but they now contain more activities that matter to me.

有时我会错过一次学习，或者比计划中睡得更晚。我不再把这些情况看作放弃整个作息的理由。相反，我会在第二天晚上重新开始。我已经明白，有用的习惯应该服务于真实生活，而不应该成为另一种压力来源。我的晚上仍然很忙碌，但现在包含了更多对我有意义的活动。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

Instead of trying to improve my entire life, I chose three simple activities: prepare dinner, spend fifteen minutes studying, and read a few pages before bed.
Instead of is followed by a gerund, while the colon introduces examples of the activities; spend is followed by an amount of time and a gerund.
Instead of后面接动名词；冒号用于引出具体活动；spend后面可以接时间长度，再接动名词表示花时间做某事。
I have learned that a useful habit should support real life, not become another source of pressure.
The present perfect have learned connects a past learning experience with the writer's present understanding, while not become contrasts two possible outcomes.
现在完成时have learned将过去的学习经历与作者现在的认识联系起来；not become用于对比两种可能的结果。
