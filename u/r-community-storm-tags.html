The Morning After the Windstorm 大风过后的早上

🧠 Learn the 6 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 6 个新词

Follow two neighbours who start talking after a windstorm, and notice how tags and short answers keep handing the conversation back. 跟着两位邻居在一场大风之后聊起来，留意附加疑问和简短回答怎样一次次把话头递回给对方。

B2 · CELPIP 7–8⏱ 20 minG13 Word order in indirect questions间接疑问句语序P10 Flat or wrong intonation; the important word is not stressed语调平，重点词不突出

📖 Key card知识卡
A tag or a short answer with the verb repeated is a small open door; a bare "yes" or "no" closes it.附加疑问、或重复动词的简短回答，是一扇半开的门；光秃秃的 yes 或 no 会把门关上。

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

The morning after the windstorm, half the street was outside in rubber boots, looking at branches. Mr. Okafor from number twelve was dragging a large limb off his lawn when Ming came out with a saw. They had lived three houses apart for two years and had never had a real conversation.

大风过后的那个早上，半条街的人都穿着雨靴站在外面看断枝。十二号的奥卡福先生正把一根大树枝从草坪上拖开，明拿着一把锯子走了出来。他们隔着三栋房子住了两年，从没好好聊过一次。

"That was quite a night, wasn't it?" he said. "It was. Our power went out at two." "Did it? Ours stayed on, oddly enough." "You're lucky. Is that from your tree?" "No, it isn't. It came from the park, I think. It's too big for one person, isn't it?"

「昨晚可真够呛，是吧？」他说。「是啊。我们家两点停电了。」「是吗？我们家倒没停，挺奇怪的。」「你运气好。这是你家树上的吗？」「不是。我想是从公园刮过来的。一个人弄不动，对吧？」

Ming offered to help, and they cut the branch into pieces small enough to carry. While they worked, the questions got easier. "You've got kids at the school on Birch Street, haven't you?" "Yes, I have. Two." "So have I. Grade three." "Have you? Then we've probably stood at the same gate a hundred times."

明主动帮忙，两人把树枝锯成能搬动的小段。干着活，问题也越来越好问了。「你家孩子在桦树街那所学校上学，是吧？」「是的，两个。」「我家也是，三年级。」「是吗？那我们大概在同一个校门口站过上百次了。」

Neither of them said anything clever. The tags and short answers did most of the work: each one handed the conversation back and made it easy to reply. A plain "yes" would have ended it. "Yes, I have. Two." left the door open.

两人谁也没说什么妙语。大部分活是附加疑问和简短回答干的：每一句都把话头递回去，让对方很容易接。光说一个 yes，对话就结束了；「Yes, I have. Two.」却把门留着。

By noon the lawn was clear. "We should do this more often, shouldn't we?" Mr. Okafor said. "Without the storm, though," said Ming. They both laughed, and the next weekend he knocked on her door with a pot of soup.

到中午草坪清干净了。「我们应该常这样，是吧？」奥卡福先生说。「不过别再刮风了，」明说。两人都笑了。下个周末，他端着一锅汤敲了她的门。

Words词

/ˈwɪndstɔːm/ n.
大风暴；狂风
a winter windstorm; after the windstorm
The windstorm knocked out power across the city. 大风让全市停了电。
/lɪm/ n.
大树枝；（人的）四肢
a tree limb; a fallen limb
A large limb fell on the fence. 一根大树枝砸在了栅栏上。
/dræɡ/ v.
拖；拽
drag sth off; drag a bag across the floor
We dragged the old sofa to the curb. 我们把旧沙发拖到了路边。
/ˈɒdli ɪˈnʌf/ adv. phr.
说来奇怪
oddly enough, it worked
Oddly enough, I slept better during the storm. 说来奇怪，刮风那晚我反而睡得更好。
/kwaɪt ə naɪt/ phr.
真够呛的一晚；不寻常的一晚
quite a day; quite a week
That was quite a week at work. 那一周上班可真够呛。
/hænd ˈbæk/ phr. v.
交回；递回
hand sth back to sb; hand the question back
The clerk handed back my passport. 职员把护照还给了我。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

"You've got kids at the school on Birch Street, haven't you?"
have got makes the tag with have: haven't you?, not don't you?
have got 的附加疑问用 have：haven't you?，不用 don't you?。
"So have I. Grade three."
So + auxiliary + subject agrees with a positive statement: I have too. The auxiliary copies have from the previous sentence.
So 加助动词加主语，表示「我也是」，助动词照抄上一句的 have。
"We should do this more often, shouldn't we?" Mr. Okafor said.
A modal makes its own tag: should → shouldn't we? Falling voice: a friendly suggestion, not a real question.
情态动词自己构成附加疑问：should 变 shouldn't we?。降调：友好的提议，不是真问。
