Report what people say, think or expect without naming them, using It is said that … and He is said to …, and choose the right infinitive for present and past. 不点名地转述别人的说法、看法或预期，用 It is said that … 和 He is said to …，并为现在和过去选对不定式。

People say the mayor will resign. 大家都说市长要辞职。
Conversational; vague source.口语，来源含糊。
It is said that the mayor will resign. 据说市长将辞职。
Formal, neutral.正式，中立。
The mayor is said to be planning to resign. 市长据说正打算辞职。
Formal, topic first, in progress.正式，话题在前，正在进行。
The mayor is going to resign. 市长要辞职了。
Stated as fact: you need a source.当事实讲，得有来源。

📏 The rule规则

📏 The rule in one line规则一句话
Two patterns report other people's views: It is + reporting verb + that + clause, and subject + is + reporting verb + to-infinitive. Use to do for the same time, to be doing for something in progress, and to have done for something earlier.转述别人的看法有两种句型：It is 加转述动词加 that 从句；主语加 is 加转述动词加不定式。同时发生用 to do，正在进行用 to be doing，更早发生用 to have done。

News, reports and careful writing use these patterns to give information without saying exactly who said it, and without taking a side. Chinese uses ju shuo or ju bao dao; English moves the source into a passive verb. It sounds more neutral than People say and less risky than stating the claim as fact.

新闻、报告和谨慎的写作用这两种句型传递信息，既不明说是谁讲的，也不表态。中文用「据说」「据报道」；英语把消息来源放进被动动词里。它比 People say 更中立，也比直接当事实陈述更稳妥。

Pattern one keeps the subject It: It is believed that the fire started in the kitchen. Pattern two puts the real topic first: The fire is believed to have started in the kitchen. The second is shorter and is preferred when the topic is a person or thing you are already talking about.

第一种用 It 作主语；第二种把真正的话题放在句首，更短，在已经谈到这个人或事物时更常用。

The infinitive carries the time. Same time as the reporting: She is said to live in Kelowna. In progress: The company is reported to be planning job cuts. Earlier: The painting is thought to have been stolen in 1990. Passive meaning needs to be done or to have been done.

不定式承担时间。与转述同时：to do；正在进行：to be doing；更早：to have done；被动意义：to be done 或 to have been done。

The reporting verb sets the tone. said and reported are neutral; believed and thought show an opinion; alleged is a legal claim not yet proved; feared adds worry; expected and due look ahead. Using alleged carefully matters: it does not mean the person did it.

转述动词决定语气。said、reported 中性；believed、thought 表看法；alleged 是未经证实的指控；feared 带担忧；expected、due 指向将来。alleged 要用得谨慎，它不等于此人确实做了。

be supposed to can work like be said to (The new café is supposed to be excellent), but it also means "expected by a rule or arrangement" (You're supposed to pay at the front). Context tells you which.

be supposed to 可以像 be said to 一样表示「据说」，也可以表示「按规定或安排应该」，靠语境区分。

Time of the reported factIt + passive + thatSubject + passive + to-infinitive
same time as nowIt is said that he lives abroad.He is said to live abroad.
in progress nowIt is reported that they are planning cuts.They are reported to be planning cuts.
earlierIt is thought that she left in May.She is thought to have left in May.

Examples例句

  • It is said that the old mill is haunted. 据说那座老磨坊闹鬼。
    Neutral hearsay.中性的传闻。
  • The old mill is said to be haunted. 那座老磨坊据说闹鬼。
    Same meaning, topic first.意思一样，话题放前。
  • It is believed that the fire started in a storage room. 据信火灾是从储藏室开始的。
    An official opinion.官方看法。
  • The fire is believed to have started in a storage room. 火灾据信起于储藏室。
    Earlier event: to have done.更早的事：to have done。
  • Around two thousand people are estimated to have attended the festival. 据估计约有两千人参加了这个节。
    An estimate about the past.对过去的估计。
  • The bank is reported to be considering a new fee for paper statements. 据报道，该银行正在考虑对纸质账单收取新费用。
    In progress: to be doing.正在进行：to be doing。
  • Interest rates are expected to stay the same until spring. 预计利率到春天都不会变。
    Looking ahead.指向将来。
  • It is generally agreed that the new bus route has helped. 大家普遍认为新公交线路有帮助。
    Wide agreement.普遍共识。
  • The building is thought to have been designed by a Scottish architect. 这栋楼据认为出自一位苏格兰建筑师之手。
    Earlier, passive meaning.更早，被动意义。
  • The suspect is alleged to have used a stolen card. 该嫌疑人被指控使用了盗来的卡。
    A claim not yet proved.尚未证实的指控。
  • Several hikers were feared to be lost in the fog. 有几名徒步者被担心在雾中迷了路。
    Worry, in the past.过去的担忧。
  • The new terminal is due to open in 2028. 新航站楼预计 2028 年启用。
    Official schedule.官方安排。
  • The restaurant on Main Street is supposed to be very good. 主街那家餐馆据说很不错。
    Informal: supposed to as hearsay.口语：supposed to 表据说。
  • It was once thought that the brain stopped changing in adulthood. 人们曾认为成年后大脑就不再变化了。
    A past belief now rejected.过去的看法，现已被推翻。

More pairs: same idea, different choice更多对比句组

She is said to work for the government. 据说她在政府工作。
Now.现在。
She is said to be working on a new book. 据说她在写一本新书。
In progress now.现在正在进行。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ He is said that he is very rich.
✓ He is said to be very rich. / It is said that he is very rich.
Do not mix the two patterns.
两个句型不要混用。
✗ The ship is believed to sink in 1912.
✓ The ship is believed to have sunk in 1912.
An earlier event needs to have done.
更早的事要用 to have done。
✗ It is said him to be a good doctor.
✓ He is said to be a good doctor.
Put the person at the start as the subject.
把人放在句首作主语。
✗ The company is reported cutting jobs.
✓ The company is reported to be cutting jobs.
After the reporting verb, use a to-infinitive.
转述动词后用 to 不定式。
✗ The man is alleged so he stole the car.
✓ The man is alleged to have stolen the car.
alleged takes to have done for a past act.
alleged 讲过去的行为接 to have done。
