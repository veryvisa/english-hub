Phones Locked Away Until Lunch 手机锁到午饭后

🧠 Learn the 5 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 5 个新词

Read a local news story about a phone ban at school and watch the present perfect link the past to now. 读一则学校禁手机的本地新闻，看现在完成时怎样把过去和现在连起来。

B2 · CELPIP 7–8⏱ 20 minG02 Present perfect and past simple confused现在完成时与一般过去时混用V12 Hedging is missing; claims sound absolute不会留余地，话说得太满

📖 Key card知识卡
Taking phones away seems to cut distraction in class, but whether it raises marks is still an open question.收走手机似乎能减少课堂上的分心，但能不能提高成绩，目前还没有定论。

Read and listen读与听

280 words
Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Since September, students at a secondary school on the edge of the city have started each day by putting their phones into a locked pouch. The pouches stay closed until lunch. The school introduced the rule as a one-year trial after teachers complained that notifications were interrupting almost every lesson.

从九月起，城边一所中学的学生每天一到校，就先把手机放进一个上锁的小袋。袋子要到午饭时才打开。老师们抱怨推送提醒几乎打断每一节课，学校随后把这条规定作为一年期的试行推出。

"In the first week, some students were furious," the principal said. "Several parents phoned to ask how they would reach their children in an emergency. We explained that the office has always taken calls, and that hasn't changed." Since then, she says, the complaints have mostly stopped.

「第一周有些学生非常生气，」校长说，「好几位家长打电话来问，万一有急事怎么联系孩子。我们解释说，学校办公室一直都接电话，这一点没有变。」她说，从那以后，投诉基本停了。

Teachers say the difference is easiest to see in the first ten minutes of class. "Before, half the room was checking their phones while I was taking attendance," one science teacher said. "Now they talk to each other, or they are just bored, and boredom is not the worst thing for a teenager." Several teachers believe their students are less distracted and that their attention span has improved, although they admit this is hard to measure.

老师们说，差别在上课头十分钟最明显。「以前我点名的时候，半个教室的人都在看手机，」一位科学老师说，「现在他们互相聊天，或者就是闲得无聊，而无聊对青少年来说并不是最坏的事。」几位老师认为学生没那么分心了，注意力持续的时间也变长了，但他们承认这很难量化。

Not everyone is convinced. Some older students say the rule treats them like children. "I'm not addicted, and I have never failed a test because of my phone," said one seventeen-year-old. "Learning to switch it off myself would be more useful than having someone else lock it away." Researchers who study screens in schools say the evidence is mixed: removing phones seems to reduce distraction, but it is less clear whether it raises marks.

并不是人人都服气。一些高年级学生说，这条规定把他们当小孩。「我没上瘾，也从没因为手机考砸过，」一名十七岁的学生说，「学会自己关机，比让别人替我锁起来更有用。」研究学校里屏幕使用的学者说，证据好坏参半：收走手机似乎能减少分心，但能不能提高成绩就不那么清楚了。

The school plans to review the rule in June. Until then, the pouches stay locked, and the most common sound in the corridor before lunch is no longer a notification but a conversation.

学校打算六月重新评估这条规定。在那之前，袋子照旧锁着；午饭前走廊里最常听到的声音，不再是推送提示音，而是说话声。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

The school introduced the rule as a one-year trial after teachers complained that notifications were interrupting almost every lesson.
Past simple for the finished events (introduced, complained); past continuous (were interrupting) for something that kept happening in the background.
已结束的事件用一般过去时（introduced, complained）；were interrupting 用过去进行时，表示当时在背景里反复发生的事。
We explained that the office has always taken calls, and that hasn't changed.
Inside reported speech the present perfect stays, because the habit started in the past and is still true now.
转述里保留现在完成时，因为这个做法从过去开始、到现在仍然成立。
