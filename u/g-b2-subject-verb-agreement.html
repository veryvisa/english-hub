Subject–verb agreement with long and collective subjects 长主语与集合名词的主谓一致

Choose the correct verb form when the subject is long, interrupted by modifiers, or a collective noun, by finding the true head noun and deciding whether the group acts as one unit or as individuals. 遇到长主语、被修饰语隔开的主语或集合名词时，能找准真正的中心词，并判断该群体是作为整体还是作为个体行动，从而选对谓语动词形式。

The team is ready to present its findings. 团队已准备好汇报其研究结果。
Singular: the team is seen as one unit with one shared state.单数：把团队看作一个整体，共享同一个状态。
The team are ready to present their findings. 团队成员们都准备好汇报他们各自的研究结果。
Plural: the focus is on the individual members, each prepared on their own.复数：关注的是每位成员，每个人各自做好了准备。
The team has won every match this season. 这支队伍本赛季每场比赛都赢了。
Singular: a collective achievement credited to the team as an institution.单数：作为一个机构的集体成就。
The team have been arguing about who should captain them. 队员们一直在争论谁该当队长。
Plural: the sentence describes disagreement among separate people.复数：描述的是不同个体之间的分歧。

📏 The rule规则

📏 The rule in one line规则一句话
The verb agrees with the head noun of the subject, not with the nearest noun, and a collective noun takes a singular verb when it acts as one unit and a plural verb when its members act individually.谓语动词与主语的中心词一致，而不是与离它最近的名词一致；集合名词作为整体行动时用单数，强调成员各自行动时用复数。

When a subject is followed by a long phrase beginning with of, with, along with, or as well as, the verb still agrees with the first noun. In 'The results of the three surveys conducted last year are surprising', the head is results, so the verb is plural. Chinese has no verb agreement, so the eye tends to latch onto the noun right before the verb. Train yourself to cross out the modifier mentally and check what remains.

当主语后面跟着以 of、with、along with、as well as 开头的长短语时，谓语仍然与第一个名词一致。例如 'The results of the three surveys conducted last year are surprising' 中中心词是 results，所以动词用复数。汉语没有动词形态变化，学习者的眼睛容易盯住紧挨着动词的那个名词。养成习惯：先在心里划掉修饰成分，再检查剩下的核心名词。

Relative clauses can hide the real subject. In 'She is one of the few lawyers who work on pro bono cases', who refers to lawyers, so the verb is plural. But in 'She is the only one of the lawyers who works on pro bono cases', the antecedent is one, so the verb is singular. The difference lies in whether the group or the single individual is what the clause describes, and 'the only one' is the usual signal for singular.

关系从句会掩盖真正的主语。'She is one of the few lawyers who work on pro bono cases' 中 who 指代 lawyers，所以用复数；而 'She is the only one of the lawyers who works on pro bono cases' 中先行词是 one，所以用单数。关键在于从句描述的是整个群体还是那个唯一的个体，'the only one' 通常是用单数的信号。

With collective nouns such as team, committee, government, and staff, choose singular when you see the group as a single body making a decision or taking an action, and plural when you stress the separate people inside it. 'The committee has reached a decision' treats it as one body, while 'The committee are arguing among themselves' pictures individuals. British English accepts both more freely, while American English leans strongly toward singular, so a consistent singular is the safer choice in formal writing.

对于 team、committee、government、staff 等集合名词，把群体看作一个整体做决定或采取行动时用单数，强调内部各个成员时用复数。'The committee has reached a decision' 把委员会看作一个整体，而 'The committee are arguing among themselves' 则描绘的是一个个成员。英式英语对两种用法都更宽容，美式英语则强烈倾向单数，所以在正式写作中保持单数是更稳妥的选择。

Some collective nouns are always plural in meaning: police, people, cattle, and the clergy take plural verbs, as in 'The police are investigating'. In contrast, nouns like news, politics, and economics look plural but take singular verbs. Learners often reverse these because they follow the written ending -s instead of the meaning. Memorise these as fixed exceptions rather than trying to derive them.

有些集合名词在意义上始终是复数：police、people、cattle、the clergy 要搭配复数动词，如 'The police are investigating'。相反，news、politics、economics 等词形看似复数，却搭配单数动词。学习者常因为只看词尾的 -s 而把这两类搞反。这些属于固定例外，应当作为整体记忆，而不要试图推导。

Expressions of quantity and proportion follow the noun that comes after them. 'A lot of the evidence is circumstantial' but 'A lot of the witnesses were nervous'; likewise 'Half of the cake has gone' and 'Half of the guests have left'. For 'a number of' the verb is plural, while 'the number of' takes a singular verb because number is the head noun. Check whether the phrase is a quantifier or a true noun phrase before choosing.

表示数量和比例的表达，谓语跟随其后面的名词。'A lot of the evidence is circumstantial'，但 'A lot of the witnesses were nervous'；同样 'Half of the cake has gone' 与 'Half of the guests have left'。'a number of' 后动词用复数，而 'the number of' 的中心词是 number，所以用单数。选择之前先判断这个短语是量词还是真正的名词短语。

Subjects joined by either...or, neither...nor, or not only...but also follow the proximity rule: the verb agrees with the noun closest to it. 'Neither the manager nor the employees were informed' but 'Neither the employees nor the manager was informed'. If the result sounds awkward, put the plural noun last, which is why writers often reorder such sentences.

由 either...or、neither...nor 或 not only...but also 连接的主语遵循就近原则：谓语与离它最近的名词一致。'Neither the manager nor the employees were informed'，但 'Neither the employees nor the manager was informed'。如果读起来别扭，可以把复数名词放在最后，这也是写作者常常调整语序的原因。

More pairs: same idea, different choice更多对比句组

A number of changes were made to the contract. 合同被做了一些修改。
Plural: 'a number of' works like 'several', so the plural noun controls the verb.复数：'a number of' 相当于 several，由复数名词决定动词。
The number of changes made to the contract was surprising. 合同被修改的数量令人惊讶。
Singular: 'the number' is the head noun, a single quantity being described.单数：'the number' 是中心词，描述的是一个单一的数量。
A great deal of the damage was caused by the flood. 大部分损失是由洪水造成的。
Singular: the verb follows the uncountable noun damage.单数：动词跟随不可数名词 damage。
A great many of the buildings were damaged by the flood. 许多建筑被洪水损坏了。
Plural: the verb follows the countable plural noun buildings.复数：动词跟随可数复数名词 buildings。
She is one of the few doctors who understand this condition. 她是少数几个了解这种病症的医生之一。
Plural: who refers back to doctors, the whole group of people described.复数：who 指回 doctors，即被描述的那群人。
She is the only one of the doctors who understands this condition. 她是这些医生中唯一了解这种病症的人。
Singular: 'the only one' is the antecedent, so the clause describes just her.单数：先行词是 'the only one'，从句只描述她一个人。
Neither the nurses nor the doctor was available. 护士和医生都不在。
Singular by proximity: the nearest noun is the singular doctor.就近原则用单数：离动词最近的名词是单数 doctor。
Neither the doctor nor the nurses were available. 医生和护士都不在。
Plural by proximity: the nearest noun is the plural nurses.就近原则用复数：离动词最近的名词是复数 nurses。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ The results of the experiment was disappointing.
✓ The results of the experiment were disappointing.
The head noun is results, not experiment. The nearest noun is a singular modifier, which tricks the ear.
中心词是 results 而不是 experiment。紧挨动词的是单数修饰成分，容易误导语感。
✗ The manager, along with his team, are planning a restructure.
✓ The manager, along with his team, is planning a restructure.
Phrases such as along with and as well as do not make the subject plural. The subject is still the manager.
along with、as well as 这类短语不会让主语变成复数，主语仍然只是 the manager。
✗ The police is investigating the case.
✓ The police are investigating the case.
Police is always plural in meaning and takes a plural verb, even though it has no -s ending.
police 在意义上始终是复数，即使没有 -s 词尾也要搭配复数动词。
✗ Politics are a complicated subject.
✓ Politics is a complicated subject.
Subjects ending in -ics that name a field of study, such as politics and economics, are treated as singular.
以 -ics 结尾、表示学科的名词，如 politics、economics，被视为单数。
✗ The number of students who fail the exam are increasing.
✓ The number of students who fail the exam is increasing.
The head noun is the number, a singular quantity. Only a number of means several and takes a plural verb.
中心词是 the number，是一个单数的量；只有 a number of 才表示若干，才搭配复数动词。
✗ She is one of the employees who has been complaining.
✓ She is one of the employees who have been complaining.
Who refers to employees, the whole group, so the relative clause verb is plural. Only 'the only one of' makes it singular.
who 指代 employees 这个整体，所以关系从句中的动词用复数；只有 'the only one of' 才会让它变成单数。
