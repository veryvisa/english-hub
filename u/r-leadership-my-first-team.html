What My First Team Taught Me 我的第一个团队教会了我什么

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

When I was first asked to lead a small team, I was thrilled and, frankly, terrified. I had been a reliable individual contributor for years, and I assumed that managing others would simply mean being more decisive. I walked into my first meeting with a long list of instructions, convinced that confidence was the main thing people needed from me.

当我第一次被要求带领一个小团队时，我既兴奋又坦白地说有些害怕。多年来我一直是一名可靠的个人贡献者，并且以为管理他人只是意味着更果断。我带着一长串指示走进第一次会议，深信人们最需要我的就是自信。

It did not go well. Although nobody objected out loud, I could sense the silence in the room, and within a few weeks a senior colleague took me aside. She suggested, gently, that I had not asked a single question since taking the job. Had I done so, she said, I might have discovered that the team already knew how to fix the problem I was so keen to solve.

结果并不顺利。虽然没有人当面反对，但我能感觉到房间里的沉默，几周之内，一位资深同事把我拉到一旁。她温和地指出，我自从上任以来没有问过一个问题。她说，如果我问过，也许就会发现团队早已知道如何解决我急于解决的那个问题。

That conversation changed my approach. I began to hold short one-to-one chats, and I forced myself to speak last in meetings. It felt uncomfortable at first, since I was used to being the person with the answers. Gradually, though, people began to bring me problems earlier, before they grew into crises.

那次谈话改变了我的做法。我开始进行简短的一对一交流，并强迫自己在会议中最后发言。起初这让我感到不自在，因为我习惯了做那个有答案的人。不过渐渐地，人们开始更早地把问题带给我，在它们演变成危机之前。

Looking back, I think my mistake was confusing authority with certainty. Leaders are not required to know everything; they are required to create the conditions in which the right answer can surface. That said, I still catch myself reaching for instructions when I am under pressure, and I suspect that habit never fully disappears.

回想起来，我认为我的错误是把权威与确定性混为一谈。领导者并不需要知道一切；他们需要创造让正确答案浮现的条件。话虽如此，当我承受压力时，我仍会发现自己下意识地想发号施令，我猜这个习惯永远不会完全消失。

Once people take ownership of a problem, they usually solve it. 一旦人们对问题负起责任，通常就能解决它。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

Had I done so, she said, I might have discovered that the team already knew how to fix the problem I was so keen to solve.
’Had I done so’ is an inverted third conditional without ‘if’, and ‘the problem I was so keen to solve’ contains a contact relative clause.
’Had I done so’ 是省略 ‘if’ 的倒装第三类条件句，'the problem I was so keen to solve' 含有省略关系代词的关系从句。
Leaders are not required to know everything; they are required to create the conditions in which the right answer can surface.
The semicolon joins two parallel passive clauses, and ‘in which’ introduces a relative clause with a fronted preposition.
分号连接两个并列的被动句，'in which' 引导介词前置的关系从句。
