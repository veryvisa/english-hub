Q: For about two months I have been feeling low. I cry easily, I wake up tired even after eight hours, I need a nap every afternoon, and I have stopped enjoying the cooking I used to love. Small things make me feel overwhelmed. My husband says it is just stress and I should be grateful for our life here. If I were stronger, would I feel like this? — Lan

问：大约两个月来我一直情绪低落。我动不动就哭，睡够八小时醒来还是累，每天下午都得睡一觉，以前爱做饭，现在也不喜欢了。小事都让我觉得招架不住。丈夫说这只是压力，我该珍惜我们在这里的生活。要是我更坚强，还会这样吗？——兰

A: Lan, thank you for writing. First, feeling like this is not a test of strength. Stress is a normal response to pressure, and it usually rises and falls with the pressure. What you describe sounds different. It has lasted for weeks, it is affecting your sleep and your pleasure in things you love, and it is not going away. Those are warning signs worth taking seriously.

答：兰，谢谢你来信。首先，有这种感觉不是在考验你坚不坚强。压力是对压力源的正常反应，通常随着压力起落。你描述的不一样：它持续了几个星期，影响了你的睡眠和对喜爱之事的乐趣，而且没有消退。这些都是值得认真对待的警示信号。

Your husband may be trying to comfort you. Many of us grew up in families where low mood was something you kept to yourself, and where talking about it felt like complaining. But if you had a cough that lasted two months, nobody would tell you to be grateful. You would see a doctor. The same is true here.

你丈夫也许是想安慰你。我们很多人在这样的家庭里长大：情绪低落是要自己憋着的，说出来就像在抱怨。可要是你咳嗽了两个月，没人会叫你知足，你会去看医生。这里也是同样的道理。

So here is what I would do if I were in your position. Book an appointment with your family doctor and say exactly what you told me, in the same words. Ask whether counselling in your own language is available; many communities offer it. And tell one person you trust, not so that they can fix it, but so that you are not coping alone.

所以，如果我处在你的位置，我会这么做：预约家庭医生，把跟我说的原原本本再说一遍。问问有没有用母语进行的心理咨询，很多社区都有。再告诉一个你信任的人，不是让对方来解决，而是让你不必一个人扛。

If at any point you feel that you might harm yourself, do not wait for an appointment: call your local crisis line or go to the nearest emergency department. I wish someone had said all this to me years ago. Asking for help is not a sign that you have failed. It is how people get better.

如果任何时候你觉得自己可能会伤害自己，别等预约：打当地的危机热线，或者去最近的急诊。我真希望多年前就有人对我说过这些。求助不说明你失败了，人就是这样好起来的。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

But if you had a cough that lasted two months, nobody would tell you to be grateful.
Unreal present used as a comparison: had in the if-clause, would in the result. The comparison makes the point without arguing.
用与现在事实相反的条件句作类比：if 从句用 had，结果句用 would。不争辩，只类比。
So here is what I would do if I were in your position.
If I were in your position is a polite frame for advice; what I would do is a noun clause in statement order.
If I were in your position 是给建议的礼貌框架；what I would do 是陈述语序的名词从句。
