Why Switching Tasks Costs More Than It Feels 切换任务的代价比感觉的大

🧠 Learn the 5 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 5 个新词

Read a short science explainer on task switching and learn the word families that carry its argument. 读一篇讲任务切换的科普短文，学会撑起论证的几个词族。

B2 · CELPIP 7–8⏱ 20 minV03 Word family: the wrong part of speech词性用错G01 Articles left out or over-used冠词漏用、滥用

📖 Key card知识卡
Every switch between tasks leaves part of your attention behind, so fewer switches usually mean better work.每切换一次任务，都会有一部分注意力留在上一件事上，所以切换越少，活通常干得越好。

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

You are writing a report. A notification appears in the corner of the screen, you glance at it, answer a quick message and go back to the report. The whole thing took thirty seconds. So why does it feel as if you have lost ten minutes?

你在写报告。屏幕角落弹出一条推送，你瞥了一眼，回了条短消息，又回到报告上。整件事花了三十秒。那为什么感觉像是丢了十分钟？

Psychologists call this the cost of task switching. When you move from one task to another, your brain has to put away the rules of the first task and load the rules of the second. Part of your attention stays behind, still thinking about the message you just answered. The result is slower work and more mistakes, even though you feel busy the whole time.

心理学家把这叫作任务切换的代价。从一件事转到另一件事时，大脑得把第一件事的规则收起来，再装上第二件事的规则。一部分注意力会留在原地，还在想你刚回的那条消息。结果是干得更慢、错得更多，尽管你一直觉得自己很忙。

A common misunderstanding is that some people are simply good at multitasking. Most studies find that people who multitask a lot are not better at it; they are often more easily distracted. Their confidence in their own flexibility is higher than their actual performance. The expectation that we can answer everything at once is the problem, not a lack of talent.

一个常见的误解是，有些人天生擅长一心多用。多数研究发现，经常一心多用的人并没有更擅长，反而往往更容易分心。他们对自己灵活性的信心，高于实际表现。问题出在「什么都能同时回」这种预期上，而不是缺天分。

The good news is that small changes bring real improvement. Turning off notifications for an hour, keeping your phone in another room and checking email at fixed times all reduce the number of switches. This is not mainly about willpower. Awareness helps too: simply noticing the urge to check your phone gives you a moment to decide not to.

好消息是，小改变就能带来实在的进步。关掉推送一小时、把手机放在另一个房间、在固定时间查邮件，都能减少切换次数。这主要不是靠意志力。觉察也有用：只要注意到自己想看手机的冲动，你就多了一刻可以决定不看。

None of this means you should never switch tasks. Some jobs require it. But if your work needs deep thought, the most reliable way to protect your attention span is to give it fewer chances to break.

这些都不是说永远不该切换任务。有些工作本来就要求切换。但如果你的工作需要深入思考，保护注意力最靠得住的办法，就是少给它被打断的机会。

/tɑːsk ˈswɪtʃɪŋ/ n.

Constant task switching made the afternoon feel twice as long. 不停地切换任务，让一下午显得有两倍长。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

Their confidence in their own flexibility is higher than their actual performance.
Two nouns built from adjectives (confident → confidence, flexible → flexibility) let the writer compare a feeling with a result in one short sentence.
两个由形容词变来的名词（confident → confidence，flexible → flexibility），让作者用一句短话把「感觉」和「结果」放在一起比。
So why does it feel as if you have lost ten minutes?
as if + present perfect: the feeling is about a loss that seems to have happened up to now.
as if 后接现在完成时：感觉上像是到此刻已经丢了什么。
