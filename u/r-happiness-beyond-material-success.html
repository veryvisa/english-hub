Beyond Material Success 超越物质成功

🧠 Learn the 4 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 4 个新词

A good life depends less on accumulating possessions than on developing meaningful relationships, personal freedom, and a sense of purpose. 美好生活与其说取决于积累物质财富，不如说取决于建立有意义的人际关系、拥有个人自由和找到人生目标。

For generations, people have associated a successful life with visible achievements: a comfortable home, a respected career, financial security, and the freedom to travel. These things certainly matter. Money can reduce anxiety, provide choices, and protect families from unexpected difficulties. Yet there is an important question worth considering: when does having more stop making life better? A person may achieve everything society admires and still feel strangely dissatisfied.

几代人以来，人们一直将成功的人生与看得见的成就联系起来：舒适的住所、受人尊敬的职业、经济保障以及旅行的自由。这些东西当然很重要。金钱能够减轻焦虑、提供选择，并保护家庭免受意外困难的影响。然而，有一个值得思考的重要问题：拥有更多东西究竟从什么时候开始不再让生活变得更好？一个人可能实现了社会所推崇的一切，却仍然感到莫名的不满足。

Psychological research suggests that material comfort contributes to happiness, particularly when basic needs are not yet met. However, the relationship between wealth and well-being is more complicated than popular slogans suggest. Once people have sufficient resources, additional possessions may bring diminishing emotional returns. The excitement of buying something new often fades, whereas meaningful relationships and a sense of purpose tend to provide more lasting satisfaction.

心理学研究表明，物质舒适有助于幸福感，尤其是在基本需求尚未得到满足的时候。然而，财富与福祉之间的关系比流行口号所暗示的更加复杂。当人们拥有足够的资源后，额外的物品可能带来越来越有限的情感回报。购买新东西带来的兴奋往往会消退，而有意义的人际关系和人生目标通常能够提供更加持久的满足感。

Consider two people with similar incomes. One spends most evenings working towards another promotion, while the other protects time for friendships, exercise, and creative interests. Neither approach is automatically superior. Ambition can give life direction, and professional achievement can be deeply rewarding. That said, if success consistently requires sacrificing sleep, relationships, and personal freedom, its overall value deserves serious examination.

想象两个人的收入相似。其中一个人把大部分晚上都花在争取下一次晋升上，另一个人则保护自己的时间，用于友谊、锻炼和创造性兴趣。两种方式都不一定更好。抱负能够为生活指明方向，职业成就也能带来深刻的满足感。话虽如此，如果成功始终要求人们牺牲睡眠、人际关系和个人自由，那么就值得认真审视它的整体价值。

Perhaps a better definition of success would include having enough resources, enough room to breathe, and enough time for the people and activities that make existence meaningful. This does not mean rejecting ambition or material comfort. Rather, it means deciding what these things are supposed to serve. A good life is not necessarily an impressive one. By and large, it is a life in which our daily choices reflect what we genuinely value.

也许，对成功更好的定义应该包括拥有足够的资源、足够的喘息空间，以及足够的时间陪伴那些让生命变得有意义的人和参与那些有价值的活动。这并不意味着拒绝抱负或物质舒适，而是意味着决定这些东西究竟应该服务于什么。美好生活不一定是令人印象深刻的生活。总的来说，它是一种日常选择能够反映我们真正重视之事的生活。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

Once people have sufficient resources, additional possessions may bring diminishing emotional returns.
The introductory time clause uses the present simple, while the main clause uses may to express a qualified possibility rather than a certainty.
句首的时间状语从句使用一般现在时，主句使用情态动词 may，表达有限定条件的可能性，而不是确定事实。
Neither approach is automatically superior.
Neither is a singular negative determiner meaning not one or the other, so it takes the singular verb is.
Neither 是表示两者都不的单数限定词，因此后面使用单数动词 is。
