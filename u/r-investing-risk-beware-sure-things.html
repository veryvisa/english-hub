Beware the Sure Thing 警惕“稳赚不赔”

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Scroll through almost any social media feed and you will soon meet someone who claims to have found an easy route to wealth. The setting is usually glamorous, the tone is relaxed, and the message is simple: join now, because this opportunity cannot fail. In my view, that last phrase should end the conversation. Any investment that offers a higher possible reward generally carries a greater chance of disappointment.

随便刷一刷社交媒体，你很快就会遇到某个自称找到了轻松致富门路的人。背景通常光鲜亮丽，语气轻松随意，传达的信息也很简单：马上加入，因为这个机会不可能失败。在我看来，听到最后这句话，对话就该结束了。任何可能带来更高回报的投资，一般也伴随着更大的失望风险。

Admittedly, not everyone who talks about money online is trying to deceive. Some explain basic ideas clearly and encourage people who might otherwise never learn about them. The trouble is that sensible advice tends to be dull. ‘Save regularly, spread your money and be patient’ rarely attracts as many viewers as a dramatic story about overnight success. We also hear far less from those who followed the same path and lost.

诚然，并非所有在网上谈钱的人都存心欺骗。有些人把基本概念讲得很清楚，也鼓励了那些原本可能永远不会去了解这些知识的人。问题在于，明智的建议往往很枯燥。“定期储蓄、分散资金、保持耐心”很少能像一夜暴富的传奇故事那样吸引那么多观众。而那些走同样的路却亏了钱的人，我们听到的声音要少得多。

What makes these promises so powerful is the fear of missing out. When others seem to be cashing in, caution can look like cowardice. Under that pressure, people may skip the questions they would normally ask. Who is making money from this recommendation? How could I lose, and how much? Can I withdraw my funds when I want to? If the answers are vague, walking away is not timid. It is simply prudent.

这些承诺之所以如此有吸引力，是因为人们害怕错过。当别人似乎都在从中获利时，谨慎看起来就像胆怯。在这种压力下，人们可能略过平时一定会问的问题。谁能从这条推荐中赚钱？我可能怎样亏钱，会亏多少？我能在想取钱的时候把资金取出来吗？如果回答含糊不清，转身离开并不是胆小，而只是审慎。

None of this means that we should avoid risk altogether. Keeping every penny in cash may feel safe, yet its purchasing power can shrink over the years. The point is to take risks that you understand, in amounts that you can bear, over a time horizon that gives you room to recover. Only then can risk become a tool rather than a trap. A genuine opportunity will survive a few awkward questions; a false one usually depends on your not asking them.

这一切并不意味着我们应该完全回避风险。把每一分钱都留作现金也许让人觉得安全，但它的购买力可能逐年缩水。关键在于：承担你理解的风险，投入你承受得起的金额，并留出足以让你恢复元气的投资期限。只有这样，风险才能成为工具而不是陷阱。真正的机会经得起几个尖锐问题的追问；虚假的机会则往往指望你不去问。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

What makes these promises so powerful is the fear of missing out.
This wh-cleft sentence uses a ‘What...’ clause as the subject so that the cause comes at the end for emphasis.
这是 what 引导的分裂句，以 What... 从句作主语，把原因放在句末加以强调。
Only then can risk become a tool rather than a trap.
Because ‘Only then’ begins the sentence, the modal ‘can’ moves in front of the subject ‘risk’.
Only then 位于句首，因此情态动词 can 要提到主语 risk 之前，构成倒装。
