A Budget That Runs Itself 一个自己运转的预算

🧠 Learn the 2 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 2 个新词

Listen to a short community talk on a budget that does not depend on willpower; notice how a speaker uses articles and phrasal verbs to sound natural. 听一段社区讲座，讲一个不靠意志力的预算；留意讲者怎样用冠词和短语动词让话听起来自然。

📖 Key card知识卡

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Good evening, everyone, and thank you for coming out on a cold night. I am going to talk for ten minutes about one idea, and it is not a spreadsheet. When my family arrived here, I set up the most detailed budget you have ever seen: forty categories, colours, formulas. It lasted three weeks.

大家晚上好，谢谢各位冒着寒夜出来。接下来十分钟我只讲一个想法，而且不是电子表格。我们一家刚来时，我做了一个你们见过的最细的预算：四十个类别，还有颜色、公式。坚持了三个星期。

The problem with most budgets is that they depend on willpower, and willpower is usually gone by Friday. So here is the alternative: a budget that runs itself. On payday, before you can spend anything, money moves automatically into three places. The first is for the bills that come every month. The second is savings, which you set aside as an emergency fund until it could cover a few months of life. The third is everything else, and that is the only account your card is connected to.

大多数预算的问题在于靠意志力，而意志力通常撑不到周五。所以换个办法：一个自己运转的预算。发薪日那天，在你能花任何一分钱之前，钱就自动转进三个地方。第一个用来付每月都来的账单。第二个是储蓄，你把它存作应急金，直到够应付几个月的生活。第三个是其余的一切，你的卡只连着这个账户。

Why does it work? Because you no longer have to make a choice every time you see something nice. If the third account is empty on the twentieth, you are not in trouble; you simply wait. You will run into months like that. Nobody gets this right the first time, and that is fine. Look at the numbers once a month, not every day, and adjust the amounts.

为什么管用？因为你不必每次看到好东西都做一次选择。要是到了二十号第三个账户空了，你也没惹上麻烦，等着就是。这样的月份你一定会遇到。没有人第一次就做对，这很正常。一个月看一次数字，不用天天看，然后调整金额。

Two small things make a big difference. First, sort out the fees: if your account charges a monthly fee, or you keep going into overdraft, deal with that before anything else. Second, if you have any debt on a credit card, pay it before you build savings, because the interest on a card is almost always higher than anything your savings will earn. That is the whole talk. Questions?

有两件小事影响很大。第一，把费用理清：如果账户收月费，或者你老是透支，先处理这个。第二，如果信用卡上有欠款，先还它再攒钱，因为信用卡的利息几乎总是比你储蓄能挣的高。我就讲这么多。有问题吗？

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

The second is savings, which you set aside as an emergency fund until it could cover a few months of life.
the second is one of a known list, so the. which… adds information about savings.
the second 指已知清单里的一项，用 the。which… 补充说明储蓄。
Second, if you have any debt on a credit card, pay it before you build savings, because the interest on a card is almost always higher than anything your savings will earn.
a credit card / a card: any card in general. the interest: the specific interest on that card.
a credit card、a card 泛指任何一张卡；the interest 特指那张卡上的利息。
