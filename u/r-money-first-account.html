What We Signed on Day One 落地第一天我们签了什么

Read a newcomer's story about a first bank account and see the present perfect and past simple tell two different parts of it. 读一位新移民开第一个银行账户的故事，看现在完成时和一般过去时各讲故事的哪一部分。

B2 · CELPIP 7–8⏱ 20 minG02 Present perfect and past simple confused现在完成时与一般过去时混用V01 Wrong collocation built word by word逐词直译的错搭配

📖 Key card知识卡

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

The week we landed, my husband and I walked into a bank branch near our rental with two passports and a folder of documents. We came out an hour later with a chequing account, two debit cards and a package of products we had not asked for. It took me six months to understand what we had signed.

落地那一周，我和丈夫带着两本护照和一夹子文件，走进租房附近的一家银行网点。一个小时后出来，我们有了一个支票账户、两张借记卡，还有一套我们没要过的产品。我花了六个月才弄明白我们签了什么。

The account had a monthly fee. It was small, so I did not notice it at first. Then I looked at a statement properly and saw that we were also paying for an overdraft service we had never used, and for a credit card with a yearly fee that nobody had explained. None of it was hidden; it was all in the papers. We had simply signed without reading.

账户有月费。数额不大，所以一开始我没注意。后来我认真看了一张对账单，才发现我们还在为一项从没用过的透支服务付钱，还有一张年费没人解释过的信用卡。这些都没藏着，全写在文件里。我们只是没读就签了。

I made a complaint, politely, at the same branch. The adviser took responsibility at once. She removed the overdraft service and moved us to an account without a monthly fee, because by then we had a regular salary going in. She also told me something I have not forgotten: "Most people never ask. The ones who ask usually get a better deal."

我在同一个网点客气地提了投诉。顾问当场认了责任，取消了透支服务，还把我们换到一个不收月费的账户，因为那时我们已经有固定工资入账了。她还说了一句我一直记得的话：「大多数人从来不问。问的人通常能拿到更好的条件。」

Since then I have read every statement in the first week of the month. It takes ten minutes. I have found two small errors and one transaction I did not recognise, which the bank reversed within days. My advice to anyone who has just arrived: open the account you need on day one, but make a decision about the extras only after you have lived here for a while.

从那以后，每个月第一周我都会把对账单读一遍，花十分钟。我发现过两处小错，还有一笔我不认识的交易，银行几天内就退回了。给刚落地的人一句建议：第一天就开你需要的账户，但附加的东西，等在这里住上一段时间再做决定。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

Then I looked at a statement properly and saw that we were also paying for an overdraft service we had never used, and for a credit card with a yearly fee that nobody had explained.
Two for phrases share paying. had never used and had explained go back before the moment she looked.
两个 for 短语共用 paying。had never used 和 had explained 都发生在她查看之前。
My advice to anyone who has just arrived: open the account you need on day one, but make a decision about the extras only after you have lived here for a while.
has just arrived: recent, linked to now. After after, the present perfect stresses that the living must be finished first.
has just arrived 表示刚刚、与现在相关。after 后用现在完成时，强调「住一段时间」要先完成。
