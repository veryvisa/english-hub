Viral Flood Photo Was Taken Three Years Ago 刷屏的水灾照片拍于三年前

🧠 Learn the 4 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 4 个新词

Read a local news report about a viral photo, and see how the present perfect and the past simple divide the story between now and then. 读一篇关于刷屏照片的本地新闻，看现在完成时和一般过去时怎样把故事分成「现在」和「当时」。

B2 · CELPIP 7–8⏱ 20 minG02 Present perfect and past simple confused现在完成时与一般过去时混用G12 Passive voice avoided or over-used被动语态不会用

📖 Key card知识卡
A real photo can still be false news: the picture was true once, and only the date and the caption have changed.照片是真的，也可能是假新闻：画面当年是真的，变的只是日期和说明文字。

Read and listen读与听

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

A photo of cars half under water on a downtown street, which was shared thousands of times over the weekend, was taken during a storm three years ago, not during Saturday's heavy rain, city officials have confirmed.

市政官员证实，一张市中心街道上汽车半截泡在水里的照片，上周末被转发了几千次，其实是三年前一场暴雨时拍的，而不是拍于周六的大雨。

The image first appeared on Saturday evening with the caption "Downtown right now. Stay home!" Within hours it had gone viral, and several residents said they had cancelled plans or driven long detours to avoid the area. "My daughter has never been so frightened of rain," one mother said. "She saw it at school and called me in tears."

这张图周六傍晚首次出现，配的说明是「市中心此刻。别出门！」。几个小时内它就刷了屏，好几位居民说，他们取消了安排，或者绕了很远的路避开那一带。「我女儿从来没有这么怕过下雨，」一位母亲说，「她在学校看到了，哭着给我打电话。」

In fact, the streets drained normally on Saturday. Fact-checkers traced the picture to a local news report from three years ago with a reverse image search. The account that posted it has since taken it down, but copies are still circulating, and some have been shared again in the last two days with new, even more alarming captions.

实际上，周六街道排水一切正常。事实核查员用以图搜图，查到这张照片出自三年前的一则本地新闻。最早发布它的账号已经删掉了帖子，但副本还在流传，过去两天里有些又被转发，配上了更吓人的新说明。

One local news site also published the photo on Saturday night and has now issued a correction. "We made a mistake. We should have checked the date before we posted it," its editor said. "We have changed our process: no image goes up until we know where it came from."

一家本地新闻网站周六晚上也发了这张照片，现在已经发布了更正。「我们犯了错。发之前应该核对日期的，」该网站的编辑说，「我们已经改了流程：不知道图片从哪来，就不发。」

The city reminds residents that real flood warnings come through official channels: its website and local radio. Officials have not said whether they will try to find the person who first shared the image. The photographer who took the original picture said it had been misleading people "for three years, every time it rains."

市政府提醒居民，真正的洪水预警走的是官方渠道：市政府网站和本地电台。官员们没有说是否会追查最早转发这张图的人。原照片的摄影者说，这张图「三年来，每逢下雨」就会误导人。

Words词

/ˈkæpʃn/ n.
（图片的）说明文字
a photo caption; add a caption; with the caption "…"
The caption said the photo was taken last night. 说明文字写着这张照片是昨晚拍的。
/kəˈrekʃn/ n.
更正
issue a correction; publish a correction; a correction to a story
The paper printed a correction the next day. 报纸第二天刊登了更正。
/ˌmɪsˈliːdɪŋ/ adj.
误导人的
misleading information; a misleading headline; mislead sb
The headline was not false, but it was misleading. 那个标题不算假，却有误导性。
/teɪk ˈdaʊn/ phr. v.
（从网上）撤下；删除
take down a post; take sth down
The video was taken down after a few hours. 视频几个小时后就被撤下了。
/ˈsɜːkjəleɪt/ v.
流传；传播
a rumour circulates; circulate online
Copies of the letter are still circulating. 那封信的副本还在流传。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

The account that posted it has since taken it down, but copies are still circulating
has since taken: present perfect with since as an adverb meaning "between then and now". What matters is the present result: the post is gone.
has since taken：现在完成时，since 作副词，意思是「从那时到现在」。重点是现在的结果：帖子已经没了。
We should have checked the date before we posted it
should have + past participle criticises a past action that did not happen. Compare: we checked (it happened) and we should have checked (it did not).
should have 加过去分词，批评过去该做却没做的事。对比：we checked（做了）与 we should have checked（没做）。
