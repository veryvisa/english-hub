Two summers ago my husband and I took our parents on their first trip to Europe. I planned the itinerary for months: nine cities in nine days, every train booked, every museum ticket printed in advance. I was proud of it. My father, I later realised, was quietly frightened of it.

前年夏天，我和丈夫带双方父母第一次去欧洲。行程我规划了好几个月：九天九座城，每趟火车都订好了，每张博物馆门票都提前打印。我挺得意。后来我才明白，我父亲其实暗暗被这个行程吓到了。

By day four, he had stopped getting off the coach. He sat in hotel lobbies with a cup of tea while the rest of us rushed through yet another old town in the middle of peak season, shoulder to shoulder with thousands of other tourists. My mother bought the same souvenir fridge magnet in three countries, and none of us could remember which cathedral was which.

到第四天，他已经不下大巴了。我们其余人在旺季里又冲进一座老城、跟成千上万的游客肩挨着肩时，他就坐在酒店大堂喝茶。我母亲在三个国家买了同一款冰箱贴纪念品，我们谁也记不清哪座大教堂是哪座。

On the long-haul flight home I tried to work out what had gone wrong. If I had planned half as much, we would have seen twice as much. If we had stayed in one place for a week, my father might have found a café he liked and gone back every morning. I wish I had asked my parents what they actually wanted, instead of deciding what a first trip should look like.

坐长途航班回家时，我试着想清楚是哪里出了错。要是我只计划一半，我们反而能看到两倍的东西。要是我们在一个地方住上一周，我父亲也许能找到一家喜欢的咖啡馆，每天早上都去。真希望当初问问父母他们到底想要什么，而不是由我来决定第一次出国该是什么样子。

Last spring we tried again. We rented a flat in one small town off the beaten track, took a slow train once, and left most days empty. My father learned to order coffee in the local language and was greeted by name by the end of the week. My mother made friends with the woman who ran the bakery.

今年春天我们又去了一次。我们在一个游客不常去的小镇租了套公寓，只坐过一趟慢车，大多数日子什么都不安排。我父亲学会了用当地话点咖啡，到周末时店里的人已经能叫出他的名字。我母亲和开面包店的那位女士成了朋友。

It cost less, it produced less waste, and it was the first holiday I did not need another holiday to recover from. I still love planning. I just plan less now, and I leave room for the day nobody planned, which is usually the one we talk about afterwards.

这次花得更少，产生的垃圾更少，而且是我第一次不需要再休个假来恢复的假期。我还是喜欢做计划，只是现在计划得少了，给那个谁也没安排的日子留出空间——事后我们常聊起的，往往就是那一天。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

If I had planned half as much, we would have seen twice as much.
Third conditional: If + had + past participle, would have + past participle for an unreal past. The two halves mirror each other: half as much / twice as much.
第三条件句：If + had + 过去分词，would have + 过去分词，说的是与过去事实相反的情况。前后两半对称：half as much／twice as much。
I wish I had asked my parents what they actually wanted, instead of deciding what a first trip should look like.
I wish + past perfect is regret about the past. what they actually wanted is an embedded question, so the word order is statement order, not what did they want.
I wish + 过去完成时，表示对过去的遗憾。what they actually wanted 是嵌入的疑问句，用陈述语序，不是 what did they want。
