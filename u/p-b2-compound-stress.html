Stress in compounds and phrasal verbs 复合词与短语动词的重音

Stress compound nouns on the first part and phrasal verbs on the particle, so that a GREENhouse is not a green HOUSE and set UP is not SETup. 复合名词重读前半，短语动词重读小品词，让 greenhouse 不变成「绿色的房子」，set up 不变成 setup。

B2 · CELPIP 7–8⏱ 20 minP08 Word stress in the wrong place词重音放错V04 Phrasal verbs avoided; the formal verb sounds stiff回避短语动词，说话太书面

📏 The rule规则

📏 The point in one line一句话要领
Compound nouns are usually stressed on the first part: BUS stop, PARKing lot, HOT dog. Adjective + noun phrases stress the noun: a hot DOG (a dog that is hot). Phrasal verbs stress the particle: turn it OFF, set UP; the matching nouns stress the first part: a SETup, a BREAKdown.复合名词一般重读前半；形容词加名词的短语重读名词；短语动词重读小品词，由它变来的名词重读前半。
  • Easy to miss易漏 ✗ a hot DOG (meaning the food) → ✓ a HOT dog

📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Open the full explanation: 5 points展开细讲：5 段讲解

In an office kitchen someone says, "There's a hot DOG in the fridge," and everyone looks for an animal. The speaker meant a HOT dog, the sausage. English uses stress to show whether two words have become one name. When they have, the first part takes the stress and the second goes quiet; when they are still a description, the noun at the end is stressed as usual.

办公室里有人说冰箱里有个 hot DOG，大家开始找动物；他说的其实是 HOT dog，香肠。英语用重音表示两个词是否已经合成一个名称：合成了就重读前半、后半放轻；还只是描述，就照常重读末尾的名词。

Compare: a BLACKboard (for writing on) and a black BOARD (any board that is black); a DARKroom (for photos) and a dark ROOM (a room with no light). Common compounds you use every day follow the first-part rule: CREDit card, PHONE number, BUS station, SHOPping mall, BIRTHday party, WAITing room.

对比：用来写字的黑板与任何黑色的板，暗房与没灯的房间。天天用的复合词都按前重规律走。

Phrasal verbs work the other way. The verb is light and the particle carries the stress: pick UP, find OUT, put it OFF, give it BACK. When the object is a pronoun, the particle still takes the stress at the end: turn it OFF. Chinese speakers tend to stress the verb because it looks like the "real" word, which makes the particle disappear and the meaning unclear: put OFF (delay) and PUT (place) are very different.

短语动词反过来：动词轻、小品词重。宾语是代词时，小品词仍在句末重读。华人常重读动词，因为它看起来是「真正的词」，结果小品词听不见，意思也含糊。

Many phrasal verbs have a noun form written as one word or with a hyphen, and the stress flips to the front: to break DOWN / a BREAKdown, to log IN / your LOGin, to work OUT / a WORKout, to check IN / the CHECK-in desk. Hearing the stress tells you whether someone is describing an action or naming a thing.

很多短语动词有对应的名词，写成一个词或带连字符，重音翻到前面。听重音就知道对方是在说动作还是在说东西。

This links to vocabulary: phrasal verbs make your English sound natural instead of stiff (put out the fire rather than extinguish), but only if people can hear the particle. A strong, clear particle is what makes a phrasal verb recognisable at speed.

这和词汇相关：短语动词让英语自然而不书面，但前提是对方听得到小品词。小品词清楚有力，快语速里短语动词才认得出。
