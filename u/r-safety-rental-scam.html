The Flat That Was Too Cheap 便宜得离谱的公寓

🧠 Learn the 4 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 4 个新词

Follow a lunchtime conversation about a rental listing that is too good to be true, and learn the phrasal verbs and conditionals people use to talk a friend out of a mistake. 跟着一段午饭时的对话，看一则好得不像真的租房广告，学会劝朋友别犯错时常用的短语动词和条件句。

B2 · CELPIP 7–8⏱ 20 minG07 Unreal conditionals and wish虚拟条件句与 wishV04 Phrasal verbs avoided; the formal verb sounds stiff回避短语动词，说话太书面

📖 Key card知识卡
An owner you cannot meet, a price far below the market and pressure to pay today are three red flags; any one of them is a reason to slow down.见不到的房东、远低于市价的租金、催你今天就付款，是三个危险信号；出现其中任何一个，都该放慢脚步。

Read and listen读与听

300 words
Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Jun was showing his friend Lena a listing on his phone over lunch. "Look at this. Two bedrooms, near the station, and the rent is about half what everything else costs. The owner is working overseas, so he can't show it, but he says he'll post the keys as soon as I pay the first month and the deposit."

午饭时，阿俊把手机上的一则房源给朋友莉娜看。「你看。两居室，离车站近，租金差不多是别处的一半。房东在国外工作，没法带看，不过他说我一付第一个月房租和押金，他就把钥匙寄过来。」

Lena put her fork down. "Half the price, an owner you can't meet, and money before you've even seen it? That's three red flags in one sentence." "He sent me a photo of his passport," Jun said. "Anyone can send a photo of a passport," Lena pointed out. "It might not even be his."

莉娜放下叉子。「半价、见不到的房东、没看房先交钱？一句话里三个危险信号。」「他给我发了护照照片。」阿俊说。「谁都能发一张护照照片，」莉娜指出，「那可能根本不是他的。」

"He also said five other people are interested, so I have to decide today." "That's the pressure part. Scammers always rush you, because the moment you slow down, you start to figure out what is really going on. If he were really the owner, he would not mind waiting a day while you check. If it looks too good to be true, it usually is."

「他还说有五个人感兴趣，所以我今天就得决定。」「这就是施压那一步。骗子总是催你，因为你一慢下来，就会开始看清到底是怎么回事。要真是房东，他不会介意你花一天去核实。看起来好得不像真的，通常就不是真的。」

"So how would I check?" "Search the photos online and see whether they appear in other listings. Look up who owns the building; in many places that's public. And never send money to someone you've never met for a flat you've never been inside. If my cousin had known that, she would still have her deposit." "Your cousin ran into this?" "Last year. Same story, different city."

「那我怎么核实？」「把照片拿到网上搜，看是不是出现在别的房源里。查一下那栋楼是谁的，很多地方这是公开信息。还有，永远别给一个没见过面的人打钱，去租一套你没进去过的房子。我表姐要是早知道这个，她的押金现在还在。」「你表姐碰上过这种事？」「去年。一样的故事，换了个城市。」

Jun went quiet, then typed a message asking whether a friend of the owner could show him the flat on Saturday. The reply came within a minute: the deal was off, because someone else had already paid. Lena picked up her fork again. "Congratulations. You've just saved yourself the most expensive cheap flat in town."

阿俊沉默了一会儿，然后打了条消息，问能不能让房东的朋友周六带他看房。不到一分钟就回了：这单不做了，因为别人已经付了钱。莉娜又拿起叉子。「恭喜。你刚刚躲过了城里最贵的一套便宜公寓。」

Words词

/ˌred ˈflæɡ/ n.
危险信号；警示迹象
a red flag; raise a red flag; spot the red flags
Being asked to pay in gift cards is a huge red flag. 被要求用礼品卡付款，是一个很大的危险信号。
/ˈskæmə/ n.
骗子（尤指诈骗钱财的）
a phone scammer; scammers target
Scammers often pretend to be someone you trust. 骗子常常冒充你信任的人。
/ˈpreʃə/ n.
压力；催逼
put pressure on sb; under pressure
Don't make big decisions under pressure. 别在被催逼的时候做大决定。
/tuː ˌɡʊd tə bi ˈtruː/ phrase
好得不像真的
sound too good to be true; look too good to be true
The job offer sounded too good to be true, and it was. 那份工作听着好得不像真的，结果确实是假的。
/lʊk ˈʌp/ phr. v.
查（资料、信息）
look up a word; look up a company
Look up the company before you sign anything. 签任何东西之前先查查这家公司。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

If he were really the owner, he would not mind waiting a day while you check.
Second conditional for an unreal present: were … would. It is a polite way of saying "he is probably not the owner". mind + -ing: mind waiting.
第二条件句，表示与现在事实相反：were … would。这是委婉地说「他很可能不是房东」。mind 后接 -ing：mind waiting。
If my cousin had known that, she would still have her deposit.
Mixed conditional: past condition (had known), present result (would still have). The deposit is still lost today.
混合条件句：条件在过去（had known），结果在现在（would still have）。押金至今没回来。
