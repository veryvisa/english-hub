What Conflict Does to the Brain 冲突对大脑做了什么

🧠 Learn the 4 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 4 个新词

Conflict triggers a stress response that narrows our thinking, so pausing and de-escalating can help negotiators reach common ground. 冲突会触发使思维变窄的应激反应，因此停顿和降温能帮助谈判者找到共同点。

When a discussion turns hostile, the body often reacts as if it were facing physical danger. Heart rate rises, muscles tense, and attention narrows to the perceived threat. Psychologists describe this as a stress response, and it evolved to help us react quickly, not to help us reason carefully.

当讨论变得充满敌意，身体往往会像面对身体危险一样做出反应。心率上升，肌肉紧绷，注意力收窄到被感知的威胁上。心理学家称之为应激反应，它的进化是为了让我们迅速反应，而不是让我们仔细推理。

The consequences for negotiation are significant. Under pressure, people tend to see fewer options, interpret neutral remarks as hostile, and cling to their opening position. Although this reaction is useful when a car swerves towards you, it is a poor tool for settling a contract dispute, where flexibility is worth more than speed.

这对谈判的影响很大。在压力下，人们往往看到的选项变少，把中性的话当作敌意，并紧抓着自己的开局立场不放。虽然这种反应在汽车朝你猛拐过来时很有用，但对解决合同纠纷却是糟糕的工具，因为在那里灵活性比速度更有价值。

Researchers who study conflict suggest that a short pause can help. Naming an emotion, even silently, appears to reduce its intensity, and a few slow breaths may give the reasoning parts of the brain time to catch up. The evidence is far from conclusive, and individual differences are large, but the practical advice is cheap and carries little risk.

研究冲突的学者认为，短暂停顿会有帮助。哪怕只是默默说出一种情绪，似乎也能减弱其强度，几次缓慢的呼吸也许能让大脑中负责推理的部分跟上。证据远不能下定论，个体差异也很大，但这一实用建议成本低、风险小。

This is one reason skilled mediators slow conversations down. By asking each side to restate what the other has said, they force a moment of reflection and make it harder for either party to hear only what they expect. The tactic helps de-escalate tension, and it often reveals common ground that was invisible while everyone was shouting.

这正是熟练的调解人要放慢对话节奏的原因之一。通过让每一方复述对方所说的话，他们迫使大家停下来反思，使任何一方更难只听到自己预期的内容。这一做法有助于缓和紧张，也常常揭示出在大家都在争吵时看不见的共同点。

Understanding the biology does not excuse bad behaviour, of course. Knowing that anger narrows our thinking simply gives us a reason to notice it early. A negotiator who recognises the warning signs, such as a racing pulse or a clenched jaw, is better placed to step back from an argument that is going nowhere and return to the real issue.

当然，了解生物学原理并不能为不良行为开脱。知道愤怒会让思维变窄，只是给了我们一个及早察觉它的理由。能识别心跳加速或下颌紧绷等警示信号的谈判者，更有条件从毫无进展的争论中抽身，回到真正的问题上。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

Although this reaction is useful when a car swerves towards you, it is a poor tool for settling a contract dispute, where flexibility is worth more than speed.
An ‘Although’ clause concedes the usefulness of the reaction first, and the non-defining relative clause beginning ‘where’ adds a contrast about the situation.
「Although」从句先承认这种反应的用处，以「where」开头的非限定性关系从句则补充了情境上的对比。
The evidence is far from conclusive, and individual differences are large, but the practical advice is cheap and carries little risk.
‘Far from’ is a strong negative meaning not at all, and the sentence concedes weak evidence before arguing for the advice with ‘but’.
「far from」是强否定，意为「根本不」，句子先承认证据薄弱，再用「but」为建议辩护。
