📏 The rule规则

🎯 The point in one line一句话要领

📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Open the full explanation: 5 points展开细讲：5 段讲解

In Chinese, linking words often come in pairs, such as one word meaning 'although' followed by another meaning 'but', or one meaning 'because' followed by another meaning 'so'. In English, only one linker is used for each relationship. Say 'Although it was raining, we went out' or 'It was raining, but we went out', never both together.

在中文里，连接词常常成对出现，例如“虽然……但是……”“因为……所以……”。英语里每种关系只用一个连接词。应该说 Although it was raining, we went out，或者 It was raining, but we went out，不能把两个连接词放在一起。

Linkers belong to different grammar groups. Conjunctions such as but, because, so and although join two clauses inside one sentence. Adverbs such as however and therefore usually start a new sentence or follow a semicolon, and they are normally followed by a comma. Mixing these groups causes run-on sentences.

连接词分属不同的语法类别。but、because、so、although 这类连词在一个句子里连接两个分句；however、therefore 这类副词通常位于新句子开头或分号之后，后面一般跟逗号。把两类混用会造成逗号粘连的病句。

Because and so show cause and result from opposite directions. Because introduces the reason, and so introduces the result. A sentence needs only one of them: 'I was tired, so I went to bed' or 'I went to bed because I was tired'. Chinese learners often add the second one automatically.

because 和 so 从相反的方向表示因果：because 引出原因，so 引出结果。一个句子只需其中一个：I was tired, so I went to bed 或 I went to bed because I was tired。华人学习者常常习惯性地再加上另一个。

Register matters. In speech and informal writing, but and so are the most natural choices. In essays, reports and emails to colleagues, however, therefore, as a result and in addition sound more formal. Using a formal linker in a casual chat can sound stiff, while using only but and so in an academic essay can sound too simple.

语体也很重要。在口语和非正式写作中，but 和 so 最自然；在论文、报告和给同事的邮件中，however、therefore、as a result、in addition 更正式。在闲聊中用太正式的连接词显得生硬，而在学术文章中只用 but 和 so 又显得太简单。

Punctuation changes with the linker. A comma usually goes before but and so when they join two full clauses. Although and because do not need a comma when the clause comes second, but a comma is needed when the clause comes first. However is followed by a comma, and a full stop or semicolon usually comes before it.

标点会随连接词而变化。but 和 so 连接两个完整分句时，前面通常加逗号；although 和 because 放在后半句时不需要逗号，放在句首时则需要逗号；however 后面要加逗号，前面通常用句号或分号。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ Although it was raining, but we went to the park.
✓ Although it was raining, we went to the park.
Learners copy the Chinese pattern that uses a pair of linking words. In English, although already shows the contrast, so but must be removed.
学习者照搬了中文“虽然……但是……”成对使用的习惯。英语里 although 已经表示转折，所以必须去掉 but。
✗ Because I was tired, so I went to bed early.
✓ Because I was tired, I went to bed early.
Because and so both mark the cause and result relationship, so using both is redundant. Choose one: 'I was tired, so I went to bed early' also works.
because 和 so 都表示因果关系，两个一起用是多余的。只选一个即可，I was tired, so I went to bed early 也是正确的。
✗ The film was long, however I enjoyed it.
✓ The film was long. However, I enjoyed it.
However is an adverb, not a conjunction, so it cannot join two clauses with only a comma. Use a full stop or semicolon before it, and a comma after it. Alternatively, use but.
however 是副词，不是连词，不能只靠逗号连接两个分句。它前面要用句号或分号，后面加逗号；也可以直接改用 but。
✗ In spite of it was cold, we went swimming.
✓ In spite of the cold, we went swimming. / Although it was cold, we went swimming.
In spite of must be followed by a noun or an -ing form, not a full clause. Use although when a subject and verb follow.
in spite of 后面必须接名词或动名词，不能接完整的从句。后面有主语加动词时，要用 although。
✗ Though he is rich, but he is not happy.
✓ Though he is rich, he is not happy.
Though works like although and already contrasts the two ideas, so but is unnecessary.
though 和 although 用法相同，本身已经表示转折，所以不需要再加 but。
✗ Since I was ill, so I stayed at home.
✓ Since I was ill, I stayed at home.
Since gives the reason, so it cannot be combined with so. This is another pair pattern borrowed from Chinese.
since 已经引出原因，不能再与 so 连用。这同样是受中文成对连接词影响的错误。
