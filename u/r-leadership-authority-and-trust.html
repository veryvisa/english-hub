Authority Is Granted, Not Claimed 权威是被赋予的，而非自封的

🧠 Learn the 4 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 4 个新词

Genuine authority in an organisation comes from the trust of others rather than from a job title. 组织中真正的权威来自他人的信任，而非职位头衔。

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

We often speak as though authority were something a title confers. Promote someone, give them a bigger office, and surely people will follow. Yet anyone who has worked in a real organisation knows that this is rarely how it works. Formal power can secure compliance, but it is trust that secures commitment.

我们常常说话时仿佛权威是头衔所赋予的东西。提拔某人，给他更大的办公室，人们自然会追随。然而，任何在真实组织中工作过的人都知道，事情很少如此运作。正式权力可以换来服从，但只有信任才能换来投入。

Trust, by contrast, is earned slowly and lost quickly. It tends to grow when leaders keep their promises, admit mistakes openly, and give credit where it is due. While it is true that some people are naturally charismatic, charisma without reliability rarely lasts. Teams are remarkably good at spotting the gap between what a leader says and what a leader does.

相比之下，信任来得缓慢，失去却很快。当领导者信守承诺、坦率承认错误、并将功劳归于应得之人时，信任往往随之增长。虽然有些人天生富有魅力，但缺乏可靠性的魅力很少能持久。团队非常善于发现领导者的言与行之间的差距。

This has practical consequences for how we choose leaders. Organisations too often reward those who deliver short-term results while ignoring how those results were achieved. A manager who hits every target by exhausting her staff is likely to leave behind a team that is hollowed out, and a reputation that does not survive her departure.

这对我们如何选拔领导者具有现实意义。组织往往奖励那些取得短期成果的人，却忽视这些成果是如何取得的。一个靠耗尽员工来完成每项指标的经理，很可能留下一支被掏空的团队，而她的声誉也不会在她离开后留存。

None of this means that formal structure is unimportant. Clear roles and lines of responsibility matter enormously. Nevertheless, a title opens the door and nothing more; what happens once the leader walks through it depends on whether the people inside are willing to believe in her.

这并不意味着正式结构无关紧要。清晰的角色和责任链条极为重要。然而，头衔只是打开了门，仅此而已；领导者走进门后会发生什么，取决于里面的人是否愿意信任她。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

Formal power can secure compliance, but it is trust that secures commitment.
This is a cleft sentence: ‘it is trust that...’ puts emphasis on ‘trust’ by splitting the clause.
这是强调句：'it is trust that...' 通过分裂句子来强调 ‘trust’。
A manager who hits every target by exhausting her staff is likely to leave behind a team that is hollowed out, and a reputation that does not survive her departure.
Two defining relative clauses modify the nouns ‘manager’ and ‘team’, and ‘is likely to’ acts as a hedge on the prediction.
两个限定性关系从句分别修饰 ‘manager’ 和 ‘team’，'is likely to' 对预测起限定作用。
