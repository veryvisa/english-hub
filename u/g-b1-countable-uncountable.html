Countable and uncountable nouns 可数与不可数名词

The learner will be able to choose appropriate articles, quantifiers, and plural forms for countable and uncountable nouns, including common exceptions and nouns with different meanings. 学习者能够根据名词的可数或不可数属性正确选择冠词、数量词和复数形式，并掌握常见例外以及具有不同可数性含义的名词。

I need some information about the course. 我需要一些关于这门课程的信息。
Information is treated as an uncountable body of knowledge.information 被视为不可数的知识内容。
I need an information about the course. 我需要一条关于这门课程的信息。
This is incorrect because information does not normally take an indefinite article.这种表达不正确，因为 information 通常不能直接与不定冠词连用。
I need a piece of information about the course. 我需要一条关于这门课程的信息。
A specific individual unit of information is being counted.这里通过 piece 将一条具体的信息作为单位进行计数。

📏 The rule规则

📏 The rule in one line规则一句话
Choose grammar according to whether you mean individual items or an undivided amount, and use a unit or container when you need to count an uncountable noun.根据表达的是独立个体还是不可分割的整体数量来选择语法；需要计算不可数名词的数量时，使用量词或容器单位。

Do not assume that every noun can take a plural ending simply because you can count the objects in real life. English often treats concepts such as advice, information, and furniture as undivided categories. When you mean the category as a whole, use the uncountable form rather than adding an -s or an indefinite article.

不要认为现实生活中能够数清的东西，英语中就一定可以直接使用复数形式。英语经常把建议、信息和家具等概念视为不可分割的类别。当你表达整个类别时，应使用不可数形式，而不是添加 -s 或不定冠词。

Choose many, a few, or several when you want to emphasize separate items or individual instances. Choose much or a little when you are concerned with the total amount rather than separate units. In ordinary affirmative sentences, a lot of is often more natural than much, even though both can express a large quantity.

当你强调独立的物品或具体事项时，选择 many、a few 或 several；当你关注总体数量而不是独立单位时，选择 much 或 a little。在普通肯定句中，a lot of 往往比 much 更自然，尽管两者都可以表示大量。

Some nouns change their countability depending on the meaning. Coffee is normally uncountable when discussing the drink in general, but a coffee can mean one cup or serving. Paper usually refers to an uncountable material, while a paper can refer to a document, essay, or academic article. Decide what the noun represents before choosing its form.

有些名词的可数性取决于具体含义。泛指咖啡这种饮料时，coffee 通常不可数，但 a coffee 可以表示一杯咖啡或一份咖啡。paper 通常表示不可数的纸张材料，而 a paper 可以表示一份文件、一篇论文或一篇学术文章。选择形式之前，先判断名词实际代表什么。

When you need to count an uncountable noun, introduce a suitable unit or container instead of changing the noun itself into a plural form. Say two pieces of advice, three bottles of water, or four items of furniture. The unit becomes plural, while the noun after of normally stays in its uncountable form.

需要计算不可数名词的数量时，应添加合适的单位或容器，而不是直接把名词变成复数。可以说 two pieces of advice、three bottles of water 或 four items of furniture。复数变化发生在单位词上，而 of 后面的不可数名词通常保持原形。

Pay special attention to nouns that are uncountable in English but commonly appear as countable nouns in other languages. Advice, information, homework, luggage, and equipment are frequent sources of mistakes. When referring to individual examples, use expressions such as a piece of information or an item of equipment rather than inventing plural forms.

特别注意那些在英语中不可数、但在其他语言中经常作为可数名词使用的词。Advice、information、homework、luggage 和 equipment 是常见的错误来源。需要表达具体的一条信息或一件设备时，使用 a piece of information 或 an item of equipment，而不是自行创造复数形式。

The difference between less and fewer depends on what is being measured. Use fewer with separately countable items, such as employees or mistakes, and less with an amount or quantity treated as a whole, such as time or money. However, less is also commonly used with measurements and amounts in everyday English, so do not apply the distinction mechanically to every numerical expression.

less 和 fewer 的区别取决于所衡量的对象。fewer 用于能够分别计数的项目，例如员工或错误；less 用于作为整体衡量的数量，例如时间或金钱。不过，在日常英语中，less 也经常用于表示测量值和总量，因此不要机械地把这一规则套用到所有数字表达中。

More pairs: same idea, different choice更多对比句组

She has a lot of experience in teaching. 她有丰富的教学经验。
Experience refers to accumulated knowledge or professional ability.experience 表示积累的知识或专业能力。
She has many experiences in teaching. 她在教学方面有许多不同的经历。
The plural form refers to separate events or individual experiences, although this wording may be less natural depending on context.复数形式表示多个独立事件或具体经历，但根据语境，这种表达可能不如其他说法自然。
She has a teaching experience. 她有一次教学经历。
The indefinite article presents experience as one particular event or type of experience.不定冠词将 experience 表示为一次特定经历或一种经历。
We need less time to finish the task. 我们需要更少的时间来完成这项任务。
Less emphasizes a smaller amount of time.less 强调时间总量更少。
We need fewer hours to finish the task. 我们需要更少的小时数来完成这项任务。
Fewer emphasizes a smaller number of separately counted hours.fewer 强调可以分别计数的小时数量更少。
We need a little time to finish the task. 我们需要一点时间来完成这项任务。
A little indicates a small but sufficient or available amount.a little 表示少量但可能足够或可以利用的时间。
We need a few hours to finish the task. 我们需要几个小时来完成这项任务。
A few indicates a small number of countable hours.a few 表示少量但可以计数的小时。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ She gave me many advices before the interview.
✓ She gave me a lot of advice before the interview.
Advice is uncountable in standard English and does not normally have a plural form. Use a lot of advice for a large amount or many pieces of advice when individual suggestions are emphasized.
Advice 在标准英语中是不可数名词，通常没有复数形式。表示大量建议时使用 a lot of advice；强调具体的一条条建议时，可以使用 many pieces of advice。
✗ I need an equipment for my new office.
✓ I need some equipment for my new office.
Equipment is an uncountable category noun, so it cannot normally follow an or take a plural ending. Use some equipment or a piece of equipment.
Equipment 是不可数的类别名词，通常不能直接与 an 连用，也不能添加复数词尾。应使用 some equipment 或 a piece of equipment。
✗ There are too much people in the meeting room.
✓ There are too many people in the meeting room.
People refers to separately countable individuals in this sentence, so many is required. Much is used with uncountable quantities.
这里的 people 表示可以分别计数的个人，因此应该使用 many。Much 用于不可数的数量。
✗ How many money do you have?
✓ How much money do you have?
Money is uncountable when referring to currency as a general amount. Use how much rather than how many; use how many with countable units such as dollars or coins.
Money 泛指金钱总量时是不可数名词，因此使用 how much，而不是 how many。如果使用 dollars 或 coins 等可数单位，则可以使用 how many。
✗ I have a lot of homeworks to finish tonight.
✓ I have a lot of homework to finish tonight.
Homework is uncountable even when it consists of several separate assignments. Do not add an -s; use assignments or pieces of homework when you need to count individual tasks.
Homework 即使包含多项独立作业，在英语中仍然是不可数名词。不要添加 -s；需要计算具体任务时，可以使用 assignments 或 pieces of homework。
✗ She bought three breads at the supermarket.
✓ She bought three loaves of bread at the supermarket.
Bread is generally uncountable when referring to the food as a substance or category. Count individual units with expressions such as loaves of bread or slices of bread.
Bread 泛指面包这种食品时通常不可数。需要计算具体数量时，可以使用 loaves of bread 或 slices of bread 等表达。
