Stress that moves in word families 词族里移动的重音

Use the texts and recordings with your coursebook; this page adds new words, grammar and practice.教材定位：English File 5e · Upper-Intermediate · 9A《The art of persuasion》；English File 5e · Upper-Intermediate · 9B《Dream cities》；English File 5e · Upper-Intermediate · 10A《Eureka!》；English File 5e · Advanced · 2A《That reminds me…》。课文与录音请配合教材学习，本页补充新词、语法与练习。

Move the stress correctly when a word changes form, and use stress to tell a noun from a verb with the same spelling. 词形变化时把重音挪对，并用重音分清拼写相同的名词和动词。

📏 The rule规则

📏 The point in one line一句话要领
Adding an ending can move the stress: PHOtograph, phoTOgrapher, photoGRAPHic. And many two-syllable words are nouns with stress on the first syllable and verbs with stress on the second: a REcord, to reCORD.加上后缀，重音可能会挪：photograph、photographer、photographic 各不相同。很多双音节词作名词重读第一节、作动词重读第二节。
  • Easy to miss易漏 ✗ I'd like to PREsent our plan. (meaning: show) → ✓ I'd like to preSENT our plan.

🗣 Say it跟读

Tap 🔊, listen, then say it aloud. Use ● to record yourself and compare.点 🔊 听，再开口跟读；点 ● 录下自己的声音对比（录音只在本机）。

photograph – photographer – photographic /ˈfəʊtəɡrɑːf fəˈtɒɡrəfə ˌfəʊtəˈɡræfɪk/
Three members, three different strong syllables. 三个成员，三个不同的强音节。
economy – economic – economics /ɪˈkɒnəmi ˌiːkəˈnɒmɪk ˌiːkəˈnɒmɪks/
CON moves to NOM. 重音从 CON 挪到 NOM。
educate – education /ˈedʒukeɪt ˌedʒuˈkeɪʃən/
-tion pulls the stress to CA. -tion 把重音拉到 CA。
analyse – analysis – analytical /ˈænəlaɪz əˈnæləsɪs ˌænəˈlɪtɪkəl/
AN, then NA, then LIT. 依次重读 AN、NA、LIT。
politics – political – politician /ˈpɒlətɪks pəˈlɪtɪkəl ˌpɒləˈtɪʃən/
POL, LIT, TI. 依次 POL、LIT、TI。
democracy – democrat – democratic /dɪˈmɒkrəsi ˈdeməkræt ˌdeməˈkrætɪk/
Each one sounds like a new word. 每个都像一个新词。
electric – electricity /ɪˈlektrɪk ˌɪlekˈtrɪsəti/
-ity: stress two syllables before the end. -ity 重读倒数第三节。
employ – employment – employer /ɪmˈplɔɪ ɪmˈplɔɪmənt ɪmˈplɔɪə/
-ment and -er leave the stress alone. -ment、-er 不改重音。
a record / to record /ə ˈrekɔːd tə rɪˈkɔːd/
Noun first, verb second. 名词重读前，动词重读后。
a present / to present /ə ˈprezənt tə prɪˈzent/
The vowels change too: /e/ becomes /ɪ/. 元音也变了。
an increase / to increase /ən ˈɪŋkriːs tʊ ɪnˈkriːs/
IN-crease, in-CREASE. 名词 IN，动词 CREASE。
an object / to object /ən ˈɒbdʒɪkt tʊ əbˈdʒekt/
The verb's first syllable is a weak /əb/. 动词第一节弱读为 /əb/。
I'd like to present the results of the analysis. /aɪd ˈlaɪk tə prɪˈzent ðə rɪˈzʌlts əv ði əˈnæləsɪs/
Verb preSENT, noun aNAlysis. 动词 preSENT，名词 aNAlysis。
Prices increased again, and nobody expected the increase. /ˈpraɪsɪz ɪnˈkriːst əˈɡen ən ˈnəʊbədi ɪkˈspektɪd ði ˈɪŋkriːs/
Verb then noun in one sentence. 一句里先动词后名词。
She works in economics, not in politics. /ʃi ˈwɜːks ɪn ˌiːkəˈnɒmɪks nɒt ɪn ˈpɒlətɪks/
NOM and POL. 分别重读 NOM 与 POL。
They want to record the meeting and keep a record of it. /ðeɪ ˈwɒnt tə rɪˈkɔːd ðə ˈmiːtɪŋ ən ˈkiːp ə ˈrekɔːd əv ɪt/
Same spelling, two stresses. 同一拼写，两种重音。

📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Open the full explanation: 5 points展开细讲：5 段讲解

A manager writes "We need to analyse the analysis" in an email and it looks fine. Said aloud with the same stress on both words, it sounds odd, because ANalyse and aNAlysis belong to the same family but carry their stress in different places. In English, a word family is not a fixed sound with endings attached; the whole word can be re-balanced.

「分析这份分析」写出来没问题，两个词读成同一个重音就别扭：analyse 与 analysis 同族，重音位置却不同。英语的词族不是固定的音再挂后缀，整个词会重新配重。

The endings tell you where to go. -tion, -ic, -ity, -ian and -ical pull the stress towards the end of the word: EDucate → eduCAtion, eCOnomy → ecoNOmic, eLECtric → elecTRIcity, POlitics → poliTIcian. Endings like -ment, -ness, -er, -ful and -ly leave the stress where it was: emPLOY → emPLOYment, CAREful → CAREfully. Learn the two groups and you can predict most families.

后缀会告诉你往哪挪。-tion、-ic、-ity、-ian、-ical 把重音往词尾方向拉；-ment、-ness、-er、-ful、-ly 不动原来的重音。记住这两组，大多数词族都能推出来。

When the stress moves, the vowels change with it. The syllable that loses the stress weakens to /ə/; the one that gains it gets a full vowel. In PHOtograph the first o is /əʊ/ and the second is /ə/; in phoTOgrapher they swap: /fəˈtɒɡrəfə/. If you move only the loudness but keep every vowel the same, the word still sounds wrong.

重音一挪，元音跟着变：失去重音的音节弱化成 /ə/，得到重音的音节变成完整元音。只挪响度、元音不变，词听起来还是不对。

Noun–verb pairs are a second pattern: a PREsent / to preSENT, an OBject / to obJECT, a PROgress / to proGRESS, an INcrease / to inCREASE. Chinese speakers often learn the noun first and use its stress for the verb, which makes I'd like to PREsent our results sound like a gift. In listening, the stress is often the only clue to which word you heard, because the spelling is identical.

名词与动词成对是第二种规律。华人常先学名词，再用名词的重音说动词，「我想展示结果」就听成「礼物」。听力上，拼写相同时重音往往是唯一的线索。

This links directly to word choice. The mistake It is very convenience is a part-of-speech error, but it is often helped along by not hearing the difference between conVEnience and conVEnient; and deMOcracy, DEmocrat, demoCRAtic are easy to mix in writing once you can no longer hear three different words. Saying the family aloud, stress and all, makes each member separate in your memory.

这和用词直接相关。词性用错，常常也是因为没听出词族成员的区别；把整个词族连重音一起读出来，每个成员在记忆里才分得开。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ I'd like to PREsent our plan. (meaning: show)
✓ I'd like to preSENT our plan.
With noun stress the listener hears "a present", a gift.
用名词重音，对方听到的是「礼物」。
✗ pho-TO-graph-ic (keeping the stress of photographer)
✓ pho-to-GRAPH-ic
-ic pulls the stress to the syllable just before it.
-ic 把重音拉到前一节。
✗ eCOnomic (copying the stress of economy)
✓ ecoNOmic
The family shares letters, not stress.
同族共享字母，不共享重音。
✗ employMENT (moving the stress onto -ment)
✓ emPLOYment
-ment is a neutral ending; the stress stays put.
-ment 是中性后缀，重音不动。

Pick three word families you use at work (for example manage, manager, management or analyse, analysis, analytical). Write a sentence that uses all members of one family, record it, and check the strong syllable of each member against the device voice. Then do the same with two noun–verb pairs in one sentence.
选三个工作中常用的词族，写一句话把一个词族的成员都用上，录音，再和设备语音逐个对强音节。再写一句同时用两对名词／动词同形词。
