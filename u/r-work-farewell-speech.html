Twenty-Two Years of Night Shifts 二十二年夜班

🧠 Learn the 6 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 6 个新词

Read a short retirement speech by a warehouse supervisor and notice how inversion, a cleft, an inverted conditional and the subjunctive give a spoken text rhythm and weight. 读一位仓库主管的退休致辞，留意倒装、分裂句、省略 if 的条件句和虚拟式怎样给口头稿带来节奏与分量。

C1 · CELPIP 9+⏱ 20 minG05 Topic-comment sentences and double subjects主题句与双主语G07 Unreal conditionals and wish虚拟条件句与 wish

📖 Key card知识卡
A good farewell speech is short, names real moments, thanks people specifically, and leaves one piece of advice. Formal structures belong at the high points, not in every sentence.好的告别致辞要短，讲真实的片段，具体地感谢人，再留下一句建议。正式结构只放在高潮处，而不是句句都用。

Read and listen读与听

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Wei Chen ran the night shift at a distribution warehouse for twenty-two years. At his retirement lunch, his manager asked him to say a few words. He had written them on two index cards and practised them in the car. This is what he said.

陈伟在一家物流仓库带了二十二年夜班。在他的退休午餐会上，经理请他讲几句话。他把讲稿写在两张卡片上，在车里练过。下面是他说的话。

"When I started here, my English was so bad that I nodded at everything. Little did I know that nodding was how I agreed to work three Christmases in a row. It was Rosa, on my second week, who sat me down and said, 'Wei, you are allowed to say no.' Had she not said that, I think I would have left within a year."

「我刚来的时候，英语差到对什么都点头。我根本不知道，正是这样点头，让我连着三个圣诞节都答应了上班。是罗莎，在我来的第二周，让我坐下来说：『阿伟，你是可以说不的。』要不是她那句话，我想我一年之内就走了。」

"Rarely does a night shift feel like a family, but this one did. We fixed a broken conveyor at four in the morning with a belt from someone's car. We covered for each other when a baby was born or a parent was sick. Not once in twenty-two years did I have to ask twice for help."

「夜班很少有家的感觉，可我们这个班有。我们凌晨四点用某人车上的一根皮带修好了坏掉的传送带。有人家里添了孩子、有人父母生病，我们就互相顶班。二十二年里，我需要帮忙从来不用开第二次口。」

"So, one piece of advice for the new supervisors. I suggest that every new person be told, in their first week, what Rosa told me. Should anyone here ever feel they cannot say no, come and find me. I will be at home, learning to sleep at night."

「所以，给新主管们一条建议。我建议每个新人在第一周都被告知罗莎对我说过的那句话。在座的谁要是觉得自己没法说不，就来找我。我会在家里，学着晚上睡觉。」

People laughed at the last line and clapped for a long time. Rosa, who had retired herself three years earlier, had come back for the lunch. She told him afterwards that it was the first speech she had ever heard at the company that nobody checked their phone during.

大家听到最后一句都笑了，掌声持续了很久。三年前自己也退休了的罗莎专程回来参加午餐会。她事后告诉他，那是她在公司听过的唯一一次没人中途看手机的致辞。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

Little did I know that nodding was how I agreed to work three Christmases in a row.
Little did I know = I had no idea; the inversion sets up the joke.
Little did I know 意为「我根本不知道」；倒装为笑点做铺垫。
It was Rosa, on my second week, who sat me down and said, 'Wei, you are allowed to say no.'
Cleft It was … who: the focus is on Rosa as the person who changed things.
分裂句 It was … who：焦点在罗莎这个改变了一切的人。
Not once in twenty-two years did I have to ask twice for help.
Not once fronted → did I have.
not once 前置，接 did I have。
I suggest that every new person be told, in their first week, what Rosa told me.
suggest that + be told: passive subjunctive; singular their for every new person.
suggest that 加 be told：被动虚拟式；every new person 用单数 their 指代。
