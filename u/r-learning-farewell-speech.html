Learning Is a Long Road 学习是一条漫长的路

🧠 Learn the 2 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 2 个新词

A teacher tells students at the end of the year that learning goes on after school and is full of ups and downs. 一位老师在学年结束时告诉学生，学习在离开学校后仍在继续，而且有起有伏。

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Good morning, everyone, and congratulations on finishing the year. When I look at your faces, I remember my own last day at school. I was sure that learning was over. I was wrong, and today I want to tell you why.

大家早上好，祝贺你们完成了这一学年。看着你们的脸，我想起了自己在学校的最后一天。我当时确信学习已经结束了。我错了，今天我想告诉你们原因。

First, school teaches you more than facts. You learned how to hand in work on time, how to work with people you did not choose, and how to keep going when a subject was boring. These skills do not appear on your certificate, but you will use them for the rest of your life.

第一，学校教给你们的不只是知识。你们学会了如何按时交作业，如何和不是自己选的人合作，以及在某门课很无聊时如何坚持下去。这些技能不会出现在你们的证书上，但你们会终生受用。

Second, nobody learns in a straight line. Some of you seemed to make progress very quickly in the autumn and then felt stuck in the spring. Others started slowly and finished strongly. If you ever feel that you are falling behind, remember that this is a normal part of learning, not a sign that you should give up.

第二，没有人是沿着一条直线学习的。你们中有些人秋天进步很快，到了春天却觉得停滞不前。另一些人起步缓慢，却有力地收尾。如果你什么时候觉得自己落后了，请记住，这是学习中很正常的一部分，并不意味着你应该放弃。

Third, be curious. The people who keep growing are usually not the cleverest. They are the ones who ask questions, read widely and pay attention to the world around them. A teacher can show you a door, but only you can decide to walk through it.

第三，保持好奇。持续成长的人通常并不是最聪明的，而是爱提问、广泛阅读、留心周围世界的人。老师可以指给你一扇门，但只有你自己能决定走进去。

So, as you leave today, do not think of this as the end of your education. It is only the end of one chapter. Take your notebooks, your questions and your friends with you. Thank you for a wonderful year, and good luck.

所以，今天离开时，不要把这看作教育的终点。这只是一个篇章的结束。带上你们的笔记本、你们的问题和你们的朋友吧。谢谢你们给了我美好的一年，祝你们好运。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

If you ever feel that you are falling behind, remember that this is a normal part of learning, not a sign that you should give up.
The sentence has an ‘if’ clause followed by an imperative, and ‘not a sign that you should give up’ adds a contrast to correct a possible wrong idea.
句子由 if 从句加祈使句构成；'not a sign that you should give up' 用来对比，纠正可能的错误想法。
A teacher can show you a door, but only you can decide to walk through it.
The two clauses are joined by ‘but’ to contrast what a teacher can do with what only the learner can do, and ‘decide to’ is followed by an infinitive.
两个分句用 but 连接，对比老师能做的和只有学习者能做的；'decide to' 后接动词不定式。
