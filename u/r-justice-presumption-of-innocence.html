Innocent Until Proven Guilty 未经证实，不应定罪

🧠 Learn the 5 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 5 个新词

The presumption of innocence protects individuals from unjust punishment by requiring the prosecution to establish guilt through reliable evidence. 无罪推定原则要求控方通过可靠证据证明被告有罪，从而保护个人免受不公正的惩罚。

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

A regional court has drawn public attention after dismissing a criminal case in which the prosecution relied heavily on indirect evidence. The judge concluded that the available material raised serious questions but did not establish guilt beyond reasonable doubt. The decision has reopened a familiar debate: should society prioritize the punishment of suspected offenders or the protection of individuals against wrongful conviction? Legal experts argue that the distinction is fundamental to a fair justice system.

一家地区法院驳回了一起刑事案件，引起了公众关注。在这起案件中，控方严重依赖间接证据。法官认为，现有材料引发了严重疑问，但未能达到排除合理怀疑的证明标准，不足以认定被告有罪。这一裁决重新引发了一个熟悉的争论：社会应该优先惩罚犯罪嫌疑人，还是保护个人免受错误定罪？法律专家认为，这种区别对于公平的司法制度至关重要。

The presumption of innocence means that a person accused of a crime must be treated as innocent unless guilt is established according to law. It places the burden of proof on the prosecution rather than requiring defendants to demonstrate their innocence. While this principle may occasionally allow a guilty person to escape punishment, abandoning it would create a much greater danger: innocent people could be convicted because suspicion was mistaken for evidence.

无罪推定意味着，被指控犯罪的人在依法被证明有罪之前，必须被视为无罪。这一原则将举证责任放在控方，而不是要求被告证明自己无罪。尽管这一原则有时可能使有罪者逃脱惩罚，但放弃它会带来更大的危险：无辜者可能因为怀疑被误认为证据而遭到定罪。

The quality of evidence is therefore crucial. Witness statements may be inaccurate, digital records may be incomplete, and confessions may be obtained under pressure. Courts must examine not only what evidence appears to show but also how it was collected and whether alternative explanations exist. In practice, the reliability of a criminal investigation tends to depend on transparent procedures, independent review, and the opportunity for the defence to challenge the prosecution's claims.

因此，证据的质量至关重要。证人的陈述可能不准确，数字记录可能不完整，认罪供述也可能是在压力下取得的。法院不仅必须审查证据表面上显示了什么，还必须审查证据是如何收集的，以及是否存在其他解释。在实践中，刑事调查的可靠性往往取决于透明的程序、独立审查，以及辩方质疑控方主张的机会。

Public opinion does not always follow the same standards as a courtroom. News reports and online discussions can create strong impressions before all the facts are known. Nevertheless, a justice system should resist pressure to deliver immediate convictions. Its legitimacy depends not simply on how many offenders are punished, but on whether decisions are reached through fair procedures. Protecting the innocent is not an obstacle to justice; it is one of its essential purposes.

公众舆论并不总是遵循法庭所采用的相同标准。新闻报道和网络讨论可能在事实尚未完全明确之前就形成强烈印象。然而，司法制度应该抵制立即定罪的压力。司法制度的合法性不仅取决于惩罚了多少罪犯，还取决于裁决是否通过公平程序作出。保护无辜者并不是实现正义的障碍，而是正义的基本目的之一。
