Council Proposes Rules for Public Algorithms 市议会提出公共算法规则

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

A city council has published a draft code of practice governing the use of automated decision-making in public services. The document, released on Tuesday after eighteen months of consultation, would require officials to explain, in plain language, how any algorithm affecting housing, benefits or policing actually reaches its conclusions.

一个市议会公布了一份规范公共服务中自动化决策使用的实践守则草案。该文件在历时十八个月的征求意见后于周二发布，将要求官员用通俗易懂的语言解释，任何影响住房、福利或治安的算法究竟是如何得出结论的。

Supporters argue that transparency is the first step towards accountability, and that the public has a right to know how it is being judged. Although automated tools tend to be faster than human clerks, they can also reproduce old prejudices hidden in historical data. If a family is wrongly refused help, they say, someone must be held accountable, and that is impossible when nobody understands the system.

支持者认为，透明是通向问责的第一步，公众有权知道自己是如何被评判的。尽管自动化工具往往比人工办事员更快，但它们也可能重现隐藏在历史数据中的旧偏见。他们说，如果一个家庭被错误地拒绝援助，就必须有人被追究责任，而当没有人理解这套系统时，这是不可能做到的。

Critics, however, warn that the rules may prove costly. Smaller departments, they note, lack the technical staff to audit complex software, and demanding detailed explanations could slow down services that residents rely on. That said, even sceptics concede that some form of human oversight is likely to become unavoidable.

然而，批评者警告说，这些规则的成本可能很高。他们指出，规模较小的部门缺乏审计复杂软件的技术人员，而要求提供详细解释可能会拖慢居民所依赖的服务。话虽如此，即使是怀疑者也承认，某种形式的人工监督很可能会变得不可避免。

Under the draft, every automated decision of consequence would carry a right of appeal to a named human reviewer. Suppliers would have to disclose what data their products were trained on, and independent auditors would be allowed to test the software for bias before it went live. Failure to comply could lead to contracts being cancelled or fines being imposed.

根据草案，每一项重要的自动化决策都将附带向一位指名的人工审查员申诉的权利。供应商必须披露其产品所使用的训练数据，独立审计人员将获准在软件上线前测试其是否存在偏见。不遵守规定可能导致合同被取消或被处以罚款。

Public hearings will run until the end of next month. On the whole, local reaction has been cautious rather than hostile, with many residents saying they simply want to know who is responsible when a machine gets it wrong. A final version is expected to be put to a vote early next year, although the timetable could still change.

公开听证会将持续到下个月底。总体而言，当地的反应是谨慎而非敌对，许多居民表示，他们只是想知道当机器出错时该由谁负责。最终版本预计将在明年初付诸表决，不过时间表仍可能变动。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

Although automated tools tend to be faster than human clerks, they can also reproduce old prejudices hidden in historical data.
The sentence opens with a concessive ‘Although’ clause, and the past participle phrase ‘hidden in historical data’ modifies ‘prejudices’ like a reduced relative clause.
句子以让步状语从句“Although...”开头，而过去分词短语“hidden in historical data”像缩略的定语从句一样修饰“prejudices”。
That said, even sceptics concede that some form of human oversight is likely to become unavoidable.
’That said’ is a written discourse marker introducing a concession, and ‘is likely to’ hedges the claim so it sounds less absolute.
“That said”是引出让步的书面话语标记，而“is likely to”对论断做了限定，使语气不那么绝对。
