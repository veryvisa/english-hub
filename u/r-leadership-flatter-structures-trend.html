Firms Flatten Their Structures, With Mixed Results 企业走向扁平化，结果喜忧参半

Read and listen读与听

210 words
Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

A growing number of mid-sized organisations are cutting layers of middle management in an attempt to speed up decision-making. Senior executives say the aim is to bring leaders closer to customers and to give frontline staff more room to act. Although the idea is hardly new, the pressure to adapt quickly has made it fashionable once again.

越来越多的中型组织正在削减中层管理层级，试图加快决策速度。高管们表示，目的是让领导者更贴近客户，并给一线员工更多行动空间。尽管这一想法并不新鲜，但快速适应的压力使它再度流行起来。

Early reports suggest the results are mixed. Teams that were given clear goals and genuine authority tend to report faster progress, whereas those left without direction often describe confusion about who is accountable. One consultant observed that removing managers is easy, but replacing the coordination they provided is far harder.

早期报告显示结果不一。获得明确目标和真正权力的团队往往反映进展更快，而那些缺乏方向的团队则常常表示不清楚该由谁负责。一位顾问指出，撤掉管理者很容易，但替代他们所提供的协调作用则困难得多。

Staff reactions have been similarly divided. Some welcome the freedom to take ownership of projects, while others feel they have been handed extra responsibility without extra support. Employees who lost their managers say they miss having someone to turn to when problems arise, and that career progression has become less clear.

员工的反应同样存在分歧。一些人欢迎自主负责项目的自由，另一些人则觉得自己被交付了额外的责任却没有得到额外的支持。失去上司的员工表示，遇到问题时怀念有人可以求助，而且职业晋升路径变得不那么清晰。

Analysts caution that structure alone is unlikely to deliver results. On the whole, organisations that communicate openly about the reasons for change, and that invest in training their remaining leaders, appear to fare better. That said, it is too early to judge whether the benefits will last once the initial enthusiasm fades.

分析人士提醒，仅靠结构调整不太可能带来成效。总体而言，那些就变革原因坦诚沟通、并投资培训留任领导者的组织似乎表现更好。不过，现在判断这些益处能否在最初的热情消退后持续，还为时过早。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

Teams that were given clear goals and genuine authority tend to report faster progress, whereas those left without direction often describe confusion about who is accountable.
A defining relative clause modifies the subject, and ‘whereas’ introduces a contrasting clause with a reduced relative (‘left without direction’) modifying ‘those’.
限定性关系从句修饰主语，'whereas' 引出对比分句，其中 ‘left without direction’ 是修饰 ‘those’ 的省略关系从句。
One consultant observed that removing managers is easy, but replacing the coordination they provided is far harder.
The gerund phrases ‘removing managers’ and ‘replacing the coordination’ act as subjects, and ‘they provided’ is a contact relative clause with the relative pronoun omitted.
动名词短语 ‘removing managers’ 和 ‘replacing the coordination’ 作主语，'they provided' 是省略了关系代词的关系从句。
