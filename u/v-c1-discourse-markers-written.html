📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Open the full explanation: 7 points展开细讲：7 段讲解

Discourse markers do more than connect sentences: they tell readers how to interpret the relationship between ideas. For example, moreover introduces additional support, whereas nevertheless signals that the next point contrasts with what has just been said. Choose a marker according to the actual logical relationship, not simply because a sentence needs a formal opening.

话语标记不仅连接句子，还告诉读者应该如何理解不同观点之间的关系。例如，moreover 用于补充支持性信息，而 nevertheless 表示接下来的内容与前文形成让步或转折。选择标记时应依据真实的逻辑关系，而不是仅仅因为句首需要一个正式表达。

Some markers introduce contrasting information, but they are not interchangeable. However usually introduces a contrasting statement, whereas conversely highlights an opposite situation or relationship. By contrast explicitly directs attention to differences between two things. Using conversely when there is merely a qualification rather than an opposite relationship can make an argument logically inaccurate.

一些话语标记都能引出对比，但它们并不能随意互换。however 通常引出与前文不同的观点；conversely 强调相反的情况或关系；by contrast 则明确引导读者比较两个事物的差异。如果只是补充限制条件，却使用 conversely，就可能使论证的逻辑关系不准确。

A useful distinction exists between adding information and strengthening an argument. Furthermore and moreover both introduce additional points, but moreover often gives the added point greater rhetorical importance. In formal writing, these expressions are particularly useful when developing an argument across several sentences or paragraphs.

补充信息和强化论证之间存在值得注意的区别。furthermore 和 moreover 都可以引出补充观点，但 moreover 往往带有更强的论证强调意味。在正式写作中，这些表达特别适合用于跨多个句子或段落逐步展开论证。

Certain markers introduce qualifications rather than straightforward agreement or disagreement. Nonetheless and nevertheless acknowledge that an earlier point remains relevant while presenting a conclusion that might seem inconsistent with it. Notwithstanding expresses a similar concession in a more formal grammatical construction, often followed by a noun phrase or gerund rather than a full clause.

某些标记用于表达限定或让步，而不是简单地表示同意或反对。nonetheless 和 nevertheless 承认前面的观点仍然成立，同时引出一个看似与之不一致的结论。notwithstanding 表达类似的让步，但语法上更正式，后面通常接名词短语或动名词，而不是完整的从句。

Markers of consequence help writers distinguish evidence from conclusions. Consequently and therefore introduce results or logical conclusions, while hence is particularly concise and common in academic or analytical writing. Hence can join a full clause, but then needs a period or semicolon before it, just like consequently and therefore; it can also introduce a noun phrase with no verb, as in 'hence the need for reform' (short for 'hence there is the need'). Accordingly often suggests that an action or decision is appropriate in response to the circumstances described. These expressions should not be used merely to mean 'next'.

表示结果的话语标记可以帮助作者区分证据和结论。consequently 和 therefore 用于引出结果或逻辑结论；hence 尤其简洁，常见于学术或分析性写作。hence 可以连接完整分句，这时前面同样需要句号或分号（和 consequently、therefore 一样）；它也可以直接引出一个没有动词的名词短语，例如 hence the need for reform（相当于 hence there is the need）。accordingly 则常暗示某项行动或决定是对前述情况作出的适当回应。这些表达不能仅仅用来表示事情的先后顺序。

Not every useful connector starts a new sentence. Although and whereas introduce a contrast or concession inside a single sentence, attached to a subordinate clause, rather than linking two separate sentences the way however or by contrast do. Separately, initially, subsequently and ultimately guide readers through the stages of a process or argument over time — what began, what followed, and what mattered in the end — rather than expressing addition, contrast or consequence.

并非所有有用的连接词都用在新句子的开头。although 和 whereas 是在同一个句子内部引出对比或让步，依附于从句，而不像 however 或 by contrast 那样连接两个独立的句子。另一方面，initially、subsequently 和 ultimately 引导读者了解一个过程或论证随时间展开的各个阶段——最初如何、后来怎样、最终的关键是什么——而不是表达补充、对比或结果关系。

Some written markers signal that the writer is narrowing or clarifying a previous statement. Specifically introduces precise details, in particular highlights one especially relevant example, and more precisely corrects or sharpens the scope of an earlier statement. These expressions help writers avoid vague generalisations and make their claims more defensible.

一些书面标记用于缩小范围或澄清前面的陈述。specifically 引出具体细节；in particular 突出某个特别相关的例子；more precisely 则修正或进一步明确先前陈述的范围。这些表达有助于避免模糊的概括，使论点更加严谨。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ The evidence is limited. Moreover, the conclusion is convincing.
✓ The evidence is limited. Nevertheless, the conclusion is convincing.
Moreover adds supporting information, while nevertheless introduces a concession or contrast. The second sentence presents a conclusion despite the limitation, not an additional supporting point.
moreover 用于补充支持性信息，而 nevertheless 表示让步或转折。第二句表达的是尽管存在局限，结论仍然具有说服力，而不是增加一个支持性论据。
✗ The policy was expensive, consequently, it was unpopular.
✓ The policy was expensive; consequently, it was unpopular.
Consequently connects two independent clauses through a cause-and-effect relationship. A comma alone cannot normally join two independent clauses in standard formal writing. Use a semicolon or separate sentences.
consequently 连接的是具有因果关系的两个独立分句。正式英语中，通常不能只用逗号连接两个独立分句。应使用分号，或者将其拆成两个句子。
✗ Although the results were promising, but further research is needed.
✓ Although the results were promising, further research is needed.
Although already introduces a concessive subordinate clause. Adding but creates an unnecessary second contrast marker. Use although with a main clause, or use but to connect two independent clauses.
although 本身已经引导让步从句，不需要再添加 but。可以使用 although 连接让步从句和主句，也可以用 but 连接两个独立分句，但不应在这种结构中重复使用两个转折标记。
✗ The first group preferred online learning, conversely the second group preferred classroom learning.
✓ The first group preferred online learning. By contrast, the second group preferred classroom learning.
By contrast is a natural choice for comparing two groups with different preferences. Conversely more strongly suggests an opposite relationship or reversal, rather than simply introducing any difference.
by contrast 很适合用于比较两个偏好不同的群体。conversely 更强调相反的关系或方向逆转，并不是所有普通差异都适合使用它。
✗ The company changed its policy, hence it introduced new rules.
✓ The company changed its policy; hence, it introduced new rules.
In this construction, hence introduces a consequence and needs appropriate punctuation. A semicolon separates the two independent clauses. Another natural option is to write 'The company changed its policy. Hence, it introduced new rules.'
在这个结构中，hence 引出结果，需要使用恰当的标点。分号可以分隔两个独立分句。另一种自然写法是将其拆成两个句子，并在 Hence 后面加逗号。
✗ Notwithstanding it was raining, the event continued.
✓ Notwithstanding the rain, the event continued.
Notwithstanding is commonly used as a preposition before a noun phrase or gerund in formal writing. To introduce a full subordinate clause, although is generally more natural.
notwithstanding 在正式写作中通常作为介词，后接名词短语或动名词。如果要引导完整的从句，通常使用 although 更自然。
