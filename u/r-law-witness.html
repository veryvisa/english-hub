The Morning I Became a Witness 那天早上我成了证人

🧠 Learn the 3 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 3 个新词

Read a first-person story about staying to be a witness after a minor crash, and learn how a story keeps its tenses in line and ends with a third conditional and I wish. 读一个小车祸后留下来作证的第一人称故事，学会讲故事时保持时态一致，并用第三条件句和 I wish 收尾。

A witness who simply tells what they saw is not taking sides; they help other people find out what really happened.证人只是说出自己看到的，并不是在选边站；他们是在帮别人弄清真相。

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

I was waiting at a crossing one morning last March when a delivery van ran into the back of a small car at the lights. Nobody was hurt, but within a minute the two drivers were out on the road, shouting, each sure the other was to blame.

去年三月的一个早上，我在路口等红灯，一辆送货车在红绿灯前撞上了一辆小轿车的车尾。没人受伤，但不到一分钟，两个司机都下了车站在路上吵，各自认定是对方的错。

My first instinct was to walk away. I had a train to catch, and I did not want to get involved in anything legal in a country where I still felt like a guest. Then the van driver told everyone around him that the car had reversed into him, which was simply not true.

我的第一反应是走开。我要赶火车，而且在一个自己还觉得像客人的国家，我不想卷进任何跟法律沾边的事。后来，货车司机对周围的人说是轿车倒车撞了他，这根本不是事实。

So I stayed. I told both drivers what I had seen and gave my name and number to the woman in the car. Two weeks later her insurer called and asked me for a short statement. I described the lights, the van and the distance between them, and I handed over a photo I had taken of the scene.

于是我留了下来。我把看到的告诉了两位司机，把名字和电话留给了轿车里的那位女士。两周后，她的保险公司打电话来，请我做一份简短的陈述。我描述了红绿灯、货车和两车之间的距离，还交出了一张我在现场拍的照片。

I had been nervous that I might have to go to court, or that I could somehow become liable for saying the wrong thing. Neither happened. A witness who tells the truth about what they saw is not taking sides, I later learned; they are helping other people figure out what happened. The person asking will usually follow up only if something is unclear.

我之前一直担心可能要上法庭，或者会因为说错话而莫名其妙地要承担责任。这两件事都没发生。我后来才知道，如实讲出自己所见的证人并不是在选边站，而是在帮别人弄清楚发生了什么。对方通常只有在哪里不清楚时才会再跟进。

I think about that morning more often than I expected. If I had caught my train, the woman would have had only her word against his. I wish I could say I stayed because I was brave. Really, I stayed because the lie annoyed me. Either way, it cost me one train and an hour, and it may have saved a stranger a great deal of trouble.

那个早上我想起的次数比我预想的多。要是我当时去赶了火车，那位女士就只能拿她的一面之词去对他的一面之词。我真希望能说我留下是因为勇敢。其实，我留下是因为那句谎话让我生气。不管怎样，它让我误了一趟火车、花了一个小时，却可能帮一个陌生人省了一大堆麻烦。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

If I had caught my train, the woman would have had only her word against his.
Third conditional: had caught … would have had. her word against his means it would be one person's story against another's, with no witness.
第三条件句：had caught … would have had。her word against his 指只有双方各执一词，没有证人。
I had been nervous that I might have to go to court, or that I could somehow become liable for saying the wrong thing.
Past perfect (had been nervous: had + been + adjective) for a feeling that came before the later past events (the call, the statement). liable for + -ing. The story stays in the past; it does not switch to I am nervous.
过去完成时（had been nervous：had + been + 形容词）表示一种早于后来那些过去事件（来电、做陈述）的情绪。liable for 后接 -ing。故事一直留在过去，不会突然转成 I am nervous。
