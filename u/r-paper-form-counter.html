At the Counter with a Two-Page Form 柜台前的两页表格

🧠 Learn the 3 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 3 个新词

Follow a mother and daughter filling in a form at a service counter, and learn the small rules that forms take for granted. 跟着一对母女在服务柜台填表，学会表格默认你懂的那些小规矩。

B2 · CELPIP 7–8⏱ 20 minV11 Numbers, dates and quantities said wrongly数字、日期、数量说错V06 Register: too formal, too casual, or too blunt语域不合：太正式、太随意或太冲

📖 Key card知识卡
Forms fail on small rules, not on English: the order of the date, no empty boxes, and names that match your ID exactly.表格出错多在小规矩，而不在英语：日期顺序、不留空格、名字与证件完全一致。

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Anna's mother had come to the counter to change the address on her records, and Anna had come to translate. The clerk handed them a two-page form. "Please fill in sections one to four. Capital letters and black ink, please."

安娜的妈妈来柜台改档案上的地址，安娜来当翻译。办事员递给她们一张两页的表格：「请填第一到第四部分。请用大写字母和黑色墨水。」

Section one was easy until "Date of birth (DD/MM/YYYY)". Her mother wrote the year first, the way she always had. "Sorry, could we have a new copy?" "No need. Just cross it out, write it again, and put your initials next to it." Then came "Previous surname". "Do I write my maiden name?" Anna asked. "Only if she has used it here. Otherwise write N/A, not applicable. Please do not leave any box empty."

第一部分一直很顺，直到「出生日期（日/月/年）」。妈妈按一贯的习惯先写了年份。「不好意思，能换一张新表吗？」「不用。划掉重写，在旁边签上姓名首字母就行。」接着是「曾用姓氏」。「要写娘家姓吗？」安娜问。「只有她在这里用过才写。否则写 N/A，即不适用。请不要留空格。」

Section four asked for supporting documents. "We have brought a utility bill and her lease. Do you need both?" "One is enough, but the name has to be the same as on the form." It was not: her mother's middle name was on the lease but not on the form. Anna had a quick word with her mother. "Could you do me a favour and write the full name, exactly as on your ID? It saves everyone a lot of time."

第四部分要证明文件。「我们带了一张水电账单和她的租约，两样都要吗？」「一样就够，但名字必须和表上的一致。」并不一致：租约上有妈妈的中间名，表上没有。安娜跟妈妈小声商量了一句。「能麻烦您把全名照证件上的一字不差写出来吗？这样大家都省事。」

At the end, the clerk checked every page, pointed to the line for the signature and the date, and gave them a receipt with a reference number. "Keep this. If you have not heard from us in two weeks, call us and give us the number." On the bus home, Anna's mother said the English had been easy. The hard part was the small rules: the order of the date, the empty boxes, the names that had to match. Next time, she said, she would fill in the form herself and take a break for coffee afterwards.

最后，办事员把每页都检查了一遍，指了指签名和日期那一行，给了她们一张带档案编号的回执。「收好。两周内没收到我们的消息，就打电话报这个编号。」回家的公交车上，妈妈说英语倒不难，难的是那些小规矩：日期的顺序、空着的格子、必须对得上的名字。她说下次她自己填，填完去喝杯咖啡歇一歇。

Words词

/fɪl ɪn/ phr. v.
填写（表格）
fill in a form; fill in your details
Please fill in both pages before you sign. 签字前请把两页都填好。
/ˌdeɪt əv ˈbɜːθ/ n.
出生日期
enter your date of birth; date of birth (DD/MM/YYYY)
Check the order of the date of birth on each form. 每张表都要核对出生日期的顺序。
/nɒt əˈplɪkəbl/ phr.
不适用（表上写 N/A）
write not applicable; mark as not applicable
If a question does not apply to you, write not applicable. 问题与你无关就写「不适用」。
/səˈpɔːtɪŋ ˈdɒkjʊmənts/ n. pl.
证明文件；佐证材料
provide supporting documents; attach supporting documents
The form will not be processed without supporting documents. 没有证明文件，表格不予处理。
/rɪˈsiːt/ n.
回执；收据
get a receipt; acknowledge receipt
Keep the receipt until the matter is closed. 事情办结之前保留回执。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

Just cross it out, write it again, and put your initials next to it.
Three commands in a row; cross out is a phrasal verb, and initials are the first letters of your names.
三个连续的祈使句；cross out 是短语动词，initials 指姓名的首字母。
One is enough, but the name has to be the same as on the form.
the same as: always the before same. has to: an outside rule, not the clerk's choice.
the same as 前固定用 the。has to 表示外部规定，不是办事员个人的要求。
