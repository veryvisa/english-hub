have something done, get something done 使役 have 与 get

📘 In your coursebook: English File 5e · Upper-Intermediate · 8A “Modern crime”. Use the texts and recordings with your coursebook; this page adds new words, grammar and practice.教材定位：English File 5e · Upper-Intermediate · 8A《Modern crime》。课文与录音请配合教材学习，本页补充新词、语法与练习。

Say that someone else does a job for you with have something done and get something done, instead of the common Chinese-English I repaired my car when a mechanic did it. 用 have something done 和 get something done 说「请别人替你做了某事」，而不是把修车师傅干的活说成 I repaired my car 这种中式英语。

📏 The rule规则

📏 The rule in one line规则一句话
have or get + object + past participle means someone else does the action for you, usually because you arrange or pay for it. It also describes bad things that happen to you: We had our car broken into.have 或 get 加宾语加过去分词，表示别人替你做了某事，通常是你安排或付钱请人做的。它也可以讲发生在你身上的坏事。

In Chinese, wo xiu le che can mean I fixed it or I had it fixed, and context decides. In English, I fixed my car says you did the work yourself. If a garage did it, say I had my car fixed or I got my car fixed. Saying I cut my hair when a hairdresser did it will make people look at your hair carefully.

中文「我修了车」可能是自己修，也可能是请人修。英语里 I fixed my car 是自己动手；修车厂修的要说 I had my car fixed 或 I got my car fixed。理发店剪的头发说成 I cut my hair，别人会仔细打量你的头发。

Word order is fixed: have + the thing + past participle. I had my teeth cleaned, not I had cleaned my teeth (that is past perfect: I cleaned them myself). This one change of order turns a service into your own action.

语序固定：have 加东西加过去分词。I had cleaned my teeth 是过去完成时，表示自己刷的。语序一换，「请人做」就变成了「自己做」。

get is more informal and common in speech, and can suggest effort or urgency: I finally got the landlord to fix the heater. We need to get this contract checked before Friday. have is neutral and slightly more formal. Both work in every tense: I'm having the windows replaced; we've had the locks changed; you should get that tooth looked at.

get 更口语、更常用，还可以带出费劲或着急的意味。have 中性，稍正式。两者各种时态都能用。

The same pattern describes something bad done to you by someone else: She had her bag stolen on the bus. We had our fence damaged in the storm. Here nobody arranged anything; the pattern just shows you were the one affected.

同样的结构也可以讲别人对你做的坏事。这里没人安排，结构只是说明你是受影响的人。

Related patterns: have someone do something (you ask a person to do it: I'll have my assistant send you the file) and get someone to do something (you persuade them: I got my son to tidy his room). Note the base verb after have someone and to after get someone.

相关句型：have someone do something（让某人做，后接原形）；get someone to do something（说服某人做，后接 to 不定式）。

PatternMeaningExample
have + object + past participlesomeone does it for youWe had the roof repaired.
get + object + past participlethe same, more informalI need to get my eyes tested.
have + object + past participle (bad event)it happened to youHe had his phone stolen.
have + person + base verbask someone to do itI'll have the nurse call you.
get + person + to + verbpersuade someoneWe got the landlord to replace the fridge.

Examples例句

  • I'm having my car serviced on Friday, so I'll take the bus. 周五我的车去保养，所以我坐公交。
    An arranged service.安排好的服务。
  • We had the whole house painted before we moved in. 搬进去之前我们请人把整栋房子刷了一遍。
    Done by painters.油漆工做的。
  • You should get that mole checked by a doctor. 那颗痣你该让医生看看。
    Advice about a service.建议请人检查。
  • She gets her hair cut every six weeks. 她每六周剪一次头发。
    A regular service.定期的服务。
  • We've just had solar panels installed on the roof. 我们刚在屋顶装了太阳能板。
    Present perfect.现在完成时。
  • I need to have these documents translated and notarized. 这些文件我得找人翻译并公证。
    Two services in one sentence.一句里两项服务。
  • He had his wallet stolen at the airport. 他在机场钱包被偷了。
    A bad event done to him.发生在他身上的坏事。
  • Our neighbours had their garage broken into last week. 我们邻居的车库上周被撬了。
    A bad event, past simple.坏事，一般过去时。
  • Where do you get your glasses made? 你在哪儿配的眼镜？
    Question form.疑问句。
  • I'll have the front desk call you a taxi. 我让前台给你叫辆出租车。
    have + person + base verb.have 加人加原形。
  • I finally got the bank to waive the fee. 我终于让银行免了那笔费用。
    get + person + to: persuasion.get 加人加 to：说服。
  • We're getting the kitchen redone next spring. 明年春天我们要重新装修厨房。
    Informal, future arrangement.口语，将来的安排。
  • Did you have the brakes checked before the trip? 出发前你检查刹车了吗？
    Asking about a service done.问有没有请人做。
  • It's cheaper to have your taxes done early in the season. 报税季早点找人报税更便宜。
    A general statement about a service.关于服务的一般说法。

More pairs: same idea, different choice更多对比句组

I had repaired the fence. 我那时已经把栅栏修好了。
Past perfect: I repaired it earlier.过去完成时：我自己更早修好的。
I had the fence repaired. 我请人把栅栏修了。
Someone repaired it for me.别人替我修的。
I had the fence damaged in the storm. 我家的栅栏在暴风雨里被毁了。
Something bad happened to it.发生的坏事。
I'll have my colleague send you the slides. 我让同事把幻灯片发给你。
I ask someone: base verb.让某人做，接原形。
I'll get my colleague to send you the slides. 我去叫同事把幻灯片发给你。
I persuade or push: to.叫某人做，接 to。
I'll have the slides sent to you. 我会让人把幻灯片发给你。
The person is not important.谁发不重要。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ I repaired my phone at the shop yesterday.
✓ I had my phone repaired at the shop yesterday. / I got my phone repaired at the shop yesterday.
If the shop did the work, say so with have or get.
店家修的，就用 have 或 get。
✗ I had cut my hair yesterday.
✓ I had my hair cut yesterday.
The object goes between have and the past participle.
宾语放在 have 和过去分词中间。
✗ We are going to have installed a new furnace.
✓ We are going to have a new furnace installed.
Same order in every tense.
各时态语序相同。
✗ I'll have my assistant to send it.
✓ I'll have my assistant send it.
have + person + base verb, without to.
have 加人后接原形，不加 to。
✗ I got the plumber fix the tap.
✓ I got the plumber to fix the tap.
get + person + to-infinitive.
get 加人后接 to 不定式。
