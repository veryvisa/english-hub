📏 The rule规则

🎯 The point in one line一句话要领
Abstract nouns such as impact, evidence, attention, responsibility, and emphasis combine with particular verbs, adjectives, and prepositions to express precise relationships, and mastering these combinations makes English sound more natural and sophisticated.impact、evidence、attention、responsibility 和 emphasis 等抽象名词通常与特定动词、形容词和介词形成固定搭配，掌握这些组合能够使英语表达更加自然、准确和成熟。

📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Open the full explanation: 5 points展开细讲：5 段讲解

Abstract nouns often have preferred verb partners. For example, we usually say "draw attention to", "take responsibility for", and "reach a conclusion" rather than choosing a verb simply because its dictionary meaning seems appropriate. Learning the entire combination is more effective than memorising isolated words.

抽象名词通常有更自然的动词搭档。例如，我们通常说 draw attention to（引起对……的注意）、take responsibility for（承担……责任）和 reach a conclusion（得出结论），而不是仅仅根据动词的字典释义随意选择。把整个搭配作为一个整体记忆，比孤立地背单词更有效。

Some abstract nouns are associated with particular adjectives. We commonly use compelling evidence, considerable impact, widespread concern, and mutual understanding. These adjective-noun combinations are often more precise than general expressions such as strong proof or big influence.

一些抽象名词通常与特定形容词搭配。常见组合包括 compelling evidence（有说服力的证据）、considerable impact（相当大的影响）、widespread concern（普遍关注）和 mutual understanding（相互理解）。这些形容词与名词的组合通常比 strong proof 或 big influence 等笼统表达更加准确自然。

Prepositions are an important part of abstract noun collocations. For instance, we say "access to information", "a lack of confidence", "in response to criticism", and "an increase in demand". Chinese learners sometimes translate prepositions directly from Chinese, but English combinations often follow conventional patterns that need to be learned individually.

介词是抽象名词搭配的重要组成部分。例如，access to information（获取信息的途径）、a lack of confidence（缺乏信心）、in response to criticism（作为对批评的回应）和 an increase in demand（需求增长）。中文学习者有时会直接按照中文习惯翻译介词，但英语搭配往往遵循约定俗成的模式，需要逐个掌握。

Pay attention to differences in register and strength. "A significant contribution" sounds appropriate in academic or professional writing, while "a huge contribution" is more conversational. Similarly, "raise serious concerns" is stronger and more formal than "cause some worries". Choosing the right collocation helps communicate not only meaning but also attitude and level of formality.

要注意搭配在语体和表达力度上的区别。a significant contribution（重要贡献）适合学术或职业写作，而 a huge contribution（巨大贡献）更偏口语。同样，raise serious concerns（引起严重担忧）比 cause some worries（造成一些担忧）更加正式，语气也更强。选择恰当的搭配，不仅能传达含义，还能体现态度和正式程度。

Certain abstract nouns have several near-synonymous verb partners, and small differences in pattern matter more than differences in meaning. "Make an impression" and "leave an impression on someone" both describe the effect someone or something has on another person's opinion, and both combine naturally with lasting ("make/leave a lasting impression"); "leave" is more often followed directly by "on someone" (leave an impression on the interviewer), while "make" is more often used on its own (made a strong impression) or before a that-clause. Learning the typical pattern, not an invented meaning gap, is what helps here.

有些抽象名词有好几个意思相近的动词搭档，重要的是搭配模式的细微差别，而不是编造出来的含义区分。make an impression 和 leave an impression on someone 都表示给别人留下印象、影响其看法，二者都能自然地搭配 lasting（make/leave a lasting impression）；leave 后面更常直接跟 on someone（leave an impression on the interviewer），而 make 更常单独使用（made a strong impression）或接 that 从句。要掌握的是典型搭配模式，不是人为编造的含义差异。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ The new technology makes a big influence on our lives.
✓ The new technology has a major influence on our lives.
In this meaning, English normally uses "have an influence on" rather than "make an influence on". Alternatively, use "make an impact on" or "have an impact on".
表示产生影响时，英语通常使用 have an influence on，而不是 make an influence on。也可以使用 make an impact on 或 have an impact on。
✗ The research provides strong evidences for the theory.
✓ The research provides strong evidence for the theory.
"Evidence" is generally an uncountable noun in standard English. Do not add an "s" when referring to evidence in general. Use "pieces of evidence" when you need to count individual items.
Evidence 在标准英语中通常是不可数名词，泛指证据时不能加复数词尾 s。如果需要表示一条条证据，可以使用 pieces of evidence。
✗ We need to pay more attention on customer feedback.
✓ We need to pay more attention to customer feedback.
The correct collocation is "pay attention to something", not "pay attention on something". The same preposition is used in "draw attention to something".
正确搭配是 pay attention to something，而不是 pay attention on something。同样，draw attention to something 也使用介词 to。
✗ She took the responsibility of organising the event.
✓ She took responsibility for organising the event.
The standard expression is "take responsibility for something". The noun is often used without an article in this general sense, and "for" introduces the task or action involved.
标准表达是 take responsibility for something。表示承担某项责任时，responsibility 通常不加冠词，而介词 for 用来引出所承担的任务或行为。
✗ The government made a conclusion after reviewing the data.
✓ The government reached a conclusion after reviewing the data.
English uses "reach a conclusion" or "come to a conclusion", not normally "make a conclusion". The verb "make" is appropriate in combinations such as "make a decision".
英语使用 reach a conclusion 或 come to a conclusion 表示得出结论，通常不说 make a conclusion。Make 适用于 make a decision（作出决定）等其他搭配。
✗ There was a significant increasing in housing costs.
✓ There was a significant increase in housing costs.
After the article "a" and the adjective "significant", this sentence requires the noun "increase", not the verb form "increasing". The natural pattern is "an increase in something".
在冠词 a 和形容词 significant 后面，这里需要名词 increase，而不是动词形式 increasing。自然的结构是 an increase in something。
