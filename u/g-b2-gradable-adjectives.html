Gradable and extreme adjectives: very tired, absolutely exhausted 可分级与极端形容词

Choose appropriate degree modifiers for gradable and extreme adjectives to express the intended intensity naturally and accurately. 能够根据形容词的程度属性选择恰当的程度修饰语，自然、准确地表达不同强度的感受和评价。

I'm very tired after the meeting. 开完会后，我非常疲惫。
Tired is gradable, and very indicates a high degree of tiredness.tired 可以分级，very 表示疲惫程度较高。
I'm absolutely exhausted after the meeting. 开完会后，我累得筋疲力尽。
Exhausted describes an extreme state, and absolutely reinforces that intensity.exhausted 表示极端疲惫，absolutely 进一步强调这种极端程度。
I'm fairly tired after the meeting. 开完会后，我有些疲惫。
Fairly indicates a moderate degree rather than extreme exhaustion.fairly 表示中等程度的疲惫，并没有达到精疲力竭的程度。
I'm completely exhausted after the meeting. 开完会后，我彻底累垮了。
Completely emphasizes the total extent of the exhausted state.completely 强调疲惫状态的彻底性。

📏 The rule规则

📏 The rule in one line规则一句话
Use very, fairly, and slightly with adjectives that allow degrees, but generally use absolutely, completely, or other appropriate absolute intensifiers with adjectives that already express an extreme degree.对于可以表示不同程度的形容词，通常使用 very、fairly、slightly 等修饰语；对于本身已经表示极端程度的形容词，通常使用 absolutely、completely 等强调程度的修饰语。

When an adjective describes a quality that can increase or decrease gradually, choose a gradable modifier such as very, fairly, slightly, or extremely. For example, very tired and slightly tired describe different positions on the same scale. Using absolutely with an ordinary gradable adjective is usually less natural unless the expression has a special conversational or emphatic meaning.

当形容词描述的性质可以逐渐增强或减弱时，选择 very、fairly、slightly 或 extremely 等可分级修饰语。例如，very tired 和 slightly tired 表示疲劳程度不同。对于普通的可分级形容词，使用 absolutely 通常不如上述表达自然，除非是在特殊的口语强调语境中。

When an adjective already describes an extreme state, avoid treating it as if it were an ordinary point on a scale. Choose absolutely exhausted rather than very exhausted in careful standard English. The intensifier absolutely emphasizes that the extreme condition has been reached, while completely can emphasize that the state is total.

当形容词本身已经表示极端状态时，不要简单地把它当作普通的程度形容词处理。在规范英语中，通常选择 absolutely exhausted，而不是 very exhausted。absolutely 强调已经达到极端程度，completely 则可以强调某种状态的彻底性。

Choose fairly or quite when you want to communicate a moderate degree rather than maximum intensity. In British English, quite with a gradable adjective often means fairly or reasonably, as in quite good. With an extreme adjective, quite can mean completely or absolutely, as in quite impossible. Context and the adjective itself determine the interpretation.

如果想表达中等程度，而不是最高强度，可以选择 fairly 或 quite。在英式英语中，quite 修饰可分级形容词时通常表示相当、还算，例如 quite good；修饰极端形容词时则可能表示完全、绝对，例如 quite impossible。具体含义取决于语境和形容词本身。

Use really when you want a flexible, conversational intensifier. Unlike absolutely, really can naturally modify both ordinary gradable adjectives and many extreme adjectives: really interesting and really exhausted. This makes it particularly useful in everyday speech, although very and absolutely may communicate the intended degree more precisely in some contexts.

如果想使用灵活、自然的口语强调方式，可以选择 really。与 absolutely 不同，really 可以自然地修饰普通可分级形容词和许多极端形容词，例如 really interesting 和 really exhausted。因此它特别适合日常交流，不过在某些语境中，very 和 absolutely 能更准确地表达预期程度。

Some adjectives can be used in both gradable and extreme senses, so do not memorize a fixed list without considering meaning. For example, cold is normally gradable, but freezing describes an extreme degree of cold. You can say very cold, absolutely freezing, or even very freezing in informal speech, although the last expression is generally less suitable for careful writing.

有些形容词根据具体含义既可以表示普通程度，也可以表示极端程度，因此不要脱离语义死记固定词表。例如，cold 通常是可分级形容词，而 freezing 表示极度寒冷。可以说 very cold、absolutely freezing；口语中偶尔也能听到 very freezing，但后一种表达通常不适合正式写作。

Do not assume that every adjective ending in -ed is gradable or that every adjective describing an emotion is extreme. Tired is gradable, whereas exhausted normally expresses a much stronger state. Similarly, angry is gradable, while furious is an extreme alternative. Choosing between them often changes not only the intensity but also the emotional force of a sentence.

不要认为所有以 -ed 结尾的形容词都是可分级的，也不要认为所有表示情绪的形容词都是极端形容词。tired 可以分级，而 exhausted 通常表示更强烈的状态。同样，angry 是可分级形容词，furious 则是表示极端愤怒的替代词。两者的选择不仅影响程度，也会改变句子的情绪力度。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ I'm very exhausted after the long journey.
✓ I'm absolutely exhausted after the long journey.
Exhausted already expresses an extreme degree of tiredness. Absolutely is the more natural intensifier in careful standard English. Really exhausted is also natural in everyday conversation.
exhausted 本身已经表示极度疲惫。在规范英语中，absolutely 是更自然的程度修饰语。日常口语中 really exhausted 也很自然。
✗ The situation is very impossible.
✓ The situation is absolutely impossible.
Impossible expresses an absolute condition rather than an ordinary degree. Use absolutely or completely instead of very in standard usage.
impossible 表示绝对不可能的状态，而不是普通的程度变化。在规范用法中，应使用 absolutely 或 completely，而不是 very。
✗ She was absolutely happy with the result.
✓ She was very happy with the result.
Happy is normally gradable, so very is a natural choice. Absolutely happy is possible in emphatic or special contexts, but it is not the neutral default.
happy 通常是可分级形容词，因此 very 是自然的选择。absolutely happy 在强调或特殊语境中可以使用，但不是中性表达中的首选。
✗ The movie was absolutely interesting.
✓ The movie was extremely interesting.
Interesting is an ordinary gradable adjective. Extremely expresses a very high degree naturally, whereas absolutely interesting is generally less idiomatic in neutral standard English.
interesting 是普通的可分级形容词。extremely 可以自然地表示极高程度，而 absolutely interesting 在中性的规范英语中通常不够地道。
✗ I'm slightly starving.
✓ I'm very hungry.
Starving normally describes an extreme degree of hunger, so slightly is an awkward combination. If the intended meaning is mild hunger, choose hungry with slightly, a little, or a bit.
starving 通常表示极度饥饿，因此与 slightly 搭配显得不自然。如果想表达轻微的饥饿，应选择 hungry，并使用 slightly、a little 或 a bit。
✗ The answer is very perfect.
✓ The answer is absolutely perfect.
Perfect is traditionally treated as an extreme or absolute adjective because it describes a state without flaws. Absolutely perfect is the conventional choice, although very perfect sometimes appears as deliberate informal emphasis.
perfect 传统上被视为极端或绝对形容词，因为它表示没有缺陷的状态。absolutely perfect 是惯常选择，不过 very perfect 有时也会作为刻意的口语强调出现。
