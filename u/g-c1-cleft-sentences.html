Cleft sentences: It was…, What I need is… 分裂句

📘 In your coursebook: English File 5e · Advanced · 10B “Make yourself at home”. Use the texts and recordings with your coursebook; this page adds new words, grammar and practice.教材定位：English File 5e · Advanced · 10B《Make yourself at home》。课文与录音请配合教材学习，本页补充新词、语法与练习。

Split one sentence into two parts to put the spotlight on the person, the thing, the time or the action that matters, the way English does with stress and structure instead of extra words. 把一句话拆成两部分，把聚光灯打在真正要紧的人、事、时间或动作上——英语靠结构和重音做强调，而不是多加词。

Ana found the leak. 安娜发现了漏水。
Plain: no focus.普通：没有焦点。
It was Ana who found the leak. 是安娜发现的漏水。
Focus on Ana, often against another name.强调安娜，常是和别人对比。
What Ana found was a leak. 安娜发现的是漏水。
Focus on what she found.强调她发现了什么。
What Ana did was find the leak and turn off the water. 安娜做的是找到漏水点并关掉水。
Focus on her actions.强调她的行动。

📏 The rule规则

📏 The rule in one line规则一句话
A cleft sentence splits one idea into two clauses so that one part gets the focus: It was my brother who called (the person), What we need is time (the thing), All I did was ask (the small action). Choose the frame by what you want the listener to notice and by what you want to correct.分裂句把一个意思拆成两个分句，让其中一部分成为焦点：It was … who 强调人；What … is 强调事物；All I did was 强调「只不过做了一件小事」。选哪种框架，看你要对方注意什么、要纠正什么。
  • Easy to miss易漏 ✗ It was my sister who she found the job. → ✓ It was my sister who found the job.

Chinese marks focus with shi … de or a particle. English does it with stress in speech, but in writing stress is invisible, so the structure has to do the work. My brother called has no focus. It was my brother who called answers the unspoken question "who?" and often corrects someone: not my father, my brother.

中文用「是……的」或语气词标焦点。英语口语靠重音，但书面上重音看不见，只能靠结构。普通句子没有焦点；It was my brother who called 回答了没说出口的「是谁？」，而且常常带有纠正的意思：不是我爸，是我哥。

It is/was X that/who … can spotlight almost any part of the sentence: the subject (It was the landlord who refused), the object (It's the paperwork that I hate), a time (It was only in March that the money arrived), a reason (It is because of the smoke that schools closed). Use who for people, that for everything; that also works for people in speech.

It is/was X that/who 几乎能强调句子的任何部分：主语、宾语、时间、原因。人用 who，其他都用 that；口语里人也可以用 that。

What … is/was (the wh-cleft) puts the known part first and saves the new part for the end, where English puts its main stress: What surprised me was the price. It is the natural way to introduce a point in a talk or a meeting: What I'd like to suggest is a trial month. For actions, use do: What we did was call the city.

What … is/was 把已知部分放前面，把新信息留到句末，正好落在英语句子的主重音上。这是开会、演讲时引出观点的自然方式。强调动作时用 do：What we did was call the city。

All I did was… and The only thing… shrink the action: All I did was ask a question means "I did very little, so your reaction is too big". The reason why … is that and The thing is… are spoken frames that prepare the listener for an explanation. Use The reason … is that, not ✗ is because, in careful writing.

All I did was… 和 The only thing… 把动作「缩小」：我只是问了个问题，你的反应太大了。The reason why … is that 与 The thing is… 是口语框架，提醒对方接下来是解释。认真写作时用 the reason … is that，避免 is because。

Do not cleft everything. One or two per paragraph is plenty; more sounds like a lawyer or a salesperson. The plain sentence is always available, and in a quick answer it is better: Who called? My brother.

不要句句都用分裂句。一段一两个足够，多了像律师或推销员。普通句子永远可用，快速回答时更好。

FrameFocusExample
It is/was X who…a personIt was Ana who found the leak.
It is/was X that…a thing, time, place, reasonIt was in May that the rules changed.
What … is/was Xa thing, new information lastWhat I need is a quiet room.
What … do/did is/was (to) Xan actionWhat we did was call the city.
All … is/was X"only this, nothing more"All I asked was a refund.
The reason (why) … is that Xan explanationThe reason we moved is that rent doubled.
The place/time where/when … is Xa place or timeThe place where I relax is the library.

Examples例句

  • It was my neighbour who called the fire department. 是我邻居打电话叫的消防队。
    Focus on the person, often correcting a wrong guess.强调人，常是纠正别人的猜测。
  • It's the waiting that makes people angry, not the decision. 让人生气的是等待，不是决定本身。
    Focus on a thing, with a contrast.强调一件事，并带对比。
  • It was only in April that the refund finally arrived. 直到四月退款才终于到账。
    Time + only: it came late.时间加 only：来得晚。
  • It is because of the smoke that the schools are closed today. 学校今天停课正是因为烟雾。
    Focus on the reason.强调原因。
  • What surprised me most was how quiet the streets were. 最让我吃惊的是街上那么安静。
    New information at the end, where stress falls.新信息放在句末，重音所在。
  • What I'd like to suggest is a one-month trial. 我想提议的是试行一个月。
    A polite way to introduce a proposal in a meeting.会议上引出提议的客气方式。
  • What we did was ask the other tenants to sign a letter. 我们的做法是请其他租户联名写信。
    Action focus: What … did was + verb.强调动作：What … did was 加动词。
  • What worries me is not the cost but the time it takes. 让我担心的不是钱，而是要花的时间。
    not … but inside a cleft.分裂句里用 not … but。
  • All I did was ask for the receipt. 我只不过是要了一下收据。
    "I did very little."「我没做什么。」
  • All you need is a library card. 你只需要一张图书证。
    "Nothing more is needed."「别的都不用。」
  • The reason we moved is that the rent doubled. 我们搬家的原因是房租翻倍了。
    The reason … is that, written style.the reason … is that，书面语体。
  • The thing is, we don't have a car. 问题是，我们没有车。
    Spoken frame before an explanation or a problem.口语框架，用在解释或说出难处之前。
  • The place where I feel most at home is the community garden. 我最有归属感的地方是社区菜园。
    Place focus.强调地点。
  • It wasn't until I had children that I understood my parents. 直到有了孩子我才理解了父母。
    It wasn't until … that: understanding came late.it wasn't until … that：明白得晚。

More pairs: same idea, different choice更多对比句组

We need more time. 我们需要更多时间。
Plain request.普通请求。
What we need is more time. 我们需要的是更多时间。
The listener expected something else, such as money.对方以为要的是别的，比如钱。
All we need is more time. 我们只需要更多时间。
"Nothing else": small and reasonable.「别的不需要」：要求小而合理。
The rules changed in May. 规定是五月变的。
Plain fact.普通事实。
It was in May that the rules changed. 规定正是在五月变的。
Focus on the time; correcting a wrong date.强调时间，纠正错误的日期。
Not until May did the rules change. 直到五月规定才变。
Inversion: formal, and stresses delay.倒装：正式，强调拖延。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ It was my sister who she found the job.
✓ It was my sister who found the job.
Who is already the subject; do not add she.
who 已经是主语，不再加 she。
✗ What I need it is a break.
✓ What I need is a break.
No extra it: the what clause is the subject.
不要多加 it：what 从句就是主语。
✗ The reason I moved is because of my job.
✓ The reason I moved is that I got a job here. / I moved because of my job.
In careful English, The reason … is that; or drop the reason.
认真的英语里用 the reason … is that，或者去掉 the reason。
✗ What we did was we called the city.
✓ What we did was call the city. / What we did was to call the city.
After What … did was, use the base verb.
what … did was 后接动词原形。
✗ It is in Burnaby where I live.
✓ It is in Burnaby that I live. / I live in Burnaby.
In an It is … that cleft, use that even for places.
It is … that 分裂句里，地点也用 that。
