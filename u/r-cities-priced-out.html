Young Families Head Out of the City 年轻家庭搬出城

🧠 Learn the 2 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 2 个新词

Read a news report on families leaving a big city, and see how the present perfect reports a trend that is still going on. 读一篇关于家庭搬离大城市的新闻，看现在完成时怎样报道一个仍在继续的趋势。

B2 · CELPIP 7–8⏱ 20 minG02 Present perfect and past simple confused现在完成时与一般过去时混用G12 Passive voice avoided or over-used被动语态不会用

📖 Key card知识卡
When housing costs push families out, the city loses more than residents: it loses the nurses, teachers and children that keep it working.住房成本把家庭挤走时，城市失去的不只是居民，还有让它运转的护士、老师和孩子。

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

For the third year in a row, more young families have left the region's largest city than have moved into it, according to a report released this week by the regional planning office. Most have not gone far. They have moved to smaller towns an hour or more away, where housing is more affordable but jobs are fewer and the commute is longer.

地区规划办公室本周发布的一份报告显示，离开本地区最大城市的年轻家庭，已经连续第三年多于搬进来的家庭。大多数人没走远，他们搬到了一小时以上车程的小镇，那里房子更负担得起，但工作少、通勤也更长。

"We did not want to leave," said one mother of two, who moved last year after the rent on their apartment went up for the second time in eighteen months. "We were priced out. We have gained a garden and lost two hours a day." She now drives to work three days a week and works from home on the other two.

「我们并不想走，」一位有两个孩子的母亲说。去年，她家公寓的房租在十八个月里第二次上涨，之后她们就搬了家。「我们是被价格挤出来的。多了一个花园，每天少了两个小时。」她现在每周三天开车上班，另外两天在家办公。

The report found that people under thirty-five have been hit hardest. Many have postponed having children or have moved back in with their parents to save for a down payment. Employers have noticed too: several hospitals and schools in the city say they have struggled to fill jobs because staff cannot afford to live nearby.

报告发现，三十五岁以下的人受冲击最大。很多人推迟了生孩子，或者搬回父母家住，攒首付。雇主们也注意到了：市里好几家医院和学校说，他们一直很难招到人，因为员工住不起附近。

Not everyone sees the trend as bad news. Some smaller towns have welcomed the newcomers, who have opened businesses and filled empty classrooms. But town officials say their roads, clinics and water systems were not built for this many people, and that services have not kept up.

并不是所有人都把这个趋势看成坏消息。一些小镇欢迎这些新来的人，他们开了店，也填满了空教室。但镇上的官员说，当地的道路、诊所和供水系统不是为这么多人建的，公共服务没能跟上。

The city has promised to approve more homes near transit and has changed some rules to allow basement suites and laneway houses in more neighbourhoods. Critics say those changes came too late for the families who have already gone.

市政府已经承诺在公共交通沿线批更多住房，也修改了一些规定，允许更多社区建地下室套间和后巷屋。批评者说，这些改变对已经离开的家庭来说来得太晚了。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

For the third year in a row, more young families have left the region's largest city than have moved into it
Present perfect for a trend measured up to now. The comparison keeps the same tense on both sides: have left … than have moved.
用现在完成时报道一个统计到现在的趋势。比较的两边时态一致：have left … than have moved。
who moved last year after the rent on their apartment went up for the second time in eighteen months
last year fixes a finished time, so the story switches to the past simple: moved, went up.
last year 指定了一个已结束的时间，所以叙述换成一般过去时：moved、went up。
