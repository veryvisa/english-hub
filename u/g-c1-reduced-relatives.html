Reduced relative clauses 省略的定语从句

Shorten who is / which was / that are clauses to a participle, an adjective phrase or a to-infinitive, so you can read dense English fast and write it without building the clause wrong. 把 who is、which was、that are 这类定语从句缩成分词、形容词短语或不定式，这样读密集的英文更快，自己写时也不会把从句搭错。

Read the sentences and decide what each one tells you. Then open the notes.先读这几句，自己判断各自传达了什么，再点开看注解。

Patients who are waiting for surgery may get a call. 正在等手术的病人可能会接到电话。
Full clause: clear, a little long.完整从句：清楚，略长。
Patients waiting for surgery may get a call. 等候手术的病人可能会接到电话。
Reduced: standard in news and notices.省略式：新闻和通知里的标准写法。
Waiting patients may get a call. 候诊病人可能会接到电话。
Single participle before the noun: shortest, loses for surgery.单个分词放名词前：最短，但丢了「等手术」。

📏 The rule规则

📏 The rule in one line规则一句话
A relative clause with be can lose the relative pronoun and be: people (who are) waiting, a letter (which was) sent last week, anyone (who is) interested. Other verbs become an -ing form: people who live here → people living here. The result is shorter and is everywhere in news, reports and forms.带 be 的定语从句可以连关系词带 be 一起省掉；其他动词变成 -ing。结果更短，新闻、报告和表格里随处可见。
  • Easy to miss易漏 ✗ The people live in this building have to move. → ✓ The people living in this building have to move. / The people who live in this building have to move.

English puts long descriptions after the noun; Chinese puts them before it with de. That is why a Chinese speaker often avoids relative clauses or builds them wrongly (✗ the man who he lives next door). Reduced relatives are the easier half: no pronoun, no tense, just the noun and a participle phrase straight after it: the man living next door.

英语把长修饰语放在名词后面，中文用「的」放在前面。所以华人常回避定语从句，或者搭错。省略式其实更容易：不用关系词、不用变时态，名词后面直接跟分词短语。

Use -ing for an active meaning, with an ongoing or general action: drivers using the bridge, families living in basement suites. Use -ed (the past participle) for a passive meaning: a form signed by both parents, homes built before 1980. The participle says nothing about time: homes built before 1980 can be in any sentence tense.

-ing 表主动，动作正在进行或一般性的；过去分词表被动。分词本身不带时间信息，时态由主句决定。

Do not reduce a clause with a single, finished past action in the active: the man who called yesterday stays as it is, not ✗ the man calling yesterday (that sounds as if he was calling over a period). -ing reductions work for states and repeated actions: anyone holding a ticket, students taking the bus.

主动意义、一次性完成的过去动作不能缩成 -ing。-ing 只适合状态或反复的动作。

Three more short forms: an adjective phrase (the documents necessary for the application, anyone interested in volunteering), a prepositional phrase (the people in the next room), and to + verb after the first, the last, the only, the next: She was the first to arrive. He is the only one to have noticed.

另外三种短式：形容词短语、介词短语，以及 the first / the last / the only / the next 之后接 to 加动词原形。

When you read, find the noun, then ask: is the next word a participle that belongs to it? The tenants affected by the fire will be moved looks like it has two verbs, but affected belongs to tenants; the main verb is will be moved. Long news sentences become easy once you see this.

阅读时先找名词，再问后面紧跟的分词是不是在修饰它。看起来有两个动词，其实前一个属于名词，后一个才是谓语。长新闻句一下子就好懂了。

Examples例句

  • People waiting for a family doctor can register online. 在等家庭医生的人可以网上登记。
    waiting = who are waiting.waiting 相当于 who are waiting。
  • Families living in basement suites were the worst hit by the flood. 住在地下室套房的家庭受洪水影响最严重。
    Active state: -ing.主动的状态：-ing。
  • The tenants affected by the fire will be moved to a hotel. 受火灾影响的租户将被安置到酒店。
    affected belongs to tenants; the main verb is will be moved.affected 属于 tenants，谓语是 will be moved。
  • Homes built before 1980 may contain asbestos. 1980 年以前建的房子可能含有石棉。
    Passive, no time in the participle.被动，分词本身不带时间。
  • Any form signed by only one parent will be returned. 只有一位家长签字的表格会被退回。
    Passive: signed by.被动：signed by。
  • Drivers using the bridge should expect delays. 走这座桥的司机要预计会延误。
    Active and current.主动，当下。
  • A letter sent last week explained the new fees. 上周寄出的一封信解释了新的收费。
    Passive past event: reduction allowed.被动的过去事件：可以省略。
  • Anyone interested in volunteering should talk to Grace. 有意做义工的人请找格蕾丝。
    Adjective phrase.形容词短语。
  • The documents necessary for the renewal are listed on page two. 续期所需的文件列在第二页。
    Adjective after the noun.形容词放在名词后。
  • The woman in the blue coat is our new neighbour. 穿蓝外套的那位女士是我们的新邻居。
    Prepositional phrase.介词短语。
  • She was the first to notice the leak. 她是第一个发现漏水的。
    the first to + verb.the first to 加动词。
  • He was the only one to have read the whole contract. 他是唯一读完整份合同的人。
    Perfect infinitive for an earlier action.完成式不定式表示在先的动作。
  • Students taking the 7:40 bus often arrive early. 坐 7 点 40 公交的学生常常早到。
    Repeated action: -ing.反复的动作：-ing。
  • The man who called this morning left a number. 今天早上打电话来的那个人留了电话号码。
    One finished active event: keep the full clause.一次性完成的主动动作：保留完整从句。

More pairs: same idea, different choice更多对比句组

The man who lives next door is a doctor. 住在隔壁的那个人是医生。
Full clause: correct.完整从句：正确。
The man living next door is a doctor. 住隔壁的那人是医生。
Reduced: shorter.省略式：更短。
The man who he lives next door is a doctor. 那个他住在隔壁的人是医生。
Wrong: who already is the subject.错误：who 已经是主语。
The report published yesterday shows rising rents. 昨天发布的报告显示房租在上涨。
Passive reduced: correct.被动省略：正确。
The report that was published yesterday shows rising rents. 那份昨天发布的报告显示房租在上涨。
Full: fine, heavier.完整：正确，但更重。
The report publishing yesterday shows rising rents. 昨天发布的报告显示房租在上涨。
Wrong: the report did not publish anything; it was published.错误：报告没有去发布什么，它是被发布的。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ The people live in this building have to move.
✓ The people living in this building have to move. / The people who live in this building have to move.
A second finite verb needs who; otherwise use -ing.
第二个变位动词需要 who，否则用 -ing。
✗ The car parking outside is mine.
✓ The car parked outside is mine.
The car does not park itself: passive parked.
车不会自己停：用被动 parked。
✗ The woman calling me yesterday was from the bank.
✓ The woman who called me yesterday was from the bank.
One finished active event: keep the full clause.
一次性完成的主动事件：保留完整从句。
✗ The interesting people can sign up here.
✓ People interested in this can sign up here. / Anyone interested can sign up here.
interesting = makes others interested; interested = feels interest.
interesting 是让人感兴趣，interested 是自己感兴趣。
✗ She was the first person arrived.
✓ She was the first to arrive. / She was the first person to arrive.
After the first, use to + verb.
the first 后接 to 加动词。
