Real conditionals and time clauses 真实条件句与时间从句

📘 In your coursebook: English File 5e · Upper-Intermediate · 4B “At your own risk”. Use the texts and recordings with your coursebook; this page adds new words, grammar and practice.教材定位：English File 5e · Upper-Intermediate · 4B《At your own risk》。课文与录音请配合教材学习，本页补充新词、语法与练习。

Talk about real possibilities and future conditions with if, unless, as long as, in case, when and as soon as, and keep will out of the condition. 用 if、unless、as long as、in case、when、as soon as 谈真实的可能和将来的条件，并且不在条件从句里用 will。

Only if they get hungry.饿了才拿。
I'll bring snacks in case the kids get hungry. 我带点零食，以防孩子们饿。
I bring them now, just in case.现在就带上，以防万一。
I'll bring snacks when the kids get hungry. 孩子们饿的时候我就把零食拿来。
I expect them to get hungry.我预计他们会饿。

📏 The rule规则

📏 The rule in one line规则一句话
In a real condition about the future, the if or time clause takes a present tense and the main clause takes will, a modal or an imperative. The linking word decides the logic: unless is "if not", as long as is "only if", and in case is "because it might happen".关于将来的真实条件，if 从句或时间从句用现在时，主句用 will、情态动词或祈使句。连接词决定逻辑：unless 是「如果不」，as long as 是「只要」，in case 是「以防」。
  • Easy to miss易漏 ✗ If it will snow tomorrow, the school will close. → ✓ If it snows tomorrow, the school will close.

📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Open the full explanation: 5 points, a table, 14 examples展开细讲：5 段讲解、一张表、14 个例句

Chinese marks future with words like hui and jiang in both halves, so learners often write If it will rain, I will stay home. In English the condition clause uses the present even though it is about the future: If it rains, I'll stay home. The same rule applies after when, as soon as, before, after, until and once.

中文前后两半都可以带「会」「将」，所以学习者常写 If it will rain, I will stay home。英语条件从句即使说将来也用现在时。when、as soon as、before、after、until、once 后同理。

if and when are not the same. When I get the job means I expect to get it. If I get the job means it is uncertain. Using when in an interview sounds confident; using if sounds modest or doubtful.

if 和 when 不同。When I get the job 表示我预计会得到；If I get the job 表示不确定。面试中用 when 显得自信，用 if 显得谦虚或没把握。

unless means if … not, and is usually followed by a positive verb: We'll miss the train unless we leave now = if we don't leave now. Do not double the negative: unless we don't leave means the opposite.

unless 等于 if … not，后面通常接肯定形式。别双重否定：unless we don't leave 意思正好相反。

as long as, provided that and on condition that set a strict condition: only if this happens. You can borrow the car as long as you fill it up. provided that is more formal and appears in contracts and policies.

as long as、provided that、on condition that 设的是严格条件：只有这样才行。provided that 更正式，常见于合同和条款。

in case is not a condition; it is a precaution. Take an umbrella in case it rains means take it now, because it might rain. Take an umbrella if it rains means take it only when it is raining. Zero conditionals (if + present, present) describe facts and rules: If you don't pay the bill by the due date, the bank charges interest.

in case 不是条件，而是预防。in case it rains 是现在就带上，因为可能下雨；if it rains 是下雨时才带。零条件句（if 加现在时，主句现在时）讲事实和规律。

Examples例句

  • If the flight is delayed, the airline will send you a text. 航班延误的话，航空公司会给你发短信。
    A real future possibility.真实的将来可能。
  • When I finish this course, I'm going to apply for the senior role. 学完这门课，我就去申请高级职位。
    I expect to finish.预计会学完。
  • We won't get a table unless we book ahead. 不提前订就订不到位子。
    unless = if not.unless 等于 if not。
  • You can work from home on Fridays as long as you are reachable by phone. 只要能打通你的电话，周五可以在家办公。
    A strict condition.严格条件。
  • Tenants may keep a pet provided that they inform the landlord in writing. 只要书面通知房东，租户可以养宠物。
    Formal, contract style.正式，合同用语。
  • Save a copy in case the system crashes. 存个备份，以防系统崩溃。
    A precaution.预防措施。
  • I'll send the invoice as soon as the work is done. 活一干完我就寄发票。
    Immediately after.一……就……。
  • Once you have your health card, you can register with a clinic. 拿到医保卡后，你就可以在诊所登记了。
    After this step is complete.这一步完成之后。
  • If you don't renew your licence on time, you have to pay a late fee. 不按时更新驾照就要交滞纳金。
    A general rule.一般规则。
  • If water gets into the wall, mould grows quickly. 墙里进了水，霉很快就长。
    A fact: zero conditional.事实：零条件句。
  • Call me if you need anything. 需要什么就给我打电话。
    Imperative in the main clause.主句用祈使句。
  • If you're going to be late, let the school know before nine. 如果要迟到，九点前通知学校。
    going to in the condition for a plan.条件里用 going to 说打算。
  • Don't sign anything until you have read every page. 每一页都读完之前什么都别签。
    until + present perfect for completion.until 加现在完成时表示完成。
  • If you should need more time, just ask. 万一需要更多时间，开口就行。
    should makes the condition less likely and more polite.should 让条件显得更不可能、更客气。

More pairs: same idea, different choice更多对比句组

We'll go to the beach if it's sunny. 天晴的话我们就去海边。
Neutral condition.中性条件。
We'll go to the beach unless it rains. 只要不下雨我们就去海边。
The plan is on; only rain stops it.计划照旧，只有下雨才取消。
We'll go to the beach as long as it's warm enough. 只要够暖和我们就去海边。
Only if it is warm.只有暖和才去。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ If it will snow tomorrow, the school will close.
✓ If it snows tomorrow, the school will close.
The if clause takes the present, even for the future.
if 从句即使说将来也用现在时。
✗ Unless you don't hurry, you will be late.
✓ Unless you hurry, you will be late.
unless already contains not.
unless 本身已含否定。
✗ Take your passport if they ask for ID.
✓ Take your passport in case they ask for ID.
A precaution before the event needs in case.
事前预防用 in case。
✗ I will tell you as soon as I will know.
✓ I will tell you as soon as I know.
Time clauses about the future take the present.
将来时间从句用现在时。
✗ If I will have time, I call you.
✓ If I have time, I'll call you.
Present in the condition, will in the result.
条件用现在时，结果用 will。
