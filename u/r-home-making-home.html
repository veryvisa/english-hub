When Does a Place Become Home? 一个地方什么时候才算家

🧠 Learn the 4 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 4 个新词

Read a reflective essay on when a new country starts to feel like home, and notice the present perfect carrying the years. 读一篇随笔，讲新的国家什么时候开始像家，留意现在完成时怎样承载这些年。

B2 · CELPIP 7–8⏱ 20 minG02 Present perfect and past simple confused现在完成时与一般过去时混用V03 Word family: the wrong part of speech词性用错

📖 Key card知识卡
Home arrives in pieces: small local facts, then people, then the moment you say "home" and mean the new place.家是一块一块到来的：先是本地的琐碎知识，再是人，最后是你说「回家」时指的是新地方的那一刻。

Read and listen读与听

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

People ask me whether Canada feels like home yet. I have lived here for eight years, and I have never found a short answer. The honest one is that home did not arrive on a date. It came in pieces.

常有人问我，加拿大有没有家的感觉了。我在这里住了八年，一直没找到一个简短的回答。老实说，家不是在某一天到来的，它是一块一块来的。

The first piece was practical. In our second winter I stopped converting prices in my head. Around the same time I learned which bus was always late and which bakery sold out by ten. A place starts to become home when you know its small, useless facts.

第一块是实际的。第二个冬天，我不再在脑子里换算价格。差不多同一时期，我知道了哪路公交总是晚点，哪家面包店十点前就卖光。当你知道一个地方那些琐碎无用的事实，它就开始变成家。

The second piece was people. For two years we went to work, came home and spoke to nobody on our street. Then a neighbour asked us to water her plants while she was away. It was nothing, and it changed everything. Since that summer we have looked after each other's keys, parcels and children. I have learned more English in her kitchen than in any classroom.

第二块是人。有两年，我们上班、回家，不和街上任何人说话。后来一位邻居请我们在她外出时帮忙浇花。这是件微不足道的事，却改变了一切。从那个夏天起，我们互相照看钥匙、包裹和孩子。我在她厨房里学到的英语，比在任何课堂上都多。

The last piece surprised me. Last year I flew back to the city where I was born, and after ten days I caught myself saying, "When I get home, I will fix the balcony door." I meant the apartment in Burnaby. I have thought about that sentence many times since. Home, it turns out, is not the place you come from or even the place you love most. It is the place where your unfinished jobs are waiting.

最后一块让我意外。去年我飞回出生的城市，十天后我发现自己在说：「等我回了家，要把阳台门修一修。」我指的是本拿比的公寓。此后我多次想起这句话。原来，家不是你来的地方，甚至不是你最爱的地方，而是你那些没做完的事在等着你的地方。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

Last year I flew back to the city where I was born, and after ten days I caught myself saying, "When I get home, I will fix the balcony door."
Past simple for a finished trip. In the quoted sentence, the time clause takes the present (get), not will get.
已结束的旅行用一般过去时。引语里时间从句用现在时 get，不用 will get。
Home, it turns out, is not the place you come from or even the place you love most.
it turns out is inserted as a comment. Two relative clauses with the pronoun dropped describe the place.
it turns out 是插入的评论语；两个省略关系代词的定语从句修饰 the place。
