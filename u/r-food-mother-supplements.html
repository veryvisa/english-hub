My Mother's Cupboard of Supplements 我妈的保健品柜子

🧠 Learn the 5 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 5 个新词

Read a son's story about his mother's supplements and see conditionals and wishes carry regret and empathy. 读一个儿子讲母亲保健品的故事，看条件句和 wish 怎样表达后悔与体谅。

B2 · CELPIP 7–8⏱ 20 minG07 Unreal conditionals and wish虚拟条件句与 wishV06 Register: too formal, too casual, or too blunt语域不合：太正式、太随意或太冲

📖 Key card知识卡
Arguing with a health claim rarely works; checking it together with the person who believes it often does.跟健康说法争辩很少管用；和相信它的人一起去核实，常常管用。

Read and listen读与听

316 words
Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

My mother keeps a cupboard of supplements in her kitchen. There is fish oil, a powder for her joints, and a herbal tea that promises to "clear the blood". Most of them arrived through the family group chat, where a relative forwards a long message every morning, usually with a headline in red and a photo of a doctor nobody has heard of.

我妈厨房里有一个装保健品的柜子：鱼油、一种护关节的粉，还有一种号称能「清血」的草本茶。大多是从家族群里来的。群里有个亲戚每天早上转一条长消息，通常配着红字标题和一张谁都没听说过的医生照片。

For years I said nothing. Then she stopped taking her blood pressure pills for a week because a message said the tea worked better. That was when I decided to bring it up. I did it badly. I called the messages rubbish, she called me arrogant, and we did not speak for two days. If I had been calmer that first time, we would have saved ourselves a lot of hurt.

好几年我什么都没说。后来她因为一条消息说这茶更管用，停了一周降压药。那时我决定把这事提出来。我提得很糟：我说那些消息是垃圾，她说我自以为是，我们两天没说话。要是第一次我冷静一点，我们本可以少受很多伤。

My second try went better. Instead of arguing about the claim, I asked her to help me figure out who had written it. We looked together. The doctor in the photo was selling the tea. The study the message mentioned did not seem to exist. I did not have to point out what that meant; she saw it herself.

第二次好多了。我没去争那个说法对不对，而是请她帮我一起弄清楚是谁写的。我们一起查。照片里的医生就是卖这茶的；消息里提到的研究好像根本不存在。我不用点明这意味着什么，她自己看出来了。

We now have a simple way to deal with new messages. Before she buys anything, she reads it to me on the phone, and we ask two questions: who is selling this, and where is the evidence? Some things pass. Her doctor is happy with the fish oil. Others she turns down on her own, which she seems to enjoy more than I expected.

现在我们有个简单的办法对付新消息。她买任何东西之前，先在电话里念给我听，我们问两个问题：谁在卖它？证据在哪里？有些东西过关了，她的医生对鱼油没意见；另一些她自己就拒了，而且她似乎比我想的更享受这个过程。

I wish I had understood earlier that she was not foolish. She was worried about getting old, and the messages offered her a feeling of control. If I were in her position, far from the country I grew up in, I might want those promises too. What she needed was not a lecture. It was a partner in checking.

我真希望自己早点明白她并不傻。她是担心变老，而那些消息给了她一种掌控感。要是我处在她的位置，远离自己长大的国家，也许我也会想要那些承诺。她需要的不是说教，而是一个一起核实的伙伴。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

If I had been calmer that first time, we would have saved ourselves a lot of hurt.
Third conditional: had been + would have saved; both halves are contrary to past fact.
第三类条件句：had been 配 would have saved，前后都与过去事实相反。
If I were in her position, far from the country I grew up in, I might want those promises too.
Unreal present with were; might is softer than would. The phrase between commas describes her position.
与现在事实相反，用 were；might 比 would 更软。两个逗号之间的短语说明她的处境。
