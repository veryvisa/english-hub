If I Had Spoken Up 如果当时我开口了

🧠 Learn the 4 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 4 个新词

Read a story of regret about a meeting and see unreal conditionals doing real work. 读一个关于会议的后悔故事，看非真实条件句在真实语境里怎么用。

📖 Key card知识卡
In many English-speaking workplaces, staying silent about a risk is worse than raising it softly.在许多英语职场，看到风险不说，比委婉地提出来更糟。

Read and listen读与听

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

There is a meeting I still think about. We were choosing a supplier, and the cheapest one had a problem that only I had noticed: their delivery dates did not match our launch. I had the numbers in front of me. I said nothing.

有一场会我至今还会想起。我们在选供应商，最便宜的那家有个只有我注意到的问题：他们的交货日期和我们的上市时间对不上。数字就在我面前。我什么也没说。

Why? Where I grew up, a junior person who contradicts a manager in public is being rude. So I waited for someone else to bring it up. Nobody did. The contract was signed, the deliveries were late, and the launch was put off by six weeks.

为什么？在我长大的地方，下属当众反驳经理是失礼的。所以我等别人来提。没有人提。合同签了，货交晚了，上市推迟了六周。

If I had spoken up that day, we would have chosen differently. I am sure of it. And if I were braver in meetings, I would not still be thinking about it now. My manager said something similar afterwards, though more kindly: "I wish you had told us. We would rather hear about a problem early than deal with it later."

如果那天我开了口，我们的选择会不一样，这一点我确信。如果我在会上更勇敢些，现在也不会还在想这件事。经理事后说了类似的话，只是更温和：「真希望你当时告诉了我们。我们宁可早点听到问题，也不愿事后收拾。」

Since then I have learned a few sentences that make it easier. "Could I point out one risk?" "I might be wrong, but the dates do not seem to match." They are soft, and they work. Speaking up is not an attack here; staying silent is the real risk. I still feel my heart beat faster before I open my mouth. I open it anyway. If only someone had taught me those sentences ten years ago.

从那以后我学会了几句让开口变容易的话。「我可以指出一个风险吗？」「我可能说得不对，但日期好像对不上。」话很软，却管用。在这里，开口不是攻击，沉默才是真正的风险。张嘴之前我的心还是会跳得更快，但我照样张嘴。要是十年前有人教过我这几句话就好了。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

We were choosing a supplier, and the cheapest one had a problem that only I had noticed: their delivery dates did not match our launch.
Past perfect had noticed goes one step back from the meeting. The colon introduces what the problem was.
过去完成时 had noticed 比开会那一刻更早一步。冒号后说明问题是什么。
And if I were braver in meetings, I would not still be thinking about it now.
Unreal present: were for all persons in careful English, with would be doing for an ongoing result.
与现在事实相反：正式用法里各人称都用 were，结果句用 would be doing 表示持续的状态。
