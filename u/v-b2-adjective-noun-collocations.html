📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Open the full explanation: 5 points展开细讲：5 段讲解

In Chinese, "big" and "strong" stretch across many nouns: big rain, big traffic, strong tea. English splits them in ways no rule predicts: heavy rain, heavy traffic, but strong tea and strong evidence. A listener understands big rain, yet it marks you at once as translating.

中文的「大」「浓」「强」能配很多名词：大雨、车多、浓茶。英语的分法没有规则可推：heavy rain、heavy traffic，却是 strong tea、strong evidence。说 big rain 对方听得懂，但一听就是在翻译。

Price, cost, salary, income and rent are amounts, so they are high or low. Goods are expensive or cheap. The price is expensive is one of the most common errors at this level, and it is easy to fix once you hear it.

价格、成本、工资、收入、房租是「数量」，所以用 high 或 low；东西本身才用 expensive 或 cheap。The price is expensive 是这个阶段最常见的错之一，听出来一次就好改。

Some pairs carry a set meaning that the words alone do not. A rough idea is an estimate, not a bad idea. A fair point means "you are right about that", and it is how people concede part of an argument politely. A close friend is about trust, not distance.

有些搭配的意思不是两个词相加：a rough idea 是大概的估计，不是坏主意；a fair point 是「这点你说得对」，是礼貌地让一步的说法；a close friend 讲的是交情，不是距离。

The article belongs to the pair too. Evidence and traffic are uncountable: no a, no plural. A serious problem, a common mistake and a big decision are countable and need a. Read the pair with its article every time you review it.

冠词也是搭配的一部分：evidence、traffic 不可数，不加 a，没有复数；a serious problem、a common mistake、a big decision 可数，要加 a。复习时连冠词一起念。

When you read, stop at every adjective in front of an abstract noun and ask: would I have chosen that word? The pairs you would have got wrong are the ones worth writing down.

阅读时，在每个抽象名词前的形容词处停一下，问自己：我会选这个词吗？会选错的那些，才值得记下来。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ There was a big rain last night.
✓ There was heavy rain last night.
Rain, snow and traffic are heavy, not big.
雨、雪、车流用 heavy，不用 big。
✗ The price of the flat is very expensive.
✓ The price of the flat is very high.
A price is an amount: high or low. The flat itself is expensive.
价格是数量，用 high/low；房子本身才 expensive。
✗ He has strong evidences.
✓ He has strong evidence.
evidence is uncountable. Say a piece of evidence for one item.
evidence 不可数；指一条用 a piece of evidence。
✗ I drink thick coffee in the morning.
✓ I drink strong coffee in the morning.
Coffee and tea are strong or weak.
咖啡和茶用 strong/weak 表示浓淡。
✗ A growing number of people is moving here.
✓ A growing number of people are moving here.
After a number of + plural noun, the verb is plural.
a number of 加复数名词后，动词用复数。
