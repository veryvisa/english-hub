Question tags, echo questions and short answers 附加疑问与简短回应

📘 In your coursebook: English File 5e · Upper-Intermediate · 1B “We may never know”. Use the texts and recordings with your coursebook; this page adds new words, grammar and practice.教材定位：English File 5e · Upper-Intermediate · 1B《We may never know》。课文与录音请配合教材学习，本页补充新词、语法与练习。

Build question tags that copy the right auxiliary, choose rising or falling intonation for what you mean, answer negative questions by the facts, and keep a conversation alive with echo questions and short answers. 让附加疑问照抄正确的助动词，用升调或降调表达你真正的意思，按事实回答否定问句，并用回声问句和简短回答让对话接下去。

📏 The rule规则

📏 The rule in one line规则一句话
A tag copies the first auxiliary and flips positive to negative (You've met Ana, haven't you?). A falling voice invites agreement; a rising voice asks a real question. Echo questions (Did you?, Is it?) show interest, and short answers repeat the auxiliary instead of a bare yes or no.附加疑问照抄第一个助动词，肯定变否定、否定变肯定。降调是请对方附和，升调是真提问。回声问句（Did you?、Is it?）表示感兴趣；简短回答重复助动词，而不是光说 yes 或 no。

Chinese has one tag for everything: dui ba or shi bu shi at the end of the sentence. English builds the tag from the verb in the sentence, so it changes every time: It's cold, isn't it? You live in Burnaby, don't you? She can drive, can't she? They didn't call, did they? Using right? or isn't it? for everything is understood, but it marks you as a learner.

中文一个「对吧」「是不是」包打天下，英语的附加疑问却从句子里的动词变出来，每次都不同。什么都用 right? 或 isn't it? 别人能听懂，但一听就是学习者。

The rules are mechanical. Copy the first auxiliary or modal; if there is none, use do, does or did. Flip the polarity. Use a pronoun. A few special cases: I am → aren't I?; Let's → shall we?; imperatives → will you? or could you?; there is → isn't there?; have got → haven't you?; words like never, hardly and nobody count as negative, so the tag is positive: You've never been to Banff, have you?

规则是机械的：照抄第一个助动词或情态动词；没有就用 do、does、did；肯否互换；用代词。几个特例：I am → aren't I?；Let's → shall we?；祈使句 → will you? 或 could you?；there is → isn't there?；have got → haven't you?；never、hardly、nobody 算否定，附加疑问用肯定。

Intonation decides the meaning. With a falling tone, the speaker is sure and wants agreement: Lovely day, isn't it? ↘. This is small talk, and the expected reply is It is or Yes, beautiful. With a rising tone, the speaker is unsure and needs information: You've paid the deposit, haven't you? ↗. The words are identical; only the voice tells you what kind of answer is wanted.

语调决定意思。降调表示说话人有把握、要你附和，这是闲聊，回 It is 就行。升调表示说话人没把握、需要信息。字面一样，只有声音告诉你对方要哪种回答。

The hardest point for Chinese speakers is answering negative questions. Chinese answers the speaker's statement: to "You didn't go?" you say "Right" (dui) and then "didn't go". English answers the facts. You didn't go, did you? If you did not go, say No, I didn't. If you did, say Yes, I did. Yes, I didn't is never possible, because yes and the verb must agree.

华人最难的是回答否定问句。中文是针对对方的说法回答，英语按事实回答。You didn't go, did you? 没去就说 No, I didn't；去了就说 Yes, I did。Yes, I didn't 永远不成立，因为 yes 和后面的动词必须一致。

Echo questions and short answers are how English keeps a conversation going. An echo copies the auxiliary of what you just heard: I've just moved here. Have you? It means "tell me more", not "I doubt you". A short answer repeats the auxiliary: Do you like it here? Yes, I do. Add So do I or Neither have I to share the same experience. A bare Yes. can sound cold in English, even when you mean nothing by it.

英语靠回声问句和简短回答把对话接下去。回声问句照抄刚听到的那句的助动词，意思是「多说点」，不是「我不信」。简短回答重复助动词。用 So do I、Neither have I 表示「我也是」。光说一个 Yes 在英语里可能显得冷淡，哪怕你没那个意思。

Examples例句

  • You work in the same building as my sister, don't you? 你和我姐姐在同一栋楼上班，对吧？
    No auxiliary: use do.没有助动词：用 do。
  • She's already left, hasn't she? 她已经走了，是吧？
    Copy has.照抄 has。
  • We can park here after six, can't we? 六点以后这里可以停车，对吧？
    Copy the modal.照抄情态动词。
  • They didn't send the invoice, did they? 他们没寄发票，是吧？
    Negative statement, positive tag.否定句配肯定附加疑问。
  • I'm on the list, aren't I? 我在名单上吧？
    I am → aren't I.I am 的附加疑问是 aren't I。
  • Let's order pizza, shall we? 我们点披萨吧？
    Let's → shall we.Let's 配 shall we。
  • Pass me that pen, could you? 把那支笔递给我好吗？
    Softens an imperative.让祈使句更客气。
  • Nobody called while I was out, did they? 我出去的时候没人打电话来吧？
    Nobody is negative; tag uses they.nobody 算否定，代词用 they。
  • There's a bus stop on this corner, isn't there? 这个路口有公交站，对吧？
    there in the tag.附加疑问里用 there。
  • Beautiful evening, isn't it? 傍晚真美，是吧？
    Falling tone: small talk.降调：闲聊。
  • "I just got back from Montreal." "Did you? How was it?" 「我刚从蒙特利尔回来。」「是吗？怎么样？」
    Echo + follow-up question.回声问句加追问。
  • "Do you like the new office?" "Yes, I do. It's much brighter." 「你喜欢新办公室吗？」「喜欢，亮多了。」
    Short answer + one more fact.简短回答再加一个信息。
  • "I've never been skiing." "Neither have I." 「我从没滑过雪。」「我也没有。」
    Agreeing with a negative.附和否定句。
  • "I take the SkyTrain every day." "So do I." 「我每天坐轻轨。」「我也是。」
    Agreeing with a positive.附和肯定句。

More pairs: same idea, different choice更多对比句组

"You didn't lock the car, did you?" "No, I didn't." 「你没锁车吧？」「对，没锁。」
Answer the facts: not locked.按事实答：没锁。
"You didn't lock the car, did you?" "Yes, I did." 「你没锁车吧？」「锁了。」
Answer the facts: locked.按事实答：锁了。
"You didn't lock the car, did you?" "Actually, I did." 「你没锁车吧？」「其实我锁了。」
Actually softens a correction.actually 让纠正更柔和。
"I've just started a new job." "Oh." 「我刚换了新工作。」「哦。」
Sounds uninterested.听起来没兴趣。
"I've just started a new job." "Have you? Where?" 「我刚换了新工作。」「是吗？在哪儿？」
Interested and keeps it going.有兴趣，对话继续。
"I've just started a new job." "Really? That's great." 「我刚换了新工作。」「真的吗？太好了。」
Also fine, a little more general.也可以，略笼统。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ You are from Shanghai, isn't it?
✓ You are from Shanghai, aren't you?
The tag copies the verb and subject of the sentence.
附加疑问照抄句子的动词和主语。
✗ He went to the meeting, wasn't he?
✓ He went to the meeting, didn't he?
No auxiliary in the past simple: use did.
一般过去时没有助动词，用 did。
✗ "You don't eat meat, do you?" "Yes, I don't."
✓ "You don't eat meat, do you?" "No, I don't."
Answer the facts; yes and no must match the verb.
按事实答，yes/no 要和后面的动词一致。
✗ You've never been to Jasper, haven't you?
✓ You've never been to Jasper, have you?
never makes the sentence negative, so the tag is positive.
never 使句子成为否定，附加疑问用肯定。
✗ "I've just moved here." "Yes."
✓ "I've just moved here." "Have you? Where from?"
A bare yes closes the conversation; an echo keeps it open.
光说 yes 会把对话关上，回声问句让它继续。

Record yourself in a one-minute small-talk exchange with a new colleague or neighbour. Use two tags with a falling tone, one with a rising tone, two echo questions and one So do I or Neither have I. Listen back and check whether your voice really went up and down where you meant it to.
