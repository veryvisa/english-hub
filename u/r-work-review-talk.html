What I Have Learned from Ten Performance Reviews 十次绩效面谈教给我的

🧠 Learn the 5 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 5 个新词

Follow a short talk about performance reviews and track every switch between have done and did. 听读一篇关于绩效面谈的短讲话，留意每一次 have done 与 did 的切换。

B2 · CELPIP 7–8⏱ 20 minG02 Present perfect and past simple confused现在完成时与一般过去时混用V03 Word family: the wrong part of speech词性用错

A performance review is a negotiation about next year, and knowing your own results is your job.绩效面谈是一场关于明年的谈判，而清楚自己的成绩是你自己的事。

Read and listen读与听

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Good afternoon, everyone. I have worked in Canada for ten years, and I have sat through ten performance reviews. I want to tell you what they have taught me, because nobody explained any of it when I arrived.

各位下午好。我在加拿大工作了十年，经历了十次绩效面谈。我想讲讲它们教会了我什么，因为我刚来的时候没有人解释过这些。

My first review was in 2016. My manager asked, "What have you achieved this year?" I answered honestly: "My team did well." In the culture I came from, that was the right answer. Here it sounded as if I had done nothing. I did not get a raise that year.

我的第一次面谈是在 2016 年。经理问：「你今年取得了什么成绩？」我老实回答：「我们团队做得不错。」在我原来的文化里，这是正确答案。在这里，听起来却像我什么也没做。那年我没有加薪。

The second year I prepared. I wrote down three things I had finished, with numbers. I said "I" instead of "we". It felt uncomfortable, almost rude. My manager nodded and wrote everything down. Since then I have never gone into a review without a list.

第二年我做了准备。我写下自己完成的三件事，带上数字。我说「我」而不是「我们」。这让我别扭，几乎觉得失礼。经理点点头，全记了下来。从那以后，我没有一次不带清单就去面谈。

Here is what I have learned. First, a review is not a judgement of your character; it is a negotiation about next year. Second, awareness of your own results is your job, not your manager's. Third, ask for one improvement you can work on, and follow up on it in three months. Managers have always valued people who are reliable, but in my experience they promote people who are both reliable and visible. I have made every one of these mistakes so that you do not have to. Thank you.

我学到的是这些。第一，面谈不是对你人品的评判，而是一场关于明年的谈判。第二，清楚自己的成绩是你的事，不是经理的事。第三，请对方指出一处你可以改进的地方，三个月后再跟进。经理一向看重可靠的人，但据我的经验，他们提拔的是既可靠又被看见的人。这些错我全犯过，为的是你们不必再犯。谢谢。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

Here it sounded as if I had done nothing.
as if + past perfect: an impression about an earlier time that was not true.
as if 后接过去完成时：给人一种关于更早时间的印象，而这印象并不属实。
Managers have always valued people who are reliable, but in my experience they promote people who are both reliable and visible.
Present perfect with always for something true from the past up to now; then present simple for a general truth.
现在完成时加 always 表示从过去到现在一直如此；后半句用一般现在时说普遍情况。
