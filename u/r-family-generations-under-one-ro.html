Read and listen读与听

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

When my grandmother moved into our home, I expected our daily routine to change. She had lived alone for years and was used to making her own decisions. My parents welcomed her, but nobody knew exactly how we would share the space. At first, even simple things caused disagreements. She preferred early dinners, while I often returned home late from work.

祖母搬进我们家时，我就预料到日常生活会发生变化。她独自生活多年，已经习惯自己做决定。父母欢迎她入住，但没有人确切知道我们应该如何共享生活空间。起初，甚至一些简单的事情也会引起分歧。她喜欢早点吃晚饭，而我经常下班很晚才回家。

The biggest challenge was our different ideas about technology. My grandmother preferred face-to-face conversations and worried that younger family members spent too much time on their phones. I thought she was being too critical. One evening, however, I showed her how to make a video call. She was delighted when she could see an old friend who lived far away. Technology suddenly became something we could enjoy together.

最大的挑战是我们对科技的不同看法。祖母更喜欢面对面交流，并担心年轻的家庭成员花太多时间看手机。我觉得她过于挑剔。然而，一天晚上，我教她如何进行视频通话。当她能够看到一位住在远方的老朋友时，她非常高兴。科技突然变成了我们可以一起享受的东西。

We gradually developed a better routine. My grandmother helped prepare dinner, and I took care of online shopping and other tasks she found difficult. My parents arranged a weekly family meal so that everyone had time to talk. While sharing a home can reduce living costs and provide practical support, it also requires patience. Each generation needs some personal space and freedom.

我们逐渐形成了更好的生活习惯。祖母帮忙准备晚餐，而我负责网上购物以及其他她觉得困难的事情。父母安排每周一次家庭聚餐，让每个人都有时间交流。虽然共同居住可以降低生活成本并提供实际帮助，但也需要耐心。每一代人都需要一定的个人空间和自由。

After several months, I began to appreciate what our arrangement offered. My grandmother shared stories about her childhood, and I learned about experiences I had never imagined. She also became more confident using technology. Our home was not always quiet, and we still disagreed sometimes. Nevertheless, we learned that a successful family is not one without differences. It is one where people are willing to understand each other.

几个月后，我开始珍惜这种家庭安排带来的好处。祖母分享了她童年的故事，让我了解到许多从未想象过的经历。她使用科技产品也变得更加自信。我们的家并不总是安静的，而且我们有时仍然会意见不合。然而，我们逐渐明白，成功的家庭并不是没有差异的家庭，而是成员愿意相互理解的家庭。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

She was delighted when she could see an old friend who lived far away.
The relative clause who lived far away provides additional information about the friend, while when introduces the time of the reaction.
who lived far away 是修饰 friend 的定语从句，而 when 引导表示反应发生时间的状语从句。
While sharing a home can reduce living costs and provide practical support, it also requires patience.
While introduces a concessive clause that presents a benefit before the main clause explains an additional requirement.
While 引导让步状语从句，先说明共同居住的好处，再由主句指出它还需要耐心。
