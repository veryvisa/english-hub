Ask the Money Desk: Why Pay in Full? 理财问答：为什么要全额还款

Read an advice column on paying a credit card in full and use unreal conditionals to compare two choices. 读一篇讲信用卡全额还款的问答专栏，用虚拟条件句比较两种选择。

📖 Key card知识卡
A credit card paid in full each month costs nothing; the minimum payment keeps you paying interest on interest.每月全额还清的信用卡不花一分钱；只还最低还款额，你就一直在为利息付利息。

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

"My card statement says I only have to make a small minimum payment each month. My husband says we should always pay the whole balance. If the bank lets me pay less, why should I pay more? Happy with the Minimum"

「我的信用卡对账单上说，我每月只需还一笔很小的最低还款额。我丈夫说应该每次都把全部欠款还清。既然银行让我少还，我为什么要多还？——最低还款就挺好」

Dear Happy, your husband is right, and the reason is interest. Whatever you do not pay by the due date starts to cost you interest, usually at a high rate, and that interest is added to the balance. Next month you pay interest on the interest. The minimum payment is designed to keep you in debt for a long time, not to help you out of it.

亲爱的「最低还款就挺好」：你丈夫说得对，原因是利息。到期日前没还的部分都会开始计息，利率通常很高，利息还会加进欠款里。下个月你就在为利息付利息。最低还款额的设计，是让你长期欠着，而不是帮你还清。

Here is a way to see it. Imagine you buy a sofa on your card and pay only the minimum. You would still be paying for that sofa long after it had worn out. If you had paid in full, the sofa would have cost exactly its price, and the card would have given you a short loan for free. That is the whole trick: a card used well costs nothing, and a card used badly is one of the most expensive loans you can have.

换个角度看。设想你刷卡买了张沙发，每月只还最低额。沙发都用旧了，你可能还在为它还钱。如果你当时全额还了，沙发就只花它本身的价钱，信用卡还白给了你一笔短期借款。窍门就在这里：用得好的信用卡一分钱不花，用得不好的信用卡是你能借到的最贵的钱之一。

So make a decision now: use the card only for what you could pay off this month. If one month you cannot pay in full, pay as much as you can, not the minimum, and take a break from the card until the balance is gone. Paying in full also has an impact on your credit score, because a low balance looks reliable to lenders. And keep the card itself: it comes with fraud protection that cash does not have.

所以现在就做个决定：只用这张卡买你这个月还得清的东西。哪个月没法全额还，就尽量多还，别只还最低额，并且在欠款还清之前先停用这张卡。全额还款对你的信用分也有好处，因为低余额在贷方眼里显得可靠。卡本身留着：它带有现金没有的防盗刷保障。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

Whatever you do not pay by the due date starts to cost you interest, usually at a high rate, and that interest is added to the balance.
Whatever you do not pay by the due date is the whole subject; its verb is starts.
Whatever you do not pay by the due date 整个是主语，谓语是 starts。
If you had paid in full, the sofa would have cost exactly its price, and the card would have given you a short loan for free.
Third conditional: had paid / would have cost for an imagined past.
第三类条件句：had paid / would have cost，想象过去的另一种情形。
