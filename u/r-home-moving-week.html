The Week Before the Move 搬家前的那一周

🧠 Learn the 6 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 6 个新词

Read a family email about a move and notice how the writer looks at the coming week from different points in time. 读一封讲搬家的家信，留意写信人怎样站在不同的时间点上看即将到来的一周。

B2 · CELPIP 7–8⏱ 20 minG08 Tense not kept consistent across a story叙事时态前后不一G12 Passive voice avoided or over-used被动语态不会用

📖 Key card知识卡
A move goes better when every job has a day and every day has a person.搬家顺不顺，看每件活有没有定好哪天做、每一天有没有人负责。

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Hi Mum, you asked for the plan, so here it is. The keys to the new place are due on Saturday morning, and the movers are coming at eight. By the time you land on Sunday evening, we will have moved everything except the piano, which needs a special team and is booked for the following Wednesday.

妈，你问我们的安排，我写下来给你。新房的钥匙定在周六上午交，搬家公司八点到。等你周日晚上落地，除了钢琴，我们会把东西都搬完。钢琴要专门的团队，订在下周三。

This week will be busy. Tomorrow and Tuesday, Mark will be packing the kitchen while I finish work. On Wednesday we are having the carpets in the new place cleaned, and on Thursday a locksmith is getting the locks changed, because nobody knows how many copies of the old keys are out there. We also need to get the furnace checked before winter, but that can wait a week.

这周会很忙。明天和周二我上班，马克在家打包厨房。周三我们请人把新房的地毯清洗一遍，周四锁匠来换锁，因为谁也不知道旧钥匙外面有多少把。暖气炉也得在入冬前找人检查，不过可以晚一周。

Please don't worry about the boxes. I know you will be thinking of the time we moved in 2009, when nothing was labelled and we spent a week looking for the kettle. This time every box has a room name and a number, and the list is on my phone.

箱子的事你别担心。我知道你会想起 2009 年那次搬家，什么都没贴标签，我们找烧水壶找了一个星期。这次每个箱子都写了房间名和编号，清单在我手机里。

When you arrive, we probably won't have unpacked much, so the guest room will be the one with a bed and very little else. By the end of your visit, though, I hope we will have hung the pictures and found the kettle on the first day.

你到的时候，我们估计还没怎么拆箱，所以客房除了一张床基本什么都没有。不过等你走的时候，我希望照片都挂好了，而且烧水壶第一天就找到了。

Will you be needing a ride from the airport, or is your friend still picking you up? Let me know by Friday. Love, Jenny

你需要我们去机场接吗，还是你朋友还去接你？周五前告诉我。爱你的珍妮

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

By the time you land on Sunday evening, we will have moved everything except the piano
By the time + present simple sets a future point; will have moved says the job is finished before it.
by the time 加一般现在时设定将来的时间点；will have moved 表示在那之前已经完成。
on Thursday a locksmith is getting the locks changed
get + object + past participle: the locksmith arranges or does the job, and the present continuous shows it is booked.
get 加宾语加过去分词；现在进行时表示已经约好。
Will you be needing a ride from the airport, or is your friend still picking you up?
The future continuous asks politely about plans without making an offer sound like pressure.
将来进行时客气地问对方的安排，不让人觉得有压力。
