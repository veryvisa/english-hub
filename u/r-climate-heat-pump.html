Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Our gas furnace is twenty years old, and the technician says it could fail any winter. My husband wants to replace it with the same kind. Our daughter keeps telling us to get a heat pump because of emissions. We have heard they do not work when it is really cold. Who is right? (Two Generations, One Furnace)

我们家的燃气暖气炉用了二十年，维修师傅说哪个冬天都可能坏。我丈夫想换一台同样的，女儿则一直说为了减排要装热泵。我们听说热泵在特别冷的时候不管用。到底谁对？（两代人，一台炉子）

You are asking at the right time. If your furnace had already broken down in January, you would have had to take whatever an installer could fit that week. Because it is still working, you can compare options calmly and figure out what suits your house.

你们问得正是时候。要是暖气炉一月份已经坏了，你们就只能装安装师傅那一周装得上的东西。正因为它还在运转，你们可以从容比较，弄清什么适合自己的房子。

First, the cold. Older heat pumps did struggle in deep winter, and that reputation has stuck. Many newer models are designed for cold climates and keep working well below freezing, although they become less efficient as the temperature drops. In the coldest regions, some households keep a backup heat source for the worst few days. Ask any installer for the model's cold-weather performance in writing.

先说冷。老式热泵在严冬里确实吃力，这个名声一直没甩掉。很多新机型是为寒冷气候设计的，在远低于零度时仍能运转，只是气温越低效率越低。在最冷的地区，有些家庭会留一个备用热源，应付最冷的那几天。不管找哪位安装师傅，都请他把机型的低温性能写下来给你。

Second, cost. A heat pump usually costs more up front, but it also cools the house, which matters more now that heat waves are longer. Running costs depend heavily on local energy prices, so get at least two quotes and ask each installer to explain them. Some governments and utilities offer rebates, so look into what applies where you live before you sign anything, because programs change.

再说钱。热泵通常前期花费更高，但它也能给房子降温；如今热浪越来越长，这一点更重要。运行成本很大程度上取决于当地的能源价格，所以至少拿两份报价，请每位安装师傅讲清楚。有些政府和公用事业公司提供返款，签任何东西之前先查查你们那里适用什么，因为这些项目常有变化。

If I were you, I would ask your daughter to help with the research. She clearly cares about the family's carbon footprint, and you clearly care about staying warm. Those goals are not as far apart as they used to be. And if you ever wish you had started sooner, remember that you started before the furnace stopped, which is more than most people manage.

如果我是你，我会请女儿帮忙查资料。她显然在乎家里的碳足迹，你们显然在乎暖和。这两个目标已经不像过去那样相距甚远了。如果哪天你们后悔没早点开始，别忘了，你们是在炉子停转之前就动手的，这已经比大多数人强了。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

If your furnace had already broken down in January, you would have had to take whatever an installer could fit that week.
Third conditional: had broken down (it did not) leads to would have had to (so you did not have to).
第三类条件句：had broken down（实际没坏）推出 would have had to（所以实际不必如此）。
Older heat pumps did struggle in deep winter, and that reputation has stuck.
did struggle: did adds emphasis, admitting the point before answering it. has stuck: the reputation began in the past and is still here.
did struggle：did 表示强调，先承认这一点再回应。has stuck：名声从过去开始，到现在还在。
