📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Open the full explanation: 5 points展开细讲：5 段讲解

Academic word lists are built from the words that appear across many subjects, not inside one. Assess, approach, factor, evidence, significant and require show up in a medical leaflet, a court decision and a business plan alike. They are the bridge between upper-intermediate English and professional reading.

学术词表收的是跨学科都出现的词，不是某一行的术语。assess、approach、factor、evidence、require 在医疗说明、法院判决、商业计划里都见得到，是中高级英语通向专业阅读的桥。

The common mistake is the wrong form, not the wrong meaning: "we did an analyse", "an assess of the risk". Each word in this unit comes with its family, and the example uses the form that causes most trouble.

常见错误是词形用错，而不是意思错：an analyse、an assess。本单元每个词都带出词族，例句专门用最容易错的那个形式。

Several words change meaning by part of speech. Approach as a verb is "come near" or "start dealing with"; as a noun it is "a way of dealing with": a new approach to teaching. Concept is an idea; conceive is to form it. Note the preposition in an approach to, a response to, the impact on.

有几个词词性一变意思就变：approach 作动词是「接近、着手」，作名词是「方法」，且配 to。注意 an approach to、a response to、the impact on 里的介词。

British spelling uses analyse and -ise; North American spelling uses analyze and -ize. Both are correct; choose one and stay with it in a document.

英式拼写用 analyse 和 -ise，北美用 analyze 和 -ize。两种都对，一份文件里统一即可。

British stress shifts in some families, which matters for listening: ANalyse but aNAlysis and anaLYtical. Say each family aloud once so that you recognise all of its forms in a lecture.

有些词族重音会移位，影响听力：ANalyse、aNAlysis、anaLYtical。每个词族大声读一遍，讲座里才认得出所有形式。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ We did an analyse of the survey.
✓ We did an analysis of the survey. / We analysed the survey.
analyse is the verb; analysis is the noun.
analyse 是动词，analysis 是名词。
✗ The bank will do an assess of your income.
✓ The bank will assess your income. / The bank will do an assessment of your income.
Verb assess, noun assessment.
动词 assess，名词 assessment。
✗ We need a new approach of the problem.
✓ We need a new approach to the problem.
an approach to.
approach 后接 to。
✗ The results are consistent to our earlier study.
✓ The results are consistent with our earlier study.
consistent with.
consistent with。
✗ We must approach to the manager.
✓ We must approach the manager.
As a verb, approach takes a direct object.
作动词时直接带宾语，不加 to。
