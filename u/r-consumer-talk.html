How to Complain So People Listen 怎样投诉别人才会听

Read and listen读与听

252 words
Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Good evening, everyone, and thank you for coming out on a cold night. For twelve years I handled complaints for a large retailer, and tonight I want to give you the same advice I used to give my own family.

大家晚上好，谢谢各位冒着寒冷出来。我在一家大型零售商处理投诉做了十二年，今晚我想把当年给自己家里人的建议同样讲给大家。

The first thing to understand is that the person at the counter usually did not cause your problem and cannot always fix it. Treat them as someone who can help you reach the person who can. Ask for their name, write it down and keep a record of every call. You would be surprised how much faster things move when someone knows they are named in your notes.

首先要明白，柜台后面的人通常不是你问题的起因，也不一定能解决它。把他们当作能帮你找到解决之人的人。问他们的名字，记下来，每一通电话都留个记录。当有人知道自己的名字写在你的笔记里，事情推进的速度会快得让你吃惊。

Second, decide what you want before you complain. A refund? A repair? An apology? People who walk in angry and leave without asking for anything specific usually get nothing. Say it in one sentence: "I'd like a full refund, or a replacement by Friday."

第二，投诉之前先想好你要什么。退款？维修？道歉？那些气冲冲进来、又什么具体要求都没提就走的人，通常什么也得不到。用一句话说出来：「我希望全额退款，或者周五前换一个新的。」

Third, read the fine print before you buy, not after. Warranties, return windows and cancellation terms are all there. And if you ever hire a contractor, get two quotes in writing. I did not, the first time, and it was the only mistake I made twice.

第三，买之前读小字条款，而不是买了之后。保修、退货期和取消条款都写在里面。还有，如果哪天要请装修工，拿两份书面报价。我第一次就没这么做，而那是我唯一犯过两次的错。

Finally, if the first answer is no, ask politely who else can look at it. Escalating is not rude; it is how the system is designed to work. And when someone does help you, tell their manager. For most front-line staff, it is the only thanks they ever hear. Now, let's take some questions.

最后，如果第一次得到的回答是不行，就客气地问一下还有谁能看看。往上反映并不失礼，这套体系本来就是这样设计的。还有，当有人真的帮了你，告诉他们的经理。对大多数一线员工来说，这是他们能听到的唯一一句感谢。好，现在我们来回答一些问题。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

The first thing to understand is that the person at the counter usually did not cause your problem and cannot always fix it.
The first thing: the with ordinal numbers. the person at the counter: the because the listener knows which person (the one in front of you).
The first thing：序数词前用 the。the person at the counter：听的人知道是哪一个（就是你面前那个），所以用 the。
I did not, the first time, and it was the only mistake I made twice.
the first time, the only: fixed uses of the. Compare: a mistake (any one) and the only mistake (just one exists).
the first time、the only：the 的固定用法。对比 a mistake（随便哪一个）和 the only mistake（只有这一个）。
