📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Open the full explanation: 5 points展开细讲：5 段讲解

English distinguishes between doing exercise and playing sports. We generally say 'do exercise', 'work out', or 'go running', but 'play tennis', 'play football', and 'play basketball'. Chinese learners sometimes use 'play' for every physical activity, but this sounds unnatural when talking about general fitness routines.

英语会区分一般锻炼和体育项目。通常说 do exercise（锻炼）、work out（健身训练）、go running（去跑步），但说 play tennis（打网球）、play football（踢足球）和 play basketball（打篮球）。中文学习者有时会把所有运动都说成 play，但描述日常健身活动时这样表达不自然。

Several expressions describe different stages of a workout. A warm-up prepares your body for exercise, a workout is the main training session, and a cool-down helps your body return to a resting state. These words are useful when explaining a complete exercise routine.

一些表达分别描述一次锻炼的不同阶段。warm-up（热身）帮助身体为运动做好准备，workout（训练）指主要锻炼过程，cool-down（整理放松）则帮助身体逐渐恢复到休息状态。这些词适合用来介绍完整的运动流程。

Fitness vocabulary often uses verbs that describe gradual improvement. You can build strength, improve endurance, increase flexibility, and maintain a healthy weight. Avoid saying 'make my endurance stronger'; 'improve my endurance' or 'build up my endurance' is more natural.

健身词汇经常使用表示逐步提升的动词。例如 build strength（增强力量）、improve endurance（提高耐力）、increase flexibility（增强柔韧性）和 maintain a healthy weight（保持健康体重）。不要说 make my endurance stronger，更自然的表达是 improve my endurance 或 build up my endurance。

Some words describe how demanding an activity is. 'Intensity' refers to how hard you are exercising, while 'stamina' is your ability to keep going over time. 'Strength' describes physical power, and 'flexibility' describes how easily your joints and muscles move. These qualities are related but not interchangeable.

有些词描述运动的强度和身体能力。intensity（强度）指锻炼有多费力，stamina（耐力）指持续运动的能力，strength（力量）描述身体的力量水平，而 flexibility（柔韧性）描述关节和肌肉活动的灵活程度。这些概念彼此相关，但不能随意互换。

Fitness instructions frequently use phrasal verbs and short chunks. 'Warm up' means prepare your body, 'stretch out' means extend your muscles, and 'push yourself' means make a greater effort. Notice that 'warm-up' is a noun, while 'warm up' is a verb.

健身指导中经常使用短语动词和固定表达。warm up 表示热身，stretch out 表示伸展肌肉，push yourself 表示让自己付出更大努力。注意，warm-up 作名词时通常带连字符，而 warm up 作动词时分开书写。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ I play exercise every morning.
✓ I exercise every morning.
Use 'exercise' as a verb for general physical activity. 'Play' is normally used with sports and games such as tennis or football.
exercise 可以直接作动词，表示锻炼身体。play 通常与网球、足球等具体体育项目或游戏搭配，不能说 play exercise。
✗ I need a good work out every morning.
✓ I need a good workout every morning.
'Workout' is written as one word when it is a noun. Keep the two-word form 'work out' only for the verb, as in 'I work out every morning'.
workout 作名词时写成一个单词。两个单词的 work out 只用作动词，例如 I work out every morning。
✗ I want to improve my stamina strength.
✓ I want to improve my stamina and strength.
'Stamina' and 'strength' describe different physical qualities. Use 'and' to connect them when you want to talk about improving both.
stamina 表示耐力，strength 表示力量，二者是不同的身体素质。如果想表达两方面都要提高，应该用 and 连接。
✗ I usually make warm-up before running.
✓ I usually do a warm-up before running.
The natural collocation is 'do a warm-up' or 'warm up'. English does not normally use 'make' with this activity.
英语中通常说 do a warm-up（做热身）或 warm up（热身），不说 make a warm-up。
✗ I go to gym for doing cardio.
✓ I go to the gym to do cardio.
Use 'the gym' when referring to a gym as a place, and use the infinitive 'to do' to explain your purpose. 'Do cardio' is the natural expression.
gym 前通常需要加 the，表示去健身房。说明目的时使用 to do，而不是 for doing。固定搭配是 do cardio。
✗ I exercise very hard because I want to keep health.
✓ I exercise regularly because I want to keep fit.
'Keep fit' means maintain good physical condition. 'Keep healthy' is also possible, but 'keep health' is incorrect because 'health' is a noun, not an adjective.
keep fit 表示保持良好的身体状态，也可以说 keep healthy，但不能说 keep health，因为 health 是名词，不能直接放在 keep 后面充当形容词。
