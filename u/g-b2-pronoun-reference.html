he, she, it, they: keeping reference straight 代词指代不出错

📘 In your coursebook: English File 5e · Advanced · 2B “Spellcheck”. Use the texts and recordings with your coursebook; this page adds new words, grammar and practice.教材定位：English File 5e · Advanced · 2B《Spellcheck》。课文与录音请配合教材学习，本页补充新词、语法与练习。

Keep every he, she, it, they and this pointing at one clear thing, in speech and in writing. 让每个 he、she、it、they、this 都指向一个清楚的对象，说和写都一样。

B2 · CELPIP 7–8⏱ 30 minG14 he / she confused in speech口语中 he、she 混

👀 Look first: what changes?先看对比：差在哪里

Read the sentences and decide what each one tells you. Then open the notes.先读这几句，自己判断各自传达了什么，再点开看注解。

Tom told Ben he could leave early. 汤姆告诉本，他可以早走。
Unclear: Tom or Ben can leave.不清楚：是汤姆还是本可以走。
Tom told Ben that Ben could leave early. 汤姆告诉本，本可以早走。
Clear by repetition; a little heavy.靠重复变清楚，稍显笨重。
Tom told Ben, "You can leave early." 汤姆对本说：「你可以早走。」
Clear and natural in speech.清楚，口语里也自然。
Ben was told he could leave early. 本被告知他可以早走。
Clear: the subject is the person who leaves.清楚：主语就是要走的人。

📏 The rule规则

📏 The rule in one line规则一句话
A pronoun is a promise that the listener already knows who or what you mean. If two people or two ideas could fit, repeat the noun, rename it, or rebuild the sentence.代词等于向对方保证「你知道我说的是谁、是什么」。只要有两个人或两件事都可能对上，就重复名词、换个说法，或把句子重新组织。

In spoken Chinese ta sounds the same for a man, a woman and a thing, so the gender choice never becomes automatic. In English a wrong he for your mother is not a small slip: for a second the listener thinks a new man has entered the story. Fix it by attaching the pronoun to the person, not to the word: picture your mother, then say she. If you catch yourself, correct it out loud and keep going: he, sorry, she.

中文口语里「他、她、它」读音相同，所以英语的性别选择很难变成本能。把妈妈说成 he 不是小口误：对方会以为故事里又冒出一个男人。纠正办法是把代词绑在人身上而不是绑在词上：先想到妈妈这个人，再说 she。说错了就当场改口继续：he, sorry, she。

When two people of the same gender share a sentence, he or she stops working. Tom told Ben he had been promoted has two readings. Choose one of three repairs: repeat the name (Tom told Ben that Ben had been promoted), use reported speech with a clear speaker (Tom said, "You've been promoted"), or put the person you mean as the subject (Ben heard from Tom that he had been promoted).

同一句里有两个同性别的人，he 或 she 就会失灵。Tom told Ben he had been promoted 有两种读法。三种修法任选：重复名字；改成直接引语让说话人清楚；或者把你要说的人放到主语位置。

It, this and that do different jobs. It points to a thing already in focus: I bought a heater. It works well. This points to a whole idea you have just said and want to keep talking about: Rents rose by a third. This means many families moved out. That points back with some distance, often to close a topic: That was the end of it. In writing, this alone is often too vague; add a noun: this increase, this decision.

it、this、that 分工不同。it 指已经在焦点上的东西；this 指刚说完、还要接着谈的整个想法；that 带距离感地回指，常用来收住话题。书面语里光秃秃的 this 往往太含糊，后面加个名词：this increase、this decision。

For a person whose gender you do not know or do not need, modern English uses singular they: If a customer calls, ask them for their reference number. It is standard in official letters and style guides. He or she is still correct but sounds heavy when repeated. He alone for everyone now sounds old-fashioned or exclusive.

性别不知道或无关紧要时，现代英语用单数 they：如果有顾客来电，问他们要档案编号。公函和文体指南里都这样用。he or she 也对，但重复几次就显得笨重；一律用 he 现在听起来过时，或有排斥意味。

Group nouns and companies take it or they. In formal writing a company or council is usually it: The council has published its plan. In speech, and in British English especially, people often say they: The bank called and they want another form. Pick one and keep it for the whole paragraph. The former and the latter are for writing only, and only when exactly two things have just been named.

集体名词和公司可以用 it 或 they。正式写作里一般用 it；口语里，尤其英式英语，常说 they。选一种，整段保持一致。the former / the latter 只用于书面，而且必须刚好刚提过两样东西。

Examples例句

  • My mother is a nurse. She works nights at the hospital. 我妈妈是护士，她在医院上夜班。
    Picture the person, then choose the pronoun.先想到这个人，再选代词。
  • My manager, Linda, said she would call me back. 我的经理琳达说她会给我回电。
    One woman named, so she is safe.只提到一位女性，she 不会有歧义。
  • When Sara met Amy at the station, Amy was in tears. 莎拉在车站见到艾米时，艾米在哭。
    Two women: repeat the name you mean.两位女性：重复你要说的那个名字。
  • Mark asked his father whether his father could lend him the car. 马克问父亲能不能把车借给他。
    Clumsy but clear; better: Mark asked his father, "Can I borrow the car?"笨但清楚；更好的说法是改成直接引语。
  • If a tenant wants to leave early, they should give written notice. 租客如果想提前搬走，应当书面通知。
    Singular they for an unknown person.未知的人用单数 they。
  • Each applicant must bring their own documents. 每位申请人都须自带文件。
    Each + their is now standard.each 配 their 现在是标准用法。
  • The city raised parking fees by half. This decision angered many shop owners. 市里把停车费提高了一半。这个决定惹恼了不少店主。
    This + noun sums up the idea.this 加名词，概括前面的想法。
  • Prices went up, wages did not, and that is why families are struggling. 物价涨了，工资没涨，这就是家庭日子难过的原因。
    That is why points back to the whole situation.that is why 回指整个情况。
  • The company says it will reply within ten days. 公司说会在十天内答复。
    Formal: a company is it.正式语体里公司用 it。
  • The bank phoned, and they need a copy of my lease. 银行来电话了，他们要我租约的复印件。
    Speech: they for the people at the bank.口语里用 they 指银行的人。
  • We looked at a house and a condo; the latter was cheaper but much smaller. 我们看了一栋独立屋和一套公寓，后者便宜但小得多。
    The latter: the second of two, writing style.the latter 指两者中后一个，书面语。
  • The neighbours and their dogs were all in the yard, and the dogs were barking. 邻居们和他们的狗都在院子里，狗在叫。
    They would be unclear; repeat the dogs.用 they 会不清楚，重复 the dogs。
  • My son and his friend practise English with each other on the bus. 我儿子和他朋友在公交车上互相练英语。
    Each other for two people doing the same thing to the other.两个人互相做同一件事用 each other。

More pairs: same idea, different choice更多对比句组

Rents rose by a third. It hit young families hardest. 房租涨了三分之一。它对年轻家庭打击最大。
It is loose here: the rent, or the rise?这里的 it 不够准：是房租还是涨幅？
Rents rose by a third. This hit young families hardest. 房租涨了三分之一。这对年轻家庭打击最大。
This takes the whole event.this 指整件事。
Rents rose by a third. This increase hit young families hardest. 房租涨了三分之一。这次上涨对年轻家庭打击最大。
This + noun: the clearest choice in writing.this 加名词：书面语里最清楚。
If a student is late, he must sign in at the office. 学生如果迟到，他必须去办公室登记。
Old-fashioned: assumes the student is male.过时：默认学生是男的。
If a student is late, he or she must sign in at the office. 学生如果迟到，他或她必须去办公室登记。
Correct but heavy if repeated.正确，但反复用就显得笨重。
If a student is late, they must sign in at the office. 学生如果迟到，他们必须去办公室登记。
Modern standard: singular they.现代标准：单数 they。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ My wife, he works in a bank.
✓ My wife works in a bank. She works in the city centre.
Two errors: the wrong gender, and a doubled subject. Say the noun once, then switch to she.
两处错：性别错了，主语也重复了。名词说一次，之后改用 she。
✗ My father called, and she said the flight was late.
✓ My father called, and he said the flight was late.
In fast speech the gender slips. Picture the person before the pronoun comes out.
说快了性别就滑。代词出口前先想到这个人。
✗ The report was late and the manager was angry. It caused problems.
✓ The report was late and the manager was angry. The delay caused problems.
It could be the report, the anger or both. Name the thing.
it 可能指报告、生气，或两者。把东西点名。
✗ Everyone should bring his passport.
✓ Everyone should bring their passport.
For a mixed or unknown group, singular their is standard.
混合或不明的群体，用单数 their 是标准用法。
✗ I have a cousin in Calgary and a friend in Edmonton. The latter is a doctor and the former is too.
✓ My cousin in Calgary and my friend in Edmonton are both doctors.
The former and the latter are a puzzle for the reader; rebuild the sentence instead.
former 和 latter 让读者解谜；不如把句子重组。
