📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Open the full explanation: 6 points展开细讲：6 段讲解

English uses different structures for large numbers and decimals. For example, 1,500 is usually read as 'one thousand five hundred', while 3.75 is read as 'three point seven five'. After 'hundred', 'thousand', and 'million', do not add a plural ending when stating an exact number.

英语中的大数字和小数有不同的读法。例如，1,500 通常读作 one thousand five hundred，而 3.75 读作 three point seven five。表示确切数字时，hundred、thousand 和 million 后面通常不加复数词尾。

Distinguish between 'a hundred' and 'hundreds of'. Use 'a hundred' for an exact quantity and 'hundreds of' for an approximate large quantity. The same pattern applies to thousand and million. For example, 'two thousand people' is exact, but 'thousands of people' is approximate.

要区分 a hundred 和 hundreds of。a hundred 表示确切的一百，而 hundreds of 表示数百个、大量的。thousand 和 million 也遵循相同规律。例如，two thousand people 表示确切的两千人，而 thousands of people 表示数千人。

Dates can be expressed in British or American English. British English commonly uses 'the fifth of June' or '5 June', while American English commonly uses 'June fifth' or 'June 5'. When writing dates in numeric form, be careful because day-month and month-day conventions differ.

日期有英式和美式表达方式。英式英语常用 the fifth of June 或 5 June，美式英语常用 June fifth 或 June 5。使用纯数字书写日期时要特别注意，因为英式和美式的日月排列顺序不同。

Use 'at' after words like 'priced' or 'sold' to state a fixed price: 'The jacket is priced at $20.' Use 'for' to say what someone paid or will pay: 'I bought it for $20.' 'Cost' itself takes no preposition at all: 'It costs $20.'

表示标价或售价时，at 常跟在 priced、sold 这类词后面，例如 The jacket is priced at $20（这件外套标价20美元）。表示实际支付的价钱时用 for，例如 I bought it for $20（我花20美元买的）。cost 后面不加任何介词，直接接价格，例如 It costs $20。

Pay attention to countable and uncountable quantities. Use 'many' with countable plural nouns, 'much' with uncountable nouns, and 'a lot of' with both. For example, 'many dollars', 'much money', and 'a lot of money' are all possible.

要注意可数名词和不可数名词的数量表达。many 用于可数名词复数，much 用于不可数名词，而 a lot of 两者都可以使用。例如，many dollars、much money 和 a lot of money 都是正确表达。

Use 'about', 'approximately', and 'around' to express estimates rather than exact figures. 'About 50 people' means the number is close to 50, not necessarily exactly 50. In everyday conversation, 'around' and 'about' are particularly common.

about、approximately 和 around 都可以表示估算，而不是精确数字。About 50 people 表示人数接近50人，不一定恰好是50人。在日常交流中，around 和 about 尤其常见。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ The population is about two thousands.
✓ The population is about two thousand.
Do not add an -s to hundred, thousand, or million when they follow an exact number. Use thousands of when you mean an approximate large quantity.
hundred、thousand 和 million 前面有具体数字时不加 -s。表示大约数千时，可以使用 thousands of。
✗ I have twenty years old.
✓ I am twenty years old.
Use the verb 'be' to express someone's age. English does not use 'have' for age, even though some other languages use a possession structure.
英语表达年龄时使用 be 动词，而不是 have。虽然有些语言使用表示拥有的结构，但英语中应说 I am twenty years old。
✗ The meeting is on Monday at 3 June.
✓ The meeting is on Monday, 3 June.
Use 'on' with a specific date or day. Use 'at' with a clock time, such as at 3 p.m. The two prepositions express different kinds of time information.
具体日期或星期几前使用 on，具体钟点前使用 at。例如 on 3 June 和 at 3 p.m.。这两个介词不能随意互换。
✗ The price is twenty dollars for each.
✓ The price is twenty dollars each.
Use 'each' directly after the price when stating a unit price. You can also say 'twenty dollars per person' or 'twenty dollars for each ticket'.
表示单价时，价格后面可以直接使用 each。也可以说 twenty dollars per person，或者 twenty dollars for each ticket。
✗ I spent twenty dollars for the book.
✓ I spent twenty dollars on the book.
Use 'spend money on something' to describe what you use your money for. Use 'pay for something' when describing the payment itself.
表示把钱花在某物上时，使用 spend money on something。表示为某物付款时，使用 pay for something。
✗ There are much people in the room.
✓ There are many people in the room.
People is a countable plural noun, so use 'many' rather than 'much'. Use 'much' with uncountable nouns such as money, time, and information.
people 是可数名词复数，因此使用 many，而不是 much。much 通常与 money、time 和 information 等不可数名词搭配。
