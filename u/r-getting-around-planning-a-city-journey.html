Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Anna: We need to get to the city museum this afternoon. Do you know how far it is from here? Ben: I checked the map earlier. It is not very far, but walking would take quite a while. We could take a bus or the underground. Anna: Which option would be easier? I do not know this area very well.

安娜：我们今天下午需要去城市博物馆。你知道它离这里有多远吗？本：我之前查看过地图。距离不是特别远，但步行需要花不少时间。我们可以乘公共汽车或地铁。安娜：哪种方式更方便？我对这个地区不太熟悉。

Ben: The bus stop is just across the street. We can take the number shown on the route information board, but we should check that it goes towards the museum. Anna: Is there a direct route? Ben: Yes, although the bus may be slower during the afternoon rush hour. The underground is faster, but we would need to walk to the station first.

本：公交车站就在街对面。我们可以乘坐路线信息牌上显示的那条线路，但应该先确认它是否开往博物馆。安娜：有直达路线吗？本：有，不过下午交通高峰期间公共汽车可能会比较慢。地铁更快，但我们需要先步行到车站。

Anna: Perhaps we should ask someone nearby. Excuse me, could you tell us where the nearest underground station is? Local resident: Certainly. Go straight ahead for two blocks, then turn left at the traffic lights. You will see the station entrance next to a large supermarket. Anna: Thank you very much. That sounds easy enough.

安娜：也许我们应该问问附近的人。打扰一下，您能告诉我们最近的地铁站在哪里吗？当地居民：当然可以。一直向前走两个街区，然后在红绿灯处左转。你们会看到车站入口就在一家大型超市旁边。安娜：非常感谢。听起来很容易找到。

Ben: Great. Let's take the underground, then. We should also check the return journey before we leave the museum. Anna: Good idea. I will save the station name on my phone. Ben: We can use the map as a guide, but we should also pay attention to signs inside the station. Anna: Agreed. A little planning will make the whole trip much more comfortable.

本：太好了。那我们就乘地铁吧。离开博物馆之前，我们也应该查看返程路线。安娜：好主意。我会把车站名称保存在手机里。本：我们可以用地图作为指引，但也应该留意车站内部的标志。安娜：同意。稍微做一点规划，就能让整个行程舒适得多。

Words词

/əˈkrɒs ðə striːt/ prep. phr.

just across the street; directly across the street
The pharmacy is directly across the street from the hotel. 药店就在酒店的街道对面。
/ˈrʌʃ aʊə/ n.
交通高峰时段
morning rush hour; afternoon rush hour
Traffic is usually heavy during the morning rush hour. 早晨交通高峰时段通常车流量很大。
/dəˈrekt/ adj.
直达的；直接的
direct route; direct service
Is there a direct train to the airport? 有直达机场的火车吗？
/ˈentrəns/ n.
入口；入口处
main entrance; station entrance
Please wait for me near the main entrance. 请在正门附近等我。

plan the return journey; return journey home
We should check the return journey before buying our tickets. 买票之前，我们应该查看返程安排。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

I do not know this area very well.
Know is a stative verb describing knowledge, so the present simple is used rather than the present continuous.
Know是表示认知状态的静态动词，因此使用一般现在时，而不是现在进行时。
We can use the map as a guide, but we should also pay attention to signs inside the station.
But connects contrasting ideas, while should expresses advice or a recommended action.
But连接两个形成对比的观点，而should表示建议或推荐采取的行动。
