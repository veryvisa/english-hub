so, such, too, enough and result clauses so、such 与结果从句

📘 In your coursebook: English File 5e · Upper-Intermediate · 3A “Flight plan”. Use the texts and recordings with your coursebook; this page adds new words, grammar and practice.教材定位：English File 5e · Upper-Intermediate · 3A《Flight plan》。课文与录音请配合教材学习，本页补充新词、语法与练习。

Choose so or such by what follows, use too for "more than is good" and enough for "as much as needed", and finish each one with the right result clause. 根据后面接什么来选 so 还是 such；too 表示「多到不好」，enough 表示「够了」；并给每一个接上正确的结果从句。

📏 The rule规则

📏 The rule in one line规则一句话
so + adjective or adverb, such + (a) + adjective + noun: so cold, such a cold day. too means more than is acceptable and often implies a negative result; enough goes after an adjective but before a noun: old enough, enough money.so 接形容词或副词，such 接（a）＋形容词＋名词：so cold、such a cold day。too 表示「超出可接受的程度」，常暗含不好的结果；enough 放在形容词后、名词前：old enough、enough money。

Chinese uses one word, tai, for both "very" and "too": tai hao le means great, but tai gui le can mean too expensive. In English, too is never a compliment. This coat is too nice sounds odd, because too means there is a problem. If you mean strong praise, use so or really: This coat is so nice.

中文的「太」既能表「很」也能表「过于」：太好了是夸奖，太贵了可能是「贵得买不起」。英语的 too 永远不是夸奖，This coat is too nice 听起来很怪，因为 too 意味着有问题。想表达强烈称赞，用 so 或 really。

The choice between so and such depends only on what comes next. Adjective or adverb alone: so. Noun phrase: such. The rent is so high but It's such a high rent. With uncountable or plural nouns, such has no article: such good advice, such friendly people. With quantity words, use so: so many people, so much traffic, so little time.

so 和 such 怎么选，只看后面接什么。单独的形容词或副词用 so；名词短语用 such。不可数或复数名词前的 such 不带冠词。数量词前用 so：so many、so much、so little。

Both can take a result clause with that, often dropped in speech: It was so cold (that) the pipes froze. It was such a long line (that) we went home. In formal writing, so + adjective + a + noun is also possible: so long a wait, but it sounds literary.

两者都能接 that 引导的结果从句，口语里 that 常省。正式书面语里也可以说 so long a wait，但显得很文。

too + adjective + to + verb says the result did not or cannot happen: He's too young to drive. for + person adds who is affected: The box is too heavy for me to lift. Do not add it at the end: too heavy to lift, not too heavy to lift it. not + adjective + enough often says the same thing more politely: He isn't old enough to drive.

too 加形容词加 to 加动词，表示结果没发生或不可能。加 for 加人说明影响到谁。结尾不要再加 it。not ... enough 常常更委婉地表达同样的意思。

enough follows adjectives and adverbs (warm enough, fast enough) but comes before nouns (enough chairs, enough time). Putting it before an adjective, as in enough big, is one of the most frequent errors from Chinese speakers. When enough is used alone, it can mean "I want no more": That's enough, thanks.

enough 放在形容词和副词后面，但放在名词前面。把它放在形容词前面，比如 enough big，是华人最常见的错误之一。单独用时可以表示「够了，别再多了」。

Examples例句

  • The view from the mountain was so beautiful that nobody spoke. 山上的景色美得大家都说不出话来。
    so + adjective + result.so 加形容词加结果。
  • It was such a beautiful view that we stayed an hour. 景色那么美，我们待了一个小时。
    such a + adjective + noun.such a 加形容词加名词。
  • The neighbours are such kind people. 邻居们人真好。
    Plural noun: no article.复数名词前不加冠词。
  • She gave me such good advice about the interview. 她给了我很好的面试建议。
    Uncountable noun: no article.不可数名词前不加冠词。
  • There were so many people at the market that we couldn't move. 市场里人多得挤不动。
    so many.so many。
  • We have so little time before the move. 搬家前我们时间太紧了。
    so little.so little。
  • This soup is too hot to eat right now. 这汤太烫，现在没法喝。
    Negative result.不好的结果。
  • The shelf is too high for my daughter to reach. 架子太高，我女儿够不着。
    for + person.加 for 加人。
  • He spoke too fast for me to understand. 他说得太快，我听不懂。
    too + adverb.too 加副词。
  • Is the apartment big enough for a family of four? 这套公寓够四口之家住吗？
    Adjective + enough.形容词加 enough。
  • We don't have enough chairs for everyone. 椅子不够大家坐。
    enough + noun.enough 加名词。
  • The water wasn't warm enough to swim in. 水不够暖，没法游泳。
    A polite negative.委婉的否定。
  • It was so cold we couldn't feel our fingers. 冷得我们手指都没知觉了。
    that dropped in speech.口语里省了 that。
  • The traffic was so bad that we missed the first half of the game. 堵车堵得我们错过了上半场。
    Cause and result.原因和结果。

More pairs: same idea, different choice更多对比句组

The jacket is so nice. 这件夹克真好看。
Praise.夸奖。
The jacket is too nice to wear for gardening. 这件夹克太好了，舍不得穿着干园艺。
Too good for the purpose.好到不适合这个用途。
The jacket is nice enough for the office. 这件夹克穿去上班够得体了。
Good enough for the purpose.符合这个用途。
He's too young to vote. 他年纪太小，不能投票。
Blunt.直接。
He isn't old enough to vote. 他还没到投票的年龄。
Softer.委婉一些。
He's so young that he can't vote yet. 他还小，暂时不能投票。
Result clause; less common here.用结果从句，这里不太常用。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ It was so a long day.
✓ It was such a long day. / The day was so long.
A noun phrase needs such.
名词短语前要用 such。
✗ The room isn't enough big.
✓ The room isn't big enough.
enough goes after adjectives.
enough 放在形容词后。
✗ This restaurant is too good!
✓ This restaurant is so good! / This restaurant is really good!
too always means a problem.
too 永远意味着有问题。
✗ The box is too heavy to lift it.
✓ The box is too heavy to lift.
Do not repeat the object at the end.
结尾不要重复宾语。
✗ There were such many people at the station.
✓ There were so many people at the station.
Quantity words take so.
数量词前用 so。
