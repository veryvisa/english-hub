In Defence of Uncomfortable History 为令人不安的历史辩护

🧠 Learn the 3 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 3 个新词

Societies should keep an honest, uncomfortable past in view, because a tidied-up version of history is a weak foundation for trust. 社会应当直面诚实而令人不安的过去，因为被粉饰过的历史是脆弱的信任根基。

We like to think of history as a fixed record, a set of facts waiting patiently in the archive. In practice, what a society remembers is the product of choices: which anniversaries are marked, which statues are raised, which chapters are taught in school. That is why the question of who decides what counts as the past is never merely academic.

我们喜欢把历史想象成一份固定的记录，一堆耐心等在档案馆里的事实。实际上，一个社会记住什么，是一系列选择的产物：纪念哪些周年，竖立哪些雕像，学校教哪些章节。这就是为什么“谁来决定什么算作过去”这个问题从来不只是学术问题。

Defenders of a comforting national story often argue that dwelling on old wrongs only divides people. There is something to this; a community consumed by grievance can lose the ability to plan for the future. That said, a past that has been tidied up is not a stable foundation. Silence tends to survive only until someone’s grandmother starts talking.

为舒适的国家叙事辩护的人常说，纠缠于旧日的过错只会分裂人们。这话有几分道理；一个被怨恨吞噬的社群可能会失去规划未来的能力。话虽如此，一个被粉饰过的过去并不是稳固的根基。沉默往往只能维持到某位祖母开口讲述的那一刻。

Consider how collective memory actually works. It is not stored in one place but passed on through family stories, school lessons, films and public ceremonies, each of which smooths some details and sharpens others. A detail that embarrasses the present is rarely deleted outright; it is simply left unmentioned until it is forgotten.

想想集体记忆实际是如何运作的。它并不存放在某一处，而是通过家庭故事、学校课程、电影和公共仪式代代相传，每一种方式都会抹平一些细节，又突出另一些。让当下难堪的细节很少被彻底删除；它只是被悄悄不再提起，直到被遗忘。

I am not suggesting that every museum should become a courtroom. Although honest history can be painful, it need not be cruel, and it certainly should not be used as a weapon against people who had no part in the events. What it should do is allow those who suffered to bear witness, and allow the rest of us to listen without rushing to defend ourselves.

我并不是说每座博物馆都应该变成法庭。诚然诚实的历史可能令人痛苦，但它不必残酷，更不应被用来攻击那些与事件毫无关系的人。它应当做的，是让受过苦的人能够作证，也让我们其余的人能够倾听，而不是急着为自己辩护。

A mature society, in my view, is one that can hold two thoughts at once: pride in what it has built, and honesty about what that cost. Nothing is more likely to weaken trust in the past than the suspicion that it has been edited for our comfort.

在我看来，成熟的社会应当能同时抱持两种想法：为自己所建成的一切感到自豪，也对其代价保持诚实。最容易削弱人们对过去的信任的，莫过于怀疑它已被为了我们的舒适而编辑过。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

That said, a past that has been tidied up is not a stable foundation.
’That said’ signals a concession before a counter-point, and the defining relative clause ‘that has been tidied up’ uses the present perfect passive to describe a past already edited.
That said 表示让步后的转折；定语从句 that has been tidied up 用现在完成时被动语态，说明过去已被人整理（粉饰）过。
Nothing is more likely to weaken trust in the past than the suspicion that it has been edited for our comfort.
A negative subject (‘Nothing’) with ‘more likely ... than’ makes a strong comparison, and ‘that it has been edited’ is a clause explaining what the suspicion is.
以否定词 Nothing 作主语，配合 more likely ... than 构成强调的比较；that it has been edited 是同位语从句，说明 suspicion 的内容。
