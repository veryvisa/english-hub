📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Open the full explanation: 4 points展开细讲：4 段讲解

Chinese learners often have the formal word from exams and the informal word from films, but not the link between them. So they write I want to obtain my results in a text to a friend, or Can you help me get the thing? to a government office. Learning both ends of a pair as one item fixes this.

华人常从考试学到正式词、从电影学到口语词，却没把两者连起来。于是给朋友发消息写 obtain，给政府部门写 get the thing。成对记就能解决。

A rough rule: the formal word is often a single Latin-based verb (postpone, purchase, require), and the informal word is often a phrasal verb or a short Old English word (put off, buy, need). The formal one is not "better"; it is colder and more distant.

粗略规律：正式词常是一个拉丁词源的单词，口语词常是短语动词或古英语短词。正式不等于更好，只是更冷、更有距离。

Most real writing sits in the middle. A work email to a colleague uses get back to you, check, sort out; a letter to a tax office or a landlord's formal notice uses respond, verify, resolve. Pick the height for the reader, then keep it: a sudden jump from reside to stuff in one paragraph is what readers notice.

大多数真实写作在中间：给同事的邮件用 get back to you、check、sort out；给税务局或正式通知用 respond、verify、resolve。按读者定高度，并保持一致：一段里忽然从 reside 跳到 stuff，读者一眼看得出。

Some formal words are fixed in official forms, and you must recognise them even if you never use them: reside (live), commence (start), cease (stop), prior to (before), in accordance with (following).

一些正式词固定出现在官方表格里，就算不用也要认得：reside、commence、cease、prior to、in accordance with。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ Hey Tom, I want to inquire if you're free for lunch.
✓ Hey Tom, are you free for lunch?
inquire is far too formal for a friend.
对朋友用 inquire 太正式。
✗ Dear Sir, I want to know how to get the stuff for my permit.
✓ Dear Sir or Madam, I am writing to ask which documents are required for my permit.
stuff and get are too casual for a formal letter.
正式信件里 stuff、get 太随意。
✗ The lease will commence on Monday, so I'll grab the keys and stuff.
✓ The lease starts on Monday, so I'll pick up the keys then.
Do not jump between heights in one sentence.
同一句里语域别乱跳。
✗ Prior to I signed, I read it.
✓ Prior to signing, I read it. / Before I signed, I read it.
prior to is a preposition: noun or -ing after it.
prior to 是介词，后接名词或 -ing。
✗ Please assist me to fill the form.
✓ Please assist me with the form. / Please help me fill in the form.
assist sb with / in doing; help sb (to) do.
assist 配 with 或 in doing。
