An Email That Took Three Drafts 一封改了三稿的邮件

🧠 Learn the 5 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 5 个新词

See how a refusal email improves over three drafts and what order makes it polite. 看一封拒绝邮件怎样三易其稿，以及什么顺序让它显得有礼。

B2 · CELPIP 7–8⏱ 20 minV06 Register: too formal, too casual, or too blunt语域不合：太正式、太随意或太冲V04 Phrasal verbs avoided; the formal verb sounds stiff回避短语动词，说话太书面

📖 Key card知识卡
Politeness in an English email is about order, not length: appreciation, answer, reason, offer.英文邮件的礼貌在于顺序而不是长度：感谢、答复、理由、替代方案。

Read and listen读与听

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Last month I had to turn down a request from a client I like. She wanted the report a week early, which was unreasonable given how late her data had arrived. My first draft of the email took two minutes and was a disaster.

上个月我不得不拒绝一位我很喜欢的客户的请求。她想提前一周拿到报告，考虑到她的数据来得那么晚，这并不合理。我的第一稿邮件只花了两分钟，是场灾难。

Draft one said: "We cannot do this. The deadline is the deadline." It was clear, and it would have ended the relationship. In English, a direct no with no cushion sounds angry, even when you are calm. A colleague read it and winced.

第一稿写的是：「这个我们做不到。期限就是期限。」意思清楚，但会断送这段合作。在英语里，没有缓冲的直接拒绝听起来像在发火，哪怕你心平气和。一位同事看了直皱眉。

Draft two went too far the other way. It was three paragraphs of apology, and the answer was hidden in the last line. My colleague pointed out that the client would have to figure out what I meant, and that a misunderstanding now would cost us more later.

第二稿又矫枉过正。三段都是道歉，答复藏在最后一行。同事指出，客户得自己琢磨我到底什么意思，而现在造成的误会以后代价更大。

Draft three did four things in five sentences. It thanked her for the request. It gave the answer and one reason: the data had arrived late. It showed willingness to help by offering a summary on the early date and the full report on the original one. And it ended with a clear next step. She replied within an hour: "That works. Thanks for the flexibility." The lesson I took away is that politeness in English is not about length. It is about order: appreciation, answer, reason, offer. Meet that expectation, and even a refusal can build confidence in your reliability.

第三稿用五句话做了四件事。感谢她提出请求；给出答复和一个理由：数据到得晚；提出提前那天先给摘要、原定日期给完整报告，表明愿意帮忙；最后写明下一步。她一小时内就回了：「可以。谢谢你的灵活处理。」我得到的教训是：英语里的礼貌与长短无关，关键在顺序：感谢、答复、理由、替代方案。满足了这个预期，连拒绝也能让对方更相信你靠得住。

Words词

/drɑːft/ n.
草稿
a first draft; write a draft
I always sleep on the first draft. 第一稿我总要放一晚再看。
/ˈkʊʃən/ n.
缓冲；垫子
a cushion against sth; soften with a cushion
A thank-you is a good cushion before a no. 说「不」之前先道谢是很好的缓冲。
/wɪns/ v.
（因难受、尴尬）皱眉蹙额
wince at sth; wince in pain
I winced when I reread my email. 重读自己的邮件时我直皱眉。
/əˌpriːʃiˈeɪʃən/ n.
感谢；欣赏
show appreciation; in appreciation of
Start with a word of appreciation. 先说一句感谢。
/ˈɡɪvən/ prep.
考虑到
given that; given the circumstances
Given the delay, we need more time. 考虑到延误，我们需要更多时间。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

She wanted the report a week early, which was unreasonable given how late her data had arrived.
which refers to the whole request, not to one noun. given here is a preposition meaning "considering".
which 指代整个请求，而不是某个名词；given 在这里是介词，意为「考虑到」。
Meet that expectation, and even a refusal can build confidence in your reliability.
Imperative + and = a condition: "If you meet that expectation, …".
祈使句加 and 相当于条件句：如果你满足这个预期，就……
