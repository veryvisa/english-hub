The Doctor Who Drank the Evidence 喝下证据的医生

Read the story of an idea that experts laughed at, and see how the present perfect links a past discovery to today. 读一个曾被专家嘲笑的想法的故事，看现在完成时怎样把过去的发现和今天连起来。

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

For most of the twentieth century, doctors were sure they knew what caused stomach ulcers: stress, spicy food and too much acid. Patients were told to slow down, drink milk and avoid curry. Many of them took medicine for years, and the pain kept coming back.

二十世纪的大部分时间里，医生们确信自己知道胃溃疡的成因：压力、辛辣食物和胃酸过多。病人被告知要放慢节奏、喝牛奶、别吃咖喱。很多人吃了好几年药，疼痛还是反复发作。

In the early 1980s, two researchers in Australia noticed something odd. Under the microscope, a curved bacterium kept appearing in samples from patients with ulcers. Their hypothesis was simple and, to most experts, ridiculous: an infection, not stress, was doing the damage. The consensus at the time was that no bacterium could survive in stomach acid, so their colleagues were deeply sceptical, and some openly laughed at them.

二十世纪八十年代初，澳大利亚的两位研究者注意到一件怪事。显微镜下，一种弯曲的细菌反复出现在溃疡病人的样本里。他们的假说很简单，在多数专家看来却很荒唐：造成损害的是感染，不是压力。当时的共识是没有细菌能在胃酸里活下来，所以同行们非常怀疑，有人还公开嘲笑他们。

Their early experiments on animals failed, and journals turned down their papers. So one of them did something most people would never try: he drank a sample of the bacteria. Within days he was sick, and tests showed the same infection. It did not prove everything, but it ruled out the idea that the bacterium was harmless.

他们早期的动物实验失败了，期刊也拒了他们的论文。于是其中一位做了件大多数人绝不会尝试的事：他喝下了一份细菌样本。几天之内他就病了，检查发现了同样的感染。这并不能证明一切，但排除了「这种细菌无害」的说法。

It still took more than ten years, and other teams had to replicate their results in much larger studies, before the medical world changed its mind. Since then, doctors have treated millions of ulcer patients with a short course of antibiotics, and the two researchers have received the highest prize in medicine. The breakthrough has also changed how doctors think about many other diseases.

医学界又花了十多年、等别的团队在规模大得多的研究里重复出他们的结果，才改变了看法。从那以后，医生已经用短疗程的抗生素治好了数以百万计的溃疡病人，两位研究者也获得了医学最高奖。这一突破还改变了医生对许多其他疾病的思考方式。

The lesson is not that experts are always wrong. Most bold ideas turn out to be wrong, and scepticism has saved us from many of them. The lesson is that evidence, not reputation, should settle the question, even when the evidence comes from someone nobody has heard of.

这件事的教训不是专家总是错的。大多数大胆的想法最后都被证明是错的，怀疑态度帮我们挡掉了其中很多。教训是：该由证据而不是名声来决定答案，哪怕证据来自一个没人听说过的人。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

The consensus at the time was that no bacterium could survive in stomach acid, so their colleagues were deeply sceptical, and some openly laughed at them.
The consensus … was that + clause: the noun is explained by a whole sentence. at the time marks a view that is no longer held.
The consensus was that 后面用整句说明共识的内容。at the time 暗示这个看法现在已经不成立。
Since then, doctors have treated millions of ulcer patients with a short course of antibiotics, and the two researchers have received the highest prize in medicine.
Since then + present perfect: a period that runs from a past point up to now, so no finished-time word like in 2005.
Since then 加现在完成时：从过去某一点延续到现在的一段时间，所以不能再加 in 2005 这种已结束的时间。
