Verb + -ing or to: the common verbs 动词后接 -ing 还是 to：常见动词

📘 In your coursebook: English File 5e · Upper-Intermediate · 6B “Music and emotion”. Use the texts and recordings with your coursebook; this page adds new words, grammar and practice.教材定位：English File 5e · Upper-Intermediate · 6B《Music and emotion》。课文与录音请配合教材学习，本页补充新词、语法与练习。

Choose confidently between -ing and to + verb after the most common verbs, and use the meaning-changing pairs (stop, remember, try, forget) correctly. 能够在常见动词后自信地选择 -ing 或 to + 动词，并正确使用会改变意思的几组动词（stop、remember、try、forget）。

Read the sentences and decide what each one tells you. Then open the notes.先读这几句，自己判断各自传达了什么，再点开看注解。

I stopped smoking. 我戒烟了。
Without extra context, this reads as ending the activity for good; smoking is the thing that stopped.没有额外语境时，这句话读作彻底结束了这个活动，停下的就是抽烟这件事。
I stopped to smoke. 我停下来抽了根烟。
I paused another action in order to smoke; smoking is the purpose.我暂停了别的事，目的是去抽烟，抽烟是新做的事。
I stopped smoking for a minute to answer the phone. 我停了一会儿烟去接电话。
Stopping with -ing can also be temporary when the context says so, but the object is still the ongoing activity.语境说明时，stop + -ing 也可以是暂时的，但停下的对象仍是正在进行的活动。

📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Verbs that describe facing, dealing with or getting past an activity that already exists in your mind, such as enjoy, avoid, finish, mind, suggest, keep and consider, take -ing. Verbs that look forward to a goal or an action that has not happened yet, such as want, decide, hope, plan, manage, refuse and promise, take to + verb. When you are unsure of a verb you have not met before, ask yourself whether the action is something you are already picturing or something you are aiming at.

表示面对、应付或结束某个已在脑海中的活动的动词，如 enjoy、avoid、finish、mind、suggest、keep、consider，后面接 -ing。表示朝向某个尚未发生的目标或动作的动词，如 want、decide、hope、plan、manage、refuse、promise，后面接 to + 动词。遇到不熟的动词时，可以问自己：这个动作是已经在想象中的，还是正要去追求的目标？

Chinese speakers often transfer the bare verb-verb pattern of their own language, so they drop the marker and say 'want go' or 'decide go'. The reverse also happens: after 'enjoy' or 'avoid', learners add 'to' because the English verb feels like a 'wanting' verb. Treat each verb as a fixed pair with its pattern, and memorise the pair rather than translating the Chinese verb.

说汉语的人常把汉语里动词连用的习惯搬过来，漏掉 to，说成 want go 或 decide go。反过来，在 enjoy 或 avoid 后面也会误加 to，因为这些词在中文里感觉像“想要”类动词。要把每个动词和它的搭配当成固定组合来记，而不是按中文意思去翻译。

Stop, remember, forget and try are the verbs where your choice changes the meaning, so choose carefully. 'Stop doing' ends an activity, while 'stop to do' pauses one thing in order to do another. 'Remember doing' looks back at a memory, while 'remember to do' is about not forgetting a task. 'Try doing' is an experiment to see if something works, while 'try to do' is an attempt at something difficult.

stop、remember、forget 和 try 是选择不同形式会改变意思的动词，要特别小心。stop doing 是结束一个活动，stop to do 是停下一件事去做另一件事。remember doing 是回想过去的经历，remember to do 是没有忘记要做的事。try doing 是试一试看是否有效，try to do 是努力去做困难的事。

Some verbs, such as like, love, hate, prefer, begin, start and continue, accept both forms with little change in meaning, so either is usually safe. A small difference remains with like and love: -ing describes enjoyment of the activity in general, while to + verb often describes a habit or a choice you consider sensible. With 'would like', 'would love' and 'would prefer', always use to + verb because they express a wish about a specific occasion.

like、love、hate、prefer、begin、start、continue 等动词两种形式都可以，意思差别很小，所以通常怎么用都不会错。like 和 love 之间仍有细微差别：-ing 表示对这个活动本身的喜爱，to + 动词常表示习惯或你认为明智的选择。would like、would love、would prefer 后面一律用 to + 动词，因为它们表达的是针对具体场合的愿望。

When a verb follows a preposition, use -ing, because to in phrases like 'look forward to', 'be used to' and 'object to' is a preposition and not part of an infinitive. This is a frequent source of errors at this level. If you can replace the following verb with a noun, such as 'look forward to the trip', then -ing is the right form.

动词出现在介词后面时用 -ing。look forward to、be used to、object to 里的 to 是介词，不是不定式符号，这是这个水平常见的错误来源。如果能把后面的动词换成名词，比如 look forward to the trip，那就应该用 -ing。

More pairs: same idea, different choice更多对比句组

I remember meeting him at a conference. 我记得在一次会议上见过他。
A memory of a past event; the meeting came first and the remembering later.回忆过去的事件，先见面，后回忆。
I remembered to meet him at the conference. 我记得要去会议上见他。
I did not forget the plan; the remembering came first and the meeting after.没有忘记这个安排，先想起来，后去见面。
I forgot meeting him, so I introduced myself again. 我忘了见过他，所以又做了一次自我介绍。
The event happened but the memory of it was lost.事情发生过，但对它的记忆丢了。
I forgot to meet him, so he waited alone. 我忘了去见他，所以他一个人在等。
The task was never done because it slipped my mind.这件事因为忘记了而根本没有做。
Try adding a little salt. 试试加点盐。
A suggestion to experiment and see whether it helps.建议做个尝试，看看是否有效。
Try to add a little salt while you stir. 搅拌的时候尽量加一点盐。
An effort at something that takes some care or difficulty.努力做一件需要小心或有难度的事。
I tried to add salt, but the box was empty. 我想加盐，但盒子空了。
The attempt may fail; the result is not guaranteed.尝试可能失败，结果没有保证。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ I want go home now.
✓ I want to go home now.
Learners carry over the pattern of two verbs side by side from their first language. English needs to before the second verb after want.
学习者把汉语中两个动词连用的习惯搬了过来。英语中 want 后面的第二个动词前必须加 to。
✗ I enjoy to read novels in the evening.
✓ I enjoy reading novels in the evening.
Enjoy is followed by -ing, never by to + verb. Learners wrongly treat it as a wanting verb because the Chinese equivalent feels like a desire.
enjoy 后面只能接 -ing，不能接 to + 动词。学习者因为中文对应词带有“想要”的感觉，误以为它是愿望类动词。
✗ She suggested to go by train.
✓ She suggested going by train.
Suggest takes -ing, not an infinitive. Learners are often confused because recommend and advise sound similar in meaning, but suggest does not allow to + verb directly.
suggest 后面接 -ing，不能直接接不定式。学习者容易和意思相近的 advise 混淆，但 suggest 后不能直接用 to + 动词。
✗ I'm looking forward to see you next week.
✓ I'm looking forward to seeing you next week.
In this phrase, to is a preposition, so the verb after it must be -ing. Learners assume that to is always part of an infinitive.
在这个短语中 to 是介词，所以后面的动词必须用 -ing。学习者误以为 to 总是不定式的一部分。
✗ He decided going to the gym every morning.
✓ He decided to go to the gym every morning.
Decide points to a future action, so it takes to + verb. Using -ing here suggests that the learner has memorised only one pattern for every verb.
decide 指向将来的行动，所以接 to + 动词。这里用 -ing 说明学习者对所有动词只记了一种搭配。
✗ I stopped to smoke last year, and I feel much better.
✓ I stopped smoking last year, and I feel much better.
Stop to + verb means pausing in order to do something. To say that you quit a habit, you need stop + -ing.
stop to + 动词表示停下来去做某事。要表达戒掉某个习惯，必须用 stop + -ing。
