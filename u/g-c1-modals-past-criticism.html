should have, needn't have, could have 过去的批评与遗憾

📘 In your coursebook: English File 5e · Upper-Intermediate · 7A “You be the judge”. Use the texts and recordings with your coursebook; this page adds new words, grammar and practice.教材定位：English File 5e · Upper-Intermediate · 7A《You be the judge》。课文与录音请配合教材学习，本页补充新词、语法与练习。

Look back at the past with the right modal + have: criticism (should have), unnecessary effort (needn't have vs didn't need to), missed chances (could have), and guesses (must have, can't have, might have). 用对「情态动词 + have」回看过去：批评（should have）、白费力气（needn't have 与 didn't need to 的区别）、错过的机会（could have）、以及推测（must have、can't have、might have）。

I didn't need to wear a suit, so I wore jeans. 我不用穿西装，所以穿了牛仔裤。
Not necessary, not done.没必要，也没做。
I needn't have worn a suit; everyone was in jeans. 我白穿西装了，大家都穿牛仔裤。
Done, but unnecessary.做了，但没必要。
I shouldn't have worn a suit; I felt silly all evening. 我真不该穿西装，整晚都觉得自己傻。
Done, and it was a mistake.做了，而且是个错误。

📏 The rule规则

📏 The rule in one line规则一句话
Modal + have + past participle looks back at the past. Should have says the right thing did not happen; could have says a possibility was not used; needn't have says you did something that turned out unnecessary; must have, can't have and might have are guesses about what happened.情态动词 + have + 过去分词用来回看过去。should have 说该做的没做；could have 说有可能却没用上；needn't have 说做了才发现不必做；must have、can't have、might have 是对过去的推测。

Chinese handles regret with words like ben lai (originally) or zao zhi dao (had I known) and keeps the verb the same. English puts it all in the modal: I should have called means "calling was the right thing and I didn't call". Without have the meaning moves to the present or future: I should call is advice for now.

中文用「本来」「早知道」表示后悔，动词不变；英语全靠情态动词。去掉 have，意思就转到现在或将来。

Should have and ought to have criticise: yourself (I should have read the contract) or others (They should have warned us). Shouldn't have criticises something that did happen (You shouldn't have shared that code). To soften criticism of someone else, use could have or might have: You could have told me is a complaint but a milder one; You might have mentioned it is very British, polite and quietly annoyed.

should have 和 ought to have 批评自己或别人；shouldn't have 批评已经发生的事。要缓和对别人的批评，就用 could have 或 might have：you could have told me 也是抱怨，但温和些。

Needn't have done and didn't need to do are a classic pair. I needn't have brought my passport = I brought it, and it was unnecessary. I didn't need to bring my passport = it was unnecessary; usually I didn't bring it (but the sentence can also cover both). In speech, many people use didn't need to for both meanings and add context: I didn't need to bring it after all.

needn't have done 和 didn't need to do 是经典一对。前者是「带了，结果白带」；后者是「不需要带」，通常也就没带。口语里很多人两种意思都用 didn't need to，再用 after all 之类补充语境。

Could have also states a real possibility that did not happen: We could have lost everything (but we didn't). Couldn't have says something was impossible, or is a strong compliment: I couldn't have done it without you. Would have belongs to conditionals and to what you were willing to do: I would have helped, but nobody asked.

could have 还表示「本来可能发生但没发生」；couldn't have 表示不可能，或是很强的赞美：没有你我做不成。would have 用在条件句里，以及表示「本来愿意做」。

For guesses about the past, choose by certainty: must have (I'm almost sure it did), might have / may have / could have (perhaps), can't have / couldn't have (I'm almost sure it didn't). The negative of must have for deduction is can't have, not mustn't have: She can't have received my email; she would have replied.

推测过去按把握程度选：must have 几乎肯定发生了；might/may/could have 也许；can't/couldn't have 几乎肯定没发生。推测时 must have 的否定是 can't have，不是 mustn't have。

Examples例句

  • I should have read the second page before I signed. 签字前我真该把第二页读一读。
    Self-criticism.自责。
  • They should have warned us that the water would be shut off. 他们本该提前告诉我们要停水。
    Criticism of others.批评别人。
  • You shouldn't have given your code to the caller. 你不该把验证码告诉那个来电者。
    Criticism of something done.批评已经做了的事。
  • You could have told me you were coming. 你要来也不提前跟我说一声。
    A complaint, softer than should have.抱怨，但比 should have 温和。
  • You might have mentioned that the party was formal. 你本可以提一句这是正式聚会的。
    Very polite, quietly annoyed.很客气，带一点不满。
  • I needn't have taken a day off; the appointment took twenty minutes. 我根本不必请一天假，预约只花了二十分钟。
    Done, and it turned out unnecessary.做了，结果没必要。
  • I didn't need to book a table; the restaurant was half empty. 我其实不用订位，餐馆一半是空的。
    Not necessary, so no need for effort.没必要。
  • The ice was thick on the road; we could have been killed. 路上结了厚冰，我们差点没命。
    Possible but did not happen.有可能发生但没发生。
  • I couldn't have passed the test without your help. 没有你的帮助我不可能通过考试。
    Strong thanks.强烈的感谢。
  • The parcel must have gone to the wrong address. 包裹肯定是寄错地址了。
    Strong guess.很有把握的推测。
  • The letter may have been delayed by the strike. 信可能是因为罢工耽误了。
    Possible explanation.可能的解释。
  • She can't have received my email; she always replies the same day. 她不可能收到了我的邮件，她总是当天回复。
    Almost sure it didn't happen.几乎肯定没发生。
  • I would have helped, but nobody asked me. 我本来愿意帮忙，可没人找我。
    Willingness in the past.过去本来愿意。
  • We ought to have saved more before buying the car. 买车之前我们真该多存点钱。
    A little more formal than should have.比 should have 稍正式。

More pairs: same idea, different choice更多对比句组

You should have called me. 你本该给我打电话的。
Direct criticism.直接的批评。
You could have called me. 你也可以给我打个电话啊。
Softer complaint.温和些的抱怨。
You might have called me. 你本可以打个电话给我的。
Polite but clearly annoyed; British.客气但明显不满；英式。
He must have missed the bus. 他肯定是没赶上车。
Almost sure.几乎肯定。
He might have missed the bus. 他可能没赶上车。
One possibility.一种可能。
He can't have missed the bus; he left an hour early. 他不可能没赶上车，他提前一个小时就出门了。
Almost sure it didn't happen.几乎肯定没发生。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ I should call you yesterday.
✓ I should have called you yesterday.
Past regret needs have + past participle.
对过去的后悔要用 have 加过去分词。
✗ You must not have told him, he looked so surprised.
✓ You can't have told him; he looked so surprised.
For a negative guess, can't have.
否定推测用 can't have。
✗ I needn't have to bring my ID.
✓ I needn't have brought my ID. / I didn't need to bring my ID.
needn't have + past participle, without to.
needn't have 加过去分词，不加 to。
✗ He should have went to the doctor.
✓ He should have gone to the doctor.
Past participle, not past simple.
用过去分词，不是过去式。
✗ I could have pass the exam if I had studied.
✓ I could have passed the exam if I had studied.
have + past participle.
have 加过去分词。
