An Email Asking for More Time 一封申请延期的邮件

🧠 Learn the 4 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 4 个新词

Read an email asking an office for more time and see how the present perfect reports what has and has not been done. 读一封向办事机构申请延期的邮件，看现在完成时怎样交代已办和未办的事。

B2 · CELPIP 7–8⏱ 20 minG02 Present perfect and past simple confused现在完成时与一般过去时混用V06 Register: too formal, too casual, or too blunt语域不合：太正式、太随意或太冲

📖 Key card知识卡
A good request for more time says what you have done, what is missing and why, and asks for one specific thing, with proof attached.好的延期申请要讲清已经做了什么、缺什么、为什么，只提一个具体请求，并附上证据。

Read and listen读与听

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Hao had been asked to send three supporting documents to a licensing office by Friday. On Wednesday he realised that one of them, a letter from his former employer, would not arrive in time. A friend told him to phone and have a word with someone. He was nervous on the phone, so he wrote instead.

阿豪被要求在周五前向一家发证机构交三份证明文件。周三他意识到，其中一份，也就是前雇主的证明信，来不及到了。朋友让他打电话找人说一声。他打电话容易紧张，就改成了写信。

"Subject: Request for more time, reference number in the line above. Dear Sir or Madam, thank you for your letter of 3 May. I have attached two of the three documents you requested. The third, a letter from my former employer, has been requested but has not yet arrived, due to circumstances outside my control. I have made every effort to get it on time, and the employer has confirmed that it will be sent next week. I have attached that email as well."

「主题：申请延期，档案编号见上一行。尊敬的先生或女士：感谢您五月三日的来信。随信附上您要求的三份文件中的两份。第三份是前雇主的证明信，已经去要了，但由于我无法控制的原因尚未收到。我已尽一切努力按时取得，前雇主也已确认下周寄出。那封确认邮件我也一并附上。」

"I would be grateful if you could grant me an extension of ten working days, or take this into account when you review my file. Please let me know if you need anything else. Thank you in advance for your help. Yours faithfully, Hao Lin." His friend read it and only changed one thing: she moved the reference number into the subject line itself, because the person who opens the email should not have to look for it.

「如能准予延期十个工作日，或在审核我的档案时考虑这一情况，我将不胜感激。如需其他材料请告知。先行致谢。林浩 敬上。」朋友看了，只改了一处：把档案编号直接放进主题行，因为打开邮件的人不该还得去找它。

The office replied two days later to acknowledge receipt and grant the extension. "I was sure I would have to explain everything on the phone," Hao said. "In the end the email did it better than I could have, because everything was on one page: what I had done, what was missing, why, and the one thing I was asking for."

两天后，对方回信确认收到，并批准了延期。「我原以为得在电话里从头解释一遍，」阿豪说，「结果邮件比我讲得好，因为所有东西都在一页上：我做了什么、缺什么、为什么，以及我只请求的那一件事。」

Words词

/ɡrɑːnt/ v.
准予；批准
grant an extension; grant a request
The office granted her two more weeks. 该机构又给了她两周时间。
/ɪkˈstenʃn/ n.
延期
ask for an extension; a short extension
You can ask for an extension before the deadline, not after it. 延期要在截止日期之前申请，而不是之后。
/ˈsɜːkəmstənsɪz/ n. pl.
情况；境况
due to circumstances outside my control; in these circumstances
In these circumstances, a short delay is normal. 在这种情况下，稍有延误是正常的。
/ɪn ədˈvɑːns/ phr.
预先；提前
thank you in advance; pay in advance
Book the appointment two weeks in advance. 提前两周预约。
/jɔːz ˈfeɪθfəli/ phr.
谨上（英式，用于 Dear Sir or Madam 开头的信）
end with Yours faithfully; Yours sincerely
Use Yours faithfully when you do not know the name. 不知道对方姓名时用 Yours faithfully。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

The third, a letter from my former employer, has been requested but has not yet arrived, due to circumstances outside my control.
Present perfect passive has been requested and has not yet arrived: the situation now. a letter from… explains the third.
现在完成时被动 has been requested 与 has not yet arrived 说明眼下的状况。a letter from… 解释 the third。
I would be grateful if you could grant me an extension of ten working days, or take this into account when you review my file.
I would be grateful if you could… is the standard formal request. take this into account: consider it.
I would be grateful if you could… 是标准的正式请求句式。take this into account：把这一点考虑进去。
