Don’t Let the Machine Take the Blame 别让机器背黑锅

🧠 Learn the 2 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 2 个新词

Because every technology reflects human choices, the people behind it, not the machine, must answer for its harms. 因为每项技术都体现了人的选择，所以应当为危害负责的是背后的人，而不是机器。

We have a habit of speaking about technology as though it had a will of its own. This is more than a quirk of language. The algorithm decided, the system failed, the software made a mistake. Such phrasing is convenient, but it quietly removes people from the picture, and that is precisely where the ethical danger lies.

我们习惯于谈论技术，仿佛它有自己的意志。这不只是语言上的小怪癖。算法做了决定，系统出了故障，软件犯了错误。这样的说法很方便，但它悄悄地把人从画面中抹去，而这恰恰是伦理风险之所在。

Every tool reflects choices made by someone: which data to collect, which goals to optimise, which errors to tolerate. While it is true that modern systems can behave in ways their designers did not foresee, unpredictability is not an excuse. A company that releases a product it cannot fully explain has, by and large, accepted the risk that comes with it.

每一种工具都体现了某个人做出的选择：收集哪些数据、优化哪些目标、容忍哪些错误。诚然，现代系统的行为可能超出设计者的预料，但不可预测并不能成为借口。一家发布了自己都无法完全解释的产品的公司，大体上就已经接受了随之而来的风险。

Consider consent. We click ‘agree’ hundreds of times a year without reading a word, and then we are told that we gave permission. This is consent in name only. Genuine consent requires that people understand what they are agreeing to and can refuse without losing access to basic services.

想想“同意”这件事。我们一年里要点击数百次“同意”，却一个字也不读，然后别人告诉我们说，我们已经给出了许可。这只是名义上的同意。真正的同意要求人们理解自己同意的内容，并且可以拒绝而不至于失去基本服务。

Nor should we imagine that regulation alone will solve the problem, however well it is drafted. Rules tend to lag behind invention, and a clever firm can usually find a way around them. That is why independent oversight matters, as does a culture in which engineers feel able to raise concerns without fear of punishment.

我们也不应想当然地认为，仅靠监管就能解决问题，无论条文起草得多么周全。规则往往落后于发明，而聪明的公司通常能找到绕开它们的办法。这就是独立监督之所以重要的原因，也是为什么需要一种让工程师敢于提出担忧而不必害怕受罚的文化。

None of this means we should fear progress. Technology has, on the whole, made life longer and easier for millions of people around the world. But the benefits do not cancel the harms, and the people who profit most should be the first to be held accountable when things go wrong. The machine cannot take the blame; we must.

这一切并不意味着我们应该害怕进步。总体而言，技术让全世界数百万人的生活更长寿、更轻松。但好处并不能抵消危害，而获利最多的人，应当在出问题时最先被追究责任。机器无法承担责任，必须由我们来承担。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

While it is true that modern systems can behave in ways their designers did not foresee, unpredictability is not an excuse.
A ‘While’ clause concedes a point, and the relative clause ‘their designers did not foresee’ has an omitted relative pronoun after ‘ways’.
“While”从句先做出让步，而“their designers did not foresee”是省略了关系词的定语从句，修饰“ways”。
Rules tend to lag behind invention, and a clever firm can usually find a way around them.
’Tend to’ and ‘usually’ are hedges that turn absolute claims into general tendencies.
“tend to”和“usually”是限定语，把绝对的论断变成一般性的倾向。
