Why Generations Think Differently 为什么不同世代想法各异

Scientists who study generations often point out that the labels we use, such as baby boomers or Generation Z, are simple ways to group people born in the same period. Those born in the same years tend to share certain experiences, such as the same technology, the same economic conditions and the same major events.

研究代际的科学家常常指出，我们使用的标签，比如婴儿潮一代或Z世代，只是把同一时期出生的人归为一组的简单方式。同一时期出生的人往往有相似的经历，比如相同的科技、相同的经济状况和相同的重大事件。

Psychologists call this the cohort effect. A person who grew up with a smartphone in hand learns to look for information, make friends and solve problems in ways that a person who grew up with only a telephone on the wall may find strange. Neither way is better; each is shaped by the world that surrounded the child.

心理学家把这称为同期群效应。一个从小手握智能手机长大的人，在查找信息、结交朋友和解决问题的方式上，可能会让一个只有墙上电话的年代长大的人觉得陌生。哪一种方式都不比另一种更好，每一种都是被围绕着孩子的世界塑造出来的。

Age matters too. Young people are generally more open to new ideas because the brain is still developing, while older adults tend to rely on experience. This is not a weakness. It is the reason why many societies value the wisdom of the old and the energy of the young.

年龄也很重要。年轻人通常对新想法更开放，因为大脑仍在发育，而年长的成年人则倾向于依靠经验。这并不是弱点。这正是许多社会既看重老年人的智慧、又看重年轻人的活力的原因。

The difficulty is that it is hard to separate the effect of age from the effect of generation. A thirty-year-old today may think differently from a thirty-year-old in the past, but we cannot be sure how much of that difference is due to age and how much to the times. For this reason, researchers follow the same people for decades, and the results so far suggest that both factors play a part.

困难在于，很难把年龄的影响和世代的影响区分开来。今天的三十岁的人，想法可能与过去的三十岁的人不同，但我们无法确定这种差异有多少归因于年龄，又有多少归因于时代。因此，研究人员会对同一批人进行几十年的跟踪，而目前的结果表明，这两个因素都起着作用。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

Young people are generally more open to new ideas because the brain is still developing, while older adults tend to rely on experience.
The sentence joins a cause (‘because’) and a contrast (‘while’) in one structure, so two groups are compared.
句子用 because 表原因、while 表对比，在一个句子里比较两类人。
A thirty-year-old today may think differently from a thirty-year-old in the past, but we cannot be sure how much of that difference is due to age and how much to the times.
In ‘how much to the times’ the words ‘is due’ are left out because they repeat the earlier part of the sentence.
how much to the times 省略了与前文重复的 is due。
