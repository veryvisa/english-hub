Ask the Career Desk: No Local Experience 职业问答：没有本地经验怎么办

🧠 Learn the 5 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 5 个新词

Read an advice column on "no local experience" and notice how the present perfect links past effort to the present situation. 读一篇关于「没有本地经验」的问答专栏，留意现在完成时怎样把过去的努力和眼下的处境连起来。

📖 Key card知识卡
"No local experience" usually hides three worries about you; answer each worry separately instead of arguing about your years abroad.「没有本地经验」背后通常是三个顾虑；逐个回应这些顾虑，而不是去争论你在国外的年头。

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

"I have worked as an accountant for twelve years in my home country. Since I arrived here eight months ago, I have applied for more than sixty jobs. Two recruiters have told me the same thing: I have no local experience. How can I get local experience if nobody will give me a job? Stuck at the Start"

「我在本国做了十二年会计。八个月前来到这里，到现在已经申请了六十多份工作。两位招聘人员跟我说的是同一句话：我没有本地经验。没人给我工作，我上哪儿去拿本地经验？——卡在起点」

Dear Stuck, you are not the first reader who has written to me with this question, and your frustration is fair. But "local experience" rarely means only the place where you worked. Hiring managers usually use it as shorthand for three worries. Will this person understand how things are done here? Will they communicate the way we do? Can anyone here vouch for them? Your twelve years answer none of those worries on paper, however good those years were.

亲爱的「卡在起点」：你不是第一个来信问这个问题的读者，你的沮丧也合情合理。但「本地经验」很少只是指你在哪儿工作过。用人经理通常拿它当三个顾虑的简称：这个人懂不懂这里的做事方式？他沟通的方式和我们一样吗？这里有没有人能为他担保？你那十二年，不管多出色，写在纸上都回答不了这三个问题。

So take the worries one at a time. For the first, do research on how your profession works here, have your credentials assessed, and take one short local course; it shows that you have made an effort to learn the local way of working. For the second, volunteer. A few months as the treasurer of a community group has given many readers local stories and a local reference. For the third, ask for informational interviews: short conversations with people in your field, where you ask questions and do not ask for a job.

所以，一个顾虑一个顾虑地解决。第一个：了解一下你这个行业在这里怎么运作，把学历资质拿去评估，再上一门短期的本地课程；这说明你下了功夫去学本地的工作方式。第二个：去做义工。在社区团体里当几个月义务司库，让很多读者有了本地的故事和本地的推荐人。第三个：约信息访谈，也就是和同行的简短交谈，你去问问题，而不是去要工作。

One reader did all three last year. She wrote to me last month to say that the volunteer role had the biggest impact: in her final interview, the hiring manager spent ten minutes asking about it and almost none on her résumé. Your experience is still your experience. You just need to give employers a local way to see it.

去年有位读者三件事都做了。她上个月来信说，影响最大的是那份义工：最后一轮面试里，用人经理花了十分钟问这段经历，她的简历几乎没被问到。你的经验依然是你的经验，你只需要给雇主一个从本地角度看见它的办法。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

Since I arrived here eight months ago, I have applied for more than sixty jobs.
Since + past simple marks the starting point; the main clause uses the present perfect because the period runs up to now.
since 后用一般过去时标出起点；主句用现在完成时，因为这段时间一直延续到现在。
Your twelve years answer none of those worries on paper, however good those years were.
none of makes the sentence negative without not. however good… means "no matter how good".
none of 让整句变成否定，不用 not。however good… 意思是「不管多好」。
