📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Open the full explanation: 5 points展开细讲：5 段讲解

Travel, trip, journey, and tour are not interchangeable. Travel often refers to the activity of travelling in general, a trip is a particular visit or period away, a journey focuses on travelling from one place to another, and a tour usually involves visiting several places or attractions.

Travel、trip、journey 和 tour 不能随意互换。Travel 通常泛指旅行这种活动；trip 指一次具体的出行；journey 强调从一个地方到另一个地方的行程；tour 通常指游览多个地点或景点。

English uses several different prepositions when discussing flights and transport. You check in for a flight, board a plane, get on a bus, and get off a train. Chinese learners sometimes translate these expressions word for word, but they are best learned as complete chunks.

英语谈论航班和交通工具时会使用不同的介词。乘飞机需要 check in for a flight、board a plane；乘公共汽车用 get on a bus；下火车用 get off a train。中文学习者有时会逐字翻译，但最好把这些表达作为完整词组记忆。

A flight can be direct, nonstop, delayed, or cancelled. Direct flights may sometimes include a stop, whereas nonstop flights do not stop between departure and arrival. This distinction is especially useful when comparing flight options.

航班可以是直达的、不中途停留的、延误的或取消的。Direct flight 有时可能包含中途停靠，而 nonstop flight 则表示从出发到抵达之间没有中途停留。比较航班选项时，这一区别尤其重要。

Some travel expressions have fixed collocations. You make a reservation, book a hotel, catch a flight, miss a connection, and pack your luggage. Using the correct verb makes your English sound much more natural than translating each Chinese word separately.

一些旅行表达具有固定搭配。例如 make a reservation（预订）、book a hotel（预订酒店）、catch a flight（赶上航班）、miss a connection（错过转机航班）以及 pack your luggage（收拾行李）。使用正确的动词，比逐字翻译中文更自然。

Tourism vocabulary often distinguishes between places, activities, and people. A tourist attraction is a place worth visiting, sightseeing is the activity of visiting interesting places, and a local guide is someone who explains the history and culture of an area. These expressions help learners describe travel experiences in greater detail.

旅游词汇通常区分地点、活动和人物。Tourist attraction 指值得参观的景点；sightseeing 指游览观光这一活动；local guide 指介绍当地历史和文化的导游。这些表达能帮助学习者更具体地描述旅行经历。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ I made a travel to Japan last year.
✓ I took a trip to Japan last year.
Travel is generally uncountable when it refers to the activity of travelling. For one particular holiday or visit, use take a trip or go on a trip.
Travel 泛指旅行活动时通常是不可数名词。要表达一次具体的旅行，应使用 take a trip 或 go on a trip，而不是 a travel。
✗ I booked a ticket and received my boarding ticket at the airport.
✓ I booked a ticket and received my boarding pass at the airport.
A ticket is what you purchase to travel, while a boarding pass is the document or digital pass that allows you to board a particular flight.
Ticket 是购买的交通票或机票；boarding pass 是允许你登上特定航班的登机牌。两者不是同一个东西。
✗ Our flight was late for three hours.
✓ Our flight was delayed by three hours.
Use delayed to describe a flight that does not leave or arrive at the scheduled time. By three hours specifies the length of the delay.
描述航班没有按计划时间起飞或抵达时，通常使用 delayed。By three hours 表示延误了三个小时。
✗ We arrived to the airport at 6 a.m.
✓ We arrived at the airport at 6 a.m.
Use arrive at for a specific place, such as an airport, station, or hotel. Use arrive in for cities and countries, such as arrive in London or arrive in Canada.
到达机场、车站或酒店等具体地点时使用 arrive at；到达城市或国家时使用 arrive in，例如 arrive in London 或 arrive in Canada。
✗ I have three luggages.
✓ I have three pieces of luggage.
Luggage is an uncountable noun in standard English. To count individual items, use pieces of luggage, bags, or suitcases.
Luggage 在标准英语中是不可数名词，不能直接加复数词尾。要表示三件行李，可以说 three pieces of luggage，也可以说 three bags 或 three suitcases。
✗ We went to sightseeing after breakfast.
✓ We went sightseeing after breakfast.
The expression is go sightseeing, without to. Sightseeing is the activity itself, rather than a destination introduced by to.
固定表达是 go sightseeing，中间不需要 to。Sightseeing 表示观光活动本身，并不是由 to 引出的目的地。
