The Neighbour Upstairs 楼上的邻居

🧠 Learn the 5 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 5 个新词

Read a personal essay about a noisy neighbour and notice how regret and imagined pasts are expressed. 读一篇关于吵闹邻居的随笔，留意遗憾与「假如当初」是怎么表达的。

Read and listen读与听

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

For our first six months in the building, the man upstairs was only a noise. Footsteps at midnight. A chair pulled across the floor. Once, at two in the morning, what sounded like bowling. My husband wanted to make a complaint to the building manager. I wanted to move.

住进这栋楼的头六个月，楼上那位先生只是一种噪音。半夜的脚步声。椅子拖过地板的声音。有一次凌晨两点，听起来像在打保龄球。我丈夫想向物业经理投诉。我想搬家。

If we had been at home, in the city where we grew up, I would have knocked on his door the first week. Here I did not know the rules. Was it rude to complain? Would he be angry? So we said nothing and grew more annoyed, which was the worst choice of all.

要是在老家，在我们长大的那座城市，第一周我就去敲他的门了。在这里我不懂规矩。抱怨会不会失礼？他会不会生气？于是我们什么也没说，越来越烦，而这恰恰是最糟的选择。

In the end a small accident solved it. A parcel for him was left at our door, and I took it upstairs. He turned out to be a nurse who worked nights. He had no idea the floor carried sound. He was so embarrassed that he bought a rug the same week, and he has brought us cookies every Christmas since.

最后是一件小小的意外解决了问题。他的一个包裹被放在了我们门口，我给他送了上去。原来他是上夜班的护士，完全不知道地板传声。他非常不好意思，当周就买了块地毯，从那以后每年圣诞都给我们送饼干。

I think about how close we came to never meeting him. If that parcel had not arrived, we might still be lying awake, writing angry letters in our heads. I wish I had gone up earlier. Most problems between neighbours are a misunderstanding, not a war, and one polite sentence would have saved us months: "I'm sure you don't realise, but the sound travels. Could we figure something out?"

我常想，我们差一点就永远不会认识他。要是那个包裹没送来，我们可能至今还躺着睡不着，在脑子里写愤怒的信。真希望我早点上楼。邻里之间的问题大多是误会，不是战争，一句客气话本可以替我们省下好几个月：「您肯定没意识到，不过声音会传下来。我们能不能想个办法？」

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

If we had been at home, in the city where we grew up, I would have knocked on his door the first week.
Third conditional: neither part happened. The phrase between commas explains at home.
第三类条件句：两部分都没有发生。逗号之间的短语解释 at home。
If that parcel had not arrived, we might still be lying awake, writing angry letters in our heads.
Mixed conditional: past condition, present result; might makes the result less certain than would.
混合条件句：过去的条件，现在的结果；might 比 would 更不确定。
