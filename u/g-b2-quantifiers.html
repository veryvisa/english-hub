Quantifiers: all, most, each, every, either 数量限定词

All students must bring ID. 所有学生都必须带身份证件。
A general rule.一般规定。
All the students in my class brought ID. 我班上所有学生都带了证件。
A specific group.具体的一群。
Every student brought ID. 每个学生都带了证件。
The whole group, as single members.整体，逐个都算。
Each student showed their ID at the door. 每个学生在门口分别出示了证件。
One at a time.一个接一个。

📏 The rule in one line规则一句话
most people is a general statement; most of the people is a known group. every sees a group as a whole, each sees its members one by one. either and neither are only for two. After each, every, either and neither, use a singular noun.most people 是泛泛而谈；most of the people 是指一个已知的群体。every 把一群看作整体，each 逐个看成员。either、neither 只用于两个。each、every、either、neither 后接单数名词。

The biggest error for Chinese speakers is most of people. The Chinese phrase for most people has no article, so learners add of by analogy with most of them. The rule: no of for a general group (most people, all children); of the / of my / of them for a specific group (most of the people at the meeting, all of my children).

华人最常见的错误是 most of people。中文「大多数人」没有冠词，学习者照着 most of them 类推加了 of。规则是：泛指一类不加 of（most people、all children）；指具体的一群用 of the / of my / of them（most of the people at the meeting、all of my children）。

every and each both take a singular noun, but they feel different. Every sweeps across the whole group and is closer to all: Every apartment in the building has a balcony. each focuses on individuals one by one, often when the number is small or the separate treatment matters: The nurse spoke to each patient. Only each can stand alone or use of: each of us, They cost twenty dollars each. For two items use each, not every.

every 和 each 都接单数名词，但感觉不同。every 把整群一扫而过，接近 all；each 逐个关注个体，常用在数量少、或「分别对待」很重要的时候。只有 each 能单用或接 of：each of us、They cost twenty dollars each。两个东西只能用 each，不能用 every。

either means one or the other of two; neither means not one and not the other; both means the two together. Either day works for me. Neither option is perfect. Both options have problems. With of + plural noun, formal English uses a singular verb (Neither of them is ready); in speech a plural verb is common and accepted.

either 是两个中任一个；neither 是两个都不；both 是两个一起。接 of 加复数名词时，正式英语用单数动词（Neither of them is ready），口语里用复数也很常见、能被接受。

no and none do different jobs. no goes before a noun: There is no parking. none stands alone or takes of: None of the parking is free. none of + plural noun can take a singular or plural verb; the singular sounds more careful in writing. Do not combine none with another negative: I didn't get none is non-standard.

no 和 none 分工不同。no 放在名词前：There is no parking。none 单独用或接 of：None of the parking is free。none of 加复数名词，动词用单数或复数都行，单数在书面语里显得更讲究。none 不能再和别的否定词连用。

all also has a position rule with verbs: it goes after be and auxiliaries, but before main verbs: We are all tired, They have all left, We all agree. This mid-sentence all (and both, each) sounds very natural in speech and is a quick upgrade from All of us agree.

all 跟动词连用时有位置规则：放在 be 动词和助动词后面，实义动词前面：We are all tired、They have all left、We all agree。句中位置的 all（以及 both、each）在口语里非常地道，比 All of us agree 更自然。

Examples例句

  • Most people in our building work from home at least one day a week. 我们楼里大多数人每周至少在家办公一天。
    General statement: no of.泛指：不加 of。
  • Most of the people at the meeting had never used the app. 会上大多数人从没用过这个应用。
    A known group: of the.已知群体：of the。
  • Every apartment in the building has its own laundry. 楼里每套公寓都有自己的洗衣设备。
    The whole set.整体来看。
  • The teacher wrote a short note to each student. 老师给每个学生都写了一张小纸条。
    One by one.逐个。
  • Each of the three bedrooms has a closet. 三间卧室各有一个衣柜。
    each of + plural noun, singular verb.each of 加复数名词，动词用单数。
  • The tickets were fifteen dollars each. 票每张十五元。
    each at the end.each 放句末。
  • I can meet on Tuesday or Thursday; either day works. 周二或周四都行，哪天都可以。
    One of two.两个中的任一个。
  • Neither car has winter tyres yet. 两辆车都还没换冬季轮胎。
    Not one, not the other.两个都不。
  • Both of my parents still drive. 我父母都还在开车。
    The two together.两个都。
  • None of the stores in the mall were open on the holiday. 节日那天商场里的店一家都没开。
    none of, plural verb in speech.none of，口语用复数动词。
  • There is no easy answer to this question. 这个问题没有简单的答案。
    no before a noun.no 放在名词前。
  • We were all surprised by the result. 结果让我们都很意外。
    all after be.all 放在 be 之后。
  • They have both applied for the same job. 他们俩都申请了同一份工作。
    both after an auxiliary.both 放在助动词之后。
  • Half of the money goes to rent. 一半的钱交了房租。
    half of the + uncountable.half of the 加不可数名词。

More pairs: same idea, different choice更多对比句组

Either option is fine with me. 两个选项我都可以。
I accept one or the other.哪个都行。
Neither option is ideal. 两个选项都不理想。
I like neither.两个都不满意。
Both options cost too much. 两个选项都太贵。
The two together.两者一起说。
There are no messages for you. 没有你的留言。

There are none. 一条也没有。
Alone.单独用。
None of the messages are for you. 这些留言没有一条是给你的。
With of.接 of。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ Most of people here speak French.
✓ Most people here speak French.
General group: no of.
泛指：不加 of。
✗ Every students has a locker.
✓ Every student has a locker.
every takes a singular noun.
every 接单数名词。
✗ Every of us agreed.
✓ Each of us agreed. / All of us agreed. / We all agreed.
every cannot take of.
every 不能接 of。
✗ I asked my two brothers, but none of them could help.
✓ I asked my two brothers, but neither of them could help.
For exactly two, use neither.
正好两个用 neither。
✗ I have two sons and every one plays hockey.
✓ I have two sons and both play hockey. / ... and each of them plays hockey.
every needs a group of three or more.
every 至少要三个。
