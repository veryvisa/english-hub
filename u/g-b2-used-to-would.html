used to, would, be used to, get used to used to 一族

Keep four look-alike forms apart: used to do for past habits and states, would do for repeated past actions in a story, and be used to or get used to doing for being or becoming familiar with something. 分清四个长得很像的说法：used to do 讲过去的习惯和状态，would do 讲故事里反复发生的过去动作，be used to 和 get used to doing 讲已经习惯和逐渐习惯。

I used to drive on the right. 我以前是靠右开车的。
Past habit, not now.过去的习惯，现在不是。
I'm used to driving on the right. 我习惯靠右开车。
It feels normal to me.对我来说很平常。
I'm getting used to driving on the left. 我在慢慢适应靠左开车。
Still adjusting.还在适应。

📏 The rule规则

📏 The rule in one line规则一句话
used to + verb says something was true or happened regularly in the past and is no longer so; would + verb also describes past habits but only actions, in a story; be used to + noun or -ing means it is familiar now, and get used to means it is becoming familiar.used to 加动词原形：过去常这样或曾经如此，现在不了；would 加动词原形也讲过去习惯，但只用于动作、多在讲故事时；be used to 加名词或 -ing 表示现在已经习惯；get used to 表示逐渐习惯。

The to in used to do is part of the verb, so a base verb follows: I used to smoke. The to in be used to is a preposition, so a noun or -ing follows: I'm used to working nights. Chinese speakers often mix these because both are translated with xiguan. Ask: am I talking about the past, or about being familiar?

used to do 里的 to 是动词的一部分，后面接原形；be used to 里的 to 是介词，后面接名词或 -ing。华人常混，因为两者都译成「习惯」。先问自己：我在讲过去，还是在讲熟不熟悉？

used to suggests a contrast with now, even when you do not say it: We used to live in Richmond implies we do not any more. If nothing has changed, use the present: I usually walk to work. For a single past event, use the past simple, not used to.

used to 暗含与现在的对比，即使不说出来。如果情况没变，就用一般现在时；过去只发生过一次的事用一般过去时，不用 used to。

would for past habits sounds warm and storytelling, and works only for actions, not states. Every summer my grandfather would take us fishing is fine; He would have a small boat is wrong, because owning is a state. Storytellers often start with used to and continue with would.

would 讲过去习惯，带着讲故事的温度，只用于动作，不用于状态。讲故事的人常先用 used to 开头，后面接着用 would。

Negatives and questions: didn't use to and Did you use to …? (no d, because did already carries the past). In writing, never used to is common and avoids the spelling problem.

否定和疑问用 didn't use to、Did you use to …?（没有 d，因为 did 已经表示过去）。书面上 never used to 很常用，也省了拼写的麻烦。

get used to describes the process, and works in any tense: I'm getting used to the rain. You'll get used to it. It took me a year to get used to driving on snow. be used to describes the result. Asking newcomers Are you used to the weather yet? is a natural small-talk line.

get used to 描述过程，各种时态都能用；be used to 描述结果。问新来的人 Are you used to the weather yet? 是很自然的寒暄。

Examples例句

  • I used to take the bus to work, but now I cycle. 我以前坐公交上班，现在骑车。
    A past habit that changed.过去的习惯，已改变。
  • There used to be a bakery on this corner. 这个街角以前有家面包店。
    A past state that is no longer true.过去的状态，现在不是了。
  • When I was a kid, my mother would wake us up with music on Saturdays. 小时候，每到周六我妈妈都会放音乐叫我们起床。
    A warm memory of a repeated action.对反复动作的温暖回忆。
  • My father would sit by the window and read the paper for hours. 我父亲常常坐在窗边一读报就是几个小时。
    Repeated past action in a story.故事里反复的动作。
  • I'm used to cold winters; I grew up in Harbin. 我习惯了冷冬，我是在哈尔滨长大的。
    Familiar now.现在已经习惯。
  • She isn't used to speaking in front of large groups. 她不习惯在很多人面前讲话。
    -ing after be used to.be used to 后接 -ing。
  • It took me months to get used to tipping in restaurants. 我花了好几个月才习惯在餐馆给小费。
    The process of becoming familiar.逐渐习惯的过程。
  • You'll soon get used to the time difference. 你很快就会适应时差的。
    Future process.将来的适应过程。
  • Did you use to play an instrument? 你以前弹过乐器吗？
    Question form without d.疑问句里没有 d。
  • I didn't use to worry about my health, but now I check my blood pressure every month. 我以前不太担心健康，现在每个月量一次血压。
    Negative past habit.过去不这样。
  • We never used to lock the door in our old town. 在我们以前住的小镇，从来不锁门。
    never used to in place of didn't use to.用 never used to 代替 didn't use to。
  • My son is getting used to his new school, but he misses his friends. 我儿子在慢慢适应新学校，不过还想念老朋友。
    A process still going on.仍在进行的适应过程。
  • Are you used to the food here yet? 你吃得惯这里的东西了吗？
    Small-talk question about familiarity.寒暄：问是否习惯。
  • I've never really got used to working from home. 我一直没真正习惯在家办公。
    Present perfect of get used to.get used to 的现在完成时。

More pairs: same idea, different choice更多对比句组

We used to have a dog. 我们以前养过一条狗。
A past state: correct.过去的状态，正确。
We would have a dog. 我们会养条狗。
Not a past habit: it sounds like an unreal or conditional sentence.不是过去习惯，听起来像假设句。
Every evening we would take the dog to the park. 每天傍晚我们都会带狗去公园。
A repeated action: would works.反复的动作，可以用 would。
I worked in a factory for two years. 我在工厂干过两年。
A finished period with a length: past simple.有长度的已结束时段，用一般过去时。
I used to work in a factory. 我以前在工厂干过。
A past situation contrasted with now, no length given.与现在对比的过去情况，不说时长。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ I am used to get up early.
✓ I am used to getting up early.
to in be used to is a preposition, so use -ing.
be used to 里的 to 是介词，接 -ing。
✗ I use to cook every day in China.
✓ I used to cook every day in China.
The past habit form is always used to in affirmative sentences.
肯定句里过去习惯总是 used to。
✗ When I was young I would live in a small village.
✓ When I was young I used to live in a small village.
would is not used for past states such as living or owning.
would 不用于居住、拥有这类过去状态。
✗ Did you used to smoke?
✓ Did you use to smoke?
After did, use the base form use.
did 后用原形 use。
✗ I used to go to Banff last summer.
✓ I went to Banff last summer.
A single past event takes the past simple.
过去一次性的事用一般过去时。
