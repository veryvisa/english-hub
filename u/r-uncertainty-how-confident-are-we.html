How Scientists Measure What They Do Not Know 科学家如何衡量“未知”

🧠 Learn the 2 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 2 个新词

Scientists express uncertainty through error ranges and replication so that readers can judge how much confidence a result deserves. 科学家通过误差范围和重复验证来表达不确定性，让读者判断一项结果值得多大信心。

When scientists report a result, they rarely give a single number. Instead they give an estimate together with a margin of error, which shows how far the true value might plausibly lie from what they measured. This habit can look like weakness, but it is actually a form of precision about what is and is not known.

科学家报告结果时，很少只给出一个数字。他们会给出一个估计值，并附上误差范围，用来表明真实值与测量值之间可能相差多远。这种习惯看似软弱，实则是对已知与未知的一种精确表达。

Uncertainty comes from several sources. Instruments are never perfect, samples are always smaller than the whole population, and living systems vary from one individual to the next. Some of this noise can be reduced by collecting more data, whereas some of it, such as the natural variability of weather, cannot be removed at all and has to be described honestly.

不确定性有多种来源。仪器永远不完美，样本总是小于整体，生命系统在个体之间也各不相同。其中一部分噪声可以通过收集更多数据来减少，而另一部分，比如天气的自然变化，根本无法消除，只能如实描述。

Replication is the second safeguard. If a finding is real, other teams working independently should be able to reproduce it. A result that appears only once, in one laboratory, is best treated as a lead rather than a fact. By contrast, a result that keeps appearing in different places, using different methods, is robust, and confidence in it grows accordingly.

重复验证是第二道保障。如果一项发现是真实的，独立工作的其他团队应当能够重现它。一个只在某个实验室出现一次的结果，最好视为线索而非事实。相反，一个在不同地方、用不同方法反复出现的结果是稳固的，人们对它的信心也会相应增长。

Even so, confidence is rarely all or nothing. Scientists tend to speak in degrees: a claim may be well supported, tentatively supported, or still open. Although this language can sound evasive to outsiders, it tells the careful reader how much weight an idea can bear and what evidence would be needed to change it.

即便如此，信心也很少是非此即彼的。科学家往往用程度来表达：一个主张可能得到充分支持、初步支持，或仍然悬而未决。这种语言在外行听来也许含糊其辞，但它告诉细心的读者，一个想法能承受多大分量，以及需要什么证据才能改变它。

Understanding all this does not require advanced mathematics. It requires only the habit of asking three questions: how large is the uncertainty, where does it come from, and has anyone else found the same thing? Readers who ask them will, on the whole, be harder to mislead.

理解这一切并不需要高深的数学，只需要养成提三个问题的习惯：不确定性有多大？它来自哪里？别人是否发现了同样的东西？总体而言，提这些问题的读者更难被误导。

Words词

/ˌreplɪˈkeɪʃən/ n.
重复验证，复现
successful replication; replication of results
Replication is what turns a surprising result into accepted knowledge. 重复验证能把一个出人意料的结果变成被接受的知识。
/ˌveəriəˈbɪləti/ n.
变异性，可变性
natural variability; high variability
The variability in the data made the trend hard to see. 数据的变异性使趋势很难看清。
/ˈtentətɪvli/ adv.
试探性地，暂时地
tentatively suggest; tentatively agree
The team tentatively concluded that the drug was safe. 团队初步得出结论，认为这种药物是安全的。
/rəʊˈbʌst/ adj.
稳固的，经得起检验的
robust results; a robust method
Only robust findings should be used to guide public policy. 只有稳固的发现才应被用来指导公共政策。
/tend tuː/ phr.
往往，倾向于
tend to speak cautiously; tend to be revised
Early estimates tend to be revised as more data arrive. 随着更多数据到来，早期的估计往往会被修订。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

Some of this noise can be reduced by collecting more data, whereas some of it, such as the natural variability of weather, cannot be removed at all and has to be described honestly.
The sentence contrasts two parts of the noise with ‘whereas’, and the phrase ‘such as the natural variability of weather’ is inserted as an example between commas.
句子用 whereas 对比噪声的两部分，'such as...' 作为举例成分插在逗号之间。
A result that appears only once, in one laboratory, is best treated as a lead rather than a fact.
The subject is a long noun phrase with a relative clause, and ‘is best treated as’ is a passive structure that gives advice.
主语是带定语从句的长名词短语，'is best treated as' 是给出建议的被动结构。
