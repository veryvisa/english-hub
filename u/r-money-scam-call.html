The Call That Was Not from the Bank 那通不是银行打来的电话

🧠 Learn the 4 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 4 个新词

Follow a fake "bank" call and learn the polite but firm phrases that end it; notice indirect questions and phrasal verbs in speech. 跟着一通冒充银行的电话，学会礼貌而坚决地结束它的说法；留意口语里的间接疑问句和短语动词。

B2 · CELPIP 7–8⏱ 20 minV06 Register: too formal, too casual, or too blunt语域不合：太正式、太随意或太冲G13 Word order in indirect questions间接疑问句语序

📖 Key card知识卡
A real bank does not ask you to read out codes and does not mind if you hang up and call the number on your card.真正的银行不会让你念验证码，也不介意你挂断后打卡背面的号码回拨。

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

It was nearly nine in the evening when Aunt Lin's phone rang. The screen showed the name of her bank. Her nephew Tom, who was staying with her that week, heard her side of the call and asked her to put it on speaker.

快晚上九点时，林阿姨的电话响了，屏幕上显示的是她那家银行的名字。那一周住在她家的外甥汤姆听到她这边的对话，让她开了免提。

"Good evening, this is the fraud department. We have noticed a suspicious transaction on your card, a purchase made in another city an hour ago. To sort out the problem, I need to verify your identity. Could you read me the code we have just sent to your phone?" Aunt Lin reached for her phone. Tom shook his head.

「晚上好，这里是反欺诈部门。我们发现您的卡上有一笔可疑交易，一小时前在另一个城市消费的。为了处理这个问题，我需要核实您的身份。您能把我们刚发到您手机上的验证码念给我吗？」林阿姨伸手去拿手机，汤姆摇了摇头。

"Sorry, could you tell me which branch you are calling from?" Tom asked. "Sir, this is urgent. If you hang up, the money will be lost." "Then I will deal with it myself," Tom said. "I am going to hang up and call the number on the back of the card." The caller tried once more, and then the line went dead.

「不好意思，能告诉我您是从哪个网点打来的吗？」汤姆问。「先生，这事很急。您要是挂了，钱就没了。」「那我自己来处理。」汤姆说，「我现在挂断，打卡背面的号码。」对方又试了一次，随后电话断了。

The real bank, reached through that number ten minutes later, confirmed what Tom had figured out. There was no suspicious transaction, and the balance was exactly as it should be. The code on her phone was a password reset code; whoever got it could have taken over her account. The agent pointed out three things real banks do not do: they do not ask you to read out codes, they do not rush you, and they do not mind when you hang up and call back. Aunt Lin now checks every statement herself, and she has written those three lines on a card next to the phone.

十分钟后，通过那个号码打通的真银行，证实了汤姆的判断：根本没有可疑交易，余额一分不差。她手机上那个验证码是重置密码用的，谁拿到它，谁就可能接管她的账户。客服指出了真银行不会做的三件事：不会让你念验证码，不会催你，你挂断再回拨，他们也不介意。林阿姨现在每张对账单都亲自看，还把这三句话写在一张卡片上，放在电话旁边。

Words词

/frɔːd/ n.
欺诈；诈骗
credit card fraud; report fraud
Report fraud to your bank at once. 发现诈骗立即报告银行。
/səˈspɪʃəs/ adj.
可疑的
a suspicious transaction; a suspicious email
If an email looks suspicious, do not click the link. 邮件看着可疑，就别点链接。
/trænˈzækʃn/ n.
交易；一笔收支
a card transaction; a transaction you do not recognise
Every transaction appears on your monthly statement. 每一笔交易都会出现在月度对账单上。
/ˈverɪfaɪ/ v.
核实
verify your identity; verify the details
The bank will verify your identity before it makes any change. 银行做任何更改前都会核实你的身份。
/hæŋ ʌp/ phr. v.
挂断电话
hang up on sb; hang up and call back
If a caller pressures you, hang up. 来电的人施压，就挂断。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

To sort out the problem, I need to verify your identity.
To + verb at the start gives the purpose. Scammers use calm, official phrases like this.
句首 To＋动词表示目的。骗子就爱用这类平静、官腔的句子。
The code on her phone was a password reset code; whoever got it could have taken over her account.
whoever = any person who. could have taken over: a past possibility that did not happen.
whoever 意为「无论谁」。could have taken over 表示过去可能发生但没发生的事。
