📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Open the full explanation: 6 points展开细讲：6 段讲解

Use 'the media' when referring to newspapers, television, radio, and news websites collectively. It is normally treated as a singular collective noun in everyday English: 'The media is covering the election.' However, 'media outlets' or 'news organizations' is often more precise when discussing individual companies.

the media 泛指报纸、电视、广播和新闻网站等媒体，日常英语中通常作为单数集合名词使用，例如 The media is covering the election（媒体正在报道这场选举）。如果要强调具体的媒体机构，可以使用 media outlets 或 news organizations。

Be careful with 'news', 'a news story', and 'an article'. 'News' is uncountable, so do not say 'a news' or 'many news'. Use 'a news story' for an individual report and 'news articles' for written pieces published by newspapers or websites.

news 是不可数名词，不能说 a news 或 many news。表示一条新闻，可以说 a news story；表示新闻网站或报纸上的文章，可以说 a news article 或 news articles。

Distinguish reporting from commenting. A journalist reports what happened, while a commentator or columnist may explain what an event means or express an opinion. The phrase 'according to' introduces information attributed to a source, whereas 'in my opinion' clearly signals a personal judgment.

要区分报道事实和发表评论。记者（journalist）报道发生了什么，而评论员（commentator）或专栏作家（columnist）可能进一步解释事件的意义或表达观点。according to 用于引出某个来源提供的信息，in my opinion 则明确表示个人看法。

The words 'bias', 'balanced', and 'objective' are useful when evaluating coverage. A report may show bias by consistently favouring one side. Balanced coverage presents relevant perspectives fairly, while objective reporting aims to separate verifiable facts from personal feelings or judgments.

bias、balanced 和 objective 是评价新闻报道时常用的词。报道如果持续偏向某一方，就可能存在偏见。平衡的报道会公平呈现相关各方的观点，而客观报道则努力将可核实的事实与个人情绪或判断区分开来。

Use 'cover a story' to mean report on an event, not physically cover something. 'Breaking news' refers to important information that is just becoming available. A story can also 'make headlines' when it receives prominent attention from major news organizations.

cover a story 在新闻语境中表示报道某个事件，而不是用东西盖住它。breaking news 指刚刚出现的重要新闻。某个事件如果受到主要媒体的突出关注，可以说 make headlines，即成为新闻头条。

Avoid assuming that something is true simply because it has been widely shared. 'Verify a claim' means check whether a statement is supported by evidence. 'Misinformation' is false or inaccurate information, whether or not someone deliberately spreads it, while 'disinformation' is deliberately created or shared to mislead people.

不要因为某条信息被大量转发，就认定它是真的。verify a claim 表示核实某个说法是否有证据支持。misinformation 指错误或不准确的信息，不一定是故意传播的；disinformation 则指为了误导他人而故意制造或传播的虚假信息。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ I read an interesting news yesterday.
✓ I read an interesting news story yesterday.
'News' is uncountable and cannot normally take 'an'. Use 'a news story' or 'a news report' when referring to one item.
news 是不可数名词，通常不能直接加 an。表示一条新闻时，应使用 a news story 或 a news report。
✗ The medias reported the election results.
✓ The media reported the election results.
'Media' is already the plural form historically, although it is commonly used as a singular collective noun in modern English. Do not add '-s' to form 'medias'.
media 在历史上本身就是复数形式，现代英语中也常作为单数集合名词使用。不要再添加 -s 写成 medias。
✗ According to I read online, the policy has changed.
✓ According to what I read online, the policy has changed.
'According to' must be followed by a noun phrase or a noun-like expression, not a complete independent clause. 'What I read online' works as a noun-like expression.
according to 后面应该接名词或具有名词性质的结构，不能直接接一个完整的独立句子。what I read online 在这里充当名词性结构。
✗ The journalist reported about the accident.
✓ The journalist reported the accident.
When 'report' means to give information about an event, it normally takes a direct object without 'about'. You can say 'report on the accident', but not normally 'report about the accident'.
report 表示报道某件事时，通常直接接宾语，不需要 about。也可以说 report on the accident，但通常不说 report about the accident。
✗ This newspaper has a strong political biased.
✓ This newspaper has a strong political bias.
'Bias' is the noun, while 'biased' is the adjective. Use 'a bias' or 'show bias' as a noun, and 'be biased' to describe something that is not neutral.
bias 是名词，表示偏见或偏向；biased 是形容词，表示有偏见的。可以说 have a bias、show bias 或 be biased。
✗ The journalist tried to make an objective news.
✓ The journalist tried to produce an objective news report.
'News' is uncountable, so 'an objective news' is incorrect. Use 'an objective news report' or simply 'objective news coverage'.
news 是不可数名词，因此 an objective news 的表达不正确。可以说 an objective news report，或者 objective news coverage。
