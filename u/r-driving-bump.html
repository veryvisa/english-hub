Five Minutes in a Parking Lot 停车场里的五分钟

🧠 Learn the 5 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 5 个新词

Follow two strangers through a small parking-lot collision and hear the polite phrasal verbs that keep it calm. 跟着两个陌生人经历一次停车场小剐蹭，听那些让场面保持平静的礼貌短语动词。

B2 · CELPIP 7–8⏱ 20 minV04 Phrasal verbs avoided; the formal verb sounds stiff回避短语动词，说话太书面V06 Register: too formal, too casual, or too blunt语域不合：太正式、太随意或太冲

📖 Key card知识卡
After a small collision, check on people first, exchange details, record everything and leave the question of fault for later.小剐蹭之后，先问人有没有事，再交换信息、把一切记下来，责任归属留到以后再说。

Read and listen读与听

277 words
Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

It was a Saturday afternoon at a busy shopping centre. Jun was backing out of a parking space when he felt a soft bump. He had checked his mirror, but the other car had been in his blind spot. The other driver, a woman in her sixties, was already getting out. Here is how the next five minutes went.

那是一个周六下午，购物中心里人很多。阿俊正在倒车出车位，突然感觉轻轻一撞。他看过后视镜，可另一辆车刚好在他的盲区里。对方司机是一位六十多岁的女士，已经下了车。下面是接下来的五分钟。

"Are you okay?" "I'm fine. Are you?" "Yes. I'm sorry, I didn't see you." "Well, I didn't see you either. Let's have a look." The damage was small: a crack in his rear light and a dent in her bumper. "I think we should exchange details and let the insurance companies sort it out," she said. "Good idea. Could I take a photo of your licence plate and your insurance card?" "Of course. I'll do the same."

「您没事吧？」「我没事，你呢？」「我也没事。对不起，我没看见您。」「我也没看见你。我们看看吧。」损坏不大：他的尾灯裂了一道，她的保险杠瘪了一块。「我想我们交换一下信息，让保险公司去处理吧，」她说。「好主意。我能拍一下您的车牌和保险卡吗？」「当然，我也拍一下你的。」

While they were writing down names and numbers, a man walking past pointed out a security camera above the parking lot. "Might help you figure out who backed into whom," he said. They both laughed, a little nervously.

他们正在记名字和电话时，一位路过的男士指了指停车场上方的监控摄像头。「说不定能帮你们弄清是谁倒车撞了谁，」他说。两人都笑了，笑得有点紧张。

Jun had read that it is usually better not to argue about fault at the scene, because insurers decide that later, and that the rules on reporting a collision differ from place to place. So he did not argue. He stayed polite, took photos of both cars from several angles and wrote down the time and place.

阿俊读到过，一般最好别在现场争论谁的责任，因为责任由保险公司事后判定；还有，事故要不要报告、怎么报告，各地规定不同。所以他没有争。他一直很客气，从几个角度给两辆车都拍了照，记下了时间和地点。

On Monday he called his insurer. It took about twenty minutes to deal with, and the agent asked him to follow up by email with the photos. The whole thing was annoying but not frightening, mostly because neither driver turned a small bump into a fight.

周一他给自己的保险公司打了电话。处理用了大约二十分钟，客服让他再发一封邮件把照片补过去。整件事很烦，但并不吓人，主要是因为两位司机都没有把一次小剐蹭变成一场争吵。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

Jun was backing out of a parking space when he felt a soft bump.
Past continuous (was backing out) for the longer action, past simple (felt) for the short event that interrupts it.
过去进行时写持续的动作，一般过去时写打断它的短暂事件。
I think we should exchange details and let the insurance companies sort it out
let + object + bare infinitive. sort it out: with a pronoun, the particle goes after it (sort it out, not sort out it).
let 后接宾语加不带 to 的不定式。宾语是代词时小品词放后面：sort it out，不说 sort out it。
