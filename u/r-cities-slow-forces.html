Why Homes Cost So Much: Four Slow Forces 房子为什么这么贵：四股慢力量

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Ask ten people why housing is expensive and you will hear ten villains: investors, landlords, banks, newcomers, the government. Economists who study housing tend to give a less satisfying answer. In most fast-growing cities, the price of a home is pushed up by a few slow forces working at the same time, and no single one explains everything.

问十个人房子为什么贵，你会听到十个「坏人」：投资客、房东、银行、新移民、政府。研究住房的经济学家给的答案往往没那么痛快：在大多数快速发展的城市，房价是由几股慢力量同时推高的，没有哪一股能解释全部。

The first is demand. When a region adds jobs and people faster than it adds homes, buyers and renters compete for the same limited supply, and prices rise. Population growth is not a problem in itself; it becomes one when building does not keep up.

第一股是需求。一个地区增加工作和人口的速度快于增加住房的速度时，买家和租客就会争夺同一批有限的房源，价格随之上涨。人口增长本身不是问题；建房跟不上时，它才成了问题。

The second is the cost of money. When interest rates are low, buyers can borrow more for the same monthly payment, and they bid up prices. When rates rise, the monthly cost of a mortgage rises with them, so a home can become harder to afford even while its price falls a little.

第二股是资金成本。利率低时，同样的月供能借到更多钱，买家就会把价格竞相抬高。利率上升时，房贷的月供也跟着涨，所以即使房价稍有下降，房子也可能更难负担。

The third and fourth are land and time. In many cities, most residential land is reserved for detached houses, so new homes have to squeeze into a small share of the map; rules that allow gentle density can change that. And building takes years. By the time new supply arrives, demand has often moved on.

第三股和第四股是土地和时间。在很多城市，大部分住宅用地只许建独立屋，新房只能挤进地图上很小的一块；允许温和加密的规定可以改变这一点。而且盖房要好几年，等新房源到位时，需求往往又变了。

None of this tells you whether to rent or buy, or where; that depends on your own situation. But it does explain why affordable housing is not something one policy can fix in a year, let alone one speech. It also explains why the condo that seemed far too expensive five years ago may look cheap to the next buyer.

这些都不能告诉你该租还是该买、该住哪里，那取决于你自己的情况。但它确实解释了，为什么住房负担问题不是一项政策一年就能解决的，更别说一场演讲了。它也解释了，为什么五年前看着贵得离谱的公寓，在下一位买家眼里可能显得便宜。
