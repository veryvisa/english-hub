📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Open the full explanation: 6 points展开细讲：6 段讲解

Town, city and village describe different types of settlements, but their boundaries are not always determined by population alone. A town is generally smaller than a city, while a village is usually a smaller rural community. Avoid assuming that every country uses exactly the same definitions.

Town、city 和 village 分别表示城镇、城市和村庄，但它们的划分不完全取决于人口数量。一般来说，town 比 city 小，而 village 通常是规模较小的乡村社区。不要认为所有国家对这些概念都有完全相同的定义。

Urban and rural are useful adjectives for comparing living environments. Urban areas tend to have more buildings, transport links and services, whereas rural areas are generally less densely populated. Remember to use urban area and rural community rather than treating urban and rural as nouns in every situation. In Canadian and American English, downtown usually refers to the central business area of a city, which is close in meaning to city centre.

Urban 和 rural 是比较居住环境时非常实用的形容词。城市地区通常有更多建筑、交通线路和公共服务，而乡村地区的人口密度通常较低。注意常见搭配是 urban area 和 rural community，不要在所有情况下都直接把 urban 和 rural 当作名词使用。在加拿大和美国英语中，downtown 通常指城市的中心商业区，含义接近 city centre。

Several words describe the physical character of a place. A suburb is a residential area outside or on the edge of a city, while a neighbourhood is a local area where people live. A district is often a larger or officially recognised section of a town or city. These words are related, but they are not interchangeable.

有几个词可以描述一个地方的空间特征。Suburb 指城市外围或边缘的住宅区，neighbourhood 指人们居住的周边社区，而 district 通常指城市或城镇中较大的区域，也可能是正式划分的行政或功能区。这些词意思相关，但不能随意互换。

English frequently uses expressions such as be located in, be surrounded by and be within walking distance of to describe where places are. Chinese speakers sometimes translate location expressions word for word. Learn the complete phrases instead of memorising individual prepositions.

英语经常使用 be located in、be surrounded by 和 be within walking distance of 等表达来介绍地点。中文学习者有时会逐字翻译方位表达，导致介词使用不自然。建议把完整短语作为整体记忆，而不是孤立地背诵介词。

Words describing the quality of a place often have different levels of formality. Lively and vibrant suggest an energetic atmosphere, while peaceful and quiet emphasise the absence of noise or activity. Picturesque describes an attractive place that looks suitable for a picture, often because of its scenery or traditional buildings.

描述地方环境的形容词在语气和侧重点上有所不同。Lively 和 vibrant 强调充满活力的氛围，peaceful 和 quiet 强调安静，而 picturesque 则形容风景或传统建筑优美、如画般迷人的地方。选择形容词时，要注意它表达的是活跃、安静还是视觉上的美感。

Some words describe how a settlement changes over time. Develop usually suggests growth or improvement, while expand focuses on becoming larger. Be careful with infrastructure, which refers to essential systems and facilities such as roads, public transport and water supplies, rather than simply individual buildings.

有些词汇用于描述聚居地随时间发生的变化。Develop 通常表示发展或改善，expand 则更强调规模扩大。Infrastructure 指道路、公共交通和供水系统等基础设施，不只是某一栋建筑或普通设施。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ I live in a countryside near the city.
✓ I live in the countryside near the city.
Countryside is an uncountable noun in this meaning, so it does not normally take the article a. Use the countryside.
Countryside 在表示乡村环境时是不可数名词，通常不能加不定冠词 a。正确表达是 the countryside。
✗ My house is located at a quiet suburb.
✓ My house is located in a quiet suburb.
Use in with a suburb when describing the area where something is situated. At is more appropriate for specific points or addresses.
表示某物位于某个郊区时，suburb 前通常使用介词 in。At 更常用于具体地点或地址。
✗ The city has many modern infrastructures.
✓ The city has a lot of modern infrastructure.
Infrastructure is generally an uncountable noun. Use much, a lot of or better infrastructure rather than making it plural in ordinary usage.
Infrastructure 通常是不可数名词。日常表达中应使用 a lot of infrastructure 或 better infrastructure，而不是随意加 s 变成复数。
✗ This area is very population dense.
✓ This area is very densely populated.
Densely populated is the standard adjective phrase. Densely is an adverb modifying populated, so the word order matters.
Densely populated 是标准的形容词短语，意思是人口稠密的。Densely 是副词，用来修饰 populated，不能随意改变词序。
✗ There are many amenities in this neighbourhood, such as park and supermarket.
✓ There are many amenities in this neighbourhood, such as parks and supermarkets.
When giving examples of facilities in general, use plural countable nouns when referring to multiple parks and supermarkets.
泛指社区中有多个公园和超市时，可数名词应使用复数形式 parks 和 supermarkets。
✗ The village is surrounded with mountains.
✓ The village is surrounded by mountains.
The standard expression is be surrounded by something. The preposition by introduces the things that surround a place.
固定搭配是 be surrounded by，介词 by 后面接环绕某地的事物。
