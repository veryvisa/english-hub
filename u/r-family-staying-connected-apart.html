Even When We Live Far Away 即使相隔遥远

🧠 Learn the 4 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 4 个新词

Families living in different places can maintain close relationships through regular communication, shared activities, and realistic expectations. 生活在不同地方的家庭成员可以通过定期沟通、共同活动和合理的期待来维持亲密关系。

Read and listen读与听

240 words
Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Mia: I feel as if I am losing touch with my parents. Since I moved to another city for work, we rarely have long conversations. I call them when I have free time, but they are sometimes busy. When we finally speak, I do not know what to say. It feels strange because we used to talk about almost everything.

米娅：我感觉自己正在逐渐与父母失去联系。自从我为了工作搬到另一个城市之后，我们很少进行长时间的交谈。我有空时会给他们打电话，但他们有时也很忙。等我们终于通上电话时，我却不知道该说些什么。这种感觉很奇怪，因为以前我们几乎什么都聊。

Daniel: I understand. My sister lives in another country, and we have had the same problem. We used to expect every phone call to be long and meaningful. That made communication feel like a responsibility instead of something enjoyable. Eventually, we decided to send each other short voice messages during the week and arrange a longer call at the weekend.

丹尼尔：我理解。我姐姐住在另一个国家，我们也遇到过同样的问题。以前我们总希望每次电话都很长，而且有意义。这使得沟通变成了一种责任，而不是一件令人愉快的事情。最后，我们决定在工作日互相发送简短的语音信息，并在周末安排一次较长的通话。

Mia: That sounds easier. Perhaps I could also share ordinary things, such as a meal I cooked or something interesting I saw on my way to work. I sometimes think that staying connected means having important news to report. But maybe everyday details are what make relationships feel natural. Even a photograph can give people something to talk about.

米娅：这听起来更容易做到。也许我还可以分享一些日常小事，比如自己做的一顿饭，或者上班路上看到的有趣事物。我有时认为，保持联系意味着必须有重要的消息可以告诉对方。但也许正是这些日常细节让关系显得自然。甚至一张照片也能为人们提供聊天的话题。

Daniel: Exactly. We also need to accept that people have different schedules. Although technology makes communication easier, it cannot remove every difficulty caused by distance. A missed call does not necessarily mean that someone does not care. What matters is making an effort over time. Regular contact, small acts of kindness, and mutual understanding are likely to keep family relationships strong.

丹尼尔：正是如此。我们还需要接受每个人的时间安排都不一样。尽管科技让沟通变得更加容易，但它无法消除距离带来的所有困难。一次没有接到的电话并不一定意味着某个人不在乎你。重要的是长期持续地付出努力。定期联系、小小的善意行为以及相互理解，很可能让家庭关系保持牢固。

Words词

/luːz tʌtʃ/ phr. v.
失去联系
lose touch with someone; gradually lose touch
I lost touch with an old classmate after graduation. 毕业后，我与一位老同学失去了联系。
/ˈsteɪ.ɪŋ kəˈnek.tɪd/ phr.
保持联系
keep staying connected; staying connected with family
Video calls help us stay connected with distant relatives. 视频通话帮助我们与远方的亲戚保持联系。
/ˈvɔɪs ˌmes.ɪdʒ/ n. phr.
语音信息
send a voice message; listen to a voice message
She sent me a voice message while travelling. 她旅行时给我发了一条语音信息。
/ˈɔː.dən.ri/ adj.
普通的；日常的
ordinary day; ordinary activities
An ordinary conversation made the difficult day feel better. 一次普通的交谈让这个艰难的一天变得好过了一些。
/ˌmjuː.tʃu.əl ˌʌn.dəˈstæn.dɪŋ/ n. phr.
相互理解
develop mutual understanding; promote mutual understanding
Mutual understanding is important in any close relationship. 相互理解对于任何亲密关系都很重要。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

Since I moved to another city for work, we rarely have long conversations.
Since introduces a time clause in the past, while the present simple describes a situation that continues into the present.
Since 引导过去时间状语从句，而主句使用一般现在时，描述延续至今的情况。
A missed call does not necessarily mean that someone does not care.
Does not necessarily is a hedging expression that avoids presenting an interpretation as an absolute fact.
does not necessarily 是一种限定表达，避免将某种解释说成绝对事实。
