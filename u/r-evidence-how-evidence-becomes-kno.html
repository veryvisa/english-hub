How Does Health Evidence Become Knowledge? 健康证据如何转化为知识？

Reliable public health knowledge develops through a continuous process of observation, comparison, critical evaluation and independent replication. 可靠的公共卫生知识通过持续的观察、比较、批判性评估和独立重复验证逐步形成。

Public health research begins with questions about patterns in human health. Researchers might investigate why certain communities experience more respiratory problems or whether a particular environmental exposure is associated with illness. They first define the question and identify what information is needed. This initial stage matters because poorly defined questions can produce misleading conclusions, even when the subsequent analysis is technically sophisticated.

公共卫生研究始于对人类健康变化模式的疑问。研究人员可能调查为什么某些社区出现更多呼吸系统问题，或者某种环境暴露是否与疾病有关。他们首先明确研究问题，并确定需要哪些信息。这一初始阶段十分重要，因为即使后续分析在技术上非常复杂，定义不清的问题仍可能产生误导性结论。

Different research designs answer different questions. Observational studies examine what happens naturally, whereas experiments introduce an intervention to investigate its effects. Neither approach is universally superior. While randomised experiments can strengthen causal inference, they may be impractical or ethically inappropriate for certain public health questions. Researchers therefore need to select a design that matches the question rather than simply choosing the most fashionable method.

不同的研究设计回答不同的问题。观察性研究考察自然发生的情况，而实验则引入干预措施来研究其影响。两种方法都不是在所有情况下更优。尽管随机实验可以增强因果推断的能力，但对于某些公共卫生问题而言，这类实验可能不切实际或不符合伦理要求。因此，研究人员需要选择与问题相匹配的设计，而不是简单地采用最流行的方法。

After collecting information, researchers analyse the results and examine possible sources of bias. They consider whether the sample represents the population, whether important variables have been overlooked and whether the findings could have occurred by chance. Statistical significance is informative, but it does not necessarily indicate practical importance. In many cases, the size of an effect and the quality of the underlying evidence matter more than a single statistical label.

收集信息后，研究人员会分析结果，并检查可能的偏差来源。他们会考虑样本是否能够代表目标人群、是否遗漏了重要变量，以及研究发现是否可能由偶然因素造成。统计显著性具有参考价值，但并不一定意味着实际意义。在许多情况下，效果大小和基础证据的质量比单一的统计标签更加重要。

Scientific knowledge becomes more robust when independent researchers examine similar questions and obtain comparable results. This process is known as replication. Nevertheless, conflicting findings do not automatically mean that one study is worthless. Differences in methods, populations and settings may explain the disagreement. On the whole, knowledge tends to develop through repeated testing and revision rather than through one supposedly definitive experiment.

当独立研究人员考察相似问题并获得可比较的结果时，科学知识就会变得更加稳固。这个过程称为重复验证。然而，研究结果相互冲突并不自动意味着其中一项研究毫无价值。研究方法、研究人群和环境的差异可能解释这种分歧。总体而言，知识往往通过反复检验和修正逐步发展，而不是依靠某一次所谓的决定性实验。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

Researchers therefore need to select a design that matches the question rather than simply choosing the most fashionable method.
The relative clause that matches the question modifies design, and rather than introduces a contrast between two choices.
关系从句 that matches the question 修饰 design，rather than 则用于对比两种选择。
On the whole, knowledge tends to develop through repeated testing and revision rather than through one supposedly definitive experiment.
The discourse marker on the whole signals a generalisation, while tends to expresses a qualified claim rather than an absolute statement.
话语标记 on the whole 表示总体判断，tends to 则表达有限定的观点，而不是绝对断言。
