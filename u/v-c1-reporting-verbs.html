📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Open the full explanation: 5 points展开细讲：5 段讲解

Compare three reports of the same words. The landlord said the heating was fixed is neutral. The landlord claimed the heating was fixed suggests you doubt it. The landlord admitted the heating was not fixed shows he said something against his own interest. One verb changes the whole story.

同一句话三种转述：said 中立；claimed 暗示你不信；admitted 说明他承认了对自己不利的事。一个动词改了整个故事。

The verbs fall into rough groups. Neutral: say, state, report, explain. Doubt: claim, allege. Agreement against interest: admit, concede, acknowledge. Strong position: insist, argue, maintain. Indirect: suggest, imply, hint. Rejection: deny, dismiss, reject.

大致分组：中立 say/state/report/explain；存疑 claim/allege；承认对己不利 admit/concede/acknowledge；坚持 insist/argue/maintain；暗示 suggest/imply/hint；否认 deny/dismiss/reject。

Each verb has its own pattern, and learners carry one pattern to all of them. Suggest takes -ing or that, never to: she suggested waiting. Deny takes -ing or that: he denied taking it. Accuse takes of: accused him of lying. Warn takes a person: warned us not to.

每个动词有自己的句型，学习者常把一个句型套到所有动词上：suggest 接 -ing 或 that，不接 to；deny 接 -ing 或 that；accuse 用 of；warn 后面先接人。

Tell needs a person; say does not. "He told that he was tired" is the classic error (V02 on the pains list). If you want a person after say, use to: she said to me that.

tell 要接人，say 不用。He told that he was tired 是经典错误。say 后面要接人得加 to。

In news and reports, the verb carries legal weight. Allege is used for accusations that have not been proved; confirm means an official source has checked. Reading these verbs closely is one of the best defences against misinformation.

在新闻报道里，转述动词有法律分量：allege 用于未经证实的指控；confirm 表示官方核实过。仔细读这些动词，是防误导信息的好办法。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ He told that he would be late.
✓ He said that he would be late. / He told me that he would be late.
tell needs a person after it; say does not.
tell 后要接人，say 不用。
✗ She suggested me to see a doctor.
✓ She suggested that I see a doctor. / She suggested seeing a doctor.
suggest never takes object + to.
suggest 不接「宾语＋to」。
✗ He denied to take the money.
✓ He denied taking the money. / He denied that he had taken the money.
deny + -ing or that.
deny 接 -ing 或 that。
✗ They accused him for lying.
✓ They accused him of lying.
accuse sb of.
accuse sb of。
✗ The study claims that sleep is important. (for a well-tested finding)
✓ The study shows that sleep is important.
claim signals doubt; use show or find for strong evidence.
claim 表存疑，有力证据用 show 或 find。
