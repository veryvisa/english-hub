📏 The rule规则

📏 The point in one line一句话要领
Fast speech is not ordinary speech played faster: small words shrink to a schwa or vanish, sounds merge across word boundaries, and only the stressed syllables keep their full shape, so the skill is to catch those and rebuild the rest from grammar and context.快语速不是把正常语速放快：小词缩成 /ə/ 或消失，词界上的音彼此融合，只有重读音节保持完整。所以本事是抓住重读音节，再用语法和语境补出其余部分。

What do you want? /ˈwɒdʒə ˈwɒnt/

Did you eat yet? /ˈdɪdʒə ˈiːt ˈjet/
/d/ + /j/ becomes /dʒ/. /d/ 加 /j/ 变成 /dʒ/。
Don't you think so? /ˈdəʊntʃə ˈθɪŋk səʊ/
/t/ + /j/ becomes /tʃ/. /t/ 加 /j/ 变成 /tʃ/。
I'm going to call them. /aɪm ˈɡʌnə ˈkɔːl əm/
going to → gonna; them → /əm/. going to 读成 gonna，them 读成 /əm/。
I should have told you. /aɪ ˈʃʊdə ˈtəʊld juː/
should have → /ˈʃʊdə/. should have 读成 /ˈʃʊdə/。
a lot of people /ə ˈlɑɾə ˈpipəl/
North American flap: a lo-da people. 北美闪音：听着像 a lo-da people。
Get it out of the car. /ˈɡeɾɪ ˈdaʊɾə ðə ˈkɑr/
Flaps and linking: ge-di-dow-da. 闪音加连读。
I don't know. /aɪ dəˈnəʊ/
The /t/ goes and don't weakens. /t/ 脱落，don't 弱化。
last night /ˌlɑːs ˈnaɪt/
/t/ between consonants drops. 辅音之间的 /t/ 脱落。
probably /ˈprɒbli/
A whole syllable disappears in casual speech. 随意说话时整整丢掉一个音节。
ten boys in the green box /ˈtem ˈbɔɪz ɪn ðə ˈɡriːm ˈbɒks/
/n/ becomes /m/ before /b/. /n/ 在 /b/ 前变成 /m/。
Could you give me a hand with this? /ˈkʊdʒə ˈɡɪv mi ə ˈhænd wɪð ˈðɪs/
Three beats: give, hand, this. 三个拍子：give、hand、this。
What are you going to do about it? /ˈwʌɾər jə ˈɡʌnə ˈdu əˈbaʊɾɪt/
Only do and bout keep their full shape. 只有 do 和 bout 保持完整。
I'd have helped if you'd asked me. /aɪdəv ˈhelpt ɪf jʊd ˈɑːst mi/
I'd have is one smudge; the participles carry it. I'd have 是一团含糊的音，靠过去分词撑住。

supposed to → s'pose-ta. supposed to 读成 s'pose-ta。

let me → lemme; can is weak. let me 读成 lemme，can 弱读。

📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Open the full explanation: 5 points展开细讲：5 段讲解

When intermediate learners say "they speak too fast", the speed is rarely the real cause. An ordinary conversation runs at about four or five syllables a second, but most of those syllables are reduced. The learner is waiting for the dictionary form of each word, and in fast speech most function words never appear in that form: what do you is /ˈwɒdʒə/, going to is /ˈɡʌnə/, I don't know can be /aɪdəˈnəʊ/ or even three vague syllables with the right melody.

中级学习者说「他们说太快了」，真正的原因很少是速度。日常对话每秒大约四五个音节，但其中大多数被弱化了。学习者在等每个词的词典读音，而快语速里大多数虚词根本不会以词典读音出现。

Learn the reductions as a fixed list, because they are predictable. Four processes do most of the work. Weak forms: and /ən/, of /ə/, to /tə/, have /əv/. Linking: get it out → ge-di-dout. Assimilation: don't you → /ˈdəʊntʃə/, did you → /ˈdɪdʒə/, ten boys → /tem bɔɪz/. Elision: a /t/ or /d/ between consonants disappears (las' night, san'wich), and whole unstressed syllables go (prob'ly, 'cause, s'pose).

把弱化当成一张固定清单来学，因为它们是可预测的。四种过程包办了大部分：弱读、连读、同化、省音。

North American speech adds one more: the flap. A /t/ or /d/ between vowels, after stress, becomes a quick tap, close to the Mandarin l said very fast: water, better, a lot of, get it, what about. If you expect a clear /t/, I'll get it sounds like I'll ge-lit and you miss the verb. Canadians use the flap as much as Americans.

北美口音还多一项：闪音。重读后、元音之间的 /t/ 或 /d/ 变成一下很快的轻弹，接近快速的汉语 l：water、better、a lot of、get it。你等清楚的 /t/，就会把 I'll get it 听成 I'll ge-lit 而漏掉动词。加拿大人和美国人一样常用闪音。

Listen top-down as well as bottom-up. Native listeners do not decode every sound; they hear the stressed words, predict the grammar around them, and fill the gaps. In I should have told you, the beats are should … told; the /ʃʊdə/ between them is grammar you already know must be there. For a Chinese speaker this means trusting structure more: when you hear a past participle after a short smudge, the smudge is very likely have or 've.

自下而上之外还要自上而下地听。母语听者并不逐个解码每个音；他们听重读词，预测周围的语法，再补空。I should have told you 里拍子在 should 和 told 上，中间那团 /ʃʊdə/ 是你本来就知道该有的语法。对华人来说，这意味着更信任结构：在一小团含糊的音后面听到过去分词，那团音很可能就是 have 或 've。

The training method that works best at this level is short, intensive dictation. Take 20 to 30 seconds of real speech with a transcript, listen once for meaning, then write down every word you can, replaying each phrase up to five times. Compare with the transcript and classify each miss: a word you did not know, a word you knew but did not recognise in its reduced form, or a word lost at a boundary. Only the second and third kinds are pronunciation problems, and they are the ones this unit's drills target.

这个阶段最有效的训练是短而密集的听写。取 20 到 30 秒带原文的真实语音，先听一遍抓意思，再逐句回放（每句最多五遍）写下每个词。对照原文给每处漏听分类：生词、认识但没认出它的弱化形式、在词界上丢了。只有后两类是发音问题，也正是本单元练习要攻的。
