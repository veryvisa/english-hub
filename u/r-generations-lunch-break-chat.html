Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Dana: You look tired, Ray. Did the new software cause trouble again? Ray: Yes, it did. I have been using the old system for twenty years, and now everything has changed. I feel like I am being left behind, and the interns seem to understand it without even trying.

黛娜：你看起来很累，雷。是新软件又出问题了吗？雷：是啊。旧系统我用了二十年，现在一切都变了。我觉得自己被落下了，而实习生们似乎不费吹灰之力就能明白。

Dana: I know what you mean, but you are not alone. When I started here, my manager used to say that young people did not know how to work hard. Now I am the one who hears that I am too old-fashioned. Ray: Really? You? Dana: Yes. It seems that every generation looks down on the next, and then gets looked down on in turn.

黛娜：我明白你的意思，不过你并不孤单。我刚来这里的时候，我的经理总说年轻人不懂得努力工作。现在轮到我被说太老派了。雷：真的吗？你？黛娜：是的。似乎每一代人都看不起下一代，然后又轮流被人看不起。

Ray: So what do I do? Dana: Ask the interns for help. They enjoy it, and you can teach them something in return. Last week one of them showed me a shortcut that saved me an hour, and I explained how to calm an angry client. Ray: That sounds like a fair deal, if they have the patience.

雷：那我该怎么办？黛娜：请实习生帮忙。他们乐意帮，你也可以反过来教他们点东西。上周其中一个教了我一个捷径，省了我一个小时，我则教了他怎样安抚愤怒的客户。雷：听起来是公平的交换，只要他们有耐心的话。

Ray: Thanks, Dana. I was afraid you would laugh at me. Dana: Never. Nobody is born knowing everything, whatever their age. If we can find some common ground, work gets easier for everyone. Ray: All right, I will try. Maybe you could sit with me this afternoon? Dana: Of course. Bring your coffee, and we will start after lunch.

雷：谢谢你，黛娜。我本来怕你会笑话我。黛娜：怎么会。没有人生来什么都懂，不管年龄多大。如果我们能找到一些共同点，每个人的工作都会轻松些。雷：好吧，我试试。也许今天下午你可以陪我坐一会儿？黛娜：当然。带上你的咖啡，午饭后我们就开始。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

Nobody is born knowing everything, whatever their age.
’Born knowing’ uses a participle phrase after the passive, and ‘whatever their age’ means ‘no matter how old they are’.
born knowing 是被动结构后接分词短语，whatever their age 意为“无论年龄多大”。
It seems that every generation looks down on the next, and then gets looked down on in turn.
The sentence changes from the active ‘looks down on’ to the passive ‘gets looked down on’ to show that roles reverse.
句子从主动 looks down on 转为被动 gets looked down on，表示角色轮换。
