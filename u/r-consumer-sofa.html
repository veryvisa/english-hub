A Sofa That Never Arrived 一张从没送到的沙发

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Subject: Order 48213, delivery missed three times. Dear Customer Care team, I am writing about my order for a three-seat sofa, placed on 2 September and paid in full. The delivery has now been missed three times, and I would like this resolved by the end of next week.

主题：订单 48213，三次送货失约。客服团队：我写信是关于我 9 月 2 日下单、已全额付款的一张三人沙发。送货到现在已经失约三次，我希望在下周末之前把这件事解决。

Here is what has happened so far. On 20 September and again on 27 September, I took a day off work and waited at home, but nobody arrived and nobody called. On 4 October the driver phoned to say the sofa had been damaged in the warehouse. Each time, your phone team apologised and promised to call back. Nobody did.

下面是到目前为止的经过。9 月 20 日和 9 月 27 日，我两次请假在家等，但没有人来，也没有人打电话。10 月 4 日，司机打电话说沙发在仓库里损坏了。每一次，你们的电话客服都道了歉，答应回电，但没有一个人回过。

I do not think my expectations are unreasonable. I understand that damage happens and that delivery dates can move. What I cannot accept is the lack of communication, which has made it impossible to plan. If there has been a misunderstanding about my address or phone number, I have confirmed both below.

我不认为我的要求不合理。我理解货物会损坏，送货日期也会变。我不能接受的是缺乏沟通，这让我完全没法安排。如果是我的地址或电话有什么误会，我已经在下面把两样都核对了一遍。

I would like you to do one of two things: confirm a firm delivery date within ten days, in writing, or cancel the order and refund the full amount to my card. I would also ask you to consider some compensation for the two days of work I have lost, or at least an apology from someone who can explain what went wrong.

希望你们做到以下两件事之一：十天内书面确认一个确定的送货日期；或者取消订单，把全款退回我的卡上。我还希望你们考虑对我耽误的两天工作作出一些补偿，至少由一位能说清问题出在哪儿的人来道个歉。

I have shopped with you for years and would like to keep doing so, but my confidence in your service has been shaken. I have kept a record of every call, with dates and the names of the staff I spoke to. If I do not hear from you by Friday, I will escalate this. Kind regards, Helen Zhou

我在你们这里买了很多年东西，也希望继续买下去，但我对你们服务的信心已经动摇了。每一次通话我都做了记录，包括日期和接电话员工的名字。如果周五前没有收到回复，我会把这件事往上投诉。此致，周海伦

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

I do not think my expectations are unreasonable.
Two word-family forms in one line: expect → expectation (noun), reasonable → unreasonable (prefix un-). Saying I do not think … unreasonable is softer than My expectations are reasonable.
一句里两个词族形式：expect → expectation（名词），reasonable → unreasonable（加前缀 un-）。说 I do not think … unreasonable 比直接说 My expectations are reasonable 更软。
What I cannot accept is the lack of communication, which has made it impossible to plan.
A what-cleft puts the key point at the end. which refers to the whole idea before it. made it impossible to plan: it stands for to plan.
what 分裂句把重点放在句末。which 指前面整件事。made it impossible to plan 里 it 代替 to plan。
