Rent or Buy? A Kitchen-Table Conversation 租还是买？厨房餐桌边的一次对话

🧠 Learn the 3 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 3 个新词

Follow a couple talking through renting and buying, and pick up the make/do/take/have collocations of a calm money conversation. 跟着一对夫妻讨论租房还是买房，学一场平心静气谈钱的对话里 make、do、take、have 的搭配。

B2 · CELPIP 7–8⏱ 20 minV01 Wrong collocation built word by word逐词直译的错搭配V10 Idioms and fixed chunks known but not used惯用语块认得但不会用

📖 Key card知识卡
Renting and buying are both risky in different ways; the useful question is what would make a choice safe enough for you.租和买各有各的风险；有用的问题是：什么条件能让这个选择对你来说足够稳妥。

Read and listen读与听

307 words
Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Li: My parents called again. They keep saying renting is throwing money away, and that we should buy as soon as we can. Daniel: I know. My mum says the same thing. But "as soon as we can" would mean a tiny condo an hour from work, or a mortgage that takes most of what we earn.

莉：我爸妈又打电话来了。他们一直说租房就是把钱往外扔，让我们能买就赶紧买。丹尼尔：我知道，我妈也这么说。可「能买就赶紧买」意味着离公司一小时车程的一间小公寓，或者一笔吃掉我们大部分收入的房贷。

Li: Then let's not make a decision tonight. We need to do research first, properly this time. If we bought now, what would we actually give up? Daniel: Our savings, mostly. The down payment would take nearly everything we have, and we would have nothing left for emergencies. And we would be stuck in one place just when my job might move.

莉：那今晚就别做决定了。我们得先好好研究一下，这次认真点。如果现在买，我们实际上要放弃什么？丹尼尔：主要是积蓄。首付几乎要用掉我们所有的钱，遇到急事就一分不剩了。而且偏偏在我的工作可能要调动的时候，我们会被困在一个地方。

Li: But rent keeps going up. We have been priced out of two neighbourhoods already. At least with a mortgage you know where you stand. Daniel: That's fair. Rising rent is a real risk, and we should take it into account. So is the interest rate going up when you renew. Neither option is safe. They are just different kinds of risk.

莉：可房租一直在涨，我们已经被挤出两个社区了。有房贷至少心里有数。丹尼尔：有道理。房租上涨是实实在在的风险，我们应该考虑进去。可续贷时利率上涨也是风险。哪个选择都不保险，只是风险不一样。

Li: OK. So what would make us comfortable buying? Daniel: A proper emergency fund on top of the down payment. A place we could still afford if one of us lost a job for a few months. And a commute we could live with for years, not just until we got tired of it.

莉：好吧。那要满足什么条件，我们才能安心买？丹尼尔：首付之外还要有一笔像样的应急金；就算我们俩有一个人几个月没工作，也还供得起；还有一段能忍好多年的通勤，而不是忍到烦了为止。

Li: That sounds sensible. I'll have a word with my parents. My dad still talks about the detached house they bought when everything was cheaper, but I think they will feel better if they know we have a plan, even if the plan is "not yet". Daniel: And let's take a break from looking at listings this weekend. Every time we scroll through them, we end up more anxious and no wiser.

莉：听起来挺有道理。我去跟爸妈聊聊。我爸还总提他们在什么都便宜的年代买的那栋独立屋，不过我想，只要他们知道我们有计划，哪怕计划是「还没到时候」，他们也会安心些。丹尼尔：还有，这个周末我们先别看房源了。每次刷完，我们都更焦虑，却一点也没更明白。

Words词

/ˈmɔːɡɪdʒ/ n.
房贷；按揭
take out a mortgage; pay off a mortgage; renew a mortgage
They took out a mortgage when they bought the flat. 他们买公寓时办了房贷。
/ˈkɒndəʊ/ n.
公寓（产权单元）
buy a condo; a one-bedroom condo
A condo downtown can cost more than a house in the suburbs. 市中心的公寓可能比郊区的独立屋还贵。
/θrəʊ ˈmʌni əˌweɪ/ v. phr.
把钱白白扔掉
throw your money away on sth
Buying cheap shoes that break is throwing money away. 买一穿就坏的便宜鞋等于白扔钱。
/weə juː ˈstænd/ phr.
自己的处境；心里有数
know where you stand; tell sb where they stand
After the meeting, at least I knew where I stood. 开完会，我至少心里有数了。
/ˈɪntrəst reɪt/ n.
利率
a high interest rate; interest rates go up
When interest rates rise, borrowing costs more. 利率上升，借钱的成本就更高。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

Rising rent is a real risk, and we should take it into account. So is the interest rate going up when you renew.
take it into account: the object it goes in the middle. So is … = that is also a risk; the verb comes before the subject after so.
take it into account：宾语 it 放中间。So is …＝那也是风险；so 开头时动词放在主语前。
Every time we scroll through them, we end up more anxious and no wiser.
every time works like whenever. no wiser = not understanding any more than before; a fixed, slightly humorous phrase.
every time 相当于 whenever。no wiser＝一点也没更明白，是个固定说法，略带幽默。
