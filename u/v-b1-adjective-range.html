📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Open the full explanation: 5 points展开细讲：5 段讲解

English has two kinds of adjectives: gradable ones like good, big and tired, and strong (extreme) ones like excellent, huge and exhausted. Gradable adjectives take 'very', 'quite' and 'really'. Strong adjectives already mean 'very good' or 'very big', so they take 'absolutely', 'really' or 'utterly' instead.

英语形容词分为两类：可分级的（如 good、big、tired）和强势/极端的（如 excellent、huge、exhausted）。可分级形容词可以用 very、quite、really 修饰；强势形容词本身已经包含“非常”的意思，所以要搭配 absolutely、really、utterly 等，而不用 very。

A very common mistake among Chinese-speaking learners is to say 'very excellent' or 'very wonderful'. The Chinese pattern of adding a degree word before almost any adjective is transferred directly into English. In English, the stronger the adjective, the less it needs a degree word, and 'very' clashes with it.

华人学习者常说 very excellent、very wonderful，这是把中文里“很＋形容词”的万能结构直接搬到英语里。在英语中，形容词越强，越不需要程度副词，very 与它们是冲突的。

Choose the adjective that matches what you are praising. Use 'delicious' for food, 'gorgeous' or 'stunning' for looks and scenery, 'brilliant' for ideas and clever people, and 'superb' or 'outstanding' for skill and performance. Precise choices make you sound more fluent than repeating 'great' every time.

要根据所称赞的对象选词：食物用 delicious，外貌和风景用 gorgeous 或 stunning，想法和聪明的人用 brilliant，技能和表现用 superb 或 outstanding。精准选词比反复说 great 更显得流利。

Register matters. 'Fantastic', 'terrific' and 'awesome' are warm and informal, good for conversation. 'Outstanding', 'exceptional' and 'superb' are more formal and suit reports, references and reviews. 'Brilliant' is very common in British English for anything impressive, even in casual speech.

语体也很重要：fantastic、terrific、awesome 比较随和、口语化，适合聊天；outstanding、exceptional、superb 更正式，适合报告、推荐信和评价；brilliant 在英式英语中非常常用，口语里也可以表示“太棒了”。

Do not stack strong adjectives or overuse them. If every sentence contains 'amazing' or 'incredible', the words lose their force. Save the strongest words for things that really impress you, and use a simple 'good' or 'pretty good' for ordinary things.

不要堆叠或滥用强势形容词。如果每句话都有 amazing 或 incredible，这些词就失去了力量。把最强的词留给真正令你印象深刻的事物，普通的事情用 good 或 pretty good 就行。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ The concert last night was very excellent.
✓ The concert last night was absolutely excellent.
Excellent is a strong adjective that already means 'very good', so it cannot take 'very'. Use 'absolutely' or 'really' instead, or just say 'excellent' alone.
excellent 是强势形容词，本身已有“非常好”的意思，不能用 very 修饰。可以用 absolutely、really，或者直接说 excellent。
✗ The view from the hotel room was very gorgeous.
✓ The view from the hotel room was absolutely gorgeous.
Learners copy the Chinese habit of putting a degree word before every adjective. With gorgeous, stunning and similar words, English prefers 'absolutely', 'really' or no modifier at all.
学习者把中文里每个形容词前都加“很”的习惯搬了过来。gorgeous、stunning 这类词在英语里应搭配 absolutely、really，或者不加修饰语。
✗ This soup is delicious very.
✓ This soup is really delicious.
Degree words come before the adjective in English, never after it. Learners sometimes place the degree word at the end because they are thinking of the word order of a Chinese comment.
英语中程度副词放在形容词前面，不能放在后面。有些学习者受中文评价句语序影响，把程度词放到了句末。
✗ Her performance was a brilliant, amazing, fantastic and incredible.
✓ Her performance was absolutely brilliant.
Stacking several strong adjectives sounds unnatural and weakens each one. Choose the single best word for the situation and let it carry the meaning.
堆叠多个强势形容词听起来不自然，还会削弱每个词的力度。选一个最合适的词，让它独立表达意思即可。
✗ The meal was fantastic, but it was very expensive, so it was a perfect meal for me.
✓ The meal was fantastic, but it was very expensive, so it was not perfect for me.
Perfect is an absolute adjective, so it cannot logically be both true and qualified by a clear drawback. When there is a problem, choose a gradable word such as good, or say it was not perfect.
perfect 是绝对性形容词，如果有明显缺点就不能再说完美。有问题时应选 good 这类可分级的词，或者说 not perfect。
