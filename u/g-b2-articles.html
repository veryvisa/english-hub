Articles: a, the or nothing 冠词：a、the 还是不用

📘 In your coursebook: English File 5e · Upper-Intermediate · 10B “Freedom of speech”. Use the texts and recordings with your coursebook; this page adds new words, grammar and practice.教材定位：English File 5e · Upper-Intermediate · 10B《Freedom of speech》。课文与录音请配合教材学习，本页补充新词、语法与练习。

Decide between a, the and no article by asking two questions, in the order a native speaker's ear asks them. 用两个问题决定 a、the 还是不用冠词，顺序和母语者的语感一致。

I'm looking for a job. 我在找工作。
Any job; the listener cannot know which.随便哪份工作，对方无从确定。
I'm looking for the job I saw online yesterday. 我在找昨天网上看到的那份工作。
One job, pinned down by the clause after it.特定的一份，被后面的从句限定。
I'm looking for work. 我在找活干。
work is uncountable: no article at all.work 不可数，不加冠词。

📏 The rule规则

📏 The rule in one line规则一句话
Ask first: can my listener tell exactly which one I mean? If yes, the. If no, ask: is it one countable thing? If yes, a/an. Otherwise use no article.先问：对方能不能确定我指的是哪一个？能，用 the。不能，再问：是不是一个可数的东西？是，用 a/an；否则不用冠词。

Chinese has no articles, so the choice feels like decoration. In English it carries information. I saw a doctor introduces someone new. I saw the doctor tells the listener they already know which one, usually the family doctor.

中文没有冠词，所以容易觉得它是装饰。在英语里它带信息：I saw a doctor 是引入一个新的人；I saw the doctor 是告诉对方「你知道是哪位」，通常指家庭医生。

A thing becomes identifiable in four ways: it was mentioned before; there is only one in the situation (the kitchen, the manager); a phrase after the noun pins it down (the email you sent); or the whole world shares it (the sun, the internet).

一个东西变得「可确定」有四种途径：前面提过；情境里只有一个（厨房、经理）；名词后面的短语把它限定了（你发的那封邮件）；全世界共用一个（太阳、互联网）。

When you speak about things in general, use a plural or an uncountable noun with no article: Dogs need exercise. Life is short. Housing is expensive. Adding the turns the statement into one about a particular group: The dogs next door need exercise.

泛指一类东西时，用复数或不可数名词，不加冠词。加上 the，句子就变成在说特定的一群：隔壁那几条狗需要运动。

A singular countable noun can almost never stand alone. It needs a, the, my, this or a number in front of it. This single check removes most article mistakes in writing: find every singular countable noun and see whether something stands before it.

单数可数名词几乎不能光着出现，前面必须有 a、the、my、this 或数词。写完后只做这一项检查就能改掉大部分冠词错误：找出每个单数可数名词，看它前面有没有东西。

Some fixed patterns are worth learning as chunks: go to work / school / bed, by bus, at home, have breakfast take no article; play the piano, in the morning, the same, the first take the; jobs take a when many people hold them: She is an engineer.

一些固定格式当作整块记：go to work、by bus、at home、have breakfast 不加冠词；play the piano、in the morning、the same、the first 用 the；很多人都能做的职业前用 a。

A few limits are worth knowing. A role that only one person holds can drop the article after be, become or elect: She is head of finance. A singular with the can also stand for a whole class, mostly for inventions and species: The bicycle changed how women travelled. And North American speakers often say play guitar, play piano with no article.

有几处例外值得知道。只有一个人担任的职务，在 be、become、elect 后可以不加冠词：She is head of finance。the 加单数也能代表整类，多用于发明和物种：自行车改变了女性的出行方式。另外北美口语常说 play guitar、play piano，不加 the。

Examples例句

  • I bought a jacket and a pair of boots. The jacket was on sale. 我买了一件外套和一双靴子。外套在打折。
    First mention a; second mention the.第一次提用 a，第二次提用 the。
  • Could you pass the salt? 能把盐递给我吗？
    There is one salt shaker on the table.桌上就那一瓶盐。
  • The advice you gave me was really useful. 你给我的建议很有用。
    advice is uncountable, but you gave me identifies it.advice 不可数，但 you gave me 把它限定了。
  • She gave me some good advice. 她给了我一些好建议。
    Uncountable and not identified: no a.不可数、未确定：不能用 a。
  • Winters in Winnipeg are long. 温尼伯的冬天很长。
    A general statement with a plural noun.用复数名词泛指。
  • My sister is a nurse at the hospital near our house. 我姐姐在我们家附近那家医院当护士。
    A job takes a; the hospital is pinned down by near our house.职业用 a；医院被 near our house 限定。
  • Society has changed, and the society we grew up in no longer exists. 社会变了，我们成长于其中的那个社会已经不存在了。
    General idea, then one particular society.先泛指，再特指。
  • The bus was late, so I took a taxi. 公交晚点了，我就打了辆出租车。
    The bus I always take; any taxi would do.公交是我每天坐的那班；出租车随便哪辆都行。
  • We need a plumber. Do you know the one who fixed the Lees' sink? 我们得找个水暖工。给李家修水槽的那位你认识吗？
    Any plumber first, then one particular person.先是随便一个，再指特定的一位。
  • There's a problem with the invoice you sent on Friday. 你周五发来的那张发票有个问题。
    The problem is new information; the invoice is pinned down.问题是新信息；发票已被限定。
  • News travels fast in a small office. 小办公室里消息传得快。
    news is uncountable and general; the office is any small one.news 不可数、泛指；办公室是随便哪个小办公室。
  • She became manager at thirty-one. 她三十一岁当上了经理。
    A single role can drop the article after become.become 后表示唯一职务时可不加冠词。
  • The smartphone has changed how we read. 智能手机改变了我们的阅读方式。
    One singular noun with the stands for the whole class.the 加单数代表整类事物。
  • He's in hospital, and his sister works at the hospital on Oak Street. 他住院了，他姐姐在橡树街那家医院上班。
    in hospital (as a patient) has no article in British English; North Americans say in the hospital.英式英语表示住院不加冠词；北美说 in the hospital。

More pairs: same idea, different choice更多对比句组

Dinner is at seven. 晚饭七点开。
The daily meal as a routine.作为日常活动的一餐。
The dinner we had on Friday was superb. 周五那顿晚饭好极了。
One particular meal both people remember.双方都记得的那一顿。
We were invited to a dinner for new staff. 我们受邀参加一场新员工晚宴。
A formal event, mentioned for the first time.一场正式活动，第一次提到。
I had a coffee with Priya this morning. 今早我和普丽娅喝了杯咖啡。
One cup: a drink is counted as a portion.一杯：饮料按份数算。
Coffee keeps me awake. 咖啡让我睡不着。
The drink in general.泛指咖啡这种饮料。
The coffee at that place is too bitter for me. 那家的咖啡对我来说太苦了。
One particular coffee, pinned down by at that place.特定的咖啡，被 at that place 限定。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ I bought car last month.
✓ I bought a car last month.
A singular countable noun cannot stand alone.
单数可数名词不能光着出现。
✗ The life is difficult for the newcomers.
✓ Life is difficult for newcomers.
General statements take no article.
泛指不加冠词。
✗ Can you give me an advice?
✓ Can you give me some advice?
advice, information, furniture, equipment and news are uncountable.
advice、information、furniture、equipment、news 都不可数。
✗ He is engineer in a tech company.
✓ He is an engineer at a tech company.
Jobs take a/an.
职业前用 a/an。
✗ I had the lunch with my manager.
✓ I had lunch with my manager.
Meals as routine events take no article.
一日三餐作为日常活动时不加冠词。
