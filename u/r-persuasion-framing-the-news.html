The Power of Framing the News 新闻框架的力量

Two news reports can describe the same event and leave readers with completely different impressions. Imagine a city introducing a new public transport policy. One headline announces that the government is investing in cleaner and more efficient travel. Another warns that commuters will face higher costs and fewer parking spaces. Both reports may contain accurate information, yet their framing encourages different emotional responses.

两篇新闻报道可以描述同一件事，却给读者留下完全不同的印象。设想某座城市正在推行一项新的公共交通政策。一篇新闻标题宣布，政府正在投资更清洁、更高效的出行方式。另一篇则警告，通勤者将面临更高的成本和更少的停车位。这两篇报道可能都包含准确的信息，但它们采用的呈现框架会引发不同的情绪反应。

Framing refers to the selection and presentation of particular aspects of an issue. Journalists cannot include every available detail, so they make decisions about which facts deserve attention. These choices are not necessarily dishonest. However, repeated emphasis on certain consequences can make them appear more important than others. Over time, audiences may begin to understand a complicated issue through a narrow perspective.

框架效应是指对某个问题的特定方面进行选择和呈现。记者不可能纳入所有可获得的细节，因此必须决定哪些事实值得关注。这些选择未必意味着不诚实。然而，反复强调某些后果，可能使它们看起来比其他后果更加重要。随着时间推移，受众可能开始通过狭窄的视角理解复杂问题。

Although journalists have a responsibility to report significant developments, the pressure to attract attention can influence editorial decisions. Headlines often highlight conflict, uncertainty or personal consequences because such elements are likely to generate clicks. While these techniques can make important stories accessible, they may also simplify complex debates. A dramatic headline is therefore not a reliable summary of the complete report.

尽管记者有责任报道重要进展，但吸引关注的压力可能影响编辑决策。标题经常突出冲突、不确定性或个人后果，因为这些元素可能带来点击量。虽然这些技巧能够让重要新闻更容易引起关注，但也可能简化复杂的争论。因此，戏剧性的标题并不能可靠地概括整篇报道。

Media analysts increasingly encourage readers to compare coverage across different sources. They recommend examining which facts are included, which perspectives are missing and whether the headline accurately represents the article. Readers should also distinguish between factual reporting and interpretation. On the whole, understanding framing does not require rejecting the media. It requires recognising that every act of presentation involves choices.

媒体分析人士越来越鼓励读者比较不同来源的报道。他们建议读者检查哪些事实被纳入、哪些观点被遗漏，以及标题是否准确反映文章内容。读者还应该区分事实报道与观点解读。总体而言，理解框架效应并不意味着要否定媒体，而是要认识到，每一种呈现方式都包含一定的选择。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

These choices are not necessarily dishonest.
The adverb necessarily modifies the adjective dishonest and softens the claim by showing that something is not automatically true.
副词 necessarily 修饰形容词 dishonest，通过表示某事并非必然如此来弱化判断。
A dramatic headline is therefore not a reliable summary of the complete report.
The linking adverb therefore introduces a logical conclusion, while the noun phrase a dramatic headline functions as the subject.
连接副词 therefore 引出逻辑结论，而名词短语 a dramatic headline 在句中充当主语。
