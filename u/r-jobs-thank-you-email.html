Two Emails After the Interview 面试之后的两封邮件

🧠 Learn the 4 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 4 个新词

Read two real-sounding emails, a thank-you note and a reply to a job offer, and learn how to raise a difficult point politely in writing. 读两封像真的一样的邮件：一封面试感谢信、一封对录用通知的回复，学会在书面里礼貌地提出难开口的事。

B2 · CELPIP 7–8⏱ 20 minV06 Register: too formal, too casual, or too blunt语域不合：太正式、太随意或太冲V04 Phrasal verbs avoided; the formal verb sounds stiff回避短语动词，说话太书面

📖 Key card知识卡
After an interview, write short and specific; when you answer an offer, give a reason, not a feeling, and leave the door open.面试后的邮件要短而具体；回复录用通知时，讲理由而不是讲感受，并且给对方留出商量的余地。

Read and listen读与听

296 words
Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Daniel had two emails to write in one week, and both made him nervous. The first went out the evening after his interview. The second came a week later, when the job offer arrived and the salary was lower than he had hoped. Here they are, with the notes that a friend who works as a recruiter gave him.

丹尼尔一周里要写两封邮件，两封都让他紧张。第一封在面试当晚发出。第二封在一周后，录用通知到了，工资比他期望的低。下面就是这两封信，附上他一位做招聘的朋友给的批注。

"Dear Ms Grant, thank you for taking the time to meet me today. I especially enjoyed hearing how your team set up the new reporting system, and it confirmed how much I would like to take on that kind of project. As promised, I have attached the sample report I mentioned. I look forward to hearing from you. Best regards, Daniel." The note: short, specific, sent the same day. One detail from the interview proves you were listening to the interviewer, not waiting for your turn.

「格兰特女士您好：感谢您今天抽时间与我面谈。我特别喜欢听您讲团队如何搭建新的报表系统，这也让我更确定自己很想承担这类项目。按约定，附上我提到的那份报告样本。期待您的回音。此致，丹尼尔。」批注：短、具体、当天发。提一个面试里的细节，证明你是在听面试官说话，而不是在等着轮到自己。

"Dear Ms Grant, thank you for the offer. I am very pleased and would like to accept the role. Before I sign, could I bring up the salary? Given the responsibilities we discussed and the salary range for similar roles, I was hoping for something closer to the top of the range. Is there any flexibility on this? I am happy to talk it through by phone." The note: one question, one reason, an open door. He did not write "I want more money", and he did not apologise.

「格兰特女士您好：感谢您的录用。我很高兴，也愿意接受这个职位。签字之前，我能谈一下薪资吗？考虑到我们谈过的职责，以及同类职位的薪资范围，我原本希望接近范围的上端。这方面有没有商量的余地？我很乐意电话里细谈。」批注：一个问题、一个理由、一扇开着的门。他没写「我想要更多钱」，也没道歉。

The hiring manager called the next morning. She could not move the salary much, but she offered an earlier pay review and a later start date, which he needed anyway. Daniel says the follow-up after the interview was the easier email, but the second one taught him more: when he pointed out a reason instead of a feeling, the conversation stayed friendly.

第二天一早，用人经理打来电话。工资她没法大调，但提出提前做一次薪资评估，并把入职日期往后推，这正好是他需要的。丹尼尔说，面试后的跟进邮件好写，第二封教给他的更多：当他讲出理由而不是情绪，谈话就一直是友好的。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

I especially enjoyed hearing how your team set up the new reporting system, and it confirmed how much I would like to take on that kind of project.
enjoy + -ing. Two embedded clauses keep statement word order: how your team set up…, how much I would like….
enjoy 后接 -ing。两个嵌入从句都用陈述语序：how your team set up…、how much I would like…。
Given the responsibilities we discussed and the salary range for similar roles, I was hoping for something closer to the top of the range.
Given = considering. I was hoping (past continuous) is softer than I hope.
Given 意为「考虑到」。I was hoping（过去进行时）比 I hope 更委婉。
