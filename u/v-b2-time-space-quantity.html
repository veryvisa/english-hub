📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Open the full explanation: 5 points展开细讲：5 段讲解

English distinguishes between points in time and periods of time. Expressions such as 'at a specific time', 'over a period of time', and 'within a certain time frame' serve different purposes. Chinese learners sometimes use 'in' and 'for' interchangeably, but 'for' usually introduces a duration, while 'in' can describe how long something takes to complete.

英语区分时间点和时间段。at a specific time、over a period of time 和 within a certain time frame 分别用于表达不同的时间概念。中国学习者有时会混淆 in 和 for，但 for 通常引出持续时间，而 in 可以表示完成某件事所花费的时间。

Distance and dimensions are often expressed through fixed measurement patterns. 'A distance of 10 kilometres' and '10 kilometres away' are both correct, but they fit different sentence structures. Similarly, 'in length', 'in width', and 'in height' describe individual dimensions, while 'the overall size' refers to the general scale of something.

距离和尺寸通常使用固定的度量结构。a distance of 10 kilometres 和 10 kilometres away 都正确，但适用于不同的句型。同样，in length、in width 和 in height 分别描述具体尺寸，而 the overall size 表示整体大小。

Speed and quantity often appear in comparisons. 'Twice as fast as', 'half the size of', and 'three times the amount' are useful patterns. Remember that English uses 'as ... as' for equal comparisons and 'than' after comparative adjectives. Avoid combining both structures unnecessarily.

速度和数量经常出现在比较句中。twice as fast as、half the size of 和 three times the amount 都是实用表达。英语使用 as ... as 表示同等程度的比较，比较级形容词后则使用 than。不要不必要地将两种结构混用。

Measurement expressions are not always interchangeable with ordinary nouns. 'Amount' generally refers to something uncountable, while 'number' refers to countable items. 'A large amount of water' and 'a large number of people' are natural combinations. Choosing the wrong expression can make an otherwise understandable sentence sound unnatural.

度量表达并不总能与普通名词随意互换。amount 通常用于不可数事物，而 number 用于可数事物。a large amount of water 和 a large number of people 都是自然搭配。即使句子能够被理解，选错表达也会使英语听起来不地道。

English often uses compact measurement phrases instead of longer explanations. For example, 'a 20-minute drive', 'a five-kilometre walk', and 'a two-hour meeting' place the measurement directly before the noun. Notice that the measurement normally remains singular when used as a compound adjective.

英语经常使用简洁的度量短语，而不是较长的解释。例如，a 20-minute drive、a five-kilometre walk 和 a two-hour meeting 都把度量信息直接放在名词前面。注意，作为复合形容词时，度量单位通常使用单数形式。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ I have lived in Canada since five years.
✓ I have lived in Canada for five years.
'For' introduces a period of time, while 'since' introduces the starting point. Use 'for five years' but 'since 2021'.
for 后面接持续时间，since 后面接起始时间点。应该说 for five years（五年），但说 since 2021（从2021年开始）。
✗ The station is 10 kilometres far from here.
✓ The station is 10 kilometres away from here.
'Far' and 'away' express distance differently. Use '10 kilometres away' or 'far from here'. Do not combine a specific distance with 'far' in this structure.
far 和 away 都可以表达距离，但句型不同。可以说 10 kilometres away，也可以说 far from here。使用具体距离时，不要在这个结构中额外加入 far。
✗ This box is twice bigger than that one.
✓ This box is twice as big as that one.
For a multiple comparison, use 'twice as + adjective + as'. Another correct option is 'twice the size of'. Avoid putting 'twice' directly before a comparative adjective such as 'bigger' in this construction.
表示倍数比较时，使用 twice as + 形容词 + as，也可以说 twice the size of。不要在这种结构中直接把 twice 放在 bigger 等比较级形容词前面。
✗ The number of water we need is increasing.
✓ The amount of water we need is increasing.
'Water' is uncountable, so 'amount' is the appropriate word. Use 'number' with countable plural nouns, such as 'the number of bottles'.
water 是不可数名词，因此应使用 amount。number 用于可数名词复数，例如 the number of bottles（瓶子的数量）。
✗ It is a 30-minutes drive to the airport.
✓ It is a 30-minute drive to the airport.
When a measurement forms part of a compound adjective before a noun, the unit is normally singular. Write 'a 30-minute drive', not 'a 30-minutes drive'.
度量单位构成复合形容词并放在名词前面时，通常使用单数形式。应该写 a 30-minute drive，而不是 a 30-minutes drive。
✗ The car is driving with a speed of 80 kilometres per hour.
✓ The car is travelling at a speed of 80 kilometres per hour.
The natural collocation is 'at a speed of'. English uses 'at' to introduce a particular speed, rather than 'with' in this expression.
表示以某个速度行驶时，固定搭配是 at a speed of。英语在这个表达中使用 at，而不是 with。
