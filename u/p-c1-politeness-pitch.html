Sounding polite: pitch range 听起来有礼貌：音高范围

Sound polite and friendly through pitch range and tune, not only through polite words, so that requests, refusals and complaints land the way you intend. 靠音域和语调显得礼貌友好，而不只靠客气的词，让请求、拒绝和投诉达到你想要的效果。

C1 · CELPIP 9+⏱ 25 minP10 Flat or wrong intonation; the important word is not stressed语调平，重点词不突出V06 Register: too formal, too casual, or too blunt语域不合：太正式、太随意或太冲

📏 The rule规则

📏 The point in one line一句话要领
In English, a wide pitch range (starting higher, moving more) sounds engaged and polite; a narrow, low range sounds bored, cold or annoyed, even with please and could you. Polite requests usually start higher and end with a gentle fall or a rise, not a short flat drop.英语里音域宽（起得高、动得多）显得投入有礼；音域窄而低显得无聊、冷淡或不耐烦，即使用了 please 和 could you。礼貌请求通常起得较高，以平缓的降调或升调收尾，而不是短促平板的下沉。
  • Easy to miss易漏 ✗ Could you please check my bill. (low, flat, short drop) → ✓ Could you please check my BILL? (higher start, gentle movement)

Could you please check my bill? /kʊdʒu ˈpliːz ˈtʃek maɪ ˈbɪl/
Start higher on Could; rise or gentle fall on BILL. Could 起高，BILL 上升或缓降。
Would you mind sending it again? /wʊdʒu ˈmaɪnd ˈsendɪŋ ɪt əˈɡen/
End with a rise: room for the other person. 句末上扬，给对方留余地。
Sorry to bother you. /ˈsɒri tə ˈbɒðə ju/
High start on SOR, wide fall. SOR 起高，宽幅下降。
I'm afraid we can't do that today... /aɪm əˈfreɪd wi ˈkɑːnt duː ðæt təˈdeɪ/
Fall-rise on TODAY: regret, not a wall. TODAY 上降升：带歉意，不是一堵墙。
Thanks so much for your help! /ˈθæŋks səʊ ˈmʌtʃ fə jə ˈhelp/
Wide range sounds sincere. 音域宽才显得真诚。
Sure, no problem! /ˈʃɔː nəʊ ˈprɒbləm/
High fall on PROB: willing. PROB 上高降：乐意。
Sure. /ˈʃɔː/
Low and short: may sound reluctant. Compare. 低而短：可能显得勉强。对比练。
Excuse me, is this seat free? /ɪkˈskjuːz mi ɪz ðɪs siːt ˈfriː/
Rise on FREE; higher start. FREE 上升，起点高一些。
I was hoping you could help me with this. /aɪ wəz ˈhəʊpɪŋ ju kʊd ˈhelp mi wɪð ðɪs/
Past tense plus a wide range softens it. 过去时加宽音域更柔和。
I've been waiting for about forty minutes. /aɪv bɪn ˈweɪtɪŋ fər əˈbaʊt ˈfɔːti ˈmɪnɪts/
Firm but calm: wide range, steady pace. 坚定而平静：音域宽、语速稳。
Do you think you could have a look at it? /də ju ˈθɪŋk ju kʊd hæv ə ˈlʊk ət ɪt/
A soft rise at the end. 句末轻柔上扬。
That would be wonderful, thank you. /ðæt wʊd bi ˈwʌndəfəl ˈθæŋk ju/
Big movement on WON. WON 上幅度大。
Could you say that again, please? /kʊdʒu ˈseɪ ðæt əˈɡen pliːz/
Rise on GAIN; not a flat demand. GAIN 上升，别平平地要求。
I'm not sure that's quite right. /aɪm nɒt ˈʃɔː ðæts kwaɪt ˈraɪt/
Fall-rise on RIGHT: polite disagreement. RIGHT 上降升：委婉的不同意。
Let me check and get back to you. /let mi ˈtʃek ən ɡet ˈbæk tə ju/
Friendly rise-and-fall, not a low monotone. 有起伏，别用低平的单调。
No worries at all! /nəʊ ˈwʌriz ət ˈɔːl/
High start, wide fall on ALL. 起高，ALL 上宽降。

📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Open the full explanation: 5 points展开细讲：5 段讲解

A customer calls a phone company and says, word for word, "Could you please check my bill." Every word is polite. But the voice stays low and flat and drops at the end, and the agent replies coolly, as if to a complaint. Later the customer says the agent was rude. Both heard the same thing: a narrow, low tune. In English that tune signals displeasure, and the listener reacts to it before the words.

客户打电话，一字一句都很客气，可声音一直低平、句末下沉，接线员就冷淡地回应，像在应付投诉；事后客户说对方没礼貌。双方听到的是同一样东西：窄而低的语调。英语里这种调表示不满，听者先对调作出反应，再听词。

Many Mandarin speakers keep a narrower overall range in English than native speakers do, partly because pitch is busy with tones on each syllable and partly because a calm, even voice is valued as polite. In English the cultural reading is reversed: an even, quiet voice can sound distant or unimpressed, and a voice that moves up and down sounds friendly and interested. Neither is better; but if you want to be heard as polite in English, you need the English signal.

很多以普通话为母语的人说英语时整体音域比母语者窄：一是音高忙着处理每个字的声调，二是平稳的声音在中文里被视为礼貌。英语的解读恰好相反：平稳低声可能显得疏远或不以为然，有起伏的声音才显得友好、感兴趣。两者没有高下，但要在英语里被听成礼貌，就得用英语的信号。

Three practical moves. Start higher: begin a request a little above your usual speaking pitch (Could you... ). Move on the key word: let the nucleus have a clear fall or rise (check my BILL). And for requests that might be a burden, end with a rise or a fall-rise, which leaves room for the other person: Would you mind sending it again↗? A short, low fall makes the same words sound like an order.

三个做法：起得高一点；在关键词上明显升或降；对可能给人添麻烦的请求，用升调或降升调收尾，给对方留余地。短促的低降会把同样的词变成命令。

Refusals and complaints need the most care. A refusal with a fall-rise and a softener sounds regretful: I'm afraid we can't do that today... The same words with a flat low fall sound final and unfriendly. When you complain, keep your range wide and your pace calm: I've been waiting for about forty minutes, and I was hoping you could help. A narrow, loud voice sounds angry; a wide, steady one sounds firm but fair.

拒绝和投诉最要小心。降升调加缓和语，拒绝听起来带歉意；同样的话用低平降调就显得生硬。投诉时保持音域宽、语速稳：窄而响显得生气，宽而稳显得坚定但讲理。

In listening, range tells you how the other person feels before they say it. If a colleague's Sure is low and short, they may not be happy with the request; ask a quick follow-up (Is that OK for you?). If a service agent's voice rises and moves, they are engaged and you can ask for more.

听力上，音域在对方开口表态之前就透露了感受。同事的 Sure 又低又短，可能并不乐意，追问一句；客服的声音有起伏，说明对方投入，可以多提要求。

✅ Check yourself测一测

✍️ Use it用起来

Write or say your answer. It is saved only in this browser.写下或说出你的答案；只存在这个浏览器里。

Record three short calls you might really make: a request (ask someone to resend a document), a refusal (say you can't attend a meeting), and a complaint (a delivery is late). Record each twice, once in your usual voice and once with a higher start and wider range. Listen back a day later and decide honestly which version you would rather receive.
录三段你可能真会打的电话：请求、拒绝、投诉。每段录两遍，一遍用平时的声音，一遍起点更高、音域更宽。隔一天回听，诚实地选出你更愿意接到的那一版。
