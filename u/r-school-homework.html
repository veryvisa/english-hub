The Homework Argument 作业之争

🧠 Learn the 5 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 5 个新词

Read a father's opinion on homework and see how unreal conditionals help him weigh two choices. 读一位父亲对作业的看法，看非真实条件句怎样帮他权衡两种选择。

B2 · CELPIP 7–8⏱ 20 minG07 Unreal conditionals and wish虚拟条件句与 wishV12 Hedging is missing; claims sound absolute不会留余地，话说得太满

📖 Key card知识卡
For young children, more homework is not the same as more learning; reading, sleep and curiosity matter more.对年幼的孩子来说，作业多不等于学得多；阅读、睡眠和好奇心更重要。

Read and listen读与听

250 words
Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Every September the same argument starts in our house. My wife thinks our daughter gets too little homework. I used to agree. In Grade 3 she had one sheet a week, and some evenings she had nothing at all. Where we grew up, children of eight spent two hours at a desk after school.

每年九月，我们家都会开始同一场争论。我太太觉得女儿的作业太少。我以前也这么想。三年级时她一周只有一张练习纸，有些晚上什么作业都没有。在我们长大的地方，八岁的孩子放学后要在书桌前坐两个小时。

If the school gave three hours a night, would she learn more? Maybe a little, for a while. But I have changed my mind. The studies I have read suggest that for young children the link between homework time and results is weak. What seems to matter more is reading at home, enough sleep, and a child who still likes learning by the age of twelve.

如果学校每晚布置三个小时，她会学得更多吗？也许会多一点，维持一阵子。但我已经改变了看法。我读过的研究显示，对年幼的孩子来说，作业时长和成绩之间的关联很弱。似乎更重要的是在家阅读、睡眠充足，以及一个到了十二岁还喜欢学习的孩子。

I also look at what she does with her free evenings. She reads, she builds things, she plays outside with the neighbours' kids, and she is making progress in English faster than we are. If she were sitting at a desk all evening, she would not have those friends. I wish I had understood this in her first year, instead of buying workbooks she hated.

我也看看她把空闲的晚上用在了哪里。她看书，搭东西，和邻居家孩子在外面玩，英语进步得比我们都快。如果她整晚坐在书桌前，就不会有这些朋友。真希望她上学第一年我就明白这一点，而不是去买那些她讨厌的练习册。

None of this means doing nothing. If she falls behind, we will add practice, and we follow her teacher's advice closely. At our table, praise for effort works better than comparing her with her cousin's rank in class.

这一切不等于什么都不做。如果她落后了，我们会加练习，也会认真照老师的建议做。在我们家的饭桌上，表扬努力比拿她和表姐的班级排名比较更管用。

My wife is not fully convinced. But we have agreed on one rule: no homework fights after eight. If only we had made that rule two years ago.

我太太并没有完全被说服。但我们约定了一条规矩：八点以后不为作业吵架。要是两年前就定下这条规矩就好了。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

If she were sitting at a desk all evening, she would not have those friends.
Unreal present with a continuous verb: were sitting in the if-clause, would not have in the result.
与现在事实相反，条件句用进行体 were sitting，结果句用 would not have。
I wish I had understood this in her first year, instead of buying workbooks she hated.
wish + past perfect for regret; instead of + -ing.
wish 加过去完成时表遗憾；instead of 后接 -ing。
