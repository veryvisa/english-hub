Do You Really Need 10,000 Steps? 真的要走一万步吗？

🧠 Learn the 5 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 5 个新词

Read a news piece about the 10,000-step target and practise reading numbers and the articles around them. 读一则关于一万步目标的新闻，练读数字以及数字周围的冠词。

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

If you wear a fitness watch, you probably know the target: 10,000 steps a day. It sounds scientific. In fact, the number seems to have started as marketing. It is usually traced to a pedometer sold in Japan in the 1960s, whose name meant roughly "10,000-step meter". It was a good round number, easy to remember. Nobody had tested it.

戴运动手表的人大概都知道那个目标：每天一万步。听起来很科学，其实这个数字似乎源于营销。人们通常把它追溯到 1960 年代日本卖的一款计步器，名字大意是「万步计」。这是个好记的整数，但当时没人检验过它。

Since then, researchers have tested it. A large analysis published in 2022 pooled data from fifteen studies and nearly fifty thousand adults. More steps were linked to a lower risk of early death, but the benefit levelled off. For people over sixty, the curve flattened at roughly six to eight thousand steps a day. For younger adults, it flattened at around eight to ten thousand.

后来研究者检验了它。2022 年发表的一项大型分析汇总了十五项研究、近五万名成年人的数据。步数越多，早逝风险越低，但好处会趋于平稳。六十岁以上的人，曲线在每天大约六千到八千步处变平；更年轻的成年人，大约在八千到一万步处变平。

That is good news for anyone who feels guilty at the end of the day with only 5,000 on the screen. The biggest gains seem to come at the low end, when a sedentary person starts moving at all. Going from 2,000 steps to 5,000 probably matters more than going from 10,000 to 13,000. The same analysis did not find clear evidence that walking faster added much, although other studies suggest pace may help.

这对那些晚上看到屏幕上只有五千步就内疚的人是好消息。最大的收益似乎在低端：久坐的人一开始动起来。从两千步到五千步，大概比从一万步到一万三千步更要紧。同一项分析没有找到走得更快会多带来多少好处的明确证据，不过别的研究认为步速可能有帮助。

There are limits to what step counts measure. They miss swimming and cycling, and they say nothing about strength or balance, which matter more and more with age. A person who walks 6,000 steps and does two short strength sessions at home may be in better shape than one who walks 12,000 steps and does nothing else.

步数能量的东西有限。它漏掉了游泳和骑车，也完全不反映力量和平衡，而这两样随年龄增长越来越重要。一个每天走六千步、在家做两次短时力量训练的人，可能比走一万两千步却什么都不做的人身体更好。

So what should you take from this? Treat 10,000 as a nice goal, not the line between healthy and unhealthy. Get off the bus one stop early, take the stairs, walk when you go to work if you can. For the first time, the number on your wrist may feel like something to enjoy rather than fear.

那该怎么看？把一万步当成一个不错的目标，而不是健康与不健康的分界线。提前一站下车，走楼梯，能走路上班就走路。头一回，手腕上的数字也许会让你觉得是种乐趣，而不是负担。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

It is usually traced to a pedometer sold in Japan in the 1960s, whose name meant roughly "10,000-step meter".
sold in Japan is a reduced relative clause; whose works for things as well as people. in the 1960s takes the.
sold in Japan 是省略的定语从句；whose 也可以指物。in the 1960s 要加 the。
The same analysis did not find clear evidence that walking faster added much, although other studies suggest pace may help.
the same always takes the; evidence that… introduces what the evidence would show.
the same 必须加 the；evidence that… 引出证据要证明的内容。
