My First Year as a School Parent 我当学校家长的第一年

🧠 Learn the 2 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 2 个新词

Read one parent's first year with a school abroad, and see the present perfect and the past simple share the work of a story. 读一位家长在国外学校的第一年，看现在完成时和一般过去时怎样分担一个故事。

A year ago my son started Grade 7 in Calgary, and I started my own first year as a school parent here. I have learned more in those twelve months than in all his years of school back home. Here are the lessons that cost me the most.

一年前，儿子在卡尔加里上了七年级，我也开始了自己在这里当学校家长的第一年。这十二个月里我学到的，比他在国内上学那些年加起来还多。下面是我付出代价最大的几课。

In September I bought a pile of workbooks, because that was what good parents did where I grew up. He never opened them. I filled in every form the school sent home, but I did not read the first newsletter properly. It said that families should protect time for rest and reading at home. I read it later and ignored it.

九月我买了一摞练习册，因为在我长大的地方，好家长都这么做。他一本也没打开。学校发回家的每张表我都填了，但第一期通讯我没好好读。上面说，家庭应该给休息和在家阅读留出时间。我后来读到了，却没当回事。

The first parent-teacher conference changed my mind. I went straight from work and asked what his rank in the class was. The teacher smiled and said there was no rank. Then she told me things I had never heard: he had helped a new student who spoke no English, and he asked the best questions in science. I had not asked about any of that.

第一次家长面谈改变了我的想法。我下了班直接过去，问他在班上排第几。老师笑了笑，说没有排名。然后她告诉我一些我从没听过的事：他帮过一个不会说英语的新同学，科学课上他提的问题最好。这些我一样都没问过。

Since then I have volunteered on two field trips, and I have met parents from eleven countries. I have also stopped asking about rank. At dinner I ask, "What was the most interesting question in class today?" Some days the answer is "nothing". Some days it lasts half an hour.

从那以后，我做过两次校外考察的志愿者，认识了来自十一个国家的家长。我也不再问排名了。吃晚饭时我问：「今天课上最有意思的问题是什么？」有时回答是「没有」，有时能聊上半个小时。

He goes to school by bus now, which he insisted on in the spring. I still worry about him. But these days I worry about the right things.

现在他坐校车上学，这是他春天时坚持要的。我还是会担心他，但如今我担心的是该担心的事。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

I have learned more in those twelve months than in all his years of school back home.
Present perfect: the year runs up to now and the lessons still count.
现在完成时：这一年一直延续到现在，学到的东西至今有效。
Then she told me things I had never heard: he had helped a new student who spoke no English, and he asked the best questions in science.
Past perfect had helped for something before the meeting; past simple asked for a general habit.
过去完成时 had helped 指面谈之前的事；一般过去时 asked 指一贯的表现。
