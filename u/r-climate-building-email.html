An Email to the Building Committee About the Heat 就高温问题写给楼宇委员会的邮件

🧠 Learn the 3 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 3 个新词

Read a resident's email to a building committee, and see how phrasal verbs keep a formal request friendly and practical. 读一位住户写给楼宇委员会的邮件，看短语动词怎样让正式请求既友好又务实。

B2 · CELPIP 7–8⏱ 20 minV06 Register: too formal, too casual, or too blunt语域不合：太正式、太随意或太冲V04 Phrasal verbs avoided; the formal verb sounds stiff回避短语动词，说话太书面

📖 Key card知识卡
A request to a committee works best with small, specific ideas and an offer to do some of the work yourself.向委员会提请求，最管用的是小而具体的方案，再加上一句「这部分我来做」。

Read and listen读与听

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Dear members of the building committee, I am writing on behalf of several residents on the upper floors to bring up something we would like to sort out before next summer. During the heat wave in July, temperatures in some units stayed uncomfortably high for days, and two older residents spent nights at a cooling centre.

楼宇委员会各位成员：我代表高层的几位住户写信，想提一件希望在明年夏天之前解决的事。七月热浪期间，有些单元里的温度连续几天都高得难受，两位年长的住户只好去避暑中心过夜。

We know there is no simple solution, and we are not asking for central air conditioning. We have come up with three smaller ideas instead. First, could the common room on the ground floor be set up as a cool room during heat warnings, with chairs, water and a fan? Second, could the building keep a voluntary list of residents who would like someone to check on them during extreme heat or wildfire smoke? Third, could we look into better filters for the ventilation system, which would also improve air quality on smoky days?

我们知道没有简单的办法，也不是要求装中央空调。我们想出了三个小一些的办法。第一，高温预警期间，一楼的公共活动室能不能布置成纳凉室，放上椅子、水和风扇？第二，楼里能不能自愿登记一份名单，记下希望在酷热或山火烟雾时有人来看看的住户？第三，能不能考虑给通风系统换更好的滤网？这样烟雾天的空气质量也会好一些。

Several residents have already offered to help. One neighbour has volunteered to run the list, and I am happy to take on the job of asking contractors for quotes on the filters, so that the committee has real figures to compare.

已经有好几位住户表示愿意帮忙。一位邻居自愿负责那份名单，我也乐意承担向承包商要滤网报价的事，这样委员会就有实际的数字可以比较。

We understand that some owners will want to know the cost before anything else, and that a heat pump for every unit is not realistic right now. That is why we have started with ideas that are cheap or free. We would simply like to make a difference before the next long hot spell, rather than after it.

我们理解有些业主首先想知道花多少钱，也明白现在给每个单元装热泵并不现实。所以我们先从便宜或不花钱的办法做起。我们只是希望在下一段长时间高温到来之前，而不是之后，做出一点改变。

Could we put this on the agenda for the March meeting? I will follow up with a short written summary beforehand. Thank you for your time and for everything you already do for the building. Kind regards, Amira Haddad, Unit 1204

能否把这件事列入三月会议的议程？会前我会再补一份简短的书面摘要。感谢各位抽出时间，也感谢大家一直以来为这栋楼做的一切。此致，阿米拉·哈达德，1204 单元

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

I am writing on behalf of several residents on the upper floors to bring up something we would like to sort out before next summer.
A formal opening that still uses two phrasal verbs: bring up (raise a topic) and sort out (solve). we would like to is a polite way to say what you want.
正式的开头里照样用了两个短语动词：bring up（提出话题）和 sort out（解决）。we would like to 是说「我们想要」的礼貌方式。
could the common room on the ground floor be set up as a cool room during heat warnings
A request as a passive question: could … be set up avoids telling the committee who must do it.
用被动疑问句提请求：could … be set up 不直接点名该由委员会谁去做。
