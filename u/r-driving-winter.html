How Winter Catches Drivers Out 冬天怎么让司机栽跟头

Every year, the first real snowfall brings the same story: cars in ditches, dented bumpers, buses running late and drivers who swear they were going slowly. Most winter crashes do not happen because the roads are impossible. They happen because people drive the way they did in October.

每年第一场真正的大雪都带来同样的故事：车滑进沟里、保险杠撞瘪、公交晚点，司机们还发誓说自己开得很慢。冬天的车祸大多不是因为路没法走，而是因为人们还在用十月的方式开车。

The physics is simple. On ice, a car needs far more distance to stop than on a dry road, often several times more. Black ice is the worst case, because it looks like an ordinary wet road. It forms most often on bridges, in shaded corners and in the early morning, when the night has been colder than the day.

道理很简单。在冰面上，车要停下来需要的距离比干路面长得多，常常是好几倍。最糟的是黑冰，因为它看上去就像普通的湿路面。它最常出现在桥面上、背阴的弯道和清晨，也就是夜里比白天冷得多的时候。

Winter tires help more than most people expect. Their rubber stays soft in the cold and keeps its grip, while all-season tires become hard when the temperature falls to around seven degrees. Some provinces require them on certain roads for part of the year, so check the local rules before you drive to work on the first icy morning.

冬季轮胎的作用比大多数人想的大。它的橡胶在寒冷中依然柔软、抓地力好；而四季胎在气温降到七度左右时就会变硬。有些省份规定一年中某段时间在某些道路上必须用冬胎，所以在第一个结冰的早晨开车上班之前，先查一下本地规定。

Then there is technique. Accelerate and brake gently, and keep your distance: leave at least twice the usual gap. If the car starts to slide, look and steer where you want to go, not at the thing you are afraid of hitting. And keep some equipment in the trunk: a brush, a scraper, a blanket and a phone charger.

然后是技巧。加速和刹车都要轻，保持车距，至少留出平时两倍的距离。如果车开始打滑，眼睛看向、方向盘也打向你想去的地方，而不是你怕撞上的东西。后备箱里再备些用品：一把扫雪刷、一个冰刮、一条毯子和一个手机充电器。

Finally, the best piece of advice is the one people least like to hear: on the worst mornings, do not drive at all. Work at home if you can, or go by bus. Nobody has ever regretted a trip they did not take.

最后，最好的一条建议也是大家最不爱听的：碰上最糟的早晨，干脆别开车。能在家上班就在家上班，或者坐公交。从来没有人后悔过一趟没出的门。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

Every year, the first real snowfall brings the same story: cars in ditches, dented bumpers, buses running late and drivers who swear they were going slowly.
the first, the same: only one is possible, so the. The list uses plural nouns with no article because they mean cars, buses and drivers in general.
the first、the same：只有一个，所以用 the。列举里的复数名词不加冠词，因为泛指。
Work at home if you can, or go by bus.
Fixed phrases with no article: at home, by bus (also by car, go to work). Adding the (by the bus) makes it sound like one particular bus.
固定说法不加冠词：at home、by bus（还有 by car、go to work）。加了 the（by the bus）就像在说某一辆特定的车。
