A Village Asks Day-Trippers to Book Ahead 小村要一日游客先预约

🧠 Learn the 5 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 5 个新词

Read a local news report about a village that limits day visitors, and notice how news uses the present perfect for a change that still matters now. 读一篇小村限制一日游客的本地新闻，留意新闻怎样用现在完成时报道一个至今仍有影响的变化。

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

For most of the year, Hollin Bay is a quiet fishing village of about two thousand people. In peak season it is something else. On a busy Saturday last August, the only road into the village was blocked by a queue of cars for three hours, and some residents could not get home from work until after dark.

一年里大部分时候，霍林湾是一个两千来人的安静渔村。到了旺季就是另一回事了。去年八月一个繁忙的周六，进村唯一的那条路被车队堵了三个小时，有些居民下班后直到天黑才回得了家。

This week the village council made a decision that has divided the community. From next summer, day-trippers will have to book a free time slot online before they drive in. Visitors who arrive without a booking will be sent to a car park outside the village and offered a shuttle bus.

本周，村议会做出了一个让村里意见分裂的决定：从明年夏天起，一日游客开车进村前必须在网上预约一个免费时段。没预约的游客会被引到村外的停车场，改乘接驳巴士。

"We have never wanted to close the door on visitors," said the council's chair. "Tourism pays a lot of wages here. But we have to take our own residents into account too. Over the past five years, short-term rentals have taken over whole streets, and young families can no longer afford to stay."

「我们从来不想把游客拒之门外，」议会主席说，「这里很多人的工资是旅游业付的。但我们也得把住在这里的人考虑进去。过去五年，短租房占掉了整条整条的街，年轻家庭已经住不起了。」

Not everyone agrees. A café owner said the booking system would have an impact on small businesses that depend on visitors who decide on the day. "People look out of the window on a sunny morning and think of us. If they have to book, they'll go somewhere else."

并非人人赞同。一位咖啡馆老板说，预约制度会影响那些靠临时起意的游客过活的小店。「人们早上看窗外天晴，就想到我们这儿来。要是还得预约，他们就去别处了。」

Researchers who study tourism say the village is not alone. Several popular destinations have tried caps on visitor numbers, with mixed results. The footprint of a visit, they point out, is not only the carbon from the drive; it is also the traffic, the rubbish and the rents. The council says it will review the scheme after the first season and make changes if necessary.

研究旅游的学者说，这个村子并不孤单。好几个热门目的地都试过限制游客人数，效果有好有坏。他们指出，一次游览的代价不只是开车排放的碳，还有交通、垃圾和房租。议会表示，第一季结束后会评估这个方案，必要时再做调整。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

This week the village council made a decision that has divided the community.
made is past simple: the vote happened at a finished moment. has divided is present perfect: the split it caused is still there now. One sentence, two time views.
made 是一般过去时：投票发生在已结束的时间点。has divided 是现在完成时：它造成的分裂现在还在。一句话里两种时间视角。
The footprint of a visit, they point out, is not only the carbon from the drive; it is also the traffic, the rubbish and the rents.
they point out is inserted between commas, a common news pattern for reporting a source. not only … ; it is also … widens the meaning of footprint.
they point out 用逗号插在句中，是新闻转述消息来源的常见写法。not only…；it is also… 把 footprint 的意思扩大了。
