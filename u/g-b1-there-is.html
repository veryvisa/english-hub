There is, there are: not there have there is 与 there are：不说 there have

You will be able to choose between 'there is/are', 'have' and a normal subject, and build these sentences with correct agreement, tense and verb forms. 你能在 there is/are、have 和普通主语句之间做出正确选择，并保证主谓一致、时态和后续动词形式正确。

There is a gym in our building. 我们楼里有个健身房。
Announces the gym as new information; the focus is on its existence.把健身房作为新信息引出，重点是“存在”。
Our building has a gym. 我们这栋楼带健身房。
The building is the topic, and the gym is presented as one of its features.话题是这栋楼，健身房是它的一项配置。
The gym is in our building. 那个健身房就在我们楼里。
The listener already knows which gym; the new information is its location.听者已经知道是哪个健身房，新信息是它的位置。
We have a gym in our building. 我们楼里有健身房可以用。
Stresses that the speaker's group has it and can use it.强调说话人这一群体拥有它、可以使用它。

📏 The rule规则

📏 The rule in one line规则一句话
Use 'there + be' to tell the listener that something new exists or happens, and use 'have' only when a real subject owns, includes or experiences it.用 there + be 告诉对方“存在或发生了某个新事物”；只有当句子里有一个真正的主语拥有、包含或经历它时，才用 have。

📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Open the full explanation: 6 points, 23 examples展开细讲：6 段讲解、23 个例句

Chinese uses one verb for both existence and possession, but English splits the two jobs. If you can name an owner or container as the subject, 'have' works: 'The flat has a balcony.' If you simply want to announce that something exists, start with 'there': 'There is a balcony at the back.' The word 'there' can never be the owner, so 'there have' followed by a thing is wrong.

中文的“有”同时表示存在和拥有，英语则分成两种说法。如果能说出一个拥有者或容纳者作主语，可以用 have：The flat has a balcony. 如果只是想说明某物存在，就用 there 开头：There is a balcony at the back. there 永远不能当“拥有者”，所以 there have 加事物是错的。

Choose 'there is' when the noun is new to the listener, which is why it usually comes with 'a', 'an', 'some', 'any', 'no' or a number. Once both of you know which thing you mean, make that thing the subject: 'The key is on the table.' A definite noun after 'there is' is possible mainly when you are reminding someone of an option, as in 'Well, there's the night bus.'

当名词对听者来说是新信息时，用 there is，所以后面常接 a、an、some、any、no 或数字。如果双方都知道指的是哪一个，就让它直接作主语：The key is on the table. there is 后接特指名词，主要用于提醒对方还有某个选择，例如 Well, there's the night bus.

The verb agrees with the noun that follows, not with 'there'. Look at the head noun: 'a lot of traffic' takes 'is', 'a lot of cars' takes 'are', and 'a number of problems' also takes 'are'. In a list, the verb normally matches the first item: 'There is a desk and two chairs.' You will hear 'there's two options' in casual speech, but avoid it in exams and formal writing.

动词要和后面的名词保持一致，而不是和 there 一致。要看中心名词：a lot of traffic 用 is，a lot of cars 用 are，a number of problems 也用 are。列举多个事物时，动词通常与第一项一致：There is a desk and two chairs. 口语里会听到 there's two options，但考试和正式写作中应避免。

Only the verb 'be' changes to show time and certainty: 'there was', 'there has been', 'there will be', 'there must be', 'there used to be'. Do not swap in 'have' as the main verb after a modal. Use 'there has been' for something that connects to the present, and 'there was' when the time is finished or stated, such as 'last night'.

表示时间和可能性时，只需要变化 be：there was、there has been、there will be、there must be、there used to be。情态动词后不要把主要动词换成 have。与现在有联系的情况用 there has been；时间已经结束或明确说出（如 last night）时用 there was。

A 'there is' clause already has its main verb, so you cannot simply add another one after the noun. Use a relative clause ('There are many people who work from home'), an '-ing' form for an action in progress ('There is a man waiting outside'), or a past participle for a passive meaning ('There are two seats left'). Often the shorter sentence 'Many people work from home' is even better.

there is 句子已经有了谓语动词，名词后面不能直接再加一个动词原形或变位动词。可以用定语从句（There are many people who work from home），用 -ing 表示正在进行的动作（There is a man waiting outside），或用过去分词表示被动含义（There are two seats left）。很多时候直接说 Many people work from home 更好。

Do not confuse 'there is' with 'it is'. 'There is' introduces the thing, while 'it is' then describes or identifies something already mentioned, or talks about weather, time and distance: 'There is a new café on our street. It is very quiet.' Compare 'There is a lot of wind today' with 'It is very windy today'.

不要混淆 there is 和 it is。there is 用来引出事物；it is 则用来描述或确认已经提到的东西，或谈天气、时间和距离：There is a new café on our street. It is very quiet. 比较 There is a lot of wind today 和 It is very windy today.

More pairs: same idea, different choice更多对比句组

There is a problem with the heating. 暖气有问题。
The problem exists now.问题现在存在。
There was a problem with the heating last week. 上周暖气出过问题。
Places the problem in a finished time period.问题发生在已经结束的时间段。
There has been a problem with the heating. 暖气出了问题。
Reports news with a present result or relevance, often to explain the current situation.通报一件与现在有关的事，常用来解释当前状况。
There might be a problem with the heating. 暖气可能有问题。
The speaker suspects a problem but is not certain.说话人怀疑有问题，但不确定。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ There have many people in the park.
✓ There are many people in the park.
Learners translate the Chinese verb meaning 'have' directly. Existence is expressed with 'there' plus 'be'.
这是把中文的“有”直接译成 have。表示存在要用 there + be。
✗ In my city has many parks.
✓ There are many parks in my city.
A prepositional phrase cannot be the subject of 'has'. Use 'there are', or say 'My city has many parks.'
介词短语不能作 has 的主语。可以用 there are，或者说 My city has many parks.
✗ There are many students study in the library.
✓ There are many students studying in the library.
The clause already has the verb 'are'. The second action needs an '-ing' form or a relative clause with 'who'.
句子已经有谓语 are，第二个动作要用 -ing 形式或 who 引导的定语从句。
✗ There will have a meeting on Friday.
✓ There will be a meeting on Friday.
After a modal such as 'will', the existential structure still needs 'be'.
will 等情态动词之后，存在句仍然要用 be。
✗ Where is my laptop? There is the laptop on your desk.
✓ Where is my laptop? It's on your desk.
The laptop is already known, so the answer should give its location with 'it' as the subject, not introduce it as new.
这台电脑是已知事物，回答应以 it 作主语说明位置，而不是把它当新信息引出。
✗ There happened a car accident near my house.
✓ There was a car accident near my house.
Do not use 'happen' after 'there' in this way. Say 'There was an accident' or 'A car accident happened near my house.'
不能这样在 there 后用 happen。应说 There was an accident，或 A car accident happened near my house.
