📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Open the full explanation: 5 points展开细讲：5 段讲解

The two people are the landlord (or property manager) and the tenant. A roommate shares the flat with you, and in British English is a flatmate. The document between landlord and tenant is the lease or tenancy agreement. You sign it, renew it and break it, and each verb has consequences in the small print.

两方是 landlord（房东，或 property manager 物业经理）和 tenant（租客）。roommate 是合租的室友，英式英语叫 flatmate。双方之间的文件是 lease 或 tenancy agreement（租约）。签约是 sign，续约是 renew，提前解约是 break，每个动词在条款里都有后果。

Money words come in pairs. The rent is monthly; the damage deposit (also called a security deposit) is paid once and returned at the end if there is no damage. A rent increase usually needs written notice under local rules. Whether utilities such as heat, water and electricity are included changes the real cost a lot.

钱的词成对出现：rent 按月付；damage deposit（也叫 security deposit，押金）一次性付，退租时没有损坏就退回。rent increase（涨租）按当地规定通常要书面通知。utilities（暖气、水、电等）是否 included（包含在内），会让实际成本差很多。

In Canada a basement suite is a self-contained flat in the lower level of a house, common and often cheaper. Ask about light, ceiling height and what happens after heavy rain. The listing will say furnished or unfurnished, and unfurnished often still includes the fridge and stove.

在加拿大，basement suite（地下室套间）是房子下层的独立单元，很常见，通常更便宜。看房时问采光、层高、下大雨后会怎样。房源会写 furnished（带家具）或 unfurnished（不带家具），而 unfurnished 往往仍包括冰箱和炉灶。

The move-in day has its own vocabulary. You do a move-in inspection with the landlord and note every mark, so the damage deposit is not used for damage that was already there. After that, problems go in a maintenance request, ideally in writing: report a leak, the heating isn't working.

入住那天有它自己的一套词：和房东一起做 move-in inspection（入住检查），把每处痕迹都记下来，免得押金被拿去赔原本就有的损坏。之后有问题就提 maintenance request（维修申请），最好书面提：report a leak、the heating isn't working。

Buying uses different words: down payment, mortgage, property tax. Keep the two sets apart. I paid the rent and I paid the mortgage tell the listener whether you are a tenant or an owner.

买房用的是另一套词：down payment（首付）、mortgage（按揭）、property tax（地产税）。两套词别混用：I paid the rent 和 I paid the mortgage，一听就知道你是租客还是房主。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ The house owner wants to raise the rent.
✓ The landlord wants to raise the rent.
The person who rents a home to you is the landlord.
把房子租给你的人叫 landlord。
✗ We paid the deposit money of damage.
✓ We paid the damage deposit.
The noun comes first in compounds: damage deposit.
复合名词修饰语在前：damage deposit。
✗ Is the rent included the utilities?
✓ Are utilities included in the rent?
utilities is plural; included in the rent.
utilities 是复数；included in the rent。
✗ I want to rent the house to live.
✓ I'm looking for a place to rent.
rent already means "pay to live in".
rent 本身就是「租来住」。
✗ We paid the rent to the bank. (as owners)
✓ We paid the mortgage.
Owners pay a mortgage, tenants pay rent.
房主还 mortgage，租客交 rent。
