You Sign More Contracts Than You Think 你签的合同比你以为的多

Most people picture a contract as a long document signed at a lawyer's desk. In everyday life you enter contracts far more often than that: when you buy a coffee, join a gym, accept a job offer or tap "I agree" in an app. A contract is simply an agreement that the law is prepared to enforce.

多数人想到合同，脑子里是律师桌上签的一份长文件。其实在日常生活里，你订立合同的次数多得多：买一杯咖啡、办健身卡、接受一份工作、在应用里点「我同意」。合同说白了就是法律愿意强制执行的协议。

In general, an agreement becomes binding when one side makes an offer, the other side accepts it, and each side gives something of value. It does not always have to be on paper. A spoken agreement can be binding too, but it is much harder to prove, which is why a short email confirming what you agreed is so useful.

一般来说，一方提出要约、另一方接受、双方各自给出某种有价值的东西，协议就有了约束力。它不一定非得写在纸上。口头协议也可以有约束力，但要证明难得多，所以一封确认双方约定内容的简短邮件特别有用。

Many disputes start with a misunderstanding rather than bad faith. Two people remember the same conversation differently, or one of them never read the terms. Reading before signing sounds obvious, but it is the only reliable protection you have. If a term seems unreasonable, ask for it to be changed before you sign, not after.

很多纠纷始于误解，而不是恶意。两个人对同一段对话记得不一样，或者其中一人根本没读条款。签字前先读，听起来理所当然，却是你唯一可靠的保护。如果某条条款看着不合理，在签之前要求修改，而不是签了之后。

Some consumer contracts come with a cooling-off period, a short window in which you can cancel without giving a reason. Which contracts have one, and for how long, depends on where you live and what you are buying, so do not assume you have one. Sales at your front door and some long-term services are common examples, but check the rules that apply to you.

有些消费合同带有冷静期，也就是一段可以不说理由就取消的短时间。哪些合同有冷静期、有多长，取决于你住在哪里、买的是什么，所以别想当然地以为自己有。上门推销和一些长期服务是常见的例子，但要查清适用于你的规定。

If something does go wrong, most contract problems settle without a court: a clear letter, a call to the business or a complaint to a consumer protection office. A little awareness of the basics, and the habit of keeping a copy of everything you agree to, will prevent more trouble than a lawyer can fix afterwards.

真出了问题，大多数合同纠纷不用上法庭就能解决：一封写清楚的信、一通打给商家的电话，或者向消费者保护机构投诉。对基本常识多一点了解，再养成把同意过的东西都留一份副本的习惯，能避免的麻烦比事后律师能补救的还多。

/ˈbaɪndɪŋ/ adj.
有（法律）约束力的
legally binding; a binding agreement
Once you sign, the agreement is binding. 一旦签字，协议就有约束力了。
/ɪnˈfɔːs/ v.
强制执行；执行（法律、规定）
enforce a contract; enforce a rule
A court can enforce the agreement if one side refuses to pay. 一方拒绝付款时，法院可以强制执行协议。
/ˌkuːlɪŋ ˈɒf ˌpɪəriəd/ n.
冷静期（可无理由取消的期限）
within the cooling-off period; a ten-day cooling-off period
She cancelled the gym contract within the cooling-off period. 她在冷静期内取消了健身房合同。
/ˌbæd ˈfeɪθ/ n.
恶意；不诚实
act in bad faith; a sign of bad faith
Changing the price after you agreed is a sign of bad faith. 谈好之后又改价，是不诚信的表现。
/ˈsetl/ v.
和解；了结（纠纷）
settle a dispute; settle without going to court
The two sides settled after one phone call. 双方一通电话就和解了。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

Some consumer contracts come with a cooling-off period, a short window in which you can cancel without giving a reason.
The phrase after the comma explains the term. in which is a formal relative pronoun after a preposition (informal: a window you can cancel in).
逗号后的短语解释了这个术语。in which 是介词加关系代词的正式说法（口语可说 a window you can cancel in）。
Reading before signing sounds obvious, but it is the only reliable protection you have.
the only always takes the. Reading before signing is a gerund subject, with no article.
the only 固定带 the。Reading before signing 是动名词作主语，不加冠词。
