📏 The rule规则

🎯 The point in one line一句话要领
A three-part phrasal verb is one block: verb + particle + preposition, with the object after the last word. Put up with it, never put it up with.三词短语动词是一个整块：动词＋小品词＋介词，宾语放在最后：put up with it，绝不说 put it up with。
  • Easy to miss易漏 ✗ I'm looking forward to see you. → ✓ I'm looking forward to seeing you.

📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Open the full explanation: 5 points展开细讲：5 段讲解

These verbs say in three words what Chinese often says with one verb or a verb plus a phrase: put up with is "endure without complaining", cut down on is "reduce how much you use". The formal single verb exists (tolerate, reduce), but in speech the three-part verb is normal and sounds less cold.

这类动词用三个词说出中文常用一个动词或一个短语表达的意思：put up with 是忍着不抱怨，cut down on 是少用一点。书面的单个动词也有（tolerate、reduce），但口语里三词短语动词才是常态，听起来没那么冷。

Word order is simple: they do not separate. The object, noun or pronoun, comes after the preposition: I can't put up with the noise, I can't put up with it. This makes them easier than two-part verbs, once you have the three words in the right order.

语序很简单：不拆开。宾语不管是名词还是代词，都放在介词后面：I can't put up with the noise、I can't put up with it。只要三个词顺序对，它们比两词短语动词还好用。

The last word is a preposition, so a verb after it takes -ing: I'm looking forward to seeing you, I never got round to calling her. Look forward to see you is the classic error, because learners hear to as the infinitive marker.

最后一个词是介词，后面接动词就要用 -ing：I'm looking forward to seeing you、I never got round to calling her。look forward to see you 是最经典的错，因为学习者把 to 听成了不定式符号。

Many describe slow pressure in daily life: keep up with bills or classmates, catch up on sleep or emails, fall behind and then make up for lost time. Grouping them by situation (money, health, work, people) makes them easier to recall when you need them.

很多三词短语动词描述的是日常里慢慢压下来的事：跟上账单或同学（keep up with）、补觉补邮件（catch up on）、落下之后再补回时间（make up for）。按场景分组（钱、健康、工作、人）记，用的时候更容易想起来。

Some are fixed with a person: get on with your colleagues (have a good relationship), look down on someone (think they are worth less). Others are fixed with a thing: run out of milk, come down with a cold. Note which slot each one fills.

有些固定接人：get on with 同事（相处得好）、look down on 某人（瞧不起）；有些固定接事物：run out of 牛奶、come down with 感冒。记住每个后面接的是人还是物。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ I'm looking forward to see you.
✓ I'm looking forward to seeing you.
to is a preposition here: use -ing.
这里的 to 是介词，接 -ing。
✗ I can't put it up with any more.
✓ I can't put up with it any more.
Three-part verbs never split.
三词短语动词不拆开。
✗ We ran out the milk.
✓ We ran out of milk.
Do not drop of.
of 不能丢。
✗ I can't stand to the noise.
✓ I can't put up with the noise.
stand takes a direct object; put up with is the phrasal option.
stand 直接接宾语；put up with 是短语动词说法。
✗ He dropped out from college.
✓ He dropped out of college.
drop out of, not from.
drop out of，不用 from。
