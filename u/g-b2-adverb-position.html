Where adverbs go 副词的位置

📘 In your coursebook: English File 5e · Upper-Intermediate · 3B “A twist in the tale”. Use the texts and recordings with your coursebook; this page adds new words, grammar and practice.教材定位：English File 5e · Upper-Intermediate · 3B《A twist in the tale》。课文与录音请配合教材学习，本页补充新词、语法与练习。

Put adverbs where English expects them: frequency words in the middle, manner and place at the end, comment adverbs at the front, and only and even next to what they focus on. 把副词放到英语习惯的位置：频率词放句中，方式和地点放句末，评论性副词放句首，only 和 even 紧挨着它们强调的词。

Only I paid for parking. 只有我付了停车费。
Nobody else paid.别人都没付。
I only paid for parking. 我只付了停车费。
I paid for nothing else.别的都没付。
I paid only for one hour of parking. 我只付了一小时的停车费。
Not for more hours.没多付时长。

📏 The rule规则

📏 The rule in one line规则一句话
Never put an adverb between a verb and its object. Frequency and certainty adverbs go before the main verb but after be and the first auxiliary; manner, place and time go at the end in that order; comment adverbs like honestly or luckily open the sentence.副词永远不要插在动词和宾语中间。频率和把握类副词放在实义动词前、be 动词和第一个助动词后；方式、地点、时间放句末，按这个顺序；honestly、luckily 这类评论性副词放句首。

📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Open the full explanation: 5 points, a table, 14 examples展开细讲：5 段讲解、一张表、14 个例句

Chinese puts almost every adverb before the verb. English allows that position for some adverbs, but never between the verb and its object. I like very much coffee and She speaks fluently English are the classic errors. The object stays glued to the verb: I like coffee very much, She speaks English fluently.

英语允许一部分副词放在动词前，但绝不允许插在动词和宾语中间。I like very much coffee、She speaks fluently English 是典型错误。宾语要紧贴着动词：I like coffee very much、She speaks English fluently。

Mid-position words are frequency (always, usually, often, never), certainty (probably, definitely) and a few others (already, still, just, also, even). They go before a main verb (I usually walk), after be (She is usually late) and after the first auxiliary (I have never been, They will probably call). In negatives, probably and definitely go before won't or don't: He probably won't come.

句中位置的副词包括频率词、把握类副词，以及 already、still、just、also、even 等。它们放在实义动词前，be 动词后，第一个助动词后。否定句里 probably、definitely 放在 won't、don't 前面：He probably won't come。

End position is for manner, place and time, usually in that order: She explained it clearly in the meeting yesterday. A long time phrase can move to the front to set the scene: Last winter, we drove to Whitehorse. sometimes, usually and often can also go at the front for emphasis or contrast: Usually I cook, but tonight we're ordering in.

句末放方式、地点、时间，通常按这个顺序。较长的时间短语可以放到句首铺垫场景。sometimes、usually、often 也能放句首，表示强调或对比。

Comment adverbs give the speaker's view of the whole sentence and go at the front with a comma: Honestly, I don't think it's worth the money. Luckily, nobody was hurt. Apparently, the office is moving. This is one of the fastest ways to sound natural in conversation and in emails, because it signals your attitude before the information arrives.

评论性副词表达说话人对整句话的看法，放在句首加逗号。这是让口语和邮件听起来自然最快的办法之一，因为它在信息到来之前先亮出了你的态度。

only and even go right before the word they focus on, and moving them changes the meaning. Only I saw the email (nobody else). I only saw the email (I did not reply). I saw only the first email (not the others). In speech, people often use the middle position and let stress show the focus, but in writing, place it exactly.

only 和 even 放在它们强调的词正前方，挪个位置意思就变。口语里常放句中、靠重音表示焦点，但书面语里要放准。

Examples例句

  • I usually take the bus, but today I drove. 我平时坐公交，不过今天开了车。
    Before the main verb.放在实义动词前。
  • She is always the first person in the office. 她总是第一个到办公室。
    After be.放在 be 后。
  • We have already sent the forms. 我们已经把表寄出去了。
    After the first auxiliary.放在第一个助动词后。
  • I have never been to Newfoundland. 我从没去过纽芬兰。
    After have.放在 have 后。
  • He probably won't be at the meeting. 他可能不来开会。
    Certainty adverb before won't.把握类副词放在 won't 前。
  • They will definitely call you back. 他们一定会给你回电话。
    After will.放在 will 后。
  • My mother speaks English quite well now. 我母亲现在英语说得挺好了。
    Object, then manner, then time.宾语，再方式，再时间。
  • I really like this neighbourhood. 我真的很喜欢这个街区。
    really before the verb.really 放在动词前。
  • I like this neighbourhood very much. 我非常喜欢这个街区。
    very much after the object.very much 放在宾语后。
  • Honestly, I'm not sure the extra fee is worth it. 说实话，我不确定这笔额外费用值不值。
    Comment adverb at the front.评论性副词放句首。
  • Luckily, the pipe burst while we were at home. 幸好水管爆的时候我们在家。
    Speaker's attitude first.先亮态度。
  • Apparently, the store is closing at the end of the month. 听说这家店月底要关门了。
    Hearsay.道听途说。
  • Even the bus driver didn't know the new route. 连公交司机都不知道新路线。
    Focus on the bus driver.强调「公交司机」。
  • I still haven't heard from the landlord. 我还是没有收到房东的消息。
    still before the negative auxiliary.still 放在否定助动词前。

More pairs: same idea, different choice更多对比句组

I usually walk to work. 我通常走路上班。
Neutral.中性。
Usually I walk to work, but this week I'm driving. 平时我走路上班，不过这周开车。
Front position sets up a contrast.放句首引出对比。
I walk to work, usually. 我走路上班，一般是这样。
End position: an afterthought in speech.放句末：口语里的补充。
He clearly explained the problem. 他把问题解释得很清楚。
Manner: the explanation was clear.方式：解释得清楚。
Clearly, he explained the problem. 很显然，他已经解释过这个问题了。
Comment: it is obvious that he did.评论：显然他解释过。
He explained the problem clearly. 他把问题解释得很清楚。
Manner at the end: most neutral.方式放句末：最中性。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ I like very much this city.
✓ I like this city very much. / I really like this city.
Never put an adverb between the verb and its object.
副词不能插在动词和宾语之间。
✗ She speaks fluently three languages.
✓ She speaks three languages fluently.
Manner goes after the object.
方式副词放在宾语后面。
✗ I always am tired on Mondays.
✓ I am always tired on Mondays.
Frequency adverbs go after be.
频率副词放在 be 后面。
✗ He won't probably come.
✓ He probably won't come.
probably goes before a negative auxiliary.
probably 放在否定助动词前。
✗ We yesterday went to the bank.
✓ We went to the bank yesterday. / Yesterday we went to the bank.
Time goes at the end or the front, not in the middle.
时间放句末或句首，不放中间。
