How Weather Affects Your Body 天气如何影响身体

🧠 Learn the 3 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 3 个新词

Weather conditions can influence sleep, physical comfort, and daily health habits, so understanding these effects helps people make sensible choices. 天气状况可能影响睡眠、身体舒适度和日常健康习惯，因此了解这些影响有助于人们作出合理选择。

Weather affects more than what we wear each day. Changes in temperature, sunlight, humidity, and wind can influence how comfortable we feel and how much energy we have. Some people feel more active on bright days, while others prefer cooler conditions. These differences are normal because individuals respond to their surroundings in different ways.

天气影响的不只是我们每天穿什么。气温、阳光、湿度和风的变化会影响我们的舒适程度以及精力水平。有些人在阳光明媚的日子里感觉更加活跃，而另一些人则更喜欢凉爽的环境。这些差异很正常，因为每个人对周围环境的反应都不一样。

Sunlight plays an important role in the body's daily rhythm. Exposure to natural light during the day helps regulate the sleep-wake cycle. In darker months, shorter days may change people's routines, especially if they spend most of their time indoors. Opening curtains in the morning and taking a walk outside can help people maintain a regular daily schedule.

阳光在人体的日常生理节律中发挥着重要作用。白天接触自然光有助于调节睡眠与清醒周期。在日照较少的月份，白天缩短可能改变人们的生活规律，尤其是那些大部分时间待在室内的人。早晨拉开窗帘并到户外散步，可以帮助人们保持规律的日常作息。

Hot weather creates different challenges. When temperatures rise, the body needs to control its internal temperature. Drinking enough water, finding shade, and avoiding intense activity during the hottest part of the day are sensible precautions. Cold weather also requires attention. Wearing suitable clothing and keeping indoor spaces comfortably warm can reduce discomfort during winter.

炎热天气会带来不同的挑战。当气温升高时，身体需要调节内部温度。饮用足够的水、寻找阴凉处，并避免在一天中最热的时候进行剧烈活动，都是合理的预防措施。寒冷天气同样需要注意。穿着合适的衣服并保持室内温度舒适，可以减少冬季的不适感。

Weather does not affect everyone in the same way, and it is difficult to make a single rule for all people. Although seasonal changes can influence sleep and activity, personal habits and living conditions also matter. On the whole, maintaining regular sleep, staying active, and checking the forecast before outdoor exercise are useful habits throughout the year.

天气并不会以相同的方式影响每个人，而且很难为所有人制定一条统一的规则。虽然季节变化可能影响睡眠和活动，但个人习惯和生活环境同样重要。总的来说，保持规律睡眠、坚持活动，以及户外锻炼前查看天气预报，都是一年四季都有用的习惯。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

Exposure to natural light during the day helps regulate the sleep-wake cycle.
The subject is the noun phrase exposure to natural light during the day; the verb helps is followed by the base form regulate, since help can take either a bare infinitive or to + infinitive.
这句话的主语是名词短语 exposure to natural light during the day；动词 helps 后面接动词原形 regulate（help 后面可以接动词原形，也可以接 to + 动词原形）。
Although seasonal changes can influence sleep and activity, personal habits and living conditions also matter.
Although introduces a concessive subordinate clause, and the main clause adds another factor that should be considered.
Although 引导让步从句，主句则补充说明另一个同样值得考虑的因素。
