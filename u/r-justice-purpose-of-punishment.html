What Is Punishment For? 惩罚究竟为了什么？

When someone commits a serious crime, public demands for punishment are often immediate and emotionally powerful. Yet deciding what punishment should achieve is a complicated question. Should it express society's condemnation, prevent future offences, protect the public, or help offenders change their behaviour? These objectives are related, but they are not identical. Understanding their differences allows us to examine criminal justice more carefully than simply asking whether a sentence is severe enough.

当有人犯下严重罪行时，公众对惩罚的要求往往立即出现，而且情绪强烈。然而，决定惩罚应该实现什么目标是一个复杂的问题。惩罚应该表达社会的谴责、防止未来犯罪、保护公众，还是帮助犯罪者改变行为？这些目标彼此相关，却并不相同。理解它们之间的区别，可以让我们比单纯追问刑罚是否足够严厉更加深入地审视刑事司法。

Retribution is based on the idea that wrongdoing deserves a proportionate response. It emphasizes accountability and the moral significance of recognizing harm. Deterrence, by contrast, focuses on influencing future behaviour. A sentence may discourage the offender from repeating a crime or warn others against similar conduct. Although deterrence is an important objective, its effectiveness depends on various social and psychological factors. The threat of punishment does not automatically change the decisions people make.

报应理论建立在这样一种观念之上：错误行为应该受到相称的回应。它强调责任承担以及承认伤害所具有的道德意义。相比之下，威慑理论关注的是影响未来行为。一项刑罚可能阻止犯罪者再次犯罪，也可能警告其他人不要从事类似行为。尽管威慑是一个重要目标，但其有效性取决于各种社会和心理因素。惩罚的威胁并不会自动改变人们作出的决定。

Rehabilitation takes a different approach. It examines the circumstances contributing to criminal behaviour and seeks to reduce the likelihood of reoffending. Education, vocational training, counselling, and stable community relationships may help individuals rebuild their lives. Nevertheless, rehabilitation should not be confused with ignoring responsibility. A person can be held accountable while receiving opportunities to develop new skills and make constructive choices. On the whole, rehabilitation is more likely to succeed when support continues beyond imprisonment.

改造采取了不同的方法。它研究导致犯罪行为的各种环境因素，并努力降低再次犯罪的可能性。教育、职业培训、咨询以及稳定的社区关系可能帮助个人重建生活。然而，改造不应与忽视责任混为一谈。一个人可以在承担责任的同时，获得学习新技能和作出积极选择的机会。总体而言，如果支持能够延续到服刑结束之后，改造就更有可能取得成功。

There is no universally accepted formula for balancing these objectives. A punishment that satisfies public anger may fail to address the causes of offending, while an excessively lenient response may undermine confidence in the law. Proportionality, individual circumstances, and public safety must all be considered. A mature justice system recognizes that punishment is not an end in itself. Its broader purpose is to protect society while respecting the dignity and rights of every person involved.

对于如何平衡这些目标，目前并不存在普遍接受的公式。一项能够满足公众愤怒情绪的惩罚，可能无法解决犯罪的根源；而过于宽松的回应则可能损害公众对法律的信心。刑罚的相称性、个人具体情况以及公共安全都必须纳入考虑。成熟的司法制度认识到，惩罚本身并不是最终目的。它更广泛的目标是在尊重所有相关人员尊严和权利的同时保护社会。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

A person can be held accountable while receiving opportunities to develop new skills and make constructive choices.
The passive construction can be held accountable expresses the possibility of accountability, while while receiving introduces a simultaneous circumstance.
被动结构 can be held accountable 表示承担责任的可能性，而 while receiving 引出同时发生的情况。
On the whole, rehabilitation is more likely to succeed when support continues beyond imprisonment.
On the whole signals a general evaluation, while the comparative construction more likely to succeed expresses a qualified prediction.
On the whole 表示总体评价，而 more likely to succeed 这一比较结构表达了带有限定条件的预测。
