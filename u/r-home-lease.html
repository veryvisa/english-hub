The Lease Nobody Reads 没人细读的租约

🧠 Learn the 3 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 3 个新词

Read an explainer on what to check in a lease, and pick up the verb + noun chunks of renting. 读一篇讲租约该看什么的说明文，收下租房里的「动词＋名词」词块。

Read three things in a lease before you sign: the money beyond the rent, how to leave, and the rules about the home.签租约前看三样：租金以外的钱、怎么退租、关于房子本身的规定。

Read and listen读与听

252 words
Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

A lease is the most expensive document most renters ever sign, and almost nobody reads it. We look at the rent, the date and the place for our signature. The rest, we assume, is the same for everyone. It is not.

租约是大多数租客签过的最贵的一份文件，却几乎没人细读。我们看一眼租金、日期和签名的位置，就以为其余部分人人都一样。并不是。

Start with the money. The rent is only the first number. Who pays for heat, water and the internet? Is there a deposit, and what must happen before you get it back? If the answer is not on paper, the advice from every tenant group is the same: ask for it in writing before you sign.

先看钱。租金只是第一个数字。暖气、水和网络谁付？有没有押金，要满足什么条件才能拿回来？如果答案没有写在纸上，每个租客组织的建议都一样：签字之前要对方写下来。

Then look at time. Most leases run for a year and then continue month by month. To leave, you normally have to give notice in writing, and the notice period is fixed. A friend of mine told his landlord by phone, moved out, and was charged an extra month. He made a complaint and lost, because the lease was clear.

再看时间。大多数租约为期一年，之后按月续。要搬走，通常得书面通知，而且通知期是固定的。我一个朋友打电话告诉了房东，搬了出去，结果被多收一个月房租。他去投诉，输了，因为租约写得明明白白。

Finally, read the rules about the home itself. Can you have a pet, a guest for a month, a bicycle in the hallway? Who repairs the dishwasher, and how fast? Take a photo of every room on the day you move in, so that nobody can say later that you did damage that was already there. None of this is exciting. But an hour with the lease can save you a year of arguments. Take the document home, make a list of questions, and do not sign until every one of them has an answer.

最后，读关于房子本身的规定。能不能养宠物、留客人住一个月、把自行车放在走廊？洗碗机谁修，多快修？搬进去那天给每个房间拍照，免得日后有人说原本就有的损坏是你弄的。这些都不有趣。但花一小时读租约，能省下一年的争执。把文件带回家，列一张问题清单，每个问题都有了答案再签。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

A lease is the most expensive document most renters ever sign, and almost nobody reads it.
most renters ever sign is a relative clause with that dropped; ever strengthens the superlative.
most renters ever sign 是省略了 that 的定语从句；ever 用来加强最高级。
Take a photo of every room on the day you move in, so that nobody can say later that you did damage that was already there.
so that gives the purpose. The last that clause describes damage.
so that 引出目的；最后一个 that 从句修饰 damage。
