Five Minutes With the Settings 花五分钟看看设置

🧠 Learn the 5 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 5 个新词

Read a conversation between a father and his teenage daughter about her new account, and hear how the present perfect asks about experience so far. 读一段父亲和十几岁女儿谈她新账号的对话，听现在完成时怎样询问「到目前为止」的经历。

B2 · CELPIP 7–8⏱ 20 minG02 Present perfect and past simple confused现在完成时与一般过去时混用V10 Idioms and fixed chunks known but not used惯用语块认得但不会用

📖 Key card知识卡
Checking settings works best as a shared habit, not a lecture: parents have photos to check too.检查设置最好是全家一起的习惯，而不是说教：父母自己也有照片要查。

Read and listen读与听

270 words
Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Dad: You've had that account for a week now. Can we look at the settings together? Ana: Dad, I'm fifteen. I've already done it. Dad: Great. Then it'll only take five minutes.

爸爸：那个账号你用了一个礼拜了。我们一起看看设置好吗？安娜：爸，我十五岁了，我早就设好了。爸爸：太好了，那五分钟就够了。

Ana: Fine. Look, it's private. Only people I accept can see my posts. Dad: Good. Who have you accepted so far? Ana: People from school, mostly. And a few people who play the same game as me. Dad: Have you met them? Ana: Not in real life. But they're harmless. We just talk about the game.

安娜：好吧。你看，是私密的，只有我通过的人才能看到我的帖子。爸爸：好。到现在你都通过了谁？安娜：大多是学校的人，还有几个跟我玩同一款游戏的。爸爸：你见过他们吗？安娜：现实里没见过。不过他们没什么坏心，我们就聊游戏。

Dad: They probably are. I'm not being paranoid; I just want you to think about what they can see. Have they seen your school name or your street anywhere? Ana: No. Well... I posted a photo at the bus stop last week. You can see the street sign. Dad: OK. That's an easy fix. Could you take that one down, or crop the sign out?

爸爸：多半是。我不是疑神疑鬼，只是想让你想想他们能看到什么。他们在哪儿看到过你的学校名或者街名吗？安娜：没有。嗯……上周我在公交站拍了张照片发了，能看到路牌。爸爸：好，这个好办。你能把那张删了，或者把路牌裁掉吗？

Ana: I can archive it. Is that enough? Dad: Yes. And one more thing. If anyone ever asks you for photos, or asks you to keep a conversation secret, tell me. You won't be in trouble. I promise. Ana: Has that happened to someone you know? Dad: Not to someone I know. But it has happened to enough kids that schools now talk about it every year.

安娜：我可以把它归档隐藏，这样行吗？爸爸：行。还有一件事：要是有人向你要照片，或者让你对聊天内容保密，告诉我。你不会挨骂，我保证。安娜：你认识的人遇到过吗？爸爸：我认识的人没有。但这种事发生在足够多的孩子身上，学校现在每年都会讲。

Ana: OK. Can I ask you something, then? Did you ask for Grandma's consent before you posted that photo of her birthday? Dad: ...That's fair. I didn't. I'll ask her tonight. Ana: And mine? You posted my baby photos for years. Dad: Then we both have some settings to check.

安娜：好。那我也能问你件事吗？你发奶奶生日那张照片之前，问过她同不同意吗？爸爸：……问得好。我没问。我今晚就问她。安娜：那我的呢？你发了我好几年的婴儿照。爸爸：那我们俩都有设置要查了。

Words词

/səʊ fɑː/ adv.
到目前为止
so far, so good; the best so far
So far, nobody has complained. 到目前为止还没人投诉。
/fɪks/ n.
解决办法
an easy fix; a quick fix; a complete fix
There is no quick fix for a bad habit. 坏习惯没有速效的解决办法。
/ˈɑːkaɪv/ v.
归档（隐藏而不删除）
archive a post; archive old emails
I archived the post instead of deleting it. 我把那条帖子归档了，没删。
/ɪn ˈtrʌbl/ adj. phr.
挨骂；惹上麻烦
be in trouble; get into trouble
You will not be in trouble for telling me. 你告诉我，不会挨骂。
/kiːp ˈsiːkrət/ v.
对……保密
keep a conversation secret; keep sth secret from sb
An adult who asks a child to keep things secret is a warning sign. 让孩子保密的成年人，本身就是一个危险信号。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

But it has happened to enough kids that schools now talk about it every year.
Present perfect has happened for repeated events up to now with no time given; enough… that gives the result.
现在完成时 has happened 表示到现在为止反复发生、不说具体时间；enough… that 引出结果。
Did you ask for Grandma's consent before you posted that photo of her birthday?
Past simple Did you ask… posted: a finished event at a known time (her birthday). Compare Have you met them? about experience up to now.
一般过去时 Did you ask… posted：一件已结束、时间明确（她生日）的事。对比 Have you met them? 问的是到现在为止的经历。
