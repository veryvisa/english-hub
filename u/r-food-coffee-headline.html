The Headline Said Coffee Adds Years 标题说咖啡能添寿

🧠 Learn the 2 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 2 个新词

Read a news piece about a coffee headline and learn how careful scientists talk about evidence. 读一则关于咖啡标题的新闻，学科学家谈证据时怎样措辞留余地。

Read and listen读与听

312 words
Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Last week a headline went round our office group chat: "Three cups of coffee a day could add years to your life." By lunchtime two colleagues had ordered a second latte, and one had forwarded the link to her parents. The study behind the headline was real. What it actually showed was much smaller.

上周，我们办公室的群里转着一条标题：「每天三杯咖啡可能让你多活好几年。」到午饭时，两位同事已经点了第二杯拿铁，还有一位把链接转给了她父母。标题背后的研究是真的，但它实际说明的东西小得多。

The team did research of the most common kind. They followed tens of thousands of adults for several years, asked what they drank, and counted who died earlier. People who drank moderate amounts of coffee did slightly better on average. But this was an observational study. It can show that two things go together; it cannot show that one causes the other.

研究团队做的是最常见的那种研究：跟踪几万名成年人好几年，问他们喝什么，再数谁去世得早。适量喝咖啡的人平均略好一点。但这是一项观察性研究，它能说明两件事同时出现，不能说明一件导致另一件。

Coffee drinkers may differ in other ways. They may be healthier to begin with, since people who are already ill often give coffee up. They may have more money or more regular jobs. Good studies try to take these differences into account, but no study can measure everything. That is why scientists use careful phrases such as "linked to" and "associated with". Headlines, which need clicks, tend to drop them.

喝咖啡的人可能在别的方面也不一样。他们可能本来就更健康，因为已经生病的人常常会戒掉咖啡；他们可能更有钱，工作更规律。好的研究会尽量把这些差别考虑进去，但没有哪项研究能把一切都量到。所以科学家用「与……有关」「与……相关」这类谨慎的说法。标题要点击量，往往把这些词删掉。

None of this means the study was useless. A single result is one piece of evidence. When the same pattern appears in many studies, in different countries, and there is a plausible reason for it, the claim becomes stronger. Science makes progress this way, slowly. This study may have an impact on what researchers test next, but it is the first step, not the last word.

这并不是说这项研究没用。单个结果只是一份证据。同一规律在许多研究、许多国家里都出现，又有说得通的原因时，这个说法才更站得住。科学就是这样一点点推进的。这项研究可能会影响研究者下一步测什么，但它只是第一步，不是定论。

So what should a reader do? Take the headline with a pinch of salt, and before you change anything, ask three questions. Who was studied? Was it an experiment or an observation? How big was the difference? For most healthy adults, coffee in moderation seems to be fine. Whether it adds years is a question the evidence cannot answer yet.

那读者该怎么办？对标题别全信，在改变任何习惯之前，先问三个问题：研究的是谁？是实验还是观察？差别有多大？对大多数健康成年人来说，适量喝咖啡看来没问题。它能不能让人多活几年，现有证据还回答不了。

/ˈhedlaɪn/ n.

/ˌɒbzəˈveɪʃənəl ˈstʌdi/ n.

/ˈevɪdəns/ n. (uncountable)
证据

/kleɪm/ n. / v.

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

Good studies try to take these differences into account, but no study can measure everything.
take ... into account splits around a long object; no study can is stronger than studies cannot always.
take ... into account 中间可以夹很长的宾语；no study can 比 studies cannot always 语气更绝对。
It can show that two things go together; it cannot show that one causes the other.
The semicolon joins two balanced halves. one ... the other is the fixed pair for two things.
分号连接两个对称的半句。one ... the other 是指两者时的固定搭配。
