An Email About Workload 一封谈工作量的邮件

🧠 Learn the 5 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 5 个新词

Read an email that raises a workload problem early and calmly, and borrow its verb–noun collocations. 读一封及早、冷静地提出工作量问题的邮件，借用其中的动名搭配。

B2 · CELPIP 7–8⏱ 20 minV06 Register: too formal, too casual, or too blunt语域不合：太正式、太随意或太冲V12 Hedging is missing; claims sound absolute不会留余地，话说得太满

📖 Key card知识卡
Raise a workload problem while it is still a problem, with facts, effects and a proposal, before it turns into burnout.趁工作量还只是个问题时提出来，带上事实、影响和建议，别等它变成倦怠。

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Subject: Could we have a word about my workload? Hi Karen, I hope your week is going well. Could we have a word this week about my workload? I wanted to put a few thoughts in writing first, so that our conversation can be useful rather than emotional.

主题：能聊聊我的工作量吗？Karen 你好，希望你这周一切顺利。这周我们能谈一下我的工作量吗？我想先把几点想法写下来，好让我们的谈话有用，而不是情绪化。

Since March I have taken responsibility for two extra projects while we hire for the open position. I was glad to help, and I think the results have been good. But for the last six weeks I have been working most evenings and part of each weekend, and I am starting to notice the effects. I find it hard to switch off at night, I am sleeping badly, and I made two careless mistakes in last week's report.

从三月起，在招人填补空缺期间，我额外负责了两个项目。我乐意帮忙，结果我觉得也不错。但最近六周，我大多数晚上和每个周末的一部分时间都在工作，开始感觉到影响了：晚上很难把脑子关掉，睡得不好，上周的报告里还出了两个粗心的错。

I am telling you this now because I would rather raise it early than wait until it becomes burnout. I am not asking to drop anything immediately. I would like us to look at the list together and decide what can wait, what someone else could take on, and what I should keep.

我现在说，是因为我宁愿早点提，也不想等到它变成倦怠。我不是要求马上放下什么。我希望我们一起看看清单，定下哪些可以缓一缓，哪些可以交给别人，哪些我该继续做。

I have also made an effort on my side. I now take a proper break at lunch, and I have stopped answering messages after nine unless something is urgent. These changes help, but they cannot fix a workload that is simply too large for one person to cope with.

我自己这边也下了功夫：现在午饭时好好休息，九点以后除非有急事不再回消息。这些改变有帮助，但解决不了一个人根本应付不来的工作量。

I would really value your view on this, including if you see the situation differently. Would Thursday afternoon work for you? Thank you for reading, and for the support you have already given me this year. Best wishes, Ming

很想听听你的看法，如果你看法不同也请直说。周四下午你方便吗？谢谢你读完，也谢谢你今年已经给我的支持。祝好，Ming

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

I am telling you this now because I would rather raise it early than wait until it becomes burnout.
would rather + bare infinitive than + bare infinitive. until takes the present for a future event.
would rather + 原形 + than + 原形。until 引导将来的事用一般现在时。
But for the last six weeks I have been working most evenings and part of each weekend, and I am starting to notice the effects.
Present perfect continuous for a tiring activity up to now; it stresses the duration, not a result.
现在完成进行时表示一直持续到现在的累人活动，强调持续时间而不是结果。
