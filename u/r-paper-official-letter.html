How to Read an Official Letter in Thirty Seconds 三十秒读懂一封公函

🧠 Learn the 4 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 4 个新词

Learn the pattern most official letters follow so that you can find the action and the date first; notice articles and long nouns. 学会大多数公函遵循的套路，先找到要你做什么和截止日期；留意冠词和长名词。

B2 · CELPIP 7–8⏱ 20 minG01 Articles left out or over-used冠词漏用、滥用V03 Word family: the wrong part of speech词性用错

📖 Key card知识卡
Read an official letter in this order: who sent it and when, the reference number, the first paragraph, then the action and the date.读公函按这个顺序：谁寄的、什么时候、档案编号、第一段，然后是要你做什么、何时之前。

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Mr Zhou brought a letter to the community centre and asked the volunteer one question: "Is this bad?" It was a request for documents from a government office, two pages long, and he had read it four times without finding the answer. The volunteer found it in thirty seconds, because official letters follow a pattern.

周先生拿着一封信来到社区中心，只问了义工一个问题：「这是坏事吗？」那是政府部门要求补交文件的信，两页长，他读了四遍也没找到答案。义工三十秒就找到了，因为公函有固定的套路。

Start at the top. The name of the sender and the date tell you who wrote and when the clock started. Next to them is a reference number. Write it down, because every phone call and every reply will need it. Then read the first paragraph. In most official letters it says why they are writing: a decision, a request, a reminder, or a notice of a change. Some letters only acknowledge that something has arrived.

从顶部开始。寄信人的名字和日期告诉你谁写的、从什么时候开始计时。旁边是档案编号，把它记下来，因为以后每次打电话、每次回复都要用。然后读第一段。大多数公函的第一段会说明来信的原因：一个决定、一项要求、一次提醒，或一份变更通知。有些信只是确认某样东西已经收到。

Next, look for the action and the date. Phrases like "you must", "please provide" and "within" are the heart of the letter, so underline them. Everything else is explanation: which rule applies, why they need the information, and what penalty may follow if you do not comply. That part matters, but it is not the first thing you need. If a form is included, the word "Enclosure" usually appears at the bottom of the letter.

接着找动作和日期。「你必须」「请提供」「在……之内」这类说法是信的核心，把它们划出来。其余都是解释：适用哪条规定、为什么需要这些信息、不照办可能有什么处罚。这部分重要，但不是你首先需要的。如果信里附了表格，信的底部通常会有「附件」字样。

Mr Zhou's letter was not bad at all. It asked for two documents within a set number of days, which was not an unreasonable request, and it ended with the same sentence many such letters end with: if you do not respond, a decision may be made on the information already on file. A little awareness of how these letters are built takes away most of the fear. The expectation is simple: find the action, find the date, and respond before it.

周先生的信一点也不坏。它要求在规定天数内补交两份文件，这个要求并不过分；结尾是很多这类信都有的那句话：如果你不回复，将根据档案中已有的信息作出决定。稍微了解这类信是怎么搭起来的，大部分恐惧就没了。要做的很简单：找到动作，找到日期，在那之前回复。

/ˈsendə/ n.

/ˈrefrəns ˌnʌmbə/ n.

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

The name of the sender and the date tell you who wrote and when the clock started.
the sender, the date: the ones on this letter, so the. when the clock started is an idiom for "when the time limit began".
the sender、the date 指这封信上的，用 the。when the clock started 是习语，意为「时限从何时开始」。
It asked for two documents within a set number of days, which was not an unreasonable request, and it ended with the same sentence many such letters end with
which comments on the whole request. many such letters end with is a relative clause with the preposition at the end.
which 评论前面整个要求。many such letters end with 是介词放在句末的定语从句。
