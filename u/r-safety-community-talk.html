Safe, Not Scared 安全，但不害怕

🧠 Learn the 2 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 2 个新词

Read a community police officer's talk on everyday safety, and notice articles with places and routines (at home, to work) and phrasal verbs that keep a public talk friendly. 读一位社区警员关于日常安全的讲话，留意地点与日常活动前的冠词用法（at home、to work），以及让公开讲话显得亲切的短语动词。

The most common crimes are also the easiest to make harder; report them, because reports decide where patrols go.最常见的案件也最容易防；报案吧，因为报案决定巡逻往哪里去。

Read and listen读与听

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Thank you for having me. When I give this talk, someone usually asks whether the neighbourhood is getting more dangerous. The honest answer is that most people here are far more likely to have a parcel stolen than to be hurt, and the most common crimes are also the easiest to make harder.

谢谢大家请我来。每次讲这个题目，总有人问社区是不是越来越危险了。老实说，这里大多数人被偷包裹的可能性远大于受伤害，而最常见的案件也最容易防。

Start at home. Thieves look for the easy option: an unlocked door, a ladder left in the garden, a parcel sitting on the step all afternoon. Ask a neighbour to bring parcels in, or have them delivered to work. A lamp on a timer is a cheap deterrent when you go away for a few days.

先从家里说起。小偷找的是最省事的目标：没锁的门、扔在院子里的梯子、在门口台阶上放了一下午的包裹。请邻居帮你把包裹拿进去，或者寄到单位。出门几天时，定时开关的台灯是一种便宜的防范手段。

Then the car. Leave nothing visible, not even coins or a charging cable. Valuables belong in your bag or at home, not in the glove box. Most break-ins take less than a minute, so a thief will simply move on to the next car if yours looks empty.

再说车。什么都别露在外面，硬币和充电线也不行。贵重物品要么放包里，要么放家里，别放在手套箱里。大多数撬车用不了一分钟，你的车看着是空的，小偷就直接去下一辆了。

Online and on the phone, the same rule applies: nobody genuine needs your password, and anyone who rushes you is a warning sign. The people who are most vulnerable are not the ones who know the least; they are the ones who are alone when the call comes. So talk to each other, and call someone before you pay anyone.

网上和电话里，规矩是一样的：真正的机构不需要你的密码，任何催你的人都是警示信号。最容易受害的不是懂得最少的人，而是电话打来时身边没人的人。所以彼此多聊聊，付钱给任何人之前先给一个人打电话。

Finally, report things. People tell me they did not want to waste our time over a stolen bike. Please file a report anyway, online or through the non-emergency line, and save the emergency number for when someone is in danger or a crime is happening right now. Reports are how we figure out where problems are and set up patrols. Several of you have brought up the lane behind the library tonight, and I will follow up on that with my team this week.

最后，请报案。有人跟我说，不想为一辆被偷的自行车浪费我们的时间。请照样报案，网上报或打非紧急电话；紧急号码留给有人遇到危险或案件正在发生的时候。我们就是靠报案弄清问题在哪里、安排巡逻的。今晚好几位提到了图书馆后面那条小巷，这周我会和同事跟进。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

Valuables belong in your bag or at home, not in the glove box.
at home takes no article (a fixed place phrase), while the glove box takes the because every car has one and we know which.
at home 不加冠词（固定的地点短语）；the glove box 加 the，因为每辆车都有一个，指哪个大家都清楚。
The people who are most vulnerable are not the ones who know the least; they are the ones who are alone when the call comes.
A contrast frame: are not the ones who … ; they are the ones who …. Useful in speaking when you correct a common belief.
对比句框：are not the ones who …；they are the ones who …。口头纠正一个常见看法时很好用。

Give a three-minute talk to newcomers in your building about staying safe. Cover home, car and phone. Use at home, to work, the same, and at least three of set up, figure out, bring up, follow up, deal with. Record it and check that you did not add the before home or work.
给你楼里的新住户做三分钟安全讲话，讲家里、车和电话三块。用上 at home、to work、the same，以及 set up、figure out、bring up、follow up、deal with 中至少三个。录下来，检查 home 和 work 前有没有多加 the。
