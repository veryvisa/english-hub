The Wrong Turn That Helped Me 那个让我受益的错误转弯

🧠 Learn the 4 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 4 个新词

Getting lost during a journey can become a valuable learning experience when travellers stay calm and seek help. 在旅途中迷路并不可怕，只要保持冷静并寻求帮助，就能把它变成宝贵的学习经历。

Read and listen读与听

250 words
Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Last summer, I visited a coastal town that I had never been to before. I planned to walk from the railway station to a small hotel near the beach. Before leaving the station, I checked my phone and felt confident that I knew the way. The journey looked simple, and I expected to arrive in less than half an hour.

去年夏天，我去了一个从未到过的海滨小镇。我计划从火车站步行到海滩附近的一家小酒店。离开车站之前，我查看了手机，并自信地认为自己已经知道路线。整个行程看起来很简单，我预计不到半小时就能到达。

Unfortunately, I took a wrong turn at a busy intersection. After walking for several minutes, I realised that the buildings around me did not match the pictures on my map. I stopped beside a small shop and checked my location again. My phone battery was running low, so I knew I needed another way to find my destination.

不幸的是，我在一个繁忙的十字路口走错了方向。走了几分钟后，我发现周围的建筑与地图上的图片不一致。我在一家小商店旁停下来，再次查看自己的位置。手机电量正在减少，所以我知道自己需要通过其他方式找到目的地。

I decided to ask the shopkeeper for directions. I explained that I was trying to reach a hotel near the beach. She smiled and told me to walk straight ahead until I reached a large park. Then I needed to turn right and follow the road towards the sea. She also pointed out a colourful building that would help me recognise the correct street.

我决定向店主问路。我解释说自己想去海滩附近的一家酒店。她微笑着告诉我，一直向前走，直到到达一个大型公园。然后向右转，沿着通往海边的道路继续走。她还指出了一栋色彩鲜艳的建筑，可以帮助我辨认正确的街道。

I finally arrived at the hotel, a little later than expected but feeling much more relaxed. Although I had made a simple mistake, I had learned an important lesson: a phone is useful, but it should not be the only source of information. Since that day, I have paid more attention to landmarks and local advice when travelling. Getting lost once has made me a more confident traveller.

我最终到达了酒店，虽然比预期晚了一点，但心情放松了许多。尽管我犯了一个简单的错误，却从中学到了重要的一课：手机很有用，但不应该成为唯一的信息来源。从那天起，我旅行时更加注意地标和当地人的建议。那次迷路经历反而让我成为一个更加自信的旅行者。

Words词

/ˌɪntəˈsekʃən/ n.
十字路口；交叉路口
busy intersection; road intersection
There is a pedestrian crossing at the next intersection. 下一个十字路口有一处人行横道。
/teɪk ə rɒŋ tɜːn/ phr.
走错方向；拐错弯
take a wrong turn; accidentally take a wrong turn
We took a wrong turn and ended up near the airport. 我们走错了方向，结果到了机场附近。
/rʌn ləʊ/ phr. v.
逐渐减少；快要用完
battery runs low; supplies run low
My phone battery is running low, so I need a charger. 我的手机电量快用完了，所以我需要一个充电器。
/riːtʃ/ v.
到达；抵达
reach the station; reach your destination
We hope to reach the campsite before sunset. 我们希望在日落前到达露营地。
/ˈkɒnfɪdənt/ adj.
有信心的；自信的
feel confident; confident traveller
After several trips, she became confident about travelling alone. 经过几次旅行后，她对独自出行变得更加自信。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

My phone battery was running low, so I knew I needed another way to find my destination.
Was running low is the past continuous tense, describing a situation in progress at that point in the story, while so introduces the result.
Was running low使用过去进行时，描述故事当时正在发生的情况，而so引出由此产生的结果。
Although I had made a simple mistake, I had learned an important lesson: a phone is useful, but it should not be the only source of information.
Although introduces a concession, and the past perfect forms had made and had learned describe experiences viewed from a later point in the past.
Although引导让步状语从句，而had made和had learned使用过去完成时，从故事中较晚的过去时间点回顾此前的经历。
