A new survey of secondary school students has found that good study habits matter more than natural talent. Researchers asked hundreds of teenagers how they prepare for lessons, and the results surprised many teachers. Most students who made steady progress said they followed a simple routine every day.

一项针对中学生的新调查发现，良好的学习习惯比天赋更重要。研究人员询问了数百名青少年如何为上课做准备，结果让许多老师感到意外。大多数取得稳步进步的学生说，他们每天都遵循一套简单的作息。

According to the report, the most successful students did not study for longer than their classmates. Instead, they used their time more carefully. They wrote short notes after each class, reviewed them at the weekend, and asked questions when they did not understand something.

根据报告，最成功的学生学习的时间并不比同学更长。相反，他们更仔细地利用时间。他们每节课后写简短的笔记，周末复习，并在不懂的时候提问。

Many students also said that it is hard to keep up with homework when a week is full of activities. One student explained that she tries to hand in her work on time because she plans each evening before she goes to bed. Others admitted that they often leave tasks until the last minute.

许多学生还说，当一周被各种活动塞满时，很难跟上作业进度。一位学生解释说，她努力按时交作业，因为她每天晚上睡前都会做计划。另一些人承认，他们常常把任务拖到最后一刻。

Teachers say the findings are useful. One head of year said that schools should teach study skills as clearly as they teach maths or history. Without these skills, even clever students can fall behind. A parent group welcomed the report but warned that rest and sleep are just as important.

老师们说这些发现很有用。一位年级主任说，学校应该像教数学或历史那样清楚地教学习方法。没有这些技能，即使聪明的学生也可能落后。一个家长团体欢迎这份报告，但提醒说休息和睡眠同样重要。

The researchers plan to repeat the survey next year. They hope to find out whether students who change their habits really do make faster progress, or whether other things, such as support at home, play a bigger part.

研究人员计划明年重复这项调查。他们希望弄清楚，改变习惯的学生是否真的进步得更快，还是家庭支持等其他因素起了更大的作用。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

Most students who made steady progress said they followed a simple routine every day.
The relative clause ‘who made steady progress’ tells us which students, and ‘they followed a simple routine’ is reported speech with the word ‘that’ left out.
关系从句 ‘who made steady progress’ 说明是哪些学生；'they followed a simple routine' 是省略了 that 的转述内容。
Without these skills, even clever students can fall behind.
’Without’ plus a noun phrase works like an ‘if not’ condition, and ‘even’ stresses that the result is surprising.
’Without’ 加名词短语相当于 ‘if not’ 的条件；'even' 强调这个结果出人意料。
