📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Open the full explanation: 5 points展开细讲：5 段讲解

Chinese has no separate small word after most adjectives and verbs, so learners pick the English preposition that "feels" right. That guess is wrong surprisingly often: good in maths for good at, the reason of for the reason for, depend of for depend on. The only fix is to store the preposition with the word.

中文的形容词、动词后面大多不跟一个独立的小词，所以学习者会凭感觉挑英语介词，而这种猜测出错率惊人：good in maths 应为 good at，the reason of 应为 the reason for，depend of 应为 depend on。唯一的办法是把介词和词一起存。

The same root can change its preposition when it changes its part of speech. You are interested in something, but you have an interest in it; you apply for a job but make an application for it. Most of the time the noun keeps the verb's preposition, but check each pair once.

同一个词根换了词性，介词可能也跟着变。interested in，名词是 an interest in；apply for，名词是 an application for。多数时候名词沿用动词的介词，但每组都要核一次。

A preposition is followed by a noun or an -ing form, never by to + verb. I'm interested in joining, not interested to join when you mean an ongoing interest. She apologised for being late, not apologised to be late.

介词后面接名词或 -ing，不接 to 加动词原形：I'm interested in joining，表示一贯兴趣时不说 interested to join；She apologised for being late，不说 apologised to be late。

Some verbs take no preposition at all where Chinese speakers add one: discuss the plan (not discuss about), enter the room, contact the office, answer the email. Some take one where you would not expect it: insist on, consist of, complain about.

有些动词不需要介词，华人却常加一个：discuss the plan（不说 discuss about）、enter the room、contact the office、answer the email。也有些动词要介词，你却想不到：insist on、consist of、complain about。

Nouns after increase, rise and fall need in for the thing that changes and of for the amount: an increase in rent of a few per cent. Mixing the two is one of the most common errors in reports.

increase、rise、fall 后面，变化的东西用 in，幅度用 of：an increase in rent of a few per cent。写报告时两者混用是最常见的错误之一。

✓ We discussed the budget.
