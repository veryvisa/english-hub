The Letter Is the Problem, Not Your English 问题在信，不在你的英语

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Every week, at the community centre where I volunteer, someone brings in an official letter and asks me what it means. The person is rarely short of English. They are short of patience with sentences like "Failure to comply with the requirements outlined herein may result in the imposition of a penalty." That sentence means: if you do not do this, you may have to pay a fine.

我在社区中心做义工，每周都有人拿着公函来问我是什么意思。这些人很少是英语不够，他们是受不了这样的句子：「未能遵守本文所列要求者，可能被处以罚款。」这句话的意思是：你不做这件事，可能要交罚款。

Plain language is not about making things childish. It is about respect for the reader's time. When readers understand the deadline, they meet the deadline. When they do not, they phone, they guess, or they put the letter aside. Offices then spend money chasing late forms, granting an extension to people who never understood the first letter, and charging late fees that nobody wanted.

说人话不是把事情幼稚化，而是尊重读者的时间。读者看懂了截止日期，就会按期办。看不懂，他们就打电话、靠猜，或者把信搁一边。结果机构还得花钱去催迟交的表格，给那些压根没看懂第一封信的人批延期，再收一笔谁都不想要的滞纳金。

If official letters were written for the people who read them, much of that cost would disappear. Imagine a letter that started with the action and the date: "Please send us these two documents by the end of next month." Then the reason, then the details. People with unusual circumstances would still call, but far fewer would. Some legal terms would stay, because they have exact meanings, but they could go in a short box at the end with a one-line explanation each.

如果公函是写给读它的人看的，这些成本大多会消失。设想一封信开头就是动作和日期：「请在下月底前把这两份文件寄给我们。」接着说原因，然后才是细节。情况特殊的人还是会打电话，但会少得多。有些法律术语会保留，因为它们含义精确，但可以放在末尾的小方框里，每个配一行解释。

Until that happens, readers need a little self-defence: find the action, find the date, respond before it, and do not be ashamed to ask. A confusing letter is not a test of your English. Expecting every reader to decode jargon is an unreasonable expectation, and clear writing is a matter of willingness more than ability. In my experience, the offices that have made the change have seen a real improvement and have never wanted to go back.

在那一天到来之前，读者需要一点自我保护：找到动作，找到日期，在日期前回复，别不好意思问。一封让人看不懂的信，考的不是你的英语。指望每个读者都能破译行话，本身就是不合理的期待；写得清楚，靠的更多是意愿而不是能力。据我所见，已经改过来的机构都看到了实实在在的改善，没有一家想再改回去。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

They are short of patience with sentences like "Failure to comply with the requirements outlined herein may result in the imposition of a penalty."
The quoted sentence hides its verbs in nouns: failure, imposition. outlined herein = described in this letter.
引号里的句子把动词藏进了名词：failure、imposition。outlined herein 即「本信所列」。
If official letters were written for the people who read them, much of that cost would disappear.
Unreal present: were written (passive) + would disappear. The writer imagines a different present.
与现在事实相反：were written（被动）＋would disappear，作者在设想另一种现状。
