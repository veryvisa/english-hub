Diphthongs that glide all the way 读满的双元音

Glide all the way through /eɪ/, /aɪ/, /əʊ/, /aʊ/ and /ɔɪ/, so that late does not become let, tired does not become tied, and coat does not become caught. 把 /eɪ/、/aɪ/、/əʊ/、/aʊ/、/ɔɪ/ 滑到底，late 不变 let、tired 不变 tied、coat 不变 caught。

B1 · CELPIP 5–6⏱ 20 minP12 /æ/ and /e/ merge (bad / bed), /eɪ/ shortened to /e/æ 与 e 不分，双元音读不满

📏 The rule规则

📏 The point in one line一句话要领
A diphthong is a movement, not a position: start on the first vowel, then move the jaw and tongue clearly towards the second, with the first part longer and stronger.双元音是一个动作，不是一个位置：从第一个元音起，下巴和舌头明显地滑向第二个元音，前半段更长更重。

📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Open the full explanation: 5 points展开细讲：5 段讲解

Mandarin has its own diphthongs (ai, ei, ao, ou), so the problem is not that the sounds are unknown. The problem is that in fast or careful speech Chinese speakers often stop the glide halfway. /eɪ/ ends up as a flat /e/: late sounds like let, paper like pepper, sale like sell. /əʊ/ ends up as /ɔː/: coat like caught, low like law.

普通话有自己的复韵母（ai、ei、ao、ou），所以问题不在于没听过这些音，而在于快说或刻意说时，华人常常滑到一半就停。/eɪ/ 变成扁平的 /e/，/əʊ/ 变成 /ɔː/。

Check the movement with a mirror. For /eɪ/ the jaw closes slightly and the lips spread as you move from /e/ to /ɪ/. For /aɪ/ the jaw starts wide open and closes. For /aʊ/ it starts open and the lips round at the end. For /əʊ/ (British) or /oʊ/ (North American) the lips must round by the end. If nothing moves in the mirror, you said a single vowel.

照镜子检查。/eɪ/ 下巴略合、嘴角向两边；/aɪ/ 从大开口到合；/aʊ/ 从开口到圆唇收尾；/əʊ/（英式）或 /oʊ/（北美）结尾必须圆唇。镜子里没动，就是读成了单元音。

The glide is shorter before voiceless consonants and longer before voiced ones or at the end of a word: right is clipped, ride is longer, rye is longest. This is one of the main cues listeners use to hear the final consonant, so a full glide also fixes many -t / -d confusions.

双元音在清辅音前短、在浊辅音前或词尾长：right 短促，ride 长一些，rye 最长。听者靠这个判断词尾辅音，所以滑满双元音也顺带解决了很多 -t/-d 混淆。

The triphthong sequences in tired, fire, hour, player and lower are also often flattened: tired becomes tied or tard. In British English these are often smoothed by native speakers too (fire close to fah), but the first vowel stays long. Keep at least two clear parts: ti-ed, fi-uh, ow-uh.

tired、fire、hour、player 里的三合元音也常被读扁：tired 成了 tied。英式母语者自己也常把它们读得平滑一些，但第一个元音保持长。至少保留两段：ti-ed、fi-uh、ow-uh。

Accent note: North American speakers glide /eɪ/ and /oʊ/ strongly; Scottish and some northern English speakers use almost pure vowels there. If you live in Canada, a full glide is the safe choice. Canadian speakers also often raise the start of /aɪ/ and /aʊ/ before voiceless sounds (about, house, price), which is why about can sound to outsiders a little like a-boat; this is covered in the unit on accents.

口音提示：北美的 /eɪ/、/oʊ/ 滑动很明显，苏格兰和英格兰北部部分口音几乎是单元音。生活在加拿大，滑满是稳妥的选择。加拿大人在清辅音前还常把 /aɪ/、/aʊ/ 的起点抬高（about、house、price），详见口音单元。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ Sorry I'm let.
✓ Sorry I'm late. /leɪt/
A flat /e/ turns late into let.
读平的 /e/，late 就成了 let。
✗ I bought a new boat. (when bought and boat sound the same)
✓ I bought /bɔːt/ a new boat /bəʊt/.
Round the lips at the end of boat to keep them apart.
boat 结尾圆唇，两个词才分得开。
✗ I'm very tied.
✓ I'm very tired. /ˈtaɪəd/
Keep the second part of the glide.
保住滑音的后半段。
✗ Please hand me the pen (meaning the cooking pan or the pain cream).
✓ Say each vowel fully: pen /pen/, pain /peɪn/, pan /pæn/.
Three different words that merge when the glide and the open /æ/ are lost.
滑音和开口 /æ/ 都丢掉，这三个词就合并了。
