Library Branch to Close for Two Years 社区分馆将关闭两年

🧠 Learn the 5 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 5 个新词

Read a local news report and notice how reporters pass on claims and estimates without naming every source. 读一篇社区新闻，留意记者怎样不逐一点名就转述说法和估计。

📖 Key card知识卡
A closed library is not only a closed building; it is a missing room for the people who had nowhere else to sit.一座图书馆关门，关掉的不只是一栋楼，还有那些没别处可坐的人的一个房间。

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

The Eastside branch library will close at the end of March for a full renovation, the city confirmed on Monday. The work is expected to take about two years, and the branch is due to reopen in the spring after next. By then, the building will have been serving the neighbourhood for almost sixty years.

市政府周一确认，东区分馆将在三月底关闭，进行全面翻修。工程预计约两年，分馆定于后年春天重新开放。到那时，这栋楼已经为社区服务了将近六十年。

The old roof is said to have leaked for at least a decade, and parts of the heating system are believed to date from the original construction. It is estimated that the branch receives several hundred visitors on a normal weekday, many of them seniors, students and parents with young children.

据说旧屋顶至少漏了十年，供暖系统的部分设备据信是建馆时的原装。据估计，分馆平常一个工作日接待几百名读者，其中很多是老人、学生和带小孩的父母。

Not everyone is convinced that two years is realistic. "Every project like this is supposed to finish on time," said one long-time volunteer, who asked not to be named. "By next winter we'll still be waiting, and the kids will have lost two years of story time."

并不是所有人都相信两年能完工。「这种工程按说都该按时完，」一位多年的义工说，她要求不具名。「到明年冬天我们还会在等，孩子们会少了两年的故事时间。」

City staff say a small pop-up library will be opened in the community centre during the closure. Books will be moved there in stages, and a weekly homework club is expected to continue in the same room. Residents are being asked which services matter most to them, and an online survey will be running until the end of February.

市里的工作人员说，关闭期间会在社区中心开一个小型临时图书馆。图书会分批搬过去，每周一次的作业辅导预计也会在同一个房间继续。市里正在征询居民最看重哪些服务，网上调查会开到二月底。

For now, the shelves are full and the reading room is busy. Whether the new building will feel like the old one is a question nobody can answer yet.

眼下，书架还是满的，阅览室还是很热闹。新楼会不会还有老楼的感觉，现在谁也答不上来。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

The old roof is said to have leaked for at least a decade
is said to have done: the report is now, the leaking was earlier and lasted a long time.
is said to have done：转述是现在，漏水是更早的事，而且持续了很久。
By then, the building will have been serving the neighbourhood for almost sixty years.
Future perfect continuous: the length of an activity up to a future point.
将来完成进行时：到将来某刻为止某活动持续了多久。
It is estimated that the branch receives several hundred visitors on a normal weekday
It is estimated that + clause gives a figure without naming who counted.
It is estimated that 加从句：给出数字而不说是谁统计的。
