The Sofa with a Deadline 带截止时间的沙发

🧠 Learn the 3 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 3 个新词

Follow two friends on the phone as one of them is pushed to buy a sofa tonight, and hear phrasal verbs doing the work of everyday persuasion. 跟着两个朋友的一通电话，其中一人正被催着今晚买沙发，听日常劝说里短语动词怎么用。

B2 · CELPIP 7–8⏱ 20 minV04 Phrasal verbs avoided; the formal verb sounds stiff回避短语动词，说话太书面V06 Register: too formal, too casual, or too blunt语域不合：太正式、太随意或太冲

📖 Key card知识卡
A seller's time limit is designed to stop you thinking; set your own deadline instead.卖家的时限就是为了让你停止思考；换成你自己定的截止时间。

Read and listen读与听

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Jun: I'm at the furniture store. They've got the sofa we liked, and the salesman says the deal ends tonight. My gut feeling says I should just buy it. Lena: Tonight? That's convenient for him. Did you measure the living room? Jun: Roughly. It'll fit. Probably.

俊：我在家具店。他们有我们看中的那款沙发，销售说这个优惠今晚就结束。我的直觉说干脆买了吧。莉娜：今晚？对他来说可真方便。你量过客厅了吗？俊：大概量过。放得下。应该吧。

Lena: Look, I'm not saying don't buy it. I'm saying don't let a deadline make the decision for you. When someone gives you a time limit, you stop weighing up options and start worrying about losing out. That's exactly what they want; it's how they deal with customers who hesitate. Jun: So what do I tell him? I can't just turn him down after he spent an hour with me.

莉娜：听着，我不是说别买。我是说别让一个截止时间替你做决定。别人给你设时限，你就不再权衡选项，开始担心错过。这正是他们想要的，他们就是这样对付犹豫的顾客的。俊：那我跟他说什么？他陪了我一个小时，我总不能直接拒绝他吧。

Lena: Of course you can. Say you need to sleep on it and you'll call him tomorrow. If the deal is real, it'll still be there, or there'll be another one next month. Stores have sales all the time. Jun: And if it isn't there tomorrow? Lena: Then you've lost a sofa, not your savings.

莉娜：当然可以。就说你要回去想一晚，明天给他打电话。要是这个优惠是真的，明天还在，要么下个月还会有别的。商店一直在打折。俊：那万一明天没了呢？莉娜：那你丢的是一张沙发，不是你的积蓄。

Jun: Fine. But you know me. If I put it off, I'll second-guess it for a month and never decide. Lena: Then let's come up with a rule. Tonight you measure the room and read the reviews. Tomorrow at lunch, if it still feels right, you buy it. No more thinking after that. Jun: A deadline of my own. I like that better than his.

俊：好吧。可你了解我。我一拖，就会反复琢磨一个月，永远决定不了。莉娜：那我们定个规矩。今晚你量房间、看评价。明天午饭时如果还觉得合适，就买。之后不再想。俊：我自己的截止时间。比他那个好。

Lena: And if you figure out it doesn't fit, you've saved yourself a very awkward return. Jun: Okay, okay. I'm walking out now. He's looking at me like I've broken his heart. Lena: He'll get over it.

莉娜：要是你发现放不下，就省了一次很尴尬的退货。俊：好好好，我这就出去。他看我的眼神就像我伤了他的心。莉娜：他会缓过来的。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

I can't just turn him down after he spent an hour with me.
turn him down: with a pronoun, it must go in the middle. just here means "simply", showing he feels it would be rude.
turn him down：代词必须放中间。这里的 just 意为「就这么」，表示他觉得这样不礼貌。
If I put it off, I'll second-guess it for a month and never decide.
First conditional: a real, likely result (put … will). put it off: delay; the pronoun again sits in the middle.
第一类条件句：真实可能的结果（put … will）。put it off：拖延，代词同样放中间。
