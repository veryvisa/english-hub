My Grandfather’s Silence 祖父的沉默

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

When I was a teenager, I used to think my grandfather had nothing to say about the past. He would sit by the window every evening, listen politely to our questions about the old country, and change the subject. I took his silence for indifference, and it did not occur to me that it might be something else.

我十几岁的时候，总觉得祖父对过去无话可说。每天傍晚他坐在窗边，礼貌地听我们问起故乡的事，然后转移话题。我把他的沉默当成了漠不关心，从没想到那可能是别的什么。

Years later, after he had died, I found a shoebox in the attic. It held a handful of photographs, a ticket stub and a letter he had never sent. None of the faces were labelled. I spent an afternoon on the floor trying to work out who these people were, and realised, with some embarrassment, that I could not name a single one.

多年后，他去世了，我在阁楼里发现了一个鞋盒。里面有几张照片、一张票根和一封他从未寄出的信。照片上的人脸都没有标注。我在地板上花了一个下午，想弄清这些人是谁，带着几分羞愧意识到，我一个也叫不出名字。

My aunt, who is the family’s unofficial archivist, filled in some of the gaps. She told me that he had left home young, that he had lost touch with most of his relatives, and that he rarely spoke about it because every memory seemed to lead back to a loss. Although I wanted a complete story, what I received was a handful of fragments, some of which contradicted each other.

姑姑是家里非正式的档案管理员，她帮我补上了一些空白。她告诉我，他很年轻就离开了家，与大多数亲戚失去了联系，而且很少谈起这些，因为每一段记忆似乎都会引向一次失去。虽然我想要一个完整的故事，但我得到的只是一把碎片，其中有些还互相矛盾。

It took me a long time to come to terms with that. I had wanted the past to be a finished book, and instead it was a box of loose pages. Yet I gradually saw that his silence was itself a kind of memory, one that bore witness to what he could not put into words.

我花了很长时间才接受这一点。我曾希望过去是一本写完的书，可它其实是一盒散页。然而我渐渐明白，他的沉默本身就是一种记忆，它见证了他无法诉诸言语的那些事。

These days I label every photograph I take, on the back, in pencil. It is a small habit, and on the whole it feels like an apology to someone who is no longer here to accept it, and a gift to whoever opens the box after me.

如今我给自己拍的每张照片都在背面用铅笔写上标注。这是个小习惯，总的来说，它像是对一个已无法接受的人的歉意，也像是留给我之后打开这个盒子的人的一份礼物。

Words词

/kʌm tə tɜːmz wɪð/ phr.
接受（令人难过的事实）；与……和解
come to terms with a loss; come to terms with the past
It took the village years to come to terms with the flood. 这个村子花了好几年才接受那场洪水带来的打击。
/bɔː ˈwɪtnəs/ phr.
见证；作证（bear witness 的过去式）
bear witness to suffering; bore witness to the events
Her diary bore witness to a difficult childhood. 她的日记见证了一段艰难的童年。
/ˈfræɡmənts/ n.
碎片；片段
fragments of memory; broken fragments
Only fragments of the original letter survived. 原信只有一些残片留存了下来。
/ɒn ðə həʊl/ adv. phr.
总的来说
on the whole positive; on the whole, though
On the whole, the trip was a success, despite the weather. 总的来说，尽管天气不好，这次旅行还是成功的。
/lɒst tʌtʃ wɪð/ phr.
与……失去联系
lose touch with friends; lose touch with reality
I lost touch with my classmates soon after we graduated. 毕业后不久，我就和同学们失去了联系。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

I had wanted the past to be a finished book, and instead it was a box of loose pages.
The past perfect ‘had wanted’ shows the wish came before the discovery, and the verb pattern ‘want + object + to-infinitive’ links the wish to what the past should be.
过去完成时 had wanted 表示这个愿望早于后来的发现；want + 宾语 + 不定式（the past to be...）表示希望过去成为什么样子。
Although I wanted a complete story, what I received was a handful of fragments, some of which contradicted each other.
’Although’ introduces a concession, ‘what I received’ is a noun clause acting as the subject, and ‘some of which’ is a non-defining relative clause about the fragments.
Although 引出让步；what I received 是名词性从句作主语；some of which 引导非限制性定语从句，指代 fragments。
