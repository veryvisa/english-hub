Present perfect or past simple 现在完成时还是一般过去时

Choose between I have done and I did without translating from the Chinese particles le and guo. 不靠「了」「过」硬套，自己判断该用 I have done 还是 I did。

I've lived in Calgary for six years. 我在卡尔加里住了六年了。
Still living there; the period reaches now.现在还住着，这段时间一直延续到现在。
I lived in Calgary for six years. 我在卡尔加里住过六年。
That period is over; I live somewhere else.那段时间已结束，现在不住那儿了。
I've been living in Calgary since March. 我从三月起一直住在卡尔加里。
Ongoing; the continuous often hints that it may not be permanent.正在持续；进行式常带「未必长久」的意味。

📏 The rule规则

📏 The rule in one line规则一句话
Past simple puts an event at a finished time. Present perfect connects a past event to now, so it does not say when the event happened.一般过去时把事情放在一个已经结束的时间点上；现在完成时把过去的事和「现在」连起来，所以不交代那件事发生在哪个时间点。

📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Open the full explanation: 6 points, a table, 13 examples展开细讲：6 段讲解、一张表、13 个例句

Chinese marks completion with le and experience with guo, but neither word says when. English makes you decide first: is the time finished (yesterday, in 2019, when I was a student) or does it still reach the present (today, this year, so far, ever)?

中文用「了」表完成、用「过」表经历，但都不交代时间。英语要你先判断：时间已经结束（昨天、2019 年、上学时），还是一直连到现在（今天、今年、到目前为止、这辈子）。

If the sentence names a finished time, or the listener already knows which occasion you mean, use the past simple. A question with when takes the past simple, because the answer will be a finished time. (Since when have you known? and the rhetorical When have I ever lied to you? are different: neither asks for a date.)

句子里有已结束的时间，或者对方已经知道你说的是哪一次，就用一般过去时。用 when 问时间就用一般过去时，因为答案是一个已结束的时间。（Since when have you known? 和反问句 When have I ever lied to you? 不是在问日期，不受此限。）

Use the present perfect for three jobs: life experience with no time given (I've been to Banff), a state that started in the past and is still true (I've lived here for six years), and a recent event whose result matters now (I've lost my keys, so I can't get in).

现在完成时管三件事：不说时间的人生经历；从过去持续到现在、现在仍成立的状态；刚发生、结果影响现在的事（钥匙丢了，所以现在进不去）。

Conversations usually open with the present perfect and continue in the past simple. The first sentence puts the topic on the table; the details then belong to one finished occasion.

对话通常用现在完成时开头，接着用一般过去时讲细节。第一句把话题摆出来，后面的细节属于那一次已结束的经历。

for works with both tenses, and the tense changes the meaning. I worked there for two years means the job is over. I've worked there for two years normally means you are still there. since is stricter: in a sentence about a period that reaches now, the main verb is in the perfect.

for 两种时态都能配，意思不同。I worked there for two years 表示已经离职；I've worked there for two years 一般表示现在还在那里。since 更严：说一段延续到现在的时间，主句动词用完成时。

You will hear North Americans use the past simple for recent news and with just, already and yet: I just sent it, Did you eat yet? It is normal speech there, not a mistake. The present perfect is also correct in North America, and it is the safer choice in writing and in exams. The rule about finished times does not change anywhere.

在北美，你会听到人们用一般过去时说刚发生的事，也和 just、already、yet 连用：I just sent it；Did you eat yet? 这在当地口语里很正常，不算错。现在完成时在北美同样正确，写作和考试里更稳妥。至于「已结束的时间用一般过去时」，哪里都一样。

Examples例句

  • I've been to Toronto twice. 我去过两次多伦多。
    No time is given: this is life experience.没说时间：这是经历。
  • I went to Toronto last March. 我去年三月去了多伦多。
    last March is a finished time.last March 是已结束的时间。
  • Have you eaten yet? — Yes, I had a sandwich at twelve. 你吃了吗？——吃了，十二点吃了个三明治。
    The question reaches to now; the answer names a finished time.问句连到现在；答句给出已结束的时间。
  • She has worked here since 2021. 她从 2021 年起一直在这儿工作。
    She still works here.现在还在。
  • She worked here from 2018 to 2021. 她 2018 到 2021 年在这儿工作。
    The period is closed.这段时间已经结束。
  • I've just sent you the file. 我刚把文件发给你了。
    Recent event; the result is that you can open it now.刚发生，结果是你现在可以打开。
  • Did you see the email this morning? 你上午看到那封邮件了吗？
    Said in the afternoon: this morning is finished.下午说这话：上午已经过去了。
  • Prices have gone up a lot this year. 今年物价涨了不少。
    this year is not finished.今年还没过完。
  • Prices went up a lot last year. 去年物价涨了不少。
    last year is finished.去年已经结束。
  • How long have you known about this? 这事你知道多久了？
    A state that reaches now.一直延续到现在的状态。
  • I've met her husband, but I don't remember where. 我见过她丈夫，但记不得在哪儿了。
    Experience; the occasion is not named.经历，没说是哪一次。
  • He grew up in Regina and has never really liked the cold. 他在里贾纳长大，却从来没真正喜欢过寒冷。
    Childhood is over; the dislike still holds.童年已结束；不喜欢一直到现在。
  • Since we moved here, the children have changed schools twice. 自从我们搬来，孩子已经换了两次学校。
    since + past simple names the start; the main verb is perfect.since 后的一般过去时点出起点，主句用完成时。

More pairs: same idea, different choice更多对比句组

Have you seen the email from HR? 你看到人事发的邮件了吗？
It matters now: you may need to act.与现在有关：你可能得处理。
Did you see the email this morning? 你今天上午看到那封邮件了吗？
this morning is finished, so past simple.上午已经过去，用一般过去时。
I sent the report on Monday. 我周一把报告发了。
A finished time is named.说出了已结束的时间点。
I've sent the report. 报告我已经发了。
No time: the result is what counts.不说时间，重要的是结果。
I worked with Dana for three years. 我和达娜共事过三年。
We no longer work together.现在不再共事。
I've worked with Dana for three years. 我和达娜共事三年了。
We still work together.现在还在共事。
I've worked with Dana before. 我以前和达娜共事过。
Experience at some time; nothing about now.以前有过的经历，没说现在如何。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ I have seen him yesterday.
✓ I saw him yesterday.
yesterday is a finished time, so the present perfect is impossible.
yesterday 是已结束的时间，不能用现在完成时。
✗ I live here for three years.
✓ I've lived here for three years.
A state that began in the past and continues now needs the present perfect, not the present simple.
从过去持续到现在的状态用现在完成时，不能用一般现在时。
✗ When have you arrived?
✓ When did you arrive?
A when question asks for a finished time.
when 问的是一个已结束的时间。
✗ I have been to Japan in 2018.
✓ I went to Japan in 2018.
Translating qu guo as have been fails as soon as a year is named.
把「去过」译成 have been，一旦带上年份就错了。
✗ My grandfather has worked as a doctor all his life. (He died in 2010.)
✓ My grandfather worked as a doctor all his life.
His life is a finished period.
他的一生是一段已结束的时间。
