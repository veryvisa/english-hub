Stop Blaming the Young 别再指责年轻人

Blaming young people is unfair, and listening to each other works better than arguing. 指责年轻人是不公平的，互相倾听比争论更有效。

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Every few years, a new label appears for the young, and every time the same complaints follow. They are lazy, they are glued to their phones, they will not commit to anything. I find this tiresome. While it is true that young people spend a lot of time online, that does not make them less capable than their parents were.

每隔几年，就会出现一个形容年轻人的新标签，而每次都伴随着同样的抱怨：他们懒惰，整天抱着手机，不肯对任何事做出承诺。我觉得这很令人厌烦。虽然年轻人确实花很多时间上网，但这并不意味着他们比父母那一代能力更差。

The generation gap is often a gap in circumstances, not in character. Today’s twenty-somethings face high rents, short contracts and a job market that changes faster than any career guide can keep up with. Anyone in their position would be likely to behave in much the same way. Calling them entitled is easy, but it ignores the facts.

代沟往往是处境上的差距，而不是性格上的差距。如今二十多岁的年轻人面对高昂的房租、短期合同，以及一个变化快到任何职业指南都跟不上的就业市场。换作任何人处在他们的位置，都很可能会有差不多的表现。说他们自视甚高很容易，但这忽视了事实。

Older people are not blameless either. Some of them are set in their ways and refuse to listen when a younger colleague suggests a better method. That said, I have met many older workers who are curious and generous, and who enjoy learning something new. I do not think either group deserves to be looked down on.

年长的人也并非毫无过错。有些人固守老习惯，当年轻同事提出更好的方法时也拒绝倾听。话虽如此，我也遇到过许多好奇而慷慨的年长同事，他们乐于学习新东西。我认为任何一方都不该被看不起。

What we need is not a winner in this argument but a habit of listening. If each side were willing to ask questions before making judgments, we would probably discover more common ground than we expect. By and large, people want the same things: security, respect and a chance to be useful.

我们需要的不是这场争论的赢家，而是倾听的习惯。如果双方都愿意在下判断之前先提问，我们大概会发现比预想中更多的共同点。总的来说，人们想要的东西是一样的：安全感、尊重，以及发挥作用的机会。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

If each side were willing to ask questions before making judgments, we would probably discover more common ground than we expect.
This is an unreal conditional: ‘if’ plus past ‘were’ in the first clause, and ‘would’ plus a verb in the main clause.
这是虚拟条件句：if 从句用过去式 were，主句用 would + 动词原形。
While it is true that young people spend a lot of time online, that does not make them less capable than their parents were.
’While’ introduces a concession, and ‘than their parents were’ leaves out the repeated word ‘capable’.
While 引出让步，than their parents were 省略了重复的 capable。
