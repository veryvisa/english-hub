Towns Struggle to Keep Pace with Ageing Residents 城镇难以跟上居民老龄化的步伐

Local councils across several rich countries are warning that an ageing population is reshaping towns faster than planners expected. A new survey of regional authorities suggests that schools are merging, bus routes are shrinking and care homes are struggling to recruit staff. Officials describe the change as gradual but relentless. Longer lives are part of the story, but falling birth rates matter just as much. Several have asked for emergency funds.

多个富裕国家的地方议会警告说，人口老龄化正在以超出规划者预期的速度重塑城镇。一项针对地区政府部门的新调查显示，学校正在合并，公交线路不断缩减，养老院则难以招到员工。官员们形容这一变化缓慢却无情。寿命延长只是原因之一，出生率下降的影响同样重大。若干议会已申请紧急资金。

The strain is most visible in health services. Hospitals report that a growing share of beds is occupied by patients who are medically fit to leave but have nowhere suitable to go. Family doctors say appointments take longer because older patients tend to have several conditions at once. Staff shortages make matters worse. While nobody disputes the value of long life, many managers admit that budgets were not designed with it in mind.

压力在医疗服务领域最为明显。医院报告称，越来越多的床位被那些医学上已可出院、却无处可去的患者占用。家庭医生表示，因为老年患者往往同时患有多种疾病，门诊时间变得更长。人手短缺使情况雪上加霜。虽然没有人否认长寿的价值，但许多管理者承认，预算在设计之初并未考虑到这一点。

Some places are responding creatively. One coastal town has converted an empty school into flats for older residents, with a nursery on the ground floor. Another has trained volunteers to check on neighbours who live alone. Early results suggest that younger families are also moving in, drawn by cheaper housing. Such schemes cannot solve the problem alone, but they ease the pressure on overstretched services.

一些地方正在富有创意地应对。一个沿海小镇把一所闲置的学校改建成老年人公寓，一楼还设有托儿所。另一个地方培训了志愿者，定期探望独居的邻居。初步结果显示，年轻家庭也被较低的房价吸引而搬了进来。这类举措单靠自身无法解决问题，但能缓解不堪重负的服务体系所承受的压力。

Central government has promised a review of how care is funded, though it has not said when it will report. Opposition members have called the delay unacceptable. Campaigners argue that, by and large, ministers have been slow to accept that the country must shoulder the burden together. Meanwhile, residents in the worst-affected villages are organising their own transport. Councils say they will keep collecting evidence.

中央政府已承诺对护理经费的筹措方式进行审查，但没有说明何时公布结果。反对派议员称这一拖延不可接受。倡导人士指出，总体而言，部长们一直迟迟不肯接受这个事实：整个国家必须共同承担这一负担。与此同时，受影响最严重的村庄里，居民们正自行组织交通。各地议会表示将继续收集证据。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

While nobody disputes the value of long life, many managers admit that budgets were not designed with it in mind.
The sentence opens with a ‘While’ clause that concedes a point, and the main clause then states the real problem; ‘with it in mind’ is a fixed phrase meaning ‘considering it’.
句子以 While 引导的从句先作让步，主句再说出真正的问题；'with it in mind' 是固定搭配，意为'考虑到它'。
Such schemes cannot solve the problem alone, but they ease the pressure on overstretched services.
The two clauses are joined by ‘but’ to balance a limitation against a benefit, and ‘alone’ works as an adverb meaning ‘by themselves’.
两个分句用 but 连接，在局限与益处之间取得平衡；alone 在此作副词，意为'单靠自身'。
