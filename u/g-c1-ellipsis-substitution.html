Ellipsis and substitution 省略与替代

📘 In your coursebook: English File 5e · Advanced · 9A “Animal rights…and wrongs?”. Use the texts and recordings with your coursebook; this page adds new words, grammar and practice.教材定位：English File 5e · Advanced · 9A《Animal rights…and wrongs?》。课文与录音请配合教材学习，本页补充新词、语法与练习。

Use ellipsis and substitution (do so, so, not, one/ones, do it/that, auxiliary-only reduction, to-infinitive reduction) to avoid repetition while keeping the reference unambiguous. 能用省略与替代(do so、so/not、one/ones、助动词省略、不定式省略等)避免重复,同时保证指代清楚。

Anyone who wants to leave may do so. 想离开的人都可以离开。
Formal and neutral; refers to a controllable action as a unit, typical of rules, notices and writing.正式、中性;把可控动作当作整体回指,常见于规定、告示和书面语。
Anyone who wants to leave may. 想离开的人都可以。
Short and slightly clipped; relies on the modal alone, natural in speech but sounds abrupt in formal documents.简短、略显生硬;仅靠情态动词,口语自然,但在正式文件中显得突兀。
Anyone who wants to leave can do it. 想离开的人都能做到。
Casual; do it points at a specific act and sounds like a task being completed, not a permission.随意;do it 指向某个具体行为,听起来像完成一项任务而非许可。

Ellipsis drops words the listener can recover from context, while substitution replaces them with a light proxy word, so the choice depends on whether the gap alone would stay clear.省略是删掉听者能从语境补出的词,替代是用一个轻量的代词性词语顶替;关键在于只留空位时指代是否仍然清楚。

Choose ellipsis when the surviving auxiliary or particle already tells the listener exactly what is missing: 'She can swim and I can too' is enough, and 'I can swim too' would sound heavier. Choose substitution with do so or do it when the verb phrase needs to be present as a unit, for example after a modal that has already been used in a different form: 'He promised to resign, and he did so on Friday.' Do so is also the natural choice in formal writing, where a bare 'did' can feel clipped.

当保留下来的助动词或小品词已经足以让听者还原被省略的内容时,选省略:'She can swim and I can too' 就够了,再说完整反而累赘。当动词短语需要作为一个整体出现时,选 do so 或 do it 替代,例如前面用的是另一种形式:'He promised to resign, and he did so on Friday.' 正式书面语中也更偏好 do so,因为单独的 did 显得过于简短。

Do so, do it and do that are not interchangeable. Do so is formal and takes only controllable actions; do it and do that are neutral and often refer to a specific action already mentioned, with 'that' pointing back more strongly. Compare 'Anyone who wishes to leave may do so' with 'Someone has to tell her, and I can't do it.' If the earlier verb is stative, none of them works well, and you should repeat the verb or restructure.

do so、do it、do that 不能随便互换。do so 偏正式,只用于可控的动作;do it 和 do that 中性,常回指已提到的具体行为,that 的回指感更强。试比较 'Anyone who wishes to leave may do so' 和 'Someone has to tell her, and I can't do it.' 如果前面的动词是状态动词,这三者都不自然,应重复动词或改写句子。

With reporting and attitude verbs, so and not replace an entire clause, but each verb has its own preferred negative. 'I think so' and 'I'm afraid so' have two negatives: 'I don't think so' for think, believe and suppose, but 'I hope not', 'I'm afraid not' and 'I guess not' for the others. Using 'I don't hope so' is a common error, because hope and be afraid attach the negation to so, which is why they take not directly.

在表示观点或转述的动词后,so 和 not 可以替代整个从句,但不同动词偏好的否定形式不同。think、believe、suppose 用 'I don't think so';而 hope、be afraid、guess 常用 'I hope not'、'I'm afraid not'、'I guess not'。'I don't hope so' 是常见错误,因为 hope 和 be afraid 是把否定放在替代词 not 上。

One and ones replace a countable noun, but only a noun that is not already specific. They work after an adjective, a determiner like 'this' or 'which', and a relative clause: 'I prefer the red one,' 'Which ones did you buy?' You cannot use one after a possessive or after a bare number as a substitute for an uncountable noun, so 'I like his one' is wrong, and 'Do you have any milk? I'd like a cold one' is also wrong, where you must repeat 'milk' or restructure.

one/ones 用来替代可数名词,且该名词本身尚不特指。它可以接在形容词、this/which 等限定词和关系从句之后:'I prefer the red one'、'Which ones did you buy?' 但不能用在所有格之后,也不能替代不可数名词,所以 'I like his one' 是错的,'I'd like a cold one'(指牛奶)也不行,需要重复 milk 或改写。

Reducing an infinitive to bare 'to' is the standard way to avoid repeating a verb phrase after want, try, hope, refuse, ought, and have, as in 'She didn't come, though I asked her to.' Keep be, have and a progressive form when they are the first auxiliary in the omitted phrase: 'He isn't working, but he should be.' Dropping the final 'be' here, which many learners do, makes the sentence ungrammatical because the stranded 'should' has nothing left to attach to.

在 want、try、hope、refuse、ought、have 等词后,只保留 to 是避免重复动词短语的标准做法,如 'She didn't come, though I asked her to.' 当被省略部分以 be、have 或进行时形式开头时,应保留这个助动词:'He isn't working, but he should be.' 许多学习者在此处省掉 be,造成句子不合语法,因为 should 之后没有任何成分可依附。

Ellipsis in coordinated clauses follows a pattern: the repeated subject, auxiliary or verb is dropped only when the structures run in parallel. 'Tom ordered the fish and Anna the steak' works because the gapped verb is identical. Gapping sounds formal or literary in speech and is unusual with long verb phrases, so in conversation it is more natural to say 'Tom had the fish, and Anna had the steak.' Avoid gapping when the two conjuncts actually need different tenses, as in 'Tom ordered the fish yesterday and Anna the steak tonight', where the missing verb cannot be read as both a past and a future form at once.

并列分句中的省略遵循一定规律:只有结构平行时,重复的主语、助动词或动词才可以省掉。'Tom ordered the fish and Anna the steak' 成立,因为被省略的动词完全相同。这种空位省略在口语中偏正式或文学化,动词短语较长时也很少见,日常更自然的说法是 'Tom had the fish, and Anna had the steak.' 当两个分句其实需要不同的时态时不要省略,如 'Tom ordered the fish yesterday and Anna the steak tonight',被省掉的动词不可能同时读作过去式和将来式。

More pairs: same idea, different choice更多对比句组

I don't think so. 我不这么认为。
Negates the opinion itself; polite disagreement or doubt, the speaker is not convinced.否定的是看法本身;委婉表示不同意或怀疑,说话人并不确信。
I think not. 我想不是。
Stiff and formal; a confident negative judgement, almost old-fashioned in everyday talk.生硬、正式;做出较肯定的否定判断,日常口语中几乎显得过时。
I'm afraid not. 恐怕不行。
Polite refusal or regretful bad news; the speaker is sorry about the negative answer.礼貌拒绝或遗憾地告知坏消息;说话人对否定的答案感到抱歉。
I hope not. 希望不会。
Expresses a wish that the thing does not happen, not doubt about whether it will.表达的是希望某事不发生,而不是怀疑它是否会发生。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ I don't hope so.
✓ I hope not.
Hope takes not directly as its negative substitute, whereas think, believe and suppose take 'don't ... so'.
hope 的否定替代形式是直接加 not,而 think、believe、suppose 才用 'don't ... so'。
✗ He isn't working, but he should.
✓ He isn't working, but he should be.
When the omitted phrase begins with be, that be must remain after the modal because the progressive cannot be reconstructed from a bare auxiliary.
被省略部分以 be 开头时,情态动词后必须保留 be,否则进行时的意思无法从光杆助动词还原。
✗ I like his one more than yours one.
✓ I like his more than yours.
One cannot follow a possessive pronoun or possessive form, which already stands alone as a noun phrase.
one 不能接在所有格代词或所有格形式之后,这些词本身已能独立充当名词短语。
✗ I wanted to go, but my boss didn't allow me to go.
✓ I wanted to go, but my boss wouldn't let me.
Repeating 'to go' is heavy, and the complement of let is easily omitted, which gives a more natural reduction; allow keeps its 'to' but would also reduce to 'didn't allow me to'.
重复 to go 显得冗赘;let 的补语可以直接省去,表达更自然;若用 allow,则应缩减为 'didn't allow me to'。
✗ A: Did she pass the exam? B: Yes, she passed.
✓ A: Did she pass the exam? B: Yes, she did.
A short answer repeats the auxiliary from the question; repeating the main verb in the past form sounds like a statement, not a reply, and is a typical transfer from Chinese verb repetition.
简短回答应沿用问句中的助动词;重复主动词的过去式更像陈述句而非回答,这是受汉语重复动词习惯影响的典型迁移错误。
✗ She likes jazz and I too like.
✓ She likes jazz, and so do I.
Addition requires so plus auxiliary with subject inversion; leaving the verb at the end follows Chinese patterns and is ungrammatical.
表示附和时需要 so + 助动词并倒装主语;把动词留在句末是汉语语序,在英语中不合语法。
