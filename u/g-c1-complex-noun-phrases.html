Long noun phrases: reading and building them 长名词短语的读与写

Find the head noun in a long English noun phrase at reading speed, and build your own phrases in the English order: short modifiers before the noun, long ones after it. 读的时候能马上找到长名词短语里的中心词；自己写时按英语的顺序搭：短修饰语放名词前，长修饰语放名词后。

👀 Look first: what changes?先看对比：差在哪里

Read the sentences and decide what each one tells you. Then open the notes.先读这几句，自己判断各自传达了什么，再点开看注解。

The families who live by the river and who were moved first were young. 住在河边并且最先被转移的那些家庭都很年轻。
Two relative clauses: clear but heavy.两个定语从句：清楚但重。
The young families living by the river were moved first. 住在河边的年轻家庭最先被转移。
Adjective before, participle after: compact.形容词在前，分词在后：紧凑。
The by-the-river young families were moved first. 河边年轻家庭最先被转移。
Wrong: Chinese order, a long modifier before the noun.错误：中文语序，长修饰语放在名词前。

📏 The rule规则

📏 The rule in one line规则一句话
An English noun phrase grows in both directions around one head noun: determiners and short adjectives go before it, and long information (prepositional phrases, participles, relative clauses) goes after it. Find the head first; everything else describes it.英语名词短语以一个中心名词为核心向两头长：限定词和短形容词放前面，长信息（介词短语、分词、定语从句）放后面。先找中心词，其余都是在描述它。

Chinese puts all modifiers before the noun and ends with the noun: zhu zai he bian de na xie nian qing de jia ting. English splits them: the young families (living by the river). A Chinese writer who keeps the Chinese order produces long strings of nouns or the wrong order (✗ the by the river living families). The rule of thumb: one or two words go before; anything with a preposition or a verb goes after.

中文把修饰语都放名词前面，最后才是名词。英语把它们拆开：短的放前，长的放后。照搬中文语序就会写出一长串名词或语序错乱。经验法则：一两个词放前面，带介词或动词的放后面。

When you read, find the head noun, then find the main verb. In The number of families on the waiting list for subsidised housing in the region has doubled, the head is number and the verb is has doubled; everything between them describes number. This is why the verb is singular: number has, not families have.

阅读时先找中心名词，再找主要动词，中间的一切都是在描述中心词。所以动词要用单数：主语是 number，不是 families。

Before the noun, English likes compound modifiers with hyphens: a two-year contract, a well-paid job, a ten-minute walk, a low-income family. The number word stays singular: a three-bedroom house, not ✗ a three-bedrooms house. Noun + noun is also common (a rent increase, a road safety campaign), but more than three nouns in a row becomes hard to read.

名词前英语喜欢用连字符复合修饰语，里面的数字后的名词用单数。名词加名词也很常见，但连续三个以上名词就难读了。

After the noun, choose the lightest form that works: a prepositional phrase (a house with a garden), a participle (people waiting for surgery), an infinitive (a decision to wait), a relative clause (the doctor who saw you last time). Put the shortest first and the longest last: a report from the city on housing costs that was published in May.

名词后的修饰语，选能用的最轻形式：介词短语、分词、不定式、定语从句。短的放前，长的放最后。

In writing, a long subject before a short verb feels top-heavy: The fact that nobody from the office had ever replied to any of our letters about the leak surprised us. Move the weight to the end: It surprised us that nobody from the office had ever replied…, or split it into two sentences. Long noun phrases are better as objects than as subjects.

写作中，长主语配短谓语会显得头重脚轻。把重量挪到句末，或拆成两句。长名词短语放在宾语位置比放在主语位置好。

Examples例句

  • The number of families on the waiting list has doubled. 排队等候名单上的家庭数量翻了一番。
    Head number → singular has.中心词 number，动词用单数 has。
  • We rented a two-bedroom apartment above a bakery. 我们在一家面包店楼上租了套两居室。
    two-bedroom: singular noun inside the compound.two-bedroom 中名词用单数。
  • She found a well-paid job with a short commute. 她找到一份通勤短、收入不错的工作。
    Compound before, prepositional phrase after.复合词在前，介词短语在后。
  • The young families living by the river were moved first. 住在河边的年轻家庭最先被转移。
    Short before, long after.短的在前，长的在后。
  • The decision to close the school was made without a public meeting. 关闭学校的决定是在没有公开会议的情况下做出的。
    decision to + verb after the noun.名词后接 decision to 加动词。
  • A report from the city on housing costs came out in May. 市里发布的一份关于住房成本的报告五月出来了。
    Two prepositional phrases, short then longer.两个介词短语，先短后长。
  • The nurse who called you last week has left the clinic. 上周给你打电话的那位护士已经离开诊所了。
    Relative clause after the head.中心词后接定语从句。
  • Parents of children under five can apply for the program. 有五岁以下孩子的家长可以申请这个项目。
    of + under five after the noun.名词后接 of 与 under five。
  • The cost of a basic monthly bus pass has gone up again. 基本月票的价格又涨了。
    Head cost; bus pass is noun + noun.中心词 cost；bus pass 是名词加名词。
  • Anyone with a question about their bill should call the number on the back. 对账单有疑问的人请拨打背面的电话。
    with a question about… after anyone.anyone 后接 with a question about…
  • It surprised us that nobody from the office had ever replied to our letters. 让我们吃惊的是，办公室从来没有人回复我们的信。
    Long subject moved to the end with it.用 it 把长主语挪到句末。
  • A ten-minute walk from the station brings you to the market. 从车站步行十分钟就到市场。
    ten-minute: singular, with a hyphen.ten-minute：单数，带连字符。
  • The main problem with the old system was the long wait for an answer. 旧制度的主要问题是等答复要等很久。
    Two heads, each with an of / for phrase.两个中心词，各带介词短语。

More pairs: same idea, different choice更多对比句组

The fact that the office never replied to any of our letters about the leak surprised us. 办公室从来没回复我们关于漏水的任何信件，这让我们很吃惊。
Top-heavy subject.主语头重脚轻。
It surprised us that the office never replied to any of our letters about the leak. 让我们吃惊的是，办公室从来没回复我们关于漏水的任何信件。
Weight moved to the end.重量挪到句末。
We wrote about the leak many times. The office never replied, which surprised us. 我们为漏水写了很多次信。办公室从来没回过，这让我们很吃惊。
Split into two sentences: best for speech.拆成两句：口语最好。
a school road safety awareness campaign 一场学校道路安全意识宣传活动
Five nouns in a row: hard to read.连续五个名词：难读。
a road safety campaign for schools 一项面向学校的道路安全宣传
Move part of it after the noun.把一部分挪到名词后面。
a campaign to make roads near schools safer 一项让学校周边道路更安全的活动
Infinitive after the noun: the clearest.名词后接不定式：最清楚。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ The number of students have increased.
✓ The number of students has increased.
The head is number, which is singular.
中心词是 number，单数。
✗ We live in a three-bedrooms house.
✓ We live in a three-bedroom house.
Inside a compound before the noun, the noun is singular.
名词前的复合修饰语里名词用单数。
✗ the in Toronto living relatives
✓ the relatives living in Toronto
Long modifiers go after the noun.
长修饰语放名词后。
✗ The reason of the delay was the weather.
✓ The reason for the delay was the weather.
reason for, cause of.
reason 配 for，cause 配 of。
✗ a very good paid job
✓ a very well-paid job
Adverb well + participle in a compound.
复合词里用副词 well 加分词。
