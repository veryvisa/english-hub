The Envelope I Did Not Open 那个我没拆的信封

🧠 Learn the 4 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 4 个新词

Read a short story about an unopened official letter and use unreal conditionals and wish to talk about a past mistake. 读一个没拆公函的小故事，用虚拟条件句和 wish 谈过去的失误。

B2 · CELPIP 7–8⏱ 20 minG07 Unreal conditionals and wish虚拟条件句与 wishG08 Tense not kept consistent across a story叙事时态前后不一

📖 Key card知识卡
In many English-speaking countries the letter is the warning: open official mail the day it arrives and write the deadline where you will see it.在许多英语国家，信本身就是提醒：公函到的当天就拆，把截止日期写在看得见的地方。

Read and listen读与听

280 words
Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

The letter arrived in March, in a plain brown envelope, and I put it on the pile by the door with the flyers. It was the renewal notice for my small business licence from the city. I meant to open it at the weekend. I put off opening it for seven weeks.

信是三月到的，一个普通的牛皮纸信封，我把它和广告传单一起放在门边那一摞上。那是市政府寄来的小生意执照续期通知。我本打算周末拆，结果一拖就是七个星期。

When I finally did, the deadline had passed eleven days earlier. The notice was polite and very clear: renew by the date shown, or the licence lapses and a late fee applies. I had to fill in a new form, pay the late fee, and wait two weeks for a decision before I could take on any new clients.

等我终于拆开，截止日期已经过了十一天。通知写得客气又清楚：在所示日期前续期，否则执照失效并收取滞纳金。我只好重新填一张表，交滞纳金，再等两周的审批，之后才能接新客户。

If I had opened the envelope the day it came, the whole thing would have taken me twenty minutes. If I were a naturally organised person, I would not need a system. I am not, so now I have one. Every official letter gets opened the same day, even if I deal with it later. I write the deadline on the outside of the envelope in red, and the envelope goes into one tray, not onto the pile. On the first day of each month I go through the tray.

如果信到的那天我就拆了，整件事二十分钟就能办完。要是我天生是个有条理的人，也就不需要什么办法了。可我不是，所以现在我有了一套：每封公函当天就拆，哪怕晚点再处理。我用红笔把截止日期写在信封外面，信封放进一个专门的文件盘，不再放到那一摞上。每个月第一天，我把文件盘过一遍。

The confusing part, I realise now, was not the English. The letter was easy to read. It was a habit I brought with me: the belief that anything important would come with a phone call or a knock on the door. Here, the letter is the call. I keep track of deadlines now as if my business depended on them, because it does. I wish I had learned that for the price of a stamp, not a late fee.

我现在明白，难的不是英语，那封信很好懂。难的是我带过来的一个习惯：以为重要的事总会有人打电话或者上门来说。在这里，信就是那通电话。现在我把截止日期当成生意的命根子来盯，因为它确实是。真希望这一课我是花一张邮票的钱学会的，而不是交一笔滞纳金。

Words词

/rɪˈnjuːəl ˈnəʊtɪs/ n.
续期通知
receive a renewal notice; send out renewal notices
The renewal notice arrives about two months before the date. 续期通知大约提前两个月寄到。
/ˈdedlaɪn/ n.
截止日期
meet a deadline; miss the deadline
We missed the deadline by one day. 我们差一天错过了截止日期。
/ˈleɪt fiː/ n.
滞纳金；逾期费
pay a late fee; a late fee applies
A late fee applies after the due date. 过了到期日要收滞纳金。
/læps/ v.
（执照、保单等）失效
let sth lapse; the licence lapses
Do not let your membership lapse. 别让会员资格失效。
/kiːp træk əv/ phr.
记着；跟踪
keep track of deadlines; keep track of spending
A simple calendar helps me keep track of every deadline. 一本简单的日历帮我记着每个截止日期。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

The notice was polite and very clear: renew by the date shown, or the licence lapses and a late fee applies.
After the colon, the notice speaks in short commands; the date shown = the date that is shown.
冒号后是通知里的简短指令；the date shown 即 the date that is shown。
If I were a naturally organised person, I would not need a system.
Unreal present: were + would. It is about who she is now, not about the past.
与现在事实相反：were＋would，说的是她现在是什么样的人，不是过去。
