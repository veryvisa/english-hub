The Day I Sold Everything 我全部卖掉的那一天

A few years ago, I opened my first investment account with money I had been saving for a deposit on a flat. I told myself that I was comfortable with risk. In reality, I had never watched my savings shrink, so I had no idea how I would react. For several months the value crept upwards, and I began to feel rather clever. As I now realise, rising prices tend to make people feel brave.

几年前，我开了第一个投资账户，投入的是我一直在为买公寓首付而攒的钱。我告诉自己，我不怕风险。实际上，我从没眼看着自己的积蓄缩水过，所以根本不知道自己会有什么反应。接连几个月，账户价值缓缓上升，我开始觉得自己挺聪明。我现在才明白，价格上涨往往会让人觉得自己很勇敢。

Then the market fell sharply. Within a fortnight, a large slice of my money had disappeared, at least on paper. I checked the figures on the bus, at my desk and in the middle of the night. News headlines predicted worse to come. Unable to sleep, I sold everything one grey morning and felt an enormous wave of relief.

接着，市场急剧下跌。不到两周，我的钱就少了一大块，至少账面上如此。我在公交车上看数字，在办公桌前看，半夜也看。新闻标题预测情况还会更糟。我无法入睡，在一个阴沉的早晨把所有投资都卖掉了，顿时感到如释重负。

The relief did not last. Over the following year, prices gradually recovered, while my cash sat on the sidelines. I kept waiting for the perfect moment to return, but it never seemed to arrive. By selling at the bottom, or somewhere close to it, I had turned a temporary fall into a permanent loss. Had I been able to ride out the storm, I would probably have been much better off.

这种轻松感并没有持续多久。接下来的一年里，价格逐渐回升，而我的现金一直在场外观望。我总在等重返市场的完美时机，可它似乎从未出现。由于在最低点或接近最低点时卖出，我把一次暂时的下跌变成了永久的亏损。要是我当时能挺过那场风暴，处境很可能会好得多。

That said, I do not think my only mistake was selling. The deeper problem was that I had invested money I would soon need. My time horizon was short, and my risk tolerance was far lower than I had imagined. Today I keep money for near-term plans in a savings account and invest only what I can leave alone for years. I still dislike seeing the numbers fall, but I no longer lose sleep over them.

话虽如此，我并不认为卖出是我唯一的错误。更深层的问题是，我拿很快就要用到的钱去投资了。我的投资期限很短，风险承受能力也远低于自己的想象。如今，我把近期计划要用的钱存在储蓄账户里，只用可以多年不动的钱投资。我仍然不喜欢看到数字下跌，但已经不会再为此失眠了。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

Had I been able to ride out the storm, I would probably have been much better off.
This inverted third conditional replaces ‘If I had been able to’ and imagines a different past result.
这是倒装的第三条件句，相当于 If I had been able to，用来假设一个与过去事实不同的结果。
Unable to sleep, I sold everything one grey morning and felt an enormous wave of relief.
The opening adjective phrase ‘Unable to sleep’ gives the reason and refers to the subject ‘I’.
句首的形容词短语 Unable to sleep 表示原因，其逻辑主语是 I。
