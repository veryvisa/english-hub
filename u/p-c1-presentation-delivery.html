Delivery for talks and meetings 演讲与会议的表达节奏

Deliver a talk or a point in a meeting so that listeners can follow the structure by ear: chunked into thought groups, with clear pauses, one main stress per group, and falling tones that close each point. 讲话或在会上发言时，让听者光凭耳朵就能跟上结构：按意群切块、停顿清楚、每组一个主重音、用降调收住每个要点。

C1 · CELPIP 9+⏱ 30 minP10 Flat or wrong intonation; the important word is not stressed语调平，重点词不突出

📏 The rule规则

📏 The point in one line一句话要领
Speak in thought groups of three to seven words, put one strong stress on the new or important word in each group, pause between groups, and let your voice fall at the end of each complete point.按三到七个词一组的意群说，每组把一个强重音放在新信息或关键词上，组与组之间停顿，每个完整要点结尾让声音降下来。
  • Easy to miss易漏 ✗ Reading the whole script at one speed and one pitch → ✓ Chunks, one nucleus each, and falls at the end of points

🗣 Say it跟读

Tap 🔊, listen, then say it aloud. Use ● to record yourself and compare.点 🔊 听，再开口跟读；点 ● 录下自己的声音对比（录音只在本机）。

Good morning, everyone. // Let's get started. /ɡʊd ˈmɔːnɪŋ ˈevriwʌn // lets ɡet ˈstɑːtɪd/
Two falls, a full pause between. 两个降调，中间完整停顿。
Last quarter / our sales in Ontario / grew by twelve per cent. /ˌlɑːst ˈkwɔːtə / ˈaʊə ˈseɪlz ɪn ɒnˈteəriəʊ / ˈɡruː baɪ ˈtwelv pə ˈsent/
Three chunks; the nucleus is the last content word of each. 三个意群，主重音在每组最后的实词上。
That's the good news. /ˈðæts ðə ɡʊd ˈnjuːz/
Fall on news, then pause before the bad news. news 降调，然后停顿再讲坏消息。
The problem isn't demand. // It's supply. /ðə ˈprɒbləm ɪznt dɪˈmɑːnd // ɪts səˈplaɪ/
Contrast stress on demand and supply. 对比重音在 demand 和 supply 上。
We tested it in Toronto, Ottawa and Montreal. /tɒˈrɒntəʊ ↗ ˈɒtəwə ↗ ən ˌmɒntriˈɔːl ↘/
Rise, rise, fall: the fall closes the list. 升、升、降，降调收住列举。
There are three reasons for this. /ðeər ə ˈθriː ˈriːzənz fə ˈðɪs/
Stress three: it tells the audience what to count. 重读 three，告诉听众要数几条。
First, / cost. /ˈfɜːst / ˈkɒst/
Short signpost, pause, one-word headline with a fall. 短提示语，停顿，一个词的标题降调。
The total came to / eighteen million dollars. /ðə ˈtəʊtəl ˈkeɪm tuː / eɪˈtiːn ˈmɪljən ˈdɒləz/
Pause before the number, then slow down. 数字前停顿，再放慢。
I'm not saying it's impossible. // I'm saying it's expensive. /aɪm ˈnɒt ˈseɪɪŋ ɪts ɪmˈpɒsəbəl // aɪm ˈseɪɪŋ ɪts ɪkˈspensɪv/
Fall-rise on impossible, firm fall on expensive. impossible 降升调，expensive 坚定降调。
Can I just add something here? /kən aɪ dʒəst ˈæd ˈsʌmθɪŋ hɪə/
Level or gently rising: you are asking for the floor. 平调或微升，是在请求发言。
Let me come back to that point at the end. /let mi ˈkʌm ˈbæk tə ðæt ˈpɔɪnt ət ði ˈend/
One chunk, fall on end. 一个意群，end 降调。
So, / to sum up: / we need more time, / not more money. /ˈsəʊ / tə ˈsʌm ˈʌp / wi ˈniːd mɔː ˈtaɪm / ˈnɒt mɔː ˈmʌni/
Four chunks; the contrast carries the final message. 四个意群，最后的对比承载核心信息。
Does that answer your question? /dəz ˈðæt ˈɑːnsə jə ˈkwestʃən/
A real question: rise at the end. 真正的提问，句末升调。
Thank you. // Happy to take any questions. /ˈθæŋk juː // ˈhæpi tə ˈteɪk eni ˈkwestʃənz/
Fall, pause, then a warm fall. 降调、停顿，再一个温和的降调。

📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Open the full explanation: 6 points展开细讲：6 段讲解

In a presentation the listener has no text to look back at. Everything they know about your structure comes from your voice: where you pause, what you stress, and whether your pitch tells them a point is finished. Many Chinese professionals prepare excellent slides and then read the script at an even pace with an even pitch. The words are correct, but the listener cannot tell the headline from the detail.

演讲时听者没有文字可以回看，对你结构的全部了解都来自你的声音：哪里停、重读什么、音高有没有告诉他这一点讲完了。很多华人专业人士幻灯片做得很好，却用平稳的速度和平稳的音高把稿子读完。用词都对，但听者分不出哪句是标题、哪句是细节。

Chunk first. Mark your script with a slash wherever the meaning naturally breaks: after an introductory phrase, before a new clause, around a list. Last quarter / our sales in Ontario / grew by twelve per cent. // That's the good news. Each chunk is said in one breath, with linking inside it and a real pause, about half a second, at the slash. A double slash marks a longer pause between points.

先切块。在稿子上意思自然断开的地方划斜线：开场短语之后、新从句之前、列举前后。每块一口气说完，块内连读，斜线处真正停顿约半秒；双斜线表示要点之间的长停顿。

Then place the nucleus. Each chunk has one word that carries the main stress, normally the last new content word: our sales in ONtario / grew by twelve per CENT. When you contrast or correct, the stress moves to the contrast: not the WEST, the EAST. A common Chinese pattern is to stress every content word equally, which sounds emphatic but flat; one strong peak per chunk is more persuasive than five medium ones.

再定主重音。每块有一个词承担主重音，通常是最后一个新的实词。对比或纠正时，重音移到对比项上。华人常见的模式是每个实词一样重，听起来用力却平；每块一个强峰值，比五个中等峰值更有说服力。

Use pitch to show structure. A fall says "this is complete, this is a fact": use it at the end of each point and for your key numbers. A rise or a fall-rise says "there is more coming" or "this is not the whole story": use it on the first items of a list (Toronto ↗, Ottawa ↗, and Montreal ↘) and on hedged points. Ending every sentence on a rise, as many nervous speakers do, makes facts sound like questions.

用音高显示结构。降调表示「讲完了、这是事实」，用在每个要点结尾和关键数字上。升调或降升调表示「后面还有」或「事情没那么简单」，用在列举的前几项和有保留的观点上。紧张的人常句句升调结尾，事实就听着像问题。

Slow down at the important places, not everywhere. Speakers who worry about their English often slow down evenly, which makes the whole talk sound heavy. Instead, keep the ordinary parts at a normal pace and slow down, with a short pause before it, for the one number, name or conclusion the audience must remember. Silence after a key sentence is a tool, not a mistake.

在重要的地方放慢，不是处处放慢。担心自己英语的人往往均匀地放慢，整场都显得沉重。正确做法是普通部分保持正常语速，只在听众必须记住的那个数字、名字或结论前稍停、放慢。关键句之后的沉默是一种工具，不是失误。

In meetings the same skills work at a smaller scale. To take the floor, start with a short signpost on a level or slightly rising tone (Can I just add something here?), then make one point in two or three chunks, and finish with a clear fall so others know you are done. Trailing off on a rise invites interruption before you have made your point.

开会时同样的技巧缩小使用。要发言，先用平调或微升的短提示语开口，然后用两三个意群讲一个观点，最后清楚地降调，让别人知道你说完了。尾音上扬、拖着不收，观点还没讲完就容易被人打断。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ Reading the whole script at one speed and one pitch
✓ Chunks, one nucleus each, and falls at the end of points
Listeners cannot hear your structure if nothing in your voice changes.
声音没有变化，听者就听不出结构。
✗ Stressing every content word: OUR SALES IN ONTARIO GREW BY TWELVE PER CENT
✓ our sales in onTARio / grew by twelve per CENT
Equal stress sounds forceful but hides the point.
平均用力听着有劲，却埋没了重点。
✗ Rising at the end of statements: Our costs fell by ten per cent?
✓ A clear fall on facts and numbers
A rise makes a fact sound uncertain.
升调让事实听起来没把握。
✗ Slowing down evenly to be clear
✓ Normal pace for ordinary content, a pause and a slower pace for the key point
Even slowness makes everything equally unimportant.
均匀放慢让一切都一样不重要。

✅ Check yourself测一测

✍️ Use it用起来

Write or say your answer. It is saved only in this browser.写下或说出你的答案；只存在这个浏览器里。

Take a one-minute update you might give at work (a project, a result, a plan). Write it out, mark every thought group with /, every point break with //, and underline one nucleus per group. Record it twice: once reading flat, once with your marks. Play both back and note which one you would rather listen to, and where your falls were missing.
拿一段工作上可能做的一分钟汇报（项目、结果或计划），写出来，用 / 标意群，用 // 标要点之间，每组划出一个主重音。录两遍：一遍平读，一遍按标记读。回放两遍，记下更愿意听哪一遍、哪里缺了降调。
