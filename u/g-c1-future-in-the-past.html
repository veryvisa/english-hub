The future seen from the past 过去将来

Use was going to, would, was about to, was to and the future perfect in the past to describe what lay ahead from a past viewpoint, and choose the one that shows whether the future happened, was planned, or was fated. 能够使用 was going to、would、was about to、was to 以及过去时间点的将来完成式，从过去的视角描述“当时的未来”，并根据未来是否实现、是计划还是命中注定来选择合适的形式。

📏 The rule规则

📏 The rule in one line规则一句话
When the narrator stands in the past and looks forward, English usually shifts future forms one step back unless that future is still ahead of the speaker now, and the choice among would, was going to, was about to and was to signals certainty, intention, immediacy or destiny.叙述者站在过去回望“当时的未来”时，只要那个未来对说话者来说已经过去，英语通常会把各种将来形式后移一步；而 would、was going to、was about to、was to 的选择则体现了确定性、意图、紧迫性或命运感。

In narrative, would is the quiet, literary choice for a future the narrator already knows has happened. 'She would become a judge' works only if we are looking back from a later vantage point. If you write 'She was going to become a judge' instead, the sentence shifts to what was intended or expected at the time, and the reader no longer knows whether it came true.

在叙事中，would 是一种含蓄、偏文学化的选择，用于叙述者已经知道结果的“当时的未来”。‘She would become a judge’只有在从之后的某个时间点回望时才成立。如果改写成 ‘She was going to become a judge’，句子就转为当时的打算或预期，读者无法判断后来是否实现。

Was going to is the everyday default for a plan or an intention that existed in the past, and it is the form that most often carries the implication 'but it did not happen'. 'I was going to call you' is an apology or an excuse. Chinese speakers often use would here because 'would' feels like the past of 'will', but 'I would call you' in this context sounds like a habit or a conditional, not an abandoned plan.

was going to 是表达过去存在的计划或打算时的日常默认形式，而且最常带有“但是没有实现”的含义。‘I was going to call you’往往是道歉或借口。中国学习者常因为 would 像是 will 的过去式而在这里误用它，但此处的 ‘I would call you’ 会被理解为习惯或条件句，而不是一个落空的计划。

Was about to marks the moment immediately before an action, and it is often interrupted by something else: 'I was about to leave when the phone rang.' It cannot be combined with a distant time expression, so 'I was about to leave next month' is unnatural. Choose it when the point is that the action was seconds away, not when you are reporting a plan.

was about to 表示动作发生前的一刹那，常常被另一件事打断：‘I was about to leave when the phone rang.’它不能和遥远的时间状语连用，所以 ‘I was about to leave next month’ 不自然。当重点是动作近在眼前，而不是汇报计划时，才选择它。

Was to carries a sense of arrangement or destiny, and it is formal. 'He was to be the last of his line' hints at fate, while 'We were to meet at six' states an arrangement that was fixed. The pattern 'was to have done' signals that the arrangement was not carried out, which is a compact way to say 'was supposed to do but did not'. Use it in formal writing or storytelling, not in casual speech.

was to 带有安排或命运的意味，且比较正式。‘He was to be the last of his line’暗示命运，而 ‘We were to meet at six’ 说明一个已经敲定的安排。‘was to have done’这一结构表示安排未能实现，是 ‘was supposed to do but did not’ 的简洁说法。它适合正式写作或讲故事，不适合日常口语。

When the future point lies inside a past frame and you need to say that something would already be finished by then, use would have + past participle. 'By the time she arrived, he knew the meeting would have ended.' This is the future perfect shifted backwards, and it is the form learners most often avoid by simplifying to a past perfect, which changes the meaning from prediction to fact.

当未来时间点处于过去的框架之内，并且要表示届时某事将已经完成，应使用 would have + 过去分词。‘By the time she arrived, he knew the meeting would have ended.’这是将来完成式的后移，学习者常常把它简化成过去完成式来回避，但这会把“预测”变成“事实”，改变了意思。

In reported speech and thought, the shift is almost mandatory when the report is still anchored in the past: 'He said he would help' not 'He said he will help', unless the future is still ahead of the speaker now. Notice that this is a choice about perspective: 'She told me she is coming tonight' is acceptable if tonight has not yet arrived and the speaker wants to show that it is still relevant.

在转述言语和想法时，只要报道仍以过去为锚点，这种后移几乎是必须的：‘He said he would help’，而不是 ‘He said he will help’，除非那个未来对说话者来说仍在前方。注意，这其实是视角的选择：如果“今晚”尚未到来，并且说话者想强调它仍然有关联，‘She told me she is coming tonight’ 也是可以接受的。

More pairs: same idea, different choice更多对比句组

She told me she would come. 她跟我说她会来。
A report anchored in the past; says nothing about whether she came.以过去为锚点的转述，没有说明她来没来。
She told me she is coming. 她跟我说她要来。
The visit is still ahead of the speaker now, so the present form keeps it live.来访对说话者而言仍在未来，用现在时保持其时效性。
She told me she was going to come. 她跟我说她打算来。
Stresses her stated plan; the listener may doubt it was carried out.强调她说过的打算，听者可能怀疑是否真的实现。
She told me she would have come by six. 她跟我说六点前她就已经到了。
A prediction about completion by a past-seen deadline.对过去视角下某个截止时间前已完成的预测。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ He said he will help me yesterday.
✓ He said he would help me yesterday.
The report is anchored in a past time marker, so the future form has to shift back to would.
转述被过去的时间状语锚定，将来形式必须后移为 would。
✗ I would call you, but I lost my phone.
✓ I was going to call you, but I lost my phone.
Would here sounds like a habit or a condition; an abandoned plan needs was going to.
此处的 would 听起来像习惯或条件，落空的计划需要用 was going to。
✗ I was about to leave next week when she called.
✓ I was going to leave the following week, but she called.
Was about to refers to the moment just before an action and cannot take a distant time expression.
was about to 指动作发生前的一刹那，不能与遥远的时间状语连用。
✗ At that time I thought it will be easy.
✓ At that time I thought it would be easy.
A thought located in the past needs the past-shifted future; a present-tense will leaves the sentence unanchored.
位于过去的想法需要用后移的将来形式，用 will 会让句子失去时间锚点。
✗ She knew that by then he finished the report.
✓ She knew that by then he would have finished the report.
A prediction about completion by a past-seen deadline needs would have plus the past participle, not a plain past tense.
对过去视角下截止时间前已完成的预测，需要 would have + 过去分词，而不是一般过去时。
✗ We were to meet at six, and we meet at six as planned.
✓ We were to meet at six, and we did meet at six as planned.
Mixing a past arrangement with a present-tense result breaks the time frame; keep the whole chain in the past.
过去的安排与现在时的结果混用会打破时间框架，整个链条都应保持过去时。
