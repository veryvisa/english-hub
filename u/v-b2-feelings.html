📏 The rule规则

🎯 The point in one line一句话要领
The precise feeling word does half the explaining for you: I was relieved tells the listener what you feared before; I was frustrated tells them something kept blocking you.准确的情绪词替你讲了一半：说 relieved，对方就知道你之前怕什么；说 frustrated，对方就知道有东西一直在挡你。

📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Open the full explanation: 5 points展开细讲：5 段讲解

Most learners have four feeling words and stretch them with very. English speakers sort feelings by cause. Frustrated is anger at being blocked; resentful is anger at being treated unfairly over time; irritated is a small, repeated annoyance. Choosing by cause is what makes the word sound right.

多数学习者只有四个情绪词，靠 very 撑场面。英语按「起因」分情绪：frustrated 是被挡住的火，resentful 是长期受不公的怨，irritated 是小而反复的烦。按起因选词才地道。

Worry also comes in grades. Uneasy is a vague feeling that something is wrong; anxious is worry about what might happen; dreading something means you know it is coming and you do not want it. Overwhelmed is not fear at all: it means there is too much to deal with.

担心也分档：uneasy 是隐约觉得不对劲；anxious 是担心可能发生的事；dread 是明知要来又不想面对；overwhelmed 根本不是害怕，是事情多到应付不过来。

Positive feelings have the same detail. Relieved follows a fear that did not come true; content is quiet satisfaction without excitement; thrilled is strong, excited pleasure; touched means someone's kindness moved you.

正面情绪同样细：relieved 是担心的事没发生；content 是不激动的满足；thrilled 是强烈的兴奋；touched 是被别人的好意打动。

The -ed / -ing pair is still the most common error at this level. The person feels -ed (I was disappointed); the thing causes it with -ing (the result was disappointing). Chinese uses one word for both, so check every time you write one.

-ed / -ing 仍是这一级最常见的错：人「感到」用 -ed，事物「令人」用 -ing。中文一个词管两头，所以每次写都要核一下。

Many feelings take a fixed preposition: ashamed of, fed up with, anxious about, grateful for something and grateful to someone. The preposition is part of the chunk; learn it with the word.

许多情绪词带固定介词：ashamed of、fed up with、anxious about、grateful for（事）／grateful to（人）。介词是词块的一部分，要连着记。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ I was very boring at the party.
✓ I was very bored at the party.
bored is how you feel; boring means you made others feel that way.
bored 是你的感受；boring 是你让别人无聊。
✗ I am very worry about my son.
✓ I'm very worried about my son. / I'm anxious about my son.
worry is a verb or noun; the adjective is worried.
worry 是动词或名词，形容词是 worried。
✗ I'm grateful to your help.
✓ I'm grateful for your help. / I'm grateful to you for your help.
grateful to a person, grateful for a thing.
对人用 to，对事用 for。
✗ I was overwhelmed because I was scared of the dog.
✓ I was terrified of the dog.
overwhelmed means too much to handle, not fear.
overwhelmed 是应付不过来，不是害怕。
✗ I'm boring of this job.
✓ I'm fed up with this job. / I'm bored with this job.
fed up with or bored with; boring of does not exist.
用 fed up with 或 bored with。
