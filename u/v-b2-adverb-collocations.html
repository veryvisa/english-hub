📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Open the full explanation: 5 points展开细讲：5 段讲解

Some adjectives already mean "very": exhausted is "very tired", delicious is "very good to eat", furious is "very angry". These extreme adjectives take absolutely, completely or totally, not very. Very exhausted sounds wrong to a native ear; absolutely exhausted sounds natural.

有些形容词本身就含「很」：exhausted 是很累，delicious 是很好吃，furious 是很生气。这类极端形容词配 absolutely、completely、totally，不配 very。very exhausted 听着别扭，absolutely exhausted 才自然。

Other adverbs come in fixed pairs. Highly goes with likely, unlikely, recommended and qualified. Deeply goes with feelings of concern and regret. Bitterly goes with disappointed and cold. Widely goes with used, known and available. Learn them as pairs, the way you learn heavy rain.

还有一些副词是固定成对的：highly 配 likely、unlikely、recommended、qualified；deeply 配担忧、遗憾一类情绪；bitterly 配 disappointed、cold；widely 配 used、known、available。像记 heavy rain 一样整对记。

Adverbs with verbs work the same way. You strongly agree or strongly disagree, firmly believe, seriously consider, sincerely hope and greatly appreciate. In emails these pairs carry tone: I greatly appreciate your help is warmer and more formal than thanks a lot.

副词配动词也是一样：strongly agree/disagree、firmly believe、seriously consider、sincerely hope、greatly appreciate。邮件里这些搭配带着语气：I greatly appreciate your help 比 thanks a lot 更郑重、更暖。

Barely and hardly are negative in meaning: I barely noticed means "I almost did not notice". Do not add not. The position matters too: these adverbs go before the main verb, but after be and after the first auxiliary: I could barely hear him.

barely、hardly 本身是否定意思：I barely noticed 是「我几乎没注意到」，不要再加 not。位置也要对：放在实义动词前、be 动词和第一个助动词后，如 I could barely hear him。

Chinese speakers often put the adverb at the end of the sentence: I agree with you strongly. That is possible, but the natural place for most of these pairs is right in front of the word they belong to.

华人常把副词放到句尾，比如 I agree with you strongly。这样说不算错，但这些搭配最自然的位置是紧贴在所修饰的词前面。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ After the flight I was very exhausted.
✓ After the flight I was absolutely exhausted.
Extreme adjectives take absolutely, not very.
极端形容词配 absolutely，不配 very。
✗ I don't barely know him.
✓ I barely know him.
barely is already negative.
barely 本身就是否定，不再加 not。
✗ It is very impossible to finish today.
✓ It is completely impossible to finish today.
impossible is absolute: completely, absolutely or simply.
impossible 是绝对词，用 completely、absolutely 或 simply。
✗ The restaurant is highly delicious.
✓ The food is absolutely delicious, and the restaurant is highly recommended.
highly goes with recommended, likely, qualified; delicious takes absolutely.
highly 配 recommended、likely、qualified；delicious 配 absolutely。
✗ I agree strongly with you that the plan is good.
✓ I strongly agree with you that the plan is good.
The adverb goes in front of the verb it belongs to.
副词放在它修饰的动词前面。
