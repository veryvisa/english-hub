📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Open the full explanation: 4 points展开细讲：4 段讲解

Linguists have shown that much of everyday English rests on a few hidden comparisons. If time is money, then you can spend, save, waste, invest and run out of time. If an argument is a fight, you can attack a position, defend a claim and win or lose a debate. Each comparison is a family of chunks.

语言学家指出，日常英语大量建立在几个隐藏的类比上。时间是钱，就能 spend、save、waste、invest、run out of 时间；争论是打仗，就能 attack 立场、defend 观点、win 或 lose 辩论。每个类比就是一族词块。

Chinese shares some of these and not others, which is where mistakes come from. Chinese also "spends" and "wastes" time, so those transfer. But English ideas are buildings that need foundations and can collapse; English plans are journeys that go off track; English feelings are temperatures: a warm welcome, a cold reply, a heated discussion.

中文和英语有的隐喻相通，有的不通，错误就出在这里。「花时间」「浪费时间」能直接搬；但英语里观点是建筑（要有 foundation、会 collapse），计划是旅程（会 off track），感情是温度（warm welcome、heated discussion）。

More is up and less is down explains a whole set of business and news verbs: prices soar, climb, plunge and bottom out; morale is high or low; spirits lift. Once you see the picture, the verb tells you the size and speed of the change.

「多是上，少是下」解释了一整套商业和新闻动词：soar、climb、plunge、bottom out；士气 high/low。看懂画面，动词本身就告诉你变化的幅度与速度。

Use these freely in speech and journalism-style writing. In formal reports, prefer one plain verb and avoid mixing pictures in one sentence: the plan collapsed under heated fire mixes a building, a temperature and a battle.

口语和新闻体写作里可以放开用；正式报告里宁可用一个直白动词，并避免一句话里混用多个画面。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ I won't use my time on this.
✓ I won't spend my time on this. / I won't waste my time on this.
Time is money: you spend it.
时间是钱：用 spend。
✗ The price flew up last month.
✓ Prices soared last month. / Prices shot up last month.
Use the fixed verb of the picture.
用该画面的固定动词。
✗ His idea was beaten down in the meeting.
✓ His idea was shot down in the meeting.
shoot down an idea is the fixed chunk.
固定说法 shoot down。
✗ The argument collapsed under heated fire and went off track.
✓ The argument collapsed when the figures were checked.
Do not mix three pictures in one sentence.
一句里别混三个画面。
✗ We have a hot discussion.
✓ We had a heated discussion.
The fixed adjective is heated.
固定用 heated。
