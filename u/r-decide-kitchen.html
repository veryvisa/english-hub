The Kitchen We Almost Didn't Finish 差点没装完的厨房

🧠 Learn the 5 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 5 个新词

Read a couple's story of a kitchen renovation and meet everyday idioms, metaphors and fixed pairs in one real-sounding account. 读一对夫妻翻修厨房的故事，在一段真实口吻的叙述里同时遇到日常习语、隐喻和固定成对词。

B2 · CELPIP 7–8⏱ 20 minV10 Idioms and fixed chunks known but not used惯用语块认得但不会用V05 One all-purpose word instead of a precise one万能词用到底（very good, a lot of things）

📖 Key card知识卡
Big household decisions rarely go to plan; what helps is a clear reason to start and a refusal to save money in the wrong place.家里的大决定很少按计划走；帮得上忙的是开工的理由要清楚，以及不在不该省的地方省钱。

    Read and listen读与听

    288 words
    Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

    My wife and I spent most of last spring arguing about a kitchen. The old one was falling apart: one cupboard door hung by a single screw, and the oven took forty minutes to heat. We had talked about renovating for years, but we were always on the fence.

    去年春天，我和妻子大半时间都在为厨房争论。旧厨房已经快散架了：一扇柜门只靠一颗螺丝挂着，烤箱要四十分钟才热。翻修的事我们说了好几年，却一直犹豫不决。

    In March we finally sat down and listed the pros and cons. A new kitchen would be expensive, and the price of materials had soared in the past few years. On the other hand, we planned to stay in the house for at least ten years, and a kitchen that worked would pay off in the long run. By and large, the list said yes.

    三月，我们终于坐下来把利弊列了出来。新厨房很贵，材料价格这几年也涨得厉害。另一方面，我们打算在这房子里至少再住十年，一个好用的厨房从长远看是值得的。总的来说，清单的答案是「做」。

    The first contractor's quote was surprisingly low. My wife asked how he could do it so cheaply, and his answer made us both uneasy. It sounded as if he would cut corners on the plumbing. We chose a more expensive firm. The bottom line was that we could not afford to do the job twice.

    第一家承包商的报价低得出奇。妻子问他怎么能这么便宜，他的回答让我们俩都心里不踏实，听起来他会在水管上偷工减料。我们选了一家贵一些的公司。说到底，这活儿我们干不起两遍。

    Even so, the project went off track almost at once. The cabinets arrived in the wrong colour, the electrician got sick, and we ran out of time before my parents' visit. When the new sink leaked onto the new floor, that was the last straw. We had a heated discussion in a kitchen with no water.

    即便如此，项目几乎一开始就偏离了轨道：橱柜送来的颜色不对，电工病了，我父母来访之前我们没时间了。新水槽漏水漏到了新地板上，那成了最后一根稻草。我们在一个没有水的厨房里大吵了一架。

    Sooner or later, though, things got back on track. The firm fixed the leak at its own cost, and by June we were cooking again. Using the new hob took some trial and error, but I have got the hang of it now. Was it worth it? On balance, yes. I would just add a month to every date a contractor gives you.

    不过事情迟早会回到正轨。公司自费修好了漏水，到六月我们又能做饭了。新灶台我是试错试出来的，现在已经上手。值不值？权衡下来，值。只是承包商给你的每个日期，都请自己再加一个月。

    Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

    On the other hand, we planned to stay in the house for at least ten years, and a kitchen that worked would pay off in the long run.
    would here is the future seen from the past (their thinking in March). pay off in the long run joins two chunks: pay off (bring a reward) and in the long run (over a long time).
    这里的 would 是「过去看将来」（三月时的想法）。pay off in the long run 连起两个词块：pay off（见回报）和 in the long run（从长远看）。
    When the new sink leaked onto the new floor, that was the last straw.
    the last straw is the small final problem after many others that makes you lose patience; that points back to the whole leak.
    the last straw 指一连串问题之后让人忍无可忍的最后一件小事；that 回指整件漏水的事。
    It sounded as if he would cut corners on the plumbing.
    as if + would: an impression, not a fact. cut corners on names the area where quality would be lost.
    as if + would 表示印象而非事实；cut corners on 后面点出在哪方面偷工。
