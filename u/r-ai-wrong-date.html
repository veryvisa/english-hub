The Email With the Wrong Date 日期写错的那封邮件

Two months into my job as an office coordinator, I used an AI tool to draft an email to forty suppliers. I gave it my notes and asked for something polite and clear. The result was better than anything I could have written myself, and I sent it after a quick read.

我当办公室协调员刚两个月时，用 AI 工具起草了一封发给四十家供应商的邮件。我把笔记给它，要一封客气、清楚的信。结果比我自己能写出来的都好，我粗读一遍就发了。

The next morning a supplier called. "You've given us until the fifteenth," he said. "Last time it was the thirtieth. Has something changed?" Nothing had changed. My notes said the thirtieth. Somewhere between my notes and the polished email, the tool had made things up, and the thirtieth had become the fifteenth. I had not noticed, because the sentence sounded so sure of itself.

第二天早上一家供应商打来电话。「你们给的期限是十五号，」他说，「上次是三十号，有什么变化吗？」什么也没变，我的笔记写的就是三十号。从我的笔记到那封漂亮的邮件之间，工具自己编了东西，三十号变成了十五号。我没发现，因为那句话写得太笃定了。

I spent that day writing forty apologies. If I had checked the dates against my notes, it would have taken two minutes. If I had pasted my notes under the draft and compared them line by line, I would have seen the mistake at once. I knew all this, of course. I just trusted the fluent English more than my own judgement.

那一整天我都在写四十封道歉信。要是我把日期和笔记对一下，两分钟就够了。要是我把笔记贴在草稿下面逐行比对，一眼就能看出错。这些我当然都知道，只是我更相信那流畅的英语，而不是自己的判断。

My manager was kinder than I expected. She said the same thing had once happened to her with a spreadsheet formula. Then she gave me a rule I still use: the more polished a draft looks, the more carefully you check the facts inside it.

经理比我预想的宽容。她说她也遇到过同样的事，是一个表格公式出的错。接着她给了我一条我至今还在用的规矩：草稿看着越精致，里面的事实就越要仔细核。

I still use the tool every day. It saves me hours. But I wish someone had told me on my first day that it does not know my suppliers, my deadlines or my company. If I were training a new coordinator now, that is the first thing I would say.

我现在每天还在用这个工具，它给我省下好几个小时。但我真希望上班第一天就有人告诉我：它不认识我的供应商，不知道我的期限，也不了解我的公司。如果现在由我来带新协调员，这会是我说的第一句话。

Words词

/ˈpɒlɪʃt/ adj.
精致的；润色过的
a polished draft; polished English
Her emails are short but polished. 她的邮件短，但很精炼。
/tʃek əˈɡenst/ v.
对照……核对
check the dates against your notes; check figures against the source
Always check the total against the receipt. 总数一定要对照收据核一遍。
/laɪn baɪ laɪn/ adv.
逐行地
compare line by line; read sth line by line
We went through the contract line by line. 我们逐行过了一遍合同。
/ˈspredʃiːt/ n.
电子表格
update a spreadsheet; a spreadsheet formula
All the numbers are in the shared spreadsheet. 所有数字都在共享表格里。
/trʌst/ v.
相信；信任
trust your judgement; trust sb completely
I trust the tool for grammar, not for facts. 语法我信这个工具，事实不信。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

Somewhere between my notes and the polished email, the tool had made things up, and the thirtieth had become the fifteenth.
Past perfect had made, had become: both changes happened before the phone call she is telling us about.
过去完成时 had made、had become：两个变化都发生在她讲的那通电话之前。
If I had pasted my notes under the draft and compared them line by line, I would have seen the mistake at once.
Third conditional with two verbs in the if clause sharing one had: had pasted… and compared.
第三类条件句，if 从句里两个动词共用一个 had：had pasted… and compared。
