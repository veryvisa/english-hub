The Rumour in My Mother's Group Chat 妈妈群聊里的那条谣言

🧠 Learn the 5 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 5 个新词

Read a story about correcting a relative's rumour, and see how unreal conditionals carry regret and second thoughts. 读一个纠正长辈谣言的故事，看非真实条件句怎样表达后悔与事后的反思。

Read and listen读与听

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Last spring my mother sent a message to the family group chat at six in the morning: a screenshot claiming that the tap water in our city was unsafe and that everyone should boil it for a week. It had no date, no name and no link, but it had three red warning signs and the words "Share before it is deleted!" By breakfast, my aunt had forwarded it to two other groups.

去年春天，妈妈早上六点往家族群里发了一条消息：一张截图，说我们市的自来水不安全，大家要烧开喝一个星期。上面没有日期、没有署名、也没有链接，却有三个红色警告标志和一句「趁还没被删赶紧转发！」。到吃早饭的时候，姨妈已经把它转到另外两个群里了。

I spent ten minutes looking for the source. The city's website said nothing about it, and neither did any local news site. In the end I found the original post. It came from another country, and it was four years old. Somebody had cropped the date off, and the rumour had gone viral all over again.

我花了十分钟找出处。市政府网站上什么都没说，本地新闻网站也没有。最后我找到了原帖：它来自另一个国家，而且是四年前发的。有人把日期裁掉了，这条谣言又从头火了一遍。

When I pointed this out in the chat, my mother was not grateful. She was embarrassed, and then she was annoyed. "If it were not true, why would so many people share it?" she wrote. I wish I had answered more gently. Instead I sent a link and the words "This is fake." Nobody replied for a day.

我在群里指出这一点时，妈妈并不领情。她先是难为情，接着就恼了。「如果不是真的，为什么那么多人转？」她写道。我真希望当时回得温和一些。可我只发了一个链接和一句「这是假的」。整整一天没人说话。

Since then I have changed my approach. I no longer correct anyone in front of the whole group. I send a private message, I start with "I was worried too when I saw this," and I ask a question instead of giving a verdict: "Do you know where it first came from?" Most of the time, the person who passed it on checks for themselves and quietly deletes it.

从那以后我换了做法。我不再当着全群的面纠正谁。我发私信，开头先说「我看到的时候也担心了」，然后提一个问题，而不是直接下结论：「你知道这最早是从哪儿来的吗？」大多数时候，转发的人会自己去查，然后悄悄删掉。

If I had done that the first time, my mother would have deleted the message herself, and nobody would have lost face. A rumour spreads because people care about each other. The correction has to show that you care too.

要是第一次我就这么做，妈妈会自己把消息删掉，谁也不会丢面子。谣言传得开，是因为人们彼此关心。纠正也得让人看到，你同样在乎。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

"If it were not true, why would so many people share it?" she wrote.
An unreal present inside a question: were for the imagined situation, would for its result. The mother uses it as an argument that the message must be true.
疑问句里的「与现在事实相反」：were 表示假设的情况，would 表示结果。妈妈用这句话来论证消息一定是真的。
Somebody had cropped the date off, and the rumour had gone viral all over again.
Two past perfects: both things happened before the writer found the post. all over again = once more, from the beginning.
两个过去完成时：两件事都发生在作者找到原帖之前。all over again＝又从头来一遍。

Write or say your answer. It is saved only in this browser.写下或说出你的答案；只存在这个浏览器里。

Write a private message of four or five sentences to a relative who shared a rumour. Say how you felt, ask one question about the source, and include one sentence with If I were you, I would…. Then read it aloud as if you were leaving a voice message.
给一位转发了谣言的长辈写一条四五句的私信：说说你的感受，问一个关于出处的问题，并用 If I were you, I would… 写一句。然后像留语音那样把它读出来。
