Dear Teacher, I take evening classes after work, and I have a problem with revision. Every time I sit down to study for a test, I pick up my phone after ten minutes. Then an hour disappears and I feel bad. My marks are getting worse, so I am starting to lose confidence. What should I do?

亲爱的老师：我在下班后上夜校，复习方面有个问题。每次坐下来准备考试，十分钟后我就会拿起手机。然后一个小时就不见了，我感觉很糟糕。我的成绩越来越差，我开始失去信心。我该怎么办？

Thank you for your honest letter. You are not alone: almost everyone finds it hard to pay attention when a phone is nearby. The first step is simple. Put your phone in another room before you start, because it is much harder to ignore something you can see.

谢谢你坦诚的来信。你并不孤单：几乎每个人在手机就在旁边时都很难集中注意力。第一步很简单。开始之前把手机放到另一个房间，因为看得见的东西要无视起来难得多。

Next, make your sessions shorter. Many people believe that longer is better, but thirty minutes of focused study is usually more useful than two tired hours. Take a five-minute break, walk around, and then begin again. When you revise for a test, give each session one clear goal, such as learning ten new words or finishing one exercise.

接下来，把每次学习的时间缩短。很多人认为越长越好，但三十分钟的专注学习通常比疲惫的两小时更有用。休息五分钟，走动一下，然后重新开始。备考复习时，给每一次学习定一个明确的目标，比如学十个新单词或做完一个练习。

Finally, keep a simple record. Write down what you finished each evening. When you see a list of small successes, you will see that you can make progress, and you will be less tempted to give up. Do not expect a perfect week at once. Even two good evenings are a step forward.

最后，做一个简单的记录。写下每天晚上完成了什么。当你看到一串小小的成功，你就会明白自己能够进步，也就不那么容易放弃了。不要指望一下子就有完美的一周。哪怕有两个晚上学得不错，也是向前迈了一步。

Please write to me again in a month and tell me how it goes. Good luck, and remember that being tired and busy is normal. What matters is that you keep trying.

请一个月后再给我来信，告诉我进展如何。祝你好运，也请记住，又累又忙是正常的。重要的是你一直在尝试。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

Many people believe that longer is better, but thirty minutes of focused study is usually more useful than two tired hours.
’More useful than’ is a comparative for a long adjective, and ‘thirty minutes of focused study’ treats a period of time as one unit, so the verb is singular.
’more useful than’ 是长形容词的比较级；'thirty minutes of focused study' 把一段时间看作一个整体，所以动词用单数。
Put your phone in another room before you start, because it is much harder to ignore something you can see.
The sentence begins with an imperative, ‘much’ makes the comparative ‘harder’ stronger, and ‘you can see’ is a relative clause that describes ‘something’.
句子以祈使句开头；much 加强比较级 harder；'you can see' 是修饰 something 的关系从句。
