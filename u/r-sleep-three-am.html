Awake at Three in the Morning 凌晨三点还醒着

🧠 Learn the 4 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 4 个新词

Read a story about waking at 3 a.m. and see how the present perfect carries a habit that is still going on. 读一个凌晨三点醒来的故事，看现在完成时怎样承载一个延续到现在的习惯。

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

For the first six months after we moved to Toronto, I woke up at three every morning. Not slowly, but suddenly, with my heart racing and a list in my head: the rent, my daughter's new school, a job I had applied for and heard nothing about. I would lie awake until five, then fall asleep just before the alarm.

搬到多伦多后的头六个月，我每天凌晨三点醒。不是慢慢醒，而是一下子醒，心跳得飞快，脑子里一张清单：房租、女儿的新学校、一份投了简历却杳无音讯的工作。我会睁着眼躺到五点，然后在闹钟响前才睡着。

I tried everything I had read about online. I gave up coffee after lunch. I bought a lavender spray. I looked at my phone "just for a minute" and found myself still scrolling an hour later. None of it worked, because I was treating it as a sleep problem. It was really a worry problem that came out at night.

网上读到的办法我都试了。午饭后不喝咖啡，买了薰衣草喷雾。我拿起手机说「就看一分钟」，一小时后发现自己还在刷。全都没用，因为我把它当成了睡眠问题。它其实是一个在夜里冒出来的焦虑问题。

What changed things was a conversation with a neighbour, another newcomer who had arrived ten years earlier. She told me that she had kept a notebook by her bed in her first years. When she woke up worrying, she wrote the worry down, plus one small thing she could do about it the next day. Then she closed the notebook. "The worry will still be there in the morning," she said. "You can deal with it then."

改变这一切的是和邻居的一次聊天。她也是新移民，十年前来的。她说刚来那几年，她床边一直放着一个本子。半夜担心着醒来，就把担心的事写下来，再加一件第二天能为它做的小事，然后合上本子。「早上焦虑还在那儿，」她说，「到时候再处理。」

I have used that notebook for two years now. Some nights I write three lines; some nights I don't wake up at all. I have also built a routine to wind down: no work email after nine, a shower, twenty pages of a dull book. I have not become a perfect sleeper. But I have stopped fearing three o'clock.

这个本子我已经用了两年。有些夜里写三行，有些夜里根本不醒。我还养成了一套睡前放松的程序：九点后不看工作邮件，洗个澡，读二十页无聊的书。我没变成睡得完美的人，但我已经不怕凌晨三点了。

If you are lying awake tonight, here is what I have learned. First, sort out what you can act on tomorrow and what you can't, and write the first kind down. Second, if you have been sleeping badly for weeks and it is affecting your days, bring up the problem with your doctor. That is not weakness. It is maintenance.

如果你今晚也睁着眼躺着，这是我学到的：第一，把明天能做点什么的和做不了什么的分开，前一种写下来。第二，如果你已经睡不好好几个星期，而且影响到了白天，跟医生提一提这个问题。这不是软弱，是保养。

/ˈmeɪntənəns/ n.

Sleep is basic maintenance for the brain. 睡眠是大脑的基本保养。
/ˈreɪsɪŋ/ adj.
（心跳、思绪）飞快的

/waɪnd daʊn/ phr. v.

Reading helps me wind down. 读书能让我放松下来。

a bedtime routine; stick to a routine; build a routine
A regular routine helps children sleep. 固定的作息帮孩子入睡。

scroll through; keep scrolling
She scrolled through the news until midnight. 她刷新闻刷到半夜。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

I have used that notebook for two years now.
Present perfect with for + period: the habit started in the past and is still going on.
现在完成时配 for + 一段时间：习惯始于过去、延续到现在。
She told me that she had kept a notebook by her bed in her first years.
Reported speech moves her past back one step: had kept.
间接引语把她说的过去再往前推一步：had kept。
