Every clause needs a subject 每个分句都要主语

Make sure every English clause has its own stated subject, including after 'because', 'when', and 'if', and with weather, time, and existence statements. 确保每个英语分句都有自己明确的主语，包括 because、when、if 之后，以及天气、时间和存在句。

B1 · CELPIP 5–6⏱ 25 minG05 Topic-comment sentences and double subjects主题句与双主语

👀 Look first: what changes?先看对比：差在哪里

Read the sentences and decide what each one tells you. Then open the notes.先读这几句，自己判断各自传达了什么，再点开看注解。

I missed the bus because I woke up late. 我错过了公交车，因为我起晚了。
Complete clause: 'because' is followed by its own subject, so the reason is stated clearly.完整分句：because 后面有自己的主语，原因表达清楚。
I woke up late and missed the bus. 我起晚了，错过了公交车。
Also correct: one shared subject with two verbs joined by 'and', so 'because' is not needed.同样正确：共用一个主语，用 and 连接两个动词，这样就不需要 because。
I missed the bus, so I was late for work. 我错过了公交车，所以上班迟到了。
Each clause keeps its own stated subject; here 'so' joins them instead of 'because'.每个分句都有自己明确的主语，这里用 so 连接两个分句。

📏 The rule规则

📏 The rule in one line规则一句话
English never drops the subject just because it is obvious from context, so every finite clause needs a subject, even an empty 'it' or 'there'.英语不会因为上下文显而易见就省略主语，每个限定分句都必须有主语，哪怕只是空主语 it 或 there。

📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Open the full explanation: 5 points, 23 examples展开细讲：5 段讲解、23 个例句

Chinese lets you drop a subject whenever the listener can recover it, and a whole chain of clauses can run on with no subject at all. English works the other way: each finite verb needs its own subject, even when it is the same person as in the previous clause. When you write a second clause, ask yourself whether it has its own subject before you move on.

汉语只要听者能推断出来，主语就可以省略，一连串分句可以完全没有主语。英语正好相反：每个限定动词都需要自己的主语，即使和上一个分句是同一个人。写第二个分句时，先问自己它有没有主语。

Subordinate clauses are where learners drop subjects most often, because the clause feels like a mere continuation. After 'because', 'when', 'if', 'although', and 'since', you must restate the subject, even if it matches the main clause. Compare 'I stayed home because was tired' with 'I stayed home because I was tired'. Only the second one is a complete clause.

从句是学习者最容易漏掉主语的地方，因为从句感觉只是前文的延续。在 because、when、if、although、since 之后必须重新写出主语，即使它与主句主语相同。比较 'I stayed home because was tired' 和 'I stayed home because I was tired'，只有后者是完整的分句。

Weather, time, distance, and general situations have no real doer, but English still demands a subject, so it supplies a dummy 'it'. Use 'It is raining', 'It is late', 'It takes two hours'. The same applies to existence: where Chinese simply says that something exists with a verb meaning 'have', English needs 'there is' or 'there are'. Leaving out 'it' or 'there' makes the sentence feel unfinished.

天气、时间、距离和一般情况没有真正的施动者，但英语仍然需要主语，所以用形式主语 it。要说 'It is raining'、'It is late'、'It takes two hours'。存在句也一样：汉语直接用动词“有”，英语则需要 there is 或 there are。省掉 it 或 there，句子就显得不完整。

A subject may only be left out in a few fixed cases: imperatives ('Close the door'), coordinated verbs that share one subject ('She opened the door and walked in'), and informal notes or messages ('Hope you are well'). Do not stretch these exceptions to ordinary sentences. In writing, if two verbs share a subject, join them with 'and' rather than separating them with a comma.

只有少数固定情况可以省略主语：祈使句（'Close the door'）、共用同一主语的并列动词（'She opened the door and walked in'），以及非正式的便条或短信（'Hope you are well'）。不要把这些例外扩展到普通句子。书面表达中，如果两个动词共用一个主语，要用 and 连接，而不是只用逗号隔开。

A related trap is using a comma to chain clauses together when the second clause has its own subject, such as 'He was tired, went home early'. If you keep the comma, the second verb still needs its subject: 'He was tired, so he went home early'. If you want to share the subject, drop the comma and use 'and': 'He was tired and went home early'.

相关的陷阱是用逗号把分句串起来，却没有给第二个分句写主语，例如 'He was tired, went home early'。如果保留逗号，第二个动词仍然需要主语：'He was tired, so he went home early'。如果想共用主语，就去掉逗号，用 and：'He was tired and went home early'。

More pairs: same idea, different choice更多对比句组

It is cold today. 今天很冷。
Dummy 'it' for weather: the sentence has a subject even though nothing actually does anything.天气用形式主语 it：虽然没有真正的施动者，句子仍有主语。
The weather is cold today. 今天天气很冷。
A full noun subject can replace the dummy 'it' when you want to name the weather explicitly.想明确提到“天气”这个词时，可以用完整名词主语代替形式主语 it。
It feels much colder today than yesterday. 今天感觉比昨天冷多了。
Dummy 'it' also works with verbs like 'feels', not only 'is'.形式主语 it 也可以搭配 feels 等动词，不限于 is。
There is a café on the corner. 拐角有一家咖啡馆。
Existence needs 'there is', not a bare verb of having.存在句需要 there is，而不是单用表示“有”的动词。
A café is located on the corner. 一家咖啡馆就在拐角处。
A full noun subject can replace 'there is' when you want to make the café itself the topic.想让咖啡馆本身成为话题时，可以用完整名词主语代替 there is。
There used to be a café on the corner. 拐角曾经有一家咖啡馆。
'There' stays as the subject when a modal or 'used to' is added before 'be'.在 be 前面加情态动词或 used to 时，there 仍然充当主语。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ Because was too expensive, I did not buy it.
✓ Because it was too expensive, I did not buy it.
The clause after 'because' needs its own subject. Learners drop it because the Chinese equivalent leaves the subject out when it is obvious.
because 之后的分句需要自己的主语。学习者之所以省略，是因为汉语在主语显而易见时会省略。
✗ Is very hot in summer here.
✓ It is very hot in summer here.
Weather and temperature statements need the dummy subject 'it'. The Chinese version has no subject, so learners leave it out in English too.
天气和温度的陈述需要形式主语 it。汉语没有主语，所以学习者在英语里也漏掉了。
✗ In my city has many parks.
✓ My city has many parks. / There are many parks in my city.
Learners translate the Chinese existence pattern, which starts with a place and a verb meaning 'have', so the English sentence ends up with a location phrase instead of a subject. Either make the place the subject or use 'there are'.
学习者直译汉语以地点开头加“有”的存在句，结果英语句子只有地点短语而没有主语。要么让地点做主语，要么用 there are。
✗ He came home, took off his coat, sat down, is very tired.
✓ He came home, took off his coat, sat down, and was very tired.
In a list of verbs the subject can be shared, but the last item must be joined with 'and' and must have the correct form. A stray verb with no subject and the wrong form is a very common run-on error.
在动词并列的结构中主语可以共用，但最后一项要用 and 连接并使用正确的形式。没有主语且形式错误的孤立动词是很常见的连写错误。
✗ I bought a new phone, but doesn't work.
✓ I bought a new phone, but it doesn't work.
After 'but' the second clause has a different subject, the phone, so it must be stated. Dropping it is natural in the Chinese sentence but not in English.
but 之后的第二个分句主语是另一个事物，即手机，所以必须写出来。汉语里省略很自然，英语里则不行。
