Pausing in thought groups 意群停顿

📘 In your coursebook: English File 5e · Upper-Intermediate · 10B “Freedom of speech”. Use the texts and recordings with your coursebook; this page adds new words, grammar and practice.教材定位：English File 5e · Upper-Intermediate · 10B《Freedom of speech》。课文与录音请配合教材学习，本页补充新词、语法与练习。

Pause where the meaning breaks, not where you run out of breath or words, so that long sentences are easy to follow and you sound in control. 在意思断开的地方停，而不是在没气或卡词的地方停，让长句好跟，听起来也从容。

B2 · CELPIP 7–8⏱ 20 minP09 Words are not linked; each one is said alone不连读，一词一顿P10 Flat or wrong intonation; the important word is not stressed语调平，重点词不突出

📏 The rule规则

📏 The point in one line一句话要领
Speak in chunks of meaning, usually three to seven words: a subject, a verb phrase, a time or place, a clause. Link everything inside a chunk, give each chunk one main stress, and pause briefly between chunks.按意思分块说话，一块通常三到七个词。块内全部连起来，每块一个主重音，块与块之间短暂停顿。
  • Easy to miss易漏 ✗ I went to the... bank. (pause after _the_) → ✓ I went to the bank. | Then...

🗣 Say it跟读

Tap 🔊, listen, then say it aloud. Use ● to record yourself and compare.点 🔊 听，再开口跟读；点 ● 录下自己的声音对比（录音只在本机）。

In most cases | it takes about a week. /ɪn ˈməʊs ˈkeɪsɪz ɪt ˈteɪks əˌbaʊt ə ˈwiːk/
Pause after the opening phrase. 开头状语后停。
The woman who called this morning | wants to reschedule. /ðə ˈwʊmən hu ˈkɔːld ðɪs ˈmɔːnɪŋ wɒnts tə riːˈʃedjuːl/
Pause after the long subject, not inside it. 长主语之后停，不在主语中间停。
If you can't make it | just let me know. /ɪf ju ˈkɑːnt ˈmeɪk ɪt dʒʌst ˈlet mi ˈnəʊ/
Slight rise on MAKE IT, fall on KNOW. 前块微升，后块降。
The code is | four | seven | two. /ðə ˈkəʊd ɪz ˈfɔː ˈsevən ˈtuː/
Pause before and between key digits. 关键数字前、数字间停。
When I first arrived | I didn't know anyone | and I didn't have a car. /wen aɪ ˈfɜːst əˈraɪvd aɪ ˈdɪdənt nəʊ ˈeniwʌn ən aɪ ˈdɪdənt hæv ə ˈkɑː/
Three groups, three nuclei. 三个意群，三个核心。
The main issue, | I think, | is timing. /ðə ˈmeɪn ˈɪʃuː aɪ ˈθɪŋk ɪz ˈtaɪmɪŋ/
I think is its own short group. I think 自成一小组。
What I'd like to know | is how long it'll take. /ˈwɒt aɪd laɪk tə ˈnəʊ ɪz haʊ ˈlɒŋ ɪtl ˈteɪk/
Pause after the what-clause. what 从句后停。
My manager, | who's usually very calm, | was furious. /maɪ ˈmænɪdʒə huːz ˈjuːʒuəli veri ˈkɑːm wəz ˈfjʊəriəs/
Commas become pauses. 逗号变成停顿。
A woman, | without her, | man is nothing. /ə ˈwʊmən wɪˈðaʊt hɜː ˈmæn ɪz ˈnʌθɪŋ/
Same words, different grouping. 同样的词，不同的分组。
A woman without her man | is nothing. /ə ˈwʊmən wɪˈðaʊt hə ˈmæn ɪz ˈnʌθɪŋ/
The other reading. 另一种读法。
Before you sign anything, | read the second page carefully. /bɪˈfɔː ju ˈsaɪn eniθɪŋ ˈriːd ðə ˈsekənd peɪdʒ ˈkeəfəli/
Rise on SIGN, fall on CARE. SIGN 上升，CARE 上降。
I went to the bank | to pay a bill. /aɪ ˈwent tə ðə ˈbæŋk tə ˈpeɪ ə ˈbɪl/
Not "I went to the... bank". 不要在 the 后面停。
As far as I know, | the office is closed on Monday. /əz ˈfɑːr əz aɪ ˈnəʊ ði ˈɒfɪs ɪz ˈkləʊzd ɒn ˈmʌndeɪ/
A fixed opener makes one group. 固定开头语自成一组。
The problem isn't the cost; | it's the time it takes. /ðə ˈprɒbləm ˈɪzənt ðə ˈkɒst ɪts ðə ˈtaɪm ɪt teɪks/
Contrast across two groups. 跨两个意群的对比。
Could you tell me | which form I need | and where to send it? /kʊdʒu ˈtel mi wɪtʃ ˈfɔːm aɪ niːd ən ˈweə tə ˈsend ɪt/
Three short groups make a long question easy. 三个短组让长问句好懂。

📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Open the full explanation: 5 points展开细讲：5 段讲解

Listen to someone reading A woman without her man is nothing in two ways. With pauses after woman and man it means one thing; with a pause after her (A woman, without her, man is nothing) it means the opposite. Pauses in speech do the work of commas in writing, and sometimes the work of brackets and full stops too. Where you pause tells the listener how the words belong together.

同一句话停顿位置不同，意思可以完全相反。口语里的停顿起书面逗号的作用，有时还起括号和句号的作用：在哪停，告诉对方哪些词是一组。

Chinese learners often pause in one of two wrong places. They stop between every word or two, because each word is being retrieved separately, or they stop in the middle of a chunk to search for the next word, for example after the or of: I went to the... bank. A pause after a grammar word sounds like hesitation; a pause after a complete chunk sounds like thinking.

华人常在两种错误位置停：每一两个词停一下，因为每个词都是单独想出来的；或者在一块中间、比如 the 或 of 之后停下来找词。在功能词后停，听起来是卡住；在一整块之后停，听起来是在思考。

Good places to pause: after a long subject (The woman who called this morning | wants to reschedule), before and after an introductory phrase (In most cases | it takes a week), between clauses (If you can't make it | just let me know), and before an important number or name so it stands out (The code is | four seven two).

好的停顿位置：长主语之后、开头的状语前后、从句之间，以及重要数字或名字之前，让它突出。

Each thought group has its own nucleus, the word with the main stress and pitch movement. Non-final groups often end with a slight rise or level tone that says "more is coming"; the last group falls. This is the same signal as in lists, and it lets the listener plan: they know when to wait and when it is their turn.

每个意群有自己的核心重音。非末尾的意群常以微升或平调结束，表示「还没完」；最后一个意群下降。这和列举的信号一样，让对方知道何时等、何时轮到自己。

If you need time to think, pause at a group boundary and fill it with a planned phrase rather than silence in the wrong place: The main issue, | I think, | is timing. In listening, thought groups are the units you should decode; trying to catch single words in fast speech is slower than catching chunks.

需要时间想，就在意群边界停，用预备好的短语填，而不是在错误位置沉默。听力上，意群才是你该解码的单位；逐词去抓比按块去抓慢。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ I went to the... bank. (pause after the)
✓ I went to the bank. | Then...
A pause inside a chunk sounds like you lost the word.
块内停顿像是忘词了。
✗ The woman who | called this morning wants | to reschedule.
✓ The woman who called this morning | wants to reschedule.
Wrong boundaries make the listener rebuild the sentence.
边界错了，对方要重新拼句子。
✗ Pausing after every word to be clear
✓ Pausing after every chunk
Too many pauses make every word equal and nothing clear.
停得太多，字字等重，反而什么都不清楚。
✗ Silent searching in the middle of a sentence
✓ A planned filler at a boundary: The main issue, I think, ...
Fill thinking time at the right place.
在对的地方用填充语换取思考时间。
