Defining and non-defining relative clauses 限定与非限定定语从句

Decide whether a relative clause identifies the noun or only adds to it, and punctuate it so the reader gets the right meaning. 判断定语从句是在「限定是哪一个」还是「补充说明」，并用标点让读者读出正确的意思。

The tenants who complained about the noise got a discount. 投诉噪音的那些租户拿到了折扣。
Only some tenants complained.只有部分租户投诉了。
The tenants, who complained about the noise, got a discount. 租户们都投诉了噪音，也都拿到了折扣。
All the tenants complained.所有租户都投诉了。
The tenants that complained about the noise got a discount. 投诉噪音的那些租户拿到了折扣。
Same as the first: that is fine in a defining clause.和第一句一样：限定从句里可以用 that。

📏 The rule规则

📏 The rule in one line规则一句话
A defining clause tells you which one (the neighbour who shovels our walk); remove it and the sentence loses its point. A non-defining clause adds a fact about something already clear (my mother, who lives in Richmond); it sits between commas and never takes that.限定性从句告诉你「是哪一个」，去掉它句子就没了重点；非限定性从句给一个已经明确的东西补充信息，放在逗号之间，而且永远不用 that。
  • Easy to miss易漏 ✗ My mother, that lives in Shanghai, calls every Sunday. → ✓ My mother, who lives in Shanghai, calls every Sunday.

Chinese puts all modifiers before the noun with de, so there is no comma to choose. In English the comma changes the facts. My sister who lives in Toronto is a nurse suggests I have more than one sister. My sister, who lives in Toronto, is a nurse means I have one sister, and I am telling you where she lives.

中文的修饰语都用「的」放在名词前，不存在用不用逗号的问题。英语里逗号会改变事实：My sister who lives in Toronto is a nurse 暗示我不止一个姐妹；My sister, who lives in Toronto, is a nurse 表示我只有一个姐妹，顺便告诉你她住哪。

In defining clauses, that is the everyday choice for things and is common for people in speech; which is also possible for things, especially in British English. When the relative word is the object, leave it out: the course I took last year, the email you sent me. Fluent speakers drop it most of the time, and keeping it can sound stiff.

在限定性从句里，指物最常用 that，口语里指人也常用 that；which 也能指物，英式英语里更多。关系词作宾语时可以省：the course I took last year、the email you sent me。母语者大多会省，留着反而显得生硬。

Non-defining clauses use who, which or whose, never that, and the relative word cannot be dropped. They are more common in writing than in speech; in conversation people often start a new sentence instead. In speech, the commas become short pauses and a lower voice, as if the clause were in brackets.

非限定性从句用 who、which、whose，不用 that，关系词也不能省。它在书面语里比口语里常见；聊天时人们往往干脆另起一句。说出来时，逗号变成短暂的停顿和略低的声调，像是加了括号。

A sentence-level which comments on the whole previous idea: The bus was cancelled, which meant we were late for the interview. This is very useful in emails and stories, but it always needs a comma, and it cannot be replaced by that or what.

句子层面的 which 评论前面整件事：The bus was cancelled, which meant we were late for the interview。写邮件、讲故事都很好用，但前面必须有逗号，也不能换成 that 或 what。

where, when and whose also start relative clauses. the clinic where I had my test, the year when we arrived, the family whose house burned down. With the reason, use why or that, or nothing: the reason we moved. Learners often write the reason is because; in careful English, the reason is that is safer.

where、when、whose 也能引导定语从句：the clinic where I had my test、the year when we arrived、the family whose house burned down。说 the reason 时用 why、that 或者都不用：the reason we moved。学习者常写 the reason is because，正式一点用 the reason is that 更稳。

Examples例句

  • The neighbour who shovels our walk every winter is eighty-two. 每年冬天帮我们铲门前雪的那位邻居八十二岁了。
    Defining: which neighbour.限定：哪位邻居。
  • My father, who never learned to drive, takes the bus everywhere. 我父亲从没学过开车，去哪都坐公交。
    Non-defining: I have one father.非限定：父亲只有一个。
  • The course I took last spring changed how I write emails. 我去年春天上的那门课改变了我写邮件的方式。
    Object relative dropped.作宾语的关系词省略了。
  • Is this the form that needs a signature? 要签名的是这张表吗？
    that for things.指物用 that。
  • Our dentist, whose office is near the station, is retiring next year. 我们的牙医明年退休，他的诊所在车站附近。
    whose in a non-defining clause.非限定从句里的 whose。
  • The clinic where I had my blood test has moved to a new building. 我验血的那家诊所搬进了新楼。
    where for a place.where 指地点。
  • We arrived in February, which is not the best month to see Winnipeg. 我们是二月到的，那可不是看温尼伯的好月份。
    Comment on the whole time.对整件事的评论。
  • The heating broke on Friday night, which meant nobody could fix it until Monday. 暖气周五晚上坏了，这就意味着周一前没人能修。
    Sentence-level which.句子层面的 which。
  • Anyone who has rented in this city knows how fast good places go. 在这座城市租过房的人都知道好房子没得多快。
    Defining after anyone.anyone 后接限定从句。
  • That's the reason we chose a smaller house. 这就是我们选了小一点房子的原因。
    the reason + no relative word.the reason 后不用关系词。
  • The person you need to speak to is on lunch until two. 你要找的那个人两点前在吃午饭。
    Object relative dropped, preposition at the end.省了关系词，介词在末尾。
  • Vancouver, where housing costs are among the highest in the country, is still growing. 温哥华的房价在全国数一数二，但城市仍在扩张。
    Non-defining where.非限定的 where。
  • Everything that the agent told us turned out to be true. 中介告诉我们的一切后来都证明是真的。
    After everything, use that or nothing.everything 后用 that 或不用关系词。
  • The students whose parents attended the meeting got extra help. 家长来开会的那些学生得到了额外的帮助。
    Defining whose.限定的 whose。

More pairs: same idea, different choice更多对比句组

My brother who works in Calgary is visiting next week. 我那个在卡尔加里工作的兄弟下周来。
I have more than one brother.我不止一个兄弟。
My brother, who works in Calgary, is visiting next week. 我兄弟下周来，他在卡尔加里工作。
I have one brother.我只有一个兄弟。
My brother is visiting next week. He works in Calgary. 我兄弟下周来。他在卡尔加里工作。
The spoken way: two sentences.口语常见：拆成两句。
The train was cancelled, which annoyed everyone. 火车取消了，这让每个人都很恼火。
Comment on the event.评论整件事。
The train that was cancelled was the last one. 被取消的那班火车是末班车。
Identifies which train.指明是哪班车。
The train, which was cancelled, was the last one. 那班火车是末班车，它被取消了。
Adds a fact about a train we already know.给已知的那班车补充信息。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ My mother, that lives in Shanghai, calls every Sunday.
✓ My mother, who lives in Shanghai, calls every Sunday.
Non-defining clauses never use that.
非限定从句不用 that。
✗ The bus was late, what made me miss the meeting.
✓ The bus was late, which made me miss the meeting.
A comment on the whole clause uses which.
评论整个句子用 which。
✗ This is the apartment which I told you about it.
✓ This is the apartment I told you about.
The relative word replaces the pronoun; do not repeat it.
关系词已经代替了宾语，不能再加 it。
✗ The reason is because the rent went up.
✓ The reason is that the rent went up.
In careful English, the reason is that.
正式英语用 the reason is that。
✗ People, who smoke, pay more for life insurance.
✓ People who smoke pay more for life insurance.
The clause tells you which people, so no commas.
从句在限定是哪些人，不加逗号。
