What Eleven Years of Tours Taught Me 带了十一年团，我学到的事

🧠 Learn the 4 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 4 个新词

Read a tour guide's short talk to new visitors and learn how the present perfect frames experience, while phrasal verbs keep the tone spoken and friendly. 读一位导游对新游客的简短讲话，学会用现在完成时铺陈经验，同时用短语动词让语气口语化、亲切。

B2 · CELPIP 7–8⏱ 20 minV06 Register: too formal, too casual, or too blunt语域不合：太正式、太随意或太冲G02 Present perfect and past simple confused现在完成时与一般过去时混用

📖 Key card知识卡
Come early, ask before you photograph people, spend where locals spend, and do less.早点来，拍人前先问，钱花在当地人花钱的地方，少安排一点。

Read and listen读与听

265 words
Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Good evening, everyone. I have been a guide in this city for eleven years, and I have taken thousands of visitors through the old market. Tonight I want to talk about the kind of visitor that locals are happy to see again, and the kind they are not.

大家晚上好。我在这座城市做导游十一年了，带过成千上万的游客逛老集市。今晚我想说说，哪种游客当地人乐意再见到，哪种不乐意。

The first thing I bring up with every group is timing. In peak season the old town is full by ten in the morning. If you come early or late, you see more, you spend less time in a queue, and the people who live upstairs can still get to work.

我跟每个团都会先提的是时间。旺季时，老城上午十点就挤满了。早点或晚点来，你能看到更多，排队时间更少，楼上住的人也还能去上班。

The second is photos. A market stall is somebody's job, not a backdrop. Ask before you take a picture of a person, and if they turn down your request, accept it. I have dealt with more than one angry shop owner who was tired of cameras in his face, and I don't blame him.

第二是拍照。集市上的摊位是别人的饭碗，不是背景板。给人拍照前先问一句，人家拒绝也要接受。我处理过不止一位被镜头怼脸、烦透了的店主发火，我不怪他们。

The third is money. A visitor who eats lunch in a family-run place leaves far more in the city than one who stays inside a resort all week. When people ask me how to reduce their footprint here, I tell them to start with where their money goes, and then think about how they get around.

第三是钱。在家庭小馆吃午饭的游客，留在这座城里的钱远比整周待在度假村里的人多。有人问我在这里怎样减少自己的影响，我说先看你的钱流向哪里，再考虑怎么出行。

Finally, slow down. Some of my guests have rushed through six countries on their itinerary and cannot tell me one thing they talked about with a local. Take on less. Sit in a square for an hour. The city will still be here tomorrow, and so, I hope, will the people who make it worth visiting.

最后，放慢脚步。我有些客人行程里一口气跑了六个国家，却说不出跟哪个当地人聊过一件什么事。少揽一点。在广场上坐一个小时。这座城市明天还在，我希望让它值得一来的那些人也还在。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

I have been a guide in this city for eleven years, and I have taken thousands of visitors through the old market.
Present perfect with for (a period up to now) and with a number (thousands) for total experience so far. No finished time is named, so past simple would sound wrong.
现在完成时：for 表示到现在为止的一段时间，数字（thousands）表示迄今累计的经验。没有提到已结束的时间，所以用一般过去时会不对。
The city will still be here tomorrow, and so, I hope, will the people who make it worth visiting.
so + auxiliary + subject (so will the people) avoids repeating will still be here. I hope is inserted between commas.
so + 助动词 + 主语（so will the people）避免重复 will still be here。I hope 用逗号插在句中。

Give a two-minute talk to visitors coming to your city. Start with how long you have lived there (present perfect), then give three tips using bring up, deal with, turn down or take on. Record it and listen for the /t/ at the end of dealt and taught.
给来你城市的游客做两分钟讲话。开头说你在那里住了多久（现在完成时），然后用 bring up、deal with、turn down 或 take on 给三条建议。录下来，听听 dealt、taught 词尾的 /t/ 有没有读出来。
