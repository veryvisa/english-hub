Who Does the Housework? 家务应该由谁来做？

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Dear Family Adviser, my partner and I both work full-time, but I seem to do most of the housework. I cook, clean, organise appointments, and remember what our children need for school. My partner says that work is exhausting and promises to help more, but the situation rarely changes. I am becoming frustrated, and small disagreements are turning into bigger arguments. What should I do?

亲爱的家庭顾问：我和伴侣都从事全职工作，但似乎大部分家务都是我在做。我做饭、打扫卫生、安排预约，还要记住孩子上学需要什么。伴侣说工作令人疲惫，并承诺会多帮忙，但情况很少发生变化。我越来越沮丧，小小的分歧也逐渐演变成更大的争吵。我应该怎么办？

Dear Reader, the first step is to recognise that housework includes more than visible tasks. Remembering appointments, planning meals, and checking school messages are also forms of family work. This mental responsibility can be tiring, even when other people do not notice it. Instead of arguing about who works harder, try making a complete list of the tasks that keep your household running.

亲爱的读者：第一步是认识到家务不仅包括看得见的劳动。记住预约时间、计划膳食和查看学校信息，也属于家庭事务。这种需要不断思考和安排的责任可能令人疲惫，即使其他人没有注意到。与其争论谁更辛苦，不如尝试列出维持家庭正常运转所需要完成的全部任务。

Next, discuss how responsibilities could be shared more fairly. You might agree that one person prepares dinner while the other cleans the kitchen. Children can also take part by putting away their toys or helping with simple household tasks. Although an equal division is not always possible, a fair arrangement should take everyone's working hours, abilities, and personal needs into account. Flexibility is often more useful than a rigid schedule.

接下来，讨论如何更加公平地分担责任。你们可以约定一个人准备晚餐，另一个人清理厨房。孩子们也可以参与其中，例如收拾自己的玩具或帮助完成简单的家务。虽然完全平均的分工并不总是可行，但公平的安排应该考虑每个人的工作时间、能力和个人需求。灵活性往往比僵化的时间表更有用。

Finally, review the arrangement regularly. A family schedule that works during a quiet month may become difficult when someone changes jobs or a child starts a new activity. Notice what is working and what needs to change. Remember that sharing responsibilities is not about keeping score. It is about creating a home where everyone feels respected, supported, and able to rest.

最后，要定期检查和调整分工。在比较清闲的月份适用的家庭安排，可能会在有人换工作或孩子开始参加新活动时变得难以执行。留意哪些安排有效，哪些地方需要改变。请记住，分担责任并不是为了计算谁做得更多，而是为了创造一个让每个人都感到受尊重、得到支持并且能够休息的家庭环境。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

Remembering appointments, planning meals, and checking school messages are also forms of family work.
Three gerund phrases form a compound subject, and the plural verb are agrees with the combined activities.
三个动名词短语构成并列主语，表示多个活动，因此使用复数动词 are。
Although an equal division is not always possible, a fair arrangement should take everyone's working hours, abilities, and personal needs into account.
Although introduces a concessive clause, while should expresses a recommendation rather than an absolute obligation.
Although 引导让步状语从句，而 should 表示建议，并非绝对的义务。
