Why We Sold Our Second Car 我们为什么卖了第二辆车

🧠 Learn the 3 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 3 个新词

Read and listen读与听

291 words
Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Two years ago my wife and I sold our second car. It was not a political decision. We worked out that it cost us a lot every month and spent most of its life parked in the driveway. Since then, I have not missed it nearly as much as I expected.

两年前我和妻子卖掉了第二辆车。这不是什么政治表态。我们算了一下，这辆车每个月花我们不少钱，大部分时间却停在车道上。从那以后，我想念它的程度远没有我原先以为的那么多。

Our situation is not everyone's. We live near a frequent bus route, my commute is twenty-five minutes each way, and our children are old enough to get around on their own. In a suburb with one bus an hour, my argument would fall apart, and I do not want to pretend otherwise.

我们的情况并不是人人都有。我们住在一条班次很密的公交线旁边，我单程通勤二十五分钟，孩子们也大到可以自己出行了。要是住在一小时才一班车的郊区，我这番道理就站不住，我也不想假装不是这样。

What surprised me was what changed. The bus takes longer, but I read on it, and the stress of merging onto the highway at rush hour has simply gone. Our kids have become far more independent; they read a timetable better than I do. We plan errands together now instead of each jumping into a car. The biggest improvement has been to our weekends, which used to disappear into driving people to places.

让我意外的是改变了什么。坐公交更慢，但我可以在车上看书，高峰时段挤上高速的压力干脆没了。孩子们独立多了，看时刻表比我还在行。现在我们一起安排要办的杂事，而不是各自跳上一辆车。最大的改善是周末，以前周末全耗在开车接送人上。

There are costs. Buses are less reliable in winter, and I have stood at a stop in minus twenty more than once. We have lost some flexibility: for trips out of town we rent a car, and for hockey we carpool with another family and take turns driving. Anyone who thinks transit is a perfect replacement for a car has not tried it with two hockey bags.

当然也有代价。冬天公交没那么靠得住，我不止一次在零下二十度的车站上等过车。我们也少了些灵活：出城就租车，打冰球就和另一家拼车、轮流开。谁要是觉得公共交通能完美替代汽车，那他一定没背着两个冰球包试过。

My advice is modest. Before you buy or replace a second car, try living without it for a month, and keep a note of what you actually use it for. You may find, as we did, that the expectation of needing it was bigger than the need itself.

我的建议很朴素：在买第二辆车或换车之前，先试着不用它过一个月，记下你实际拿它干了什么。你可能会像我们一样发现，觉得需要它的预期，比真实的需要大。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

Since then, I have not missed it nearly as much as I expected.
Since then + present perfect: from the sale until now. I expected is past simple because the expecting happened at one past time, before the sale.
since then 加现在完成时：从卖车到现在。I expected 用一般过去时，因为那种预期发生在卖车之前的某个时候。
Anyone who thinks transit is a perfect replacement for a car has not tried it with two hockey bags.
The subject is long: Anyone who thinks … for a car. The verb has not tried agrees with anyone (singular). Present perfect: never, up to now.
主语很长：Anyone who thinks … for a car。动词 has not tried 跟 anyone（单数）一致。现在完成时表示到现在为止从没有过。
