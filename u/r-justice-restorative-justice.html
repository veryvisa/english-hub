Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

When my neighbour's workshop was damaged during a burglary, the immediate consequences were practical and emotional. Tools had disappeared, repairs were expensive, and the sense of security that had made the neighbourhood feel familiar was suddenly weakened. The person responsible was eventually identified. At first, everyone expected the usual legal process to provide a clear answer. Yet the experience raised another question: what would actually help repair the harm?

当我邻居的工作室在一次入室盗窃中遭到破坏时，随之而来的后果既有实际层面，也有情感层面。工具不见了，维修费用高昂，而原本让社区显得熟悉和安全的感觉也突然减弱了。责任人最终被确认。起初，大家都希望通常的法律程序能够给出明确答案。然而，这段经历引出了另一个问题：究竟怎样才能真正弥补伤害？

A community mediator explained the idea of restorative justice. Rather than concentrating exclusively on punishment, this approach examines the harm caused, the needs of the victim, and the responsibilities of the offender. Participation is generally voluntary and should involve appropriate safeguards. While a restorative process cannot replace every criminal proceeding, it may provide something that a conventional sentence does not: an opportunity for meaningful communication and acknowledgement.

一名社区调解员向我们解释了恢复性司法的理念。这种方法并非只关注惩罚，而是研究已经造成的伤害、受害者的需求以及犯罪者的责任。参与通常是自愿的，并且应该具备适当的保障措施。尽管恢复性司法程序不能取代所有刑事诉讼，但它可能提供传统刑罚无法提供的东西：真正沟通和承认伤害的机会。

The process began with separate conversations. The victim described the financial loss and explained why the incident had affected his daily life. The offender was asked to listen without interrupting and to consider the consequences of his actions. Eventually, both agreed to take part in a carefully facilitated meeting. That said, nobody was promised forgiveness, and the victim retained the right to withdraw. The purpose was accountability, not emotional pressure.

这一过程从分别交谈开始。受害者描述了经济损失，并解释这起事件为什么影响了自己的日常生活。犯罪者被要求认真倾听，不得打断，并思考自己行为造成的后果。最终，双方同意参加一场经过谨慎协调的会面。话虽如此，没有人承诺必须给予宽恕，受害者也保留退出的权利。其目的是承担责任，而不是施加情感压力。

During the meeting, the offender acknowledged the damage and agreed to contribute to the repair costs through an arrangement both parties considered reasonable. The victim did not describe the experience as forgiveness, but said that being heard had helped restore a sense of control. The outcome was not perfect, and it did not erase the original offence. Nevertheless, restorative justice tends to be most meaningful when participants are safe, informed, and genuinely willing to engage.

在会面过程中，犯罪者承认了造成的损害，并同意按照双方认为合理的安排承担部分维修费用。受害者没有将这段经历描述为宽恕，但表示有人倾听了自己的感受，这帮助他重新获得了掌控感。结果并不完美，也没有抹去最初的犯罪行为。然而，当参与者感到安全、充分了解情况并且真正愿意参与时，恢复性司法往往最有意义。
