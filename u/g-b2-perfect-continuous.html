Present perfect simple or continuous 现在完成时与完成进行时

📘 In your coursebook: English File 5e · Upper-Intermediate · 2A “Call the doctor!”. Use the texts and recordings with your coursebook; this page adds new words, grammar and practice.教材定位：English File 5e · Upper-Intermediate · 2A《Call the doctor!》。课文与录音请配合教材学习，本页补充新词、语法与练习。

Choose between I've done and I've been doing by asking what you want the listener to notice: the result, or the activity and how long it has gone on. 想清楚你要对方注意什么：是结果，还是这件事本身和它持续了多久，再在 I've done 和 I've been doing 之间选。

B2 · CELPIP 7–8⏱ 30 minG02 Present perfect and past simple confused现在完成时与一般过去时混用

👀 Look first: what changes?先看对比：差在哪里

Read the sentences and decide what each one tells you. Then open the notes.先读这几句，自己判断各自传达了什么，再点开看注解。

I've painted the bedroom. 卧室刷好了。
The job is done; look at the result.活干完了，请看结果。
I've been painting the bedroom. 我一直在刷卧室。
This explains the smell or the paint on me; it may not be finished.解释油漆味或身上的漆，不一定刷完了。
I've painted two walls of the bedroom. 卧室我刷了两面墙。
A count of finished units.数已完成的部分。

📏 The rule规则

📏 The rule in one line规则一句话
The present perfect simple points at a result or a number you can count; the continuous points at an activity that has filled a period up to now, often still going or with visible traces.现在完成时指向结果或数得出的数量；现在完成进行时指向一段一直持续到现在的活动，往往还没停，或者留下了看得见的痕迹。

Both forms connect the past to now, so the question is not "past or present" but "what is the point of my sentence?" I've painted the kitchen is about the finished kitchen. I've been painting the kitchen explains the paint on my hands, and the job may not be done.

两种形式都把过去和现在连起来，所以要问的不是「过去还是现在」，而是「我这句话想说明什么」。I've painted the kitchen 讲的是刷好了的厨房；I've been painting the kitchen 解释的是我手上的油漆，活不一定干完了。

Numbers and counts take the simple form. I've written six emails this morning, not I've been writing six emails. A count means you are looking at completed units. Duration takes the continuous: I've been writing emails all morning.

有数量、有件数就用一般完成时：I've written six emails，不说 I've been writing six emails。数件数就是在看一件件完成的东西。讲持续多久用进行时：I've been writing emails all morning。

With for and since, many verbs work in both forms with almost the same meaning: I've lived here for ten years and I've been living here for ten years. The continuous sounds more temporary or more recent in feeling; the simple sounds more settled. With always and permanent situations, prefer the simple: I've always lived in cities.

配 for 和 since 时，很多动词两种形式都行，意思几乎一样。进行式听起来更像暂时的、近来的状态；一般式听起来更稳定。和 always 或永久的情况搭配时，优先用一般式。

State verbs (know, believe, own, belong, understand, seem) normally stay in the simple form: I've known her since school. A few verbs change meaning in the continuous: I've been thinking (considering) is fine, I've thought is a finished opinion.

状态动词（know、believe、own、belong、understand、seem）一般只用一般完成时。少数动词用进行式时意思会变：I've been thinking 是「一直在考虑」，没问题；I've thought 是已经形成的看法。

The continuous also carries a feeling. Who's been using my laptop? suggests annoyance at something you can see. You've been working too hard is concern based on what you notice now. In a complaint letter, we have been waiting for six weeks stresses the length of the wait better than any adjective.

进行式还带着情绪。Who's been using my laptop? 是看到痕迹后的不满；You've been working too hard 是看到对方现状后的关心。投诉信里 we have been waiting for six weeks 比任何形容词都更能强调等了多久。

Short, single actions do not go in the continuous for duration: I've been losing my keys means it keeps happening, not one loss. Use the simple for one event: I've lost my keys.

短暂的、一次性的动作不用进行式表持续。I've been losing my keys 的意思是「老是丢」，不是丢了一次；只丢一次要用 I've lost my keys。

You want to sayFormExample
A finished result that matters nowhave + past participleI've fixed the printer.
How many, how muchhave + past participleShe's read four of his novels.
An activity over a period up to nowhave been + -ingI've been reading his novels all month.
Visible traces of a recent activityhave been + -ingYour eyes are red. Have you been crying?
A state verb over a periodhave + past participleWe've known each other since 2010.
Something repeated and annoyinghave been + -ingSomeone has been parking in my spot.

Examples例句

  • I've been trying to call the clinic since nine, but the line is always busy. 我从九点就一直在给诊所打电话，可总是占线。
    Repeated attempts up to now.到现在为止反复在尝试。
  • We've called the clinic three times this week. 这周我们给诊所打了三次电话。
    A count, so the simple form.有次数，用一般完成时。
  • Sorry about the mess. We've been clearing out the garage. 不好意思，乱七八糟的，我们一直在清车库。
    The mess is the trace of the activity.乱是活动留下的痕迹。
  • We've cleared out the garage, so there's room for the car now. 车库清好了，现在车放得进去了。
    The result matters now.重点是结果。
  • She's been working at the hospital for twelve years. 她在那家医院工作了十二年了。
    Duration up to now; she still works there.持续到现在，她还在那里工作。
  • He's always worked nights, so he finds the day shift strange. 他一直上夜班，所以觉得白班很别扭。
    A long-term, settled pattern.长期、稳定的情况。
  • How long have you been learning English? 你学英语多久了？
    Asking about the length of an activity.问一个活动持续了多久。
  • How many words have you learned this month? 这个月你学了多少个词？
    Asking about a count.问数量。
  • I've known my landlord for years, so I just sent him a text. 我认识房东好多年了，所以直接给他发了条短信。
    A state verb stays simple.状态动词用一般式。
  • It's been raining since Sunday, and the basement is starting to smell. 从周日起一直在下雨，地下室开始有味道了。
    A weather activity still going on.天气活动还在继续。
  • Someone's been taking my lunch from the office fridge. 有人老是从办公室冰箱里拿我的午饭。
    Repeated and annoying.反复发生，令人恼火。
  • I've been thinking about what you said, and I think you're right. 我一直在想你说的话，觉得你说得对。
    think here means consider, so the continuous is fine.这里 think 是「考虑」，可以用进行式。
  • Prices have gone up again, so we've been cooking at home more. 物价又涨了，所以我们最近在家做饭多了。
    A result, then a recent habit.先说结果，再说近来的习惯。
  • The kids have been playing outside all afternoon, which is why they're so muddy. 孩子们整个下午都在外面玩，所以才一身泥。
    The continuous explains what you can see.用进行式解释眼前的情况。

More pairs: same idea, different choice更多对比句组

I've lived in Burnaby for eight years. 我在本拿比住了八年了。
Neutral and settled.中性，稳定。
I've been living in Burnaby for eight years. 我在本拿比已经住了八年。
Same facts, but feels like it might change.事实一样，但感觉可能会变。
I lived in Burnaby for eight years. 我在本拿比住过八年。
Finished: I no longer live there.已经结束，现在不住那里了。
Have you finished the report? 报告写完了吗？
Asking about the result.问结果。
Have you been working on the report? 你一直在写报告吗？
Asking about the activity, perhaps because you look tired.问的是这件事本身，也许因为你看起来很累。
How much of the report have you written? 报告你写了多少了？
Asking about the amount done.问完成了多少。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ I am living here for five years.
✓ I have been living here for five years.
Chinese uses no tense change here, but English needs a perfect form to link the past to now.
中文不变时态，英语要用完成形式把过去接到现在。
✗ I have been writing three reports today.
✓ I have written three reports today.
A number of finished items takes the simple form.
有件数的完成量用一般完成时。
✗ I have been knowing him since university.
✓ I have known him since university.
know is a state verb and does not go in the continuous.
know 是状态动词，不用进行式。
✗ Since two hours I wait for the bus.
✓ I have been waiting for the bus for two hours.
Use for with a length of time, since with a starting point, and a perfect continuous verb.
一段时间用 for，起点用 since，动词用完成进行式。
✗ I've been losing my phone yesterday.
✓ I lost my phone yesterday.
yesterday is a finished time, and losing a phone is a single event.
yesterday 是已结束的时间，丢手机是一次性动作。
