📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Open the full explanation: 6 points展开细讲：6 段讲解

A compound noun combines two or more words to name one person, object, place, or concept. The first word often describes or identifies the second: a coffee shop is a type of shop, and a swimming pool is a type of pool. Chinese learners should pay attention to the main noun, which usually comes last.

复合名词由两个或多个单词组合而成，用来表示一个人、物品、地点或概念。第一个词通常修饰或限定第二个词，例如 coffee shop 是一种商店，swimming pool 是一种泳池。中文学习者要注意，核心名词通常位于最后。

Compound nouns can be written as one word, two separate words, or hyphenated words. For example, bedroom is written as one word, bus stop as two words, and mother-in-law with hyphens. There is no single spelling rule that works for every compound, so learners should learn common combinations as complete vocabulary items.

复合名词可以写成一个单词、两个分开的单词，或用连字符连接。例如 bedroom 写成一个词，bus stop 写成两个词，mother-in-law 则使用连字符。复合词没有适用于所有情况的统一拼写规则，因此应将常见组合当作完整词汇记忆。

Compound adjectives often combine a noun, adjective, or number with another word to describe a person or thing. Examples include user-friendly, full-time, and five-year-old. When these expressions appear before a noun, a hyphen often makes the relationship between the words clearer.

复合形容词通常由名词、形容词或数字等与其他词组合而成，用来描述人或事物。例如 user-friendly、full-time 和 five-year-old。当这些表达放在名词前面时，通常使用连字符，使词语之间的关系更加清楚。

A common mistake is changing the order of words based on Chinese sentence structure. In English, the modifying element generally comes before the main noun in a compound. Say a car park, not a park car, and a school bus, not a bus school.

中文学习者常见的错误是按照中文语序排列复合词。在英语中，修饰成分通常放在核心名词之前。应该说 a car park，而不是 a park car；应该说 a school bus，而不是 a bus school。

Some compound adjectives include a number and a singular noun, such as a ten-minute break or a three-bedroom house. The noun remains singular even when the number is greater than one because the whole expression functions as an adjective. Compare a ten-minute walk with a walk of ten minutes.

有些复合形容词由数字和名词组成，例如 a ten-minute break 和 a three-bedroom house。即使数字大于一，名词仍然使用单数形式，因为整个结构充当形容词。可以对比 a ten-minute walk 和 a walk of ten minutes。

Not every compound adjective drops its hyphen after a linking verb. Some, such as free range (The chickens are free range) or hands on (The training is very hands on), are often written without one in that position. Well-established compounds like user-friendly and well-known keep the hyphen in every position, including after is, as the examples in this set show. Follow standard dictionary usage rather than guessing from a single pattern.

并非所有复合形容词在系动词后都会去掉连字符。有些词，例如 free range（The chickens are free range）或 hands on（The training is very hands on），在这个位置常常不加连字符。而像 user-friendly 和 well-known 这类固定搭配，无论放在哪个位置（包括 is 之后）都保留连字符，本组例句就是如此。应参考标准词典的写法，而不要只凭一种规律猜测。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ I am looking for a job part time.
✓ I am looking for a part-time job.
When part-time describes a noun, it normally comes before the noun and is hyphenated. The expression can also follow a verb without a hyphen: I work part time.
part-time 修饰名词时，通常放在名词前并使用连字符。放在动词后作状语时，可以不加连字符，例如 I work part time。
✗ She has a five-years-old daughter.
✓ She has a five-year-old daughter.
A number-based compound adjective uses a singular noun, even when the number is greater than one. The noun does not take a plural ending inside the compound.
由数字构成的复合形容词中，名词始终使用单数形式，即使数字大于一也不加复数词尾。
✗ He works in a shop coffee.
✓ He works in a coffee shop.
In this compound noun, coffee identifies the type of shop. English generally places the modifying element before the main noun.
在这个复合名词中，coffee 用来说明商店的类型。英语通常把修饰成分放在核心名词之前。
✗ This software is very user-friend.
✓ This software is very user-friendly.
The established compound adjective is user-friendly, not user-friend. The ending -ly is part of the adjective and should not be removed.
固定复合形容词是 user-friendly，而不是 user-friend。这里的 -ly 是形容词的一部分，不能省略。
✗ We need a long term plan.
✓ We need a long-term plan.
Long-term is conventionally hyphenated when used as an adjective before a noun. Consistent spelling makes compound adjectives easier to recognise.
long-term 放在名词前作形容词时，通常使用连字符。保持规范拼写有助于读者识别复合形容词。
✗ My mother in law lives nearby.
✓ My mother-in-law lives nearby.
Mother-in-law is a conventional hyphenated compound noun. Its plural form is mothers-in-law, with the main noun taking the plural ending.
mother-in-law 是一个固定使用连字符的复合名词。它的复数形式是 mothers-in-law，复数词尾加在核心名词 mother 上。
