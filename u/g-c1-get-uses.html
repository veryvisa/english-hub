get: become, passive, causative and phrasal get 的各种用法：变化、被动、使役与短语

📘 In your coursebook: English File 5e · Advanced · 3A “Online or IRL?”. Use the texts and recordings with your coursebook; this page adds new words, grammar and practice.教材定位：English File 5e · Advanced · 3A《Online or IRL?》。课文与录音请配合教材学习，本页补充新词、语法与练习。

Choose and use get accurately to express changes of state, passive events, causation, and common phrasal meanings in different contexts. 能够根据语境准确使用 get 表达状态变化、被动事件、使役关系及常见短语含义。

The situation became worse after the announcement. 公告发布后，情况变得更糟。
A relatively formal description that emphasizes the transition into a new state.较正式的表达，强调状态发生转变。
The situation got worse after the announcement. 公告发布后，情况变得更糟。
A more conversational description of the resulting change.更口语化的表达，突出变化后的结果。
The situation was worse after the announcement. 公告发布后，情况更糟了。
A description of the condition at a particular time, without emphasizing the process of change.描述某个时间点的状态，不强调变化过程。

📏 The rule in one line规则一句话
Get is especially useful when presenting a change, an event affecting someone, or an action arranged through another person, while the surrounding words determine which meaning is intended.Get 的关键不在于记住一个固定中文意思，而在于根据后面的形容词、过去分词、宾语或副词判断它表达的是状态变化、被动经历、安排他人做事还是短语动词。

📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Open the full explanation: 6 points, 24 examples展开细讲：6 段讲解、24 个例句

Choose get rather than become when describing a noticeable change in everyday situations, especially in conversation. Become is usually more formal and often emphasizes the transition itself, whereas get makes the result sound immediate and natural. For example, get tired focuses on reaching a tired state during an activity.

描述日常生活中明显的状态变化时，可以优先考虑 get，而不是 become。Become 通常更正式，也更强调状态转变本身；get 则更自然地突出变化后的结果。例如，get tired 强调活动过程中逐渐变得疲惫。

Choose get plus a past participle when the subject experiences an event, particularly when the event is unexpected, inconvenient, or personally significant. Compared with the be passive, the get passive often makes the event feel more dynamic and emphasizes what happened to the subject. It is especially common with verbs such as hurt, caught, fired, and robbed.

当主语经历某个事件，尤其是事件具有意外性、不便性或个人影响时，可以使用 get 加过去分词。与 be 被动语态相比，get 被动语态往往更突出事情发生的过程，以及主语受到的影响。它常见于 hurt、caught、fired 和 robbed 等动词。

Choose get something done when you arrange for another person to perform a task, rather than simply describing an action you perform yourself. The construction emphasizes the completed result or the arrangement, and the person doing the work may remain unspecified. This is useful when discussing repairs, services, paperwork, and professional assistance.

当你安排别人完成某项工作，而不是强调自己亲自动手时，可以使用 get something done。这个结构突出工作完成的结果或安排过程，实际执行者可以不提及。它特别适用于维修、服务、文件办理和专业协助等场景。

Choose get someone to do something when you cause, persuade, or arrange for a particular person to act. Unlike make someone do something, get someone to do something often suggests persuasion, cooperation, or successful arrangement rather than direct authority. The infinitive must include to.

当你促使、说服或安排某个人采取行动时，可以使用 get someone to do something。与 make someone do something 不同，get someone to do something 通常暗示说服、合作或成功安排，而不是直接命令。这里的不定式必须带 to。

Choose get something rather than have something when the meaning is simply obtaining, receiving, or acquiring an item or opportunity. Get is particularly natural in informal conversation, while obtain and acquire are more formal alternatives. The intended meaning depends heavily on the object and surrounding context.

当意思只是获得、收到或取得某件物品或机会时，可以使用 get something。Get 在日常对话中尤其自然，而 obtain 和 acquire 更正式。具体含义需要结合宾语及上下文判断。

Choose a phrasal verb with get when the particle or preposition creates a meaning that cannot be understood by translating get alone. For example, get over can mean recover from a problem, while get through can mean finish a difficult task or successfully contact someone. Learn these combinations as complete expressions rather than translating each word separately.

当 get 与副词或介词组合后产生新的含义时，应将其作为完整短语动词理解，而不是单独翻译 get。例如，get over 可以表示从困难中恢复，get through 可以表示完成艰难任务或成功联系某人。学习时应结合具体语境记忆整个搭配。

More pairs: same idea, different choice更多对比句组

The employee was fired after the investigation. 调查结束后，这名员工被解雇了。
A relatively neutral passive statement focusing on the employment decision.较中性的被动表达，重点是解雇这一决定。
The employee got fired after the investigation. 调查结束后，这名员工被解雇了。
A more conversational and event-focused description of what happened to the employee.更口语化的表达，突出员工经历了被解雇这一事件。
The employee fired the manager after the investigation. 调查结束后，这名员工解雇了经理。
An active construction in which the employee performs the action.主动语态，员工是实施解雇行为的人。
I repaired my car yesterday. 我昨天修好了自己的汽车。
The speaker explicitly did the repair themselves; no arrangement with another person is implied.说话者自己完成了维修，没有暗示安排别人去做。
I got my car repaired yesterday. 我昨天找人把汽车修好了。
The speaker arranged for the car to be repaired, usually by someone else.说话者安排别人修好了汽车，通常由他人执行。
My car got repaired yesterday. 我的汽车昨天修好了。
The car experienced the repair event; the arrangement and repairer are not emphasized.汽车经历了维修这一事件，不强调安排者或维修人员。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ I got become angry when I heard the news.
✓ I got angry when I heard the news.
Get and become are alternative linking verbs in this construction. Do not combine them before the adjective. Use get angry for a natural conversational description of a change in emotion.
Get 和 become 在这里都是连接动词，不能在形容词前叠加使用。日常描述情绪变化时直接说 get angry 即可。
✗ He got marry last year.
✓ He got married last year.
The expression requires the past participle married, not the base verb marry. Get married is the standard expression for entering marriage.
这个表达需要使用过去分词 married，而不是动词原形 marry。Get married 是表示结婚的标准搭配。
✗ My laptop got broke during the flight.
✓ My laptop got broken during the flight.
A get passive requires a past participle after get. The correct participle of break is broken, not broke, which is its simple past form.
Get 被动语态中的 get 后面需要接过去分词。Break 的过去分词是 broken，而 broke 是过去式，不能用于这里。
✗ I got my hair cutted yesterday.
✓ I got my hair cut yesterday.
The causative construction requires a past participle. Cut has the same form in the base, past, and past participle forms, so cutted is incorrect.
使役结构中需要使用过去分词。Cut 的原形、过去式和过去分词形式相同，都是 cut，不能写成 cutted。
✗ I got my assistant prepare the report.
✓ I got my assistant to prepare the report.
When a person is the object of get in a causative construction, use the to-infinitive. The pattern is get someone to do something, not get someone do something.
当某人是 get 使役结构的宾语时，后面的动词必须使用带 to 的不定式。正确结构是 get someone to do something。
✗ I got to my car repaired yesterday.
✓ I got my car repaired yesterday.
The causative construction places the object directly after get. The word to does not belong between get and the object. Get to a place is a different construction meaning reach a destination.
使役结构中，宾语应直接放在 get 后面，get 和宾语之间不需要 to。Get to a place 是另一个表示到达某地的结构。
