📏 The rule规则

🎯 The point in one line一句话要领
The difference is grammar, not meaning: tell needs a person after it, say does not; talk is a conversation, speak is the act or the language.区别在语法，不在意思：tell 后面要接人，say 不用；talk 是交谈，speak 是开口这个动作或说某种语言。

📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Open the full explanation: 5 points展开细讲：5 段讲解

Say and tell both report words, so Chinese gives one verb for both. The rule is about the listener. Tell puts the person first: She told me (that) the office was closed. Say puts the words first, and if you add a person you need to: She said (to me) that the office was closed. He told that... and He said me... are both wrong.

say 和 tell 都是转述话语，中文一个「说」就够了。规则看听话的人：tell 先接人，She told me (that) the office was closed；say 先接话，加人要用 to，She said (to me) that the office was closed。He told that… 和 He said me… 都错。

Tell also lives in fixed chunks where no listener is named: tell the truth, tell a lie, tell a story, tell the difference, tell the time. Say has its own: say sorry, say goodbye, say no. Learn these as units, not as exceptions.

tell 还有一批不需要接人的固定搭配：tell the truth、tell a lie、tell a story、tell the difference、tell the time。say 也有自己的：say sorry、say goodbye、say no。当成整块记，别当例外记。

Talk is two-way and usually takes about and to/with: We talked about the lease, I talked to my landlord. Speak is more formal and one-way, as in a meeting or on the phone: Could I speak to the manager?. Languages always take speak: She speaks three languages.

talk 是双向的，常带 about 和 to/with：We talked about the lease、I talked to my landlord。speak 更正式、偏单向，比如开会或打电话：Could I speak to the manager?。说语言一律用 speak：She speaks three languages。

Remind is what someone else does to your memory: Remind me to call the school. Remember is what your own memory does: I remembered to call. Chinese often uses one word for both, so learners say Please remember me to call, which in English means something else entirely.

remind 是别人提醒你：Remind me to call the school；remember 是你自己想起来：I remembered to call。中文常一个「记得／提醒」混着用，于是有人说 Please remember me to call，这在英语里完全是另一个意思。

Borrow and lend point in opposite directions: you borrow from someone, someone lends to you. Hear happens to you; listen to is something you do on purpose. Can you lend me your pen? and I heard a noise, so I listened carefully show both pairs at once.

borrow 和 lend 方向相反：你向别人 borrow，别人 lend 给你。hear 是声音自己传进来，listen to 是你主动去听。Can you lend me your pen? 和 I heard a noise, so I listened carefully 一下子把两组都说清了。

🆚 Easy to mix up易混对比

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ He told that he was tired.
✓ He said that he was tired. / He told me that he was tired.
tell needs a person after it.
tell 后面必须接人。
✗ She said me the news.
✓ She told me the news. / She said the news to me.
say cannot take a person as a direct object.
say 不能直接接人作宾语。
✗ Please remember me to buy milk.
✓ Please remind me to buy milk.
remind sb to do. Remember me to sb means "say hello to them from me".
用 remind sb to do；remember me to sb 是「替我向某人问好」。
✗ Can I lend your car this weekend?
✓ Can I borrow your car this weekend?
You borrow from the owner; the owner lends.
向主人借用 borrow；主人借出用 lend。
✗ I'm listening music.
✓ I'm listening to music.
listen to needs to before the object.
listen 接宾语要加 to。
