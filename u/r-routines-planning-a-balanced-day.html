Read and listen读与听

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Maya: You always seem busy. Do you ever have time for yourself? Daniel: Not as much as I would like. I work during the day, help my children with their homework, and deal with household tasks in the evening. By the time everything is finished, I am usually too tired to do anything else. Maya: Perhaps you need a more realistic daily routine.

玛雅：你看起来总是很忙。你有自己的时间吗？丹尼尔：没有我希望的那么多。我白天工作，晚上辅导孩子做作业，还要处理家务。等所有事情都完成时，我通常已经太累，什么也不想做了。玛雅：也许你需要一个更加切实可行的日常作息。

Daniel: I have tried making schedules before, but they never seem to work. I write down everything I want to do, including exercise, reading, and learning a new skill. Then something unexpected happens, and the whole plan falls apart. Maya: That sounds like you are planning every minute. Why not leave some free time between activities? A good schedule should allow for changes.

丹尼尔：我以前也试过制定日程，但似乎从来没有成功过。我把所有想做的事情都写下来，包括锻炼、阅读和学习新技能。然后总会发生一些意想不到的事情，整个计划就被打乱了。玛雅：听起来你把每一分钟都安排好了。为什么不在活动之间留一些空闲时间呢？好的日程应该允许调整。

Daniel: That makes sense. I could prepare things for the next morning before going to bed. I could also take a short walk after lunch instead of sitting at my desk all afternoon. Maya: Exactly. You do not need to change everything at once. Start with one or two activities that matter most to you. Once they become part of your routine, you can consider adding something else.

丹尼尔：有道理。我可以在睡觉前准备好第二天早上需要的东西。我也可以在午饭后散一会儿步，而不是整个下午都坐在办公桌前。玛雅：正是如此。你不需要一次改变所有事情。先从一两项对你最重要的活动开始。一旦它们成为你日常作息的一部分，你就可以考虑增加其他内容。

Daniel: What if I cannot follow the plan every day? Maya: Do not worry about being perfect. Although a routine can provide structure, it should not control your entire life. If a family activity takes longer than expected, adjust your schedule. You might also choose a simpler version of your plan on particularly busy days. The aim is to build a routine that supports you, not one that makes you feel guilty.

丹尼尔：如果我不能每天都按照计划执行怎么办？玛雅：不要担心自己做得不够完美。虽然作息能够提供规律，但它不应该控制你的整个生活。如果家庭活动花费的时间超过预期，就调整你的日程。特别忙碌的日子里，你也可以选择一个更简单的计划版本。我们的目标是建立一种能够支持你的作息，而不是让你感到内疚的作息。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

Although a routine can provide structure, it should not control your entire life.
Although introduces a concessive clause, and the modal verb should expresses advice or a recommended way of behaving.
Although引导让步状语从句；情态动词should在这里表示建议或推荐的行为方式。
Once they become part of your routine, you can consider adding something else.
Once introduces a time clause, similar to 'when' or 'after', marking the point at which the first event has happened; consider is followed by a gerund rather than a to-infinitive.
Once引导时间状语从句，作用和when、after类似，标出前一件事已经发生的那个时间点；consider后面接动名词，而不是带to的不定式。
