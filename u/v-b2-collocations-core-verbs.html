📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Open the full explanation: 5 points展开细讲：5 段讲解

In Chinese one verb, zuo, covers decisions, research and effort alike. English splits them: make a decision, do research, make an effort. There is no rule that predicts every case, which is why these pairs are learned as chunks.

中文里一个「做」能配很多名词：做决定、做研究、做努力。英语把它们拆开，而且没有一条规则能预测全部，所以要整块记。

A rough guide helps with new cases. make tends to create something that did not exist: a decision, a plan, a complaint, a mistake, progress. do tends to carry out an activity or a task: research, homework, business, damage, a favour.

遇到新搭配时有个大致的方向：make 多指造出原本没有的东西；do 多指执行一项活动或任务。

take, have and give are "light verbs". The noun carries the meaning and the verb only makes a sentence possible: take a break, have an impact, give feedback. Advanced writers use them to pack an action into a noun that can then be described: have a lasting impact, give honest feedback.

take、have、give 是「轻动词」：意思在名词上，动词只负责成句。高级写作用它们把动作装进名词，再给名词加修饰。

The article is part of the chunk. make a decision, make an effort and take a break need a/an; make progress, do research, do damage, give feedback and give notice take none, because those nouns are uncountable.

冠词也是词块的一部分：decision、effort、break 要 a/an；progress、research、damage、feedback、notice 不可数，不加。

When you read, underline the verb in front of every abstract noun. Ten minutes of this with any article will give you more collocations than a list.

阅读时把每个抽象名词前面的动词画出来。随便拿一篇文章这样做十分钟，比背词表收获多。

🆚 Easy to mix up易混对比

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ I did a big mistake in the report.
✓ I made a big mistake in the report.
Mistakes, decisions and plans are "created": make.
mistake、decision、plan 是「造出来的」，用 make。
✗ We must do a decision today.
✓ We must make a decision today.
Never do a decision.
没有 do a decision 这个说法。
✗ She gave me a good feedback.
✓ She gave me good feedback.
feedback is uncountable: no a, no plural.
feedback 不可数，不加 a，没有复数。
✗ The new rule makes a big influence to us.
✓ The new rule has a big impact on us.
have an impact / influence on, with on.
have an impact on，介词用 on。
