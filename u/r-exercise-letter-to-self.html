A Letter to Myself at Forty 写给四十岁的自己

🧠 Learn the 3 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 3 个新词

Read a letter from a 62-year-old to her younger self and see unreal conditionals carry advice and regret. 读一封六十二岁写给年轻自己的信，看虚拟条件句怎样承载建议与遗憾。

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Dear Hui, You are forty, you have just taken on a bigger job, and you think exercise is something you will do later, when the children are older and work is calmer. I am writing from sixty-two to tell you that later does not come by itself. You have to make room for it.

亲爱的慧：你四十岁，刚接下一份更重的工作，觉得运动是以后的事，等孩子大一点、工作松一点再说。我从六十二岁写信告诉你：以后不会自己来，你得给它腾出地方。

I know what your days are like. You sit at a desk for nine hours and in the car for two. You keep putting off the swimming lessons you promised yourself. Your back is stiff in the mornings, and you tell yourself that is just age. It isn't. Much of it is sitting.

我知道你的日子是什么样：在桌前坐九个小时，在车里坐两个小时。你答应自己的游泳课一拖再拖。早上背是僵的，你跟自己说这只是年纪。不是的，很大一部分是坐出来的。

If I could change one thing, I would start strength training at your age, not at fifty-five, which is when I finally began. If I had started earlier, I would probably have avoided the shoulder injury and the year of physiotherapy that came with it. I would also have learned, twenty years sooner, that a short walk after dinner does more for my mood than another hour of email.

如果能改变一件事，我会在你这个年纪就开始力量训练，而不是等到五十五岁才终于开始。要是早点开始，我大概就能躲过那次肩伤和随之而来的一年理疗。我也会早二十年明白：晚饭后散一小会儿步，比再看一小时邮件对心情好得多。

Here is what worked when I did start. Warm up properly; your joints will thank you. Pace yourself: three short sessions a week beat one heroic one. Find something you enjoy, because you will only keep up what you like. For me that was a walking group and a strength class. The progress came quickly at first, then levelled off, and that was fine. It all builds up.

我真开始以后，这些管用：好好热身，关节会感谢你。量力而行，一周三次短练胜过一次拼命。找你喜欢的，因为你只会坚持喜欢的事。对我来说，是一个健走小组和一门力量课。进步起初来得快，后来平稳了，这也没关系。一点一点都攒着。

You do not have to figure out everything at once. Start with ten minutes. Deal with the excuses one by one. And when you finally catch up with me, I hope you will be stronger than I was at your age. With love and some impatience, Hui (62)

你不用一下子想清楚所有事。从十分钟开始，一个一个对付那些借口。等你终于赶上我的时候，希望你比我在你这个年纪时更强壮。爱你，也有点替你着急。慧（62 岁）

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

If I had started earlier, I would probably have avoided the shoulder injury and the year of physiotherapy that came with it.
Third conditional; probably sits between would and have, which keeps the claim honest.
第三类条件句；probably 放在 would 和 have 之间，让话不说太满。
If I could change one thing, I would start strength training at your age, not at fifty-five, which is when I finally began.
Unreal present with could; which is when adds information about the age just mentioned.
与现在事实相反用 could；which is when 补充说明刚提到的那个年龄。
