The First Week: What Nobody Tells You 入职第一周：没人告诉你的事

Read an advice column about starting a new job and collect the phrasal verbs offices run on. 读一篇入职建议专栏，收下办公室里天天用的短语动词。

The first week is for building a map of people, not for proving yourself.入职第一周是用来摸清人的，不是用来证明自己的。

Read and listen读与听

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Most advice about a new job is about the interview. Almost none of it is about the first week, when you have the job and no idea how anything works. I have started at five companies in two countries, and the first week has never gone the way I expected.

关于新工作的建议大多在讲面试，几乎没有人讲第一周：工作到手了，却完全不知道一切怎么运转。我在两个国家的五家公司入过职，第一周从来没有按我预想的走。

The first surprise is how little anyone has prepared. Your laptop has not been set up, your manager is in meetings, and the colleague who was supposed to hand over the project left on Friday. Do not take it personally. Use the empty hours to figure out who does what. Write names down. People notice when you remember them.

第一个意外是大家几乎什么都没准备。电脑没装好，经理在开会，本该向你交接项目的同事上周五就走了。别往心里去。用这些空档弄清楚谁负责什么，把名字记下来。你记得别人，别人是会注意到的。

The second surprise is that nobody will tell you the rules that matter. When do people take a break? Is it fine to bring up a problem in a meeting, or should you have a word with someone privately first? You will only find out by watching. If you run into something you do not understand, ask one person, quietly, and follow up with a short thank-you message.

第二个意外是，真正要紧的规矩没人会告诉你。大家什么时候休息？能在会上直接提出问题，还是该先私下跟人说一声？只有靠观察才知道。碰到不懂的事，悄悄问一个人，事后再发一条简短的感谢消息。

Finally, resist the urge to prove yourself at once. New staff often take on too much, make a mistake, and then spend a month sorting it out. Make an effort to do one small thing well instead. By Friday you will not have made much progress, and that is normal. What you will have is a map: who decides, who helps, and who simply talks. Everything after the first week depends on that map.

最后，忍住马上证明自己的冲动。新人常常揽下太多，出了错，再花一个月收拾。不如用心把一件小事做好。到周五你不会有多大进展，这很正常。你手里会有一张地图：谁拍板，谁帮忙，谁只是说说。第一周之后的一切都靠这张地图。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

Almost none of it is about the first week, when you have the job and no idea how anything works.
when adds a description of the first week. have has two objects: the job and no idea how anything works.
when 引出对「第一周」的补充说明；have 带两个宾语：the job 和 no idea how anything works。
What you will have is a map: who decides, who helps, and who simply talks.
What you will have is the subject (a cleft that saves the key word for the end). The three who clauses spell out the map.
What you will have 作主语（把关键词留到句末的分裂结构），冒号后三个 who 从句说明这张地图的内容。
