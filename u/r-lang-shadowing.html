Like a Shadow 像影子一样跟读

🧠 Learn the 4 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 4 个新词

Listen in on two learners before a library conversation circle and pick up the phrasal verbs they use about practice and problems. 听两个学习者在图书馆会话小组开始前聊天，学会他们谈练习和难处时用的短语动词。

B2 · CELPIP 7–8⏱ 20 minV04 Phrasal verbs avoided; the formal verb sounds stiff回避短语动词，说话太书面V10 Idioms and fixed chunks known but not used惯用语块认得但不会用

📖 Key card知识卡
Shadowing a short recording puts whole chunks in your mouth, so they come out on their own when you need them.跟读一段短录音能把整块的词组装进嘴里，需要时它们会自己冒出来。

Read and listen读与听

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Rosa: Sorry I'm late. I ran into my neighbour on the way and she wanted to catch up. Has the conversation circle started? Ken: Not yet, the coordinator is still setting up the chairs. How's your English going? Last time you said you felt stuck.

罗莎：抱歉来晚了。路上碰到邻居，她想聊聊近况。会话小组开始了吗？肯：还没，组织者还在摆椅子。你英语学得怎么样？上次你说感觉卡住了。

Rosa: Still stuck, honestly. I understand almost everything, but when I speak, I'm so slow. I translate word for word from my first language in my head. I'll never be fluent like this. Ken: I used to do that too. Have you tried shadowing? It's the thing that finally worked for me. Rosa: Shadowing? Like following someone?

罗莎：老实说，还是卡着。我几乎都听得懂，可一开口就特别慢。我在脑子里从母语逐字翻译。这样下去我永远说不流利。肯：我以前也这样。你试过影子跟读吗？最后对我管用的就是它。罗莎：影子？像跟踪别人那样？

Ken: Sort of. You play a short recording, a minute or two of a podcast, and you repeat it out loud just behind the speaker, like a shadow. Same speed, same rhythm. At first you can't keep up at all. After a week with the same clip, you start to figure out where the stress goes and which words get swallowed.

肯：差不多。你放一小段录音，播客里一两分钟就行，然后紧跟在说话人后面大声重复，像影子一样。同样的语速，同样的节奏。一开始你根本跟不上。同一段练一周，你就开始弄明白重音落在哪、哪些词被吞掉了。

Rosa: Doesn't that just turn you into a parrot? Ken: That's what I thought. But it puts whole chunks in your mouth, not single words. Last month I was in a meeting and "can I just point out" came out on its own. I didn't have to come up with it. It was just there. Rosa: That's the part I want. I always feel so self-conscious when I'm building a sentence.

罗莎：那不就把人变成鹦鹉了吗？肯：我原来也这么想。可它让你嘴里装进的是整块的词组，不是单个的词。上个月我在一个会上，「我能不能指出一下」自己就冒出来了。我不用去想它，它就在那儿。罗莎：我要的就是这个。我边造句边说的时候总是特别不自在。

Ken: Bring it up with the group today. Half of them have tried it, and someone will have a good podcast to recommend. Oh, they're starting. Rosa: Okay. I'll ask for one about cooking. At least I'll enjoy the input.

肯：今天在小组里提一下吧。他们一半人都试过，总会有人推荐一个好播客。哦，开始了。罗莎：好。我要一个讲做饭的。至少我会享受这些输入。

Ten minutes of shadowing a day improved her rhythm. 每天十分钟影子跟读改善了她的节奏。

Don't translate the menu word for word. 别逐字翻译菜单。

She became fluent after two years of daily practice. 每天练习两年后，她说得流利了。
/aʊt ˈlaʊd/ adv.

Say the sentence out loud three times. 把这句话大声说三遍。

English rhythm comes from strong and weak syllables. 英语的节奏来自强弱音节。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

I ran into my neighbour on the way and she wanted to catch up.
run into sb: meet by chance (inseparable, so ran into her, never ran her into). catch up with no object: talk about recent news.
run into sb：偶遇（不可拆，说 ran into her）。catch up 不带宾语：聊聊近况。
After a week with the same clip, you start to figure out where the stress goes and which words get swallowed.
figure out + indirect question: normal word order (where the stress goes, not where does the stress go). get swallowed is an informal passive.
figure out 后接间接疑问，用陈述语序。get swallowed 是口语化的被动。
