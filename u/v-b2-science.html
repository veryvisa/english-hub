📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Open the full explanation: 6 points展开细讲：6 段讲解

Scientific research often follows a sequence: identify a problem, form a hypothesis, conduct an experiment, collect data, analyse the results, and draw a conclusion. These expressions frequently appear together in academic writing and discussions of research.

科学研究通常遵循一定流程：确定问题、提出假设、开展实验、收集数据、分析结果并得出结论。这些表达经常一起出现在学术写作和科研讨论中。

A hypothesis is a proposed explanation that can be tested, whereas a theory is a broader explanation supported by substantial evidence. Chinese learners sometimes use these two words interchangeably, but in scientific contexts they describe different levels of explanation.

hypothesis 指可以通过实验检验的假设，而 theory 指得到大量证据支持的系统性解释。中文学习者有时会混淆这两个词，但在科学语境中，它们代表不同层次的解释。

The words evidence, data, and results are related but not identical. Data refers to collected information, results describe what an investigation produces, and evidence is information used to support or challenge a claim. Evidence is generally uncountable, so avoid saying 'an evidence'.

evidence、data 和 results 意思相关，但并不相同。data 指收集到的信息，results 指研究产生的结果，evidence 则是用来支持或质疑某个观点的信息。Evidence 通常是不可数名词，因此不要说 an evidence。

Scientific verbs have particular collocations. Researchers conduct experiments, carry out research, collect data, analyse samples, and publish findings. Using these combinations makes scientific English sound more natural than translating each Chinese verb separately.

科学英语中的动词有固定搭配。研究人员会进行实验、开展研究、收集数据、分析样本并发表研究发现。掌握这些组合，比逐字翻译中文动词更符合英语习惯。

Scientific discoveries are not always immediate or final. Researchers may make a breakthrough, identify a new pattern, or develop a new technology, but later studies can challenge earlier explanations. Expressions such as further research and remain uncertain help communicate scientific caution.

科学发现并不总是立即出现，也不一定是最终结论。研究人员可能取得突破、发现新的规律或开发新技术，但后续研究也可能挑战早期解释。使用 further research 和 remain uncertain 等表达，可以体现科学讨论应有的审慎态度。

The verb prove is stronger than suggest or indicate. A single experiment may provide evidence for an explanation without proving it conclusively. In academic English, researchers often use cautious expressions such as the findings suggest that or the results indicate that.

prove 的语气比 suggest 或 indicate 强得多。单次实验可能为某种解释提供证据，但未必能彻底证明它。在学术英语中，研究人员经常使用 the findings suggest that 或 the results indicate that 等谨慎表达。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ The scientists made an experiment about climate change.
✓ The scientists conducted an experiment on climate change.
Make is not used with experiment. The standard verbs are conduct, carry out, or do an experiment, and on is the more appropriate preposition when describing the subject of an experiment.
experiment 不能与 make 搭配。标准动词是 conduct、carry out 或 do an experiment，描述实验研究的主题时，通常使用介词 on。
✗ The researchers collected many evidences.
✓ The researchers collected a lot of evidence.
Evidence is normally an uncountable noun. Use a piece of evidence or several pieces of evidence when you need to count individual items.
evidence 通常是不可数名词，不能直接加复数词尾。需要表达具体数量时，可以说 a piece of evidence 或 several pieces of evidence。
✗ The experiment proved the theory, but more research is needed.
✓ The experiment provided evidence for the theory, but more research is needed.
Prove suggests that something has been established conclusively. A single experiment usually provides supporting evidence rather than proving an entire scientific theory.
prove 表示已经确凿地证明某件事。单次实验通常只是为某个科学理论提供支持性证据，而不是彻底证明整个理论。
✗ The scientists suggested us that the results were incorrect.
✓ The scientists suggested that the results were incorrect.
Suggest is not normally followed by an indirect object in this structure. Use suggest that plus a clause, or suggest doing something.
suggest 在这种结构中通常不能直接接人作为间接宾语。正确结构是 suggest that 加从句，或者 suggest doing something。
✗ We made a research about the new material.
✓ We conducted research on the new material.
Research is normally uncountable in this meaning, so do not use a research. Conduct research is a standard academic collocation, and on introduces the research topic.
research 表示研究活动时通常是不可数名词，因此不能说 a research。conduct research 是标准学术搭配，而 on 用来引出研究主题。
✗ The results indicate us that the treatment works.
✓ The results indicate that the treatment works.
Indicate normally takes a clause introduced by that or a noun phrase as its object. It does not need an indirect object such as us in this sentence.
indicate 通常直接接 that 引导的从句或名词短语，不需要在这里添加 us 这样的间接宾语。
