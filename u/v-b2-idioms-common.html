📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Open the full explanation: 4 points展开细讲：4 段讲解

Many learners meet idioms in lists like raining cats and dogs, which almost nobody under seventy says. The idioms worth learning are short, flexible and used daily: on the same page, a long shot, call it a day. They feel like ordinary words to native speakers, which is why they are easy to miss in listening.

很多人学习语从 raining cats and dogs 这类列表开始，可现在几乎没人这么说。值得学的习语短、灵活、天天用；母语者觉得它们就是普通说法，所以听力里很容易漏掉。

Idioms are fixed. You can change the tense and the pronoun (she's been on the fence for weeks), but not the nouns: on the wall does not mean on the fence. If you are not sure of the exact words, use a plain sentence instead.

习语是固定的：时态和代词可以变，名词不能换。记不准原词就别用，换成普通句子。

Most everyday idioms belong to spoken and informal writing: chat, email to a colleague, a talk. In a formal report, cut corners becomes reduce quality to save time. Knowing the plain meaning lets you move between the two.

日常习语多属口语和非正式书面：聊天、给同事的邮件、讲话。正式报告里 cut corners 要换成「为省时间降低质量」。知道直白意思，才能在两种语域间切换。

Idioms often soften what you say. It's not really my cup of tea is kinder than I don't like it; it's a bit of a long shot is gentler than it probably won't work. That softening is the real reason to learn them.

习语常常是为了说得软一点：not my cup of tea 比 I don't like it 客气；a long shot 比「多半不行」温和。这才是学它们的真正理由。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ It's raining cats and dogs today.
✓ It's pouring today. / It's really coming down today.
Textbook idiom; almost nobody uses it now.
课本习语，现在几乎没人说。
✗ I'm still on the wall about it.
✓ I'm still on the fence about it.
Idioms are fixed; you cannot swap the noun.
习语固定，名词不能换。
✗ The bottom line of our analysis is shown in Table 2.
✓ The main conclusion of our analysis is shown in Table 2.
the bottom line is informal; use plain words in formal writing.
bottom line 偏口语，正式写作换成直白说法。
✗ This exam was a piece of cakes.
✓ This exam was a piece of cake.
Fixed form: a piece of cake, singular.
固定为单数。
✗ I'm getting the hang on it.
✓ I'm getting the hang of it.
The preposition is of.
介词是 of。
