When I first moved to a place with four distinct seasons, I thought winter would be the hardest part of the year. I came from a warmer climate and had never experienced such long periods of cold weather. During my first autumn, I was surprised by how quickly the leaves changed colour. The streets became beautiful, and the air felt fresh.

当我第一次搬到一个四季分明的地方时，我认为冬天会是一年中最难熬的季节。我来自一个气候较温暖的地区，从未经历过如此漫长的寒冷天气。在第一个秋天，我惊讶地发现树叶变色的速度如此之快。街道变得美丽，空气也显得格外清新。

Winter arrived sooner than I expected. The days became shorter, and I had to wear a warm coat, gloves, and boots whenever I went outside. At first, I stayed indoors because I did not enjoy the cold. However, a neighbour invited me to join a weekend walking group. I discovered that fresh air and regular exercise could improve my mood.

冬天比我预想的来得更早。白天变短了，每次出门我都必须穿上暖和的外套、戴上手套并穿好靴子。起初，因为不喜欢寒冷，我一直待在室内。然而，一位邻居邀请我参加周末徒步小组。我发现，新鲜空气和规律运动能够改善我的心情。

Spring brought another surprise. Small flowers appeared in gardens, birds became more active, and the temperature slowly increased. I started spending more time outside and planted vegetables in a small garden. Summer was different again. Although some afternoons were too hot for outdoor exercise, long evenings gave me more opportunities to meet friends and enjoy local parks.

春天又带来了另一份惊喜。花园里出现了小花，鸟儿变得更加活跃，气温也逐渐升高。我开始花更多时间待在户外，并在一个小花园里种植蔬菜。夏天又有所不同。虽然有些下午太热，不适合户外锻炼，但漫长的傍晚让我有更多机会与朋友见面并享受当地公园。

I no longer think of one season as better than all the others. Each season offers something different, and each requires a little preparation. I check the forecast, dress in layers when necessary, and plan activities around the weather. Living through these changes has made me more patient and flexible. I have learned that adapting to a new climate takes time, but it can also be enjoyable.

我不再认为某一个季节比其他所有季节都好。每个季节都有不同的特点，也都需要一些准备。我会查看天气预报，在需要时分层穿衣，并根据天气安排活动。经历这些变化让我变得更有耐心，也更加灵活。我明白了，适应新的气候需要时间，但这个过程也可以充满乐趣。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

Although some afternoons were too hot for outdoor exercise, long evenings gave me more opportunities to meet friends and enjoy local parks.
Although introduces a contrast, while the adjective pattern too hot for explains that the temperature was excessive for an activity.
Although 引导让步状语从句，而 too hot for 这一形容词结构表示天气过热，不适合进行某项活动。
I have learned that adapting to a new climate takes time, but it can also be enjoyable.
The present perfect have learned connects a past experience with a lesson that remains relevant now, and adapting is a gerund functioning as the subject of the that-clause.
现在完成时 have learned 将过去的经历与现在仍然适用的认识联系起来，而 adapting 是动名词，在 that 从句中作主语。
