📏 The rule规则

🎯 The point in one line一句话要领
Native speakers say pros and cons, never cons and pros. The order is part of the word, and getting it right is one of the quickest ways to sound fluent.母语者只说 pros and cons，从不说 cons and pros。语序本身就是词的一部分，说对它是最快显得流利的办法之一。

📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Open the full explanation: 4 points展开细讲：4 段讲解

A binomial is two words joined by and or or in a fixed order: sooner or later, ups and downs, by and large. Chinese has the same kind of pairs (dongxi, maimai), and their order is fixed too. Reversing an English pair sounds as odd as reversing a Chinese one.

binomial 是用 and 或 or 连起来、语序固定的两个词。中文也有这类词（东西、买卖），顺序同样不能倒。英语倒过来说，和中文倒过来一样别扭。

Some pairs mean something neither word means alone. By and large means "in general"; odds and ends are small leftover things; touch and go means the result was uncertain. Learn these as single words.

有些成对词的意思不是两个词相加：by and large 是「总体而言」，odds and ends 是零碎东西，touch and go 是结果难料。当一个词记。

Sentence frames are the bigger version: a fixed shell with an open slot. The thing is, …, It's not that …; it's just that …, The more …, the more …, What I mean is …. They give a listener a map of your argument before the content arrives, which makes you easier to follow.

句框架是更大的固定件：一个外壳加一个空位。它们在内容到来前先给听者一张论点地图，让人更容易跟上你。

Frames are especially useful when you need a moment to think. The thing is or What I mean is buys you two seconds without the silence or the um that makes speech sound unsure.

需要想一想的时候，句框架尤其管用：说 The thing is 或 What I mean is，你多了两秒，又不会冷场或一直「嗯」。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ We discussed the cons and pros.
✓ We discussed the pros and cons.
The order of a binomial is fixed.
成对词语序固定。
✗ Later or sooner they will find out.
✓ Sooner or later they will find out.
Fixed order: sooner or later.
固定为 sooner or later。
✗ The more I practise, I get more confident.
✓ The more I practise, the more confident I get.
Both halves need the + comparative at the front.
两半都要以 the + 比较级开头。
✗ I'm sick and tired with the delays.
✓ I'm sick and tired of the delays.
The preposition is of.
介词用 of。
✗ The thing is that, I don't have time.
✓ The thing is, I don't have time. / The thing is that I don't have time.
Use either the comma or that, not both.
逗号和 that 二选一。
