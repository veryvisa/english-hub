A Letter Home After Three Years 三年后寄回家的一封信

🧠 Learn the 5 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 5 个新词

Read a letter home after three years abroad and see how the present perfect carries "up to now" while the past simple tells finished moments. 读一封出国三年后寄回家的信，看现在完成时怎样承载「到现在为止」，一般过去时怎样讲已经结束的时刻。

B2 · CELPIP 7–8⏱ 20 minG02 Present perfect and past simple confused现在完成时与一般过去时混用G08 Tense not kept consistent across a story叙事时态前后不一

📖 Key card知识卡
Belonging is built from small kindnesses and routines; missing home does not go away, it becomes part of having two homes.归属感由一件件小小的善意和日常建起来；想家不会消失，它成了拥有两个家的一部分。

Read and listen读与听

250 words
Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Dear Xiao Yu, it has been three years since I left, and I have owed you a proper letter for most of them. Messages are not the same. So here is a real one, written slowly on a Sunday morning while the snow comes down outside.

小雨：我离开已经三年了，这三年里大部分时间我都欠你一封像样的信。短消息不一样。所以这是一封真正的信，在一个周日早上慢慢写成，窗外正下着雪。

You asked last time whether I have settled in. The honest answer is: mostly. I have found a job I like, I have learned to drive on icy roads, and I have stopped converting every price in my head. The first winter was hard. I cried in a supermarket once, because I could not find the right kind of rice, and it suddenly felt like proof that I did not belong.

你上次问我有没有安顿下来。老实说：大体上算是。我找到了一份喜欢的工作，学会了在结冰的路上开车，也不再在脑子里换算每一个价格了。第一个冬天很难熬。有一次我在超市里哭了，因为找不到合适的米，那一刻它突然像是证明我不属于这里。

What changed? Small things, mostly. A neighbour brought soup when I was ill. A colleague asked me to teach her the dish I took to a party, and now she makes it better than I do. I joined a hiking group and went every Saturday for a year, even on days when I did not feel like it.

是什么变了？大多是小事。我生病时，一位邻居送来了汤。一位同事请我教她做我带去聚会的那道菜，现在她做得比我还好。我参加了一个徒步小组，一年里每个周六都去，哪怕有些日子根本不想去。

I still get homesick, usually in autumn, when I know everyone at home is eating mooncakes together. I have not found a cure for that, and I am not sure I want one. Missing home seems to be part of having two homes.

我还是会想家，通常在秋天，我知道家里人都在一起吃月饼的时候。这一点我还没找到解药，也不确定自己想要。想家，好像就是拥有两个家的一部分。

Write back and tell me everything: your new flat, your mother's health, whether the noodle shop near our old school is still open. I want to know all of it. Love, Ning

回信吧，什么都告诉我：你的新房子、你妈妈的身体、我们老学校旁边那家面馆还开不开。我全都想知道。爱你的，宁

I stopped converting every price into yuan. 我不再把每个价格都换算成人民币了。
/pruːf/ n.

proof that...; living proof
One bad day is not proof that you made a mistake. 一个糟糕的日子并不能证明你错了。
/fiːl laɪk/ v. phr.

feel like doing sth; not feel like it
I did not feel like cooking, so we ate out. 我不想做饭，所以我们出去吃了。
/kjʊə/ n.

a cure for sth; no cure
There is no cure for missing home. 想家没有解药。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

You asked last time whether I have settled in.
Past simple asked for the moment of the question; present perfect have settled because the question is about up to now.
asked 用一般过去时，指提问的那一刻；have settled 用现在完成时，因为问的是到现在为止。
I have not found a cure for that, and I am not sure I want one.
Present perfect negative: not yet, up to now. one = a cure.
现在完成时否定：到现在还没有。one 指 a cure。

Write a letter of about 150 words to a friend in your home town. Use the present perfect three times for what has changed up to now, the past simple twice for single moments, and end with three questions about their life.
给家乡的朋友写一封约 150 词的信。用三次现在完成时讲到现在为止的变化，两次一般过去时讲具体的时刻，最后问三个关于对方生活的问题。
