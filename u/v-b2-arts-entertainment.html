📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Open the full explanation: 5 points展开细讲：5 段讲解

English uses different verbs for experiencing different kinds of art. We usually read a book, watch a film, listen to music and look at a painting. Chinese learners sometimes use watch for all visual media or listen to when discussing a live performance, so learning these verb-noun combinations together helps make descriptions more natural.

英语会根据艺术形式使用不同的动词。通常说 read a book（读书）、watch a film（看电影）、listen to music（听音乐）和 look at a painting（看画）。中文学习者有时会用 watch 泛指所有视觉媒体，或在讨论现场演出时误用 listen to，因此一起掌握这些动宾搭配有助于表达更加自然。

When evaluating creative works, distinguish between entertaining, engaging and thought-provoking. Entertaining means enjoyable, engaging suggests that something holds your attention, and thought-provoking means that it makes you think deeply. A film can be entertaining without being particularly meaningful, while a serious documentary may be thought-provoking rather than entertaining.

评价文艺作品时，要区分 entertaining、engaging 和 thought-provoking。Entertaining 表示有娱乐性、令人愉快；engaging 表示引人入胜、能够吸引注意力；thought-provoking 表示发人深省。电影可以很好看，却不一定有深刻内涵；严肃的纪录片则可能更发人深省，而不是以娱乐性见长。

The words audience, viewers, listeners and readers refer to people who experience different forms of creative work. Audience is especially common for live performances, although it can also refer to the people watching a film or programme. Viewers are associated with visual media, listeners with audio, and readers with written material.

Audience、viewers、listeners 和 readers 分别指接触不同艺术或媒体形式的人。Audience 尤其常用于现场演出的观众，也可以指电影或节目的观众。Viewers 通常指观看视觉媒体的人，listeners 指听众，readers 则指读者。

Several useful expressions describe how a work is created or presented. A performance can receive critical acclaim, a film can feature a talented cast, and an artist can exhibit their work in a gallery. Notice that acclaim is normally used in expressions such as receive critical acclaim rather than simply receive many good comments.

一些常用表达可以描述作品的创作和呈现方式。例如，演出可以获得评论界的高度赞誉（receive critical acclaim），电影可以由优秀演员阵容出演（feature a talented cast），艺术家可以在画廊展出作品（exhibit their work）。注意，acclaim 常用于 receive critical acclaim 这样的固定搭配，而不是简单地表示收到很多好评。

The word performance is broader than concert or play. It can refer to acting, dancing, singing or playing an instrument in front of an audience. Similarly, exhibition usually refers to a public display of artworks or other objects, while an exhibit is one individual item shown at an exhibition.

Performance 的含义比 concert（音乐会）或 play（戏剧）更广，可以指在观众面前进行表演、舞蹈、演唱或乐器演奏。同样，exhibition 通常指艺术品或其他物品的公开展览，而 exhibit 可以指展览中展示的一件具体物品。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ I watched a book about modern art yesterday.
✓ I read a book about modern art yesterday.
Use read for books and written material. Watch is used for films, television and other moving visual content.
读书使用 read，而不是 watch。Watch 通常用于电影、电视和动态影像。
✗ The movie has a very interesting history.
✓ The movie has a very interesting plot.
History refers to real past events or the development of something. Plot describes the sequence of events in a fictional story or film.
History 指真实的历史或事物的发展历程；plot 指小说或电影中的情节。这里想表达电影情节有趣，应使用 plot。
✗ The film received many critical acclaims.
✓ The film received critical acclaim.
Acclaim is normally an uncountable noun in this expression. The natural collocation is receive critical acclaim, without many or a plural ending.
在 receive critical acclaim 这一表达中，acclaim 通常是不可数名词，不能说 many critical acclaims。
✗ I am very interesting in contemporary art.
✓ I am very interested in contemporary art.
Interested describes how a person feels, while interesting describes something that creates interest. Use be interested in when expressing your own enthusiasm.
Interested 描述人的感受，interesting 描述事物令人感兴趣的特点。表达自己对当代艺术感兴趣，应使用 be interested in。
✗ The film is adopted from the novel of the same name.
✓ The film is adapted from the novel of the same name.
Adopted means taken in or formally accepted, such as adopting a child or a policy. Adapted means changed to suit a new form, which is the correct word for a film based on a novel.
adopted 表示收养或正式采纳，例如领养孩子或采纳某项政策；adapted 表示为适应新形式而改编，才是“电影改编自小说”应使用的词。
✗ We went to see an art performance in the gallery.
✓ We went to see an art exhibition at the gallery.
An exhibition is a public display of artworks. A performance is an event in which people act, dance, sing or play music. Use at a gallery when referring to the venue.
Exhibition 指艺术品展览，performance 指现场表演。描述在美术馆看展览时，应使用 an art exhibition at the gallery。
