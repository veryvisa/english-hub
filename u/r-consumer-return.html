Not a Return, a Defect 不是退货，是质量问题

🧠 Learn the 5 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 5 个新词

Follow a customer returning faulty headphones after the return window and learn the collocations that turn a complaint into a request. 跟着一位顾客在退货期过后去退坏耳机，学会把抱怨变成请求的那几个搭配。

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Wei had bought a pair of wireless headphones three weeks earlier. Last Friday the left side stopped working. He found the receipt, put everything back in the box and went to the customer service counter.

阿伟三周前买了一副无线耳机。上周五左边那只不响了。他找出收据，把东西全放回盒子里，去了客服柜台。

"Hi, I bought these here about three weeks ago, and the left earphone has stopped working. I have the receipt." "Okay, let me see. Our return window is fourteen days, so I can't give you a refund, I'm afraid." "I understand. But I haven't changed my mind about them; they're faulty. Isn't that covered by the warranty?" "The warranty is with the manufacturer. You'd have to send them away."

「您好，我大概三周前在这里买的，左边耳机不响了。我有收据。」「好，我看看。我们的退货期是十四天，恐怕没法给您退款。」「我明白。可我不是改主意了，是东西有毛病。保修不管吗？」「保修是厂家负责的，您得寄给他们。」

"That could take weeks. I'm not trying to make a complaint about you, and you've been very helpful, but I'd like the store to take responsibility for something it sold that broke in three weeks. Could I have a word with the manager?" "Sure, one moment."

「那可能要好几周。我不是在投诉您，您已经帮了很多忙了，但这东西是店里卖的，三周就坏了，我希望店里能负起责任。我能跟经理说两句吗？」「当然，请稍等。」

The manager came over, listened and tested the headphones herself. "You're right, that's a defect. I can't refund them outside the window, but I can give you a replacement today, or store credit." Wei took the replacement pair.

经理过来了，听完之后自己试了试耳机。「您说得对，这是质量问题。过了退货期我没法退款，但今天可以给您换一副新的，或者给您店内积分。」阿伟要了一副新的。

Walking out, he thought about what had worked. He had brought the receipt, stayed calm, separated the person at the counter from the policy and asked for something specific. He had also prepared one sentence that took the store's rules into account: "I know the return window is fourteen days, but this isn't a return; it's a defect."

走出店门，他想了想是什么起了作用。他带了收据，保持冷静，把柜台后的人和规定分开，还提了一个具体的要求。他还事先准备了一句把店里规矩考虑在内的话：「我知道退货期是十四天，但这不是退货，是质量问题。」

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

I'm not trying to make a complaint about you, and you've been very helpful, but I'd like the store to take responsibility for something it sold that broke in three weeks.
Two collocations: make a complaint about, take responsibility for. The middle clause praises the person before the request, so the request is aimed at the store, not at her.
两个搭配：make a complaint about、take responsibility for。中间那句先肯定对方，请求就落在店上，不落在她身上。
I know the return window is fourteen days, but this isn't a return; it's a defect.
I know … but accepts the rule first, then shows why this case falls outside it. The semicolon contrasts return and defect.
I know … but 先承认规定，再说明这件事不在规定范围内。分号对比 return 和 defect。
