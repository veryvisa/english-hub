An Email About Sharing the Drive 一封提议拼车的邮件

🧠 Learn the 5 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 5 个新词

Read an email that proposes a carpool to colleagues and learn the make/take/give collocations that keep a plan clear and polite. 读一封向同事提议拼车的邮件，学会让计划既清楚又客气的 make / take / give 搭配。

B2 · CELPIP 7–8⏱ 20 minV06 Register: too formal, too casual, or too blunt语域不合：太正式、太随意或太冲V01 Wrong collocation built word by word逐词直译的错搭配

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Subject: Sharing the drive to the new office. Hi all, since the office moved to the business park last month, at least six of us have been driving forty minutes each way from the east side, mostly alone. I would like to suggest a carpool, and I wanted to share the idea before anyone makes a decision about buying a parking pass.

主题：一起开车去新办公室。大家好：自从上个月公司搬到商务园区，我们至少有六个人每天从东区单程开四十分钟，大多是一人一车。我想提议拼车，也想在大家决定买停车证之前先把这个想法说出来。

Here is the idea: two cars, three people each, leaving from the community centre parking lot at 7:30. We would take turns driving, one week each, so nobody does more than their share. The driver for the week takes responsibility for being on time; passengers make an effort to be at the meeting point five minutes early. If someone is sick or working late, they let the group know by text before seven.

想法是这样的：两辆车，每车三人，七点半从社区中心停车场出发。我们轮流开车，每人一周，这样谁也不会多出力。当周的司机负责准时，乘客尽量提前五分钟到集合点。谁生病或要加班，七点前在群里发条短信说一声。

A few practical things. First, please check with your own insurer whether regular carpooling changes anything for you; I have checked mine, but policies differ, so I would rather not guess. Second, we should take the weather into account: on mornings with black ice or heavy snow, the driver can cancel and we all work at home. Third, nobody should feel stuck. If it does not work for you, you can drop out any week, no explanation needed.

几件实际的事。第一，请各自问一下自己的保险公司，经常拼车对你有没有影响；我问过我的，但各家保单不同，我不想乱猜。第二，我们得把天气考虑进去：碰上有黑冰或大雪的早晨，司机可以取消，大家在家上班。第三，谁都不该觉得被绑住。不合适的话，哪一周都可以退出，不用解释。

I know a carpool is not for everyone. Some people use the drive to think or to call family, and that is fine. But if we can turn six cars into two, we save money, rush-hour stress and a fair amount of fuel.

我知道拼车不适合每个人。有人喜欢在开车时想事情或给家里打电话，这完全没问题。但如果能把六辆车变成两辆，我们就能省钱，少受高峰期的罪，也省下不少油。

If you are interested, reply by Thursday, and please give honest feedback on the times. I would rather change the plan now than start something people quietly hate. Thanks, Priya

有兴趣的话请在周四前回复，也请对时间安排说说真实想法。我宁愿现在就改计划，也不想开始一件大家心里都讨厌的事。谢谢，普丽亚

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

I would like to suggest a carpool, and I wanted to share the idea before anyone makes a decision about buying a parking pass.
make a decision about + -ing. before anyone makes: present simple after before, even about the future. I wanted to is a polite past, softer than I want to.
make a decision about 后接 -ing。before 之后用一般现在时表示将来。I wanted to 是委婉的过去式，比 I want to 软。
The driver for the week takes responsibility for being on time; passengers make an effort to be at the meeting point five minutes early.
Two collocations set side by side: take responsibility for + -ing, make an effort to + verb. The semicolon balances the duties of each side.
两个搭配并列：take responsibility for 加 -ing，make an effort to 加动词原形。分号把双方的责任摆平。
