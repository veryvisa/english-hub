Noise Complaints Rise as City Reviews Its Bylaw 噪音投诉增多，市里修订规定

🧠 Learn the 5 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 5 个新词

Read a city news report on noise rules, and learn word-formation pairs (misunderstand / misunderstanding, flexible / flexibility, aware / awareness) that news uses to explain a policy. 读一篇关于城市噪音规定的新闻，学会新闻解释政策时常用的构词对（misunderstand／misunderstanding、flexible／flexibility、aware／awareness）。

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

The city has received more noise complaints this year than at any time in the past decade, and councillors are now reviewing the bylaw that sets quiet hours. Most complaints involve late parties, early construction and, more and more often, dogs left alone all day.

今年市里收到的噪音投诉是近十年来最多的，市议员们正在修订规定安静时段的那条地方法规。投诉大多涉及深夜聚会、清早施工，以及越来越多的整天被独自留在家的狗。

A bylaw is a local rule made by a city or town rather than by a higher level of government. Breaking one is not usually a crime in the everyday sense, but it can lead to a warning or a fine. In many cities, officers respond to complaints rather than patrol the streets, so a rule can be broken for months until a neighbour picks up the phone.

地方法规是由市或镇、而不是更高一级政府制定的本地规则。违反它在日常意义上通常不算犯罪，但可能被警告或罚款。在很多城市，执法人员是接到投诉才出动，而不是上街巡查，所以一条规定可能被违反好几个月，直到某个邻居拿起电话。

That system has created its own problems. One officer said many calls come from a misunderstanding: new residents assume that noise is allowed until a certain hour everywhere, or that a single loud evening is enough for a fine. "People's expectations are often unrealistic in both directions," she said. "Some expect us to arrive in five minutes; others think anything goes on a Saturday."

这套做法也带来了自己的问题。一位执法人员说，很多来电源于误解：新居民以为到处都是到某个钟点前可以吵，或者以为吵一个晚上就够罚款了。「人们的预期往往两头都不现实，」她说，「有人指望我们五分钟就到，有人觉得周六干什么都行。」

Councillors are considering more flexibility for weekend events and a stricter line on construction that starts before dawn. Residents who disagree with a ticket can usually dispute it by asking for a review within a set number of days, and that number is printed on the ticket itself.

市议员们正在考虑对周末活动更灵活一些，对天亮前就开工的施工更严一些。不同意罚单的居民，通常可以在规定天数内申请复核来提出异议，天数就印在罚单上。

The city also plans an awareness campaign this spring. Its message is simple: before you call, try talking to your neighbour, and save the complaint for noise that is unreasonable or keeps happening. Most noise, the officer said, stops when someone politely asks, and a complaint that turns a neighbour into an enemy is a high price for one quiet night.

市里还计划今年春天开展一次宣传活动，信息很简单：打电话之前，先试着和邻居谈谈，投诉留给过分的、或者反复发生的噪音。那位执法人员说，大多数噪音只要有人客气地提一句就停了，为了一个安静的夜晚把邻居变成仇人，代价太高。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

In many cities, officers respond to complaints rather than patrol the streets, so a rule can be broken for months until a neighbour picks up the phone.
rather than joins two verbs in the same form (respond … rather than patrol). can be broken is a modal passive.
rather than 连接两个同形的动词（respond … rather than patrol）。can be broken 是情态动词被动式。
People's expectations are often unrealistic in both directions
expectation (noun of expect); unrealistic is un- + realistic. in both directions: too high for some, too low for others.
expectation 是 expect 的名词；unrealistic 是 un- 加 realistic。in both directions：有人期望过高，有人过低。
