Checking a Claim in Sixty Seconds 六十秒核实一条说法

🧠 Learn the 3 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 3 个新词

Read a short explainer on how professionals check a claim, and collect the make/do/take/have collocations it runs on. 读一篇讲专业人士怎样核实说法的科普短文，收集文中 make、do、take、have 的搭配。

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Professional fact-checkers do not read a suspicious post more carefully than the rest of us. They read it less. In one study, researchers asked experienced fact-checkers and university students to judge unfamiliar websites. The students stayed on the page, studying the design and the "About us" section. The fact-checkers left almost at once and opened new tabs to find out what other sources said about the site. They reached better answers in a fraction of the time.

专业的事实核查员读一条可疑帖子，并不比我们读得更仔细，反而读得更少。在一项研究里，研究人员请有经验的核查员和大学生判断一些陌生网站。学生们留在页面上，研究版面设计和「关于我们」那一栏。核查员几乎马上就离开，打开新的标签页，去看别的来源怎么评价这个网站。他们只用了一小部分时间，就得出了更准确的判断。

You do not need to do research for an hour before you share something. Three quick moves are usually enough. First, stop. A post full of outrage is designed to stop you thinking, so take a short break before you react. Second, look for the source: who first said this, and are they in a position to know? Third, search for the claim itself and see whether credible news organisations or official bodies are reporting it.

转发之前，你不必花一个小时做研究，通常三个快动作就够了。第一，停下来。一条满是愤怒的帖子，本来就是设计来让你停止思考的，所以反应之前先稍停一下。第二，找出处：最早是谁说的？他们有没有条件知道？第三，直接搜索这条说法本身，看看可信的新闻机构或官方机构有没有在报道。

Images need one extra step. Many photos that go viral are real but out of context: a flood from another year, a crowd from another country, an old picture with a new caption. Even video is no longer proof, now that convincing deepfakes are easy to make. A reverse image search, which most phones can now do, often shows where and when a picture first appeared online.

图片还要多一步。很多刷屏的照片本身是真的，只是被断章取义：别的年份的洪水、别的国家的人群、配了新说明的旧照片。如今以假乱真的深度伪造视频很容易做，连视频也不再能当证据。用以图搜图（现在多数手机都能做），常常能查到一张图最早是在什么时候、什么地方出现在网上的。

None of this makes you an expert, and it will not catch everything. Take your own reaction into account as well: most of us check the claims we dislike far more carefully than the ones we agree with. If a screenshot fits what you already believe a little too neatly, that is exactly the one to check.

这些都不会让你变成专家，也不能把所有问题都抓出来。还要把你自己的反应也考虑进去：我们大多数人核查不喜欢的说法，要比核查赞同的说法仔细得多。如果一张截图和你原本的想法吻合得有点过于严丝合缝，那恰恰是最该核实的那一张。

The habit has an impact beyond your own phone. Every time you make a decision not to forward something unchecked, one chain of sharing stops with you.

这个习惯的影响不止于你自己的手机。每一次你决定不转发没核实过的东西，就有一条转发链在你这里断掉了。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

The fact-checkers left almost at once and opened new tabs to find out what other sources said about the site.
at once = immediately. what other sources said is an indirect question, so it keeps normal word order: subject, then verb.
at once＝立刻。what other sources said 是间接疑问，用陈述语序：主语在前，动词在后。
If a screenshot fits what you already believe a little too neatly, that is exactly the one to check.
a little too neatly softens the warning. the one to check = the one you should check; that points back to the whole situation.
a little too neatly 让提醒听起来不那么重。the one to check＝该核实的那一张；that 指代前面整个情形。
