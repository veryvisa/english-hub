What One Flight Leaves Behind 一趟航班留下了什么

🧠 Learn the 2 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 2 个新词

Read a plain explainer on where a trip's carbon really comes from, and learn the noun and adjective forms that let you hedge a claim instead of overstating it. 读一篇讲旅行碳排放到底从哪来的说明文，学会用名词和形容词形式给结论留余地，而不是把话说满。

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Ask people what makes a holiday "green", and most talk about hotel towels and plastic straws. Those things matter, but for a typical long-haul trip they are small. The largest part of a traveller's carbon footprint usually comes from getting there and getting back.

问人们什么样的假期算「环保」，多数人会说酒店毛巾和塑料吸管。这些当然有关系，但对一趟典型的长途旅行来说，它们只是小头。旅行者碳足迹里最大的一块，通常来自去程和回程。

Flying is unusual because its emissions are concentrated in a few hours. A single return flight across an ocean can produce more carbon than many people create by driving for months. Short flights tend to be worse per kilometre, because take-off burns a lot of fuel, while long ones are worse in total. Estimates vary widely, so it is wise to treat any precise figure with some care.

飞行的特别之处在于，排放集中在短短几个小时里。一趟跨洋往返航班产生的碳，可能比很多人开几个月车还多。短途航班按每公里算往往更糟，因为起飞很耗油；长途航班则是总量更大。各方估算差别很大，所以对任何精确数字都最好谨慎看待。

Public awareness has grown, and so has the market for carbon offsets: you pay a little extra, and someone plants trees or funds a clean energy project. Researchers are divided on how reliable these schemes are. Some projects are well run; others would have happened anyway, or the trees are cut down a few years later. An offset may be better than nothing, but it is not the same as not producing the emissions at all.

公众的意识提高了，碳补偿的市场也跟着长起来：你多付一点钱，就有人去种树或资助清洁能源项目。研究者对这些项目有多可靠看法不一。有的项目运作得很好；有的本来就会做，或者树过几年就被砍了。买碳补偿也许聊胜于无，但和根本不排放不是一回事。

The bigger improvement usually comes from changing the trip itself. Taking the train for shorter distances, flying less often but staying longer, and choosing a direct route instead of two connections can all shrink the footprint. Flexibility helps too: if your dates can move, you can sometimes find a slower, cleaner route at a competitive price.

更大的改善通常来自改变旅行本身。短途坐火车、少飞几次但每次待久一点、选直飞而不是转两次机，都能减少碳足迹。灵活也有帮助：日期能挪的话，有时能以有竞争力的价格找到一条慢一点、干净一点的路线。

None of this means people should stop travelling. It means adjusting our expectations: one long, unhurried trip may give more than three short ones, and leave less behind. Before you book, it is worth asking what the itinerary is for, not only where it goes.

这些都不是说人们该停止旅行，而是要调整预期：一趟从容的长假，可能比三趟短途收获更多，留下的也更少。订票之前，值得问问这个行程是为了什么，而不只是去哪里。

/ˌlɒŋ ˈhɔːl/ adj.
长途的（多指远程航班）
a long-haul flight; long-haul destinations
Long-haul flights make up a small share of trips but a large share of emissions. 长途航班在出行次数里占比小，在排放里占比大。
/ɪˈmɪʃnz/ n. (pl.)
排放（物）
carbon emissions; cut emissions; produce emissions
Trains usually produce far fewer emissions per passenger than planes. 按每位乘客算，火车的排放通常比飞机少得多。
/ˈkɑːbən ˈɒfset/ n.
碳补偿（花钱抵消自己的排放）
buy a carbon offset; an offset scheme
The airline offered a carbon offset for a small fee at checkout. 航空公司在结账时提供付少量费用的碳补偿选项。
/aɪˈtɪnərəri/ n.
行程（安排）
a flexible itinerary; a detailed itinerary
A flexible itinerary makes it easier to choose the train. 行程有弹性，坐火车就容易安排了。
/ˈkɒnsntreɪtɪd/ adj.
集中的
concentrated in a few areas; highly concentrated
Most of the damage is concentrated in a few busy weeks. 大部分破坏集中在几个繁忙的星期里。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

Researchers are divided on how reliable these schemes are.
divided on + an embedded question: how reliable these schemes are keeps statement order (subject before are). reliable is the adjective; the noun is reliability.
divided on 后接嵌入疑问句：how reliable these schemes are 用陈述语序（主语在 are 前）。reliable 是形容词，名词是 reliability。
Short flights tend to be worse per kilometre, because take-off burns a lot of fuel, while long ones are worse in total.
tend to softens the claim (hedging). while here means whereas, contrasting two measures: per kilometre vs in total.
tend to 让结论变软（留余地）。这里的 while 等于 whereas，对比两种衡量：按公里与按总量。
