An Email to the Teacher About Class Photos 就班级照片给老师的一封邮件

🧠 Learn the 3 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 3 个新词

Read a parent's polite email to a teacher about class photos online, and practise work phrasal verbs and article choices in a formal request. 读一位家长就网上班级照片写给老师的礼貌邮件，在正式请求里练职场短语动词和冠词的选择。

B2 · CELPIP 7–8⏱ 20 minV06 Register: too formal, too casual, or too blunt语域不合：太正式、太随意或太冲V04 Phrasal verbs avoided; the formal verb sounds stiff回避短语动词，说话太书面

📖 Key card知识卡
A firm request can still be warm: say what you appreciate, state the facts, ask for one clear action.坚定的请求也可以温和：先说你感谢什么，再摆事实，最后提一个清楚的要求。

Read and listen读与听

295 words
Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Subject: Class photos on the school's page. Dear Ms Grant, I hope you are well. My son, Leo, is in your Grade 3 class. I am writing about the photos from last Friday's field trip, which appeared on the school's public social media page on Monday. Leo is clearly visible in two of them, and his first name is in one caption.

主题：学校主页上的班级照片。格兰特老师您好：希望您一切都好。我儿子利奥在您的三年级班上。我写信是为了上周五校外参观的照片，这些照片周一发在了学校的公开社交媒体主页上。利奥在其中两张里看得很清楚，有一张的配文里还有他的名字。

I want to say first that I understand why the school shares these pictures. Most parents enjoy them, and I can see the effort that goes into them. My concern is narrower. When Leo started at the school in September, I filled in the consent form, which asks for parents' permission, and ticked the box that says photos may be used inside the school only. I may have misunderstood how the form works, so I would be grateful if you could check.

首先我想说，我理解学校为什么分享这些照片。大多数家长都喜欢，我也看得出这背后花的心思。我担心的是一个更具体的问题。利奥九月入学时，我填了那份征求家长许可的同意书，勾的是「照片仅限校内使用」。也可能是我误解了这张表的用法，所以想麻烦您核实一下。

Would it be possible to take down the two photos, or to crop Leo out? I would also like to understand how the school deals with consent for online posts. If a parent has said no, how do the staff who post the photos know? I am not raising this to cause trouble, and I am happy to bring it up at the next parents' meeting if that would be more useful.

能否把这两张照片撤下，或者把利奥裁掉？我也想了解学校在网上发帖时怎么处理同意的问题：如果有家长不同意，负责发照片的老师怎么知道？我提这件事不是要找麻烦；如果在下一次家长会上提出来更合适，我也很乐意。

I know you are very busy, so there is no need to reply at length. A short note to confirm that the photos have been sorted out would be enough. I will follow up with the office if I have not heard back by the end of next week.

我知道您很忙，不必长篇回复，简短告知照片已处理好就够了。如果下周末前还没收到回音，我会再联系学校办公室。

Thank you for everything you do for the class. Leo has loved the trips so far, and he talks about the last one every day at home. Kind regards, Lin Zhao

感谢您为全班做的一切。到目前为止，利奥每次出游都很喜欢，上一次的事他在家天天都在讲。此致，赵琳

Words词

/kənˈsent/ n.
同意；许可
give consent; a consent form; without consent
Schools need written consent before they publish a child's photo. 学校发布孩子的照片前需要书面同意。
/tɪk/ v.
打钩；勾选
tick a box; tick the right answer
Tick the box if you agree. 同意请在方框里打钩。
/ˈnærəʊ/ adj.
窄的；范围小的
a narrow question; in a narrow sense
My question is narrower than it sounds. 我的问题比听上去要具体。
/ət leŋθ/ adv.
详细地；长篇地
reply at length; talk at length about sth
He explained the rules at length. 他把规定讲得很详细。
/krɒp aʊt/ phr. v.
（从照片里）裁掉
crop out a face; crop sb out of a photo
She cropped her ex out of every picture. 她把前任从每张照片里都裁掉了。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

When Leo started at the school in September, I filled in the consent form, which asks for parents' permission, and ticked the box that says photos may be used inside the school only.
the school, the consent form, the box: each is one specific thing the teacher already knows, so the is used every time.
the school、the consent form、the box 都是老师已经知道的特定事物，所以都用 the。
I am not raising this to cause trouble, and I am happy to bring it up at the next parents' meeting if that would be more useful.
raise and bring up both mean "mention a topic"; using both avoids repeating one verb. the next + noun takes the.
raise 和 bring up 都是「提出话题」，换着用避免重复；the next 加名词要用 the。
