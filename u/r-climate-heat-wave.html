Why Heat Waves Hit Older People Hardest 热浪为什么最伤老人

In a long heat wave, the people most at risk are rarely the ones you see on the news fainting at an outdoor concert. They are usually older people alone at home, in upper-floor apartments without air conditioning or good ventilation, where the temperature indoors keeps climbing for days after it has started to fall outside.

在漫长的热浪里，最危险的人很少是新闻里在露天音乐会上晕倒的那些。他们通常是独自在家的老人，住在没有空调、通风也不好的高层公寓里；外面已经开始降温了，屋里的温度还要往上爬好几天。

Age changes the body in ways that make heat more dangerous. Older people sweat less, so the body cools itself less effectively. Many feel thirst later, so they drink less than they need. Some common medicines for blood pressure and the heart can also affect how the body handles heat. None of this is visible from the outside, and many people do not feel unwell until they are seriously ill.

年龄会让身体发生一些变化，使高温变得更危险。老人出汗少，身体散热就差；很多人口渴的感觉来得晚，水喝得比需要的少；一些常见的降压药和心脏病药，也会影响身体应对高温的能力。这些从外面都看不出来，很多人直到病得很重才觉得不舒服。

The most dangerous part is often the night. If the air does not cool down after sunset, the body never gets a chance to recover. The first hot day is uncomfortable; the third and fourth nights in a row, during a long hot spell, are when emergency rooms fill up.

最危险的往往是夜里。如果日落后空气不降温，身体就一直没有机会恢复。第一个热天只是难受；一段长时间高温里连续的第三、第四个夜晚，才是急诊室爆满的时候。

Cities have learned from the worst summers. Many now open cooling centres in libraries and community centres, extend the hours of public pools and send volunteers to check on people who live alone. Some keep a list of residents who have asked for a call during extreme weather. The most effective measure, however, is the same in every city: someone who knows you and notices when something is wrong.

各个城市从最糟的那几个夏天里吸取了教训。很多城市现在会在图书馆和社区中心开设避暑中心，延长公共泳池的开放时间，派义工去探望独居的人。有些城市还留着一份名单，记下那些希望在极端天气时接到电话的居民。不过最有效的办法在哪座城市都一样：有个认识你的人，能察觉到你哪里不对劲。

So if an older relative or neighbour lives alone, a short visit or call each day of a heat wave matters more than any advice on the news. Ask whether their home is cooler than the street, and whether they have somewhere to go. It may be the only check they get that day.

所以，如果有长辈或邻居独居，热浪期间每天去看一眼或打个电话，比新闻里的任何建议都重要。问问他们屋里是不是比街上凉快，有没有地方可去。这也许是他们那天得到的唯一一次问候。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

They are usually older people alone at home, in upper-floor apartments without air conditioning or good ventilation
at home takes no article. air conditioning and ventilation are uncountable, so there is no an.
at home 不加冠词。air conditioning 和 ventilation 是不可数名词，不用 an。
The most effective measure, however, is the same in every city
the same always takes the. however sits in the middle, between commas, to mark a contrast without starting the sentence with it.
the same 一定带 the。however 放在句中、用逗号隔开，表转折而不放句首。
