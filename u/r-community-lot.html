Residents Divided over Empty Lot 一块空地，两种声音

🧠 Learn the 5 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 5 个新词

Read a local news report and notice how the present perfect links a long story to tonight's meeting. 读一篇社区新闻，留意现在完成时怎样把一段长故事接到今晚的会议上。

B2 · CELPIP 7–8⏱ 20 minG02 Present perfect and past simple confused现在完成时与一般过去时混用V12 Hedging is missing; claims sound absolute不会留余地，话说得太满

📖 Key card知识卡
A neighbourhood argument is rarely between good and bad; it is usually between two things people both need.街坊之间的争论很少是好与坏之争，通常是两样大家都需要的东西之争。

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

More than two hundred residents filled the community centre on Tuesday night to discuss what should happen to the empty lot at the end of Maple Street. The lot has been fenced off since a gas station closed there eight years ago, and the city has now proposed two options: a community garden with a small playground, or forty paid parking spaces.

周二晚上，两百多名居民挤满了社区中心，讨论枫树街尽头那块空地该怎么处理。八年前那里的加油站关门后，这块地就一直围着栏杆。现在市里提出了两个方案：建一个带小游乐场的社区花园，或者建四十个收费停车位。

Most speakers supported the garden. "My kids have grown up looking at a fence," said one father who has lived in the neighbourhood since 2015. Several older residents said that a garden would give people who usually keep to themselves a reason to get to know each other, and help newcomers feel they belong.

大多数发言者支持建花园。「我的孩子是看着一道栏杆长大的，」一位 2015 年起就住在这一带的父亲说。几位年长的居民说，花园能让平时不怎么来往的人有个互相认识的理由，也能让新来的人觉得自己属于这里。

Not everyone agreed. A few people raised minor worries about noise, and two shop owners argued that customers have complained about parking for years, and that some have already stopped coming. "We are not against gardens," one of them said. "We are against losing the people who keep this street alive." A petition for the parking option has collected over three hundred signatures, according to its organisers.

并不是所有人都赞成。有几个人提了些关于噪音的小顾虑，两位店主则说，顾客抱怨停车难已经好几年了，有些人已经不来了。「我们不反对花园，」其中一位说，「我们反对的是失去让这条街活下去的人。」据组织者说，支持停车场方案的请愿书已经征集到三百多个签名。

City staff said they had not made a decision yet, would take both views into account and hoped to sort out the details before the vote. They also pointed out that a garden would need volunteers to look after it, and asked anyone who wanted to get involved to sign up at the door. By the end of the evening, sixty people had signed up, including one of the shop owners.

市里的工作人员说，他们还没有做决定，会把两方的意见都考虑进去，希望在投票前把细节理清楚。他们还指出，花园需要义工来照料，请想参与的人在门口登记。到会议结束时，已经有六十人报了名，其中包括一位店主。

The council is expected to vote in February. Until then, the fence stays where it has stood for eight years.

市议会预计在二月投票。在那之前，那道栏杆还会立在原地，就像过去八年一样。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

The lot has been fenced off since a gas station closed there eight years ago, and the city has now proposed two options
has been fenced off since + past simple: a state that started at a past moment and is still true. closed … ago is a finished past event, so it stays in the past simple.
has been fenced off since 加一般过去时：从过去某刻开始、现在仍然如此的状态。closed … ago 是已结束的过去事件，所以用一般过去时。
By the end of the evening, sixty people had signed up, including one of the shop owners.
By + a past time takes the past perfect: the signing up was complete before that point.
by 加过去的时间点用过去完成时：报名在那个时间点之前已经完成。
