📏 The rule规则

🎯 The point in one line一句话要领
Spoken markers carry no content, only direction: they tell the listener "I'm correcting", "I'm going back", "I'm softening". Without them, fluent grammar still sounds like reading aloud.口语话语标记不带内容，只指方向：告诉对方「我在纠正」「我要拉回来」「我在缓一下」。没有它们，语法再流利也像在念稿。
  • Easy to miss易漏 ✗ Actually, I live in this city. (meant as "the fact is") → ✓ I live in this city, in fact. / As it happens, I live in this city.

📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Open the full explanation: 5 points展开细讲：5 段讲解

Chinese speakers often move between ideas with nothing, or with and then and so for everything. English speakers use a different small word for each move. Anyway closes a side story and goes back to the main point. By the way opens a side topic. Speaking of... jumps to a related topic.

华人说话转换话题时常常什么都不用，或者处处用 and then、so。英语母语者每种转向用一个不同的小词：anyway 结束岔开的话、回到正题；by the way 开个旁支；speaking of… 跳到相关话题。

Actually is a false friend. It does not mean "in reality, as a matter of interest" as often as learners think; in speech it usually signals a correction or a surprise: Actually, it's on Thursday, not Friday. It is also a soft way to disagree: Actually, I think it's cheaper online.

actually 是个假朋友。口语里它多半不是「其实、实际上」那种陈述义，而是表示纠正或意外：Actually, it's on Thursday, not Friday。它也是委婉表示不同意的方式：Actually, I think it's cheaper online。

I mean repairs what you just said: It was fine. I mean, the food was good, but it was too loud. The thing is introduces the real problem: I'd like to come. The thing is, I don't have a car. To be honest prepares the listener for something they may not like.

I mean 用来修正刚说的话：It was fine. I mean, the food was good, but it was too loud。The thing is 引出真正的问题：I'd like to come. The thing is, I don't have a car。To be honest 提前打招呼，接下来的话对方可能不爱听。

Listener markers matter as much as speaker markers. I see, Right, Fair enough and Let me think show you are following, accepting a point, or buying time. In the speaking test and in real meetings, a silent pause while you plan sounds like you are lost; Let me think sounds like you are thinking.

听话人的标记和说话人的一样重要。I see、Right、Fair enough、Let me think 表示你在跟、接受了对方的点、或在争取时间。思培口语和真实会议里，一边想一边沉默听着像卡住了；说一句 Let me think 听着像在思考。

Use them, but not as fillers. You know and sort of every five words make you sound vague. One marker at each turn in the conversation is about right, and it should match the move you are making.

要用，但别当口头禅。每五个词一个 you know、sort of，听着含糊。大致是每次转向用一个，而且要和你正在做的那一步对得上。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ Actually, I live in this city. (meant as "the fact is")
✓ I live in this city, in fact. / As it happens, I live in this city.
In speech, Actually at the start usually sounds like a correction.
口语里句首的 Actually 多半听着像在纠正对方。
✗ Anyway, by the way, so, I went home.
✓ Anyway, I went home.
One marker per move.
一次转向一个标记。
✗ You know, it was, you know, sort of, you know, nice.
✓ It was nice, actually. I mean, better than I expected.
Markers should point somewhere, not fill silence.
标记要指方向，不是填空白。
✗ (silence for five seconds)
✓ Let me think. Probably the second one.
A marker shows you are thinking, not lost.
用标记表明你在思考，而不是卡壳。
✗ Mind you, the flat is small. (as the first sentence)
✓ The flat is great. Mind you, it's small.
Mind you adds a contrast to something already said.
mind you 是给前面的话加个转折，不能开头就用。
