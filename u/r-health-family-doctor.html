Ask the Nurse: Do I Really Need a Family Doctor? 问护士：我真的需要家庭医生吗？

🧠 Learn the 5 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 5 个新词

Read a health advice column and notice when English uses "the", "a" or no article at all. 读一篇健康问答专栏，留意英语什么时候用 the、a，什么时候不用冠词。

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Dear Nurse, I have been here for eight months. I am healthy, I hardly ever get sick, and when I do, I go to a walk-in clinic. My friends keep telling me to find a family doctor. Do I really need one? Healthy in Vancouver

护士您好：我来这里八个月了。我身体健康，几乎不生病，病了就去免预约诊所。朋友们一直劝我找个家庭医生。我真的需要吗？温哥华的健康人

Dear Healthy, you do not need a family doctor for a cough or a sore throat. A walk-in clinic or a pharmacist can deal with most of those. But a family doctor is not really for the days you are sick. It is for the years you are well.

健康人您好：咳嗽、喉咙痛，你不需要家庭医生，免预约诊所或药剂师大多能处理。可家庭医生其实不是为你生病的那几天准备的，而是为你健康的那些年。

Think of what a family doctor holds: your history, your test results, the medicine you take, and the information from every specialist you have seen. When something changes slowly, like blood pressure or a mole that grows a little each year, the same doctor seeing you over time is the one most likely to notice. A walk-in doctor meets you for the first time, every time.

想想家庭医生手里有什么：你的病史、你的检查结果、你在吃的药，以及你看过的每位专科医生给的信息。当某样东西变化得很慢，比如血压，或者一颗每年长大一点的痣，多年来一直给你看病的同一位医生最有可能察觉。免预约诊所的医生每次见你都是第一次。

In most places the family doctor is also the door to the rest of the system. A referral to a specialist usually goes through them, and many tests need their signature. Without one, people often end up in an emergency room with problems that a normal appointment could have handled.

在大多数地方，家庭医生也是通往整个体系的那扇门。转诊到专科医生通常要经过他们，许多检查也需要他们签字。没有家庭医生，人们常常带着本来一次普通门诊就能处理的问题，最后进了急诊室。

My advice: get on a waiting list now, while you are well, because it can be long. Ask friends whether their doctor is taking new patients, check your local health website, and at the first visit bring a list of the medicine you keep at home. Then go back once a year, even when nothing hurts. The Nurse

我的建议：趁身体好，现在就去排队，因为可能要等很久。问问朋友他们的医生收不收新病人，看看本地的卫生网站，第一次就诊时带上你家里常备药的清单。之后每年去一次，哪怕哪儿都不痛。护士

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

When something changes slowly, like blood pressure or a mole that grows a little each year, the same doctor seeing you over time is the one most likely to notice.
No article with blood pressure in general; a mole is one of many; the same doctor always takes the.
泛指血压不加冠词；a mole 指众多中的一颗；the same doctor 固定用 the。
Without one, people often end up in an emergency room with problems that a normal appointment could have handled.
one = a family doctor. could have handled = possible in the past but did not happen.
one 指家庭医生。could have handled 表示过去本可以、但没有发生。
