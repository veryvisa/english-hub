what, whatever, whoever as noun clauses 名词性从句

Choose accurately between what, whatever, whoever and similar noun-clause openers, and use them to make compact, precise statements about unspecified things and people. 能准确区分 what、whatever、whoever 等名词性从句引导词，并用它们简洁地谈论不确定的事物和人。

I'll cook what you like. 我会做你喜欢的菜。
Points to specific dishes you are known to like; the speaker knows your preferences.指向你已知喜欢的具体菜品，说话人了解你的口味。
I'll cook whatever you like. 你喜欢什么我就做什么。
Open choice: you decide freely, and the speaker is flexible.选择完全开放，由你决定，说话人很灵活。
I'll cook whichever dish you like from this menu. 我会做你从这份菜单里挑的任何一道菜。
The choice is open but limited to a known set of options.选择自由，但限于已知的有限选项。
Whatever you like, I'll cook it. 不管你喜欢什么，我都会做。
Concessive adverbial clause: emphasizes that the speaker's willingness does not depend on your choice.让步状语从句，强调说话人的意愿不受你选择的影响。

What means 'the thing that', whoever means 'the person who, no matter who', and whatever means 'anything that, no matter what', so each one fills a noun slot and also acts as a subject or object inside its own clause.what 相当于「……的那个东西」，whoever 是「不管是谁的那个人」，whatever 是「不管什么的任何东西」；它们既充当整个句子里的名词成分，又在从句内部充当主语或宾语。

📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Open the full explanation: 6 points, 24 examples展开细讲：6 段讲解、24 个例句

Use what when the thing is specific but unnamed, and whatever when the thing is open-ended. 'I'll tell you what he said' points to one actual statement; 'I'll tell you whatever he says' covers any statement he might make. The -ever form carries an element of free choice or indifference, which is why it often appears with verbs like want, need, choose, prefer and like.

what 指向具体但没有点明的那个东西，whatever 指向不限范围的任何东西。「I'll tell you what he said」指他说过的某一句话；「I'll tell you whatever he says」则涵盖他可能说的任何话。-ever 形式带有任意选择或无所谓的意味，所以常和 want、need、choose、prefer、like 搭配。

A noun clause with what is not a relative clause, and the difference matters. A relative clause modifies a noun that already exists in the sentence ('the book that I read'), while what contains its own antecedent ('what I read'). Learners often write 'the thing what' or 'all what', which doubles up the antecedent; the correct choices are 'what', 'the thing that', or 'all that'.

what 引导的名词性从句不是定语从句，这点很关键。定语从句修饰句中已有的名词（the book that I read），而 what 自己就包含了先行词（what I read）。学习者常写出重复先行词的 the thing what 或 all what，正确的选择是 what、the thing that 或 all that。

Whoever and whomever differ in the role they play inside their own clause, not in the role of the whole clause. In 'Give it to whoever needs it most', the whole clause is the object of 'to', but inside the clause whoever is the subject of 'needs', so the subject form is correct. In everyday English whomever is rare and whoever is often used even for objects, but in careful writing the clause-internal role decides.

whoever 与 whomever 的区别取决于它在自己从句内部的角色，而不是整个从句在大句中的角色。「Give it to whoever needs it most」里，整个从句是 to 的宾语，但 whoever 在从句内是 needs 的主语，所以用主格。日常英语中 whomever 很少见，宾格位置也常用 whoever，但在正式书面语里应按从句内部角色来选。

Choose whichever instead of whatever when the options come from a limited, known set. 'Take whichever seat you like' implies there are particular seats in view, while 'take whatever seat you like' sounds as if the choice is wide open. This is a small distinction, but it signals that you can see the options, which sounds more precise and natural in instructions and offers.

选项来自有限且已知的范围时，用 whichever 而不是 whatever。「Take whichever seat you like」暗示眼前有具体的几个座位，而「take whatever seat you like」听起来选择范围完全开放。差别虽小，却表明你看得到这些选项，在指示和邀请中更精确自然。

Whatever can also work as an adverbial meaning 'no matter what', not only as a noun clause. In 'Whatever you decide, I'll support you', the clause is a concession attached to the main clause, and you could rephrase it with 'no matter what you decide'. In 'I'll support whatever you decide', it is the object of 'support' and the paraphrase 'no matter what' does not work. Checking this paraphrase is a quick way to tell the two uses apart.

whatever 除了引导名词性从句，还能作让步状语，意为「无论什么」。「Whatever you decide, I'll support you」中，从句是附加在主句上的让步成分，可以改写成 no matter what you decide；而「I'll support whatever you decide」中，它是 support 的宾语，不能用 no matter what 替换。用这个替换法就能快速分辨两种用法。

Reach for a what-clause when you want to put emphasis at the end of the sentence, as in pseudo-cleft sentences. 'What surprised me was her calm' places the new, important information last, which reads more naturally than 'Her calm surprised me' when you are building a point. At C1, this pattern is a key tool for controlling focus in both speaking and writing.

想把强调的信息放到句末时，可以用 what 从句构成伪分裂句。「What surprised me was her calm」把新的重要信息放在最后，在铺陈观点时比「Her calm surprised me」更自然。到 C1 阶段，这个句型是控制语篇焦点的重要手段，口语和写作中都适用。

More pairs: same idea, different choice更多对比句组

Who broke the window is still unknown. 谁打破了窗户仍然不清楚。
Who is a question word: the identity is the open issue.who 是疑问词，焦点是身份本身未知。
Whoever broke the window must pay for it. 打破窗户的人必须赔偿。
Whoever means 'the person who, whoever it is'; the identity is unknown but a consequence applies to that person.whoever 意为「不管是谁，打破窗户的那个人」，身份未知但后果由此人承担。
The person who broke the window must pay for it. 打破窗户的那个人必须赔偿。
A relative clause with a definite antecedent; the speaker treats the person as a particular individual.带明确先行词的定语从句，说话人把此人视为一个特定的个体。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ All what he said was true.
✓ All that he said was true.
What already contains its own antecedent, so it cannot follow 'all'. After 'all', 'everything' or 'the only thing', use that, or drop the head word and use 'what he said'.
what 本身已包含先行词，不能接在 all 后面。all、everything、the only thing 之后应该用 that，或者去掉前面的词，直接说 what he said。
✗ I will give the ticket to whomever wins the draw.
✓ I will give the ticket to whoever wins the draw.
The form depends on the role inside the clause, not on the preposition before it. Here the word is the subject of 'wins', so the subject form is needed. Learners often choose the object form because it follows a preposition.
形式取决于词在从句内部的角色，而不是前面的介词。这里它是 wins 的主语，所以要用主格 whoever。学习者常因前面有介词而误用宾格。
✗ What is the best way is to practice every day.
✓ What works best is practising every day.
A what-clause is already a full noun clause acting as the subject, so adding a second full subject-verb phrase creates a clash. Keep one clear structure: the what-clause, then 'is', then the complement.
what 从句本身就是充当主语的完整名词性从句，再叠加一个完整的主谓结构会造成冲突。保持一个清晰的结构：what 从句 + is + 表语。
✗ Whatever you do it, I will support you.
✓ Whatever you do, I will support you.
Whatever is already the object of 'do' inside its clause, so adding 'it' repeats the object. Learners sometimes insert a pronoun because the Chinese pattern repeats the idea in the main clause.
whatever 在从句内已经是 do 的宾语，再加 it 就重复了宾语。学习者有时因为汉语习惯在主句中再次指代而多加一个代词。
✗ I don't know that he wants.
✓ I don't know what he wants.
A noun clause that is missing its object needs a word that fills that gap and also introduces the clause. That cannot do this job, because it only links the clause and plays no role inside it, so what is required.
缺少宾语的名词性从句需要一个既引导从句、又在从句中充当成分的词。that 只起连接作用，在从句中不充当成分，所以必须用 what。
✗ Whoever you ask, he will say the same thing.
✓ Whoever you ask, they will say the same thing.
Whoever is unspecified and can refer to anyone, so a singular 'he' wrongly assumes the person is male. Use 'they' for an unknown person.
whoever 指代不特定的任何人，用单数 he 会误设这个人是男性。指代不确定的人时应使用 they。
