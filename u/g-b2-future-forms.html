Future forms: will, going to, present tenses 将来的几种说法

📘 In your coursebook: English File 5e · Advanced · 8B “Don’t miss it!”. Use the texts and recordings with your coursebook; this page adds new words, grammar and practice.教材定位：English File 5e · Advanced · 8B《Don’t miss it!》。课文与录音请配合教材学习，本页补充新词、语法与练习。

Pick the future form that matches how you see the future event: a decision made now, a plan, an arrangement, a timetable, or a prediction. 按你怎么看这件将来的事来选说法：当场的决定、已有的打算、约好的安排、时刻表，还是预测。

I'll talk to the teacher. 我去跟老师谈。
Just decided, perhaps right after hearing the problem.刚决定，也许刚听说这个问题。
I'm going to talk to the teacher. 我打算去跟老师谈。
I made up my mind before now.我早就打定主意了。
I'm talking to the teacher on Monday. 我周一要跟老师谈。
It is arranged; the teacher knows.已经约好，老师也知道。

📏 The rule规则

📏 The rule in one line规则一句话
will is for decisions made now, promises and predictions from opinion; going to is for plans already made and predictions from evidence; the present continuous is for fixed arrangements with other people; the present simple is for timetables.will 用于当场决定、承诺和凭看法的预测；going to 用于早有的打算和有迹象的预测；现在进行时用于和别人约好的安排；一般现在时用于时刻表。

Chinese uses yao, hui and dasuan freely, and the listener works out the meaning. In English the choice tells the listener something: I'll call the landlord sounds like you just decided; I'm going to call the landlord says you decided earlier; I'm calling the landlord at four says it is arranged.

中文「要」「会」「打算」混着用，听的人自己体会。英语里选哪种说法本身就在传递信息：I'll call 听起来是刚决定；I'm going to call 是早就想好了；I'm calling … at four 是已经约好。

Offers, promises, requests and refusals at the moment of speaking take will: I'll carry that for you. I won't tell anyone. Will you hold the door? Using going to here sounds as if you had planned it, which can be strange or even cold.

说话当下的主动帮忙、承诺、请求、拒绝都用 will。这时用 going to 听起来像早有计划，会显得奇怪甚至冷淡。

Both will and going to make predictions. going to suggests you can see it coming: Look at those clouds. It's going to pour. will is more about what you believe or expect: I think the price will go up next year. With probably, I'm sure, I expect, will is the usual choice.

will 和 going to 都能做预测。going to 暗示眼前有迹象；will 更多是你相信、预期的事。和 probably、I'm sure、I expect 连用时通常用 will。

The present continuous needs a fixed arrangement, usually with a time and other people involved: We're meeting the lawyer on Thursday. It does not work for things nobody controls: say It's going to rain, not It's raining tomorrow.

现在进行时表将来需要一个定好的安排，通常有时间、有别人参与。没人能控制的事不能这样说。

The present simple is for timetables and official schedules: The train leaves at 7:10. The course starts on 6 January. It also appears after when, as soon as, before, after and until when they talk about the future: I'll call you when I land, not when I will land.

一般现在时用于时刻表和正式日程。在 when、as soon as、before、after、until 引导、指向将来的从句里也用一般现在时。

For something very near, use be about to: Don't hang up, I'm about to send it. For something officially planned, especially in news, use be due to: The new bridge is due to open in spring.

马上就要发生的事用 be about to；官方计划好的事，尤其新闻里，用 be due to。

How you see itFormExample
Decision at the moment of speakingwillHang on, I'll get a pen.
Offer, promise, refusalwill / won'tI won't be late, I promise.
Plan already decidedbe going toWe're going to sell the car.
Prediction from evidence nowbe going toThat shelf is going to fall.
Prediction from opinionwillI think rents will keep rising.
Fixed arrangement with otherspresent continuousI'm seeing the dentist on Friday.
Timetable or official schedulepresent simpleThe office opens at 8:30.
Very near futurebe about toThe film is about to start.

Examples例句

  • The phone's ringing. I'll get it. 电话响了，我来接。
    Decided at that moment.当下决定。
  • I'm going to ask for a raise at my review next month. 下个月考评时我打算提加薪。
    A plan made earlier.事先的打算。
  • I'm having lunch with my manager on Thursday. 周四我和经理一起吃午饭。
    A fixed arrangement.约好的安排。
  • The last ferry leaves at 9:40, so we can't stay long. 末班渡轮九点四十开，所以我们不能待太久。
    A timetable.时刻表。
  • Watch out! You're going to drop those plates. 小心！盘子要掉了。
    Evidence you can see.眼前的迹象。
  • I expect the meeting will run late; it always does. 我估计会议会拖时间，每次都这样。
    Prediction from experience.凭经验预测。
  • Don't worry, I won't forget your birthday this year. 放心，今年我不会忘了你的生日。
    A promise.承诺。
  • The car won't start. I think the battery is dead. 车打不着火，我觉得电瓶没电了。
    won't for refusal, even by a machine.won't 表示拒绝，机器也能用。
  • We'll let you know as soon as we hear from the insurer. 一有保险公司的消息就通知你。
    Present simple after as soon as.as soon as 后用一般现在时。
  • My daughter is starting university in September. 我女儿九月上大学。
    Arranged and fixed.已定的安排。
  • The prime minister is due to speak at noon. 总理定于中午讲话。
    Officially scheduled.官方安排。
  • Quick, get in. The bus is about to leave. 快上车，车马上要开了。
    Very near future.马上发生。
  • Are you going to apply, or are you still thinking about it? 你打算申请，还是还在考虑？
    Asking about someone's plan.问对方的打算。
  • Shall I open the window? 要不要我开窗？
    An offer with shall I.用 shall I 主动提出帮忙。

More pairs: same idea, different choice更多对比句组

It'll be cold tomorrow, I think. 我觉得明天会冷。
An opinion or a forecast you heard.看法或者听来的预报。
It's going to be cold. Look at the frost on the car. 要冷了，你看车上结霜了。
Evidence right now.眼前有迹象。
The cold front is due to arrive on Tuesday. 冷锋预计周二到达。
Official, news-style language.正式的新闻说法。
The workshop starts at ten. 讲座十点开始。
Fixed timetable.固定日程。
The workshop is starting late because the speaker is stuck in traffic. 讲座要晚开始，因为讲者堵在路上。
A changed arrangement.安排有变。
The workshop is about to start. Find a seat. 讲座马上开始，找个位子。
Very near.马上。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ I will tell you when I will arrive.
✓ I will tell you when I arrive.
After when about the future, use the present simple.
when 引导的将来从句用一般现在时。
✗ A: The printer is out of paper. B: OK, I'm going to get some.
✓ A: The printer is out of paper. B: OK, I'll get some.
A decision made at that moment takes will.
当场的决定用 will。
✗ It's snowing tomorrow.
✓ It's going to snow tomorrow.
Weather is not an arrangement, so the present continuous does not work.
天气不是安排，不能用现在进行时。
✗ I will see the doctor at 3 tomorrow, it is booked.
✓ I'm seeing the doctor at 3 tomorrow.
A booked appointment sounds most natural in the present continuous.
已预约的事最自然的说法是现在进行时。
✗ Do you go to the party on Saturday?
✓ Are you going to the party on Saturday?
Asking about someone's arrangement takes the continuous, not the present simple.
问对方的安排用进行时，不用一般现在时。
