Why We Stay Until the End of a Bad Film 烂片为什么舍不得走

Read an explainer on the sunk cost trap and use make/take/have collocations to talk about choices. 读一篇讲沉没成本陷阱的说明文，用 make、take、have 搭配谈选择。

Read and listen读与听

315 words
Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

You paid for a cinema ticket, and forty minutes into the film you know it is terrible. Do you leave? Most people stay. They tell themselves that leaving would waste the money, even though the money is gone whether they stay or not. Economists call this the sunk cost trap: we let what we have already spent make the decision for us.

你买了电影票，看了四十分钟就知道这片子很烂。你走吗？大多数人会留下。他们对自己说，走了钱就白花了，可不管走不走，钱都已经花出去了。经济学家把这叫作沉没成本陷阱：我们让已经花掉的东西替我们做决定。

The trap is not only about money. People stay in courses they hate because they have already done two years. Companies keep putting money into projects that are clearly failing because so much work has gone into them already. In each case, the past is being treated as a reason, when the only question that matters is what happens next.

这个陷阱不只关乎钱。人们留在自己讨厌的课程里，因为已经读了两年。公司不断往明显失败的项目里砸钱，因为已经在里面投入了那么多心血。每一种情况下，过去都被当成了理由，可唯一要紧的问题是接下来会怎样。

Why are we like this? One reason is that quitting feels like admitting a mistake, and nobody enjoys that. Another is that we want to look consistent to others. Staying is also the default: it needs no new decision. Stopping halfway can look weak, even when it is the wise option.

我们为什么会这样？一个原因是放弃感觉像承认错误，没人喜欢那样。另一个是我们想在别人眼里显得前后一致。留下也是默认的做法：它不需要做新的决定。半途停下可能显得软弱，哪怕那是明智的选择。

A simple question can help. Imagine you had not spent anything yet. Knowing what you know now, would you start? If the answer is no, the money and time you have already put in should not be taken into account. They cannot be recovered, and they should not have an impact on what you choose. In hindsight, many people wish they had asked this question sooner.

一个简单的问题能帮上忙。设想你还什么都没花。以你现在知道的情况，你还会开始吗？如果答案是不会，那么已经投进去的钱和时间就不该被考虑进去。它们收不回来，也不该影响你的选择。事后回看，很多人希望自己早点问了这个问题。

This does not mean we should give up on everything that gets hard. Plenty of good things, from learning an instrument to building a business, feel like a waste of time in the middle. The point is to weigh up the future honestly: the cost of going on against what you expect to gain. Sometimes the best thing you can do is make an effort and continue. Sometimes it is to walk out of the cinema.

这并不是说遇到难处就该放弃。很多好事，从学一门乐器到创业，做到一半都感觉像在浪费时间。关键是诚实地权衡未来：继续下去的代价，对比你预计能得到的。有时最好的做法是加把劲继续；有时则是走出电影院。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

Economists call this the sunk cost trap: we let what we have already spent make the decision for us.
let + object + bare verb (make, not to make). what we have already spent is a noun clause acting as the object.
let 后接宾语加动词原形（make，不是 to make）。what we have already spent 是名词从句作宾语。
If the answer is no, the money and time you have already put in should not be taken into account.
Passive of take … into account: the object moves to the front and into account stays after the verb.
take … into account 的被动：宾语提到前面，into account 留在动词后。
