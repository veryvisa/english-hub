An Email About Thursday's Call 关于周四那通电话的一封邮件

🧠 Learn the 3 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 3 个新词

Read a warm family email after a near miss with a "grandchild in trouble" scam, and learn how I wish and conditionals soften regret, while core-verb collocations make a request clear. 读一封险些上当后写给父母的家书，学会用 I wish 和条件句缓和遗憾，用核心动词搭配把请求说清楚。

Subject: About Thursday's call, and a family code word. Dear Mum and Dad, I have been thinking about Thursday all weekend, and I want to write it down while it is fresh, partly for you and partly for me.

主题：关于周四那通电话，以及一个家庭暗号。亲爱的爸妈：这个周末我一直在想周四的事，想趁着记忆还新把它写下来，一半是写给你们，一半是写给我自己。

When you rang me, Mum, you were halfway to the bank. A young man had called you, crying, saying he was Leo, that he had been in a car accident on holiday and needed money for a lawyer before the end of the day. He begged you not to tell me because he was embarrassed. You were sure it was his voice.

妈，你给我打电话时，已经在去银行的半路上了。一个年轻人打电话给你，哭着说他是里奥，说他度假时出了车祸，今天之内要钱请律师。他求你别告诉我，因为他觉得丢人。你很确定那是他的声音。

Please don't feel foolish. These scammers are very good at what they do, and the pressure they put on people is enormous. If you had walked into the bank five minutes earlier, the money would have gone. What saved us was that you hesitated and decided to ring me first. I wish every grandparent did the same.

请别觉得自己傻。这些骗子很擅长干这一行，他们给人施加的压力是巨大的。要是你早五分钟走进银行，钱就没了。救了我们的是你犹豫了一下，决定先给我打个电话。真希望每个祖父母都这样做。

So I want to ask you both to do a favour for all of us. Let's agree a family code word, something only we know, and never write it anywhere online. If anyone calls claiming to be one of us and asking for money, ask for the word. A real grandson will not mind; a scammer will hang up. And please make an effort to call one of us before you make a decision about money, however urgent the caller says it is.

所以我想请你们俩为我们大家帮个忙。我们定一个家庭暗号，只有我们知道，绝不写在网上任何地方。谁打电话来自称是我们家的人、又开口要钱，就问他暗号。真孙子不会介意，骗子会挂电话。还有，在为钱的事做决定之前，请尽量先给我们中的一个人打电话，不管对方说得多急。

I also had a word with your bank. They said staff are trained to ask questions when a customer wants to withdraw a large sum in a hurry, and they were glad we told them. Leo, by the way, was at home all week, perfectly fine and very upset that someone had used his name. Love, Anna

我还跟你们的银行谈了谈。他们说，客户急着取一大笔钱时，员工受过训练会多问几句，他们很高兴我们告诉了他们这件事。顺便说一句，里奥这一周都在家，好好的，知道有人冒用他的名字，他很生气。爱你们的，安娜

/ˈkəʊd wɜːd/ n.

Our family has a code word for emergencies. 我们家有一个应急用的暗号。
/ˈhezɪteɪt/ v.
犹豫

If something feels wrong, it is fine to hesitate. 觉得不对劲时，犹豫一下没关系。
/wɪðˈdrɔː/ v.

/ˈskæmə/ n.
骗子
a phone scammer; scammers pretend to be
The scammer knew the grandson's name from social media. 骗子是从社交媒体上知道孙子名字的。
/ˈpreʃə/ n.
压力；催逼
put pressure on sb; give in to pressure
The caller put so much pressure on her that she nearly paid. 打电话的人逼得太紧，她差点就付了。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

If you had walked into the bank five minutes earlier, the money would have gone.
Third conditional for a past that did not happen: had walked … would have gone. It shows how close the family came to losing the money.
第三条件句，说一件没发生的过去：had walked … would have gone。写出了家里离丢钱有多近。
And please make an effort to call one of us before you make a decision about money, however urgent the caller says it is.
Two make collocations: make an effort, make a decision. however urgent … it is means "no matter how urgent".
两个 make 搭配：make an effort、make a decision。however urgent … it is 意思是「不管多急」。
