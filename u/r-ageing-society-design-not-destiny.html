A Problem of Design, Not Destiny 是设计问题，而非命中注定

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

It has become fashionable to describe an ageing society as a looming disaster. I think that framing is lazy. Longer life expectancy is, after all, one of the great achievements of modern medicine and public health, and it is odd to treat success as a threat.

把老龄社会形容成迫在眉睫的灾难，已经成了一种时髦。我认为这种说法太过偷懒。毕竟，预期寿命的延长是现代医学和公共卫生的重大成就之一，把成功当成威胁，未免奇怪。

That said, the pessimists are not entirely wrong. A shrinking workforce is likely to put pressure on public finances, and the old model of one generation quietly funding another cannot continue unchanged. Nobody should pretend the transition will be painless. But the arithmetic only looks frightening if we assume that people will retire at the same age, in the same way, as their grandparents did.

话虽如此，悲观者也并非全无道理。劳动力萎缩很可能给公共财政带来压力，而一代人默默供养另一代人的旧模式，也无法原封不动地延续下去。没有人该假装这一转变会毫无痛感。但只有当我们假定人们仍像祖辈那样在同样的年龄、以同样的方式退休时，这些算术才显得可怕。

Consider what older people actually do. Many care for grandchildren, volunteer in their communities and keep working part-time long after formal retirement. Treating them purely as a cost ignores the unpaid labour that holds families together. A retired engineer mentoring apprentices is an asset, not a drain. A fairer debate would ask how to make better use of their skills rather than how to shoulder the burden of their existence.

不妨看看老年人实际上在做什么。许多人照看孙辈，在社区里做志愿者，在正式退休很久之后仍兼职工作。把他们单纯看作成本，就忽视了维系家庭的无偿劳动。一位退休工程师指导学徒，是一笔资产，而不是负担。更公平的讨论应该问：如何更好地发挥他们的技能，而不是问怎样承担他们存在所带来的负担。

Of course, goodwill is not a policy. Policy follows attitudes, and attitudes can change. Employers must stop treating experience as a liability, and governments must invest in prevention so that extra years are healthy ones. Although the task is demanding, an ageing population is by and large a problem of design, not destiny.

当然，好意不等于政策。政策随观念而动，而观念是可以改变的。雇主必须不再把经验当作包袱，政府则必须投资于预防，让多出来的岁月是健康的岁月。尽管任务艰巨，人口老龄化总体上是设计问题，而非命中注定。

Younger readers should care too. They will, one day, be the older generation, and the systems we build now are the ones that will look after them. A society that values its elders is, in the end, building its own future security.

年轻读者也应该关心这件事。总有一天，他们会成为老一代，而我们现在建立的制度，正是将来照顾他们的制度。一个尊重长者的社会，说到底是在为自己未来的安全打基础。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

But the arithmetic only looks frightening if we assume that people will retire at the same age, in the same way, as their grandparents did.
The ‘if’ clause states the condition under which the main clause is true, and ‘did’ replaces the repeated verb ‘retired’ to avoid repetition.
if 从句给出主句成立的条件；句末的 did 代替了重复的动词 retired，以避免重复。
Although the task is demanding, an ageing population is by and large a problem of design, not destiny.
The ‘Although’ clause concedes difficulty, while ‘by and large’ hedges the main claim so that it does not sound absolute.
Although 从句承认任务艰巨，而 by and large 对主句的论断加以限定，使之不显得绝对。
