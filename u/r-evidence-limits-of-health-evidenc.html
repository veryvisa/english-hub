When Evidence Is Not the Whole Story 当证据并不能说明一切

🧠 Learn the 5 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 5 个新词

Sound public health decisions require scientific evidence, but they must also account for uncertainty, social conditions and competing values. 合理的公共卫生决策需要科学证据，同时也必须考虑不确定性、社会条件和相互竞争的价值观。

Public discussions about health often revolve around a deceptively simple question: What does the evidence say? Scientific findings are indispensable, yet they rarely provide a complete answer to complex social problems. A study may establish an association without demonstrating causation, or reveal an average effect that conceals substantial differences between population groups. Evidence must therefore be interpreted, not merely repeated.

公共卫生讨论经常围绕一个看似简单的问题展开：证据说明了什么？科学发现不可或缺，但它们很少能够为复杂的社会问题提供完整答案。一项研究可能发现某种关联，却无法证明因果关系；也可能揭示平均效果，却掩盖不同人群之间的显著差异。因此，证据需要经过解读，而不能只是被重复引用。

Consider a programme designed to encourage healthier behaviour. Its evaluation may show that participants improved their habits, although those who volunteered may already have been more motivated than the wider population. This selection effect complicates the interpretation. While the programme may genuinely help, its apparent success could partly reflect differences that existed before the intervention began.

以一项旨在鼓励健康行为的计划为例。评估可能显示参与者改善了自己的习惯，但主动报名的人可能本来就比普通人群更有动力。这种选择效应使结果更难解释。尽管该计划可能确实有所帮助，但它表面上的成功也可能部分反映了干预开始之前就已经存在的差异。

There is also a question of distribution. A policy may improve average health outcomes while placing additional burdens on people with limited income, disabilities or restricted access to healthcare. Such consequences are not always captured by conventional statistical measures. On the whole, evidence-based policy tends to be more defensible when decision-makers examine both aggregate benefits and unequal effects.

此外，还存在利益分配问题。一项政策可能改善整体平均健康结果，却给收入有限、存在残障或医疗服务获取受限的人带来额外负担。传统统计指标并不总能反映这些后果。总体而言，如果决策者同时考察总体收益和不平等影响，以证据为基础的政策通常会更有说服力。

This does not mean that scientific evidence should be abandoned whenever disagreement arises. Rather, the appropriate response is to make assumptions explicit, disclose limitations and explain which values influence the final decision. Public health is both a scientific and a social undertaking. Good decisions are not necessarily free from controversy, but they should be open to scrutiny and revision as better evidence emerges.

这并不意味着一旦出现分歧就应该放弃科学证据。相反，恰当的做法是明确说明假设、披露局限，并解释哪些价值观影响了最终决策。公共卫生既是一项科学事业，也是一项社会事业。好的决策未必完全没有争议，但随着更好的证据出现，它们应当接受审查并作出调整。
