What One Household Can and Cannot Do 一个家庭能做什么、做不了什么

Read and listen读与听

296 words
Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Thank you all for coming. When the library asked me to talk about climate change, I almost said no, because most talks on this subject leave people feeling either guilty or hopeless. I would like you to leave tonight with neither feeling, and with a clearer idea of where your own choices make a difference.

谢谢大家来。图书馆请我来讲气候变化时，我差点推掉了，因为这个题目的讲座大多让人听完不是内疚就是绝望。我希望大家今晚离开时两种感觉都没有，而是更清楚自己的选择在哪些地方真正起作用。

Let me start with an honest point. The idea of a personal carbon footprint was made popular partly by advertising that encouraged people to focus on their own habits rather than on the energy system as a whole. That does not mean personal choices are useless. It means we should take both into account: what we do at home, and what we ask of the people who make larger decisions.

先说一句实在话。「个人碳足迹」这个说法之所以流行，部分是因为一些广告鼓励人们只盯着自己的习惯，而不是整个能源系统。这并不是说个人选择没用，而是说两边都要考虑：我们在家做什么，以及我们对那些做更大决定的人提出什么要求。

At home, a few choices have much more impact than the rest. How you heat the house, how far you drive and how often you fly usually matter more than switching off lights or rinsing yogurt pots. If you are replacing a furnace anyway, look at a heat pump. If you are choosing where to live, a shorter commute can do more than years of careful recycling.

在家里，有几个选择的影响远大于其他。怎么取暖、开车开多远、多久坐一次飞机，通常比随手关灯、冲洗酸奶盒重要得多。如果反正要换暖气炉，看看热泵。如果正在选住处，通勤短一点，作用可能比多年认真分类回收还大。

Beyond the home, the most useful thing most of us can do is talk. Ask your building, your workplace and your local council what they are doing about heat waves, wildfire smoke and emissions. Make the effort to go to one public meeting a year. The people who decide bus routes and building rules notice who turns up.

在家门之外，我们大多数人能做的最有用的事就是开口。问问你住的楼、你的单位、你所在的市议会，他们在怎样应对热浪、山火烟雾和排放。每年花点力气去参加一次公开会议。决定公交线路和建筑规定的人，会注意到谁来了。

Finally, be patient with yourself. Nobody can take responsibility for the whole problem, and nobody needs to. We are making progress, slowly and unevenly, and that progress came from millions of ordinary decisions, not from a few perfect people. Now, I am happy to take questions.

最后，对自己耐心一点。没有人能为整个问题负全责，也没有人需要这样做。我们在进步，虽然缓慢、也不均衡，而这些进步来自千千万万个普通的决定，而不是几个完美的人。好，现在欢迎提问。

Flying is the biggest part of their carbon footprint. 坐飞机是他们碳足迹里最大的一块。
/meɪk ə ˈdɪfrəns/ v. phr.

make a big difference; make a real difference to sth
A short daily walk can make a real difference. 每天散一小会儿步就能起很大作用。
/ˈhəʊpləs/ adj.

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

It means we should take both into account: what we do at home, and what we ask of the people who make larger decisions.
take … into account splits around its object both. After the colon, two what clauses explain what both means. ask sth of sb = expect it from them.
take … into account 把宾语 both 夹在中间。冒号后两个 what 从句说明 both 指什么。ask sth of sb＝对某人提出要求。
a shorter commute can do more than years of careful recycling
A comparison of effect: do more than = have a bigger result than. years of + noun stresses a long effort.
比较作用大小：do more than＝比……效果更大。years of 加名词，强调长期的努力。
