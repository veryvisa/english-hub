The passive in all tenses 各时态的被动

📘 In your coursebook: English File 5e · Upper-Intermediate · 8A “Modern crime”. Use the texts and recordings with your coursebook; this page adds new words, grammar and practice.教材定位：English File 5e · Upper-Intermediate · 8A《Modern crime》。课文与录音请配合教材学习，本页补充新词、语法与练习。

Use the passive in any tense when the action or the person affected matters more than who did it, and form it correctly in the continuous, perfect and modal forms. 当动作或受影响的人比做事的人更重要时，在任何时态里用被动，并且把进行、完成、情态各形式的被动写对。

They are painting our building. 他们在粉刷我们的楼。
Active: focus on the workers.主动：重点在工人。
Our building is being painted. 我们的楼正在粉刷。
Passive: focus on the building.被动：重点在楼。
Our building is painted. 我们的楼是刷过漆的。
A state, not the process.状态，不是过程。

📏 The rule规则

📏 The rule in one line规则一句话
The passive is be in the needed tense + past participle. Choose it when the doer is unknown, obvious or unimportant, when you want to keep the same topic at the start of each sentence, or when a formal or impersonal tone is needed.被动＝相应时态的 be 加过去分词。做事的人不知道、显而易见或不重要时，想让每句话开头保持同一个话题时，或者需要正式、客观的语气时，用被动。

Chinese often leaves the doer out without changing the verb (fangzi mai le), or uses bei mainly for bad things. English has no such limit: the passive is neutral, and it is the normal way to talk about processes, rules and news. For fangzi mai le, the natural English is The house has been sold.

中文常省掉施事而动词不变（「房子卖了」），「被」字多用于不好的事。英语被动是中性的，是讲流程、规则和新闻的常规说法。

Every tense has a passive, and the trick is to change only be. They are repairing the road → The road is being repaired. They have repaired it → It has been repaired. They will repair it → It will be repaired. The past participle never changes.

每种时态都有被动，诀窍是只变 be，过去分词不变。

The passive moves known information to the front and new information to the end, which makes text flow. The new library opened in May. It was designed by a local firm keeps the library as the topic. Starting the second sentence with A local firm designed it jumps to a new topic.

被动把已知信息放前面、新信息放后面，让文字连贯。第二句用被动，话题一直是图书馆；用主动就跳到了新话题。

Add by + doer only when the doer is new and important information. My bike was stolen needs no by someone. The bridge was designed by a team of students is worth saying.

只有做事的人是重要的新信息时才加 by。自行车被偷，不用说 by someone；桥是学生团队设计的，这才值得说。

Some verbs cannot be passive because they take no object: happen, arrive, die, seem, disappear, occur. The accident was happened is a common error. get + past participle is an informal passive for events, often unplanned: He got fired. Our flight got cancelled.

不及物动词没有被动：happen、arrive、die、seem、disappear、occur。get 加过去分词是口语里的被动，多用于意外的事件。

TenseActivePassive
present simpleThey clean the office daily.The office is cleaned daily.
present continuousThey are cleaning the office.The office is being cleaned.
past simpleThey cleaned the office.The office was cleaned.
past continuousThey were cleaning the office.The office was being cleaned.
present perfectThey have cleaned the office.The office has been cleaned.
past perfectThey had cleaned the office.The office had been cleaned.
futureThey will clean the office.The office will be cleaned.
modalThey must clean the office.The office must be cleaned.
modal perfectThey should have cleaned the office.The office should have been cleaned.

Examples例句

  • Your application is being reviewed, and you will be contacted within ten business days. 你的申请正在审核，十个工作日内会联系你。
    Present continuous and future passive: official tone.进行时和将来时被动：官方语气。
  • The pool has been closed for repairs since August. 游泳池从八月起就因维修关闭了。
    Present perfect passive.现在完成时被动。
  • When we arrived, the furniture had already been delivered. 我们到的时候，家具已经送到了。
    Past perfect passive.过去完成时被动。
  • The road was being widened, so we took the long way round. 那条路正在拓宽，所以我们绕远路走。
    Past continuous passive.过去进行时被动。
  • Passwords must be changed every ninety days. 密码每九十天必须更换一次。
    Modal passive for a rule.情态被动讲规则。
  • The form should have been signed by both parents. 这份表本应由父母双方签字。
    Modal perfect passive: it was not done.情态完成被动：没做到。
  • Thousands of homes were left without power after the storm. 暴风雨后数千户人家断电。
    News style, doer not needed.新闻语体，不需要施事。
  • Rice is grown in more than a hundred countries. 一百多个国家种水稻。
    A general fact.一般事实。
  • The painting was bought by a museum in Montreal. 这幅画被蒙特利尔一家博物馆买下了。
    by + new, important doer.by 引出重要的新信息。
  • All visitors are asked to sign in at reception. 所有访客请在前台登记。
    Polite, impersonal instruction.客气、不针对个人的指示。
  • He got caught speeding on the highway. 他在高速上超速被抓了。
    Informal get passive.口语 get 被动。
  • The results won't be announced until next week. 结果下周才会公布。
    Negative future passive.否定将来被动。
  • The kids love being read to at bedtime. 孩子们喜欢睡前有人给他们读书。
    -ing passive after a verb.动词后接 -ing 被动。
  • I don't want to be disturbed for the next hour. 接下来一个小时我不想被打扰。
    Infinitive passive.不定式被动。

More pairs: same idea, different choice更多对比句组

Someone has hacked my email account. 有人黑了我的邮箱。
Active with a vague subject.主动，主语含糊。
My email account has been hacked. 我的邮箱被黑了。
Passive: the natural choice.被动：最自然的说法。
My email account got hacked. 我的邮箱让人黑了。
Informal, conversational.口语。
The mistake was made in the accounts department. 错误出在会计部。
Neutral: no one is blamed by name.中性，不点名。
Someone in the accounts department made the mistake. 会计部有人犯了这个错。
Points closer to a person.更接近于指向某个人。
We made a mistake. 我们犯了一个错。
Taking responsibility.主动承担责任。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ The accident was happened at night.
✓ The accident happened at night.
happen has no object, so it has no passive.
happen 不及物，没有被动。
✗ The road is repairing now.
✓ The road is being repaired now.
The road does not repair anything; use is being + past participle.
路不会修东西，要用 is being 加过去分词。
✗ My car has stolen.
✓ My car has been stolen.
The present perfect passive needs been.
现在完成时被动要有 been。
✗ The house sold last month by my uncle.
✓ The house was sold last month by my uncle. / My uncle sold the house last month.
Passive needs be in the right tense.
被动要有相应时态的 be。
✗ English is speaking in many countries.
✓ English is spoken in many countries.
Use the past participle, not -ing.
用过去分词，不用 -ing。
