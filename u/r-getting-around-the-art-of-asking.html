The Art of Asking for Directions 问路也是一种艺术

Many travellers believe that a good map is all they need to get around. However, even the best navigation application cannot answer every question. A street may be closed, a bus stop may have moved, or a walking route may be difficult to recognise. In these situations, the ability to ask for directions becomes extremely valuable.

许多旅行者认为，只要有一张好地图，就足以在城市中出行。然而，即使是最好的导航应用，也无法回答所有问题。某条街道可能已经封闭，公共汽车站可能搬迁，步行路线也可能难以辨认。在这些情况下，问路的能力就显得格外宝贵。

The way we ask a question matters. Instead of saying, 'Where is the station?' in a hurried or unfriendly voice, travellers can begin with 'Excuse me' and continue with 'Could you tell me how to get to the station?' This small change makes the request sound more polite. It also gives the other person a moment to pay attention.

我们提问的方式很重要。旅行者不必用匆忙或不友好的语气说“车站在哪里？”，而可以先说“打扰一下”，然后接着问“您能告诉我怎么去车站吗？”这个小小的改变会让请求听起来更加礼貌，也能给对方一点时间集中注意力。

Good communication also means listening carefully. Someone might say, 'Go straight ahead, turn left at the traffic lights, and you will see a bank on your right.' Rather than trying to remember every word, travellers should identify the main actions and landmarks. If necessary, they can politely ask the speaker to repeat or explain a particular part.

良好的沟通也意味着认真倾听。有人可能会说：“一直往前走，在红绿灯处左转，然后你会在右边看到一家银行。”旅行者不必努力记住每一个词，而应该找出主要动作和地标。如有必要，可以礼貌地请对方重复或解释某个部分。

While digital navigation is convenient, speaking to local people can provide details that a map may miss. Of course, not everyone has the time or ability to help, so travellers should remain patient and respectful. By and large, a combination of clear questions, careful listening and a little confidence tends to make getting around much easier.

虽然电子导航很方便，但与当地人交谈可以获得地图可能遗漏的细节。当然，并不是每个人都有时间或能力提供帮助，因此旅行者应该保持耐心和尊重。总的来说，将清楚的提问、认真倾听和一点自信结合起来，通常会让出行变得容易得多。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

Rather than trying to remember every word, travellers should identify the main actions and landmarks.
Rather than is followed by a gerund here and introduces an alternative to the action in the main clause.
Rather than在这里后接动名词，引出一种替代主句动作的做法。
While digital navigation is convenient, speaking to local people can provide details that a map may miss.
While introduces a concessive clause, and the relative clause beginning with that modifies the noun details.
While引导让步状语从句，而以that开头的定语从句修饰名词details。
