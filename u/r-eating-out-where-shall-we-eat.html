Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Mia: Hi, Tom. Are you free tonight? I would like to try the new noodle place near the station. Tom: Sure, but I heard it is always busy. Should we book a table first? Mia: Good idea. I will call them now and ask for seven o’clock.

米娅：嗨，汤姆。你今晚有空吗？我想去试试车站附近新开的那家面馆。汤姆：行啊，不过听说那儿总是很忙。我们要不要先订位？米娅：好主意。我现在就打电话，订七点。

Mia: They said the only free table is outside. Is that all right? Tom: It is a bit cold for that. Could we try somewhere else? Mia: There is a Thai restaurant next door. It is cheaper than the noodle place, and I think the portions are bigger. Tom: That sounds better. Is there a vegetarian option? I stopped eating meat last year.

米娅：他们说只剩外面的位子了。可以吗？汤姆：这么冷，坐外面有点不合适。我们能换一家吗？米娅：隔壁有一家泰国餐厅，比那家面馆便宜，我觉得分量也更大。汤姆：听起来更好。有素食选择吗？我去年开始不吃肉了。

Mia: I looked at the menu online. There are three vegetable curries on the menu, and the tofu salad looks nice too. Tom: Great. What do you usually order at Thai places? Mia: Usually something spicy, but I cannot eat too much chili. Last time I cried at the table, and the waiter had to bring me a lot of water. Tom: Then let us ask for the mild version.

米娅：我在网上看了菜单。菜单上有三种蔬菜咖喱，豆腐沙拉看起来也不错。汤姆：太好了。你在泰国餐厅一般点什么？米娅：通常点辣的，但我吃不了太多辣椒。上次我在桌边辣哭了，服务员还得给我拿好多水。汤姆：那我们就要微辣版吧。

Tom: One more thing. Is it a good idea to arrive early? Mia: Yes. The restaurant fills up quickly on Fridays, so we should get there before the crowd. If our table is not ready, we can wait at the bar and have a cold drink. Tom: I will leave the office at six, then. Mia: Great. I am already hungry!

汤姆：还有一件事。早点到是不是更好？米娅：是的。这家餐厅周五客人很快就满了，所以我们得赶在人多之前到。如果我们的桌子还没准备好，可以在吧台等，喝杯冷饮。汤姆：那我六点下班。米娅：太好了。我已经饿了！

Mia: Okay. Shall we meet at half past six? Tom: Perfect. Who is paying tonight? Mia: Let us split the bill, as usual. Tom: Fine, but dessert is on me, because you chose the restaurant. Mia: Deal! See you soon.

米娅：好的。我们六点半碰头怎么样？汤姆：完美。今晚谁买单？米娅：像往常一样AA吧。汤姆：行，不过甜点我请，因为是你选的餐厅。米娅：一言为定！一会儿见。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

It is cheaper than the noodle place, and I think the portions are bigger.
Short adjectives take -er before ‘than’ to compare two things, and ‘I think’ makes the opinion softer.
短形容词加 -er 再接 than 来比较两个事物；'I think' 让观点语气更柔和。
Last time I cried at the table, and the waiter had to bring me a lot of water.
The past simple ‘cried’ tells a finished event, and ‘had to’ expresses a necessity in the past.
过去式 cried 讲已经结束的事件，'had to' 表示过去的必须。
