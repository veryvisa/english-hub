Does Good Taste Need Education? 良好的品味需要教育吗？

🧠 Learn the 4 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 4 个新词

Artistic taste benefits from education and critical discussion, but genuine appreciation cannot be reduced to conformity with established standards. 艺术品味能够从教育和批判性讨论中受益，但真正的欣赏不能被简化为遵循既定标准。

People frequently describe someone's taste as refined, sophisticated, or poor. Such labels suggest that appreciation can be ranked according to a shared standard. Yet who establishes that standard? In many societies, museums, universities, critics, and collectors influence which works receive recognition. Their judgements may reflect considerable knowledge, but they also reveal the values and assumptions of particular cultural environments.

人们经常用高雅、成熟或低俗来评价某人的品味。这些标签似乎暗示，艺术欣赏可以按照共同标准进行排名。然而，这些标准究竟由谁制定？在许多社会中，博物馆、大学、评论家和收藏家都会影响哪些作品得到认可。他们的判断可能体现了丰富的知识，但也反映出特定文化环境中的价值观和假设。

Education can certainly expand our ability to appreciate art. Learning about composition, symbolism, historical movements, and artistic techniques gives viewers a richer vocabulary for describing what they see. Nevertheless, knowing the correct terminology does not automatically produce an authentic response. A person who can explain every technical feature of a painting may still feel emotionally disconnected from it.

教育确实能够拓展我们欣赏艺术的能力。学习构图、象征意义、艺术运动和创作技巧，可以让观众拥有更丰富的词汇来描述所见。不过，掌握正确的专业术语并不会自动产生真实的感受。一个能够解释画作所有技术特点的人，仍然可能在情感上与作品缺乏联系。

The problem emerges when education becomes a mechanism of exclusion. If people believe that only specialists can appreciate difficult works, they may hesitate to express their own opinions. This attitude tends to turn cultural knowledge into social status rather than a resource for exploration. While expertise deserves respect, it should encourage curiosity instead of creating an invisible boundary between qualified observers and ordinary visitors.

当教育变成排斥他人的机制时，问题便会出现。如果人们认为只有专家才能欣赏复杂作品，他们可能会不愿表达自己的观点。这种态度往往会使文化知识变成社会地位的象征，而不是探索艺术的资源。虽然专业能力值得尊重，但它应该鼓励好奇心，而不是在合格的观察者和普通参观者之间制造无形的界限。

A more productive understanding of good taste would combine knowledge, openness, and self-awareness. We should be able to explain why a work appeals to us while recognising that another person's response may be equally reasonable. Ultimately, artistic maturity is not about collecting prestigious preferences. It is about developing the confidence to examine our assumptions, encounter unfamiliar visual language, and revise our opinions when new perspectives become available.

对良好品味更有建设性的理解，应该结合知识、开放态度和自我认知。我们既应该能够解释某件作品为何吸引自己，也应该承认别人的反应可能同样合理。归根结底，艺术上的成熟并不是积累令人羡慕的偏好，而是培养审视自身假设的信心，接触陌生的视觉语言，并在获得新视角时调整自己的观点。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

Their judgements may reflect considerable knowledge, but they also reveal the values and assumptions of particular cultural environments.
The modal verb may signals a qualified claim, while the coordinating conjunction but introduces a contrasting consideration.
情态动词 may 表示有限定的判断，而并列连词 but 引出与前面观点形成对照的另一层考虑。
If people believe that only specialists can appreciate difficult works, they may hesitate to express their own opinions.
This is a first conditional-style structure with a present-tense condition and may expressing a possible consequence; the that-clause functions as the object of believe.
这是带有现实条件特点的结构，使用一般现在时表示条件，并用 may 表示可能结果；that 从句充当 believe 的宾语。
