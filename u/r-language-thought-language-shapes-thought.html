Does Language Shape the Way We Think? 语言会塑造我们的思维吗？

🧠 Learn the 5 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 5 个新词

Language influences how people organise experience and direct attention, although it does not completely determine what they can think. 语言会影响人们组织经验和分配注意力的方式，但并不能完全决定人们能够思考什么。

Imagine describing a familiar street to someone who has never visited it. You might begin with its buildings, its direction, or the people who gather there. The choices you make reveal something about how you organise experience. Language is more than a system for naming objects. It provides categories, distinctions and familiar patterns that help us interpret the world. When we learn a new language, we also encounter alternative ways of describing ordinary situations.

想象一下，向一个从未到访过的人描述一条熟悉的街道。你可能从建筑物、方向或聚集在那里的人们说起。你所作的选择揭示了自己组织经验的某些方式。语言不仅仅是一套为事物命名的系统。它提供分类、区别和熟悉的表达模式，帮助我们理解世界。当我们学习一门新语言时，也会接触到描述日常情境的其他方式。

Researchers have investigated whether linguistic structures influence habitual attention. Some languages require speakers to express information about time, gender, number or spatial relationships more regularly than others. Such grammatical requirements may encourage speakers to notice particular features. For example, speakers who routinely describe directions using geographical orientation may become more attentive to their surroundings. That said, linguistic patterns are only one influence among many, including education, environment and personal experience.

研究人员一直在探讨语言结构是否会影响习惯性的注意方式。有些语言比其他语言更经常要求说话者表达时间、性别、数量或空间关系等信息。这些语法要求可能促使说话者留意某些特征。例如，经常使用地理方位描述方向的人，可能会更加关注周围环境。尽管如此，语言模式只是众多影响因素之一，教育、环境和个人经历同样发挥作用。

This relationship is sometimes discussed through the idea of linguistic relativity. In its moderate form, the theory suggests that language influences habitual thought without placing an absolute limit on mental ability. A person can understand a distinction even when their first language does not routinely express it. Learning vocabulary for unfamiliar emotions, for instance, may help someone identify experiences that previously felt difficult to describe. Language can therefore make certain ideas more accessible without making other ideas impossible.

这种关系有时通过语言相对论这一概念来讨论。在较温和的版本中，该理论认为语言会影响习惯性思维，但不会对人的心理能力设置绝对限制。即使母语通常不表达某种区别，一个人仍然能够理解它。例如，学习描述陌生情绪的词汇，可能帮助某人识别以前难以表达的体验。因此，语言能够让某些观念更容易被理解，却不会使其他观念变得不可能。

The practical implications are important for education and communication. When people encounter unfamiliar expressions, they may also encounter unfamiliar ways of categorising experience. By comparing languages, learners can become more conscious of assumptions they previously took for granted. On the whole, language is best understood as a flexible cognitive resource rather than a prison for thought. It guides attention, provides shared concepts and makes some interpretations easier, while human imagination remains capable of moving beyond established linguistic boundaries.

这一观点对教育和交流具有重要的现实意义。当人们接触陌生表达时，也可能接触到陌生的经验分类方式。通过比较不同语言，学习者能够更加清楚地意识到自己过去视为理所当然的假设。总的来说，语言最好被理解为一种灵活的认知资源，而不是限制思维的牢笼。它引导注意力、提供共享概念，并使某些解释更加容易，但人类的想象力仍然能够超越既有的语言边界。
