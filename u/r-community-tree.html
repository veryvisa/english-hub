A Note About the Maple Tree 关于那棵枫树的一封信

🧠 Learn the 4 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 4 个新词

Read a note to new neighbours about an overhanging tree and see how unreal conditionals make a request firm but friendly. 读一封写给新邻居、关于越界树枝的便条，看非真实条件句怎样让请求既坚定又友好。

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Dear Sandra and Tom, I hope you are both well, and that the new baby is letting you sleep a little. We have not had a proper chance to talk since you moved in, so I thought I would write instead of knocking on your door at a bad time.

亲爱的桑德拉和汤姆：希望你们一切都好，也希望小宝宝能让你们睡上一会儿。你们搬来后我们还没好好聊过，所以我想写几句，免得在不合适的时候去敲门。

The reason I am writing is the large maple on your side of the fence. Over the summer, several of its branches have grown out over our garage, and last week's wind brought one of them down onto our car. Luckily it only did minor damage, a scratch on the door, and I am not asking you to pay for it.

我写信是因为篱笆你们那边的那棵大枫树。整个夏天，有几根树枝长到了我们车库上方，上周刮风，其中一根掉下来砸在我们车上。幸好只是小损伤，车门上一道划痕，我也不是要你们赔。

If the branches were a little shorter, I would not worry at all. But if that branch had fallen a metre to the left, it would have gone through the garage window, and I would rather sort this out now than after a winter storm. Would you be open to having the branches on our side cut back? I know a tree service that a few people on the street have used, and I would be happy to share the cost.

要是树枝短一点，我根本不会担心。可要是那根树枝往左偏一米掉下来，就会砸穿车库的窗户。我宁愿现在就把这事解决，也不想等到冬天的暴风雪之后。你们愿意把伸到我们这边的树枝修剪一下吗？我知道一家街上好几户都用过的修树公司，费用我很乐意分担。

I should say that we love the tree. It gives both gardens shade, and our kids have spent hours watching the squirrels in it. I wish we had talked about it in the spring, when the branches were lighter. Please feel free to drop by any evening this week, or reply to this note, whichever suits you.

我得说一句，我们很喜欢这棵树。它给两家的院子都遮了阴，我们的孩子看树上的松鼠一看就是几个小时。真希望我们春天就聊过这件事，那时树枝还轻一些。这周哪天晚上都欢迎过来坐坐，或者回这张便条，哪种方便都行。

Best wishes, and welcome again to the street. We are glad to have you as neighbours. Mei and David, number 14

祝好，再次欢迎你们搬到这条街来，很高兴和你们做邻居。14 号的阿美和大卫

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

But if that branch had fallen a metre to the left, it would have gone through the garage window
Third conditional: if + past perfect, would have + past participle, for a past that did not happen. It makes the risk concrete without blaming anyone.
第三类条件句：if 加过去完成时、would have 加过去分词，说过去没发生的事。它把风险说具体，又不指责任何人。
I wish we had talked about it in the spring, when the branches were lighter.
wish + past perfect: regret about the past. It shares the responsibility; the writer does not say you should have told us.
wish 加过去完成时：对过去的遗憾。这是把责任一起担，而不是说「你们早该告诉我们」。
