Why Groups Decide the Way They Do 群体为何这样决策

🧠 Learn the 3 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 3 个新词

Research on group behaviour suggests that how a team is structured often shapes its decisions more than the intelligence of its members. 关于群体行为的研究表明，团队的结构往往比成员的智力更能塑造其决策。

Read and listen读与听

220 words
Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

It seems reasonable to assume that a group of clever people will make clever decisions. Psychologists who study teams have found, however, that this is far from guaranteed. Groups tend to fall into predictable traps, and the structure of a meeting can matter as much as the ability of those sitting in it.

认为一群聪明人会做出明智的决定似乎合情合理。然而，研究团队的心理学家发现，这远非必然。群体往往会落入可预见的陷阱，会议的结构与参会者的能力同样重要。

One well-documented problem is conformity. When a senior figure speaks first, others are inclined to fall in line, even if they privately hold different views. The result is a false consensus that feels comfortable but hides useful disagreement. Another is the tendency to discuss information that everyone already shares, while unique knowledge held by one member goes unmentioned.

一个有充分记录的问题是从众。当资深人物先发言时，其他人倾向于随声附和，即使他们私下持有不同看法。结果是一种令人舒适却掩盖了有益分歧的虚假共识。另一个问题是倾向于讨论大家已经共享的信息，而某位成员掌握的独特知识却无人提及。

Fortunately, simple remedies exist. Asking members to write down their views before discussion begins reduces the pull of early opinions. Assigning someone to argue the opposite case, sometimes called a devil’s advocate, encourages the group to test its assumptions rather than simply confirm them. While none of these techniques is a cure-all, each is likely to improve the quality of debate.

幸运的是，存在简单的补救办法。让成员在讨论开始前写下自己的观点，可以减弱早期意见的牵引力。指派某人为相反立场辩护，有时称为唱反调者，能鼓励群体检验自己的假设而非一味确认。虽然这些技巧都不是万能药，但每一种都可能提升辩论的质量。

The broader lesson is that good leadership involves designing the conditions for good thinking, not merely supplying answers. A leader who can create an environment where people feel safe to dissent is, in effect, building the team’s collective intelligence. By and large, the evidence points in that direction.

更广泛的启示是，良好的领导包括为良好的思考创造条件，而不仅仅是提供答案。能够营造让人们敢于表达异议的环境的领导者，实际上是在构建团队的集体智慧。总的来说，证据指向这个方向。

/fɔːl ɪn laɪn/ phr.

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

When a senior figure speaks first, others are inclined to fall in line, even if they privately hold different views.
’Be inclined to’ is a hedged way of describing a tendency, and ‘even if’ introduces a concessive clause.
’be inclined to’ 是描述倾向的限定性说法，'even if' 引出让步分句。
A leader who can create an environment where people feel safe to dissent is, in effect, building the team’s collective intelligence.
The subject is a long noun phrase with two embedded relative clauses, and the parenthetical ‘in effect’ sits between the verb parts.
主语是带有两个嵌套关系从句的长名词短语，插入语 ‘in effect’ 位于动词成分之间。
