📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Open the full explanation: 5 points展开细讲：5 段讲解

Chinese pairs linkers: suiran... danshi, yinwei... suoyi. English uses one per idea. Although it was raining, we went out is complete; adding but makes it wrong. Because it was late, we took a taxi is complete; adding so makes it wrong. This is the most frequent linker error for Chinese speakers.

中文的连接词成对用：「虽然……但是」「因为……所以」。英语一个意思只用一个。Although it was raining, we went out 已经完整，再加 but 就错；Because it was late, we took a taxi 已经完整，再加 so 就错。这是华人最常犯的连接词错误。

Although, even though, while and whereas join two clauses. Despite and in spite of are prepositions: they take a noun or an -ing form, not a clause. Despite the rain or Despite it raining, but not Despite it was raining. If you need a clause after despite, add the fact that.

although、even though、while、whereas 连接两个分句；despite 和 in spite of 是介词，后面接名词或 -ing，不接从句：Despite the rain、Despite it raining，不能说 Despite it was raining。一定要接从句就加 the fact that。

However, even so, on the other hand, as a result and therefore connect two sentences. They start the second sentence, usually with a comma, or sit after a semicolon. It was cheap, however it broke is a comma splice; write It was cheap. However, it broke.

however、even so、on the other hand、as a result、therefore 连接两个句子：放在第二句句首，通常后跟逗号，或者放在分号之后。It was cheap, however it broke 是逗号粘连句，要写成 It was cheap. However, it broke.

Cause: because + clause, because of and due to + noun, since + clause when the reason is already known. Result: so + clause in speech, as a result and therefore in writing, which means to add a consequence to the same sentence. So that is purpose, not result: I left early so that I could park.

原因：because 接从句，because of 和 due to 接名词，since 接从句、用于对方已知的理由。结果：口语用 so 接从句，书面用 as a result、therefore，which means 在同一句里补上后果。so that 表目的，不表结果：I left early so that I could park。

While and whereas compare two facts side by side: My husband likes the city, whereas I prefer the suburbs. On the other hand needs a first hand: use it for the second side of the same question, not as a general but.

while 和 whereas 用来并列比较两个事实：My husband likes the city, whereas I prefer the suburbs。on the other hand 需要有「一方面」在前：用来讲同一个问题的另一面，不是万能的 but。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ Although it was cold, but we went to the park.
✓ Although it was cold, we went to the park.
One linker per idea: drop but.
一个意思一个连接词，删掉 but。
✗ Because the shop was closed, so we went home.
✓ Because the shop was closed, we went home. / The shop was closed, so we went home.
Because or so, not both.
because 和 so 二选一。
✗ Despite it was raining, we went out.
✓ Despite the rain, we went out. / Despite the fact that it was raining, we went out.
despite + noun or -ing.
despite 接名词或 -ing。
✗ The price is high, however many people buy it.
✓ The price is high. However, many people buy it.
However starts a new sentence or follows a semicolon.
however 开启新句，或放在分号后。
✗ I study hard so that I passed the exam.
✓ I studied hard, so I passed the exam.
so that is purpose; so is result.
so that 表目的，so 表结果。
