/l/, /n/ and /r/, and the dark l l、n、r 与词尾 l

Separate /l/ from /n/ and /r/, and keep the dark l at the end of words like feel, will and school. 分清 /l/ 与 /n/、/r/，并保住 feel、will、school 这类词尾的暗 l。

📏 The rule规则

📏 The point in one line一句话要领
/n/ sends air through the nose, /l/ sends it round the sides of the tongue, and /r/ never lets the tongue touch the roof of the mouth; at the end of a word the l is still there, as a dark, back sound./n/ 气流走鼻腔，/l/ 气流从舌头两侧出，/r/ 舌头始终不碰上腭；词尾的 l 也要发，是一个靠后、偏暗的音。

📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Open the full explanation: 5 points展开细讲：5 段讲解

Speakers from Hunan, Sichuan, Hubei, Fujian, Jiangxi, and parts of Guangdong and Anhui often do not separate l and n in their own dialect, so light and night, low and no, lice and nice can swap in English. If this is not your problem, skip to the dark l below; if it is, it is the first thing to fix, because the swap changes very common words.

湖南、四川、湖北、福建、江西及广东、安徽部分地区的方言常常 l、n 不分，到英语里 light 与 night、low 与 no、lice 与 nice 就会互换。如果你没有这个问题，直接看下面的暗 l；如果有，先修它，因为换掉的都是高频词。

The test for /n/ and /l/ is to pinch your nose. Say a long nnnn: if you pinch your nose the sound stops, because /n/ needs the nose. Say a long llll: pinching changes nothing, because the air leaves round the sides of the tongue. For /l/ the tongue tip is on the ridge behind the top teeth but the sides of the tongue drop down.

测试法是捏住鼻子。拉长发 nnnn，捏鼻子声音就断了，因为 /n/ 靠鼻腔；拉长发 llll，捏鼻子没影响，因为气从舌头两侧出。/l/ 舌尖抵上齿龈，舌两侧放低。

/r/ and /l/ confusion is less common for Mandarin speakers than for Japanese or Korean speakers, but the English /r/ is still often made too much like Mandarin r in ri, with friction. Pull the tongue tip back and up without touching anything, round the lips a little, and let the vowel come out of that shape. right, road, really.

华人的 r、l 混淆比日韩学习者少，但英语 /r/ 常被发得像汉语「日」的声母，带摩擦。舌尖往后上方卷起、不碰任何地方，嘴唇略圆，元音从这个口型里出来。

The dark l is the bigger problem for almost everyone. At the end of a word or before a consonant (feel, will, milk, people, help), English /l/ becomes darker: the back of the tongue rises, and to a Chinese ear it sounds close to a short o or u. Learners either drop it (I'll becomes I, will becomes wi) or replace it with a full vowel (milk as mi-o-k). Both cost meaning: I'll call without the l is I call, a different tense.

暗 l 几乎是所有人的更大问题。在词尾或辅音前，英语 /l/ 变暗：舌后部抬起，华人听着像短促的「哦」或「呜」。学习者要么吞掉（I'll 成了 I），要么换成一个完整元音（milk 读成 mi-o-k）。两种都伤意思：I'll call 吞掉 l 就是 I call，时态变了。

To make the dark l, finish the vowel, then lift the tongue tip to the ridge behind the teeth while the back of the tongue stays high. You do not need to release it. In fast speech some native speakers barely touch the ridge at all, which is why people can sound like peepo in casual speech; as a listener, expect it.

发暗 l：元音结束后舌尖抬到齿龈，同时舌后部保持抬高，不必放开。快语速里有些母语者舌尖几乎不碰齿龈，所以随意说话时 people 听着像 peepo；听的时候要有准备。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ I see you tomorrow. (dark l dropped from I'll)
✓ I'll see you tomorrow.
Without the l the sentence is present tense, which sounds like a habit, not a plan.
吞掉 l 就成了现在时，听着像习惯而不是安排。
✗ We had a night lunch.
✓ We had a light lunch.
/n/ for /l/ changes the word; check with your nose.
用 /n/ 代 /l/ 就换了词，捏鼻子检查。
✗ mi-o-k, peo-pu
✓ milk /mɪlk/, people /ˈpiːpəl/
The dark l is a tongue movement, not an extra vowel.
暗 l 是舌头动作，不是多出来的元音。
✗ I need to collect my answer.
✓ I need to correct my answer.
/l/ for /r/ gives a real but wrong word.
/r/ 读成 /l/ 就成了另一个真实存在的词。
