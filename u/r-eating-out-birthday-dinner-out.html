A Birthday Dinner Out 外出过生日的晚餐

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Last month, I wanted to take my parents out for dinner to celebrate my father’s birthday. I chose a small Italian restaurant near my office because my colleague said the pasta there was wonderful. I called on Monday to book a table for three, and the waiter told me it was fully booked until Friday. I was disappointed, but I took a Friday evening slot anyway.

上个月，我想带父母出去吃晚饭，庆祝爸爸的生日。我选了公司附近一家小小的意大利餐厅，因为同事说那里的意面很棒。我周一打电话想订一张三人桌，服务员告诉我周五之前都订满了。我有点失望，但还是订下了周五晚上的时段。

On Friday we arrived ten minutes early, and the room was warm and noisy. My mother looked at the menu for a long time. She does not eat much meat, so she asked the waiter what was on the menu for vegetarians. He suggested a mushroom risotto, and she ordered it happily. My father chose a steak because it was his birthday, and I had the pasta my colleague had recommended.

周五我们提前十分钟到了，餐厅里温暖而嘈杂。妈妈看了很久的菜单。她不太吃肉，所以问服务员菜单上有什么适合素食者的菜。他推荐了蘑菇烩饭，妈妈高兴地点了。爸爸因为是生日选了牛排，我则点了同事推荐的那份意面。

The food took almost forty minutes to arrive, which was longer than I expected. My father joked that he was getting older while he waited. When the plates finally came, everything smelled fantastic. The steak was a little too salty, but the pasta was even better than my colleague had promised, and my mother said the risotto was the best she had ever eaten.

上菜花了差不多四十分钟，比我预想的要久。爸爸一边等一边开玩笑说自己都变老了。盘子终于端上来时，一切闻起来都很香。牛排有点太咸，但意面比同事说的还要好，妈妈说那份烩饭是她吃过最好吃的。

At the end, the waiter brought a small cake with one candle, and the whole room sang. My father looked embarrassed but pleased. When the bill came, I wanted to pay, but my father said we should split the bill. We argued politely for a minute and then agreed. It was not a perfect evening, but I think my parents will remember it for a long time.

最后，服务员端来一块插着一支蜡烛的小蛋糕，整个餐厅的人都唱起了歌。爸爸看起来既尴尬又高兴。结账时我想付钱，但爸爸说我们应该AA。我们礼貌地争了一分钟，然后达成一致。这不是一个完美的夜晚，但我想父母会记很久。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

I chose a small Italian restaurant near my office because my colleague said the pasta there was wonderful.
The word because introduces the reason for the choice, and the clause ‘my colleague said the pasta there was wonderful’ is a reported statement with the word that left out.
because 引出选择的原因；the pasta there was wonderful 是省略了 that 的宾语从句，转述同事的话。
The steak was a little too salty, but the pasta was even better than my colleague had promised, and my mother said the risotto was the best she had ever eaten.
The sentence joins three clauses; it uses ‘even better than’ for a comparison and the past perfect in ‘had promised’ and ‘had ever eaten’ to show earlier time and experience.
句子由三个分句连接；'even better than' 表示比较，'had promised' 和 ‘had ever eaten’ 用过去完成时表示更早的时间和经历。
