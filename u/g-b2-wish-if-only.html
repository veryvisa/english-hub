wish, if only, it's time, would rather wish 与 would rather

Express regret, longing and complaint with wish, if only, it's time and would rather, and put the verb one step back in time the way English expects. 用 wish、if only、it's time、would rather 表达后悔、渴望和不满，并按英语的习惯把动词往过去退一步。

📏 The rule规则

📏 The rule in one line规则一句话
After wish and if only, use the past for a present wish, the past perfect for a past regret, and would for a wish that someone else would change; after it's time and I'd rather + another person, use the past to talk about now.wish 和 if only 后：对现在的愿望用过去式，对过去的后悔用过去完成式，希望别人改变用 would；it's time 和 I'd rather 后接另一个人时，用过去式说现在的事。

Like unreal conditionals, these structures show distance from reality by moving the verb back. I wish I had more time is about now: I do not have it. I wish I had left earlier is about the past: I did not leave earlier. Chinese yaoshi … jiu hao le covers both, so English learners have to choose the time on purpose.

和虚拟条件句一样，这些结构靠把动词往过去退一步表示与事实有距离。中文「要是……就好了」现在和过去都能用，所以用英语时要有意识地选时间。

wish + would is a complaint or request about someone else's behaviour, or about something outside your control: I wish my neighbours would turn the music down. I wish it would stop raining. Do not use it about yourself: I wish I would is wrong; say I wish I could.

wish 加 would 是对别人行为或你控制不了的事的不满或期盼。不能用来说自己：说 I wish I could，不说 I wish I would。

if only is the same as wish but stronger and more emotional, and often stands alone as an exclamation: If only I'd listened to her! It also starts the first half of an unreal conditional: If only we had insurance, this would be easy.

if only 和 wish 一样，但更强烈、更有情绪，常单独成感叹句。它也可以引出虚拟条件句的前半句。

it's time + past means something should already be happening: It's time we talked about the budget. It's high time adds impatience. It's time to go (infinitive) is neutral and has no criticism.

it's time 加过去式表示某事早该开始了；It's high time 带着不耐烦。It's time to go 用不定式，中性，没有责备意味。

would rather means prefer. Same subject: base verb (I'd rather walk). Different subject: past form (I'd rather you didn't smoke in the car). This second pattern is a polite way to say no or to ask someone not to do something, and is very common in English workplaces.

would rather 表示宁愿。主语相同接原形；主语不同接过去式。后一种是客气地拒绝或请别人别做某事的说法，在英语职场很常见。

You meanFormExample
I want the present to be differentwish / if only + pastI wish I lived closer to work.
I regret the pastwish / if only + past perfectI wish I hadn't said that.
I want someone or something to changewish + wouldI wish you would listen.
I want an ability I don't havewish + couldI wish I could speak French.
It should be happening alreadyit's time + pastIt's time we left.
I prefer something myselfwould rather + base verbI'd rather stay in tonight.
I prefer someone else to do somethingwould rather + subject + pastI'd rather you called first.

Examples例句

  • I wish I knew more about how taxes work here. 我真希望自己更懂这里的税是怎么回事。
    A present wish: I don't know enough.对现在的愿望：了解不够。
  • I wish we had a bigger kitchen. 要是我们的厨房大一点就好了。
    Present wish about a state.对现在状态的愿望。
  • I wish I had taken more photos of my grandparents. 真后悔没给祖父母多拍些照片。
    Past regret.对过去的后悔。
  • I wish I hadn't signed that contract so quickly. 真后悔那么快就签了那份合同。
    Regret about something done.后悔做了某事。
  • I wish the bus would come. We've been here for twenty minutes. 公交车快来吧，我们都等了二十分钟了。
    Impatience about something outside our control.对控制不了的事不耐烦。
  • I wish you wouldn't check your phone at dinner. 希望你吃饭时别看手机。
    A complaint about someone's habit.对别人习惯的不满。
  • I wish I could take a month off. 要是我能休一个月就好了。
    A wished-for ability.希望有的能力。
  • If only I had kept the receipt! 要是我留着收据就好了！
    Strong regret as an exclamation.强烈的后悔，感叹句。
  • If only the clinic were open on Sundays. 要是诊所周日也开门就好了。
    Strong present wish.强烈的现在愿望。
  • It's time we thought about where our parents will live when they get older. 我们早该考虑父母老了住哪里了。
    It should already be happening.早该开始了。
  • It's high time the city fixed this road. 市里早就该修这条路了。
    Impatience.不耐烦。
  • It's time to go. The car's outside. 该走了，车在外面。
    Neutral, with the infinitive.中性，用不定式。
  • I'd rather take the train than drive in this snow. 这么大的雪，我宁愿坐火车也不开车。
    Same subject: base verb.同一主语接原形。
  • I'd rather you didn't share this with the team yet. 这事先别跟团队说，好吗？
    Different subject: past form; a polite request.不同主语接过去式，客气的请求。
  • Would you rather meet in person or online? 你想当面谈还是线上谈？
    Asking about preference.问偏好。

More pairs: same idea, different choice更多对比句组

I'd rather go home. 我宁愿回家。
My own preference.我自己的偏好。
I'd rather you went home. 我倒希望你回家去。
I want you to do it, said politely.希望你这样做，说得客气。
It's time you went home. 你早该回家了。
You should have gone already: a mild criticism.你早该走了，轻微的责备。
It's time to go home. 该回家了。
Neutral.中性。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ I wish I can speak English like a native.
✓ I wish I could speak English like a native.
After wish, move can back to could.
wish 后 can 要退成 could。
✗ I wish I didn't go to that party last night.
✓ I wish I hadn't gone to that party last night.
Regret about the past needs the past perfect.
对过去的后悔用过去完成式。
✗ I wish I would be taller.
✓ I wish I were taller. / I wish I was taller.
wish + would is for other people or outside events, not your own state.
wish 加 would 用于别人或外部事件，不用于自己的状态。
✗ It's time we go to bed.
✓ It's time we went to bed. / It's time to go to bed.
it's time + subject takes the past form.
it's time 加主语时接过去式。
✗ I'd rather you don't call after ten.
✓ I'd rather you didn't call after ten.
With a different subject, would rather takes the past form.
主语不同时 would rather 接过去式。
