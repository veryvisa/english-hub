Compound nouns and possessives: a car key, the car's owner 复合名词与所有格

📘 In your coursebook: English File 5e · Advanced · 9B “Hands off our dishes!”. Use the texts and recordings with your coursebook; this page adds new words, grammar and practice.教材定位：English File 5e · Advanced · 9B《Hands off our dishes!》。课文与录音请配合教材学习，本页补充新词、语法与练习。

Choose naturally between noun compounds and possessive forms to express relationships such as purpose, category, ownership, authorship, and association. 能够根据用途、类别、所属关系、所有权和关联等不同语义，自然地选择名词复合结构或所有格结构。

The car owner called the police. 一位车主报了警。
Car owner classifies the person by their relationship to cars in general.car owner 将这个人归类为车主，强调一般身份或类别。
The car's owner called the police. 这辆汽车的车主报了警。
The possessive identifies the owner of a particular car.所有格指出某一辆特定汽车的车主。
The cars' owners called the police. 这些汽车的车主报了警。
The plural possessive identifies the owners of multiple specific cars.复数所有格指出多辆特定汽车各自的车主。

📏 The rule规则

📏 The rule in one line规则一句话
Use noun compounds when the first noun classifies or specifies the type, purpose, or function of the second noun, and use possessives when the relationship involves ownership, personal association, authorship, or a clearly identifiable possessor.当前面的名词表示后面名词的类别、用途或功能时，通常使用名词复合结构；当关系涉及所有权、个人关联、作者身份或明确的所属者时，通常使用所有格。

Choose a noun compound when the first noun tells the reader what kind of thing the second noun is. A car key is a type of key, and a school bus is a type of bus. The first noun normally remains singular even when the thing it refers to is plural: car keys, not cars keys.

当第一个名词说明第二个名词属于什么类别时，选择名词复合结构。car key 是一种钥匙，school bus 是一种公交车。即使第一个名词所指的对象有多个，它通常仍然使用单数形式，例如 car keys，而不是 cars keys。

Choose the possessive when you want to identify who owns something or who is personally connected to it. The car's owner identifies the owner of a particular car, whereas a car owner describes a general category of person. The distinction becomes important when the sentence refers to a specific individual or relationship rather than a type.

当你想说明某物属于谁，或者某人和某物有什么个人关联时，选择所有格。the car's owner 指某辆特定汽车的车主，而 a car owner 指车主这一类人。当句子强调具体的人或所属关系，而不是类别时，这一区别尤其重要。

Do not assume that every relationship involving people requires an apostrophe. A student record is a record classified by its connection to students, while a student's record highlights the record belonging to or associated with one particular student. The choice depends on whether you are naming a document category or emphasizing an individual's connection to it.

不要认为只要涉及人物就必须使用撇号。student record 是按照与学生的关系命名的一类记录，而 a student's record 强调这份记录属于或关联某一位特定学生。选择取决于你是在命名文件类别，还是在强调个人与文件的关系。

Use possessives naturally with people, organizations, animals, and expressions of time when the relationship is one of belonging, responsibility, or association. The company's decision emphasizes the company as the decision-maker, while a company decision can simply classify a decision as a business-related one. Both forms can be grammatical, but their focus differs.

当关系表示归属、责任或关联时，人物、组织、动物和时间表达通常可以使用所有格。the company's decision 强调公司是作出决定的主体，而 a company decision 也可以只是将某个决定归类为商业决策。两种结构都可能符合语法，但强调重点不同。

In longer noun compounds, the final noun carries the main grammatical role, while the preceding nouns narrow down its meaning. For example, a car insurance policy is a type of insurance policy, and car insurance is a type of insurance. Avoid adding apostrophes simply because several nouns appear together.

在较长的名词复合结构中，最后一个名词承担主要的语法功能，前面的名词逐步限定其含义。例如，car insurance policy 是一种保险合同，而 car insurance 是一种保险。不要仅仅因为几个名词连续出现就添加撇号。

A possessive does not always mean legal ownership. Expressions such as John's idea, the company's reputation, and yesterday's meeting describe authorship, association, or time. When a noun compound would sound like a classification rather than a relationship with a particular person or event, the possessive is often the clearer choice.

所有格并不总是表示法律意义上的所有权。John's idea、the company's reputation 和 yesterday's meeting 分别表达创作归属、组织关联和时间关系。当名词复合结构听起来像是在表示类别，而不是与某个具体人物或事件的关系时，所有格往往更清楚。

More pairs: same idea, different choice更多对比句组

We reviewed the company policy. 我们审查了公司政策。
Company policy classifies the policy as a business or organizational policy.company policy 将该政策归类为公司政策。
We reviewed the company's policy. 我们审查了这家公司的政策。
The possessive emphasizes the policy belonging to or established by a particular company.所有格强调这项政策属于或由某家特定公司制定。
We reviewed the companies' policy. 我们审查了这些公司的政策。
The plural possessive indicates that multiple companies are associated with the policy.复数所有格表示这项政策与多家公司有关。
She submitted a student record. 她提交了一份学生档案。
Student record identifies a type of record used for student information.student record 表示一种用于记录学生信息的档案类别。
She submitted the student's record. 她提交了这名学生的档案。
The possessive connects the record to one particular student.所有格将这份档案与某一名特定学生联系起来。
She submitted the students' records. 她提交了这些学生的档案。
The plural possessive connects multiple records to multiple students.复数所有格表示这些档案分别与多名学生有关。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ I lost my cars key.
✓ I lost my car key.
The first noun in a noun compound normally stays singular, even when it refers to a category involving many objects. The word car classifies the type of key rather than showing possession.
名词复合结构中的第一个名词通常使用单数形式，即使它所指的类别涉及许多物品也是如此。car 在这里表示钥匙的类别，而不是所有关系。
✗ The cars owner is waiting outside.
✓ The car's owner is waiting outside.
A singular possessor normally requires apostrophe plus s. Without the apostrophe, cars is simply a plural noun and does not express the intended ownership relationship.
单数所有者通常需要使用撇号加 s。没有撇号时，cars 只是复数名词，无法表达这里需要的所属关系。
✗ The childrens' school is nearby.
✓ The children's school is nearby.
The plural noun children is irregular and does not end in s. Its possessive form therefore takes apostrophe plus s, not an apostrophe alone.
children 是不规则复数名词，词尾没有 s，因此它的所有格需要使用撇号加 s，而不能只加撇号。
✗ I need to buy a cars key.
✓ I need to buy a car key.
A noun compound uses the singular form of its modifying noun in this expression. The article a belongs to the entire compound, not just to the final noun.
在这个名词复合结构中，修饰名词 car 使用单数形式。冠词 a 修饰整个 car key 结构，而不只是最后一个名词。
✗ The companys manager called me yesterday.
✓ The company's manager called me yesterday.
When a singular organization is the possessor, the possessive form needs an apostrophe before s. Companys is not the correct spelling of this possessive.
当单数组织作为所属者时，所有格需要在 s 前面加撇号。companys 不是正确的所有格拼写。
✗ The students's records are ready.
✓ The students' records are ready.
For a regular plural noun already ending in s, add only an apostrophe to form the possessive. Adding another s produces an unnecessary ending.
对于以 s 结尾的规则复数名词，构成所有格时只需在词尾添加撇号，不需要再添加一个 s。
