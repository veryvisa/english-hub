Should We Change Our Weekend Plans? 我们应该改变周末计划吗？

🧠 Learn the 3 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 3 个新词

A conversation between two friends shows how checking weather forecasts and considering alternatives can help people make better weekend plans. 两位朋友的对话展示了查看天气预报和考虑替代方案如何帮助人们更好地安排周末活动。

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Maya: Have you checked the weather forecast for Saturday? I was hoping we could go hiking in the mountains. Daniel: I checked it this morning. It looks as if we might get heavy rain in the afternoon. The morning should be mild, but the conditions could change quickly. Maya: That is disappointing. I have been looking forward to this trip all week.

玛雅：你查看星期六的天气预报了吗？我本来希望我们可以去山里徒步。丹尼尔：我今天早上查过了。看起来下午可能会下大雨。早晨应该比较温和，但天气状况可能迅速变化。玛雅：真让人失望。我整个星期都在期待这次旅行。

Daniel: I understand, but we should think about safety first. The mountain paths may become slippery after heavy rain, and visibility could be poor. Maya: You are right. I do not want us to take unnecessary risks. Perhaps we could leave earlier and finish before the rain starts. Daniel: That might work, although we should check the updated forecast before making a final decision.

丹尼尔：我理解，但我们应该首先考虑安全。大雨过后，山间小路可能会变得湿滑，而且能见度可能较差。玛雅：你说得对。我不希望我们承担不必要的风险。也许我们可以早点出发，在下雨前结束活动。丹尼尔：这可能行得通，不过我们在最终决定之前应该查看最新的天气预报。

Maya: What if the rain arrives earlier than expected? We could spend the day at the local museum instead. Daniel: That is a good alternative. We could also have lunch at the small cafe nearby. Maya: Great idea. I would still prefer hiking, but there is no point in ignoring the forecast. We can save the mountain trip for another weekend.

玛雅：如果雨比预期来得更早怎么办？我们可以改为在当地博物馆度过这一天。丹尼尔：这是个不错的替代方案。我们还可以在附近的小咖啡馆吃午餐。玛雅：好主意。我还是更喜欢徒步，但忽视天气预报没有意义。我们可以把登山旅行留到另一个周末。

Daniel: Exactly. We should prepare for both possibilities. Let's bring light jackets and check the forecast again on Friday evening. Maya: Agreed. If the weather improves, we can go hiking. Otherwise, we will visit the museum. Daniel: Perfect. Having a backup plan makes the weekend easier to organise, especially when the weather is unpredictable. Maya: I am glad we have a plan that works either way.

丹尼尔：没错。我们应该为两种可能性做好准备。我们带上轻便夹克，并在星期五晚上再次查看天气预报吧。玛雅：同意。如果天气好转，我们就去徒步。否则，我们就参观博物馆。丹尼尔：太好了。有一个备用计划会让周末更容易安排，尤其是在天气难以预测的时候。玛雅：我很高兴我们有一个无论哪种情况都可行的计划。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

I have been looking forward to this trip all week.
The present perfect continuous have been looking forward to describes an expectation that started in the past and continues up to the present.
现在完成进行时 have been looking forward to 表示从过去开始并持续到现在的期待。
If the weather improves, we can go hiking.
This is a first conditional structure using the present simple in the if-clause and can plus the base verb in the main clause.
这是第一条件句结构，if 从句使用一般现在时，主句使用 can 加动词原形。
