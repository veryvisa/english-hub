An Email About Focus Hours 一封谈专注时段的邮件

🧠 Learn the 5 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 5 个新词

Read a team leader's email that sets up quiet focus hours and practise work phrasal verbs and article choices. 读一位组长设立「专注时段」的邮件，练习职场短语动词和冠词的选择。

B2 · CELPIP 7–8⏱ 20 minV04 Phrasal verbs avoided; the formal verb sounds stiff回避短语动词，说话太书面G01 Articles left out or over-used冠词漏用、滥用

📖 Key card知识卡
A protected block of quiet time is a team decision, not a test of each person's willpower.一段受保护的安静时间是团队的决定，而不是考验每个人的意志力。

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Subject: Trying focus hours in March. Hi everyone, at last week's meeting several of you brought up the same problem: it is hard to finish anything serious when the chat window never goes quiet. I have been thinking about it since then, and I would like to set up a simple trial for the month of March.

主题：三月试行专注时段。大家好，上周开会时好几位提到同一个问题：聊天窗口从不安静，很难把正经事做完。我一直在想这件事，想在三月份搞一个简单的试行。

From nine to eleven every morning, we will treat the chat as closed unless something is urgent. You can mute notifications, close your email or work at home if that helps. If a customer needs an answer, the person on support that day will deal with it and follow up with you after eleven. Anything truly urgent can still reach you by phone.

每天上午九点到十一点，除非有急事，聊天就当作关闭。你可以把推送静音、关掉邮箱，或者觉得有用就在家办公。客户要回复的话，当天值班的同事会先处理，十一点后再跟你对接。真有急事，照样可以打电话找到你。

I know some of you worry that this will slow things down. It may, at first. Many people feel an almost instant urge to check the chat, and resisting it takes practice. But the last two projects ran into delays partly because nobody had a clear block of time to think, and I would rather figure out whether this helps than keep guessing.

我知道有人担心这会拖慢进度。一开始也许会。很多人几乎立刻就有看聊天的冲动，忍住它需要练习。但前两个项目延期，部分原因就是没人有一整段时间好好想事情。我宁可弄清楚这样有没有用，也不想一直瞎猜。

At the end of the month I will send round a short survey. Be honest. If the focus hours turn out to be a waste of time, we will drop them. If they help, we will talk about whether to keep the same hours or move them.

月底我会发一份简短的问卷，请如实填。如果专注时段证明是浪费时间，我们就取消；如果有用，我们再商量是保持同样的时段还是调整。

One request: please do not make it a test of willpower. Put your phone in a drawer, not just face down on the desk. Thanks, Mei

一个请求：请别把它变成意志力测试。把手机放进抽屉，而不只是扣在桌上。谢谢，梅

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

at last week's meeting several of you brought up the same problem: it is hard to finish anything serious when the chat window never goes quiet.
bring up = mention a topic; the same problem needs the, because same points to one problem everyone knows.
bring up＝提起；the same problem 必须带 the，因为 same 指大家都知道的那一个问题。
But the last two projects ran into delays partly because nobody had a clear block of time to think, and I would rather figure out whether this helps than keep guessing.
run into delays = meet problems you did not plan for; would rather A than B takes the bare verb on both sides.
run into delays＝遇到意料之外的拖延；would rather A than B 两边都用动词原形。
