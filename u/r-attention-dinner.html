"Dad, You're Not Here" 「爸，你人不在这儿」

🧠 Learn the 5 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 5 个新词

Read a father's story about his phone at dinner and see unreal conditionals carry regret and advice. 读一位父亲讲饭桌上看手机的故事，看非真实条件句怎样表达后悔和建议。

Read and listen读与听

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Last winter my daughter, who was nine, asked me a question at dinner. I said "Mm-hm" without looking up. She waited, then said quietly, "Dad, you're not here." I was holding my phone under the table, scrolling through work messages that could easily have waited until morning.

去年冬天，我九岁的女儿在饭桌上问了我一个问题。我头也没抬，「嗯嗯」了一声。她等了一会儿，小声说：「爸，你人不在这儿。」我当时在桌子底下拿着手机，翻着完全可以等到第二天早上再看的工作消息。

I wanted to defend myself. I work long hours; the messages were from my manager; I was only glancing at them. But she was right. If someone had filmed our dinners that month, the video would have shown a man looking down at a small screen while his family talked around him. I would have been embarrassed to watch it.

我想为自己辩解：我工作时间长，消息是经理发的，我只是瞄几眼。可她说得对。要是有人把我们那个月的晚饭拍下来，视频里会是一个男人低头盯着小屏幕，家人在他身边说着话。我自己看了都会难为情。

So we made a rule. From six to eight, every phone in the house goes into a basket by the door, mine included. The first week was harder than I expected. I kept reaching for something in my pocket that was not there. I felt the urge to check the way you feel an itch. By the second week, the urge had started to wear off.

于是我们定了条规矩：六点到八点，家里每部手机都放进门边的篮子，我的也不例外。第一周比我想的难。我总是伸手去掏口袋里并不在那儿的东西。想看手机的冲动就像身上发痒。到了第二周，这股冲动开始减退。

Nothing terrible happened at work. My manager never noticed. What did change was dinner. We talk longer now, and my daughter tells me things she used to keep to herself. I wish I had made the rule years earlier, when her brother was small and asking me the same kind of questions.

工作上什么糟糕的事也没发生，经理根本没察觉。真正变了的是晚饭。我们现在聊得更久，女儿会跟我说以前憋在心里的事。真希望我早几年就定下这条规矩，那时她哥哥还小，也总问我同样的问题。

I am not against phones. I still use mine for hours every day. But if I were starting again as a parent, I would put the basket by the door on the first day, before the habit had a chance to grow.

我并不反对手机，每天照样用好几个小时。但如果让我重新当一次父母，我会在第一天就把篮子放在门边，不给这个习惯长起来的机会。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

If someone had filmed our dinners that month, the video would have shown a man looking down at a small screen while his family talked around him.
Third conditional: had filmed + would have shown. Nobody filmed them; the writer imagines the past to judge himself.
第三类条件句：had filmed + would have shown。其实没人拍过，作者假想过去，借此审视自己。
But if I were starting again as a parent, I would put the basket by the door on the first day, before the habit had a chance to grow.
Second conditional with a continuous verb (were starting): an imagined present situation, used here to give advice.
第二类条件句用进行体（were starting）：假想的现在情境，这里用来给建议。
