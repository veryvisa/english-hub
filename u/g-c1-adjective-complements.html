Adjective + to, -ing or that-clause 形容词后接 to、-ing 还是 that 从句

Choose confidently between adjective + to-infinitive, adjective + -ing (with a preposition) and adjective + that-clause, so that each sentence says exactly what you mean about a person's attitude, a situation or a fact. 能够在形容词后准确选择 to 不定式、介词加 -ing 还是 that 从句，让句子精确表达你对人物态度、情境或事实的意思。

I'm afraid of speaking in public. 我害怕公开演讲。
Fear of an activity or its possible result; the activity itself is what frightens me.害怕某项活动或其可能的后果；让我恐惧的就是这项活动本身。
I'm afraid to speak in public. 我不敢公开演讲。
Hesitation to take the action; it suggests I tend to avoid doing it.不敢采取行动；暗示我倾向于回避这件事。
I'm afraid that I will speak badly in public. 我担心自己公开演讲会讲不好。
Worry about a predicted outcome, expressed as a full proposition with its own subject.对预期结果的担忧，用带有自己主语的完整命题来表达。

📏 The rule规则

📏 The rule in one line规则一句话
After an adjective, a to-infinitive usually points to the action or reaction the adjective is about, a preposition plus -ing names the activity or issue being judged, and a that-clause states a proposition the person has an attitude toward.形容词后，to 不定式通常指向与该形容词相关的动作或反应，介词加 -ing 点明被评价的活动或事项，that 从句则陈述当事人对其持有态度的一个命题。

📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Open the full explanation: 5 points, 24 examples展开细讲：5 段讲解、24 个例句

Choose a that-clause when the adjective expresses a mental stance toward a whole fact or proposition, such as sure, certain, aware, convinced or relieved. You are not talking about one action but about a state of affairs, so a full clause with its own subject and tense is needed. Compare 'I'm sure that she left' with 'She's sure to leave': the first is your belief about a past event, the second is a prediction about her future behaviour.

当形容词表达对整个事实或命题的心理态度时，如 sure、certain、aware、convinced、relieved，应选 that 从句。你谈论的不是某一个动作，而是一种事态，所以需要带自己主语和时态的完整从句。比较 'I'm sure that she left' 与 'She's sure to leave'：前者是你对过去事件的判断，后者是对她未来行为的预测。

A to-infinitive after an emotion adjective usually reports the direct experience that triggered the feeling, often the exact moment it happened: 'I was relieved to hear the results.' A that-clause instead states the fact itself as a proposition, which can stand at some distance from the speaker's direct experience and can carry its own subject: 'I was relieved that the results were clear.' Use the that-clause when you are reporting the fact rather than the moment of experiencing it, or when the clause needs a subject of its own.

情感形容词后的 to 不定式通常报告触发这种感受的直接经历，往往是那个具体的瞬间，如 'I was relieved to hear the results.'。that 从句则把这件事当作一个命题来陈述，可以与说话人的直接经历有一定距离，也可以带有自己的主语：'I was relieved that the results were clear.'。当你是在陈述事实本身而不是经历那一刻，或从句需要自己的主语时，就用 that 从句。

Adjectives that take a preposition, such as afraid of, proud of, fond of, capable of, tired of and keen on, need -ing, because the preposition requires a noun-like form. The choice between 'afraid of making' and 'afraid to make' is real: of -ing describes a fear of a possible outcome, while to-infinitive describes hesitation to perform an act. 'I'm afraid of falling' is about a feared result, whereas 'I'm afraid to jump' signals reluctance to perform the act, often meaning I will not do it.

带介词的形容词，如 afraid of、proud of、fond of、capable of、tired of、keen on，后面要接 -ing，因为介词后需要名词性形式。'afraid of making' 与 'afraid to make' 的选择是有实际差别的：of 加 -ing 描述对可能后果的恐惧，而 to 不定式描述对实施这个动作本身的犹豫。'I'm afraid of falling' 说的是怕出现的后果，'I'm afraid to jump' 则表示不敢跳，往往意味着我不会去跳。

Adjectives describing a property of a thing, such as easy, hard, impossible, worth and ready, take a to-infinitive where the grammatical subject is actually the object of that verb: 'This rule is hard to explain.' Do not add an object pronoun after the verb. When the adjective describes a person's quality or attitude, as in 'She is hard to please' versus 'She is reluctant to please', the structure looks identical but the meaning relation changes completely. Always ask whether the subject is doing the action or receiving it.

描述事物属性的形容词，如 easy、hard、impossible、worth、ready，后接 to 不定式时，语法主语实际上是该动词的宾语：'This rule is hard to explain.' 动词后不要再加宾语代词。当形容词描述人的品质或态度时，如 'She is hard to please' 与 'She is reluctant to please'，结构看似相同，意义关系却完全不同。要始终问自己：主语是在做这个动作，还是在承受这个动作。

Some adjectives accept both patterns with a clear difference in tone. 'It is important to be honest' is a general principle about behaviour, while 'It is important that he be honest' or 'that he is honest' focuses on a specific person and often sounds more formal or demanding. At C1, use the that-clause with a subjunctive or should when you want to sound like a policy, a rule or a formal recommendation, and the infinitive when you want a smoother, more general statement.

有些形容词两种结构都可以接，语气却有明显差别。'It is important to be honest' 是关于行为的一般原则，而 'It is important that he be honest' 或 'that he is honest' 聚焦于某个具体的人，通常更正式、要求感更强。在 C1 水平，想表达规定、规则或正式建议时，用带虚拟语气或 should 的 that 从句；想表达更顺畅的一般性陈述时，用不定式。

More pairs: same idea, different choice更多对比句组

I was surprised to see him at the party. 我在聚会上看到他很惊讶。
Surprise at a direct, personal experience of seeing him.对亲眼看到他这一亲身经历感到惊讶。
I was surprised that he came to the party. 他来参加聚会，我很惊讶。
Surprise at the fact itself, with some distance; I might have only heard about it.对事实本身感到惊讶，带有一定距离感；我也许只是听说的。
I was surprised at his coming to the party. 我对他来参加聚会感到惊讶。
Formal and noun-like; focuses on the event as a topic of reaction.较正式、偏名词化；把这件事当作反应的对象来强调。
She is certain to win the election. 她肯定会赢得选举。
The speaker predicts the outcome; certainty belongs to the speaker.说话人预测结果；确定性来自说话人。
She is certain that she will win the election. 她确信自己会赢得选举。
The certainty belongs to her; it describes her belief, which may be wrong.确定性属于她本人；描述她的信念，而这种信念可能是错的。
She is certain of winning the election. 她对赢得选举很有把握。
Her confidence about the result, in a compact and slightly formal form.她对结果的把握，形式简洁，略显正式。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ This problem is difficult to solve it.
✓ This problem is difficult to solve.
With adjectives like difficult, easy or hard, the subject is already the object of the verb, so adding an object pronoun duplicates it. Learners often add it because the Chinese sentence naturally repeats the object.
在 difficult、easy、hard 这类形容词后，主语本身已是动词的宾语，再加宾语代词就重复了。学习者常因为中文句子自然会重复宾语而误加。
✗
✓ I'm interested in learning about history.
Interested takes the preposition in followed by -ing for a general interest. The to-infinitive form 'interested to learn' means 'I would be curious to find out', which usually refers to a specific piece of news.
interested 表示一般兴趣时用介词 in 加 -ing。'interested to learn' 的意思是“很想知道”，通常指某条具体消息。
✗
✓ I'm afraid of making mistakes in the exam, so I checked carefully.
The learner means fear of a result, not unwillingness to act. Afraid to implies refusing to perform the action, which does not fit a sentence about checking carefully.
学习者想表达的是怕出现某个后果，而不是不敢做某事。afraid to 暗示不敢去做，这与“仔细检查”的语境不符。
✗
✓ I'm sure that the plan will work.
Sure does not take a person with to before the that-clause. Learners sometimes build a pattern from the Chinese structure that places the person first. If you need a person, use a different adjective or phrase such as 'I assured him that'.
sure 后不能在 that 从句前用 to 加人称。学习者有时受中文把人放在前面的结构影响而造出这种句型。如果需要带上这个人，应换用别的表达，如 'I assured him that'。
✗
✓ She is used to getting up early.
In be used to, the word to is a preposition, so it needs -ing or a noun. Learners confuse it with 'used to get up', which describes a past habit.
在 be used to 中，to 是介词，后面要接 -ing 或名词。学习者容易把它与表示过去习惯的 'used to get up' 混淆。
✗
✓ It is important that he be honest.
After a that-clause with important or essential, use the base form of the verb or should plus the base form, or an ordinary tense in less formal English. Mixing to-infinitive into the clause is never correct.
在 important、essential 之后的 that 从句里，用动词原形或 should 加原形，非正式英语中也可用普通时态。在从句中混入 to 不定式是不对的。
