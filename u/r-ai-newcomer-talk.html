Will AI Take My Job? AI 会抢走我的工作吗

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Good evening, everyone. When I was asked to speak tonight, the organisers told me that the question you ask most often is a simple one: will AI take my job? I want to give you an honest answer, which is that nobody knows for certain, and anyone who sounds completely sure is probably selling something.

大家晚上好。受邀今晚来讲时，主办方告诉我，你们问得最多的问题很简单：AI 会抢走我的工作吗？我想给一个诚实的回答：谁也说不准，而听上去十拿九稳的人，多半是在推销什么。

What we can say is this. The tools are very good at tasks that follow a pattern: drafting routine emails, summarising long documents, putting together the first version of a report. They are much weaker at the parts of a job that involve judgement, relationships and responsibility. Someone still has to make the decision, take responsibility when it goes wrong, and answer for it to a customer or a manager.

能说的是这些。这些工具很擅长有规律的活：起草日常邮件、总结长文件、拼出报告的初稿。工作里涉及判断、人际关系和担责的部分，它们就弱得多。最后拍板的、出了错担责的、向客户或经理交代的，仍然得是人。

For newcomers, I think this changes the advice in two ways. First, learn the tools now. Many of you spend hours polishing your English in emails; a good assistant can take some of that work away, and you can put the time into relationships at work. Second, invest in the human parts. The ability to have a word with a worried client, or to notice that a number looks wrong and check it against the source, is becoming more valuable, not less.

对新移民来说，我认为这让建议有两点变化。第一，现在就学会用这些工具。你们很多人花几个小时在邮件里打磨英语；好的助手能替你分掉一部分，省下的时间可以用在职场关系上。第二，在「人」的那部分下功夫。能和一位着急的客户谈上几句，能发现某个数字不对劲并对照原始来源核一遍，这些能力的价值在上升，而不是下降。

You do not need to become a programmer. You need to make the effort to understand what these tools can and cannot do, and to keep your own judgement switched on. In my experience, the people who struggle are not the ones who use AI. They are the ones who trust it completely, and the ones who refuse to touch it.

你不需要变成程序员。你需要花点力气弄懂这些工具能做什么、不能做什么，并让自己的判断一直开着。在我看来，吃力的不是用 AI 的人，而是两种人：完全信它的，和碰都不肯碰它的。

Take the information you hear tonight, including mine, with some caution. The tools change every few months. The habit of checking, though, will be useful for the rest of your career. Thank you.

今晚听到的信息，包括我说的，都请带点保留地听。这些工具几个月就变一次，但核对的习惯，会让你受用一整个职业生涯。谢谢大家。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

I want to give you an honest answer, which is that nobody knows for certain, and anyone who sounds completely sure is probably selling something.
an honest answer takes an because honest starts with a vowel sound (the h is silent).
honest 的 h 不发音，以元音开头，所以用 an。
The ability to have a word with a worried client, or to notice that a number looks wrong and check it against the source, is becoming more valuable, not less.
The subject is long (The ability… source); the verb is becoming agrees with ability. have a word with sb = talk briefly, often about a problem.
主语很长（The ability… source），谓语 is becoming 和 ability 一致。have a word with sb＝和人简短谈一下，常指谈问题。
