Backyard Counts Show Birds Arriving Earlier 后院数鸟：鸟儿来得更早了

🧠 Learn the 5 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 5 个新词

Read a news report on a volunteer science project and use the noun forms that make news English sound careful and precise. 读一则关于志愿者科学项目的新闻，学会让新闻英语显得严谨精确的名词形式。

Read and listen读与听

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Every spring for the last twenty-two years, a retired school teacher in a small Prairie town has written down the date she sees the first robin in her backyard. Her notebooks, along with records from thousands of other volunteers, are now part of a citizen science project that suggests some birds are arriving earlier than they used to.

过去二十二年的每个春天，草原地区一个小镇上的一位退休教师都会记下她在后院看到第一只知更鸟的日期。她的笔记本，连同成千上万其他志愿者的记录，如今都成了一个公民科学项目的一部分。这个项目显示，有些鸟比过去来得早了。

The project collects simple observations: which species, where and when. On its own, a single backyard means very little. Put together, the data covers a whole continent and several decades of springs, something no university team could afford to collect by itself.

项目收集的是简单的观察记录：什么鸟、在哪里、什么时候。单看一个后院说明不了什么。汇总起来，这些数据覆盖整个大陆、几十个春天，这是任何大学团队靠自己都负担不起的采集量。

Researchers say the reliability of amateur records was once a serious concern. Some volunteers confuse similar species, and people tend to report more birds on sunny weekends. To deal with this, the project checks unusual reports, compares them with nearby sightings and gives less weight to observers with few records. "The improvement over the last decade has been huge," said one of the scientists who analyses the data. "We now have real confidence in the overall pattern."

研究者说，业余记录的可靠性曾经是个大问题。有些志愿者会把相似的鸟种搞混，人们也往往在晴朗的周末报告更多的鸟。为了应对，项目会核查异常记录，与附近的观察对比，对记录很少的观察者给较低的权重。「过去十年的改进非常大，」一位分析这些数据的科学家说，「现在我们对总体趋势真的有信心了。」

The findings are cautious. Not every species is arriving earlier, and the researchers are careful about the causes. Warmer springs are the leading explanation, but they have not ruled out other factors, such as changes in where birds stop to feed on the way north.

研究结论是谨慎的。并不是每种鸟都提前到了，研究者对原因也很小心。较暖的春天是最主要的解释，但他们并没有排除其他因素，比如鸟在北飞途中停歇觅食的地点变了。

Organisers say the project has another benefit: awareness. People who count birds start to notice the world around them, and many say their expectations of what an ordinary backyard holds have changed completely. Anyone can join. The only requirement is a willingness to look out of the window and write down what you see.

组织者说这个项目还有另一个好处：意识。数鸟的人开始留意身边的世界，很多人说，他们对一个普通后院里有什么的预期完全变了。谁都可以加入，唯一的要求是愿意看看窗外，把看到的记下来。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

Researchers say the reliability of amateur records was once a serious concern.
reliable → reliability: the noun lets the sentence talk about a quality as a thing. was once: true in the past, no longer the main worry.
reliable 变 reliability：用名词把「可靠」当成一个话题来谈。was once：过去如此，现在不再是主要担忧。
The only requirement is a willingness to look out of the window and write down what you see.
a willingness to do: the noun keeps the infinitive of its adjective (willing to do). what you see is a noun clause, the thing you write down.
a willingness to do：名词保留形容词 willing to do 的不定式。what you see 是名词从句，作 write down 的宾语。
