Reporting with nouns: the claim that, his refusal to 用名词转述：the claim that、his refusal to

Use reporting nouns such as 'the claim that' and 'his refusal to' to report statements, decisions, and actions with precision and an appropriate level of formality. 能够使用 the claim that 和 his refusal to 等转述名词，准确、得体地概述他人的陈述、决定和行为，并控制表达的正式程度。

The manager claimed that the figures were accurate. 经理声称这些数字是准确的。
A direct reporting verb emphasizes the manager's act of making the claim.直接使用转述动词，强调经理作出这一声称的行为。
The manager's claim that the figures were accurate was questioned. 经理关于这些数字准确的说法受到了质疑。
The reporting noun turns the statement into a topic that can be evaluated.转述名词把这一说法变成可以进一步评价和讨论的对象。
It was claimed by the manager that the figures were accurate. 有人声称这些数字是准确的，而这一声称来自经理。
The passive reporting construction foregrounds the reported content rather than the speaker.被动转述结构突出被报告的内容，而不是说话者本人。

📏 The rule规则

📏 The rule in one line规则一句话
Choose a reporting noun when you want to package a statement, attitude, decision, or action as a concept, especially when the noun makes the source, degree of certainty, or evaluation more important than the original verb.当你希望把陈述、态度、决定或行为概括为一个概念时，选择转述名词结构；尤其当信息来源、确定程度或评价比原来的动词更重要时，这种表达更合适。

📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Open the full explanation: 6 points, 22 examples展开细讲：6 段讲解、22 个例句

Choose a reporting noun when the content needs to function as the subject or object of a longer sentence. Instead of repeatedly using verbs such as claim, refuse, or suggest, you can write 'The claim that the system is unreliable has caused concern.' This is particularly useful in academic writing, reports, and formal discussions.

当一项陈述需要充当长句的主语或宾语时，可以选择转述名词，而不是反复使用 claim、refuse 或 suggest 等动词。例如，The claim that the system is unreliable has caused concern. 这种结构特别适合学术写作、报告和正式讨论。

Use a noun followed by that when the content is a complete proposition with its own subject and finite verb. The noun identifies the type of information being reported, while the that-clause supplies its content. Do not replace that with an infinitive when the reported content needs its own tense, subject, or modal verb.

当转述内容是一个包含独立主语和限定动词的完整命题时，使用名词加 that 从句。名词说明信息的性质，that 从句补充具体内容。如果转述内容需要保留自己的时态、主语或情态动词，就不要随意改成不定式。

Use a possessive or determiner followed by a noun and an infinitive when you want to identify whose action, decision, or intention is involved. For example, 'their refusal to cooperate' focuses on the people responsible for the refusal. This is often more compact and formal than saying 'they refused to cooperate.'

当你希望明确指出是谁采取了某种行动、作出了决定或表达了意愿时，可以使用物主限定词或其他限定词加名词和不定式。例如，their refusal to cooperate 强调拒绝合作的主体。这通常比直接说 they refused to cooperate 更简洁、正式。

Choose the reporting noun carefully because different nouns express different attitudes toward the reported information. A claim may be disputed, an allegation may imply an accusation that has not been established, and an admission suggests that someone has acknowledged something. Replacing one noun with another can change the writer's stance even when the that-clause remains identical.

选择转述名词时要特别注意其评价色彩。claim 可以表示有争议的主张，allegation 通常暗示尚未证实的指控，而 admission 表示某人承认了某件事。即使 that 从句完全相同，替换名词也可能改变作者的立场。

Use reporting nouns to avoid making an unsupported statement sound like an established fact. Compare 'The company is losing money' with 'The claim that the company is losing money has not been verified.' The second version clearly attributes the information to a claim rather than presenting it as the writer's own conclusion.

使用转述名词还可以避免把未经证实的信息写成既定事实。比较 The company is losing money 和 The claim that the company is losing money has not been verified. 后者明确说明这只是一个主张，而不是作者自己确认的结论。

Be careful when choosing between a noun followed by that and a noun followed by an infinitive. Some nouns naturally introduce a complete reported proposition, while others describe an action that someone intends, attempts, or refuses to perform. Use 'the decision that the project should be postponed' for a proposition and 'the decision to postpone the project' for the action itself.

要注意区分名词加 that 从句和名词加不定式。有些名词适合引出完整的转述命题，另一些名词则描述某人打算、尝试或拒绝实施的动作。the decision that the project should be postponed 强调一个完整判断，而 the decision to postpone the project 强调推迟项目这一行动。

More pairs: same idea, different choice更多对比句组

They refused to release the documents. 他们拒绝公布这些文件。
The verb focuses directly on their act of refusing.动词直接强调他们拒绝公布文件的行为。
Their refusal to release the documents caused concern. 他们拒绝公布这些文件的行为引发了担忧。
The noun phrase presents the refusal as an event or issue that caused a consequence.名词短语把拒绝行为作为一个引发后果的事件或问题来呈现。
The documents were not released because they refused to do so. 由于他们拒绝公布，这些文件最终没有公开。
The clause emphasizes the consequence and gives the refusal as its reason.从句强调结果，并将拒绝行为作为原因。
She suggested that the deadline should be extended. 她建议延长截止日期。
The reporting verb emphasizes the act of making a suggestion.转述动词强调她提出建议这一行为。
Her suggestion that the deadline should be extended was accepted. 她提出的延长截止日期的建议得到了接受。
The noun phrase makes the suggestion itself the object of evaluation or acceptance.名词短语将建议本身作为评价或接受的对象。
The deadline was extended following her suggestion. 在她提出建议后，截止日期得到了延长。
The prepositional phrase presents the suggestion as background information.介词短语将建议作为背景信息，而重点放在实际结果上。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ The claim what the policy is unfair has attracted attention.
✓ The claim that the policy is unfair has attracted attention.
A reporting noun such as claim normally introduces a complete proposition with that, not what. What would introduce a different type of clause and does not fit this structure.
claim 等转述名词通常使用 that 引导完整陈述，而不是使用 what。what 会引出不同类型的从句，不符合这里的结构。
✗ His refusal of answer the question caused confusion.
✓ His refusal to answer the question caused confusion.
The noun refusal is followed by an infinitive when it identifies an action that someone declines to perform. The preposition of cannot directly introduce this infinitive.
refusal 后面接不定式，表示某人拒绝实施的动作。不能使用介词 of 直接引出不定式。
✗ Her claim that the company was cheated the customers was rejected.
✓ Her claim that the company had cheated the customers was rejected.
The that-clause needs a grammatically complete finite clause. In the incorrect version, was is inserted before cheated even though the intended meaning requires an active past perfect verb.
that 从句必须构成语法完整的限定从句。错误句在主动语态的 cheated 前误加了 was，正确表达应使用过去完成时 had cheated。
✗ The suggestion that to reduce costs should be considered.
✓ The suggestion that costs should be reduced should be considered.
The noun suggestion can introduce a that-clause with its own subject and finite verb. The original sentence incorrectly mixes a that-clause with an infinitive structure and also lacks a clear grammatical subject.
suggestion 后可以接包含独立主语和限定动词的 that 从句。错误句混用了 that 从句和不定式结构，同时缺少清晰的语法主语。
✗ Their decision that move abroad surprised us.
✓ Their decision to move abroad surprised us.
When decision refers to the action someone chooses to take, it normally takes an infinitive. A that-clause requires a complete proposition, such as 'that they should move abroad.'
decision 表示某人选择采取的行动时，通常接不定式。如果使用 that 从句，就需要完整的命题，例如 that they should move abroad。
✗ The allegation that he had stolen the money was confirmed as a fact.
✓ The allegation that he had stolen the money was investigated but not confirmed.
An allegation is an accusation, not proof. Treating the noun itself as confirmation confuses the existence of an accusation with the truth of its content.
allegation 表示指控，并不等于证据。把指控本身说成事实已经得到确认，混淆了指控存在与指控内容真实这两个概念。
