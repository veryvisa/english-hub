Narrative tenses and the past perfect 叙事时态与过去完成时

📘 In your coursebook: English File 5e · Upper-Intermediate · 3A “Flight plan”. Use the texts and recordings with your coursebook; this page adds new words, grammar and practice.教材定位：English File 5e · Upper-Intermediate · 3A《Flight plan》。课文与录音请配合教材学习，本页补充新词、语法与练习。

Tell a story in English so the listener always knows what happened first, what was already going on, and what had been going on for a while. 用英语讲故事时，让听的人始终知道哪件事先发生、哪件事当时正在进行、哪件事已经持续了一段时间。

📏 The rule规则

📏 The rule in one line规则一句话
The past simple moves the story forward; the past continuous sets the scene; the past perfect steps back to something earlier; the past perfect continuous shows how long something had been going on before the main event.一般过去时推进故事；过去进行时铺场景；过去完成时往回退一步讲更早的事；过去完成进行时说明主要事件之前某事已经持续了多久。

Chinese often relies on words like yijing (already) and zhiqian (before) and leaves the verb unchanged. English puts the order on the verb. Once you have chosen a moment in the past, anything earlier than that moment can take had done.

中文常靠「已经」「之前」交代先后，动词本身不变；英语把先后标在动词上。一旦选定了过去某个时刻，比它更早的事就可以用 had done。

You do not need the past perfect every time something is earlier. If the events are told in order, or a word like before or after already makes the order clear, the past simple is enough: I locked the door and left. Use had done when you jump back, or when the order would otherwise be confusing.

并不是只要更早就必须用过去完成时。按顺序讲，或者 before、after 已经交代了先后，用一般过去时就够了。往回跳的时候，或者不用就会弄不清先后的时候，才用 had done。

The past perfect changes meaning in pairs like these. When I arrived, the meeting started means it started as I walked in. When I arrived, the meeting had started means I was late. Good storytellers use this to show what someone missed.

过去完成时会改变意思。When I arrived, the meeting started 是我进门时会议才开始；When I arrived, the meeting had started 是我迟到了。会讲故事的人用这个区别交代「错过了什么」。

The past continuous is background, often interrupted by a past simple event: I was cooking when the power went out. Two background actions at the same time take while and two continuous verbs: While I was cooking, the kids were doing homework.

过去进行时是背景，常被一个一般过去时的事件打断。两个同时进行的背景动作用 while 加两个进行式。

The past perfect continuous explains a state at that moment by the activity that led to it: Her eyes were red. She had been crying. It also gives duration up to a past moment: I had been driving for six hours when I finally stopped.

过去完成进行时用之前持续的活动来解释当时的状态（她眼睛红了，之前一直在哭）。它也说明到过去某刻为止持续了多久。

In longer stories, switch to the past perfect for the first verb or two of a flashback, then return to the past simple; the listener stays in the earlier time without a string of had.

长故事里，回忆段落开头用一两个过去完成时，接着就回到一般过去时，听的人知道还在更早的时间里，不必一路 had 到底。

Job in the storyFormExample
Main events, in orderpast simpleShe opened the letter and sat down.
Background, scenepast continuousIt was raining and the bus was late.
Earlier than the story momentpast perfectShe had already paid the bill.
Duration up to the story momentpast perfect continuousShe had been waiting for an hour.
Interrupted actionpast continuous + past simpleI was cooking when the phone rang.

Examples例句

  • When we got to the airport, the flight had already closed. 我们到机场时，航班已经停止值机了。
    We were too late.我们来晚了。
  • When we got to the airport, the flight closed. 我们一到机场，航班就停止值机了。
    It closed at the moment we arrived.就在我们到的那一刻关闭。
  • I was walking the dog when I noticed smoke coming from a neighbour's roof. 我正遛狗的时候，看到邻居屋顶冒烟。
    Background interrupted by an event.背景被一个事件打断。
  • While my husband was talking to the officer, I was taking photos of the damage. 我丈夫跟警察说话的时候，我在拍损坏的照片。
    Two background actions at once.两个同时进行的背景动作。
  • She had never seen snow before she moved to Edmonton. 搬到埃德蒙顿之前，她从没见过雪。
    Experience up to a past point.截至过去某刻的经历。
  • By the time the ambulance arrived, a nurse who lived nearby had stopped the bleeding. 救护车到的时候，住在附近的一位护士已经把血止住了。
    By the time marks the story moment.by the time 标出故事时刻。
  • I had been working there for three years when the company was sold. 公司被卖掉时，我在那里已经干了三年。
    Duration up to a past event.截至过去事件的持续时间。
  • The floor was wet. Someone had been mopping it. 地板是湿的，有人刚拖过。
    An activity explains the state.用之前的活动解释当时的状态。
  • He realised he had left his passport in the hotel safe. 他意识到护照落在酒店保险箱里了。
    A verb of thinking, then an earlier event.思维动词后接更早的事。
  • I locked the door, put the keys in my bag and walked to the station. 我锁上门，把钥匙放进包里，走去车站。
    Events in order: no past perfect needed.按顺序讲，不需要过去完成时。
  • We had only been living there a month when the pipes burst. 我们才在那里住了一个月，水管就爆了。
    Short duration before a sudden event.突发事件前只持续了很短时间。
  • It was the first time I had ever spoken at a public meeting. 那是我第一次在公开会议上发言。
    It was the first time takes the past perfect.It was the first time 后接过去完成时。
  • After she had read the contract twice, she signed it. 她把合同读了两遍之后才签。
    Optional past perfect stresses the order.这里可用可不用，用了更强调先后。

More pairs: same idea, different choice更多对比句组

I didn't recognise him. He grew a beard. 我没认出他。他留起了胡子。
Confusing: sounds like the beard came later.让人糊涂：听起来像后来才留的。
I didn't recognise him. He had grown a beard. 我没认出他，他留了胡子。
Clear: the beard was already there.清楚：胡子早就有了。
I didn't recognise him at first because he had been growing a beard for months. 一开始没认出他，因为他已经留了几个月胡子。
Adds the length of the process.加上了过程的长度。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ When I arrived, he already left.
✓ When I arrived, he had already left.
He left before the moment of arrival, and already signals that order.
他在我到之前就走了，already 正说明了先后。
✗ I was knowing her for years when we became business partners.
✓ I had known her for years when we became business partners.
know is a state verb; duration before a past event needs the past perfect.
know 是状态动词；截至过去事件的持续用过去完成时。
✗ Yesterday I had gone to the bank and then I had bought groceries.
✓ Yesterday I went to the bank and then I bought groceries.
Events in order take the past simple; had is not a general past marker.
按顺序发生的事用一般过去时；had 不是泛用的「过去」标记。
✗ While I cooked, the phone was ringing.
✓ While I was cooking, the phone rang.
The long action is the background; the short one is the event.
长的动作是背景，短的才是事件。
✗ It was the first time I visited a Canadian hospital.
✓ It was the first time I had visited a Canadian hospital.
It was the first time looks back over experience up to that moment.
It was the first time 回看截至那时的经历。
