Modals of deduction: must, might, can't have 推测情态

📘 In your coursebook: English File 5e · Upper-Intermediate · 7A “You be the judge” · English File 5e · Advanced · 4B “Sound effects”. Use the texts and recordings with your coursebook; this page adds new words, grammar and practice.教材定位：English File 5e · Upper-Intermediate · 7A《You be the judge》；English File 5e · Advanced · 4B《Sound effects》。课文与录音请配合教材学习，本页补充新词、语法与练习。

Show how sure you are about a guess, present or past, with must, might, could, can't and their perfect forms, instead of adding maybe to every sentence. 用 must、might、could、can't 及其完成形式表达你对推测有多大把握（现在或过去），而不是每句都加 maybe。

He must be at work. 他肯定在上班。
About ninety per cent sure: there is a reason.大约九成把握，有依据。
He might be at work. 他可能在上班。
About fifty per cent: one possibility among others.大约五成，一种可能。
He can't be at work. 他不可能在上班。
Almost sure he is not: there is a reason.几乎肯定不在，有依据。
Maybe he is at work. 也许他在上班。
Correct but flat; gives no sense of the evidence.没错但平淡，听不出依据。

📏 The rule规则

📏 The rule in one line规则一句话
must means you are almost sure it is true, can't means almost sure it is not, and might, may and could mean it is possible. Add have + past participle to make the same guesses about the past.must 表示几乎肯定是，can't 表示几乎肯定不是，might、may、could 表示有可能。加 have 加过去分词，就是对过去的同样推测。

Chinese speakers often reach for maybe and I think, which works but sounds flat and repetitive. A modal carries both the guess and the degree of certainty in one word: She must be at the gym is stronger than Maybe she is at the gym, and the listener hears you have a reason.

华人常用 maybe 和 I think，能用但平淡重复。情态动词一个词就同时表达推测和把握程度：She must be at the gym 比 Maybe she is at the gym 更有把握，听的人知道你有依据。

The opposite of deductive must is can't, not mustn't. He must be home; the lights are on becomes He can't be home; the car isn't there. mustn't is a rule (do not do it), not a deduction.

推测意义上 must 的反义是 can't，不是 mustn't。mustn't 是禁止，不是推测。

For the past, keep the modal and change the verb: must have left, might have forgotten, can't have seen or couldn't have seen. must have and can't have are conclusions from evidence; might have and could have are open possibilities.

讲过去，情态动词不变，动词改成 have 加过去分词。must have、can't have 是根据证据下的结论；might have、could have 是还开着的可能性。

could have done has a second meaning: something was possible but did not happen. You could have called me! is a complaint, not a guess. Context decides; in a story about a near miss, We could have been killed means we were not.

could have done 还有第二个意思：本来可以、但没发生。You could have called me! 是抱怨，不是推测。在讲险些出事的故事里，We could have been killed 意思是并没有出事。

Use the continuous to guess about an activity: She must be driving; she isn't answering. They might have been sleeping when we knocked. In formal writing, may is common for possibility and sounds cautious: The delay may have been caused by the storm.

推测一个正在进行的活动用进行式。正式写作里 may 常用来表示可能，语气谨慎。

Examples例句

  • You've been travelling all day. You must be exhausted. 你奔波了一整天，一定累坏了。
    A confident guess from evidence.有依据的肯定推测。
  • That can't be Kevin's car. He sold it last month. 那不可能是凯文的车，他上个月卖了。
    Almost sure it is not true.几乎肯定不是。
  • The package might be at the front desk. Have you checked? 包裹可能在前台，你看过没有？
    An open possibility.一种可能。
  • She's not picking up. She could be in a meeting. 她不接电话，可能在开会。
    Possible explanation.可能的解释。
  • The ground is wet. It must have rained overnight. 地是湿的，夜里肯定下过雨。
    A past conclusion from evidence.根据证据对过去下结论。
  • I can't find my umbrella. I might have left it on the train. 我找不到伞了，可能落在火车上了。
    Past possibility.过去的可能。
  • He can't have read the email; he's asking the same question again. 他不可能看过那封邮件，又在问同样的问题。
    Past: almost sure it did not happen.过去：几乎肯定没发生。
  • They couldn't have known about the change; nobody told them. 他们不可能知道这个变动，没人告诉他们。
    Same meaning as can't have.和 can't have 意思一样。
  • The delay may have been caused by a signal problem. 延误可能是信号问题造成的。
    Cautious, formal possibility.谨慎、正式的可能。
  • You could have told me you were coming! I'd have cooked. 你要来怎么不早说！我会做饭的。
    A complaint: it was possible but did not happen.抱怨：本来可以但没做。
  • She must have been waiting for ages; the coffee in front of her is cold. 她肯定等了很久了，面前的咖啡都凉了。
    Past continuous deduction.对过去持续活动的推测。
  • The kids are very quiet. They must be watching something. 孩子们很安静，肯定在看什么东西。
    Present continuous deduction.对正在进行的活动的推测。
  • That was close. We could have hit that deer. 好险，我们差点撞上那头鹿。
    A near miss: it did not happen.险些发生。
  • It might not have been the same man; it was dark. 可能不是同一个人，当时天黑。
    Negative possibility in the past.过去的否定可能。

More pairs: same idea, different choice更多对比句组

She must have missed the bus. 她肯定没赶上公交。
A conclusion about the past.对过去的结论。
She might have missed the bus. 她可能没赶上公交。
A possibility about the past.对过去的可能推测。
She could have taken the bus, but she walked. 她本可以坐公交，但她走路去了。
Possible but not chosen: not a guess.本可以但没选：不是推测。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ He mustn't be home; his car is gone.
✓ He can't be home; his car is gone.
The negative of a confident guess is can't; mustn't is a prohibition.
肯定推测的否定是 can't；mustn't 是禁止。
✗ She must forget the meeting yesterday.
✓ She must have forgotten the meeting yesterday.
A guess about the past needs have + past participle.
对过去的推测要用 have 加过去分词。
✗ Maybe he might be sick.
✓ He might be sick. / Maybe he is sick.
maybe and might say the same thing; use one.
maybe 和 might 意思重复，用一个。
✗ It can't have been rained last night.
✓ It can't have rained last night.
rain is not passive here; use have + past participle only.
这里 rain 不是被动，只用 have 加过去分词。
✗ You must had been tired.
✓ You must have been tired.
After a modal, always the base form have.
情态动词后永远用原形 have。
