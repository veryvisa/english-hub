Alternatives to if: provided, unless, supposing, but for if 的替代说法

📘 In your coursebook: English File 5e · Advanced · 6B “Can’t live without it”. Use the texts and recordings with your coursebook; this page adds new words, grammar and practice.教材定位：English File 5e · Advanced · 6B《Can’t live without it》。课文与录音请配合教材学习，本页补充新词、语法与练习。

Choose precisely among provided, unless, supposing and but for instead of defaulting to if, and use each with the right tense and register. 能在 provided、unless、supposing、but for 之间准确选择，而不是一律用 if，并配上正确的时态和语体。

We'll hire him if he accepts the salary. 如果他接受这个薪水，我们就雇他。
A neutral statement of a possibility, with no particular attitude to it.中性地陈述一种可能，不带任何态度。
We'll hire him provided he accepts the salary. 只要他接受这个薪水，我们就雇他。
The salary is a firm term; acceptance is a requirement we are setting.薪水是硬性条款，接受是我们设定的前提。
We'll hire him unless he rejects the salary. 除非他拒绝这个薪水，否则我们就雇他。
Hiring is the default; refusal is the only thing that stops it.雇用是默认结果，只有拒绝才会让它作罢。
Supposing he rejects the salary, who else could we hire? 假如他拒绝这个薪水，我们还能雇谁？
We are imagining a scenario in order to test a plan.我们在设想一种情形，用来检验计划。

📏 The rule in one line规则一句话
These words each add a meaning that a plain if does not carry: provided limits the condition, unless names the only exception, supposing invites imagination, and but for removes a cause from the past or present.这几个词各自带着 if 没有的意思：provided 强调限定条件，unless 指出唯一例外，supposing 邀请对方设想，but for 则是把某个原因从过去或现在拿掉。

📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Choose provided (that) when the condition is something you want to happen and you are really setting a term. 'We'll sign provided the price stays fixed' sounds like a negotiated limit, while 'if the price stays fixed' merely states a possibility. Because of this, provided fits contracts, permissions and promises, and it feels odd when the condition is unwelcome: 'provided it rains' sounds strange unless you actually want rain.

当条件是你希望发生、并且真的在设定条款时，用 provided (that)。'We'll sign provided the price stays fixed' 听起来是谈判里划下的底线，而 if 只是陈述一种可能。因此 provided 适合合同、许可和承诺；条件本身不受欢迎时它就显得别扭，比如除非你真想下雨，否则说 'provided it rains' 很奇怪。

Choose unless when you want to present one single exception to an otherwise fixed plan. Unless means roughly 'if not', but it does not work well in every case. It is natural for 'We'll go unless it rains', yet it is wrong for a result that depends on a negative event you are emotionally reacting to: 'I'll be upset unless he comes' is fine, but 'I'll be happy unless he comes' is not what you mean, so use 'if he doesn't come' there.

当你想在一个原本确定的计划里指出唯一的例外时，用 unless。它大致相当于 if not，但不能处处互换。'We'll go unless it rains' 很自然；但当结果是你对某个否定事件的情绪反应时就不行了，例如想表达'他不来我就高兴'，要用 'I'll be happy if he doesn't come'，因为 unless 只能表示'除非例外出现，否则结果成立'。

Choose supposing (or suppose) when you want the listener to picture a scenario, usually to test a plan or an idea. It is often a question or a challenge: 'Supposing they refuse, what then?' Unlike if, it carries no commitment to likelihood and often opens an informal or discursive turn, so it suits brainstorming and objections more than formal writing, where 'in the event that' or 'should they refuse' is safer.

当你想让对方在脑中设想一个场景、通常是为了检验计划或想法时，用 supposing（或 suppose）。它常常是问句或质疑：'Supposing they refuse, what then?' 与 if 不同，它不表示可能性高低，常引出一个口语化、讨论式的转折，所以适合头脑风暴和提出反对意见，正式书面语里用 'in the event that' 或 'should they refuse' 更稳妥。

Choose but for when a noun phrase stands for the cause that did not or does not exist, so the result would be different. 'But for your help, we would have failed' means 'if it had not been for your help'. It is formal, takes a noun or -ing form rather than a clause, and is nearly always paired with a would-type main clause, so it fits thank-you speeches and reports that credit a specific factor.

当一个名词短语代表那个没有发生或并不存在的原因、从而结果会不同时，用 but for。'But for your help, we would have failed' 等于 'if it had not been for your help'。它很正式，后面接名词或 -ing 形式而不是从句，主句几乎总是 would 一类的结构，所以适合致谢和强调某个具体因素的报告。

Mind the tense rules, because that is where mistakes show. Provided, unless and supposing all use a present tense for future meaning in real conditions: 'unless he calls', not 'unless he will call'. For unreal situations, provided and supposing can take past forms ('Supposing you had known'), while unless is mostly kept for real or open conditions, so in a counterfactual about the past, 'if … had not' or but for is the better choice.

注意时态规则，错误往往出在这里。在真实条件句里，provided、unless、supposing 表示将来时都用一般现在时：'unless he calls'，不能说 'unless he will call'。非真实情形下，provided 和 supposing 可以用过去形式（'Supposing you had known'），而 unless 主要用于真实或开放的条件，因此对过去的反事实，用 'if … had not' 或 but for 更合适。

Examples例句

  • You can borrow the car provided you bring it back by six. 只要你六点前把车开回来，就可以借。
  • We'll proceed with the merger provided the regulators approve it. 只要监管机构批准，我们就推进合并。
  • Providing that the weather holds, the match will start on time. 只要天气不变，比赛将准时开始。
  • She agreed to speak provided her name was kept out of the report. 她同意发言，条件是报告中不出现她的名字。
  • Employees may work remotely provided that they are reachable during core hours. 只要核心时段能联系上，员工可以远程办公。
  • I'll lend you the money provided you pay it back within a year. 我可以借钱给你，条件是一年内还清。
  • The deal will go ahead unless either side withdraws. 除非任何一方退出，否则交易将继续进行。
  • Unless I hear from you by Friday, I'll assume you're not coming. 除非周五前收到你的消息，否则我就当你不来了。
  • Don't call me unless it's an emergency. 除非有紧急情况，否则别给我打电话。
  • The restaurant is always full unless there's a big match on. 除非有大型比赛，否则这家餐厅总是满座。
  • Unless prices fall soon, many families will struggle to cope. 除非价格很快下降，否则许多家庭将难以应对。
  • She won't sign unless the contract is revised. 除非合同修改，否则她不会签字。
  • Supposing the funding falls through, what's our fallback? 假如资金落空，我们的备用方案是什么？
  • Suppose he refuses to move, what would you do then? 假设他不肯搬走，你会怎么办？
  • Supposing you had won the lottery, would you have quit your job? 假如你当时中了彩票，你会辞职吗？
  • Supposing we arrive late, will they still let us in? 万一我们迟到了，他们还会让我们进去吗？
  • Supposing it turned out to be a mistake, who would take the blame? 假如这最终是个错误，谁来担责？
  • But for the storm, we would have arrived on schedule. 要不是那场风暴，我们本来能准时到达。
  • But for her quick thinking, the fire could have spread to the whole block. 要不是她反应快，大火可能已蔓延到整个街区。
  • But for the scholarship, he would never have been able to study abroad. 要不是有那笔奖学金，他根本不可能出国留学。
  • But for your advice, I might have made a serious mistake. 要不是你的建议，我可能已经犯了严重错误。
  • But for the traffic, the journey would take only twenty minutes. 要不是堵车，这段路程只需二十分钟。
  • But for the pandemic, the company would still be expanding. 要不是疫情，这家公司现在仍在扩张。
  • As long as you keep the receipt, you can return the item at any time. 只要你留着收据，随时都可以退货。

More pairs: same idea, different choice更多对比句组

If you hadn't helped, we would have lost the contract. 如果你当时没帮忙，我们就丢掉合同了。
A standard past counterfactual built on a full clause.以完整从句构成的标准过去反事实。
But for your help, we would have lost the contract. 要不是你帮忙，我们就丢掉合同了。
Formal and compact; the noun phrase credits one specific cause.正式而简洁，用名词短语把功劳归给一个具体原因。
Had it not been for your help, we would have lost the contract. 要不是你帮忙，我们就丢掉合同了。
Formal inverted form with the same meaning, usually with a clause after it.正式的倒装形式，意思相同，后面通常带从句。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ We will go out unless it will rain.
✓ We will go out unless it rains.
Unless introduces a real condition about the future, so it takes a present tense, never will.
unless 引导关于将来的真实条件，要用一般现在时，不能加 will。
✗ I'll be delighted unless he doesn't come.
✓ I'll be delighted if he doesn't come.
Unless already contains a negative meaning, so adding not creates a double negative and changes the sense. Use if … not when the negative event itself is the cause of the result.
unless 本身含有否定意义，再加 not 就成了双重否定、意思也变了；当否定事件本身就是结果的原因时，要用 if … not。
✗ But for he helped us, we would have failed.
✓ But for his help, we would have failed.
But for must be followed by a noun phrase or an -ing form, not by a full clause. For a clause, use if it had not been that or had it not been for.
but for 后面只能接名词短语或 -ing 形式，不能接完整从句；要接从句，请用 if it had not been that 或 had it not been for。
✗ Provided it will be sunny, we'll have the picnic.
✓ Provided it's sunny, we'll have the picnic.
Like other conditional words, provided takes a present tense for future meaning. Using will in the condition clause is a typical mistake.
和其他条件连词一样，provided 表示将来时用一般现在时；在条件从句里用 will 是常见错误。
✗ Provided it rains, the match will be cancelled.
✓ If it rains, the match will be cancelled.
Provided suggests that the condition is something wanted or required. Rain is an unwelcome possibility, so a neutral if is the natural choice.
provided 暗示条件是被期望或被要求的东西；下雨是不受欢迎的可能，所以用中性的 if 才自然。
✗ Supposing he would come, we would be happy.
✓ Supposing he comes, we'll be happy.
After supposing, the tense follows the same logic as if. Use the present for an open possibility; save would for a clearly hypothetical situation.
supposing 之后的时态逻辑与 if 相同：开放的可能性用一般现在时，只有明显的假设情形才用 would。
