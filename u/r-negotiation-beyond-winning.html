Why Winning Is the Wrong Goal 为什么「赢」是错误的目标

🧠 Learn the 2 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 2 个新词

Treating negotiation as a contest to be won damages relationships, whereas focusing on each side’s real interests tends to produce better outcomes. 把谈判当作一场要赢的较量会损害关系，而关注双方的真实利益往往能带来更好的结果。

We are taught to think of negotiation as a contest, with a winner who walks away satisfied and a loser who does not. That picture is seductive, but it is misleading. Yet the contest metaphor persists, partly because it flatters our competitive instincts. In most real disputes, the parties will have to deal with each other again, and a victory that humiliates the other side tends to be expensive in the long run.

我们从小被教导，把谈判看作一场较量：赢家满意离场，输家则不然。这种图景很诱人，却具有误导性。然而较量这一比喻依然流行，部分原因是它迎合了我们的竞争本能。在大多数现实纠纷中，各方还得再打交道，而让对方颜面扫地的胜利，长远看往往代价高昂。

Consider what happens when someone refuses to concede even a minor point. The other side reads this as a signal of contempt, and the conversation quickly stops being about the issue and starts being about pride. While firmness is sometimes necessary, rigidity is rarely a strategy; it is usually a reflex dressed up as principle.

想想看，当有人连一个小问题都不肯让步时会发生什么。对方会把这解读为轻蔑的信号，对话很快就不再围绕问题本身，而变成围绕面子。虽然在某些时候坚定是必要的，但僵硬很少是一种策略；它通常只是披着原则外衣的条件反射。

A better question than ‘How do I win?’ is ‘What does each side actually need?’ Positions are what people announce; interests are what they cannot do without. Two neighbours quarrelling over a fence may, on closer inspection, simply want privacy and a fair share of the cost, and once that is clear, common ground appears almost by itself.

比「我怎样才能赢」更好的问题是「双方究竟需要什么」。立场是人们公开宣布的东西，利益则是他们不可或缺的东西。两位为篱笆争吵的邻居，细究之下也许只是想要隐私和公平分担费用，一旦这一点清楚了，共同点几乎会自己浮现。

None of this means that leverage is irrelevant. Those with more options are, by and large, in a stronger position, and pretending otherwise would be naive. But leverage is best used quietly. The negotiator who keeps reminding the other side of their weakness may win the point and lose the relationship.

这并不意味着筹码无关紧要。总体而言，选择更多的一方处境更强，假装不是这样未免天真。但筹码最好低调使用。总是提醒对方处于弱势的谈判者，也许赢了这一点，却输了这段关系。

So the next time you sit down to bargain, resist the urge to keep score. Aim instead for an outcome that both sides can live with, and judge success by whether you would be willing to negotiate with them again.

所以下次坐下来讨价还价时，克制住记分的冲动。转而追求双方都能接受的结果，并以你是否愿意再次与对方谈判来衡量成功。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

While firmness is sometimes necessary, rigidity is rarely a strategy; it is usually a reflex dressed up as principle.
The ‘While’ clause concedes a point before the main clause qualifies it, and the semicolon joins a second clause that restates the idea in sharper terms.
「While」从句先作让步，主句再加以限定，分号连接的第二个分句用更尖锐的措辞重申观点。
Positions are what people announce; interests are what they cannot do without.
Each ‘what’ clause acts as a noun phrase, and ‘do without’ ends with a preposition because the object is the word ‘what’ at the front.
每个「what」从句都相当于名词短语，「do without」以介词结尾，因为其宾语就是前面的「what」。
