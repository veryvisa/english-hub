Relative clauses with prepositions and quantifiers 带介词与数量词的定语从句

📘 In your coursebook: English File 5e · Advanced · 10A “On your marks, set, go!”. Use the texts and recordings with your coursebook; this page adds new words, grammar and practice.教材定位：English File 5e · Advanced · 10A《On your marks, set, go!》。课文与录音请配合教材学习，本页补充新词、语法与练习。

Place the preposition in a relative clause where the situation wants it, at the end in speech or before which and whom in formal writing, and use most of whom, some of which to pack two facts into one sentence. 根据场合安排定语从句里的介词：口语放句末，正式书面语放在 which、whom 前；并用 most of whom、some of which 把两件事装进一句话。

The landlord we spoke to was very helpful. 我们谈过的那位房东很热心。
Everyday speech.日常口语。
The landlord who we spoke to was very helpful. 我们谈过的那位房东很热心。
Still informal; who added.仍然口语，加了 who。
The landlord to whom we spoke was very helpful. 与我们交谈的那位房东很热心。
Formal writing.正式书面语。

📏 The rule规则

📏 The rule in one line规则一句话
The preposition that belongs to the verb has two homes: at the end of the clause (the person I spoke to), which is normal in speech, or before which/whom (the person to whom I spoke), which is formal. Quantifier + of which/whom adds a fact about part of a group.跟着动词的介词有两个位置：放在从句末尾（the person I spoke to），口语里很正常；或者放在 which/whom 前面（the person to whom I spoke），显得正式。数量词加 of which/whom，用来补充说明一组人或物中的一部分。

The preposition comes from the verb or adjective inside the clause: speak to, rely on, be worried about. Chinese learners often lose it altogether: the company I applied instead of the company I applied to. A quick test is to rebuild the full sentence: I applied to the company. If the preposition is there, it must also be in the relative clause.

介词来自从句里的动词或形容词：speak to、rely on、be worried about。很多人干脆把它丢了：the company I applied，而不是 the company I applied to。快速检验法：把完整句子还原出来，I applied to the company；原句有介词，定语从句里就必须有。

In everyday English the preposition goes at the end and the relative word is usually dropped: the landlord we deal with, the town she grew up in. This is not a mistake. The old school rule against ending a sentence with a preposition sounds stiff in speech and in most emails.

日常英语里介词放句末，关系词通常省掉：the landlord we deal with、the town she grew up in。这不是错。老派的「句末不放介词」规则，在口语和大多数邮件里听起来反而别扭。

In formal writing, such as a contract, a cover letter or a report, the preposition moves to the front, and then you must use which for things or whom for people, never that or who: the period during which you may cancel, the officer to whom the complaint was sent. Some verbs resist this, especially phrasal verbs: the problem we looked into, not into which we looked.

在合同、求职信、报告这类正式书面语里，介词提前，这时指物必须用 which，指人用 whom，不能用 that 或 who：the period during which you may cancel、the officer to whom the complaint was sent。有些动词不接受这种挪动，特别是短语动词：the problem we looked into，而不说 into which we looked。

Quantifier relatives join a group and a fact about part of it: We interviewed twelve people, most of whom had never used the service. The pattern is some / many / most / all / none / both / neither / one / two of + whom for people or which for things, and always after a comma. Use whose in the same way for possession: three neighbours, one of whose dogs barks all night.

数量词定语从句把「一群」和「其中一部分的情况」连起来：We interviewed twelve people, most of whom had never used the service。句型是 some/many/most/all/none/both/neither/one/two of 加 whom（指人）或 which（指物），前面一定有逗号。表所属时同样可用 whose。

These structures let one sentence do the work of two, which is why they appear so often in news and reports. In speech, people usually split them: We talked to twelve people. Most of them had never used it. Both are correct. Pick the formal one when you are writing for a reader who expects precision.

这些结构能用一句话做完两句话的事，所以在新闻和报告里特别常见。口语里人们一般拆开说：We talked to twelve people. Most of them had never used it。两种都对；给讲究精确的读者写东西时用正式那种。

Examples例句

  • That's the colleague I was telling you about. 那就是我跟你说过的那位同事。
    Everyday: preposition at the end.日常：介词在末尾。
  • Is this the bus stop you were waiting at? 你是在这个车站等车吗？
    The verb is wait at.动词是 wait at。
  • The town my parents grew up in no longer has a train station. 我父母长大的那个小镇已经没有火车站了。
    grow up in a place.grow up in 某地。
  • I finally found a dentist I can rely on. 我终于找到一个靠得住的牙医。
    rely on.rely on。
  • You have a ten-day period during which you may cancel without a fee. 您有十天的期限，期间可免费取消。
    Formal: preposition first.正式：介词提前。
  • The department to which the request was sent has not replied. 收到申请的那个部门还没回复。
    Formal, things: which.正式，指物用 which。
  • The supervisor with whom I discussed the schedule has since left. 跟我讨论过排班的那位主管后来离职了。
    Formal, people: whom.正式，指人用 whom。
  • We looked at eight apartments, most of which were too small. 我们看了八套公寓，大部分都太小。
    Part of a group of things.一组物中的一部分。
  • The class has twenty students, half of whom work full time. 这个班有二十个学生，一半人全职工作。
    Part of a group of people.一组人中的一部分。
  • She has two brothers, neither of whom lives in Canada. 她有两个兄弟，都不住在加拿大。
    neither of whom: zero out of two.两个都不。
  • The clinic sent three letters, none of which arrived. 诊所寄了三封信，一封都没到。
    none of which.一封都没有。
  • We met several families, one of whose children goes to our school. 我们认识了几户人家，其中一家的孩子在我们学校上学。
    whose for possession.whose 表所属。
  • This is the issue we need to look into before Friday. 这是周五前我们需要调查的问题。
    Phrasal verb: preposition stays at the end.短语动词：介词留在末尾。
  • The reason for which the claim was refused is explained in section 4. 理赔被拒的原因在第四节有说明。
    Very formal: written style.非常正式的书面体。

More pairs: same idea, different choice更多对比句组

We invited fifteen neighbours. Most of them came. 我们请了十五位邻居，大多数都来了。
Spoken: two sentences.口语：两句话。
We invited fifteen neighbours, most of whom came. 我们请了十五位邻居，其中大多数都来了。
Written: one sentence.书面：一句话。
We invited fifteen neighbours, and most came. 我们请了十五位邻居，大多数来了。
Neutral middle way.折中说法。
the window in which you can return it 可以退货的那段时间
Formal.正式。
the window you can return it in 可以退货的那段时间
Informal and natural.非正式，也很自然。
the window when you can return it 可以退货的那段时间
when for time, no preposition.用 when 表时间，不需要介词。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ This is the company I applied last month.
✓ This is the company I applied to last month.
apply to a company; the preposition stays.
apply to 某公司，介词不能丢。
✗ The officer to who I spoke was kind.
✓ The officer to whom I spoke was kind. / The officer I spoke to was kind.
After a preposition, use whom, or move the preposition to the end.
介词后用 whom，或者把介词放到末尾。
✗ The house in that we lived was sold.
✓ The house in which we lived was sold. / The house we lived in was sold.
that cannot follow a preposition.
介词后不能接 that。
✗ I have two sisters, both of them are teachers.
✓ I have two sisters, both of whom are teachers. / I have two sisters. Both of them are teachers.
A comma cannot join two full sentences; use whom or a full stop.
逗号不能连接两个完整句子，用 whom 或句号。
✗ The bed which I sleep in it is too soft.
✓ The bed I sleep in is too soft.
Do not repeat the object after the preposition.
介词后不要再重复宾语。
