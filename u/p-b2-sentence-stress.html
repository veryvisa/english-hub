Sentence stress and rhythm 句重音与节奏

Use the texts and recordings with your coursebook; this page adds new words, grammar and practice.教材定位：English File 5e · Upper-Intermediate · 1B《We may never know》；English File 5e · Upper-Intermediate · 3A《Flight plan》；English File 5e · Upper-Intermediate · 5B《I wish…》；English File 5e · Upper-Intermediate · 10B《Freedom of speech》。课文与录音请配合教材学习，本页补充新词、语法与练习。

Speak in English rhythm: stress the words that carry the message, squeeze the rest, and let one word in each phrase stand out most. 用英语的节奏说话：重读传达信息的词，压缩其余的词，每个短语里让一个词最突出。

B2 · CELPIP 7–8⏱ 25 minP07 Every syllable gets the same weight; no schwa每个音节一样重，没有弱读P10 Flat or wrong intonation; the important word is not stressed语调平，重点词不突出

📏 The rule规则

📏 The point in one line一句话要领
In an English sentence the stressed words come at roughly regular beats, however many small words lie between them; and in each phrase one stressed word, usually the last new piece of information, gets the main pitch movement.英语句子里重读的词大致按均匀的拍子出现，中间夹多少小词都一样；每个短语里有一个重读词承载主要的音高变化，通常是最后一个新信息。
  • Easy to miss易漏 ✗ I LEFT MY KEYS IN THE CAR. (every word equal) → ✓ I left my KEYS in the CAR.

CATS eat FISH. /ˈkæts iːt ˈfɪʃ/
Three beats. 三拍。
The CATS will have EATen the FISH. /ðə ˈkæts wɪl əv ˈiːtən ðə ˈfɪʃ/
Still three beats, same time. 仍是三拍，时间一样。
I NEED to CALL the BANK. /aɪ ˈniːd tə ˈkɔːl ðə ˈbæŋk/
to and the go into the gaps. to、the 塞进拍子间。
I left my KEYS in the CAR. /aɪ ˈleft maɪ ˈkiːz ɪn ðə ˈkɑː/
Main movement on CAR, the last new word. 主要音高变化在最后一个新信息 CAR 上。
Where did you LAST have them? /ˈweə dɪdʒu ˈlɑːst hæv ðəm/
them is old information, so the nucleus moves to LAST. them 是旧信息，核心重音挪到 LAST。
We've been WAITing for an HOUR. /wiv bɪn ˈweɪtɪŋ fər ən ˈaʊə/
Four small words between two beats. 两拍之间夹四个小词。
WHAT do you THINK of it? /ˈwɒt də ju ˈθɪŋk əv ɪt/
Question word and main verb take the beats. 疑问词和主要动词踩拍子。
I'd have TOLD you if I'd KNOWN. /aɪd əv ˈtəʊld ju ɪf aɪd ˈnəʊn/
Only two beats; the rest is grammar. 只有两拍，其余是语法。
She DIDn't SAY anything. /ʃi ˈdɪdənt ˈseɪ eniθɪŋ/
The negative auxiliary is stressed. 否定的助动词要重读。
Can you SEND me the FILE by FRIday? /kən ju ˈsend mi ðə ˈfaɪl baɪ ˈfraɪdeɪ/
Three beats; main movement on FRI. 三拍，核心在 FRI。
It's NOT as BAD as it LOOKS. /ɪts ˈnɒt əz ˈbæd əz ɪt ˈlʊks/
as is weak /əz/ both times. 两个 as 都弱读。
I'm SORry I'm LATE. The TRAFfic was TERrible. /aɪm ˈsɒri aɪm ˈleɪt ðə ˈtræfɪk wəz ˈterəbəl/
Two phrases, two nuclei: LATE and TER. 两个短语，两个核心重音。
The MEETing has been MOVED to the AFterNOON. /ðə ˈmiːtɪŋ həz bɪn ˈmuːvd tə ði ˌɑːftəˈnuːn/
has been shrinks to /həz bɪn/. has been 压缩。

should have sounds like /ʃədəv/. should have 读成 /ʃədəv/。
I'll be BACK in TEN MINutes. /aɪl bi ˈbæk ɪn ˈten ˈmɪnɪts/
Main movement on MIN. 核心在 MIN。

📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Open the full explanation: 5 points展开细讲：5 段讲解

Read these two aloud and time them: CATS eat FISH and The CATS will have EATen the FISH. A fluent speaker takes almost the same time for both, because both have three beats. The extra words are squeezed into the gaps. Mandarin keeps each syllable about the same length, so the second sentence comes out nearly twice as long and every word sounds equally important. That even pace is what English listeners describe as "flat" or "reading aloud".

两句读出来计时：流利的人几乎用一样的时间，因为都是三拍，多出来的词被挤进拍子之间。普通话每个音节长度差不多，第二句就长了将近一倍，每个词都一样重。英语听者说的「平」「像在念」就是这种匀速。

Which words get a beat? Content words: nouns, main verbs, adjectives, adverbs, negatives like not and never, and question words like what and why. Which do not? Grammar words: articles, pronouns, auxiliaries, prepositions and conjunctions. These go weak (see the weak-forms unit) and run together with their neighbours.

实词踩拍子：名词、主要动词、形容词、副词、not 和 never 这类否定词、what 和 why 这类疑问词。功能词不踩拍子：冠词、代词、助动词、介词、连词，它们弱读并和旁边的词连起来。

Not all beats are equal. In each phrase, one beat is the nucleus: the pitch falls or rises there, and it is the loudest. By default it is the last content word: I left my KEYS in the CAR. But if the last word is old information, the nucleus moves back: I've lost my keys. — Where did you LAST have them? Putting the nucleus on them would sound as if keys were a surprise.

拍子之间也有轻重。每个短语里有一个核心重音：音高在那里升或降，也最响。默认落在最后一个实词上；如果最后一个词是旧信息，核心重音就往前挪。

Rhythm is also how people listen. A listener with a full head of numbers and names catches the beats and fills in the rest. If every word has equal weight, they cannot tell what to keep, and they ask you to repeat even when each word was correct. And when you listen, aim for the beats: in I'd have told you if I'd known, the words you must catch are TOLD and KNOWN; everything else can be rebuilt from grammar.

节奏也是对方听的方式：听者抓住拍子，其余靠推。字字等重，他不知道该记哪个，即使每个词都对也会让你重复。反过来，你听的时候也盯拍子，其他的用语法补出来。

Practise with your hand. Tap once on each capital word at an even speed, and fit the small words in between without slowing the taps. Start with short phrases, then add words without adding time.

用手练：每个大写词敲一下，速度均匀，把小词塞进两次敲击之间、不放慢。先短语，再加词不加时间。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ I LEFT MY KEYS IN THE CAR. (every word equal)
✓ I left my KEYS in the CAR.
Equal weight hides the message and makes you sound as if you are reading.
字字等重淹没信息，听起来像在念稿。
✗ I've lost my keys. Where did you last have THEM?
✓ Where did you LAST have them?
Stressing old information sounds like a contrast you did not mean.
重读旧信息，听起来像在对比，并非本意。
✗ She didn't SAY anything (with didn't weak)
✓ She DIDn't SAY anything.
Negative auxiliaries keep their stress; a weak didn't can be heard as did.
否定助动词要重读，弱读可能被听成 did。
✗ Slowing down to give every small word its full form
✓ Keep the beats even and squeeze the small words
Slower is not clearer if the rhythm is lost.
节奏丢了，放慢也不会更清楚。
