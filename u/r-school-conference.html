Fifteen Minutes with the Teacher 和老师的十五分钟

🧠 Learn the 4 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 4 个新词

Follow a parent through a fifteen-minute meeting with a teacher and learn how questions, not demands, get you a plan. 跟着一位家长走完和老师的十五分钟会面，学会用提问而不是要求换来一个计划。

B2 · CELPIP 7–8⏱ 20 minV06 Register: too formal, too casual, or too blunt语域不合：太正式、太随意或太冲G13 Word order in indirect questions间接疑问句语序

📖 Key card知识卡
Ask about the child before the marks, bring up one concern, and leave with a plan and a date to follow up.先问孩子，再问分数；只提一个担忧；带着计划和跟进日期离开。

Read and listen读与听

280 words
Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Mr Zhao had fifteen minutes with his son's teacher, and he had planned every one of them. Leo was in Grade 5, his reading marks had dropped this term, and Mr Zhao wanted to know why. He had also decided not to start with the marks.

赵先生和儿子的老师只有十五分钟，每一分钟他都计划好了。利奥上五年级，这学期阅读成绩下滑了，赵先生想知道原因。他还打定主意，不从分数谈起。

"Thanks for meeting me, Ms Patel. Before we look at the report card, could you tell me how Leo is doing in class in general?" "Of course. He's kind, he works hard in maths, and he gets on well with the others. In reading he has fallen behind a little. He doesn't often ask for help, so I didn't notice until the last test."

「谢谢您见我，帕特尔老师。看成绩单之前，能不能先说说利奥在班上总体怎么样？」「当然。他很善良，数学很用功，和同学相处得很好。阅读方面他稍微落后了一点。他不太开口求助，所以直到上次测验我才发现。」

"That's what I wanted to bring up. At home he reads Chinese books every evening, but he avoids English ones. Is there anything we can do to help him catch up?" "Yes. Twenty minutes of English reading a night, with a book he chooses himself, would make a big difference. And if you ask him to tell you about it afterwards, even better."

「这正是我想提的。他在家每晚都读中文书，却躲着英文书。我们能做点什么帮他赶上来吗？」「能。每晚二十分钟英文阅读，书让他自己挑，效果会很明显。读完再让他讲给你听，就更好了。」

"Could you point out the kind of book that suits his level?" "I'll send a list home on Friday. Let's follow up in a month, by email or in person, and see how he's getting on." "That works. One more thing: is it okay if he asks you questions after class? I think he's afraid it's rude." "It's not rude at all. I'd love him to."

「您能指点一下适合他水平的书是哪一类吗？」「我周五把书单带回家给你。一个月后我们再跟进一下，发邮件或者面谈都行，看看他进展如何。」「好的。还有一件事：他下课后问您问题可以吗？我觉得他怕那样不礼貌。」「一点也不失礼，我很希望他来问。」

On the way out, Mr Zhao realised the meeting had gone better than he had feared. He had asked questions instead of making demands, and he had left with a plan and a date. The marks had hardly come up at all.

出门时，赵先生意识到这次会面比他担心的顺利得多。他是在提问，而不是提要求；离开时手里有了计划和日期。分数几乎都没怎么谈到。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

Before we look at the report card, could you tell me how Leo is doing in class in general?
could you tell me + statement order: how Leo is doing, not how is Leo doing.
could you tell me 后接陈述语序：how Leo is doing，不是 how is Leo doing。
He had asked questions instead of making demands, and he had left with a plan and a date.
Past perfect looks back from the moment he walks out; instead of + -ing.
过去完成时从他走出门那一刻往回看；instead of 后接 -ing。
