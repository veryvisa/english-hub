Stress that corrects and contrasts 对比与纠正重音

Move the main stress to the word you are correcting or contrasting, so that one sentence can carry several different meanings without extra words. 把主重音挪到你要纠正或对比的词上，同一句话不加词就能表达不同的意思。

B2 · CELPIP 7–8⏱ 25 minP10 Flat or wrong intonation; the important word is not stressed语调平，重点词不突出

📏 The rule规则

📏 The point in one line一句话要领
Stress the word that is new or different and leave the rest flat: I said TUESday, not Thursday. Whichever word gets the main stress tells the listener what you are correcting.重读新的或不同的那个词，其余放平。哪个词得到主重音，就告诉对方你在纠正什么。
  • Easy to miss易漏 ✗ Tuesday at ten. (correcting, but with the same flat rhythm) → ✓ TUESday at ten.

🗣 Say it跟读

Tap 🔊, listen, then say it aloud. Use ● to record yourself and compare.点 🔊 听，再开口跟读；点 ● 录下自己的声音对比（录音只在本机）。

I said TUESday, not Thursday. /aɪ sed ˈtjuːzdeɪ nɒt ˈθɜːzdeɪ/
Jump on TUES; keep not Thursday lower. TUES 上跳，后半句压低。
It's fifTEEN, not fifty. /ɪts fɪfˈtiːn nɒt ˈfɪfti/
Stress the part that differs: TEEN. 重读不同的那一部分：TEEN。
I wanted the BLUE one. /aɪ ˈwɒntɪd ðə ˈbluː wʌn/
one stays flat after BLUE. BLUE 之后的 one 放平。
I didn't say HE took it. /aɪ ˈdɪdənt seɪ ˈhiː tʊk ɪt/
Someone else took it. 是别人拿的。
I didn't say he TOOK it. /aɪ ˈdɪdənt seɪ hi ˈtʊk ɪt/
He borrowed it, perhaps. 也许是借的。
I didn't SAY he took it. /aɪ ˈdɪdənt ˈseɪ hi tʊk ɪt/
I only hinted it. 我只是暗示过。
It's not FOR him, it's FROM him. /ɪts nɒt ˈfɔː hɪm ɪts ˈfrɒm hɪm/
Prepositions take strong forms. 介词用强读式。
I DID lock the door. /aɪ ˈdɪd ˈlɒk ðə dɔː/
Stressed did means "I really did". 重读 did 表示「我真的锁了」。
We need the report by FRIday. /wi niːd ðə rɪˈpɔːt baɪ ˈfraɪdeɪ/
The point is the deadline. 重点是期限。
We need the rePORT by Friday. /wi niːd ðə rɪˈpɔːt baɪ ˈfraɪdeɪ/
The point is the report itself. 重点是报告本身。
Not the second floor. The THIRD floor. /nɒt ðə ˈsekənd flɔː ðə ˈθɜːd flɔː/
floor is flat both times. 两个 floor 都放平。
Is it Mrs Chen or MS Chen? /ɪz ɪt ˈmɪsɪz tʃen ɔː ˈmɪz tʃen/
The title is the point. 重点在称谓。
I don't want a SMALLER flat, I want a CHEAPer one. /aɪ dəʊnt wɒnt ə ˈsmɔːlə flæt aɪ wɒnt ə ˈtʃiːpə wʌn/
Two contrast words in one sentence. 一句里两个对比词。
You booked it for May? I asked for MARCH. /ju ˈbʊkt ɪt fə ˈmeɪ aɪ ˈɑːskt fə ˈmɑːtʃ/
Correct with stress, not volume of the whole sentence. 靠重音纠正，不是整句提高音量。
DO sit down. /ˈduː sɪt ˈdaʊn/
Stressed do makes an invitation warmer. 重读 do 让邀请更热情。
It's not the price, it's the TIMing. /ɪts nɒt ðə ˈpraɪs ɪts ðə ˈtaɪmɪŋ/
First contrast item stressed too, but lower. 第一个对比项也重读，但低一些。

📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Open the full explanation: 5 points展开细讲：5 段讲解

At a clinic desk the receptionist says, "So that's Thursday at ten." The patient answers, "Tuesday at ten," with the same flat rhythm as before. The receptionist hears agreement and books Thursday. In English the correction lives in the stress: "TUESday at ten." Repeating the right word is not enough; it has to stand out.

前台说「那就周四十点」，病人用原来那种平平的节奏回答「周二十点」，前台听成了同意，订了周四。英语里纠正靠重音：只重复对的词不够，它必须突出。

Mandarin shows contrast mostly with extra words, such as bú shì ... ér shì ("not ... but") or a well-placed jiù. English often does it with stress alone. I didn't say he took it has seven meanings depending on which word is stressed. If you rely only on extra words, your English sounds heavy; if you use neither words nor stress, the contrast disappears.

普通话多用词来表达对比。英语常常只靠重音。只靠加词，英语显得笨重；既不加词也不用重音，对比就没了。

How to do it: make the contrast word clearly louder and longer, with a big pitch jump, usually a fall from high. Then keep everything after it low and flat, even words that would normally be stressed. That flat tail is what makes the contrast obvious. I wanted the BLUE one has one almost silent after BLUE.

做法：对比词明显更响、更长，音高大跳，通常从高处降下来；之后的词都压低放平，连平时该重读的词也放平。正是这个平尾巴让对比显得清楚。

Contrast also hits words that are normally weak. Pronouns, auxiliaries and prepositions take the strong form when they are the point: It's not for HIM, it's from him. — I DID lock it. A stressed auxiliary like did or do is how English says "really, I insist".

平时弱读的词被对比时也会重读并用强读式。重读的 did、do 就是英语里「真的、我坚持」的说法。

In listening, the stressed word is your shortcut to the speaker's point. When a colleague says We need the report by FRIday, the deadline is the issue; We need the REPORT by Friday means the report itself was forgotten. Listen for which word jumps, and answer that word.

听力上，被重读的词就是对方要点的捷径。听哪个词跳起来，就回应那个词。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ Tuesday at ten. (correcting, but with the same flat rhythm)
✓ TUESday at ten.
Without the jump, a correction sounds like agreement.
没有音高跳跃，纠正听起来像同意。
✗ I want THE BLUE ONE. (stressing everything to sound firm)
✓ I want the BLUE one.
Stressing every word removes the contrast instead of strengthening it.
每个词都重读，反而没了对比。
✗ It's not for him, it's from him. (weak /fə/ and /frəm/)
✓ It's not FOR him, it's FROM him.
Contrast needs the strong forms.
对比要用强读式。
✗ No, no, not that, the other one is what I mean. (piling up words)
✓ No, the OTHer one.
One stressed word does the job of a long correction.
一个重读词就顶一长串纠正。

✍️ Use it用起来

Write or say your answer. It is saved only in this browser.写下或说出你的答案；只存在这个浏览器里。

Take one plain sentence, for example "My sister drove me to the airport on Monday." Record it five times, each time stressing a different word, and after each version say in one short phrase what you are correcting ("not my brother", "not the station"...). Then play one version to a friend without telling them which, and see if they pick the right correction.
取一个普通句子，录五遍，每遍重读不同的词，每遍之后用一个短语说出你在纠正什么；再放给朋友听，看对方能否选出对的纠正。
