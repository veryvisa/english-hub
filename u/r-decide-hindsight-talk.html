Good Decisions, Bad Outcomes 好决定，坏结果

🧠 Learn the 5 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 5 个新词

Read a short community talk about judging decisions fairly, and notice how the present perfect connects a past failure to the speaker's life now. 读一篇社区讲座讲稿，讲怎样公平地评判决定；注意现在完成时怎样把过去的失败和讲者现在的生活连起来。

📖 Key card知识卡
Judge a decision by what you knew at the time, not by how it turned out.评判一个决定，看的是你当时知道什么，而不是结果如何。

Read and listen读与听

312 words
Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Thank you all for coming. I want to start with a question. Have you ever made a decision that turned out badly and then blamed yourself for months? I have. Five years ago I left a stable job to start a small café, and two years later the café closed. For a long time I called that the worst decision of my life, and it took me years to get over it.

谢谢大家来。我想先问个问题：你们有没有做过一个结果很糟的决定，然后自责了好几个月？我有。五年前我辞了一份稳定的工作去开一家小咖啡馆，两年后店关了。很长一段时间里，我都说那是我这辈子最糟的决定，花了好几年才走出来。

Then a friend who plays poker said something that has stayed with me ever since. She said that good players judge their decision-making by the information they had at the time, not by the result. You can play a hand well and still lose, because luck is part of the game. In hindsight everything looks obvious, but nobody makes decisions in hindsight.

后来一个打扑克的朋友说了一句话，我一直记到现在。她说好牌手评判自己的决策，看的是当时手上有的信息，而不是结果。一手牌你打得很好也可能输，因为运气本来就是游戏的一部分。事后看，什么都显而易见，可没有人是在事后做决定的。

So I went back and looked at my decision honestly. I had done my research. I had talked to people in the business, and I had taken the risks into account. What I had not expected was a new competitor opening across the street. Was that bad luck or bad planning? Partly both, I think. But it was not foolish, and I have stopped second-guessing it.

于是我回头诚实地看了看那个决定。我做过调研，和行内的人聊过，也把风险考虑进去了。我没料到的是对街新开了一家竞争对手。那是运气差还是计划差？我想两者都有一点。但那不是愚蠢的决定，我已经不再反复怀疑它了。

Since then I have kept two simple habits. Before a big decision, I write down what I know, what I do not know, and what would make me change my mind. Afterwards, I read the note again, whatever the result. It has made me more honest with myself, and much less afraid of losing out on the next opportunity just because the last one went wrong.

从那以后我一直保持两个简单的习惯。做大决定之前，我写下我知道什么、不知道什么、什么会让我改变主意。事后，不管结果如何，我都把那张纸再读一遍。这让我对自己更诚实，也不那么害怕仅仅因为上一次失败就错过下一个机会。

Some regret is useful; it tells us what we care about. But regret that only replays the result teaches us nothing. Judge the process, forgive the luck, and give yourself a time limit for blaming yourself. Mine was far too long. I hope yours is shorter.

有些后悔是有用的，它告诉我们自己在乎什么。可是只会回放结果的后悔，什么也教不了我们。评判过程，原谅运气，给自责定一个期限。我的太长了，希望你们的短一些。

Words词

/ˈhaɪndsaɪt/ n.
事后的认识；后见之明
in hindsight; with hindsight
In hindsight, we should have left earlier. 事后看，我们应该早点出发。
/dʒʌdʒ/ v. phr.
以……来评判
judge sb by their actions; judge a book by its cover
Don't judge a school by its website. 别凭网站评判一所学校。
/ˈsteɪbəl/ adj.

a stable job; a stable income
After years of short contracts, she wanted something stable. 做了多年短期合同后，她想要份稳定的工作。
/kəmˈpetɪtə/ n.

A competitor opened a shop on the same street. 一个竞争对手在同一条街开了店。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

Then a friend who plays poker said something that has stayed with me ever since.
Past simple for the moment she spoke (said), present perfect for the effect that continues to now (has stayed … ever since).
说话的那一刻用一般过去时 said；影响持续到现在用现在完成时 has stayed … ever since。
I had done my research. I had talked to people in the business, and I had taken the risks into account.
Past perfect: things finished before the decision she is looking back at. do research and take … into account keep their usual verbs in every tense.
过去完成时：在她回看的那个决定之前已完成的事。do research、take … into account 在任何时态里都保持原搭配。
