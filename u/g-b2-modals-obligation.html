Obligation and advice in the present and past 义务与建议（含过去）

📘 In your coursebook: English File 5e · Advanced · 7A “Is that a fact?”. Use the texts and recordings with your coursebook; this page adds new words, grammar and practice.教材定位：English File 5e · Advanced · 7A《Is that a fact?》。课文与录音请配合教材学习，本页补充新词、语法与练习。

Say exactly how strong a duty or a piece of advice is, now and in the past, and avoid the classic mix-ups of mustn't and don't have to, didn't need to and needn't have. 准确表达义务或建议的强弱（现在与过去），避开 mustn't 与 don't have to、didn't need to 与 needn't have 这些经典混淆。

B2 · CELPIP 7–8⏱ 30 minV06 Register: too formal, too casual, or too blunt语域不合：太正式、太随意或太冲

You mustn't tell Mum. 不许告诉妈妈。
Forbidden: it is a secret.禁止：这是秘密。
You don't have to tell Mum. 你不用告诉妈妈。
Not necessary; your choice.不必，你自己决定。
You shouldn't tell Mum. 你最好别告诉妈妈。
Advice against it.建议别说。

📏 The rule规则

📏 The rule in one line规则一句话
must and have to are strong obligation; mustn't forbids; don't have to and don't need to mean it is not necessary; should and ought to advise; had better warns. For the past, had to is obligation, should have done is criticism or regret, and needn't have done means you did something unnecessary.must 和 have to 是强义务；mustn't 是禁止；don't have to、don't need to 是不必；should、ought to 是建议；had better 是警告。讲过去：had to 是当时必须，should have done 是批评或后悔，needn't have done 是做了不必做的事。

In the present, must often comes from the speaker or a written rule (You must wear a seat belt; I must call my mother), while have to sounds like an outside fact (I have to work on Saturday). In speech, have to and have got to are far more common; must in conversation can sound strict.

现在时里，must 常出自说话人本人或书面规定，have to 更像外部事实。口语里 have to、have got to 常用得多，对话里用 must 可能显得严厉。

The negatives are not opposites of each other. You mustn't park here means it is forbidden. You don't have to park here means there are other options. Chinese buyong means not necessary, which is don't have to, not mustn't.

两个否定式意思完全不同。mustn't 是禁止；don't have to 是不必、还有别的选择。中文「不用」是不必，对应 don't have to，不是 mustn't。

must has no past form for obligation. Use had to: I had to show my ID. should have done looks back at the right thing that did not happen: I should have read the contract. shouldn't have done is regret for something you did.

表义务的 must 没有过去式，用 had to。should have done 回看应该做却没做的事；shouldn't have done 是后悔做了不该做的事。

didn't need to do and needn't have done differ. I didn't need to queue usually means I did not queue, because it was unnecessary. I needn't have queued means I queued, and it turned out to be unnecessary. In North American English, didn't need to is far more common for both; needn't have sounds British or formal.

didn't need to do 通常表示没必要、所以没做；needn't have done 表示做了，结果发现没必要。北美英语两种情况都更常用 didn't need to；needn't have 听起来偏英式或正式。

had better is not polite advice; it carries a warning of a bad result: You'd better leave now or you'll miss the ferry. To a manager or a stranger, You might want to or It might be worth is softer. be supposed to talks about what is expected or arranged, often when it does not happen: I was supposed to start on Monday, but my permit hadn't arrived.

had better 不是客气的建议，暗含不这样就有麻烦。对上司或陌生人，用 You might want to、It might be worth 更委婉。be supposed to 讲的是按理、按安排应该怎样，常用在没照做的时候。

Examples例句

  • You must keep your receipt; the store won't take returns without it. 一定要保留收据，没有收据商店不退货。
    A firm rule.硬性规定。
  • I have to leave early on Thursdays to pick up my son. 每周四我得早点走，去接儿子。
    An outside fact about my life.生活里的客观安排。
  • You mustn't leave children alone in the car. 不准把孩子单独留在车里。
    Forbidden.禁止。
  • You don't have to bring anything. We've got plenty of food. 你什么都不用带，我们吃的多着呢。
    Not necessary.不必。
  • We had to wait three hours at the border. 我们在边境等了三个小时。
    Past obligation.过去不得不。
  • I should have checked the weather before we set off. 出发前我应该查一下天气的。
    Regret: I did not check.后悔没查。
  • You shouldn't have paid in cash; there's no record now. 你不该付现金的，现在没有记录了。
    Criticism of something done.批评做了的事。
  • I didn't need to print the ticket; they scanned it on my phone. 我不用打印票，他们直接扫我手机。
    Not necessary, and I did not print it.没必要，所以没打印。
  • I needn't have printed the ticket; they just scanned my phone. 我其实没必要打印票，他们只扫了手机。
    I printed it for nothing.白打印了。
  • You'd better call the bank today, or they'll freeze the card. 你最好今天给银行打电话，不然卡会被冻结。
    A warning of a bad result.警告后果。
  • You might want to ask for that in writing. 你也许该要一份书面的。
    Soft, polite advice.委婉的建议。
  • The parcel was supposed to arrive yesterday. 包裹本该昨天到的。
    Expected but did not happen.本该如此但没发生。
  • You're not supposed to eat in the computer lab. 电脑室里不让吃东西。
    A rule people know but may break.大家都知道的规矩。
  • Do we have to book in advance, or can we just walk in? 我们得提前预约，还是直接去就行？
    Asking about necessity.问是否必要。
  • You ought to get that cough checked. 你那咳嗽应该去看看。
    Advice, a little more formal than should.建议，比 should 稍正式。

More pairs: same idea, different choice更多对比句组

I didn't have to take the test. 我不用考那个试。
It was not required, so I did not take it.不要求，所以没考。
I needn't have taken the test. 我其实没必要去考那个试。
I took it, then learned it was not required.考了，后来才知道不用考。
I should have taken the test. 我本该去考的。
I did not take it, and that was a mistake.没考，是个错误。
You should back up your photos. 你应该备份照片。
Ordinary advice.一般建议。
You'd better back up your photos before the update. 更新前你最好备份照片。
A warning: something bad may happen.警告：可能出事。
It might be worth backing up your photos. 也许值得备份一下照片。
Gentle and polite.温和客气。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ You mustn't pay now; you can pay later.
✓ You don't have to pay now; you can pay later.
mustn't forbids; for "not necessary" use don't have to.
mustn't 是禁止；「不必」用 don't have to。
✗ Yesterday I must go to the hospital.
✓ Yesterday I had to go to the hospital.
must has no past form for obligation.
表义务的 must 没有过去式。
✗ You should told me earlier.
✓ You should have told me earlier.
Criticism of the past needs should have + past participle.
批评过去要用 should have 加过去分词。
✗ You had better to see a doctor.
✓ You had better see a doctor.
had better is followed by the base verb, without to.
had better 后接原形，不加 to。
✗ We didn't need to hurry, so we hurried for nothing.
✓ We needn't have hurried. / We hurried, but we didn't need to.
If you did it anyway, use needn't have or say so clearly.
如果做了，用 needn't have，或把「做了」说清楚。
