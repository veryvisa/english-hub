Intonation that shows attitude 表达态度的语调

📘 In your coursebook: English File 5e · Upper-Intermediate · 3B “A twist in the tale” · English File 5e · Advanced · 3A “Online or IRL?” · English File 5e · Advanced · 7A “Is that a fact?”. Use the texts and recordings with your coursebook; this page adds new words, grammar and practice.教材定位：English File 5e · Upper-Intermediate · 3B《A twist in the tale》；English File 5e · Advanced · 3A《Online or IRL?》；English File 5e · Advanced · 7A《Is that a fact?》。课文与录音请配合教材学习，本页补充新词、语法与练习。

Hear and use the tunes that carry attitude (interest, doubt, reservation, surprise, boredom), so that your words and your tone say the same thing. 听出并用上表达态度的语调（感兴趣、怀疑、保留、惊讶、无聊），让你的话和语气说的是同一件事。

C1 · CELPIP 9+⏱ 25 minP10 Flat or wrong intonation; the important word is not stressed语调平，重点词不突出V06 Register: too formal, too casual, or too blunt语域不合：太正式、太随意或太冲

📏 The rule规则

📏 The point in one line一句话要领
The same words change meaning with the tune: a high fall sounds definite or enthusiastic, a low fall sounds flat or bored, a fall-rise means "yes, but...", and a rise-fall sounds impressed or ironic. Native listeners judge attitude by the tune before the words.同样的词随语调改变意思：高降显得确定或热情，低降显得平淡或无聊，降升是「是的，可是……」，升降显得佩服或讽刺。母语者先凭语调判断态度，再听词。

That's great! /ðæts ˈɡreɪt/
High fall. Start high on GREAT and fall all the way. 高降。从高处在 GREAT 上一直降下来。
That's great. /ðæts ˈɡreɪt/
Low fall. Start low: it sounds bored. 低降。低起，听起来无聊。
It's quite good... /ɪts kwaɪt ˈɡʊd/
Fall-rise. The rise at the end means "but". 降升。句末上扬表示「但是」。
Really! /ˈrɪəli/
Rise-fall. Up, then down: impressed. 升降。先升后降：佩服。
Really? /ˈrɪəli/
Rise. A simple rise: a real question. 升调。单纯上升：真在问。
In principle, yes. /ɪn ˈprɪnsəpəl ˈjes/
Fall-rise on yes. Agreement with a reservation. 降升（落在 yes 上）。带保留的同意。
I can do Monday. /aɪ kən duː ˈmʌndeɪ/
Fall-rise on Mon. Hidden: "but not other days". 降升（落在 Mon 上）。暗含「别的日子不行」。
That's an interesting idea. /ðæts ən ˈɪntrəstɪŋ aɪˈdɪə/
Fall-rise. Polite doubt. 降升。礼貌的怀疑。
That's an interesting idea! /ðæts ən ˈɪntrəstɪŋ aɪˈdɪə/
High fall. Real interest. 高降。真感兴趣。
Oh, you've finished. /əʊ juv ˈfɪnɪʃt/
Rise-fall on fin. Surprised, maybe pleasantly. 升降（落在 fin 上）。惊讶，可能是惊喜。
Fine. /ˈfaɪn/
Low fall. Can sound annoyed: be careful. 低降。可能听起来不高兴，小心。
Fine! /ˈfaɪn/
High fall. Warm agreement. 高降。爽快同意。
It's not bad. /ɪts nɒt ˈbæd/
Fall-rise. Lukewarm; there is a "but". 降升。不温不火，还有下文。
For now, that's fine. /fə ˈnaʊ ðæts ˈfaɪn/
Fall-rise on now. Later may be different. 降升（落在 now 上）。以后可能不同。
Thank you so much! /ˈθæŋk ju səʊ ˈmʌtʃ/
High fall on much. Wide range sounds sincere. 高降（落在 much 上）。音域宽才显得真诚。
Well, you could try. /wel ju kʊd ˈtraɪ/
Fall-rise on try. Doubtful it will work. 降升（落在 try 上）。怀疑会成功。

📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Open the full explanation: 5 points展开细讲：5 段讲解

A new hire says "That's great" when the team lead announces a new project. The words are positive, but the pitch is low and falls only a little, and the team lead later asks whether something is wrong. In English a low, narrow fall on a positive word sounds unenthusiastic, even sarcastic. The words were right; the tune contradicted them, and listeners believe the tune.

新同事听到新项目时说 That's great，词是正面的，音高却低、只降了一点，组长后来问他是不是有什么不满。英语里正面词配低而窄的降调，听起来不热情，甚至讽刺。词对，语调却和词唱反调，而听者相信语调。

Mandarin listeners are trained to hear pitch as tone on each syllable, not as attitude across a phrase, so this layer is easy to miss in both directions. When speaking, Chinese speakers often keep a narrow range and sound flat; when listening, they take the words literally and miss that That's an interesting idea with a fall-rise means "I don't agree".

母语为普通话的人习惯把音高听成每个字的声调，而不是整句的态度，所以这一层两头都容易漏：说的时候音域窄、显得平；听的时候只按字面理解。

Four tunes to know. High fall (start high, fall to low): definite, warm, enthusiastic: That's GREAT! Low fall (start low, fall a little): neutral, final, possibly bored: That's great. Fall-rise (fall, then rise at the end): reservation, a hidden "but": It's quite good..., I like the colour... (but not the price). Rise-fall (go up, then down): impressed, surprised, or ironic: Really!, He did?

四个调：高降表示确定、热情；低降表示中性、终结，可能无聊；降升表示保留，暗含「但是」；升降表示佩服、惊讶或讽刺。

The fall-rise is the one that matters most at work. It lets you agree partly without saying "no": In principle, yes. / For now, that's fine. / I can do Monday. (but not Tuesday). Native speakers use it constantly to be diplomatic, and they hear it in your speech even if you did not mean it. If you always end with a small rise, you may sound as though you always have a reservation.

职场上最重要的是降升调：不说 no 也能部分同意。母语者常用它来表达委婉；就算你无意，他们也会在你的话里听出保留。习惯句末小上扬，可能让你听起来总有保留。

To widen your range, exaggerate in practice: start the high fall at the top of your voice and drop to the bottom. Real speech will come out at about half that size, which is usually just right. Record the same short sentence with all four tunes and check that a friend can name the attitude without seeing you.

想扩大音域，练习时就夸张：高降从嗓音最高处落到最低处。真说话时会缩到一半左右，通常正好。把同一个短句用四种调各录一遍，让朋友不看你、只听声音说出态度。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ That's great. (low, narrow fall when you are pleased)
✓ That's GREAT! (high fall)
A low fall on a positive word sounds bored or sarcastic.
正面词配低降，听起来无聊或讽刺。
✗ Taking "That's an interesting idea" (fall-rise) as praise
✓ Hearing the reservation
The fall-rise often means polite disagreement.
降升调常是委婉的不同意。
✗ Ending every sentence with a small rise
✓ Clear falls for definite statements
A habitual rise can sound uncertain or reserved.
习惯性上扬会显得没把握或有保留。
✗ Fine. (flat, said to a customer)
✓ Fine, no problem! (high fall)
A flat fine can sound like irritation.
平平的 fine 可能听起来不耐烦。

把四个短句各用四种调录一遍（共 16 段），打乱顺序放给朋友或同事听，请对方写下每段听出的态度，数一数和你本意相符的有几段，没对上的重录。
