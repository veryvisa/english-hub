Cohesion: this, such, the former, doing so 语篇衔接

📘 In your coursebook: English File 5e · Advanced · 1B “Job description” · English File 5e · Advanced · 3B “History in the making”. Use the texts and recordings with your coursebook; this page adds new words, grammar and practice.教材定位：English File 5e · Advanced · 1B《Job description》；English File 5e · Advanced · 3B《History in the making》。课文与录音请配合教材学习，本页补充新词、语法与练习。

Use reference devices such as this, such, the former/the latter and doing so to link sentences precisely without repeating nouns or leaving the reference vague. 能够使用 this、such、the former/the latter 和 doing so 等指代手段精确衔接句子，避免重复名词或指代含糊。

👀 Look first: what changes?先看对比：差在哪里

Read the sentences and decide what each one tells you. Then open the notes.先读这几句，自己判断各自传达了什么，再点开看注解。

The board cut the budget. This angered the staff. 董事会削减了预算。这激怒了员工。
Bare 'this' points to the whole event of cutting the budget, so the reader must infer the referent.单独的 this 指整个削减预算的事件，读者需要自己推断所指。
The board cut the budget. This decision angered the staff. 董事会削减了预算。这一决定激怒了员工。
Adding a noun labels the referent as a decision, so the reader knows exactly what is being discussed.加上名词后把所指定性为一个决定，读者准确知道在谈什么。
The board cut the budget. Such a move angered the staff. 董事会削减了预算。这样的举动激怒了员工。
'Such' treats the budget cut as an example of a type of action, which invites a more general comment.such 把削减预算看作一类行为的例子，引向更概括的评论。
The board cut the budget. In doing so, it angered the staff. 董事会削减了预算。这样做，它激怒了员工。
'Doing so' focuses on the board's action and links it directly to a consequence caused by the same agent.doing so 聚焦董事会的行为，并把它与同一主体造成的后果直接联系起来。

📏 The rule规则

📏 The rule in one line规则一句话
Cohesive reference words let a later clause point back to an earlier idea, and a good writer picks the one that names exactly what is being referred to, whether a single noun, a whole clause, or an action.衔接指代词让后文回指前文的内容，好的写作者会选择恰好能指明所指对象的那个词，不论所指是一个名词、整个从句还是一个动作。
  • Easy to miss易漏 ✗ The government raised fuel taxes. This made many people angry. This also caused prices to rise. This led to protests. → ✓ The government raised fuel taxes. This move angered many people and pushed prices up, which in turn led to protests.

📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Open the full explanation: 5 points, 23 examples展开细讲：5 段讲解、23 个例句

A bare 'this' at the start of a sentence can point to a noun, a whole clause or an entire paragraph, and readers must guess which. At C1, add a noun after it ('this approach', 'this shift', 'this failure') whenever the earlier text contains more than one possible referent. Keep the bare form only when the referent is the obvious idea you have just finished stating.

句首单独的 this 可能指一个名词、一个从句甚至整段话，读者只能猜。到了 C1，只要前文存在不止一个可能的所指，就应在 this 后加名词（this approach、this shift、this failure）。只有当所指显然就是刚说完的那个观点时，才保留单独的 this。

'Such' does two jobs that learners blur. As a determiner it points back to a type ('such measures', 'such a view') and carries the meaning 'of the kind just mentioned'. It is different from 'this', which points to the specific item itself. Choose 'such' when you are generalising from the example to a category, and 'this' when you are talking about that very case.

such 有两个用法，学习者常常混淆。作限定词时，它回指一类事物（such measures、such a view），意思是“刚才提到的那一类”。它不同于 this，this 指向具体的那一个。从例子概括到一类情况时用 such，谈论那一个具体情形时用 this。

'The former' and 'the latter' are worth using only when two items have just been named and repeating them would be clumsy. They force the reader to look back, so they become tiring when the two nouns are far apart or when there are three or more items. If the reader has to search for the referent, repeat the noun or use a short descriptive phrase instead.

the former 和 the latter 只适用于刚刚提到两个对象、重复名词会显得笨拙的时候。它们迫使读者回头查找，所以当两个名词相隔很远或者对象多于两个时就会让人疲惫。如果读者得去找所指对象，就应重复名词或改用简短的描述性短语。

'Doing so' replaces a verb phrase that has already been stated, usually after a preposition or participle: 'By doing so, the firm cut costs.' It points to an action, not a noun, so it cannot be swapped with 'this' or 'it' in the same slot. Its subject must be the same as the main clause subject, otherwise you get a dangling structure, so check who actually performs the action.

doing so 替代前文已经说过的动词短语，通常用在介词或分词之后：By doing so, the firm cut costs。它指代的是动作而非名词，所以在同一位置不能换成 this 或 it。它的逻辑主语必须与主句主语一致，否则会形成悬垂结构，因此要检查到底是谁做出这个动作。

A related choice is between 'this' and 'it'. Use 'it' when the referent is a single clear noun already in focus, and 'this' when you want to pick up a whole idea and comment on it, as in 'This raises a further question.' Using 'it' for a whole clause often feels vague, while using 'this' for a simple noun can sound heavy and over-formal.

另一个选择是 this 与 it。当所指是已在焦点中的单个清晰名词时用 it；想把整个观点拎出来加以评论时用 this，例如 This raises a further question。用 it 指整个从句常常显得含糊，而用 this 指简单名词又会显得沉重、过于正式。

Examples例句

  • The council banned cars from the centre. This decision divided local shopkeepers. 市议会禁止汽车进入市中心。这一决定使当地店主意见不一。
  • Several firms offered discounts to loyal customers, but such incentives rarely changed buying habits. 几家公司向老客户提供折扣，但这类激励措施很少改变购买习惯。
  • Both Smith and Jones studied the fall in birth rates; the former blamed housing costs, the latter blamed changing values. 史密斯和琼斯都研究了出生率下降；前者归咎于住房成本，后者归咎于价值观的变化。
  • The company moved its call centre abroad and, in doing so, lost much of its local goodwill. 公司把呼叫中心迁到国外，这样做使它失去了许多本地好感。
  • Prices rose sharply last winter. This trend is likely to continue. 去年冬天价格急剧上涨。这一趋势很可能会持续下去。
  • Critics say the law is too vague. Such criticism overlooks how courts have applied it. 批评者说这项法律太模糊。这类批评忽视了法院实际适用它的方式。
  • She refused to sign the contract, and by doing so she kept her freedom to negotiate later. 她拒绝签合同，这样做让她保留了日后继续谈判的自由。
  • Tea and coffee are both widely traded, but the latter is far more volatile in price. 茶和咖啡都被广泛交易，但后者的价格波动要大得多。
  • The experiment failed to produce clear results. This failure forced the team to rethink its method. 实验没有得出明确结果。这次失败迫使团队重新思考方法。
  • Employers who monitor staff emails risk damaging trust, and doing so may also breach privacy law. 监控员工电子邮件的雇主有损害信任的风险，这样做还可能违反隐私法。
  • Such was the pressure on the minister that she resigned within a week. 部长承受的压力如此之大，以至于一周内就辞职了。
  • Remote work saves commuting time. This is why many parents prefer it. 远程办公节省通勤时间。这就是许多家长偏好它的原因。
  • The report recommends investing in rail and in cycling lanes; the former is costly, whereas the latter is cheap and quick. 报告建议投资铁路和自行车道；前者成本高，而后者便宜又见效快。
  • Users may ignore the warning, but doing so can void the warranty. 用户可能会无视警告，但这样做可能使保修失效。
  • Some governments subsidise fuel heavily. Such policies distort the market and discourage investment in renewables. 一些政府大量补贴燃料。这类政策扭曲市场，并抑制对可再生能源的投资。
  • Interest rates have been frozen for two years. This has left many borrowers in a difficult position. 利率已经冻结了两年。这让许多借款人陷入困境。
  • He turned down the transfer to Vancouver, and in doing so kept his seniority in the Toronto office. 他拒绝了调往温哥华的机会，这样做保住了他在多伦多办公室的工龄资历。
  • We tried both a loan and a grant. The former carried heavy interest, while the latter required a long application. 我们尝试了贷款和助学金两种方式。前者利息沉重，而后者申请周期很长。
  • Nobody warned residents about the flood risk, and this lack of communication proved costly. 没有人就洪水风险向居民发出警告，这种沟通缺失代价高昂。
  • Parents can read to their children every night. Doing so builds vocabulary long before school begins. 父母可以每晚给孩子读书。这样做在孩子入学很早之前就能积累词汇量。
  • There are countless misleading headlines, and such headlines spread faster than corrections. 误导性标题数不胜数，而这类标题传播得比更正快。
  • The new tax was introduced without consultation. This, more than anything, explains the public anger. 新税在没有咨询的情况下出台。公众的愤怒主要就是由此造成的。
  • Investors who panic and sell at the bottom lock in their losses; those who wait, in doing so, often recover them. 在谷底恐慌抛售的投资者会锁定亏损；而选择等待的人，这样做往往能把亏损赚回来。

More pairs: same idea, different choice更多对比句组

Our firm sells software and hardware, and it is the hardware that brings in most of the profit. 我们公司卖软件和硬件，带来大部分利润的是硬件。
Repeating the noun is clearest when only one item matters and the reader should not have to look back.只有一个对象重要时，重复名词最清楚，读者不用回头查找。
Our firm sells software and hardware, and the latter brings in most of the profit. 我们公司卖软件和硬件，后者带来大部分利润。
'The latter' is compact and formal, and it is safe because exactly two items were just named.the latter 简洁正式，因为刚好只提到了两个对象，所以不会有歧义。
Our firm sells software, hardware and services, and the latter brings in most of the profit. 我们公司卖软件、硬件和服务，后者带来大部分利润。
With three items, 'the latter' is ambiguous or technically wrong, so this version shows the risk of using it.有三个对象时，the latter 有歧义甚至不准确，这个句子显示了误用的风险。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ The government raised fuel taxes. This made many people angry. This also caused prices to rise. This led to protests.
✓ The government raised fuel taxes. This move angered many people and pushed prices up, which in turn led to protests.
Learners repeat a bare 'this' at the start of every sentence, as a Chinese writer might repeat the same topic word, and the referent becomes unclear. Vary the device by adding a noun or using a relative clause with 'which'.
学习者像中文写作重复同一个话题词那样，在每个句首都用单独的 this，所指变得不清楚。应加名词或用 which 引导的关系从句来变换手法。
✗ Many students use phones in class. Doing so is distracting for the teacher and other students.
✓ Many students use phones in class. Such behaviour is distracting for the teacher and other students.
'Doing so' needs a clear performer of the action and works best when it follows the same subject. Here the sentence starts a general comment on a habit, so a noun phrase with 'such' or 'this' reads more naturally.
doing so 需要有明确的动作执行者，最好紧跟同一主语。这里是对一种习惯做总体评论，用 such 或 this 加名词更自然。
✗ I compared the Toronto office and the Calgary office, and the former one is bigger.
✓ I compared the Toronto office and the Calgary office, and the former is bigger.
'The former' and 'the latter' already stand for a whole noun phrase, so adding 'one' is redundant. Learners add it because they think a noun-like word needs a noun partner.
the former 和 the latter 本身就代表整个名词短语，再加 one 是多余的。学习者误以为类似名词的词需要搭配一个名词。
✗ He didn't pay the fine. By doing so it, he was arrested.
✓ He didn't pay the fine. As a result, he was arrested.
'Doing so' replaces a verb phrase and takes no object, and it also expresses an action performed, not an omission. For a negative event, use a connector of result instead, or name the action explicitly.
doing so 代替动词短语，后面不能再带宾语，而且它表示做出的行为而非没做的事。对于否定情形，应改用表示结果的连接词，或明确说出这个行为。
✗ There are such many problems in the city that we cannot solve them quickly.
✓ There are so many problems in the city that we cannot solve them quickly.
Learners translate the Chinese intensifier meaning 'so' as 'such' everywhere. 'Such' attaches to a noun phrase ('such problems', 'such a long wait'), but 'so' goes before 'many', 'much', 'few' and adjectives.
学习者把汉语中表示“这么”的程度词到处译成 such。such 接名词短语（such problems、such a long wait），而 so 用在 many、much、few 和形容词之前。
✗ Researchers surveyed workers and managers. The later were more optimistic.
✓ Researchers surveyed workers and managers. The latter were more optimistic.
The words 'later' and 'latter' look and sound similar, and learners often confuse them in writing. 'The latter' refers to the second of two items, whereas 'later' means afterwards.
later 和 latter 外形和读音相近，学习者在写作中常混淆。the latter 指两者中的第二个，而 later 的意思是“之后”。
