Introductory it: It's worth…, I find it hard to… 形式主语与形式宾语 it

Use introductory it accurately to evaluate situations and express opinions, difficulty, importance, and value without confusing grammatical subjects and objects. 准确使用引导性 it 来评价情况，并表达观点、难度、重要性和价值，避免混淆语法主语与宾语。

📏 The rule规则

📏 The rule in one line规则一句话
Use introductory it when a real subject or object is a long infinitive or that-clause, especially when you want to foreground an evaluation or judgment before giving its content.当真正的主语或宾语是较长的不定式或 that 从句时，使用形式 it，尤其是在需要先表达评价或判断、再交代具体内容时。

📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Choose It is + adjective + to-infinitive when you want to evaluate an action as a whole. It is often more natural than beginning with a long infinitive clause, particularly in explanations, recommendations, and formal writing. The infinitive at the end tells the reader exactly which action is being evaluated.

当你想整体评价某个行为时，使用 It is + 形容词 + to do。与直接用较长的不定式短语开头相比，这种结构通常更自然，尤其适用于解释、建议和正式写作。句末的不定式说明具体评价的是哪项行为。

Choose It is worth + -ing when you want to recommend an activity because its benefits justify the time, money, or effort. Do not automatically use a to-infinitive after worth. The noun or gerund following worth identifies the activity or thing that deserves attention.

当你想表示某项活动值得投入时间、金钱或精力时，使用 It is worth + 动名词。不要在 worth 后机械地使用不定式。worth 后面的名词或动名词说明什么事情值得投入。

Choose I find it + adjective + to-infinitive when you are reporting your personal experience or judgment about doing something. This is particularly useful when you want to distinguish your experience from a general fact. The word it represents the later infinitive clause, not the person or object experiencing the difficulty.

当你想表达自己对做某事的实际感受或判断时，使用 I find it + 形容词 + to do。这个结构特别适合区分个人体验与普遍事实。it 指代后面的不定式从句，而不是经历困难的人或物。

Choose I find it + adjective + that-clause when your judgment concerns a fact, situation, or development rather than an action you perform. The that-clause supplies the content of your judgment. Compare this with the infinitive pattern: use the infinitive for an activity and the that-clause for a proposition or reported situation.

当你的判断针对某个事实、情况或发展，而不是自己执行的动作时，可以使用 I find it + 形容词 + that 从句。that 从句提供判断的具体内容。与不定式结构相比，不定式侧重行为，而 that 从句侧重一个事实或陈述。

Use It is + adjective + that-clause when you want to emphasize a judgment about a complete statement. This pattern is especially common with adjectives such as important, essential, obvious, surprising, and unfortunate. Do not replace the that-clause with an infinitive unless the meaning and grammatical relationship remain appropriate.

当你想强调对一个完整陈述的判断时，使用 It is + 形容词 + that 从句。important、essential、obvious、surprising 和 unfortunate 等形容词经常采用这种结构。只有在意义和语法关系都合适时，才能把 that 从句改成不定式。

Distinguish introductory it from the ordinary pronoun it. In It is difficult to solve the problem, it introduces the evaluation and the infinitive gives the actual subject. In I find it difficult to solve the problem, it is the grammatical object of find, while the infinitive explains what is difficult.

要区分形式 it 和普通代词 it。在 It is difficult to solve the problem 中，it 引出评价，真正的主语是后面的不定式。在 I find it difficult to solve the problem 中，it 是 find 的语法宾语，后面的不定式说明困难所在。

Examples例句

  • It is worth checking the figures before making a decision. 在做决定之前核实这些数字是值得的。
  • It is worth spending a little more on a reliable laptop. 多花一点钱买一台可靠的笔记本电脑是值得的。
  • It is not worth arguing about such a minor issue. 为这样一个小问题争论不值得。
  • It is difficult to explain the decision without more information. 没有更多信息，很难解释这个决定。
  • It is important to keep a record of every payment. 保存每笔付款的记录很重要。
  • It is essential to understand the conditions before signing the contract. 签合同之前了解相关条件至关重要。
  • It is possible to complete the project within three weeks. 在三周内完成这个项目是有可能的。
  • It is easy to overlook small differences in the two reports. 人们很容易忽略两份报告中的细微差别。
  • It is unusual to receive such a detailed response so quickly. 这么快就收到如此详细的回复并不常见。
  • It is surprising that nobody noticed the mistake earlier. 令人惊讶的是，之前竟然没有人发现这个错误。
  • It is clear that the current system needs improvement. 很明显，现有系统需要改进。
  • It is important that every applicant provides accurate information. 每位申请人都提供准确的信息非常重要。
  • I find it difficult to concentrate in a noisy environment. 我发现自己很难在嘈杂的环境中集中注意力。
  • I find it useful to review my notes before each meeting. 我发现每次会议前复习笔记很有用。
  • She found it impossible to finish the assignment in one evening. 她发现不可能在一个晚上完成这项任务。
  • We find it hard to predict how customers will respond. 我们发现很难预测客户会如何回应。
  • They consider it essential to protect personal information. 他们认为保护个人信息至关重要。
  • The new software makes it easier to organize large amounts of data. 新软件让整理大量数据变得更加容易。
  • The flexible schedule makes it possible to attend evening classes. 灵活的时间安排使参加晚间课程成为可能。
  • I think it strange that he has not replied to the message. 我觉得奇怪的是，他还没有回复这条消息。
  • We believe it necessary to discuss the risks before proceeding. 我们认为在继续之前讨论风险是必要的。
  • I find it encouraging that more people are participating in the program. 我觉得越来越多的人参与这个项目令人鼓舞。
  • It takes time to develop confidence in a new language. 培养使用一门新语言的信心需要时间。
  • It would be helpful to receive the documents in advance. 如果能提前收到这些文件，将会很有帮助。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ It is worth to read the full report.
✓ It is worth reading the full report.
Worth is normally followed by a noun or an -ing form, not a to-infinitive. Chinese-speaking learners often transfer the general pattern for saying that an action is worthwhile without checking the complement required by worth.
worth 后面通常接名词或动名词，而不是带 to 的不定式。中文学习者容易直接套用表示值得做某事的一般表达，而忽略 worth 本身对后续结构的要求。
✗ I find difficult to understand his explanation.
✓ I find it difficult to understand his explanation.
The verb find needs an object before the adjective in this pattern. The introductory object it anticipates the infinitive that explains what is difficult.
在这个结构中，find 后面需要先有宾语，再接形容词。形式宾语 it 预指后面解释困难内容的不定式。
✗ I find it difficult understanding his explanation.
✓ I find it difficult to understand his explanation.
When an adjective follows the object it in this construction, the usual complement is a to-infinitive. Do not confuse this pattern with verbs and expressions that independently take gerunds.
在这个结构中，形式宾语 it 后接形容词时，通常使用带 to 的不定式。不要将其与本身可以接动名词的动词或表达混淆。
✗ Is important to check the details.
✓ It is important to check the details.
English normally requires an explicit grammatical subject in this construction. Chinese can leave the subject unexpressed, but the corresponding English sentence needs introductory it.
英语在这个结构中通常需要明确的语法主语。中文可以省略主语，但相应的英语句子需要形式主语 it。
✗ It makes easy to use this application.
✓ It makes it easy to use this application.
The causative pattern make it + adjective + infinitive requires an object before the adjective. The second it anticipates the infinitive, whereas the first it must refer to an identifiable subject or situation.
使役结构 make it + 形容词 + 不定式要求在形容词前有一个宾语。第二个 it 预指后面的不定式，而第一个 it 必须指代明确的主语或情况。
✗ I find it is difficult to work under pressure.
✓ I find it difficult to work under pressure.
The adjective complement pattern uses find + it + adjective + infinitive, without inserting is. If you want a that-clause instead, say I find that it is difficult to work under pressure.
形容词补足语结构使用 find + it + 形容词 + 不定式，中间不能插入 is。如果想使用 that 从句，可以说 I find that it is difficult to work under pressure。
