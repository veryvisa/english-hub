What the Snow Review Found 暴风雪复盘发现了什么

🧠 Learn the 6 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 6 个新词

Read a local news report on a town's review of a winter storm and see how reports use inversion, inverted conditionals, the subjunctive and long noun phrases to state findings and recommendations. 读一篇本地新闻，报道小镇对一场冬季暴风雪应对的复盘；看报告怎样用倒装、省略 if 的条件句、虚拟式和长名词短语陈述发现与建议。

C1 · CELPIP 9+⏱ 20 minG05 Topic-comment sentences and double subjects主题句与双主语G07 Unreal conditionals and wish虚拟条件句与 wish

📖 Key card知识卡
An official review separates what happened, why it happened and what should change. Read the recommendations first: they tell you what the writers think went wrong.官方复盘会把「发生了什么」「为什么」「该改什么」分开写。先读建议部分：从中能看出撰写者认为哪里出了问题。

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

When forty centimetres of snow fell on the town in a single night last January, some streets were not cleared for four days. Residents in the hillside neighbourhoods, many of them older people living alone, could not reach a pharmacy or a bus stop. This week the town council published an independent review of what went wrong.

去年一月，小镇一夜之间下了四十厘米的雪，有些街道四天都没清出来。住在山坡社区的居民，其中很多是独居老人，去不了药房，也到不了公交站。本周，镇议会发布了一份关于问题出在哪里的独立复盘报告。

The review is blunt. Only on the third day did the town ask a private company for extra ploughs, although the forecast had warned of heavy snow a week earlier. Nor was there a list of residents who might need help. Volunteers who offered to check on neighbours had no way of knowing where to start.

报告措辞直率。直到第三天，镇上才请私人公司加派扫雪车，尽管天气预报一周前就警告过有大雪。也没有一份需要帮助的居民名单。主动提出去看望邻居的志愿者不知道该从哪里开始。

"Had the contract been signed in December, most of the hillside would have been open by the second morning," the report says. It also notes that the lack of clear information made the situation worse: the town's website was updated only twice in four days.

报告写道：「如果合同在十二月就签了，山坡区大部分道路第二天早上就能通行。」报告还指出，信息不清让情况更糟：镇政府网站四天里只更新了两次。

The review recommends that the town sign snow contracts before the start of each winter, and that a voluntary register of residents with limited mobility be kept by the community centre. It is essential, the authors write, that the register be checked every autumn. Should a similar storm hit again, volunteers will receive a list of streets within hours.

报告建议镇上在每个冬季开始前签好扫雪合同，并由社区中心保管一份行动不便居民的自愿登记册。撰写者写道，登记册必须每年秋天核对一次。如果再遇到类似的暴风雪，志愿者几小时内就能拿到一份街道名单。

At a public meeting on Tuesday, a retired nurse who spent the storm phoning her neighbours said the report was fair. "Nobody needed to be a hero," she said. "We just needed a list."

在周二的一次公众会议上，一位暴风雪期间一直给邻居打电话的退休护士说，这份报告很公道。「没人需要当英雄，」她说，「我们只是需要一份名单。」

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

Only on the third day did the town ask a private company for extra ploughs, although the forecast had warned of heavy snow a week earlier.
Only on the third day fronted → did the town ask; the inversion stresses how late the action came.
only on the third day 前置后接 did the town ask，倒装强调行动来得太晚。
The review recommends that the town sign snow contracts before the start of each winter, and that a voluntary register of residents with limited mobility be kept by the community centre.
Two subjunctive clauses after recommends that: bare sign (not signs) and passive be kept. The head of the long subject is register.
recommends that 后两个虚拟式从句：原形 sign（不是 signs）与被动 be kept。长主语的中心词是 register。
"Had the contract been signed in December, most of the hillside would have been open by the second morning," the report says.
= If the contract had been signed: an unreal past in report style.
相当于 If the contract had been signed：报告语体的过去虚拟。
