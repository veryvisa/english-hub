Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

A new food hall opened in the town centre on Saturday, and by lunchtime the queues stretched out of the door. The hall has twelve small stalls, each selling a different kind of food, from fresh dumplings to Mexican tacos and homemade ice cream. The owners say they want to give local cooks a chance to start a business without paying for a whole restaurant.

周六，镇中心新开了一家美食广场，到午饭时间，排队的人已经排到了门外。广场里有十二个小摊位，每个卖不同的食物，从新鲜饺子、墨西哥卷饼到自制冰淇淋应有尽有。店主们说，他们希望让本地厨师有机会创业，而不必自己开一整间餐厅。

Visitors seemed pleased with the idea. One customer said she liked being able to choose her own meal, because her friends usually disagree about what to eat. Another said the prices were lower than in most restaurants, and the portions were big enough to share. However, some people complained that the hall was too crowded and that it was hard to find a free seat.

游客们似乎很喜欢这个主意。一位顾客说，她喜欢能自己挑选餐点，因为朋友们常常在吃什么上意见不一。另一位说，这里的价格比大多数餐厅低，分量也大到可以分着吃。不过，也有人抱怨广场太拥挤，很难找到空位。

Unlike a traditional restaurant, the food hall has no waiters. Customers order at each stall and wait for their name to be called. There is also no need to book a table, which is useful for families with small children. A spokesperson said the hall will try to add more seats if the crowds continue. Meanwhile, a few stall owners said they had to prepare food all night to be ready for the opening.

与传统餐厅不同，美食广场没有服务员。顾客在各个摊位点餐，等叫到自己的名字。这里也不用订位，这对带小孩的家庭很方便。一位发言人说，如果人流持续不断，广场会尽量增加座位。与此同时，一些摊主说，为了赶上开业，他们整夜都在备菜。

Local cooks are hoping the hall will bring them new customers. One stall owner, who used to cook only for friends and neighbours, said she was nervous but excited. ‘I have always dreamed of having my own menu,’ she said. The hall is open every day, and the owners plan to hold a cooking class next month.

本地厨师希望这家广场能给他们带来新顾客。一位摊主以前只给朋友和邻居做饭，她说自己既紧张又兴奋。“我一直梦想有自己的菜单，”她说。广场每天营业，店主们还计划下个月举办一次烹饪课。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

Unlike a traditional restaurant, the food hall has no waiters.
Unlike plus a noun phrase starts the sentence to contrast two things, and ‘no waiters’ is a negative made with the word no.
Unlike 加名词短语放在句首，用来对比两个事物；'no waiters' 用 no 构成否定。
Customers order at each stall and wait for their name to be called.
’Wait for ... to be called’ joins wait for with a passive infinitive, so the focus is on the name, not on who calls it.
’wait for ... to be called’ 把 wait for 和被动不定式连用，重点在名字而不在叫名字的人。
