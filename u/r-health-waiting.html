Why the Wait Feels Longer Than It Is 为什么等待显得比实际更长

🧠 Learn the 1 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 1 个新词

Read an opinion piece on waiting times and watch unreal conditionals argue a point; collect collocations for complaining well. 读一篇讲排队等待的评论，看非真实条件句怎样帮作者论证；收集「有效投诉」的搭配。

Read and listen读与听

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Almost every newcomer I know has a waiting story: the referral that took months, the specialist who could not see them until spring, the long night in an emergency room. Some of these stories point to real problems. But I think part of the pain comes from what we compare the wait with.

我认识的新移民几乎人人都有一个排队的故事：转诊等了好几个月，专科医生要到春天才能看，急诊室里熬过的漫长一夜。有些故事指向的是真问题。但我认为，痛苦有一部分来自我们拿等待和什么作比较。

Where many of us grew up, you could walk into a large hospital, pay at a window and see a specialist the same day. If the system here were built like that, some waits would be shorter. But patients would pay more at the counter, and the family doctor, who knows your whole history, would be pushed out of the picture. Systems built around family doctors make a decision to send patients through one door first.

在我们很多人长大的地方，你可以走进一家大医院，在窗口交钱，当天就看上专家。如果这里的体系也这样建，有些等待会短一些。但病人在柜台要付得更多，而了解你全部病史的家庭医生会被挤到一边。以家庭医生为中心的体系，是有意让病人先走同一扇门。

That does not mean every wait is fine. Waits have a real impact on people living with pain or worry, and if you believe your case has been handled badly, you have every right to make a complaint. Before that, ask whether the doctor has taken your situation into account: "Is this referral marked as urgent? What should I do if things get worse while I wait?"

这不等于每一次等待都没问题。对带着疼痛或忧虑生活的人，等待的影响是真实的；如果你认为自己的情况被处理得不好，你完全有权投诉。在那之前，先问问医生有没有考虑到你的情况：「这个转诊标了紧急吗？等的时候情况变严重，我该怎么办？」

I wish someone had told me this in my first year. I would have saved myself a lot of anger. If I were arriving now, I would make an effort to find a family doctor in the first month, keep a list of my symptoms, and use the pharmacist for small things.

真希望第一年就有人告诉我这些，那我能省下许多怒气。如果我现在才来，我会在第一个月就努力找一位家庭医生，把症状记成清单，小毛病就找药剂师。

The wait will not disappear. Comparing it with a system you have left only makes it feel longer.

等待不会消失。拿它和你已经离开的体系作比较，只会让它显得更长。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

If the system here were built like that, some waits would be shorter.
Unreal present: were (not was) in careful English, would in the result. The writer does not think it is built like that.
与现在事实相反：正式用法用 were 而不是 was，结果句用 would。作者认为现实并非如此。
I wish someone had told me this in my first year.
wish + past perfect: regret about the past. Compare I wish someone would tell me (a wish about the future).
wish 加过去完成时：对过去的遗憾。对比 I wish someone would tell me（对将来的希望）。
