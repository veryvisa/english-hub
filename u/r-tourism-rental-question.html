Ask the Travel Desk: Is Our Holiday Rental the Problem? 旅行问答：我们订的度假屋是问题所在吗

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Dear Travel Desk, next month my sister and I are taking our four children to a seaside town for a week. We have found a short-term rental that is cheaper than any hotel and has a kitchen, which matters a lot with four kids. Then a friend pointed out that places like this are pushing locals out of their own neighbourhoods. Now I feel guilty. Should we cancel? Mei, by email

旅行问答：下个月我和妹妹要带四个孩子去一个海边小镇住一周。我们找到一间短租房，比哪家酒店都便宜，还有厨房，带四个孩子这很重要。后来一个朋友指出，这类房子正在把当地人挤出他们自己的社区。现在我很内疚。我们该取消吗？——美，来信

Dear Mei, you are not the first reader to run into this question, and you will not be the last. The honest answer is that it depends on the town and on the listing, and it takes about ten minutes of looking to figure out which kind you have found.

亲爱的美：你不是第一个碰到这个问题的读者，也不会是最后一个。老实说，这取决于是哪个镇、哪套房源，花十分钟左右看一看，就能弄清你找到的是哪一种。

The problem is not holiday rentals as such. It is whole buildings that used to be homes for local families and are now rented to visitors all year round. In some towns this has helped drive up rents so far that nurses and teachers cannot afford to live near their work. A spare room in a family home, or a flat the owner lives in for part of the year, is a different thing.

问题不在度假屋本身，而在那些原本住着本地人家、如今全年租给游客的整栋楼。在有些小镇，这已经把房租推高到护士和教师住不起工作地附近的地步。家里的一间空房，或者房主一年里有一段时间自己住的公寓，是另一回事。

So look at the listing again. Does the host live there or nearby? Does the same host run twenty other properties? Has the town set up a registration system, and does the listing show a licence number? If the answers point to a real neighbour earning some extra income, book it with a clear conscience.

所以再看一遍房源：房东住在那里或附近吗？同一个房东是不是还经营着二十套别的房子？镇上有没有设立登记制度，房源上有没有许可证号？如果答案指向一位赚点外快的真邻居，那就安心地订吧。

Wherever you stay, the money you spend matters as much as the bed you sleep in. Eat in local cafés rather than at a resort, buy fruit at the market, and treat the street as somebody's home, because it is. Enjoy the week, and do not let the guilt follow you back.

不管住在哪里，你花的钱和你睡的床一样重要。去本地咖啡馆吃饭，别窝在度假村里，在集市上买水果，把这条街当作别人的家——因为它就是。好好享受这一周，别让内疚跟着你回来。

Words词

/ˌʃɔːt tɜːm ˈrentl/ n.
短租房（按天或按周出租）
book a short-term rental; short-term rental rules
Many cities now require a licence for every short-term rental. 现在很多城市要求每套短租房都有许可证。
/ˈləʊklz/ n. (pl.)
当地人
ask the locals; popular with locals
If you want a good café, ask the locals, not the hotel. 想找好咖啡馆，问当地人，别问酒店。
/draɪv ˈʌp/ phr. v.
推高（价格、成本）
drive up prices; drive up rents
Demand from visitors can drive up prices in small towns. 游客的需求可能把小镇的物价推高。
/əz ˈsʌtʃ/ phrase
本身；就其本身而言
not the problem as such; tourism as such
I don't dislike tourists as such; I dislike the traffic. 我并不讨厌游客本身，我讨厌的是堵车。
/wɪð ə ˌklɪə ˈkɒnʃəns/ phrase
问心无愧地；安心地
do something with a clear conscience
Once I checked the listing, I could book it with a clear conscience. 查过房源之后，我就能安心地订了。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

you are not the first reader to run into this question, and you will not be the last.
the first … to do and the last take the because they are unique positions in a series. run into here means meet a problem by chance.
the first … to do 和 the last 用 the，因为它们是序列里唯一的位置。run into 在这里指偶然碰上一个问题。
If the answers point to a real neighbour earning some extra income, book it with a clear conscience.
point to (show, suggest) differs from point out (tell someone a fact). a real neighbour earning… is a shortened relative clause: who is earning.
point to（表明、指向）和 point out（向别人指出一个事实）不同。a real neighbour earning… 是省略的定语从句：who is earning。
