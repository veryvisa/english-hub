What the Front of the Box Leaves Out 包装正面没说的事

Pick up a box of breakfast cereal and the front will speak to you in large, happy words: "high in fibre", "made with whole grains", "no artificial colours". Each claim may be true. The problem is what the front leaves out. For a more reliable picture, turn the box over.

拿起一盒早餐麦片，正面会用又大又欢快的字跟你说话：「高纤维」「全谷物制成」「不含人工色素」。每个说法都可能是真的，问题在于正面没说什么。想看到更可靠的情况，把盒子翻过来。

The nutrition label on the back starts with the serving size, and this is where most misunderstanding begins. A serving might be forty grams, which is about half the portion many adults pour into a bowl. If you eat twice the serving, you get twice the sugar, twice the salt and twice the calories. Every number below depends on that first line.

背面的营养标签从每份分量开始，大多数误解就从这里开始。一份可能是四十克，大约是许多成年人倒进碗里那一份的一半。吃了两份，糖、盐和热量就都翻倍。下面每个数字都取决于第一行。

Next, look at the percentages. Labels in many countries show how much of a day's suggested amount one serving gives you. On Canadian labels the official rule of thumb is simple: five per cent or less is a little, fifteen per cent or more is a lot. You do not need to remember exact daily limits; you only need to know whether a number is small or large.

接着看百分比。许多国家的标签会标出一份食物占一天建议摄入量的多少。加拿大标签的官方经验法则很简单：百分之五或以下算少，百分之十五或以上算多。不必记住确切的每日上限，只要知道一个数字是大是小。

Then read the ingredients, which are listed from the largest amount to the smallest. Sugar often hides under several names, such as glucose syrup, dextrose or fruit juice concentrate. When three of these appear, the total can be high even if none of them comes first. Processed foods are not all bad, but the longer and stranger the list, the more carefully you should read it.

然后读配料表，配料按含量从多到少排列。糖常常藏在好几个名字下面，比如葡萄糖浆、右旋糖或浓缩果汁。三个同时出现时，即使哪个都不排第一，加起来也可能很多。加工食品并不都坏，但配料表越长越陌生，就越该仔细读。

None of this requires a science degree, only a little awareness and the willingness to spend twenty seconds in the aisle. Your expectation should not be a perfect food; it should be an honest picture. Small improvements, such as choosing the box with half the sugar, add up over a year. And you will shop with more confidence once the front of the box stops doing your thinking for you.

这些都不需要理科学位，只需要一点意识，并且愿意在货架前多站二十秒。你该期待的不是完美的食物，而是一幅诚实的图景。小小的改进，比如选含糖减半的那盒，一年下来会积少成多。等包装正面不再替你思考，你买东西也会更有底气。
