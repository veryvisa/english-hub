Four Hours at the Walk-in Clinic 在免预约诊所的四个小时

🧠 Learn the 2 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 2 个新词

Follow one first visit to a walk-in clinic and see how the present perfect and the past simple divide a story into "then" and "up to now". 跟着一次第一回去免预约诊所的经历，看现在完成时和一般过去时怎样把故事分成「当时」和「到现在为止」。

📖 Key card知识卡
A walk-in visit is short and is not first come, first served; bring a list of your symptoms and expect advice, not a drip.免预约门诊看得很快，也不是先到先看；带上症状清单，期待的是建议，不是打点滴。

Last March I woke up with a sore throat and a fever that would not go down. I had lived in Toronto for two years by then, and I had never seen a doctor here. I did not have a family doctor, so a friend told me to try a walk-in clinic rather than end up in an emergency room. I got there at eight. There were already eleven people in the waiting room.

去年三月，我一觉醒来喉咙痛，烧也退不下去。那时我已经在多伦多住了两年，却从没在这里看过医生。我没有家庭医生，朋友就让我去免预约诊所试试，免得最后进了急诊室。我八点到的，候诊室里已经坐了十一个人。

The receptionist asked for my health card and a short description of my symptoms. "How long have you had the fever?" she asked. "Since Saturday," I said, and then, because I was nervous, I added that I had taken some medicine from home. She wrote it down without comment. Later I understood why that detail mattered: the doctor needed to know what was already in my body.

前台要了我的医疗卡，让我简单说说症状。「烧了多久了？」她问。「从周六开始。」我说。然后因为紧张，我又补了一句，说我吃过从国内带来的药。她没说什么，记了下来。后来我才明白这个细节为什么要紧：医生得知道我体内已经有什么药。

I waited almost four hours. The nurse called people in an order that made no sense to me until I realised it was not first come, first served. A man with chest pain went in before all of us, and nobody complained. That is triage: the most urgent case goes first, whatever time you arrived.

我等了将近四个小时。护士叫人的顺序我起初完全看不懂，后来才意识到这里不是先到先看。一个胸口痛的男人比我们所有人都先进去，没有人抱怨。这就是分诊：不管你几点到，最紧急的先看。

The doctor spent eight minutes with me. She looked at my throat, asked three questions and wrote a prescription. I had expected tests and perhaps a drip, because that is what a visit meant where I grew up. "Rest, drink plenty of water, and come back if it gets worse," she said. I felt almost cheated.

医生在我身上花了八分钟。她看了看我的喉咙，问了三个问题，开了一张处方。我原以为要做检查，也许还要打点滴，因为在我长大的地方，看病就是这样。「多休息，多喝水，严重了再来。」她说。我几乎觉得自己被敷衍了。

I have been to that clinic four times since then, and I have learned to bring a short list: my symptoms, when they started, and what I have already taken. The visits are still short. But they are no longer a mystery, and I have stopped expecting a drip.

从那以后我又去过那家诊所四次，学会了带一张简短的清单：症状、什么时候开始的、已经吃过什么。看病还是很快，但不再是一头雾水，我也不再指望打点滴了。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

I had lived in Toronto for two years by then, and I had never seen a doctor here.
Past perfect looks back from that March morning; by then fixes the point in the past.
过去完成时从那个三月早上往回看；by then 定住过去的那个时间点。
I have been to that clinic four times since then, and I have learned to bring a short list: my symptoms, when they started, and what I have already taken.
Present perfect for experience up to now (four times since then); past simple started for a finished moment.
现在完成时讲到现在为止的经历（从那以后四次）；started 用一般过去时，是已经结束的时间点。
