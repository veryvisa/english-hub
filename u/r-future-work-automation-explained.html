How Automation Reshapes Jobs 自动化如何重塑工作

🧠 Learn the 2 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 2 个新词

Automation changes jobs by taking over some tasks within them, so the real question is which tasks are exposed and who is helped to adjust. 自动化通过接管工作中的部分任务来改变职业，因此真正的问题是哪些任务受到冲击，以及谁能得到帮助去调整。

Economists often describe automation as a process of unbundling. A job is not a single block of activity but a collection of tasks, and machines tend to take over the ones that are predictable and repetitive. This is why researchers prefer to ask which tasks are exposed, rather than which occupations will disappear.

经济学家常把自动化描述为一个拆解的过程。一份工作不是单一的一整块活动，而是一组任务的集合，机器往往会接管其中可预测、重复性高的那些。这就是为什么研究者更愿意问哪些任务受到冲击，而不是哪些职业将会消失。

Consider an accountant. Entering figures into a spreadsheet can now be handled by software, yet advising a nervous client about risk still depends on empathy and judgement. As routine work shrinks, the human share of the job shifts towards the parts that machines find hard, which is one reason the same job title can mean something quite different a decade later.

以会计为例。把数字录入电子表格如今可以由软件处理，但就风险向一位紧张的客户提供建议，仍然依赖共情与判断力。随着常规工作缩减，工作中属于人的部分会转向机器难以胜任的内容，这也是同一个职位名称十年后可能意味着相当不同的工作的原因之一。

Although this pattern is reassuring, it is not automatic. Gains from new tools are unevenly distributed, and on the whole they flow first to people who already hold a flexible skill set. Workers whose tasks are easy to codify can lose out unless they receive support to adapt to a changing role.

虽然这一模式令人安心，但它并非自动发生。新工具带来的收益分配并不均衡，总体而言，它们首先流向那些已经拥有灵活技能组合的人。任务容易被规范化的工作者，如果得不到支持去适应不断变化的角色，就可能吃亏。

Another effect is the rise of what scientists call platform labour, in which an algorithm assigns short pieces of work to individuals. It offers freedom to some and insecurity to others. Because the rules are written into software rather than contracts, workers can find it surprisingly difficult to challenge a decision, or even to learn how it was made.

另一个影响是科学家所说的平台劳动的兴起，即由算法把零散的短期工作分配给个人。它给一些人带来自由，给另一些人带来不安全感。由于规则写进了软件而不是合同，工作者可能会惊讶地发现，质疑一项决定，甚至弄清它是如何做出的，都相当困难。

The science of work, in short, points to a future of continuous adjustment rather than sudden collapse. Whether that adjustment feels like opportunity or threat is likely to depend less on the technology itself than on the institutions that surround it.

简而言之，关于工作的研究指向的是一个持续调整而非骤然崩塌的未来。这种调整给人的感觉是机遇还是威胁，很可能更少取决于技术本身，而更多取决于围绕它的各种制度。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

As routine work shrinks, the human share of the job shifts towards the parts that machines find hard, which is one reason the same job title can mean something quite different a decade later.
The opening ‘As’ clause expresses a change happening at the same time, and the non-defining relative clause with ‘which’ comments on the whole idea before it.
开头的 As 从句表示同时发生的变化，带 which 的非限定性关系从句则是对前面整个内容的评论。
Because the rules are written into software rather than contracts, workers can find it surprisingly difficult to challenge a decision, or even to learn how it was made.
The sentence uses a passive ‘are written’ in a reason clause, then a ‘find it + adjective + infinitive’ pattern with a dummy object ‘it’.
句子在原因从句中使用被动式 are written，主句则用 find it + 形容词 + 不定式结构，其中 it 是形式宾语。
