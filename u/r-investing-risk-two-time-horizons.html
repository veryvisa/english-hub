Same Money, Different Plans 同样的钱，不同的打算

🧠 Learn the 4 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 4 个新词

A suitable investment plan depends on when the money is needed and how much uncertainty its owner can live with. 合适的投资计划取决于何时需要用钱，以及钱的主人能承受多大的不确定性。

Read and listen读与听

290 words
Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Nina: I’ve finally saved a decent sum, and I want it to grow. My cousin invests almost everything in shares, so I was thinking of copying her. Omar: Hold on a moment. Before you copy anyone, tell me what the money is for and when you’ll need it. Nina: Part of it is for a course I’m starting next autumn. The rest is for much later, perhaps even retirement.

妮娜：我总算攒下了一笔可观的钱，想让它增值。我表姐几乎把所有钱都投进了股市，所以我在考虑照着她做。奥马尔：等一下。在模仿任何人之前，先告诉我这笔钱是做什么用的，什么时候要用。妮娜：一部分是明年秋天开始上课的费用，其余的要很久以后才用，也许要到退休时。

Omar: Then you’ve got two different time horizons. If I were you, I’d keep the course fees somewhere stable. Shares can drop sharply, and there may not be enough time for them to recover. Nina: But the returns on cash are tiny. Isn’t that a risk too? Omar: It is. Rising prices can quietly eat into your savings. Still, for a bill that’s due soon, certainty is usually worth more than growth.

奥马尔：那你就有两个不同的投资期限。换作是我，我会把学费放在稳妥的地方。股票可能大幅下跌，而且未必有足够的时间涨回来。妮娜：可是现金的收益太少了。这不也是一种风险吗？奥马尔：是的。物价上涨会悄悄侵蚀你的积蓄。不过，对于很快就要支付的账单，确定性通常比增值更有价值。

Nina: Fair enough. What about the long-term money? Omar: You can probably afford more ups and downs there, but it depends on your risk tolerance. Imagine opening your account and finding that a quarter of it has vanished. What would you do? Nina: Honestly? I’d panic. I might sell the lot. Omar: In that case, a more cautious mix is likely to suit you better, even though it may grow more slowly.

妮娜：有道理。那长期不用的钱呢？奥马尔：那部分钱大概可以承受更大的起伏，但要看你的风险承受能力。想象一下，你打开账户，发现四分之一的钱不见了。你会怎么办？妮娜：说实话？我会慌。我可能会全部卖掉。奥马尔：那样的话，更稳健的组合很可能更适合你，尽管它增长得可能慢一些。

Nina: So there’s no single right answer? Omar: Not really. A plan only works if you can stick to it when things look gloomy. Build up an emergency fund first, spread your money widely, and check the fees, because even small charges add up. Nina: And my cousin? Omar: Her approach may be perfectly sensible for her. It’s what you can live with that matters. Nina: All right. I’ll work out my dates before I choose anything.

妮娜：所以没有唯一的正确答案？奥马尔：确实没有。只有在形势看起来不妙时你仍能坚持，计划才会奏效。先建立应急资金，把钱广泛分散，还要查看费用，因为小额收费也会积少成多。妮娜：那我表姐呢？奥马尔：她的做法对她来说也许完全合理。重要的是你自己能接受什么。妮娜：好吧。我先把用钱的时间理清楚，再做选择。

/ˈtaɪm həˌraɪznz/ n. pl.

different time horizons; short time horizons
The two partners were working with very different time horizons. 两位合伙人考虑的时间跨度大不相同。
/ˌiːt ˈɪntuː/ phr. v.
侵蚀，耗掉
eat into savings; eat into profits
Long journeys to work eat into my free time. 漫长的通勤耗掉了我的空闲时间。
/ˌʌps ən ˈdaʊnz/ n. phr.
起伏，波动
daily ups and downs; the ups and downs of life
Every friendship has its ups and downs. 每段友谊都有起有落。
/ˈstɪk tə/ phr. v.
坚持，遵守
stick to a plan; stick to a budget
It is easier to stick to a routine when it is simple. 日常安排简单一些，就更容易坚持。
/ˌæd ˈʌp/ phr. v.
积少成多，累积起来
costs add up; quickly add up
Buying snacks every day can soon add up. 天天买零食，花费很快就会累积起来。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

It’s what you can live with that matters.
This cleft sentence uses ‘It is ... that’ to emphasise the clause ‘what you can live with’.
这是一个分裂句，用 It is ... that 结构强调 what you can live with 这个从句。
Imagine opening your account and finding that a quarter of it has vanished.
’Imagine’ is followed by two -ing forms, ‘opening’ and ‘finding’, joined by ‘and’.
imagine 后接由 and 连接的两个动名词 opening 和 finding。
