Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

When I first visited a modern art gallery, I felt strangely uncomfortable. The paintings seemed unfinished, the sculptures looked like ordinary objects, and the explanations appeared unnecessarily complicated. I remember standing in front of a large abstract canvas and wondering whether the artist had simply forgotten to complete it. My friend, who had studied art history, seemed genuinely fascinated. I began to suspect that we were looking at two different things.

第一次参观现代艺术画廊时，我感到一种奇怪的不自在。那些画似乎没有完成，雕塑看起来像普通物品，而作品说明又显得过于复杂。我记得自己站在一幅巨大的抽象画前，怀疑艺术家是不是忘记完成作品了。我的朋友学过艺术史，却显得十分着迷。我开始怀疑，我们看到的似乎是两种不同的东西。

Instead of dismissing my reaction, my friend asked me to describe what I noticed first. I mentioned the colours, the uneven shapes, and a small area of bright red. She then explained how the artist had explored movement and emotional tension through visual language. I did not suddenly fall in love with the painting, but I became interested in the decisions behind it.

我的朋友没有否定我的反应，而是让我描述自己最先注意到的东西。我提到了颜色、不规则的形状，以及一小块鲜红色。随后，她解释了艺术家如何通过视觉语言探索运动感和情感张力。我并没有突然爱上这幅画，但开始对作品背后的创作选择产生兴趣。

Over the following months, I visited several exhibitions and started reading about different artistic movements. I discovered that cultural context could transform my understanding of objects I had previously considered ordinary. That said, my preferences did not change in a straight line. Some celebrated works still left me indifferent, while a few modest photographs affected me deeply. My taste seemed to be expanding rather than becoming more predictable.

在接下来的几个月里，我参观了几场展览，并开始阅读有关不同艺术运动的资料。我发现，文化背景能够改变我对过去认为普通的物品的理解。话虽如此，我的偏好并没有沿着一条直线改变。一些备受赞誉的作品仍然让我无动于衷，而几张不起眼的照片却深深打动了我。我的品味似乎是在拓展，而不是变得更加可预测。

Today, I still dislike certain styles, and I no longer feel obliged to pretend otherwise. However, I have learned to separate immediate preference from thoughtful judgement. A work does not have to appeal to me personally to deserve attention. By and large, becoming more knowledgeable has made me less confident about declaring something worthless and more willing to ask questions. Perhaps that is what developing taste really means.

如今，我仍然不喜欢某些艺术风格，也不再觉得自己有义务假装喜欢它们。然而，我已经学会区分即时偏好与经过思考的判断。一件作品即使不符合我的个人喜好，也可能值得关注。总的来说，知识的增长让我不再轻易断言某件作品毫无价值，也更愿意提出问题。也许，这才是真正培养品味的意义。
