📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Open the full explanation: 5 points展开细讲：5 段讲解

Many newcomers know the nouns, account, loan, interest, but not the verbs that go with them. You open and close an account, withdraw and deposit cash, transfer money, take out a loan and pay it off. Without the verbs, every conversation becomes a description.

很多新来的人认识名词（account、loan、interest），却不会和它们搭配的动词：开户、销户是 open/close an account，取钱存钱是 withdraw/deposit cash，转账是 transfer money，贷款是 take out a loan、还清是 pay it off。没有动词，每次对话都只能绕着描述。

Account names differ between countries. In Canada, the everyday account is a chequing account; in the UK it is a current account; in the US a checking account. A savings account is the same everywhere. Use the local word at the counter and you will be understood faster.

账户名称各国不同：加拿大日常账户叫 chequing account，英国叫 current account，美国叫 checking account；savings account 各地都一样。在柜台用当地的叫法，对方懂得更快。

Credit vocabulary is where mistakes cost money. The minimum payment keeps the account in good standing but leaves interest on the rest. The due date is the last day to pay without a late payment on your record. To build credit is to create a history of paying on time, which is what a credit check looks at.

信用相关的词说错了是要花钱的。minimum payment（最低还款）能保住账户状态，但剩下的部分照样计息。due date 是不留 late payment（逾期）记录的最后一天。build credit 是建立按时还款的记录，credit check（信用审查）看的就是这个。

Interest is uncountable when it means the money: pay interest, the interest rate. Money is uncountable too: a lot of money, not many moneys. Fee and charge are countable: a monthly fee, bank charges.

interest 指利息这笔钱时不可数：pay interest、the interest rate。money 也不可数：a lot of money，不说 many moneys。fee、charge 可数：a monthly fee、bank charges。

An overdraft is permission to go below zero in your account, and it usually comes with interest or a fee. It is not the same as a credit card, and the bank will often call it overdraft protection. Read the terms; the wording is designed to sound like a service.

overdraft（透支）是允许你账户余额低于零，通常要付利息或手续费。它不等于信用卡，银行常叫它 overdraft protection（透支保护）。看清条款，这种措辞故意让它听起来像一项服务。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ I want to take some money from my account. (at the counter)
✓ I'd like to withdraw some cash.
The banking verb is withdraw.
银行用语是 withdraw。
✗ The interest is very expensive.
✓ The interest rate is very high.
A rate is high or low.
利率用 high/low。
✗ I borrowed a loan from the bank.
✓ I took out a loan from the bank.
You take out a loan; you borrow money.
贷款用 take out a loan；借钱用 borrow money。
✗ I have many moneys in the savings.
✓ I have a lot of money in my savings account.
money is uncountable.
money 不可数。
✗ I paid the loan off it last year.
✓ I paid it off last year. / I paid off the loan last year.
One object only, pronoun in the middle.
只有一个宾语，代词放中间。
