Two Years Below Ground 地下室里的两年

🧠 Learn the 3 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 3 个新词

Read a newcomer's story about living in a basement suite, and see how unreal conditionals and wish weigh up choices made years ago. 读一位新移民讲住地下室套间的故事，看非真实条件句和 wish 怎样回头掂量多年前的选择。

Our first home in Canada was a basement suite with three small windows at the level of the grass. From the kitchen I could see our landlord's tomatoes and, in winter, the boots of anyone walking to the back door. We paid much less than the apartments downtown cost, and for two years, even squeezed into two small rooms, we had exactly what we needed.

我们在加拿大的第一个家是一间地下室套间，三扇小窗和草地一样高。从厨房望出去，能看见房东种的番茄；到了冬天，能看见每个走向后门的人的靴子。我们付的房租比市中心的公寓少得多，那两年，哪怕挤在两个小房间里，我们要的也都有了。

It was not always comfortable. The ceiling was so low that my husband learned to duck without thinking, and in the first spring a heavy rain left water across the bedroom floor. If our landlord had been less decent, that flood would have turned into a long argument. Instead he came down in his slippers at midnight, apologised, and paid for the repair without being asked.

住得并不总是舒服。天花板矮得我丈夫学会了下意识低头；第一个春天，一场大雨让卧室地板上全是水。要是房东不那么厚道，那次进水早就变成一场没完没了的争执了。可他半夜穿着拖鞋下来，道了歉，没等我们开口就付了修理费。

People sometimes ask whether I regret not renting something bigger. I do not. If we had spent more on rent in those first years, we would not have been able to save for a down payment, and we would still be renting today. The basement bought us time to find jobs, learn the city and work out which neighbourhood we actually wanted.

有人问我后不后悔当初没租个大点的地方。我不后悔。要是头几年在房租上多花钱，我们就攒不下首付，到今天还在租房。那间地下室给我们换来了时间：找工作、熟悉这座城市、弄清我们到底想住哪个社区。

What I do wish is that someone had told us how to read a lease properly. I wish I had asked who paid for the heating, and whether the suite was a legal unit. We were lucky. Friends of ours rented a suite that turned out not to be registered with the city, and when the owner sold the house, they had very little time to move out.

我真正希望的，是当初有人教过我们怎么好好读租约。我真希望当时问过暖气费谁付、这个套间是不是合法单元。我们算是运气好。我们的朋友租的套间后来才发现没在市政府登记，房主卖房时，他们只有很短的时间搬走。

Now that we own a small townhouse, I sometimes miss the tomatoes. If I could give one piece of advice to a newcomer family, it would be this: a cheaper place is not a worse start. It is a cheaper start, as long as you know what you are signing.

现在我们有了一套小联排屋，有时我还会想念那些番茄。如果能给新移民家庭一句建议，那就是：便宜的住处不是更差的起点，只是更便宜的起点，只要你清楚自己签的是什么。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

If we had spent more on rent in those first years, we would not have been able to save for a down payment, and we would still be renting today.
A mixed conditional: the if part is about the past (had spent), the second result is about now (would still be renting today).
混合条件句：if 部分说过去（had spent），第二个结果说现在（would still be renting today）。
What I do wish is that someone had told us how to read a lease properly.
What I do wish is … puts the real regret at the end for emphasis; do adds contrast with I do not regret the basement.
What I do wish is … 把真正的遗憾放到句末来强调；do 和前面「不后悔住地下室」形成对比。
