/v/ and /w/ v 与 w

Keep /v/ and /w/ apart, so that very, vine and vest never come out as wery, wine and west. 把 /v/ 和 /w/ 分开，very、vine、vest 不再说成 wery、wine、west。

B1 · CELPIP 5–6⏱ 15 minP03 /v/ becomes /w/v 读成 w

📏 The rule规则

📏 The point in one line一句话要领
/v/ is made with the top teeth on the bottom lip and a buzz; /w/ uses no teeth at all, only rounded lips that open into the vowel./v/ 是上齿轻咬下唇、振声带出声；/w/ 完全不用牙齿，只靠双唇收圆再张开滑向元音。

📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Open the full explanation: 5 points展开细讲：5 段讲解

Many Chinese speakers have one sound for both letters. In Mandarin, the w of wei or wan is pronounced by some speakers with the lips rounded and by others with a light lip-teeth contact, and both count as the same sound. So the difference between vest and west feels unimportant. In English it is a full word difference: vine and wine, veil and whale, vet and wet.

很多华人用一个音同时代表这两个字母：普通话 wei、wan 里的 w，有人圆唇、有人轻碰唇齿，都算同一个音，所以 vest 和 west 的区别感觉不重要。在英语里这就是两个词。

The check is physical. Put a finger on your lower lip. For /v/ the top teeth must touch the lip and you should feel a buzz under the finger. For /w/ the teeth stay out of the way, the lips push forward into a small circle, like the start of Mandarin wu, and then spread as the vowel starts.

检查方法是身体上的。手指放在下唇上：发 /v/ 时上齿碰到下唇、手指能感到振动；发 /w/ 时牙齿不参与，双唇前突成小圆、像「乌」的开头，然后随元音展开。

The opposite error also exists: speakers who learned "v is lip-teeth" sometimes put teeth on every w, so wine becomes vine and we becomes vee. Practise the two sounds side by side rather than only drilling /v/.

也有反方向的错：学过「v 要咬唇」的人有时每个 w 都咬，wine 成了 vine、we 成了 vee。两个音要对照着练，别只练 /v/。

At the end of a word, /v/ is often weakened by learners to /f/ or dropped: I've, have, five, leave. Keep a short buzz there. It matters because I've got and I got, leave and leaf, five and fife are told apart by this ending, and the vowel before a voiced /v/ is a little longer.

词尾的 /v/ 常被弱化成 /f/ 或吞掉：I've、have、five、leave。词尾要留一点振动。I've got 与 I got、leave 与 leaf 就靠这个词尾区分，而且浊音 /v/ 前的元音稍长。

The hardest combinations put the two next to each other: vowel, view with, very well, we've waited. Slow down, then speed up while keeping the movements separate.

最难的是两个音挨着出现：vowel、very well、we've waited。先放慢，再加速，动作始终分开。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ It was wery good.
✓ It was very good.
Without teeth on the lip, very becomes a non-word; listeners notice it in the most common English intensifier.
不咬唇 very 就成了不存在的词，而它是最常用的程度副词，很显眼。
✗ We need a wet for the dog.
✓ We need a vet for the dog.
/w/ for /v/ produces a different word.
用 /w/ 代 /v/ 就成了另一个词。
✗ I want a glass of vine.
✓ I want a glass of wine.
Over-correction: no teeth for /w/.
矫枉过正：/w/ 不碰牙齿。
✗ I got it already (meaning I have it).
✓ I've got it already.
A dropped final /v/ changes the grammar the listener hears.
吞掉词尾 /v/ 会改变对方听到的语法。
