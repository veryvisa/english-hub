Nominalisation: from clause to noun phrase 名词化

Turn a verb or adjective idea into a noun phrase when you need to sum up, compare or argue in writing, and turn it back into verbs when you speak or want to be clear. 写作中需要概括、比较或论证时，把动词或形容词的意思变成名词短语；口头表达或追求清楚时再把它变回动词。

C1 · CELPIP 9+⏱ 30 minV03 Word family: the wrong part of speech词性用错V06 Register: too formal, too casual, or too blunt语域不合：太正式、太随意或太冲

👀 Look first: what changes?先看对比：差在哪里

Read the sentences and decide what each one tells you. Then open the notes.先读这几句，自己判断各自传达了什么，再点开看注解。

Prices rose sharply, so many families cut back. 物价大涨，很多家庭因此节省开支。
Spoken: verbs, clear cause.口语：动词，因果清楚。
The sharp rise in prices led many families to cut back. 物价大涨让很多家庭节省开支。
Written: one noun phrase as subject.书面：一个名词短语作主语。
The sharp rise in prices led to a reduction in household spending. 物价大涨导致家庭支出减少。
Very written: two nouns, nobody acting.很书面：两个名词，没有人在行动。

📏 The rule规则

📏 The rule in one line规则一句话
Nominalisation packs a whole clause into a noun phrase: prices rose sharply → the sharp rise in prices. It lets you make that idea the subject of the next sentence and build an argument on it. It also hides who did what, so use it for summing up, not for telling a story.名词化把一整个分句压进一个名词短语里，这样就能让这个意思当下一句的主语，在上面搭建论证。它也会把「谁做了什么」藏起来，所以用于概括，而不是讲故事。
  • Easy to miss易漏 ✗ It is very convenience to live near the station. → ✓ It is very convenient to live near the station. / The convenience of living near the station is worth the rent.

📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Open the full explanation: 5 points, a table, 13 examples展开细讲：5 段讲解、一张表、13 个例句

Speech runs on verbs: The city closed the pool, so people were angry. Writing that argues runs on nouns: The closure of the pool caused anger. The noun version turns an event into a thing you can discuss, count and compare: The closure was the third this year. That is why reports, news analysis and academic English are full of nouns ending in -tion, -ment, -ance, -ity, -al.

口语靠动词推进，论证型写作靠名词。名词版把一个事件变成可以讨论、计数、比较的「东西」。所以报告、新闻分析和学术英语里满是 -tion、-ment、-ance、-ity、-al 结尾的名词。

The process has three steps. Find the verb or adjective (rose, sharp). Change it to a noun (rise) and the adverb to an adjective (sharp). Attach the old subject with of or in, or as a possessive (the rise in prices, the city's decision). Learn which preposition goes with which noun: an increase in, a demand for, a reduction in, the cause of, a lack of, access to, a response to.

步骤有三：找到动词或形容词；把它变成名词，副词变形容词；用 of、in 或所有格把原来的主语接上。每个名词配什么介词要专门记。

Chinese learners often know the noun but use the wrong word class, especially with -tion / -tive and -ence / -ent: ✗ It is very convenience, ✗ the decide of the council. The test: after the or a you need a noun; after be or very you need an adjective. Learn word families in sets of three: decide / decision / decisive, depend / dependence / dependent, able / ability / enable.

华人常常认得名词却用错词性。判断方法：the 或 a 后面要名词，be 或 very 后面要形容词。词族三个一组地记。

Too much nominalisation makes writing heavy and evasive: The implementation of the reduction of staff was effected hides who cut whose jobs. Government letters do this, and the plain-language movement fights it. Good writers mix: a noun phrase to sum up (This delay…), then verbs with real people to explain (…meant that families waited two more months).

名词化过度会让文字沉重、躲闪，看不出谁做了什么。公文常这样，「简明语言」运动就是在反对它。好的写法是混用：先用名词短语概括，再用有真人做主语的动词句来解释。

Use nominalisation to link sentences. End one sentence with an event as a verb, and start the next with the same event as a noun: The landlord raised the rent by 15%. This increase was well above the legal limit. This is the written version of the old-before-new principle.

用名词化来衔接句子：上一句用动词讲一件事，下一句用名词形式把同一件事拿来当主语。这就是「旧信息在前」原则的书面版。

Examples例句

  • The sudden closure of the clinic left two thousand patients without a doctor. 诊所突然关门，让两千名病人没了医生。
    Event as subject: closure of.事件作主语：closure of。
  • The sharp rise in rents has pushed many students out of the city. 房租大涨把许多学生挤出了城市。
    rise in + the thing that rose.rise in 加上涨的东西。
  • The council's decision surprised almost everyone. 市议会的决定几乎让所有人意外。
    Possessive for the old subject.用所有格接原来的主语。
  • There is a growing demand for evening classes. 夜校的需求越来越大。
    demand for.demand for。
  • Their refusal to answer made the situation worse. 他们拒绝回答，让情况更糟。
    refusal to + verb.refusal to 加动词。
  • A lack of affordable childcare keeps many parents out of work. 缺少负担得起的托儿服务，让很多家长无法工作。
    a lack of.a lack of。
  • The reliability of the trains is the main reason we sold the car. 火车靠得住，是我们卖掉车的主要原因。
    Adjective reliable → noun reliability.形容词 reliable 变名词 reliability。
  • The landlord raised the rent by 15%. This increase was well above the guideline. 房东把房租提高了 15%。这次涨幅远远超过指导线。
    Verb, then noun to link.先用动词，再用名词衔接。
  • Our failure to plan for winter cost us a lot in repairs. 我们没为冬天做准备，结果修理花了不少钱。
    failure to + verb.failure to 加动词。
  • Access to a family doctor is still a problem in many towns. 在很多小镇，看家庭医生仍然是个难题。
    access to.access to。
  • The arrival of the new manager changed the mood in the office. 新经理的到来改变了办公室的气氛。
    arrive → arrival.arrive 变 arrival。
  • A reduction in bus service would hurt shift workers the most. 减少公交班次最伤倒班工人。
    reduction in.reduction in。
  • His dependence on his car became clear when it broke down. 车坏了他才看出自己多依赖车。
    depend on → dependence on.depend on 变 dependence on。

More pairs: same idea, different choice更多对比句组

The city cut the library's hours, and students lost a place to study. 市里缩短了图书馆开放时间，学生没了学习的地方。
Clear: who did what.清楚：谁做了什么。
The reduction in library hours meant a loss of study space for students. 图书馆开放时间缩短意味着学生失去学习空间。
Nominal: good for a report, hides who decided.名词化：适合报告，但隐去了决策者。
The implementation of hour reductions resulted in study space loss. 时长缩减的实施导致学习空间流失。
Over-nominalised: heavy and evasive.名词化过度：沉重而躲闪。
The bus is not reliable, so I drive. 公交不靠谱，所以我开车。
Speech.口语。
Because of the unreliability of the bus, I drive. 由于公交不可靠，我开车。
Written.书面。
The bus's unreliability is why I drive. 公交不可靠，就是我开车的原因。
Written and compact; noun as the focus.书面而紧凑；名词成为焦点。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ It is very convenience to live near the station.
✓ It is very convenient to live near the station. / The convenience of living near the station is worth the rent.
After very you need an adjective; use the noun as a subject.
very 后要形容词；名词适合作主语。
✗ The decide of the council was unfair.
✓ The decision of the council was unfair. / The council's decision was unfair.
After the you need the noun decision.
the 后要名词 decision。
✗ There was an increase of rents last year.
✓ There was an increase in rents last year.
increase in the thing; increase of + amount: an increase of 10%.
increase in 后接上涨的东西，increase of 后接数量。
✗ The lack of money was a problem for the reason of the closure.
✓ The lack of money led to the closure. / The closure happened because there was no money.
Too many nouns; use a verb to show the cause.
名词太多；用动词显示因果。
✗ His depend on the car was a problem.
✓ His dependence on the car was a problem.
depend (verb) → dependence (noun).
动词 depend 变名词 dependence。
