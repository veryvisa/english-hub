Reflexive pronouns, each other and generic you 反身代词、each other 与泛指的 you

📘 In your coursebook: English File 5e · Advanced · 2B “Spellcheck”. Use the texts and recordings with your coursebook; this page adds new words, grammar and practice.教材定位：English File 5e · Advanced · 2B《Spellcheck》。课文与录音请配合教材学习，本页补充新词、语法与练习。

Choose correctly between reflexive pronouns, each other, and personal pronouns, and use generic you (or one, we, they) with the right tone and reference. 能在反身代词、each other 和普通人称代词之间做出正确选择，并恰当使用泛指的 you（以及 one、we、they）。

The students introduced themselves to the teacher. 学生们向老师介绍了自己。
Each student introduced his or her own self; the action is directed back at the subject.每个学生介绍的是他自己；动作回到了主语身上。
The students introduced each other to the teacher. 学生们向老师互相介绍了对方。
The students introduced one another; the action goes between different people in the group.学生们介绍的是彼此；动作发生在群体中不同的人之间。
The students introduced them to the teacher. 学生们把他们介绍给了老师。
The pronoun refers to some other people, not the students.代词指的是另外一些人，而不是学生本人。

📏 The rule规则

📏 The rule in one line规则一句话
A reflexive pronoun is used only when the object is the same person as the subject, each other when two or more people act on one another, and generic you when the speaker means people in general rather than one specific listener.只有宾语与主语是同一个人时才用反身代词；多人互相作用时用 each other；说话人指泛泛的人而不是具体听话人时用泛指的 you。

📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Choose a reflexive pronoun when the subject and the object of the same clause are the same person: She blamed herself. If the object is a different person, or if the pronoun sits inside a prepositional phrase that describes location or accompaniment, English often prefers a plain object pronoun: She put the bag next to her, not next to herself. A useful test is whether the pronoun could possibly refer to someone else; if it could, you need the plain pronoun.

当同一个分句中的主语和宾语是同一个人时，用反身代词：She blamed herself。如果宾语是另一个人，或者代词出现在表示位置、陪伴的介词短语中，英语通常更倾向于用普通宾格代词：She put the bag next to her，而不是 next to herself。一个简单的检验方法是：这个代词有没有可能指别人？如果有可能，就要用普通代词。

Reflexives are also used for emphasis, and in that role they can be dropped without changing the grammar: I fixed it myself means that nobody helped me. Compare this with I fixed myself, which is a different and odd statement. Learners often confuse the emphatic use with the true object use, so ask whether the pronoun receives the action or only stresses who performed it.

反身代词还可以用来强调，这时即使去掉，句子语法也不受影响：I fixed it myself 的意思是没人帮我。把它和 I fixed myself 对比，后者意思完全不同而且很奇怪。学习者常把强调用法和真正的宾语用法混淆，所以要问：这个代词是承受动作，还是仅仅强调动作的执行者。

Use each other (or one another, which is slightly more formal) when two or more people each do the action to the other: They help each other. The structure is not the same as a reflexive plural, since They hurt themselves says that each person hurt his or her own self, while They hurt each other says that they hurt one another. Each other is also not a subject, so Each other love is impossible, and it needs a preposition where the verb requires one: talk to each other, not talk each other.

当两个或更多的人彼此对对方做出同一个动作时，用 each other（one another 稍微正式一些）：They help each other。这和复数反身代词不同：They hurt themselves 说的是每个人伤了自己，而 They hurt each other 说的是彼此伤害。each other 也不能做主语，所以 Each other love 是不可能的；动词需要介词时要带上介词：talk to each other，而不是 talk each other。

Generic you lets the speaker describe what happens to people in general, and it sounds natural and conversational: You never know what will happen. Choose one for a formal or written style, but be aware that it can sound stiff or old-fashioned in speech. Choose we when the speaker wants to include himself or herself in the group, and they when the group is other people, such as an institution or a different community. The trap is that generic you can unintentionally sound like advice or accusation to the actual listener, so switch to one or people when you want to avoid that.

泛指的 you 让说话人描述一般人的情况，听起来自然、口语化：You never know what will happen。正式或书面风格可以选 one，但要注意在口语中它可能显得生硬或过时。想把说话人自己包括在内时用 we，指其他人（比如机构或别的群体）时用 they。陷阱在于，泛指的 you 可能无意中让真正的听话人觉得是在给建议或指责，想避免这种情况时就改用 one 或 people。

Some verbs that take a reflexive in Chinese do not take one in English, and the reverse is also true. Verbs such as relax, concentrate, behave, and feel are not normally followed by a reflexive when the meaning is simply that the person does it: Relax, I feel tired. By contrast, some verbs need a reflexive when the object is the subject: enjoy yourself, help yourself, introduce yourself. Check the verb itself rather than translating word by word from Chinese.

有些汉语中要带反身成分的动词，英语里不带，反过来也有这种情况。像 relax、concentrate、behave、feel 这类动词，如果意思只是某人做这件事，通常不接反身代词：Relax, I feel tired。相反，有些动词在宾语就是主语时需要反身代词：enjoy yourself、help yourself、introduce yourself。要检查动词本身，而不是从汉语逐字翻译。

  • She wrapped a blanket around herself and sat by the window. 她用毯子裹住自己，坐在窗边。
  • The machine switches itself off after ten minutes of inactivity. 这台机器在闲置十分钟后会自动关机。

  • If you cannot make a decision, you can end up wasting a lot of time. 如果你无法做出决定，最终可能会浪费很多时间。
  • The neighbours do not talk to one another any more. 邻居们之间不再说话了。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ I feel myself very tired today.
✓ I feel very tired today.
Feel as a linking verb describing a state does not take a reflexive; the reflexive is only used when feel takes an object, as in I could feel myself shaking.
feel 作系动词表示状态时不接反身代词；只有 feel 带宾语时才用，例如 I could feel myself shaking。
✗ We talked each other for hours.
✓ We talked to each other for hours.
The verb talk needs the preposition to before each other, because each other is the object of the preposition, not of the verb directly.
动词 talk 后面的 each other 需要介词 to，因为 each other 是介词的宾语，不是动词的直接宾语。
✗ The two sisters look at themselves and laughed. (meaning they looked at one another)
✓ The two sisters looked at each other and laughed.
Themselves means each sister looked at her own reflection, whereas each other means they looked at one another.
themselves 表示每位姐妹看的是自己的映像，而 each other 才表示她们互相看对方。
✗ I concentrated myself on my homework.
✓ I concentrated on my homework.
Concentrate is not normally used with a reflexive pronoun when the meaning is simply that the person focuses; the reflexive form is a common Chinese-influenced error.
当意思只是某人集中注意力时，concentrate 通常不接反身代词；加上反身代词是常见的受汉语影响的错误。
✗ He put the book beside himself.
✓ He put the book beside him.
After a preposition of location, English normally uses the plain object pronoun, since the person and the book are in different positions; the reflexive would sound like an unusual emphasis.
在表示位置的介词后，英语通常用普通宾格代词，因为人和书处于不同位置；用反身代词会显得强调得很奇怪。
✗ One should always trust your instincts.
✓ One should always trust one's instincts.
Once you start with one, you should stay with one and one's; mixing in your makes the reference unclear and the register inconsistent.
一旦用 one 开头，就要一直用 one 和 one's；混入 your 会使指代不清、语体不一致。
