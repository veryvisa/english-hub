Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

A new survey has found that the so-called generation gap may be smaller than most people think. Researchers asked three age groups about money, work and family life, and the answers overlapped far more often than they expected. The study, which was carried out online over several months, included people from cities and small towns.

一项新调查发现，所谓的代沟可能比大多数人想象的要小。研究人员询问了三个年龄组关于金钱、工作和家庭生活的看法，答案重合的频率远超他们的预期。这项研究在线上进行了数月，参与者来自城市和小城镇。

Young adults and their grandparents, in particular, agreed that having a stable home matters more than owning expensive things. Many of the older people said they had grown up with very little, yet they did not look down on younger people who rent for years. ‘Life moves quickly,’ one retired teacher said. ‘We have to keep up with it.’

尤其是年轻人和他们的祖父母，都认为拥有一个稳定的家比拥有昂贵的东西更重要。许多老人说他们小时候家里一贫如洗，但他们并不看不起那些多年租房的年轻人。“生活变化得很快，”一位退休教师说，“我们得跟上它。”

There were differences, of course. Younger respondents were far more likely to say they feel under pressure at work, and they tended to worry about housing costs. Older respondents, on the other hand, were more concerned about being left behind by technology. What surprised the team most was how often young people said they would ask their parents for advice.

当然也有差异。年轻受访者更有可能表示工作压力大，也更倾向于担心住房成本。另一方面，年长受访者更担心被科技甩在后面。最让研究团队意外的是，年轻人竟然经常说会向父母寻求建议。

The researchers say the findings suggest that families should spend less time arguing about differences and more time looking for common ground. They plan to repeat the study next year with a larger group of participants. One researcher added that the results should be read with care, because people do not always describe their own views accurately.

研究人员表示，调查结果表明，家人应该少花时间争论分歧，多花时间寻找共同点。他们计划明年用更大的参与者群体重复这项研究。一位研究人员补充说，解读结果时应当谨慎，因为人们并不总能准确描述自己的观点。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

A new survey has found that the so-called generation gap may be smaller than most people think.
The present perfect ‘has found’ introduces a that-clause, and the modal ‘may be’ makes the claim cautious.
现在完成时 has found 后接 that 从句，情态动词 may be 让结论显得谨慎。
Older respondents, on the other hand, were more concerned about being left behind by technology.
’On the other hand’ marks a contrast, and ‘concerned about’ is followed by a passive gerund, ‘being left behind’.
on the other hand 表示对比，concerned about 后接被动动名词 being left behind。
