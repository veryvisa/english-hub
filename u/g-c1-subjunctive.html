The subjunctive: I suggest he go 虚拟式

Use the base-form subjunctive after verbs and adjectives of demand and importance (suggest that he go, it is essential that she be), and know where British English prefers should and where plain English avoids both. 在表示要求、重要性的动词和形容词后用原形虚拟式（suggest that he go、it is essential that she be）；并知道英式英语何时偏好 should，什么时候简明英语两者都不用。

Bare subjunctive: formal, North American.原形虚拟式：正式，北美用法。
I suggest that she should call the clinic. 我建议她应当给诊所打电话。
should: common in British English.should：英式英语常见。

Maybe she could call the clinic? 要不她给诊所打个电话？

📏 The rule规则

📏 The rule in one line规则一句话
After words that demand or stress importance (suggest, recommend, insist, require, essential, vital), the that clause uses the bare verb for every person: I suggest that he call the office. No -s, no past tense, and be instead of is/are. British English often uses should instead; everyday speech often avoids the clause altogether.在表示要求或强调重要性的词（suggest、recommend、insist、require、essential、vital）之后，that 从句里各人称都用动词原形：不加 -s，不用过去式，be 代替 is/are。英式英语常用 should 代替；日常口语则常干脆不用这个从句。
  • Easy to miss易漏 ✗ I suggest you to call the bank. → ✓ I suggest that you call the bank. / I suggest calling the bank.

The subjunctive is invisible most of the time: I suggest that they call looks like the normal present. It only shows with third person singular (that he call, not calls), with be (that she be present), with the negative (that he not drive), and after a past main verb (We insisted that he stay, not stayed). These are the places to watch.

虚拟式大多数时候看不出来，只有四处显形：第三人称单数不加 -s；用 be；否定直接加 not；主句是过去时，从句也不变过去式。

It belongs to formal and official English: rules, decisions, recommendations, minutes of meetings, letters from lawyers and offices. The committee recommends that the school be closed for repairs. The landlord requires that all tenants give 30 days' notice. In a formal complaint it makes you sound precise and serious.

它属于正式和公文英语：规章、决定、建议、会议纪要、律师和机构的信。在正式投诉里用它显得严谨、郑重。

British English often replaces it with should + verb: I suggest that he should call the office. Both are correct in Britain; American and Canadian official writing prefers the bare form. What is not correct in careful writing is the ordinary present after these verbs when the meaning is a demand: ✗ I insist that he pays is common in British speech but marked as informal.

英式英语常用 should 加动词替代，两种都对；北美公文更偏好原形。认真写作时，表示要求的语境里用普通现在时（insist that he pays）会被视为口语化。

In conversation, most people step around the structure: I suggest calling the office, You should call the office, We want him to stay, It's important for everyone to come. These are often better choices when you speak. Note: suggest never takes to: ✗ I suggest you to call.

日常对话里大多数人会绕开这个结构，改用 suggest + -ing、should、want sb to、important for sb to。这些在口语里往往是更好的选择。注意 suggest 后面不能接 sb to do。

Watch the double meaning of insist and suggest. He insisted that she was right (indicative) reports a belief: he said firmly that she was right. He insisted that she be on time (subjunctive) reports a demand. The report suggests that prices are rising is a claim; The report suggests that prices be capped is a recommendation.

注意 insist 和 suggest 的双重意思：后接陈述语气是在说一个看法，后接虚拟式是在提要求。报告 suggests that prices are rising 是一种说法；suggests that prices be capped 是一个建议。

A few fixed phrases keep the old subjunctive: if need be, be that as it may, come what may, so be it, as it were, and if I were. Use them as wholes.

少数固定短语保留了古老的虚拟式，整句记住使用即可。

TriggerBare subjunctiveBritish alternativeSpoken alternative
suggestI suggest that he call.…that he should call.I suggest calling. / He should call.
recommendWe recommend that she be seen today.…that she should be seen…She should be seen today.
insist (demand)They insisted that he stay.…that he should stay.They made him stay.
requireThe rules require that each tenant give notice.…should give…Each tenant has to give notice.
essentialIt is essential that everyone be on time.…should be…Everyone needs to be on time.
negativeWe ask that you not park here.…that you should not park…Please don't park here.

Examples例句

  • I suggest that he call the office before Friday. 我建议他周五前给办公室打电话。
    call, not calls.用 call，不用 calls。
  • The doctor recommended that she be seen by a specialist. 医生建议她去看专科。
    be seen: passive subjunctive.be seen：被动虚拟式。
  • We insisted that the landlord fix the heater within a week. 我们坚持要求房东一周内修好暖气。
    Past main verb, but fix stays bare.主句过去时，fix 仍用原形。
  • The rules require that every tenant give 30 days' notice. 规定要求每位租客提前 30 天通知。
    Official English.公文英语。
  • It is essential that the form be signed by both parents. 表格必须由父母双方签字。
    It is essential that … be.it is essential that … be。
  • It is vital that nobody enter the building until it is inspected. 在检查完之前，任何人都绝对不能进入大楼。
    enter, not enters.用 enter，不用 enters。
  • We ask that you not leave bags in the hallway. 请勿把袋子放在走廊。
    Negative: not + bare verb.否定：not 加原形。
  • The committee proposed that the pool remain open in August. 委员会提议泳池八月继续开放。
    remain, bare.remain 用原形。
  • The school asked that each child bring a water bottle. 学校要求每个孩子带一个水瓶。
    bring, not brings.用 bring。
  • I suggest that he should apply again next year. 我建议他明年再申请一次。
    British should version.英式 should 版本。
  • I'd suggest applying again next year. 我建议明年再申请一次。
    Spoken: suggest + -ing.口语：suggest 加 -ing。
  • It's important for everyone to arrive by eight. 大家八点前到很重要。
    Spoken: for + person + to.口语：for 加人再加 to。
  • Our recommendation is that the program be extended for a year. 我们的建议是该项目延长一年。
    Noun + that + subjunctive.名词加 that 加虚拟式。
  • We will wait until Monday and, if need be, call a lawyer. 我们等到周一，必要时再找律师。
    Fixed phrase if need be.固定短语 if need be。

More pairs: same idea, different choice更多对比句组

He insisted that she was right. 他坚持说她是对的。
Indicative: a belief he stated firmly.陈述语气：他坚持的看法。
He insisted that she be on time. 他坚决要求她准时。
Subjunctive: a demand.虚拟式：一个要求。
He insisted on her being on time. 他坚持要她准时。
insist on + -ing: the same demand, less formal.insist on 加 -ing：同样是要求，没那么正式。
It is essential that every visitor sign in. 每位访客都必须登记。
Formal notice.正式告示。
Every visitor must sign in. 访客必须登记。
Plain rule.简明的规定。
Please sign in when you arrive. 到达时请登记。
Friendly instruction.友好的提示。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ I suggest you to call the bank.
✓ I suggest that you call the bank. / I suggest calling the bank.
suggest never takes object + to.
suggest 后不接宾语加 to。
✗ The doctor recommended that he takes a week off.
✓ The doctor recommended that he take a week off.
Bare form for every person.
各人称都用原形。
✗ It is important that she is there on time.
✓ It is important that she be there on time. / It is important for her to be there on time.
In formal writing, be; in speech, the for … to pattern.
正式写作用 be；口语用 for … to。
✗ We insisted that he stayed for dinner.
✓ We insisted that he stay for dinner.
The past main verb does not change the subjunctive.
主句过去时不影响虚拟式。
✗ We ask that you don't park here.
✓ We ask that you not park here.
Negative subjunctive: not + verb, no do.
虚拟式否定：not 加动词，不用 do。

Write a three-sentence request to a building manager, school or city office about a real problem: one sentence with I request that… or We ask that…, one with It is essential that…, and a final plain sentence explaining why. Then say the same request to a friend in one spoken sentence without the subjunctive.
