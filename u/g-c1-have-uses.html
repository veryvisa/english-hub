have: main verb, auxiliary and fixed phrases have 的实义、助动与固定用法

Choose accurately among have as a main verb, an auxiliary verb, and part of a fixed expression according to the intended meaning, tense, emphasis, and level of formality. 能够根据表达意图、时态、强调重点和正式程度，准确选择 have 作为实义动词、助动词或固定表达的一部分。

I have a new laptop. 我有一台新笔记本电脑。
Have expresses possession; the laptop belongs to the speaker.have 表示拥有，笔记本电脑属于说话者。
I have bought a new laptop. 我已经买了一台新笔记本电脑。
Have is an auxiliary; the purchase is presented as a completed event relevant to the present.have 是助动词，强调购买已经完成，并与现在有关。
I have to buy a new laptop. 我必须买一台新笔记本电脑。
Have to expresses necessity or obligation rather than possession or a completed purchase.have to 表示必要性或义务，而不是拥有或已经完成购买。

The key is to distinguish whether have carries its own meaning, supports another verb to express a perfect relationship, or belongs to an established expression whose meaning cannot be worked out by translating have literally.关键在于判断 have 是独立承担词义、辅助其他动词表达完成时关系，还是属于一个不能简单按字面翻译的固定表达。

📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Open the full explanation: 6 points, 24 examples展开细讲：6 段讲解、24 个例句

Choose have as a main verb when possession, relationships, characteristics, obligations, or experiences are the actual information you want to communicate. In these cases, have carries lexical meaning and normally forms questions and negatives with do in ordinary present and past simple sentences. Do not add do when have is functioning as an auxiliary in a perfect construction.

当你要表达拥有、关系、特征、义务或经历时，选择实义动词 have。此时 have 本身承担词义，在一般现在时和一般过去时的普通句子中，疑问句和否定句通常需要借助 do。不要把这种 do 的规则套用到完成时助动词 have 上。

Use have as an auxiliary when you want to connect an earlier event or experience with a later reference point. The present perfect is particularly useful when the past event matters to the present, rather than when you simply want to locate an event at a finished time in the past. Compare I have lost my keys with I lost my keys yesterday.

当你需要把较早发生的事件或经历与后来的参照时间联系起来时，使用助动词 have。现在完成时尤其适合表达过去事件对现在仍有意义的情况，而不是单纯定位一个已经结束的过去时间。比较 I have lost my keys 和 I lost my keys yesterday。

In perfect constructions, have is followed by a past participle, not an ordinary past-tense form. This distinction becomes especially important with irregular verbs, because forms such as gone, written, and taken may look very different from their past-tense counterparts. The auxiliary changes according to the subject and tense, while the participle remains in its required form.

在完成时结构中，have 后面必须接过去分词，而不是普通过去式。对于不规则动词，这一点尤其重要，因为 gone、written 和 taken 等形式与对应的过去式可能差异很大。助动词根据主语和时态变化，而后面的动词必须使用规定的过去分词形式。

Use have to when you want to express an obligation that often comes from circumstances, rules, or practical necessity. Unlike must, have to makes the obligation sound more externally imposed in many contexts. Be particularly careful with the negative: do not have to means something is unnecessary, whereas must not means something is prohibited.

当义务来自客观情况、规定或实际需要时，通常使用 have to。与 must 相比，have to 在许多语境中更突出外部要求。尤其要注意否定形式：do not have to 表示没有必要，而 must not 表示禁止。

Use have got to in conversational English to express a strong obligation or necessity, especially when speaking about the present or near future. It is generally less suitable for formal writing and extended descriptions of repeated obligations. In contrast, have got can also express possession, as in I have got two sisters.

在口语中，have got to 常用于表达强烈的义务或必要性，尤其是涉及现在或近期未来的情况。它通常不太适合正式写作或描述长期反复发生的义务。另一方面，have got 也可以表达拥有，例如 I have got two sisters。

Choose fixed expressions with have when the verb combines with a noun to describe an activity, experience, or social interaction. Expressions such as have a look, have a chat, and have a break often describe what someone does rather than what someone possesses. Their natural meanings are usually learned as complete combinations.

当 have 与名词组合，用来表达活动、经历或社交互动时，应优先考虑固定搭配。例如 have a look、have a chat 和 have a break 描述的是某人做什么，而不是拥有某种东西。这些表达通常需要作为完整搭配来学习。

More pairs: same idea, different choice更多对比句组

She has three meetings today. 她今天有三个会议。
Has is a main verb indicating that meetings are scheduled for her.has 是实义动词，表示她今天安排有三个会议。
She has attended three meetings today. 她今天已经参加了三个会议。
Has is an auxiliary in the present perfect, emphasizing completed meetings within the current day.has 是现在完成时的助动词，强调今天已经完成的会议。
She has to attend three meetings today. 她今天必须参加三个会议。
Has to expresses an obligation to attend the meetings.has to 表示她有义务参加这些会议。
I do not have enough money. 我没有足够的钱。
Do not have negates possession or availability.do not have 否定拥有或可用性。
I have not saved enough money. 我还没有存够钱。
Have not saved expresses an unfinished or incomplete action using the present perfect.have not saved 使用现在完成时，表示存钱这一行动尚未完成。
I do not have to save more money. 我不必再存更多钱了。
Do not have to removes the obligation to save more.do not have to 表示没有继续存钱的义务。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ Do you have ever visited Canada?
✓ Have you ever visited Canada?
When have is the auxiliary in the present perfect, it moves before the subject to form a question. Do is not added to this construction.
当 have 是现在完成时的助动词时，疑问句中需要将 have 提到主语之前，不能再添加 do。
✗ I have went to the office.
✓ I have gone to the office.
The present perfect requires a past participle after have. Went is the simple past form, while gone is the past participle.
现在完成时要求 have 后面接过去分词。went 是一般过去式，gone 才是过去分词。
✗ She don't have a driving licence.
✓ She doesn't have a driving licence.
When have expresses possession in the simple present, third-person singular subjects require does not. The main verb remains in its base form.
当 have 表示拥有时，第三人称单数主语需要使用 does not，后面的实义动词保持原形。
✗ I haven't to work tomorrow.
✓ I don't have to work tomorrow.
In ordinary contemporary English, have to forms its negative with do-support. Haven't to is not the normal standard construction.
在现代标准英语中，have to 的否定形式通常借助 do 构成。haven't to 不是常规的标准表达。
✗ You don't have to smoke here.
✓ You must not smoke here.
If the intended meaning is prohibition, use must not. Do not have to means that smoking is unnecessary, not that it is forbidden.
如果想表达禁止，应使用 must not。do not have to 表示没有必要做某事，并不表示禁止。
✗ I have a look to the report before the meeting.
✓ I have a look at the report before the meeting.
Fixed expressions with have usually take a specific preposition; have a look is followed by at, not to. Learners sometimes transfer the preposition from a different fixed expression.
have 的固定搭配通常搭配特定的介词：have a look 后接 at，而不是 to。学习者有时会把别的固定搭配里的介词搬过来用。
