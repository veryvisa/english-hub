Why Populations Grow Older 人口为什么会变老

🧠 Learn the 4 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 4 个新词

Populations age because life expectancy rises while birth rates fall, and this slow, predictable shift can be measured and planned for. 人口老龄化是因为预期寿命上升而出生率下降，这种缓慢而可预测的转变可以被测量，也可以提前规划。

Why are populations ageing at all? Two forces work together. First, life expectancy has risen because of cleaner water, better nutrition, vaccines and improved treatment of heart disease and infection. Second, couples in most wealthy countries now have fewer children than their parents did. This combination is new in human history.

人口为什么会老龄化？有两股力量共同起作用。第一，由于饮水更清洁、营养更好、有了疫苗，心脏病和感染的治疗也有所改进，预期寿命提高了。第二，大多数富裕国家的夫妇如今生育的孩子比父母那一代少。这种组合在人类历史上是前所未有的。

Demographers capture this with a simple picture. Imagine a population drawn as a pyramid, with many young people at the base and few old people at the top. As birth rates fall and survival improves, the base narrows and the top widens, until the shape looks more like a column or even an upside-down pyramid. Many countries are somewhere along this path.

人口学家用一幅简单的图来描述这一点。想象把人口画成一座金字塔，底部是众多年轻人，顶部是少数老年人。随着出生率下降、存活率提高，底部变窄、顶部变宽，直到形状更像一根柱子，甚至一座倒置的金字塔。许多国家正走在这条路上的某个阶段。

Why does this matter? Economists often track the dependency ratio, which compares the number of people who are usually outside paid work, such as children and retirees, with those of working age. A rising ratio tends to mean that each worker supports more people, although the picture changes if more older adults stay in employment.

这为什么重要？经济学家常常追踪抚养比，即通常不在有薪工作中的人（如儿童和退休者）与劳动年龄人口之比。比值上升往往意味着每位劳动者要供养更多人，不过如果有更多老年人继续就业，情况就会改变。

Biology adds a further layer. Ageing is not a single disease but a gradual accumulation of cellular damage, and researchers are still debating how far it can be slowed. On the whole, current evidence suggests that healthy years can be extended, but there is no sign that human lifespan is about to be rewritten.

生物学又增添了一层。衰老不是单一的疾病，而是细胞损伤逐渐累积的过程，研究者仍在争论它能被延缓到什么程度。总体而言，目前的证据表明健康的岁月可以延长，但没有迹象表明人类寿命上限即将被改写。

For societies, the practical lesson is that an ageing population is a slow, predictable process rather than a sudden shock. That predictability is an advantage, since planners can see the changes coming decades ahead. The difficulty is not knowledge but the political will to act while the pressure still seems distant. Some already shoulder the burden of a very elderly population, while others are only beginning.

对社会而言，实际的启示是：人口老龄化是一个缓慢且可预测的过程，而不是突然的冲击。这种可预测性是一种优势，因为规划者可以提前几十年看到变化的到来。困难不在于知识，而在于在压力看似遥远时就采取行动的政治意愿。有些国家已经在承担非常高龄的人口所带来的负担，而另一些才刚刚开始。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

As birth rates fall and survival improves, the base narrows and the top widens, until the shape looks more like a column or even an upside-down pyramid.
The sentence uses a time clause with ‘As’ for parallel changes, followed by ‘until’ to show the end result of those changes.
句子用 As 引导的时间状语从句表示同时发生的变化，再用 until 引出这些变化的最终结果。
A rising ratio tends to mean that each worker supports more people, although the picture changes if more older adults stay in employment.
’Tends to’ hedges the main claim, and the ‘although’ clause adds a qualification that contains its own ‘if’ condition.
tends to 对主句论断加以限定，although 从句补充了一个保留条件，其中又嵌套了 if 条件句。
