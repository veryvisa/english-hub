Easy to Start, Hard to Stop 开通容易取消难

🧠 Learn the 5 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 5 个新词

Read a news report about hard-to-cancel subscriptions and see how the present perfect reports a trend that is still going on. 读一篇关于难以取消的订阅的新闻，看现在完成时怎样报道仍在持续的趋势。

Read and listen读与听

262 words
Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Complaints about subscriptions that are easy to start but hard to cancel have risen sharply over the past year, according to a report released on Monday by a national consumer group. The group said it has received more complaints about cancellations than about any other single issue since it began keeping records.

据一个全国性消费者团体周一发布的报告，过去一年里，关于「开通容易、取消难」的订阅的投诉急剧增加。该团体说，自从它开始做记录以来，关于取消订阅的投诉比任何其他单一问题都多。

Many of the complaints describe a familiar pattern. A customer signs up for a free trial online in two clicks, forgets about it, and is charged when the trial ends. When they try to cancel, the button is hidden several pages deep, or they are told they must phone during office hours. "I have spent longer cancelling a streaming service than I spent choosing it," one woman told researchers.

许多投诉讲的是同一个熟悉的套路：顾客在网上点两下就开通了免费试用，转头忘了，试用一结束就被扣钱。等他们想取消时，按钮藏在好几层页面之后，或者被告知只能在办公时间打电话。「取消一个视频订阅花的时间，比我当初挑它的时间还长，」一位女士对研究人员说。

Several governments have already introduced or proposed "click to cancel" rules, which require cancelling to be as easy as signing up. Industry groups have argued that some of these rules go too far, and that customers sometimes cancel by mistake. The report suggests that clearer reminders before a trial ends may help both sides.

一些国家的政府已经出台或提议了「一键取消」规定，要求取消和开通一样容易。行业团体则认为，有些规定管得太宽，而且顾客有时会误取消。报告认为，在试用结束前给出更清楚的提醒，可能对双方都有好处。

The group's advice for consumers has not changed. Put the end date of every free trial in your calendar, read the fine print before you enter card details, and check your statements every month. If a company makes cancelling difficult, keep a record of every attempt, with dates and screenshots, and ask your card provider whether it can help.

该团体给消费者的建议一直没变：把每个免费试用的到期日记进日历，输入卡号之前读一读小字条款，每月核对账单。如果一家公司让取消变得很难，就把每一次尝试都记下来，附上日期和截图，再问问发卡机构能不能帮忙。

The report's authors admit that their figures only include people who chose to complain. The real number of unwanted subscriptions, they said, is likely to be much higher.

报告作者承认，他们的数字只包括选择了投诉的人。他们说，真正不想要的订阅数量可能要高得多。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

The group said it has received more complaints about cancellations than about any other single issue since it began keeping records.
has received … since it began: present perfect for the period up to now, past simple for the starting point. In reported news, has (not had) shows the situation is still true.
has received … since it began：现在完成时表示到现在为止的一段时间，一般过去时表示起点。新闻转述里用 has 而不是 had，说明情况至今仍是如此。
"I have spent longer cancelling a streaming service than I spent choosing it," one woman told researchers.
The same verb twice: have spent (her experience up to now, still annoying) and spent (a finished event in the past).
同一个动词用了两次：have spent（到现在为止的经历，仍让人恼火），spent（过去已完成的事）。
