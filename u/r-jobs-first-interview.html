The Interview I Prepared for Wrongly 那场准备错了方向的面试

🧠 Learn the 5 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 5 个新词

Read a story about preparing for the wrong kind of interview, and use unreal conditionals to talk about what you would do differently. 读一个面试准备错了方向的故事，用虚拟条件句说如果重来会怎么做。

B2 · CELPIP 7–8⏱ 20 minG07 Unreal conditionals and wish虚拟条件句与 wishV12 Hedging is missing; claims sound absolute不会留余地，话说得太满

📖 Key card知识卡
Many interviews in English-speaking countries ask for stories, not facts: prepare short accounts of what you did and what happened.英语国家的很多面试要的是故事而不是事实：准备几段简短的经历，讲你做了什么、结果如何。

Read and listen读与听

275 words
Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

The call came on a Tuesday: I was on the shortlist for an analyst job, and the interview was on Friday. I spent three evenings memorising my résumé. I could recite every date, every job title, every number. I thought that was what preparation meant.

电话是周二打来的：我进了一个分析师职位的候选名单，周五面试。我花了三个晚上背自己的简历，每个日期、每个职位名称、每个数字都能张口就来。我以为准备就是这个意思。

The hiring manager did not ask about a single date. She said, "Tell me about a time you had to deal with a difficult client." Then: "What would you do if two managers gave you opposite instructions?" I had no stories ready. I gave general answers, like "I always try to communicate clearly", and I could see the interviewer on her left stop writing.

用人经理一个日期都没问。她说：「讲一次你不得不应付难缠客户的经历。」接着问：「如果两位经理给你相反的指示，你会怎么做？」我一个故事都没准备。我给的都是泛泛的回答，比如「我总是尽量沟通清楚」，我看得见她左边那位面试官停下了笔。

If I had known what kind of interview it was, I would have prepared stories, not facts. Many employers here use behavioural questions. They believe that the way you dealt with a problem in the past is the best guide to how you will deal with the next one. A good answer is a short story: the situation, what you did, and the result. If I were doing that interview today, I would bring up the time I sorted out a billing error for an angry customer and kept the account.

要是我当时知道那是什么样的面试，我就会准备故事，而不是事实。这里很多雇主用行为面试题。他们相信，你过去怎么处理问题，最能预示你下一次会怎么处理。好的回答是一个短故事：当时的情况、你做了什么、结果怎样。如果今天让我再面一次，我会提起那次帮一位愤怒的客户解决账单错误、最后保住了这个客户的事。

I did not get that job. But I came up with a system for the next one: six stories, each on its own card, each showing one quality the posting asked for. I rehearsed them aloud until they sounded like talking, not reading. Two interviews later, I got a job offer. I wish someone had told me sooner that the interviewer already has your résumé. What they want is to hear you think.

那份工作我没拿到。但我为下一次想出了一套办法：六个故事，各写一张卡片，每个故事对应启事里要求的一种品质。我把它们大声练到听起来像在说话、而不是在念稿。又面了两次，我拿到了录用通知。真希望早点有人告诉我：面试官手里已经有你的简历了，他们想听的是你怎么思考。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

They believe that the way you dealt with a problem in the past is the best guide to how you will deal with the next one.
The subject of the that clause is long: the way you dealt with a problem in the past. Its verb is is.
that 从句的主语很长：the way you dealt with a problem in the past，谓语是 is。
If I were doing that interview today, I would bring up the time I sorted out a billing error for an angry customer and kept the account.
Unreal present with were doing. the time (that) I sorted out… is a relative clause without that.
与现在事实相反，用 were doing。the time I sorted out… 是省略了 that 的定语从句。
