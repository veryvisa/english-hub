📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Open the full explanation: 5 points展开细讲：5 段讲解

English distinguishes carefully between cooking methods. Food can be grilled, roasted, baked, fried or steamed, and these verbs are not interchangeable. For example, we usually roast vegetables in an oven, grill meat over direct heat, and steam fish using hot water vapour.

英语对烹饪方式有比较明确的区分。食物可以用 grilled、roasted、baked、fried 或 steamed 等方式制作，这些词不能随意互换。例如，蔬菜通常用烤箱烘烤，肉类可以直接炙烤，鱼则可以用蒸汽蒸熟。

Food descriptions often combine an adjective with a particular noun. Common combinations include tender meat, crispy skin, fresh ingredients, rich sauce and mild flavour. Chinese learners sometimes use delicious too frequently, but more precise adjectives make descriptions sound more natural and informative.

描述食物时，英语经常使用形容词与特定名词搭配，例如嫩滑的肉、酥脆的外皮、新鲜的食材、浓郁的酱汁和清淡的味道。中文学习者有时过度使用 delicious，其实使用更具体的形容词能让表达更加自然、准确。

When eating out, several useful chunks help you communicate politely. You can ask for a table, make a reservation, order a dish, ask for the bill or request a takeaway. Notice that we normally say make a reservation rather than do a reservation.

在外面吃饭时，有一些实用短语可以帮助你礼貌地交流。你可以询问是否有座位、预订餐桌、点菜、要求结账或打包带走。注意，英语通常说 make a reservation，而不是 do a reservation。

Some food-related words have different meanings depending on context. A dish can mean a prepared meal or a physical plate, while a course refers to one part of a meal, such as a starter or dessert. A meal is the complete eating occasion, such as breakfast, lunch or dinner.

一些食物相关词汇会根据语境产生不同含义。dish 可以表示一道菜，也可以表示餐盘；course 指一顿饭中的一道，例如前菜或甜点；meal 则表示一餐，例如早餐、午餐或晚餐。

English uses countable and uncountable nouns differently when discussing food. Ingredients such as rice, bread, cheese and meat are generally uncountable. To refer to individual portions, use expressions such as a bowl of rice, a slice of bread, a piece of cheese or two portions of meat.

英语中的食物名词有可数和不可数之分。rice、bread、cheese 和 meat 通常是不可数名词。如果要表示具体数量，可以使用 a bowl of rice（一碗米饭）、a slice of bread（一片面包）、a piece of cheese（一块奶酪）或 two portions of meat（两份肉）。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ I cooked the fish by steam.
✓ I steamed the fish.
Steam is a verb when describing this cooking method. Use steamed directly rather than the expression by steam.
描述蒸这种烹饪方式时，steam 可以直接作动词，过去式是 steamed。不要使用不自然的 by steam。
✗ I want to make a reservation for two persons.
✓ I'd like to make a reservation for two people.
People is the natural plural form in everyday restaurant conversations. For a reservation, for two is also a common and concise expression.
在日常餐馆交流中，people 是更自然的复数表达。预订座位时也可以直接说 for two，简洁而地道。
✗ The meat is very delicious and very soft.
✓ The meat is delicious and really tender.
Tender is the more precise adjective for meat that is easy to chew. Soft is possible, but it does not describe the desirable texture of cooked meat as naturally.
Tender 是形容肉质鲜嫩、容易咀嚼的更准确表达。Soft 虽然也能表示柔软，但描述烹饪后的肉质时，tender 通常更加自然。
✗ Please give me two breads.
✓ Could I have two slices of bread, please?
Bread is generally uncountable. Use a unit such as slices, pieces or loaves when referring to specific quantities.
Bread 通常是不可数名词。表示具体数量时，需要使用 slices、pieces 或 loaves 等量词。
✗ We ordered a main dish and two side foods.
✓ We ordered a main course and two side dishes.
Main course and side dish are standard restaurant expressions. Side food is not a natural way to refer to accompanying dishes.
Main course 和 side dish 是餐馆场景中的标准表达。Side food 不是表达配菜的自然说法。
✗ The restaurant's food tastes very blandly.
✓ The food at the restaurant tastes very bland.
Taste is a linking verb when describing flavour, so it is followed by an adjective rather than an adverb. Bland describes the food itself.
Taste 在表示尝起来怎么样时属于系动词，后面应接形容词，而不是副词。因此应该使用 bland，而不是 blandly。
