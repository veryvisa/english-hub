-ing or to with a change of meaning 接 -ing 与 to 意思不同

Choose between -ing and to after remember, forget, stop, try, regret, mean and go on, where the choice changes what actually happened. 在 remember、forget、stop、try、regret、mean、go on 后面选 -ing 还是 to；选错了，说的就是另一件事。

📏 The rule规则

📏 The rule in one line规则一句话
After these verbs, -ing points back to an action that already happened or is already going on; to points forward to an action that has not happened yet, or that was the aim.在这几个动词后面，-ing 指向已经发生、正在进行的动作；to 指向还没发生的动作，或者想达到的目的。
  • Easy to miss易漏 ✗ I forgot locking the door, so I went back. → ✓ I forgot to lock the door, so I went back.

Most Chinese learners memorise lists: enjoy takes -ing, want takes to. That works until a small group of verbs accepts both, with two different meanings. I stopped smoking means I no longer smoke. I stopped to smoke means I paused something else so that I could have a cigarette. The verb is the same; the event is opposite.

很多人背清单：enjoy 接 -ing，want 接 to。可有一小组动词两样都能接，意思却不同。I stopped smoking 是戒烟了；I stopped to smoke 是停下手里的事去抽一根。动词相同，发生的事正好相反。

The time rule covers most of the group. remember doing and forget doing look back at a memory: I'll never forget seeing the Rockies for the first time. remember to do and forget to do look forward to a task: Remember to lock the back door. regret doing is sorry about the past; regret to say or regret to inform you is a formal way of delivering bad news now.

时间规则管住了这组里的大多数。remember/forget doing 回看一段记忆；remember/forget to do 向前看一件该做的事。regret doing 是为过去后悔；regret to say / regret to inform you 是正式场合现在要宣布坏消息。

try is about effort versus experiment. try to open the window means it is difficult and you may fail. try opening the window means opening it is easy; you are testing whether it solves the real problem, such as a hot room. When a colleague says Have you tried restarting it? they are suggesting an experiment, not doubting your strength.

try 区分「费力」和「试一试」。try to open 是窗户难开，可能开不了；try opening 是开窗很容易，试试看能不能解决真正的问题，比如屋里太热。同事说 Have you tried restarting it?，是建议你试个办法，不是怀疑你有没有力气。

mean doing means involve: A new job in Calgary would mean moving the kids. mean to do means intend: I meant to call you, but the day ran away from me. go on doing continues the same activity; go on to do moves to the next thing in a sequence, often in a career or a talk: She started as a cashier and went on to manage three stores.

mean doing 是「意味着、牵涉到」；mean to do 是「本打算」。go on doing 是接着做同一件事；go on to do 是接下来做下一件事，常说职业经历或讲话的顺序。

A useful check when you are unsure: ask whether the action after the verb is before, during or after the main verb. Before or during, -ing. After, or the purpose, to. The rule is not perfect for every verb in English, but for this group it gives the right answer almost every time.

拿不准时自问：后面的动作是在主动词之前、同时还是之后？之前或同时，用 -ing；之后或者是目的，用 to。这个规则不是对所有动词都灵，但对这一组几乎每次都对。

Verb+ -ing+ to
remember / forgeta memory of a past actiona task you need to do
stopthe activity endsyou pause in order to do something
tryan experiment to solve a probleman effort that may fail
regretsorry about something you didformal: sorry to announce bad news
meanit involves thisyou intend to do it
go oncontinue the same activitymove on to the next activity

Examples例句

  • I remember signing the lease, but I don't remember reading the part about pets. 我记得签了租约，可不记得读过关于宠物的那一段。
    Two memories of the past.两段过去的记忆。
  • Please remember to sign page four before you send it back. 寄回之前请记得在第四页签名。
    A task still to do.一件还没做的事。
  • I forgot to renew my parking permit, and I got a ticket the next morning. 我忘了续停车证，第二天早上就吃了罚单。
    The task never happened.那件事根本没做。
  • She'll never forget meeting her daughter's teacher for the first time. 她永远不会忘记第一次见女儿老师的情景。
    A strong memory.一段深刻的记忆。
  • He stopped checking his phone at dinner, and the conversations got longer. 他吃饭时不再看手机，聊天时间就长了。
    The habit ended.习惯停止了。
  • On the drive to Banff we stopped to buy coffee and stretch our legs. 开车去班夫的路上，我们停下来买咖啡、活动活动腿。
    The purpose of the stop.停下来是为了做这件事。
  • I tried to call the clinic all morning, but the line was always busy. 我一上午都想打给诊所，但一直占线。
    Effort that failed.努力了，没成功。
  • If the printer jams again, try turning it off for a full minute. 打印机要是再卡纸，试试关机整整一分钟。
    An experiment to solve a problem.试个办法解决问题。
  • I regret not asking more questions at the interview. 我后悔面试时没多问几个问题。
    Sorry about the past.为过去后悔。
  • We regret to inform you that the course has been cancelled. 我们遗憾地通知您，该课程已取消。
    Formal bad news, now.正式场合宣布坏消息。
  • Taking the earlier flight would mean leaving the house at four. 坐早一班的飞机就意味着四点出门。
    mean + -ing = involve.mean 加 -ing＝意味着。
  • Sorry, I didn't mean to interrupt you. 对不起，我不是故意打断你。
    mean + to = intend.mean 加 to＝本意、打算。
  • The speaker went on talking long after the time was up. 时间到了很久，演讲者还在接着讲。
    Same activity continued.同一件事接着做。
  • After her degree in nursing, she went on to run a home-care business. 读完护理学位后，她接着开了一家居家护理公司。
    The next stage in a sequence.人生的下一阶段。

More pairs: same idea, different choice更多对比句组

Did you remember to turn off the stove? 你记得关炉子了吗？
Checking a task.确认一件该做的事做了没有。
Do you remember turning off the stove? 你记不记得自己关过炉子？
Checking a memory.确认脑子里有没有那段记忆。
I don't remember turning it off, so let's go back. 我不记得关过，我们回去看看吧。
No memory, so the task is in doubt.没有记忆，所以不确定做没做。
I tried to sleep, but the neighbours were too loud. 我想睡，可邻居太吵了。
Effort, and it failed.努力了，没睡着。
I tried sleeping with earplugs, and it helped. 我试了戴耳塞睡，有用。
An experiment that worked.试了个办法，奏效了。
Try going to bed at the same time every night. 试试每晚同一时间上床。
Advice: test this method.建议：试试这个办法。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ I forgot locking the door, so I went back.
✓ I forgot to lock the door, so I went back.
The locking never happened, so it is a task you forgot, not a memory.
锁门这件事根本没做，是忘了做，不是忘了记忆。
✗ I stopped to smoke last year.
✓ I stopped smoking last year.
stop to smoke means you paused to have a cigarette. To say you quit, use -ing.
stop to smoke 是停下来抽一根；说戒烟要用 -ing。
✗ Sorry, I didn't mean hurting you.
✓ Sorry, I didn't mean to hurt you.
When you talk about your intention, mean takes to.
说「本意」时 mean 接 to。
✗ I regret to tell her the truth last night.
✓ I regret telling her the truth last night.
Sorry about a past action takes -ing; regret to tell is for bad news you are giving now.
后悔过去做的事用 -ing；regret to tell 是现在要说坏消息。
✗ I remember to meet her at a party in 2019.
✓ I remember meeting her at a party in 2019.
A memory of the past takes -ing; remember to meet would be a task for the future.
回忆过去用 -ing；remember to meet 是将来要做的事。
