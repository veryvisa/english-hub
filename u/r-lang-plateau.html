The Intermediate Plateau 中级平台期

Many adult learners describe the same experience. In the first year, progress is fast and easy to see: you go from knowing nothing to ordering food, asking for directions and getting by at work. Then, somewhere around the intermediate level, everything seems to slow down. You study just as hard, but you feel stuck. Teachers call this the intermediate plateau.

很多成年学习者都描述过同样的经历。第一年进步很快、看得见：从什么都不会，到能点餐、问路、工作中应付得过去。然后，大约到了中级，一切都好像慢了下来。你学得一样用功，却感觉卡住了。老师们把这叫作中级平台期。

Part of the problem is arithmetic. The most common thousand words cover a large share of everyday conversation, so learning them makes a big difference quickly. The next thousand appear much less often, so each new word has less of an impact on what you understand. You are still making progress; it is just harder to notice.

问题的一部分是算术。最常用的一千个词覆盖了日常对话的很大一部分，所以学会它们很快就见效。接下来的一千个词出现得少得多，所以每个新词对你理解程度的影响就小了。你仍然在进步，只是更难察觉。

The other part is what we choose to practise. At the plateau, many learners keep doing what worked before: memorising single words from lists and studying grammar rules. But fluent speakers do not build sentences one word at a time. They use chunks, ready-made groups of words such as "take something into account" or "make an effort". Knowing the word "effort" is not the same as knowing which verb goes with it.

另一部分是我们选择练什么。到了平台期，很多学习者还在做以前管用的事：从单词表上背单个的词、学语法规则。可说得流利的人不是一个词一个词地造句的。他们用词块，也就是现成的词组，比如 take something into account、make an effort。认识 effort 这个词，不等于知道该配哪个动词。

People who do research on second-language learning have a simple suggestion: a great deal of input on topics you care about. When you read and listen to many texts on one theme, the same chunks come back again and again, and you start to notice them without trying. Adults have an advantage here, because they can choose topics they really need.

研究二语习得的学者有个简单的建议：在你关心的话题上大量输入。当你就一个主题读和听很多篇文章时，同样的词块会一再出现，你不刻意也会开始注意到它们。成年人在这一点上有优势，因为他们能挑自己真正需要的话题。

The plateau is not a wall. It is a sign that the easy part is over and the interesting part has started. Make a decision about what you want to use English for, and make an effort to read, listen and speak in that area every day. Progress will come back; you may just need a better way to measure it.

平台期不是一堵墙。它说明容易的部分已经过去，有意思的部分开始了。想清楚你要用英语做什么，然后每天下功夫在这个领域里读、听、说。进步会回来的；你可能只是需要一个更好的方法来衡量它。
