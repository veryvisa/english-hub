What My Doorbell Camera Taught Me 门铃摄像头给我的教训

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Last winter I installed a smart doorbell with a built-in camera, mainly because I was tired of missing parcels. The setup took ten minutes, and I felt rather pleased with myself. It never occurred to me to read the lengthy terms and conditions, which I accepted with a single tap.

去年冬天，我安装了一个带摄像头的智能门铃，主要是因为我受够了总是错过包裹。安装只花了十分钟，我对自己相当满意。我从没想过要读那些冗长的条款，只是轻轻一点就接受了。

A few weeks later, a neighbour mentioned, rather awkwardly, that my camera could see part of her garden. I had not thought about that. Worse, when I looked into the settings, I discovered that the footage was being stored on remote servers and, by default, shared with unnamed partners for ‘product improvement’. Nobody had asked for my consent in any meaningful sense.

几周后，一位邻居相当尴尬地提到，我的摄像头能拍到她花园的一部分。我之前根本没想过这一点。更糟的是，当我查看设置时，发现录像被存储在远程服务器上，并且默认会与未具名的合作方共享，用于“产品改进”。从任何有实际意义的角度说，都没有人征求过我的同意。

I felt uncomfortable, and not only for my neighbour’s sake. Although I had bought the device to feel safer, I had unintentionally turned my street into a source of data for people I would never meet. Who was responsible if that footage leaked? The manufacturer insisted it was not; the terms, naturally, said so in small print.

我感到不安，而且不只是出于对邻居的考虑。虽然我买这个设备是为了更有安全感，但我无意中把我们的街道变成了向我永远不会见到的人提供数据的来源。如果录像泄露了，谁该负责？厂商坚称不是它；条款当然也用小字这样写着。

So I spent a weekend fixing what I could. I switched off the sharing option, narrowed the camera’s field of view, and set the footage to delete itself after a few days. My neighbour and I also had a long, friendly chat, and she was relieved to hear that I had listened. That conversation, I think, mattered more than any setting.

于是我花了一个周末尽力修正。我关闭了共享选项，缩小了摄像头的视野范围，并设置录像在几天后自动删除。我和邻居也进行了一次漫长而友好的交谈，她听到我愿意倾听后松了一口气。我觉得，那次交谈比任何设置都更重要。

I still use the doorbell, and it has, on the whole, done its job well. But the experience changed how I think about gadgets. Convenience is rarely free; somebody pays, and it is often the person who never agreed to anything. These days I read the small print first, and I expect companies to be held accountable for what they quietly collect.

我仍在使用这个门铃，总体而言，它完成得不错。但这次经历改变了我看待小玩意儿的方式。便利很少是免费的；总有人要付出代价，而这个人往往是从未同意过任何事的人。如今我会先读细则，也期望企业对它们悄悄收集的东西负起责任。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

Although I had bought the device to feel safer, I had unintentionally turned my street into a source of data for people I would never meet.
An ‘Although’ clause gives the concession, both clauses use the past perfect for earlier actions, and ‘I would never meet’ is a relative clause without a pronoun.
“Although”从句表示让步，两个分句都用过去完成时表示更早的动作，“I would never meet”是省略关系词的定语从句。
It never occurred to me to read the lengthy terms and conditions, which I accepted with a single tap.
’It never occurred to me to...’ means the idea never came to mind, and the non-defining clause ‘which I accepted...’ adds extra information about the terms.
“It never occurred to me to...”表示根本没想到，而非限制性从句“which I accepted...”补充说明条款的信息。
