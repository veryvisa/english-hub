Q: I am 48, and I have put on about eight kilos since I moved to Canada eleven years ago. My colleagues have all cut out carbs this year, and they say I should stop eating rice. But I have eaten rice at almost every meal my whole life. I have tried skipping it for a week, and I was miserable. Is rice really the problem? — Wei

问：我 48 岁，十一年前搬来加拿大以后胖了大约八公斤。同事们今年都戒了碳水，说我该别吃米饭了。可我这辈子几乎顿顿吃米饭。我试过一周不吃，难受得要命。米饭真的是问题所在吗？——伟

A: Wei, you have asked a question that many readers have sent me this year, so thank you. The short answer is that rice on its own is rarely the whole story. When people move countries, many things change at once: portions get bigger, we drive instead of walking, and processed snacks become part of the day. Eleven years ago you probably walked much more. That change alone can explain a lot.

答：伟，今年很多读者都问过我这个问题，谢谢你来信。简短的回答是：单单米饭很少是全部原因。人换了国家，很多事会同时变：份量变大了，走路变成了开车，加工零食成了日常的一部分。十一年前你大概走路多得多，光这一点就能解释不少。

Diets that cut out a whole food group often work for a few months, mainly because people end up eating less overall. The trouble is that most people cannot keep them up. Your colleagues have lost weight since the spring; the real test is where they will be in two years. A diet you can live with for the rest of your life beats a perfect diet you give up in March.

戒掉一整类食物的节食法常常能管用几个月，主要是因为人们总体上吃少了。麻烦在于大多数人坚持不下去。你的同事从春天到现在瘦了；真正的考验是两年后他们怎样。一种你能过一辈子的吃法，胜过三月就放弃的完美节食。

So instead of cutting rice out, try changing the portion. Use a smaller bowl, fill half your plate with vegetables, and choose brown or mixed rice some of the time. Keep eating the meals you love, in moderation. Small, steady changes are less exciting than a new diet, but they are far easier to keep.

所以与其戒米饭，不如改份量：用小一号的碗，半盘装蔬菜，有时换成糙米或杂粮饭。爱吃的饭菜照吃，适量就好。细水长流的小改变没有新节食法那么刺激，但容易坚持得多。

One more thing. If you have gained weight quickly, or you have been unusually tired or thirsty, see your family doctor before you change anything. A simple check-up is better than guessing. I have seen many readers spend a year on a diet when the real answer was waiting in the doctor's office.

还有一点：如果你体重涨得很快，或者近来格外容易累、容易口渴，改变任何习惯之前先去看家庭医生。做个简单的检查，好过瞎猜。我见过不少读者花了一年节食，而真正的答案一直在医生的诊室里等着。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

I am 48, and I have put on about eight kilos since I moved to Canada eleven years ago.
Present perfect for the result up to now (have put on), past simple after since for the starting point (moved).
现在完成时表示到现在的结果（have put on），since 后面用一般过去时表示起点（moved）。
Your colleagues have lost weight since the spring; the real test is where they will be in two years.
since the spring forces the present perfect; where they will be is a statement-order question used as a noun.
since the spring 要求现在完成时；where they will be 是陈述语序的名词性疑问从句。
