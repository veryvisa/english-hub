📏 The rule规则

📏 The point in one line一句话要领
English is spoken in beats. Content words carry the beat; to, of, and, can, for, a, the shrink to a schwa between beats.英语按拍子说。实词踩在拍子上；to、of、and、can、for、a、the 在拍子之间缩成中央元音 /ə/。
  • Easy to miss易漏 ✗ I CAN swim. (meaning: I am able to) → ✓ I can SWIM.
  • Next下一步 Linking words together连读

a cup of TEA /ə ˈkʌp əv ˈtiː/
of is /əv/, almost "cuppa tea". of 读 /əv/，几乎连成 cuppa tea。
I WENT to the BANK. /aɪ ˈwent tə ðə ˈbæŋk/
to the is two quick schwas. to the 是两个很快的 /ə/。
FISH and CHIPS /ˈfɪʃ ən ˈtʃɪps/
and shrinks to /ən/. and 缩成 /ən/。
She can SWIM. /ʃi kən ˈswɪm/
Weak can /kən/; the beat is on swim. can 弱读，拍子在 swim 上。
She CAN'T swim. /ʃi ˈkɑːnt ˈswɪm/
can't keeps its full vowel: that is how listeners tell them apart. In North America it is /kænt/. can't 保持饱满的元音，听者就靠这个区分；北美读 /kænt/。
I'm WAITing for the BUS. /aɪm ˈweɪtɪŋ fə ðə ˈbʌs/
for the /fə ðə/. for the 读 /fə ðə/。
What are you LOOKing at? /ˈwɒt ə ju ˈlʊkɪŋ æt/
Sentence-final at is strong /æt/. 句末的 at 用强读式。
It was GOOD, but it was exPENsive. /ɪt wəz ˈɡʊd bət ɪt wəz ɪkˈspensɪv/
was /wəz/, but /bət/. was、but 都弱读。
I've GOT to GO. /aɪv ˈɡɒtə ˈɡəʊ/
got to runs together. got to 连成一体。
TELL them to CALL me at HOME. /ˈtel ðəm tə ˈkɔːl mi ət ˈhəʊm/
Three weak words between the beats. 拍子之间有三个弱读词。
Do you WANT some of THESE? /də ju ˈwɒnt səm əv ˈðiːz/
do you /də ju/, some of /səm əv/. do you、some of 都弱读。
I should have TOLD you. /aɪ ʃəd əv ˈtəʊld ju/
should have sounds like "should of" /ʃədəv/. should have 听起来像 should of。
Yes, I CAN. /ˈjes aɪ ˈkæn/
Short answer: strong form. 简短回答用强读式。
It's FROM my manager, not FOR her. /ɪts ˈfrɒm maɪ ˌmænɪdʒə nɒt ˈfɔː hə/
Contrast makes from and for strong. 对比使 from 和 for 都变成强读。

📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Open the full explanation: 5 points展开细讲：5 段讲解

In Mandarin every syllable gets roughly the same weight. Carried into English, this makes speech sound flat and, more seriously, hides which words matter. English listeners find the message by listening for the stressed words.

普通话每个音节分量大致相同。带到英语里，话就显得平，更严重的是听不出哪些词重要。英语听者靠重读的词抓意思。

About forty common words have two pronunciations. The strong form is the one you learned first. The weak form, usually with /ə/, is the one used nine times out of ten: to /tə/, for /fə/, of /əv/, and /ən/, can /kən/, was /wəz/, at /ət/, from /frəm/, them /ðəm/.

约四十个常用词有两种读音。你最早学的是强读式；十有八九用的是带 /ə/ 的弱读式：to /tə/、for /fə/、of /əv/、and /ən/、can /kən/、was /wəz/、at /ət/、from /frəm/、them /ðəm/。

Use the strong form mainly in three places: at the end of a sentence (What are you looking AT?), for contrast (I said TO him, not FROM him), and in short answers (Yes, I CAN).

用强读式主要有三种情况：句末、表示对比、简短回答。

This is also a listening skill. If you expect /kæn/ and the speaker says /kən/, you may not notice the word at all. Train your ear with the drills below: listen for the beat first, then for what is squeezed between the beats.

这也是听力技能。你等的是 /kæn/，对方说的是 /kən/，就可能根本没听到这个词。先听拍子，再听拍子之间被挤扁的部分。

Practise by tapping. Tap the table on each capital word and fit the small words between taps without slowing down.

用敲桌子练：每个大写的词敲一下，把小词塞进两次敲击之间，不要放慢。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ I CAN swim. (meaning: I am able to)
✓ I can SWIM.
A stressed can sounds like can't to many listeners.
重读的 can 很容易被听成 can't。
✗ A CUP OF TEA (every word equal)
✓ a cup of TEA
Equal weight hides the message and sounds robotic.
字字一样重会淹没重点，听起来像机器。
✗ I want TO go TO the bank.
✓ I WANT to GO to the BANK.
to is /tə/ unless it ends the sentence.
to 读 /tə/，除非在句末。
