How Bias Gets Into Algorithms 偏见如何进入算法

🧠 Learn the 3 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 3 个新词

Algorithmic bias usually comes from the data and the measures chosen, and it can be reduced through testing and human oversight but never fully removed. 算法偏见通常来自数据和所选的衡量标准，可以通过测试和人工监督来减少，但无法彻底消除。

When people hear that an algorithm is biased, they often picture a programmer deliberately favouring one group over another. In reality, the process is usually far less dramatic. Bias tends to enter a system quietly, through the data used to teach it. Understanding how this happens is essential to deciding who should answer for the result.

当人们听说某个算法存在偏见时，往往会想象有程序员故意偏袒某一群体。实际上，这个过程通常平淡得多。偏见往往悄悄地进入系统，通过用来训练它的数据。了解这是如何发生的，对于判断应由谁对结果负责至关重要。

Machine-learning systems work by finding patterns in large collections of past examples. Suppose a hiring tool is trained on decades of records in which managers mostly promoted people of one background. The software will notice that pattern and treat it as a sign of quality, even though it reflects an old prejudice rather than genuine ability.

机器学习系统的工作方式，是在大量过去的例子中寻找规律。假设一个招聘工具是用几十年的记录训练的，而这些记录中经理们大多提拔的是同一背景的人。软件会注意到这种规律，并把它当作优秀的标志，尽管它反映的是一种旧偏见，而不是真正的能力。

A second source of bias is the choice of what to measure. Engineers often use a convenient substitute for the thing they really care about, such as using past arrests to estimate future crime. While such proxies are practical, they may simply record where police have looked most often, so the system ends up confirming its own assumptions.

偏见的第二个来源是对衡量对象的选择。工程师常常用一个方便的替代指标来代替他们真正关心的东西，比如用过去的逮捕记录来估计未来的犯罪。这类替代指标固然实用，但它们可能只是记录了警方最常关注的地方，于是系统最终只是在印证它自己的假设。

Researchers have developed several ways to detect these problems. One approach is to compare how a system treats different groups and check whether error rates differ sharply. Another is to ask outside experts to test the software before release. Neither method is perfect, but both make hidden patterns easier to see, which is the first step towards fixing them.

研究人员已经开发出几种发现这些问题的方法。一种是比较系统对不同群体的处理方式，检查错误率是否差异悬殊。另一种是请外部专家在发布前测试软件。这两种方法都不完美，但都能让隐藏的模式更容易被看到，而这是纠正它们的第一步。

It would be wrong to conclude that bias can be removed completely. Human societies are unequal, and data drawn from them is likely to carry traces of that inequality. The realistic goal is not a perfectly neutral machine but a system that is monitored, questioned and corrected, with clear human oversight at every stage.

如果由此得出偏见可以被彻底消除的结论，那就错了。人类社会是不平等的，取自其中的数据很可能带有这种不平等的痕迹。现实的目标不是一台绝对中立的机器，而是一套受到监测、质疑和纠正的系统，并在每个阶段都有明确的人工监督。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

Bias tends to enter a system quietly, through the data used to teach it.
’Tends to’ hedges the claim, and the reduced relative clause ‘used to teach it’ modifies ‘data’.
“tends to”对论断做了限定，而缩略定语从句“used to teach it”修饰“data”。
While such proxies are practical, they may simply record where police have looked most often, so the system ends up confirming its own assumptions.
The sentence concedes a point with ‘While’, hedges with ‘may’, and uses ‘ends up’ plus an -ing form to show an unintended result.
句子用“While”做让步，用“may”做限定，并用“ends up + -ing”表示意料之外的结果。
