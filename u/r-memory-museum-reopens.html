Town Museum Reopens with a Different Story 小镇博物馆以新的叙述重新开放

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

A small museum in a coastal town reopened on Saturday after two years of renovation, and its new main gallery has already provoked lively debate. Where the old display celebrated the harbour’s rise as a trading centre, the revised version devotes equal space to the workers, migrants and families whose labour made that prosperity possible.

一座海滨小镇的博物馆经过两年翻修，于周六重新开放，新的主展厅已引发热烈争论。旧展览歌颂的是港口作为贸易中心的崛起，而修订后的展览则给予那些以劳动成就了这份繁荣的工人、移民和家庭同等的篇幅。

The curator said the aim was not to replace one version of the past with another. Instead, the team wanted visitors to see how the town’s collective memory had been shaped by what earlier generations chose to preserve, and by what they quietly left out. Several letters and photographs that had spent decades in storage are now displayed for the first time.

策展人表示，目标并不是用一种历史叙述取代另一种。相反，团队希望参观者看到，小镇的集体记忆是如何被前几代人选择保存的东西塑造的，又是如何被他们悄悄略去的东西塑造的。几封信件和照片在库房里放了几十年，如今首次展出。

Reactions have been mixed. While many residents welcomed the changes, some older visitors said the new gallery made the town look ashamed of its own achievements. A local historian, speaking at the opening, argued that such disagreement is by and large a healthy sign, since a museum that nobody disputes is likely to be a museum that nobody really examines.

反应不一。虽然许多居民欢迎这些改变，但一些年长的参观者说，新展厅让小镇看起来为自己的成就感到羞愧。一位本地历史学者在开幕式上发言称，这样的分歧总体上是健康的信号，因为一座无人质疑的博物馆很可能也是无人真正审视的博物馆。

One exhibit in particular has drawn crowds: a wall of recorded interviews in which elderly residents bear witness to the years when the harbour was closed and many families left. Visitors can listen to the recordings, add their own recollections on cards, and compare their accounts with the official records beside them.

有一件展品格外吸引人：一面录音访谈墙，年长的居民在其中为港口关闭、许多家庭离去的那些年作证。参观者可以收听录音，在卡片上写下自己的回忆，并把自己的叙述与旁边的官方记录对照。

The museum’s board says it will review visitor feedback after six months and may adjust the displays. Whatever the outcome, the reopening has shown that a town’s past is rarely settled once and for all; it tends to be renegotiated each time a new generation walks through the door.

博物馆董事会表示，六个月后将审阅参观者的反馈，并可能调整展陈。无论结果如何，这次重新开放都表明，一个小镇的过去很少会一劳永逸地定论；每当新一代人走进大门，它往往都要被重新商榷。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

Instead, the team wanted visitors to see how the town’s collective memory had been shaped by what earlier generations chose to preserve, and by what they quietly left out.
The sentence uses a how-clause with a past perfect passive, and two ‘by what’ clauses that act as noun clauses naming the factors behind the shaping.
句中用 how 引导的从句，谓语是过去完成时被动语态；两个 by what 引导名词性从句，说明记忆是被什么塑造的。
Where the old display celebrated the harbour’s rise as a trading centre, the revised version devotes equal space to the workers, migrants and families whose labour made that prosperity possible.
’Where’ introduces a contrast between old and new, and ‘whose labour’ is a relative clause that attaches ownership of the labour to the workers, migrants and families.
Where 在此表示新旧对比（而不是地点）；whose labour 是关系从句，表明劳动属于前面提到的工人、移民和家庭。
