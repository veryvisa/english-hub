Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Dear Adviser, my manager has asked our team to start using an automated tool that scores job applicants. I am uneasy, but I do not want to seem old-fashioned or difficult. Am I right to worry, and should I say anything?

亲爱的顾问：我的经理要求我们团队开始使用一个给求职者打分的自动化工具。我感到不安，但又不想显得守旧或难相处。我的担心有道理吗？我应该说点什么吗？

Your unease is reasonable, and it is not the same as being against progress. Tools of this kind can save time, and by and large they are probably harmless when used sensibly. That said, a score that looks objective can hide bias inherited from the data behind it, so it deserves the same scrutiny you would give to a human opinion.

你的不安是合理的，这并不等同于反对进步。这类工具可以节省时间，在合理使用的情况下，大体上可能是无害的。话虽如此，一个看似客观的分数可能隐藏着从背后数据继承而来的偏见，因此它值得你像对待人的意见一样加以审视。

Start with questions rather than objections, because they invite answers instead of arguments. Ask what the tool actually measures, who tested it for fairness, and whether a person will review every rejection. If the answers are vague, that in itself is useful information. A good employer should be able to explain how decisions are made and who is accountable for them.

先提问题而不是提反对意见，因为问题引来的是回答而不是争论。问问这个工具究竟衡量什么，谁测试过它的公平性，以及每一次拒绝是否都会有人复核。如果回答含糊其辞，这本身就是有用的信息。一个好的雇主应该能够解释决策是如何做出的，以及由谁来负责。

You might also suggest a trial period in which the software’s recommendations are compared with those of human reviewers. Although this takes extra effort, it tends to reveal problems early, before real applicants are affected. Raising the point as a practical safeguard, rather than as a criticism, will make it easier for colleagues to listen.

你也可以建议设置一个试用期，把软件的推荐结果与人工审核者的结果进行比较。虽然这需要额外的努力，但它往往能及早暴露问题，在真正的求职者受到影响之前。把这一点作为切实的保障措施而不是批评来提出，会让同事们更容易听进去。

Finally, remember that speaking up is part of being a responsible professional, and part of building a workplace that others can trust. Nobody expects you to be an expert in machine learning, but everyone benefits when someone asks sensible questions about oversight and fairness. It is likely to be uncomfortable for a moment; it is also likely to be appreciated later. Good luck.

最后，请记住，发声是作为一名负责任的职场人士的一部分，也是营造一个让他人信任的工作环境的一部分。没有人期望你成为机器学习专家，但当有人就监督和公平性提出合理的问题时，所有人都会受益。这在当下可能让人有些不自在，但日后也很可能会受到赞赏。祝你好运。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

That said, a score that looks objective can hide bias inherited from the data behind it, so it deserves the same scrutiny you would give to a human opinion.
’That said’ introduces a concession, the past participle phrase ‘inherited from the data’ modifies ‘bias’, and ‘you would give’ is a relative clause with the pronoun omitted.
“That said”引出让步，过去分词短语“inherited from the data”修饰“bias”，“you would give”是省略了关系代词的定语从句。
Although this takes extra effort, it tends to reveal problems early, before real applicants are affected.
The ‘Although’ clause concedes a cost, ‘tends to’ hedges the benefit, and ‘before... are affected’ is a time clause using the present passive.
“Although”从句承认代价，“tends to”限定了好处，而“before... are affected”是使用一般现在时被动语态的时间状语从句。
