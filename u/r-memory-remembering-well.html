Remember Well, Remember Honestly 好好记住，诚实地记住

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Good evening, everyone. When I was asked to say a few words about the past, my first instinct was to talk about dates and events. But I have taught history for many years, and I have come to believe that the dates are the easy part. The hard part is deciding what to do with them.

大家晚上好。当有人请我就过去说几句话时，我的第一反应是谈日期和事件。但我教了多年历史，逐渐相信日期是容易的部分，难的是决定拿它们怎么办。

Every community keeps a collective memory, and by and large it serves us well. It gives us stories to be proud of, names to honour and reasons to look after one another. Yet it also has a habit of taking the comfortable parts for granted and letting the uncomfortable parts fade. Nobody plans this; it simply happens, the way a path forms across a field.

每个社群都保有集体记忆，总体而言它对我们是有益的。它给我们值得骄傲的故事、值得敬重的名字，以及彼此照应的理由。然而它也有个习惯：把舒适的部分视为理所当然，让不舒适的部分淡去。没有人刻意如此；它只是自然发生，就像田野上走出一条小路。

So I would like to ask something of the young people here tonight. Whenever you are given a tidy story about the past, ask who is missing from it. Ask who wrote it down, and who was not allowed to. Were you to find the answer uncomfortable, that would not be a reason to stop; it would be a sign that you are finally looking at the whole picture.

所以今晚我想对在座的年轻人提一个请求。每当有人给你一个整齐的过去的故事，就问问谁不在其中。问问是谁写下了它，又有谁不被允许书写。倘若你发现答案令人不安，这并不是停下的理由；这恰恰说明你终于看到了全貌。

I should add a word of caution. While it is true that honest history can hurt, it is equally true that anger is a poor guide to accuracy. The aim is not to find someone to blame; it is to bear witness faithfully, to record what happened as carefully as we can, and to accept that our understanding is likely to change as new evidence comes to light.

我还要提醒一句。诚然诚实的历史可能令人受伤，但同样真实的是，愤怒并不是通往准确的好向导。目标不是找出一个该被责怪的人，而是忠实地作证，尽可能认真地记录发生过的事，并接受随着新证据浮出水面，我们的理解很可能会改变。

Thank you for listening. Remember well, and remember honestly; the two are not always the same, but they are worth the effort of bringing them together.

感谢聆听。请好好地记住，也请诚实地记住；这两者并不总是一回事，但值得我们努力把它们结合起来。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

Were you to find the answer uncomfortable, that would not be a reason to stop; it would be a sign that you are finally looking at the whole picture.
’Were you to find’ is an inverted conditional meaning ‘if you were to find’, used for a hypothetical situation, and the main clause uses ‘would’ to state its consequence.
Were you to find 是倒装条件句，相当于 if you were to find，表示假设；主句用 would 说明可能的结果。
Yet it also has a habit of taking the comfortable parts for granted and letting the uncomfortable parts fade.
’Have a habit of’ is followed by two gerund phrases joined by ‘and’; ‘take ... for granted’ places its object in the middle, and ‘letting ... fade’ uses a bare infinitive after the object.
have a habit of 后接由 and 连接的两个动名词短语；take ... for granted 的宾语插在中间；letting ... fade 中宾语后接不带 to 的不定式。
