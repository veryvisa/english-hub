The key is to understand how English divides time and space into specific points, surfaces, and areas rather than translating Chinese expressions word for word.本组重点学习 at、on、in 等常见介词在时间和地点表达中的用法。关键是理解英语如何将时间和空间划分为具体时点、表面和区域，而不是逐字翻译中文表达。

📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Open the full explanation: 6 points展开细讲：6 段讲解

For time, at usually refers to a precise point, on refers to a particular day or date, and in refers to a longer period. Think of a clock time, a calendar day, and a larger time period. For example, we say at 7:00, on Monday, and in November.

表示时间时，at 通常用于具体时刻，on 用于某一天或具体日期，in 用于较长的时间段。可以把它们理解为时钟上的时间点、日历上的某一天和更大的时间范围。例如：at 7:00、on Monday、in November。

For place, at often describes a specific location or point, in describes being inside an area or enclosed space, and on describes contact with a surface. For example, someone can be at the station, in the station building, or on the platform. The correct choice depends on the intended meaning.

表示地点时，at 常用于表示具体位置或地点，in 表示在某个区域或封闭空间内部，on 表示位于某个表面上。例如，一个人可以在车站这个地点、在车站建筑物内部，或在站台上。具体选择取决于想表达的含义。

Some expressions follow fixed patterns rather than simple logical rules. We say in the morning, in the afternoon, and in the evening, but at night. We also say at the weekend in British English and on the weekend in American English. Learn these expressions as complete chunks.

有些表达遵循固定搭配，而不是简单的逻辑规则。例如，我们说 in the morning、in the afternoon、in the evening，但说 at night。英国英语通常使用 at the weekend，美国英语通常使用 on the weekend。建议把这些表达作为完整词组记忆。

English often uses a preposition where Chinese uses a direct time or place expression without one. For example, English requires on before a weekday in expressions such as on Friday. However, not every time expression needs a preposition: we say next Friday, last year, and this morning without adding one.

中文在表达时间和地点时经常不需要介词，而英语通常需要。例如，表示星期几时，英语通常要在前面加 on，如 on Friday。但并非所有时间表达都需要介词，例如 next Friday、last year 和 this morning 前面都不加介词。

Some prepositions change the meaning of an expression depending on context. At school usually describes someone's general activity or status as a student, while in the school emphasizes being inside the school building or grounds. Similarly, at work describes a person's work location or situation, while in the office emphasizes being inside an office.

有些介词会根据语境改变表达的含义。at school 通常表示在学校上学或处于学校这一活动状态，而 in the school 更强调身处学校建筑物或校园内部。同样，at work 表示在工作场所或工作状态中，in the office 则更强调在办公室里面。

The preposition since introduces a starting point, while for introduces a length of time. We say since 2020 but for six years. These expressions commonly appear with the present perfect when describing an activity or situation that began in the past and continues now.

since 用来引出时间起点，for 用来表示持续时间。例如：since 2020 表示从2020年开始，for six years 表示持续六年。这两个表达经常与现在完成时连用，描述从过去开始并持续到现在的情况。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ I get up on 7 o'clock every morning.
✓ I get up at 7 o'clock every morning.
Use at for a precise clock time. On is normally used for particular days and dates.
表示具体钟点时使用 at，而 on 通常用于具体的星期几和日期。
✗ My birthday is in Monday.
✓ My birthday is on Monday.
Use on before a particular weekday. In is used with months, years, and longer periods.
具体某一天或星期几前使用 on。in 通常用于月份、年份和较长的时间段。
✗ I was born on 1995.
✓ I was born in 1995.
Use in with a year because a year is a longer time period, not a single calendar day.
年份前使用 in，因为年份表示一个较长的时间段，而不是日历上的某一天。
✗ I usually study in night.
✓ I usually study at night.
At night is the standard expression. It is a fixed collocation that learners should memorize.
at night 是表示在夜间的固定表达，需要整体记忆，不能直接套用 in the morning 的结构。
✗ I will see you on next Friday.
✓ I will see you next Friday.
Do not normally use a preposition before next, last, this, or every when these words introduce a time expression.
next、last、this 和 every 引导时间表达时，前面通常不加介词。
✗ She is waiting in the bus stop.
✓ She is waiting at the bus stop.
At the bus stop identifies the location where someone is waiting. In would suggest being inside an enclosed space.
at the bus stop 表示在公交车站这个地点等候。in 通常强调身处某个封闭空间内部。
