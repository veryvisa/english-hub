Why Did Nobody Believe Him? 为什么当时没人信他

Read and listen读与听

290 words
Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Maya: Dad, look at this map. South America and Africa really do fit together like two pieces of a puzzle. Why did it take scientists so long to notice? Dad: They did notice. A weather scientist said it more than a hundred years ago. He argued that the continents had once been joined and had slowly moved apart. Most geologists laughed at him.

玛雅：爸，你看这张地图。南美洲和非洲真的像两块拼图一样拼得上。科学家怎么花了那么久才注意到？爸爸：他们注意到了。一百多年前，一位气象学家就说过。他主张各大洲曾经连在一起，后来慢慢分开了。大多数地质学家都嘲笑他。

Maya: But he was right! If I had been a geologist back then, I would have believed him. Dad: Would you? He had good evidence: careful observations, the same fossils on both sides of the ocean, the same kinds of rock. But he could not explain how whole continents could move. Without a mechanism, most experts thought the idea was just a nice story. They were sceptical, and to be fair, that was their job.

玛雅：可他是对的！要是我当年是地质学家，我会相信他。爸爸：会吗？他有不错的证据：仔细的观察、大洋两岸相同的化石、同类的岩石。可他解释不了整块大陆怎么能移动。没有机制，大多数专家觉得这个想法只是个好听的故事。他们持怀疑态度，说句公道话，那本来就是他们的职责。

Maya: So what changed? Dad: New data. Decades later, ships mapped the ocean floor and found a long chain of underwater mountains where new rock was forming. Suddenly there was a way for the continents to move. Within about twenty years, the idea went from a joke to the textbook.

玛雅：那后来什么变了？爸爸：新数据。几十年后，船只测绘了海底，发现了一长串海底山脉，新的岩石正在那里形成。一下子，大陆就有了移动的途径。大约二十年里，这个想法从笑话变成了教科书内容。

Maya: That's so unfair. He was ahead of his time and nobody took him seriously. Dad: He died before the breakthrough, so he never saw it. If he were alive today, he would probably be amazed by how much we know. But I'm not sure the others were simply wrong. If scientists accepted every bold hypothesis without a mechanism, they would believe a lot of nonsense too.

玛雅：这太不公平了。他超前于时代，却没人当回事。爸爸：他在突破到来之前就去世了，所以没能看到。他要是活到今天，大概会惊讶我们知道了这么多。不过我不确定其他人就是简单地错了。要是科学家接受每一个没有机制的大胆假说，他们也会相信一大堆胡说八道。

Maya: So being right isn't enough. You have to be able to show how it works. Dad: Exactly. And sometimes you have to wait for someone else to make the discovery that backs you up.

玛雅：所以光是对还不够，还得能说明它是怎么运作的。爸爸：没错。有时候还得等别人做出那个支持你的发现。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

If I had been a geologist back then, I would have believed him.
Third conditional: if + had done, would have done for an imagined past. She was not a geologist, so both halves are unreal.
第三类条件句：if + had done，would have done，设想过去。她并不是地质学家，两部分都不真实。
If scientists accepted every bold hypothesis without a mechanism, they would believe a lot of nonsense too.
Second conditional: if + past, would + verb for an imagined general situation now, not a past event.
第二类条件句：if + 过去式，would + 动词原形，设想现在的普遍情况，不是讲过去。
