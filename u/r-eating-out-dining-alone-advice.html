Dining Alone 一个人下馆子

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Dear Alex, I have just moved to a new city for work, and I do not know anyone yet. After work I am often hungry, but I feel shy about going to a restaurant alone. I am afraid that people will look at me and think I have no friends. So most evenings I buy a sandwich and eat it at home. Is this normal? What should I do? Lonely in the City

亲爱的艾历克斯：我刚因为工作搬到一座新城市，还不认识任何人。下班后我常常很饿，但一想到自己一个人去餐厅就觉得害羞。我怕别人看着我，觉得我没有朋友。所以大多数晚上，我都买个三明治回家吃。这正常吗？我该怎么办？——城市里的孤单人

Dear Lonely, First of all, you are not alone. Many people feel the same way, and it is much more common than you think. In fact, most diners are too busy with their own food and phones to notice anyone else. Eating out alone can even be more relaxing than eating with a group, because you can choose any dish you like and take your time.

亲爱的孤单人：首先，你并不孤单。很多人有同样的感受，这比你想象的常见得多。事实上，大多数食客忙着吃自己的饭、看自己的手机，根本没空注意别人。一个人下馆子甚至比和一群人一起更放松，因为你可以点任何喜欢的菜，也可以慢慢来。

Start small. Try a casual place at lunchtime, when the room is bright and busy. A noodle shop, a café with a counter, or a bakery with window seats is a good choice, because you do not need to book a table and nobody expects you to stay long. Bring a book if it makes you feel safer. Look at what is on the menu before you go, so that you can order without stress.

从小处开始。先试试午餐时间的休闲小店，那时店里明亮又热闹。面馆、有吧台的咖啡馆、或带靠窗座位的面包店都是不错的选择，因为你不用订位，也没人指望你坐很久。如果带本书会让你更有安全感，就带上。去之前先看看菜单上有什么，这样点菜时就不会有压力。

Also, talk to the staff. A friendly waiter can recommend a dish, and a short chat can make the evening warmer. Over time, you may find that you enjoy these quiet meals. Some people even say that trying new food alone helped them feel more confident in a new city. After a few weeks, you might feel ready to invite a colleague to join you, and then you can split the bill and share a few dishes.

另外，多和店员聊聊。友善的服务员可以推荐菜，简短的聊天也会让夜晚更温暖。慢慢地，你可能会发现自己喜欢上这种安静的用餐。有些人甚至说，一个人尝试新食物让他们在新城市里更有自信。几周后，你也许就准备好邀请一位同事一起来，那时你们可以AA付账，再分着吃几道菜。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

In fact, most diners are too busy with their own food and phones to notice anyone else.
The pattern ‘too + adjective + to + verb’ means something is not possible; here it means that diners do not notice other people.
’too + 形容词 + to + 动词' 表示因太……而不能；这里指食客忙得没空注意别人。
Eating out alone can even be more relaxing than eating with a group, because you can choose any dish you like and take your time.
A gerund phrase (‘Eating out alone’) is the subject, and it is compared with another gerund phrase using ‘more relaxing than’.
动名词短语 ‘Eating out alone’ 作主语，并用 ‘more relaxing than’ 与另一个动名词短语比较。
