📏 The rule规则

🎯 The point in one line一句话要领
At home and in shops, people say turn off the light and fill in the form, not extinguish or complete. The phrasal verb is the normal word, not the casual one.在家里、在店里，大家说 turn off the light、fill in the form，不说 extinguish、complete。短语动词才是正常说法，不是随便的说法。

📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Open the full explanation: 5 points展开细讲：5 段讲解

Chinese speakers often avoid phrasal verbs and reach for one formal verb instead: remove the rubbish, ignite the stove, awaken. Grammatically fine, but at home it sounds like a manual. Everyday English runs on two-word verbs: throw away the rubbish, turn on the stove, wake up.

华人常回避短语动词，改用一个书面动词：remove the rubbish、ignite the stove、awaken。语法没错，但在家里说出来像念说明书。日常英语靠的是两个词的动词：throw away the rubbish、turn on the stove、wake up。

Many of these verbs are separable. With a noun, both orders work: pick up the kids or pick the kids up. With a pronoun, the pronoun must go in the middle: pick them up, never pick up them. This is the single most common phrasal verb error at B2.

这些动词很多是可拆分的：宾语是名词时两种语序都行，pick up the kids 或 pick the kids up；宾语是代词时必须放中间，pick them up，绝不能说 pick up them。这是 B2 阶段最常见的短语动词错误。

Some are not separable, and some have no object at all. Look after the baby keeps the object at the end: look after her. Wake up, eat out and break down need no object. When you learn a new one, learn a sentence with a pronoun, and the pattern comes with it.

有些不可拆分，有些根本不带宾语。look after the baby 宾语永远在后：look after her。wake up、eat out、break down 不带宾语。学一个新的，就学一句带代词的例句，语序规则就跟着记住了。

One verb, several meanings: pick up is to collect a person, to lift something, and to learn something without lessons (I picked up some Cantonese at work). Go off is an alarm ringing and milk turning bad. Context decides, so store each meaning with its own example.

一个短语动词常有几个意思：pick up 可以是接人、拿起东西、不经正式学习就学会（I picked up some Cantonese at work）。go off 可以是闹钟响，也可以是牛奶变质。靠语境区分，所以每个意思各配一个例句存。

In shops and on the phone these verbs are fixed scripts: Can I try this on?, Sorry, I have to hang up now, I'll drop it off tomorrow. Say them as one breath, with the stress on the small word: try it ON, drop it OFF.

在店里和电话里，这些短语动词就是固定台词：Can I try this on?、Sorry, I have to hang up now、I'll drop it off tomorrow。一口气说出来，重音落在小词上：try it ON、drop it OFF。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ My son called, so I picked up him at school.
✓ My son called, so I picked him up at school.
Pronoun objects go in the middle of a separable verb.
可拆分短语动词的代词宾语放中间。
✗ Please close the light when you leave.
✓ Please turn off the light when you leave.
Lights and machines are turned off, not closed.
灯和电器用 turn off，不用 close。
✗ Can I try on it?
✓ Can I try it on?
Same rule: try it on.
同一条规则：try it on。
✗ I will look the baby after tonight.
✓ I will look after the baby tonight.
look after cannot be split.
look after 不可拆。
✗ Our car was broken down on the highway.
✓ Our car broke down on the highway.
break down has no object and no passive.
break down 不带宾语，也没有被动。
