Why Losses Hurt More 为什么亏损更让人难受

🧠 Learn the 3 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 3 个新词

Our tendency to feel losses more sharply than gains can distort investment decisions, but good habits help to manage it. 我们对亏损的感受往往比对收益更强烈，这会扭曲投资决策，但良好的习惯有助于控制这种倾向。

Imagine that someone offers you a simple bet on a coin toss. If it lands heads, you win a certain sum; if it lands tails, you lose exactly the same amount. Most people turn it down, even though the odds are perfectly even. Psychologists call this tendency loss aversion: a loss usually feels considerably more painful than an equal gain feels pleasant.

设想有人邀请你参加一个简单的抛硬币赌局。如果正面朝上，你赢得一笔钱；如果反面朝上，你输掉完全相同的金额。尽管胜负机会完全均等，大多数人还是会拒绝。心理学家把这种倾向称为损失厌恶：亏损带来的痛苦，通常远远超过同等收益带来的快乐。

Researchers suggest that this bias may have helped our ancestors survive. For someone with little food in store, losing a week’s supply could be fatal, whereas finding an extra week’s supply was merely welcome. A brain that treated threats as urgent therefore had an advantage. The same instinct, however, can be unhelpful when the threat is a falling number on a screen.

研究人员认为，这种偏差可能曾帮助我们的祖先生存下来。对于存粮很少的人来说，失去一周的口粮可能是致命的，而多找到一周的口粮只不过是件好事。因此，把威胁当作紧急情况处理的大脑占有优势。然而，当威胁只是屏幕上一个不断下跌的数字时，同样的本能就可能帮倒忙。

In investing, loss aversion tends to show itself in two ways. First, people who check their accounts every day see many small drops, and each one stings. As a result, they are likely to become more cautious than their goals require. Second, some investors refuse to sell a poor investment because selling would make the loss feel real. They wait for the price to recover, even when there is little reason to expect it.

在投资中，损失厌恶往往表现为两种形式。第一，每天查看账户的人会看到许多小幅下跌，每一次都令人刺痛。结果，他们很可能变得过于谨慎，超出了实现目标所需的程度。第二，有些投资者不肯卖掉表现糟糕的投资，因为一旦卖出，亏损就显得真实了。他们等着价格回升，即使几乎没有理由抱这种期望。

Although the bias cannot simply be switched off, it can be managed. One useful step is to decide on your time horizon, meaning how long you can leave the money untouched, before you invest anything. Another is to write down the conditions under which you would sell. Looking at your account less often also helps. None of these habits removes risk, but they make it less likely that a moment of fear will make the decision for you.

虽然这种偏差无法简单地关掉，但可以加以控制。一个有用的做法是，在投入任何资金之前先确定投资期限，也就是这笔钱可以多久不动用。另一个做法是写下你会在什么条件下卖出。少看账户也有帮助。这些习惯都不能消除风险，但能降低一时的恐惧替你做决定的可能性。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

Another is to write down the conditions under which you would sell.
’Another’ stands for ‘another useful step’, and ‘under which’ introduces a relative clause with the preposition placed before the pronoun.
Another 代替 another useful step；under which 引导介词前置的定语从句。
None of these habits removes risk, but they make it less likely that a moment of fear will make the decision for you.
In ‘make it less likely that...’, ‘it’ is a placeholder object that points forward to the that-clause.
在 make it less likely that... 结构中，it 是形式宾语，真正的宾语是后面的 that 从句。
