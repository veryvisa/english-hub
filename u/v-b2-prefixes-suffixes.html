📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Open the full explanation: 5 points展开细讲：5 段讲解

Reading at B2 and above means meeting many words for the first time. Most of them are a familiar root plus a prefix or suffix: unaffordable, rethink, misleading. Splitting the word before reaching for a dictionary keeps you reading and trains the habit that native speakers use without noticing.

B2 以上的阅读会不停遇到新词，其中大多是熟悉的词根加前后缀。先拆词再查字典，阅读不断档，也练出母语者无意中在用的习惯。

Prefixes change meaning but not part of speech. un-, in-/im-/ir-/il- and dis- make opposites; mis- means "wrongly"; re- means "again"; over- and under- mean "too much" and "not enough"; pre- and post- mean "before" and "after".

前缀改意思、不改词性：un-、in-/im-/ir-/il-、dis- 表相反；mis- 表「错」；re- 表「再」；over-/under- 表「过度/不足」；pre-/post- 表「之前/之后」。

Suffixes change the part of speech, and this is where Chinese learners lose marks: "It is very convenience", "I am interesting in it". Noun endings include -tion, -ment, -ness, -ity; adjective endings include -able, -ful, -less, -ive, -ous; verbs often end in -ise or -en; adverbs in -ly.

后缀改词性，华人最常在这里丢分。名词后缀 -tion、-ment、-ness、-ity；形容词后缀 -able、-ful、-less、-ive、-ous；动词常见 -ise、-en；副词 -ly。

The opposite prefix is not predictable, so learn it with the word: unable but inability, dishonest, irregular, illegal, impossible. A rough guide: im- before p and m, ir- before r, il- before l.

相反前缀不可预测，要跟词一起记：unable 却是 inability。大致规律：p、m 前用 im-，r 前用 ir-，l 前用 il-。

Guessing from parts has limits. Inflammable means "burns easily", not "cannot burn"; invaluable means "extremely valuable". When a guess makes the sentence strange, check it.

拆词猜义有局限：inflammable 是「易燃」，invaluable 是「极其宝贵」。猜出来句子说不通，就去查。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ It is very convenience to live here.
✓ It is very convenient to live here.
After be + very you need the adjective; convenience is the noun.
be very 后要形容词，convenience 是名词。
✗ His unability to listen annoys everyone.
✓ His inability to listen annoys everyone.
unable but inability: learn the prefix with each word.
unable 的名词是 inability，前缀要逐词记。
✗ The fire report says the material is inflammable, so it is safe.
✓ The material is non-flammable, so it is safe.
inflammable means it burns easily; the prefix does not mean "not" here.
inflammable 意为易燃，这里的 in- 不是「不」。
✗ We need to strong our team.
✓ We need to strengthen our team.
The verb from strong is strengthen.
strong 的动词是 strengthen。
✗ I misunderstanded you.
✓ I misunderstood you.
The prefix keeps the irregular past: understood → misunderstood.
加前缀后不规则变化不变。
