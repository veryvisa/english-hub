Reading a Report Card Without Panic 不慌不忙读成绩单

🧠 Learn the 5 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 5 个新词

Learn to read the scale and the comments on a report card, and meet the word families teachers use: expect, rely, willing, improve. 学会读成绩单上的等级和评语，认识老师常用的词族：expect、rely、willing、improve。

Read and listen读与听

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

The first report card from a school abroad can be confusing. Where many parents grew up, a report was a list of scores and a rank in the class. Here there may be no rank at all, letters instead of numbers, and two pages of comments. Parents often read the comments as polite filler. They are not. They are where the teacher tells you what to do next.

国外学校的第一张成绩单可能让人摸不着头脑。在许多家长长大的地方，成绩单就是一串分数加班级排名。这里可能根本没有排名，用字母代替数字，外加两页评语。家长常把评语当成客套话。其实不是，评语正是老师告诉你下一步做什么的地方。

Start with the scale. Many schools use letters or levels, and each school explains its own. A level that sounds average may mean "meets expectations for the grade", which is exactly where a child should be. A misunderstanding here is common: parents see a B or a three and think their child is failing.

先看评分标准。许多学校用字母或等级，各校各有说明。一个听起来很普通的等级，可能意思是「达到本年级的预期」，而这正是孩子该在的位置。这里的误会很常见：家长看到 B 或 3，就以为孩子要不及格了。

Then read the comments for the verbs. "Is developing", "with support" and "beginning to" mean the skill is not there yet. "Consistently", "independently" and "reliable" mean it is. A phrase like "shows a willingness to take risks in discussion" is real praise: your child speaks up even when not sure of the answer.

然后看评语里的动词。「正在发展」「在帮助下」「开始能够」表示这项能力还没到位。「一贯」「独立地」「可靠」表示已经到位。像「愿意在讨论中大胆尝试」这样的话是真正的表扬：你的孩子即使拿不准答案也敢开口。

Next, look for the area of improvement the teacher names. There are usually only one or two, and teachers choose them carefully. That is where to put your effort at home, not on everything at once, and it is worth asking the teacher which approach works in class.

接着，找出老师点名需要改进的地方。通常只有一两处，老师是仔细挑的。这才是你在家该使劲的地方，而不是什么都一起抓；也值得问问老师，课堂上用的是什么方法。

Finally, share the good parts with your child first. Children who hear what they do well build confidence, and confidence makes the hard parts easier to fix. If something is unclear, ask at the next parent-teacher conference instead of guessing.

最后，先把好的部分讲给孩子听。听到自己哪里做得好的孩子会建立信心，有了信心，难的部分也更容易补上。哪里看不明白，就在下一次家长会上问，别去猜。

拿一句真实或想象的评语，比如「开始能在写作中组织思路」。给孩子写三句话：一句表扬、一个需要改进的地方、一个在家的小计划。用上 expect、rely 或 improve 词族里的两个词。
