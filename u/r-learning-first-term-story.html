The Term I Almost Gave Up 我差点放弃的那个学期

🧠 Learn the 3 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 3 个新词

After a hard start at a new school, the writer made progress by asking for help and changing the way of studying. 在新学校艰难起步之后，作者通过求助和改变学习方法取得了进步。

When I started at my new school, I could hardly follow the lessons. The teachers spoke quickly, and my classmates joked with each other in a way I did not understand. I felt tired and lonely, and after two weeks I wanted to go home.

刚转到新学校时，我几乎听不懂课。老师们说得很快，同学们互相开的玩笑我也不明白。我又累又孤独，两周后就想回家了。

The worst problem was homework. I could not keep up with the reading, and I often forgot to hand in my work. One day my teacher asked me to stay after class. I expected her to be angry, but she only asked me what the hardest part was.

最糟糕的是作业。我跟不上阅读量，还常常忘记交作业。有一天，老师让我下课后留下来。我以为她会生气，可她只问我最难的部分是什么。

I told her the truth. I was spending hours looking up every new word in a dictionary, and by the time I finished a page, I was too tired to think about the meaning. She showed me another way. First I should read the whole page quickly, then underline only the words that appeared more than once.

我对她说了实话。我花了好几个小时在词典里查每一个生词，等读完一页时，累得根本没法思考意思。她教了我另一种方法：先快速读完整页，然后只给出现不止一次的词画线。

It felt strange at first, but I got used to it. Little by little, the reading became easier, and I began to make progress. I also joined a small study group, where we explained difficult ideas to each other. Teaching someone else turned out to be the best way to learn.

起初感觉很奇怪，但我慢慢习惯了。一点一点地，阅读变得容易了，我也开始有进步。我还加入了一个小型学习小组，大家互相讲解难点。事实证明，教别人是最好的学习方式。

Now I look back on that term with a smile. I did not become perfect, but I stopped feeling afraid. My advice to anyone in a new school is simple: ask for help early, because most teachers are happy to give it.

现在我带着微笑回顾那个学期。我没有变得完美，但我不再害怕了。我对任何一个刚进新学校的人的建议很简单：尽早求助，因为大多数老师都乐意帮忙。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

I was spending hours looking up every new word in a dictionary, and by the time I finished a page, I was too tired to think about the meaning.
’Was spending hours looking up’ uses the past continuous with an -ing form for a repeated activity, and ‘too tired to think’ means so tired that thinking was impossible.
’was spending hours looking up’ 用过去进行时加 -ing 形式表示反复进行的活动；'too tired to think' 表示累到无法思考。
Teaching someone else turned out to be the best way to learn.
The -ing phrase ‘Teaching someone else’ is the subject, and ‘turned out to be’ shows a result that was not expected at first.
动名词短语 ‘Teaching someone else’ 作主语；'turned out to be' 表示起初没想到的结果。
