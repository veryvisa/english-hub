be to, be about to, be due to: arranged futures be to、be about to、be due to：安排好的将来

📘 In your coursebook: English File 5e · Advanced · 8B “Don’t miss it!”. Use the texts and recordings with your coursebook; this page adds new words, grammar and practice.教材定位：English File 5e · Advanced · 8B《Don’t miss it!》。课文与录音请配合教材学习，本页补充新词、语法与练习。

Choose accurately between 'be to', 'be about to' and 'be due to' when talking about arranged, imminent or scheduled future events, and use each in its proper register. 在谈论已安排、即将发生或按计划应发生的事件时，准确区分并使用 be to、be about to 和 be due to，并注意各自的语体。

The president is to visit the factory on Monday. 总统定于周一视察工厂。
A fixed official arrangement, reported in a formal, news-style register.官方已定的安排，语体正式，类似新闻报道。
The president is due to visit the factory on Monday. 总统预计周一视察工厂。
Expected according to a schedule; slightly more open to change than 'is to'.按日程预计发生，比 is to 更容易有变数。
The president is about to visit the factory. 总统马上要视察工厂了。
The visit is minutes away; no scheduling information is given.视察就在眼前，不涉及日程安排。
The president is going to visit the factory on Monday. 总统周一要去视察工厂。
A neutral, everyday statement of intention or plan, without the official tone.中性、日常的计划或打算，没有官方口吻。

📏 The rule规则

📏 The rule in one line规则一句话
'Be to' presents a future as fixed by an authority, plan or fate; 'be about to' marks the moment just before something happens; 'be due to' says something is expected at a scheduled time.be to 表示由权威、计划或命运决定的将来；be about to 表示事情发生前的一刻；be due to 表示按时间表预计会发生。

📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Choose 'be to' when the future event has been fixed by someone with the power to fix it: an organiser, a government, a boss or a regulation. It sounds formal and is common in news reports and official announcements. In speech, 'is going to' or 'will' usually sounds more natural, so 'be to' signals that you are reporting a decision rather than predicting.

当将来的事由有权安排的一方（组织者、政府、上司或规章）定下时，用 be to。它语体正式，常见于新闻报道和官方通告。口语里 is going to 或 will 更自然，所以用 be to 是在传达一项决定，而不是做预测。

'Be to' also carries two meanings that 'be going to' cannot: an order or instruction ('You are to report at nine') and destiny ('He was never to see his home again'). Learners who only know it as a plain future miss that the speaker is imposing or narrating something inevitable. The past form 'was to' in the second sense is typical of storytelling and looks back on events already known to be inevitable.

be to 还有 be going to 没有的两种含义：命令或指示（You are to report at nine），以及命运注定（He was never to see his home again）。只把它当普通将来时的学习者会漏掉说话人在下令或讲述不可避免之事。第二种用法中的过去形式 was to 是讲故事的典型写法，是站在已知结局的立场回顾。

'Be about to' is about timing, not planning. It means the action will start within moments, so it is incompatible with a distant time adverbial: 'She is about to leave next month' is wrong. Use it for interruptions and coincidences, often with 'when' or 'just as': 'I was about to call you when you walked in.' Adding 'just' makes the immediacy stronger.

be about to 关乎时间点，不关乎计划。它表示动作马上就要开始，因此不能与遥远的时间状语连用：She is about to leave next month 是错的。它常用于被打断或巧合的场合，多与 when 或 just as 搭配：I was about to call you when you walked in。加上 just 则更强调紧迫。

Choose 'be due to' when a schedule or expectation exists, such as a timetable, a deadline or a medical estimate: 'The train is due to arrive at six.' It does not say the event is certain, only that it is expected at that time, so delays are easy to mention afterwards. Do not confuse it with 'due to' meaning 'because of', which is a preposition followed by a noun and has nothing to do with the future.

当存在时间表或预期（时刻表、截止日期、医生的预计）时，用 be due to：The train is due to arrive at six。它不保证一定发生，只说预计在那个时间发生，所以之后说延误也很自然。不要把它与表示“由于”的介词 due to 混淆，后者后接名词，与将来无关。

In negative sentences the three structures diverge sharply. 'Is not to' is a prohibition ('You are not to touch the exhibits'), 'is not about to' means someone firmly refuses ('I am not about to apologise'), and 'is not due to' simply says nothing is expected yet. Learners often pick the wrong one and accidentally issue an order or an emphatic refusal when they meant a neutral statement.

在否定句中三者差别很大。is not to 是禁令（You are not to touch the exhibits），is not about to 表示坚决不肯（I am not about to apologise），is not due to 只是说预计还没到时候。学习者常选错，结果无意中下了命令或表达了强烈拒绝，而本意只是中性陈述。

Perfect forms such as 'was to have done' and 'was about to do' show a plan that did not happen. 'The meeting was to have started at ten' implies it did not. Be careful with 'was due to', which can leave the outcome open, while 'was to have' almost always signals failure. Use these forms when you want to report a cancelled or unfulfilled arrangement concisely.

完成式如 was to have done 表示原定计划没有实现。The meeting was to have started at ten 暗示会议没有如期开始。注意 was due to 可以不交代结果，而 was to have 几乎总是表示落空。想简洁地说明某项安排被取消或未兑现时，就用这些形式。

Examples例句

  • The Prime Minister is to address the nation tonight. 首相今晚将向全国发表讲话。
  • You are to report to the manager before you start work. 你开始工作前必须先向经理报到。
  • The new library is to open in September. 新图书馆定于九月开放。
  • He was never to see his hometown again. 他注定再也见不到家乡了。
  • If we are to meet the deadline, we need more staff. 如果我们要赶上截止日期，就需要更多人手。
  • Visitors are not to enter the laboratory without permission. 未经许可，参观者不得进入实验室。
  • The two leaders are to meet in Geneva next week. 两国领导人下周将在日内瓦会面。
  • I was about to leave when the phone rang. 我正要离开，电话响了。
  • Hurry up, the film is about to start. 快点，电影马上就要开始了。
  • She looked as if she was about to cry. 她看上去像是要哭了。
  • I am not about to give up after all this effort. 我费了这么大力气，可不打算现在放弃。
  • The plane is about to take off, so please fasten your seat belts. 飞机即将起飞，请系好安全带。
  • We were just about to order when the waiter came over. 我们刚要点菜，服务员就走了过来。
  • The train is due to arrive at 6.15. 火车预计六点十五分到达。
  • Her baby is due to be born in March. 她的宝宝预产期在三月。
  • The report is due to be submitted by Friday. 报告应在周五之前提交。
  • The results are due to be announced this afternoon, but nobody knows exactly when. 结果预计今天下午公布，但没人知道具体时间。
  • The flight was due to land at noon, but it was delayed by fog. 航班原定中午降落，但因大雾延误了。
  • The meeting was to have started at ten, but the chairman was stuck in traffic. 会议原定十点开始，但主席被堵在路上了。
  • Rent is due to be paid on the first of each month. 租金应于每月一号支付。
  • The bridge is due to reopen once the repairs are finished. 桥梁修缮完成后预计重新开放。
  • They were to have been married that summer, but the war intervened. 他们本来要在那年夏天结婚，但战争打乱了一切。
  • No one is to leave the building until the alarm has been checked. 警报检查完毕之前，任何人不得离开大楼。

More pairs: same idea, different choice更多对比句组

You are not to open the box. 你不许打开这个盒子。
A prohibition issued by an authority.有权威者发出的禁令。
I am not about to open the box. 我才不打算打开这个盒子。
A firm refusal by the speaker.说话人的坚决拒绝。
The box is not due to be opened until Friday. 这个盒子预计要到周五才会被打开。
A neutral statement that opening is not expected before Friday, not a refusal or a prohibition.中性陈述：预计不会早于周五打开，既不是拒绝，也不是禁止。
The play was to start at eight. 这出戏原定八点开演。
Past arrangement; the outcome is left open and the context decides.过去的安排，结果未明，由上下文决定。
The play was to have started at eight. 这出戏本该八点开演。
The arrangement was not carried out.该安排未能实现。
The play was about to start when the power failed. 戏正要开演时停电了。
An interrupted moment just before the start.开演前一刻被打断。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ She is about to graduate next June.
✓ She is due to graduate next June.
'Be about to' requires immediacy and cannot take a distant time expression. A scheduled event months away calls for 'be due to' or 'be to'.
be about to 要求事情迫在眼前，不能与遥远的时间状语连用。几个月后的既定安排应使用 be due to 或 be to。
✗ The delay was due to happen because of the storm.
✓ The delay was due to the storm.
Learners mix the prepositional 'due to' meaning 'because of' with the scheduling 'be due to + verb'. Only the second is followed by a bare verb and refers to an expected future event.
学习者容易混淆表示“由于”的介词 due to 与表示预计的 be due to + 动词。只有后者接动词原形，指预计发生的将来之事。
✗ I am not to go to the party, I have too much work.
✓ I am not going to the party, I have too much work.
'Am not to' sounds like someone has forbidden you to go. For a personal decision or plan, use 'be going to' or the present continuous.
am not to 听起来像是有人禁止你去。表达个人决定或计划，应使用 be going to 或现在进行时。
✗ The train is about to arrive at 6 o'clock tomorrow.
✓ The train is due to arrive at 6 o'clock tomorrow.
A specific clock time in the future points to a timetable, so 'be due to' fits. 'Be about to' cannot be combined with a precise future time.
将来的具体钟点指向时刻表，应使用 be due to。be about to 不能和精确的将来时间连用。
✗ The meeting was to have started and it started on time.
✓ The meeting was to start at ten and it started on time.
'Was to have + past participle' signals that the plan failed, so it contradicts the idea that the meeting took place as planned. Use plain 'was to + infinitive' for a plan that was carried out.
was to have + 过去分词表示计划落空，与“会议如期举行”相矛盾。计划得以实现时，应用普通的 was to + 动词原形。
✗ He is about to buying a new car.
✓ He is about to buy a new car.
All three structures are followed by the bare infinitive with 'to'. Learners sometimes add an -ing form after 'to' because they treat it as a preposition.
这三种结构后面都接带 to 的不定式。学习者有时把 to 当成介词，错误地加上 -ing 形式。
