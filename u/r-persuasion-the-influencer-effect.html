When Influence Becomes Advertising 当影响力变成广告

Online creators can shape consumer behaviour, making transparency and audience awareness essential. 网络创作者能够影响消费者行为，因此透明度和受众意识至关重要。

The modern media environment has created a new kind of persuasive voice: the online creator. Unlike traditional advertisements, creator content often appears within ordinary conversations about travel, technology, food or everyday life. Followers may develop a sense of personal connection with someone they watch regularly. This relationship can make recommendations feel more authentic than conventional commercial messages.

现代媒体环境创造了一种新型的说服性声音：网络创作者。与传统广告不同，创作者发布的内容通常融入有关旅行、科技、食品或日常生活的普通对话中。粉丝可能会与自己经常观看的人建立一种个人联系。这种关系会让推荐显得比传统商业信息更加真实。

There is nothing inherently wrong with creators earning income through partnerships. Businesses need ways to communicate with potential customers, and audiences may genuinely appreciate useful product demonstrations. However, transparency matters. If a recommendation is financially motivated, viewers should be able to recognise that relationship. Without clear disclosure, commercial persuasion can be mistaken for independent personal advice.

创作者通过合作获得收入本身并没有什么问题。企业需要与潜在客户沟通的方式，而受众也可能确实喜欢有用的产品演示。然而，透明度十分重要。如果一项推荐受到经济利益驱动，观众就应该能够识别这种关系。如果缺乏明确披露，商业说服就可能被误认为独立的个人建议。

The problem becomes more complicated when creators build an identity around being ordinary and relatable. A carefully planned promotional message may look spontaneous, while a polished commercial arrangement may be presented as a friendly suggestion. Although audiences are not necessarily passive or easily deceived, repeated exposure to persuasive content can gradually influence their expectations and spending habits.

当创作者围绕普通、亲切的形象建立个人身份时，问题就会变得更加复杂。经过精心策划的宣传信息可能看起来很自然，而经过包装的商业合作也可能被呈现为朋友之间的建议。尽管受众并非必然被动或容易受骗，但反复接触说服性内容可能逐渐影响他们的期望和消费习惯。

Platforms also have a responsibility to make commercial relationships understandable. Clear labels, accessible explanations and consistent disclosure practices can help users evaluate what they are seeing. Nevertheless, responsibility should not rest entirely with platforms or creators. Consumers can develop healthier habits by checking independent reviews, comparing alternatives and questioning unusually enthusiastic claims. By and large, trust is more sustainable when influence is accompanied by honesty.

平台也有责任让商业关系变得容易理解。清晰的标签、易于获取的说明和一致的披露做法可以帮助用户评估自己看到的内容。不过，责任不应该完全落在平台或创作者身上。消费者可以通过查看独立评论、比较不同选择以及质疑异常热情的宣传说法来培养更健康的习惯。总体而言，当影响力伴随着诚实时，信任才更具可持续性。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

If a recommendation is financially motivated, viewers should be able to recognise that relationship.
The if clause expresses a real condition, while should be able to combines advice with the idea of capability.
if 从句表达真实条件，而 should be able to 结合了建议与能力的含义。
Although audiences are not necessarily passive or easily deceived, repeated exposure to persuasive content can gradually influence their expectations and spending habits.
Although introduces a concession, and the subject repeated exposure is a nominalised expression describing an ongoing experience.
Although 引导让步状语从句，主语 repeated exposure 则是一个名词化表达，用来描述持续发生的接触行为。
