For years, I imagined that happiness was waiting somewhere in the future. I would feel satisfied when I received a promotion, moved into a larger apartment, or finally took the journey I had been planning. Each achievement produced a brief sense of excitement, followed by another goal. Without noticing it, I had turned everyday life into a waiting room for a better future.

多年来，我一直认为幸福正在未来的某个地方等待着我。当我得到晋升、搬进更大的公寓，或者终于开始计划已久的旅行时，我就会感到满足。每一次成就都会带来短暂的兴奋，随后又出现下一个目标。在不知不觉中，我把日常生活变成了等待更美好未来的候车室。

The turning point came during an ordinary weekend. A planned trip was cancelled because of bad weather, leaving me with an unexpected free afternoon. At first, I felt disappointed. Then I prepared a simple lunch, walked through a nearby park, and spent an hour reading by the window. Nothing remarkable happened, yet I felt unusually peaceful. I began to question why I had overlooked such experiences.

转折点发生在一个普通的周末。由于天气不好，原定的旅行被取消了，我因此意外获得了一个空闲的下午。起初，我感到失望。后来，我准备了一顿简单的午餐，在附近的公园散步，并在窗边读了一个小时的书。没有发生任何特别的事情，但我感到格外平静。我开始思考自己为什么一直忽略这些体验。

I started keeping a small journal, recording three things that had made each day worthwhile. Some entries described conversations with friends; others mentioned the smell of fresh bread or the satisfaction of completing a difficult task. This practice did not eliminate my problems. However, it helped me pay attention to positive experiences that would otherwise have disappeared into the background. Gradually, my expectations became less demanding.

我开始写一本小日记，记录每天三件值得珍惜的事情。有些记录描述了与朋友的交谈，另一些则提到了新鲜面包的香气，或者完成一项困难任务后的满足感。这种做法并没有消除我的问题。然而，它帮助我关注那些原本可能被忽略的积极体验。渐渐地，我的期待不再那么苛刻。

I still have ambitions, and I still enjoy working towards future goals. While gratitude cannot solve every difficulty, it tends to change the way we experience the present. I have also learned that contentment is different from complacency. It does not require abandoning improvement; it simply allows us to appreciate what already exists. Happiness, I suspect, is partly the ability to stop postponing our enjoyment of life.

我仍然有抱负，也仍然享受为未来目标努力的过程。虽然感恩无法解决所有困难，但它往往能够改变我们体验当下的方式。我也逐渐明白，知足与自满并不相同。知足并不要求我们放弃进步，它只是让我们能够欣赏已经拥有的东西。我想，幸福在某种程度上就是不再一味推迟享受生活的能力。

Words词

/kənˈtentmənt/ n.
满足感；知足
find contentment; a sense of contentment
She found contentment in a quiet life close to nature. 她在亲近自然的宁静生活中找到了满足感。
/ˈtɜːnɪŋ pɔɪnt/ n. phr.
转折点
reach a turning point; a major turning point
Moving abroad became a turning point in his personal development. 移居国外成为他个人成长中的一个转折点。
/ˌwɜːθˈwaɪl/ adj.
值得的；有价值的
a worthwhile experience; worthwhile activities
Teaching children to read is a worthwhile activity. 教孩子们阅读是一项有价值的活动。
/ˈɡrætɪtjuːd/ n.
感恩；感激
express gratitude; a feeling of gratitude
He expressed gratitude for the support of his neighbours. 他对邻居们的支持表示了感激。
/pəˈspəʊn/ v.
推迟；延后
postpone a decision; postpone a meeting
They decided to postpone their holiday until the weather improved. 他们决定将假期推迟到天气好转以后。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

Without noticing it, I had turned everyday life into a waiting room for a better future.
The past perfect had turned describes an earlier, gradually developing situation viewed from a later point in the past.
过去完成时 had turned 描述从过去某个较晚时间点回望时，已经逐渐形成的更早状态。
While gratitude cannot solve every difficulty, it tends to change the way we experience the present.
The sentence combines a concessive while-clause with the hedging expression tends to, making the claim balanced rather than absolute.
句子结合了表示让步的 while 从句和限定表达 tends to，使观点更加平衡，而不是绝对化。
