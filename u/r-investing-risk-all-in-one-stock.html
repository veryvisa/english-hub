Should I Bet It All? 我该孤注一掷吗？

🧠 Learn the 5 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 5 个新词

Before buying a popular stock, a saver should build a safety cushion, consider when the money is needed and avoid relying on a single investment. 在购买热门股票之前，储蓄者应先建立安全缓冲，考虑何时需要用钱，并避免依赖单一投资。

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Dear Money Mentor, a company that everyone at my office is talking about has seen its share price climb for months. I have some savings sitting in the bank, earning very little, and I am tempted to put all of them into this one stock. My friends say that waiting would be foolish. What do you think? Restless Saver

理财顾问您好：我办公室里人人都在谈论一家公司，它的股价已经连涨好几个月。我有一些积蓄放在银行里，收益很低，我很想把它们全部投到这一只股票上。朋友们说再等下去就太傻了。您怎么看？——坐不住的储户

Dear Restless Saver, I understand the temptation, because standing still while others appear to be getting rich is uncomfortable. However, a price that has risen quickly can fall just as quickly. Past gains tell you what has already happened; they cannot tell you what will happen next. By the time a stock has become office gossip, much of the good news is likely to be reflected in its price.

坐不住的储户你好：我理解这种诱惑，因为看着别人似乎在发财，自己却原地不动，确实不好受。然而，涨得快的价格也可能跌得一样快。过去的收益只能告诉你已经发生了什么，无法告诉你接下来会发生什么。等到一只股票成了办公室里的谈资，很多利好消息很可能已经反映在股价里了。

Before you invest anything, set aside an emergency fund that covers your essential expenses for a while, and pay close attention to any expensive debts. This safety cushion means that a broken boiler or a sudden job loss will not force you to sell at a bad time. Then ask yourself when you will need the rest. If the answer is soon, investing it in shares may not be appropriate at all.

在投资之前，先留出一笔能支付一段时间基本开销的应急资金，并且要密切关注任何高成本的债务。有了这个安全缓冲，锅炉坏了或突然失业时，你就不会被迫在糟糕的时机卖出。然后问问自己，剩下的钱什么时候要用。如果答案是很快就要用，那么把它投进股市可能根本不合适。

Should you decide to go ahead, do not put all your eggs in one basket. Spread your money across different companies, industries and types of investment, so that one disappointment cannot wipe you out. While diversification will not protect you from every fall, it tends to soften the blow. If you still want to back your favourite company, limit it to a small sum that you could afford to lose. Your friends may be right about the stock, but they will not be paying your bills if they are wrong.

如果你决定投资，不要把所有鸡蛋放在一个篮子里。把钱分散到不同的公司、行业和投资类型上，这样一次失利就不至于让你血本无归。虽然分散投资不能让你躲过每一次下跌，但它往往能减轻打击。如果你仍想支持自己看好的公司，就把金额限制在亏得起的小数目内。你的朋友们对这只股票的看法也许是对的，但万一他们错了，替你付账单的可不是他们。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

Should you decide to go ahead, do not put all your eggs in one basket.
’Should you decide’ is a formal inverted conditional meaning ‘If you should decide’, followed by an imperative.
Should you decide 是正式的倒装条件句，相当于 If you should decide，后接祈使句。
By the time a stock has become office gossip, much of the good news is likely to be reflected in its price.
’By the time’ introduces a time clause, while ‘is likely to be reflected’ combines hedging with a passive infinitive.
By the time 引导时间状语从句；is likely to be reflected 把委婉限定与被动不定式结合在一起。
