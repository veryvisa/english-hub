Smart People Share False Stories Too 聪明人也会转发假消息

🧠 Learn the 4 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 4 个新词

Read an opinion column on why people share false stories, and notice the nouns built from verbs and adjectives that carry its argument. 读一篇讨论人们为什么转发假消息的评论，留意那些由动词和形容词变来、撑起论证的名词。

Most people who share false stories can tell true from false; what they lack in the moment is attention, not intelligence.大多数转发假消息的人其实分得清真假；他们那一刻缺的是注意力，不是智力。

We like to think that people who fall for false stories and rumours are simply not very bright. The evidence does not support that. Researchers who study online sharing have found that many people who pass on misleading headlines can tell true from false perfectly well when they are asked to judge them. The problem is not intelligence. It is attention.

我们总以为，被假消息和谣言骗到的人就是不太聪明。证据并不支持这种看法。研究网络转发的学者发现，很多转发误导性标题的人，在被请去判断时，其实完全分得清真假。问题不在智力，而在注意力。

Scrolling is fast, and our feeds reward speed. A headline full of outrage triggers a gut feeling before careful thought has a chance to start, and the share button is always one tap away. Stories taken out of context circulate fastest of all, because nobody takes them at face value for long enough to wonder. In that moment, most of us are not asking "Is this true?" We are asking "Will my friends react to this?"

刷屏的速度很快，信息流奖励的也是快。一个满是愤怒的标题，会在认真思考还来不及开始时就勾起本能反应，而转发按钮永远只差一下点击。被断章取义的内容传得最快，因为没有人会停在表面意思上久到起疑。那一刻，我们大多数人问的不是「这是真的吗」，而是「朋友们会对这个有反应吗」。

That is why a small nudge can help. In several experiments, people who were asked to rate the accuracy of a single neutral headline before they began scrolling went on to share noticeably fewer false stories. Nobody lectured them. A small reminder simply put accuracy back into their minds.

所以，一个小小的提醒就能起作用。在几项实验里，被请在开始刷屏前给一个中性标题的准确性打分的人，之后转发的假消息明显少了。没有人给他们上课，只是一个小提醒，就把「准不准」重新放回了他们脑子里。

This should change how we talk about the problem. Calling people stupid creates misunderstanding and makes them defensive; it rarely leads to any improvement. A better approach is to build awareness: to show friends and relatives how the trick works, and to admit that it works on us too. Confidence is part of the problem. The people most sure that they would never be fooled are often the least willing to check, and willingness to check is the whole game.

这应该改变我们谈论这个问题的方式。骂别人蠢只会制造误解、让人起戒心，很少带来任何改进。更好的办法是提高意识：让亲友看清这套把戏怎么运作，并承认它对我们自己也管用。自信本身就是问题的一部分。最确信自己绝不会上当的人，往往最不愿意去核实，而愿不愿意核实，恰恰是关键所在。

I am not suggesting that we stop sharing news. I am suggesting a pause of a few seconds and one honest question: would I still share this if it came from a source I did not trust? Most days, that may be enough.

我并不是建议大家别再分享新闻。我建议的是停几秒钟，问自己一个诚实的问题：如果这是一个我不信任的来源发的，我还会转吗？大多数时候，这也许就够了。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

The people most sure that they would never be fooled are often the least willing to check
Two superlatives balance each other: most sure … the least willing. would never be fooled is a passive with would, because being fooled is only imagined.
两个最高级互相对照：most sure……the least willing。would never be fooled 是带 would 的被动，因为「上当」只是假想。
people who were asked to rate the accuracy of a single neutral headline before they began scrolling went on to share noticeably fewer false stories
The long subject runs all the way to scrolling; the main verb is went on to share (= did next, as a result).
主语很长，一直到 scrolling 才结束；谓语是 went on to share（接着就……，作为结果）。
