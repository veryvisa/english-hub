Dear Law Desk, three weeks ago a moving company moved us across town. When we unpacked, my grandmother's dining table had a cracked leg and a deep scratch across the top. I have made a complaint by phone twice, and each time I was told someone would "look into it". Nobody has taken responsibility. What can I actually do? Frank, by email

法律问答：三周前，一家搬家公司帮我们搬到城另一头。拆箱时发现，我祖母留下的餐桌断了一条腿，桌面上还有一道很深的划痕。我已经打电话投诉了两次，每次都被告知会有人「去查一下」。至今没人承担责任。我到底能做什么？——弗兰克，来信

Dear Frank, I'm sorry about the table. The steps below are general; the details vary from place to place, so check the rules where you live, or talk to a free legal clinic, before you act.

亲爱的弗兰克：桌子的事很遗憾。下面说的是一般做法，各地细节不同，动手之前请查一下你所在地的规定，或者去免费法律诊所问问。

First, put everything in writing. Phone calls leave no trace. Send a short, polite letter or email that says what happened, when, what the damage is and what you want: a repair, a replacement or payment, with a date by which you expect a reply. This is often called a demand letter, and it is surprising how many disputes settle at this stage.

第一，一切落成文字。电话不留痕迹。寄一封简短、礼貌的信或邮件，写明发生了什么、什么时候、损坏情况，以及你要什么：修理、换新或赔钱，并写上你希望对方在哪天之前答复。这通常叫索赔函，令人意外的是，很多纠纷在这一步就和解了。

Second, gather your evidence: photos of the damage, your contract, any receipts and a written repair quote. Read the contract for limits on what the mover is liable for. Some movers only pay according to weight, not value, unless you bought extra coverage, so it is worth knowing where you stand.

第二，收集证据：损坏处的照片、合同、各种收据，以及一份书面维修报价。看看合同里对搬家公司赔偿责任有什么限制。有的搬家公司只按重量赔、不按价值赔，除非你买了额外保障，所以值得弄清自己处在什么位置。

If they still do not respond, small claims court is designed for disputes like yours. You often do not need a lawyer, and the forms are written for ordinary people. One warning: there is a limitation period, a deadline for starting a claim, and once it has passed you may lose the right to claim at all. So do not let "we'll look into it" run out the clock. Make a decision soon about how far you want to take this.

如果对方仍不回应，小额索偿法庭就是为你这类纠纷设的。你往往不需要律师，表格也是写给普通人填的。提醒一句：有诉讼时效，也就是提起索赔的期限，一旦过了，你可能就完全失去索赔的权利。所以别让一句「我们去查查」把时间耗光。尽快决定你打算把这件事推进到哪一步。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

I have made a complaint by phone twice, and each time I was told someone would "look into it".
have made … twice (present perfect: experience so far, may happen again) vs was told (past simple passive: each finished call). look into: investigate.
have made … twice（现在完成时：到目前为止的经历，还可能再有）对比 was told（一般过去时被动：每一通已结束的电话）。look into：调查。
there is a limitation period, a deadline for starting a claim, and once it has passed you may lose the right to claim at all.
The phrase between commas explains the legal term in plain words. once it has passed uses the present perfect after a time word for a future moment.
逗号之间的短语用大白话解释了法律术语。once it has passed 在时间连词后用现在完成时，指将来的某个时刻。
