Why Muscle Matters More After Fifty 五十岁以后，肌肉为什么更要紧

Most people think of muscle as something for young men in gyms. In fact, leg strength may be one of the most reliable signs of how independent you will be at eighty. From middle age onwards, adults who do no strength work slowly lose muscle mass and strength, and the loss tends to speed up later in life. When it becomes serious, doctors call it sarcopenia.

大多数人以为肌肉是健身房里年轻男人的事。其实，腿部力量可能是预示你八十岁时能否自理最可靠的信号之一。从中年起，不做力量训练的成年人会慢慢流失肌肉量和力量，到了晚年流失往往加快。严重到一定程度，医生称之为肌少症。

Why does it matter? Muscle does more than lift shopping bags. Strong legs help you get out of a chair, climb stairs and catch yourself when you trip. Muscle also helps the body manage blood sugar. A sedentary life speeds up the loss; regular strength training slows it down and can even reverse part of it, at almost any age.

为什么要紧？肌肉不只是用来提购物袋的。腿有力，你才能从椅子上站起来、爬楼梯、绊一下时稳住自己。肌肉还帮身体调节血糖。久坐的生活加速流失；规律的力量训练能减缓它，甚至在几乎任何年龄都能逆转一部分。

The good news is that improvement can come quickly. In many studies, people in their seventies and eighties who started simple strength training became noticeably stronger within a few months. You do not need a gym. Body-weight exercises, such as standing up from a chair ten times, or elastic bands at home, are a good place to start. Many guidelines suggest at least two sessions a week.

好消息是改善可以来得很快。在许多研究里，七八十岁才开始做简单力量训练的人，几个月内就明显变壮了。你不需要健身房。自重练习，比如从椅子上站起来十次，或在家用弹力带，都是不错的起点。许多指南建议每周至少练两次。

Walking is excellent for the heart, but on its own it does little for strength. Stretching keeps your flexibility, which matters too, and eases stiff joints, but it is not the same thing. The aim is to make the muscles work a little harder than they are used to, then gradually increase the effort. Your joints usually benefit as well, because stronger muscles support them.

走路对心脏极好，但光走路对力量帮助不大。拉伸保持柔韧性，这也重要，还能缓解关节僵硬，但它不是一回事。目标是让肌肉比习惯的稍微累一点，再逐步加量。关节通常也会受益，因为更强的肌肉能撑住它们。

A sensible expectation is not to look like an athlete. It is to carry your own groceries at eighty-five, and to have the confidence to walk on uneven ground. If you have heart problems, joint pain or another health condition, ask your doctor or a physiotherapist to help you choose safe exercises first.

合理的期待不是练成运动员的样子，而是八十五岁还能自己提菜，走坑洼的路也有底气。如果你有心脏问题、关节疼痛或其他健康状况，先请医生或物理治疗师帮你挑安全的练习。

In fact, leg strength may be one of the most reliable signs of how independent you will be at eighty.
one of the most + adjective + plural noun; how independent you will be is a statement-order clause after of.
