📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Open the full explanation: 5 points展开细讲：5 段讲解

Many Chinglish errors come from translating each word separately. Learners say 'open the light' because the Chinese verb for 'open' also covers switching on electrical things. English uses 'turn on' or 'switch on' for lights, TVs and computers, and 'open' mainly for doors, windows, boxes and files.

许多中式英语错误来自逐词翻译。学习者说 'open the light'，是因为中文的“开”也可以用于电器。英语中灯、电视、电脑要用 turn on 或 switch on，open 主要用于门、窗、盒子和文件。

False friends are words that look or sound similar to a Chinese-English gloss you learned, but mean something different. For example, 'sympathy' is not the same as being 'sympathetic' to an idea, and 'eventually' does not mean 'possibly'. Always check a new word in a dictionary example, not only in a translation list.

假朋友是指看起来或学起来像某个中文释义、实际意义却不同的词。例如 eventually 不是“可能”，而是“最终”。学新词时要看词典例句，不要只看中文释义列表。

Some Chinese ideas need a whole English chunk, not one word. The Chinese phrase for 'take medicine' becomes 'take medicine' in English, but 'eat medicine' sounds odd. Likewise 'do exercise' is less natural than 'get some exercise' or 'work out'. Learn the verb together with its noun partner.

有些中文概念在英语里需要整个搭配，而不是单个词。“吃药”要说 take medicine，说 eat medicine 就怪。同理，do exercise 不如 get some exercise 或 work out 自然。要把动词和它的名词搭档一起记。

Politeness and register also cause trouble. Saying 'I want you to give me a discount' sounds demanding, while 'Would it be possible to get a discount?' sounds polite. Chinese often shows politeness through context or tone, but English usually needs softer chunks such as 'would you mind' or 'I was wondering if'.

礼貌程度和语体也会出问题。说 'I want you to give me a discount' 听起来像命令，而 'Would it be possible to get a discount?' 就礼貌得多。中文常靠语境或语气表达礼貌，英语通常需要 would you mind、I was wondering if 这类更委婉的搭配。

Another common pattern is adding words English does not need, or leaving out words English requires. Learners say 'I very like it' or 'He has 30 years old'. English needs 'really like' or 'like it very much', and uses 'be' with age: 'He is 30'. Check the verb, the adverb position and the small grammar words each time.

另一个常见问题是多加或漏掉英语需要的词。学习者会说 'I very like it' 或 'He has 30 years old'。英语要说 really like 或 like it very much，年龄用 be：He is 30。每次都要检查动词、副词位置和小语法词。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ Please open the light.
✓ Please turn on the light.
Learners translate the Chinese verb for 'open' directly. In English, 'open' is for doors, windows and files, while lights and appliances are turned on.
学习者直接翻译中文的“开”。英语中 open 用于门、窗、文件，灯和电器要用 turn on。
✗ I ate medicine this morning.
✓ I took medicine this morning.
The Chinese verb for 'eat' is used with medicine, but English uses the collocation 'take medicine'.
中文说“吃药”，但英语的固定搭配是 take medicine，不能用 eat。
✗ I will possibly find a job.
✓ I will eventually find a job.
Some learners think 'eventually' means 'maybe'. It actually means 'in the end, after some time', and it describes something that does happen.
有些学习者以为 eventually 是“可能”。它的意思是“最终”，指经过一段时间后确实发生的事。
✗ He is a very sensible person, and he cries when people criticise him.
✓ He is a very sensitive person, and he cries when people criticise him.
'Sensible' looks like the Chinese idea of 'sensitive' but means 'practical and wise'. Use 'sensitive' for people who are easily hurt or affected.
sensible 的意思是“明智的”，不是“敏感的”。表示容易受伤或反应强烈要用 sensitive。
✗ I very like English songs.
✓ I really like English songs. / I like English songs very much.
In English, 'very' cannot directly modify a verb like 'like'. Use 'really' before the verb or 'very much' after the object.
英语中 very 不能直接修饰动词 like。要在动词前用 really，或在宾语后用 very much。
✗ My grandfather has 80 years old.
✓ My grandfather is 80 years old.
Chinese can express age with a verb that sounds like 'have'. English uses 'be' with age.
中文表达年龄的动词让学习者想到 have，但英语年龄要用 be。
