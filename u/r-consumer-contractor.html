Our Bathroom Is Half Finished 卫生间装修做了一半

🧠 Learn the 4 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 4 个新词

Read an advice column about a contractor who disappeared and see how unreal conditionals help give advice without blame. 读一篇关于装修工失联的建议专栏，看非真实条件句怎样帮人给建议又不指责。

📖 Key card知识卡
Decide what you really want, put the agreement in writing, and give a firm date before you reach for a bad review.先想清楚你真正要什么，把约定落成书面，给出确定的期限，然后再考虑写差评。

Read and listen读与听

308 words
Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

"We hired a contractor to redo our bathroom. He took a deposit, did about half the work and has not come back for five weeks. He answers our texts with 'next week, I promise.' My husband wants to post a bad review everywhere. I just want a working shower. What should we do?" — Half a Bathroom

「我们请了一位装修工重做卫生间。他收了定金，干了大约一半的活儿，然后五个星期没再来。我们发短信，他就回『下周，保证』。我丈夫想到处发差评，我只想有个能用的淋浴。我们该怎么办？」——半个卫生间

Dear Half a Bathroom, I am sorry. This is one of the most common complaints I receive. Before anything else, send one clear email that sets out what was agreed, what has been done, what is left and the date by which you expect him back. Keep it calm. If this ever ends up in front of anyone else, that email will matter.

亲爱的「半个卫生间」：很遗憾。这是我收到最多的投诉之一。别的先不说，发一封清楚的邮件，写明当初约定了什么、已经做了什么、还剩什么，以及你希望他哪天之前回来。语气要平静。万一这件事以后要拿给别人看，这封邮件会很重要。

Then think about what you really want. If your goal were only to punish him, a review would do it. But you want a bathroom. Many contractors who disappear have simply taken on too many jobs at once, and a firm date sometimes brings them back. If he does not return by that date, follow up with a short note saying you will hire someone else to finish, and ask a local consumer protection office what your options are where you live.

然后想想你真正要的是什么。如果你的目的只是惩罚他，写个差评就够了。可你要的是卫生间。很多失联的装修工只是同时接了太多活，一个确定的期限有时就能把他们叫回来。如果到那天他还没回来，就再发一条短信跟进，说你会另请人来收尾，再问问当地的消费者保护机构，在你住的地方你有哪些选择。

For next time, and for every reader planning work: get at least two quotes, check references, never pay most of the price up front, and link payments to stages of the work. I know it is easy to say this now. If I had followed my own advice the first time I renovated, I would have saved myself a whole summer.

下次装修，也给每位正打算装修的读者：至少拿两份报价，核实推荐人，绝不预付大部分款项，按施工阶段分期付款。我知道现在说这些很容易。要是我第一次装修时照自己的建议做了，就能省下整整一个夏天。

As for the review, wait until the job is finished or clearly abandoned, then write what happened, not how you felt. Facts are fairer, and other people deal with them better. A review written that way may also help you resolve the next dispute faster.

至于差评，等活儿干完或者明显被撂下了再写，写发生了什么，而不是你有多生气。事实更公道，别人也更好处理。这样写出来的评价，说不定还能帮你更快解决下一次纠纷。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

If your goal were only to punish him, a review would do it.
Second conditional: were … would. It is unreal because her goal is not only to punish him. The columnist uses it to show a choice without telling her she is wrong.
第二类条件句：were … would。说它不真实，是因为她的目的并不只是惩罚他。专栏作者用它摆出选择，而不直接说她错了。
If I had followed my own advice the first time I renovated, I would have saved myself a whole summer.
Third conditional about the writer's own past: the advice feels less like a lecture when the writer admits a mistake.
关于作者自己过去的第三类条件句：作者先认自己的错，建议就不像说教。
