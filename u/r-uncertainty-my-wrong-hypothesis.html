The Afternoon My Hypothesis Failed 我的假设失败的那个下午

🧠 Learn the 2 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 2 个新词

A young researcher learns that being proved wrong is not a disaster but the real beginning of scientific work. 一位年轻研究者认识到，被证明错误并非灾难，而是科学工作真正的开端。

Read and listen读与听

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

For almost two years I was convinced that I understood how a certain kind of soil bacteria responds to heat. My supervisor had warned me not to fall in love with my own hypothesis, but I ignored her. I thought I was being confident, when in fact I was simply being stubborn.

将近两年来，我深信自己了解某类土壤细菌对高温的反应。导师曾警告我不要爱上自己的假设，但我没有理会。我以为自己是自信，其实只是固执。

The turning point came one grey afternoon in the lab. I had repeated an experiment for the fifth time, and the results contradicted everything I had written in my draft. At first I assumed that I had made a mistake. I checked the equipment, redid the calculations, and even blamed the weather, but the pattern would not go away.

转折点出现在实验室里一个阴沉的下午。我第五次重复同一个实验，结果与我草稿里写的一切相矛盾。起初我以为是自己出了错。我检查了设备，重新做了计算，甚至怪到天气头上，但这个规律就是不消失。

What I felt then was not excitement but something closer to shame. Having built a whole thesis on one idea, I did not know how to explain that it might be wrong. It was my supervisor who changed my mind. Rather than being disappointed, she smiled and said that a result which surprises you is usually worth more than one which confirms what you expected.

当时我感受到的不是兴奋，而是更接近羞耻。整篇论文都建立在一个想法上，我不知道该如何解释它可能是错的。改变我想法的是导师。她没有失望，反而笑着说，一个让你吃惊的结果，通常比一个证实你预期的结果更有价值。

Over the following months I learned to treat my conclusions as provisional. I began writing down not only what I thought but how sure I was, and what evidence would make me change my view. While this felt awkward at first, it gradually became a habit, and my work improved because of it.

在接下来的几个月里，我学会了把自己的结论视为暂定的。我开始不仅写下我的想法，还写下自己有多确定，以及什么证据会让我改变看法。起初这让人别扭，但渐渐成了习惯，我的工作也因此改进。

I still think about that afternoon. Admitting that I was wrong did not end my career; it began it. Science, I now believe, is by and large a conversation in which being corrected is a form of being helped, and the sooner we accept that, the less it hurts.

我至今仍会想起那个下午。承认自己错了并没有终结我的职业生涯，反而开启了它。我现在相信，科学总体上是一场对话，在其中被纠正是一种被帮助；我们越早接受这一点，就越不痛苦。

Words词

/haɪˈpɒθəsɪs/ n.
假设，假说
test a hypothesis; a working hypothesis
She revised her hypothesis after the third experiment failed to match it. 第三次实验与之不符后，她修改了自己的假设。
/ˈstʌbən/ adj.
固执的
a stubborn refusal; stubborn about
He was too stubborn to admit that the data pointed the other way. 他太固执，不肯承认数据指向相反的方向。
/ˌkɒntrəˈdɪktɪd/ v.
与……相矛盾
contradict the evidence; directly contradict
The new figures contradicted the claim made in the press release. 新的数字与新闻稿中的说法相矛盾。
/prəˈvɪʒənəl/ adj.
暂定的，临时的
provisional conclusions; a provisional view
Treat the early results as provisional until they have been checked. 在核实之前，请把早期结果视为暂定的。
/baɪ ənd lɑːdʒ/ phr.
总体上，大体而言
by and large satisfied; by and large correct
By and large, my predictions turned out to be right. 总体而言，我的预测被证明是对的。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

Having built a whole thesis on one idea, I did not know how to explain that it might be wrong.
The perfect participle clause ‘Having built...’ shows an action completed before the main clause and shares its subject ‘I’.
完成时分词结构 Having built... 表示先于主句发生的动作，并与主句共用主语 I。
Rather than being disappointed, she smiled and said that a result which surprises you is usually worth more than one which confirms what you expected.
’Rather than’ takes an -ing form, and the reported clause uses ‘one’ to avoid repeating ‘result’ and ‘usually’ to soften the claim.
Rather than 后接 -ing 形式；宾语从句用 one 代替 result 以避免重复，并用 usually 缓和语气。
