What Did You Really Mean? 你真正想表达什么？

Read and listen读与听

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Maya, a new employee, was discussing a project with Daniel, her colleague from another cultural background. She presented a proposal and asked whether he thought it was realistic. Daniel paused before replying, 'That could be an interesting approach.' Maya interpreted the response as enthusiastic approval. However, Daniel had intended to express reservations without openly rejecting her suggestion. Neither person was being dishonest; they were relying on different conventions for expressing disagreement.

新员工玛雅正在与来自另一种文化背景的同事丹尼尔讨论一个项目。她提出一份方案，并询问他是否认为方案切实可行。丹尼尔停顿了一下，然后回答：这可能是一种有趣的方法。玛雅将这句话理解为热情的赞同。然而，丹尼尔原本是想在不公开否定她建议的情况下表达保留意见。两个人都没有不诚实，他们只是依赖不同的表达异议的惯例。

In some communication settings, direct disagreement is considered efficient and respectful because it makes problems visible. In others, speakers tend to soften criticism to preserve social harmony and protect relationships. Neither approach is universally superior. While direct language can reduce ambiguity, it may also sound unnecessarily confrontational. Conversely, indirect language can maintain goodwill, although it sometimes leaves listeners uncertain about the speaker's actual position.

在某些交流环境中，直接表达异议被认为高效且尊重他人，因为这种方式能够让问题显现出来。在另一些环境中，说话者往往会缓和批评，以维持社会和谐并保护人际关系。两种方式都不是在所有情况下更优越的选择。虽然直接表达可以减少歧义，但也可能显得没有必要地咄咄逼人。相反，间接表达能够维持善意，不过有时会让听者无法确定说话者的真实立场。

'I thought you liked the proposal,' Maya said when they met again. 'I did like parts of it,' Daniel replied. 'I was concerned about the schedule, but I didn't want to dismiss your work.' Maya realised that she had treated a positive adjective as a complete endorsement. Daniel, meanwhile, recognised that his carefully softened response had failed to communicate the practical importance of his concerns. Both began to clarify what they meant rather than assuming that shared vocabulary guaranteed shared understanding.

再次见面时，玛雅说：我以为你喜欢这个方案。丹尼尔回答：我确实喜欢其中的一些部分。我担心的是时间安排，但我不想否定你的工作。玛雅意识到，自己把一个积极的形容词当成了全面认可。与此同时，丹尼尔也认识到，他经过谨慎缓和的回答未能传达自己担忧的实际重要性。两人开始澄清自己的真实意思，而不再假设使用相同的词汇就一定意味着理解一致。

They agreed to use more explicit questions in future meetings. Instead of asking whether an idea was realistic, Maya would ask which aspects needed improvement. Daniel would distinguish between genuine approval and polite acknowledgement. Such adjustments are likely to make collaboration more effective, although they cannot eliminate every misunderstanding. Cultural competence does not require memorising fixed rules about entire populations. It involves observing individual preferences, checking interpretations and remaining willing to revise one's assumptions.

他们同意今后的会议中使用更加明确的问题。玛雅不再笼统地询问方案是否切实可行，而是询问哪些方面需要改进。丹尼尔则会区分真正的赞同与礼貌性的认可。这样的调整可能使合作更加有效，尽管它们无法消除所有误解。文化能力并不要求人们记住关于整个群体的固定规则，而是要求观察个人偏好、核实自己的理解，并愿意修正原有假设。
