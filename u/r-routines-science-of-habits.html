How Habits Take Shape 习惯是如何形成的

🧠 Learn the 4 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 4 个新词

Habits develop through repeated behaviour, environmental signals, and rewards that encourage people to repeat an action. 习惯通过重复行为、环境提示以及鼓励人们再次行动的奖励逐渐形成。

Have you ever opened your phone without thinking, even when you had no particular message to check? This is a familiar example of habitual behaviour. A habit is an action that we perform regularly, sometimes with very little conscious attention. Habits help the brain manage everyday activities without requiring us to make a fresh decision each time.

你是否曾经不假思索地打开手机，即使当时并没有特别想查看的消息？这是习惯性行为的一个常见例子。习惯是我们经常进行的一种行动，有时几乎不需要有意识地思考。习惯帮助大脑处理日常活动，使我们不必每次都重新做出决定。

Many habits follow a simple pattern involving a cue, an action, and a reward. A cue is a signal that reminds us to do something. For example, seeing a coffee machine may remind someone to prepare a drink. The action is making the coffee, and the reward might be enjoying its taste or feeling more awake. Repeating this pattern can strengthen the connection between the signal and the behaviour.

许多习惯遵循一个简单的模式，其中包括提示、行动和奖励。提示是一种提醒我们做某事的信号。例如，看到咖啡机可能会让某人想起要准备一杯饮料。行动就是制作咖啡，而奖励可能是享受它的味道，或者感觉更加清醒。重复这种模式可以加强信号与行为之间的联系。

The environment also plays an important role. If healthy food is easy to see and reach, people may be more likely to choose it. Similarly, placing a book on a desk can encourage reading. This suggests that changing the environment may sometimes be easier than trying to change behaviour through willpower alone. Small adjustments can make desirable actions more convenient.

环境也发挥着重要作用。如果健康食品容易看见和拿到，人们可能更愿意选择它。同样，把一本书放在桌子上也能鼓励阅读。这说明，有时改变环境可能比单纯依靠意志力改变行为更容易。小小的调整可以让理想的行动变得更加方便。

However, habit formation is not identical for everyone. The time needed depends on the behaviour, the person, and the circumstances. While repetition is important, simply repeating an action does not guarantee that it will become permanent. A realistic goal, a supportive environment, and regular practice tend to make the process easier. When a habit becomes established, it can help people use their attention for other tasks.

然而，习惯的形成过程并非每个人都完全相同。所需时间取决于行为本身、个人情况以及具体环境。虽然重复很重要，但仅仅重复某个行动并不能保证它一定会成为永久习惯。现实的目标、支持性的环境和规律的练习通常会让这个过程更加容易。当一种习惯逐渐稳定下来时，它可以帮助人们把注意力用于其他任务。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

Habits help the brain manage everyday activities without requiring us to make a fresh decision each time.
Without is a preposition followed by the gerund requiring; require here takes an object (us) plus a to-infinitive (to make), so the phrase means 'without needing us to make...'.
without 是介词，后接动名词 requiring；require 接宾语 us 和不定式 to make，整个短语的意思是“不需要我们做出……”。
The time needed depends on the behaviour, the person, and the circumstances.
Needed is a reduced relative clause equivalent to that is needed, and depends on is a verb followed by its dependent preposition.
Needed是省略了that is的缩略定语从句，相当于that is needed；depends后面需要搭配介词on。
