Is That Really His Voice? 那真是他的声音吗？

🧠 Learn the 4 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 4 个新词

Follow two colleagues working out whether a shocking video is real, and pick up the phrasal verbs people use to handle a problem at work. 跟着两位同事判断一段惊人的视频是不是真的，学职场里处理问题时常用的短语动词。

Read and listen读与听

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Maya: Have you seen this clip? It's the CEO, saying the downtown office is closing in March and everyone there will be let go. It's been forwarded to half the department. Jonas: Hang on. Where did it come from? Did it come through any company channel?

玛雅：你看到这段视频了吗？是 CEO，说市中心办公室三月关闭，那里的人全部裁掉。半个部门都转了。约纳斯：等一下。它是从哪来的？走的是公司正式渠道吗？

Maya: No, someone from my old team sent it to me. But it's definitely his face and his voice. Listen to the way he says "folks". He always says that. Jonas: That's exactly what worries me. Voices are easy to copy now. A few minutes of someone speaking in public is enough to make a convincing deepfake. Before we fall for it, let's figure out where it started.

玛雅：不是，我以前组里的人发给我的。可那绝对是他的脸、他的声音。你听他说 folks 的样子，他一直这么说。约纳斯：我担心的正是这个。现在声音很容易仿。一个人在公开场合讲几分钟话，就够做出一段以假乱真的深度伪造视频。在上当之前，我们先弄清楚它是从哪开始的。

Maya: OK, but what if it's real? People on that team need to know. Jonas: Then they'll hear it from HR, not from a forwarded video. Look at the background. It's the same room as the town hall last spring. I think someone took that recording and put new words on top. Can you check whether his lips match the sound around the twenty-second mark?

玛雅：好吧，可万一是真的呢？那个组的人得知道。约纳斯：那他们会从人事部听到，而不是从一段转发的视频里。你看背景，和去年春天全员大会是同一个房间。我觉得有人拿了那段录像，在上面配了新的话。你能看看二十秒左右那里，他的嘴型和声音对不对得上吗？

Maya: You're right. It's slightly off. And he never gives a date like that in real meetings. I almost sent this to my sister, who works there. Jonas: Let's not deal with it on our own. I'll bring it up with our manager and point out the background. Could you follow up with the security team? They'll want to know who is passing it on.

玛雅：你说得对，有点对不上。而且他在真正的会上从来不这样报日期。我差点把它发给在那儿上班的妹妹。约纳斯：我们别自己处理。我去跟经理提这件事，把背景的问题指出来。你能跟进一下安全团队吗？他们会想知道是谁在传。

Maya: Fine. I'll hold off on telling anyone else until they get back to us. It's scary how easily I took it at face value. Jonas: It's scary how good the fakes are getting. My rule now is simple: the more shocking the message, the more boring the place it should come from.

玛雅：好。在他们回复之前，我先不跟任何人说。我那么轻易就信了，真吓人。约纳斯：吓人的是这些假东西做得越来越像了。我现在的规矩很简单：消息越惊人，它就越该来自一个越无聊的地方。

Words词

/ˈdiːpfeɪk/ n.
深度伪造（AI 伪造的音视频）
a convincing deepfake; spot a deepfake
It took experts a week to prove the video was a deepfake. 专家花了一周才证明那段视频是深度伪造的。
/let ˈɡəʊ/ v. phr.
解雇；裁掉（委婉）
be let go; let staff go
Twenty people were let go last month. 上个月有二十个人被裁了。
/həʊld ˈɒf/ phr. v.
暂缓；先不做
hold off on sth; hold off doing sth
Let's hold off on booking until we know the dates. 日期定下来之前先别订。
/ɡet ˈbæk tə/ phr. v.
回复（某人）
get back to sb; get back to you on sth
I'll get back to you by Friday. 我周五前回复你。
/ˈtʃænl/ n.
渠道；途径
official channels; through the right channel
Please send complaints through the proper channel. 投诉请走正规渠道。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

the more shocking the message, the more boring the place it should come from.
the more …, the more …: two things change together. The verb is can be left out of the first half.
the more……, the more……：两件事一起变化。前半句的 is 可以省略。
Can you check whether his lips match the sound around the twenty-second mark?
whether starts an indirect yes/no question, so the word order stays normal: his lips match, not do his lips match.
whether 引出间接的一般疑问句，语序用陈述语序：his lips match，不是 do his lips match。
