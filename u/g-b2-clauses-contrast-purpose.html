Clauses of contrast and purpose 让步与目的从句

📘 In your coursebook: English File 5e · Upper-Intermediate · 9A “The art of persuasion”. Use the texts and recordings with your coursebook; this page adds new words, grammar and practice.教材定位：English File 5e · Upper-Intermediate · 9A《The art of persuasion》。课文与录音请配合教材学习，本页补充新词、语法与练习。

Link a contrast or a purpose with the right connector, and fit the grammar after it: a clause after although, a noun or -ing after despite, a verb after to, a subject after so that. 用对表示让步和目的的连接词，并让后面的语法跟上：although 后接从句，despite 后接名词或 -ing，to 后接动词，so that 后接带主语的从句。

Although it was cold, we walked to the market. 虽然冷，我们还是走路去了市场。
Clause.接从句。
Despite the cold, we walked to the market. 尽管冷，我们还是走路去了市场。
Noun.接名词。
It was cold, but we walked to the market anyway. 天很冷，不过我们还是走路去了市场。
Spoken and simple.口语、简单。

📏 The rule规则

📏 The rule in one line规则一句话
Although and even though take a full clause; despite and in spite of take a noun or -ing. For purpose, to and in order to take a verb, so that takes a new subject and usually can, could or will, and in case means "as a precaution, because it might happen".although 和 even though 后接完整从句；despite 和 in spite of 后接名词或 -ing。表目的时，to 和 in order to 后接动词；so that 后接新的主语，通常配 can、could 或 will；in case 的意思是「以防万一」。

📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Chinese pairs connectors: suiran ... danshi (although ... but) and yinwei ... suoyi (because ... so). English uses only one of each pair. Although it was raining, but we went out is the single most common linking error. Choose one: Although it was raining, we went out or It was raining, but we went out.

中文的连词常成对出现，英语一对里只用一个。Although it was raining, but we went out 是最常见的连接错误。二选一：Although it was raining, we went out，或者 It was raining, but we went out。

The four contrast words differ in strength and grammar. although is neutral. even though is stronger and stresses surprise: Even though she had a fever, she went to work. though is the spoken form and can come at the end of a sentence: It's expensive. Worth it, though. despite and in spite of need a noun or -ing: despite the rain, despite feeling tired. To use a clause after them, add the fact that.

四个让步词力度和语法不同。although 中性；even though 更强，强调出乎意料；though 是口语形式，可以放在句末；despite 和 in spite of 后面要接名词或 -ing，想接从句得加 the fact that。

whereas and while compare two facts that are both true, without surprise: My husband likes the city, whereas I prefer small towns. This is not the same as although. If you can swap the two halves and keep the meaning, you probably want whereas.

whereas 和 while 对比两件都成立的事，不含意外：My husband likes the city, whereas I prefer small towns。这和 although 不同。如果把前后两半对调意思不变，多半该用 whereas。

For purpose, use to + verb when the subject is the same: I called early to get an appointment. in order to is more formal and clearer at the start of a sentence. For a negative purpose, to alone sounds wrong; use so as not to or in order not to: We whispered so as not to wake the baby. When the subject changes, use so that: I'll leave the key under the mat so that you can get in.

表目的、主语相同时用 to 加动词。in order to 更正式，放句首更清楚。否定目的不能只用 to，要用 so as not to 或 in order not to。主语变了就用 so that。

in case is not if. Take an umbrella in case it rains means take it now, because rain is possible. Take an umbrella if it rains means only take it when it is already raining. After in case, use a present tense for the future, never will: in case the flight is late, not in case it will be late.

in case 不等于 if。Take an umbrella in case it rains 是现在就带上，因为可能下雨；Take an umbrella if it rains 是下雨了才带。in case 后面用现在时表将来，不用 will。

Examples例句

  • Although the apartment was small, it got a lot of light. 公寓虽小，采光却很好。
    Neutral contrast.中性的让步。
  • Even though he had lived here for ten years, he still felt like a guest. 尽管他在这里住了十年，还是觉得自己像个客人。
    Stronger, surprising.更强，带意外。
  • The food was great. The service was slow, though. 菜很好，不过上菜慢。
    Spoken though at the end.口语 though 放句末。
  • Despite the snow, the buses ran on time. 尽管下雪，公交车还是准点。
    despite + noun.despite 加名词。
  • In spite of feeling nervous, she gave a clear presentation. 尽管紧张，她的展示讲得很清楚。
    in spite of + -ing.in spite of 加 -ing。
  • Despite the fact that prices went up, sales stayed strong. 尽管涨价，销售依然强劲。
    Clause after despite the fact that.despite the fact that 后接从句。
  • My son loves winter, whereas my daughter counts the days to summer. 我儿子喜欢冬天，而我女儿天天盼着夏天。
    Two facts compared.对比两个事实。
  • I went to the pharmacy early to avoid the lunch rush. 我早早去了药房，避开午间高峰。
    Purpose, same subject.目的，主语相同。
  • In order to qualify, you must have lived in the province for a set period. 要符合资格，你必须在本省住满规定的时间。
    Formal purpose.正式的目的表达。
  • We took our shoes off at the door so as not to mark the new floor. 我们在门口脱了鞋，以免弄脏新地板。
    Negative purpose.否定的目的。
  • I'll send the address by text so that you can find it easily. 我把地址发短信给你，这样你好找。
    New subject: so that.主语变了：so that。
  • She spoke slowly so that her parents could follow. 她讲得很慢，好让父母听得懂。
    Past: so that ... could.过去：so that ... could。
  • Save the confirmation email in case there's a problem with the booking. 留着确认邮件，以防预订出问题。
    Precaution.以防万一。
  • Keep some cash in the car in case your card doesn't work. 车里放点现金，以防卡刷不了。
    Present tense after in case.in case 后用现在时。

More pairs: same idea, different choice更多对比句组

Bring a jacket in case it gets cold. 带件外套，以防变冷。
Take it now, as a precaution.现在就带上，以防万一。
Bring a jacket if it gets cold. 要是变冷了就带件外套。
Only when it is already cold.冷了才带。
Bring a jacket so that you don't get cold. 带件外套，免得着凉。
The purpose of bringing it.带外套的目的。
I moved closer to work to save time. 我搬到离单位近的地方，为了省时间。
Same subject.主语相同。
I moved closer to work so that my kids could walk to school. 我搬到离单位近的地方，好让孩子能走路上学。
New subject.主语换了。
I moved closer to work so as not to spend two hours in traffic. 我搬到离单位近的地方，免得每天堵两小时。
Negative purpose.否定的目的。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ Although I studied hard, but I failed the test.
✓ Although I studied hard, I failed the test. / I studied hard, but I failed the test.
English uses only one connector for one contrast.
英语一个让步只用一个连接词。
✗ Despite it was raining, we played.
✓ Despite the rain, we played. / Although it was raining, we played.
despite takes a noun or -ing, not a clause.
despite 接名词或 -ing，不接从句。
✗ I'm saving money for buying a house.
✓ I'm saving money to buy a house.
Purpose uses to + verb, not for + -ing.
表目的用 to 加动词，不用 for 加 -ing。
✗ Take some water in case you will get thirsty.
✓ Take some water in case you get thirsty.
No will after in case.
in case 后不用 will。
✗ I left early for not missing the bus.
✓ I left early so as not to miss the bus. / I left early so that I wouldn't miss the bus.
Negative purpose: so as not to or so that ... not.
否定目的用 so as not to 或 so that ... not。
