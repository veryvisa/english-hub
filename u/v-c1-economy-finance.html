📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Open the full explanation: 6 points展开细讲：6 段讲解

Distinguish between the economy and the financial system. The economy includes the production, distribution, and consumption of goods and services, while the financial system concerns money, credit, banking, and investment. Economic growth can improve employment opportunities, but it does not automatically mean that every household becomes wealthier.

注意区分经济体系和金融体系。经济涵盖商品与服务的生产、分配和消费，而金融体系主要涉及货币、信贷、银行业务和投资。经济增长可能改善就业机会，但并不意味着每个家庭都会自动变得更加富裕。

Economic indicators often pair with predictable verbs: inflation rises or falls, interest rates increase or decrease, unemployment rises or falls, and consumer confidence improves or weakens. Remember that inflation refers to a general increase in prices, whereas an increase in the price of one particular product does not necessarily indicate inflation.

经济指标通常搭配固定的动词：通货膨胀上升或下降、利率提高或降低、失业率上升或下降、消费者信心改善或减弱。Inflation指整体物价水平持续上涨，而某一种商品单独涨价并不一定代表通货膨胀。

Use income, expenditure, savings, and disposable income to describe household finances. Income is money received, expenditure is money spent, and savings are the portion of income not spent. Disposable income refers to money available after direct taxes have been deducted, so it is not necessarily the same as total income.

可以使用income、expenditure、savings和disposable income描述家庭财务。Income是收入，expenditure是支出，savings是未花掉的收入。Disposable income指扣除直接税后可支配的收入，因此不能与总收入混为一谈。

Financial vocabulary often distinguishes between an amount owed and the cost of borrowing. Debt is money that must be repaid, while interest is the additional amount paid for borrowing money. A mortgage is a loan secured against property, and a repayment is an amount paid towards reducing a debt.

金融词汇通常区分欠款本金和借款成本。Debt是需要偿还的债务，interest是借钱所支付的利息。Mortgage是以房产作为抵押的贷款，repayment则是用于偿还债务的款项。

Investment expressions frequently use verbs such as make, generate, earn, and incur. Investors make a profit, generate returns, earn interest, and incur losses. A return measures the gain or loss on an investment, whereas a profit is the amount remaining after relevant costs have been deducted.

投资领域经常使用make、generate、earn和incur等动词。投资者可以获得利润、产生投资回报、赚取利息，也可能蒙受损失。Return衡量投资收益或亏损，而profit通常指扣除相关成本后剩余的利润。

Financial risk and financial security are not opposites in every situation. A diversified portfolio spreads investments across different assets to reduce exposure to any single risk. Liquidity describes how easily an asset can be converted into cash without a substantial loss in value.

财务风险和财务安全并不总是简单的对立关系。Diversified portfolio指将资金分散投资于不同资产，以降低对单一风险的暴露。Liquidity指资产在不发生重大价值损失的情况下转换成现金的难易程度。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ The inflation of food prices has made life difficult.
✓ Rising food prices have made life difficult.
Inflation normally refers to a general increase in the price level. When describing the rising prices of particular products, rising prices or price increases is usually more natural.
Inflation通常指整体物价水平上涨。描述某些特定商品涨价时，使用rising prices或price increases通常更加自然。
✗ I have many expenditures every month.
✓ I have a lot of monthly expenses.
Expenditure is often used as an uncountable noun or in formal discussions of total spending. Expense is more natural when referring to individual household bills and costs.
Expenditure通常作为不可数名词使用，或者用于正式讨论总体支出。Expense更适合指家庭中的具体账单和各项开销。
✗ I want to put some money aside for buy a house.
✓ I want to put some money aside to buy a house.
Put money aside takes a purpose phrase: 'for' before a noun or -ing form (for a deposit, for saving), or 'to' before the base verb (to buy a house). Here 'for' is wrongly followed by a bare verb.
Put money aside 后接表示目的的短语：for 后接名词或动名词（for a deposit、for saving），或 to 后接动词原形（to buy a house）。这里 for 后面错误地接了动词原形。
✗ I have paid out my mortgage.
✓ I have paid off my mortgage.
Pay off means to repay a debt completely. Pay out usually means to distribute or provide money, often as compensation, insurance payments, or dividends.
Pay off表示还清债务。Pay out通常表示支付或发放款项，常用于赔偿金、保险赔付款或股息等场景。
✗ The bank gave me a cheap interest rate.
✓ The bank offered me a low interest rate.
Interest rates are normally described as high or low, not cheap or expensive. Cheap is more appropriate for the cost of a product or service.
利率通常用high或low描述，而不是cheap或expensive。Cheap更适合形容商品或服务价格便宜。
✗ I have to return the debt next month.
✓ I have to repay the debt next month.
Repay is the natural verb for returning borrowed money. Return usually refers to giving something back, while repay specifically refers to paying back money that is owed.
Repay是偿还借款和债务的自然表达。Return通常表示归还某件物品，而repay专门用于偿还所欠的款项。
