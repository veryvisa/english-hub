The Culture Shock Curve Is Not a Curve 文化冲击曲线并不是一条曲线

🧠 Learn the 4 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 4 个新词

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Most guides to moving abroad show the same picture: a curve. First comes the honeymoon, when everything is new and exciting. Then comes the dip, when the language tires you and the food is never quite right. Then a slow climb, and finally a line that stays flat and happy. It is a neat picture. For many people it is not what happens.

大多数出国指南都画着同一张图：一条曲线。先是蜜月期，一切新鲜又兴奋。然后是低谷，语言让你疲惫，吃的东西总差那么一点。然后慢慢爬升，最后是一条平稳、愉快的直线。图很整齐，可对许多人来说，事情并不是这样发生的。

People who work with newcomers often describe something messier: the line moves up and down for years. A bad week at work, a call from a parent who is ill, a week of isolation in a dark winter: each can send someone back into the dip, even in the fifth year. This is not failure. It is the normal shape of settling in.

和新移民打交道的人常常描述一种更乱的情形：这条线会上上下下好几年。工作上糟糕的一周、生病的父母打来的一通电话、漫长冬夜里孤零零的一周，都可能把人重新送回低谷，哪怕到了第五年。这不是失败，这就是安顿下来的正常样子。

What does change is how fast people come back up. The first dip may last months; later ones may last days. Two things seem to help most. One is awareness: knowing that the low mood has a name and that it passes. The other is flexibility: being ready to adapt, keeping some old habits and dropping others, rather than choosing between "my culture" and "theirs".

真正会变的，是人回升的速度。第一次低谷可能持续几个月，后来的也许只有几天。有两样东西似乎最有帮助。一是意识：知道这种低落有名字，也知道它会过去。二是灵活：愿意调整，保留一些旧习惯、放下另一些，而不是在「我的文化」和「他们的文化」之间二选一。

Expectations matter too. People who arrive expecting a smooth curve often feel they are doing something wrong when the dip returns. People who expect a zigzag are less surprised, and they recover with more confidence.

预期也很重要。带着「平滑曲线」的预期来的人，低谷一回来，常会觉得自己哪里做错了。预期是锯齿线的人没那么意外，恢复时也更有信心。

So if you feel homesick in your third year, it does not mean you made the wrong choice. It means you are human, living in two places at once, and still finding your way home in both.

所以，如果你第三年还会想家，这不说明你选错了。这说明你是个普通人，同时活在两个地方，还在两边都慢慢找回家的路。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

A bad week at work, a call from a parent who is ill, a week of isolation in a dark winter: each can send someone back into the dip, even in the fifth year.
The list comes first and the colon leads to the point; each gathers the list into one subject. the fifth year: ordinals take the.
先列举，冒号后才是要点；each 把前面的列举收成一个主语。序数词前用 the：the fifth year。
The other is flexibility: being ready to adapt, keeping some old habits and dropping others, rather than choosing between "my culture" and "theirs".
flexibility = flexible + -ity; rather than + -ing matches the other -ing forms.
flexibility＝flexible 加 -ity；rather than 后接 -ing，与前面几个 -ing 平行。
