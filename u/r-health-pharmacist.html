Ask the Pharmacist First 先问药剂师

🧠 Learn the 5 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 5 个新词

Listen in on a conversation at a pharmacy counter and pick up the phrasal verbs and indirect questions that make asking for help easy. 旁听一次药房柜台前的对话，学会让求助变轻松的短语动词和间接疑问句。

B2 · CELPIP 7–8⏱ 20 minV04 Phrasal verbs avoided; the formal verb sounds stiff回避短语动词，说话太书面G13 Word order in indirect questions间接疑问句语序

📖 Key card知识卡
For small problems, the pharmacist is often the fastest first stop; tell them everything you are already taking.小毛病先找药剂师往往最快；把你正在吃的东西都告诉他们。

Read and listen读与听

295 words
Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Lin had a cough that would not go away, and the earliest appointment with a doctor was nine days off. A neighbour pointed out that pharmacists here can answer more questions than most people think. So on her way back from work, Lin stopped at the pharmacy counter.

阿琳咳嗽一直不好，最早的医生预约要等九天。邻居提醒她，这里的药剂师能回答的问题比大多数人想的多。于是下班回家的路上，阿琳在药房柜台前停了下来。

"Hi. I wonder if you could help me. I've had a cough for about two weeks, and I can't figure out whether I need to see a doctor." "Sure. Is it a dry cough, or are you bringing anything up?" "Dry, mostly. It's worse at night." "Any fever? Any trouble breathing?" "No fever. I had some aches at the start, but they've gone."

「您好，不知道您能不能帮我一下。我咳嗽两周左右了，搞不清要不要去看医生。」「当然。是干咳，还是有痰？」「基本是干咳，晚上更厉害。」「发烧吗？呼吸困难吗？」「不发烧。刚开始身上有点酸痛，现在好了。」

"Are you taking anything for it at the moment?" "Just some herbal tea I brought from home, and a cough syrup a friend gave me." "Could I see the syrup? Okay, this one can make you sleepy, so don't take it before you drive. Let me check it against your other medicine." "Thanks. I didn't know whether it mattered."

「现在在吃什么药吗？」「只喝了点从国内带来的草本茶，还有朋友给的一瓶止咳糖浆。」「能让我看看糖浆吗？好，这种会让人犯困，开车前别喝。我对照一下你吃的其他药。」「谢谢。我不知道这也有关系。」

"For a dry cough like yours, this usually helps at night. But if it isn't better in a week, or if you run into any trouble breathing, see a doctor straight away. Don't wait for your appointment. A walk-in clinic can deal with that, and so can an emergency room if it's serious." "And should I tell the doctor about the syrup?" "Yes, bring the bottle. It saves time."

「像你这种干咳，这个药晚上一般管用。不过一周还不好，或者出现任何呼吸困难，马上去看医生，别等预约。免预约诊所能处理，严重的话就去急诊。」「那我要不要告诉医生糖浆的事？」「要，把瓶子带上，省时间。」

Lin walked out with the right medicine and a clearer idea of when to worry. Later she noticed something about her own English: she had said "I can't figure out whether I need a doctor", not "Do I need a doctor?" Asking indirectly felt more polite, and the pharmacist had sorted it out in five minutes.

阿琳带着对症的药和「什么时候该担心」的清楚概念走了出来。后来她注意到自己英语里的一点：她说的是「我搞不清要不要看医生」，而不是「我要看医生吗？」间接地问让人觉得更礼貌，药剂师五分钟就帮她把事情理清了。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

I've had a cough for about two weeks, and I can't figure out whether I need to see a doctor.
whether I need keeps statement word order inside a question. figure out = find the answer by thinking.
whether I need 在疑问里保持陈述语序。figure out＝想明白。
But if it isn't better in a week, or if you run into any trouble breathing, see a doctor straight away.
run into trouble = meet a problem you did not expect; the imperative see gives clear advice.
run into trouble＝碰上意外的问题；祈使句 see 给出明确建议。
