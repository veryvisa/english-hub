Read and listen读与听

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

A team of researchers studying polar ice announced on Tuesday that the ice sheet is thinning faster than earlier models suggested, although they were careful to describe their findings as provisional. At a briefing for journalists, the lead scientist said the measurements came from a limited number of sites and would need to be confirmed by further work.

一个研究极地冰层的团队周二宣布，冰盖变薄的速度比早先的模型所显示的更快，不过他们谨慎地强调，这些发现只是暂定的。在面向记者的简报会上，首席科学家说，测量数据来自数量有限的观测点，还需要更多研究加以证实。

The headline figure came with a wide margin of error, and the team spent much of the briefing explaining what that means. A number such as this one is not a single point, they said, but a range within which the true value is likely to lie. On the whole, the range was narrower than it had been a decade ago, which the scientists described as a sign of progress rather than a final answer.

这个核心数字附带着很大的误差范围，团队在简报会上花了不少时间解释其含义。他们说，这样的数字并不是一个单点，而是一个区间，真实值很可能落在其中。总体而言，这个区间比十年前更窄，科学家称这是进步的标志，而不是最终答案。

Not everyone was reassured. One independent expert, who had not been involved in the work, warned that dramatic headlines tend to travel faster than careful caveats. While she welcomed the new data, she argued that the public should not be asked to treat every revision as a crisis or, equally, as proof that scientists cannot be trusted.

并非所有人都感到放心。一位未参与此项研究的独立专家警告说，耸动的标题往往比谨慎的附加说明传播得更快。她欢迎这些新数据，但同时认为，不应要求公众把每一次修订都当作危机，同样也不应把它当作科学家不可信的证据。

The research team plans to repeat its measurements next season with additional instruments. If the results hold up, the estimate will be published in a peer-reviewed journal. If they do not, the scientists said, they will say so openly, because correcting the record is part of how the process is meant to work.

研究团队计划在下个季节用更多仪器重复测量。如果结果经得起检验，这一估计将发表在同行评审期刊上；如果经不起，科学家们说，他们会公开说明，因为纠正记录本来就是这一过程运作的一部分。

For now, the findings are being treated as a warning rather than a verdict. Local planners have been asked to consider several possible outcomes instead of relying on one prediction, a request the team says reflects good practice in any field where the evidence is still developing.

目前，这些发现被视为一种警示，而不是定论。当地规划者被要求考虑几种可能的结果，而不是依赖单一预测；团队表示，在证据仍在发展的任何领域，这都是良好的做法。

Words词

/prəˈvɪʒənəl/ adj.
暂定的，临时的
a provisional result; provisional approval
The committee gave provisional approval to the plan while it waited for more data. 委员会在等待更多数据期间，对该计划给予了暂时批准。
/ˈmɑːdʒɪn əv ˈerə/ n.
误差范围
a small margin of error; within the margin of error
The two candidates were so close that the gap fell within the margin of error. 两位候选人非常接近，差距落在误差范围之内。
/tend tuː/ phr.
往往，倾向于
tend to overreact; tend to assume
People tend to remember dramatic stories better than careful explanations. 人们往往更容易记住戏剧性的故事，而不是严谨的解释。
/həʊld ʌp/ phr. v.
经得起检验，站得住脚
hold up under scrutiny; hold up well
Her argument did not hold up once the original figures were checked. 一旦核对了原始数据，她的论点就站不住脚了。
/ˈkæviæts/ n.
附加说明，限制条件
with a few caveats; attach caveats to
The report is useful, but it comes with several important caveats. 这份报告很有用，但附有几项重要的限制条件。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

A number such as this one is not a single point, they said, but a range within which the true value is likely to lie.
The reporting clause ‘they said’ is inserted mid-sentence, and ‘within which’ is a formal preposition-plus-relative-pronoun that links the range to where the true value may lie.
插入语 ‘they said’ 打断主句；'within which' 是“介词+关系代词”的正式结构，说明真实值可能落在哪个区间内。
While she welcomed the new data, she argued that the public should not be asked to treat every revision as a crisis or, equally, as proof that scientists cannot be trusted.
The opening ‘While’ clause makes a concession, and the main clause then uses a passive ‘should not be asked to’ with two parallel complements joined by ‘or’.
开头的 While 从句表示让步；主句用被动结构 should not be asked to，并用 or 连接两个并列成分。
