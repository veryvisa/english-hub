Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

A city office that answers residents' questions about building permits has spent the past three months testing an AI writing assistant. Staff use it to draft replies to routine emails, which they then check and edit before anything is sent.

一家负责解答居民建筑许可问题的市政办公室，过去三个月一直在试用一款 AI 写作助手。员工用它起草日常邮件的回复，再核对、修改，然后才发出。

The office started the trial in August, after the number of emails it received had almost doubled in two years. "At one point we had a backlog of several weeks," said the manager in charge of the project. "Since we began using the tool, the average waiting time has fallen to a few days." She stressed that every reply is still read and signed by a person.

办公室八月开始试用，此前两年里收到的邮件数量几乎翻了一番。「有一阵子积压了好几周，」负责这个项目的经理说，「用上这个工具以后，平均等待时间降到了几天。」她强调，每一封回复仍由人来读、来签。

Staff reactions have been mixed. Some say the drafts take away the most boring part of the job. Others have found mistakes that worried them. In one case the tool wrote that an application could be submitted online when, in fact, it could only be submitted in person. An employee caught the error before the email went out. "It sounded completely confident," she said. "That is what makes it dangerous."

员工的反应好坏参半。有人说草稿替他们拿走了工作里最枯燥的部分；也有人发现了让他们担心的错误。有一次，工具写道某项申请可以网上提交，而实际上只能当面提交。一位员工在邮件发出前发现了这个错。「它听起来非常笃定，」她说，「这正是危险的地方。」

The office has written a short set of rules. Staff must not paste residents' personal details into the tool, must verify every date and requirement against the official page, and must rewrite anything that does not sound human. Residents are not told when a reply was drafted with AI help, a decision that some staff have questioned.

办公室定了几条简短的规矩：员工不得把居民的个人信息粘贴进工具；每个日期和要求都要对照官方页面核实；读起来不像人话的地方必须重写。居民不会被告知哪封回复是借助 AI 起草的，一些员工对这个决定提出了疑问。

The office will report on the trial in the new year. So far, no resident has complained about a reply. Whether that is because the replies are good or because nobody has noticed is a question the office cannot yet answer for certain.

办公室将在新年公布试用结果。到目前为止，还没有居民投诉过哪封回复。这是因为回复写得好，还是因为没人察觉，办公室眼下还说不准。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

The office started the trial in August, after the number of emails it received had almost doubled in two years.
Past simple for the start date; past perfect had doubled for the change that came before it.
开始的时间用一般过去时；在那之前已发生的增长用过去完成时 had doubled。
Whether that is because the replies are good or because nobody has noticed is a question the office cannot yet answer for certain.
The whole Whether… or… clause is the subject; the main verb is is a question.
整个 Whether… or… 从句作主语，谓语是 is a question。
