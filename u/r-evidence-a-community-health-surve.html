What Our Community Survey Revealed 社区调查告诉了我们什么

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

When I first volunteered for a neighbourhood health project, I assumed that collecting information would be relatively straightforward. We planned to ask residents about access to local healthcare, common health concerns and the difficulties they experienced when seeking advice. I expected people to provide similar answers. Instead, our first conversations revealed how differently individuals understood health, risk and the availability of support.

当我第一次参加社区健康项目的志愿工作时，我以为收集信息会相对简单。我们计划询问居民如何获得当地医疗服务、常见的健康问题，以及他们寻求建议时遇到的困难。我原以为大家会给出相似的回答。然而，最初的交流却让我们发现，不同的人对健康、风险和支持服务的可获得性有着截然不同的理解。

Some residents described long waiting experiences, while others said that they had difficulty understanding medical information. A few people avoided answering questions because they worried that their responses might affect their privacy. Although our questionnaire had been carefully prepared, it did not capture every concern. We therefore changed our approach and allowed participants to explain their experiences in their own words.

一些居民描述了漫长的等待经历，另一些人则表示难以理解医疗信息。还有少数人因为担心回答会影响个人隐私而不愿作答。尽管我们的问卷经过了精心准备，但它并没有涵盖所有人的顾虑。因此，我们调整了调查方式，让参与者用自己的语言解释个人经历。

The responses suggested that access to healthcare depended on more than the physical presence of a clinic. Transport, language, working hours and confidence in healthcare providers also mattered. However, our participants were not a perfect representation of the whole neighbourhood. People with particularly strong opinions may have been more willing to participate. Our findings were therefore useful indicators, not definitive measurements of community health.

回答表明，获得医疗服务不仅取决于附近是否有诊所。交通、语言、工作时间以及对医疗服务提供者的信任程度同样重要。然而，我们的参与者并不能完美代表整个社区。意见特别强烈的人可能更愿意参与调查。因此，我们的发现是有用的参考信号，而不是社区健康状况的最终测量结果。

At the end of the project, we presented the findings to local service providers. We explained what residents had told us, but also described the limitations of our survey. On the whole, community feedback tends to be more valuable when it is interpreted alongside other evidence. I learned that listening is not simply a friendly gesture; it is part of building a reliable picture of public health needs.

项目结束时，我们向当地服务提供者介绍了调查结果。我们解释了居民告诉我们的情况，同时也说明了调查的局限。总体而言，当社区反馈与其他证据结合解读时，它往往更有价值。我逐渐认识到，倾听不仅仅是一种友善的行为，也是建立可靠公共卫生需求图景的一部分。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

Although our questionnaire had been carefully prepared, it did not capture every concern.
Although introduces a concessive clause, and the past perfect passive had been prepared indicates an action completed before the past reference point.
Although 引导让步状语从句，过去完成时被动结构 had been prepared 表示在过去参照时间之前已经完成的动作。
People with particularly strong opinions may have been more willing to participate.
May have been expresses a tentative deduction about a possible past situation, rather than a confirmed fact.
May have been 表示对过去可能情况的推测，而不是已经证实的事实。
