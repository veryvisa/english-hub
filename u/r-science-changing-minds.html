Q: Last year I read that eggs were bad for the heart. This year a headline says an egg a day is fine. My husband now says scientists cannot make up their minds, so he ignores all of it. How do I know which findings to take seriously? — Confused in Calgary

问：去年我读到鸡蛋对心脏不好。今年一条新闻标题说每天一个鸡蛋没问题。我丈夫现在说科学家拿不定主意，所以他全都不理。我怎么知道哪些研究结论值得当回事？——卡尔加里的困惑读者

A: Your husband has noticed something real, but he has drawn the wrong conclusion. Changing its mind is not a weakness of science; it is the whole point. Researchers do research on a question and publish their findings, and then other teams try to replicate them. When a result holds up again and again, the field makes progress. When it does not, the field corrects itself.

答：你丈夫注意到的是真问题，但他下错了结论。改变看法不是科学的弱点，恰恰是它的意义所在。研究者就一个问题做研究、发表结论，然后别的团队尝试重复。一个结果一次又一次站得住，这个领域就前进了；站不住，这个领域就自我纠正。

The trouble is that headlines rarely tell you where a study sits in this process. A single small study is an early hypothesis, not a verdict. Before you make a decision based on it, take a few things into account. How many people were studied? Was it an observational study, which can only show a link, or a trial, which can test cause and effect? Have other teams found the same thing?

麻烦在于，新闻标题很少告诉你一项研究处在这个过程的哪一步。一项小研究只是早期假说，不是定论。在据此做决定之前，先考虑几件事：研究了多少人？是只能显示关联的观察性研究，还是能检验因果的试验？别的团队有没有得出同样的结果？

Food research is especially hard, because people eat thousands of different things and rarely remember them accurately. That is why advice on eggs, coffee or wine changes so often, while the consensus on smoking has barely moved in decades. The stronger the evidence, the less one new study will have an impact on it.

饮食研究尤其难，因为人们吃的东西成千上万，也很少记得准。这就是为什么关于鸡蛋、咖啡或葡萄酒的建议变得那么频繁，而关于吸烟的共识几十年来几乎没动过。证据越强，一项新研究对它的影响就越小。

So make an effort to look past the headline. Ask whether a finding has been backed up by other teams, and treat surprising single studies with friendly scepticism. Ignoring all of it is easy, but it means throwing away the results that have survived the hardest tests.

所以，下点功夫看透标题。问一问某个结论有没有得到别的团队的支持，对出人意料的单项研究保持友好的怀疑。全都不理很容易，但那意味着把那些经受住最严格检验的结果也一起扔掉了。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

Researchers do research on a question and publish their findings, and then other teams try to replicate them.
do research on, never make research; research is uncountable, so no a and no -s.
说 do research on，不说 make research；research 不可数，不加 a 也不加 -s。
The stronger the evidence, the less one new study will have an impact on it.
The + comparative …, the + comparative …: as one thing grows, the other changes. have an impact on takes on, not to.
The 比较级…，the 比较级…：一方增加，另一方随之变化。have an impact 后接 on，不接 to。
