Is My English Good Enough to Volunteer? 我的英语够去做义工吗

🧠 Learn the 4 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 4 个新词

Read an advice column for a retired newcomer and pick up the phrasal verbs that make spoken requests sound natural. 读一篇写给退休新移民的建议专栏，学会让口头请求听起来自然的短语动词。

B2 · CELPIP 7–8⏱ 20 minV04 Phrasal verbs avoided; the formal verb sounds stiff回避短语动词，说话太书面V06 Register: too formal, too casual, or too blunt语域不合：太正式、太随意或太冲

📖 Key card知识卡
Volunteering does not wait for perfect English; it is one of the fastest ways to get it.做义工不用等英语完美，它本身就是把英语练好最快的路之一。

Read and listen读与听

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

"I moved to Canada two years ago to help my daughter with her children. They are at school all day now, and I have time on my hands. I would like to volunteer, maybe at the library or the community centre, but I am worried that my English is not good enough. Is it rude to sign up if I cannot always understand people?" — Lin

「我两年前来加拿大帮女儿带孩子。现在孩子们整天在学校，我手头空了。我想去做义工，图书馆或者社区中心都行，可又担心自己的英语不够好。要是我不能总听懂别人的话，去报名会不会失礼？」——琳

Dear Lin, it is not rude at all. Volunteer coordinators deal with this question every week, and many of them were newcomers themselves. What they need most is someone who turns up on time and keeps their promises. Your English will improve faster with them than without them.

亲爱的琳：一点也不失礼。义工协调人每周都要处理这样的问题，他们当中很多人自己当年也是新来的。他们最需要的，是准时到场、说话算数的人。跟他们在一起，你的英语会比自己闷着练进步得快。

Start small. Ask the coordinator to set up a short chat before you sign up, and bring up your English honestly: "I understand most things, but I may ask you to repeat." That one sentence takes the pressure off both of you. Then take on a task with a clear routine, such as sorting books or putting out chairs for events. You will hear the same phrases again and again, which is exactly how a language sticks.

从小处开始。报名前请协调人安排一次简短的聊天，坦白提一下你的英语：「大部分我都听得懂，但可能会请您再说一遍。」这一句话就能让你们俩都松口气。然后接一个有固定流程的活儿，比如整理图书或者活动前摆椅子。同样的话你会一遍遍听到，语言就是这样记住的。

Once you have settled in, look for a role where your other skills count. Many centres run programmes for older newcomers and are often short of people who speak a second language. If you can help a family figure out a form or a bus route in Mandarin or Cantonese, you are not a volunteer with weak English; you are exactly the person they were looking for.

等你适应下来，就去找一个能用上你其他本事的岗位。很多社区中心都给年长的新移民办活动，常常缺会说第二种语言的人。如果你能用普通话或粤语帮一家人弄明白一张表格或一条公交线路，那你就不是英语差的义工，而正是他们一直在找的人。

One last thing. The people you meet there will be your neighbours, too. Some will stay acquaintances, a few may become friends, and all of them will look out for you in small ways. I know of no quicker way to get to know a neighbourhood from the inside.

最后一句。你在那里遇到的人，也会是你的邻居。有的会一直是点头之交，有几个也许会成为朋友，而他们都会在小事上照应你。我想不出比这更快从里面了解一个街区的办法。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

Ask the coordinator to set up a short chat before you sign up, and bring up your English honestly
Three phrasal verbs in one line: set up (arrange), sign up (register), bring up (mention a subject). In speech they sound friendlier than arrange, register and mention.
一句里三个短语动词：set up 安排，sign up 报名，bring up 提起。口语里它们比 arrange、register、mention 更亲切。
If you can help a family figure out a form or a bus route in Mandarin or Cantonese, you are not a volunteer with weak English; you are exactly the person they were looking for.
A real condition (can … are), not an unreal one. figure out: understand after some effort. The semicolon sets two descriptions side by side.
这是真实条件句（can … are），不是虚拟。figure out：费点劲弄明白。分号把两种说法并排放。
