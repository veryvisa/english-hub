Prepositions after verbs, and no preposition at all 动词后的介词与不用介词

Learn the preposition together with the verb as one chunk (depend on, apply for, complain about), and stop adding prepositions to verbs that take a direct object (discuss, contact, enter, attend). 把动词和它的介词当作一个词块来记（depend on、apply for、complain about），并且不再给直接接宾语的动词加介词（discuss、contact、enter、attend）。

📏 The rule规则

📏 The rule in one line规则一句话
Many verbs need a fixed preposition before their object, and the preposition is part of the meaning: apply for a job but apply to a company. A smaller group of very common verbs takes the object directly, with no preposition at all: discuss the plan, contact the office, enter the room.很多动词接宾语前要一个固定的介词，介词本身就是意思的一部分：apply for a job，但 apply to a company。另有一小组非常常用的动词直接接宾语，不要任何介词：discuss the plan、contact the office、enter the room。

The errors here come in two directions. First, Chinese speakers drop the preposition because the Chinese verb does not need one: I am waiting the bus, It depends the weather. Second, they add one where the Chinese has a helper word, for example from gen ... taolun (discuss with): We discussed about the price, Please contact with the office. Both errors are easy to hear and easy to fix, but only if you learn verbs as chunks.

这里的错误有两个方向。一是丢介词，因为对应的中文动词不需要：I am waiting the bus、It depends the weather。二是多加介词，因为中文里有「跟……讨论」「和……联系」这样的辅助词：We discussed about the price、Please contact with the office。两种错都很容易被听出来，也很好改，前提是把动词当成词块来记。

The preposition often changes the meaning. apply for the thing you want (a job, a permit), apply to the place that decides (a school, a company). agree with a person or an opinion, agree on a decision you reach together, agree to a proposal or a request. think about is to consider; think of is to have an idea or an opinion: What do you think of the new manager?

介词常常改变意思。apply for 申请的东西，apply to 做决定的机构。agree with 人或观点，agree on 共同做出的决定，agree to 一个提议或请求。think about 是考虑，think of 是想到、有看法。

The no-preposition verbs are worth memorising as a short list, because they are frequent in emails and meetings: discuss, contact, enter, attend, reach, marry, approach, answer, emphasise, lack, resemble, call (someone), email (someone), join (a group). The noun forms sometimes do take a preposition: a discussion about, in contact with, an emphasis on, a lack of.

不带介词的动词值得作为一张短清单背下来，因为在邮件和会议里很常见。它们的名词形式有时却要介词：a discussion about、in contact with、an emphasis on、a lack of。

Some verbs take a person and a thing with different prepositions: apologise to someone for something, complain to someone about something, thank someone for something, blame someone for something, remind someone about / of something. In a complaint email, getting these right makes you sound in control: I am writing to complain about the delay.

有些动词同时带人和事，介词不同：apologise to sb for sth、complain to sb about sth、thank sb for sth、blame sb for sth、remind sb about/of sth。投诉邮件里把这些用对，会显得你心里有数。

When the object is a verb, use -ing after the preposition: I'm thinking about changing jobs, She insisted on paying, We succeeded in finding a place. Learners often write insist to pay or succeed to find because they expect to after a verb. If the chunk has a preposition, the next verb is -ing.

宾语是动作时，介词后用 -ing。学习者常写 insist to pay、succeed to find，因为以为动词后都接 to。只要词块里有介词，后面的动词就是 -ing。

✓ We need to discuss the budget. / We need to talk about the budget.

discuss 直接接宾语。
✗ Please contact with the front desk.
✓ Please contact the front desk. / Please get in touch with the front desk.
contact has no preposition; get in touch with does.
contact 不加介词；get in touch with 才加。
✗ It depends the price.
✓ It depends on the price.
depend always needs on.
depend 必须加 on。
✗ I'm waiting the doctor.
✓ I'm waiting for the doctor.
wait for someone.
wait for 某人。
✗ He insisted to pay.
✓ He insisted on paying.
