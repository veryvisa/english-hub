Emphasis with stress and word order together 重音与语序一起做强调

📘 In your coursebook: English File 5e · Advanced · 10B “Make yourself at home”. Use the texts and recordings with your coursebook; this page adds new words, grammar and practice.教材定位：English File 5e · Advanced · 10B《Make yourself at home》。课文与录音请配合教材学习，本页补充新词、语法与练习。

Combine word order and stress to put focus exactly where you want it: cleft sentences (What I need is…, It was… that…), fronting (This I can accept.), and the intonation each one needs to sound natural rather than written. 把语序和重音结合起来，把焦点放在想放的地方：分裂句（What I need is…、It was… that…）、前置（This I can accept.），以及让它们听起来自然而不像书面语所需的语调。

📏 The rule规则

📏 The point in one line一句话要领
Word order chooses which part of the sentence becomes the focus, and stress makes the listener hear it: in a cleft or fronted sentence the focus word gets the main stress, and everything after it is said lower and quicker.语序决定句子哪一部分成为焦点，重音让听者听到它：在分裂句或前置句里，焦点词承担主重音，焦点之后的部分说得更低、更快。
  • Easy to miss易漏 ✗ This book I think it is useful. → ✓ I think this book is useful. / This book I found useful; that one I didn't.

🗣 Say it跟读

Tap 🔊, listen, then say it aloud. Use ● to record yourself and compare.点 🔊 听，再开口跟读；点 ● 录下自己的声音对比（录音只在本机）。

It was the MANager who called, not the client. /ɪt wəz ðə ˈmænɪdʒə hu kɔːld ˈnɒt ðə ˈklaɪənt/
Stress manager; who called is low and quick. 重读 manager，who called 低而快。
It's the PRICE that worries me. /ɪts ðə ˈpraɪs ðət ˈwʌriz mi/
Not worRIES: the worry is already known. 不重读 worries，担心是已知信息。
What we need is more TIME. /wɒt wi niːd ɪz mɔː ˈtaɪm/
Level first half, tiny pause, fall on time. 前半句平，稍停，time 降调。
What I don't understand is WHY. /wɒt aɪ dəʊnt ˌʌndəˈstænd ɪz ˈwaɪ/
One-word payoff with a full fall. 一个词的答案，完整降调。
What happened was / the system CRASHED. /wɒt ˈhæpənd wəz / ðə ˈsɪstəm ˈkræʃt/
A pseudo-cleft that introduces a story. 用来引出事情经过的分裂句。
All I said was that it's LATE. /ˈɔːl aɪ sed wəz ðət ɪts ˈleɪt/
All gets a secondary stress; late the nucleus. all 次重音，late 主重音。
The reason I'm calling is the INVOICE. /ðə ˈriːzən aɪm ˈkɔːlɪŋ ɪz ði ˈɪnvɔɪs/
A useful opener for phone calls. 打电话时好用的开头。
This I can ACCEPT; that I can't. /ˈðɪs aɪ kən əkˈsept / ˈðæt aɪ ˈkɑːnt/
Fronted contrast: stress this and that, no extra it. 前置对比：重读 this 和 that，不加 it。
That point I'll come back to LATER. /ˈðæt ˈpɔɪnt aɪl kʌm ˈbæk tə ˈleɪtə/
Fronting links to what was just said. 前置承接刚说的内容。
I DID send it. Check your spam folder. /aɪ ˈdɪd ˈsend ɪt/
Emphatic did with a strong fall. 强调的 did，强降调。
Not ONCE did they call us back. /ˈnɒt ˈwʌns dɪd ðeɪ ˈkɔːl əs ˈbæk/
Formal inversion; stress once. 正式倒装，重读 once。
Only THEN did we see the problem. /ˈəʊnli ˈðen dɪd wi ˈsiː ðə ˈprɒbləm/
Stress then; the rest falls away. 重读 then，其余渐弱。
It wasn't until MONDAY that they replied. /ɪt ˈwɒznt ənˌtɪl ˈmʌndeɪ ðət ðeɪ rɪˈplaɪd/
Monday is the news, not replied. 新信息是 Monday，不是 replied。
What I'd suggest is that we WAIT a week. /wɒt aɪd səˈdʒest ɪz ðət wi ˈweɪt ə ˈwiːk/
A softer way to make a recommendation. 提建议时较委婉的说法。

📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Open the full explanation: 5 points展开细讲：5 段讲解

Mandarin moves a topic to the front freely, as in a word-for-word this book, I feel very useful. Carried into English this gives This book I think it is useful, which has a double subject and is the error behind G05. English does allow fronting, but under conditions: the fronted part is usually contrastive or links to what was just said, there is no extra pronoun, and it carries a strong stress followed by a pause or a fall. This book I found useful; the other one I didn't.

普通话很容易把话题提前：这本书我觉得很有用。照搬到英语就成了 This book I think it is useful，出现双主语，这就是 G05 背后的错误。英语也允许前置，但有条件：前置部分通常是对比的或承接上文的，不再加代词，并带强重音、后面停顿或降调。

Cleft sentences are the safer, more frequent tool. It-clefts pick out one item and exclude others: It was the manager who called, not the client. The focus (the MANager) carries the nucleus; the who/that clause after it is said low and fast, because it is information the listener already has. Wh-clefts (pseudo-clefts) build suspense and then deliver: What we need is more TIME. The first part is said on a level or rising tone, often with a tiny pause after is, and the payoff gets the fall.

分裂句更稳妥、也更常用。It 分裂句挑出一项、排除其他；焦点承担主重音，后面的 who/that 从句说得低而快，因为那是听者已知的信息。Wh 分裂句先制造悬念再给答案：前半句平调或微升，is 后常稍停，答案部分降调。

The most common delivery error is stressing the wrong part. Learners often put the strongest stress on the clause that follows the focus: It was the manager who CALLED. That makes the sentence sound as if called is new, which contradicts the structure you chose. Rule of thumb: in a cleft, the stress belongs to the word right after It was or right after is.

最常见的表达错误是重音放错地方：学习者常把最强重音放在焦点后面的从句上，这让 called 听起来像新信息，与所选结构自相矛盾。经验法则：分裂句里，重音属于 It was 后面或 is 后面紧接的那个词。

In speech, stress alone can often do the job of a cleft: the MANager called already means "the manager, not someone else". Clefts add weight and formality, and they help when the stressed word is not in a natural position for stress, or when a sentence is long. Use stress alone in casual talk; use clefts when you need the listener to notice, as in meetings, complaints, and corrections.

口语里光靠重音常常就能起到分裂句的作用。分裂句增加分量和正式感，在重读词不处于自然重读位置、或句子很长时尤其有用。随意交谈用重音就够了；开会、投诉、纠正时要让对方注意到，就用分裂句。

Other emphatic patterns follow the same principle. Auxiliary do for contrast: I DID send it; check your spam folder. Negative inversion in formal speech: Not once did they CALL us. Fronted adverbials: Only THEN did we realise. In each one, the structure points at a word and the voice must land on it; without the stress, these sound like grammar exercises read aloud.

其他强调句式同理：用助动词 do 表示对比、正式场合的否定倒装、前置状语。每种都是结构指向某个词，声音必须落在那个词上；不重读，听起来就像在朗读语法练习。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ This book I think it is useful.
✓ I think this book is useful. / This book I found useful; that one I didn't.
English fronting has no extra pronoun and is used for contrast, not as a default topic.
英语前置不加多余代词，用于对比，不是默认的话题结构。
✗ It was the manager who CALLED.
✓ It was the MANager who called.
The cleft points at manager; stressing called contradicts it.
分裂句指向 manager，重读 called 就自相矛盾。
✗ What we need IS more time (stress on is, then a flat end)
✓ What we need is / more TIME.
The payoff after is carries the fall.
is 后面的答案承担降调。
✗ Not once they called us.
✓ Not once did they call us.
A fronted negative needs inversion.
否定词前置要倒装。

✍️ Use it用起来

Think of a recent misunderstanding at work or with a service (a wrong date, a missing payment, the wrong person contacted). Record a 30-second correction using at least one it-cleft, one wh-cleft and one emphatic do. Then play it back and check that each focus word, and only that word, carries the strongest stress.
回想最近工作或办事中的一次误会（日期错了、款项没到、联系错了人）。录一段 30 秒的纠正，至少用一个 It 分裂句、一个 Wh 分裂句和一个强调的 do。回放，检查每个焦点词、且只有那个词承担最强重音。
