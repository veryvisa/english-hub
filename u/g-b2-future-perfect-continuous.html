Future continuous and future perfect 将来进行与将来完成

📘 In your coursebook: English File 5e · Upper-Intermediate · 4A “Riding the storm”. Use the texts and recordings with your coursebook; this page adds new words, grammar and practice.教材定位：English File 5e · Upper-Intermediate · 4A《Riding the storm》。课文与录音请配合教材学习，本页补充新词、语法与练习。

Look at the future from a point in time: say what will be in progress then, and what will already be finished by then. 站在将来某个时间点上说话：那时什么事正在进行，到那时什么事已经完成。

At 10 a.m. I'll write the report. 我上午十点写报告。
I start at ten.十点开始写。
At 10 a.m. I'll be writing the report. 上午十点我正在写报告。
I'm in the middle of it at ten.十点时正写着。
By 10 a.m. I'll have written the report. 上午十点前我就把报告写完了。
It is done before ten.十点之前写完。
By 10 a.m. I'll have been writing the report for three hours. 到上午十点，我写报告就写了三个小时了。
How long I'll have been at it.到那时写了多久。

📏 The rule规则

📏 The rule in one line规则一句话
Use will be doing for an activity in progress at a future time, or for something that will happen as a matter of course; use will have done for something complete before a future deadline, and will have been doing for the length of an activity up to that point.will be doing 表示将来某时正在进行的活动，或顺理成章会发生的事；will have done 表示在将来某个期限之前已经完成；will have been doing 表示到那时某活动已经持续了多久。
  • Easy to miss易漏 ✗ By next year I will finish my degree. → ✓ By next year I will have finished my degree.

📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Open the full explanation: 6 points, a table, 14 examples展开细讲：6 段讲解、一张表、14 个例句

These forms are a camera placed in the future. Pick the moment first, often with at this time tomorrow, by Friday, by the time you arrive or in two years' time. Then ask: will the action be going on at that moment, or will it already be over?

这几种形式就像把镜头放到将来。先选时间点（明天这个时候、周五前、你到的时候、两年后），再问：那一刻动作正在进行，还是已经结束？

will be doing also says something will happen anyway, as part of the normal course of events, without being a decision or a request. I'll be passing the post office, so I can drop it off sounds natural and relaxed; I'll pass the post office sounds like a new decision.

will be doing 还能表示「本来就会发生」的事，不是决定也不是请求。I'll be passing the post office 听起来自然随意；I'll pass the post office 像是刚做的决定。

That is why Will you be using the car tonight? is a polite question: it asks about plans without asking a favour. Will you use the car tonight? can sound like a request or pressure.

所以 Will you be using the car tonight? 很客气：问的是对方的安排，不是要人帮忙。Will you use the car tonight? 可能听起来像请求或施压。

will have done needs a deadline: by, by the time, before, in ten years. Without one it sounds odd. By June we will have paid off the car is complete before June; In June we will pay off the car just names the time of the final payment.

will have done 需要一个期限（by、by the time、before、in ten years），没有期限就别扭。By June 表示六月前已还清；In June 只说最后一笔在六月付。

will have been doing stresses the length of an activity up to a future point, often in anniversaries and complaints: In March I'll have been working here for ten years. It also expresses an assumption about now: Don't call her yet; she'll have been driving all day.

will have been doing 强调到将来某刻为止持续了多久，常用于纪念日和抱怨。它也能表示对现在的推测：先别打电话，她肯定开了一整天车了。

After by the time, when, before and until, the future time clause takes a present tense: By the time you get here, we'll have eaten, not by the time you will get here.

by the time、when、before、until 后面指将来的从句用现在时。

Point of viewFormExample
In progress at a future momentwill be + -ingAt nine tomorrow I'll be sitting my exam.
Will happen anyway, as plannedwill be + -ingWe'll be leaving at six as usual.
Polite question about plansWill you be + -ing?Will you be needing a receipt?
Finished before a future deadlinewill have + past participleBy Friday they'll have fixed the roof.
Duration up to a future pointwill have been + -ingBy May I'll have been studying for a year.
Assumption about now or the pastwill have + past participleThey'll have landed by now.

Examples例句

  • This time next week, we'll be lying on a beach in Mexico. 下周这个时候，我们就躺在墨西哥的海滩上了。
    In progress at a future moment.将来某刻正在进行。
  • Don't call between two and four. I'll be driving. 两点到四点之间别打电话，我在开车。
    Busy at that time.那段时间在忙。
  • Will you be working from home on Friday? 周五你在家办公吗？
    A polite question about plans.客气地问对方安排。
  • I'll be seeing Linda at the meeting anyway, so I can give her the file. 反正开会时我会见到琳达，可以把文件给她。
    Happens as a matter of course.顺理成章会发生。
  • By the time you get home, I'll have cooked dinner. 你到家时，我已经把晚饭做好了。
    Finished before your arrival.在你到家之前完成。
  • By the end of the year, we'll have saved enough for the deposit. 到年底，我们就攒够首付了。
    A deadline with by.by 加期限。
  • The renovation won't have finished by December, so we'll stay with my sister. 装修到十二月还完不了，所以我们住我姐姐家。
    Negative: not complete before the deadline.否定：期限前完不成。
  • In June, I'll have been living in Canada for five years. 到六月，我在加拿大就住满五年了。
    Duration up to a future point.截至将来某刻的持续。
  • She'll have been waiting for an hour by the time the doctor sees her. 医生看到她的时候，她已经等了一个小时了。
    Duration, with a complaint in the tone.持续时间，语气里带着抱怨。
  • It's eight o'clock. The kids will have gone to bed by now. 八点了，孩子们这会儿应该已经睡了。
    An assumption about now.对现在的推测。
  • How much will you have spent on the trip by the time you're back? 等你回来，这趟旅行要花掉多少？
    A question about a total before a future point.问将来某刻前的总量。
  • Next year the plant will have been operating for half a century. 明年这家工厂就运营满半个世纪了。
    An anniversary.纪念日。
  • At midnight the system will be updating, so the site will be down. 半夜系统在更新，所以网站会停。
    A scheduled process in progress.预定的过程正在进行。
  • Before the end of the course, you'll have written about forty short essays. 课程结束前，你会写出大约四十篇短文。
    A count completed before a future point.将来某刻前完成的数量。

More pairs: same idea, different choice更多对比句组

Will you use the meeting room this afternoon? 你下午要用会议室吗？
Can sound like a request or a check on you.可能听起来像请求或查问。
Will you be using the meeting room this afternoon? 下午你会用会议室吗？
A polite question about plans.客气地问安排。
Are you going to use the meeting room this afternoon? 你下午打算用会议室吗？
Asks about an intention.问意图。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ By next year I will finish my degree.
✓ By next year I will have finished my degree.
by + a future time means complete before then, so use will have done.
by 加将来时间表示在那之前完成，用 will have done。
✗ When you will arrive, we will have left.
✓ When you arrive, we will have left.
A future time clause takes the present tense.
将来时间从句用现在时。
✗ Tomorrow at 8 I will have a meeting with the client, so I can't pick you up. (meaning: I'll be in the middle of it)
✓ Tomorrow at 8 I'll be meeting the client, so I can't pick you up.
To say you are busy at that time, use will be doing.
说那个时间在忙，用 will be doing。
✗ In March I will work here for ten years.
✓ In March I will have been working here for ten years.
Duration up to a future point needs the perfect.
截至将来某刻的持续要用完成形式。
✗ By the time we get there, the shop will have been closing.
✓ By the time we get there, the shop will have closed.
A single finished event takes will have done, not the continuous.
一次性完成的事用 will have done，不用进行式。
