📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Open the full explanation: 5 points展开细讲：5 段讲解

Weather and climate are related but not interchangeable. Weather describes short-term atmospheric conditions, such as rain, wind and temperature, while climate refers to typical weather patterns in a region over many years. A common mistake is saying that one unusually cold winter disproves global warming; a single season is weather, not climate.

Weather（天气）和 climate（气候）有关，但不能互换。Weather 描述短期大气状况，例如降雨、风和温度；climate 指某个地区多年形成的典型天气模式。常见错误是认为某个异常寒冷的冬天能够证明全球变暖不存在；单个季节属于天气现象，而不是长期气候趋势。

Many weather expressions use predictable adjective-noun combinations. We say heavy rain, strong winds, thick fog and freezing temperatures rather than powerful rain or big fog. Learning these combinations as complete chunks makes descriptions sound more natural and helps avoid direct translation from Chinese.

许多天气表达使用固定的形容词与名词搭配。我们说 heavy rain（大雨）、strong winds（强风）、thick fog（浓雾）和 freezing temperatures（冰冻气温），而不是 powerful rain 或 big fog。将这些搭配作为完整词块记忆，可以让表达更自然，也能避免从中文直接翻译造成的错误。

Environmental discussions often involve cause-and-effect language. Expressions such as contribute to climate change, lead to habitat loss and have an impact on wildlife help explain relationships between human behaviour and environmental consequences. Remember that contribute to and lead to are followed by a noun or an -ing form, not an infinitive.

讨论环境问题时，经常需要使用因果关系表达。contribute to climate change（加剧气候变化）、lead to habitat loss（导致栖息地丧失）和 have an impact on wildlife（对野生动物产生影响）可以解释人类行为与环境后果之间的联系。注意 contribute to 和 lead to 后面接名词或动名词，而不是动词不定式。

Environmental vocabulary includes both scientific terms and everyday expressions. Global warming refers specifically to the long-term rise in Earth's average temperature, while climate change covers broader changes in climate patterns. Pollution describes harmful substances or conditions affecting the environment, and conservation focuses on protecting natural resources and wildlife.

环境词汇既包括科学术语，也包括日常表达。Global warming（全球变暖）特指地球平均气温的长期上升，而 climate change（气候变化）涵盖更广泛的气候模式变化。Pollution（污染）指影响环境的有害物质或状况，conservation（保护）则侧重于保护自然资源和野生动物。

Some environmental expressions describe gradual processes, while others describe sudden events. Drought and desertification often develop over time, whereas a flood or a storm can cause serious damage within hours. Use expressions such as become increasingly severe, pose a serious threat and recover from damage to discuss the scale and development of environmental problems.

有些环境表达描述逐渐发生的过程，另一些则描述突然发生的事件。干旱和土地荒漠化通常需要一段时间才会形成，而洪水或风暴可能在数小时内造成严重破坏。可以使用 become increasingly severe（变得日益严重）、pose a serious threat（构成严重威胁）和 recover from damage（从破坏中恢复）来讨论环境问题的程度和发展过程。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ The climate is very hot today.
✓ The weather is very hot today.
Weather describes conditions on a particular day. Climate describes long-term patterns in a region. Use weather when talking about today's temperature.
Weather 描述某一天的天气状况，climate 描述一个地区的长期气候模式。谈论今天的气温时应该使用 weather。
✗ There was a big rain last night.
✓ There was heavy rain last night.
Heavy rain is the natural collocation for intense rainfall. Big rain is not normally used in standard English.
Heavy rain 是表示强降雨的自然搭配。标准英语中通常不使用 big rain。
✗ The government wants to reduce the carbon footprints of the country.
✓ The government wants to reduce the country's carbon footprint.
Carbon footprint is commonly used as a singular expression when referring to the total emissions of an individual, organisation or country. The possessive form makes the relationship clearer.
Carbon footprint 通常用单数表示个人、组织或国家产生的温室气体排放总量。使用所有格形式可以更清楚地表达所属关系。
✗ Renewable energies are becoming more popular.
✓ Renewable energy is becoming more popular.
Renewable energy is normally an uncountable expression when referring to the general concept. Use renewable energy sources when you want to identify different types of energy.
泛指可再生能源这一概念时，renewable energy 通常是不可数名词。想强调不同类型的能源时，可以使用 renewable energy sources。
✗ Factories contribute to increase air pollution.
✓ Factories contribute to increasing air pollution.
The preposition to in contribute to is followed by a noun or an -ing form, not a bare infinitive. Contribute to increasing pollution is the correct structure.
contribute to 中的 to 是介词，后面接名词或动名词，而不是动词原形。因此应该说 contribute to increasing pollution。
✗ Many animals are in danger to disappear.
✓ Many animals are in danger of disappearing.
The expression in danger of is followed by a noun or an -ing form. In danger of disappearing is a natural way to describe the risk of extinction.
固定表达 in danger of 后面接名词或动名词。in danger of disappearing 是描述动物面临灭绝风险的自然表达。
