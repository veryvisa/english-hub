A Library Where Strangers Come to Talk 陌生人来聊天的图书馆

🧠 Learn the 1 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 1 个新词

Read a local news story about a library conversation circle; notice phrasal verbs in a news style and the passive where the doer does not matter. 读一篇讲图书馆英语会话圈的本地新闻，留意新闻文体里的短语动词，以及不关心动作者时用的被动语态。

📖 Key card知识卡
A free, informal place to talk every week can do more against isolation than another class.一个每周可以聊天、免费又随意的地方，对抗孤独的作用可能比多上一门课还大。

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Every Tuesday evening, about thirty people sit at round tables in the back room of a public library and talk. Nobody is giving a lesson. The rules are simple: speak English, ask questions, and stay for the full hour. The conversation circle was set up four years ago by two volunteers who had once been newcomers themselves.

每周二晚上，大约三十个人围坐在一家公共图书馆后屋的圆桌旁聊天。没有人在讲课。规矩很简单：说英语、提问题、待满一小时。这个会话圈是四年前由两位志愿者创办的，她们自己当年也是新移民。

"We came up with the idea after a long winter," said one of the founders, a retired nurse. "We both knew the feeling of going a whole week without a real conversation. Isolation does more damage than people think." The library agreed to provide the room, and the circle has since grown to three evenings a week.

「我们是在一个漫长的冬天之后想出这个主意的。」创办人之一、一位退休护士说，「我们俩都知道一整周没有一次真正交谈是什么滋味。孤独造成的伤害比人们以为的大。」图书馆同意提供房间，会话圈此后扩大到每周三个晚上。

Topics are chosen by the group. One week it might be job interviews; the next, how to deal with a difficult landlord or what people do on public holidays. Regulars often take on new members, sitting with them until the first meeting feels less awkward. Some have run into each other at the supermarket and gone for coffee; others now meet at weekends.

话题由大家来定。这一周可能是求职面试，下一周可能是怎样应付难缠的房东，或者大家公共假日都做什么。常客们常常主动带新成员，陪他们坐着，直到第一次聚会不那么尴尬。有些人在超市碰上了，就一起去喝咖啡；还有些人现在周末也约着见面。

Organisers say the circle is not a replacement for language classes. What it offers is a routine and a sense of belonging for people who are still settling in. "People come for the English," said a volunteer who joined in the first month. "They stay because somebody notices when they are missing."

组织者说，会话圈不能替代语言课。它提供的是一种日常节奏，以及给还在安顿中的人的一份归属感。「大家是为了英语来的，」一位第一个月就加入的志愿者说，「留下来，是因为他们没来的时候，有人会注意到。」

The library has turned down offers to charge a fee. Staff say the circle works because it is free and informal, and because nobody has to be good at English to sit down. Anyone who feels like trying can turn up on a Tuesday.

图书馆拒绝了收费的提议。工作人员说，会话圈之所以有效，是因为它免费、随意，也因为没人需要英语很好才能坐下来。谁想试试，周二来就行。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

The conversation circle was set up four years ago by two volunteers who had once been newcomers themselves.
Passive was set up puts the circle first; by adds who did it. Past perfect had been goes back before the circle began.
被动语态 was set up 把会话圈放在句首；by 引出动作者。过去完成时 had been 回溯到会话圈成立之前。
What it offers is a routine and a sense of belonging for people who are still settling in.
A what-clause as subject puts the focus on the end of the sentence.
用 what 从句作主语，把重点放到句末。
