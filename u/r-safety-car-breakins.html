Car Break-ins Climb at Shopping Centre Car Parks 购物中心停车场车辆被撬增多

🧠 Learn the 5 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 5 个新词

Read a short police news report about car break-ins, and notice how news mixes the present perfect, the past simple and the passive to report crime. 读一篇关于车辆被撬的警方新闻，留意新闻报道案件时怎样混用现在完成时、一般过去时和被动语态。

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Police are asking drivers to clear their cars of anything worth stealing after a sharp rise in break-ins at shopping centre car parks over the holiday season. Officers have received more reports in the past month than in the whole of last spring, a spokesperson said on Tuesday.

节日季期间，购物中心停车场的车辆被撬案件明显增多，警方呼吁司机把车里值钱的东西都清走。一名发言人周二表示，过去一个月警方收到的报案比去年整个春天还多。

Most of the thefts follow the same pattern. A driver leaves a bag or a laptop on the back seat, goes into a shop for twenty minutes and comes back to a broken window. "Thieves walk along the rows and look for anything visible," the spokesperson said. "Even a jacket can make them think there is a wallet in the pocket."

大多数盗窃案都是一个套路：司机把包或笔记本电脑留在后座，进店二十分钟，回来车窗就碎了。「小偷沿着一排排车走，找任何看得见的东西，」发言人说，「哪怕一件外套，也会让他们以为口袋里有钱包。」

The cost is often greater than what was taken. A new window takes days to arrange, and a break-in can do damage to people's sense of safety that lasts much longer. Several victims told reporters they had stopped shopping after dark.

损失往往比被偷的东西更大。换一块车窗要好几天才安排得上，而被撬一次对人们安全感的伤害会持续得更久。好几位受害者告诉记者，他们已经不在天黑后购物了。

Police say the best deterrent is simple: leave nothing in view, and do not move valuables to the boot after you park, because someone may be watching. Drivers who have been victims of a break-in are asked to file a report online or through the non-emergency line, even if nothing of value was taken. "Every report helps us take the pattern into account when we plan patrols."

警方说，最好的防范很简单：车里什么都别露在外面，也别停好车后再把贵重物品挪进后备箱，因为可能有人在看。车辆被撬过的司机，即使没丢值钱东西，也请在网上或通过非紧急电话报案。「每一份报案都帮助我们在安排巡逻时把规律考虑进去。」

The shopping centre's management said it has made an effort to improve lighting and has hired extra security staff. Some shoppers remain unconvinced. "They have put up more signs," said one woman whose window was smashed last week. "I would rather see more people."

购物中心管理方表示，已经努力改善照明，并加雇了保安。一些顾客仍不放心。「他们多挂了几块牌子，」一位上周车窗被砸的女士说，「我宁愿多看到几个人。」

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

Drivers who have been victims of a break-in are asked to file a report online or through the non-emergency line, even if nothing of value was taken.
Two passives: are asked (the police ask), was taken (by an unknown thief). News uses the passive when the doer is obvious or unknown.
两个被动：are asked（是警方在请求）、was taken（偷的人不知道是谁）。施动者很明显或不知道时，新闻用被动。
The cost is often greater than what was taken.
what was taken is a noun clause: "the things that were taken". greater than compares two amounts.
what was taken 是名词性从句，等于「被拿走的东西」。greater than 比较两个量。
