Perfect aspect across tenses 贯穿各时态的完成体

Choose accurately among present perfect, past perfect, future perfect and perfect non-finite forms to show that one situation is anterior to, or still relevant at, a reference point. 能够准确选用现在完成、过去完成、将来完成以及完成体非谓语形式，表明某一情形先于参照时间点发生，或在该时间点仍有关联。

I lived in Calgary for five years. 我在卡尔加里住过五年。
The period is closed; I no longer live there, and the focus is on a past stretch of time.这段时期已经结束，我现在不再住那里，重点是过去的一段时间。
I've lived in Calgary for five years. 我在卡尔加里已经住了五年。
The period reaches up to now; I still live there.这段时期延续到现在，我仍然住在那里。
I had lived in Calgary for five years when I got the transfer. 我接到调任通知时，已经在卡尔加里住了五年。
The five years end at a past reference point (the transfer).这五年截止于一个过去的参照点（调任）。
I'll have lived in Calgary for five years by September. 到九月我就在卡尔加里住满五年了。
The five years will be complete at a future reference point.这五年将在一个未来的参照点上达到完整。

📏 The rule规则

📏 The rule in one line规则一句话
The perfect aspect does not locate an event in time by itself; it links an earlier situation to a reference point, which may be now, a past moment, a future moment, or the time of another verb.完成体本身并不给事件定位，而是把一个较早的情形与某个参照点联系起来，这个参照点可以是现在、过去某刻、将来某刻，或另一个动词所在的时间。

📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

The key decision is where your reference point sits. If it is the moment of speaking and the earlier situation still matters, use the present perfect; if you have narrated your way back to a past moment and need to reach further back, use the past perfect. The tense of 'have' moves with the reference point, while the participle always carries the 'earlier than that' meaning.

关键在于参照点放在哪里。参照点是说话时刻且先前的情形仍有影响，就用现在完成；如果叙述已经回到过去某一刻，还需要再往前追溯，就用过去完成。助动词 have 的时态随参照点变化，而过去分词始终承载“早于该点”的意思。

Choose the perfect over the simple past when the result, relevance or unfinished time frame is the point. 'I lost my keys' is a closed event; 'I've lost my keys' tells the listener the problem exists now. Once you add a finished time marker such as 'yesterday' or 'in 2019', the present perfect becomes impossible because the time frame has been closed off.

当重点是结果、现实关联或尚未结束的时间段时，选完成体而不是一般过去时。“I lost my keys”只是陈述一个已结束的事件，“I've lost my keys”则告诉对方问题此刻仍存在。一旦加上 yesterday、in 2019 这类已结束的时间标记，现在完成就不能用了，因为时间框架已被封闭。

The past perfect is not a 'more past' simple past to be sprinkled over every earlier event. Use it when the order of events would otherwise be unclear or when the earlier event explains the later one. If a narrative is already clearly sequenced by 'then' or 'after', or the events are told in the order they happened, the simple past is cleaner and more natural.

过去完成不是可以随意撒在每个更早事件上的“更过去的一般过去时”。只有在事件先后顺序否则不清楚，或较早事件用来解释较晚事件时才用它。如果叙述已经靠 then、after 等明确排序，或按发生顺序讲述，一般过去时更简洁自然。

The future perfect and 'would have' forms are about completion by a later point or about unreal pasts. 'By Friday I'll have finished' stresses that the work will be done before the deadline, whereas 'I'll finish on Friday' does not. Modal perfects such as 'must have', 'can't have' and 'should have' shift the speaker's attention to judgments about what happened earlier, so they express deduction or regret rather than ordinary time.

将来完成和 would have 结构表示到某个未来时间点已经完成，或表示与过去事实相反的情形。“By Friday I'll have finished”强调在截止前已经做完，而“I'll finish on Friday”没有这层意思。情态完成式如 must have、can't have、should have 把注意力转到对先前事情的推断或遗憾上，表达的是推测或后悔，而不是普通的时间关系。

Perfect non-finite forms ('having finished', 'to have been') make anteriority explicit when the main verb alone would not. 'Having read the report, she objected' shows the reading came first and may be the reason she objected; 'Reading the report, she objected' suggests the two happened together. Use the perfect form only when that sequence or cause matters, because it can sound heavy otherwise.

完成体非谓语形式（having finished、to have been）在主动词本身无法体现先后时，把先行关系明确出来。“Having read the report, she objected”表明先读后反对，甚至可能是反对的原因；“Reading the report, she objected”则暗示两者同时发生。只有先后或因果确实重要时才用完成体形式，否则显得笨重。

Continuous and simple perfects differ in what they foreground. 'I've been writing the report' highlights the activity, its duration and possible incompleteness, while 'I've written the report' highlights the finished product. With stative verbs like 'know' or 'own', the simple perfect is the normal choice for a state continuing up to the reference point.

完成进行体和完成体强调的内容不同。“I've been writing the report”突出活动本身、持续时间以及可能尚未完成，“I've written the report”则突出已完成的成果。对于 know、own 这类状态动词，表示状态持续到参照点时通常用简单完成体。

More pairs: same idea, different choice更多对比句组

She was exhausted because she ran the marathon. 她筋疲力尽，因为她跑了马拉松。
Perfectly natural; 'because' already implies the race came first, so the plain past leaves the sequence to be inferred rather than marking it.完全自然；because 本身已暗示比赛在先，一般过去时只是把先后顺序留给读者推断，而不明确标出。
She was exhausted because she had run the marathon. 她筋疲力尽，因为她之前跑了马拉松。
The past perfect makes the earlier event explicit, which is useful when the order might otherwise be unclear.过去完成明确标出较早发生的事件，适合在顺序否则会不清楚时使用。
She was exhausted because she had been running all morning. 她筋疲力尽，因为整个上午一直在跑。
Highlights the prolonged activity as the cause, not a completed race.强调长时间的活动是原因，而不是一场已完成的比赛。
He must be late. 他肯定是迟到了。
A deduction about the present situation.对当前情况的推断。
He must have been late. 他当时肯定是迟到了。
A deduction about an earlier situation.对先前情况的推断。
He should have been on time. 他本该准时的。
Criticism or regret: he was not on time.批评或遗憾：他没有准时。
He can't have been late. 他不可能迟到了。
Strong negative deduction about the earlier situation.对先前情况的强否定推断。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ I have seen him yesterday.
✓ I saw him yesterday.
A finished time marker closes the time frame, so the present perfect cannot be used; English needs the simple past.
yesterday 这类已结束的时间标记封闭了时间框架，不能与现在完成连用，英语要用一般过去时。
✗ When did you have finished the course?
✓ When did you finish the course?
'When' asks for a specific past time, which requires the simple past rather than the perfect.
when 询问具体的过去时间，需用一般过去时而不是完成体。
✗ She has been to Japan last year.
✓ She went to Japan last year.
'Last year' is a completed period; the perfect cannot combine with it, even though the trip may still be relevant.
last year 是已结束的时间段，即使这次旅行仍有影响，也不能与完成体连用。
✗ By the time I got there, the film already started.
✓ By the time I got there, the film had already started.
The film started before the reference point ('got there'), so the past perfect is required to show the earlier event.
电影开始在参照点（我到达）之前，所以必须用过去完成来表示更早的事件。
✗ I will finish the report by tomorrow, so you can read it then.
✓ I will have finished the report by tomorrow, so you can read it then.
When the point is completion before a deadline, the future perfect makes the 'already done by then' meaning explicit; the simple future only states a plan.
强调在截止时间之前已经完成时，将来完成能把“到时已做完”的意思讲明白；一般将来时只是陈述计划。
✗ If I would have known, I would have helped.
✓ If I had known, I would have helped.
In the unreal past conditional, the if-clause takes the past perfect, and 'would have' appears only in the main clause.
在与过去事实相反的条件句中，if 从句用过去完成，would have 只出现在主句中。
