When I started working at a public research centre, I assumed that understanding its rules would be enough to do my job well. The employee handbook explained reporting lines, approval procedures, and professional responsibilities. I read it carefully and expected that daily decisions would follow the written guidance. Within a few weeks, however, I discovered that understanding an institution requires more than memorising its regulations. I also needed to understand how people interpreted them.

刚开始在一家公共研究中心工作时，我以为只要理解这里的规则，就足以做好工作。员工手册解释了汇报关系、审批程序和职业责任。我认真阅读了手册，并认为日常决策都会遵循书面指南。然而，几周后我发现，理解一个制度不仅仅是记住规章。我还需要了解人们如何解释这些规则。

One morning, I submitted a proposal through the official system. Every required document was included, but the request was returned because another department needed to comment first. The handbook mentioned this possibility, although the relevant section was difficult to find. A colleague explained that the process had developed over time to prevent conflicting decisions. What initially appeared to be unnecessary bureaucracy turned out to have a practical institutional purpose.

一天早上，我通过官方系统提交了一份提案。所有要求的文件都已附上，但申请还是被退回，因为另一个部门需要先提出意见。手册虽然提到了这种可能性，但相关内容很难找到。一位同事解释说，这个流程是随着时间逐渐形成的，目的是防止决策相互冲突。起初看起来不必要的官僚程序，后来被证明具有实际的制度目的。

I gradually noticed another distinction. Formal rules described who was responsible, but informal habits influenced how quickly people responded and which questions they considered important. Some colleagues preferred written explanations, while others relied on brief conversations before making a recommendation. These habits were not necessarily wrong. Nevertheless, they could create unequal experiences for employees who lacked access to informal networks. The institution's official procedures therefore did not tell the whole story.

我逐渐注意到了另一个区别。正式规则说明了谁应该负责，但非正式习惯会影响人们回应的速度，以及他们认为哪些问题重要。有些同事更喜欢书面解释，另一些人则倾向于在提出建议前进行简短交谈。这些习惯不一定是错误的。然而，它们可能让无法接触非正式人际网络的员工获得不同的体验。因此，该机构的正式程序并不能说明全部情况。

After several months, I became more comfortable with the system. I still questioned procedures that seemed unnecessarily complicated, but I had learned to ask why they existed before suggesting changes. By and large, institutions tend to work through a combination of written rules, shared expectations, and accumulated experience. Although personal relationships can make work easier, fair procedures remain essential. I now see institutional competence as the ability to preserve useful traditions while correcting practices that no longer serve their purpose.

几个月后，我逐渐熟悉了这套制度。我仍然会质疑那些看起来过于复杂的程序，但我已经学会在提出改变之前，先询问这些程序存在的原因。总体而言，制度通常依靠书面规则、共同预期和长期积累的经验共同运作。尽管个人关系能够让工作更顺利，但公平的程序仍然不可或缺。我现在认为，制度能力就是在保留有用传统的同时，纠正那些已经失去实际作用的做法。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

The handbook mentioned this possibility, although the relevant section was difficult to find.
Although introduces a subordinate concessive clause that contrasts the existence of information with the difficulty of locating it.
Although 引导让步从句，对比信息确实存在与相关内容难以找到这两个事实。
By and large, institutions tend to work through a combination of written rules, shared expectations, and accumulated experience.
The sentence uses a discourse marker to qualify the generalisation, followed by tend to and a three-part coordinated noun phrase.
该句使用话语标记限定一般性判断，随后通过 tend to 表达倾向，并使用三个并列的名词短语。
