📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Open the full explanation: 5 points展开细讲：5 段讲解

Chinese medical verbs do not travel. We kai yao and chi yao, so learners say "the doctor opened medicine" and "I ate the pill". In English the doctor prescribes medicine or gives you a prescription, and you take it, whether it is a tablet, a syrup or an injection.

中文的就医动词搬不过去：「开药」「吃药」直译就错。英语里医生 prescribe（开处方），你 take 药，不管是片剂、糖浆还是打针。

English describes pain with the body part and a verb or an -ache noun: my back hurts, I've got a headache, my knee is sore. Painful is for things that cause pain: a painful injection, a painful week. "My head is painful" sounds like a textbook, not a patient.

英语描述疼痛用「部位＋hurt」或 -ache 名词，或 sore。painful 用来形容带来疼痛的事物。「My head is painful」听着像课本，不像病人。

Time words matter more than adjectives. It started on Sunday, it comes and goes, it has got worse since yesterday, it flares up at night. A precise timeline helps a doctor more than "very, very bad".

时间信息比形容词更有用：什么时候开始、时好时坏、从昨天起加重、晚上发作。清楚的时间线比「非常非常疼」更帮得上医生。

Many illness chunks are phrasal and informal: you come down with a cold, you get over the flu, a rash clears up. In a clinic these are normal, not too casual. Save the formal versions (contract an infection, recover fully) for writing.

很多疾病词块是短语动词，口语化：染上感冒、好了、疹子消了。在诊所里这样说很正常，不嫌随便；正式说法留给书面。

The appointment system has its own verbs: you book an appointment (not order or make one reservation), you get a referral to a specialist, you get something checked. Learn them as chunks so you can use them on the phone without thinking.

预约系统有自己的动词：book 预约、get a referral 拿转诊、get something checked 去查一下。整块记住，打电话时张口就来。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ My head is very painful.
✓ I've got a bad headache. / My head really hurts.
For your own body, use hurt, sore or an -ache noun; painful describes what causes pain.
说自己身上疼用 hurt、sore 或 -ache；painful 形容带来疼痛的事物。
✗ The doctor opened some medicine for me.
✓ The doctor prescribed some medicine. / The doctor gave me a prescription.
kai yao is prescribe; open is a word-by-word translation.
「开药」是 prescribe，open 是逐字直译。
✗ I ate the medicine after dinner.
✓ I took the medicine after dinner.
You take medicine in any form.
吃药一律用 take。
✗ Can I order an appointment for tomorrow?
✓ Can I book an appointment for tomorrow?
You book or make an appointment; you order food.
预约用 book 或 make，order 是点菜、订货。
✗ I have fever.
✓ I've got a fever. / I'm running a temperature.
a fever needs the article; a temperature here means a high one.
fever 要加 a；run a temperature 里的 temperature 指体温偏高。
