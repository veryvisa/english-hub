📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Open the full explanation: 5 points展开细讲：5 段讲解

Many marketing words form fixed verb + noun collocations, and the verb is rarely the one you would guess from Chinese. English speakers launch a product, run a campaign, build a brand, target an audience and gain market share. Learn the whole chunk instead of the noun alone, because a wrong verb sounds unnatural even when the meaning is clear.

许多营销词汇是固定的“动词+名词”搭配，而且动词往往和中文直觉不同。英语里说 launch a product、run a campaign、build a brand、target an audience、gain market share。要整块记忆，而不是只记名词，因为动词一旦用错，即使意思能懂也会显得不地道。

Chinese learners often use one word, such as 'advertisement', for everything. In English, the countable noun 'an ad' or 'an advertisement' is one piece of promotion, 'advertising' is the uncountable activity or industry, and 'a campaign' is a planned series of activities with a goal. Choosing the right noun makes your sentence sound professional.

中国学习者常常用一个词（如 advertisement）概括一切。在英语中，an ad / an advertisement 是可数名词，指一条广告；advertising 是不可数名词，指广告活动或广告行业；a campaign 则是有明确目标的一系列有计划的活动。选对名词，句子才显得专业。

Several pairs look similar but differ in meaning: 'customer' buys from a business, 'client' receives a professional service, and 'consumer' is the end user of a product. Likewise 'the market' often means a group of buyers or a commercial sector (as in 'the Asian market'), while 'a market' can also mean a physical marketplace; 'sales' (the total of what is sold, or the selling activity itself) is different from 'marketing' (everything done to create demand).

有几组词外形相近但含义不同：customer 是向企业购买的人，client 是接受专业服务的客户，consumer 是产品的最终使用者。同样，the market 常指买家群体或某个商业领域（如 the Asian market），而 a market 也可以指实际的集市场所；sales 指销售额或销售活动本身，marketing 指为创造需求所做的一切工作。

Register matters in this field. Business English often prefers short, confident verbs and noun phrases such as 'boost sales', 'cut costs' and 'raise awareness'. Phrases like 'brand awareness' and 'customer loyalty' are neutral and appear in reports, emails and meetings, while overly dramatic Chinese-style expressions such as 'greatly improve the sales amount' sound unnatural.

这个领域很讲究语体。商务英语偏好简短有力的动词和名词短语，如 boost sales、cut costs、raise awareness。brand awareness、customer loyalty 这类表达较中性，常见于报告、邮件和会议；而像 greatly improve the sales amount 这种中式直译就显得不自然。

Pay attention to prepositions that follow these words. We say 'advertise a product', but 'advertise for staff'; we say 'a demand for a product', 'an increase in sales', 'a campaign against waste' and 'compete with a rival for market share'. Chunks keep the correct preposition attached, which saves you from guessing each time.

要留意这些词后面的介词。我们说 advertise a product，但 advertise for staff（登广告招人）；说 a demand for a product、an increase in sales、a campaign against waste、compete with a rival for market share。整块记忆能把正确的介词一并带上，免得每次靠猜。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ We made a big advertising to attract more customers.
✓ We ran a big advertising campaign to attract more customers.
'Advertising' is uncountable, so it cannot take 'a'. Use 'an ad', 'an advertisement' or 'an advertising campaign' for a single, countable item, and use 'run' with 'campaign'.
advertising 是不可数名词，不能加 a。指单独一条广告要用 an ad / an advertisement，指整场活动用 an advertising campaign，并且与 campaign 搭配的动词是 run。
✗ Our company wants to enlarge the market share of the new product.
✓ Our company wants to gain market share with the new product.
We 'gain', 'win', 'increase' or 'lose' market share. 'Enlarge' is not the usual verb here, and 'market share' is normally used without 'the' when speaking generally.
市场份额一般用 gain、win、increase 或 lose 来搭配，enlarge 不是常用动词；泛指时 market share 通常不加 the。
✗ The company gave a big promotion to its sales manager.
✓ The company ran a big promotion on its products to attract shoppers.
In marketing, 'promotion' means a sales activity such as a discount, and the natural verb is 'run'. The meaning 'a move to a higher job' is a different sense, so the context must be clear; to avoid confusion, say 'promoted the sales manager' for a job move.
在营销语境中，promotion 指折扣等促销活动，常搭配动词 run；“职位晋升”是另一个意思，所以语境必须清楚。表示职位晋升时更清晰的说法是 promoted the sales manager。
✗ The consumers of our law firm are mostly small businesses.
✓ The clients of our law firm are mostly small businesses.
A professional service such as law, accounting or consulting has 'clients', not 'consumers'. 'Consumer' refers to the end user of a product, such as food or electronics.
律师、会计、咨询等专业服务的对象叫 clients，而不是 consumers。consumer 指食品、电子产品等商品的最终使用者。
✗ The new ad improved our sales amount greatly.
✓ The new ad boosted our sales significantly.
'Sales amount' is a literal translation. English uses 'sales' or 'sales figures', and a short strong verb like 'boost' sounds natural; 'greatly' is also weaker than 'significantly' in business writing.
sales amount 是直译。英语用 sales 或 sales figures，再配 boost 这样简洁有力的动词才自然；在商务写作中，significantly 也比 greatly 更得体。
✗ We will make a new product in the market next month.
✓ We will launch a new product on the market next month.
Use 'launch' for introducing a product, and the fixed phrase is 'on the market', not 'in the market'. 'Make' only means to manufacture and does not describe a public release.
推出新产品要用 launch，并且固定搭配是 on the market 而不是 in the market。make 只表示“制造”，不能表达公开上市。
