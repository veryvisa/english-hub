Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

Ana: You look exhausted, Ben. Did you stay up late again? Ben: Yes, I did. I had to finish an essay, and I only managed to hand in the essay this morning. I hate working at night, but it is the only time my flat is quiet.

安娜：你看起来累坏了，本。你又熬夜了吗？本：是的。我得写完一篇文章，今天早上才勉强交上。我讨厌晚上工作，可那是我的公寓里唯一安静的时候。

Ana: Why don’t you come to our study group? We meet in the library every Tuesday. Ben: I’m not sure. I tried a group last year, and everyone just talked about other things. Ana: This one is different. We have a rule: the first half hour is for work only, and then we can chat.

安娜：你为什么不来我们的学习小组呢？我们每周二在图书馆见面。本：我不太确定。我去年试过一个小组，大家净聊别的事。安娜：这个不一样。我们有个规矩：前半小时只学习，之后才可以聊天。

Ben: Does it really help? Ana: For me, yes. I used to find it hard to pay attention in lectures, but explaining a topic to other people makes me listen more carefully. Also, it is easier to keep up with the course when someone reminds you about deadlines.

本：这真的有帮助吗？安娜：对我来说有。我以前总觉得上课很难集中注意力，但向别人讲解一个话题会让我听得更认真。而且，有人提醒你截止日期时，更容易跟上课程。

Ben: Fair enough. But I’m worried that I will be the slowest one there. Ana: Nobody cares about that. Everyone makes mistakes, and you soon get used to admitting them out loud. Last month I got a question completely wrong, and the others just laughed and helped me fix it.

本：有道理。可我担心自己会是那里最慢的一个。安娜：没人在意这个。每个人都会犯错，你很快就会习惯大大方方地承认错误。上个月我有一道题完全答错了，其他人只是笑了笑，然后帮我改正。

Ben: All right, I’ll come next Tuesday. Should I bring anything? Ana: Just your notes and a pen. Oh, and some snacks, if you can. Ben: Deal. If it works, I might even start to hand in my essays a day early.

本：好吧，我下周二去。需要带什么吗？安娜：带上笔记和一支笔就行。哦，可以的话再带点零食。本：说定了。如果有用，我甚至可能开始提前一天交论文。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

I used to find it hard to pay attention in lectures, but explaining a topic to other people makes me listen more carefully.
’Used to’ describes a past habit that has changed, ‘find it hard to’ is a fixed pattern, and ‘makes me listen’ follows the pattern make plus object plus bare infinitive.
’used to’ 表示已经改变的过去习惯；'find it hard to' 是固定句式；'makes me listen' 是 make + 宾语 + 不带 to 的动词原形。
If it works, I might even start to hand in my essays a day early.
This is a real condition: ‘if’ plus the present simple, with ‘might’ showing that the result is possible but not certain.
这是真实条件句：if 加一般现在时，might 表示结果可能发生但不确定。
