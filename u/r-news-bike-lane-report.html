Did the Bike Lane Really Hurt the Shops? 自行车道真的害了小店吗？

🧠 Learn the 5 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 5 个新词

Read how a shop owner checks a story about a local report, and see academic collocations and reporting verbs doing the real work. 读一位店主怎样核查一则关于本地报告的报道，看学术搭配和转述动词怎样承担真正的判断。

📖 Key card知识卡
A news story reports a study in one line; the study itself usually says something smaller and more careful. Read the verbs the authors chose.报道用一行字转述一项研究，研究本身通常说得更小、更谨慎。看作者选了哪些动词。

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

When the city painted a bike lane along our street last year, my neighbour who runs the bakery was furious. In March a local website published a story that seemed to prove her right. It claimed that a new report showed sales on the street had fallen by a fifth since the lane opened. She printed it and taped it to her till. I run the hardware shop two doors down, and I wanted to know whether it was true, so I went looking for the report itself.

去年市里在我们这条街上画了自行车道，开面包店的邻居气坏了。三月，一家本地网站登了篇报道，似乎证明她是对的：报道称一份新报告显示，自行车道开通以来这条街的销售额下降了五分之一。她把报道打印出来贴在收银台上。我在隔两家开五金店，想知道这是不是真的，于是去找了报告原文。

The researchers had carried out a survey of forty shop owners on three streets and asked them how business had changed. Owners on our street did report lower sales. But that raises a question the story ignored: what else happened that year? A large office nearby closed in the same month, and two hundred workers stopped buying lunch here.

研究者对三条街上的四十位店主做了问卷，问他们生意有什么变化。我们这条街的店主确实报告销售下降。可这引出了一个报道忽略的问题：那一年还发生了什么？附近一栋大写字楼同月关门，两百个上班族不再来这儿买午饭。

The authors were careful. The findings suggest that some shops lost customers, they wrote, but the survey cannot show that the lane was the cause. They also pointed out that a street with no lane lost almost as much, and they warned that asking owners to remember past sales can pose a risk of bias, because people recall bad months more clearly than good ones.

作者很谨慎。他们写道，研究结果显示一些店确实流失了顾客，但问卷无法证明原因是自行车道。他们还指出，一条没有自行车道的街损失几乎一样多；并提醒说，让店主回忆过去的销售额可能带来偏差，因为人们对坏月份记得比好月份清楚。

So I wrote a short letter to the website. There is no strong evidence that the lane hurt the shops, I wrote, only a question that deserves more research. Studies in other cities have found mixed results, so the worry is not silly, but one survey of forty owners is not enough to draw a firm conclusion.

于是我给网站写了封短信。我写道：没有有力证据表明自行车道害了这些店，只有一个值得进一步研究的问题。其他城市的研究结果不一，所以这种担心并不荒唐，但四十位店主的一次问卷不足以下定论。

The website printed my letter a week later. My neighbour has not taken her printout down, but she has taped mine next to it.

一周后网站登了我的信。邻居没把她的打印件撕下来，不过把我的也贴在了旁边。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

But that raises a question the story ignored: what else happened that year?
raise a question means "make people ask it". The colon introduces the question itself, and the story ignored is a relative clause with that left out.
raise a question 是「引出疑问」。冒号后面就是那个问题；the story ignored 是省略了 that 的定语从句。
Studies in other cities have found mixed results, so the worry is not silly, but one survey of forty owners is not enough to draw a firm conclusion.
draw a firm conclusion: the adjective goes between the verb and the noun of the collocation. The sentence first grants the other side a point, then limits it with but.
draw a firm conclusion：形容词插在搭配的动词和名词之间。整句先让对方一步，再用 but 收住。
They also pointed out that a street with no lane lost almost as much, and they warned that asking owners to remember past sales can pose a risk of bias, because people recall bad months more clearly than good ones.
Two reporting verbs: point out that adds a fact the reader may miss; warn that adds a possible danger.
两个转述动词：point out that 补一个读者可能忽略的事实，warn that 补一个可能的危险。
