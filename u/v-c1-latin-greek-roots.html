📖 Why, and when细讲：为什么、什么时候用

Open the full explanation: 6 points展开细讲：6 段讲解

Latin and Greek roots often provide the central meaning of a word, while prefixes and suffixes modify that meaning or determine the grammatical function. For example, the Greek root bio means life and appears in biology, biography and biodiversity; geo means earth or land and appears in geography and geopolitical; chron means time and appears in chronological and chronicle. Recognising the root gives you a useful starting point, even when you have never encountered the complete word before.

拉丁语和希腊语词根通常提供单词的核心含义，而前缀和后缀则改变其含义或决定词性。例如，希腊语词根 bio 表示生命，出现在 biology（生物学）、biography（传记）和 biodiversity（生物多样性）中；geo 表示土地或地球，出现在 geography（地理学）和 geopolitical（地缘政治的）中；chron 表示时间，出现在 chronological（按时间顺序的）和 chronicle（编年史）中。即使以前没有见过完整单词，识别词根也能帮助你推测词义。

A single root can appear in several different words belonging to different parts of speech, not just different inflections of one word. For instance, spect means look or observe and appears in the verb inspect, the noun spectator, and the nouns perspective and retrospective. These words are related, but they do not have interchangeable meanings. Learn the root alongside the complete word and its typical context rather than assuming that every word containing the same root means exactly the same thing.

同一个词根可以出现在属于不同词性的多个单词中，而不只是同一个单词的不同变形。例如，spect 表示看或观察，出现在动词 inspect（检查）、名词 spectator（观众），以及名词 perspective（视角）和 retrospective（回顾性的）中。这些单词存在词源联系，但不能互换使用。学习时应将词根、完整单词及其典型语境结合起来，而不要认为所有包含相同词根的单词意思完全相同。

Some roots have several related forms because of historical spelling changes or their movement between Latin, Greek and English. For example, scrib and script both relate to writing, as seen in describe, manuscript and transcription. Chinese learners should recognise these variations instead of expecting every related word to preserve an identical sequence of letters.

由于历史上的拼写变化，以及词汇在拉丁语、希腊语和英语之间的演变，一些词根会出现不同形式。例如，scrib 和 script 都与书写有关，可以在 describe（描述）、manuscript（手稿）和 transcription（转录）中看到。中文学习者应识别这些词根变体，而不是期待所有相关单词都保留完全相同的字母组合。

Root knowledge is a vocabulary-building tool, not a substitute for checking a dictionary. A familiar root can suggest a word's general meaning, but prefixes, suffixes and historical developments may produce meanings that are not obvious from the individual parts. Port means carry and tract means pull or draw: transport (port) now means a specific system for moving people or goods, not just 'carry' in general, and attract (tract) keeps the idea of 'pulling' but now describes interest or appeal rather than a physical pull.

词根知识是扩展词汇的工具，不能代替查词典。熟悉的词根可以提示单词的大致含义，但前缀、后缀和历史演变可能产生无法单纯通过词素组合推断的意思。port 表示携带，tract 表示拉、拽：transport（含 port）现在特指运送人或货物的系统，而不只是泛泛的「携带」；attract（含 tract）仍保留「拉」的核心意象，但现在描述的是引起兴趣或吸引力，而不是物理上的拉拽。

Greek roots are particularly common in scientific, medical and technical vocabulary, while Latin roots appear widely in academic, legal and everyday English. However, this is a general tendency rather than a strict rule. Micro means small (microeconomics) and macro means large (macroeconomic); tele means far or distant, so telescope literally combines 'far' with the root for 'see'; photo means light and graph means write or record, so photography literally means 'recording with light'. Latin roots such as bene (good), mal (bad) and cred (believe) are useful in ordinary discussions and formal writing: benevolent shows good will, malfunction is a bad or failed function, and credible means able to be believed.

希腊语词根在科学、医学和技术词汇中尤其常见，而拉丁语词根则广泛出现在学术、法律和日常英语中。不过，这只是总体趋势，并非严格规则。micro 表示小（microeconomics 微观经济学），macro 表示大（macroeconomic 宏观经济的）；tele 表示远，所以 telescope（望远镜）字面上就是「远」加上表示「看」的词根；photo 表示光，graph 表示写或记录，所以 photography（摄影）字面意思就是「用光记录」。bene（好）、mal（坏）、cred（相信）等拉丁语词根在日常讨论和正式写作中也很实用：benevolent（仁慈的）体现善意，malfunction（故障）是「坏的」或失灵的功能，credible（可信的）表示可以被相信。

A root-formed word that works as both a noun and a verb often shifts its stress between the two: the noun stresses the first syllable and the verb stresses the second. Transport (port, carry) is a clear example: 'TRANSport' as a noun (public transport) but 'transPORT' as a verb (to transport goods). Saying the wrong one is a common pronunciation mistake, not a spelling mistake.

一个由词根构成、同时能作名词和动词的单词，常常在名词和动词之间移动重音：名词重音落在第一个音节，动词重音落在第二个音节。Transport（含词根 port，意为携带）就是典型例子：作名词时读 'TRANSport'（public transport），作动词时读 'transPORT'（to transport goods）。读错重音是常见的发音错误，不是拼写错误。

⚠️ Common slips for Chinese speakers华人常错

✗ I need a transcription of this contract into French.
✓ I need a translation of this contract into French.
Transcription means converting speech or recorded material into written text in the same language; moving a text into a different language is translation, not transcription, even though the two words sound similar.
Transcription 指把讲话或录音转换成同一种语言的文字，而把文本转换成另一种语言是 translation（翻译），不是 transcription，尽管这两个词读音相近容易混淆。
✗ The manager inspected about the safety procedures.
✓ The manager inspected the safety procedures.
Inspect is a transitive verb and takes a direct object without about. Chinese learners sometimes add about because they are thinking of the structure talk about something. Use inspect something, not inspect about something.
Inspect 是及物动词，后面直接接宾语，不需要 about。中文学习者有时会受到 talk about something 这一结构的影响而误加 about。正确结构是 inspect something，而不是 inspect about something。
✗ From my perspective, the problem is very serious, but I respect your perspective view.
✓ From my perspective, the problem is very serious, but I respect your point of view.
Perspective already means a particular way of viewing something. Adding view immediately after it is redundant. Use perspective or point of view, rather than combining the two expressions.
Perspective 本身就表示看待事物的角度或观点，在其后再加 view 会造成重复。可以使用 perspective 或 point of view，不要将两种表达机械地组合起来。
✗ The new policy aims to attract to foreign investment.
✓ The new policy aims to attract foreign investment.
Attract is a transitive verb and takes its object directly, with no preposition in between. Chinese learners sometimes insert to by analogy with phrases like appeal to, but attract never takes to before its object.
Attract 是及物动词，后面直接接宾语，中间不加介词。中文学习者有时会类比 appeal to 这类结构而误加 to，但 attract 后面永远不接 to 再跟宾语。
✗ The machine has malfunction.
✓ The machine has a malfunction.
As a noun, 'malfunction' is countable and normally needs a determiner such as 'a', as in 'a malfunction'. (The verb form needs no article: 'The machine malfunctioned.')
Malfunction 作名词时是可数名词，通常需要限定词，例如 a malfunction。（作动词时不需要冠词：The machine malfunctioned.）
✗ We need a credible evidence to support our argument.
✓ We need credible evidence to support our argument.
'Evidence' is uncountable, so it cannot take 'a' before it, even with an adjective in between. 'Credible' is the right adjective here; the error is the article, not the word class.
Evidence 是不可数名词，即使前面加了形容词，也不能用 a。Credible 这个形容词用得对，错的是冠词 a，不是词性。
