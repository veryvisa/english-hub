Two Forces That Decide When You Sleep 决定你何时入睡的两股力量

Why can you feel wide awake at midnight after a long day, and then struggle to keep your eyes open at three in the afternoon? Sleep scientists describe two forces that work together: sleep pressure and the body clock. Knowing them explains a great deal.

为什么忙了一整天，半夜反而精神十足，下午三点却眼皮打架？睡眠科学家说有两股力量在共同起作用：睡眠压力和生物钟。了解它们能解释很多事。

Sleep pressure builds up from the moment you wake. The longer you are awake, the more of a chemical called adenosine collects in the brain, and the sleepier you feel. Sleep clears it away. Caffeine works by blocking the signal, not by removing the chemical, which is why tiredness can return all at once when the coffee wears off. A long nap late in the day also releases some of the pressure, so you may lie awake at night.

睡眠压力从你醒来那一刻开始累积。醒着的时间越长，大脑里一种叫腺苷的物质积得越多，你就越困。睡眠把它清掉。咖啡因是挡住这个信号，而不是清除这种物质，所以咖啡劲一过，疲倦会一下子涌回来。傍晚睡个长午觉也会释放掉一部分压力，于是夜里可能躺着睡不着。

The body clock is a rhythm of roughly twenty-four hours that tells the body when to be alert and when to rest. Its strongest signal is light, especially morning light. Bright screens late at night send the opposite message. This is why going to bed and getting up at the same time every day, even at weekends, helps so many people: the clock learns what to expect.

生物钟是一个大约二十四小时的节律，告诉身体什么时候该清醒、什么时候该休息。它最强的信号是光，尤其是早晨的光。深夜明亮的屏幕发出的是相反的信号。所以每天同一时间睡觉、同一时间起床，周末也不例外，对很多人都有帮助：生物钟学会了该期待什么。

Jet lag shows what happens when the two forces disagree. After a long flight east, your sleep pressure may be high, but your clock still thinks it is the middle of the day. For shift workers, who go to work when the clock says sleep, the conflict never fully ends, and they need extra care with light, meals and naps.

时差说明两股力量不一致时会怎样。往东飞了长途之后，你的睡眠压力可能很高，生物钟却还以为是大白天。倒班的人在生物钟喊「睡觉」的时候去上班，这种冲突从来没有完全结束，他们在光线、吃饭和小睡上都要格外注意。

In practice, most advice about sleep comes down to helping one force or the other. A regular routine trains the clock, and daylight in the morning sets it. Limiting caffeine after lunch and keeping naps short protect the pressure. A calm hour to wind down before bed gives both a chance to agree. The only thing you cannot rush is time: a new routine usually needs a few weeks.

说到底，大多数睡眠建议都是在帮其中一股力量。规律的作息训练生物钟，早晨的日光校准它；午饭后少喝咖啡、午觉睡短一点，保护睡眠压力；睡前留一小时平静下来，让两者有机会一致。唯一急不来的是时间：新的作息通常要几个星期才见效。
