The Experiment That Went Wrong 出了错的那次实验

🧠 Learn the 3 new words of this unit, one card at a time逐张学本单元的 3 个新词

Read a lab technician's story about a failed experiment and pick up the phrasal verbs people use to talk about problems at work. 读一位实验室技术员讲一次失败实验的故事，学会工作中谈问题常用的短语动词。

📖 Key card知识卡
A mistake you report can become a question worth asking; a mistake you hide is only a mistake.说出来的错误可能变成一个值得问的问题；藏起来的错误就只是错误。

Hear the whole topic in a row整个主题连着听

In my second year as a lab technician in Canada, I ran into the kind of problem that makes you want to hide. I was growing cells for a study on wound healing, and every dish I touched seemed to go wrong. The cells grew too fast, then died. My supervisor had a report due, and I was the person slowing everything down.

在加拿大当实验室技术员的第二年，我遇到了那种让人想躲起来的问题。我在为一项伤口愈合研究培养细胞，可我碰过的每个培养皿好像都出了错。细胞先是长得太快，然后死掉。导师有一份报告要交，而我就是那个拖慢一切的人。

For two weeks I tried to sort it out by trial and error. I changed the temperature, then the timing, then the brand of gloves. Nothing worked. I did not want to bring it up at the weekly meeting, because where I trained, a technician who reported a failure was a technician who had been careless.

两个星期里，我靠反复试错想把它解决掉。我改了温度，又改了时间，再换了手套的牌子。都不管用。我不想在每周例会上提这件事，因为在我受训的地方，报告失败的技术员就是粗心的技术员。

In the end a postdoc noticed my face and asked what was going on. Together we figured out that one bottle of growth liquid had been mixed with the wrong chemical by accident. It was not my fault, but that was not the interesting part. She pointed out that the cells in the "bad" dishes had closed a scratch twice as fast as normal.

最后一位博士后注意到我的脸色，问我怎么了。我们一起弄清楚了：有一瓶培养液被意外混进了错误的化学品。不是我的错，但这还不是有意思的地方。她指出，那些「坏」培养皿里的细胞把一道划痕愈合得比平常快一倍。

We did not make a discovery that day, and I want to be honest about that. We came up with a question. The lab spent the next year following up on it, and the effect held up, but it was much smaller than we had hoped. Still, the project would not exist if we had thrown those dishes away.

那天我们并没有做出什么发现，这一点我想说实话。我们想出的是一个问题。实验室接下来一年都在跟进它，这个效应站住了脚，但比我们期待的小得多。不过，如果当时把那些培养皿扔了，就不会有这个项目。

I still come across people who think science is a straight line from idea to answer. Mine was a mess of mistakes, and my mistakes were data too. Now when something goes wrong, I write it down and say it out loud at the meeting.

我现在还会碰到有人以为科学是从想法到答案的一条直线。我的经历是一团错误，而我的错误也是数据。现在一出问题，我就记下来，在会上说出来。

Words词

/ˌtraɪəl ənd ˈerə/ n. phr.
反复试错
by trial and error; a process of trial and error
I learned to cook rice by trial and error. 我是靠反复试错学会煮饭的。
/baɪ ˈæksɪdənt/ adv. phr.
意外地；偶然
discover sth by accident; happen by accident
Many useful things were found by accident. 许多有用的东西是偶然发现的。
/dɪˈskʌvəri/ v. + n.
有所发现；做出发现
make an important discovery; make the discovery that
She made the discovery while cleaning her old samples. 她是在清理旧样本时有了这个发现。
/kʌm əˈkrɒs/ phr. v.
偶然碰到；遇见
come across a problem; come across an article
I came across an interesting study last week. 上周我偶然看到一项有意思的研究。
/ˈdeɪtə/ n.
数据；资料
collect data; the data shows
We need more data before we decide. 决定之前我们需要更多数据。

Long sentences, unpacked长句拆解

Together we figured out that one bottle of growth liquid had been mixed with the wrong chemical by accident.
figure out that + clause: find the reason. had been mixed is past perfect passive: it happened before they found it, and nobody knows who did it.
figure out that 后接从句＝弄清原因。had been mixed 是过去完成时被动：发生在他们发现之前，也不知道是谁弄的。
I did not want to bring it up at the weekly meeting, because where I trained, a technician who reported a failure was a technician who had been careless.
bring it up: with a pronoun, the object goes between the two parts. The story stays in the past: trained, reported, had been.
bring it up：宾语是代词时放在两部分中间。整段叙事保持过去时：trained、reported、had been。
