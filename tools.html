Tools工具

Practice tools that work on what you have already read and learned here.这些练习工具用的都是你在本站读过、学过的内容。

CELPIP listening and speaking思培听说

CELPIP as the ruler for what you learn here: all 8 speaking tasks with answers at three levels, the 6 listening parts, real-test-style clips, and a weekly check on the topic you studied.把思培当作检验学习成果的尺子：口语 8 个任务、每题三个级别的示范，听力 6 个部分、真题风格音频，每周用本周主题的题目检验所学。

My corpus个人语料

Every text you finish joins your own corpus. See how many times you have met each chunk, in which texts and in which sentences, what is close to ten meetings, and which text to read next to get there.读完的每篇文章都进你自己的语料库：每个词块见过几次、在哪几篇、原句是什么，哪些快到十次，再读哪一篇能补上。

Sentence gym句式健身房

Take a plain sentence and rebuild it: inversion, cleft sentences, participle clauses, nominalisation and more. Every answer is checked at once, and you see the same structure in real sentences from the texts on this site.把一句平淡的句子改成高级结构：倒装、分裂句、分词从句、名词化等。即时判对，并显示同一结构在本站文章里的真实出现。

Upgrade ladders说法升级梯

Swap the words everyone uses for sharper ones, one step at a time: everyday → B2 → C1. Each step has collocations, examples, the tone it carries and when not to use it.把人人都会的说法一级级换成更准的说法：常用 → B2 → C1。每一级都有搭配、例句、语气差别，以及什么时候不该用。

Listen by topic窄听：按主题连着听

Every topic text read sentence by sentence: follow along, loop one line, slow it down, take dictation, then record yourself and compare.每个主题的文章逐句朗读：跟着看、单句循环、变速、听写判分，再录下自己来对比。

New Concept English companion新概念英语配套

Book 1 to Book 4, one page per lesson: the lesson's new words with full cards, checks and spaced review, how the hard sentences are built, grammar points, patterns, and a method for dictation and retelling. Use it alongside your coursebook.第一到第四册，一课一页：本课新词（完整学习卡、小测、间隔复习）、难句结构、语法点、句型、听写与复述的方法。配合教材使用。

Mistake radar华人错误雷达

The 38 slips Chinese speakers make most, as practice: find the mistake, rewrite the sentence, hear the difference. A radar chart shows where you are weakest and which unit to study next.华人最常犯的 38 类错误，做成练习：找错、改写、听辨。雷达图显示你哪里最弱、接下来该学哪个单元。

Click-a-word reader点词阅读器

Paste any English text or drop a .txt, .md, .srt or .vtt file. It is laid out for easy reading; click any word for a full word card, see which words are above your level and get a word list for the text. Nothing leaves your browser.粘贴任意英文，或拖入 .txt、.md、.srt、.vtt：排成好读的版式；点任何词出完整学习卡，标出超出你级别的词，一键生成本篇生词表。文字不离开你的浏览器。

Your route (new home, sample)学习路线（新首页样板）

One route from intermediate to advanced: find your starting station in ten minutes, then one topic a week with words by meaning, sentence patterns, reading, speaking and a writing check that runs in your browser.从中级到高级的一条主路线：十分钟找到起点，然后一周一个主题：按意思学词、句式、阅读、口语，加一个在浏览器里运行的写作体检。

Text spine: which coursebooks, in what order课文主线：用哪几套、怎么配

What New Concept English got right, which modern British, American and National Geographic series do it better for listening and speaking, and how they fit with English File without repeating it.新概念好在哪里；哪些英美与国家地理的现代体系在听说上做得更好；怎样和 English File 配合而不打转。

Squeeze a text课文榨干

Bring any text or transcript you own (and its audio). Close reading line by line, new words by frequency band, a retelling outline, a summary frame, dictation and shadowing. Nothing leaves your browser.拿你手上的任意课文或转写（可配音频）：逐句精读、按频段找生词、复述提纲、摘要框、听写、跟读。全部在你的浏览器里处理，不上传。

Shadowing studio跟读录音室

Shadow any text sentence by sentence: hear the model, record yourself, play both back. Then answer CELPIP-style speaking tasks against the clock, rate yourself on the four dimensions and watch the curve move.任选一篇文章逐句跟读：听示范、录自己、两段对比回放。再按思培形式限时作答口语题，四个维度自评，看曲线怎么走。

TED talk lessonsTED 演讲课

Real talks, studied like a coursebook text: chunks before you listen, a listening plan with time stamps, 10+ questions, a note frame, shadowing lines and CELPIP-style speaking tasks.真实演讲当课文来学：听前词块、带时间点的听力计划、10 道以上理解题、笔记框、跟读句和仿思培口语题。

Word study (new layout, sample)单词学习（新版样板）

Twenty words with several meanings, each meaning with its own sentences from Tatoeba, our own writing and classic books. Three ways to study: meaning cards, sentence first, flashcards.二十个多义词与易混词：每个意思配自己的例句（Tatoeba、本站自写、经典作品）。三种学习方式可切换试用：义项卡、例句先行、闪卡。

Word pages词条页

One page per word for the mid-frequency and academic words that come after the first 2,800: meaning, pronunciation, collocations, word family, at least eight example sentences, where it comes from, and the sentences on this site where it appears.最常用的 2800 词之后的中频词与学术词，一词一页：释义、读音、搭配、词族、至少八条例句、词源，以及它在本站文章里出现的原句。
