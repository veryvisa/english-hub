Lesson 48 · Planning a Share Portfolio 规划股票投资组合

📘 New Concept English · Book 4 · Lesson 48 “Planning a Share Portfolio”新概念英语 第四册 Lesson 48《Planning a Share Portfolio》

C1🧠 25 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
There is never any shortage of people offering a tip that will make you rich by Friday. The serious investor needs something duller: a holding with a shape and a purpose. Ask five brokers and you will get five answers - there is no single right portfolio, though there are plainly wrong ones, such as putting everything into one company called Periwigs. Settle the basics first (the mortgage, the pension, the insurance, enough cash within reach), then set your aims by your age and your temperament: an older investor wants to keep the capital and lift the income, while someone young, secure and not easily frightened can afford to be bolder. There is one golden rule, and it is to spread the risk. This is writing that gives advice, and it is a model of how to hedge in English: an if-clause, then should, may, might, or you'd probably.
「一夜暴富」的荐股人到处都是，认真的投资者却需要一个有结构、有目标的投资组合。问五个经纪人会得到五种答案——没有唯一正确的组合，但有明显错误的做法（比如把钱全押在一家叫 Periwigs 的公司）。先处理好按揭、养老金、保险和现金储备，再按年龄和性格定目标：年长者保本、增收；年轻、财务稳、心大的人可以进取一些。黄金法则只有一条：分散风险。本课是理财建议文，教你用英语给出有条件、有分寸的建议：if 从句 + should / may / might / you'd probably。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. What separates the serious investor from the one who is really gambling?认真的投资者和赌徒式投资者有什么区别？
  2. Why is there no single right way to build a portfolio?为什么说构建投资组合没有唯一「正确」的方法？
  3. How should an older investor's holdings differ from a younger one's?年长的投资者和年轻的投资者的投资方式有什么不同？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

The credit in the older edition of Book 4 names a magazine and a date: Investors Chronicle, March 23 1990 - a British weekly for private investors - and the passage carries a footnote of its own, telling the reader that Periwigs is an invented company. No writer is named, which is usual for a magazine's own advice pages. We have the credit line and the footnote from the scanned book; we did not reach the issue itself, so we cannot say which page or column it came from, and the writer is unknown to us.

旧版第四册的署名给了一份杂志和一个日期：Investors Chronicle，1990 年 3 月 23 日——一份英国面向个人投资者的周刊；课文自己还带一条脚注，告诉读者 Periwigs 是虚构的公司名。没有署作者名，杂志自家的理财版面通常如此。署名行与脚注我们从扫描本上读到；那一期本身没有取到，所以说不出它出自哪一页哪一栏，作者也不详。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

Read the date before you read the advice. On 23 March 1990 the British base rate was 15 per cent, where it had stood since the previous October and where it stayed until sterling joined the European exchange rate mechanism that autumn; the cut then was to 14. A deposit account in that spring paid a double-digit return, which is why the list offered to the cautious reader - government stock, cash on deposit, the income shares of certain trusts - was a real menu and not a counsel of despair. Those were also the last weeks before a recession that took the housing market with it. And one item on the list is worth marking. The piece recommends, among the safer holdings, the income shares issued by split capital trusts: the sector where, a decade later, a scheme that had been sold as safe collapsed, and where the regulator and some twenty firms eventually put up 194 million pounds, agreed in December 2004, for investors - many of them holding what had been bought to pay school fees. Nothing in the article is wrong about them. It simply could not know.

先看日期，再看建议。1990 年 3 月 23 日，英国基准利率是 15%，从上一年 10 月起就停在那里，直到那年秋天英镑加入欧洲汇率机制时才下调到 14%。那个春天，一个存款账户给的是两位数的回报——所以给谨慎读者的那张清单（政府债、现金存款、某些信托的收益类份额）是一张真实的菜单，不是无奈之举。那也是一场把房地产市场一起带走的衰退之前的最后几个星期。清单上有一项值得标出来：文章推荐了「拆分资本型投资信托的收益类份额」——而正是这个板块，十年后出了事：一种曾被当作安全产品卖出去的东西崩了；监管机构与约二十家公司最终在 2004 年 12 月同意拿出 1.94 亿英镑赔付投资者，其中许多人买它原本是为了付学费。文章关于它们的说法没有错，它只是不可能知道。

✨ How it is written赏析

This is a hard thing to write well, and the page does it with three devices worth copying. The first is the invented company. Instead of warning against putting everything in one place, the writer gives that one place a ridiculous name, and the argument is won by the sound of the word: nobody will ever put all their money into Periwigs. The fiction is even footnoted, which makes the joke more solid rather than less. The second is the five brokers. A statement that there is no single right answer is weak; five respectable men, given the same facts, returning five different answers is a demonstration, and it also protects the writer from the thing a reader most wants and cannot be given. The third is the grading of the verbs, which is the real technique of the page. Advice is laid out as a ladder - what you should do first, what you would probably then do, what you may decide, what you might include - and the modal does the work of a disclaimer without sounding like one. There is one more flourish, in the bracket that adds, after all of it, or perhaps any at all: the aside takes back more than the sentence seemed to be giving. If you have to write advice for strangers, this is the model: a real example made absurd, a demonstration instead of a claim, and a verb chosen to carry exactly as much weight as you can defend.

这类文字很难写好，而这一页用了三手值得照抄的办法。第一是那个虚构的公司：作者没有去警告「不要把钱全放在一个地方」，而是给那「一个地方」起了一个荒唐的名字，于是这场论证靠一个词的声音就赢了——没有人会把全部的钱投进 Periwigs。这个虚构还专门加了脚注，而这反倒让玩笑更实，不是更虚。第二是那五位经纪人：「没有唯一正确答案」这句话本身很弱；而五位有口碑的人拿到同样的事实、交回五个不同的答案，就是一次演示，同时也保护了作者，免得去给读者最想要、却给不了的那样东西。第三是动词的分级，这才是这一页真正的技术：建议被摆成一道梯子——你应该先做什么、接着你多半会怎么做、你可以决定什么、你不妨放进什么——情态动词干了免责声明的活，却不像免责声明。还有一处小花招，在那个括号里：「全部的钱，或者也许任何一分钱」——这句插话收回去的，比句子看起来给出的更多。如果你必须给陌生人写建议，这就是范本：一个被弄荒唐了的真实例子、一次演示而不是一句断言，以及一个只承担你能承担的分量的动词。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

One sentence on this page has outlasted everything else, and it is the last one: spread your risk. Everything above it is dated, and its dating is instructive. The piece takes for granted that your job is to choose holdings, with help from a broker, and the thirty-five years since have gone to the opposite answer - a single cheap fund that holds the whole market and chooses nothing. The scoreboard is why. In the 2024 figures for the United States, about 65 per cent of actively managed large-company funds did worse than the index over the year, and over the fifteen years to the end of 2024 there was no category in which most active managers beat their benchmark; of the funds that finished in the better half in 2022, only about 9 per cent stayed there in each of the next two years. Put beside the opening paragraph, that last number is the sharpest thing here: the writer tells the serious investor to leave the tipsters to the gamblers, and the data say that the professionals' good years do not predict their next ones either. Two other parts have aged in opposite directions. His sequence - deal with the mortgage, the pension, the insurance and the cash you can reach, and only then think about shares - is now standard advice almost everywhere, and the collapse of the splits a decade later is an argument for taking it even more literally than he meant. But the shape of his menu depends on that 15 per cent, and a reader who copies it into a world of low deposit rates has copied the furniture and not the plan. Which is the general lesson, and the honest one: the reasoning on this page is worth keeping and the holdings are not, and what anybody should actually own is a question for somebody licensed who knows their situation, not for a magazine, and not for a lesson in a textbook. For Chinese readers the one thing this page cannot supply is the part that was missing from the British market too in 1990: the cheap fund that holds everything is a recent arrival in most places, and the argument about whether to pick shares at all is being had now, with thirty-five years of scoreboards that the writer of this page did not have.

这一页上有一句话活得比其余一切都长，就是最后那一句：分散风险。它上面的全部内容都带着年份，而「带着年份」这件事本身很有教益。文章想当然地认为，你的活是在经纪人帮助下挑持仓；而此后的三十五年走向了相反的答案——一只便宜的基金，持有整个市场，什么都不挑。原因在成绩单上：美国 2024 年的数字里，约 65% 的主动管理大盘基金当年落后于指数；而在截至 2024 年底的十五年里，没有任何一个类别里多数主动管理人跑赢了基准；2022 年排在前一半的基金中，只有约 9% 在之后两年里每年都还留在前一半。把最后这个数放到开篇那一段旁边，它是这里最锋利的东西：作者叫认真的投资者把荐股人留给赌徒，而数据说，专业人士的好年份也预测不了他们的下一个年份。另外两部分则朝相反方向老去。他的次序——先把按揭、养老金、保险和随手可取的现金处理好，然后才去想股票——今天几乎在所有地方都是标准建议；而十年后拆分信托的崩塌，是一个「比他本意更照字面执行」的理由。但他那张菜单的形状，是靠那 15% 撑起来的；谁把它照搬到低存款利率的世界里，搬走的是家具，不是方案。这就是一般性的、也是诚实的那一课：这一页上的推理值得留下，持仓不值得；而任何人到底该持有什么，是一个问题——该问一位了解你处境的持牌专业人士，不该问一份杂志，也不该问一本教材里的一课。对中国读者，这一页唯一给不了的，正是 1990 年的英国市场也还缺的那样东西：持有一切的便宜基金在多数地方都是近年才到的，而「到底要不要挑股票」这场争论正在发生，手里握着三十五年的成绩单——写这一页的人并没有。

💬 Discuss and write讨论与写作
  1. Rewrite the paragraph about the five brokers for a field you know well, where experts also disagree. Keep the method: do not claim that there is no single answer, demonstrate it. Six sentences.
    把「五位经纪人」那一段改写到一个你熟悉、专家同样意见不一的领域里。保留它的办法：不要去断言「没有唯一答案」，而是把它演示出来。六句话。
  2. Go through the lesson marking every should, would probably, may and might, and say what each one is doing. Then write three sentences of advice of your own about something you know, one at each strength.
    把课文里的每一个 should、would probably、may、might 都标出来，说一说它们各自在干什么。然后就你熟悉的一件事，用这三种强度各写一句你自己的建议。
  3. The one line here that has not aged is the last one. Pick a piece of advice you were given years ago and ask which part was the reasoning and which part was the furniture of its time.
    这一页上没有过时的只有最后一句。挑一条多年前有人给你的建议，问一问：哪一部分是它的推理，哪一部分只是它那个年代的家具？

Sentence 2第 2 句 (begins开头 “But if you …”)

  • The main clause is an imperative, with the if-clause in front of it.祈使句作主句，前面是 if 条件从句。
  • leave something to somebody: hand it over to them.leave sth to sb：把……留给某人。
  • those plus a prepositional phrase: the people who have spare cash. The infinitive after money tells you what the money is for.those with money to fritter：those + 介词短语（「那些有闲钱可以挥霍的人」）；to fritter 是不定式作定语修饰 money。

Sentence 4第 4 句 (begins开头 “But exactly how …”)

  • go about plus a gerund: set about doing it. about here is a preposition.go about + 动名词：着手做（about 是介词）。
  • exactly sharpens the question word: how, precisely.exactly 修饰疑问词 how，「到底怎样」。

Sentence 7第 7 句 (begins开头 “-- even if …”)

  • even if: supposing that you do.even if：即使（假设的让步）。
  • The preposition has four objects, three of them nouns and the fourth a what-clause: a noun clause can stand in a list with nouns.about 的宾语是四个并列成分，前三个是名词，最后一个是 what 从句——名词性从句可以和名词并列。

Sentence 10第 10 句 (begins开头 “would have suggested …”)

  • would have suggested: the condition is unstated but understood - if you had put the question to them, not one of them would have said it.would have suggested：虚拟语气，「（假如你去问了）他们谁也不会建议……」，暗含条件 if you had asked them。
  • suggest doing: suggest takes a gerund, never an infinitive.suggest doing：建议做某事（suggest 后接动名词，不接不定式）。
  • sink money into something: pour it in. The bracket is the joke: never mind all of it, perhaps not a penny.sink money into sth：把钱砸进……；括号里 or perhaps any 是幽默：别说全部，可能一分都不该投。

Sentence 16第 16 句 (begins开头 “So preserving your …”)

  • Two gerund phrases joined as subject, so the verb is plural: these are two separate things to do.两个动名词短语并列作主语，指两件事，谓语用复数 are。

Sentence 20第 20 句 (begins开头 “you may decide …”)

  • but only if: and not unless.but only if：但前提是……（只在……条件下）。
  • be blessed with: be lucky enough to have.be blessed with：有幸具有。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

Giving advice with conditionals: if ..., you should / may / might / you'd probably ...用条件句给建议：if … , you should / may / might / you'd probably …
To advise somebody, use an if-clause to set out their situation, and then pick the modal that carries the right strength: should (you ought to) is the strongest, then you'd probably (most people in your position would), then may (you could consider it), then might (you may as well).
给建议时，用 if 从句设定读者的情况，主句用不同的情态动词控制语气：should（应该）＞ you'd probably（多半会）＞ may（可以考虑）＞ might（不妨）。

All of these are real conditions - the reader may well be in that situation - so the if-clause takes the present simple and there is nothing subjunctive about it.

这些 if 从句都是真实条件（读者可能就是这种情况），从句用一般现在时，不是虚拟。

  • If you feel tired, you should take a break.
  • If you have time, you might visit the museum.
Gerunds as subject, and suggest / go about plus doing动名词作主语与 suggest / go about + doing
A gerund or gerund phrase can be the subject of a sentence, naming an action or an activity; when two of them are joined by and, they are two things and the verb is plural. A number of verbs and phrases take only a gerund after them.
动名词（短语）可以作主语，表示一件事或一种行为；两个动名词由 and 连接表示两件事时谓语用复数。另有一批动词和短语后只接动名词。

A single gerund subject takes a singular verb: Spreading your risk is the golden rule.

单个动名词主语用单数：Spreading your risk is the golden rule.

The gerunds after verbs in this lesson: the one after would have suggested, and the one after go about. Compare It is no use doing, Lesson 46.

本课后接动名词的：would have suggested sinking；go about achieving；L46 It is no use doing。

Verbs that take only a gerund: suggest, avoid, enjoy, finish, mind, consider, risk, keep, admit, deny.

常见只接动名词的动词：suggest, avoid, enjoy, finish, mind, consider, risk, keep, admit, deny。

  • Swimming is good exercise.
  • She suggested going by train.（✗ suggested to go）
even if against even thougheven if 与 even though
even if supposes something that may or may not be so; even though states something that is so.
even if ＝「即使（假设可能发生或不知是否属实）」；even though ＝「虽然、尽管（说的是事实）」。

Compare: Even though he gave them all the information, they gave him five different answers - here he did give it.

对比：Even though he gave them all the information, they gave him five different answers.（事实：他确实给了）

Neither is used with but, and an even if clause can refer to the future while staying in the present simple.

两者都不和 but 连用；even if 从句可以表将来，用一般现在时。

  • I'll go even if it rains.（下不下雨还不知道）
  • She went out even though it was raining.（确实在下雨）

Summary writing摘要写作

In no more than 80 words, write a paragraph of advice: how should a serious investor plan a portfolio, and how should an older investor's differ from a younger one's?

用不超过 80 个词写一段投资建议：认真的投资者应怎样规划股票投资组合？年长和年轻的投资者有何不同？

Points a good summary covers好摘要要覆盖的要点
  • A serious investor does not gamble on shares but holds a planned portfolio with stated aims; there is no one right way to build it, though there are clearly wrong ones.认真的投资者不要赌博式炒股，而要有结构、有目标的投资组合；没有唯一正确的方法，但有明显错误的方法。
  • Deal with the mortgage, the pension, the insurance and the cash reserves first, and only then set aims to suit the circumstances and the temperament.先处理好按揭、养老金、保险和现金储备，再根据个人情况和心理定目标。
  • An older investor puts keeping the capital and raising the income first, which points to low-risk shares, government stock and deposits.年长者以保本和增加收入为主，选低风险股票、国债、存款等。
  • Somebody young, financially secure and cheerful by nature can be bolder and add growth shares; the golden rule is still to spread the risk.年轻、财务稳健、心态乐观者可以更进取，加入成长股；黄金法则是分散风险。
Our model summary本站范文

Serious investors should avoid get-rich-quick gambles and build a planned portfolio with clear aims. There is no single right portfolio, though there are clearly wrong ones. After securing mortgages, pensions, insurance and cash reserves, investors should set goals that suit their circumstances and temperament. Older people should protect capital and seek income through low-risk investments, while younger, confident investors may add some risky growth shares. Above all, spread your risk.（70 词）
