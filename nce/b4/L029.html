Lesson 29 · The Hovercraft 气垫船

📘 New Concept English · Book 4 · Lesson 29 “The Hovercraft”新概念英语 第四册 Lesson 29《The Hovercraft》

C1🧠 21 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
A machine that counts neither as a boat nor as an aircraft: it is held up by a layer of air one or two feet deep and 'flies' just above water or ground. The lesson is laid out in the standard order of technical exposition - invention, then principle, then trial, then prospects. Two things to watch in the language. First, how the writer places a new thing by using phrases like something in between and no more than. Second, the string of can / could / may well at the end, which grades the possibilities from the ones already in hand to the ones furthest off.
一种既不算船、也不算飞机的交通工具：靠一层一两英尺厚的空气垫托起，贴着水面或地面「飞」。本课按发明 → 原理 → 试验 → 应用前景的顺序写，是科技说明文的标准骨架。语言上注意两点：一是作者怎样用 something in between、no more than 这类短语给新事物「定位」；二是结尾一连串 can / could / may well 表示由近到远的可能性。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. Why has it been so hard to decide whether this machine belongs with ships, with aircraft or with land vehicles?为什么人们很难决定气垫船该归入船、飞机还是陆地车辆？
  2. What problem of ordinary ships was Cockerell trying to solve, and what was his answer?科克雷尔想解决的是普通船的什么问题？他的办法是什么？
  3. Why can this machine fly and yet not fly any higher?气垫船为什么「能飞却不能飞得更高」？

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

The real chronology is a little later than the lesson's. Cockerell, a radio engineer who had moved to boat-hiring on the Norfolk Broads, tested the principle in 1955 with a cat-food tin set inside a coffee tin and air blown through the gap - accounts differ as to whether the air came from a hair dryer or a vacuum cleaner, and he weighed the lift on kitchen scales. The idea was then classified by the British government and stayed secret until 1958, when similar work abroad made the secrecy pointless; only after that was he introduced to the National Research Development Corporation, which paid for the patent rights and ordered a full-size craft from Saunders-Roe on the Isle of Wight. So the lesson's date of 1953 and its picture of an inventor taking his idea to government and industry compress three years of waiting and a period under official secrecy into one sentence. The trial the writer calls a sensation was genuinely one: the machine crossed the Channel on 25 July 1959, the fiftieth anniversary, chosen deliberately, of the first flight across it by aeroplane.

真实的时间线比课文稍晚。科克雷尔是无线电工程师，后来到诺福克水乡做船艇租赁；1955 年他用一个猫粮罐套在咖啡罐里、从缝隙吹气来验证原理——吹风机还是吸尘器各源说法不一，他用厨房秤称升力。随后这个想法被英国政府列为机密，一直保密到 1958 年，因为国外出现类似研究，保密已无意义；此后他才被介绍给国家研究开发公司，由后者买下专利权，并向怀特岛的桑德斯-罗公司订造全尺寸船。所以课文里「1953 年」以及「发明人向政府和工业界提出设想」的画面，把三年的等待和一段官方保密压缩成了一句话。作者称为「轰动」的那次试验确实是轰动：那台机器在 1959 年 7 月 25 日横渡英吉利海峡，而这一天是刻意选的——首次飞机飞越海峡的五十周年。

✨ How it is written赏析

This is how to explain a machine in one page. The order never varies: who thought of it, what the idea was, what problem it solves, how the solution works, what happened when it was tried, what it might be used for. Notice that the principle gets two sentences and no formulas, and that the only number in the explanation is the thickness of the air - one or two feet - which is the number that makes the thing surprising. A reader remembers one figure, so choose it. Notice next how often the writer reaches for quotation marks: a pad of air, a machine that 'flies', a flying fruit-bowl, a hovertrain. Each pair of marks does the same job - borrowing a familiar word for something that has no word yet - and together they tell you that the language had not caught up with the invention. The best single device, though, is the placing of the new thing between two old ones: neither ship nor aircraft, but something in between. When you have to explain anything unfamiliar, name the two familiar things it sits between and the reader is oriented in one sentence. The ending, with its three graded modals and its dash, is the period's house style for technology - and it is also where the writing is weakest, because enthusiasm is not evidence.

这就是在一页之内解释一台机器的做法。顺序从不乱：谁想出来的、设想是什么、解决什么问题、办法怎样运作、试验结果如何、可以用在哪里。注意原理只占两句，没有公式；而解释里唯一的数字是气垫的厚度——一两英尺——这正是让人吃惊的那个数字。读者只记得住一个数，所以要挑对。再注意作者多么频繁地伸手去取引号：空气的「垫」、会「飞」的机器、「会飞的果篮」、「气垫火车」。每一对引号都在干同一件事——为还没有名字的东西借一个熟悉的词；合起来它们告诉你：语言还没追上这项发明。不过最好的一招是把新东西放在两样旧东西之间：既不是船也不是飞机，而是介于其间。你要解释任何陌生事物时，说出它夹在哪两样熟悉的东西之间，一句话就能让读者定位。结尾那三级情态动词加破折号，是那个年代写科技的通用腔调——也是这篇文章最弱的地方，因为热情不等于证据。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The page ends with three predictions, and sixty years is long enough to mark them. Regular services did appear and then nearly disappeared. The large car-carrying craft worked the Channel for decades and made their last crossing in 2000, beaten by fuel costs, by the tunnel and by fast catamarans; what survives is a ten-minute passenger run between Portsmouth and the Isle of Wight, which its operator describes as the only year-round scheduled passenger hovercraft service left in the world. The air-cushion liner across the Atlantic never came, for the reason the writer does not mention: a cushion has to be paid for continuously, since the engines carry the weight of the craft every second it is up, and that makes the machine thirsty and loud. The third prediction is the interesting one. A train that touches nothing and runs at the speed he names did arrive - but held up by magnets, not by air. The line in Shanghai opened to passengers on 1 January 2004 and ran commercially at 431 km per hour, which is close to his figure in miles; a Japanese test train reached 603 km per hour in 2015. And then the honest twist: the Shanghai service cut its cruising speed to 300 km per hour in 2021. So the function he foresaw was right, the mechanism was wrong, and even the function has pulled back. That is the lesson worth taking from this page: a forecast that names a function ages better than a forecast that names a machine, and both should be written with the confidence graded, as he grades his, so that a later reader can tell which part you were sure of.

这一页以三个预言结尾，而六十年足够给它们打分。定期航线确实出现过，然后几乎消失：运汽车的大型气垫船在英吉利海峡干了几十年，2000 年作了最后一次横渡，被燃料成本、海底隧道和高速双体船打败；留下来的是朴次茅斯到怀特岛的十分钟客运航线，运营方称它是世界上仅存的全年定期客运气垫船航线。横渡大西洋的气垫班轮从未出现，原因是作者没有提的那一条：气垫得一直花钱买——只要船浮着，发动机每一秒都在承担整船的重量，这让它既耗油又吵。第三个预言才有意思：一种什么都不接触、速度正如他所说的列车确实来了——但托住它的是磁，不是空气。上海那条线 2004 年 1 月 1 日载客通车，商业运行速度达每小时 431 公里，换成英里与他给的数字很接近；日本的试验列车 2015 年跑到每小时 603 公里。然后是如实的转折：上海那条线 2021 年把巡航速度降到每小时 300 公里。所以他预见的功能对了，机制错了，而连功能也往回收了一步。这一页值得带走的教训正是这个：点明「功能」的预测比点明「机器」的预测更耐老；而两者都该像他那样把把握程度分级写出来，好让后来的读者看得出你当初对哪一部分有底。

💬 Discuss and write讨论与写作
  1. Pick one machine you use every day and describe it in three sentences to someone who has never seen it, naming the two familiar things it sits between.
    挑一件你每天都用的机器，用三句话向没见过它的人描述，并说出它夹在哪两样熟悉的东西之间。
  2. Write 120 words predicting one change in transport in your city over the next twenty years. Grade your confidence as the writer does - what can happen, what could happen, what may well happen.
    写 120 词，预测你所在城市未来二十年交通上的一项变化。像作者那样把把握程度分级：什么现在就能做到、什么或许可能、什么很可能发生。
  3. The air-cushion train lost to the magnetic one. When two technologies promise the same thing, what decides which one survives?
    气垫火车输给了磁悬浮。当两种技术承诺同一件事时，决定谁活下来的是什么？

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 2第 2 句 (begins开头 “the strangest of …”)

  • After the comma comes an absolute construction: the strangest of them brings its own subject and takes being, which is the same as saying and the strangest of them is perhaps this one.逗号后是独立主格：the strangest of them（自带主语）+ being …，= and the strangest of them is perhaps the hovercraft。
  • means has the same form in both numbers; with many it is plural here.means 单复数同形，这里与 many 连用是复数。

Sentence 7第 7 句 (begins开头 “Ever since, people …”)

  • ever since: from that time until now, which is why the verb is in the present perfect.ever since：从那以后一直，与现在完成时 have had 连用。
  • have difficulty (in) doing something: the in may be left out, but the -ing form stays.have difficulty (in) doing：做某事有困难，in 可省。

Sentence 10第 10 句 (begins开头 “His answer was …”)

  • The complement is an infinitive (the subject is answer, meaning the way out), built like the only way is to do in Lesson 1.表语用不定式 to lift（主语是 answer，表「办法」，与 L1 的 the only way is to do 同构）。
  • by making it ride: by + doing for the means; after make the infinitive has no to.by making it ride：by + doing 表手段；make sth do 不带 to。

Sentence 12第 12 句 (begins开头 “It 'flies', therefore, …”)

  • The verb is in quotation marks because this is not flight in the proper sense; the writer is borrowing the word.'flies' 加引号：不是真正的飞，只是作者借用。
  • water or ground: in apposition to the surface.water or ground：the surface 的同位语。
  • over which it rides: preposition + which, from ride over a surface.over which it rides：介词 + which 引导定语从句（ride over the surface）。

Sentence 20第 20 句 (begins开头 “and the railway …”)

  • may well: very probably - a good deal more confident than may alone.may well：「很可能」，比 may 更有把握。
  • riding on its air cushion ...: a present participle phrase describing the train.riding on its air cushion …：现在分词短语作后置定语/补充说明 hovertrain。
  • which it never touches: a non-defining relative clause about the single rail.which it never touches：非限制性定语从句修饰 a single rail。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

Degrees of guessing: can, could, may well, might推测的分寸：can, could, may well, might
Ranked by confidence: will (certain) > may well (very probably) > may (possibly) > could / might (just possibly); can states an ability or a general possibility.
情态动词按把握程度排列：will（肯定）＞ may well（很可能）＞ may（可能）＞ could / might（或许可能，把握最小）；can 讲「具有某种能力/一般可能性」。

may well do has a second sense: you have every reason to - You may well be surprised. Keep it apart from may as well do, which means there is nothing better to do.

may well do 还有一个意思：「完全有理由……」：You may well be surprised.（你感到吃惊是很自然的）；而 may as well do ＝「不妨」。

For guesses about the past use may/might/could + have done (see Lesson 1); here the subject is the present and the future.

推测过去用 may/might/could + have done（L1 已讲），这里集中讲对现在和将来的推测。

  • Prices may well rise again next year.
  • Take an umbrella — it might rain.
  • Smoking can cause cancer.（一般性的可能）
have difficulty (in) doing, and gerunds after prepositionshave difficulty (in) doing 及动名词作介词宾语
A verb after a preposition always takes the -ing form; in have difficulty / trouble / a problem (in) doing the in is often dropped, but the -ing stays.
介词后面接动词，一律用动名词（-ing）；have difficulty/trouble/a problem (in) doing 中 in 常省，但动词仍用 -ing。

Common partners: be good at doing, insist on doing, look forward to doing (to is a preposition here), be used to doing (be accustomed to), object to doing.

常见搭配：be good at doing、insist on doing、look forward to doing（to 是介词！）、be used to doing（习惯于）、object to doing。

Easily got wrong: look forward to seeing you (not to see); I'm used to getting up early (not to get up).

易错：look forward to seeing you（✗ to see）；I'm used to getting up early（✗ to get up）。

  • She had no trouble finding the house.
  • We are looking forward to hearing from you.
no more than / not more than / no less than
no more than = only (it sounds few); not more than = at most (a plain upper limit); no less than = as much or as many as (it sounds a lot).
no more than ＝ only（嫌少）；not more than ＝ at most（客观上限）；no less than ＝ as much/many as（嫌多、强调多）。

Compare: He has no more than ten dollars (only ten, and that is little) with He has not more than ten dollars (ten at the most, perhaps fewer).

对比：He has no more than ten dollars.（只有 10 美元，嫌少）／ He has not more than ten dollars.（最多 10 美元，可能更少）。

No less than fifty people came: a full fifty, more than expected.

no less than 50 people came. 足足有 50 人来了（嫌多、惊讶）。

  • The journey took no more than an hour. 路上只花了一个小时。
  • No less than 500 people attended. 竟有 500 人参加。

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • … the strangest of them being … ……其中最……的大概是……
    We visited many cities, the most beautiful of them being Kyoto.
  • have difficulty (in) deciding whether … 难以决定是否……
    I had difficulty deciding whether to accept the job.
  • something in between A and B 介于 A 和 B 之间的东西
    A tablet is something in between a phone and a laptop.
  • … may well be … ……很可能是……
    The future of cars may well be electric.

用不超过 80 个词介绍气垫船：谁发明的、原理是什么、试验结果如何、有哪些用途和前景。

Points a good summary covers好摘要要覆盖的要点
  • In 1953 Cockerell put forward the idea of holding a craft up on a pad of low-pressure air.1953 年科克雷尔提出用低压空气垫托起船体的设想。
  • The aim was to cut the drag of the waves by lifting the hull clear of the water on a layer of air one or two feet deep.目的是减少波浪阻力，让船离开水面，贴着一两英尺厚的气垫行驶。
  • The first trials in 1959 made a great stir, and the machine later went across the Channel.1959 年首次试验引起轰动，后来横渡了英吉利海峡。
  • It suits large regions with poor roads, and it might one day cross the Atlantic or run on a rail as an air-cushion train.适合交通不便的大地区；未来可能横跨大西洋，或发展为气垫火车。
Our model summary本站范文

In 1953 Christopher Cockerell proposed a craft supported on a cushion of air. To overcome the wave resistance that slows ships down, he lifted the vessel just above the surface, so that it rides on air pumped through jets underneath. The first trials in 1959 caused a sensation, and the craft later crossed the Channel. Hovercraft are useful where transport is poor, and they may one day cross the Atlantic or become high-speed trains.（74 词）
