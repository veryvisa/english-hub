🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

This is a despatch from one short-lived scientific fashion. In the early 1960s the Soviet press carried a run of reports on what was called skin sight or dermo-optical perception: people, almost always young women, who could supposedly name colours and read print through the skin of the fingers. The case that travelled furthest was Rosa Kuleshova, a young woman from the Urals, who was written up in the Soviet press, examined by Soviet psychologists, and profiled by Life magazine in 1964. Vera Petrova, the girl in this lesson, belongs to the same wave. The story reached British readers through the BBC, and that matters: in 1963 a radio despatch from Moscow was almost the only channel through which a Western audience heard what Soviet laboratories were doing, and the correspondent was in no position to run the tests again himself.

这是一场短命的科学风潮留下的一篇通讯。1960 年代初，苏联报刊连续报道所谓「皮肤视觉」（dermo-optical perception）：有人——几乎都是年轻女性——据称能用手指的皮肤辨色、读字。传得最远的案例是乌拉尔地区的年轻女子罗莎·库列绍娃（Rosa Kuleshova），苏联报刊写过她，苏联心理学家检验过她，1964 年《生活》（Life）杂志为她做过特写。本课里的女孩薇拉·彼得罗娃属于同一波。这个故事是经 BBC 传到英国读者那里的，这一点很要紧：1963 年，一则来自莫斯科的广播通讯几乎是西方读者了解苏联实验室动向的唯一渠道，而记者本人根本没有条件把那些测试重做一遍。

✨ How it is written赏析

Read this as a lesson in how the passive voice distributes responsibility. Count the agents: her father was the first to notice, a special commission put her through tests, and then it was also found that. Almost every important verb is passive, and in most of them the person doing the finding has vanished. The effect is to make the findings look as though they belong to nobody — and therefore to everybody. That is exactly why the style feels authoritative, and exactly why it is worth being suspicious of. A reporter writing it was found that is not claiming the finding; he is passing it on while standing to one side. Notice also the ordering of evidence. The homely detail comes first (a child's hands on a safe door, old newspapers in bundles), because a small specific picture is what makes a reader willing to believe the laboratory part that follows. Then come the official nouns: institute, commission, ministry. Each one is a borrowed guarantee, and none of them is a test the reader can check.

把这篇当作一堂关于被动语态如何分配责任的课来读。数一下施动者：这项能力「最先被她父亲注意到」，她「被一个专门委员会施以一系列测试」，「还被发现」。几乎每个关键动词都是被动，而其中多数句子里，做出判断的那个人消失了。效果是：这些结论看上去不属于任何人——因而属于所有人。这正是这种文体读起来权威的原因，也正是它值得怀疑的原因。一个记者写下「据发现」时，他并没有认领这个发现，他只是在转述，同时站到一边。还要注意证据的排序。家常细节放在最前（一个孩子的手搭在保险柜门上、捆成一捆捆的旧报纸），因为一个具体的小画面才能让读者愿意相信后面的实验室部分。接着才是官方名词：研究所、委员会、卫生部。每一个都是借来的担保，而没有一个是读者能自己验证的测试。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The claim did not survive. Blindfolds leak. In 1966 Martin Gardner argued in Science that the whole class of skin-sight performances rested on a conjuring technique old enough to have a name, peeping down the side of the nose, which blindfolds of the usual kind do nothing to prevent. Controlled Soviet retests reported in Nature in 1970 found that Kuleshova had been caught peeping, and that once full precautions were in place her scores collapsed. No laboratory has since demonstrated colour or print recognition through the skin. Read the lesson again with that in hand and the giveaway is sitting in plain sight in the last two sentences: the ability worked only while her eyes stayed covered, and stopped as soon as her fingers got damp. The report presents these as curious properties of a gift. They are better read as the conditions under which the trick works and the conditions under which it does not — a blindfold to peep past, and dry fingers, because a damp fingertip is no use for feeling the edge of a card or for sliding a cloth. The useful habit is not to decide in advance whether skin sight is possible, but to ask of any claimed ability: which of its stated limits protect the claim rather than describe it? That one question would have saved a great deal of laboratory time, and it costs nothing to apply to the next marvel you read about.

这个说法没有活下来。眼罩是漏的。1966 年，马丁·加德纳在《科学》上论证：整类「皮肤视觉」表演靠的是一种老到有专名的戏法——从鼻梁侧面往下偷看，而常见的眼罩完全防不住。1970 年《自然》报道的苏联对照复测发现，库列绍娃「被当场抓到偷看」，一旦防护做足，她的成绩就崩了。此后没有任何实验室能重现用皮肤辨色或读字。带着这些再读一遍课文，破绽就明摆在最后两句里：这种能力只在蒙眼时有效，手一湿就停止。报道把这两点当作天赋的奇异属性来呈现。更合理的读法是：它们一个是戏法成立的条件，一个是戏法失效的条件——需要一个能偷看出去的眼罩，还需要干的手指，因为湿指尖摸不出卡片边缘，也不好把布滑开。有用的习惯不是预先判定皮肤视觉可不可能，而是对任何声称的异能都问一句：它自己列出的限制条件里，哪些是在保护这个说法，而不是在描述它？这一个问题本可以省下大量实验室时间，而且你下次读到新的奇闻时，照样可以免费用上。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 1第 1 句 (begins开头 “Several cases have …”)

  • The present perfect passive, which goes naturally with recently.现在完成时被动语态，配合 recently。
  • The modifier is split off from its noun: cases ... of people, with in Russia recently pushed in between. English moves a long modifier behind the verb so the sentence is not top-heavy.定语被拆开：cases … of people，中间插入了 in Russia recently——英语为避免「头重脚轻」，把长修饰语放到谓语后面。

  • Two relative clauses opening with who are joined by but; both are non-defining, and both describe Vera.两个 who 引导的非限制性定语从句由 but 并列，都修饰 Vera。
  • In front of a noun, 11-year-old takes hyphens and keeps year singular.11-year-old 作前置定语时连字符连接，year 用单数。

Sentence 8第 8 句 (begins开头 “Vera's curious talent …”)

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

The passive in reporting: It is reported / was found that ...报道体的被动语态：It is reported / was found that…
News writing and research reports often leave out who did the thing and use the passive to put the fact first. Findings commonly take the form It + be + past participle + that clause, or subject + be + past participle + to-infinitive.
新闻与研究报告常把「谁做的」隐去，用被动语态突出事实；结论常用 It + be + 过去分词 + that 从句，或「主语 + be + 过去分词 + to do」。

The two forms are interchangeable: It is said that he is ill. = He is said to be ill. If the reported action came earlier, use the perfect infinitive: It is said that he was ill. = He is said to have been ill.

两种等价句式：It is said that he is ill. = He is said to be ill. 若从句动作在前，用完成式：It is said that he was ill. = He is said to have been ill.

  • It is believed that the painting is a fake. = The painting is believed to be a fake.
  • She was given a medal. / A medal was given to her.（双宾语动词的两种被动）
Noun phrases used as conjunctions: the moment / the instant / every time名词短语作连词：the moment / the instant / every time
the moment, the minute, the instant, immediately and directly can all work as conjunctions meaning as soon as; every time and each time mean 'whenever'.
the moment, the minute, the instant, immediately, directly 都可当连词用，＝ as soon as「一……就……」；every time / each time ＝「每当」。

In the clause, the simple present carries future meaning: I'll call you the moment I arrive (not will arrive).

从句里用一般现在时表将来：I'll call you the moment I arrive.（不用 will arrive）

  • The minute I saw him, I knew something was wrong.
  • Every time I visit her, she cooks for me.
Shortened adverbial clauses: when / while / if / though + participle or adjective状语从句的省略：when / while / if / though + 分词或形容词
When the clause has the same subject as the main clause and contains a form of be, you can leave out both the subject and be, keeping only the conjunction with a participle, an adjective or a prepositional phrase.
当状语从句主语与主句主语相同、且从句里有 be 动词时，可同时省略主语和 be，只留连词 + 分词/形容词/介词短语。

In this lesson: except when (she was) blindfolded she lacked the ability ...

本课：except when (she was) blindfolded she lacked the ability…

Common cases: when (it is) necessary / if (it is) possible / while (I was) walking / though (he was) tired.

常见：when (it is) necessary / if (it is) possible / while (I was) walking / though (he was) tired。

Careful: you cannot shorten the clause when the subjects differ. When finished, the teacher collected the papers leaves the reader asking who finished.

注意：主语不同时不能省（× When finished, the teacher collected the papers.——是谁完成？）

  • When asked about the money, he said nothing.
  • Though tired, she kept working.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • Several cases have been reported of people who … 已有报道称出现了若干……的案例
    Several cases have been reported of patients who recovered without treatment.
  • be able to do …, and, stranger still, … 能……，更奇怪的是还能……
    He could play the piano at four and, stranger still, compose music.
  • except when … 除了在……的时候
    I never drink coffee except when I'm working late.
  • … ceased the moment … ……一……就停止了
    The noise ceased the moment the teacher walked in.

  • only when blindfolded; ability ceased when her hands were wet

Summary writing摘要写作

In no more than 80 words, report Vera's unusual ability: how it came to light, what was tested, and what limited it. Keep the tone of a news report and use the passive at least twice.

用不超过 80 个词报道薇拉的特殊能力：怎样被发现、测试了什么、有什么限制。用报道语气，至少用两处被动语态。

Points a good summary covers好摘要要覆盖的要点
  • Her father found it first: with her hands on a locked safe she said there were old newspapers tied up inside.她父亲先发现：她摸着锁着的保险柜，说出里面捆着旧报纸。
  • A research institute and a commission from the Ministry of Health then tested her.一个科研所和卫生部的委员会对她进行了测试。
  • She read a newspaper through a screen light could not pass through, and named shapes and colours with her elbow, her foot, her knees and her shoulders.她能隔着不透明屏幕读报，用肘部、脚、膝、肩辨认图形和颜色。
  • The ability worked only while her eyes were covered, and it went the moment her hands got wet.只有蒙眼时她才有这种能力，手一湿能力就消失。
