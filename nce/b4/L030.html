Lesson 30 · Exploring the Sea-Floor 海底探险

📘 New Concept English · Book 4 · Lesson 30 “Exploring the Sea-Floor”新概念英语 第四册 Lesson 30《Exploring the Sea-Floor》

C1🧠 25 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
A hundred years ago people knew how the surface of the sea was shaped and where the rocks lay close inshore, and most of them simply supposed the bottom was flat. From the first deep sounding in 1839, through the systematic measurements of 1869 and the Challenger expedition, to the companies laying cables across the ocean, the truth about the shape of the sea floor came out a step at a time: the continents turn out to be tablelands sitting on top of the sea bed, with the shelf and then the slope falling away from the shore. This is geographical exposition - long sentences, many technical terms - so it is the right place to practise finding the frame of a sentence first and hanging the modifiers on afterwards.
一百年前，人们只知道海面的形状和近岸浅水的暗礁，至于海底，大多数人想当然地以为它是平的。从 1839 年第一次深海测深、1869 年「豪猪」号系统测量，到「挑战者」号考察和铺设海底电缆，海底地形的真相一步步揭开：大陆其实是「叠」在海床上的高原，大陆架、大陆坡层层下降。本课是地理说明文，长句多、术语多，正好练习「先找主干、再挂修饰」。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. To what two things was our knowledge of the oceans limited a hundred years ago?一百年前人们对海洋的认识局限在哪两方面？
  2. What made research on the ocean bed move so quickly after 1869?是什么让海底研究在 1869 年以后迅速发展？
  3. Where does the real boundary between the continents and the oceans lie?大陆与海洋真正的分界线在哪里？

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

The writer was one of the men doing the work he describes. Thomas Frohock Gaskell (1916-1995) was a British geophysicist who sailed on the 1950-52 voyage of the second ship to carry the name Challenger, where his team made seismic measurements of the rock under the ocean and sounded the deepest place yet measured, in the trench that now bears the ship's name; he published a book about those voyages in 1960. Before the voyage he had been chief petroleum physicist for the company that became British Petroleum, and he went back to its exploration department afterwards. That is worth knowing when you read his paragraphs on the continental shelf, because the shelf is where the oil is: the writer is a working geophysicist explaining the shape of the sea floor to the general reader, and the part of it that mattered most to his employer is the part he describes in most detail.

作者是他所描述的那项工作的参与者之一。托马斯·弗罗霍克·加斯克尔（1916–1995）是英国地球物理学家，参加了 1950–52 年第二代「挑战者」号的航行；他的小组用地震法测量海底岩层，并测出了当时已知最深处——今天那条海沟以这艘船命名；1960 年他出版了一本关于这些航行的书。出海之前，他是后来成为英国石油公司的那家公司的首席石油物理学家，航行结束后又回到其勘探部门。读他讲大陆架的那几段时，这一点值得记住：石油就在大陆架上。一位在职的地球物理学家向普通读者解释海底的形状，而他写得最细的那一部分，恰好是他雇主最在意的那一部分。

✨ How it is written赏析

The page is organised as a history and then a picture, and the history is doing a particular job: it starts with what people wrongly assumed, so that every measurement afterwards reads as a correction. If you want a reader to feel that knowledge has been won, begin with the error. Then watch how the second half builds a landscape out of comparisons rather than figures. The continents become tablelands standing nearly three miles up; the shelf becomes a gentle slope running out from the shore; the real frontier is not a line on the surface but the foot of a steeper drop. Those are all ordinary words, and they do more work here than the measurements in fathoms, which a modern reader cannot feel at all. Two details of technique. The writer puts the place first and the verb before its subject when he comes to the shelf, which makes the reader's eye travel outward from the shore in the order the sentence unfolds. And he grades his certainty honestly: features are probably vertical, the long tail at the foot of a slope almost certainly comes from material washed off the land. Keeping probably and almost certainly in their right places is what separates a scientist writing for the public from an enthusiast.

这一页先讲历史、再画图景，而那段历史在干一件特定的事：它从「人们错以为什么」开始，于是此后每一次测量读起来都是一次纠正。想让读者感到知识是挣来的，就从错误讲起。再看后半篇怎样用比较而不是数字搭出一片地貌：大陆成了高出海底近三英里的高原；大陆架成了从岸边缓缓伸出的斜坡；真正的边界不是海面上的一条线，而是一道更陡的坡脚。这些全是普通词，在这里比用英寻写的测量数字管用得多——后者现代读者根本没有感觉。另有两处技术：写到大陆架时，作者把地点放在句首、谓语放在主语之前，于是读者的目光随句子展开而从岸边往外走；而他对确定程度的处理很诚实：崖壁「大概」是垂直的，坡脚的那段长长的收尾「几乎肯定」来自大陆被侵蚀后冲到深水里的物质。把「大概」和「几乎肯定」各放在该放的位置，这正是为公众写作的科学家与爱好者的区别。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

Everything here is still true, and the piece is now a snapshot of the moment just before the big idea arrived. Gaskell describes the shape of the Atlantic floor without the explanation, because the explanation was still being fought over: Marie Tharp, working from soundings at Columbia, became convinced in 1952 that a rift valley ran down the middle of the ridge, Bruce Heezen at first dismissed her reading as girl talk, and the physiographic map that made the floor visible to everyone appeared in 1957 - while her name was left off several of the key papers between 1959 and 1963. Within a few more years the rift and the magnetic stripes beside it had become the evidence for sea-floor spreading, and the sea bed stopped being a shape and became a process. Two of his numbers are also worth converting. He writes in fathoms, and his 2,500-3,500 fathoms is roughly 4,600 to 6,400 metres; the deepest sounding he and his shipmates took in 1951 is in the trench now named after their ship, and the present figures for that deep run between about 10,900 and 11,000 metres depending on the survey. A Chinese submersible reached 10,909 metres there in November 2020, which is the same place measured in a different way. Finally, the mapping he calls enough has turned out to be a beginning: the international Seabed 2030 project now reports that something like 28 to 29 per cent of the sea floor has been surveyed to modern resolution, after five million square kilometres were added in one recent year. The flat sea bed of the Victorians is gone, but most of the real one has still not been seen properly.

这里讲的一切至今成立，而这篇文章现在成了「大想法到来前一刻」的快照。加斯克尔描述了大西洋海底的形状，却没有给出解释，因为解释当时仍在争论之中：玛丽·撒普在哥伦比亚大学据测深数据作图，1952 年确信洋中脊正中有一道裂谷；布鲁斯·希森起初把她的判读贬为「女人的闲话」；让海底第一次被所有人看见的地貌图在 1957 年问世——而 1959 至 1963 年间几篇关键论文没有署她的名字。再过几年，这道裂谷和它两侧的磁条带成了海底扩张的证据，海底从此不再只是一个形状，而成了一个过程。他的两个数字也值得换算：他用英寻写，2500–3500 英寻约合 4600 至 6400 米；而他和同船者 1951 年测到的最深处，就在今天以那条船命名的海沟里，如今各次测量给出的数值在约 10900 至 11000 米之间。2020 年 11 月，一艘中国载人潜水器在那里下到 10909 米——同一个地方，另一种测法。最后，他称之为「足够」的测绘，后来证明只是开始：国际「海床 2030」项目报告说，按现代分辨率完成测量的海底约占 28% 至 29%，而其中有近五百万平方公里是最近一年里新增的。维多利亚时代那片平坦的海底已经消失，但真实的那一片，大部分还没有被好好看过。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

Three ways of saying not untilnot until 的三种写法
English has three shapes for it: a negative main clause with until; the cleft It was not until ... that ...; and Not until at the head with partial inversion in the main clause.
「直到……才……」在英语里有三种形式：主句否定 + until；强调句 It was not until … that …；Not until 放句首 + 主句部分倒装。

3 Inverted: Not until 1869 was a series of deep soundings obtained (partial inversion in the main clause; the not until part itself is not inverted).

3 倒装句：Not until 1869 was a series of deep soundings obtained.（主句部分倒装；从句 not until … 本身不倒装）

The commonest mistakes: putting a second negative into the that-clause of the cleft, or inverting inside the cleft.

最常见错误：在强调句的 that 从句里再写否定，或在强调句里倒装。

  • I didn't realize my mistake until he pointed it out.
  • It was not until he pointed it out that I realized my mistake.
  • Not until he pointed it out did I realize my mistake.
The passive infinitive: to be done不定式的被动式：to be done
When the subject of the infinitive is the receiver of the action, use to be done; it is common after enable / allow / order somebody or something to be done, after be likely to be done, and after want something to be done.
不定式的逻辑主语是动作的承受者时，用 to be done；常见于 enable/allow/order sb/sth to be done、be likely to be done、want sth to be done 等。

How to decide: ask whether the noun does the action or has it done to it. A map cannot draw itself, so to be drawn.

判断：问「这个名词是做动作，还是被做」——map 不会自己画，所以 to be drawn。

There is also a continuous passive, to be being done (rare), and a perfect passive, to have been done, for something that happened earlier: He is said to have been promoted.

另有进行被动 to be being done（少见）和完成被动 to have been done（表先于主句的被动）：He is said to have been promoted.

  • The house is going to be sold next month.
  • She asked to be allowed to leave early.
The with-construction (with + object + complement)with 复合结构（with + 宾语 + 补足语）
with + noun + participle, adjective, prepositional phrase or adverb works as an adverbial of attendant circumstance, cause or description, and the noun and the complement stand in a subject-predicate relation.
with + 名词 + 分词/形容词/介词短语/副词，作伴随、原因或描写的状语；名词与补足语之间有逻辑主谓关系。

Choose the complement by the relation: active or ongoing takes -ing (with the engine running); passive or completed takes -ed (with his arms folded); adjectives and prepositional phrases are also possible (with the door open / with a book in her hand).

补足语形式看关系：主动/进行 → -ing（with the engine running）；被动/完成 → -ed（with his arms folded）；also adj./介词短语（with the door open / with a book in her hand）。

How it differs from the absolute construction: where either is possible the sense is much the same, and the with-form is commoner and more conversational.

与独立主格的区别：with 可加可不加时意思基本相同，with 结构更口语、更常用。

  • He fell asleep with the light on.
  • With so many people watching, she felt nervous.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • be confined to … 局限于……
    His interest in sport is confined to watching football on TV.
  • It was not until … that … 直到……才……
    It was not until 1869 that deep soundings were obtained.
  • put sth at sb's disposal 把……交给某人随意使用
    The company put a car at our disposal.
  • enable sth to be done 使某事得以完成
    New software enables the data to be checked in seconds.

Summary writing摘要写作

In no more than 80 words, answer: how did knowledge of the sea floor grow step by step, and what do we know of its shape today?

用不超过 80 个词概括：人们对海底的认识是怎样一步步发展的？今天我们对海底地形知道些什么？

Points a good summary covers好摘要要覆盖的要点
  • A hundred years ago people knew the surface and the dangers of shallow water inshore, and most supposed the bottom was flat.一百年前，人们只知道海面形状和近岸浅水的航行危险，多以为海底是平的。
  • From 1869 depths were measured and samples taken in a systematic way, and the Challenger expedition made the sea floor a proper field of study.1869 年起开始系统测深和取样，「挑战者」号考察使海底研究成为正式学科。
  • Cable-laying confirmed how deep the ocean is and how varied its floor, and there are now enough measurements to draw a relief map of that ocean.铺设海底电缆证实海洋很深、海底地形起伏巨大，现在已能画出大西洋地势图。
  • The continents are tablelands standing on the sea bed; the shelf slopes gently away from the shore, and the real boundary is at the foot of the steeper slope.大陆是叠在海床上的高原；大陆架缓缓下降，真正的分界在大陆坡脚下。
Our model summary本站范文

A century ago people knew only the surface of the sea and the dangers of shallow coastal water, and most assumed the sea-bed was flat. Systematic deep soundings began in 1869, and the Challenger expedition made the sea-floor a serious science. Cable-laying later showed how deep and varied the ocean is. We now see the continents as tablelands resting on the sea floor, sloping gently down to a steeper continental slope that meets the deep ocean.（76 词）
