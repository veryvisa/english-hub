🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
From Man, the Unknown (1935) by Alexis Carrel, a French surgeon and Nobel prize winner. He attacks modern industry for chasing the lowest cost and the largest output while taking no account at all of the body or the mind of the people who do the work; tall buildings go up only for the income each square foot can bring, the streets are narrow and dark, full of fumes and noise, and the people living there settle for comfort without seeing what has been taken away from them. The language is fierce critical argument: the passive voice keeps pushing people into the object slot, and long strings of nouns and participles build up the weight of the city.
选自法国医生、诺贝尔奖得主亚历克西·卡雷尔的《人之奥秘》（Man, the Unknown, 1935）。他批评现代工业只追求最低成本、最大产量，完全不顾工人身心；城市高楼只为每平方英尺收益而建，街道窄暗、废气弥漫、噪声不断，人们却安于舒适，不知道被剥夺了生活的必需。语言是强烈的批判性议论：被动语态突出「人被忽视」，长串名词和分词描绘城市的压迫感。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. What idea is modern industry built on, and what does it leave out of account?现代工业以什么观念为基础？忽视了什么？
  2. What decides the shape and the size of a tall city building?摩天大楼的形状和尺寸取决于什么？
  3. How does the author describe the streets of a modern city?作者怎样描写现代城市的街道？

The book, published in 1935 as L'Homme, cet inconnu, was an international best-seller. We have not checked which edition or page the textbook used.

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

Two things about the writer belong next to this passage. The first is that he was a famous laboratory scientist, which is why the argument comes dressed in words like physiological and descendants: he is not complaining about cities as a resident, he is diagnosing them as a doctor. The second is harder. The book that contains this passage is the same book in which he argues that modern life is letting the weak survive and that societies should act on that, and historians who have read it describe it as a work of eugenics; a few years later a body for the study of human problems was created by decree of the Vichy regime in 1941 with him at its head. After the Liberation he was accused of collaboration, and he died in Paris in November 1944 before any case against him was heard. Nothing in that record makes his description of a street wrong. It does mean that the warm phrase the good of its inhabitants needs a question attached: which inhabitants?

关于作者，有两件事该和这段文字放在一起。第一，他是著名的实验室科学家，所以论证会穿上 physiological、descendants 这类词：他不是以居民身份抱怨城市，而是以医生身份给城市下诊断。第二件更难说。收录本段的那本书，也正是他论述现代生活让弱者存活下来、社会应就此采取行动的那本书；读过它的历史学者称之为一部优生学著作。几年之后，维希政权 1941 年以法令设立了一个研究「人的问题」的机构，由他出任首脑。解放后他被指控通敌，1944 年 11 月在巴黎去世，案件未及审理。这些记录都不会让他对一条街的描写变错。但它确实让「为居民的利益」这句温和的话需要追加一个问题：哪些居民？

✨ How it is written赏析

The paragraph is a lesson in how to make a judgement feel like an observation. Look at who acts in each sentence. The factory, the conception, the necessity and the dimensions all take the subject slot; the workers, the inhabitants and we are all objects, or sit inside a passive, or are simply left out. That grammar is the argument: people here are things that get built around. Then the adjectives arrive in a run, and notice that none of them measures anything. Monstrous, toxic, banal, ceaselessly: each one is a verdict, and the sentence is long enough that four verdicts go past before a reader can object to any single one. It is effective, and it is exactly the trick to watch for in writing that wants your agreement. Ask of any such sentence: which word here could be checked, and which one is a vote?

这一段教的是「怎样让一个判断读起来像一个观察」。看每句话里是谁在动作。工厂、观念、需要、尺寸，都占着主语位置；工人、居民、我们，都是宾语、藏在被动语态里，或者干脆不出现。这个语法本身就是论点：这里的人是被绕着建起来的东西。接着形容词成串涌来，而且留意，它们一个也不测量什么。Monstrous、toxic、banal、ceaselessly：每一个都是裁决，而句子足够长，四个裁决先后掠过，读者还来不及对其中任何一个提出异议。这很有效，也正是那种「想要你点头」的文字里最该警惕的手法。对任何这样的句子都可以问一句：这里哪个词是能核的，哪个词只是一张选票？

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

On the measurable half he was right, and the numbers took another eighty years to arrive. The World Health Organization halved its own guideline for fine particles in 2021, from ten micrograms per cubic metre of air as an annual average down to five, and reported the following year that about ninety-nine per cent of the world's people breathe air above its limits; of the urban population it was able to assess, more than half live where fine particles run at more than six times the guideline, and only around seventeen per cent live where the level is met. So the street he describes is not a period piece. What has changed is the direction of the remedy. He wanted fewer and better people; the planners who inherited his complaint want fewer and better streets, and their tools are tree cover, speed limits, buildings turned to let light down to the pavement, and a shop within a short walk. Same diagnosis, opposite prescription. That contrast is worth holding on to, because a correct description of a problem tells you nothing at all about who is entitled to fix it or how.

可测量的那一半，他说对了，而数字又过了八十年才到。世界卫生组织 2021 年把自己的细颗粒物准则砍掉一半：年均值从每立方米十微克降到五微克；次年报告称全球约 99% 的人呼吸的空气超过其限值；在它能评估的城市人口里，一半以上住在细颗粒物超过准则六倍以上的地方，只有约 17% 住在达标的地方。所以他描写的那条街不是旧照片。变了的是药方的方向。他要的是更少、更好的人；继承了他这份抱怨的规划者要的是更少、更好的街——他们的工具是树冠覆盖、限速、把楼体转个方向让光落到人行道上、以及走几步就有的一家店。诊断相同，药方相反。这个对照值得记住，因为「对问题的描述正确」完全不能告诉你谁有资格去修它、该怎么修。

💬 Discuss and write讨论与写作
  1. Walk one street you use every day and count what the author counts: width, light, air, noise, crowd. Which of the five is worst where you live, and who decided it?
    走一趟你每天都走的街，按作者数的那几项数一遍：宽度、采光、空气、噪声、人流。你住的地方哪一项最差？这是谁定下来的？
  2. Write 120 words describing a place you dislike, using only words that could be checked by somebody else. Then rewrite it in 60 words using four verdict adjectives. Which version would you rather have signed your name to?
    用 120 词写一个你不喜欢的地方，只用别人能核实的词。再用 60 词重写一遍，用四个下裁决的形容词。哪一版你更愿意署名？
  3. The writer and today's city planners agree on the symptoms and disagree on everything else. What does that tell you about taking a diagnosis and a remedy from the same source?
    作者和今天的城市规划者在症状上一致，在其他一切上相反。关于「诊断和药方取自同一个来源」，这件事能告诉你什么？

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 1第 1 句 (begins开头 “In the organization …”)

  • A present perfect passive, stressing that it has been left out of account right up to now.现在完成时被动，强调「至今一直被忽视」。

Sentence 3第 3 句 (begins开头 “in order that …”)

  • in order that + may or can: a clause of purpose, more formal than so that.in order that + may/can：目的状语从句（比 so that 正式）。
  • as much ... as possible: the greatest amount there can be.as much … as possible：尽可能多。

Sentence 5第 5 句 (begins开头 “and without giving …”)

  • Two without phrases side by side, one with a noun and one with an -ing form.两个 without 短语并列：without + 名词 / without + 动名词。
  • Two layers of modifier, one past participle phrase describing the effects and another inside it describing the way of living.produced on … by …：过去分词后置定语修饰 effects；imposed by the factory：再修饰 mode of existence——两层后置定语套叠。

Sentence 7第 7 句 (begins开头 “The shape and …”)

  • Two of + -ing phrases side by side, both describing the same noun.两个 of + 动名词并列，都修饰 necessity。

Sentence 11第 11 句 (begins开头 “While they enjoy …”)

  • while: at the same time as; here with a contrast built in, since they enjoy the comfort and do not see the loss.while：在……的同时；这里带对比意味（享受着舒适，却不知……）。
  • be deprived of: to have something necessary taken away.be deprived of：被剥夺。

Sentence 13第 13 句 (begins开头 “torn by the …”)

  • full of ...: an adjective phrase placed after the noun it describes.full of…：形容词短语后置修饰 streets。
  • Two more past participle phrases side by side, still describing the streets. One of them is strikingly physical: the noise does not merely fill the street, it tears it.torn by… / thronged by…：两个过去分词短语并列，继续修饰 streets；torn 用得生动——噪声把街道「撕裂」。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

The present perfect passive: has/have been done现在完成时被动：has/have been done
It says that up to now something has been done to the subject, with the result still standing. Critical writing uses it to keep people in the object slot.
强调「到现在为止某事一直被……/已经被……」，结果延续到现在；批评性文字常用它突出「人被忽视」。

Compare the simple past: that would place the neglect at some time in the past with no claim about the present.

对比：was neglected（过去某时被忽视，与现在无关）。

  • The bridge has been closed since last week.
  • Nothing has been done to help them.
in order that / so that + may/can；as … as possible
in order that (formal) and so that both open a clause of purpose, usually with may, might, can or could. in order to and so as to take the bare verb, and only work when the subject is the same.
in order that（正式）与 so that 引导目的状语从句，从句常用 may/might/can/could；in order to / so as to 后接动词原形（主语相同时用）。

With a different subject you cannot use in order to; use in order for somebody to do instead.

主语不同时不能用 in order to：× in order to the workers earn… ；可用 in order for sb to do。

  • He spoke slowly so that everyone could understand.
  • Please reply as soon as possible.
deprive / rob / cure / remind / inform + sb + of + sth
A family of patterns, verb + person + of + thing: deprive somebody of something, rob somebody of something, cure somebody of something, remind somebody of something, inform somebody of something, warn somebody of something.
一组「动词 + 人 + of + 物」结构：deprive sb of sth（剥夺）、rob sb of sth（抢夺）、cure sb of sth（治愈）、remind sb of sth（使想起）、inform sb of sth（通知）、warn sb of sth（警告）。

Note rob against steal: you rob somebody of something, but you steal something from somebody.

注意 rob 与 steal：rob sb of sth / steal sth from sb。

  • The accident deprived him of his sight.
  • This song reminds me of my childhood.

Points a good summary covers好摘要要覆盖的要点
  • The way industrial life is organized leaves out entirely what the factory does to the body and the mind of the people in it.工业组织完全忽视了工厂对工人身心的影响。
  • Modern industry runs on the lowest cost and the largest output, so that a few can earn as much as possible, with no account taken of human nature or of the next generation.现代工业以最低成本、最大产量为原则，只为少数人赚钱，不顾人的本性及其后代所受的影响。
  • Tall buildings follow the income each square foot can earn and the taste of whoever will rent, and crowds are packed into enormous structures.城市高楼只按每平方英尺收益和租户喜好来建，人群被挤进巨大建筑。
  • People settle for comfort and dull luxury without seeing what has been taken away from them.人们享受舒适与平庸的奢华，却不知自己被剥夺了生活必需。
  • The streets are narrow and dark, thick with fumes and noise, pressed with crowds, and plainly not laid out for the good of anybody who lives there.城市街道狭窄阴暗、充满废气噪声、人潮拥挤——显然不是为居民的利益规划的。
Our model summary本站范文

Industry has ignored its effect on workers' bodies and minds. It aims at maximum production at minimum cost so that a few can grow rich, with no thought for human nature or future generations. Cities, too, ignore people: skyscrapers are designed only for maximum rent, crowding huge numbers together. Enjoying comfort, people fail to see what they lack, while their dark, noisy, polluted and crowded streets show that cities are not planned for their good.（75 词）
