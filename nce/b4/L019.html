Lesson 19 · The Stuff of Dreams 梦的本质

📘 New Concept English · Book 4 · Lesson 19 “The Stuff of Dreams”新概念英语 第四册 Lesson 19《The Stuff of Dreams》

C1🧠 21 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
Why do we sleep? The author reasons by elimination: not to rest the body (a short spell of lying or sitting does that, and the body in fact works best when it keeps moving); not to rest the brain either (the instrument shows that the total amount of brain activity does not fall during sleep). The decisive evidence comes from Dement's experiment with eye movements: people broken in on during those periods developed personality disorders, while people broken in on at other times did not. The conclusion: what matters is not having your sleep disturbed but having your dreaming disturbed. This is a model academic argument, and were it not for and it is not ... that ... but ... are both typical of the register.
人为什么要睡觉？作者用排除法推理：不是为了让身体休息（躺一会儿就够了，身体反而需要活动）；也不是为了让大脑休息（脑电图显示睡眠中大脑总活动量并不减少）；关键证据来自德门特的眼动实验——快速眼动期被打断的人会出现人格障碍，其他时段被打断的人却没事。结论：要紧的不是睡眠被打扰，而是做梦被打扰。这是学术议论文的样板，语言上 were it not for、it is not … that … but … 都很典型。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. Why is sleep more than a way of resting the body?为什么说睡眠不只是让身体休息？
  2. Why does the idea that the brain needs a rest fail to stand up?「大脑需要休息」这个假设为什么站不住？
  3. What did Dement's experiment establish?德门特的实验得出了什么结论？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

The attribution line in the original Book 4 reads 'Christopher Evans, The stuff of dreams, from The Listener'. The Listener was the BBC's weekly magazine, where talks broadcast on radio were printed for readers, which explains the spoken rhythm of the argument. Evans was a British psychologist who also worked with computers, and he is remembered for the suggestion that dreaming is what the brain does when it goes over its own programs, the way a machine is taken off line to be checked. He died before finishing the book that set the idea out in full.

旧版第四册课末署名作「Christopher Evans, The stuff of dreams, from The Listener」。The Listener 是 BBC 的周刊，把电台播出的讲座印出来给读者看——这正解释了本篇论证里那种口语节奏。埃文斯是英国心理学家，同时做计算机工作；他留下的说法是：做梦就是大脑在检查自己的程序，像机器下线自检那样。他在那本完整阐述这个想法的书写完之前去世了。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

The experiment described here was real and recent when the piece was written. Fast eye movements during sleep had been noticed in a Chicago laboratory in the early fifties, and in 1960 the American researcher named in the passage published a paper in Science on what happens when people are not allowed to have them: volunteers were woken each time the movements began, for three to seven nights running, and when they were finally left alone they spent markedly more of the night in that state than before. That last result, the rebound, is the part of the study that has never been in doubt; every later experiment finds the same rising pressure as the deprivation goes on. The claim the passage leans on hardest, that the volunteers started to come apart as people, is the part that did not travel so well.

本篇描述的实验，在文章写作时既是真的，也是新的。睡眠中的快速眼动在五十年代初由芝加哥一间实验室注意到；1960 年，文中点名的那位美国研究者在《科学》上发表论文，讲不让人进入这一状态会发生什么：志愿者每次眼动开始就被叫醒，连续三到七夜；等终于让他们安睡时，他们处于那种状态的时间明显比之前多。最后这个结果——反弹——是整项研究从未被怀疑的部分；后来每一个实验都发现，剥夺越久，压力越高。本篇倚得最重的那项说法——志愿者开始作为「人」出问题——反倒是走得不太远的那部分。

✨ How it is written赏析

This is argument by elimination, and the shape is worth stealing whole. Establish that there is something to explain, and that it matters, because so much of life is spent on it. Then take the obvious answer, rest for the body, and remove it with two quick facts. Then take the next obvious answer, rest for the brain, and remove that with an instrument reading. Only then bring in the experiment, which now lands with the weight of being the only candidate left. Notice the hedging as you go: would seem to be, it looks as if, to a degree, more or less, there is no evidence that. A beginner reads these as weakness. They are the opposite: each one marks exactly how far the writer is willing to be held, which is what buys him the right to end hard, with a flat sentence saying what the thing was not and what it was. Hedge the steps, not the conclusion.

这是排除法论证，整个形状值得整套拿走。先立住「有一件事需要解释，而且重要」，因为生命中有那么多时间花在它上面。再拿最显然的答案——让身体休息——用两个干脆的事实把它排除。再拿下一个显然的答案——让大脑休息——用一台仪器的读数把它排除。只有到这时才引出实验，而它此刻带着「只剩这一个候选」的重量落下。一路留意那些限定说法：would seem to be、it looks as if、to a degree、more or less、there is no evidence that。初学者把它们读成软弱，其实恰恰相反：每一个都精确标出作者愿意被追究到哪一步——正是这些让他有权在结尾下硬话，用一个平直的句子说清那件事不是什么、是什么。限定每一步，不要限定结论。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The first half of his argument has aged well and the last step has not. Nobody now explains sleep as rest, and the reason is the direction he was pointing in: sleep is maintenance, and maintenance is work. A 2013 paper in Science put a mechanism under that. In sleeping mice the space between brain cells opens up and fluid washes through it, carrying out waste including the protein that collects in Alzheimer's disease; the clearance runs faster asleep than awake, though you should know that the published commentary says about twice as fast while the university's own account says almost ten times, and that this is mouse work. His own computer image belongs on the same shelf, since checking over your programs is also maintenance rather than rest, so his instinct was sound even where his evidence was not. Where the ground has shifted is under the strong claim. Later human work on this kind of deprivation has mostly looked at memory and attention, and even there the results are still argued over; nobody reproduces the picture of a personality breaking up after a few nights. And there is an awkward fact from the clinic: the commonest antidepressant drugs cut this state of sleep sharply and are taken for months or years, while several effective ones leave it alone altogether. That pair is hard to square with the idea that losing it quickly damages the person. So read the passage as a first-rate piece of reasoning with one overconfident last line, and keep the question he was really asking, which nobody has finished answering: what is the work that only sleep can do?

他论证的前半经得起时间，最后一步没有。今天没人再把睡眠解释为休息，理由正是他指的那个方向：睡眠是维护，而维护是工作。2013 年《科学》上的一篇论文为此装上了机制。睡着的小鼠脑细胞之间的空隙张开，液体从中冲过，带走代谢废物，其中包括在阿尔茨海默病中堆积的那种蛋白；清除速度睡时快于醒时——不过要知道，发表的评论说「约快一倍」，而该校自己的通稿说「近十倍」，而且这是小鼠实验。他自己那个计算机比喻也该摆在同一层架子上，因为「检查自己的程序」同样是维护而不是休息；所以即使他的证据不牢，他的直觉是对的。真正塌下去的是那条强断言底下的地基。后来关于这类剥夺的人体研究大多看记忆与注意，而且连那里的结果至今还在争；没有人再复现「几夜之后人格开始解体」那幅图景。临床上还有一个尴尬的事实：最常用的抗抑郁药会大幅压低这一段睡眠，而且要吃几个月甚至几年，同时另有几种有效药完全不动它。这一对事实很难与「迅速失去它就会损伤这个人」的说法对上。所以把本篇读成一篇一流的推理加上一句过于自信的末句，并且把他真正在问的那个问题留着——那个问题至今没人答完：有哪一样工作，只有睡眠能做？

💬 Discuss and write讨论与写作
  1. The writer removes two obvious answers before offering his own. Take a question you argue about with family and try the same order: what are the two obvious answers, and what removes them?
    作者在给出自己的答案之前先排除了两个显然的答案。拿一件你和家人争论的事，照同样的顺序试一遍：那两个显然的答案是什么？拿什么把它们排除？
  2. Write 120 words of cautious academic English on any claim, using would seem to, there is no evidence that and more or less, and then finish with one flat unhedged sentence.
    就任何一个断言写 120 词的谨慎学术英语，用上 would seem to、there is no evidence that、more or less，然后用一个不加限定的平直句子收尾。
  3. A result that holds (the rebound) and a result that does not (the personality damage) came from the same experiment. What would you check before trusting the second kind of claim?
    同一个实验里出了一个站得住的结果（反弹）和一个站不住的结果（人格损伤）。在相信后一类说法之前，你会先核什么？

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

The inverted subjunctive with if left out: Were / Had / Should省略 if 的虚拟倒装：Were / Had / Should
When a subjunctive condition contains were, had or should, if can be dropped and the verb moved in front of the subject. In the negative, not is not contracted and follows the subject: Were it not for ..., Had it not been for ...
虚拟条件句中含 were、had、should 时可省 if，把它们提到主语前；否定时 not 不缩写，放在主语后：Were it not for…, Had it not been for…

Three versions: Were I you, I would accept. (now) / Had I known, I would have come. (past) / Should it rain, the match will be cancelled. (future, unlikely)

三种：Were I you, I would accept.（现在）/ Had I known, I would have come.（过去）/ Should it rain, the match will be cancelled.（将来，可能性小）

  • Should you need help, please call me.
  • Had he not warned us, we would have been hurt.
need doing / want doing / deserve doing: an active form with a passive senseneed doing / want doing / deserve doing：主动形式表被动
After need, want, require and deserve, an -ing form carries a passive meaning: The car needs washing = The car needs to be washed.
need, want, require, deserve 后接动名词时，动名词有被动含义：The car needs washing = The car needs to be washed。

You cannot say needs being washed.

注意不能说 needs being washed。

  • The garden wants watering.
  • His bravery deserves praising. / deserves to be praised.
The cautious tone of academic prose: would seem / it looks as if / more or less学术文体的谨慎语气：would seem / it looks as if / more or less
Academic writing is slow to state anything absolutely, and uses would seem, appear to, it looks as if, there is no evidence that, more or less and to a degree to pitch a claim lower.
学术写作不轻易下绝对结论，常用 would seem, appear to, it looks as if, there is no evidence that, more or less, to a degree 等降调表达。

And it still keeps a hard conclusion for the end: the flat sentence saying what the thing was not and what it was.

同时保留有力的结论：The implications … were that it was not … but …。

  • It would seem that the plan has failed.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • It is fairly clear that …, and because …, … would seem to be … 相当清楚的是……，而且由于……，……似乎很……
    It is fairly clear that reading matters, and because children spend so little time on it, the problem would seem to be serious.
  • This might be a plausible … were it not for … 要不是……，这本是个说得通的……
    This might be a plausible excuse were it not for the photos.
  • It was not A that mattered, but B 要紧的不是 A，而是 B
    It was not the price that mattered, but the delay.

Keywords for retelling复述关键词

  • sleep takes so much time — it must have an important function
  • not rest for the body: brief rest is enough; tissues need activity
  • not rest for the brain: EEG shows no less total activity
  • Dement: bursts of eye-movements during sleep
  • woken then → dreaming; woken at other times → no dreams
  • deprived of eye-movement sleep → personality disorders: dreaming matters

Summary writing摘要写作

In no more than 80 words, sum up the author's chain of reasoning: what the function of sleep is not, and what the experiment showed.

用不超过 80 个词概括作者的推理过程：睡眠的功能不是什么，以及实验证明了什么。

Points a good summary covers好摘要要覆盖的要点
  • Sleep takes up so much time that its function must be important, yet it is more than rest for the body: a short spell of lying or sitting will rest the body, which in any case works best when it keeps moving.睡眠时间很长，必有重要功能，但它不只是让身体休息——身体短暂躺坐就能休息，且需要经常活动。
  • Nor is it rest for the brain: the instrument shows the pattern of activity changing while the total amount does not fall.也不是让大脑休息——脑电图显示睡眠中大脑活动方式改变，总量却不减少。
  • Dement found that eye movements come round at intervals through the night, and that people woken then are usually in the middle of a dream.德门特发现睡眠中周期性出现眼动，此时被叫醒的人大多正在做梦。
  • Those broken in on at those moments for several nights running developed personality disorders, while those broken in on at other times were much the same as before.眼动期被打断数夜的人出现人格障碍，其他时段被打断的人基本不受影响。
  • The conclusion: what mattered was the dreaming being disturbed, not the sleep.结论：要紧的是梦被打扰，而不是睡眠被打扰。
Our model summary本站范文

Sleep must have an important function, but it is not simply rest for the body, which can rest briefly and works best when active. Nor is it rest for the brain, since recordings show its total activity does not fall during sleep. Dement found that people woken during periods of eye-movement were usually dreaming, and those repeatedly deprived of such sleep developed personality disorders. So it is disturbed dreaming, not disturbed sleep, that matters.（74 词）
