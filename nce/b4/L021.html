Lesson 21 · William S. Hart and the Early Western Film 威廉·S·哈特和早期「西部」影片

📘 New Concept English · Book 4 · Lesson 21 “William S. Hart and the Early Western Film”新概念英语 第四册 Lesson 21《William S. Hart and the Early Western Film》

C1🧠 26 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
A critical essay about a film-maker: Hart may be the greatest of the Western stars because he played in no other kind of film, and he invented the standard shape of the Western, with the good-bad man at its centre, a man at odds with himself and with the frontier closing in on him. The writer then explains the appeal: here myth and fact really do meet, because men trained to fight nature were helpless against politicians, bankers, fences and laws. The last lines turn to the present: we live among hypocrisy and disorder, and we still look for rules we can live by. What this lesson teaches is how a critical essay climbs: the man, then the formula, then the historical root, then the feelings of the audience, and finally why any of it matters now.
一篇影评式的文化随笔：哈特是最伟大的西部片明星，因为他只演西部片；他创造了西部片的基本程式——「好坏人」式的主角，一个与自己、与边疆环境冲突的个人。作者进一步解释为什么观众喜欢：西部片里神话与现实在一处相交——习惯与自然搏斗的人，被政客、银行家和篱笆、法律打下马来。结尾回到今天：我们生活在虚伪与混乱中，仍想要一套可以依循的准则。学这一课学评论文的递进：人物 → 程式 → 历史根源 → 观众心理 → 当下意义。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. Why does the writer rank Hart above the other Western stars?为什么说哈特可能是最伟大的西部片明星？
  2. What is special about the hero Hart created?哈特塑造的主角有什么特点？
  3. Why could early audiences forgive a hero who robbed a bank or shot a sheriff?为什么早期观众能原谅主角抢银行、射杀警长？

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

Carl Foreman (1914-1984) was not a film historian but a screenwriter with a wound. He wrote High Noon (1952), was called before the House Committee on Un-American Activities, took the Fifth Amendment rather than name colleagues, found himself on the Hollywood blacklist and moved to Britain, where he worked without credit on The Bridge on the River Kwai (1957); the Academy admitted whose Oscar that had been only in 1984, the day before he died. So the lines here about a man pushed out by respectable people, and forgiven by the crowd for breaking their rules, were written by someone describing his own trade. Hart himself (1864-1946) came to the screen from the stage, was already past fifty when he started, made his last picture in 1925, and left a ranch north of Los Angeles that is now a public park and museum. By then audiences had moved on to faster and prettier riders.

卡尔·福尔曼（1914–1984）不是电影史家，而是一个带着伤的编剧。他写了《正午》（High Noon，1952），被传到众议院「非美活动调查委员会」作证，宁可援引宪法第五修正案也不肯供出同行，于是上了好莱坞黑名单，去了英国，为《桂河大桥》（1957）写剧本却不署名；学院承认那座奥斯卡属于他，是在 1984 年——他去世前一天。所以课文里那个被体面人挤出去、又被观众原谅了违法行为的人，是一个在写自己行当的人写出来的。哈特本人（1864–1946）是舞台演员出身，入行时已过五十，1925 年拍完最后一部片子，在洛杉矶以北留下一座农场，今天是公园和博物馆。那时观众早已转向更快、更漂亮的骑手。

✨ How it is written赏析

The essay is built on paradox. Each name for the hero holds two opposite words inside one phrase: a man who is good and bad at the same time, an outlaw who turns noble by accident, a cowboy who is honest and framed all the same. You cannot take such a phrase apart, and that is the point - the character exists only in the contradiction. Three other moves here are worth copying. First, the list of four types is closed by in short, which turns a catalogue into a definition. Second, the writer concedes the strongest objection before he makes his own claim: yes, that corner of the American past has been dressed up more absurdly than any other, and even so one real conflict lay underneath. A concession placed first makes the claim that follows sound measured rather than fond. Third, the ending changes pronoun. Until then the audience is they, people of another century; then comes a three-word sentence in the first person, and the subject of the essay is suddenly the reader. The last line piles up abstract nouns - aggression, hypocrisy, disorder - and lands on something small and concrete: a rule a person can hold on to.

这篇文章建在悖论上。给主角起的每个名字，都把两个反义词塞进一个短语：一个同时是好人和坏人的人，一个因偶然而显得高贵的逃犯，一个诚实却仍被诬陷的牛仔。这样的短语拆不开，而这正是用意所在——人物只存在于矛盾之中。另有三处手法值得照搬。第一，四种类型的清单用 in short 收口，把罗列变成定义。第二，作者先承认最强的反对意见，再提出自己的主张：是的，美国历史上这一角被涂抹得比别处更荒唐，可底下仍有一桩真实的冲突。把让步放在前面，后面的主张就显得有分寸，而不像偏爱。第三，结尾换了人称。此前观众是「他们」，是另一个世纪的人；接着来了一句三个词的第一人称短句，文章的对象忽然变成读者本人。最后一句把抽象名词堆起来——侵略、虚伪、混乱——落在一件具体而微小的东西上：一个人抓得住的规矩。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

Two parts of the argument need checking today. The first is the claim of first-hand knowledge. The record of Hart's boyhood on the plains is thin: a standard reference says he was brought up in the Dakotas until he was sixteen, a museum page and the National Portrait Gallery add that he had Sioux playmates and learned to speak Lakota, while a scholarly sketch calls him self-dramatizing and says little is firmly known about those years. Part of what the essay takes for experience was publicity, and Hart was his own best publicist. The second is a silence. The people who attack, and who are beaten in the last reel, appear in the essay as scenery, the obstacle that lets an outsider become a hero. That is how the genre worked for fifty years, and it is the part that has changed most: a 2022 film was released with a full Comanche-language soundtrack, and a comedy series written, directed and acted by Indigenous film-makers ran for three seasons, so the frontier is now also told from the side that used to be the weather. For a Chinese reader the near relative is wuxia. A lone swordsman with a private code, at odds with officials and merchants, forgiven his violence because the law has been bought - the shape is almost the same, which suggests that the hunger the essay describes is not American but general. One caution: that everybody wants a rule of that kind is the writer's assertion, not a measured finding, and it is worth asking what evidence would test it.

这篇论证有两处今天需要重检。第一是「亲历」的说法。哈特在平原上的童年记录很薄：权威辞书说他在达科他长到十六岁，博物馆网页与史密森学会国家肖像馆补充说他有苏族玩伴、学会说拉科塔语，而一篇学术传略称他「爱自我戏剧化」，并说那几年其实所知甚少。课文当成经历的东西，有一部分是宣传，而哈特是自己最好的宣传员。第二是一段沉默。那些来进攻、又在最后一卷里被打败的人，在文中是布景，是让局外人成为英雄的障碍。这就是这个类型片五十年的做法，也是今天变化最大的地方：2022 年有一部影片发行了全科曼奇语版本；一部由原住民编、导、演的喜剧剧集播了三季——边疆如今也从过去只当天气的那一边讲出来了。对中国读者来说，最近的亲戚是武侠：独行的侠客有自己的一套准则，与官府和商人过不去，因为律法已被买通，他的暴力就得到原谅——形状几乎相同，这说明课文讲的那种渴望不是美国特有的，而是普遍的。一点提醒：「人人都想要这样一套规矩」是作者的断言，不是经过衡量的结论，值得追问什么证据才能检验它。

  • the conflict ...: an appositive that explains what the arena is.the conflict…：同位语，说明 arena。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

nothing but / anything but / all but
nothing but = only; anything but = not at all; all but = almost. The three look alike and mean quite different things, so they are easy to mix up.
nothing but＝只有，仅仅；anything but＝绝不是，根本不；all but＝几乎（= almost）。三者形近义远，极易混。
  • He is anything but a hero.（他根本不是英雄）
  • The project is all but finished.（几乎完成了）
  • I could do nothing but wait.（只能等）——nothing but 后接动词原形。
The auxiliary do/does/did for emphasis助动词 do/does/did 强调谓语
A stressed do/does/did in front of the verb of a positive sentence means really, indeed; it often comes after a concession.
在肯定句的谓语动词前加 do/does/did（重读），表示「确实、的确」，常用于先让步后肯定的语境。

It is also used in requests, to make them warmer: Do come in!

也用于祈使句表恳切：Do come in!

  • I know you don't believe me, but I did see him.
  • She does look tired today.
A negative word with a comparative = a superlative否定词 + 比较级 = 最高级
no, nothing, nobody, never and hardly used with a comparative carry the meaning of a superlative: No ... has been more ... than ...; I have never seen a better film.
no, nothing, nobody, never, hardly 等与比较级连用表示最高级的意思：No … has been more … than …；I've never seen a better film.

Other shapes: Nothing is more important than health. / I couldn't agree more (= I agree completely).

其他形式：Nothing is more important than health. / I couldn't agree more.（我完全同意）

  • Nothing is more precious than time.
  • I couldn't be happier.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • It was X who created …, and devised … 正是 X 创造了……，并设计了……
    It was Jobs who created the modern smartphone market and devised its basic design.
  • … ; in short, … ……；简而言之，……
    He was late, rude and unprepared; in short, a disaster.
  • Although no … has been more …, … did … 虽然没有哪个……比它更……，但……确实……
    Although no city has been more often criticized, it did manage to host the Games well.

Summary writing摘要写作

In no more than 80 words, sum up what Hart gave to the Western, and why the Western appealed to audiences then and appeals to us now.

用不超过 80 个词概括哈特对西部片的贡献，以及西部片为什么吸引过去和现在的观众。

Points a good summary covers好摘要要覆盖的要点
  • Hart played in Westerns only, and for ten years from 1914 nobody could match him.哈特只演西部片，1914 至 1924 年无人能及。
  • He created the standard shape of the Western and its good-bad hero, a man at odds with himself and with the frontier.他创造了西部片的基本程式和「好坏人」式的主角——与自身和边疆环境冲突的个人。
  • As a child he had seen the old West while it was dying out, so his hero grew out of real memory as well as out of myth.他童年亲历过正在消逝的西部，人物植根于真实经历与神话。
  • In the Western, myth and fact meet in the struggle between one man and an advancing civilisation, and the audience takes the outsider's side.西部片里神话与现实交汇于个人与侵入的文明之间的冲突；观众同情这位局外人。
  • Audiences then wanted to escape into a simpler age; in today's disorder we want rules of our own to live by.当年的观众想逃回一个简单的时代，今天身处混乱世界的我们也想要一套准则。
Our model summary本站范文

William S. Hart, who acted only in Westerns, dominated the genre from 1914 to 1924. He created its basic formula and its hero, the good-bad man in conflict with himself and the frontier. Having known the old West as a child, he drew on both its history and its myths, especially the struggle between the individual and advancing civilization. Audiences then, like us today, enjoyed escaping to a simpler time with a clear code to live by.（77 词）
