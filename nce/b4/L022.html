Lesson 22 · Knowledge and Progress 知识与进步

📘 New Concept English · Book 4 · Lesson 22 “Knowledge and Progress”新概念英语 第四册 Lesson 22《Knowledge and Progress》

C1🧠 22 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
Why has progress become such a loud idea in the modern world? The writer draws a precise line: in intelligence and in morality people have not improved at all, and the only real gain is in the stock of knowledge - from speech to writing, libraries, printing and then science, a thin flow that has become a flood, with every new finding put to use at once. But knowledge cuts both ways: gunners use science on men's bodies while surgeons a short distance away use it to mend them. The question left open is what happens if that double use goes on as the power grows. The shape to learn here is definition, then history, then turn, then warning, and the metaphors (thin flow to stream to flood, and interest earned on interest) are worth studying on their own.
为什么「进步」观念在现代世界如此突出？作者给出一个精确的区分：人类的智力和道德并没有普遍提高，进步只发生在知识的积累上——从口语、文字、图书馆、印刷到科学，涓涓细流成了洪流，而且新知识立刻被用于实际。但知识是双刃剑：炮手用科学粉碎人体，近旁的外科医生用科学修复人体。问题是：知识越来越强大，这种双重用途继续下去会怎样？这是定义—历史—转折—警示结构的议论文，比喻（trickle → stream → torrent，compound interest）用得精彩。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. In which two areas does the writer say people have not improved, and in which area have they?作者认为人类在哪两方面没有进步？在哪方面有巨大进步？
  2. What stages did the growth of knowledge pass through?知识增长经历了哪几个阶段？
  3. Why does the writer call knowledge a weapon with two edges, and what example does he give?为什么说知识是「双刃剑」？作者举了什么例子？

课文署名 G. N. M. Tyrrell，出自他的《人的人格》（The Personality of Man，据旧版第四册课末署名）。该书是 1947 年的鹈鹕丛书（Pelican）平装本，部分编目作 1946 年。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

These paragraphs are the opening move of a book about something else. George Nugent Merle Tyrrell (1879-1952) trained as a radio engineer and a mathematician, then gave his working life to psychical research, and he was president of the Society for Psychical Research in 1945-46. His Pelican book argues that inspiration, mysticism, telepathy and mediumship belong inside any full account of human personality, and the passage you have just read lays the ground for that argument: if what grows is only the store of facts, while judgement and character stand still, then the sciences must have left out a side of us. The date matters too. A paperback published in 1947 was written in the first two years after Hiroshima, so the gunners and the surgeons were not a figure of speech but recent memory, and the question in the last sentence was being asked in every newspaper.

这几段是另一本书的开场。乔治·纳杰特·默尔·泰瑞尔（1879–1952）学的是无线电工程与数学，后半生都投在灵异研究上，1945–46 年任英国心灵研究学会（SPR）会长。他这本鹈鹕丛书主张：灵感、神秘体验、心灵感应、通灵都应算进对「人」的完整描述，而你刚读的这几段是为那个主张铺路——如果增长的只有事实的库存，判断力与品性原地不动，那科学必定漏掉了人的一面。年份也有分量：1947 年的平装本写在广岛之后的头两年，所以炮手与外科医生不是修辞，是刚过去的记忆；最后一句的问题，当时每份报纸都在问。

✨ How it is written赏析

Watch five moves. The piece opens with a question and answers it in the next breath, so the reader is inside the argument before the first paragraph ends. Then comes a concession - no general gain in two things - placed so that the real claim sounds narrow and therefore trustworthy. The history that follows is five stages in five short sentences: speech, writing, libraries with education, printing, science. Nothing is explained twice. The whole acceleration is then carried by one metaphor that is allowed to grow, from a thin flow to a stream to a flood, with a second figure from banking beside it, interest earned on interest. One growing metaphor does more than three clever unrelated ones. Finally the ending refuses to conclude: a rhetorical question sets two men side by side using the same tool in opposite directions, and the last sentence is itself a question handed to the reader. If you want the technique in one line: concede, narrow the claim, let one image carry the speed, and stop before the answer.

看五处手法。开篇先提问，紧接着就答，读者在第一段结束前已被拉进论证里。随后是让步——有两样东西并没有普遍提高——放在这个位置，使真正的主张显得范围很窄，因而可信。接下来的历史是五句话五个阶段：口语、文字、图书馆与教育、印刷、科学，没有一处重复解释。整个加速过程由一个任其生长的比喻承担：细流、溪流、洪流，旁边配一个银行业的比喻——利息又生利息。一个会生长的比喻，胜过三个互不相关的聪明比喻。最后，结尾拒绝下结论：一个反问把两个人并置，同一件工具朝相反方向使用；而末句本身是一个交到读者手上的问题。技巧压成一句话：先让步，再收窄主张，让一个意象承担速度，在答案之前停住。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

Three parts are worth testing today, and one of them turns back on the author. First, the accumulation he describes has itself become a research subject, and the findings are disputed. A 2023 paper in Nature argued that papers and patents have grown steadily less disruptive over decades; a working paper the same year showed that most of the patent decline disappears once a measurement artefact is corrected - backward citations to patents before 1976 had been cut off - and that the count of highly disruptive patents has in fact risen since 2008. So the honest summary is: the store grows beyond doubt, the effect of each addition is an open quarrel. Second, the line that character has not improved at all is the weakest in the passage, because it is asserted and never measured. There is a serious debate: one body of work finds long declines in several kinds of violence, while critics answer that the statistics of war have such heavy tails that no trend can be read from them yet. Both sides are worth reading before you repeat either. Third, the author's own next step failed the test he himself invites. The psychical research he spent his life defending has not produced repeatable results: a much-discussed 2011 experiment on seeing the future was followed by three pre-registered attempts at Edinburgh, Goldsmiths and Hertfordshire which found nothing at all. A clean argument about knowledge is no guarantee about the subject it is built to defend. For a Chinese reader there is one more thing in his list of stages. Printing comes before the sudden rise in tempo - and printing was in use in China centuries before Europe, which is exactly the question Joseph Needham spent decades on: why the systematic, self-feeding phase began where it did. That question is still open, and the passage, written for a 1947 reader, does not notice that it is a question at all.

今天有三处值得检验，其中一处反过来打在作者自己身上。第一，他说的那种积累本身已成了研究对象，而结论有争议：2023 年《自然》上一篇论文认为，几十年来论文与专利的「颠覆性」持续下降；同年一篇工作论文指出，专利那部分下降在校正一个测量假象后几乎消失——1976 年以前的被引数据被截断了——而高颠覆性专利的数量自 2008 年起其实在上升。所以如实的说法是：库存确实在涨，每一件新增的效用则还在吵。第二，「品性毫无提高」这句是全文最弱的一句，因为它只是断言，从未度量。这里有一场正经的争论：一派研究发现若干类暴力长期下降，批评者回应说战争的统计分布尾部太厚，现在还读不出趋势。两边都该读过再去重复任何一边。第三，作者自己的下一步没能通过他自己邀请的检验：他毕生捍卫的灵异研究至今拿不出可重复的结果——2011 年一项广受讨论的「预知」实验，随后在爱丁堡、金匠学院和赫特福德三处做了预注册重复，什么也没测到。一个关于知识的干净论证，并不保证它要捍卫的那个题目也干净。对中国读者还有一处：他的阶段表里，印刷排在速度骤升之前——而印刷在中国早用了几百年，这正是李约瑟花几十年追问的问题：为什么那个系统化、自我喂养的阶段偏偏从那里开始。这个问题至今没有答案，而写给 1947 年读者的这几段，根本没意识到它是个问题。

💬 Discuss and write讨论与写作
  1. Name one thing you can do that your grandparents could not, and one judgement they made better than you. Which list is longer, and what does that do to the writer's distinction between knowing and being wise?
    说出一件你会做而你祖父母不会做的事，再说出一件他们判断得比你好的事。哪张清单更长？这对作者「知识」与「智慧」的区分意味着什么？
  2. Write 120 words about one technology you use every day: name one use that helps and one that harms, then say who you think should choose between them, and why.
    写 120 词谈一样你每天用的技术：说出一种有益的用法和一种有害的用法，再说你认为该由谁来做选择，为什么。
  3. The passage ends with a question instead of an answer. Write the answer the author avoided, in three sentences, and mark which of the three you could be wrong about.
    文章以问题而非答案结尾。用三句话写出作者回避的那个答案，并标出其中哪一句你可能是错的。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 3第 3 句 (begins开头 “Although mankind has …”)

  • A concession set against a claim: no general improvement on one side, extraordinary progress on the other.让步对比：没有普遍提高 vs 有非凡进步。
  • make progress in something: to advance in that field; progress is uncountable.make progress in：在……方面取得进步；progress 不可数。

Sentence 8第 8 句 (begins开头 “for knowledge could …”)

  • with + noun: here it marks the time or the cause, meaning as something arrived.with + 名词：表时间/原因（随着……）。
  • not only ... but also joins two past participles, which share the same could be.not only … but also 连接两个过去分词，共用 could be。

Sentence 10第 10 句 (begins开头 “the growth of …”)

  • The interest-on-interest figure: libraries make education possible, education fills libraries, and each round is bigger than the last.复利比喻：图书馆促进教育，教育又充实图书馆，越滚越快。
  • which refers to the growth (or to the whole process): a non-defining relative clause.which 指 the growth（或整个过程），非限制性定语从句。

Sentence 17第 17 句 (begins开头 “What is called …”)

  • What is called ...: what people call something; a noun clause used as the subject.What is called…：所谓的……（主语从句）。
  • not A but B: after but the words the result are left out, and only the of-phrase remains (see Lesson 6).not A but B：but 后省略 the result，只保留 of…（见第 6 课）。
  • applied to practical life: a past participle phrase describing the noun in front of it.applied to practical life：过去分词后置定语。

Sentence 22第 22 句 (begins开头 “As is so …”)

  • as works as a relative pronoun standing for the whole main clause; only as, never which, can come first in the sentence.as 作关系代词，指代后面整个主句内容；放句首时只能用 as，不能用 which。

Sentence 25第 25 句 (begins开头 “Could any spectacle, …”)

  • A rhetorical question with a comparative carries the force of a superlative: could anything be more ...? means that this is the most ... of all.反问 + 比较级 = 最高级（还有比这更……的吗？＝这最……）。
  • that of: that stands for the noun spectacle; gunners using science ... is a gerund with its own subject.that of：that 代替 spectacle；gunners using science…：动名词复合结构（gunners 是逻辑主语）。
  • while marks the contrast; close at hand means only a short distance away.while 表对比；close at hand 近在咫尺。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

Non-defining clauses with as (As is known / As has been said)as 引导的非限制性定语从句（As is known / As has been said）
as can act as a relative pronoun standing for the whole main clause, and its clause may come first, in the middle or at the end; which can also stand for a whole clause, but only after the main clause.
as 可作关系代词指代整个主句内容，从句可放句首、句中、句尾；which 也可指代整句，但只能放在主句之后。

Common set phrases: as is known to all, as has been said, as was expected, as we all know.

常见套语：as is known to all（众所周知）、as has been said、as was expected、as we all know。

The difference: an as-clause agrees with the main clause; when the fact is contrary to expectation, use which: He failed, which was unexpected.

区别：as 从句与主句意思一致（顺承）；表示与预期相反时用 which：He failed, which was unexpected.

  • As is known to all, the earth goes round the sun.
  • He was late again, as was expected.
The present continuous passive: is being done现在进行时被动：is being done
For something that is being done to a thing at this moment or in this period, use am/is/are being + past participle; the past form is was/were being done.
「此刻/现阶段正在被……」用 am/is/are being + 过去分词；过去进行时被动 was/were being done。
  • The bridge is being repaired this week.
  • When I arrived, the patient was being examined.
Rhetorical questions and the negative with a comparative: Could any ... be more ...?修辞反问与「否定 + 比较级」：Could any … be more …?
A question in form, a strong statement in force: Could anything be more absurd? = Nothing could be more absurd. = This is absurd in the highest degree.
用疑问形式表达强烈的肯定：Could anything be more absurd? = Nothing could be more absurd. = This is extremely absurd.

The logic is the same as the negative with a comparative in Lesson 21; only the form has changed to a question.

与第 21 课的「否定 + 比较级」同理，只是换成了反问。

  • Who could be kinder than my grandmother?（没有人比她更善良）
  • What could be simpler?

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • Why does … loom so large …? Surely because … 为什么……如此突出？无疑是因为……
    Why does money loom so large in our lives? Surely because everything has a price.
  • What is called '…' is not the result of …, but of … 所谓的「……」并不是……的结果，而是……的结果
    What is called 'talent' is not the result of luck, but of years of practice.
  • Could any spectacle be more … than that of …? 还有什么景象比……更……？

⑥ Dictation and retelling听写与复述

  1. Listen to the whole recording once with the book closed. Say in one sentence what it is about.合上书完整听一遍录音，用一句话说出讲了什么。
  2. Dictate sentence by sentence: pause after each sentence and write it down. Replay a sentence at most three times.逐句听写：每句后暂停写下来，同一句最多重放三遍。
  3. Check against the text in your coursebook. Mark every mistake by type: a word you did not know, a word you knew but did not hear (linking, weak forms), or spelling.对照教材课文自查，把每个错按类型标出：不认识的词、认识却没听出的词（连读、弱读）、拼写。
  4. Words you did not hear go into your word book above. Re-listen to those sentences and shadow them.没听出的词加进上面的生词本；把那几句再听、跟读。
  5. Retell from the keywords below without looking at the text, then compare and retell once more.只看下面的关键词、不看课文复述一遍，对照后再复述一遍。

Keywords for retelling复述关键词

  • progress looms large because one kind of progress is real
  • no improvement in intelligence or morality — only in knowledge
  • speech → writing (stored) → libraries and education (compound interest) → printing
  • science: trickle → stream → torrent; knowledge turned to practical account
  • modern civilization = knowledge applied to life
  • a two-edged weapon: gunners vs surgeons — what next?

Summary writing摘要写作

In no more than 80 words, sum up the writer's analysis of progress and the question he leaves us with.

用不超过 80 个词概括作者对「进步」的分析和他提出的问题。

Points a good summary covers好摘要要覆盖的要点
  • People have not grown wiser or better in general; the real advance is in what they know.人类的智力和道德没有普遍提高，真正的进步在于知识的积累。
  • Knowledge began to grow with speech, writing let it be stored, libraries and education fed each other, printing sped it up, and science raised the rate sharply.知识从口语传递开始增长，文字使其可储存，图书馆与教育相互促进，印刷加速，科学使速度骤增。
  • Each new finding is put to use at once, and what we call modern civilisation is the result of that use, not of a balanced growth of human nature.新知识一经获得立即用于实际；所谓现代文明是知识应用于生活的结果。
  • Knowledge cuts both ways and is serving good and evil alike, as the gunners and the surgeons show.知识是双刃剑，正被不加区别地用于善与恶，如炮手与外科医生。
  • The question to face is what will happen if that double use continues while the power keeps growing.必须认真思考：力量日增的知识若继续被这样双重使用会怎样。
