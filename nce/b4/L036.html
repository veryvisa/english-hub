Lesson 36 · The Cost of Government 政府的开支

📘 New Concept English · Book 4 · Lesson 36 “The Cost of Government”新概念英语 第四册 Lesson 36《The Cost of Government》

C1🧠 22 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
Why must a divided country spend heavily on governing itself? The author argues it through a firm. Where everybody can be trusted to do the job, one person to a post is enough; where nobody can, you have to hire crowds of managers, checkers and foremen, the running costs climb, and there is less left to pay out to the owners or to put back into the business. A nation works the same way: a loyal and responsible people does not need armies of police and officials. This is a textbook argument by analogy, and it keeps using to the extent that, correspondingly and as ... so ... — the English for 'one quantity moving with another'.
一个四分五裂的国家，政府开支为什么必然很高？作者拿企业作比：员工个个可靠负责，一个岗位一个人就够了；谁都信不过，就得雇一大批管理员、检查员、工头，管理费用水涨船高，能分的红利、能投的发展资金就少了。国家同理：人民忠诚负责，就不需要成群的警察和公务员。本课是典型的类比论证，句式上反复使用 to the extent that、correspondingly、as … so …，表达「一个量跟着另一个量变化」。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. Why does a lack of unity cut the resources available for development?国家不团结为什么会减少可用于发展的资源？
  2. Under what condition are a firm's running costs low?企业的管理费用在什么条件下才会低？
  3. What does the author want the comparison with a firm to prove about a nation?作者用企业作类比，想说明国家的什么道理？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

The lesson is credited to Raymond Frost's book The Backward Society, published in 1961 by St Martin's Press in New York; a review appeared in the American Political Science Review the following year. We found no biography of the author, so who he was is not verified.

课文署名 Raymond Frost 的《The Backward Society》，1961 年由纽约 St Martin's Press 出版，次年《American Political Science Review》有书评。我们没找到作者生平，他是什么人未核。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

The year matters. In 1961 dozens of countries were becoming independent within a few years of each other, the word development had just turned into a profession with its own institutions and loans, and the standard question was why some poor countries grew and others did not. The fashionable answers were about capital: not enough saving, not enough machinery, not enough foreign money. This author goes a different way and makes the answer social. What a country lacks, on his account, is not machines but the ability to leave people alone to do their work, and the bill for that shortage arrives as the cost of government. The chapter is also, quietly, a piece of writing from the end of empire: the colonial state had been mostly a watching and tax-collecting machine, and his readers in 1961 could see what it had cost and what little development it had left behind.

年份很重要。1961 年，几十个国家在短短几年内相继独立，「发展」刚变成一门带着机构和贷款的专业，而标准问题是：为什么有些穷国长起来了，有些没有。当时流行的答案都关于资本：储蓄不足、机器不足、外资不足。这位作者走了另一条路，把答案放到社会关系上：一个国家缺的不是机器，而是「可以放手让人把活干完」的能力，而这项欠缺的账单，就以政府开支的形式送来。这一章同时是一篇帝国末期的文字：殖民地政府主要是一台监视与征税的机器，1961 年的读者看得见它花了多少钱、又留下了多少发展。

✨ How it is written赏析

The structure is a single analogy carried all the way through, and it is carried honestly, which is rarer than it sounds. Watch the order. He states the claim about nations in two sentences, then says outright that he will illustrate it by comparison, then builds the business case in full - cost, overheads, who checks whom, what is left for dividends - and only then returns to the nation with a single sentence saying the case is exactly parallel, repeating the structure point for point, police standing where the foremen stood. Nothing is smuggled across. The weakness of arguing by analogy is that the reader cannot see the join; this author puts the join in the middle of the page and invites you to inspect it. If you ever argue this way, copy that: announce the comparison before you use it, finish one side before you start the other, and keep the two halves in the same order so the reader can check them against each other. The other thing to steal is the sentence that costs him nothing and buys a lot - the aside that these resources are pitifully small to begin with in a poor country. One clause, and the whole argument acquires stakes.

结构是一个贯穿到底的类比，而且贯穿得很老实，这比听起来少见。注意顺序：他先用两句话把关于国家的主张说完，然后明说「我要用比较来说明它」，接着把企业那一侧建完整——成本、管理费用、谁查谁、还剩多少可分红——再回过头来说「国家完全一样」，并逐点重复同一个结构，警察站在工头原来的位置上。没有任何东西被偷运过去。类比论证的毛病是读者看不见接缝；这位作者把接缝摆在纸面中央，请你检查。如果你也这样论证，就照搬：用之前先宣布这个比较，把一侧讲完再开另一侧，两侧保持同样的顺序，让读者能对着看。另一处值得偷的，是那句花费极小、收益极大的插话——穷国可用的资源本来就少得可怜。一个从句，整个论证就有了分量。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The mechanism he describes is real, and it now has a name and a price tag. Economists call it the cost of monitoring, and the cross-country work on trust points the same way: in a well-known 1997 study of twenty-nine market economies, a one-standard-deviation rise in how far people said their countrymen could be trusted went with growth higher by more than half a standard deviation, and the same work found trust stronger where incomes were higher and more equal and where institutions kept rulers from preying on people. Note that last clause, because it reverses his arrow. He treats disunity as the cause and heavy government as the consequence. The evidence allows the other direction just as well: a state that watches, checks and controls teaches people that nobody is to be trusted, and then needs more watching. Both arrows are probably live at once, which is what makes the trap a trap. The formula also fails on its face in one big way. If a high cost of government meant few resources for development, the countries that raise and spend the largest share of national income through their governments would be the poorly developed ones, and they are the opposite: the Nordic states tax and spend near the top of the world and sit near the top of every development ranking. What they have is not a small government but a cheap-to-run one - a state people do not need to be policed into complying with. So the thing to measure is not the size of the bill but what the bill buys: how much of it goes on watching rather than on building. For Chinese readers the lesson is close to a familiar proverb about costly officials, and worth testing against two cases a generation apart - a village where contracts are kept because everyone knows everyone, and a city where strangers transact all day because a payment system and a court stand behind them. The first is cheap and small; the second is expensive to build and makes everything else cheap. His analogy has no room for that second kind of spending, and that is where it runs out.

他描述的机制是真的，今天还有了名字和价码：经济学把它叫做监督成本，而跨国研究的方向一致——1997 年一项关于 29 个市场经济体的著名研究发现，「国人之间可以信任」的程度每升高一个标准差，经济增长高出半个标准差以上；同一研究还发现，收入更高、更均等，以及制度能约束统治者掠夺的地方，信任更强。注意最后这一句，因为它把他的箭头反了过来：他把不团结当原因、把庞大政府当结果；证据同样允许反方向——一个一直监视、检查、控制的政府，教会人们谁都不可信，于是需要更多的监视。两个箭头很可能同时活着，这正是这个陷阱难出的原因。这个公式还在一个大处当场失效：如果政府开支高就意味着可用于发展的资源少，那么通过政府征收与花掉最大份额国民收入的国家应该是发展落后的，而事实恰恰相反——北欧国家的税收与支出占比在世界前列，各项发展排名也在前列。他们拥有的不是一个小政府，而是一个运行便宜的政府：一个不需要靠警察逼人配合的政府。所以该量的不是账单的大小，而是这张账单买到了什么——其中多少花在监视上，多少花在建设上。对中国读者，这一课接近「冗官之费」那类老话，值得拿两个相隔一代的例子来检验：一个村子里合约靠「谁都认识谁」维持；一座城市里陌生人整天成交，背后站着一套支付系统和一个法院。前者便宜而小，后者建起来贵，却让别的一切都变便宜。他的类比里没有第二种支出的位置，而那正是它走到尽头的地方。

  2. Count the checking in something you take part in - a workplace, a school, a building committee. Who checks whom, and what would have to be true for one of those layers to be removed? Write 120 words.
    数一数你参与的某个组织里有多少层检查——单位、学校、业主委员会：谁在查谁？要去掉其中一层，得先有什么条件成立？写 120 词。
  3. Nordic states spend a very large share of national income and are highly developed. Does that refute the lesson, or does it only mean the author was measuring the wrong thing?
    北欧国家花掉国民收入中很大一块，发展水平却很高。这是否推翻了课文的说法，还是只说明作者量错了对象？

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 1第 1 句 (begins开头 “If a nation …”)

  • it is left to somebody to do: the job falls to them. The it is formal and the infinitive is the real subject.it is left to sb to do：「得由某人来……」，it 形式主语，真正主语是 to hold it together。
  • hold something together: to keep it from falling apart.hold sth together：维系、使不散。
  • The spelling essentialy in the original is a misprint; the word is essentially.课文原文 essentialy 是印刷错误，正确拼写是 essentially。

Sentence 3第 3 句 (begins开头 “And it should …”)

  • Another formal it, with the how clause as the real subject.it 形式主语，真正主语是 how 引导的主语从句 how small those resources are。
  • The passive with a modal, should not be forgotten, sounds more detached and formal than We should not forget.被动 + 情态 should not be forgotten：比 We should not forget 更客观正式。

Sentence 9第 9 句 (begins开头 “The administrative overheads …”)

  • to the extent that: in so far as. The more reliable the staff, the lower the cost.to the extent that：在……程度上，即员工越可靠，费用越低。
  • working in the business: a present participle phrase placed after everyone to limit it.working in the business：现在分词作后置定语修饰 everyone。
  • can be trusted to do: a passive with an infinitive — they can be relied on to do it.can be trusted to do：被动 + 不定式，「可以被信任去……」。
  • The that clause modifies way: in the manner that does most for the firm.that 定语从句修饰 way：「以最能促进公司利益的那种方式」。

Sentence 12第 12 句 (begins开头 “and to exercise …”)

  • such ... as ...: as is the relative pronoun and it is the subject inside its own clause — the kind of initiative that belongs to their own area of work.such … as …：as 是关系代词，在从句中作主语（as falls within their sphere）——「属于他们职权范围内的那种主动性」。
  • they can each be trusted: each stands in apposition to they and sits after the modal.they can each be trusted：each 作主语 they 的同位语，放在情态动词后。

Sentence 13第 13 句 (begins开头 “It will be …”)

  • have somebody doing: to keep someone engaged in it (object plus present participle).have sb doing：使某人在做（宾语 + 现在分词作补语）。
  • After without come three infinitives in parallel, all describing the extra man: to check, to keep, to report.without 后面接三个并列的不定式（查他、管住他、再往上报告），都作 another man 的后置定语。
  • It will be low: the It stands for overheads, which the author treats as one single thing, though strictly it should be they. The slip belongs to speech.开头 It will be low 的 It 指 overheads——作者把 overheads 当作单数整体看待（一般应为 they），属于口语化的不严谨。

Sentence 17第 17 句 (begins开头 “As administrative overheads …”)

  • As ..., so ...: as one thing changes, so does the other; the two amounts move together.As …, so …：「随着……，……也……」，表示两个量同步变化。
  • Two infinitives in parallel at the end, both describing the money: some to pay out, some to put back in.句末两个并列的不定式都修饰 money：一部分用来分红，一部分直接投进发展。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

As ..., so ...: two quantities moving togetherAs …, so …：此消彼长的比例对应
As A ..., so B ... means 'as A does this, B does that too'. The main clause after so may be inverted or not; both are correct.
As A …, so B …＝「随着 A……，B 也相应……」；so 引导的主句常倒装或不倒装，两种都对。

In this lesson the clause after so keeps the ordinary word order.

本课：As administrative overheads rise, so …（不倒装，主句照常语序）。

The inverted form: As the wind rose, so did the waves. (so + auxiliary + subject, used when the main clause is short)

倒装形式：As the wind rose, so did the waves.（so + 助动词 + 主语，用于简短的主句）

Another common use means 'just as one thing is true, so is the other': Just as the French love their wine, so the British love their tea.

另一种常见用法表「正如……，……也……」：Just as the French love their wine, so the British love their tea.

The same idea with the + comparative: The higher the overheads, the lower the earnings.

同义表达：the + 比较级, the + 比较级——The higher the overheads, the lower the earnings.

  • As prices go up, so demand goes down.
  • Just as a business needs trust, so does a nation.
to the extent that and to some extentto the extent that / to some extent：表程度
to the extent that means 'in so far as', and the clause says what the degree depends on; to some extent and to a large extent are the ready-made phrases for 'partly' and 'largely'.
to the extent that ＝「在……的程度上；就……而言」，从句说明程度的依据；to some/a large extent 是常用短语「在某种/很大程度上」。

In this lesson the phrase ties the two together: how low the cost is depends on how far everyone can be trusted.

本课：管理费用低到什么程度，取决于人人是否可信——to the extent that 把两者绑在一起。

And again: in so far as the people can be counted on, the state needs no crowds of police and officials.

又一处：To the extent that 人民可靠，政府就不需要成群的警察和公务员。

It can be replaced by insofar as, or in so far as, which are more formal still.

可换成 insofar as（正式）或 in so far as。

  • I agree with you to some extent.
  • To the extent that he works hard, he deserves the money.
The passive infinitive, and phrasal verbs in the passive: can be trusted to do, can be relied on to do被动不定式 + 短语动词的被动：can be trusted to do / can be relied on to do
modal + be + past participle + to do means 'can be counted on to do it'. When a phrasal verb goes into the passive, its preposition or adverb stays with it and cannot be dropped.
「情态动词 + be + 过去分词 + to do」表「能被……去做」；短语动词（rely on, look after, laugh at）变被动时介词/副词保留，不能丢。

rely on somebody becomes somebody can be relied on (the on must stay); look after the children becomes the children are looked after.

rely on sb → sb can be relied on（on 不能省）；look after the children → the children are looked after。

The same family: be expected to do, be supposed to do, be required to do — all of them 'somebody expects or requires it of you'.

同类：be expected to do、be supposed to do、be required to do——都是「被期望/要求做某事」。

  • He can be relied on to keep a secret.
  • The old man was looked after by his daughter.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • it is left to sb to do 得由某人来做
    If the parents do nothing, it is left to the teachers to help the child.
  • A is analogous to B A 类似于 B
    The heart is analogous to a pump.

Keywords for retelling复述关键词

  • disunited nation → government must hold it together → high cost, less for development
  • comparison: a business and its administrative overheads
  • trustworthy staff → one man per job → low overheads

Summary writing摘要写作

Write no more than 80 words: how does the author use the example of a firm to show that a divided nation must spend heavily on government?

用不超过 80 个词概括：作者如何用企业的例子说明一个不团结的国家政府开支必然很高？

Points a good summary covers好摘要要覆盖的要点
  • If a nation does not hold together of itself, the government has to hold it; that costs money, and the money for development shrinks by as much.国家不团结，就得靠政府维系，政府开支增加，用于发展的资源相应减少。
  • A firm's administrative costs stand in the same relation to the firm as the cost of government does to a country.企业的管理费用相当于国家的政府开支。
  • Where the staff can be trusted, one person to a post is enough and costs stay low; where they cannot, crowds of supervisors are needed, costs climb, and profits, dividends and investment all shrink.员工可靠负责，一人一岗即可，管理费用低；员工不可信，就需要大批管理人员，费用上升，利润、红利和投资减少。
  • A nation is no different: a loyal, responsible people needs no armies of police and officials, while a divided one has to be watched and controlled, and that comes dear.国家也一样：人民忠诚负责，就不需要大量警察和公务员；否则政府必须监督控制，开支过高。
Our model summary本站范文

A disunited nation must be held together by its government, which raises the cost of government and leaves fewer resources for development. A business shows why. If every employee can be trusted, one person per job is enough and administrative costs stay low. If nobody can be trusted, armies of supervisors are needed, and profits, dividends and investment fall. Similarly, a loyal people needs few police, but a disunited one must be controlled at great expense.（76 词）
