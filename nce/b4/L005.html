🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

The title of the column tells you what the piece is. "Out of the Air" was a weekly column in The Listener, the BBC magazine that ran from January 1929 to its last issue on 3 January 1991, and it commented on what had just been on the radio. So this is not a treatise on the young; it is a columnist writing against a phrase that was in the air that week. The phrase itself is the point: by the late 1950s and the 1960s, teenagers in Britain had money of their own for the first time, their own music, their own clothes and their own newspapers, and adults responded by inventing a category for them. "The problem of youth" was a stock heading, in the way that a screen-time panic is a stock heading now. Knowing that explains why the essay opens with the phrase in quotation marks: the writer is not introducing a topic, he is refusing one.

专栏名字本身就说明了这篇是什么。Out of the Air 是《听众》周刊的一个每周专栏——《听众》是 BBC 的杂志，1929 年 1 月创刊，1991 年 1 月 3 日出最后一期——专门评论刚刚播出的广播节目。所以这不是一篇论青年的专著，而是一个专栏作家在跟那一周流行的一句话较劲。那句话本身就是要害：到 1950 年代末和 1960 年代，英国的少年人第一次手里有了自己的钱、自己的音乐、自己的服装和自己的报纸，成年人的反应是为他们发明一个类别。「青年问题」是当时的通用标题，就像今天的「孩子玩手机」是通用标题一样。知道这一点，就明白文章为什么把那句话加了引号开头：作者不是在引入一个话题，而是在拒绝一个话题。

✨ How it is written赏析

The opening three sentences do a piece of work worth studying. Sentence one quotes the phrase and attributes it to nobody in particular (people are always talking about). Sentence two casts doubt on whether the thing exists at all, and inside the doubt turns the accusation round. Sentence three refuses the premise outright by stating something nobody can deny: that the young are human beings. In nine lines the writer has taken a question that was asked about young people and made it a question about the people asking. If you ever have to answer a loaded question — why are your generation so lazy, why is your country so aggressive — that is the shape to use: do not supply an answer, dispute whether the thing being asked about is real, and ask who benefits from the framing. Two other devices repay attention. One is the pair of mirrored sentences about the future, which look balanced and are not: the same adjective is applied to both men, and only the preposition changes, so the reader does the demolition. The other is the long run of hard words near the end — conceited, ill-mannered, presumptuous, fatuous. The writer concedes everything his opponent could say about the young, piles it up, and then refuses to draw the expected conclusion. Granting your opponent's facts and denying the inference is a stronger move than disputing the facts.

开头三句做了一件值得研究的事。第一句引出那句话，却不归给任何具体的人（人人都在谈）。第二句怀疑这件事究竟存不存在，并在怀疑里把指控反转了方向。第三句干脆拒绝前提，办法是陈述一件谁都无法否认的事：年轻人到底是人。九行之内，作者把一个关于年轻人的问题，变成了一个关于提问者的问题。你哪天要应付一个预设立场的问题——你们这代人为什么这么懒、你们国家为什么这么好斗——用的就是这个形状：不要供上答案，先争论被问的那件事是否真实，再问这个提法对谁有利。另有两处手法值得留意。一是关于前途的那对镜像句子：看起来对称，其实不是——同一个形容词用在两个人身上，只换了介词，拆台的活让读者自己干。二是接近结尾处那串重词：自负、无礼、放肆、愚昧。作者把对手能说出的关于年轻人的坏话全盘承认、堆起来，然后拒绝得出预期的结论。承认对手的事实而否认他的推论，比直接否认事实有力。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The essay's main empirical bet has since been tested, and it won. In 2019 John Protzko and Jonathan Schooler published five preregistered studies in Science Advances under the title "Kids these days". Their finding is close to this writer's: adults judge today's children harshly, and they do it selectively — the more authoritarian a person is, the more likely they are to say the young have lost respect for authority; the better read a person is, the more likely they are to say the young no longer read. The researchers attribute the effect to a biased memory of one's own childhood rather than to deliberate unfairness, and they note that it would explain why complaints about the young have sounded the same for thousands of years. Worth noting, in fairness to the critics, is that the same study did not find the effect for intelligence, which the authors take as evidence that the complaint is not simply a general feeling of superiority. The part of the essay that has aged is the picture of what the young are like. The writer admires them for having no devotion to material things and for not being anxious social climbers. That reads oddly now: surveys of young people in rich countries find them more anxious about money, housing and work than their parents were at the same age, for the plain reason that the housing and the job are harder to get. The character he was describing was partly a product of a particular decade — full employment, cheap rent, a rising curve — and not a property of being twenty. So keep the argument and let go of the portrait. The argument is that the complaint tells you more about the complainer than about the young; the portrait was a snapshot of one lucky cohort.

这篇随笔最主要的那个事实性赌注后来被检验过，而且它赢了。2019 年，John Protzko 与 Jonathan Schooler 在《科学进展》上以《Kids these days》为题发表了五项预注册研究。结论与这位作者很接近：成年人对今天的孩子评价严苛，而且评得有选择——一个人越威权，就越可能说年轻人不尊重权威；一个人读书越多，就越可能说年轻人不读书了。研究者把这个效应归因于人对自己童年的记忆有偏差，而不是故意不公，并指出这可以解释为什么几千年来对年轻人的抱怨听起来都一样。为对批评者公平起见，也要说：同一项研究在「智力」这一项上没有发现这个效应，作者据此认为这种抱怨不只是一种笼统的自觉优越。这篇随笔真正过时的部分是它对年轻人的画像。作者赞赏他们不迷恋物质、不是焦虑的向上攀爬者。今天读来有点怪：富裕国家对年轻人的调查发现，他们对钱、住房和工作的焦虑超过他们父母在同龄时的程度，原因很朴素——房子和工作更难拿到了。他描述的那种性格，部分是某一个年代的产物：充分就业、房租便宜、曲线向上，而不是「二十岁」这件事本身的属性。所以留下论证，放掉画像。论证是：这种抱怨告诉你的，关于抱怨者的比关于年轻人的多；而画像只是某一个幸运世代的一张快照。

💬 Discuss and write讨论与写作
  1. Write down the complaint you most often hear about your own generation, then ask the study's question of the person who says it: what are they good at, and is the complaint about exactly that?
    写下你最常听到的、关于你自己这一代人的抱怨，然后对说这话的人问一遍那项研究的问题：他擅长的是什么？这个抱怨针对的正好是那件事吗？
  2. The writer concedes every bad thing said about the young and still refuses the conclusion. Pick an argument you are on the losing side of and try the same move in 150 words.
    作者承认了所有关于年轻人的坏话，仍然拒绝那个结论。挑一个你处于下风的争论，用 150 词试一次同样的做法。
  3. Is his portrait of the young true where you live — free of money worries, indifferent to status? If not, what changed: the young, or their circumstances?
    他对年轻人的那幅画像，在你生活的地方成立吗——不愁钱、不在意地位？如果不成立，变的是年轻人，还是他们的处境？

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

The cleft sentence: It is ... that / who ...强调句 It is … that/who …
Put the part you want to stress between It is or It was and that (who, for people), and leave the rest of the sentence in its normal order after it. Take away It is ... that and what remains is still a complete sentence.
把要强调的成分放在 It is/was 与 that（指人可用 who）之间，其余部分照原语序放在后面；去掉 It is … that 后句子仍然完整。

How to recognise one: remove it is and that or who. If the sentence still reads properly, it is a cleft sentence; if it does not, you are probably looking at a relative clause or a subject clause.

判断强调句：去掉 it is 和 that/who，句子仍通顺 → 强调句；不通顺 → 可能是定语从句或主语从句。

You can stress a subject, an object or an adverbial, but not the main verb: It was in 1990 that I met her. / It was because he was ill that he left.

可强调主语、宾语、状语（不能强调谓语动词）：It was in 1990 that I met her. / It was because he was ill that he left.

  • It is the teachers, not the students, who need to change.
  • It was not until midnight that he came back.
The unreal form after as if / as thoughas if / as though 的虚拟
To describe something contrary to present fact, as if takes a past tense (with were for be); contrary to past fact, it takes the past perfect. When the thing described is genuinely possible, the ordinary indicative is fine.
as if 后描述与现在事实相反的情况用一般过去时（be 用 were），与过去事实相反用过去完成时；如果是真实可能的情况，也可用陈述语气。

The time relations: He talks as if he knew everything (he does not know). / He talked as if he had seen a ghost (he had not seen one).

时间关系：He talks as if he knew everything.（现在不知）/ He talked as if he had seen a ghost.（之前没见过）

  • She looks as if she is tired.（真的可能累了，用陈述语气）
  • He treats me as if I were a child.
elder and older; the young and the old (the + adjective)elder / older；the young / the old（the + 形容词）
the + adjective names a class of people and takes a plural verb when it is the subject: the young are ..., the old are ... elder works only before a noun or as a noun meaning an older relative, and never takes than; older does.
the + 形容词表示一类人，作主语时谓语用复数：the young are…, the old are…；elder 只作定语或名词（长辈），不接 than，older 可接 than。

Keywords for retelling复述关键词

  • "the problem of youth"? — if any, older people create it
  • the young are human beings like their elders
  • one difference: glorious future before vs splendid future behind
  • as a teenager: young and uncertain; being a problem = an identity
  • young people: free, not social climbers, not materialistic — "cosmic beings"
  • no clichés about respect for elders; argue with them as equals

Summary writing摘要写作

In no more than 80 words, sum up the writer's view of young people. Bring in three things: what he makes of the phrase 'the problem of youth', what he admires in the young, and the rule he follows in dealing with them.

用不超过 80 个词概括作者对青年的看法。要写出：他对「青年问题」的态度、他欣赏青年的地方、他与青年相处的原则。

Points a good summary covers好摘要要覆盖的要点
  • The writer doubts there is any such problem; and if there is, it is the old who have made it.作者怀疑存在所谓青年问题；即使有，也是老年人制造的。
  • Young and old are alike human; the one difference is whether the good years lie ahead or behind.青年和老人都是人，区别只在于前途在前还是在后。
  • As a boy he felt only raw and unsure, and being treated as a problem would at least have given him someone to be.他少年时只觉得自己稚嫩不安，被当作「问题」反而能获得一种身份感。
  • What he admires is their freedom, their indifference to petty ambition and to possessions, which puts them closer to where life begins.他欣赏青年的自由、不庸俗、不追逐物质，觉得他们更接近生命本源。
  • Even when a young person is rude or full of himself, the writer does not fall back on the old saying about respecting your elders; he argues it out on level terms.即使青年无礼或自负，他也不搬出「尊老」的老话，而是平等地与之争论。
Our model summary本站范文

The writer doubts whether youth is a problem; if it is, older people create it. The young are just like their elders, except that their future lies ahead. He finds young people exciting because they are free, unambitious in a mean way and not materialistic. Even when a young person is rude or foolish, he does not demand respect for age but argues with him as an equal.（68 词）
