Lesson 37 · The Process of Ageing 衰老过程

📘 New Concept English · Book 4 · Lesson 37 “The Process of Ageing”新概念英语 第四册 Lesson 37《The Process of Ageing》

C1🧠 31 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
A human being is least likely to die at twelve. After that, vigour leaks away — too slowly to notice at first, and at the end so fast that no amount of care and no amount of medicine can keep us here. That is ageing. The author, a biologist, goes one step further: people take ageing for granted, as though it were the kettle going cold or a pair of shoes going through at the sole, and it is not like that at all. A watch cannot mend itself; a human being once could. Ageing is the loss of the power of self-repair. This is scientific argument, with long sentences and clauses inside clauses: with each one, find out first what the it stands for and what the that is doing.
人在 12 岁时最不容易死；此后活力一点点流失，起初察觉不到，最后陡到再怎么保养、医学再怎么发达也活不下去——这就是衰老。作者（生物学家）进一步指出：人们以为衰老像水壶变凉、鞋子磨破一样「理所当然」，其实不然；手表不会自我修复，人却曾经能，衰老的本质是丧失自我修复能力。本课是科学说理文，句子长、从句层层嵌套，很多句子要先找 it 指代什么、that 引导的是什么。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. At what point in life is death least likely, and why?人一生中什么时候死亡的可能性最小？为什么？
  2. Why does the author say human ageing cannot be compared with a watch wearing out?作者为什么说人的衰老和手表的磨损不能类比？
  3. What would happen if we kept the vigour we have at twelve?如果人一直保持 12 岁时的活力，会怎样？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

The lesson is credited to Alex Comfort and to his book The Process of Ageing, which appeared in 1964 as a reworking of his earlier study of senescence, first published in 1956. Comfort (1920-2000) was among the leading gerontologists of the second half of the century, and is now remembered mainly for a quite different book he wrote in 1972 about sex.

课文署名 Alex Comfort 与他的《The Process of Ageing》，该书 1964 年出版，是他 1956 年那本关于衰老的研究的改写本。康福特（1920–2000）是二十世纪后半叶最重要的老年学家之一，而今天多数人记得他，是因为他 1972 年写的另一本题材完全不同的书。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

The author had two careers that pulled in opposite directions, and both are in this page. One was laboratory biology: he spent years measuring how long animals live and arguing that senescence is a biological process with causes, not a fact of nature to be accepted. The other was a lifetime of public argument against authority of every kind: he was a lifelong pacifist who took part in civil disobedience. Put the two together and the tone of the lesson makes sense. The sentence that matters is the quiet one about the kettle and the shoes. For centuries, treating ageing as self-evident had a consequence: nothing was investigated, because nobody investigates the obvious. His move is to deny the obviousness, and a research programme follows from that denial. That is also why he insists on pulling the watch analogy apart instead of simply dropping it. The analogy is the enemy: as long as a person is a machine running down, there is nothing to study.

作者有两条互相拉扯的生涯，这一页里两条都在。一条是实验室生物学：他花了多年测量各种动物活多久，主张衰老是一个有原因的生物学过程，不是一件该接受的自然事实。另一条是毕生对各种权威的公开反抗：他是终身的和平主义者，参加过非暴力抗命。把两条放在一起，这一课的语气就说得通了。真正要紧的是关于水壶和鞋子的那句平淡的话：几百年来把衰老当作「理所当然」有一个后果——没有人去研究它，因为没有人研究显而易见的事。他的动作是否认这个「显而易见」，而一个研究纲领就从这个否认里长出来。这也是他为什么非要把手表那个类比拆开、而不是直接丢掉：类比就是敌人——只要人还是一台正在走停的机器，就没有什么可研究的。

✨ How it is written赏析

Notice what he does with the analogy, because it is the best thing in the passage. He does not say the comparison is wrong. He grants it first, and generously: most animals really do age, a wound watch and the sun really do run down according to the second law. Only then does he take the watch apart in three steps - one that has merely run down remains the same instrument and takes another winding; an old one is worn past the point of mending; and no such instrument could ever have mended itself, having no living parts, only metal that friction eats away. The reader is left holding the distinction the author wanted all along, and feels it was his own idea. The second device is the one number at the end. After a page of qualitative argument he drops in a figure - keep the vigour of twelve and it would take some seven centuries for half of us to go - and that single number does more to make ageing look like a disease, rather than a destiny, than any amount of insistence could. Save your strongest number for last, and let the reader do the arithmetic of the implication.

注意他怎么处理那个类比，这是全篇最漂亮的地方。他不说这个比较错了，而是先大方承认：多数动物确实会老，上了弦的表和太阳确实按第二定律耗尽能量。然后才分三步拆表：走停的表还是表，可以再上弦；旧表磨损到不值得修；而表永远不可能自我修复，因为它没有活的零件，只有被摩擦一点点吃掉的金属。读者最后手里握着作者一开始就想给他的那个区分，却觉得是自己想到的。第二招是结尾那一个数字：一页定性论证之后，他丢进一个数——保持十二岁的活力，要七百年左右我们才死掉一半——这一个数字让衰老看起来像疾病而不像命运，效果超过任何强调。把最有力的数字留到最后，让读者自己算出它的含义。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

His biology has aged well; his arithmetic has not. The idea that ageing is the loss of the power of repair is now the organizing idea of the field, which lists a dozen named processes - damage to DNA, shortening telomeres, worn-out protein recycling, cells that stop dividing but will not leave, stem cells that run out - and every one of them is a repair system failing rather than a part wearing thin. Note what that framing allows him to claim and what it does not. It allows ageing to be studied as a mechanism; it does not promise that the mechanism can be switched off, and no demonstration in a human being has yet shown that it can. The number is where the lesson needs correcting, and pleasantly so. Seven hundred years implies an annual risk of death of about one in a thousand, which is roughly what a twelve-year-old in Britain faced when he wrote. A child of that age in a rich country today faces about one in seven thousand - the American rate for ages five to fourteen in 2023 was 14.7 deaths per hundred thousand - and running his own sum on that figure gives not seven hundred years but something near four thousand seven hundred. The point survives, enormously strengthened: whatever kills us, it is not the hazard a healthy child lives with. One more thing has moved. He writes of a virtual limit, and the longest life ever documented, 122 years, has stood since 1997 and remains the ceiling in practice. But whether the limit is a wall or a slope is now a live argument: a 2018 study of nearly four thousand Italians aged a hundred and five and over found the risk of dying stops climbing and stays flat past that age, and that the straight-line fit drawn through the sixties and seventies badly overshoots it, which would mean there is no fixed wall, only a probability so punishing that almost nobody gets through. For Chinese readers there is a sharp local version of his point. Within one lifetime, life expectancy in China went from the mid-forties to the high seventies, and almost all of that came from not dying young - infections, childbirth, childhood illness - rather than from ageing more slowly. That is his distinction made visible on a national scale: we have become very good at the accidents and the diseases he brackets out, and no better at all at the decline he is actually writing about.

他的生物学老得很好，他的算术没有。「衰老就是修复能力的丧失」如今是整个领域的组织性想法，而这个领域列出了十来个有名字的过程——DNA 损伤、端粒缩短、蛋白质回收系统失灵、不再分裂又不肯离场的细胞、耗尽的干细胞——每一个都是一套修复系统在失效，而不是某个零件被磨薄。要注意这个框架允许他主张什么、不允许什么：它允许把衰老当成机制来研究，但并不承诺这个机制能被关掉，而在人身上至今没有任何实证说明它能。需要更正的是那个数字，而更正的结果很愉快：七百年意味着每年约千分之一的死亡风险，大致就是他写作时英国一个十二岁孩子面对的水平。今天富裕国家同龄孩子面对的大约是七千分之一——2023 年美国 5–14 岁的死亡率是每十万人 14.7——用他自己的算法代入这个数，得到的不是七百年，而是接近四千七百年。他的论点活了下来，而且强得多：无论最后杀死我们的是什么，都不是一个健康孩子日常面对的那种风险。还有一件事变了：他说存在一个「实际上的限度」，而有记载的最长寿命 122 岁自 1997 年起无人超过，实际上仍是天花板；但这个限度是一道墙还是一个坡，如今有争论——2018 年一项针对近四千名 105 岁以上意大利人的研究发现，死亡风险在那个年龄之后停止攀升、保持平直，而按六七十岁那段画出的直线会大幅高估它，那意味着没有固定的墙，只有一个苛刻到几乎没人穿得过去的概率。对中国读者，他的论点有一个很尖锐的本地版本：在一代人之内，中国的预期寿命从四十几岁升到七十几岁，而这几乎全部来自「不再年轻时死掉」——传染病、生育、儿童疾病——而不是来自衰老变慢。这正是他那个区分在国家尺度上的显形：我们在他括号外的那些意外与疾病上变得非常擅长，而在他真正要写的那种衰退上一点也没有变好。

💬 Discuss and write讨论与写作
  1. The author brackets out wars, accidents and diseases to get at ageing itself. Try the same separation on a person you know who is old: which of their difficulties are ageing, and which are illness or circumstance that happen to arrive with it?
    作者把战争、意外和疾病括到外面，才谈得上衰老本身。拿这个区分去看你认识的一位老人：他的哪些困难属于衰老，哪些其实是恰好同时到来的疾病或处境？
  2. If his sum were run again today it gives thousands of years rather than seven hundred, because children are so much safer now. Write 120 words on which of the two numbers makes the better argument, and why.
    如果今天重算他那笔账，得到的是几千年而不是七百年，因为孩子如今安全得多。写 120 词：两个数字里哪一个更有说服力，为什么？
  3. His point is that no machine of metal can mend itself. What repairs are you making in yourself right now without knowing it, and what would you notice first if one of them stopped?
    他说表永远不可能自我修复。此刻你身上有哪些修复正在无声进行？如果其中一项停了，你最先会察觉到什么？

  • which we all make: a relative clause describing discoveries.which we all make：定语从句修饰 discoveries。

  • the fact that man ages: an appositive clause saying what the fact is.the fact that man ages：同位语从句。
  • Inside the object clause, the subject carries two of phrases, so its verb turns up very late.assumed 的宾语从句里，主语 the process … 后带两个 of 短语，谓语 was 很晚才出现。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

The formal it: when the real subject is a that clauseit 作形式主语：真正的主语是 that 从句
When the subject is a that clause, an infinitive or a gerund and it runs long, English puts it in the subject's place and sends the real subject to the end, so that the sentence is not top-heavy.
当主语是 that 从句、不定式或动名词且较长时，用 it 占主语位置，把真正的主语放到句末，避免头重脚轻。

In this lesson the real subject is the three that clauses at the end: that we must decline, that we shall at last die of old age, and that the rate hardly varies from one person to another. They are far too long to stand at the front.

本课：It is one of … discoveries …，真正的主语是后面三个并列的 that 从句（我们必然这样衰退、我们终将「老死」、衰老速率人人相差不大）——太长了，只能放后面。

Telling it from a cleft sentence: take away the It is ... that. If what is left cannot stand as a sentence, the it was a formal subject; if it can, the sentence was a cleft one (Lesson 26).

与强调句区分：去掉 It is … that 后，剩下的部分不能组成完整句子 → 形式主语句；能组成 → 强调句（L26）。

Do that here and what remains will not stand on its own, so this it is a formal subject.

本课这一句去掉 It is … that 之后剩下的部分不成句，所以是形式主语，不是强调句。

The common frames: It is clear / obvious / true that ...; It is a pity that ...; It seems that ...; It is said that ...

常见句型：It is clear/obvious/true that …；It is a pity that …；It seems that …；It is said that …

  • It is a pity that you can't come.
  • It is well known that smoking is harmful.
the + comparative, the + comparativethe + 比较级, the + 比较级
'the more ..., the more ...'. Each half opens with the + comparative, followed by its subject and verb, either of which may be left out.
「越……就越……」，两个分句各以 the + 比较级开头，后接主语和谓语（常可省略）。

In this lesson the half-clause the older we get implies the other half: the more likely we are to die.

本课：…the older we get（隐含「越老，就越可能死」）。

The key point of form: whatever belongs with the comparative moves forward with it — The more books you read, the more you know (books travels with more; not The more you read books).

结构要点：比较级后面的成分紧跟着它前移——The more books you read, the more you know.（books 跟着 more 前移，✗ The more you read books）

The first half often takes the present simple for a future meaning, while the second may take will: The harder you work, the more you will earn.

前句常用一般现在时表将来，后句可用将来时：The harder you work, the more you will earn.

Both halves can be cut right down: The sooner, the better.

可省略：The sooner, the better.（越快越好）

  • The older we grow, the wiser we become.
  • The more you practise, the better your English will be.
Positive in form, negative in sense: have yet to, all but, no longer, least含否定意味的肯定形式：have yet to, all but, no longer, least
Some patterns carry no not and still mean a negative, or something close to one. Turn them over in your head as you read.
有些结构形式上没有 not，意思却是否定或接近否定，读的时候要转换过来。

Four of them in this lesson: has yet to for 'not so far', no longer for 'not any more', all but for 'all except', and least for 'smallest of all'.

本课四处：has yet to reach …（还没有达到）、can live no longer（不能再活下去）、all but the most … fatal（除最致命的以外，全都能克服）、the likelihood is least（可能性最小）。

all but has two uses: with a noun it means 'all except'; with an adjective or a participle it means 'almost' — The work is all but finished.

all but 有两个意思：+ 名词 ＝「除……外全部」；+ 形容词/动词 ＝「几乎」：The work is all but finished.（几乎完成了）

anything but means the exact opposite: He is anything but a fool.

anything but ＝「绝不」：He is anything but a fool.（他绝不是傻瓜）

Points a good summary covers好摘要要覆盖的要点

Our model summary本站范文

The human body is strongest, and least likely to die, at twelve. After that it slowly loses vigour until, whatever care we take, we can live no longer; this is ageing. It happens at much the same rate in everyone, so most people die between 65 and 80. We tend to think ageing is like a machine wearing out, but a watch cannot repair itself, while we once could. Ageing is the gradual loss of that power.（77 词）
