📘 New Concept English · Book 4 · Lesson 27 “The vasa”新概念英语 第四册 Lesson 27《The vasa》

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
In 1628 the Swedish king's new flagship, whose size and guns he had settled himself, left harbour on her first voyage with the whole city watching and the guns of the fleet saluting - and a few minutes later a squall tipped her over, water poured in through the open gun-ports, and she went down in her home port. This is narrative description: the first half is loaded with splendour (ablaze, majestic, a riot of carved gods), and then the pace changes abruptly (listing, heaved, sliding, crashing). The thing to study here is how the writer opens a scene out, layer by layer, with absolute constructions and participles.
1628 年，瑞典国王亲自定下尺寸和火炮的旗舰「瓦萨」号首航出港，全城围观、礼炮齐鸣——几分钟后，一阵狂风让它侧倾、海水从炮门灌入，就在母港里沉没。本课是叙事描写文：前半段堆满华丽的形容（ablaze, majestic, riot of carved gods），后半段节奏骤然加快（listing, heaved, sliding and crashing）。学这一课，重点看作者怎样用独立主格和分词把一个画面一层层铺开。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. Why is the ship called a floating work of art?「瓦萨」号为什么被称作「漂浮的艺术品」？
  2. What did the ordnance officer order once the ship began to lean, and why did it not work?船开始倾斜后，军械官采取了什么措施？为什么没用？
  3. What is ironic about the writer's last few words, which name the port where the ship had been built?文章最后点出这艘船沉在建造它的那个港口，有什么讽刺意味？

The date of the piece we could not confirm; Saunders also published a book under that title in the early 1960s: not verified.

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

This is salvage journalism, written while the work was going on. The wreck was found in 1956 by Anders Franzen, an engineer who had spent years reading old records and probing the harbour mud with a core sampler, and the hull was lifted in April 1961 after cables had been washed through tunnels under it. A reader of the BBC weekly in those years was following the story in instalments, which is why the piece spends so long on the splendour of the ship: the salvage was news, and the ship had to be made worth saving in the reader's mind first. Two facts about the day itself belong here, because the lesson leaves them out. Before the voyage an admiral watched thirty men run from side to side across the deck to test her; after three runs the rolling was so bad that he stopped the test. He did not stop the voyage, and he did not tell the king. The ship had been built tall and narrow to carry two full tiers of guns, and there was too little weight low down to hold her upright.

这是一篇「打捞现场」的新闻写作，写于工程进行之中。沉船由工程师安德斯·弗兰岑在 1956 年找到——他读了多年旧档，又用取样管在港泥里一处处探；1961 年 4 月，在船底冲出隧道、穿入钢缆之后，船体被整体吊起。那几年读 BBC 周刊的人是分期追这条新闻的，这也解释了文章为什么在船的华美上花那么多笔墨：打捞是新闻，而船得先在读者心里值得被救。关于出事那天，还有两件该放在这里的事实，课文没有写。首航之前，一位海军将领看着三十个人在甲板上来回奔跑做稳定性试验；跑了三趟，船晃得太厉害，他叫停了试验。他没有叫停首航，也没有报告国王。为装两层满编炮位，这船造得又高又窄，而低处的压载重量不足，扶不住它。

✨ How it is written赏析

The piece is two pieces of writing joined at a hinge. The first part is an accumulation: a long subject that keeps the verb waiting, a catalogue of carved gods and demons and mermaids, colours named rather than described, and then four absolute constructions in a row - the flags, the pennants, the sails, the gilded stern - so that the ship is assembled in front of the reader like a camera moving in. Everything in that half is still, even the motion. Then the wind freshens and the grammar changes with it. Sentences get shorter, the verbs do the work and they are all verbs of weight and slide, and the long list that was carvings a moment ago is now cargo, ballast, ammunition and people going the same way at once. Two technical points to steal. First, the writer calls the ship she throughout, which is why the ending can be so cruel in six words: the harbour where she was born. Second, notice what he does not do: there is no explanation, no engineering, no blame. The description carries the judgement, and a reader who has just been shown gun-ports left open does not need to be told what they will do.

这篇文章是两段写法接在一个铰上。前半是堆积：长主语让谓语一直等着，雕像清单一个接一个（神、魔、骑士、人鱼），颜色只点出名字而不加描摹，然后连着四个独立主格——旗、三角旗、帆、镀金的船尾——像镜头推近一样，把这条船在读者眼前组装起来。那半篇里的一切都是静的，连动作也是静的。接着风变紧了，语法跟着变：句子变短，动词开始干活，而且全是重量与滑动的动词；一分钟前还是雕刻清单的那串列举，现在变成了货物、压舱物、弹药和人同时朝一边去。有两处技术值得偷。第一，作者全篇用「她」称这条船，所以结尾那六个词才那么狠：她出生的那个港口。第二，注意他没做什么：没有解释，没有工程分析，没有追责。判断由描写承担——刚被告知炮门全开的读者，不需要别人再说那会发生什么。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The description is reliable; one number in it is not. The best accounts put between about 150 and 250 people on board, crew with wives and children invited for the occasion, and about thirty deaths; the remains of at least fifteen people were found in and around the hull in 1961. The four hundred in the lesson is too high, and it matters, because the sentence uses that figure as part of the weight that slid to one side. The cause is better understood now than the piece lets on: too narrow a hull for two gun decks, too little ballast, and a king who had settled the dimensions himself and was waiting for his flagship. The failed stability test shows that at least two senior officers knew. There is also a part of the story the writer could not know. Raising the ship started a second, slower emergency. Sulphur compounds left in the timbers by the harbour mud met the air and turned into sulphuric acid - estimates of around two tonnes of it in the wood, with much more possible if all the sulphur oxidises - so the preserved hull has spent decades being treated, measured and held in a climate-controlled hall, and the conservation question is still open. For a Chinese reader the obvious comparison is Nanhai One, the Southern Song merchant ship lifted out of the sea off Guangdong in December 2007 inside a steel caisson, mud and all, and excavated indoors in a museum built for it. The Swedish choice was to raise the ship and fight chemistry afterwards; the Chinese choice was to lift the sea bed with the ship and do the digging in a hall where the air can be controlled from the first day. Both are expensive; the second is the one that learned from the first.

描写是可靠的，其中一个数字不是。较可信的记述说船上约有 150 至 250 人（船员，以及应邀同行的妻儿），死亡约三十人；1961 年在船体内外至少发现十五人的遗骸。课文里的「四百人」偏高，而这重要——因为那句话正是用这个数字充当滑向一侧的重量。沉因今天也比文章透露的清楚：为两层炮甲板而把船造得过窄、压载不足，再加上一位亲自定下尺寸、正等着自己旗舰的国王。失败的稳定性试验说明至少两位高级军官是知道的。还有一部分是作者当年不可能知道的：把船吊起来，开启了第二场更慢的险情——港泥留在木材里的硫化物遇到空气，变成硫酸，有估计说木头里约有两吨，若硫全部氧化还会更多；于是这具保存下来的船体几十年来一直在被处理、被测量，被放在控温的大厅里，而保护方案至今没有定论。对中国读者来说，最明显的对照是「南海一号」：这条南宋商船于 2007 年 12 月在广东海域被整体装进钢沉箱、连泥一起吊出，运进为它建的博物馆，在室内发掘。瑞典的选择是先把船捞上来、再与化学作斗争；中国的选择是把海床连船一起抬走，在一个从第一天起就能控制空气的大厅里慢慢挖。两种都很贵；后一种是从前一种身上学来的。

💬 Discuss and write讨论与写作
  1. The admiral stopped the test and said nothing to the king. If you had been in his place, what exactly would you have done, and what would it have cost you?
    那位将领叫停了试验，却没对国王说什么。如果你在他的位置上，你具体会怎么做？这会让你付出什么代价？
  2. Write 120 words describing something splendid that failed - a building, a product, an event - using the shape of this lesson: build it up slowly, then let short sentences take it apart.
    写 120 词，描述一件华美而失败的东西（建筑、产品、活动都行），照这一课的结构来：先慢慢堆起来，再用短句把它拆掉。
  3. Is it better to raise a wreck or to leave it where it lies? Give one reason on each side before you answer.
    沉船该捞起来还是留在原地？先给出支持两边的各一条理由，再回答。

Sentence 4第 4 句 (begins开头 “the story of …”)

  • must be here is a guess about the present: it surely counts as one of them.must be 表推测（现在）：「一定算得上」。
  • one of the + superlative + plural noun.one of the + 最高级 + 复数名词：one of the strangest tales。
  • English often refers to a ship as she and her.船在英语里常用 she/her 指代：her maiden voyage。

Sentence 8第 8 句 (begins开头 “then at the …”)

  • The nickname stands in apposition to the king's name.'The Northern Hurricane'：同位语，国王的绰号。
  • had dictated: a past perfect - the king had settled all this before the events of the story.had dictated：过去完成时，船出事前（1628 年前）国王已经亲自定下了这些。

Sentence 21第 21 句 (begins开头 “Then the cannons …”)

  • thunder is used here as a transitive verb, a livelier word than fire.thunder 作及物动词：「隆隆地鸣放」，生动替代 fire。
  • to which: preposition plus relative pronoun (from reply to something), with the preposition moved in front of which.to which：介词 + 关系代词引导定语从句，= and the Vasa fired in reply to it（reply to sth 中的 to 提到 which 前）。

Sentence 27第 27 句 (begins开头 “As the wind …”)

  • there came ...: there + an intransitive verb brings on something new, and is more dramatic than putting the squall first as the subject.there came …：there + 不及物动词（came）引出新出现的事物，比 a sudden squall came 更有戏剧性。
  • listing to port: a present participle giving the result, and telling us what the strange movement was.listing to port：现在分词作结果/伴随状语，补充说明「奇怪的动作」是什么。

Sentence 28第 28 句 (begins开头 “The Ordnance officer …”)

  • order something to be done: the object and the infinitive are in a passive relation, since the guns are the things being moved.order sb/sth to be done：宾语和不定式是被动关系（大炮「被」搬），用不定式被动 to be heaved。
  • to counteract the lean: an infinitive of purpose.to counteract the list：不定式作目的状语。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

The absolute construction独立主格结构
A noun or pronoun + a participle (or an adjective, or a prepositional phrase) carries its own subject, different from the subject of the main clause, and works as an adverbial of attendant circumstance, cause or condition.
名词/代词 + 分词（或形容词、介词短语），自带逻辑主语，不与主句主语一致，作状语表伴随、原因、条件等。

How to spot it: the flags are not the subject of the main clause, so the participle has to bring its own subject with it - that is what absolute means.

判断：flags 与主句主语 she 不同，所以分词前必须带上自己的「主语」flags——这就是「独立」的意思。

It can be rewritten as a clause (as her flags were flying ...); the absolute form is tighter and suits description.

它可以改写成从句：as her flags were flying …；独立主格更紧凑，适合描写。

  • The work done, we went home.（= When the work was done）
  • He sat there, his eyes fixed on the screen.
  • Weather permitting, the match will be held tomorrow.
Relative clauses with preposition + which/whom介词 + which/whom 引导定语从句
When the relative pronoun is the object of a preposition, the preposition may be placed in front of which or whom; which preposition you need is decided by the verb or noun inside the clause.
关系代词在从句里作介词宾语时，介词可提到 which/whom 前；介词由从句中的动词或名词搭配决定。

How to choose: put the antecedent back into the clause - they fired in reply to the salute - and the preposition appears.

怎么选介词：把先行词放回从句里还原——the Vasa fired in reply to the salute → to which。

After a preposition only which (things) or whom (people) is possible, never that or who.

介词后只能用 which（物）或 whom（人），不能用 that 或 who。

Phrasal verbs are usually not split: the child (whom) I look after, not after whom I look.

短语动词（look after, put up with）通常不拆：the child (whom) I look after，不说 after whom I look。

  • This is the house in which I was born.
  • The man to whom you spoke is our manager.
There + an intransitive verb (come, stand, lie, live ...)There + 不及物动词（come, stand, lie, live …）
Besides be, there can be followed by come, appear, stand, lie, live, exist or remain - verbs of being and appearing - to bring something new into the picture.
there 后除了 be，还可接 come、appear、stand、lie、live、exist、remain 等表示存在、出现的不及物动词，用来引出「新出现的东西」。

The verb agrees with the noun that follows: There remain three questions.

谓语与后面的名词一致：There remain three questions.

The pattern belongs to narrative and description, where it makes a scene more vivid than a plain subject and verb.

这种句子多用于叙事、描写，比普通主谓句更有画面感。

  • Once upon a time there lived an old fisherman.
  • There stands a statue in the middle of the square.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • be intended to do 原本打算/指望……（常暗示没做成）
    The bridge was intended to last a hundred years.
  • more … than any previous … 比以往任何……都更……
    The new phone is lighter than any previous model.
  • be ablaze with … 被……照得通明、五彩缤纷
    The city was ablaze with lights.
  • seal sb's/sth's fate 注定了……的命运
    That single mistake sealed his fate.

Summary writing摘要写作

In no more than 80 words, tell the story of the loss: start with where the ship came from and end with what happened on the day.

用不超过 80 个词叙述「瓦萨」号沉没的经过：从它的来历写到首航当天发生的事。

Points a good summary covers好摘要要覆盖的要点
  • She was the royal flagship, her size and guns settled by the king himself, richly decorated and carrying sixty-four bronze guns.「瓦萨」号是瑞典国王亲自定下规格的皇家旗舰，装饰华丽，载有 64 门大炮。
  • On 10 August 1628 she set out on her first voyage, with the city watching and guns firing a salute.1628 年 8 月 10 日首航，全城围观，鸣炮致敬。
  • A squall pushed her over to the left, and moving the guns across to the other side did no good.一阵狂风使船向左舷倾斜，把大炮移到右舷也没用。
  • Cargo, ballast, ammunition and people all slid the same way, and water came in through the lower gun-ports.货物、压舱物、弹药和人都滑向左边，下层炮门进水。

Our model summary本站范文

The Vasa, a richly decorated warship designed on the orders of King Gustavus Adolphus, set out on her maiden voyage in 1628 while crowds watched. Soon after a gun salute, a sudden squall made her list to port. Moving the cannon to the other side failed; everything on board slid to port, water poured in through the lower gun-ports, and she sank in Stockholm harbour. She was not found until 1956.（71 词）
