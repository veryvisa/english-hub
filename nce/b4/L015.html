Lesson 15 · Secrecy in Industry 工业中的保密

📘 New Concept English · Book 4 · Lesson 15 “Secrecy in Industry”新概念英语 第四册 Lesson 15《Secrecy in Industry》

C1🧠 19 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
The writer names two reasons why research inside industry works badly, an atmosphere of secrecy and a researcher with no freedom, and this passage deals only with the first. Secrecy cuts a scientist off from everyone doing the same work; how tight it is varies from firm to firm, and the chemical trade is the worst of all, to the point where nobody dares borrow a book, in case a rival reads the borrowing records and guesses what is being looked into. The style is formal academic argument: in so far as, of such ... nature that, for fear (that) ... should are all worth copying.
作者讨论工业研究效率低的两个原因：保密气氛和研究人员缺乏自由，本课只讲前者。保密让科学家无法与同行交流；保密程度因公司而异，化学工业尤甚，甚至到了不敢去图书馆借书的地步——怕被对手从借书记录里猜出研究方向。文体是正式的学术议论文：in so far as, of such … nature that, for fear (that) … should 等结构值得模仿。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. What are the two things that work against the effectiveness of research inside industry?影响工业科研效率的两个因素是什么？
  2. Why does the chemical trade care about secrecy more than other industries do?为什么化学工业特别注重保密？
  3. Why are some companies unwilling to borrow books from a library?有些公司为什么不愿意从图书馆借书？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

The attribution line in the original Book 4 reads 'J. D. Bernal, The Social Function of Science'. Bernal was an X-ray crystallographer at Cambridge and later London, and one of the most political scientists of his generation; the book, published by Routledge in 1939, grew out of an essay of the year before and argued that research should be planned for public ends rather than left to chance and private money. A retrospective in Nature calls it a book that set itself directly against the ideal of pure science done for its own sake.

旧版第四册课末署名作「J. D. Bernal, The Social Function of Science」。贝尔纳是先在剑桥、后在伦敦工作的 X 射线晶体学家，也是他那一代最有政治色彩的科学家之一；这本书 1939 年由 Routledge 出版，由前一年的一篇长文扩展而成，主张科研应为公共目的作规划，而不该交给偶然和私人资金。《自然》杂志的一篇回顾说，这本书把自己直接摆在「为科学而科学」那种纯科学理想的对面。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

Read the date and the argument changes shape. In 1939 industrial research in Britain was young, small and almost entirely privately owned, and the writer's complaint is not that secrecy is dishonest but that it is wasteful: a chemist who cannot talk to the man doing the same work in the next building has to find the same answer twice. Behind the complaint sits a political programme. He wanted research directed, funded and published as a public activity, and within a few years the war gave Britain a demonstration of what directed science could do, from radar to penicillin production. That is the argument's strongest moment and also its blind spot, because the same years produced the most tightly sealed research project in history.

读出年代，论证的形状就变了。1939 年的英国工业研究还年轻、规模小、几乎全是私营；作者抱怨保密，不是说它不诚实，而是说它浪费：一个化学家没法跟隔壁楼里做同一件事的人说话，同一个答案就得被找两遍。抱怨背后有一套政治主张。他希望科研被统一指挥、公共拨款、公开发表；几年之后，战争正好给英国演示了一遍「有组织的科学」能做什么——从雷达到青霉素量产。这是这套论证最有力的时刻，也是它的盲点，因为同一批年份里还产生了史上封得最严的那个研究项目。

✨ How it is written赏析

The passage is a good place to learn the register of serious argument, because every sentence is doing one job and signposting it. Two causes are named in the first sentence and the second one is left with its verb missing, which is normal in formal parallel structures and worth imitating. Then comes a chain of qualifications: in so far as, naturally, considerably, often enough. A careless reader takes these for padding; in fact each one narrows a claim so that it cannot be knocked down by a single counter-example. The last move is the best. Instead of summing up, the writer produces one small concrete scene, a man who will not put his name on a library card, and lets the reader draw the conclusion. Argue in the abstract, close with one image: that order is far more persuasive than the reverse.

本篇是学「正经论证的语体」的好地方，因为每一句都在做一件事，而且把这件事标示出来。第一句点出两个原因，第二个原因干脆省掉系动词——这在正式的并列结构里很常见，值得模仿。接着是一串限定：in so far as、naturally、considerably、often enough。读得快的人当它们是废话；其实每一个都在收窄一项断言，让它不至于被一个反例一击即倒。最后一手最漂亮：作者不作总结，而是给出一个很小的具体场景——有人不肯把自己的名字写在借书卡上——然后让读者自己得结论。抽象处讲理，收尾给一个画面：这个顺序比反过来有说服力得多。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The waste he describes is real and his remedy lost. Secrecy did not fade away; it acquired a courthouse. In the United States the Defend Trade Secrets Act of 2016 created, for the first time, a federal civil claim for taking a trade secret, and filings moved accordingly: around nine hundred actions a year through 2009 to 2016, then one thousand one hundred and thirty-four in 2017, and of just over thirteen thousand federal trade-secret matters filed between 2016 and 2025 about nine and a half thousand brought the new claim. The commonest case is not a spy but a departing employee. Meanwhile the open side of science grew too, through preprints and public funding rules, so a working scientist today lives in both regimes at once: publish the paper, say nothing about the process. The library card has modern descendants, and they are worse. Who your firm is recruiting, which cloud service your bills show, which conference your people attended, which compound you have ordered: each is a line on somebody's card, and none of them is something you can decline to sign. His question therefore still pays: when you cannot say what you are working on, how much of the cost is paid by you, and how much by everybody else?

他描述的浪费是真的，而他的解法输了。保密没有消失，它拿到了法庭。美国 2016 年的《保护商业秘密法》第一次为「窃取商业秘密」设立联邦民事诉因，案件数随之变化：2009 至 2016 年平均每年约九百起，2017 年升到 1,134 起；2016 至 2025 年联邦法院受理的一万三千余件商业秘密案件中，约九千五百件提出了这项新诉因。最常见的案子不是间谍，而是跳槽的员工。与此同时科学的开放一侧也在长：预印本、公共资金的公开要求。于是今天的科研人员同时活在两套制度里：论文照发，工艺一字不说。借书卡有了现代后代，而且更糟。你所在的公司在招什么人、账单上显示你用哪家云服务、你的人去了哪个会、你订购过哪种试剂：每一条都是别人卡上的一行，而且没有一条是你可以拒签的。所以他的问题至今有回报：当你不能说自己在做什么，这代价有多少由你付，有多少由其他所有人付？

💬 Discuss and write讨论与写作
  1. Think of something you cannot discuss outside work. Who gains from that silence, and what is the cost to people outside the firm?
    想一件你在单位之外不能谈的事。谁从这种沉默中获益？单位之外的人付出了什么代价？
  2. Write 120 words of formal argument on any question, using in so far as, to such an extent that and for fear that once each, and finish with one concrete scene instead of a summary.
    就任何一个问题写 120 词的正式论证，in so far as、to such an extent that、for fear that 各用一次，并以一个具体场景收尾，不作总结。
  3. The writer assumes openness speeds research up. Name a case where keeping quiet for a while clearly helped, and say what made it different.
    作者默认公开会加快研究。举一个「先保密一段时间明显有好处」的例子，并说清它特殊在哪里。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 2第 2 句 (begins开头 “One is the …”)

  • one ... the other ...: of two things, this is the first and that is the second.one … the other …：两者中的一个……另一个……
  • The linking verb is left out after the other; in a parallel structure the repeated verb can be dropped.the other 后省略了 is——并列结构里重复的系动词可省略。

Sentence 5第 5 句 (begins开头 “it naturally limits …”)

  • in so far as: to the extent that.in so far as：在……范围内。
  • limit somebody from something: to leave somebody unable to do it. prevent or keep somebody from is commoner.limit sb from sth：使某人无法……（更常用 prevent/keep sb from）。

Sentence 8第 8 句 (begins开头 “Some of the …”)

  • be of + noun does the work of an adjective: of such ... nature here means so general and so basic.be of + 名词 = 形容词（of such … nature = so general and fundamental）。
  • such ... that: a clause of result.such … that：结果从句。
  • it is ... to do: an empty subject; the infinitive is made negative by putting not in front of to.it is … to do：形式主语；not to keep them secret 是不定式的否定式（not 放 to 前）。

Sentence 10第 10 句 (begins开头 “Yet a great …”)

  • An -ing form after the noun, describing the processes.depending on such research：现在分词作后置定语。
  • at which: preposition + which, describing the stage.at which：介词 + which，修饰 stage。

Sentence 13第 13 句 (begins开头 “where chance discoveries …”)

  • A non-defining where clause describing the chemical industries.where 非限制性定语从句修饰 chemical industries。
  • than they do: do stands in for the repeated verb (see Lesson 13).than they do：do 替代 play a part（见第 13 课）。

Sentence 16第 16 句 (begins开头 “because they are …”)

  • have + object + past participle: to get something done to it, here to get a name written down.have + 宾语 + 过去分词：使某事被做（让名字被登记）。
  • as having done ...: as somebody who has already done it; having done marks the earlier action.as having taken out…：作为「已借出……」的人；having done 表先发生。
  • for fear (that) ... should do: in case; should can be dropped.for fear (that) … should do：唯恐……；should 可省（虚拟）。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

such / so ... that, and be of + abstract nounsuch / so … that 与 be of + 抽象名词
such + (a) + adjective + noun + that; so + adjective or adverb + that; be of + noun (importance, value, use, help, nature...) does the work of be + the matching adjective.
such + (a) + 形容词 + 名词 + that；so + 形容词/副词 + that；be of + 名词（importance, value, use, help, nature…）= be + 相应形容词。
  • This book is of great value. = This book is very valuable.
  • It was such a cold day that we stayed in. = It was so cold a day that…（较书面）
for fear (that) … should / lest；in so far as
for fear (that) and lest open a negative clause of purpose, in case or so as to avoid; the clause often takes should + the bare verb, and should can be dropped. for fear of takes a noun or an -ing form. in so far as / insofar as means to the extent that.
for fear (that) / lest 引导否定目的状语从句，「唯恐、以免」，从句常用 should + 动词原形（可省 should）；for fear of + 名词/动名词。in so far as / insofar as ＝ 在……的范围内。
  • He wrote down the address for fear (that) he should forget it.
  • She spoke quietly for fear of waking the baby.
one … the other …；have sth done
Of two things use one for the first and the other for the second; with three or more, one ... another ... the third, or some ... others. have + object + past participle means to get something done to it, or to suffer it.
两者中一个用 one，另一个用 the other；三者以上：one … another … the third / some … others …。have + 宾语 + 过去分词：让别人做/遭遇某事。
  • I have two sisters: one is a nurse, the other a teacher.
  • I had my hair cut yesterday.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • In so far as …, it naturally … 只要……，自然就会……
    In so far as a meeting has no clear purpose, it naturally wastes everyone's time.
  • … are of such … nature that … ……性质如此……，以至于……
    Some problems are of such a personal nature that they cannot be discussed in class.
  • This applies particularly to …, where … 这一点在……尤其如此，因为在那里……
    This applies particularly to small towns, where everyone knows everyone else.
  • … for fear … should … 唯恐……会……
    She kept the letter for fear her parents should find it.

Summary writing摘要写作

In no more than 80 words, set out the problem of secrecy in industrial research: why it costs efficiency, how far it varies, why the chemical trade is the worst case, and what the extreme looks like.

用不超过 80 个词说明工业研究中的保密问题：为什么影响效率、程度如何、化学工业为何最甚、极端情况是什么。

Points a good summary covers好摘要要覆盖的要点
  • An atmosphere of secrecy, and researchers with no freedom, both weaken the effectiveness of research inside industry.保密气氛与研究人员缺乏自由，削弱了工业科研的效率。
  • Once a piece of work is secret, the people on it can hardly talk to anyone doing the same thing abroad, at a university, or even in another department of the same company.研究一旦保密，科研人员就难以与国内外、大学乃至本公司其他部门的同行交流。
  • How tight it is varies: some basic work is better off open, many methods are kept quiet until a patent is granted, and more are never patented at all.保密程度各异：有些基础研究公开反而有利，但许多工艺在拿到专利前严格保密，更多工艺干脆永不申请专利。
  • The chemical trade depends more than others on discoveries nobody planned, so it keeps the tightest hold.化学工业靠偶然发现的成分大，所以尤其保密。

Our model summary本站范文

Secrecy greatly reduces the value of industrial research, since it cuts scientists off from colleagues in other countries, universities and even other departments. Its degree varies: some fundamental research is better made public, but many processes are kept secret until patented, and others are never patented. Chemical firms, which depend heavily on chance discoveries, are the most secretive; some will not even borrow library books for fear rivals might guess their research.（72 词）
