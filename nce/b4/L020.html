🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

Two habits of mind date this piece more precisely than any word in it. The first is the way Nature is written as a person: she selects, she concocts, she works from an improved formula. That is not carelessness, it is the ordinary popular language of the time for talking about evolution, and it quietly imports the idea of a designer choosing between options. The second is the confidence of the brackets. The writer knows of exactly one venomous lizard, states it in passing, and moves on; the bracket is where an author puts what he does not expect to be questioned. Both habits are worth noticing before you judge the argument, because the argument rests on them: a chooser who chose only snakes is a much stranger fact than a chemistry that happened to persist in some lineages and not others.

有两个思维习惯，比文中任何一个词都更准确地标出这篇文章的年代。第一是把「自然」写成一个人：她挑选、她调配、她按改良配方工作。这不是随手写错，而是当时谈演化的通俗语言，并且悄悄引进了「有一个设计者在诸选项之间挑选」的念头。第二是括号里的那份自信。作者只知道一种有毒蜥蜴，顺口一提就过去了；括号正是作者放「料想不会被质疑的东西」的地方。在评判这个论证之前，这两个习惯都值得留意，因为论证正架在它们上面：一个「只挑了蛇」的挑选者，比「某些支系里碰巧留下了这套化学、另一些没有」要古怪得多。

✨ How it is written赏析

The whole passage is driven by questions the writer has no intention of answering, and that is a technique, not a failing. Count them: how did it happen, why only one group, why so strong, why two kinds. Each question opens a paragraph and each one hands the reader the author's agenda while sounding like curiosity. Between the questions the tone keeps dropping into comedy, with the venom described as a luxury and a bite set against the indignity of wrestling a grown rabbit, so that heavy material moves lightly. Then two short set phrases do the real structural work. So to speak marks an idea he will not defend, and Be that as it may dismisses the whole preceding paragraph in four words and turns to what he actually wants to say. If you are writing about something you do not fully understand, this is the honest shape to use: ask the question you cannot answer, say plainly which parts are guesswork, and keep the reader with you by being funny about it rather than vague.

整段的推动力是一串作者并不打算回答的问题，这是一种手法，不是缺陷。数一数：是怎样发生的、为什么只有这一类、为什么这么强、为什么分两种。每个问题领起一段，每个问题都把作者的议程交到读者手上，听起来却像好奇。问题之间，语气不断往喜剧里落——把毒液说成奢侈品，把「一口」拿去跟「同一只大兔子扭打」的失体面相比，于是沉重的材料走得很轻。接着是两个短固定说法在干真正的结构活。Be that as it may 用四个词把上一整段撇掉，转向他真正要说的事；另一处则用 so to speak 标出一个他不打算辩护的想法。如果你要写一件自己并没有完全弄懂的事，这就是诚实的形状：问出你答不了的问题，明说哪些部分是猜的，并且靠幽默而不是含糊把读者留住。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

His central puzzle has partly dissolved, because the premise inside the brackets was wrong. By 2006 the count of reptile groups known to have evolved a venom delivery system stood at two, the advanced snakes and the helodermatid lizards, which is two species of lizard and not one; and the paper that reported this went further, proposing that the chemistry arose a single time in a common ancestor of snakes, monitors and iguanas something like a hundred and seventy million years ago, which would make venom far older and far more widespread than snakes. That single-origin proposal is itself disputed, with other workers arguing for several independent origins and pointing out how hard homology is to demonstrate here. Either way the question changes: not why Nature gave it only to snakes, but why so many other lineages kept the machinery switched down. And his last line needs answering too. He says the speed with which the nerve poison kills a human being means nothing, since none of it was aimed at us. As natural history that is correct, and as a conclusion it is the wrong way round. The World Health Organization puts bites at about 5.4 million people a year, of whom between 1.8 and 2.7 million are actually envenomed, with somewhere between 81,000 and 138,000 deaths, and its 2019 strategy sets out to halve the deaths and the permanent injuries by 2030, with three million courses of regionally matched treatment a year as one of the targets. The same report notes that the official counts are too low: in one comparison in a Sri Lankan district, five deaths in eight never reached the hospital figures at all. The chemistry was not meant for us. The farm workers it kills are the part that means something.

他的核心谜题有一部分自己化掉了，因为括号里那个前提是错的。到 2006 年，已知演化出毒液输送系统的爬行类支系计为两个：高等蛇类，和毒蜥科蜥蜴——那是两种蜥蜴，不是一种；而报告这件事的那篇论文走得更远，提出这套化学在蛇、巨蜥与鬣蜥的共同祖先身上只出现过一次，约在一亿七千万年前——这就意味着毒液比蛇古老得多、分布也广得多。这个「单一起源」的提法本身也有争议：另一些研究者主张多次独立起源，并指出在这里要证明同源非常困难。无论哪一说，问题都变了：不是「自然为什么只给了蛇」，而是「为什么那么多别的支系把这套机器一直开得很小」。他的末句也需要一个回答。他说神经毒素杀死一个人的速度说明不了什么，因为这一切本来都不是冲着我们的。作为博物学，这话对；作为结论，它把顺序弄反了。世界卫生组织估计每年约 540 万人被咬，其中 180 万至 270 万人真被注入毒液，死亡在 8.1 万到 13.8 万之间；它 2019 年的战略要在 2030 年前把死亡与永久伤残减半，目标之一是每年三百万剂按区域配型的治疗。同一份报告指出官方数字偏低：斯里兰卡某地区的一次比对中，八成死亡里有五成从未进入医院统计。这套化学不是为我们准备的。它杀死的那些农业劳动者，才是说明得了什么的那部分。

💬 Discuss and write讨论与写作
  1. The writer treats one fact as settled inside a bracket, and that bracket is where he is wrong. Find a bracket or an aside in something you have read recently and ask how the author knows it.
    作者把一个事实当成定局塞进括号，而他错的地方正在那个括号里。在你最近读的东西里找一个括号或插入语，问一问作者凭什么知道它。
  2. Write 120 words on a subject you do not fully understand, using three questions you cannot answer, one aside marked so to speak, and Be that as it may to turn to your real point.
    就一件你并没有完全弄懂的事写 120 词：用三个你答不了的问题、一处用 so to speak 标出的插话，并用 Be that as it may 转到你真正要说的那一点。
  3. Snakebite kills far more people than most risks that frighten us, and almost nobody talks about it. What makes a danger famous?
    蛇咬致死的人远多于多数让我们害怕的风险，而几乎没人谈它。是什么让一种危险变得有名？

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 1第 1 句 (begins开头 “How in came …”)

  • The whole how clause is the subject of the sentence, and is is the verb. Note that the printed text has one letter wrong in that clause.整个 how 从句作主语（主语从句），谓语是 is。课文录入错字：in 应为 it。
  • it came about that ...: this is how it happened; it is an empty subject and the that clause is the real one.it came about that…：……是怎样发生的；it 是形式主语，真主语是 that 从句。

Sentence 2第 2 句 (begins开头 “Over the periods …”)

  • A relative clause; defy analysis means it cannot be analysed.that defies analysis：定语从句；defy analysis 难以分析。

Sentence 4第 4 句 (begins开头 “they could have …”)

  • could have done: they would have been able to, and in fact they did otherwise.could have done：过去本可以（实际上用了毒）。
  • just as ... still do: do stands in for the whole verb phrase already used (see Lesson 13).just as … still do：do 替代 catch and live on prey（见第 13 课）。

Sentence 10第 10 句 (begins开头 “In fact, it …”)

  • would be: a subjunctive, since no such thing is the case.would be：虚拟（假如食肉动物都有毒的话）。
  • though opens a clause of concession.though 引导让步。

Sentence 14第 14 句 (begins开头 “some snakes manufactured …”)

  • An adjective phrase after the noun (see Lesson 17); that stands in for the noun already named.different … from that of others：形容词短语后置（见第 17 课）；that 代替 poison。
  • as different as A is from B: as unlike each other as those two things are.as different as A is from B：像 A 与 B 那样不同。
  • An -ing phrase, standing side by side with the adjective phrase and describing the same noun.and having different effects：现在分词短语，与前面的形容词短语并列修饰 poison。

Sentence 16第 16 句 (begins开头 “One poison acts …”)

  • one ... the other (see Lesson 15), with the verb left out of the second half.one … the other（见第 15 课），第二个分句省略谓语。

Sentence 20第 20 句 (begins开头 “It is said …”)

  • the + comparative + of the two: of two things, the one that is more so; the is required.the + 比较级 + of the two：两者中较……的一个，要加 the。
  • Two that clauses side by side as the real subject; the second that cannot be dropped.两个 that 从句并列作真主语（第二个 that 不能省）。
  • so to speak: as it were; an aside marking a form of words the writer will not defend.so to speak：可以说，插入语。

Sentence 21第 21 句 (begins开头 “Be that as …”)

  • Be that as it may: a fixed subjunctive phrase meaning however that may be, used to set the whole of the last paragraph aside.Be that as it may：虚拟式固定说法，「尽管如此、不管怎样」（= however that may be）。
  • do its business: take effect; a mild way of saying it kills.do its business：起作用（委婉说法：致死）。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

Subject clauses, and it as an empty subject主语从句与 it 作形式主语
A clause can be the subject (What/How/Why/Whether ... is ...), but when that makes the sentence top-heavy, English usually puts it in the subject slot and sends the clause to the end.
从句可以作主语（What/How/Why/Whether … is …），但句子头重时常改用 it 作形式主语，把从句移到句尾。

Note: a whether clause can stand first as subject, but an if clause cannot: Whether he will come is uncertain. (not If he will come is uncertain.)

注意：whether 引导的主语从句放句首可以，if 不行：Whether he will come is uncertain.（× If he will come is uncertain.）

  • What he said surprised everyone.
  • It is still unknown why the plane crashed.
Comparing two: the + comparative + of the two, and by far两者比较：the + 比较级 + of the two；by far
When only two things are in play, the one that is more so is the + comparative + of the two. by far strengthens a comparative or a superlative.
两者比较时，「两个中较……的那个」用 the + 比较级 + of the two；by far 加强比较级或最高级（远远……）。

Note: the superlative needs three or more: the most primitive of all.

注意：三者以上才用最高级：the most primitive of all。

  • She is the taller of the two sisters.
  • This is by far the best book I have read.
Fixed subjunctive phrases: Be that as it may / so be it / come what may固定的虚拟式套语：Be that as it may / so be it / come what may
English keeps a handful of old subjunctive phrases in which the verb has its bare form: Be that as it may (however that may be), So be it (let it be so), Come what may (whatever happens), Long live ..., God bless you.
英语保留了一些古老的虚拟式套语，动词用原形：Be that as it may（尽管如此）、So be it（就这样吧）、Come what may（无论发生什么）、Long live…（……万岁）、God bless you。

What it does: it grants what has just been said and turns to what the writer really means to say.

功能：承认前面的说法，但转入作者真正想说的。

  • I may be wrong. Be that as it may, we must act now.
  • Come what may, I will stand by you.

  2. Dictate sentence by sentence: pause after each sentence and write it down. Replay a sentence at most three times.逐句听写：每句后暂停写下来，同一句最多重放三遍。
  3. Check against the text in your coursebook. Mark every mistake by type: a word you did not know, a word you knew but did not hear (linking, weak forms), or spelling.对照教材课文自查，把每个错按类型标出：不认识的词、认识却没听出的词（连读、弱读）、拼写。
  4. Words you did not hear go into your word book above. Re-listen to those sentences and shadow them.没听出的词加进上面的生词本；把那几句再听、跟读。
  5. Retell from the keywords below without looking at the text, then compare and retell once more.只看下面的关键词、不看课文复述一遍，对照后再复述一遍。

Keywords for retelling复述关键词

  • saliva → poison that defies analysis
  • not needed for survival: a luxury — one bite
  • why only snakes? cats would benefit; a two-edged weapon

Summary writing摘要写作

In no more than 80 words, sum up the puzzles about snake poison and the difference between the two kinds.

用不超过 80 个词概括关于蛇毒的几个谜和两种蛇毒的区别。

Points a good summary covers好摘要要覆盖的要点
  • How a snake turned mild digestive saliva into poison is still unknown, and the poison still cannot be analysed.蛇怎样把温和的消化唾液变成毒液至今是谜，毒液今天仍难以分析。
  • Poison was not forced on them by the struggle to survive, since snakes without it catch food too; for a snake it is a luxury, and one bite is the whole effort.毒液不是生存竞争逼出来的，无毒蛇也能捕食；毒对蛇只是一种奢侈，一咬即可。
  • Other meat eaters would gain by having it, yet only snakes got it, along with one lizard, and some of it was made extraordinarily strong.别的食肉动物有毒也会受益，大自然却只选了蛇（和一种蜥蜴），还造出毒性极强的毒。
  • There are two kinds: one acting on the nerves, from the mambas and cobras, and one acting on the blood, from the vipers and rattlesnakes.蛇毒分两种：神经毒素（曼巴、眼镜蛇）和血液毒素（蝰蛇、响尾蛇）。
  • The nerve kind kills a person faster, but that proves nothing, since the poison was developed against rats and mice, on which the blood kind acts almost at once.神经毒素对人起效更快，但这说明不了什么，蛇毒本是对付鼠类的，血液毒素对它们几乎立刻生效。
Our model summary本站范文

Nobody knows how snakes' mild digestive saliva became a poison that still defies analysis. Snakes did not need it to survive; it simply lets them kill with one bite, and other carnivores would have benefited too. Snake poisons differ completely: mambas and cobras produce a nerve poison, while vipers and rattlesnakes produce a blood poison. The nerve poison kills men faster, but that is irrelevant, as both evolved against small prey, which viper poison kills almost at once.（78 词）
