Lesson 17 · A Man-made Disease 人为的疾病

📘 New Concept English · Book 4 · Lesson 17 “A Man-made Disease”新概念英语 第四册 Lesson 17《A Man-made Disease》

C1🧠 26 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
A piece of ecological history. Early settlers brought the European rabbit to Australia, where it had no enemies and overran the continent; scientists then used a virus that attacks this one animal and only this one, spread it by mosquito, and cut the numbers sharply. The irony is that the man-made disease was carried back to Europe by a French doctor, spread through France and Britain, and threatened both wild rabbits and the fur trade. The key word of the whole passage is ironically: people solve one problem and manufacture the next. The last sentence, asking whether we can control a disease of our own making, is still the central difficulty of biological control.
一段生态史：早期移民把欧洲兔子带到澳大利亚，兔子没有天敌、泛滥全洲；科学家用只感染这种兔子的黏液瘤病毒、再借蚊子传播，大幅压低了兔子数量。讽刺的是，这种人造疾病又被一位法国医生带回欧洲，扩散到法国和英国，威胁到野兔和养兔皮毛业。全文的关键词是 ironically：人类解决一个问题，又制造出新问题。最后一句「人能否控制自己发明的疾病」至今仍是生物防治的核心难题。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. Why did the rabbit become such a plague in Australia?兔子在澳大利亚为什么会泛滥成灾？
  2. How did the disease come to spread across the whole of Australia?黏液瘤病是怎样在澳大利亚全境传播的？
  3. What happened once the disease arrived back in Europe?这种疾病回到欧洲后造成了什么后果？

Ritchie Calder was a Scottish journalist who spent his life explaining science to newspaper readers and later became a peer; the book carries that title and that period, the middle 1950s, when biological control was new and hopeful. We have not located a copy, so the year of publication is not verified here.

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

The numbers behind the passage are worth having. The European rabbit reached Victoria in 1859, when a grazier had a shipment sent out to his property at Barwon Park; accounts differ on how many animals were actually released, with one count putting twenty-four on the ship and the national museum saying thirteen wild rabbits went out. Within seven years fourteen thousand had been shot on that one estate, and by 1880 they were across the Murray and into New South Wales. Myxomatosis went back into trial in 1950 and took hold after the summer rains bred the mosquitoes that carried it; the Australian national archives put the fall in the rabbit population over the first two years at from six hundred million down to one hundred million, while the national museum prefers a figure of around five hundred million killed. Either way it is the largest deliberate kill of a wild animal in history, and it is why the writer's tone is so confident.

这段背后的数字值得知道。欧洲兔 1859 年抵达维多利亚：一位牧场主把一批兔子运到自己在巴旺园（Barwon Park）的产业；放出的数量各源不一，一说船上是 24 只，澳大利亚国家博物馆说放出的是 13 只野兔。七年之内，仅这一处产业就射杀了一万四千只；到 1880 年它们已越过墨累河进入新南威尔士。黏液瘤病 1950 年重新进入试验，夏季降雨孵出传病的蚊子之后站稳了脚；澳大利亚国家档案馆把头两年兔子数量的下降记为从六亿降到一亿，国家博物馆则取「死了约五亿只」这个数。无论取哪个数，这都是史上最大规模的一次有意扑杀野生动物，也正是作者语气如此自信的原因。

✨ How it is written赏析

The whole passage turns on one word placed at the start of a paragraph, and everything before it is built to make that word land. Up to that point the sentences march in order: the animal arrives, it breeds, it overruns, a weakness is found, a carrier is found, the numbers fall. The verbs are all active and all human, so the reader is carried along inside the project. Then Ironically, and with it the direction reverses: the continent that received the pest sends the cure back as a plague. Notice the symmetry in that sentence, one thing handed over as a pest and another taken back as a pestilence, with two words so nearly alike that the reader hears the trap close. And then the ending is not a conclusion but a question, which is braver than it looks: the writer has just told you a success story and he declines to call it one.

整段文字转在一个放在段首的词上，而它前面的一切都是为了让这个词落得响。在它之前，句子按顺序行进：动物到来、繁殖、泛滥、找到弱点、找到传播者、数量下降。动词全是主动的，全是人做的，读者就被带进这项工程里面。接着是 Ironically，方向随之反转：当初收下害兽的那块大陆，把解药当瘟疫送了回来。留意那句话里的对称——一样东西作为害兽被送出，另一样作为瘟疫被收回，两个词几乎同形，读者听得见陷阱合上的声音。而结尾不是结论，是一个问题，这比看起来更有勇气：作者刚讲完一个成功故事，却拒绝把它叫做成功。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

His closing question has an answer now, and it is: only for a while at a time. The rabbits did not stay susceptible, which he already saw coming, and so a second virus was brought in, released in Australia in 1996; by 2017 that one needed help too, and in the first week of March a Korean variant known as K5 went out nationally, chosen because it could get past the partial protection a harmless related virus was giving the animals. The result was a knockdown averaging about a third of the population, uneven from place to place, and spreading less readily than the first release had in the mid-nineties. At one monitored site the outbreaks keep coming back, and more often, which researchers describe as an arms race still under way. So biological control is not a cure but a treadmill: each new agent buys perhaps twenty years, and resistance is the price of success, because the animals that survive are precisely the ones that could. The honest reading of the lesson today is that the writer asked the right question and slightly misframed it. The question is not whether people can control a disease they invented. It is whether they can keep inventing fast enough, and whether they should.

他结尾那个问题现在有答案了，答案是：一次只能管一阵。兔子没有一直保持易感——这一点他已经预见到——于是又引进了第二种病毒，1996 年在澳大利亚放出；到 2017 年这一种也需要帮手，3 月第一周，一个称为 K5 的韩国变种在全国范围放出，选它是因为它能绕过一种无害近亲病毒给兔子带来的部分保护。结果是种群平均压低约三分之一，各地参差不齐，而且扩散不如九十年代中期第一次放出时顺畅。在一个受监测的点位，疫情反复回来，而且越来越频繁，研究者称这是一场仍在进行的军备竞赛。所以生物防治不是治愈，而是跑步机：每一种新手段大概买来二十年，而抗性正是成功的代价——活下来的动物恰恰就是能活下来的那些。今天如实地读这一课，会说作者问对了问题，但框得略偏。问题不是人能不能控制自己发明的疾病，而是人能不能一直发明得够快，以及该不该这样做。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 2第 2 句 (begins开头 “This rabbit had …”)

  • The comma before so that marks a result, not a purpose.so that 前有逗号，表结果（所以），不是目的。
  • An adjective phrase after the noun, standing in for a relative clause.characteristic of rabbits：形容词短语后置修饰 abandon，= which is characteristic of rabbits。
  • that here means the well-known kind of.that promiscuous abandon：that 表「众所周知的那种」。

Sentence 4第 4 句 (begins开头 “It caused devastation …”)

  • by + an -ing form gives the means, and two are joined here.by doing 表方式，两个并列。
  • might have maintained: could have fed them but did not; a modal perfect used for something that never happened (see Lesson 1).might have maintained：本可以养活（实际没有），虚拟的情态完成式（见第 1 课）。

Sentence 6第 6 句 (begins开头 “By infecting animals …”)

  • The understood subject of the by + -ing phrase is the scientists, while the subject of the passive main clause is the outbreaks. Strictly this dangles, but expository prose tolerates it (see Lesson 7).by doing 的逻辑主语（科学家）与主句被动的主语（epidemics）不同——这在严格语法上是「悬垂」，但在说明文中被容忍（见第 7 课）。
  • let somebody or something loose: to set them free.let sb/sth loose：放出，释放。

Sentence 8第 8 句 (begins开头 “So while the …”)

  • while here marks a contrast, and the two past continuous verbs set the two policies of the same years side by side.while 表对比（而）；两个过去进行时并列，突出同时期反差。
  • this one stands for the kind of mosquito just named.this one 指这种蚊子。

Sentence 13第 13 句 (begins开头 “Ironically, Europe, which …”)

  • A non-defining relative clause in the past perfect: the animal had been sent out first.which had bequeathed … to Australia：非限制性定语从句，过去完成时（先送去兔子）。

Sentence 17第 17 句 (begins开头 “and it spread …”)

  • Two where clauses side by side, both describing the same country.两个 where 定语从句并列，都修饰 Britain。
  • An adjective phrase dropped in as extra information.equally susceptible to the disease：形容词短语作插入的补充说明。

Sentence 19第 19 句 (begins开头 “The question became …”)

  • one stands for a question, and of + whether opens the clause.one = a question；of whether…：介词 + whether 从句。
  • A relative clause in the past perfect with the relative pronoun left out.he had invented：过去完成时，省略 that 的定语从句。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

so that: result or purpose?so that：表结果还是表目的？
Purpose: usually no comma before so that, and the clause often has can, could, may, might or will. Result: usually a comma before so that, and the clause simply states what followed, much like so.
表目的：so that 前一般无逗号，从句常有 can/could/may/might/will；表结果：so that 前常有逗号，从句是陈述事实，相当于 so（所以）。

Purpose: He saved money so that he could buy a house.

目的：He saved money so that he could buy a house.

  • It rained heavily, so that the match was cancelled.（结果）
  • Speak clearly so that everyone can hear you.（目的）
Adjective phrases after the noun, and be characteristic of / be susceptible to形容词短语后置与 be characteristic of / be susceptible to
Once an adjective brings along something of its own, a prepositional phrase or an infinitive, it has to follow the noun: a man proud of his son / an animal susceptible to disease.
形容词带了自己的补足成分（介词短语、不定式）时，必须放在名词后面：a man proud of his son / an animal susceptible to disease。

Common members of the family: be typical of / be characteristic of / be susceptible to / be responsible for / be full of.

常见：be typical of / be characteristic of / be susceptible to / be responsible for / be full of。

  • This is a problem typical of big cities.
  • Children eager to learn should be encouraged.
while for contrast, and what a comment adverb such as Ironically does to a paragraphwhile 的对比用法与 Ironically 的篇章作用
while can mark time (at the moment when), contrast (whereas) or concession (although). Comment adverbs such as ironically, interestingly and unfortunately stand at the head of the sentence and declare the writer's attitude to the whole of it.
while 可表时间（当……时），也可表对比（而，然而）、让步（虽然）；ironically, interestingly, unfortunately 等评注性副词放句首，表明作者对整句事实的态度。
  • While I like the colour, I don't like the design.（让步）
  • Ironically, the fire station burned down.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • … so that it multiplied with … characteristic of … ……于是以……特有的……繁殖起来
    The weeds had no competitors, so that they spread with the speed characteristic of invasive plants.
  • … which might have maintained … 本可以养活/维持……的
    They wasted money which might have built a new school.
  • Ironically, A, which had …, acquired … 具有讽刺意味的是，曾经……的 A 却得到了……
    Ironically, the company that had warned others about hackers was itself hacked.

  2. Dictate sentence by sentence: pause after each sentence and write it down. Replay a sentence at most three times.逐句听写：每句后暂停写下来，同一句最多重放三遍。
  3. Check against the text in your coursebook. Mark every mistake by type: a word you did not know, a word you knew but did not hear (linking, weak forms), or spelling.对照教材课文自查，把每个错按类型标出：不认识的词、认识却没听出的词（连读、弱读）、拼写。
  4. Words you did not hear go into your word book above. Re-listen to those sentences and shadow them.没听出的词加进上面的生词本；把那几句再听、跟读。
  5. Retell from the keywords below without looking at the text, then compare and retell once more.只看下面的关键词、不看课文复述一遍，对照后再复述一遍。

Keywords for retelling复述关键词

  • settlers introduced the European rabbit; no natural enemies
  • it overran the continent, burrowing and eating the herbage
  • myxomatosis: infect rabbits and let them loose
  • a mosquito carried the disease — Australia encouraged it
  • rabbits developed resistance; problem manageable
  • ironically, a French doctor → France and Britain; can man control it?

Summary writing摘要写作

In no more than 80 words, tell the story of this disease: from the rabbit overrunning Australia, through the deliberate spread of the virus, to its unintended arrival in Europe.

用不超过 80 个词讲述黏液瘤病的故事：从兔子在澳大利亚泛滥，到疾病被人为传播，再到它意外传到欧洲。

Points a good summary covers好摘要要覆盖的要点
  • Early settlers brought the European rabbit to Australia, where it had no enemies, overran the continent and destroyed the grazing.早期移民把欧洲兔引入澳大利亚，兔子没有天敌，泛滥全洲，毁坏牧草。
  • Scientists found that this one animal was open to a deadly virus, set off local outbreaks by hand, then let mosquitoes carry it across the continent, and the numbers fell sharply.科学家发现这种兔子易感染致命的黏液瘤病，先人工制造局部流行，后借蚊子传遍全洲，兔子数量锐减。
  • The animals gradually toughened up, so wiping them out became unlikely, though the problem looked as if it could be held.兔子逐渐产生抵抗力，难以根绝，但问题有望可控。
  • A French doctor brought the disease onto his own land, and from there it spread through France and Britain, putting wild rabbits and the fur trade at risk.一位法国医生把这病引进自己的庄园，它扩散到法国和英国，危及野兔和皮毛业。
  • The question turned into this: can people control a disease of their own making?问题变成：人能否控制自己发明的疾病。
