Lesson 33 · Education 教育

📘 New Concept English · Book 4 · Lesson 33 “Education”新概念英语 第四册 Lesson 33《Education》

C1🧠 27 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
We say education is the great opportunity of modern life. Yet a tribal society with no books turns out to be the more democratic school: what the tradition holds is shared by everyone and required of everyone, no matter what the family has; the child grows up beside its parents, and there is no such thing as juvenile crime. The author works by contrast — on one side a carefully designed state system that arrived very late (Germany 1642, France 1806, England 1876, as he gives the dates), on the other side the equal start of a tribe. Watch how many words he puts in quotation marks ('invest', 'interest', 'college', 'civilized'): in English that is the standard way to mark irony, or a word borrowed from somebody else.
我们总说「教育是现代人最大的机会」，可没有书本的部落社会反而更「民主」：传统知识人人共享、人人必学，不看家里有没有钱，孩子在父母身边长大，没有「少年犯罪」。作者用反衬手法：一边是精心设计却姗姗来迟的现代义务教育（德国 1642、法国 1806、英国 1876），一边是原始部落的「平等起点」。注意作者大量使用引号（'invest' 'interest' 'college' 'civilized'）——这是英语里表示反讽或借用的常见手段。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. Why do modern states 'invest' in education, and what 'interest' do they expect?现代国家为什么要「投资」教育？它们期待得到什么「利息」？
  2. In what ways does the author think a bookless tribal education beats a modern one?作者认为没有书本的部落教育在哪些方面比现代教育优越？
  3. What do the dates of the compulsory-schooling laws show?义务教育各国立法的年份说明了什么？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

The passage comes from The Origin of Things by Julius Lips, published in New York in 1947; the German edition, Vom Ursprung der Dinge, followed in Leipzig in 1951. Lips was a German ethnologist who lost his museum post in Cologne in 1933 for speaking against the new regime, left for the United States in 1934, and is best known for The Savage Hits Back (1937).

课文取自尤利乌斯·利普斯（Julius Lips）的《The Origin of Things》，1947 年在纽约出版；德文本《Vom Ursprung der Dinge》1951 年在莱比锡出版。利普斯是德国民族学家，1933 年因公开反对纳粹失去科隆的博物馆职位，1934 年流亡美国，最有名的书是 1937 年的《The Savage Hits Back》。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

Lips wrote as a refugee, and that matters for how you read him. His best-known book had collected the carvings, masks and figures in which colonized peoples portrayed the European - the white trader with his bottle, the officer with his gun - and turned the ethnographer's gaze around. A man who had just watched a literate, school-building, concert-going nation vote itself into barbarism had good reason to doubt that schooling and civilization are the same thing. So the chapter is not really a report on tribal life. It is an argument aimed at his readers: look at what you call an opportunity, then look at who actually gets it, and then look at how recently you even decided that every child should. The praise of the forest is the stick he uses to hit the lecture hall with.

利普斯是以流亡者的身份写作的，这一点影响怎么读他。他最有名的那本书收集了被殖民者刻出来的欧洲人——拿酒瓶的白人商人、持枪的军官——把民族志的目光反转了过来。一个刚刚看着一个识字率高、到处建学校、听音乐会的民族投票走向野蛮的人，完全有理由怀疑「上学」与「文明」是不是一回事。所以这一章其实不是一份关于部落生活的报告，而是冲着读者来的论辩：先看看你口中的「机会」，再看看它实际落到谁手里，再看看你是多么晚才认定每个孩子都该有。他赞美森林，是为了拿这根棍子敲讲堂。

✨ How it is written赏析

Three devices carry the piece, and all three are worth borrowing. The first is the quotation mark used as a tone of voice: invest, interest, college, civilized, happy few. He never says the words are wrong; he just holds each one at arm's length and lets you hear it. The second is the sentence that loses its own subject - education is announced, then wanders through a dash and a sarcastic appositive about wisdom you can buy, and only afterwards does the question arrive. That broken shape mimics the sprawl of the thing he is describing. The third is the false concession. He grants that without schools we would still have doctors, lawyers, weddings and births, and only then names what would really be different, which is the inner furniture: memory, practical psychology, getting along with the people around you. Give the other side the easy half of the argument, and the half you keep sounds like the honest one.

全文靠三样手法，都值得借用。第一是把引号当语气用：invest、interest、college、civilized、happy few——他从不说这些词是错的，只是把每一个捏在手上举远一点，让你自己听出味道。第二是一个丢了主语的句子：先宣布「教育」，然后穿过一个破折号和一句讽刺「智慧可以花钱买」的同位语，问题才姗姗出现——这种破掉的句形模仿的正是他所描述的那个庞杂体系。第三是假让步：他承认没有学校我们照样会有医生、律师、婚礼和出生，然后才点出真正会不一样的东西——人内部的家具：记性、实用心理、与周围人相处的本事。把容易的那一半让给对方，自己留下的那一半听起来就像老实话。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The argument has one strong half and one weak half, and the dates are where it leaks. Gotha's famous school ordinance of the 1640s was the decree of one small duchy, not of a country called Germany, and Prussia's general school regulation came only in 1763. France did not make schooling compulsory in 1806 - that year belongs to Napoleon's imperial university - but in 1882, after the 1881 law had made it free. In England the 1870 act built schools without compelling attendance; compulsion arrived in 1880. In other words the three dates in the lesson are earlier than the laws they are supposed to name, which weakens his point about lateness while leaving his point about reluctance standing. The weak half is the forest. A society where every child learns the whole tradition is also a society where no child can learn anything else, where the teacher is your father and cannot be appealed against, and where what counts as knowledge is fixed; and the claim that jungles know no juvenile delinquency is the sort of absence that an outsider staying three months would be the last to see. The strong half has outlived him. The gap he complained about is still measured: UNESCO counted 273 million children and young people out of school in 2024, and adds that about 13 million more in the worst conflict-hit countries are missing from the count. For Chinese readers the comparison is immediate. China passed its compulsory education law in 1986, which on the lesson's own timeline is extremely late, and reached near-universal nine-year schooling within about two decades. That speed is the real answer to Lips: the equal start he admired is not a gift of the forest, it is a thing a state can decide to buy, and the question is only when.

这个论证有一半很硬，一半很软，而漏水的地方正是那几个年份。哥达那份有名的学校条例出在 1640 年代，是一个小公国的法令，不是一个叫「德国」的国家的法令；普鲁士的普通学校规程要到 1763 年。法国并不是 1806 年实行义务教育——那一年属于拿破仑的帝国大学——而是 1882 年，在 1881 年先行免费之后。英国 1870 年的法案建了学校但没有强制入学，强制要到 1880 年。换句话说，课文里三个年份都比它们所指的法律更早，这反而削弱了他「来得太晚」的论点，而「不情愿」这一层仍然站得住。软的那一半是森林：一个每个孩子都学完整套传统的社会，也是一个孩子学不到别的东西的社会，老师就是你父亲、无处申诉，什么算知识是定死的；而「丛林里没有青少年犯罪」这种「没有」，恰恰是一个待三个月的外来者最不可能看见的。硬的那一半活得比他久：他抱怨的那个缺口今天仍在被测量——联合国教科文组织统计 2024 年有 2.73 亿儿童与青少年失学，并说明冲突最严重的那些国家还有约 1300 万没算进去。对中国读者，这个对照就在眼前：中国 1986 年通过义务教育法，按课文自己的时间轴算是极晚，却在约二十年里做到了九年教育的近乎普及。这个速度才是对利普斯真正的回答：他羡慕的「平等起点」不是森林的恩赐，而是一个国家可以决定买下来的东西，问题只在于什么时候买。

💬 Discuss and write讨论与写作
  1. The author puts invest and interest in quotation marks because he dislikes treating education as a return on money. Yet that is how most governments and most parents now argue for it. Can you defend spending on schools without using the language of returns?
    作者给 invest 和 interest 加引号，是不喜欢把教育当成资金回报。可今天多数政府和多数家长恰恰是这样为教育说话的。你能不用「回报」这套话，为办学花钱辩护吗？
  2. Pick one thing you learned outside school that your schooling could never have given you, and one thing school gave you that no family could. Write 120 words comparing what each cost.
    挑一件学校绝不可能教给你、你在校外学到的东西，再挑一件任何家庭都给不了、学校给了你的东西，写 120 词比较它们各自的代价。
  3. He says a tribal child is never hurried. Where does the hurry in your own education come from - the exams, the parents, the job market - and what would you give up to remove it?
    他说部落里的孩子从不匆忙。你自己受教育过程中的「匆忙」来自哪里——考试、父母，还是就业市场？要去掉它，你愿意放弃什么？

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 2第 2 句 (begins开头 “A man without …”)

  • many of us believe: an aside, the same as Many of us believe that a man without ...many of us believe：插入语，相当于 Many of us believe that a man without …。
  • deprived of ...: a past participle phrase adding more about the man (= who is deprived of ...).deprived of …：过去分词短语作补充说明（= who is deprived of …）。

Sentence 4第 4 句 (begins开头 “Convinced of the …”)

  • Convinced of ...: a past participle as an adjunct of cause (= Because they are convinced of ...), and its implied subject is the states.Convinced of …：过去分词作原因状语（= Because they are convinced of …），逻辑主语是 modern states。
  • 'invest' and 'interest' in quotation marks: education is being treated as a financial investment, and the marks carry the irony.'invest' 和 'interest' 加引号：把教育比作金融投资，带反讽。
  • in the form of ...: the shape the return takes. The who clause after it describes those young people: the state puts money in and the interest it draws out is people.in the form of …：以……的形式；后面的 who 定语从句修饰那群「开明的年轻人」——国家投下钱，收回的「利息」是人。

Sentence 7第 7 句 (begins开头 “punctuated by textbooks—those …”)

  • The sentence opens with a topic left hanging: Education has no verb of its own, and after the dash the thread is picked up again by its (= education's). The effect is close to speech.句首的 Education … 是「悬垂的话题」，后面没有自己的谓语，在破折号后由 its（= education's）接回——一种口语化的修辞结构。
  • An appositive for textbooks, and a sarcastic one: wells of wisdom that you can buy over a counter.those purchasable wells of wisdom：教科书的同位语，讽刺「智慧之井居然可以花钱买」。
  • what would ... be like without ...: an implied conditional (without = if there were no ...).what would … be like without …：含蓄虚拟（without = if there were no …）。

Sentence 12第 12 句 (begins开头 “If our educational …”)

  • A conditional against present fact: were fashioned in the if clause (passive), would have in the main clause.与现在事实相反的虚拟条件句：从句 were fashioned（被动），主句 would have。
  • the most ... imaginable: the adjective comes after the noun and sharpens the superlative — the most of its kind that can be thought of.the most … imaginable：imaginable 后置，强调「所能想象的最……」。

Sentence 17第 17 句 (begins开头 “There are no …”)

  • while here marks a contrast, 'whereas'; it is not 'during the time that'.while 表对比（「而」），不是「当……时」。
  • The inserted if clause is the author's careful reservation: where there is no script, the word illiterate does not really apply.插入的条件句是作者的严谨保留：没有文字的民族，谈「文盲」这个词本身就不合适。
  • 'civilized' in quotation marks: an irony aimed at nations that call themselves civilized and have no such law.'civilized' 加引号：反讽这些国家并不像自称的那样文明。

Sentence 19第 19 句 (begins开头 “This shows how …”)

  • how long it was before ...: an object clause built out of It was a long time before ... (a long time passed before we ...).how long it was before …：宾语从句，来自 It was a long time before …（过了很久才……）。
  • deem it necessary to do: it is a formal object and the real object is the infinitive phrase that follows.deem it necessary to do：it 是形式宾语，真正宾语是 to make sure …。
  • The past participle phrase at the end modifies knowledge: what a lucky minority had piled up over centuries.文末的过去分词短语作后置定语，修饰 knowledge：那是几个世纪里由少数幸运者积累起来的知识。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

Conditionals against present fact与现在事实相反的虚拟条件句
If + subject + past tense (be becomes were), then subject + would / could / might + bare infinitive — for a present or future situation that is untrue or unlikely.
If + 主语 + 过去式（be 用 were），主语 + would/could/might + 动词原形——假设现在或将来不真实、不太可能的情况。

An implied conditional needs no if: without its benefits does the work of if there were no education, and the would clauses that follow all hang on that assumption.

含蓄虚拟：没有 if 也能表示假设——without its benefits 就等于 if there were no education；后面一连串 we would have … 都承着这个假设。

The passive in the if clause: were + past participle.

从句被动形式：were + 过去分词（were fashioned）。

Note the contrast: for a real possibility, use the indicative — If it rains, we will stay at home.

注意：真实可能的条件用陈述语气：If it rains, we will stay at home.

  • If I were you, I would accept the offer.
  • Without water, nothing could live.
The past participle as an adjunct of cause过去分词作原因状语
A past participle phrase at the front of a sentence often gives the cause, the time or the condition. Its implied subject is the subject of the main clause, and the relation is passive or a state.
过去分词（短语）放句首作状语，常表示原因、时间或条件，逻辑主语就是主句主语，与它是被动或状态关系。

Many participles of this kind work as plain adjectives: convinced, tired, worried, moved.

很多这类过去分词其实相当于形容词：convinced（确信的）、tired（疲倦的）、worried（担心的）、moved（感动的）。

The implied subject must match: Seen from the hill, the town looks beautiful. (the town is seen) / not Seen from the hill, we found the town beautiful.

逻辑主语必须一致：Seen from the hill, the town looks beautiful.（town 被看到）；✗ Seen from the hill, we found the town beautiful.

Compare the present participle: Interested in history, he reads a lot. (he feels the interest) / The book is interesting. (the book causes it)

与现在分词对比：Interested in history, he reads a lot.（他感兴趣）／ The book is interesting.

  • Moved by her story, everyone was in tears.
  • Tired after the long walk, she went straight to bed.
It was (a long time) before ...: how long it tookIt was (a long time) before …：过了多久才……
It + be + a length of time + before ... means 'it took that long before ...'; the negative It was not long before ... means 'it was not long until ...'.
It + be + 时间 + before …＝「过了（多久）才……」；否定式 It was not long before … ＝「不久就……」。

This lesson uses it inside an object clause: how long it was before ... — that is, a very long time passed before we thought it necessary.

本课用的是它的宾语从句形式 how long it was before …（＝过了很久我们才认为有必要……）。

Matching the tenses: the past takes It was ... before + a past tense; the future takes It will be ... before + a present tense (no will in the clause).

时态对应：过去用 It was … before + 过去式；将来用 It will be … before + 一般现在时（从句不用 will）。

Keep it apart from since: It is three years since we met looks back to a past point, while before looks forward to how long the wait is.

区分：It is three years since we met.（自从……已经三年）——since 回看过去，before 看「要等多久」。

  • It was two years before he came back.
  • It will be a long time before we meet again.
  • It was not long before the police arrived.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • lay less stress on A and more on B 少强调 A，多强调 B
    Schools should lay less stress on exams and more on thinking.
  • So much is certain: that … 有一点是肯定的：……
    So much is certain: that prices will keep rising.
  • the most … imaginable 能想象到的最……的
    It was the worst weather imaginable.
  • be entitled to an equal start 有权拥有平等的起点
    Every child is entitled to an equal start in life.

Summary writing摘要写作

Write no more than 80 words: what advantages does the author find in a bookless tribal education compared with a modern one?

用不超过 80 个词概括：作者认为没有书本的部落教育与现代教育相比，有哪些优点？

Points a good summary covers好摘要要覆盖的要点
  • Modern society treats education as the chief opportunity in life, and states spend on it to raise future leaders.现代社会把教育视为头等机会，国家投资教育以培养领导人才。
  • In a tribal society the inherited knowledge belongs to everyone and is taught to everyone, so each member starts level with the rest.在部落社会，传统知识人人共享、人人必学，每人都有平等的起点。
  • A tribe has no illiterates, while compulsory schooling was made law very late, and in some countries still has not been.部落里没有文盲；现代义务教育却很晚才立法，有些国家至今没有。
  • Tribal education costs no money and is never hurried; the child grows up under its parents' eyes, and juvenile crime is unknown.部落教育不取决于金钱，没有匆忙，孩子在父母关注下成长，没有青少年犯罪。
Our model summary本站范文

We regard education as one of the greatest modern opportunities, and states invest in it to produce leaders. Yet bookless tribal societies are more democratic: their traditional knowledge is taught to everyone, so all members have an equal start, and nobody is "illiterate". Compulsory schooling, by contrast, came late in Europe and still does not exist everywhere. Tribal education costs nothing, is unhurried, and keeps children with their parents, so there is no juvenile delinquency.（75 词）
