Lesson 7 · Bats 蝙蝠

📘 New Concept English · Book 4 · Lesson 7 “Bats”新概念英语 第四册 Lesson 7《Bats》

C1🧠 21 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
A piece of popular science. The writer's subject is how bats find their way by echo, but he takes a detour through human inventions first — shout and listen for the echo, tap a ship's hull to sound the depth, use echoes to find fish — and only then comes back to bats and the comparison with radar. Working from the familiar towards the unfamiliar is standard practice in science writing. In the language, watch the further ... the longer ..., the inversion in So was born ..., and the steady use of the passive.
科普说明文。作者要讲蝙蝠的回声定位，却先绕到人类发明——喊一声听回声、敲船壳测水深、用回声找鱼群——最后才回到蝙蝠，并与雷达类比。这种「由熟悉到陌生」的铺垫是科普写作的常用策略。语言上注意 the further … the longer …、So was born …（倒装）和大量被动语态。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. How does echo-sounding apparatus work out the depth of the sea?回声测深仪是怎么测出海水深度的？
  2. Why is going from the sea bottom to a shoal of fish called a comparatively simple step?从测海底到找鱼群，为什么说是「相对简单的一步」？
  3. What do bats use echo-location for?蝙蝠用回声定位做什么？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

The lesson is credited to Maurice Burton, from Curiosities of Animal Life (attribution line in the original Book 4). Burton (1898-1992) was a zoologist at the Natural History Museum in London and a prolific popular science writer; the book was published by Ward, Lock in 1952.

课文署名 Maurice Burton，选自《动物生活奇观》（Curiosities of Animal Life）（据旧版第四册课末署名）。Burton（1898–1992）是伦敦自然史博物馆的动物学家，也是产量很高的科普作家；该书 1952 年由 Ward, Lock 出版。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

Put a date on this and the whole shape of the piece makes sense. The book came out in 1952, which is about ten years after the thing it describes was established and only a few years after radar had come out of the war as public knowledge. Lazzaro Spallanzani had shown in the 1790s that blinded bats still flew accurately while deafened ones did not, but nobody could say what they were using, because the signal is above the range of human hearing. The answer waited on an instrument. In 1938 Donald Griffin, then an undergraduate at Harvard, took bats to the physicist G. W. Pierce, who had built a detector for ultrasound, and the bats turned out to be loudly calling in a register no one in the room could hear. Griffin and Robert Galambos dated the discovery to 1940 and published it in 1941; Griffin later gave the thing its name, echo-location. That is why a 1952 writer explains bats by way of ships. Echo-sounding and radar were the familiar marvels of his readers' lifetime, and bats were the strange new case. A reader today has the comparison the other way round: the bat is the famous example and sonar is the technical footnote.

给这篇定个年代，它的整个结构就说得通了。书出版于 1952 年，距它所描述的事情被确立大约十年，而距雷达作为战争遗产成为公众知识只有几年。斯帕兰扎尼在 1790 年代就证明：弄瞎的蝙蝠仍能准确飞行，弄聋的则不能；但没人能说出它们用的是什么，因为那个信号高于人耳的听觉范围。答案要等一台仪器。1938 年，还在哈佛读本科的 Donald Griffin 把蝙蝠带到物理学家 G. W. Pierce 那里——Pierce 造了一台超声探测器——结果发现蝙蝠正在一个屋里没人听得见的音区里大声叫喊。Griffin 与 Robert Galambos 把这项发现定在 1940 年，1941 年发表论文；Griffin 后来给这件事起了名字：echo-location（回声定位）。这就是一位 1952 年的作者为什么要绕着船来解释蝙蝠。回声测深和雷达是他的读者这一生中熟悉的奇迹，蝙蝠才是陌生的新案例。今天的读者两头正好相反：蝙蝠是那个有名的例子，而声呐成了技术脚注。

✨ How it is written赏析

The detour is the lesson. A writer with a strange fact to convey has two choices: define it, or build it. Burton builds it, and he builds it in five steps that each add exactly one thing. Shout at a wall and an echo returns — everyone has done this. Put the wall further off and the echo is later — now you have time as a measurement. Tap a hull instead of shouting, and the sea floor instead of a wall — now the method is an instrument. Put a shoal of fish where the sea floor was — now it finds moving targets. Give the echo a shape and you can name the species — now it discriminates. Only at that point does the bat appear, and by then the reader has built the bat's ability himself and has nothing left to be told. Count how little is actually asserted about bats: two sentences. Everything that makes those two sentences intelligible was installed beforehand, using things the reader already owned. If you ever have to explain something your reader has no picture of, resist the definition. Find the thing they have done themselves — shouted in a tunnel, heard a delay — and add one variable at a time until the strange thing is the obvious next step.

这篇真正要教的就是那段绕路。一个作者手里有个陌生的事实，有两条路：定义它，或者搭建它。伯顿选择搭建，而且分五步，每一步只加一件东西。对着墙喊一声，回声返回——这是人人都做过的。把墙挪远，回声来得更晚——现在你有了用时间作度量。改成敲船壳而不是喊，改成海底而不是墙——现在这个方法成了一台仪器。把鱼群放到海底的位置上——现在它能找会动的目标。给回声一个形状，就能叫出鱼种——现在它能分辨。只到这一步，蝙蝠才出场，而此时读者已经自己把蝙蝠的本事搭好了，没有什么还需要被告知。数一下关于蝙蝠本身真正断言了多少：两句话。让这两句话可被理解的全部东西，都是事先用读者本来就有的材料装进去的。你哪天要解释一件读者脑子里没有画面的事，别急着下定义。去找他们自己做过的那件事——在隧道里喊过一声、听过那个延迟——然后一次只加一个变量，直到那件陌生的事成为顺理成章的下一步。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

Two things in the piece need updating, and they point in opposite directions. The small one is the word often in the comparison with radar. In 1952 the resemblance was a striking analogy; it has since become a working relationship, and the traffic now runs the other way. Engineers study bat calls in order to design better sonar and radar, because a bat solves in a few grams of tissue problems that remain hard in hardware: separating its own echo from the calls of hundreds of neighbours, tracking a target that is itself taking evasive action, and adjusting the call from a broad sweep to a narrow probe as the insect comes closer. The larger correction is to the word utilitarian in the opening sentence. Burton draws a clean line: some animal sounds are language, and the bat's voice is strictly a tool. That line has not held. Bat calls carry information about the individual that made them — species, and in several species identity and sex — and some bats use distinct social calls to argue over roosting space, to claim food, and to address particular individuals. Egyptian fruit bats have been recorded directing different call types at different individuals in the colony. So the instrument also happens to be a signature, and the division the lesson opens with turns out to be a spectrum rather than a boundary. His last sentence, meanwhile, has aged into an understatement: the moths that bats hunt evolved ears tuned to bat calls, and some reply with clicks that jam the bat's sonar, which means we are reading not one invention but an arms race several million years old.

这篇里有两处需要更新，而且方向相反。小的那处是与雷达作比较时用的那个「常常」。1952 年这个相似只是一个醒目的类比；此后它变成了一种工作关系，而且流向反了过来。工程师研究蝙蝠的叫声，为的是设计更好的声呐和雷达，因为蝙蝠用几克组织就解决了硬件里至今很难的问题：把自己的回声从几百个邻居的叫声中分离出来；跟踪一个本身正在做规避动作的目标；在昆虫靠近时把叫声从宽扫改成窄探。更大的更正落在开篇那个「实用的」上。伯顿划了一条干净的线：有些动物叫声是语言，而蝙蝠的声音纯粹是工具。这条线没有守住。蝙蝠的叫声携带着发声个体的信息——种类，在若干物种里还包括身份与性别；一些蝙蝠会用专门的社交叫声争栖息位置、占食物，甚至对特定个体说话。有记录显示埃及果蝠会对群体中不同个体发出不同类型的叫声。所以那台「仪器」同时也是一个签名，而课文开篇的那个划分，到头来是一道渐变光谱，不是一条边界。与此同时，他的最后一句已经成了保守说法：被蝙蝠捕食的蛾子演化出了对准蝙蝠叫声的耳朵，有些还用咔嗒声回应、干扰蝙蝠的声呐——这意味着我们读到的不是一项发明，而是一场几百万年的军备竞赛。

💬 Discuss and write讨论与写作
  1. Explain something you understand well to someone who has no picture of it, using Burton's method: start from something they have done themselves and add one variable at a time. 200 words.
    用伯顿的办法，向一个脑子里完全没有这个画面的人解释一件你很懂的事：从他自己做过的事开始，一次只加一个变量。200 词。
  2. Where would you draw the line between a signal that is a tool and a signal that is language? Does your line survive the bat?
    你会把「作为工具的信号」和「作为语言的信号」的界线划在哪里？你的界线经得起蝙蝠这个例子吗？
  3. Pick an animal ability that engineers have copied. What did the engineers have to give up that the animal does not, and why?
    挑一种被工程师模仿过的动物本领。工程师不得不放弃了什么，而动物并不需要放弃？为什么？

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 1第 1 句 (begins开头 “Not all sounds …”)

  • Not all ... is a partial negative: some of those sounds are language and some are not. It does not mean that none of them is.Not all … ＝ 部分否定：「并非所有声音都是语言」，不是「所有声音都不是语言」。

Sentence 4第 4 句 (begins开头 “The further off …”)

  • the + comparative, the + comparative, as in Lesson 3; is has been left out of the first half.the + 比较级, the + 比较级（见第 3 课），前半句省略了 is。
  • further off: at a greater distance. elapse takes no object and so has no passive.further off：离得更远；elapse 不及物，没有被动。

Sentence 5第 5 句 (begins开头 “A sound made …”)

  • by measuring ... says how it is done, and its understood subject is a person, while the main clause is passive. This is normal in scientific English and is accepted.by measuring…：方式状语，其逻辑主语是「人」，主句却是被动——这是科技英语常见写法，可接受。

Sentence 6第 6 句 (begins开头 “So was born …”)

  • Full inversion: So, Thus or Hence + verb + subject, used when the subject is long, to announce that something came into being.完全倒装：So / Thus / Hence + 谓语 + 主语，主语较长时用，表「于是诞生了……」。

Sentence 10第 10 句 (begins开头 “With experience, and …”)

  • it is a dummy subject; the real subject is the pair of infinitives, not only to ... but to ...it 是形式主语，真主语是 not only to … but to … 两个不定式。

Sentence 13第 13 句 (begins开头 “This echo-location in …”)

  • the principle of which: preposition + which opening a non-defining relative clause, = and its principle is similar.the principle of which：介词 + which 引导非限制性定语从句，= and its principle is similar。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

Partial negation: not all / not every / not both / not always部分否定：not all / not every / not both / not always
With not, the words all, every, both, always and entirely give 'not in every case'. For a complete negative, use none, no, neither or never instead.
all, every, both, always, entirely 与 not 连用表「并非都」；要表全部否定改用 none, no, neither, never。

Watch the word order: All that glitters is not gold. Although not comes later, the sense is still partial — not everything that glitters.

注意语序：All that glitters is not gold.（闪光的未必都是金子）——not 虽然在后，仍是部分否定。

  • Not everyone likes football.
  • I don't know both of them.（只认识其中一个）
Inversion after So / Thus, and preposition + whichSo / Thus + 谓语 + 主语 的倒装与「介词 + which」
To state a result or bring something new on stage, you can put so, thus, here or there first and the verb before the subject. With preposition + which or whom, the preposition is chosen by the verb or noun it belongs to.
表示结果或引出新事物时，可把 so/thus/here/there 放句首，谓语放在主语前（完全倒装）；介词 + which/whom 引导定语从句时，介词随动词或名词搭配决定。

the principle of which = whose principle; in formal writing of which often follows the noun in this way.

the principle of which = whose principle；正式文体中 of which 常放在名词之后。

  • Here comes the bus.（主语是名词时倒装；代词则不倒装：Here it comes.）
  • He has two sons, both of whom are doctors.
The passive in scientific writing, and by doing as an adverbial of means科技英语中的被动与「方式状语 by doing」
Explanations of how something works commonly use the passive, with a thing as subject; by doing says by what means, and its understood subject is usually people in general.
说明原理时常用被动（主语是物）；by doing 表方式，其逻辑主语常是泛指的「人」。

In this lesson: A sound ... will be reflected ...; by measuring the time interval ... the depth can be calculated.

本课：A sound … will be reflected …；by measuring the time interval … the depth can be calculated。

In your own writing, a passive main clause can carry a by doing phrase whose doer is never named; just avoid the dangling participle that leaves the reader guessing.

写作时，若主句为被动，by doing 的逻辑主语可以是没出现的执行者；但避免引起歧义的悬垂分词。

  • The distance can be measured by timing the echo.
  • Water can be purified by boiling it.

Summary writing摘要写作

In no more than 80 words, explain the principle of echo-location and what it is used for, from human echo-sounding through to bats.

用不超过 80 个词解释回声定位的原理和应用，从人类的回声测深一直写到蝙蝠。

Points a good summary covers好摘要要覆盖的要点
  • A shout meets a wall or a hillside and comes back; the further off the barrier, the later the echo arrives.喊声遇到墙壁或山坡会返回回声，障碍越远回声来得越晚。
  • A tap on a ship's body sends sound to the sea floor, and timing the gap gives the depth: that is the echo-sounding instrument.敲击船体的声音被海底反射，测出时间间隔就能算出水深——这就是回声测深仪。
  • Fish throw sound back as well, so a shoal can be found, and the kind of fish told from the shape of the echo.鱼群也能反射声音，于是可以找到鱼群，甚至根据回声分辨鱼种。
  • Some bats give out high thin cries and use what comes back to miss obstacles and take insects in flight.某些蝙蝠发出尖叫声，凭回声避开障碍、捕捉飞虫。
  • Radar works on much the same principle.这种回声定位与雷达原理相似。
Our model summary本站范文

Sound is reflected by solid objects, and the further the object, the longer the echo takes to return. By timing echoes from the sea bottom, ships measure depth with echo-sounding apparatus. Shoals of fish also reflect sound, so they can be located and even identified by their echoes. Certain bats use the same principle: they emit squeaks and use the echoes to avoid obstacles and catch insects, much as radar works.（71 词）
