📘 New Concept English · Book 4 · Lesson 13 “The Search for Oil”新概念英语 第四册 Lesson 13《The Search for Oil》

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
An oil engineer describing a well in the first person: why the tower has to be tall, how the pipe turns, how a core sample is taken, how mud holds back the pressure underground, and why nobody in the trade wants the romantic black fountain. This is process exposition: written in working order, thick with technical nouns, with when / once / until threading the steps together. A good lesson for learning how to explain a working procedure in English.
一位石油工程师用第一人称介绍钻井：井架为什么要高、钻杆怎样转动、怎样取岩芯样本、怎样用泥浆控制地下压力，以及为什么不要「井喷」那种浪漫景象。这是过程说明文：按操作顺序写，大量技术名词，句子靠 when / once / until 把步骤串起来。很适合学「怎样用英语说明一个工作流程」。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. Why is there no need to send people down a well, as there is in other kinds of mining?为什么采油不需要像采矿那样派人下井？
  2. Why does the tower above the hole have to be so tall?井架为什么要造得很高？
  3. Why do people in the oil trade want to avoid a blowout?石油工人为什么要避免井喷？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

The attribution line in the original Book 4 reads 'T. F. Gaskell, The Search for the Earth's Minerals, from Discovery'. Tom Gaskell (1916-1995) was a British geophysicist: chief petroleum physicist for the Anglo-Iranian company that became British Petroleum, and chief scientist of the 1950-52 voyage of HMS Challenger, the survey linked to the discovery of the deepest point in the ocean. Discovery was a British popular-science monthly; we have not located the article itself, so the date is not verified.

旧版第四册课末署名作「T. F. Gaskell, The Search for the Earth's Minerals, from Discovery」。汤姆·加斯克尔（1916–1995）是英国地球物理学家：曾任盎格鲁-伊朗石油公司（后来的英国石油）首席石油物理学家，并担任 1950–1952 年英国皇家海军「挑战者」号环球海洋考察的首席科学家——与发现世界最深海沟点有关的那次考察。Discovery 是英国的通俗科学月刊；原文我们没找到，所以发表年代未核。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

This is an engineer talking shop in a popular magazine, and the date shows in every detail. In the 1950s a well was a vertical hole; steel pipe turned from the top by an engine at the surface, and a quarter of a mile of it hanging in the hole was why the tower had to be tall. Britain had no oil of its own to speak of, so the writer's working life had been spent in Iran and at sea, and the appetite for this kind of article was part of a wider postwar confidence that the earth could be surveyed, mapped and tapped by instrument rather than by luck. Note what he takes for granted: that the geologist reads the rock from a cylinder brought up by hand, and that a well which blows out is a nuisance rather than a catastrophe.

这是一位工程师在通俗杂志上讲本行，年代感写在每一个细节里。五十年代的油井是一个垂直的洞：钢管由地面发动机从顶上带动旋转，洞里垂着四百米长的管子，这才是塔要造高的原因。英国本土几乎没有石油，所以作者的职业生涯多半在伊朗和海上度过；这类文章有市场，也属于战后那种更广的自信——地球可以靠仪器去测、去画、去开采，而不再靠运气。注意他默认的两件事：地质学家靠人工取上来的一截岩芯来读地层；井喷是件讨厌事，不是一场灾难。

✨ How it is written赏析

Process writing has its own grammar, and this passage is a clean model of it. The passive voice does most of the work, because what matters is what happens to the pipe and the rock, not who happens to be holding the wrench: a sample is obtained, the pressure must be under control. Time words carry the sequence instead of first, second, third: when the site has been chosen, once the oil is reached, until it can be led away, every so often. The pronoun we is the voice of a trade rather than of a man, which is why the piece can sound both personal and impersonal at once. And look at the ending: he names the picture the reader already has in mind, the black fountain, and then refuses it. Rejecting a cliche is a strong way to close, far stronger than a summary.

过程说明文有自己的语法，本篇是个干净的范本。被动语态承担了大部分工作，因为要紧的是管子和岩石经历了什么，而不是谁正握着扳手：取出一个样品、压力必须受控。顺序不靠 first、second、third，而靠时间词来带：选定井位之后、一旦打到油层、直到能把它导走、每隔一段时间。第一人称 we 代表的是一个行当而不是一个人，所以这篇文章能同时显得既亲切又客观。再看结尾：他先点出读者心里早有的那个画面——黑色喷泉——然后拒绝它。否掉一个陈词滥调是很有力的收尾，比总结一遍强得多。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

Two things have moved since. Depth and shape: the well no longer goes straight down. Guinness records the Z-44 well at the Chayvo field off Sakhalin at 12,376 metres drilled in August 2012, and that figure is length along the hole, not depth below the rig; later wells on the same project ran longer still, and a hole that turns sideways for kilometres is now routine, which is what made shale oil and gas worth producing at all. Pressure control, the quiet subject of his last paragraph, turned out to deserve a much larger place. In April 2010 the Macondo well in the Gulf of Mexico blew out, eleven men on the rig were killed, and the United States government later put the discharge at 4.9 million barrels. The remedies had the same names he uses: heavy fluid pumped down to hold the formation back, tried once in June and again in August. So his engineering is still right, and his tone is the part that dates. He treats the loss of oil as the reason to avoid a blowout. Today the reason is the eleven names, the coastline and the decade of litigation, and the oil is almost an afterthought.

此后变了两件事。一是深度和形状：井不再是直着往下打。吉尼斯记录库页岛外海柴沃油田的 Z-44 井为 12,376 米，2012 年 8 月钻成——而且这是沿井眼量的长度，不是井口往下的垂深；同一项目后来的井更长，而横着拐几公里的井眼如今是常规操作，正是它让页岩油气有了开采价值。二是压力控制，他最后一段轻描淡写带过的那件事，后来证明值得更大的篇幅。2010 年 4 月墨西哥湾的马孔多井井喷，钻井平台上 11 人死亡，美国政府后来把外泄量定为 490 万桶。补救手段的名字跟他用的一样：往下泵重质流体把地层压住，6 月试了一次，8 月又试一次。所以他的工程判断至今成立，带年代印记的是语气。他把「油白流掉」当作要避免井喷的理由；今天的理由是那 11 个名字、那条海岸线和十年的诉讼，油几乎成了末节。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 2第 2 句 (begins开头 “But we do …”)

  • as opens a clause of manner or comparison; the repeated verb has been dropped and only the modal is left, which is the standard English way of avoiding repetition.as 引导方式/比较状语从句，后面省略了重复的 send man down，只留情态动词 must——英语避免重复的典型手段。
  • man here means human beings in general and takes no article; most writers now use people.man：泛指「人」，无冠词，现在多用 people。

Sentence 4第 4 句 (begins开头 “My particular experience …”)

  • do more to do something than ...: to contribute more towards it than something else does.do more to do … than …：在……方面比……贡献更大。
  • than any other + singular noun: compared with every other member of the group, so the sense is superlative.than any other + 单数名词：与同类中其他任何一个比较，表最高级含义。

Sentence 7第 7 句 (begins开头 “It has to …”)

  • The object is long, so it has been moved behind both verbs and their adverbials (end weight). The plain order would put it straight after the first verb.宾语 great lengths of drill pipe 很长，被移到两个动词及其状语之后（尾重原则）：本应 lower great lengths of drill pipe into the ground。
  • The which clause has two passive verbs joined by and.which 定语从句里两个并列被动谓语。

Sentence 12第 12 句 (begins开头 “It cuts a …”)

  • Inversion inside a relative clause: the normal order would end with the preposition and which. The subject is long and carries a relative clause of its own, so it goes to the end.定语从句内部倒装：正常语序 the strata … can be seen from which；主语 the strata 很长（还带定语从句），所以放到最后。

Sentence 13第 13 句 (begins开头 “Once we get …”)

  • once here is a conjunction meaning as soon as.once：连词，一旦。
  • either from gas or water: an inserted note on where the pressure comes from.either from gas or water：插入说明压力来源。

Sentence 16第 16 句 (begins开头 “We want it …”)

  • want somebody or something to do: a complex object.want sb/sth to do：复合宾语。
  • in a ... manner: the written way of saying it, standing in for an adverb.in a … manner：以……的方式，书面替代副词（= controllably）。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

Using an auxiliary or a modal in place of the repeated part (ellipsis)用助动词/情态动词代替重复部分（省略）
English dislikes repetition: when the second half of a sentence would repeat the verb of the first, only the auxiliary or modal is kept (do/does/did, can, must, will, have...) and the rest is left out.
英语不喜欢重复：后半句与前半句谓语相同时，只保留助动词或情态动词（do/does/did, can, must, will, have…），其余省略。

Common after as, than, so, too and neither: She works harder than I do. / He can swim, and so can I.

常与 as, than, so, too, neither 等连用：She works harder than I do. / He can swim, and so can I.

  • I didn't want to go, but I had to.（省略 go，保留 to）
  • If you haven't finished, you should have.
End weight: long objects moved back, and inversion in a relative clause尾重原则：长宾语后移与定语从句倒装
Keep the sentence light at the front and heavy at the end: push long, complicated parts (objects, subjects) towards the end. To do that you may move the object, or invert the order inside a clause.
句子要「头轻脚重」：长而复杂的成分（宾语、主语）往句尾放；为此可调整宾语位置或在从句中倒装。

Same shape: On the wall hung a portrait of the founder of the school. (adverbial of place in front, full inversion after it)

类似：On the wall hung a portrait of the founder of the school.（地点状语前置 + 倒装）

  • He explained to us the reasons why he had refused the offer.
  • Among the guests was a famous writer who had just returned from Africa.
Linking words for a process: when / once / until / every so often / by means of过程说明常用连接：when / once / until / every so often / by means of
When you write up a procedure, use time words to mark the steps and conditions: when, once (as soon as), until, every so often (from time to time), by means of (with the help of).
写流程时用时间连词标出步骤和条件：when（当……时）、once（一旦）、until（直到）、every so often（不时、每隔一段时间）、by means of（借助）。
  • Once you understand the rule, the exercises are easy.
  • We communicate by means of signals.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • … has done more to … than any other … ……对……的贡献比其他任何……都大

Summary writing摘要写作

In no more than 80 words, set out the stages of drilling an oil well, from choosing the site to getting the flow under control.

用不超过 80 个词按步骤说明石油钻井的过程，从选址到控制油流。

Points a good summary covers好摘要要覆盖的要点
  • Oil wells are the deepest holes of all, up to 25,000 feet, but they are narrow borings under a foot across, so nobody has to go down.油井是最深的井，可达 25000 英尺，但只是直径不到一英尺的钻孔，不需要派人下去。
  • Once the site is settled, a tall tower is built to lower and raise long strings of pipe, which an engine at the surface turns and which carry a cutting tool at the lower end.选好位置后在地面立高大井架，用来放下和提起很长的钻杆；钻杆由顶部发动机带动旋转，底部装钻头。
  • From time to time a hollow tool brings up a core of rock, and the geologist reads the layers from it.不时用取芯钻头取出岩芯，地质学家据此了解地层。
  • At the oil, gas or water pressure pushes the oil upwards, and that pressure has to be held by heavy fluid pumped round.钻到油层后，气或水的压力会把油推上来，必须用循环泥浆控制压力。
  • A blowout wastes oil and gas, so the oil is kept below until it can be led away under control.要避免浪费油气的井喷，把油留在井下直到可以有控制地导出。
Our model summary本站范文

Oil wells, up to 25,000 feet deep, are narrow borings, so nobody needs to go down. A tall derrick is built to lower and raise long drill pipes, which an engine rotates. Samples of rock are taken now and then so that geologists know which strata have been reached. When oil is found, gas or water pressure pushes it up, so mud is circulated to control it and prevent a wasteful gusher.（72 词）
