Lesson 45 · Of Men and Galaxies 人与星系

📘 New Concept English · Book 4 · Lesson 45 “Of Men and Galaxies”新概念英语 第四册 Lesson 45《Of Men and Galaxies》

C1🧠 23 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
Human beings won their contest with the other animals long ago - the last rival was the mosquito - and the contest now is between people and between groups. What has changed is the setting that decides who wins: it is no longer material but mental. The writer, the astronomer Fred Hoyle, offers a test case. Morocco and California lie at much the same latitude, with much the same climate and much the same resources, and they have turned out utterly differently; the difference is in what their inhabitants think. Ideas are dangerous things: twenty or thirty pages about the atomic bomb, handed over around 1925, would have been enough to change the history of the world. This is a philosophical argument, each link fastened to the last, full of conditionals and guarded claims, and it ends in a modest joke against itself: we are all prisoners of the age we live in.
人类早已赢了与其他生物的竞争（最后一个对手是蚊子），现在的竞争是人与人、群体与群体之间的——但决定胜负的环境已不再是物质的，而是思想。作者（天文学家弗雷德·霍伊尔）举例：摩洛哥和加利福尼亚纬度、气候、资源都差不多，发展却天差地别，原因在居民头脑里的想法。思想是危险的：1925 年前后，二三十页关于原子弹的资料就足以改变世界历史。本课是哲理性议论文，作者的论证一环扣一环，大量使用表示程度与推测的虚拟语气，结尾带着谦逊的自嘲：我们都是当代世界的「囚徒」。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. What, in the writer's view, is the environment that now decides our evolution?作者认为现在决定人类进化的环境是什么？
  2. What does the comparison of Morocco with California prove?摩洛哥和加利福尼亚的例子说明了什么？
  3. Why are ideas dangerous, and why can the writer not give an example?为什么说思想是危险的？作者为什么举不出例子？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

The lesson comes from Fred Hoyle's Of Men and Galaxies, published by the University of Washington Press in Seattle in 1964 - a thin book of seventy-three pages, priced at $2.95, made out of the third series of the John Danz Lectures, which Hoyle delivered at that university. Prometheus Books reissued it in 2005. We confirmed the publisher, the year, the length and the lecture origin from a review in Physics Today and from catalogue records; we have not read the lectures themselves, so we cannot say which of the three this passage belongs to.

课文出自弗雷德·霍伊尔（Fred Hoyle）的《Of Men and Galaxies》，1964 年西雅图华盛顿大学出版社出版——一本七十三页的小书，定价 2.95 美元，内容是他在该校所作的第三辑 John Danz 讲座。2005 年 Prometheus Books 重印。出版社、年份、页数与「讲座稿」的来历我们核过（《Physics Today》书评与图书馆目录）；讲座原文未读，所以说不出这段属于三讲中的哪一讲。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

It matters that this is a lecture and that the lecturer was an astronomer, not an economist or a historian. Hoyle was then one of the best-known scientists in Britain, a man who had worked out where the chemical elements are manufactured inside stars, who argued to the end of his life for a universe with no beginning, and who, in trying to make the rival theory sound absurd on the radio, gave it the name everybody now uses. He also wrote science fiction and talked on the air a great deal, and he was used to standing in front of an audience with a large idea and no equations. All of that is audible here: the confident sweep, the readiness to walk straight into economics and anthropology without credentials, the willingness to say I and to make himself the man losing an argument with a bear. And the date, 1964, sits two years after the autumn when the two nuclear powers came as close as they ever came to using what they had. When this lecturer says that a few pages of information could have turned the world over, nobody in that hall needed the point explained.

「这是一篇讲演，而讲演者是天文学家、不是经济学家或历史学家」这件事很重要。霍伊尔当时是英国最有名的科学家之一：他算出了化学元素是在恒星内部造出来的，一生都主张宇宙没有开端；而他为了在广播里把对立的理论说得荒唐，反倒给它起了今天人人都在用的那个名字。他还写科幻、常上广播，习惯了带着一个大想法、不带方程式站在听众面前。这些在这一页上都听得见：那种自信的大跨步，那种不要资历就径直走进经济学与人类学的劲头，以及愿意说「我」、愿意把自己写成那个跟熊吵输了的人。而 1964 这个年份，离那个两个核大国最接近动用手里东西的秋天，只隔了两年。当这位讲演者说几页资料就能把世界翻过来，台下没有人需要别人解释这句话。

✨ How it is written赏析

The page is built as a funnel, and watching it narrow is the lesson. It opens on the whole of evolution, comes down to one man and one bear, comes down again to two places on a map, and finishes on twenty or thirty pages of paper. Each step is smaller and more concrete than the one before, and the last one is small enough to hold. Notice what the bear is for. A lecturer about to make a very large claim first makes a joke at his own expense, and the joke buys two things: the audience's goodwill, and the right to say I without sounding grand. Notice too how carefully he grades what he will and will not assert. He says he would like to give an example of a destructive idea and fortunately cannot - and that single word, fortunately, is doing a great deal of work, since it turns an admission of failure into a piece of good news about the world. Then comes the finest move on the page: having said that only an outsider can bring in a dangerous thought, he classes himself with the insiders, a prisoner of his own time who can get only a short way past the patterns he was trained in. The argument is thereby applied to the man making it, which is both honest and, as rhetoric, very hard to answer. Two things to take for your own writing: when you are about to claim something enormous, pay for it first with a small admission; and if your argument applies to you, say so before the reader notices.

这一页是按漏斗造的，而看着它一层层收窄就是一课。它从整个进化开场，收到一个人和一头熊，再收到地图上的两个地方，最后落在二三十页纸上。每一步都比上一步更小、更具体，而最后那一步小到可以拿在手里。注意那头熊是干什么用的：一个即将提出极大主张的讲演者，先拿自己开了个玩笑；这个玩笑买到两样东西——听众的善意，以及说「我」而不显得自大的权利。也注意他如何小心地给「要断言什么、不断言什么」分级。他说他想举一个破坏性思想的例子，而「幸好」举不出来——而「幸好」这一个词干了很多活：它把一次承认失败变成了一条关于世界的好消息。接着是全页最漂亮的一手：他刚说完只有局外人才能带进危险的思想，就把自己归进了局内人——一个被自己时代关着的囚徒，只能在受训得来的模式之外想一点点。于是这个论证被用在了提出它的人身上；这既诚实，作为修辞又极难反驳。两件事可以搬进自己的写作：你要提一个极大的主张之前，先用一个小小的承认把它付掉；而如果你的论证也适用于你自己，就在读者发现之前自己说出来。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

His main claim won, and then it was taken away from him. The idea that two places dealt the same physical hand can end up far apart, and that the explanation lies in something other than soil and sunshine, is now the mainstream position in the study of why countries are rich or poor; the Nobel prize in economics went in 2024 to three economists for exactly this line of work. But the winning version does not say what he said. The non-material thing that decides it is not the contents of people's heads; it is institutions - who is allowed to own what, who can be taxed, who can be overthrown - and one of the best-known findings in that literature is that regions which were the richer ones five hundred years ago are the poorer ones now, because of the arrangements their colonizers left behind. Thoughts matter in that account, but as the output of rules rather than as a free-floating cause, and his own example is the awkward case: Morocco and California differ in colonial history, in property law and in half a century of state spending, and a reader in 1964 was invited to see a difference of mentality where there was also a difference of institutions. His second claim, about the twenty or thirty pages, has aged differently again. It is almost right and importantly wrong. Almost right, because the information hazard he describes is now a recognized category and the central worry of several fields at once, from dual-use biology to the publication of machine-learning capabilities. Importantly wrong, because the pages he imagines mostly existed and were published: the physics of fission had been published openly in the journals before the war, and what separated a paper from a weapon was industrial - the American programme cost about 1.9 billion dollars by the end of 1945 and employed some 125,000 people at its peak. An idea on its own does not turn the world over. An idea plus a state with money does. That correction matters today, because the same argument is being had again about whether certain kinds of knowledge should be published at all, and the honest answer depends on whether the hard part is the idea or the factory. For Chinese readers the most useful thing here may be the last sentence rather than the argument. A man who had spent his working life being told he was wrong about the universe - and who was, on that question - says that he cannot think more than a little way outside the patterns he was trained in. Anyone who has sat an examination in one country and then worked in another knows which of the two statements on this page is easier to verify.

他的主张赢了，然后被人从他手里拿走了。「两个被发的物质牌面相同的地方，可以走到相差极远的地步，而解释不在土壤与日照里」——这在今天研究「国家为什么富或穷」的领域里已是主流立场；2024 年的诺贝尔经济学奖正是给了这条线上的三位经济学家。但获胜的那个版本并不是他说的那个：决定结局的非物质因素不是人们头脑里的内容，而是制度——谁可以拥有什么、谁可以被征税、谁可以被推翻；而这套文献里最有名的发现之一是，五百年前较富的那些地区今天反而较穷，原因在于殖民者留下的安排。在那套解释里思想仍然重要，但它是规则的产物，不是一个自由漂浮的原因；而他自己的例子恰恰是尴尬的那一个：摩洛哥与加利福尼亚的差别不只在心态，也在殖民史、在产权法、在半个世纪的公共支出上，而 1964 年的读者被引着只看见了前者。他的第二个主张——那二三十页——老去的方式又不一样：它几乎是对的，而且错在要紧处。几乎对，是因为他描述的「信息危害」今天已是一个被正式命名的范畴，同时是好几个领域的核心忧虑，从两用生物学到机器学习能力的公开发表。错在要紧处，是因为他设想的那些页大多已经存在、而且已经发表：核裂变的物理在 1930 年代末就在公开期刊上了，把一篇论文和一件武器分开的东西是工业——那个美国计划到 1945 年底花掉约 19 亿美元，高峰期雇了约十二万五千人。一个想法本身不会把世界翻过来，一个想法加上一个有钱的国家才会。这个修正今天要紧，因为同样的争论正在重演：某些知识到底该不该公开发表；而诚实的答案取决于难的那一半是想法，还是工厂。对中国读者，这一页上最有用的也许不是那个论证，而是最后一句。一个一辈子被人说「你对宇宙的看法错了」的人——而在那个问题上他确实错了——说他没法在自己受训得来的模式之外多想一点。凡是在一个国家考过试、又在另一个国家工作过的人，都知道这一页上的两句话里哪一句更容易验证。

💬 Discuss and write讨论与写作
  1. Take two places you know that were dealt similar hands and turned out differently. List what differs: beliefs, rules, money, history. Which of them would you put first, and what would make you change your order?
    挑两个你熟悉的、条件相近而结果不同的地方。把差别列出来：观念、规则、钱、历史。你会把哪一项放在第一位？什么样的证据会让你改变这个排序？
  2. He says some knowledge is too dangerous to publish, and today several fields argue about exactly that. Write 120 words taking a side, and make the best case for the other side first. Decide, in your answer, whether the hard part is having the idea or building the thing.
    他说有些知识危险到不该发表，而今天好几个领域正为这件事争论。写 120 词选一边，先把另一边最有力的说法写出来。在回答里给出你的判断：难的那一半是想出那个念头，还是把东西造出来。
  3. He ends by calling himself a prisoner of his own time. Name one thing you believe that you think people fifty years from now will find strange, and say why you believe it anyway.
    他最后把自己称作自己时代的囚徒。说出一件你相信、而你认为五十年后的人会觉得奇怪的事，并说明你为什么还是相信它。

  • dispute something with somebody: contest it against them.dispute sth with sb：与某人争夺某物。

Sentence 17第 17 句 (begins开头 “not so much …”)

  • not so much A but B: B is nearer the truth than A. The standard form is not so much A as B, and the writer has used but instead.not so much A but B：「与其说是 A，不如说是 B」；标准形式是 not so much A as B，作者用了 but。
  • even here presses the point: not even the difference in the people is the main cause.even：「甚至」，强调连人种差异都不是主因。

Sentence 22第 22 句 (begins开头 “the most destructive …”)

  • it is the formal subject (Lesson 37).It is well known that …：形式主语（L37）。
  • The where-clause is an adverbial of place: in places where this has happened.where … ：地点状语从句（「在……的地方」）。
  • not from A but from B: not from the first source but from the second (Lesson 32).not from A but from B：不是来自 A 而是来自 B（L32）。

Sentence 26第 26 句 (begins开头 “you are conditioned …”)

  • Inside the because-clause sits a when-clause.because 从句里又套 when 从句。
  • to such a degree that ...: so far that, with the consequence following.to such a degree that …：到了……的程度，以至于（结果状语）。
  • The sense: free speech is possible precisely because people living inside a community find it so hard to think of anything genuinely destructive.意思：正因为身在其中的人很难想出真正有破坏性的思想，社会才敢允许言论自由。

Sentence 34第 34 句 (begins开头 “would have been …”)

  • A past participle after the noun, carrying a condition with it: if they had been handed over.handed to …：过去分词作后置定语，同时含条件意味（= if they had been handed to …）。
  • would have been: contrary to what actually happened, since nobody handed over any such pages in 1925.would have been：与过去事实相反的虚拟（1925 年并没有人给出这些资料）。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

Unreal conditions in the past: would have done与过去事实相反的虚拟：would have done
For something that did not happen: if plus had done, with would, could or might plus have done in the main clause. The condition can also hide inside a noun phrase, a prepositional phrase or a participle.
假设过去没发生的事：If + had done，主句 would/could/might + have done；条件也可以藏在名词短语、介词短语或分词里（含蓄条件）。

Compare Lesson 33, where the condition is contrary to present fact (were / would do). Here everything moves one step back: the past tense becomes had done, and would do becomes would have done.

与 L33（与现在事实相反：were / would do）对比：时间往前推一格——过去式 → had done；would do → would have done。

Mixed conditions put the condition in the past and the result in the present: If he had taken the medicine, he would be well now.

混合虚拟：条件在过去、结果在现在：If he had taken the medicine, he would be well now.

  • If I had known your number, I would have called you.
  • Without your help, I could not have finished the work.（含蓄条件）
not so much A as B: more B than Anot so much A as B：与其说是 A，不如说是 B
not so much A as B says that B fits the facts better than A, and the two must be parallel in form (nouns, prepositional phrases or clauses).
not so much A as B 表示 B 比 A 更符合实际；A、B 是平行成分（名词、介词短语、从句都可以）。

A related phrase, not so much as, means not even: He didn't so much as say thank you.

相关：not so much as ＝「连……都不」：He didn't so much as say thank you.（他连谢谢都没说）

Compare not A but B (Lesson 32), which rejects A outright; not so much A as B only says that A is not the main thing.

与 not A but B（L32，「不是 A 而是 B」，完全否定 A）比：not so much A as B 只是说 A 不是主要原因。

  • He is not so much a writer as a journalist.
  • The problem is not so much the money as the time.
Noun plus infinitive: the first / the last / the only ... to do名词 + 不定式作定语：the first / last / only … to do
After a noun preceded by an ordinal, a superlative, or the only, the last or the next, an infinitive often follows as a modifier, meaning the first, the last or the only one that does it.
名词前有序数词、最高级、the only / the last / the next 等时，后面常用不定式作定语，表示「第一个/最后一个/唯一……的」。

The phrase in Lesson 1 about the only way is a different structure, with the infinitive as complement.

L1 中的 the only way … is to recount 是另一种结构（不定式作表语）。

For a passive sense, use to be done: He was the first man to be sent into space.

被动关系用 to be done：He was the first man to be sent into space.

Infinitives after other nouns work in the same way: a book to read, nothing to do, the man to ask.

其他名词后的不定式：a book to read（要读的书）、nothing to do、the man to ask（该问的人）。

  • She was the first woman to win the prize.
  • He is always the last to leave the office.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • … must have been critical ……当时一定至关重要
    In the early years, his support must have been critical.
  • not so much because of A, but because of B 与其说是因为 A，不如说是因为 B
    He failed not so much because of bad luck, but because of poor planning.
  • to such a degree that … 到了……的程度，以至于……
    He was tired to such a degree that he fell asleep standing up.

Keywords for retelling复述关键词

Summary writing摘要写作

In no more than 80 words: what does the writer think is now the most important part of our environment, and what examples does he use to show the power of ideas?

用不超过 80 个词概括：作者认为现代人类社会中最重要的环境因素是什么？他用哪些例子说明思想的力量？

Points a good summary covers好摘要要覆盖的要点
  • The contest with the other animals is over; the contest between people is not, but the setting that decides our evolution is no longer mainly a material one.人与其他生物的竞争已经结束，人与人之间的竞争仍然激烈，但决定进化的环境不再主要是物质的。
  • What we believe shapes that setting: Morocco and California were dealt much the same natural hand and have ended up far apart, and the difference lies in what the people there think.环境主要由我们的信念决定：摩洛哥和加州自然条件相近，发展迥异，原因在于居民的思想。
  • Ideas are dangerous: what the white man did most damage with was not weapons but ideas, and only an outsider can put a dangerous idea into a community.思想是危险的：白人对原始文化最大的破坏来自思想；只有局外人才能注入危险的思想。
  • Around 1925, twenty or thirty pages about the bomb would have been enough to change history; and we, prisoners of our own age, can hardly think of such ideas ourselves.1925 年前后二三十页原子弹资料就能改变历史；我们都是当代世界的囚徒，很难想出这样的思想。
Our model summary本站范文

Man has defeated other creatures, but competition between people continues, and it is now shaped less by the physical environment than by beliefs. Morocco and California have similar natural conditions yet very different development because their people think differently. Ideas are dangerous: they harmed primitive cultures more than weapons, and a few pages on the atomic bomb could have changed history in 1925. Yet our minds, prisoners of our age, cannot easily imagine such ideas.（75 词）
