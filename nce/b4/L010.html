🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
An American magazine report from 1989 (its 'future' is our past): chip-design tools would let engineers make chips at their desks, so garage start-ups might come back; Asian engineers were becoming the core of the Valley and could use language and culture to link it with Pacific Rim markets. The fun of reading it now is checking the predictions: which came true and which did not. The language is typical American news writing: long appositives, participle phrases of result, and figures of speech (back to the future, near lock, in droves).
上世纪八九十年代之交的美国杂志报道（文中的「未来」就是我们今天的过去）：芯片设计工具让工程师能在办公桌上做芯片，车库创业可能卷土重来；亚裔工程师成为硅谷主力，能借助语言和文化联通环太平洋市场。读它的乐趣在于回看预测：哪些说中了（台湾芯片厂挑战日本），哪些没说中。语言上是典型的美式新闻：长同位语、分词结果状语、比喻（back to the future, near lock, in droves）。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. Why might technology trends push Silicon Valley 'back to the future'?技术趋势为什么可能把硅谷「推回未来」？
  2. What special advantage do Asian entrepreneurs have?亚裔创业者有什么独特优势？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

The lesson is an edited news feature from the American weekly US News and World Report, issue of October 2, 1989 (attribution in the original Book 4); no author is named.

课文是美国新闻周刊《美国新闻与世界报道》1989 年 10 月 2 日一期的报道节选（据旧版第四册课末署名），未署作者。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

In 1989 American business was anxious about Japan. Japanese firms had won the memory-chip market — they held more than 70 per cent of commodity DRAM sales until about 1990 — and many Americans feared the US would lose the whole industry. This article offers two reasons for hope. The first is Carver Mead, the Caltech professor whose work on very-large-scale integration (VLSI) showed that chip design could be separated from chip manufacturing; if design becomes cheap and fast, small teams can compete again. The second is people: immigrant engineers from China, Taiwan, Hong Kong, Korea, the Philippines and India. A decade later the sociologist AnnaLee Saxenian measured this: Chinese and Indian engineers were running about a quarter (24 per cent) of the Valley's technology companies founded between 1980 and 1998.

1989 年的美国商界很焦虑日本：日本企业拿下了内存芯片市场——直到 1990 年前后，通用 DRAM 七成以上的份额都在日本手里——很多美国人担心整个产业都要丢掉。这篇报道给出两个乐观的理由。一是卡弗·米德：这位加州理工教授在超大规模集成电路（VLSI）上的工作，证明芯片设计可以和芯片制造分开；设计一旦又便宜又快，小团队就能重新参与竞争。二是人：来自中国大陆、台湾、香港、韩国、菲律宾和印度的移民工程师。十年后，社会学家萨克森宁（AnnaLee Saxenian）做了量化：1980 到 1998 年创办的硅谷科技公司里，约四分之一（24%）由华裔和印度裔工程师掌舵。

✨ How it is written赏析

This is magazine journalism, and it shows in the rhythm. The writer opens with a pun on a film title (Back to the Future, 1985), packs a person's whole career into one appositive, and uses a homely comparison — designing a chip is like making a newsletter on a Macintosh — so that a general reader can picture a technical change. Then comes a single fragment, And on Asians., which turns the paragraph from technology to people in three words. Notice how the article moves from a named expert, to a general trend, to named examples, and ends on a joke about a retirement village. If you write a report, borrow the pattern: an expert's claim, the trend behind it, two concrete cases, and a short closing line with an image.

这是杂志新闻的写法，节奏感很强。开头用电影名玩双关（《回到未来》，1985），一个同位语就装下一个人的整段履历，再用家常的比方——设计芯片就像在 Mac 上做一份简报——让普通读者看得见一项技术变化。接着一个只有三个词的省略句 And on Asians.，一下把话题从技术转到人。注意文章的推进：点名的专家 → 背后的趋势 → 点名的例子，最后用「养老院」开个玩笑收尾。写报道可以借这个结构：专家的判断、背后的趋势、两个具体案例、一句带画面的短结尾。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

Reading it thirty-five years later is a lesson in prediction. Mead was right about design: today most chip companies are fabless — they design at their desks and pay a foundry to manufacture. But the winner of that split was not a garage. It was a foundry in Taiwan, TSMC, founded in 1987, which now makes most of the world's most advanced chips. The memory market did leave Japan, but it went mainly to Korea: Samsung became the largest DRAM maker in 1992. And the prediction about people came true beyond the article's hopes. The lesson for a reader: an article can see the direction of change and still get the geography wrong. For Chinese readers, the story of engineers who use two languages and two networks to build businesses across the Pacific is also the story of many families today.

三十五年后再读，是一堂关于预测的课。米德在设计上说对了：今天多数芯片公司是「无晶圆厂」模式——在办公桌上设计，花钱请代工厂制造。但这场分工的赢家不是车库，而是 1987 年创立的台湾代工厂台积电，如今全世界最先进的芯片大多由它生产。内存市场确实离开了日本，却主要去了韩国：三星在 1992 年成为全球最大的 DRAM 厂商。关于「人」的预测则超额实现。给读者的启示是：一篇文章可能看准了变化的方向，却看错了地理。对中国读者来说，工程师凭两种语言、两张人脉网跨太平洋创业，这也正是今天许多家庭的故事。

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 2第 2 句 (begins开头 “Carver Mead, a …”)

  • a pioneer in ... and a professor of ...: a long appositive telling us who Mead is.a pioneer in…and a professor of…：长同位语，介绍 Mead 的身份。
  • After notes, a noun clause with that left out; that enable ... is a relative clause describing workstations.notes 后省略 that 的宾语从句；that enable…：定语从句修饰 workstations。
  • much the way (that) an editor creates ...: a clause of manner, 'very like an editor making a newsletter on a Mac'. 'and editor' in the source text is a typing error for 'an editor'.much the way (that) an editor creates…：方式状语，「很像编辑在 Mac 上做简报那样」。原文 and editor 是课文录入错字，应为 an editor。

  • without being penalized: preposition + passive gerund.without being penalized：介词 + 动名词被动式。

Sentence 7第 7 句 (begins开头 “And on Asians. …”)

  • The short sentence And on Asians. leaves words out: = And the industry thrives on Asians, continuing thrive on anarchy.上一句 And on Asians. 是省略句，= And the industry thrives on Asians，承接 thrive on anarchy。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

Present participles for result现在分词作结果状语
A present participle phrase at the end of a sentence can show the natural result of what came before, often strengthened with thus / thereby; its subject can be the whole earlier event.
句末的现在分词短语可表示前面动作自然带来的结果，常用 thus / thereby 加强；逻辑主语可以是前面整件事。

Compare: for an unexpected result use only to do: He hurried to the station, only to find the train had left.

区别：表「意料之外」的结果用 only to do：He hurried to the station, only to find the train had left.

  • The storm destroyed the bridge, cutting off the village.
  • Prices rose sharply, causing widespread anger.
Four common uses of as: while / in the role of / like / becauseas 的四种常见用法：随着／作为／像……那样／因为
As a conjunction, as can mean time and change in step (while), manner (like, the way), or reason (because); as a preposition it gives a role (in the role of).
as 作连词可表时间与同步变化（随着）、方式（像……那样）、原因（因为）；作介词表身份（作为）。

Related: as for reason is weaker than because and usually comes first: As it was late, we went home.

相关：as 表原因时语气弱于 because，常放句首：As it was late, we went home.

  • As he grew older, he became wiser.（随着）
  • Do as I say.（像……那样）
Causative patterns: enable / allow / letenable / allow / let 等使役结构
enable/allow/permit/help sb to do; let/make/have sb do (no to); in the passive make takes to again: He was made to wait.
enable/allow/permit/help sb to do；let/make/have sb do（不带 to）；被动时 make 要还原 to：He was made to wait。
  • The scholarship enabled her to study abroad.
  • Don't let the children play with matches.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • X thrives on … X 靠……兴旺／越……越起劲
    Some people thrive on pressure.
  • much the way … 很像……那样
    She plans her week much the way an engineer designs a machine.
  • let one's imagination soar without being penalized by … 放开想象，不必为……付出代价
    Cheap 3D printing lets designers experiment without being penalized by costly mistakes.
  • get/give sb a jump on … in doing … 在……方面抢先于……
    Early research gave the company a jump on its rivals in launching the product.
  • X may prove … X 可能会证明是……
    The setback may prove a blessing in disguise.

Summary writing摘要写作

In no more than 80 words, sum up the article's two predictions about Silicon Valley: how technology will change chip design and start-ups, and what role Asian engineers will play.

用不超过 80 个词概括文章对硅谷未来的两点预测：技术如何改变芯片设计与创业，亚裔工程师扮演什么角色。

Points a good summary covers好摘要要覆盖的要点
  • New workstations let engineers design and produce chips at their desks, quickly and cheaply.新工作站让工程师在桌上就能快速、低成本地设计和生产芯片。
  • Failure costs less, which may lead to a new wave of garage start-ups and help the US bring products out faster.失败代价变小，可能催生新一代车库创业，帮美国更快推出新产品。
  • Asian engineers already make up most of the technical staff at many Valley firms, and many more are graduating.亚裔工程师已占很多硅谷公司技术人员的多数，且大批毕业。
  • They can use language and culture to link with Pacific Rim markets, for example by building a factory in Taiwan to challenge Japan's hold on memory chips.他们能凭语言和文化联通环太平洋市场，例如在台湾建厂挑战日本内存芯片的垄断。
Our model summary本站范文

New workstations let engineers design and make chips on their desks in days for a few hundred dollars. With failures no longer costly, inventors may create a new wave of garage start-ups and help America beat foreign rivals to market. Silicon Valley also depends on Asian engineers, who already form the majority at many firms and can use their languages and customs to link the Valley with Pacific Rim markets.（70 词）
