Lesson 8 · Trading Standards 贸易标准

📘 New Concept English · Book 4 · Lesson 8 “Trading Standards”新概念英语 第四册 Lesson 8《Trading Standards》

C1🧠 19 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
Business reporting in the house style of The Economist: what blocks trade between America and Europe is no longer the tariff but each side's own testing rules — the same electric razor has to be tested twice. The piece opens on a quarrel about chicken to catch the reader, then moves to double testing, then to why the talks are hard, then to the difference in the two sides' thinking. Marks of the style: the quotation turned inside the sentence (claim officials in Brussels / ask businesses ...), the cleft sentence, and workshop metaphors (put sand in the wheels, hammer out). Anyone doing business across the Canada-US border will recognise the problem.
选自《经济学人》式的财经报道：美欧之间阻碍贸易的已不是关税，而是各自的检测标准——同一个电动剃须刀要在两边各测一遍。文章用「鸡肉之争」开头吸引读者，再讲双重检测、谈判难点、双方思路差异。语言特点：插入式引语（claim officials in Brussels / ask businesses…）、强调句、口语化比喻（put sand in the wheels, hammer out）。对做加拿大—美国跨境生意的人也很有现实感。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. What is it that mainly gets in the way of trade between rich countries now?现在妨碍富国之间贸易的主要是什么？
  2. Why do firms think the second round of testing is pointless?为什么企业认为双重检测没有必要？
  3. Where do America and the EU disagree about how the deal should be built?美国和欧盟在怎样达成协议上有什么分歧？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

The lesson is taken from The Economist of 24 May 1997 (attribution line in the original Book 4). Articles in that paper carry no by-line, so there is no author to name.

课文选自 1997 年 5 月 24 日的《经济学人》（据旧版第四册课末署名）。该刊文章不署作者名，因此无从点名作者。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

This is a news story with a deadline in it, and the deadline makes it datable to the week. The piece was published on 24 May 1997 and says both sides are hoping to close the deal before a summit four days away. What they were negotiating was a mutual recognition agreement: not common standards, but an arrangement under which each side accepts the other's test results, so that one certificate serves both markets. The sectors on the table were the ones the lesson names and a few more — telecommunications equipment, electromagnetic compatibility, electrical safety, medical devices, pharmaceutical manufacturing practice, and recreational boats. The chicken quarrel in the opening lines was not a stray detail either; poultry, hormone-treated beef and later genetically modified crops were the running sores of transatlantic trade for the next twenty years, because they are cases where the two sides disagree not about test results but about what counts as evidence of safety.

这是一篇带着截稿期限的新闻，而那个期限让它可以精确到那一周。文章发表于 1997 年 5 月 24 日，文中说双方希望在四天后的峰会前完成。他们谈的是一份互认协议：不是统一标准，而是各方接受对方的检测结果，使一张证书能同时用于两个市场。当时桌面上的部门就是课文点到的那些，再多几个——电信设备、电磁兼容、电气安全、医疗器械、药品生产规范、游艇。开头那场鸡肉之争也不是顺手的细节：禽肉、用过激素的牛肉，以及后来的转基因作物，是此后二十年跨大西洋贸易的长期伤口，因为在这些问题上双方分歧的不是检测结果，而是「什么才算安全的证据」。

✨ How it is written赏析

The piece is a worked example of how to make an institutional story readable, and the method is visible in the first two lines. It opens with two voices arguing — Brussels says the chickens are not fit for European tables, the Americans say their birds are clean — before a single fact about regulation appears. That is deliberate. Trade policy has no characters, so the writer borrows some. Then watch the scale drop. From chickens we go to a razor and a dialysis machine, two objects you can hold, and the absurdity of the system is delivered through them rather than argued. The paper simply remarks that a shaver passed as safe on one side of the ocean is not very likely to kill anyone on the other. The sentence is funny because it states the obvious in the flattest possible way, and the flatness is the joke. Two other habits are worth copying. The paper puts the reader's own question in the mouth of the firms in the story: companies on either shore are made to ask why a single product has to be tested twice over. That gets the obvious objection raised and answered without the writer ever speaking in his own voice. And the ending is not a conclusion but a contrast of temperaments: the Americans would happily sign one deal at a time, the EU wants general principles. That turns a procedural disagreement into a difference of character, which is far easier to remember than either position.

这篇是「怎么把制度性题材写得读得下去」的一个标本，办法在开头两行就看得见。它以两个声音争吵开场——布鲁塞尔说这些鸡配不上欧洲人的餐桌，美国人说我们的禽肉没问题——而关于监管的第一个事实还没出现。这是故意的。贸易政策没有人物，于是作者借来几个。接着看尺度的下落。从鸡到一把剃须刀和一台透析机，两件你能拿在手里的东西，而制度的荒谬是通过它们交付的，不是论证出来的。文章只是顺口说了一句：在大洋这一边被判定安全的剃须刀，在那一边大概也不至于把人电死。这句话好笑，是因为它用最平的语气说出最明显的事实，而那种平就是笑点。另有两个习惯值得照搬。这家报纸把读者自己的问题塞进故事里人物的嘴里——「大西洋两岸的企业于是问：一次就够，为什么要两次？」——这样最明显的质疑被提出又被回答，而作者从未用自己的声音说话。还有，结尾不是结论，而是两种性情的对照：美国人乐意一份一份签，欧盟要通用原则。这把一场程序分歧变成了性格差异，而性格差异比任何一方的立场都好记得多。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The deal was done, and the lesson's own deadline turned out to be wrong, which is the first useful thing to notice. The agreement was concluded on 20 June 1997, not by the summit four days after publication; it was signed in London on 18 May 1998, approved by the EU Council that June, and the sectors came into operation over the following years rather than all at once. So read the sentence about finishing in time for the summit as what it is: a negotiator's hope quoted in a newspaper, not a fact. Journalism reports intentions in the same grammar it uses for events, and a reader has to keep the two apart. The deeper point is that the mutual recognition agreement solved only the easy half of the problem. Testing twice is a duplication, and duplication can be negotiated away. What cannot be negotiated away so easily is the chicken: the EU objects to the chlorine wash used in American poultry processing, and that is a disagreement about what safe means, not about whether a test was properly run. Every later attempt ran onto the same rock. The Transatlantic Trade and Investment Partnership, launched under a mandate agreed in June 2013 and intended to go much further than mutual recognition, was suspended at the end of 2016 after fifteen rounds, and in April 2019 the EU Council declared the 2013 mandate obsolete. Twenty-two years after this article, the two largest rich-country markets had got their razors recognised and still had not settled their chickens. The lesson to carry away is about which kinds of difference are negotiable: where both sides want the same outcome and merely duplicate the work, agreement is administrative; where they disagree about what the outcome should be, calling it a technical barrier does not make it one.

协议签成了，而课文里那个期限后来并不成立——这是第一件值得注意的事。协议于 1997 年 6 月 20 日谈成，而不是在文章发表四天后的峰会上；1998 年 5 月 18 日在伦敦签署，同年 6 月经欧盟理事会批准，各部门随后几年陆续生效，而不是一次全部落地。所以要把那句「希望在峰会前完成」读作它本来的身份：一位谈判者的希望被报纸引用了，而不是一个事实。新闻用同一种语法报道意图和事件，读者得自己把两者分开。更深一层的点是：这份互认协议只解决了问题容易的那一半。重复检测是冗余，而冗余可以谈掉。谈不掉的是那只鸡：欧盟反对美国禽肉加工中使用的氯水清洗，这是关于「安全」指什么的分歧，不是关于某项检测是否规范执行的分歧。后来的每一次尝试都撞上同一块礁石。《跨大西洋贸易与投资伙伴关系协定》（TTIP）依据 2013 年 6 月通过的授权启动，目标远超互认，谈了十五轮后于 2016 年底中止，2019 年 4 月欧盟理事会宣布 2013 年的授权已经过时。本文发表二十二年后，这两个最大的富国市场把剃须刀互认下来了，鸡还是没谈成。可以带走的教训是关于「哪一类分歧可以谈」：双方想要同一个结果、只是把活干了两遍，达成一致是行政问题；双方对结果本身应该是什么有分歧时，把它叫作技术壁垒并不能让它变成技术壁垒。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

The reporting inversion in news English: say / claim / ask + subject新闻英语的引述倒装：say / claim / ask + 主语
When the reporting phrase comes in the middle of the sentence or at the end, verbs such as say, claim, ask and reply usually go before the subject, especially when that subject is a noun. With a pronoun subject there is no inversion.
引述语放在句中或句末时，常把 say/claim/ask/reply 等放在主语前（主语是名词时尤其如此）；主语是代词时一般不倒装。

With a pronoun: ..., he said. Not said he, except in deliberately old-fashioned writing.

代词主语：…, he said.（不说 said he，除非是古旧文体）

  • "The plan will fail," said the minister.
  • "We are ready," she replied.
Clauses opening with where, and do meaning 'be enough'where 引导的状语从句与 do 的「够用」义
where can open a clause of place, and it can also open a clause of circumstance, = in a situation in which. As an intransitive verb, do means to be enough, to serve.
where 可引导地点状语从句，也可引导「条件/情况」状语从句（= in a situation where）；do 作不及物动词＝够用、行。

Others of the same kind: Where there is a will, there is a way. / Where possible, use simple words.

其他：Where there is a will, there is a way. / Where possible, use simple words.

  • This room will do for a meeting.
  • That will do! 够了！
Where enough goes, and what follows itenough 的位置与结构
enough follows an adjective or adverb (old enough) but normally goes in front of a noun (enough money). For the result, use enough to do; American newspapers also write enough that.
enough 修饰形容词、副词时放在其后（old enough）；修饰名词时一般放在前（enough money）；结果用 enough to do，也有 enough that（美式新闻）。

In standard British usage this would be so complex that ... or complex enough to make a deal difficult.

标准英式：so complex that… / complex enough to make a deal difficult。

  • She is old enough to vote.
  • We don't have enough time to finish.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • It is A, far more than B, that … 正是 A，而远不是 B，在……
    It is lack of sleep, far more than stress, that causes his headaches.
  • As it happens, … 碰巧，其实……
    As it happens, I know the person you are talking about.
  • why have two … where one would do? 一个就够，何必要两个？
    Why hold two meetings where one would do?
  • hammer out … covering, say, … 敲定涵盖比如……的……
    They hammered out rules covering, say, food labels and packaging.

Summary writing摘要写作

In no more than 80 words, sum up what blocks trade between America and the EU, what the two sides are doing about it, and why it is proving hard.

用不超过 80 个词概括美欧贸易的障碍以及双方为消除它所做的努力和遇到的困难。

Points a good summary covers好摘要要覆盖的要点
  • What chiefly blocks trade between rich countries is the gap between their national rules, not the tariff.富国之间的贸易障碍主要是国家标准不同，而不是关税。
  • An electric razor built to the EU standard still has to be tested in America, and the same the other way round, so everything is tested twice.例如符合欧盟标准的电动剃须刀仍须经美国检测，反之亦然，造成重复检测。
  • Both sides accept the principle of recognising each other's tests, and hope to sign before the summit on 28 May.双方原则上同意互认检测，希望在 5 月 28 日峰会前达成协议。
  • The hard part is how to build the deal: America would sign industry by industry, while the EU wants one set of general principles that can also be extended to other countries.困难在于协议的构建方式：美国想按行业逐个签，欧盟要一套通用原则并可推广到他国。
Our model summary本站范文

Trade between rich countries is hindered less by tariffs than by different national regulations, which force products such as electric razors and dialysis machines to be tested twice. America and the EU agree in principle to end double testing and hope to sign a deal before their May summit. But it will be hard: America wants separate agreements for each industry, while the EU wants general principles that could cover many products and countries.（74 词）
