Lesson 25 · Non-Auditory Effects of Noise 噪音的非听觉效应

📘 New Concept English · Book 4 · Lesson 25 “Non-Auditory Effects of Noise”新概念英语 第四册 Lesson 25《Non-Auditory Effects of Noise》

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
Can noise drive people mad? The author first criticises both sides — people in industry will not even admit that noise can harm, while campaigners against noise like to use weak evidence. Then he takes apart a newspaper story about a typist 'driven into a mental hospital', showing that it cannot tell cause from effect. Finally he draws a conclusion from a large US Navy study. It is a model scientific argument: claim → counter-example → method → evidence → a careful, limited conclusion. Notice how carefully he chooses his words: merely, does not prove, but it does mean.
噪音会把人逼疯吗？作者先各打五十大板——工业界根本不承认噪音有害，反噪音的人又爱拿蹩脚证据说事；接着拆穿一则「打字员被逼进精神病院」的报道，指出它分不清因果；最后用美国海军的大样本研究下结论。这是一篇标准的科学论证文：主张 → 反例 → 方法 → 证据 → 有限度的结论。注意作者措辞的分寸感：merely、does not prove、but it does mean。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. Why does the author think it a pity that the campaign against noise is linked with bad science?作者为什么说「把减噪运动和坏科学绑在一起」是可惜的？
  2. What is wrong with the typist story? Why can it not tell cause from effect?打字员的故事错在哪里？为什么说它「分不清因果」？
  3. What did the US Navy study show, and what did it not show?美国海军的研究证明了什么、没有证明什么？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

The lesson is taken from "Non-auditory effects of noise" by D. E. Broadbent, from Science Survey (attribution in the original Book 4). A medical review lists a Broadbent paper of this title in 1961, in Advancement of Science; which version the book used is not confirmed.

课文选自 D. E. 布罗德本特的《噪音的非听觉效应》，出自 Science Survey（据旧版第四册课末署名）。一篇医学综述的参考文献把同名文章列在 1961 年的 Advancement of Science 上；教材用的是哪个版本未核。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

Donald Broadbent (1926–1993) was one of the founders of modern cognitive psychology. He spent his career at the Medical Research Council's Applied Psychology Unit in Cambridge, which he later directed, and much of his early research, done for the Royal Navy, was about how people perform under noise. That explains the voice of this essay: a scientist who takes noise seriously, but refuses to let a good cause rest on bad evidence. It was written when Britain was beginning to argue about noise from jet aircraft and new roads. The "Services" in the first sentence are Britain's armed forces, and the large navy study he describes looked at men who worked on aircraft carriers.

唐纳德·布罗德本特（1926–1993）是现代认知心理学的奠基人之一。他一生在剑桥的英国医学研究理事会应用心理学研究室工作，后来出任主任；早期研究很多是为英国皇家海军做的：人在噪音下怎样工作。这就解释了本文的口吻：一位认真对待噪音的科学家，却不肯让好的事业建立在坏证据上。文章写于英国开始为喷气机和新公路的噪音争论的年代。第一句里的 the Services 指英国三军；他介绍的海军大样本研究，调查的是在航空母舰上工作的人。

✨ How it is written赏析

This is a model of fair scientific argument in under 400 words. Broadbent begins by criticising both camps, which earns the reader's trust. He then picks one vivid example — a dramatic photograph with a sad caption — and calmly shows its weakness: the story cannot tell whether noise caused the illness or whether complaints about noise were part of the illness. Notice his comparison with a patient who believes the neighbours are plotting against her; it makes the logic clear without any technical words. He then says what good evidence would look like, gives it, and states a limited conclusion. Watch the hedges: merely, does not prove, but it does mean. The final comparison with an orphanage gives the reader a sense of scale, which is often more useful than a yes or no.

这是一篇不到 400 词的、公平的科学论证范文。布罗德本特先把两边都批评一遍，赢得读者信任；然后挑一个生动的例子——一张戏剧化的照片配一句煽情的图片说明——冷静地指出它的软肋：这个故事分不清是噪音导致了病，还是抱怨噪音本身就是病的一部分。注意他拿「怀疑邻居合伙诽谤自己的病人」作类比，不用一个术语就把逻辑讲清。接着他说明好证据应该长什么样，给出证据，再下一个有限度的结论。留意他的分寸词：merely、does not prove、but it does mean。最后拿孤儿院作比，给读者一个「危险有多大」的量级感，这往往比一句「是或否」更有用。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

On his main question Broadbent has largely held up: sixty years later, the World Health Organization's 2018 review still rated the evidence that environmental noise causes mental illness as mostly of very low quality. But the field has moved on in another direction. The same WHO guidelines found stronger evidence that long-term traffic noise is linked to heart disease and disturbed sleep, and recommended keeping road traffic noise below about 53 decibels. So the fair update is: noise is probably not a common cause of madness, but it is a real health hazard of a quieter kind. The method is the lasting lesson. Chinese readers will know the type of story he criticises — the shocking headline built on one case. His questions work on any of them: which way does cause run, and what would a study of many people show?

在主要问题上，布罗德本特的结论大体站得住：六十年后，世界卫生组织 2018 年的综述仍把「环境噪音导致精神疾病」的证据大多评为极低质量。但研究在另一个方向上前进了：同一份世卫指南发现，长期交通噪音与心脏病、睡眠受扰的关联证据更强，并建议把道路交通噪音控制在约 53 分贝以下。所以如实的更新是：噪音大概不是常见的致疯原因，却是一种更隐蔽的真实健康危害。真正经久的是方法。中国读者对他批评的那类报道并不陌生——用一个个案撑起的惊悚标题。他的两个问题对付哪一篇都管用：因果到底朝哪个方向？研究大量人群会看到什么？

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

do / does / did for emphasis助动词 do / does / did 强调谓语
In a positive sentence, do/does/did before the main verb (in its base form) means 'really, indeed'; it adds emphasis or contrasts with something negative said before.
在肯定句的实义动词前加 do/does/did（动词用原形）＝「确实、的确」，用来强调或与前文的否定形成对比。

Tense and person go on the auxiliary: He does work hard. (present, third person) / I did tell you. (past)

时态和人称落在助动词上：He does work hard.（现在、第三人称单数）／ I did tell you.（过去）。

Commands can take do to sound warm and sincere: Do come in!

祈使句也能加 do 表恳切：Do come in!

  • I did send the email — check your spam folder. 我确实发了邮件。
  • She doesn't like jazz, but she does enjoy classical music.
what-clauses as subjectwhat 引导的主语从句
A what-clause can act as the subject of a sentence: what = the thing(s) that, with statement word order inside; it often forms the emphatic pattern What ... is ....
what 从句可以整个当主语用，what = the thing(s) that，从句内用陈述语序；常构成「What … is …」的强调句式。

what itself plays a part inside the clause (here it is the subject); if the clause is complete without it, use that: That noise affects people is possible.

what 在从句里自身充当成分（此处作主语）；如果从句缺的是完整句子，就用 that：That noise affects people is possible.

The verb is usually singular; with a plural complement, speech sometimes uses a plural verb, but writing usually keeps the singular.

谓语一般用单数；表语是复数名词时口语中也见复数，但书面多用单数。

  • What surprised me most was his calmness.
  • What we need is more time, not more money.
on + doing: as soon ason + doing：一……就……
on/upon + gerund = as soon as ..., two actions one right after the other; the subject of the main clause must be the one doing the -ing action.
on/upon + 动名词＝as soon as …，表示两个动作紧接着发生；主句主语必须就是 doing 的逻辑主语。

Keep the subject the same: On arriving home, I found the door open. (I arrived); not On arriving home, the door was open. (a door does not arrive home)

逻辑主语一致：On arriving home, I found the door open.（我到家）；不能说 On arriving home, the door was open.（门不会「到家」）。

Compare: in doing = in the course of doing; on doing = immediately after doing.

比较：in doing＝在做……的过程中；on doing＝刚一做……就。

  • On hearing the news, she burst into tears.
  • Upon arrival, please report to the front desk.（也可接名词）

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • regard A as B 把 A 看作 B
    Many managers regard training as a waste of money.
  • The snag (in …) is that … ……的问题/症结在于……
    The snag in this plan is that nobody wants to pay for it.
  • What is needed is … 真正需要的是……
    What is needed is a clear and simple rule.
  • That does not prove …; but it does mean … 这不能证明……，但确实说明……
    That does not prove he is guilty; but it does mean we should ask more questions.

Keywords for retelling复述关键词

  • two extremes: industry ignores noise; anti-noise people use weak evidence
  • pity: noise abatement is a good cause, but bad science discredits it
  • allegation: noise causes mental illness — the typist story
  • snag: cause or symptom? (compare a patient who thinks neighbours persecute her)
  • proper method: study large numbers — US Navy on aircraft carriers
  • result: no effect found → effect, if any, very small; an orphanage is a real hazard

Summary writing摘要写作

In no more than 80 words, sum up: does the author think noise causes mental illness, and on what evidence? (First give the other side's argument, then why it fails, and finally what reliable research concludes.)

用不超过 80 个词概括：作者认为噪音是否会导致精神疾病？他的依据是什么？（先写反方的论据，再写为什么不成立，最后写可靠研究的结论。）

Points a good summary covers好摘要要覆盖的要点
  • Some people claim noise causes mental illness, using single cases (such as the typist who went into a mental hospital) as proof.有人说噪音导致精神病，常以个案（如打字员进精神病院）为证。
  • Such cases cannot separate cause from effect: complaining about noise may just be a symptom of the illness.这类个案分不清因果：抱怨噪音可能只是病的症状。
  • The reliable method is to study large numbers of people living with noise.可靠的办法是研究大量生活在噪音环境中的人。
  • A US Navy study of men on aircraft carriers (and earlier British and American studies) found no effect.美国海军对航母人员的研究（及更早的英美研究）未发现影响。
  • Conclusion: any effect of noise is very small, far less dangerous than growing up in an orphanage.结论：噪音即使有影响也极小，远不如在孤儿院长大危险。
Our model summary本站范文

It is often claimed that noise causes mental illness, and stories such as that of a typist driven into a mental hospital are offered as proof. But such stories cannot separate cause from effect, since complaints about noise may simply be a symptom. A large-scale study of men on noisy aircraft carriers found no effects, confirming earlier research. Noise may harm mental health, but only very slightly — far less than an orphanage upbringing.（74 词）
