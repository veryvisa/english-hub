Lesson 38 · Water and the Traveller 水与旅行者

📘 New Concept English · Book 4 · Lesson 38 “Water and the Traveller”新概念英语 第四册 Lesson 38《Water and the Traveller》

C1🧠 22 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
How do you drink safely away from home? This lesson is a travel-health guide: first why water gets contaminated, then one piece of advice after another — stay with hot drinks and sealed brands you know, see the bottle opened in front of you, boiling is the surest method, turn down ice and cold drinks of unknown origin, and never count on alcohol to make water safe. Practical writing of this kind has a very distinct voice: imperatives everywhere (Make sure ..., Refuse ..., Beware of ...) and a steady stream of should and can with the passive. Learn these and you can write notices, instructions and letters of advice that sound like the real thing.
出门旅行，水怎么喝才安全？本课是一份旅行健康指南：先讲水为什么会被污染，再给出一条条具体建议——喝热饮和知名品牌瓶装饮料、当面开瓶、烧开最保险、冰块和来路不明的冷饮都要拒绝、酒精不能消毒饮用水。这类实用说明文的语言特点非常鲜明：大量祈使句（Make sure… Refuse… Beware of…）和 should / can + 被动式；学会它们，你就能写出地道的英文须知、说明书和建议信。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. How does drinking water usually become contaminated?饮用水通常是怎样被污染的？
  2. What does the writer suggest travellers drink, and what should they do about ice and cold drinks?文章建议旅行者喝什么？怎样处理冰块和冷饮？
  3. Why should alcohol not be counted on to make water safe?为什么不能指望用酒精给饮用水消毒？

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

This is writing with a job to do, and the job explains the style. A handbook of this kind is read in a hurry, often by someone already on a trip and already worried, so every paragraph has to be self-contained and every sentence has to end in something you can do. Notice what the author does not do: he never tells a story, never names a country, and never says how likely any of this is. That is deliberate. Name a country and the advice dates, and the reader whose destination is not named assumes they are safe. The chemistry in the passage is sound and is the part most often got wrong in travel talk: alcohol does kill germs, but only in a narrow band of strength, and ordinary drinking spirits are far below it - the figure of 95 proof works out to under half alcohol by volume, because proof on the American scale is twice the percentage. Methylated spirit, which is strong enough, is poisoned on purpose so that it cannot be drunk.

这是带着任务写的文字，任务决定了文体。这类手册是匆忙中读的，读者往往已经在路上、已经在担心，所以每一段都得自成一体，每一句都得落在一件「能照做的事」上。注意作者不做什么：他不讲故事，不点任何国家的名字，也不说这些事发生的概率有多大。这是刻意的——点了国名，建议就会过时，而目的地没被点到的读者会以为自己安全。文中的化学是对的，也正是旅行闲谈里最常说错的一处：酒精确实杀菌，但只在一个很窄的浓度带里，而喝的烈酒远在它之下——「95 proof」折算下来不到五成，因为美制 proof 是酒精体积百分比的两倍。真正够浓的工业酒精，则被故意掺了毒，为的就是让人不能喝。

✨ How it is written赏析

The structure is worth copying exactly. One paragraph of causes, then advice in descending order of certainty: what is safest (hot and sealed), what is nearly as safe (boiled), what to do when you have neither (hot tap water left to cool), and what never works (alcohol). Within that, each instruction carries its reason in the same breath - do not add ice, because ice can be no safer than the water behind it; prefer fizzy drinks, because the acid helps a little. A rule with its reason attached survives being passed on; a bare rule does not. Two small devices do a lot of work. The first is the modal passive, which lets the writer give an order without being bossy: the bottle should be opened, not you must make them open it. The second is the single softened imperative - refuse politely - which is the only place a person appears in the whole passage, and the only moment the writer admits that the hard part of this advice is social rather than medical. Turning down a drink someone has poured for you is the instruction readers are most likely to break.

结构值得原样照搬：一段讲成因，然后按确定性从高到低给建议——最安全的（热的、密封的）、几乎同样安全的（煮沸的）、两者都没有时怎么办（烫手的自来水放凉）、以及永远不管用的（酒精）。而在每条建议内部，理由都和指令同时给出：别加冰，因为冰的安全最多等于它背后那些水；选带气的，因为酸性稍微帮点忙。带着理由的规则能被转述下去，光秃秃的规则不能。两个很小的手法做了很多事。第一是情态被动，它让作者下指令而不显得专横：说「瓶子应当被打开」，而不是「你必须让他们打开」。第二是那句被软化的祈使句——礼貌地拒绝——这是全文唯一出现「人」的地方，也是作者唯一承认这条建议的难处在社交而不在医学的时刻：拒绝别人倒给你的饮料，正是读者最可能违反的那一条。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The chemistry and the physics still hold; the strategy has been downgraded. Boiling remains the first recommendation of the public health agencies, with one refinement the lesson does not have: bring it to a rolling boil for a minute, and for three minutes high up, because water boils cooler as you climb. Everything else on his list has been overtaken by equipment he could not buy in 1989 - chlorine dioxide tablets, hollow-fibre filters fine enough to stop bacteria and protozoa, and ultraviolet pens - which between them make the bottled drink the least good of the options rather than the best, once the plastic is counted. The real correction is larger and more interesting. The whole genre of advice this page belongs to has been tested, and it does not perform. Reviews covering seven studies found no relationship between how faithfully travellers followed the eating and drinking rules and how often they fell ill; the public health manuals now say plainly that people who follow the rules still get sick, and that the strongest predictor of illness is simply where you went, with the hygiene of whoever handled your food - something no traveller controls - mattering more than anything on the traveller's own checklist. So the honest reading is: this is good advice about water and an incomplete theory of risk. Keep the boiling and the sealed bottle, drop the belief that carefulness is protection, and carry the treatment for when it happens anyway. For Chinese readers there is a familiar version of all this. Drinking boiled water and carrying a flask is an old habit across China, and it was public health policy before the words existed; the habit solves exactly the problem on this page. What it does not solve - and what the evidence says matters more - is the kitchen you cannot see, whether that kitchen is in Delhi, in Guangzhou, or three blocks from your own home.

化学和物理仍然成立，策略被降级了。煮沸依然是公共卫生机构的首选，只多了一条课文没有的细节：烧到大滚、保持一分钟，高海拔地区三分钟，因为海拔越高水的沸点越低。他清单上的其他东西都被 1989 年买不到的装备超过了——二氧化氯片、细到能挡住细菌与原生动物的中空纤维滤芯、紫外线笔——这些东西加在一起，使「买瓶装饮料」从最好的选项变成最差的那个，只要把塑料也算进账。真正的更正更大、也更有意思：这一页所属的整个建议体裁已经被检验过，而它表现不佳。覆盖七项研究的综述没有发现「旅行者多认真遵守饮食规则」与「生病频率」之间有关系；今天的公共卫生手册直说：照规则做的人照样生病，而最强的预测因素只是「你去了哪里」，还有替你处理食物的人卫生如何——这恰恰是旅行者控制不了的，而它比旅行者自己清单上的任何一条都更要紧。所以如实的读法是：这是关于水的好建议，加上一套不完整的风险理论。留下煮沸和密封瓶装，丢掉「小心就等于安全」这个信念，同时把万一发生时的药带上。对中国读者，这一切都有个熟悉的版本：喝开水、带水壶是中国长期的习惯，在有这些词之前它就是公共卫生政策，而这个习惯正好解决这一页上的问题。它解决不了的，恰恰是证据说更要紧的那一样——你看不见的那间厨房，无论那厨房在德里、在广州，还是在你家三个街区之外。

Sentence 1第 1 句 (begins开头 “Contamination of water …”)

  • be due to + noun: caused by. Four noun phrases follow in parallel, with or before the last.be due to + 名词：由于；后接四个并列名词短语，最后用 or 连接。
  • The whole sentence has one verb, is, and everything else is a noun phrase. This packing of information into nouns is the signature of technical English.全句只有一个谓语 is，其余都是名词短语——这是科技英语「名词化」的典型写法，信息密度高。

Sentence 3第 3 句 (begins开头 “Even if a …”)

  • even if: a concession — granting the point and going on.even if：即使；让步。
  • not always: a partial negative, 'not in every case'.not always：部分否定，「并不总是」。
  • by the time ...: a time clause meaning 'by the moment when'.by the time …：到……时候（为止），引导时间状语从句。

Sentence 7第 7 句 (begins开头 “It is best …”)

  • it is best to do: the best course is to. keep to: confine yourself to.it is best to do：最好……；keep to：只限于（只喝）。
  • What follows the dash is the reason why a known brand can be trusted. The original prints standard ... are, which is a slip for standards ... are.破折号后说明理由（为什么品牌瓶装饮料可靠）。课文 standard … are 原文有误，应为 standards … are。

Sentence 9第 9 句 (begins开头 “Make sure that …”)

  • An imperative with Make sure and two that clauses in parallel; the second that cannot be dropped, or the boundary between the clauses is lost.祈使句 Make sure + 两个并列的 that 宾语从句，第二个 that 不能省（省了就分不清从句的界限）。
  • in your presence: while you are watching.in your presence：当着你的面。

Sentence 14第 14 句 (begins开头 “Ice is only …”)

  • only as ... as ...: no more ... than. The safety of the ice can rise no higher than the safety of the water.only as … as …：「只和……一样……」，即冰的安全取决于水。
  • from which it is made：be made from water → from which。
  • should not be put: a modal with the passive. be known to be safe: the passive of the pattern know something to be (Lesson 34 had the active form).should not be put：情态被动；be known to be safe：被动复合宾语（L34 讲过主动形式 know sth to be）。

Sentence 24第 24 句 (begins开头 “tap water that …”)

  • too hot to touch: so hot that you cannot touch it.too hot to touch：烫得没法摸（too … to）。
  • can be left to cool: a modal, a passive and an infinitive together — you may leave it standing until it cools.can be left to cool：情态 + 被动 + 不定式，「可以放着让它凉」。
  • safe to drink: an adjective with an infinitive, where the subject is the object of the drinking. Never write safe to be drunk.safe to drink：形容词 + 不定式，主语 water 是 drink 的逻辑宾语（不能写成 safe to be drunk）。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

The modal passive in instructions: should / can / must be done说明文里的情态被动：should / can / must be done
A modal with be and a past participle states a rule, a recommendation or a possibility without naming who does it. This is the backbone of manuals, guides and public notices.
情态动词 + be + 过去分词，用来客观地给出规则、建议或可能性，不必说出「谁」去做——这是说明书、指南、公告最常用的句式。

Degrees of force: must be done (required) is stronger than should be done (advised), which is stronger than can be done (permitted or possible); must not be done is a prohibition.

语气强弱：must be done（必须）＞ should be done（应该）＞ can be done（可以）；否定 must not be done＝禁止。

A phrasal verb in the passive: be relied upon (Lesson 36), where the preposition must stay.

被动 + 短语动词：be relied upon（L36 讲过），介词不能丢。

  • All forms must be signed by a parent.
  • Mobile phones should be switched off during the exam.
as ... as for equality, and the words that modify itas … as 同级比较及其修饰
as + the plain form of an adjective or adverb + as means 'to the same degree'. In front of it you can put just, nearly, almost, only or a multiple; the negative is not as, or not so, ... as.
as + 形容词/副词原级 + as 表「和……一样」；前面可加 just, nearly, almost, only, twice 等修饰，否定式 not as/so … as。

In this lesson: no safer than the water it was made from, which sets a ceiling rather than a level.

本课：only as safe as 制作它的那些水——冰的安全最多等于水的安全。

The modifier changes the meaning: just as safe as (exactly the same), nearly as safe as (close, but below), twice as expensive as (double — see Lesson 35 on multiples).

修饰语改变意思：just as safe as（完全一样安全）；nearly as safe as（差不多一样安全，但不及）；twice as expensive as（贵一倍，L35 讲过倍数）。

Set phrases with it: as soon as possible, as far as I know, as long as (provided that), as well as (and also).

固定用法：as soon as possible；as far as I know；as long as（只要）；as well as（以及）。

  • This box is not so heavy as that one.
  • He is just as clever as his sister.
The imperative: the voice of guides and warnings祈使句：指南和警告的语气
An imperative begins with the plain form of the verb and leaves you out; the negative takes Don't or Never. Beware of, Make sure and Be sure to are the stock openings for written instructions.
祈使句用动词原形开头、省略主语 you；否定用 Don't / Never；Beware of / Make sure / Be sure to 是写须知的高频开头。

Three of them in this lesson, with make sure, refuse and beware.

本课三处：Make sure that …（当面开瓶）、Refuse politely …（拒绝来路不明的冷饮）、Beware of …（当心甲醇）。

Softening it: politely after the verb takes the edge off, and Please does the same; for a wholly impersonal tone, switch to should (Travellers should avoid ...).

委婉程度：Refuse politely … 在动词后加 politely 软化；也可用 Please …；更客观就换成 should 句（Travellers should avoid …）。

An imperative with and or or: Boil the water, and it will be safe (do it and this follows); Boil the water, or you may fall ill (fail to, and this follows).

祈使句 + and/or：Boil the water, and it will be safe.（照做就……）；Boil the water, or you may fall ill.（否则……）

refuse takes its object directly, and when the object is long the adverb can sit in between: Refuse politely any cold drink ...

refuse 后直接接宾语；宾语长时可把副词放中间：Refuse politely any cold drink …

  • Never drink water from an unknown source.
  • Be sure to check the voltage before you plug in.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • … is usually due to … ……通常是由于……
    Most accidents are usually due to carelessness.
  • A is only as … as B A 最多也就跟 B 一样……
    A team is only as good as its coach.

Keywords for retelling复述关键词

  • causes of contamination: poor sanitation, sewage, leaks, industrial or farm waste
  • safe at the source ≠ safe at the tap; intermittent supplies are suspect

Summary writing摘要写作

Write no more than 80 words of drinking-water advice for a traveller: why is the water unsafe, what should they drink, and what should they avoid?

用不超过 80 个词写一份给旅行者的饮水须知要点：水为什么不安全？应该喝什么、避免什么？

Points a good summary covers好摘要要覆盖的要点
  • Poor sanitation near the source, waste emptied into it, leaks into the pipes and industrial or farm waste all contaminate water; even water that is safe at the source may not be safe at the tap.水源附近卫生差、污水排放和泄漏、工农业废物都会污染水；源头安全的自来水到了水龙头也未必安全。
  • On a short trip, drink no tap water and nothing untreated; stay with hot drinks and sealed drinks of a known brand, opened in front of you.短期旅行不喝自来水或未处理的水，最好喝热饮和知名品牌的瓶装、罐装饮料，当面开瓶。
  • Boiling is a sound method; turn down cold drinks of unknown origin, and leave out ice unless the water behind it is safe.煮沸是好办法；拒绝来路不明的冷饮，冰块不安全就别加。
Our model summary本站范文

Water can be polluted by poor sanitation, sewage or industrial and farm waste, and even water that is safe at its source may not be safe at the tap. Travellers should avoid tap water and keep to hot drinks or well-known bottled drinks, opened in their presence. Boiling is always reliable. Avoid cold drinks of unknown origin and doubtful ice, and never rely on alcohol to purify water. Very hot tap water, once cooled, is usually safe.（77 词）
