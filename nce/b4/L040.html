📘 New Concept English · Book 4 · Lesson 40 “Waves”新概念英语 第四册 Lesson 40《Waves》

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
A wave looks like a wall of water coming at you, but the water does not travel with it: the wave passes through the water, and a particle at the surface only draws a circle and comes back. This lesson mixes poetry with physics. It opens by calling waves the offspring of a quarrel between sea and sky; in the middle it defines crest, trough, height, wave length and period; at the end it gives the wave a life, a death and a return to the sea. Read it for two things: how a definition sentence is built, and how -ing phrases paint one continuous movement.
海浪看起来像一堵水墙朝你涌来，其实水并没有跟着浪走——浪「穿过」水，水面上的一个微粒只是在原地画了一个圈。本课把诗意描写和科学说明揉在一起：开头说浪是「海洋与大气搏斗的孩子」「无限的签名」，中间给出波峰、波谷、波高、波长、周期的定义，最后又把浪比作有生有死的「生命」。读这一课，一要学定义句怎么写，二要看分词短语怎样把一个连续动作描绘出来。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. What does a wave seem to be doing that it is not really doing?海浪给观察者造成了什么错觉？
  2. How does a single particle of water at the surface move while a wave goes past?一个水面上的微粒在海浪经过时是怎样运动的？
  3. Which three natural forces set these waves going?海浪是由哪三种自然原因产生的？

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

Of the forty-eight pieces in this book, this is one of the very few with no author at all, and that tells you what kind of writing it is. A magazine of that sort sold on photographs, and its first paragraph had to hold a reader who had not come looking for science. The subject helps. A wave is the one piece of physics that every reader has already watched for hours, so the writer can open with something you have seen and let the definitions in afterwards, under cover. The editors of the Chinese edition answered the opening in kind: the picture printed beside the lesson is not a diagram of crests and troughs but waves by Utagawa Hiroshige, a woodblock artist who died in 1858, a century before anyone measured a sea properly. A diagram would have taught the five terms faster. The print tells you why the first paragraph exists.

这本书四十八篇里，极少数完全没有作者的就是这一篇，而这件事本身说明了它是什么性质的文字。那类杂志靠照片卖，第一段必须留住一个本来不是来看科学的读者。题目帮了它：海浪是每个读者都已经看过几小时的物理，所以作者可以从你见过的画面开始，把定义掩护着带进来。中文版的编者也以同样的方式回应了这个开头——印在课文旁边的不是波峰波谷示意图，而是歌川广重的浪，一位 1858 年去世的浮世绘画家，比人类第一次把海量准的时间早了一个世纪。若论教会那五个术语，示意图快得多；而这幅画告诉你，第一段为什么要存在。

✨ How it is written赏析

The piece is built in three voices and they do not blend. First a hymn: sun, then wind, then sea, each handing a message to the next. Then a glossary, and this is the real engineering of the page - five terms defined inside a single sentence, each definition tucked into a bracket, so the reader is handed a dictionary without ever stopping to read one. Then an elegy, in which the wave is given a life, a short spell of being somebody, and a death. The best move is the proof. Rather than assert that the water stays behind, the writer asks what the world would look like if it did not, and lets a shoreline full of racing debris make the argument for him. Notice the rhythm too: the first and last sentences are long and full of open vowels, the technical middle is flat and clipped, and reading the page aloud shows you exactly where the joins are. One more lesson, not intended by anyone. The printed line from both maturity to death is not English; the sense the paragraph needs is from birth to death. The lesson itself already flags awave as a misprint. Two slips on one page: printed texts are fallible, and a learner who notices that has learned something more useful than a definition.

这一页有三种嗓音，而且并不融合。先是颂歌：太阳、风、海，一个把消息交给下一个。接着是词汇表，这才是这一页真正的工程——五个术语在同一个句子里定义完，每个定义塞进一个括号，于是读者拿到了一本词典，却从来没有停下来读词典。最后是哀歌：浪被给了一生、一段短暂的「成为某一个」的时间，和一次死亡。最妙的一步是它的证明方式：作者没有断言水会留在原处，而是问「如果不是这样，世界会是什么样子」，让一片东西全往岸上狂奔的画面替他说话。节奏也值得看：首句与末句长、开口元音多，中间的技术段落平而短；朗读一遍，接缝在哪里就都听得出来。还有一课是谁都没打算教的：印出来的 from both maturity to death 不是英文，这一段需要的意思是「从生到死」。而课文自己已经标出 awave 是排印错误。同一页两处错：印刷品是会错的，注意到这件事的学习者，学到的东西比一个定义更有用。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The physics has held. The orbiting-particle picture is still what is taught, and the point that a wave carries energy rather than water is the key to something the lesson does not mention. Its own list of causes includes movements of the earth; what it does not say is how differently that kind of wave behaves. In the deep ocean a tsunami is barely a metre high and crosses an ocean at over 800 kilometres an hour, which is why a ship can pass over one and feel nothing, and why it only rears up where the water shallows. The lesson's central insight is exactly what explains that: nothing is travelling but the energy. Where the page is too tidy is in the word undulating. The smooth regular sea of 1987 was also an engineering assumption, and rogue waves were sailors' tales until the first day of 1995, when a downward-pointing laser on the Draupner platform in the North Sea recorded a wave 25.6 metres high in a sea whose significant wave height was about 12 metres. The platform had been built for a calculated once-in-ten-thousand-years wave of around 20 metres. A second caution is about numbers. The record wave measured by a buoy, 19 metres in February 2013 between Iceland and Britain, is a significant wave height, which means the average of the highest third over a period, so individual waves in that hour were taller. Any figure about waves needs its definition attached. For Chinese readers there is a neat exception to go and look at. The Qiantang bore is the thing the lesson says a wave is not: in that funnel-shaped estuary the incoming tide really does arrive as a step and the water behind it really is deeper, so water is carried bodily upstream at something like ten to twenty kilometres an hour. The bore is a different animal from a wind wave, and knowing why the two are different is worth more than either one alone.

物理部分站住了。水面微粒画圆的图像今天还是这么教的，而「浪搬运的是能量，不是水」这一点，正是课文没提到的另一件事的钥匙。它自己列的成因里有地壳运动，但没说那种浪的行为有多不一样：在深海，海啸只有一米上下高，以每小时八百公里以上穿过大洋——所以船从它上面过去什么也感觉不到，而它只在水变浅的地方才立起来。解释这件事的恰恰就是本课的核心洞见：除了能量，什么都没有在移动。这一页过于整齐的地方是 undulating 这个词。1987 年那种光滑规则的海面也曾是工程上的假设，而「疯狂巨浪」在 1995 年元旦之前一直是水手的传说——那一天，北海 Draupner 平台上一台朝下的激光测到一个 25.6 米高的浪，而当时的有效波高约 12 米；那座平台的设计依据是一个约 20 米、理论上一万年一遇的浪。第二点提醒是关于数字的：浮标测到的最高记录——2013 年 2 月冰岛与英国之间的 19 米——是「有效波高」，即一段时间内最高三分之一的平均值，所以那一小时里单个的浪比它更高。凡是关于浪的数字，都得把口径一起带上。对中国读者还有一个值得专门去看的例外：钱塘江大潮正是课文说「浪不是那样」的那样——在那个喇叭口的河口，涨潮真的以一道台阶的形态推进，台阶后面的水真的更深，于是水被整体带着往上游走，速度大约每小时十到二十公里。潮涌和风浪是两种东西，而知道它们为什么不同，比只知道其中任何一种都值钱。

💬 Discuss and write讨论与写作
  1. Stand at a shore, or watch a video of one, and try to see what this lesson says is there: the water staying while the shape goes by. Describe in six sentences what you actually see, and where your eye tells you something different from the physics.
    到海边站一会儿，或者看一段海边的视频，试着看出本课说的那件事：形状过去了，水留了下来。用六句话写下你真正看到的，以及你的眼睛在哪一处告诉了你跟物理不一样的东西。
  2. The writer proves his point by imagining the opposite - if the water really moved, the shore would be a catastrophe. Take a claim you want to make about something you know well and prove it the same way, in 120 words: describe the world we would be living in if the claim were false.
    作者用「设想相反的情形」来证明自己的论点：如果水真的跟着走，岸边就是一场灾难。挑一件你熟悉的事、一个你想立的主张，用同样的办法在 120 词里证明它：描述一下假如这个主张不成立，我们会生活在什么样的世界里。
  3. This page has two misprints in it. Find them, decide what each should have been, and then answer the harder question: how did you know?
    这一页上有两处排印错误。把它们找出来，判断本来应该是什么，然后回答更难的那一问：你是怎么知道的？

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 1第 1 句 (begins开头 “Waves are the …”)

  • A linking-verb sentence. The complement is a noun phrase, and the phrase after the comma stands in apposition to it, adding a second image.系表结构，表语 the children of …，后面 the ongoing signatures of infinity 是同位语，再补一个比喻。
  • Both nouns are metaphors: the comparison is made directly, with no like and no as.「孩子」「签名」都是隐喻（metaphor），没有用 like / as。

Sentence 2第 2 句 (begins开头 “Rays from the …”)

  • The -ing phrase here is an adverbial of result: and so it wakes it up.awakening …：现在分词短语作结果状语（「从而唤醒它」）。
  • awaken sb/sth to sth means bring them into that state. The four to-phrases are parallel, and each is a little longer than the one before, so the rhythm opens out.awaken sb/sth to …：使……意识到/进入……；四个 to 短语排比，节奏由短到长。

Sentence 9第 9 句 (begins开头 “and a period …”)

  • Each bracket holds a definition. the time (that) it takes ... is a relative clause describing time, built on the pattern it takes somebody time to do something.括号里都是定义；the time (that) it takes a wave crest to travel …：定语从句修饰 time，从句用 it takes sth (time) to do 句型。
  • The printed awave is a misprint for a wave.课文 awave 是印刷错误，应为 a wave。

Sentence 12第 12 句 (begins开头 “If the water …”)

  • An unreal condition about now: the if-clause takes the past continuous, and the main clause takes would be doing, which keeps the picture in motion.与现在事实相反的虚拟条件句，从句用过去进行时，主句用 would be doing（进行式的虚拟，强调「正在发生」的画面）。
  • Formal writing prefers were moving in the if-clause; was moving is common in speech.正式文体从句应用 were moving；口语里 was 也常见。

Sentence 15第 15 句 (begins开头 “drawing the particle …”)

  • Another -ing phrase after a noun, working as a postmodifier, like a which-clause.passing through deep water：现在分词作后置定语。
  • cause something to do something: make it happen.cause sth to do：使……做。
  • The last -ing phrase runs beside the main verb and describes the process; first ... then ... then ... and then takes the particle right round its circle.drawing …：现在分词作伴随/结果状语，first … then … then … and then … 依次描述一个完整的圆周。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

Present participle phrases: postmodifier or adverbial?现在分词短语：后置定语还是状语？
An -ing phrase that follows a noun and tells you which one is a postmodifier. An -ing phrase marked off by a comma, giving the result of the main verb or what goes with it, is an adverbial.
-ing 短语紧跟在名词后、说明「哪一个」的是后置定语；用逗号隔开、说明动作的结果或伴随情况的是状语。

Test it: if a which/that clause can replace it with no change of meaning, it is a postmodifier; if and plus a finite verb can replace it, it is an adverbial.

判断方法：能换成 which/that 从句且意思不变 → 定语；能换成 and + 谓语 → 状语（and awakens it …）。

An -ing adverbial of result means a result that follows naturally. For a result that comes as a surprise, use only to do: He hurried to the station, only to find the train had left.

结果状语的分词动作是「自然而然的结果」，如果是意料之外的结果，用不定式 only to do：He hurried to the station, only to find the train had left.

  • The girl sitting by the window is my sister.（定语）
  • The rain lasted a week, causing serious floods.（结果状语）
Definition sentences in expository writing: A is the B that/which ...说明文的定义句：A is the B that/which …
The standard shape: the term + is + the class it belongs to + what marks it off (a relative clause or a prepositional phrase). The class has to be right, and the mark has to separate it from everything else in that class.
下定义的标准句式：被定义词 + is + 上位概念（类别）+ 限定成分（定语从句、介词短语）；类别要准，限定要把它和同类区分开。

The shape is class (point / distance / time) plus distinguishing feature (highest / vertical / flat / between two crests).

结构：类别（point / distance / time）＋ 区分特征（highest / vertical / horizontal / between two crests）。

Other useful frames: X is the ... that/which ...; X refers to ...; X is defined as ...; By X we mean ...

常用句式：X is the … that/which …；X refers to …；X is defined as …；By X we mean …

  • A thermometer is an instrument that measures temperature.
Once as a conjunction: as soon as something has happenedOnce 引导的条件/时间从句
once as a conjunction means as soon as, or the moment that. It introduces a clause of condition or of time, usually with the present perfect or the simple present, and it stresses that the second thing follows the first.
once 作连词 ＝「一旦……（就）」，引导条件或时间状语从句，从句常用现在完成时或一般现在时，强调前一动作完成后后一情况就随之出现。

as soon as puts the weight on timing; once puts it on the condition - given that it has happened, this follows.

与 as soon as 的区别：as soon as 强调时间上「一……就」；once 侧重条件「一旦（做了）就……」。

The main clause can be future while the once-clause stays present or present perfect: Once you have learned to ride, you will never forget.

主句是将来时，从句仍用现在时或现在完成时：Once you have learned to ride, you will never forget.

  • Once you understand the rule, it's easy.
  • Once the work is finished, we can go home.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • … gives the impression of … ……给人……的印象/错觉
    The room gives the impression of being larger than it is.
  • In actuality … 实际上……
    It looks easy, but in actuality it takes years of practice.
  • … are among the most … ……属于最……之列
    These paintings are among the most valuable in the world.
  • in a continual attempt to … 不断地试图……
    He works late every night in a continual attempt to catch up.

Summary writing摘要写作

In no more than 80 words, describe what an ocean wave is made of, how it moves, and what starts it.

用不超过 80 个词说明海浪的特征、运动方式和产生原因。

Points a good summary covers好摘要要覆盖的要点
  • A wave has a crest, a trough, a height, a wave length and a period.海浪的基本特征是波峰、波谷、波高、波长和周期。
  • It looks like a moving wall, but it travels through the water and leaves the water roughly in place.海浪看似一堵水墙涌来，其实是穿过水体，水基本留在原处。
  • A particle at the surface goes round in a circle as the wave passes, and ends where it began.水面微粒随浪经过作大致圆周运动，最后回到起点。
  • Wind, movements of the earth and the pull of the moon and of the sun start waves; gravity then drives them and tries to flatten the sea again.海浪由风、地壳运动或地震、月亮和太阳的引力产生；之后重力推动它们，试图使海面恢复平坦。
Our model summary本站范文

An ocean wave has a crest, a trough, a height, a wave length and a period. Although it looks like a wall of water rushing towards the shore, the wave actually passes through the water, which stays roughly where it was: a surface particle simply moves in a circle and returns to its start. Waves are caused by wind, earth tremors and the pull of the moon and sun, and gravity then drives them towards a flat sea.（78 词）
