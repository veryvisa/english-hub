📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

The lesson is credited to George Orwell, under the title "The sporting spirit" (attribution line in the original Book 4). The essay first appeared in the London weekly Tribune on 14 December 1945, and was collected in Shooting an Elephant and Other Essays in 1950.

课文署名 George Orwell，题为 The sporting spirit（据旧版第四册课末署名）。该文 1945 年 12 月 14 日首发于伦敦周刊《论坛》（Tribune），1950 年收入文集《射象》（Shooting an Elephant and Other Essays）。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

The essay has an occasion, and knowing it changes how the piece reads. In November 1945, three months after the war ended, the Soviet football club Dynamo Moscow toured Britain and played four matches, against Chelsea, Cardiff City, Arsenal and Glasgow Rangers, winning two and drawing two. The tour was staged as a gesture of wartime friendship between allies, and it was reported as having produced bad feeling instead, on the terraces and on the pitch. Orwell wrote in December, with that fresh. So the opening line is not a general complaint about sport; it is aimed at a specific official claim that had just been made and had just failed. The other date in the piece, 1936, is the Berlin Olympics, which the German government ran as a showcase. Orwell's angle on nationalism is consistent across his work of these years: he was writing Animal Farm and the essays on national feeling in the same period, and his quarrel is always with the group loyalty that lets people stop thinking, not with the activity the loyalty attaches to.

这篇文章有一个具体的由头，知道它，读法就变了。1945 年 11 月，战争结束三个月后，苏联足球俱乐部莫斯科迪纳摩访问英国，打了四场比赛，对手是切尔西、卡迪夫城、阿森纳和格拉斯哥流浪者，两胜两平。这次访问是作为盟国之间战时友谊的姿态安排的，而报道称它反而制造了恶感——看台上和场上都有。奥威尔 12 月写下此文，事情还热着。所以开篇那句不是对体育的笼统抱怨，它针对的是一个刚刚被提出、又刚刚失败的官方说法。文中另一个年份 1936 指柏林奥运会，德国政府把它办成了一场橱窗展示。奥威尔对民族主义的切入角度在这几年的作品里是一贯的：他同期在写《动物庄园》和一批论民族情感的随笔，他的争执对象始终是那种让人停止思考的群体忠诚，而不是忠诚所附着的那项活动。

✨ How it is written赏析

The argument has an unusual joint in it, and that joint is the whole technique. Orwell offers evidence and then says he does not need it: granted the record, he writes, one could work the conclusion out from first principles anyway. Most writers put evidence at the centre; he puts a mechanism at the centre and uses the evidence only to show the mechanism running. The mechanism is built in three steps on the village green. Step one: a game played for its own sake, no sides that stand for anything, and it is simply enjoyable. Step two: attach a name to each side. Step three: make losing a disgrace for everyone whose name is attached. He has isolated the variable. The thing that turns play into something uglier is not competition, not money, not the crowd — it is representation, standing for a group. That is why the essay still bites eighty years on, and why it applies well beyond sport. Note too how the last sentence spends its force. He does not accuse the players; he turns to the spectators, and then past them to the nations behind, and ends on three plain words — running, jumping, kicking — set against national virtue. Reducing the grand thing to its physical movements, and letting the gap do the work, is a trick worth keeping.

这篇论证里有一个不寻常的接点，而那个接点就是全部技术。奥威尔先给出证据，接着说他并不需要它：就算不知道那些例子，单凭一般原理也能推出结论。多数作者把证据放在中心；他把一个机制放在中心，证据只用来演示这个机制如何运转。机制在村头草地上分三步搭起来。第一步：为游戏本身而玩，分出的两边不代表任何东西，于是单纯有趣。第二步：给每一边挂上一个名字。第三步：让输球成为所有被这个名字挂上的人的耻辱。他把变量隔离出来了。把游戏变成某种更丑的东西的，不是竞争、不是金钱、也不是观众，而是「代表」——代表一个群体。这就是为什么八十年后这篇文章仍然咬得住人，也是为什么它的适用范围远远超出体育。还要注意最后一句把力气花在哪里。他不指责球员，而是转向观众，再越过观众指向背后的国家，最后落在三个最朴素的词上——跑、跳、踢球——与「民族品德」并列。把一件庄严的事还原成它的物理动作，让落差自己起作用，这个手法值得留着。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

His mechanism has held up better than his tone. The clearest test came in 1969, when a World Cup qualifying series between El Salvador and Honduras was followed within weeks by a short war between them, the one still called the Football War — a case that fits his argument, provided one is careful about the direction of cause: the matches inflamed a quarrel that was already there over migration and land, they did not create it. That caveat matters, because the opposite case is just as easy to find. Sport has repeatedly been used to open doors that politics had closed: the American table tennis team's visit to China in 1971, which preceded the thaw between the two governments; the Korean teams marching together at Olympic opening ceremonies; the 1995 Rugby World Cup in South Africa, which Nelson Mandela used deliberately as an instrument of reconciliation. None of these refutes Orwell's analysis, and all of them qualify his conclusion. The reason they can coexist is that representation cuts both ways: if standing for a group is what makes a match dangerous, it is also what makes a handshake between two teams worth a headline. Orwell saw the first half of that and not the second. The honest reading today is that sport is an amplifier, not a cause. It takes whatever relation two groups already have and makes it louder, which is why the same fixture can produce a riot in one decade and a thaw in the next. His closing warning survives intact in any case: the people to watch are not the players, and the test is not what happens on the pitch but what the crowd and the country make of it afterwards.

他的机制比他的调子站得更住。最清楚的检验来自 1969 年：萨尔瓦多与洪都拉斯之间的世界杯预选赛之后几周，两国打了一场短暂的战争，至今被称为「足球战争」——这个案例符合他的论证，但须留意因果方向：比赛点燃了一场本已存在的争端（起因是移民与土地），并没有制造它。这一点很重要，因为反向的例子同样好找。体育屡屡被用来打开政治关上的门：1971 年美国乒乓球队访华，发生在两国政府关系解冻之前；朝韩代表团在奥运开幕式上共同入场；1995 年南非橄榄球世界杯，纳尔逊·曼德拉有意把它当作和解的工具。这些都没有推翻奥威尔的分析，却都给他的结论加了限制。它们能够并存，是因为「代表」这件事两面都切：如果说代表一个群体是让一场比赛变危险的东西，那它同样是让两队握手上得了头条的东西。奥威尔看到了前一半，没看到后一半。今天如实的读法是：体育是放大器，不是起因。它把两个群体本来就有的关系放大变响，这就是为什么同一场对阵在某个十年引发骚乱，在下一个十年带来解冻。无论如何，他结尾的那句警告原样有效：要盯的不是球员，检验也不在场上，而在赛后观众和国家把它当成了什么。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

Verbs of perception + object + do / doing / done感官动词 + 宾语 + do / doing / done
hear, see, watch, notice and feel take an object followed by do (the whole action), doing (part of it, in progress) or done (the action done to the object).
hear/see/watch/notice/feel + 宾语 + do（全过程）/ doing（正在进行的片段）/ done（宾语被动）。

In the passive, the bare infinitive takes to again: I saw him enter the room becomes He was seen to enter the room. The participle stays as it is: He was seen entering ...

被动时 do 要还原 to：I saw him enter the room. → He was seen to enter the room.（doing 被动时保留：He was seen entering…）

  • I heard someone call my name.（听到了整个动作）
  • I heard my name called.（名字被叫）
Concession and condition: even if / if only / unless让步与条件：even if / if only / unless
even if supposes something and often goes with an unreal form; even though states a fact; if only expresses a wish, or means 'provided only that'; unless means 'except if', that is, if ... not.
even if＝即使（假设性让步，常配虚拟）；even though＝尽管（事实）；if only＝要是……就好了／只要……；unless＝除非（= if not）。

First case here: Even if one didn't know ..., one could deduce it — an unreal concession.

本课 ①：Even if one didn't know…, one could deduce it.（虚拟让步）

Second: if only the common peoples could meet ..., they would have no inclination ... — an unreal condition.

本课 ②：if only the common peoples could meet…, they would have no inclination…（虚拟条件）

  • Even though it was raining, we went out.（事实：确实在下雨）
  • If only I had listened to you!（表遗憾）
not A but B, and the impersonal onenot A but B 与 one 的泛指
not A but B joins two elements of the same kind, noun against noun, phrase against phrase. one means 'anybody at all' and belongs to formal writing; the pronouns that follow are one and one's.
not A but B 连接两个对等成分（名词对名词、介词短语对介词短语）；one 泛指「任何人」，正式文体用，后续代词用 one / one's。

Keep not only A but (also) B ('both ... and') apart from not A but B ('the second, not the first').

注意 not only A but (also) B（不但……而且……）与 not A but B（不是……而是……）的区别。

  • He came not to help but to complain.
  • One should keep one's promises.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • Even if one didn't know …, one could deduce it from … 就算不知道……，也能从……推断出来
    Even if one didn't know the facts, one could deduce them from common sense.
  • … has little meaning unless … 除非……，否则……没什么意义
    Practice has little meaning unless you know your weak points.
  • As soon as …, as soon as …, … 一旦……，一旦……，就……（排比递进）
    As soon as money is involved, as soon as reputations are at stake, friends become rivals.

Keywords for retelling复述关键词

  • common belief: sport creates goodwill between nations
  • the writer: amazed — contests lead to hatred (1936 Olympics)
  • village green: fun; prestige involved → savage instincts
  • international level = mimic warfare
  • the real problem: spectators and nations who take games seriously

Summary writing摘要写作

In no more than 80 words, sum up why the writer thinks international sport cannot build friendship between nations. Keep the shape of the argument: the view opposed, his answer, his reasons, his conclusion.

用不超过 80 个词概括作者为什么认为国际体育比赛不能促进国家间的友谊。注意写出「对方观点—作者反驳—理由—结论」的结构。

Points a good summary covers好摘要要覆盖的要点
  • Some people hold that games make nations friendly; the writer does not.有人认为体育能促进国家间的友好，作者不同意。
  • Both the record (the 1936 Olympics, for one) and plain reasoning point the other way: these contests stir up intense hatred.事实（如 1936 年奥运会）和推理都表明国际比赛会引起强烈仇恨。
  • Every modern sport is a contest; once reputation and the honour of a group are at stake, the fiercest fighting instincts come up.现代体育都有竞争性；一旦牵涉到名誉和集体荣辱，最野蛮的好斗本能就会被激起。
  • At that level a match is a rehearsal for war.国际体育实际上是模拟战争。
  • What deserves more notice is the temper of the crowd and of the countries behind it: they rage over absurd fixtures and treat them as a measure of national character.更值得注意的是观众和国家的态度：他们为荒唐的比赛狂怒，并把它当成民族品德的考验。
Our model summary本站范文

Some people think sport creates goodwill between nations, but the writer disagrees. Experience and reasoning both show that international contests cause hatred. Modern sports are competitive; once prestige is involved and people fear being disgraced, savage instincts are aroused. International sport is really mimic warfare, and the spectators and nations behind them, who treat these absurd games as tests of national virtue, are even more significant than the players.（69 词）
