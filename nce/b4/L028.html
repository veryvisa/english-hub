Lesson 28 · Patients and Doctors 病人与医生

📘 New Concept English · Book 4 · Lesson 28 “Patients and Doctors”新概念英语 第四册 Lesson 28《Patients and Doctors》

C1🧠 23 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
Ours is an age that doubts everything except a bottle of medicine. A patient does not feel treated unless something comes home in his hand, and a busy doctor is glad enough to hand it over; even a great man of letters set off to rescue a sick friend with what was left of his wife's prescription in his pocket. The tone is English dry humour: the sentences are long and perfectly serious, and the mockery hides inside a few phrases - only too ready, in all probability, and the last four words about trouble to themselves. The long sentences make this a good lesson for practising how to find the frame of a sentence.
我们这个时代什么都怀疑，唯独对「一瓶药」深信不疑。病人非要带点药回家才觉得看过病，忙碌的医生也乐得开药打发；连大文豪卡莱尔都揣着妻子吃剩的药去「救」朋友。作者用的是英式冷幽默：句子又长又正经，讽刺藏在 only too ready、in all probability、at no inconvenience to themselves 这些措辞里。本课长句多，是练习找主干的好材料。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. The writer calls this a doubting age; what does he say people still believe in completely?作者说「这是一个怀疑的时代」，但人们对什么仍然深信不疑？
  2. Why do doctors so readily hand out medicine instead of advice about how to live?医生为什么总是乐于给病人开药，而不是给生活建议？
  3. What does the story about Carlyle show, and what does the last sentence say patients really want?卡莱尔的故事说明了什么？最后一句「病人真正要的是什么」？

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

Two words date this page exactly: the Health Services. The British National Health Service opened in 1948 with free prescriptions, the drug bill rose at once, and a charge per item had to be brought in within a few years. So the writer is not making a timeless remark about human nature; he is a doctor inside a new public system, watching a bill climb and watching crowded out-patient departments, and his examples are drawn from the queue in front of him. The Carlyle story carries another date with it. A Victorian man of letters could walk to a friend's house with a half-used bottle in his pocket because medicines then were not labelled, measured or restricted in any way we would recognise; what the writer tells as a comic anecdote about blind faith was, in its own century, ordinary practice.

有两个词把这一页钉在确切的年代上：Health Services。英国国民保健署 1948 年开张，处方免费，药费立刻上涨，几年之内不得不按件收费。所以作者不是在讲一条关于人性的永恒道理：他是一位身在新公共体系内部的医生，看着账单往上爬，看着门诊挤满人，他的例子来自眼前那条队伍。卡莱尔的故事自带另一个年代印记：一位维多利亚时代的文人能把半瓶吃剩的药揣在兜里走到朋友家，是因为当时的药没有我们今天认得出的标签、剂量和管制；作者当作「盲信」来讲的笑话，在他那个世纪其实是常规做法。

✨ How it is written赏析

The humour here is made out of grammar, not jokes. Every sentence is long, balanced and perfectly polite, and the mockery is slipped in as a single phrase inside the politeness: the doctor is only too ready, the friend in all probability accepted, the patient asks to be cured with no trouble to himself. Say any of those aloud with a straight face and you will hear it. Three devices are worth taking away. First, the writer proves a general claim with one hard fact (a bill that keeps rising) and one story (a famous man with a bottle in his pocket), and nothing else - a cheap and strong pair. Second, he uses lists of three objects - a bottle, a box, a jar - and then repeats the set later on; a repeated list becomes a label for the whole habit. Third, look at the last sentence. It explains the advantage of medicine in a flat, kind tone, and then a dash opens and the real indictment comes in eight words. If you want to end an argument hard, put the mild statement first and let the dash do the work.

这里的幽默是用语法做出来的，不是靠笑话。每一句都长、都匀整、都客气到无懈可击，而讽刺就以一个短语的形式塞进这份客气里：医生「非常乐意」；朋友「很可能」收下了；病人要求「不给自己添任何麻烦」就被治好。把这几处板着脸念出来，你就听见了。有三处手法值得拿走。第一，作者证明一个普遍论断，只用一个硬事实（一张不断上涨的账单）加一个故事（一位名人兜里的药瓶），别的什么都没有——又便宜又有力。第二，他把三件东西列成一串（一个瓶、一个盒、一个罐），后面再整组重复一次；重复的清单会变成整个习惯的标签。第三，看末句：它用平和善意的语气解释吃药的好处，然后破折号一开，真正的控诉用八个词抵达。想把论证结得狠，就先放温和的陈述，让破折号去干活。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The forecast in the middle of this page has been confirmed beyond anything the writer imagined. In England in 2024-25 the community pharmacies and appliance contractors dispensed about 1.26 billion prescription items at a cost of around 11.2 billion pounds, and the count rose again on the year before. But the sneer at the bottle of medicine needs a correction, because the thing he treats as pure superstition turns out to be a real effect. Trials of so-called open-label placebos, in which patients are told plainly that the tablets contain no active drug, still find benefit against no treatment across several conditions; a 2025 review pooled sixty randomised trials. Two cautions belong with that finding: most of the outcomes are symptoms the patient reports, not disease measured from outside, and none of it licenses deception. What the ritual seems to do is change how a symptom is experienced, which is not nothing and is not a cure. The habit also has a price he could not have costed. Handing over an antibiotic to end a consultation is one of the engines of drug resistance, and attempts to break the pattern run into the demand side rather than the supply side. China tried the sharpest version of that experiment: from 2016 several provinces banned intravenous antibiotics in adult out-patient departments, because patients had come to treat a drip as the mark of proper treatment, exactly as the patients in this lesson treated a bottle. Prescribing in those out-patient departments fell - and the published evaluation found the demand moved sideways, into emergency rooms, wards and primary care, with doctors finding ways round the rule. The bottle in this lesson has a Chinese cousin, and sixty years later it is still the patient's expectation, not the doctor's pen, that is hardest to change.

这一页中段的预言，被证实的程度超出作者的想象：2024–25 年度，英格兰社区药房与器具供应商配出的处方约 12.6 亿件，费用约 112 亿英镑，件数比上一年又涨。不过他对「一瓶药」的讥笑需要一处更正：他当作纯粹迷信的东西，后来被发现是真实的效应。「公开标签安慰剂」的试验——明确告知患者药片不含有效成分——在多种病况下仍发现其优于不治疗；2025 年的一篇综述汇总了六十项随机试验。这个发现要配两条提醒：大多数结局是患者自报的症状，而非外部测得的疾病指标；而且这一切都不为欺骗提供任何许可。这套仪式似乎改变的是症状被体验的方式，这既不是「什么都没有」，也不是治愈。这个习惯还有一笔他当年算不出的代价：为结束一次问诊而递出抗生素，是耐药性的主要推手之一，而想打破这个模式，撞上的是需求一侧而不是供给一侧。中国做过这个实验最硬的版本：2016 年起多个省份禁止成人门诊静脉输注抗生素，因为患者已把「打点滴」当成正规治疗的标志，正如这一课里的患者把一瓶药当成标志。那些门诊的处方量确实下降了——而已发表的评估发现，需求横向转移到了急诊、住院和基层，医生也找到了绕过规定的办法。这一课里的药瓶有个中国表亲；六十年过去，最难改的仍然是患者的期待，不是医生的笔。

💬 Discuss and write讨论与写作
  1. When you last went to a doctor, what would have made you feel the visit was worth it? Be honest about whether a prescription was part of it.
    回想你上一次看医生：什么会让你觉得这趟没白跑？老实说，一张处方是不是其中一项？
  2. Advice about food, sleep and habits is free, hard to follow and rarely thanked for. Write 120 words on how you would make a doctor's advice as satisfying as a bottle of medicine.
    关于饮食、睡眠和习惯的建议不花钱、难照做、也少有人感谢。写 120 词：你会怎么让医生的建议像一瓶药那样令人满意？
  3. If a tablet with nothing in it makes a patient feel better, should a doctor be allowed to give it, and what would you want to be told?
    如果一片什么都没有的药片能让病人感觉好转，医生该不该开它？你希望医生怎么告知你？

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 2第 2 句 (begins开头 “but although our …”)

  • The main clause says that our confidence stays what theirs was; remains is a linking verb and theirs stands for their confidence.主句：our confidence … remains the same as theirs，remain 系动词；theirs = their confidence。
  • The sentence runs but + concession + main clause. Using but together with although is a written way of pressing the contrast; in Chinese only one of the two needs to be translated.句子结构是「but + 让步从句 + 主句」，but 与 although 同现属于强调转折的书面用法，中文翻译时只译一个即可。

Sentence 8第 8 句 (begins开头 “they have not …”)

  • unless = if ... not: they feel they have had no proper treatment if they cannot carry something home.unless = if … not：除非能带点药回家，否则就觉得没得到充分治疗。

Sentence 12第 12 句 (begins开头 “There is no …”)

  • no + comparative + than: a negative comparative with the force of a superlative - this is the quickest way of all.no + 比较级 + than：比较级的否定表示最高级，= giving them what they ask for is the quickest method。
  • After than comes by giving ..., matching the idea of a method in the first half.than 后接 by giving …，与 method of disposing 的方式意义对应。
  • what they are asking for: a noun clause as object.what they are asking for：宾语从句。

Sentence 17第 17 句 (begins开头 “Nor is it …”)

  • Two layers at once: a cleft sentence (it is only the ignorant ... who ...) with the negative Nor moved to the front, which forces partial inversion.两层结构：强调句 It is only the ignorant … who has such faith …；前面加否定词 Nor，引起部分倒装 → Nor is it …。
  • The sense is that faith in the medicine bottle is not confined to the uneducated - which prepares the way for a famous man as the next example.意思：「对药瓶如此迷信的，也不只是无知、没受过教育的人。」承上启下，引出名人卡莱尔。

Sentence 18第 18 句 (begins开头 “It is recounted …”)

  • It is recounted of somebody that ...: there is a story told about somebody; it is an empty subject and the that-clause is the real one; of here means about.It is recounted of sb that …：「关于某人有这样一段记载……」，it 是形式主语，真正主语是 that 从句；of = about。
  • an indisposition of Mrs Carlyle's: a double possessive.an indisposition of Mrs. Carlyle's：双重所有格。

Sentence 30第 30 句 (begins开头 “and that is …”)

  • demand something of somebody: to require it from them; the what-clause is the complement.demand sth of sb：向某人要求某事；what 宾语从句作表语。
  • After the dash, the passive infinitive stands in apposition to what, and it is the sharpest stroke in the piece.破折号后的不定式被动 to be cured 是 what 的同位说明——全文的点睛之笔。
  • at no inconvenience to themselves: without the least trouble to themselves; at no cost and at no risk are built the same way.at no inconvenience to themselves：不给自己添任何麻烦；at no cost / at no risk 同构。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

Partial inversion after a negative at the head of a clause: Nor / Neither / Not only / Never ...否定词放句首的部分倒装：Nor / Neither / Not only / Never …
When a negative or half-negative word (nor, neither, never, seldom, hardly, not only, little, no sooner) stands first, the clause takes partial inversion: the auxiliary, modal or be goes in front of the subject.
否定或半否定词（nor, neither, never, seldom, hardly, not only, little, no sooner …）放句首时，句子用部分倒装：助动词/情态动词/be 提到主语前。

Both nor and neither at the head of a clause mean and not, and both need inversion; nor is also used, as here, to carry a negative idea forward into a new stretch of argument.

nor 和 neither 在句首都表「也不」，都要倒装；nor 还常用来接续前文的否定意思，像本课这样开启新的一段论述。

Partial inversion is not full inversion: only the auxiliary moves, and the main verb stays where it was - Never have I seen such a thing.

部分倒装 ≠ 完全倒装：只把助动词提前，实义动词留在原位——Never have I seen such a thing.

Note that with not only ... but also at the head, only the not only half is inverted: Not only did he come, but he also brought a gift.

注意：not only … but also 放句首时，只有 not only 那半句倒装：Not only did he come, but he also brought a gift.

  • I have never been to Paris, nor do I want to go.
  • Seldom does he complain.
  • Little did I know what was waiting for me.
A negative comparative = a superlative: no + comparative + than比较级的否定＝最高级：no + 比较级 + than
no, nothing or never with a comparative and than means that nothing else is more so - that is, it is the most of all.
no / nothing / never + 比较级 + than … 表示「没有比……更……的」，即最高级含义。

Common shapes: Nothing is more important than ...; I have never seen a better film; He couldn't be happier (he is extremely happy).

常见句式：Nothing is more important than …；I have never seen a better film.；He couldn't be happier.（他高兴极了）

Keep it apart from no more ... than: A is no more B than C is means that A is as little B as C is - a quite different sense.

与「no more … than」区分：A is no more B than C is ＝ A 和 C 一样都不是 B（双重否定），意思完全不同。

  • Nothing is more precious than time.
  • I couldn't agree more.（我完全同意）
unless ＝ if … not
unless opens a clause of condition meaning if not; the present tense in it refers to the future, and because it is negative in itself you do not add not.
unless 引导条件从句，意为「除非，如果不」；从句用一般现在时表将来，本身已含否定，不再加 not。

A way to read it: put if ... not in its place - if they cannot carry a remedy home, they feel they have not been treated.

理解技巧：把 unless 换成 if … not 读一遍：if they are not able to carry home a remedy, they feel they have not received treatment。

Do not use another negative after unless (not unless you don't come), and do not use will in the clause (not unless it will rain).

unless 后不能再跟否定（✗ unless you don't come）；也不能用 will（✗ unless it will rain）。

  • You will fail unless you work harder.
  • Don't call me unless it's urgent.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • … shows no signs of … ……没有……的迹象
    The rain shows no signs of stopping.
  • be only too ready / glad to do 非常乐意（甚至太乐意）做
    The shop was only too glad to take our money.
  • Nor is it only … who … 也并非只有……才……
    Nor is it only the young who use social media.
  • make no demands on … beyond … 除了……外，对……毫无要求
    The job makes no demands on him beyond turning up on time.

Summary writing摘要写作

In no more than 80 words, answer: how much faith do people today put in medicines, what fact and what example does the writer use, and what do patients really want?

用不超过 80 个词概括：现代人对药物抱有怎样的信心？作者用什么事实和例子说明？病人真正想要的是什么？

Points a good summary covers好摘要要覆盖的要点
  • People have lost their faith in much that their forefathers believed, but not in medicine, and the rising drug bill proves it.人们对许多事物失去了信心，却仍迷信药物；药费年年攀升可以为证。
  • Out-patients are not satisfied unless something comes home with them, and overworked doctors would rather hand it over than spend time on advice about how to live.门诊病人非要带回药品才满意，忙碌的医生也乐于用开药打发他们，而不是给生活建议。
  • Educated people are no different: Carlyle set out to help a sick friend with the remains of his wife's prescription.有知识的人也一样：卡莱尔带着妻子剩下的药去看病中的朋友。
  • The attraction of medicine is that it asks nothing of the patient except a moment of a nasty taste.吃药的好处是除了忍受一下怪味，对病人没有任何要求。
  • What patients want is to be made well without any trouble to themselves.病人想要的是不费自己任何力气就被治好。
Our model summary本站范文

Although ours is a sceptical age, we still trust medicine, as the soaring drug bill shows. Most patients feel untreated unless they take home pills or ointment, and busy doctors gladly supply them rather than give advice on diet and habits. Even the educated share this faith: Carlyle once took his wife's leftover medicine to a sick friend. Medicine asks nothing of patients except a moment's bad taste — and they want a cure without effort.（76 词）
