Lesson 35 · Space Odyssey 太空探索

📘 New Concept English · Book 4 · Lesson 35 “Space Odyssey”新概念英语 第四册 Lesson 35《Space Odyssey》

C1🧠 29 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
Why will the spaceport of the future sit on the Moon and not on Earth? One word: gravity. The Moon has an eightieth of our planet's mass, and it takes only about a fifth of the speed to get away from it, so a cargo leaving the Moon for Earth orbit costs a fraction of the energy of the short hop up from the ground. Once there is a base up there, settling Mars moves from 'possible but not worth doing' to 'I said all along it was a good idea'. This lesson is full of numbers, multiples and proportions — the best text in the book for practising how English says them.
为什么未来的「太空港」在月球而不在地球？答案只有一个字：引力。月球质量只有地球的八十分之一，逃逸速度每秒 1.5 英里，地球却要 7 英里——从月球飞到地球轨道，比从地面飞上 200 英里高的轨道还省 97% 的能量。有了月球基地，移民火星就会从「可能但不值得」变成「我早就说是好主意」。本课满是数字、倍数和比例，是练习英语倍数、分数表达的最佳课文。

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

The original credits the piece to the magazine 7 DAYS and dates it 19 February 1989. We have not been able to trace that issue, so the magazine and the author are not verified; the Clarke quotation inside the passage is well documented, though the book it comes from is not.

旧版课末署名是杂志 7 DAYS，日期 1989 年 2 月 19 日。我们没能查到这一期，所以刊物与作者未核；文中引的克拉克那段话有可靠出处，但出自哪本书未核。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

Read the date and the argument makes sense. In February 1989 the space-station plans were being drawn, the Apollo landings were twenty years back, and the talk in aerospace circles was all about the Moon as a staging post rather than as a destination. The piece is an argument from arithmetic, and its author picked the right numbers. Getting off our planet needs about seven miles per second; getting off the Moon needs about a fifth of that, because the Moon holds an eightieth of the mass and pulls with a sixth of the surface force. Since the energy of a launch rises with the square of the speed, a fifth of the speed is a very small fraction of the energy, which is where the ninety-seven per cent comes from. The oxygen claim is sound too: oxygen is locked in the rock itself, chemically bound, and on the usual estimates lunar soil is about two-fifths oxygen by weight, though we have not checked that figure against the Apollo sample analyses themselves.

把日期和论证放在一起看就通了。1989 年 2 月，空间站方案正在画图，阿波罗登月过去了二十年，而航天圈里谈的是把月球当中转站、不是当目的地。这篇是用算术说话，作者挑的数字是对的：离开地球大约要每秒七英里，离开月球只要五分之一左右，因为月球的质量是地球的八十分之一、表面引力是六分之一。而发射所需的能量随速度的平方增长，速度只要五分之一，能量就只剩很小一块——百分之九十七就是从这里来的。氧气那一条也站得住：氧并不是以气体形式存在，而是化学地锁在岩石里，按常见估算月壤按重量约有五分之二是氧——这个数我们没有回到阿波罗样品分析原文去核。

✨ How it is written赏析

The piece is built on one trick worth stealing: hand the reader a number they can feel. A quarter of a million miles against two hundred miles, and the shorter trip is the expensive one - that reversal does more work than any adjective could. Then the exclamation mark, which is the writer admitting the figure looks absurd, followed at once by the admission that the figure looks absurd and the assurance that the sum behind it is simple. Conceding that your own fact sounds mad, and then standing by it, buys more trust than insisting. The Clarke quotation is placed where it is for a reason: it turns a physics argument into a story about people, and it gives the reader a slot to put themselves in. And notice the last paragraph, which abandons numbers entirely for red deserts, savage storms, a canyon that dwarfs the one in Arizona and a mountain that dwarfs Everest. Arithmetic earns the attention; the pictures make you want it. Most good persuasive writing ends on the picture, not on the sum.

这篇有一招值得偷：给读者一个能用身体感觉到的数字。二十五万英里对二百英里，而短的那一趟才贵——这个反转比任何形容词都管用。接着是那个感叹号，等于作者承认「这数字看着离谱」，紧跟着就是「听起来也许难以置信，但很容易算」。先承认自己的事实听起来像疯话，再站在它后面，这比一味强调更能换来信任。克拉克那段引语摆在那个位置也有道理：它把一个物理论证变成关于人的故事，并且给读者留了一个「我在第几阶段」的位子。还要注意最后一段：数字全部撤走，只剩红色沙漠、猛烈沙暴、比大峡谷更宽的裂谷、比珠峰高一倍以上的山。算术换来注意力，画面让你想去。好的说服性文字大多收在画面上，不收在算式上。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The physics has held and the schedule has not. Thirty-seven years on, nobody lives permanently in space in the sense the author meant: there has been someone aboard a station continuously since late 2000, and a second station since 2021, but the crews are visitors on rotation, not residents, and the number has never passed about a dozen at a time. By Clarke's ladder, Mars is still stuck on the second rung - possible, but not worth doing - and the lesson's own condition for moving to the third has not been met. Two things have gone better than the author knew. Water ice was confirmed in the permanently shadowed craters at the lunar poles, which matters more than oxygen from rock, because water splits into both halves of a rocket's fuel. And on Mars an instrument the size of a toaster on a rover showed the trick can be done away from Earth at all: it pulled oxygen out of the thin carbon-dioxide air sixteen times, 122 grams in total, up to twelve grams an hour, and finished in 2023. That is enough to keep a person breathing for a few hours, and it is the first oxygen ever made on another planet. One number in the lesson deserves a correction. Mars is called 160 times as far away as the Moon, which holds only when the two planets happen to be close: at its nearest Mars is about 145 times as far as the Moon, and on average more like 580 times. The figure flatters the trip. For Chinese readers the interesting development is that the Moon-first logic of this 1989 article is now Chinese policy as much as anyone's: a mission brought back 1,935.3 grams of rock and soil from the far side of the Moon in June 2024, the first samples ever taken from that side, and the published work on them includes how to get water and oxygen out of lunar soil. The article guessed that whoever learns to refuel at the Moon owns the Solar System. It did not guess who would be doing the learning.

物理站住了，时间表没有。三十七年过去，没有人以作者说的那种方式长住太空：自 2000 年底起空间站上一直有人，2021 年起又多了一座，但船上是轮换的访客、不是居民，同时在轨人数从未超过十来个。按克拉克那三级台阶，火星仍停在第二级——可能，但不值得做；课文自己设的「进入第三级」的条件并没有达成。有两件事比作者知道的更好：月球两极永久阴影坑里的水冰已被确认，这比从岩石里取氧更要紧，因为水一拆就是火箭燃料的两半；而在火星上，一台面包机大小的仪器证明了这件事在地球之外也做得成——它从稀薄的二氧化碳大气里取出氧气，前后十六次、共 122 克，最高每小时 12 克，2023 年结束任务。这个量够一个人呼吸几小时，却是人类第一次在另一个行星上制出氧气。课文里有一个数字该改：说火星比月球远 160 倍，只有在两颗行星正好靠近时才成立——最近时约为月球距离的 145 倍，平均则在 580 倍上下。这个数字把行程说轻了。对中国读者，有意思的是这篇 1989 年文章「先上月球」的逻辑，如今也是中国的路线：2024 年 6 月，一次任务从月球背面取回 1935.3 克岩石与土壤，是人类第一次从那一面取样，而围绕这些样品发表的研究里就包括怎样从月壤中取水、取氧。文章猜到了谁学会在月球加油，谁就拥有太阳系；它没猜到会是谁在学。

💬 Discuss and write讨论与写作
  1. Put yourself on Clarke's ladder for one idea you keep hearing about - living on Mars, cities under the sea, uploading a mind. Which rung are you on, and what single piece of evidence would push you up one?
    就一个你总听人说起的想法——移居火星、海底城市、意识上传——把自己放到克拉克那三级台阶上：你在第几级？哪一条证据能把你往上推一级？
  2. The author sells Mars with pictures: red deserts, a canyon wider than the Grand Canyon, a mountain far taller than Everest. Write 120 words selling somewhere you know well, using only what a visitor would see, and no adjectives of praise.
    作者是用画面在卖火星：红色沙漠、比大峡谷更宽的裂谷、远高于珠峰的山。写 120 词推销一个你熟悉的地方，只写访客能看见的东西，不用一个赞美的形容词。
  3. Spending on a Moon base is often attacked as money that should go to people on Earth. Make the strongest version of that attack, then answer it in a way its author would accept.
    建月球基地的花费常被批评为「这笔钱该花在地球上的人身上」。先把这种批评写成最强的版本，再用提出者自己能接受的方式回答它。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 1第 1 句 (begins开头 “The Moon is …”)

  • be likely to do: very probably going to.be likely to do：很可能……。
  • supplying the rocket fuels ...: a present participle phrase adding what the hub would do.supplying the rocket fuels …：现在分词短语作后置定语/补充说明（月球作为枢纽，提供燃料）。

Sentence 5第 5 句 (begins开头 “it requires 97% …”)

  • an eightieth: one part in eighty. English fractions use a cardinal number on top and an ordinal underneath.an eightieth：八十分之一（分数：分子基数词 + 分母序数词）。
  • 97% less energy ... than ...: an amount of difference, then the comparative, then than; the two things compared are the two journeys.97% less energy … than …：数量差 + 比较级 + than，比较的两项是两段旅程。
  • it requires ... to travel ...: a formal it, with the infinitive as the real subject.it requires … to travel …：it 形式主语，真正主语是不定式。

Sentence 11第 11 句 (begins开头 “it takes much …”)

  • it takes + energy or time + to do: doing it costs that much.it takes + 能量/时间 + to do：做……需要……。
  • than it does on Earth: does stands in for the whole verb phrase so that it need not be said twice.than it does on Earth：does 替代 takes energy to accelerate，避免重复。
  • The imperative in brackets asks the reader to recall how the astronauts moved on the surface. Memory is used as evidence, and it works better here than one more number would.括号里那句祈使句（叫读者回想阿波罗宇航员在月面上那种轻快的跳跃）是拿读者的记忆当证据，比再给一个数字有效。

Sentence 12第 12 句 (begins开头 “Moon dwellers will …”)

  • at ... of the cost: at introduces a price or a rate.at … of the cost：以……的花费；at 表价格、比率。
  • their terrestrial cousins: a joke — the relatives back on Earth, that is, the rest of us.their terrestrial cousins：「地球上的表亲」，幽默地指地球人。

Sentence 17第 17 句 (begins开头 “The idea of …”)

  • The appositive for Mars uses a multiple with a comparative: that many times as far away.火星的同位语用「倍数 + 比较级 + than」：远 160 倍。
  • when ... are living: a time clause uses the present continuous for a future meaning, never will be living.when … are living：时间状语从句用现在进行时表将来，不用 will be living。

Sentence 24第 24 句 (begins开头 “cloudless skies, savage …”)

  • After of comes a string of nouns in parallel, each describing the planet and each able to take modifiers of its own.of 后一连串名词并列描述火星，每个都可带修饰。
  • chasms wider than ...: a comparative adjective phrase placed after its noun.chasms wider than …：形容词比较级短语后置修饰名词。
  • To say 'over double the height of', put more than twice in front of the as ... as frame.「比某物高一倍以上」的写法：在 as … as 框架前面加 more than twice。
  • After hence the verb is left out (= hence it gets its name), and the nickname follows the dash.hence 后面省略了动词（＝ hence it gets its name），破折号后面接的就是那个外号。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

Multiples and fractions倍数与分数的表达
English has three usual patterns for multiples: A is three times as big as B / A is three times bigger than B / A is three times the size of B. Fractions use a cardinal number and an ordinal: a sixth, two-thirds, an eightieth.
英语倍数常用三种格式：A is three times as big as B／A is three times bigger than B／A is three times the size of B；分数用「基数词 + 序数词」：a sixth, two-thirds, an eightieth。

This one lesson carries nearly all of them: a fraction with an ordinal, two fractions in words, a percentage of difference, a percentage of cost, a multiple with a comparative, and a multiple in front of the as ... as frame.

本课一网打尽：an eightieth（八十分之一）、a sixth（六分之一）、97% less energy（少 97% 的能量）、at three percent of the cost（花费只有 3%）、160 times more distant（远 160 倍），以及拿珠峰比高度那句里的 more than twice。

When the top of a fraction is more than one, the ordinal takes an s: two-thirds, three-quarters (a quarter is the usual word for a fourth).

分数分子大于 1，序数词加 s：two-thirds, three-quarters（四分之一常用 quarter）。

times is used from three upwards; for two use twice, and for a half use half as big as.

times 用于三倍及以上，两倍用 twice，「一半」用 half：half as big as。

Saying how much something has fallen: reduce by a third means a third was taken off; reduce to a third means two thirds were taken off. The two prepositions say very different things.

「减少了几分之几」：reduce by a third（减少了三分之一）／ reduce to a third（减到三分之一）——by 与 to 差别很大。

  • Mars is about half the size of the Earth.
  • This room is three times as large as that one.
it takes ... to do, and do or that standing in for something already saidit takes … to do 与比较中的替代词 do / that
it takes + (somebody) + time, energy or effort + to do. In a comparison, the second half uses do, does or did for the verb phrase, and that or those for the noun.
it takes + (sb) + 时间/能量/努力 + to do；做比较时，第二个分句用 do/does/did 替代前面的动词短语，用 that/those 替代名词。

In this lesson the second half uses does for the whole verb phrase, so that it need not be repeated.

本课：it takes much less energy …than it does on Earth（does ＝ takes energy to accelerate）。

For a plural noun use those: The roads in the city are better than those in the country.

复数名词用 those：The roads in the city are better than those in the country.

  • It took us three hours to finish.
  • She runs faster than her brother does.
  • The climate of Vancouver is milder than that of Toronto.
Time and condition clauses: the present tense for a future meaning时间、条件状语从句用现在时表将来
After when, before, after, until, as soon as, if and unless, the main clause may be future but the clause itself takes the present simple or present continuous — never will.
when, before, after, until, as soon as, if, unless 等引导的状语从句，主句是将来时，从句用一般现在时或现在进行时（不用 will）。

In this lesson the main clause is future while the when clause stays in the present continuous.

本课：主句用 will move …，而 when 从句用 are living（不是 will be living）。

Keep it apart from when introducing an object clause ('at what time'), where will is fine: I don't know when he will come.

注意区分：when 引导宾语从句（「什么时候」）时可以用 will：I don't know when he will come.

The same goes for if meaning 'whether' in an object clause: I wonder if it will rain.

if 引导宾语从句（「是否」）也可用 will：I wonder if it will rain.

  • I'll call you as soon as I arrive.
  • We will start when everyone is ready.

Summary writing摘要写作

Write no more than 80 words: why would the Moon become the industrial centre of the Solar System, and what does that mean for settling Mars?

用不超过 80 个词概括：为什么月球会成为太阳系的工业中心？这对移民火星有什么意义？

Points a good summary covers好摘要要覆盖的要点
