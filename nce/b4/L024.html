Lesson 24 · Beauty 美

📘 New Concept English · Book 4 · Lesson 24 “Beauty”新概念英语 第四册 Lesson 24《Beauty》

C1🧠 27 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
One of the hardest pieces of writing in Book 4. A young man watches a sunset, cannot say what he feels, and concludes that he has been shown a door into another world. The writer grants him part of his case: great art, and nature in certain moods, really do give us a glimpse of something finer, and the glimpse is beyond words, because our words were made for this world. But if beauty does mean something, we should not try to read the meaning off it, for measured in human meanings beauty has none. The sentences are long, the insertions many, the rhetoric strong (double negatives; life compared to a cheap flame among the cold light of the stars). Finding the frame of each sentence matters more here than learning the words.
第四册最难读的哲理散文之一。年轻人看到日落，无法表达心中的感动，于是断定那是通往另一个世界的门。作者承认：伟大的艺术和某些时刻的自然，确实让人瞥见一个「更美的世界」，而这种美超出语言；但如果美真有意义，我们不应去解释它——用人类的意义去衡量，美是没有意义的。句子长、插入成分多、修辞强（双重否定的「没有哪片天蓝得不指向更蓝」、把生命比作星光里一簇庸俗的火苗），读懂主干比记生词更重要。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. Why does the young man take a sunset for a door into another world?年轻人看到日落时为什么会认为那是通往另一个世界的大门？
  2. Why is language so poor at describing such moments?为什么语言难以描述审美体验？
  3. What does the writer finally tell us to do about beauty?作者最后的结论是什么：我们应该怎样对待美？

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

Cyril Edwin Mitchinson Joad (1891-1953) was the best-known philosopher in Britain in the 1940s, not because of his books but because of the radio. He sat on the BBC panel programme The Brains Trust, where listeners sent in questions and three or four learned men answered them live, and his habit of opening with a request to define the terms made him a household voice. This passage is that voice in its Sunday clothes: popular philosophy written to be read aloud. It also sits on a road Joad was travelling. He had made his name as a sceptic, and in his last years he published a book describing his return to Christian belief, so the beauty that points beyond this world is not a decoration in his argument but the hinge of it. One more fact belongs here, because the essay is about human meanings. In 1948 Joad was caught travelling in a first-class railway carriage on a third-class ticket, was convicted, and lost his place at the BBC. The man who warned us not to read meanings into beauty was himself read, for the rest of his life, through a railway fare.

西里尔·埃德温·米钦森·乔德（1891–1953）是 1940 年代英国最知名的哲学家，靠的不是书，是广播。他是 BBC 节目《智囊团》（The Brains Trust）的常驻嘉宾：听众寄来问题，三四位学者现场作答；他开口先要求「先说清你这个词指什么」的习惯，使他成了家喻户晓的声音。这几段就是那个声音穿上礼服的样子——写来供朗读的通俗哲学。它也落在乔德当时正走的那条路上：他以怀疑论者成名，晚年出书讲述自己回到基督信仰，所以「指向彼岸」的美在他的论证里不是装饰，而是枢纽。还有一件事该放在这里，因为这篇文章讲的正是「人的意义」：1948 年，乔德被查出持三等票坐一等车厢，被定罪，失去了 BBC 的位置。那个劝我们别给美强加意义的人，此后一生被人用一张火车票来读解。

✨ How it is written赏析

The method here is suspension. The writer keeps putting off the main clause: three if-clauses in a row, each carrying a metaphor to be denied, with in a word at last letting the sentence land. Read that passage aloud and you can feel the technique working on you, which is the first thing to learn from it and the first thing to be careful about. Then look at the two other devices. Double negatives do the praising: not one June sky, not one sunset, is fine enough to stop us wanting a finer, so the compliment arrives as a refusal. And the old inversions after yet and nor lift the tone a step above ordinary prose. Finally count the words in the last sentence. After clauses that run to fifty and sixty words, the essay ends on nine flat ones, with no image in them at all. If you want one lesson in shape from this page, it is that: build the long thing, then stop short, and let the short sentence carry the claim. The risk is also worth naming, because this page shows it too. A reader carried along by sound may not notice that the central step - I cannot say what I feel, therefore what I feel comes from somewhere else - has not been argued at all.

这里的手法是「悬置」：作者一再推迟主句——三个 if 从句接连排开，每个都抬出一个比喻再加以否认，最后由 in a word 让整句落地。把那一段读出声，你能感到这套手法正在你身上起作用；这是从它那里要学的第一件事，也是要当心的第一件事。再看另两处。双重否定承担了赞美：没有哪片六月的天、没有哪场日落美得让人不再想要更美的——赞语以拒绝的形式送到。yet 与 nor 之后的旧式倒装，又把调子抬高了一级。最后数一数末句的词数：在动辄五六十词的长句之后，全文以九个平白的词收尾，里面一个意象也没有。如果从这一页只学一件关于结构的事，就是它：先把长的搭起来，然后骤然停住，让那个短句承担主张。风险也值得点明，因为这一页同样展示了它：被声音带着走的读者，可能不会注意到最关键的一步——「我说不出我的感受，所以这感受来自别处」——其实根本没有被论证。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

Start with the logic, because the essay invites the question. The step from I cannot express this to this is a door into another world is not an argument; it is an inference from a limit of language to a fact about the universe. Joad half knows it, and his last move protects the conclusion from any test: do not interpret, do not try to utter it. A claim that may not be examined cannot be wrong, and cannot be useful either. Since 1942 some of this has become measurable, and the findings cut both ways. Experiments on shared taste find that agreement between strangers is strong for faces and for landscapes and much weaker for works of art, so the common ground he assumed is real for his June sky and thin for his great art. Brain imaging finds that strong aesthetic experience engages regions of medial prefrontal cortex that belong to the default-mode network, the same system that is busy when we think about ourselves - which is why a sunset can feel like a message addressed to you personally, and also why that feeling is no evidence about the sunset. For a Chinese reader the shape of his conclusion will be familiar long before the English is. What can be spoken of is not the thing itself; words do not exhaust the sense. The old formula is nearly the same, but its use is different: in the Chinese tradition the unsayable is usually handed over to practice - to the brush, the strings, the long walk - rather than to a world beyond this one. Set the two side by side and you can see what is being claimed and what is only being felt.

先说逻辑，因为这篇文章自己把问题提了出来。从「我说不出」到「这是通往另一个世界的门」，这一步不是论证，而是从语言的界限推出关于宇宙的事实。乔德半知道这一点，而他最后一招把结论保护起来，使它无法被检验：不要解释，不要试图说出。一个不许审查的主张不会错，也不会有用。1942 年以来，其中一部分已经可以度量，而结果两面都切。关于「共同趣味」的实验发现：陌生人之间的一致度在面孔和自然景观上很高，在艺术作品上则低得多——所以他预设的共识，对那片六月的天是真的，对「伟大的艺术」却很薄。脑成像发现，强烈的审美体验会调动内侧前额叶中属于「默认模式网络」的区域，也就是我们想自己的事时活跃的那套系统——这既解释了为什么一场日落会像一封写给你本人的信，也说明这种感觉对日落本身并不构成证据。对中国读者来说，他结论的形状在英文之前就熟悉了：可以说出来的就不是那个东西本身，言不尽意。旧的说法几乎相同，用处却不同：在中国的传统里，不可说的部分通常被交给实践——交给笔、交给弦、交给一段长路——而不是交给另一个世界。把两者并排放，就看得出哪一部分是主张，哪一部分只是感受。

💬 Discuss and write讨论与写作
  1. Describe something you have seen or heard that you could not put into words at the time. Try now, in five sentences, and then say what the attempt lost.
    说一件你当时说不出口的所见或所闻。现在用五句话试着说出来，再说说这次尝试丢掉了什么。
  2. People agree about beautiful faces and beautiful landscapes much more than about paintings or music. Why might that be, and does it make one kind of beauty more real than the other?
    人们对美的面孔和美的风景的看法，比对绘画或音乐一致得多。可能是什么原因？这是否使其中一种美比另一种更「真实」？
  3. Write 120 words answering the writer: if beauty may not be interpreted, what should a critic, a teacher or a guide do for the rest of us?
    写 120 词回应作者：如果美不可解释，那么评论家、老师或导游该为我们其他人做什么？

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 2第 2 句 (begins开头 “unable to understand …”)

  • unable to ...: an adjective phrase giving the reason, slipped in between and and the main verb.unable to…：形容词短语作原因状语，插在 and 与 concludes 之间。
  • that it rouses in him: a relative clause; that lies beyond is another, describing the world.that it rouses in him：定语从句；that lies beyond：定语从句修饰 world。

Sentence 4第 4 句 (begins开头 “It is difficult …”)

  • it is difficult for somebody to do something: it is an empty subject standing for the infinitive.it is difficult for sb to do：形式主语。
  • the suggestion that ...: a noun clause in apposition to suggestion; a light that shines ... is a relative clause.the suggestion that…：同位语从句；a light that shines…：定语从句。
  • different and ... in some way higher: two adjectives joined, with a clause of reason pushed in between them.different and … in some way higher：形容词并列，中间插入原因状语从句。

Sentence 8第 8 句 (begins开头 “And, though the …”)

  • though ... yet: an old-fashioned pair; modern English does not put but or yet after though.though … yet：古雅的呼应（现代英语 though 后通常不用 but/yet）。
  • When yet opens the clause the writer inverts the verb for emphasis, which gives a literary ring rather than a modern one.yet 放句首表转折时，此处用了倒装 do they convey——强调与文学化。
  • greater than we have known: a comparative phrase placed after the two nouns it describes.greater than we have known：形容词短语后置修饰 beauty and serenity。

Sentence 10第 10 句 (begins开头 “for language, which …”)

  • for: because; another means another world.for：因为；another = another world。

Sentence 12第 12 句 (begins开头 “That all great …”)

  • that opens a noun clause used as the subject; in that position the that cannot be left out. The same idea can be written It is undeniable that ...that 引导主语从句，放句首时 that 不能省；也可改为 It is undeniable that…

Sentence 14第 14 句 (begins开头 “There is no …”)

  • no ... so ... that ... not: two negatives make a strong positive, so every June sky points to a bluer one.no … so … that … not：双重否定＝任何……都……（每一片六月的蓝天都让人想到更蓝的天）。

Sentence 24第 24 句 (begins开头 “beauty means something …”)

  • Three if-clauses are laid one on another; the first two deny a string of metaphors, and in a word closes the series.三个 if 条件从句层层铺排，前两个用比喻的否定（世界不是坏笑话、生命不是庸俗的火光……），最后 in a word 收束。

Sentence 26第 26 句 (begins开头 “nor should we …”)

  • After nor the subject and the auxiliary change places.nor 后倒装（should we）。
  • invest A with B: to give A the quality of B. Here the object is long, so it is moved to the end, after the with-phrase.invest A with B：赋予 A 以 B；这里宾语 that which we cannot grasp 很长，后移到 with significance 之后（尾重）。
  • that which = what: the thing that (we cannot grasp).that which = what：那种（我们无法把握的）东西。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

Noun clauses as subject: That ... is ...; that which = what主语从句 That … is …；that which = what
When a that-clause is the subject and stands first, the that cannot be dropped; more often English uses it as an empty subject instead (see Lesson 20). that which is the written form of what: the thing that.
that 引导的主语从句放句首时 that 不可省；更常见的是用 it 作形式主语（见第 20 课）。that which 是 what 的书面说法：「……的东西」。
  • That he is honest is clear to everyone. = It is clear to everyone that he is honest.
  • Don't believe that which you cannot prove. = Don't believe what you cannot prove.
Double negatives: no ... so ... that ... not / never ... without双重否定：no … so … that … not / never … without
A negative on a negative makes a strong positive: There is no ... so ... that it does not ... means that every one of them does; never ... without doing means every time; cannot help doing means you are unable to stop.
否定 + 否定 = 强烈的肯定：There is no … so … that it does not …＝所有……都……；never … without doing＝每……必……；cannot help doing＝禁不住。
  • There is no rule so strict that it has no exceptions.（任何规则都有例外）
  • They never meet without quarrelling.（每次见面都吵）
Literary inversion: yet do they / nor should we文学性倒装：yet do they / nor should we
For emphasis or for rhythm, written English may invert (auxiliary before subject) after yet, nor, neither, only, not until and never at the head of a clause.
为了强调或节奏，书面语在 yet、nor、neither、only、not until、never 等词放句首时可用部分倒装（助动词提到主语前）。

Inversion after nor, neither, never and only is still ordinary in modern prose; inversion after yet is rare and belongs to older, more formal writing.

现代散文里 nor/neither/never/only 句首的倒装仍很常用；yet 句首倒装已罕见，属于古雅修辞。

  • Never have I seen such a beautiful sunset.
  • Only then did I realize my mistake.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • … and, unable to …, concludes that … ……，由于无法……，就断定……
    He failed the test and, unable to accept it, concluded that the teacher was unfair.
  • That … is undeniable. ……是不可否认的
    That technology has changed how we read is undeniable.
  • There is no … so … that it does not … 没有任何……如此……以至于不……（＝每个……都……）
    There is no city so rich that it has no poor.

Summary writing摘要写作

In no more than 80 words, sum up the writer's view of beauty. Hold on to the frame of each sentence and do not be led away by the metaphors and insertions.

用不超过 80 个词概括作者关于美的看法。注意抓主干，不要被比喻和插入成分牵着走。

Points a good summary covers好摘要要覆盖的要点
  • A strong experience of beauty, like the young man's sunset, leaves us feeling we have seen a light from a higher world.强烈的审美体验（如年轻人看日落）让人觉得瞥见了另一个更高世界的光。
  • That beauty goes beyond what we have known, imagined or can describe, because our language was made for this world and not for another.这种美超出了我们知道、想象和能描述的范围，因为语言是为这个世界造的。
  • All great art, and nature in some moods, can hint at a world beyond, and the hint leaves a longing and a regret that cannot be named.一切伟大艺术、某些时候的自然，都有暗示「彼岸世界」的力量，留下难以名状的向往与惆怅。
  • If beauty does mean something, we still should not try to interpret it, nor force a meaning on what we cannot grasp.如果美确有意义，我们也不应试图解释它，不该把意义强加于我们无法把握的东西。
  • Measured in human meanings, beauty has no meaning at all.用人类的意义来衡量，美是没有意义的。
