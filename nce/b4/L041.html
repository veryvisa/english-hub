Lesson 41 · Training Elephants 训练大象

📘 New Concept English · Book 4 · Lesson 41 “Training Elephants”新概念英语 第四册 Lesson 41《Training Elephants》

C1🧠 35 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
There are two ways of training an elephant: the hard way, which means putting it to work and beating it until it obeys, and the patient way, which costs more time and gives you a good-tempered animal that stays loyal for years. The writer is plainly on the patient side. The key, he says, is that one elephant should answer to one man. A newly caught elephant is first roped up with a tame elephant beside it, then sent to a training camp to take in the feel of the place, and only then trained, amid rubbing hands and a slow chant. This is a process description, held together by markers: the first essential, the next stage, and then its own training begins. The observation is close, and the writer's dry humour shows through (ho! my son).
训练大象有两种办法：硬的——一边干活一边打，直到它听话；软的——更有耐心，换来一头脾气好、多年忠心的大象。作者显然站在「软」的一边：训象的关键是让一头大象只认一个驯象人，被捉来的野象先拴着、让驯好的象陪着，再送进训练场「感受气氛」，然后在抚摸和单调的吟唱中开始训练。本课是过程说明文，用 The first essential / The next stage / When … its own training begins 串起步骤；描写细腻，又不乏作者的冷幽默（ho! my son）。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. What comes of each of the two ways of training an elephant?训练大象的两种方法各有什么后果？
  2. Why is the bond between one man and one animal said to decide whether the training works?为什么说驯象人与大象之间的关系是训练成功的关键？
  3. What has to happen to a newly caught elephant before its training can start?新捉来的野象到训练场后要经过哪些步骤才开始训练？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

The lesson is taken from Richard Carrington's Elephants: A Short Account of Their Natural History, Evolution and Influence on Mankind, published in London by Chatto & Windus in 1958 and reviewed that year in the conservation journal Oryx; a Pelican paperback followed in 1962. We confirmed the book, the publisher and the year from catalogue and review records, but we have not read the chapter itself, so we cannot say which page the passage comes from.

课文选自理查德·卡林顿（Richard Carrington）的《Elephants: A Short Account of Their Natural History, Evolution and Influence on Mankind》，1958 年伦敦 Chatto & Windus 出版，同年保护类期刊《Oryx》有书评；1962 年出了 Pelican 平装本。书名、出版社与年份我们核到了（图书馆目录与书评），但没有读到原章，所以说不出这段在第几页。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

Read the date first. In 1958 this was not a piece about cruelty to animals; it was a piece about labour. Elephants were still working animals across India and Burma, hauling timber out of forest that no machine could enter, and a book for the general reader could describe how one was broken in the way another book might describe how a horse is shod. That is why the passage reads so oddly today: nobody in it is defending anything. The capture it describes was then ordinary practice, and the economics are stated without embarrassment - an animal of fifteen to twenty is chosen because it can start paying for its food at once. The law has since moved. In India the Asian elephant is now listed in the first schedule of the 1972 wildlife act, and taking such an animal is prohibited except under narrow stated exceptions. The method survives mainly in tourism and in the remaining timber camps, which is where almost all the modern evidence comes from.

先看年份。1958 年，这不是一篇关于虐待动物的文章，而是一篇关于劳动的文章。那时候印度和缅甸的大象还是干活的牲口，把机器进不去的林子里的木头拖出来；一本写给普通读者的书描述怎么驯服一头象，和另一本书描述怎么给马钉蹄铁，性质是一样的。所以这段文字今天读起来才这么别扭：里面没有一个人在为什么事辩护。它描述的捕捉在当时是常规做法，经济账也说得毫无尴尬——挑十五到二十岁的，因为它立刻就能挣回自己的饭钱。法律后来变了：在印度，亚洲象已列入 1972 年野生动物保护法的第一附表，捕捉这类动物除少数明文例外一律禁止。这套方法如今主要留在旅游业和尚存的木材营地里，而现代的证据几乎全部来自那里。

✨ How it is written赏析

Watch what the writer does in his second paragraph, because it is the most useful move on the page. He has just described beating an elephant into obedience, and his verdict is that, morals aside, the method is simply stupid. He puts the morals aside on purpose. A reader who does not care how an elephant feels still cares about an animal that will kill a man in ten years' time, and so the argument is built to win that reader rather than the reader who already agrees. The rest of the technique is quieter. He never uses a word like cruel; he simply sets the vocabulary of tenderness beside the vocabulary of the tool, and lets them stand next to each other - the hands rubbing the skin, the slow chant, the pet names, and a long metal-tipped stick. And then the last sentence delivers the result in the flat voice of a manual: the trunk grows too sore to swing, so the animal keeps it curled. Nothing is denounced. The sentence is terrible precisely because it is written as a technical outcome. In your own writing both moves are worth stealing: argue on the ground your opponent stands on, and let a fact finish the paragraph instead of an adjective.

看作者第二段里的那一手，这是整页上最有用的动作。他刚刚描述了把一头象打到服从，然后给出判决：撇开道德因素不谈，这是一种愚蠢的训练方法。他是故意把道德挪到一边的。一个并不在乎大象什么感受的读者，仍然在乎十年后会杀人的那头畜生；于是这个论证是冲着那种读者造的，而不是冲着本来就同意他的人。其余的手法更安静：他从不用「残忍」这类词，只是把温柔的词汇和工具的词汇并排摆着——摩擦皮肤的手、缓慢的吟唱、亲昵的称呼，和一根带尖铁头的长棍。最后一句则用操作手册的平直语气交出结果：鼻子疼得挥不动了，于是它自己卷起来。全程没有一句谴责。这个句子之所以可怕，恰恰因为它是以技术结论的形式写出来的。这两手都值得偷进自己的写作：在对手站的那块地上跟他讲理；段落的收尾交给一个事实，而不是一个形容词。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The central claim can now be tested with numbers, and it holds better than the writer could have known. Most of the evidence comes from Myanmar's timber elephants, whose working records go back to the middle of the last century. A study of 5,150 animals from 1951 to 2000 found that elephants taken from the wild carried a higher risk of death than elephants born in captivity, whatever the capture method, and lived a median of three to seven years less. A second study followed 1,947 calves through the age at which taming begins and found the risk of death rising by more than half at the age of four. So his prudential argument - the hard way does not even pay - is now a measured effect rather than an opinion, and his other claim, that the bond with one keeper carries the training, matches a welfare literature that treats a stable handler relationship as central. What the lesson leaves out is the question that has replaced his. He asks how best to tame; nobody now asks that first. The wild population is thought to be somewhere between forty and fifty thousand, the species is listed as endangered, and more than sixty per cent of the habitat that once suited it has gone since 1700. India's own count in 2025, done from DNA rather than from sightings, came to about 22,400. The argument has moved to whether elephants should be kept at all, and, where they are, whether the handler should work beside the animal or behind a barrier. Here the honest answer is that the evidence is thinner than the quarrel: a veterinary review describes the two systems and their trade-offs, most captive elephants in Asia are still handled directly, and there is very little head-to-head welfare research to settle it. For Chinese readers there is a useful parallel in how working animals left ordinary life at home within one or two generations - the water buffalo and the cart horse went out with the tractor, and the question of how to break them in simply stopped being asked. Our page is a photograph of the moment just before a trade becomes a controversy.

核心主张今天可以用数字检验，而且它比作者能知道的更站得住。证据大半来自缅甸的木材象，它们的工役档案从上世纪中叶开始。一项覆盖 1951 至 2000 年、5,150 头象的研究发现：从野外捕来的象，不论用哪种捕法，死亡风险都高于圈养出生的象，中位寿命少三到七年。另一项研究跟踪了 1,947 头幼象经过开始驯服的年龄，发现四岁那年死亡风险上升了五成以上。于是他那个功利论证——硬办法根本不划算——今天不再是看法，而是量出来的效应；他另一个主张，即训练靠的是与唯一驯象人的关系，也与把「稳定的人象关系」当作核心的动物福利文献对得上。课文缺的是那个取代了他的问题。他问的是怎样驯得最好，今天没有人先问这个：野生种群估计在四万到五万之间，物种列为濒危，1700 年以来适宜栖息地已消失六成以上；印度 2025 年改用 DNA 方法自己数了一次，约 22,400 头。争论已经移到「到底该不该养」，以及在养的地方，驯象人该站在象身边还是隔着围栏。这里诚实的答案是证据比争吵薄：一份兽医界的综述列出了两种制度各自的代价，亚洲绝大多数圈养象仍是人直接接触，而真正一对一比较福利结果的研究非常少。对中国读者，有一个可用的对照：家里的役用动物也在一两代之内退出了日常生活——水牛和拉车的马随拖拉机一起走了，「怎么驯」这个问题就直接不再被问起。我们这一页，是一门手艺变成一场争议之前的最后一张照片。

💬 Discuss and write讨论与写作
  1. The writer deliberately sets the moral question aside and argues that the cruel method is simply stupid. Was that the stronger way to win his reader, or did he give something up? Argue one side in about 120 words, and put the best version of the other side first.
    作者故意把道德问题挪开，只论证残忍的办法「愚蠢」。这是赢得读者更有力的做法，还是他放弃了什么？用大约 120 词支持一方，但先把另一方最有力的说法写出来。
  2. Pick a job that has almost disappeared where you live - a craft, a trade, an animal that used to work. Describe in six sentences how it was done, in the flat voice of this lesson, with no adjectives of judgement. Then say what the flat voice did to the reader.
    挑一件在你生活的地方几乎消失了的活计——一门手艺、一个行当、一种曾经干活的动物。用本课那种平直的语气、不带任何评价性形容词，用六句话写出它是怎么做的。然后说一说，这种平直的语气对读者起了什么作用。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 1第 1 句 (begins开头 “Two main techniques …”)

  • which introduces a non-defining relative clause whose antecedent is the two techniques; in call A B the object has moved to the front as which.which 引导非限制性定语从句，指 two main techniques；call A B 中宾语 which 已提前。
  • respectively lines the two methods up with the two names, in that order.respectively：「分别」，把两种方法依次对应 the tough 和 the gentle。

Sentence 2第 2 句 (begins开头 “The former method …”)

  • the former means the first of two things just mentioned; the writer could have written the latter instead of naming the gentle method again.the former：前者（指 the tough）；后文 The gentle method 本可以写成 the latter。
  • consist of takes two gerunds here, joined by and.consist of + 两个并列动名词 setting … and beating …。
  • what is expected of him is a noun clause as object; expect something of somebody means require it from them.what is expected of him：what 宾语从句；expect sth of sb 对某人期望某事。

Sentence 4第 4 句 (begins开头 “for it produces …”)

  • apart from means leaving that aside (Lesson 31).apart from：撇开……不谈（L31）。
  • for here introduces a supporting reason, and belongs to written style.for：表补充原因（书面）。
  • may well means it is quite likely (Lesson 29); turn plus a bare noun means become, as in turn traitor or turn professional.may well：很可能（L29）；turn + 名词（不加冠词）：变成，如 turn traitor / turn professional。

Sentence 9第 9 句 (begins开头 “There are even …”)

  • The who-clause has two predicates in parallel: have refused and (have) pined.who 从句两个并列谓语：have refused … and (have) pined …。
  • Inside the when-clause an adverbial of means has been dropped in, saying how it came about.when 从句里插入方式状语 by some unavoidable circumstance。
  • be deprived of means to have something taken away (Lesson 33).be deprived of：被剥夺（L33）。

Sentence 23第 23 句 (begins开头 “it is customary …”)

  • it is customary for sb to do: for brings in the doer of the infinitive, and the infinitive itself is passive.it is customary for sb to do：for 引出不定式的逻辑主语；to be placed 是不定式被动。
  • are being trained: present continuous, passive.are being trained：现在进行时被动。

Sentence 30第 30 句 (begins开头 “This is supposed …”)

  • be supposed to do here means it is believed to - and the writer has his doubts, since the next sentence says the animal is not taken in.be supposed to do：「据认为会……；理应……」——这里带一丝怀疑（作者下一句就说大象并不吃这一套）。
  • endearing epithets are pet names, such as the ones quoted.endearing epithets：亲昵的称呼，如 'ho! my son'。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

the former / the latter, and respectivelythe former / the latter 与 respectively
the former means the first of the two just mentioned, the latter the second; respectively pairs off two sets of things in order.
the former 指前面提到的两者中的第一个，the latter 指第二个；respectively 用于把两组事物按顺序一一对应。

the former / the latter are for two items only; with three or more use the first / the last.

the former / the latter 只用于两者；三者以上用 the first / the last。

The verb agrees with the noun referred to: the former is ... (singular noun) / the latter are ... (plural noun).

谓语数随所指的名词：the former is …（单数名词）／ the latter are …（复数名词）。

Position of respectively: usually at the end of the clause, or right beside the pair it sorts out: Tom and Jane are 10 and 12 respectively.

respectively 位置：通常放在句末或被对应的那组词之前/后：Tom and Jane are 10 and 12 respectively.

  • Tea and coffee are both popular; the former is cheaper, the latter stronger.
  • The first and second prizes went to Anna and Ben respectively.
It is + adjective + for sb to do: for brings in the doer of the infinitiveIt is + adj. + for sb to do：for 引出不定式的逻辑主语
When you need to say who performs the infinitive, put for plus a noun or pronoun in front of it. The commonest frames are it is + adjective or noun + for sb to do, too ... for sb to do, and enough ... for sb to do.
不定式动作的执行者要说出来时，在不定式前用 for + 名词/代词；常见于 it is + adj./n. + for sb to do，以及 too … for sb to do、enough … for sb to do。

for or of? If the adjective describes the thing done (easy, important, necessary, customary), use for. If it describes the person (kind, clever, stupid, careless), use of: It is kind of you to help.

for 与 of 的区别：形容词描述事情（easy, important, necessary, customary）用 for；形容词描述人的品质（kind, clever, stupid, careless）用 of：It is kind of you to help.

The same phrase can also serve as a purpose or as a subject: The best thing is for you to rest. / For him to win would be a miracle.

也可作目的、主语：The best thing is for you to rest.／ For him to win would be a miracle.

  • It is important for children to sleep enough.
  • The box was too heavy for me to lift.
The two senses of be supposed to dobe supposed to do 的两层意思
be supposed to do means either (1) ought to, by rule or arrangement - often hinting that it did not happen - or (2) is believed to, the same as be believed to. This lesson uses sense (2), in a doubting tone.
be supposed to do ① 「理应、按规定应该」（常暗示实际上没做）；② 「据认为、据说」（= be believed to）。本课是第 ② 种，带怀疑口吻。

Sense (1): You are supposed to be at work at nine. / You were supposed to call me!

第 ① 种：You are supposed to be at work at nine.（你本该九点上班）／ You were supposed to call me!（你本该给我打电话的！）

The negative, be not supposed to do, means it is not allowed: You're not supposed to smoke here.

否定 be not supposed to do ＝「不许、不该」：You're not supposed to smoke here.

A perfect infinitive puts the report in the past: He is supposed to have been rich once.

完成式表过去的「据说」：He is supposed to have been rich once.

  • This drug is supposed to cure headaches.（据说能治头痛）
  • We are supposed to hand in our essays today.（按规定应该今天交）

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • The former method consists of … 前一种方法就是……
    The former method consists of learning lists of words by heart.
  • Apart from any moral considerations, … 撇开道德因素不谈，……
    Apart from any moral considerations, cheating simply doesn't work.
  • The first essential in … is to … ……的首要条件是……
    The first essential in learning to swim is to relax.
  • It is customary for … to … 按惯例……要……
    It is customary for the bride's father to make a speech.

Keywords for retelling复述关键词

  • two methods: tough (beating) → resentful, maybe a man-killer; gentle → loyal for years
  • first essential: one mahout for each elephant; elephants feel personal affection
  • best age to capture: 15–20 — ready to work but hard to tame
  • captive roped to a tree, screams, refuses food; a tame elephant calms it
  • moved to the training centre between two trained elephants; left alone to settle
  • training: stick with a metal point, rubbing, a soothing chant, "ho! my son" — the trunk becomes sore and is curled up

Summary writing摘要写作

In no more than 80 words: what are the two ways of training an elephant, which one does the writer prefer, and how is a newly caught animal brought round?

用不超过 80 个词概括：训练大象有哪两种方法？作者赞成哪一种？新捉来的野象如何被逐步驯服？

Points a good summary covers好摘要要覆盖的要点
  • The hard way uses the stick, and it leaves an animal full of grudges that may even kill; the patient way takes time and gives you a gentle, loyal elephant.「硬」的方法靠打，会使大象怨恨甚至伤人；「软」的方法需要耐心，能得到温顺忠诚的大象。
  • The first requirement is one keeper and one elephant, and everything turns on the bond between them.首要条件是给大象指定唯一的驯象人，二者的关系是成功的关键。
  • An animal of 15 to 20 is the cheapest to take, but the hardest to bring round; at first it is roped up with a tame elephant for company until it will eat.最经济的是捉 15–20 岁的象，但它们难驯服，开始时野象拴着、由驯服的象陪伴，直到肯吃东西。
  • At the camp it is left in peace to settle, and then trained with rubbing, chanting and pet names, while a pointed stick keeps its swinging trunk under control.被送到训练场后先静养适应，然后在抚摸、吟唱和亲昵称呼中开始训练，用尖棍制服它挥舞的鼻子。
Our model summary本站范文

Elephants are trained either by beating, which makes them resentful and dangerous, or by patience, which produces loyal, good-natured animals. The key is to give each elephant a single trainer. Elephants of 15 to 20 are the best to catch, but they resist at first, so the captive is tied up beside a tame elephant until it eats. Then, at a training centre, it is allowed to settle and is trained with soothing chants and a pointed stick.（78 词）
