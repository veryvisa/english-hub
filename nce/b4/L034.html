Lesson 34 · Adolescence 青春期

📘 New Concept English · Book 4 · Lesson 34 “Adolescence”新概念英语 第四册 Lesson 34《Adolescence》

C1🧠 25 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
A child praises a friend's house, and the parents hear an attack on their cooking and their furniture; they cannot resist a cold remark — and from then on the child tells them nothing. The author writes as a psychiatrist: losing your illusions about your parents is unavoidable in these years, and it is exactly the sign that the child is growing up and forming a judgment of its own. What parents should do is admit they were wrong, not stand on their authority. The sentences here come in cause-and-effect chains: watch the joints — and makes them resolve, but they seldom realize, and therefore would not.
孩子夸别人家好，父母就觉得是在贬自己的厨艺和家具，忍不住冷嘲热讽——结果孩子从此什么都不跟家里说。作者是心理学家的口吻：青春期对父母「幻灭」是必然的，这恰恰说明孩子在长大、在形成独立判断；父母该做的是坦然认错，而不是端着权威。本课句子多是「原因—后果」链，练习时盯住 and makes them resolve、but they seldom realize、and therefore would not 这些逻辑节点。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. Which parental reaction makes a child stop talking about its friends?父母的哪种反应会让孩子从此不再谈论自己的朋友？
  2. Why is a child's disillusionment with its parents unavoidable to some degree?为什么孩子对父母的「幻灭」在某种程度上不可避免？
  3. What kind of parent do adolescents respect, and what can they not forgive?青少年尊重什么样的父母？不能原谅父母的什么？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

The passage comes from Journey through Adolescence (1957) by Doris Odlum. Odlum (1890-1985) was an English psychiatrist who worked mainly with children and adolescents, chaired the British Medical Association's psychological medicine group, pressed for mental health to be part of the new National Health Service, and from 1974 was life president of the Samaritans, the telephone service for people in despair.

课文取自多丽丝·奥德勒姆（Doris Odlum）1957 年的《Journey through Adolescence》。奥德勒姆（1890–1985）是英国精神科医师，主要治疗儿童与青少年，当过英国医学会心理医学组主席，主张把精神卫生纳入新建的国民保健体系，1974 年起担任撒玛利亚会（为绝望者设的电话求助机构）终身会长。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

Two things about 1957 explain the tone. First, the Victorian father the author mentions was not history to her readers; he was their own father. A British parent of 1957 had been raised under the rule that a child's real feelings were not the parents' business, and was now being told the opposite by a woman doctor writing in plain words for the general public. Second, her clinical work was with young people in trouble, and much of her life went into a service for people who had no one to tell. That is why one sentence in the lesson carries more weight than it looks: children then were too frightened to let their parents know how they really felt. From where she sat, a child who cannot speak is not merely a sad household; it is a risk. The practical advice follows from the clinic, not from a theory of the family.

关于 1957 年有两件事能解释这篇的语气。第一，她提到的那种维多利亚式父亲，对当时的读者不是历史，就是他们自己的父亲：1957 年的英国家长，从小被教的规矩是「孩子真实的感受不是父母该管的事」，而现在有一位女医生用大白话告诉他们正相反。第二，她的临床工作对象就是出了问题的年轻人，她一生很大一部分精力投在一个专为「无人可说」的人设立的求助机构上。所以课文里有一句的分量比看上去重：那时候的孩子太胆怯，不敢让父母知道自己真实的感受。坐在她那个位置上看，一个说不出话的孩子不只是家里不和睦，而是一个风险。文中的建议出自诊室，不是出自某种家庭理论。

✨ How it is written赏析

The paragraph is one long chain of consequences, and every link is a verb: the child praises, the parents are annoyed, a spiteful remark follows, the child is shocked, the child resolves, the child goes quiet, the parents complain. Nothing is asserted about character; it is all sequence, and the reader is left to notice that each step is small and reasonable and the end result is a wall. Then comes the move that makes the piece useful. She takes the event the parent feels as rejection and renames it: this is observation and independent judgment arriving on schedule. Notice also how she disarms the defence before it can be raised, granting in advance that the parents may be perfectly good ones and perfectly decent people besides, so that no reader can answer 'but I am a good parent'. If you ever have to tell someone they are making things worse, that is the order to use: grant that they are not bad, describe the sequence, rename the event, and only then advise.

这一段是一条长长的因果链，每一环都是一个动词：孩子称赞，父母不悦，一句刻薄话跟上，孩子震惊，孩子决定，孩子沉默，父母抱怨。全篇没有一句断定谁的品性如何，全是顺序；读者自己会发现，每一步都很小、都挺合理，而尽头是一道墙。接着是让这篇文章真正有用的一招：她把父母体验为「被嫌弃」的那件事重新命名——这是观察力和独立判断按时到场。还要注意她怎样先把对方的辩词拆掉：无论他们作为父母还是作为个人有多好——于是没有读者能回一句「可我是好家长」。如果你哪天必须告诉某人「你正在把事情弄坏」，顺序就该这样：先承认对方不坏，再描述顺序，再给事件改名，最后才给建议。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The mechanism has held up and the calendar has not. Punishing a child for an honest remark teaches the child to conceal, and that is now ordinary findings rather than one doctor's opinion; so is her distinction between the parent who demands obedience and allows no argument and the parent who is warm, firm and willing to explain himself - the second produces better outcomes on most measures, and the vocabulary she uses for the first is the vocabulary that research still uses. One caution belongs with that, and it matters here: most of those findings come from Western samples, and reviewers who look at Asian, Arab, African American and Latin American families keep finding that a firmer, more directive style can go with good outcomes there, so the ranking is not a law of nature. What has moved is the length of the journey. She writes as though it ends when school does. A much-cited 2018 argument in a Lancet paediatrics journal proposes that adolescence now runs from about 10 to 24, because puberty begins earlier than it did and the social markers - first job, leaving home, marriage - arrive later, with brain maturation continuing into the twenties. That adds a decade to her book and changes the subject of the negotiation: not whether a fifteen-year-old may stay out, but what authority a parent has over a twenty-three-year-old who lives at home. Her closing judgment has also aged in an interesting way. In 1957 she thought her own time had swung to the opposite extreme and called that healthier. Seventy years later the complaint has reversed again: the worry is no longer the silent father but the parent who never stops managing, with the phone as the leash. For Chinese families abroad there is a harder version of her problem. Disillusionment arrives bundled with language. A child may outrun its parents in English within two years, and the parents' authority can be contradicted by a school letter the child has to translate for them. Admitting you were wrong is one thing; admitting it to someone who is also your interpreter is another, and it is exactly where her advice costs most and is worth most.

机制站住了，时间表没站住。为一句老实话惩罚孩子，教会的是隐瞒——这在今天是普通的研究结论，不再是某位医生的一家之言；她对两类父母的区分也是：一类要求服从、不许争辩，一类温和、有要求、愿意解释自己——后者在多数指标上结果更好，而她用来称呼前者的那个词，正是研究至今在用的词。这里要加一条告诫，而且对本文读者尤其相关：这些结论多半出自西方样本，而研究亚裔、阿拉伯、非裔美国与拉美家庭的综述反复发现，在那些家庭里更严、更指令式的方式也常与好结果同行——所以这个排序不是自然法则。变了的是这段旅程的长度。她写得像是学校读完就结束。2018 年一份被广泛引用的柳叶刀系儿科期刊文章主张，青春期如今大致从 10 岁延伸到 24 岁：发育开始得比过去早，而社会标记——第一份工作、搬出去住、结婚——来得更晚，大脑成熟一直持续到二十几岁。这给她那本书加了十年，也换掉了谈判的题目：不再是十五岁能不能晚归，而是一个二十三岁还住在家里的人，父母有多大权力。她最后那句判断老去的方式也很有意思：1957 年她认为自己的时代已经摆到了另一个极端，并说这样更健康；七十年后抱怨又反过来了——今天忧虑的不是沉默的父亲，而是永不停手的管理者，手机就是那根绳。对海外华人家庭，她的难题有一个更难的版本：幻灭是和语言捆在一起来的。孩子可能两年内英语就跑到父母前面，而父母的权威会被一封需要孩子翻译的学校来信当场推翻。承认自己错了是一件事；向一个同时是你翻译的人承认，是另一件事——而这恰恰是她的建议代价最大、也最值钱的地方。

💬 Discuss and write讨论与写作
  1. The lesson says children cannot forgive a parent who denies what the child knows to be true. Think of a time an adult in your life did admit a mistake to you. What changed afterwards, and what would have happened if they had not?
    课文说，孩子不能原谅明知有错却不承认的父母。想一次你身边的大人真的对你认了错，之后有什么变化？要是他没认，又会怎样？
  2. If adolescence now stretches to about 24, write 120 words on where you think a parent's say should end, and on what grounds - money, the roof, or nothing at all.
    如果青春期现在延伸到约 24 岁，写 120 词：你认为父母说话的分量应该到哪里为止？依据是什么——钱、住处，还是什么都不算。
  3. She thought the danger of her time was the father who would not come down from his authority. Name the danger of ours, and say what a parent would have to give up to avoid it.
    她认为她那个时代的危险是不肯从权威上下来的父亲。说出我们这个时代的危险，并说清父母要放弃什么才能避开它。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 1第 1 句 (begins开头 “Parents are often …”)

  • Parents carries two verbs in parallel, are often upset and are foolish enough to ...; the and regard it as ... in between still belongs to the when clause, with parents as its subject.主语 Parents 带两个并列谓语：are often upset … 和 are foolish enough to …；中间的 and regard it as … 属于 when 从句（主语还是 parents）。
  • be foolish enough to do: foolish to the point of doing it.be foolish enough to do：愚蠢到……的地步。

Sentence 4第 4 句 (begins开头 “Such a loss …”)

  • The subject is two abstract nouns joined by and, loss ... and descent ..., but they are treated as one thing, so the verbs are singular.主语是两个并列抽象名词 loss … and descent …，但被看作一件事，谓语用单数 shocks / makes。
  • make somebody resolve that ...: after make, the infinitive drops its to.make sb resolve that …：make 后的不定式不带 to。

Sentence 6第 6 句 (begins开头 “Before very long …”)

  • will be complaining: the future continuous, picturing the parents going on and on about it when the time comes.will be complaining：将来进行时，描绘「到时候会一直抱怨」的情景。
  • bring something on oneself: to have asked for it.bring sth on oneself：自作自受。

Sentence 9第 9 句 (begins开头 “however good and …”)

  • Between the subject and its verb sits a concessive clause, however ... may be: no matter how good they are.主语 disillusionment 与谓语 is 之间插入让步从句 however … may be（无论多好）。

Sentence 11第 11 句 (begins开头 “unless the parents …”)

  • The it refers to the high ideal; can hardly hope to means it has almost no chance of.it 指 the high ideal；can hardly hope to：几乎不可能。

Sentence 21第 21 句 (begins开头 “What the child …”)

  • What ... is ...: a subject clause (see Lesson 25).What … is …：主语从句（L25 讲过）。
  • know somebody or something to be ...: an object with its complement, the same as knows that they are true.know sb/sth to be …：复合宾语，= knows that they are true。
  • these charges points back to the four words before it: wrong, ignorant, unfair, unjust.these charges 指上文的 wrong, ignorant, unfair, unjust。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

such ... that and so ... thatsuch … that 与 so … that
Both mean 'so ... that'. such goes with a noun (such + a/an + adjective + singular noun, or such + adjective + plural or uncountable noun), while so goes with an adjective or an adverb.
都表「如此……以至于」：such 修饰名词（such + a/an + adj. + 单数名词 / such + adj. + 复数或不可数名词），so 修饰形容词或副词（so + adj./adv.）。

This lesson uses such ... that: the ideal a child holds is set so high that reality can hardly live up to it.

本课用的是 such … that：孩子心里那个理想化的形象「高」到几乎经不起现实的检验。

The two can swap: such a high ideal = so high an ideal (with so the article moves).

可以互换的结构：such a high ideal ＝ so high an ideal（so + adj. + a + 名词，冠词位置变了）。

Before many, much, few and little, use so: so many people, so little time (not such many); but such a lot of.

many/much/few/little 前用 so：so many people, so little time（✗ such many）；但 such a lot of。

Lesson 1 covered so ... that; here the point is such, and how to turn one into the other.

L1 讲过 so … that；本课重点在 such 和两者的转换。

  • It was such a hot day that we stayed indoors.
  • It was so hot a day that we stayed indoors.
  • There were so many people that we couldn't get in.
Object plus complement: know / believe / consider somebody to be ...复合宾语：know / believe / consider sb to be …
Verbs of knowing and judging can take an object followed by to be and a complement, which says the same as a that clause but sounds more formal.
表「认为、知道、发现」的动词可接「宾语 + to be + 补语」，相当于一个 that 从句；结构较正式。

In this lesson: knows them to be true is the formal equivalent of knows that they are true.

本课：knows them to be true（＝ knows that they are true）。

The common verbs are believe, consider, find, know, suppose and prove; think is rarely used this way.

常见动词：believe, consider, find, know, suppose, prove, think（think 少用于此结构）。

The passive is commoner: He is believed to be honest. / The story was proved to be false.

被动形式更常见：He is believed to be honest.／ The story was proved to be false.

After consider and find, the to be is often left out: I consider him (to be) a fool.

consider/find 后的 to be 常可省：I consider him (to be) a fool.

  • We believe him to be the best candidate.
  • The ring was found to be a fake.
adjective or adverb + enough to do, and too ... to doadj./adv. + enough to do 与 too … to do
enough goes after an adjective or adverb and before a noun: old enough to vote, enough money to buy. too + adjective + to do means 'too ... for that to be possible'.
enough 放在形容词/副词之后、名词之前：old enough to vote / enough money to buy；too + adj. + to do 表「太……而不能」。

One of them in this lesson: foolish enough to let ... — foolish to the point of letting the child see the annoyance.

本课一处：foolish enough to let …（愚蠢到让孩子看出来自己生气了）。

And the other: too cowed to let ... — too frightened to let the parents know how they felt. The lesson happens to carry both patterns.

另一处：too cowed to let …（太胆怯，不敢让父母知道自己真实的感受）——同一课里两个结构正好成对。

Note that too ... to carries its own negative; but only too and all too + adjective mean 'very' (see only too ready in Lesson 28).

注意：too … to 自带否定；但 only too / all too + adj. 是「非常」（L28 讲过 only too ready）。

  • She is old enough to travel alone.
  • He was too tired to walk any further.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • be foolish enough to … 愚蠢到竟然……
    He was foolish enough to lend her all his savings.
  • regard sth as a slur on … 把……看作对……的诋毁
    She regarded his remark as a slur on her family.
  • have brought this on oneself 这是自作自受
    Don't complain — you've brought this on yourself.
  • … is to some degree inevitable ……在某种程度上不可避免
    Some conflict between generations is to some degree inevitable.
  • It is always wiser to face up to … 勇敢面对……总是更明智的
    It is always wiser to face up to your mistakes.

Summary writing摘要写作

Write no more than 80 words: how should parents handle an adolescent's criticism and loss of illusions, and why?

用不超过 80 个词概括：父母应该怎样对待青春期孩子对他们的批评与幻灭？为什么？

Points a good summary covers好摘要要覆盖的要点
  • Parents often take praise of another home badly and answer with a sneer; the child is shocked and stops telling them anything that matters.父母常因孩子称赞别家而生气、讽刺，这会让孩子震惊，从此不再跟父母说心里话。
  • The ideal picture a child holds of its parents cannot survive a realistic look, so some disillusionment is bound to come.孩子对父母的理想化形象经不起现实检验，幻灭在某种程度上不可避免。
  • It is a sign of growth and of a judgment forming; parents who expect it are not hurt by it and do not push the child into opposition.这是孩子成长、形成独立判断的标志；父母若有准备就不会受伤或逼孩子对抗。
  • Children respect a parent who owns up to a mistake and cannot forgive one who denies what the child knows to be true; facing facts is always the wiser course.孩子尊重肯认错的父母，不能原谅明知有错却不承认；面对现实总是更明智。
Our model summary本站范文

When adolescents praise other homes, parents often take offence and show it, which shocks children into secrecy. Since children idealize their parents, some disillusionment is inevitable, but it is a sign that they are growing up and judging for themselves. Parents who understand this will not feel hurt or provoke opposition. Teenagers respect parents who admit their faults and cannot forgive those who deny them, so facing reality honestly is always the wiser course.（74 词）
