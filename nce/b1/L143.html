Lesson 143–144 · A Walk Through the Woods 穿过森林

📘 New Concept English · Book 1 · Lesson 143–144 “A Walk Through the Woods”新概念英语 第一册 Lesson 143–144《A Walk Through the Woods》

B1🧠 22 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
The writer lives in an old town ringed by lovely woods, a well-known place for day trips. Every Sunday crowds arrive from the city to look round the town and stroll among the trees. Visitors have long been told to keep the place neat, and baskets for rubbish stand under the trees, yet people still drop their waste anywhere. Last Wednesday the writer went walking there and came home upset. There were seven dead cars and three old fridges; the baskets held nothing, while paper, cigarette butts, tyres, bottles and rusty cans lay all around. In the middle of the mess lay a sign warning that litter louts would face the law. This is a short piece of writing with an ironic ending that teaches the present perfect passive, be covered with, a what-clause as subject, make someone feel something, and anyone who.
讲述人住在一座被美丽森林环绕的古老小镇，那里是有名的风景区，每逢星期天都有成百上千的城里人来逛镇子、穿林子散步。游客早被要求保持森林整洁，树下也放了垃圾篮，可人们还是随处乱扔。上星期三他去林中散步，看到的情景让他很难过：七辆旧汽车、三台旧冰箱，垃圾篮空空如也，地上满是纸片、烟头、旧轮胎、空瓶子和锈罐头。就在垃圾堆里，他发现一块牌子，上面写着：「凡在林中乱扔垃圾者，将被起诉！」本课是一篇带讽刺结尾的短叙述，学现在完成时的被动 have been asked / have been placed、be covered with、What I saw 作主语、make sb + 形容词 与 anyone who …。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. Why do crowds visit the town every Sunday?小镇为什么每个星期天都有很多游客？
  2. What did the writer find among the trees?讲述人在林子里看见了什么？
  3. Why is the sign so ironic?那块牌子为什么显得可笑？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander; New Concept English, Book 1, Longman, 1967. This is the final lesson of Book One; the town and the woods are not named and seem to be invented.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为教材自撰，见《新概念英语》第一册（朗文，1967），是第一册的最后一课。小镇和森林都没有名字，看来是虚构的。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

In the 1960s car ownership in Britain was growing fast, and day trips from the cities to the countryside became a Sunday habit for many families. Litter was a growing public worry, and the Keep Britain Tidy campaign had begun in 1955. Dumping old cars and fridges in the countryside was a real problem, because getting rid of them properly cost money; laws against dumping cars came only later, with the Civic Amenities Act of 1967.

1960 年代英国拥有汽车的家庭快速增加，从城里开车到乡下一日游成了很多家庭的周日习惯。乱扔垃圾日益成为公众担心的问题，「让英国保持整洁」运动在 1955 年就开始了。往乡下扔旧汽车和旧冰箱是真问题，因为正规处理要花钱；禁止丢弃废车的法律要到后来才有，即 1967 年的《市政设施法》。

✨ How it is written赏析

The text moves from praise to disappointment to irony in eleven sentences. It begins like a tourist brochure, beauty spot and lovely woods, then shows the rules and the baskets, which raise our hopes. The long list in sentence 12 piles up the rubbish so that the reader feels the weight of it, and the last sentence places the warning sign itself among the litter: the rule has become part of the mess it was meant to stop. It is a gentle ending for a book of lessons, with a small moral left for the reader to draw.

这篇短文在十一句里从赞美走到失望，再走到讽刺。开头像旅游手册——风景区、美丽的森林——接着写规定和垃圾篮，让我们抱有希望。第 12 句的一长串清单把垃圾一件件堆上来，让读者感到它的分量；最后一句把那块警告牌本身放进垃圾堆：规定成了它本想制止的那堆乱象的一部分。作为一册书的结尾，这样的收束很温和，把一点小小的寓意留给读者自己去想。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

Canadians who love trails will recognise both sides of this story: popular parks fill up on weekends, and many have a pack it in, pack it out rule, with no bins at all, because bins attract bears and overflow. Illegal dumping still happens; in British Columbia most cities offer free or low-cost drop-off days for old appliances and mattresses, which is the practical answer to the fridges in the woods. For learners, signs are a quick way to see the passive in real life: look for will be fined, will be towed and are prohibited the next time you are out.

爱走步道的加拿大人能认出这个故事的两面：热门公园周末人满为患，很多公园实行「带进来什么就带出去什么」的规定，根本不设垃圾桶，因为垃圾桶会招来熊，还容易满溢。非法倾倒至今仍有；卑诗省多数城市提供免费或低价的旧电器、旧床垫回收日，这就是对林中旧冰箱的实际回应。对学习者来说，标牌是在现实中看被动语态的捷径：下次出门留意 will be fined、will be towed 和 are prohibited。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

The passive in different tenses被动语态的几种时态
The passive is a form of be plus the past participle, and the tense is carried by be: is or are done, was or were done, has or have been done, will be done. The present perfect passive stresses that something has already been done by now.
被动语态 = be 的某种形式 + 过去分词，时态由 be 决定：一般现在 is/are done，一般过去 was/were done，现在完成 has/have been done，将来 will be done。现在完成被动强调「现在已经被做了」。

Sentence 3 has is surrounded, sentences 6 and 8 have been asked and placed, sentence 12 was covered, and sentence 13 will be prosecuted.

本课：第 3 句 is surrounded，第 7、8 句 have been asked / placed，第 12 句 was covered，第 13 句 will be prosecuted。

  • The road has been closed because of the snow.
  • The winners will be announced on Friday.
A what-clause as subjectWhat I saw … 作主语
what plus a subject and verb can work as a noun, meaning the thing that: What I saw, What she said, What you need. As a subject it usually takes a singular verb.
what + 主语 + 动词 可以整体当名词用，意思是「……的东西」：What I saw、What she said、What you need。它作主语时，谓语一般用单数。

In sentence 9, What I saw is the subject.

本课：第 9 句 What I saw 作主语。

  • What she told me was a surprise.
  • What you need is a good night's sleep.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • … is surrounded by … ……被……环绕。
    The farm is surrounded by mountains.
  • People have been asked to … 人们已经被要求……
    People have been asked to save water.
  • What I heard made me … 我听到的事让我……
    What I heard made me very angry.
  • The floor was covered with … 地上满是……
    The floor was covered with toys.
  • Anyone who … will be … 凡……者将被……
    Anyone who parks here will be fined.

Our model summary本站范文

The writer lives in an old town ringed by lovely woods, and every Sunday crowds come from the city to walk there. Visitors are asked to keep the place clean, and bins stand under the trees, yet people still drop waste anywhere. Last Wednesday he found seven dead cars and three fridges. The bins were empty, but rubbish lay everywhere. In the middle of it was a sign warning that litterers would face the law.（75 词）
