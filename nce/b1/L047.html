Lesson 47–48 · A Cup of Coffee 一杯咖啡

📘 New Concept English · Book 1 · Lesson 47–48 “A Cup of Coffee”新概念英语 第一册 Lesson 47–48《A Cup of Coffee》

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
Christine asks Ann whether she enjoys coffee and would like some. Ann says yes to both; she takes sugar but no milk, because she prefers it black. Christine then asks whether Ann enjoys biscuits and offers her one, and Ann accepts. This lesson is questions and answers when you have a guest. It teaches Do you ...? questions in the present simple with the short answer Yes, I do, the difference between like and want, any in questions, and how to accept and refuse with Yes, please and No, thank you.
克里斯廷问安喜不喜欢咖啡、要不要来一杯。安说喜欢，要一杯；要糖，不要牛奶——她不喜欢咖啡里加奶，喜欢喝黑咖啡。克里斯廷又问她喜不喜欢饼干、要不要一块，安说要。本课是待客时的一问一答，学一般现在时的 Do you …? 问句与简略回答 Yes, I do.、like 和 want 的区别、疑问句里的 any，以及 Yes, please. / No, thank you. 怎么接受和谢绝。

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander; New Concept English, Book 1, Longman, 1967.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为教材自撰，见《新概念英语》第一册（朗文，1967）。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

This is the lesson where Book 1 turns to the present simple with do, after many lessons built on be and can. The setting, one woman offering another a hot drink and a biscuit, was the most ordinary social exchange in a British home, and the questions follow the real order of the ritual: first the drink, then what goes in it, then something to eat with it. In the 1960s instant coffee was spreading fast in Britain, and taking it black was less usual than with milk, which is why Ann has to explain her choice.

第一册在用 be 和 can 撑了很多课之后，从这一课转向带 do 的一般现在时。场景是一位女士请另一位喝杯热饮、吃块饼干，这是英国家庭里最普通的社交往来；问题的顺序也照着真实的待客礼数：先是饮料，再问里面加什么，再配点吃的。1960 年代速溶咖啡在英国迅速普及，而当时喝咖啡加奶比不加奶常见，所以安要解释一下自己的选择。

✨ How it is written赏析

The dialogue is a pattern drill dressed as hospitality. Almost every question has the same shape, so the learner hears Do you ...? six times and answers with do until it becomes automatic. The writer keeps it from being boring by giving Ann one real opinion, the only full sentences she says, about how she likes her coffee. That small personal touch is placed exactly in the middle, between the two halves of yes and no, and it turns a list of answers into a person with tastes.

这段对话是一套句型操练，外面套着待客的衣服。几乎每个问题都是同一个形状，读者听六遍 Do you ...?、用 do 回答，直到成为条件反射。作者让它不显得枯燥的办法，是给安一个真实的个人看法——她全场仅有的两句完整句子，讲她喜欢怎样喝咖啡。这一点个人色彩恰好放在正中间，在「要」与「不要」两半之间，把一串回答变成了一个有口味的人。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The grammar and the offers are exactly what Canadians say today, though a host would more often ask Would you like a coffee? than a plain Do you want one?, which can sound blunt to a guest. Learners should notice the asymmetry in polite replies: yes goes with please and no goes with thank you, and getting them backwards is a common slip. In Canada biscuit is understood, but most people say cookie for a sweet one; a biscuit in North America is usually a soft bread roll, which surprises many newcomers.

语法和待客的说法今天加拿大人照样在用，只是主人更常问 Would you like a coffee?，而不是 Do you want one?，后者对客人可能显得直白。读者要注意礼貌回答里的不对称：接受配 please，谢绝配 thank you，弄反是常见的口误。在加拿大人们听得懂 biscuit，但甜饼干多数人说 cookie；北美的 biscuit 通常指一种松软的小面包，很多新移民第一次都会吃惊。

💬 Discuss and write讨论与写作

    和同伴表演招待客人：给对方三样东西，用 Would you like ...? 提问，并用正确的接受或谢绝来回答。
  2. Write five sentences about how you like your tea or coffee, using like, don't like and want.
    写五句话说你喜欢怎样喝茶或咖啡，用上 like、don't like 和 want。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 3第 3 句 (begins开头 “Do you like …”)

  • A question in the present simple: Do, then the subject, then the plain verb. The short answer uses do: Yes, I do or No, I don't, never Yes, I like.一般现在时的问句：Do + 主语 + 动词原形？简答用 do：Yes, I do. / No, I don't.，不说 Yes, I like。

Sentence 5第 5 句 (begins开头 “Do you want …”)

  • like asks about her general taste; want asks whether she would like one now.like 问「平时喜不喜欢」，want 问「现在要不要」。
  • To accept, Yes, please is the usual answer.接受用 Yes, please.，不说 Yes, thank you（后者不错，但 Yes, please 更常见）。

Sentence 9第 9 句 (begins开头 “Do you want …”)

  • any in a question; No, thank you to refuse politely.疑问句里用 any；谢绝用 No, thank you.。

Sentence 11第 11 句 (begins开头 “I don't like …”)

  • The negative is don't with the plain verb.否定：don't + 动词原形。
  • black coffee has no milk; in Britain coffee with milk is called white coffee.black coffee：不加奶的咖啡，与 white coffee（加奶）相对。

Sentence 15第 15 句 (begins开头 “Do you want …”)

  • one stands for a biscuit, so the noun is not said again.one 代替 a biscuit，避免重复。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

The present simple: Do you ...? and short answers一般现在时：Do you …? 与简略回答
With ordinary verbs, the present simple question is Do, or Does for he, she and it, then the subject and the plain verb. The short answers are Yes, I do and No, I don't. The negative is don't with the plain verb.
普通动词的一般现在时问句用 Do（第三人称单数用 Does）+ 主语 + 动词原形；简答 Yes, I do. / No, I don't.；否定 I don't + 动词原形。

Sentences 3, 6, 8, 10, 14 and 16 all begin with Do you, and sentence 11 is the negative.

本课：第 4、6、8、10、14、16 句都是 Do you …?；第 11 句是否定。

  • Do you eat meat? — No, I don't.
  • Does your brother like football? — Yes, he does.
like and want; Yes, please and No, thank youlike 与 want；Yes, please / No, thank you
like is about what you enjoy in general; want is about what you wish to have now. To accept an offer say Yes, please; to refuse say No, thank you.
like 说平时的喜好，want 说此刻想要；回答别人的提议，接受用 Yes, please.，谢绝用 No, thank you.。

First comes the question about taste, then the offer; sentence 6 accepts and sentence 10 refuses.

本课：先问喜不喜欢咖啡，再问要不要一杯；第 6 句接受，第 10 句谢绝。

  • I like cake, but I don't want any now. I'm full.
  • More tea? — Yes, please. / No, thank you.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • Do you like …? — Yes, I do. 你喜欢……吗？——喜欢。
    Do you like jazz? — Yes, I do.
  • Do you want a …? — Yes, please. 你要一……吗？——好的，谢谢。
    Do you want a sandwich? — Yes, please.
  • Do you want any …? — No, thank you. 你要……吗？——不用了，谢谢。
    Do you want any ice? — No, thank you.
  • I don't like … in my …. 我不喜欢在……里放……
    I don't like ice in my drink.
  • I like … black. 我喜欢不加奶的……
    I like my tea black.
