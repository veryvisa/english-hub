Lesson 31–32 · Where's Sally 萨莉在哪里？

📘 New Concept English · Book 1 · Lesson 31–32 “Where's Sally”新概念英语 第一册 Lesson 31–32《Where's Sally》

B1🧠 15 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
Jean asks Jack where Sally is. He tells her Sally is out in the garden, sitting beneath a tree, and that Tim is there as well, on his way up into its branches. Jean misses this and has to ask again who it is. Last comes the dog: it is out there too, dashing over the lawn behind a cat. This lesson is questions and answers about what is happening right now. The grammar is questions in the present continuous (What's she doing? and Who's ...?), short answers such as Yes, he is and Tim is, the spelling of verbs with -ing, and three prepositions: under, across and after.
琼问杰克萨莉在哪儿。杰克说她在花园里，坐在树下；蒂姆也在，正往树上爬。琼没听清，又问了一遍是谁在爬树。最后问到狗：狗也在花园里，正穿过草地去追一只猫。本课是一问一答描述眼前正在发生的事，学现在进行时的问句（What's she doing? / Who's …?）、简略回答（Yes, he is. / Tim is.）、动词加 -ing 的拼写，以及 under、across、after 三个介词。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. What are Sally, Tim and the dog each doing?萨莉、蒂姆和狗各自在做什么？
  2. Why does Jean say I beg your pardon?琼为什么要说 I beg your pardon?
  3. In sentence 17, what does run after mean?第 17 句的 run after 是什么意思？

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander; New Concept English, Book 1, Longman, 1967.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为教材自撰，见《新概念英语》第一册（朗文，1967）。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

This is the first lesson in Book 1 to use the present continuous for something going on outside the room, and the picture does most of the work: the learner looks at a garden scene and the dialogue names what each figure is doing. Book 1 runs in pairs, so the lesson after it drills the same tense with many verbs and many people. The family here, Sally, Tim and their parents, reappears through the middle of the book, which lets later lessons reuse the scene and the names without fresh explanation.

这是第一册里第一次用现在进行时描述屋外正在发生的事，插图承担了大部分工作：读者看着一幅花园图，对话一个一个说出图里每个人在做什么。第一册是成对编排的，所以后一课用很多动词、很多人物把同一个时态练个遍。这里的一家人——萨莉、蒂姆和他们的父母——在全书中段反复出现，后面的课可以直接沿用场景和名字，不用重新交代。

✨ How it is written赏析

The dialogue is a guessing game with a picture. One speaker can see the garden and the other cannot, so every question is a real question and every answer gives new information. The single misunderstanding in the middle, where Jean has to ask again, is there on purpose: it forces the question with who as subject, which learners find hard, and it shows the short answer in action. The lesson ends on movement, the dog dashing after something, which gives the scene a little life and leaves the learner wanting to know whether the cat gets away.

这段对话像是对着一幅图玩猜谜：一个人看得见花园，另一个看不见，所以每个问题都是真问题，每个回答都带来新信息。中间唯一的误会——琼要再问一遍——是有意安排的：它逼出了 who 作主语的问句，这恰恰是读者觉得难的地方，同时示范了简略回答怎么用。全课收在一个动作上：狗冲出去追东西，让画面活了起来，读者也会想知道那只猫跑掉没有。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The grammar here is exactly what English speakers use today, and the short answers are worth copying closely: Chinese speakers often answer Yes or the whole sentence again, and the short form with the auxiliary, Yes, he is or Tim is, sounds far more natural. One thing has shifted. I beg your pardon is still polite, but in everyday speech in Canada most people say Sorry? or Pardon?, and I beg your pardon with a falling voice can sound like offence has been taken. Learners should know all three and use Sorry? in daily life.

这里的语法就是今天英语母语者在用的，简略回答尤其值得照着学：中国读者常常只答 Yes，或者把整句话再说一遍，而带助动词的短答——Yes, he is、Tim is——听起来自然得多。有一点变了：I beg your pardon 仍然礼貌，但在加拿大的日常口语里，多数人说 Sorry? 或 Pardon?；而用降调说 I beg your pardon，听起来像是觉得被冒犯了。三种说法都要认得，日常生活里用 Sorry? 就好。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 6第 6 句 (begins开头 “What's she doing? …”)

What's she doing?

  • A question in the present continuous: question word, then be, then the subject, then the -ing form. What's is short for What is.现在进行时的特殊疑问句：疑问词 + be + 主语 + 动词 -ing？What's = What is。
  • It asks about this moment, so the answer is in the same tense: She's sitting ...问的是「此刻在做什么」，所以答句也用进行时：She's sitting …。

Sentence 11第 11 句 (begins开头 “I beg your …”)

I beg your pardon?

  • Said with a rising voice, it politely asks someone to repeat what they said. In speech many people simply say Pardon? or Sorry?没听清时请对方重复，用升调，很客气；口语里也可只说 Pardon? 或 Sorry?。

Sentence 12第 12 句 (begins开头 “Who's climbing the …”)

Who's climbing the tree?

  • Here who is itself the subject, so no other subject is added.who 本身就是主语，所以不用再加主语：Who is climbing …?，不说 Who is he climbing。
  • The short answer keeps only the subject and be. Saying the whole thing again is possible but sounds heavy.简略回答只留主语 + be：Tim is.，不说 Tim is climbing the tree 也可以，但更自然的是短答。

Sentence 17第 17 句 (begins开头 “It's running after …”)

It's running after a cat.

  • run after means to chase: after here means following in order to catch.run after = 追赶；after 在这里表示「跟在后面想抓住」。
  • run doubles its n before -ing: running.run 加 -ing 要双写 n：running。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

Questions and short answers in the present continuous现在进行时的问句与简略回答
For a yes/no question put be before the subject, and answer Yes, he is or No, he isn't. For a wh- question use What, Where or Who, then be. When who is the subject it goes straight on to is and the -ing form.
一般疑问句把 be 提到主语前：Is Tim in the garden? Is he climbing …?；回答 Yes, he is. / No, he isn't.。特殊疑问句：What/Where/Who + be + …？who 作主语时直接接 is + -ing。

Sentence 6 asks what she is doing and sentence 12 asks who is climbing; sentences 9 and 13 are both short answers.

本课：第 6 句问「她在做什么」，第 12 句问「是谁在爬」，第 9 句与第 13 句都是简略回答。

  • Is your mother cooking? — Yes, she is.
  • Who's singing in the kitchen? — My sister is.
Spelling the -ing form动词 -ing 的拼写
Most verbs simply add -ing, as in climbing. A silent e is dropped first, as in writing. A short verb ending in one vowel and one consonant doubles the consonant: sitting, running.
一般直接加 -ing：climb → climbing；不发音的 e 去掉再加：write → writing；重读闭音节、一个元音字母 + 一个辅音字母结尾的，双写末尾辅音：sit → sitting，run → running。

Sentence 7 has sitting and sentence 16 has running, both doubled, while sentence 10 has climbing with nothing changed.

本课：第 7 句 sitting、第 16 句 running 双写，第 10 句 climbing 直接加。

  • She is swimming in the lake.
  • They are making a cake.
The prepositions under, across and after介词 under、across、after
under gives a position below something; across means from one side to the other, often with run, walk or swim; after with run means to chase.
under 表示位置在下面；across 表示从一边到另一边（常和 run、walk、swim 连用）；after 和 run 连用表示追赶。

Sally sits beneath the tree in sentence 7; the dog crosses the lawn in sentence 16 and chases the cat in sentence 17.

本课：萨莉坐在树下（第 7 句），狗跑过草地（第 16 句）、去追猫（第 17 句）。

  • The ball is under the car.
  • A fox is running across the field.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • Where's …? — She's in the …. ……在哪儿？——她在……里。
    Where's Grandma? — She's in the kitchen.
  • What's … doing? — She's …ing. ……在做什么？——她在……。
    What's Ben doing? — He's reading.
  • Who's …ing? — … is. 谁在……？——……在。
    Who's knocking? — The postman is.
  • What about the …? ……呢？（问另一个人或物）
    What about your sister? Is she coming?
  • It's running after a …. 它在追一只……
    The puppy is running after a ball.
