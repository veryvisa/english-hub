Lesson 11–12 · Is This Your Shirt 这是你的衬衫吗？

📘 New Concept English · Book 1 · Lesson 11–12 “Is This Your Shirt”新概念英语 第一册 Lesson 11–12《Is This Your Shirt》

B1🧠 16 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
A teacher holds up a shirt and asks who it belongs to. Dave says it is not his, because his is blue. The teacher wonders whether it is Tim's, and Dave says perhaps, as Tim's is white. Tim is called over, it is indeed his, and the teacher throws it to him with a quick Catch! This lesson is one round of claiming lost property: asking whose it is, saying no and describing your own, guessing, confirming, and handing it back. The grammar is the question word whose, the possessive 's, my, your, his and her, and how to tell when 's means is and when it means belonging to.
老师手里拿着一件衬衫，问是谁的。戴夫说不是他的，他的是蓝色的；老师问会不会是蒂姆的，戴夫说也许是，蒂姆的衬衫是白的。老师叫来蒂姆，果然是他的，说一声「接住！」把衬衫扔给了他。本课是认领失物的一轮问答：问是谁的、否认并说明自己的、猜测、确认、交还。学 Whose …? 问所属、名词所有格 's、物主代词 my/your/his/her，以及 's 到底是 is 还是「的」。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. Whose shirt is white?谁的衬衫是白色的？
  2. How does Dave show the shirt is not his?戴夫怎么证明那件衬衫不是他的？
  3. In sentence 12, do the two 's mean the same thing?第 12 句里两个 's 意思一样吗？

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

The picture in the book shows a teacher and two boys, and the shirt has most likely been left behind after a games lesson. In British schools of the time a male teacher was called sir by his pupils, which is why every reply ends in that word. The drill lesson that follows brings in family words such as father, mother, brother and sister, so that the learner can use his and her about real people.

书里的插图是一位老师和两个男孩，衬衫大概是体育课后落下的。那个年代的英国学校里，学生称男老师为 sir，所以每句回答都以这个词收尾。后面那课操练加了父亲、母亲、兄弟、姐妹这些家庭称谓，让读者能拿 his 和 her 说真实的人。

✨ How it is written赏析

The small puzzle at the centre of the lesson is a matter of evidence. Dave cannot simply say it is not his; he gives a reason, the colour of his own, and then a second piece of evidence, the colour of his friend's. The teacher acts on the guess and checks it with the owner. It is a tiny model of how to settle a question: claim, reason, check. And the same short sound, 's, does two jobs in one line, which makes this lesson the right place to learn to tell them apart.

课中心那个小谜题其实是证据问题。戴夫不只说「不是我的」，他给出理由——自己那件的颜色，又给出第二条线索——朋友那件的颜色。老师根据这个猜测去找本人核实。这是解决问题的一个小模型：说法、理由、核实。同一个短短的 's 在一行里干了两件事，所以这一课正是学会分辨它们的地方。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

One habit Chinese students bring into English classrooms is calling the teacher Teacher, as in Good morning, Teacher. In English this sounds odd. British pupils say sir or miss; in Canada students usually say Mr, Mrs or Ms with the family name, and at university many lecturers are happy to be called by their first name. The respect is carried by tone and manners, not by the job title. Lost property is handled differently too: most Canadian schools keep a lost and found box, and writing your name on the label inside your clothes saves a lot of questions like the ones in this lesson.

中国学生常把一个习惯带进英语课堂：叫老师 Teacher，比如 Good morning, Teacher。在英语里这听起来很怪。英国学生说 sir 或 miss；加拿大学生通常用 Mr、Mrs 或 Ms 加姓，大学里不少老师也乐意被直呼名字。尊重靠语气和礼貌体现，不靠职业称呼。失物的处理也不同：加拿大多数学校有失物招领箱；在衣服里的标签写上名字，能省掉很多本课这样的问答。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 4第 4 句 (begins开头 “Whose shirt is …”)

Whose shirt is that?

  • Whose with a noun at the front asks who owns something. You can also say Whose is that shirt?Whose + 名词放句首，问「……是谁的」。也可说 Whose is that shirt?。
  • whose sounds the same as who's, but who's means who is.whose 和 who's 读音相同，意思不同：who's = who is。

Sentence 9第 9 句 (begins开头 “My shirt's blue. …”)

My shirt's blue.

  • Here 's is short for is: My shirt is blue.这里的 's 是 is 的缩写：My shirt is blue.。
  • Dave shows the shirt is not his by giving the colour of his own.戴夫用说出自己衬衫的颜色来证明那件不是他的。

Sentence 10第 10 句 (begins开头 “Is this shirt …”)

Is this shirt Tim's?

  • Tim's here is the possessive, meaning Tim's one; the word shirt is left out after it.Tim's 这里是名词所有格「蒂姆的（东西）」，后面省略了 shirt。
  • A name with 's shows who owns something: Tim's shirt, Anna's hat.人名 + 's 表示所属：Tim's shirt、Anna's hat。

Sentence 12第 12 句 (begins开头 “Tim's shirt's white. …”)

Tim's shirt's white.

  • There are two 's here. The first means belonging to Tim; the second is short for is. Look at what follows: a noun after it means possession, while an adjective after it usually means is.一句里两个 's：第一个是所有格「蒂姆的」，第二个是 is 的缩写。看位置：名词后接名词是所有格，后接形容词常是 is。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

Whose ...? and the possessive 'sWhose …? 与名词所有格 's
To ask who owns something, say Whose plus the noun, then is this or is that. Answer with a name and 's: It's Tim's, or It's Tim's shirt.
问东西是谁的：Whose + 名词 + is this/that?；回答用 人名 + 's：It's Tim's (shirt).。

Sentence 4 asks with whose, and sentences 10 and 12 use Tim's.

本课：第 4 句用 Whose 提问，第 10 句与第 12 句用了 Tim's。

  • Whose car is that? — It's my father's.
  • Whose pen is this? — It's Lucy's.
my, your, his and her物主代词：my / your / his / her
Put my, your, his or her before a noun to show who owns it: I gives my, you gives your, he gives his, she gives her. They never go with a or the, so a my shirt is wrong.
物主代词放在名词前表示所属：I → my，you → your，he → his，she → her。不能和 a/the 一起用（不说 a my shirt）。

In sentences 7 and 8 Dave says my shirt; in sentence 5 the teacher asks about your shirt.

本课：第 7、8 句戴夫说 my shirt；第 5 句老师问 your shirt。

  • This is Tom. His tie is red.
  • This is Jane. Her blouse is white.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • Whose … is this/that? 这／那……是谁的？
    Whose phone is that on the table?
  • It's not my …. My …'s (colour). 不是我的……，我的是……色的。
    It's not my cap. My cap's red.
  • Is this … X's? — Perhaps it is. 这……是 X 的吗？——也许是。
    Is this umbrella Ben's? — Perhaps it is.
  • Here you are. Catch! 给你，接着！
    Here you are, Sam. Catch!
