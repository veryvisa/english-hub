Lesson 113–114 · Small Change 零钱

📘 New Concept English · Book 1 · Lesson 113–114 “Small Change”新概念英语 第一册 Lesson 113–114《Small Change》

B1🧠 15 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
On a bus, the conductor calls for fares. A man wants to go to Trafalgar Square and holds out a ten-pound note, which the conductor cannot break. Has he anything smaller? No. The conductor tries the other passengers: one has nothing, another has nothing either, a lady cannot help, and nor can the woman beside her. So the man has to leave the bus, and the conductor jokes that everyone aboard must be rich. Two passengers protest that they are not: they have coins after all. This lesson teaches some, any, no and none, either and neither, So have I and Neither can I, two meanings of change, except, and get off.
公共汽车上，售票员说：请买票！一位乘客要去特拉法尔加广场，递上一张十英镑的钞票。售票员找不开，问他有没有零钱——没有。售票员去问别的乘客：一位先生说一点也没有，另一位说他也没有，一位女士也找不开，她旁边的人说我也不能。售票员只好请那位乘客下车：没有一个乘客能找开这张钞票，他们全是百万富翁！只有两个人说：除了我们——我有零钱，我也有。本课学some / any / no / none、either 与 neither、So have I / Neither can I、change 的两种意思、except 与 get off。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. Where is the passenger going, and what does he give the conductor?那位乘客要去哪里？他给了售票员什么？
  2. Which passengers does the conductor ask, and what do they say?售票员问了几位乘客？他们怎么回答？
  3. Who has small change in the end?最后谁有零钱？

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

London buses of the time, including the famous red double-decker Routemaster, carried a conductor as well as a driver. The conductor walked through the bus collecting fares in coins and issuing paper tickets from a machine, and carried only a small bag of change. A ten-pound note was a large sum for a bus ride then, far more than most people would carry in coins, so refusing it was quite fair. Trafalgar Square, in the centre of London, was and is a major stop on many routes. Conductors disappeared from most London routes by the 1980s, and today London buses take no cash at all.

当时的伦敦公交车，包括著名的红色双层「路霸」，除了司机还配有售票员。售票员在车厢里走动收硬币车费，用机器出纸质车票，身上只带一小袋零钱。在那时，十英镑钞票对一趟公交车来说是一大笔钱，远超大多数人身上的硬币，所以找不开拒收合情合理。特拉法尔加广场位于伦敦市中心，过去和现在都是许多线路的大站。到 1980 年代，伦敦大多数线路已取消售票员，如今伦敦公交车完全不收现金。

✨ How it is written赏析

The lesson is a chain of refusals, and each refusal teaches a different way to say no. The conductor walks down the bus, and with every passenger the same answer comes back in a new form: got none, haven't got any either, can't, neither can I. The repetition is the joke as well as the drill. Then the conductor gives up and turns the refusals into a sarcastic compliment, and the last two passengers break the pattern with the positive forms the lesson has been holding back. The ending works because the reader has heard no five times and is waiting for a yes.

这一课是一串拒绝，每一次拒绝教一种说「没有」的方式。售票员沿着车厢一路问下去，每个乘客回来的都是同一个答案、换一种说法：一点也没有、我也没有、不能、我也不能。重复本身既是笑点也是操练。然后售票员认输，把这一串拒绝变成一句挖苦的恭维，最后两个乘客用课文一直压着没出场的肯定说法打破了格局。结尾之所以有效，是因为读者听了五次「没有」，正等着一个「有」。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

Cash is fading fast: in Metro Vancouver you pay transit fares with a Compass card, a bank card or a phone, and bus drivers cannot give change at all; if you pay cash, the machine keeps any extra. So the problem in the lesson has turned around: today you may be stuck without a card rather than without coins. Small change is still a useful phrase for coins in general, and Do you have change for a twenty? is common at a market or for a parking meter. For learners, Neither can I and So have I are worth practising until they are automatic, because Chinese speakers often say Me too after a negative, where Me neither is needed.

现金正迅速退场：在大温哥华，乘公交用 Compass 卡、银行卡或手机付费，公交司机根本不找零，付现金的话，多投的钱机器也不退。所以课文里的难题反过来了：今天你可能缺的是卡，而不是硬币。small change 仍是指零钱硬币的常用说法，Do you have change for a twenty? 在集市上或投停车计时器时也很常见。对学习者来说，Neither can I 和 So have I 要练到脱口而出，因为中国人常在否定句后说 Me too，其实该说 Me neither。

Sentence 6第 6 句 (begins开头 “I can't change …”)

  • change a note means give smaller money for it.change a note：把大面额钞票换开、找零。

Sentence 7第 7 句 (begins开头 “Haven't you got …”)

  • A negative question shows surprise or hope.否定疑问句：难道你没有……吗？
  • small change is coins; got no means haven't got any.small change：零钱。I've got no … = I haven't got any …。

Sentence 13第 13 句 (begins开头 “I've got none. …”)

  • An older British way to ask.Have you any …?：英式老派问法，= Have you got any …?。
  • none stands alone without a noun.none：一点也没有，单独用，不跟名词。
  • either at the end of a negative sentence.either：也（否定句），放句末。

Sentence 17第 17 句 (begins开头 “Neither can I. …”)

  • Neither, then the helper verb, then the subject, to agree with a negative.Neither + 助动词 + 主语：……也不能，跟着前面的否定句。

Sentence 19第 19 句 (begins开头 “You must get …”)

  • get off means leave the bus.get off：下车。must：必须。
  • none of plus a plural noun.none of + 名词复数：……中没有一个。

Sentence 24第 24 句 (begins开头 “So have I. …”)

  • except means not including.except：除了……。
  • So, then the helper verb, then the subject, to agree with a positive.So + 助动词 + 主语：……也是，跟着前面的肯定句。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

some, any, no and none
some is for positive sentences, any for negatives and questions. no with a noun means not any. none stands alone, and none of goes before a plural noun or pronoun.
some：肯定句。any：否定句、疑问句。no + 名词 = not any + 名词（I've got no change = I haven't got any change）。none 单独用，不跟名词（I've got none）；none of + 复数名词或代词（none of us）。

Sentences 7, 9, 14, 21 and 24 move through any, no, none, none of and some.

本课：第 7 句 any，第 8 句 no，第 13 句 none，第 20 句 none of，第 23 句 some。

  • There are no buses after midnight.
  • I wanted some milk, but there was none left.
So do I and Neither do I
To agree with a positive, say So with the helper verb and then the subject. To agree with a negative, say Neither with the helper verb and the subject, or use not ... either. The helper verb matches the first sentence.
同意肯定句：So + 助动词 + 主语（I've got some. — So have I.）。同意否定句：Neither + 助动词 + 主语（I can't. — Neither can I.），或 主语 + 助动词 + not + either（I can't either）。助动词跟前一句一致：have / can / do / am。

Sentence 14 uses not ... either, sentence 17 has Neither can I, and sentence 24 has So have I.

本课：第 14 句是 not … either，第 17 句是 Neither can I，第 24 句是 So have I。

  • I love hockey. — So do I.
  • I wasn't at the meeting. — Neither was I.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • Sorry, I can't change a … bill. 抱歉，我找不开……面额的钞票。
    Sorry, I can't change a hundred-dollar bill.
  • Haven't you got anything smaller? 你没有小一点的钱吗？
    Haven't you got anything smaller, sir?
  • I've got none, I'm afraid. 恐怕我一点也没有。
    I've got none, I'm afraid. I paid by card.
  • None of us can … 我们谁也不能……
    None of us can speak Japanese.
  • I've got some coins. — So have I. 我有硬币。——我也有。
    I've got some coins. — So have I.

In no more than 60 words, tell what happened on the bus when a man could not pay with small money.

用不超过 60 个词讲讲公共汽车上找零的经过。

Points a good summary covers好摘要要覆盖的要点

Our model summary本站范文

A man on a bus tried to pay his fare with a ten-pound note. The conductor could not change it, and the man had no coins. The conductor asked several passengers, but nobody could help, so the man had to get off. The conductor joked that everyone was rich, but then two people said they had coins.（57 词）
