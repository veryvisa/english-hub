Lesson 101–102 · A Card From Jimmy 吉米的明信片

📘 New Concept English · Book 1 · Lesson 101–102 “A Card From Jimmy”新概念英语 第一册 Lesson 101–102《A Card From Jimmy》

B1🧠 18 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
A grandmother asks her granddaughter Penny to read out a card from Jimmy. Jimmy has just reached Scotland and is staying at a youth hostel. The old lady is hard of hearing, so Penny passes each line on: he says he has just got there, he says he is staying at a hostel, he belongs to the hostel association. What else? He will send a proper letter soon and hopes they are all fine. Is that it? You cannot fit much on a card, Penny tells her. This lesson teaches reported speech with he says that, how the pronouns and tenses shift, and also speak up, what else, and the tag question does he?
奶奶让孙女彭妮给她念吉米寄来的明信片：吉米刚到苏格兰，住在青年招待所。奶奶耳背，听不清，彭妮就一句句转述：他说他刚到苏格兰，他说他住在青年招待所——他是青年招待所协会的会员。还写了什么？他说很快会写信，希望大家都好。就这些？明信片上本来就写不了多少，妈妈。本课学间接引语：He says (that) he's just arrived、He says he'll write、He hopes (that) …，以及人称和时态怎么跟着变；另学 Speak up、What else、反意疑问句 does he?
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. Where is Jimmy, and where is he staying?吉米在哪里？住在什么地方？
  2. Why does Penny have to say each line twice?为什么彭妮要把每句话再说一遍？
  3. Is grandmother happy with the card?奶奶对这张明信片满意吗？

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander; New Concept English, Book 1, Longman, 1967.

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

The Youth Hostels Association of England and Wales was founded in 1930, with Scotland's own association formed a year later, to give young people cheap beds while they walked or cycled through the countryside. By the 1960s a membership card let a teenager stay in simple shared rooms across Britain for a few shillings a night, often in return for a small chore in the morning. Picture postcards were the cheapest and quickest way to tell the family you had arrived, and a short message on the back was normal; a full letter would follow later. A grandmother living with her family was also common at the time.

英格兰与威尔士青年招待所协会成立于 1930 年，苏格兰的协会一年后成立，目的是让年轻人在徒步或骑车穿越乡间时有便宜的床位。到 1960 年代，凭一张会员卡，青少年花几个先令就能在全英各地住一晚简朴的合住房，早上常常要帮着做点小杂务。图画明信片是告诉家里人「我到了」最便宜也最快的办法，背面写短短几句是常态，长信随后再寄。奶奶和儿孙住在一起，在当时也很普遍。

✨ How it is written赏析

The lesson is a machine for reported speech, and the deafness is the engine. Every line Jimmy wrote has to be read aloud and then said again, so the learner sees the direct form and the reported form side by side, with only the pronouns changing. The grandmother's interruptions keep the drill from feeling like a drill: she mishears an abbreviation, she complains, she asks for more. Her last remark is a small joke about the young, and Penny's reply defends her brother with simple logic. The card's own closing line sits between them like the end of the message it is.

这一课是一台间接引语的练习机，而奶奶的耳背就是它的发动机。吉米写的每一句都得先念一遍、再转述一遍，学习者因此能看到直接说法和间接说法并排摆着，只有人称在变。奶奶的打断让这段操练不像操练：她听错一个缩写，她抱怨，她追问还有没有。她最后那句是对年轻人的小小调侃，彭妮的回答用简单的道理替哥哥辩护。明信片的落款夹在两人之间，正像一段消息的结尾。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The scene is easy to update: today Jimmy would send a short text or a photo, and grandmother would still complain that he says nothing. The skill the lesson teaches, passing on someone's words to a third person, is one of the most useful in daily life, especially for families who help older parents with English in Canada, reading letters from the bank or a doctor's message and explaining them. When you report something that is still true, the tense need not change: she says she is fine. Chinese learners often forget the pronoun shift and say he says I will write, which confuses the listener about who will write.

这个场景很容易换成今天：吉米会发一条短信或一张照片，奶奶照样会抱怨他什么都没说。课文教的本领——把一个人的话转述给第三个人——是日常生活里最有用的能力之一，对在加拿大帮年长父母应付英语的家庭尤其如此：念银行的来信、转述医生的留言、再解释给他们听。转述一件仍然成立的事，时态不必变：she says she is fine。中国学习者常忘记换人称，说成 he says I will write，让听的人搞不清到底是谁要写。

💬 Discuss and write讨论与写作
  1. Read a short message from a friend aloud, then report it to a partner sentence by sentence, starting with She says.
    把朋友的一条短消息念出来，再以 She says 开头一句句转述给同伴。
  2. Write a five-line postcard from a trip you would like to take, then rewrite it in reported speech.
    写一张五行的明信片，写一次你想去的旅行，再把它改写成间接引语。

Sentence 8第 8 句 (begins开头 “You know he's …”)

  • You know reminds someone of a fact they already know.You know …：你知道的嘛，提醒对方已知的事。
  • The what? asks someone to repeat a word you did not catch.The what?：……什么？没听清某个词时的追问。

Sentence 12第 12 句 (begins开头 “What else does …”)

  • else comes straight after what.What else …?：还有什么……？else 放在疑问词后面。

Sentence 17第 17 句 (begins开头 “He says he'll …”)

  • The pronouns change to fit who is speaking: I becomes he, you becomes we.I'll → he'll；you → we：人称跟着说话人换。

  • A negative statement takes a positive tag, and the speaker expects agreement.反意疑问句：前否后肯，doesn't … does he?，期待对方同意。
  • Is that all? asks if there is more.Is that all?：就这些吗？

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

Reported speech after says间接引语（主句用 says）
When the reporting verb is in the present, such as says or hopes, the tense in the reported clause stays the same. Only the pronouns and possessives change to fit the new speaker: I becomes he, you becomes we, my becomes his.
直接引语 'I'm staying at a hostel.' → 间接引语 He says (that) he's staying at a hostel.。主句是现在时 says / hopes 时：从句时态不变，人称和物主代词按说话人换（I → he，you → we，my → his）。

Sentences 4 and 7, 14 and 18, and 15 and 19 are pairs: each gives Jimmy's words and then Penny's version of them.

本课：第 4 句和第 6 句、第 13 句和第 17 句、第 14 句和第 18 句，每对都是同一个意思的直接与间接说法。

  • 'I like my new job.' → She says she likes her new job.
  • 'We'll call you.' → They say they'll call us.
Tag questions反意疑问句
A positive statement takes a negative tag, and a negative statement a positive one. The tag uses the same helper verb and a pronoun. A falling tone means you expect the listener to agree.
肯定句 + 否定尾巴：It's cold, isn't it?；否定句 + 肯定尾巴：He doesn't say much, does he?。尾巴用和前句一致的助动词与代词。语调下降表示期待对方同意。

Sentence 21 has a negative statement with a positive tag.

本课：第 21 句是前否后肯。

  • You're from Richmond, aren't you?
  • She can't swim, can she?

  • X doesn't write much, does he? X 写得不多，是吧？
