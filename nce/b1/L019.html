Lesson 19–20 · Tired and Thirsty 又累又渴

📘 New Concept English · Book 1 · Lesson 19–20 “Tired and Thirsty”新概念英语 第一册 Lesson 19–20《Tired and Thirsty》

B1🧠 18 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
A mother asks her children what is wrong, and they say they are tired and thirsty. She tells them to sit down for a while and asks if they feel better; not yet. Then they spot a man selling ice cream, she buys two, and once they have eaten them the children say thank you and that they are fine now. This lesson is a small scene of looking after someone: ask what is wrong, offer a fix, then check again. The grammar is What's the matter?, the imperatives Sit down and Look!, There's for pointing out what is in a place, and all right with now.
妈妈问孩子们怎么了，他们说又累又渴。妈妈让他们坐下歇会儿，问好些了没有——还没有。这时孩子们看见一个卖冰淇淋的，妈妈买了两个；冰淇淋很好吃，孩子们道了谢，说现在好了。本课是照顾别人的一段小对话：问出了什么事、给个办法、再问一次好没好。学 What's the matter?、祈使句 Sit down / Look!、There's … 「那儿有……」，以及 all right 和 now。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. What does Mum do that makes the children say thanks?妈妈做了什么，让孩子们道谢？
  2. Mother asks twice whether they are all right; are the two answers the same, and why?妈妈问了两次「好了吗」，两次的回答一样吗？为什么？
  3. When do you use There's, as in sentence 10, and Here you are, as in sentence 12?第 10 句的 There's 和第 12 句的 Here you are 分别用在什么时候？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander; New Concept English, Book 1, Longman, 1967. The ice cream seller is a familiar figure from British parks and seaside towns of the time.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为教材自撰，见《新概念英语》第一册（朗文，1967）。卖冰淇淋的人是那个年代英国公园和海滨小镇常见的身影。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

In Britain, and later in Canada, ice cream vans drove round parks and streets in summer playing a short tune, and children would run to them with coins. The scene here is a family day out, probably in a park, on a hot day. The drill lesson after it adds pairs of opposite adjectives such as full and empty, heavy and light, open and shut, so that the learner can describe the state of things as well as people.

在英国，后来在加拿大也一样，夏天冰淇淋车会放着一小段音乐绕公园和街道转，孩子们攥着硬币跑过去。这里的场景是一家人出游，大概在公园里，天气很热。后面那课操练加了几对反义形容词：满与空、重与轻、开与关，让读者不只能描述人，也能描述物的状态。

✨ How it is written赏析

The lesson is built on one question asked twice. The first time, the answer is no, and the problem is still open. In between comes a small surprise, the ice cream seller, and the same question gets a yes. Nothing else is needed to show that things have changed: the shift from No, we aren't to Yes, we are carries the whole story. It is a good example of how a story can move with almost no new words, simply by repeating a line under new conditions.

这课是围绕同一个问题问两次搭起来的。第一次回答是「没有」，问题悬着；中间出现一个小惊喜——卖冰淇淋的人；同一个问题再问，回答变成「好了」。不需要别的话来说明情况变了：从 No, we aren't 到 Yes, we are，整个故事就讲完了。这是很好的例子：几乎不用新词，只要在新条件下重复一句话，故事就能往前走。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

One small detail matters for Chinese families. The children thank their mother for the ice cream, and English-speaking parents teach this from an early age, even inside the family. In many Chinese homes, saying thanks to your own parents can feel too formal, as if you were guests. Neither habit is wrong, but children growing up in Canada will hear thanks at home all the time and may find a home without it cold. Also note the British Mum; in Canada and the United States it is Mom, and children's books from each country spell it their own way.

有个小细节对中国家庭有意义。孩子们为冰淇淋谢了妈妈。英语国家的父母从小就教这一点，在家里也一样。而在很多中国家庭，对自己的父母说谢谢会显得太客气，像客人。两种习惯都没有错，但在加拿大长大的孩子会天天在家里听到 thanks，可能会觉得不说谢谢的家有点冷。还要注意英式的 Mum；在加拿大和美国是 Mom，两国的童书各按各的拼。

    写一段短对话：朋友又累又饿。用上 What's the matter?、一个祈使句和 There's。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 3第 3 句 (begins开头 “What's the matter …”)

What's the matter, children?

  • What's the matter? asks what is wrong. It shows concern and is not a complaint.What's the matter? 问「怎么了？出什么事了？」，是关心，不是责备。
  • Add with to ask about a person or a thing: What's the matter with your leg?加 with 可以问具体的人或物：What's the matter with your leg?。

Sentence 6第 6 句 (begins开头 “Sit down here. …”)

Sit down here.

  • An imperative starts with the plain verb and has no subject; it sounds natural between family and friends.祈使句以动词原形开头，没有主语；对家人朋友这样说很自然。
  • To a guest or a stranger, Please sit down or Have a seat is more polite.对陌生人或客人更礼貌的说法：Please sit down. / Have a seat.。

Sentence 10第 10 句 (begins开头 “There's an ice …”)

There's an ice cream man.

  • There is, or There's, with a singular noun says that something or someone is in a place.There is（There's）+ 单数名词：说某处「有」某物或某人。
  • An ice cream man sells ice cream; the noun ice cream works like an adjective here.ice cream man 是卖冰淇淋的人，名词 ice cream 在这里作定语。

Sentence 11第 11 句 (begins开头 “Two ice cream …”)

Two ice creams, please.

  • As single portions ice cream is countable, so two ice creams; as a kind of food it is uncountable, as in I like ice cream. The text file we use drops the -s here, but it should be ice creams.指「一份一份」的冰淇淋时可数，复数加 -s：two ice creams；泛指这种食物时不可数：I like ice cream.。我们的课文底稿此处少了 -s，读的时候按 ice creams。

Sentence 16第 16 句 (begins开头 “Yes we are …”)

Yes, we are, thank you!

  • Adding thank you after the short answer thanks the person for caring.简略回答后再加一句 thank you，是对关心的回应。
  • Earlier, in sentence 8, the answer was no; now it is yes, so the ice cream has worked.前面第 8 句是 No, we aren't.，这里变成肯定，说明冰淇淋起了作用。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

There is / There's: saying something is in a placeThere is / There's：某处有……
Use There is or There's with a singular noun, and There are with a plural noun, to say for the first time that something is in a place.
There is（There's）+ 单数名词；There are + 复数名词。用于第一次提到某物「在那儿」。

Sentence 10, where the children point something out to their mother, uses this pattern.

本课：第 10 句孩子们指给妈妈看的就是这个句型。

  • There's a bus stop near my house.
  • There are two cats in the garden.
The imperative: Sit down. / Look!祈使句：Sit down. / Look!
Start with the plain verb and no subject to ask, invite, warn or order. Add please to be more polite. For the negative, use Don't with the verb.
用动词原形开头、不带主语，表示请求、邀请、提醒或命令；加 please 更礼貌。否定用 Don't + 动词。

Sentences 6 and 10 are both imperatives: one tells the children to sit, the other tells someone to look.

本课：第 6 句与第 9 句都是祈使句，一个让人坐下，一个让人看。

  • Open the window, please.
  • Don't eat too fast!

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • What's the matter, …? — We're / I'm … 怎么了？——我们／我……
    What's the matter, Ben? — I'm hungry.
  • Sit down here. 坐这儿吧。
    Sit down here, Grandpa, it's nice and quiet.
  • Look! There's a/an … 看！那儿有……
    Look! There's a rainbow!
  • Two …s, please. 请来两个……
    Two coffees, please.
  • Are you all right? — Yes, I'm fine now. 你没事吧？——现在没事了。
    Are you all right, Mia? — Yes, I'm fine now, thanks.

Summary writing摘要写作

In no more than 40 words, tell how the mother makes her children feel better.

用不超过 40 个词讲讲妈妈怎样让孩子们好起来的。

Points a good summary covers好摘要要覆盖的要点
  • The children are tired and thirsty, so their mother lets them sit and rest.孩子们又累又渴，妈妈让他们坐下休息。
  • Resting does not help; then they see the ice cream seller, and she buys two.休息一会儿还不行；他们看见卖冰淇淋的，妈妈买了两个。
  • The ice creams taste good, the children feel better, and they thank her.冰淇淋很好吃，孩子们好了，向妈妈道谢。

The children were tired and thirsty. Their mother told them to sit down, but they still felt bad. Then they saw a man selling ice cream, and she bought two. The ice creams were nice, and the children thanked her.（40 词）
