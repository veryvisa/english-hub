Lesson 57–58 · An Unusual Day 不平常的一天

📘 New Concept English · Book 1 · Lesson 57–58 “An Unusual Day”新概念英语 第一册 Lesson 57–58《An Unusual Day》

B1🧠 19 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
The same family, the same hours, and yet nothing is as usual. At eight the children, normally driven to school, are walking there. At ten Mrs Sawyer, normally at home in the morning, is out at the shops. At four she is having her afternoon tea outside rather than indoors. At six the children are not doing homework but playing outside. At nine Mr Sawyer has put the paper aside for a good book. This lesson is usual habits set against today. It teaches how the present simple, with usually, and the present continuous, with today and at the moment, share the work, by car and on foot, telling the time with It is ... o'clock, and time words such as this morning, this evening and tonight.
还是索耶一家，还是那几个钟点，可今天什么都不一样。八点：孩子们平时坐车上学，今天走路去。十点：索耶太太上午一般待在家，今天去商店。四点：她下午通常在客厅喝茶，今天在花园里喝。六点：孩子们傍晚一般做作业，今天没做，正在花园里玩。九点：索耶先生晚上通常看报，今晚没看，正在读一本有趣的书。本课是「平常」与「今天」的对照，学一般现在时（usually）与现在进行时（today、at the moment）的分工、by car 与 on foot、It is … o'clock 报时，以及 this morning / this evening / tonight 这类时间说法。

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander; New Concept English, Book 1, Longman, 1967.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为教材自撰，见《新概念英语》第一册（朗文，1967）。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

This lesson only makes sense beside the one before it, which set out the Sawyers' ordinary routine in the present simple. Here the same family is followed through the same day, and at each hour the routine is broken. It is a classic way to teach the two present tenses, and Alexander uses it at exactly the point where learners know both forms but tend to mix them up. The question printed before the text asks about tonight, so the learner must find the one tense that answers it.

这一课必须和上一课并读：上一课用一般现在时写下了索耶一家的日常作息，这一课跟着同一家人走过同样的一天，每到一个钟点都打破常规。这是教两种现在时的经典办法，亚历山大正好用在读者两种形式都学过、却最容易混用的时候。课文前印的那个问题问的是「今晚」，读者必须找出能回答它的那一个时态。

✨ How it is written赏析

The structure is a strict repeating frame: a clock time, a habit, then today's change. Five times in a row the reader hears usually followed by but, and by the third round the pattern is so clear that the tense choice becomes automatic. What keeps it from being only a drill is that the changes grow more personal through the day. The morning changes are about travel and shopping; the evening ones are small pleasures, children playing instead of working and a father choosing a good book over the paper. The title promises something unusual, and the day delivers a quiet holiday mood.

结构是一个严格重复的框架：一个钟点、一个习惯、然后今天的变化。连着五次，读者听到 usually 之后跟着 but；到第三轮，模式清楚到时态选择变成了自动反应。让它不只是操练的，是变化越到晚上越私人：上午的变化是交通和购物，晚上的变化是小小的乐事——孩子不写作业去玩、父亲放下报纸挑了本好书。标题许诺了「不平常」，这一天给出的是一种安静的放假心情。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The tense contrast is the most useful thing in this part of Book 1, and it is still a frequent error for Chinese speakers, because Chinese marks the difference with separate time words rather than with the verb. In English the verb itself must change, and getting it wrong can change the meaning: I work at a bank is my job, while I'm working at a bank suggests something temporary. Learners in Canada will use this contrast every day, in small talk such as What are you doing this weekend? and in describing their jobs.

这种时态对照是第一册这一段里最有用的东西，至今仍是中国读者的常见错误，因为中文靠「正在」「今天」这样的词来区分，而不是靠动词本身。英语里动词必须变，用错还可能改变意思：I work at a bank 是我的职业，而 I'm working at a bank 暗示这只是暂时的。在加拿大的读者每天都会用到这种对照，比如闲聊里的 What are you doing this weekend?，或描述自己的工作时。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 4第 4 句 (begins开头 “The children go …”)

  • every day goes with the present simple, for the habit; today goes with the continuous, for the exception.every day 配一般现在时（习惯）；today 配现在进行时（今天的例外）。
  • by car takes no article. Walking is on foot, not by foot.by car 不加冠词；步行说 on foot，不说 by foot。

Sentence 7第 7 句 (begins开头 “Mrs. Sawyer usually …”)

  • in the morning means any morning; this morning means today's.in the morning（任何一个上午）对 this morning（今天上午）。
  • go to the shops means go shopping.go to the shops：去商店买东西。

Sentence 11第 11 句 (begins开头 “But this afternoon, …”)

  • Starting with But picks up the habit in sentence 10; this is common in speech and simple narrative.句首的 But 承接上一句（第 10 句）的习惯，口语和简单叙述里常这样用。

Sentence 15第 15 句 (begins开头 “At the moment, …”)

  • at the moment means now and goes with the continuous.at the moment = now，和现在进行时连用。

Sentence 19第 19 句 (begins开头 “At the moment, …”)

  • The negative continuous is be with not and the -ing form.进行时的否定：be + not + -ing。
  • interesting begins with a vowel sound, so an.interesting 以元音开头，用 an。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

Present simple and present continuous: usually versus now一般现在时与现在进行时：平常 vs 此刻
Use the present simple for habits, with usually, every day and in the morning. Use the present continuous for what is happening now or today, especially when it differs from the habit, with today, this morning, at the moment and tonight.
习惯和经常的事用一般现在时，常和 usually、every day、in the morning 连用；此刻或今天正在发生的、与平常不同的事用现在进行时，常和 today、this morning、at the moment、tonight 连用。

Every pair gives the habit first and then today: sentences 4 and 6, 8 and 9, 11 and 12, 14 to 16, and 18 to 20.

本课：每一对句子都是先「平常」后「今天」：第 4–5 句、第 7–8 句、第 10–11 句、第 13–15 句、第 17–19 句。

  • I usually walk to work, but today I'm taking the bus.
  • She drinks coffee every morning. At the moment she's drinking tea.
Means of travel: by with a vehicle, on foot交通方式：by + 交通工具，on foot
by with a vehicle, without an article: by car, by bus, by train, by plane. Walking is on foot. With an article the preposition changes: in the car, on the bus.
by + 交通工具（不加冠词）：by car、by bus、by train、by bike、by plane；步行说 on foot。用了冠词就换介词：in the car、on the bus。

Sentences 4 and 6 set by and on side by side.

本课：第 4 句与第 5 句一个 by、一个 on，正好对照。

  • I go to work by train.
  • It's a nice day, so let's go on foot.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • It is … o'clock. 现在……点。
    It is seven o'clock.
  • … usually …, but today … is …ing. ……通常……，但今天在……
    Lily usually cycles to work, but today she is taking the bus.
  • In the afternoon … usually …. But this afternoon …. 下午……通常……，但今天下午……
    In the afternoon Grandpa usually sleeps. But this afternoon he is fishing.
  • At the moment, … are …ing in the …. 此刻……正在……里……
    At the moment, the boys are swimming in the lake.
  • … is not …ing tonight. ……今晚没在……
    Mum is not cooking tonight.
