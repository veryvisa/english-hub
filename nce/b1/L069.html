Lesson 69–70 · The Car Race 汽车比赛

📘 New Concept English · Book 1 · Lesson 69–70 “The Car Race”新概念英语 第一册 Lesson 69–70《The Car Race》

B1🧠 17 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
Every year there is a motor race close to the speaker's town. The one held in 1995 was a big event, with a crowd several hundred strong, and he and his wife went along, as did their friends Julie and Jack. In the photo you can spot them on the left of the crowd. Twenty cars took part, from Britain, France, Germany, Italy, America and Japan, and the finish was a thriller: Billy Stewart won in car fifteen, with five others close on his tail. Driving home, his wife told him to slow down, because he was no racing star. This lesson is telling a past event from a photo. It teaches there is, there was and there were, hundreds of, adjectives of nationality, be standing to describe a picture, and a command with so plus an adverb.
说话人住的小镇附近每年都有一场汽车赛。1995 年那场特别大，来了好几百人，他和妻子也在场，朋友朱莉和杰克也去了。照片里能在人群左边找到他们。参赛的二十辆车来自英国、法国、德国、意大利、美国和日本，结尾很精彩：冠军是开 15 号车的比利·斯图尔特，另外五辆紧跟其后。回家路上，妻子对他说：别开那么快，你又不是比利·斯图尔特！本课是看照片讲一件过去的事，学 there is / there was / there were、hundreds of、国籍形容词、be standing 描述照片，以及 so + 副词的祈使句。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. How many people and how many cars were at the race in 1995?1995 年的比赛有多少人、多少辆车？
  2. Who won, and what was the number of his car?冠军是谁？他开的是几号车？
  3. What does the wife mean by her last words?妻子最后那句话是什么意思？

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

Motor racing was hugely popular in Britain in the 1960s, when British drivers and teams were among the best in the world and small local circuits drew big crowds on summer weekends. A race with cars from six countries reflects the European and international flavour of the sport. The name Billy Stewart may be a nod to the Scottish driver Jackie Stewart, who became one of the great champions of the period, though the book does not say so. The list of countries also tells a story about industry: Japanese cars were only starting to be sold abroad in the 1960s, and their place on the list would have seemed modern.

1960 年代的英国赛车极受欢迎，当时英国车手和车队位居世界前列，夏季周末的小型地方赛道也能吸引大批观众。一场有六国车辆参加的比赛，反映了这项运动的欧洲与国际色彩。比利·斯图尔特这个名字可能是在致敬苏格兰车手杰基·斯图尔特——他后来成为那个时代的伟大冠军之一，不过书里并没有这么说。国家名单还透露出工业变迁：日本车在 1960 年代才刚开始出口，把它列进名单在当时显得很新潮。

✨ How it is written赏析

The text is told by someone pointing at a photograph, and the tenses follow his finger. The race happens every year, so it is in the present; the big race was in the past, so it is in the past; but the people in the picture are still standing there, so they are in the present continuous. The middle of the lesson is a list, and the list builds up a sense of a great international event. Then the last two lines bring everything back down to a small domestic joke, as the wife reminds her husband that watching a champion does not make you one.

这篇课文是一个人指着照片讲出来的，时态跟着他的手指走。比赛每年都有，所以用现在时；那场大赛在过去，所以用过去时；但照片里的人还站在那里，所以用现在进行时。课文中段是一张清单，这张清单营造出一场国际盛事的感觉。最后两句又把一切拉回一个小小的家庭玩笑：妻子提醒丈夫，看过冠军比赛，并不会让你变成冠军。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The wife's joke is still very much alive: almost every driver has heard a version of it after a sports event or an action film. The grammar of there was and there were remains one of the most useful structures for telling stories, and Chinese learners often mix it with have, saying in my town have a race instead of there is a race. The list of nationalities is also worth practising, because the adjective is often different from the country name. Today the cars would more likely be Korean or electric, and Canada itself hosts a Formula One race in Montreal every summer, a good topic for practising the same patterns.

妻子那句玩笑至今仍很鲜活：几乎每个司机在看完体育比赛或动作片后都听过类似的话。there was / there were 的语法仍是讲故事最有用的结构之一，中国学习者常把它和 have 混用，说 in my town have a race，而不是 there is a race。国籍形容词也值得练，因为形容词常和国名不同。今天的参赛车更可能有韩国车或电动车，而加拿大自己每年夏天在蒙特利尔举办一站一级方程式大奖赛，正好拿来练同样的句型。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 3第 3 句 (begins开头 “There is a …”)

  • there is says something exists; it happens every year, so the present is used.there is：存在句「有」。每年都有，所以用现在时。

Sentence 5第 5 句 (begins开头 “There were hundreds …”)

  • For the past, use there was with a singular noun and there were with a plural.说过去用 there was（单数）/ there were（复数）。
  • hundreds of means very many; with an exact number we say two hundred people.hundreds of：数以百计的；具体数字时说 two hundred people，不加 -s、不加 of。

Sentence 9第 9 句 (begins开头 “We are standing …”)

  • The speaker is describing a photo, so the present continuous is used even though the race is past.这是在描述一张照片，所以用现在进行时，虽然比赛在过去。

Sentence 11第 11 句 (begins开头 “There were English …”)

  • Nationality adjectives take a capital; and joins the last two.国籍形容词首字母大写；最后两项用 and 连接。

Sentence 15第 15 句 (begins开头 “Five other cars …”)

  • The figure comes after number; just behind means very close behind.car number fifteen：号码放在 number 后面。just behind：紧跟在后。

Sentence 16第 16 句 (begins开头 “On the way …”)

  • Don't plus a bare verb gives a negative command; so quickly means as fast as that.Don't + 动词原形：否定祈使句。so quickly：那么快。
  • The wife uses the winner's name as a joke: her husband is no racing driver.妻子拿冠军的名字开玩笑：你又不是赛车手。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

there is, there was and there were
To say that something exists, use there is or are for now and there was or were for the past. Questions put was or were first, and negatives often add any.
表示「某处有某物」：现在用 there is / are，过去用 there was（单数或不可数）/ there were（复数）。问句 Was there …? / Were there …?；否定 there wasn't / weren't any。

Sentence 3 is in the present, and sentences 4, 6, 11 and 12 are in the past.

本课：第 3 句用现在，第 5、6、11、12 句用过去。

  • There was a big storm last night.
  • Were there many people at the concert? — Yes, there were.
Adjectives of nationality国籍形容词
The country and its adjective are often different words. Both take a capital, and the adjective goes before the noun.
国家名与国籍形容词常不同：England → English，France → French，Germany → German，Italy → Italian，America → American，Japan → Japanese，China → Chinese，Canada → Canadian。都大写，放在名词前。

Sentence 11 lists cars from six countries in one breath.

本课：第 11 句一口气列了六种车。

  • I love Italian food.
  • She drives a German car.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • There is a … near our … every … 我们……附近每……都有一场……
    There is a farmers' market near our school every Saturday.
  • There were hundreds of … there. 那里有好几百……
    There were hundreds of children there.
  • You can see us in … We are … 在……里能看到我们，我们正……
    You can see us in this photo. We are sitting by the lake.
  • X was in car number … X 开的是……号车
    My brother was in seat number twelve.
  • Don't … so …! You're not …! 别那么……！你又不是……！
    Don't eat so fast! You're not in a contest!
