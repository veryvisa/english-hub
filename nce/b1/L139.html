Lesson 139–140 · Is That You, John 是你吗，约翰？

📘 New Concept English · Book 1 · Lesson 139–140 “Is That You, John”新概念英语 第一册 Lesson 139–140《Is That You, John》

B1🧠 16 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
Graham Turner phones and asks for John; the voice says it is John. So Graham asks him to pass a message to Mary: he and his wife will be late for dinner. Mary had invited the two of them, and Graham promised to arrive at six, but his boss now needs him for extra work, so he must stay late and has no idea when he can leave. Also, his wife would like to know whether Mary needs a hand. The other man says he has no idea what Graham means. Graham checks: John Smith? Yes. The engineer? Yes. With the Overseas Engineering Company? No. This John Smith is the engineer fixing Graham's own phone line. This is a phone call to the wrong man that teaches indirect questions with when and if, tag questions such as isn't it, aren't you and don't you, want someone to do, and the phone word speaking.
格雷厄姆·特纳打电话找约翰，对方说是我。格雷厄姆就让他转告玛丽：今晚吃饭他们要晚到——玛丽请了他和夏洛特，他说好六点到，可老板要他加班，他得留在办公室，也不知道几点完。对了，他妻子还想问问玛丽要不要帮忙。对方说听不懂他在说什么。格雷厄姆一问：你是约翰·史密斯？是。工程师约翰·史密斯？对。在海外工程公司上班吧？不是——「我是电话工程师约翰·史密斯，正在修你的电话线。」本课是一通打错了人的电话，学间接疑问句（when 引导的、if 引导的）、反意疑问句（isn't it / aren't you / don't you）、want sb to do 和电话用语 speaking。

  2. Why can't Graham get there on time?格雷厄姆为什么不能准时去吃饭？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander; New Concept English, Book 1, Longman, 1967. The characters and the Overseas Engineering Company are invented.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为教材自撰，见《新概念英语》第一册（朗文，1967）。人物和「海外工程公司」都是虚构的。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

In 1960s Britain the telephone network was run by the General Post Office, and its repair staff were called telephone engineers; they often tested a line by picking up calls on it. John Smith was, and still is, one of the most common names in England, so a mix-up between two John Smiths was a natural joke. Dinner invitations were usually arranged by phone between the wives, which is why Graham's message is full of Mary and Charlotte.

1960 年代英国的电话网由邮政总局经营，维修人员叫「电话工程师」，他们修线时常会接起线上的电话来测试。约翰·史密斯过去和现在都是英格兰最常见的名字之一，所以两个约翰·史密斯搞混，是很自然的笑话。请人吃饭通常由两家的太太在电话里约定，所以格雷厄姆的话里满是玛丽和夏洛特。

✨ How it is written赏析

The joke is held back by Graham's confidence: he talks for six sentences before he lets the other man speak. Then the tag questions do the detective work, each one narrowing the identity, until the last no reveals the truth. The final line also explains why the man answered at all: he is working on Graham's own phone, so the call never reached the right house.

笑话被格雷厄姆的自信「憋」住了：他一口气说了六句，才让对方开口。接着由反意疑问句来做侦探的活，每一问都把身份缩小一圈，直到最后那个 No 揭开真相。最后一句还解释了对方为什么会接电话：他正在修格雷厄姆自己的电话，所以这通电话根本没打到该去的人家。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

Indirect questions are one of the most useful polite forms in Canadian English. At a front desk, Can you tell me where the washroom is? sounds much softer than Where is the washroom?, and learners who keep question order inside them, as in Do you know where is the bank?, sound foreign at once. On the phone, always check who you are speaking to before you give a message or personal details; scam calls in Canada often rely on callers who say yes, speaking, to whatever name they hear.

间接疑问句是加拿大英语里最有用的礼貌句式之一。在前台说 Can you tell me where the washroom is? 比 Where is the washroom? 柔和得多；在里面还保留疑问语序（Do you know where is the bank?）的学习者，一开口就显得是外国人。打电话时，传话或说个人信息之前一定先确认对方是谁；加拿大的诈骗电话常常就是对你说出的任何名字都应一句「是我」。

💬 Discuss and write讨论与写作
  1. Turn five direct questions into polite indirect ones with Could you tell me … or Do you know ….
    用 Could you tell me … 或 Do you know … 把五个直接问句改成礼貌的间接问句。
  2. Role-play a phone call with a partner where one of you has the wrong number; check the identity with three tag questions.
    和同伴演一通打错的电话，用三个反意疑问句确认对方身份。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

Indirect questions间接疑问句
When a question goes after I don't know, I wonder, Do you know or wants to know, use statement order, with the subject before the verb and no do, does or did. Wh-questions keep the wh-word; yes-no questions use if or whether. The final mark follows the whole sentence.
把问题放进 I don't know / I wonder / Do you know / wants to know 后面时，用陈述语序（主语在前，动词在后），也不用 do / does / did。特殊疑问句保留 when、what 等；一般疑问句用 if 或 whether。句末标点看整句：I don't know … 用句号。

Sentence 12 has when I'll finish, sentence 13 if Mary needs, and sentence 14 what you're talking about.

本课：第 12 句 when I'll finish，第 13 句 if Mary needs，第 14 句 what you're talking about。

  • Do you know where the station is?
  • I wonder if she got my email.
Tag questions反意疑问句
A tag question adds a short question to a statement to check it: positive statement, negative tag, and the other way round. The tag repeats the helper verb: be uses is or are, ordinary verbs use do, does or did, and will uses won't. A falling tone means the speaker is fairly sure; a rising tone means a real question.
陈述句后面加一个短问句来求证：前肯后否，前否后肯。反意部分用和前面一致的助动词：be 用 is/are，实义动词用 do/does/did，will 用 won't。语调下降＝很有把握，只求确认；语调上升＝真的不确定。

Sentence 15 has isn't it, sentence 17 aren't you, and sentence 19 don't you.

本课：第 15 句 isn't it，第 17 句 aren't you，第 19 句 don't you。

  • You live in Richmond, don't you?
  • It won't rain, will it?

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • Is that you, …? — Yes, speaking. 是……吗？——我就是。
    Is that you, Helen? — Yes, speaking.
  • Please tell … I'll be late. 请转告……我会晚到。
    Please tell Mr Lee I'll be late.
  • My boss wants me to … 老板要我……
    My boss wants me to work on Saturday.
  • I don't know when … 我不知道什么时候……
    I don't know when the next train leaves.
  • She wants to know if … 她想知道……是否……
    She wants to know if you can come early.

Our model summary本站范文

Graham Turner phoned and asked for John. He wanted John to tell Mary that he and his wife would be late for her dinner, because his boss needed him for extra work and he had no idea when he would leave. His wife also wondered whether Mary needed help. The man was puzzled. He was John Smith, but not that one: he was the engineer fixing Graham's own phone line.（70 词）
