Lesson 137–138 · A Pleasant Dream 美梦

📘 New Concept English · Book 1 · Lesson 137–138 “A Pleasant Dream”新概念英语 第一册 Lesson 137–138《A Pleasant Dream》

B1🧠 20 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
Brian is filling in his football pools coupon and feels sure of a prize this week. Julie says he always says so, yet they have never won a penny. She asks what he would do with a big win. He offers her a mink coat; she does not want one, she wants to travel. Fine: a trip round the globe in the finest hotels, then a large country house with a lovely garden. Julie asks what happens when the money runs out and they are poor again. Brian's answer is to win the pools once more. Julie says it is a nice dream, but it all hangs on one small word. This is a couple daydreaming together, and it teaches the first conditional, If plus present simple with will in the main clause, What will you do if, want to do, depend on and try and do.
布赖恩在填足球彩票，说这周肯定能中点什么；朱莉说你每次都这么说，可我们从来没中过。她问中了大奖怎么办，布赖恩说给她买件貂皮大衣，她说不要，她想看看世界。好，那就环游世界、住最好的酒店，回来在乡下买栋大房子，带漂亮花园……朱莉问：钱花光了，我们又穷了，怎么办？布赖恩说：那就再去中一次彩票。朱莉说：「这是个美梦，可一切都取决于『如果』！」本课是一对夫妻做白日梦，学真实条件句 If + 一般现在时，主句 will + 动词原形、What will you do if …?、want to do、depend on 与 try and do。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. What is Brian busy with?布赖恩在做什么？
  2. What does Julie want, and what does she not want?朱莉想要什么，不想要什么？
  3. What is Brian's plan once the money is gone?钱花光了，布赖恩打算怎么办？

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander; New Concept English, Book 1, Longman, 1967. Brian and Julie are invented characters; the text prints the name as Brain in some copies, a printing slip for Brian.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为教材自撰，见《新概念英语》第一册（朗文，1967）。布赖恩和朱莉是虚构人物；有的版本把名字印成 Brain，是 Brian 的印刷错误。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

The football pools were Britain's great weekly gamble from the 1920s until the National Lottery began in 1994. Millions of people filled in a coupon each week, guessing which matches would end in a draw, and posted it with a small stake; a collector often came to the door. A big win could change a family's life overnight, and newspapers ran photos of winners holding giant cheques. Doing the pools on a Saturday was a normal family habit, much like buying a lottery ticket today.

足球彩票从 1920 年代起是英国人每周的大赌注，直到 1994 年国家彩票开办。每周有几百万人填一张彩票单，猜哪几场比赛会打平，附上一小笔钱寄出去，常常还有收单人上门来取。中个大奖能让一家人一夜之间改变命运，报纸会登出获奖者举着巨大支票的照片。周六填足球彩票是普通家庭的习惯，就像今天买一张乐透。

✨ How it is written赏析

The text is a ladder of if-sentences, each one climbing a little higher into fantasy: a coat, a world trip, the finest hotels, a country house. Julie keeps pulling the dream back to earth, first by refusing the coat and then by asking what happens when the money runs out. Brian's answer, win again, is the comic peak, and her last line names the grammar point of the whole lesson.

课文是一架由 if 句搭成的梯子，每一句都往幻想里再爬高一点：一件大衣、一次环球旅行、最好的酒店、乡下的大房子。朱莉不停地把梦往地上拉——先是不要大衣，再问钱花光了怎么办。布赖恩那句「再中一次」是喜剧的顶点，而她的最后一句话正好点出整课的语法点。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The most common learner mistake here is putting will after if, as in If it will rain; English uses the present simple for the condition, and the will goes only in the result. For readers in Canada, the story has a practical side too: lottery winners are often advised to get financial and legal advice first, because studies of big winners show that many spend the money within a few years. Lottery wins in Canada are not taxed, but the interest earned on them later is, which is a nice point to discuss in English.

这里学习者最常犯的错是在 if 后面加 will，比如 If it will rain；英语的条件句用一般现在时，will 只放在结果里。对加拿大读者来说，这个故事也有实用的一面：常有人建议中了大奖先去找财务和法律顾问，因为对大奖得主的研究显示，很多人几年内就把钱花完了。在加拿大中彩票的奖金本身不交税，但之后用奖金赚到的利息要交税，这是一个用英语讨论的好话题。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentence 5第 5 句 (begins开头 “I'm sure we'll …”)

  • I'm sure plus a clause.I'm sure + 从句：我肯定……
  • something in a positive sentence.肯定句用 something。

Sentence 6第 6 句 (begins开头 “You always say …”)

  • One negative is enough: never with anything.never + anything：一个否定词就够，不说 never win nothing。
  • always and never with the present simple show habit.always / never 配一般现在时表习惯。

Sentence 7第 7 句 (begins开头 “What will you …”)

  • In the if-clause use the present simple; the main clause has will.条件状语从句：if 后用一般现在时，主句用 will。

Sentence 8第 8 句 (begins开头 “If I win …”)

  • buy someone something takes two objects.buy sb sth：给某人买某物，双宾语。
  • When the if-clause comes first, writing usually adds a comma.if 从句在前时，书面一般用逗号隔开。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

The first conditional: If plus present, will plus base verb真实条件句：If + 一般现在时，will + 动词原形
To talk about a possible future event and its result, use the present simple in the if-clause and will plus the base verb in the main clause. Do not use will in the if-clause. The if-clause can come first, with a comma, or second, usually with none.
讲将来可能发生的事和它的结果：if 从句用一般现在时，主句用 will + 动词原形。if 从句里不用 will。从句可在前（加逗号），也可在后（一般不加逗号）。

Sentences 7, 9, 13, 16 and 18 all use this pattern.

本课：第 8、9、13、16、18 句都是这个句型。

  • If it snows, we'll stay at home.
  • I'll call you if I need help.
something and anything with never or notnever / not … anything 与 something
Use something and someone in positive sentences, and anything and anyone in negatives and most questions. never is already negative, so it takes anything, not nothing; standard English avoids two negatives.
肯定句用 something / someone，否定句和疑问句一般用 anything / anyone。never 本身是否定词，后面用 anything，不用 nothing（英语不双重否定）。

Sentence 5 has something; sentence 6 has never ... anything.

本课：第 5 句 something，第 6 句 never … anything。

  • I bought something for you.
  • He never says anything at meetings.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • What will you do if …? 如果……你会怎么办？
    What will you do if the flight is cancelled?
  • If I get the job, I'll … 如果我得到这份工作，我会……
    If I get the job, I'll buy a car.
  • I don't want a … I want to … 我不要……我想……
    I don't want a big party. I want to have a quiet dinner.
  • We'll try and … 我们会设法……
    We'll try and finish by Friday.
  • It all depends on … 全看……
    It all depends on the weather.

Our model summary本站范文

Brian was filling in his pools coupon, sure of a prize this week, though they had never won before. He offered Julie a mink coat, but she wanted to travel instead. So he planned a world trip, the finest hotels and a large country house. If the money ran out, he would win again. Julie said it was a lovely dream, but it all hung on if.（67 词）
