🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
After the exams Richard says they went fine; he thinks he passed English and maths, as the questions were simple. Gary found those two papers harder than he liked and hopes he has not failed. French, he is sure, went badly: he managed sixteen questions and the others were beyond him. He hates French tests and expects a poor result. Richard tells him to look on the bright side, then tells a story about the boy at the next desk, who put his name on the paper and then just stared at it for three hours. This lesson teaches enough and too with adjectives, pass and fail, I hope I haven't, the rest, tag questions, and cheer up.
考完试，理查德说还不错，英语和数学大概都及格了，题目很容易。加里却说英语和数学的卷子对他来说不够容易，但愿别不及格；法语他肯定没考过：会做十六道，剩下的太难。法语考试真糟糕，他肯定分数很低。理查德让他别泄气，又讲了个笑话：他旁边那个人在卷子顶上写了名字，然后对着卷子坐了三个小时，一个字也没写。本课学形容词 + enough / too + 形容词 + for sb、pass / fail、I hope I haven't …、the rest、反意疑问句 aren't they? 与 cheer up。

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander; New Concept English, Book 1, Longman, 1967.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为教材自撰，见《新概念英语》第一册（朗文，1967）。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

In Britain at the time, sixteen-year-olds sat the General Certificate of Education at Ordinary level, known as O-levels, in each subject separately, and each subject could have more than one written paper. Results came weeks later by post, so the talk after an exam was all guessing, as it is here. A French paper was a normal part of school, because French was the main foreign language taught in British schools. A three-hour paper was not unusual, and students sat in silent rows in a school hall, which is how Richard can see what the boy at the next desk is doing.

当时在英国，十六岁的学生要参加普通教育证书普通级考试，通常叫 O-level，各科分开考，一门科目可能有不止一张笔试卷。成绩几周后才邮寄到家，所以考后的议论全是猜测，正如课文里那样。法语卷子是学校的常规科目，因为法语是英国学校教的主要外语。一张卷子考三个小时并不稀奇，学生在学校礼堂里一排排静静坐着，所以理查德能看到旁边那个人在做什么。

✨ How it is written赏析

The two boys are set up as opposites, and the grammar draws the line between them. Richard is calm and uses short, modest words; Gary piles up negatives, with not enough, couldn't, too difficult, hate, and low mark all in a few lines. The same exam looks easy to one and hard to the other, which is exactly the job too and enough do: they always measure against a person. Richard's cure for his friend's worry is not an argument but a story, and its last line lands on the word he did not write, which makes everyone else's result look good.

两个男孩被设计成一正一反，而语法替他们划清界线。理查德平静，用词简短而谦虚；加里堆砌否定，not enough、couldn't、too difficult、hate、low mark 几句话里全有。同一场考试，在一个人眼里容易、在另一个人眼里难，这正是 too 和 enough 的功用：它们总是拿一个人来衡量。理查德安慰朋友靠的不是讲道理，而是讲故事，最后一句落在那个人一个字也没写上，让其他所有人的成绩都显得不错了。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The worry after an exam has not changed, but in British Columbia today it often comes with a different shape: provincial graduation assessments in numeracy and literacy, and course marks spread over the whole year, so a single bad paper matters less than it did. In Canada students talk about grades or marks and percentages rather than pass and fail alone. For learners, the word order of enough is the main trap: Chinese puts the word for enough before the adjective, so learners say enough big instead of big enough. Not too bad is also worth learning; it is the most common modest answer in Canadian English, and it usually means things went well.

考后的担心没有变，但在今天的不列颠哥伦比亚省，它换了样子：有全省统一的计算与读写毕业评估，课程成绩分散在全年，一张卷子考砸，分量不如从前重。在加拿大，学生谈论的是分数、等级和百分比，而不只是及格与不及格。对学习者来说，enough 的词序是主要陷阱：中文「够」放在形容词前，学习者便说成 enough big，而不是 big enough。Not too bad 也值得学，它是加拿大英语里最常见的谦虚回答，通常意思是挺好。

    跟同伴讲你考过的最难的一次试：什么太难，什么还算容易。
  2. Write six sentences about yourself with too and enough, for example I am old enough to vote.
    用 too 和 enough 写六句关于自己的话，比如 I am old enough to vote。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentence 5第 5 句 (begins开头 “I think I …”)

  • Not too bad is a modest way to say quite good.Not too bad：还不错（英式含蓄说法）。
  • pass in a subject is British; pass the subject works too.pass in + 科目（英式）；也可说 pass English。

Sentence 8第 8 句 (begins开头 “The English and …”)

  • enough goes after the adjective, and for shows who it is about.形容词 + enough + for sb：对某人来说足够……。enough 放在形容词后。
  • How about you? turns the same question to the other person.How about you?：你呢？

Sentence 9第 9 句 (begins开头 “I hope I …”)

  • The present perfect fits a result not yet known.I hope I haven't failed：但愿没考砸（结果到现在还不知道，用现在完成时）。
  • The past simple for an exam that is over and done.failed the French paper：法语卷子没考过，已成定局用一般过去时。

Sentence 13第 13 句 (begins开头 “But I couldn't …”)

  • could and couldn't for past ability; the rest means the other questions.could / couldn't：过去能 / 不能。the rest：其余的（题）。
  • too plus an adjective means more than is good for someone.too + 形容词 + for sb：对某人来说太……。

Sentence 15第 15 句 (begins开头 “French tests are …”)

  • A positive statement with a negative tag.前肯后否的反意疑问句。
  • a low mark means a poor score.a low mark：低分。

Sentence 23第 23 句 (begins开头 “He didn't write …”)

  • at the top of the page.at the top of：在……顶端。
  • A finished period in the past takes the past simple.for three hours：过去持续了三个小时，用一般过去时。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

too and enoughtoo 与 enough
too goes before an adjective and means more than is right. enough goes after an adjective but before a noun: old enough, enough money. not easy enough is close in meaning to too hard.
too + 形容词（+ for sb / to do）：太……而不能。形容词 + enough（+ for sb / to do）：足够……可以。enough 要放在形容词后面，名词前面：old enough，enough money。not + 形容词 + enough ≈ too + 反义形容词。

Sentence 8 uses not enough after an adjective and sentence 14 uses too with for me, and they say almost the same thing.

本课：第 8 句是 not … enough，第 14 句是 too … for me，两句意思相近。

  • This coffee is too hot to drink.
  • She isn't tall enough to reach the shelf.
pass and fail; I hope ...pass 与 fail；I hope …
pass and fail take the exam or subject as object. When the result is known, use the past simple. When you are still waiting, use I hope with the present perfect.
pass / fail + 考试或科目：pass the test、fail maths。考试结果已知用一般过去时（I failed），结果还不知道、只是担心时说 I hope I've passed，或否定的 I hope + haven't failed。

Sentence 5 is a guess that he passed, sentence 9 is a hope, and sentence 10 is a firm belief that he failed.

本课：第 5 句说自己大概及格了，第 9 句是但愿，第 10 句是认定没过。

  • I hope I've passed my driving test.
  • She failed chemistry last year but passed it this year.
