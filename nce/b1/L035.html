Lesson 35–36 · Our Village 我们的村庄

📘 New Concept English · Book 1 · Lesson 35–36 “Our Village”新概念英语 第一册 Lesson 35–36《Our Village》

B1🧠 17 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
The speaker shows some photos of his village. It lies in a valley, between two hills, beside a river. In the next photo he and his wife are strolling by the water; they are the pair on the left, and a boy is swimming to the far side. The last shows the school, with a park next to it on the right; children are leaving the school and several are heading into the park. This lesson is pointing at photos and saying where things are. It teaches This is and Here is another, the place prepositions between, beside, along and on the left or right, and into and out of for movement in and out.
「我」拿出几张照片介绍自己的村子：村子在山谷里，夹在两座小山之间，挨着一条河。第二张照片里，「我」和妻子在河岸上散步，在左边；河里有个男孩在游泳横渡。第三张是学校，旁边是公园，在右边；一些孩子正从楼里出来，有的走进了公园。本课是指着照片讲方位，学 This is / Here is another、方位介词 between、beside、along、on the left/right，以及表示进出的 into 和 out of。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. Where is the village? Say it with three prepositions.村子在什么地方？用三个介词说出来。
  2. Which side of the photo are the speaker and his wife on, and what are they doing?「我」和妻子在照片的哪一边？在做什么？
  3. Are the children leaving the park or entering it?孩子们是从公园出来，还是进公园？

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander; New Concept English, Book 1, Longman, 1967.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为教材自撰，见《新概念英语》第一册（朗文，1967）。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

The village of the lesson is the English countryside as many town dwellers of the 1960s imagined it: a valley, two hills, a river, a school with a park beside it. Showing photographs to a visitor was then a real social ritual, done with prints passed from hand to hand, and it is a natural way to bring in This is and Here is another. The lesson sits in the run of Book 1 that teaches prepositions through pictures, and the drill lesson after it adds more movement words in the same pattern, so that the learner ends with a small kit for describing any place.

课文里的村子是 1960 年代许多城里人心目中的英国乡村：山谷、两座小山、一条河、一所旁边有公园的学校。那时候给客人看照片是真实的社交习惯——冲印出来的照片一张张递过去——用来引出 This is 和 Here is another 再自然不过。这一课处在第一册「借图学介词」的那一段，后面那课操练按同样的格局再加一批表示运动的词，读者学完就有了一套描述任何地方的小工具。

✨ How it is written赏析

The three photos do three different jobs. The first is still and wide, a map of the whole place in three short sentences. The second brings in people and two kinds of movement, one along the water and one across it, so the learner sees the difference side by side. The third ends with movement in opposite directions, out of one place and into another, which is the hardest pair and so comes last. The speaker keeps placing himself in the scene, on the left, as if pointing with a finger, and that small detail makes the description feel like talk rather than a list.

三张照片各干一件事。第一张是静止的远景，三句短话画出整个地方的地图。第二张带进了人和两种运动——一个沿着水，一个横过水——让读者把两者的差别并排看清楚。第三张收在方向相反的运动上：从一个地方出来、进到另一个地方，这是最难的一对，所以放在最后。说话人不断把自己放进画面里，在左边，就像在用手指着，这个小细节让描写听起来像在聊天，而不是开清单。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The prepositions are timeless, and the two that cause Chinese learners most trouble are both here. Along and across are both translated with words for going past, so they get mixed up; the picture of a man walking by the river while a boy swims to the far side fixes the difference. Beside and besides look almost the same but mean next to and apart from. Today people show photos on a phone rather than as prints, but the language is unchanged: This is, Here is another, and on the left are still exactly what you would say.

这些介词不会过时，而最让中国读者头疼的两对都在这里。along 和 across 都容易被理解成「经过」，于是常常混用；一个人在河边走、一个男孩游向对岸，这幅画面正好把差别钉住。beside 和 besides 长得几乎一样，意思却是「旁边」和「除了」。今天人们给别人看照片是在手机上，而不是拿冲印照片，但语言没变：This is、Here is another、on the left，照样是你会说的话。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 4第 4 句 (begins开头 “Our village is …”)

  • In sentence 6, on a river does not mean floating on it; it means beside it.第 6 句的 on a river 不是「在河面上」，而是「在河边、临河」。

Sentence 8第 8 句 (begins开头 “My wife and …”)

  • When you mention yourself with someone else, English puts I last.My wife and I：说到自己和别人时，英语习惯把 I 放后面。
  • along means following a line; banks is plural because a river has two sides, or a long stretch of side.along 表示沿着一条线走；banks 用复数，指河的两岸或长长的岸边。

Sentence 11第 11 句 (begins开头 “He is swimming …”)

  • across is not along: it means from one side to the far side.across 和 along 不同：横着从这一岸到对岸。

Sentence 16第 16 句 (begins开头 “Some children are …”)

  • come out of means moving from inside to outside; go into means moving inside.come out of：从里面出来；go into：进到里面去。
  • some of them means part of that group.some of them：他们中的一些人。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

Prepositions of place表示位置的介词
in for inside, on for on top of or beside a river, between for the space separating two things, beside for right next to, and on the left or on the right for a side.
in（在……里）、on（在……上；临河）、between（两者之间）、beside（紧挨着）、on the left / on the right（在左边 / 右边）。

The village is placed with three of these in sentences 4 to 7, and the school and park with two more in sentences 14 and 16.

本课：村子 in a valley、between two hills、on a river（第 4–6 句）；学校 beside a park，公园 on the right（第 14–15 句）。

  • The cinema is between the bank and the hotel.
  • My seat is on the left, beside the window.
Prepositions of movement表示运动方向的介词
along means following a line, across means from one side to the other, into means moving inside, and out of means moving outside. They go with walk, swim, come and go.
along（沿着）、across（横穿）、into（进入）、out of（从……出来），常与 walk、swim、come、go 连用。

Sentence 8 follows the bank, sentence 11 crosses the water, and sentences 16 and 18 show one movement out and one in.

本课：第 8 句沿河岸走，第 11 句游过河，第 17、18 句一出一进。

  • We walked along the beach.
  • A man came out of the shop and got into a taxi.
This is ... and Here is another ...
Use This is ... to show the first thing, and Here is another ... for the next one of the same kind. another takes a singular noun.
介绍第一件东西用 This is …；再拿出一件同类的用 Here is another …。another 后接单数名词。

Sentence 3 shows the first photo, and sentences 7 and 13 use another for the next ones.

本课：第 3 句介绍第一张照片，第 7 句和第 12 句用 another 引出下一张。

  • This is my brother. Here is another photo of him.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • This is a photo of my …. 这是我的……的照片。
    This is a photo of my new flat.
  • Our … is between two …. 我们的……在两个……之间。
    Our flat is between two shops.
  • Here's another photo of …. 这是……的另一张照片。
    Here's another photo of the lake.
  • … is beside a … . The … is on the right. ……在……旁边，……在右边。
    The café is beside a bank. The bank is on the right.
  • Some of them are heading into …. 他们中有些人正走进……
    Some of them are heading into the cinema.
