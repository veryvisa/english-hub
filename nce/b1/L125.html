Lesson 125–126 · Tea for Two 两个人的茶

📘 New Concept English · Book 1 · Lesson 125–126 “Tea for Two”新概念英语 第一册 Lesson 125–126《Tea for Two》

B1🧠 17 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
Susan calls Peter inside for tea, but he says he cannot come yet: the garden needs water first. The ground is very dry, just like last summer, when he had the job every single day. Susan decides to drink her tea alone. A moment later Peter walks in. Rain has started, so there is no watering to do and he can join her after all. This is a homely talk between husband and wife that teaches must and have to, the past form had to, don't need to, by myself, and instead.
苏珊叫彼得进屋喝茶，彼得说不行，得先给花园浇水：天太干了，去年夏天也这样，他天天都得浇。苏珊只好说那她自己喝。没过一会儿彼得就进来了——外面下雨了，不用浇水了，正好可以喝茶。本课是夫妻间的家常对话，学must 与 have to（必须）、had to（过去必须）、don't need to（不必）、by myself 和 instead。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. Why won't Peter come in straight away?彼得为什么不肯马上进屋？
  2. What did Peter do last summer?去年夏天彼得做了什么？
  3. What changed Peter's plan in the end?最后是什么让彼得改了主意？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander; New Concept English, Book 1, Longman, 1967. The title borrows the name of a well-known 1920s song, Tea for Two, from the musical No, No, Nanette.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为教材自撰，见《新概念英语》第一册（朗文，1967）。标题借用了 1920 年代名曲《Tea for Two》，出自音乐剧《No, No, Nanette》。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

Afternoon tea at home was a fixed point of the British day, and a garden was a matter of pride for many families in the suburbs. Dry summers meant long evenings with a watering can or hose; in very dry years local water boards banned hoses altogether. The weather is the most common British topic of talk, so a sudden shower that solves a problem is a very British happy ending.

在家喝下午茶是英国人一天里固定的时刻，郊区很多家庭以自家花园为傲。干旱的夏天意味着傍晚要拎着水壶或拿水管浇很久；特别干的年份，地方供水部门会干脆禁用水管。天气是英国人最常聊的话题，所以一场及时雨解决难题，是很英国式的圆满结局。

✨ How it is written赏析

The dialogue sets up a small conflict, tea against duty, and the weather settles it without anyone having to give way. Each step is carried by a modal: must for Peter's sense of duty, had to for the memory of last year, and don't need to for the relief. The last line, with instead, makes a neat full circle back to the tea.

对话设了一个小冲突——喝茶还是尽责——最后由天气来解决，谁都不用让步。每一步都由情态动词推动：must 是彼得的责任感，had to 是去年的回忆，don't need to 是如释重负。最后一句的 instead 又干净利落地回到了茶。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The biggest trap here is mustn't. Many learners think it means not necessary, but it means not allowed; a Canadian sign that reads You must not park here is a rule, while You don't need to book is good news. Hearing the difference saves real trouble with parking, work rules and exams. In the Lower Mainland, summer watering restrictions are common, so the scene is quite familiar: city websites list which days each address may water.

这一课最大的坑是 mustn't。很多学习者以为它是「不必」，其实是「不准」；加拿大标牌上写 You must not park here 是规定，而 You don't need to book 是好消息。分清这两种说法，在停车、工作规定、考试上都能省掉真麻烦。大温地区夏天常有浇水限制，所以这一幕很眼熟：市政网站会列出每个地址哪几天可以浇水。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 5第 5 句 (begins开头 “I must water …”)

  • must plus the base verb shows the speaker feels it is necessary.must + 动词原形：必须，表示说话人自己觉得非做不可。
  • first means before anything else.first：先（再做别的）。

Sentence 6第 6 句 (begins开头 “Do you have …”)

  • To ask whether something is necessary, Do you have to is common.问「必须吗」，常用 Do you have to …?
  • I'm afraid I must is a polite way to insist.I'm afraid I must：恐怕必须，礼貌地坚持。

Sentence 13第 13 句 (begins开头 “I had to …”)

  • must has no past form, so the past is had to.must 没有过去式，过去「必须」用 had to。

Sentence 20第 20 句 (begins开头 “That means you …”)

  • mustn't means it is not allowed, not unnecessary.不能说 mustn't 表示「不必」；mustn't 是「不准」。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

must and have tomust 与 have to
Both mean it is necessary. must often shows the speaker's own view, while have to often points to a rule or a situation. must has only a present form; for the past and future use had to and will have to. Questions are usually Do you have to?
两者都表示「必须」。must 偏说话人自己的看法（我觉得必须），have to 偏外部情况或规定（不得不）。must 只有现在形式，过去和将来用 had to / will have to。问句常说 Do you have to …?

Sentence 5 has must, sentence 6 Do you have to, and sentence 13 the past form had to.

本课：第 5 句 must，第 6 句 Do you have to，第 13 句过去式 had to。

  • I must call my mum tonight.
  • We had to wait two hours at the airport.
Not necessary: don't need to, don't have to, needn't不必：don't need to / don't have to / needn't
To say something is not necessary, use don't need to, don't have to or needn't plus the base verb. mustn't means something quite different: it is not allowed.
表示「不必」，用 don't need to、don't have to 或 needn't + 动词原形。mustn't 意思完全不同，是「不准、禁止」。

Sentence 20 uses don't need to.

本课：第 20 句用 don't need to。

  • It's Saturday. You don't have to get up early.
  • You mustn't park here — it's a bus stop.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • I must … first. 我得先……
    I must finish this email first.
  • Do I have to … now? 我现在就得……吗？
    Do I have to pay now?
  • Last year I had to … every week. 去年我每周都得……
    Last year I had to drive to Surrey every week.
  • You don't need to … 你不必……
    You don't need to bring anything.
  • I'll … by myself. 我自己……
    Fine, I'll go to the movies by myself.

Our model summary本站范文

Susan asked Peter to come in for tea, but he said the garden was very dry and he must water it first. Last summer he had watered it every day. Susan said she would drink alone. Soon Peter came in: rain was falling, so the watering was not necessary and he could have tea.（54 词）
