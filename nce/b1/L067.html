Lesson 67–68 · The Weekend 周末

📘 New Concept English · Book 1 · Lesson 67–68 “The Weekend”新概念英语 第一册 Lesson 67–68《The Weekend》

B1🧠 15 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
Two women meet in the street. One has just been to the butcher's and the other to the shop that sells fruit and vegetables. Mrs. Johnson asks after Jimmy, who is fine now, and checks whether he missed school last week; he did, for four days running. Asked about her own family, she says happily that they are off to the country for three days, staying with her mother from Friday to Sunday. Her friend says how lucky they are. This lesson is small talk in the street: where you have been, how the children are, how the family is, and plans for the weekend. It teaches the past of be with was and were in questions and answers, shop names like the butcher's, be absent from, the days of the week, and exclamations such as Aren't you lucky!
两位太太在街上碰见。一位刚从肉铺出来，另一位去的是蔬菜水果店。约翰逊太太问吉米今天怎么样——很好；上周他是不是没去上学？是的，周一到周四都没去。问起家里人，约翰逊太太高兴地说：她们一家要去乡下过三天，周五、周六、周日都住在她母亲家。对方羡慕地说：你们真幸运！本课是街头寒暄：问在哪儿、问孩子、问家人、说周末计划。学 be 的过去式 was / were 的问答、the butcher's 这类「店铺所有格」、be absent from、星期名称，以及 Aren't you lucky! 这种感叹。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. Which shop has each woman just been to?两位太太刚才各去了哪家店？
  2. Which days did Jimmy miss school last week, and how does that link to the last lessons?吉米上周哪几天没上学？这和前几课有什么联系？
  3. How do the Johnsons plan to spend their weekend?约翰逊一家周末要做什么？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander; New Concept English, Book 1, Longman, 1967.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为教材自撰，见《新概念英语》第一册（朗文，1967）。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

In a British town of the 1960s, shopping meant a walk down the high street from one small shop to the next: the butcher for meat, the greengrocer for fruit and vegetables, the baker for bread. Supermarkets were only beginning to appear, so meeting a neighbour between shops was a daily event and a natural place for news. A long weekend in the country, staying with a parent, was a common pleasure for town families who did not own a holiday home. The four days Jimmy missed are the week in bed ordered by the doctor two lessons ago.

在 1960 年代的英国小城，买东西就是沿着商业街从一家小店走到下一家：肉铺买肉，蔬果店买水果蔬菜，面包店买面包。超市才刚刚出现，所以在两家店之间碰到邻居是每天都有的事，也是交换消息的天然场所。到乡下父母家过一个长周末，是没有度假屋的城里人家常见的享受。吉米缺的那四天课，正是两课前医生要求的卧床。

✨ How it is written赏析

The lesson is a model of how small talk moves. It starts with the place where you meet, goes on to a question about the other family's child, then to a polite question about everyone's health, and only then lets the speaker share her own good news. Each turn is short, and the answers repeat the verb of the question, which is exactly how natural English short answers work. The three days are listed twice, once as a number and once by name, and the excitement comes from the repetition rather than from any adjective. The friend's last line is a negative question used as an exclamation, a very English way of being pleased for someone.

本课示范了寒暄是怎么推进的。先从碰面的地方说起，接着问对方的孩子，再礼貌地问全家身体，最后才让说话人讲出自己的好消息。每一轮都很短，答句重复问句里的动词，这正是自然英语简答的方式。那三天被说了两遍，一次是数字，一次是星期名，兴奋感来自重复，而不是形容词。朋友最后那句是用否定疑问句表达的感叹，是很英式的「替别人高兴」的说法。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The shops in this lesson have mostly disappeared from Canadian life, replaced by supermarkets, though some cities still have butchers and fruit shops, and many Chinese families in Vancouver shop at small Asian grocers in the same way. The grammar of short answers is still essential: Canadians answer Were you at the game? with Yes, I was, not just Yes. Two words have shifted: in Canada people say on the weekend rather than at the weekend, and How are you keeping? sounds old-fashioned; How are you all doing? is the everyday choice. Asking about someone's children before your own news is still good manners everywhere.

本课里的那些小店大多已从加拿大生活中消失，被超市取代，不过有些城市还有肉铺和水果店，温哥华的许多华人家庭也照样在小型亚洲食品店买东西。简答的语法依然必不可少：加拿大人回答 Were you at the game? 会说 Yes, I was，而不只是 Yes。有两个说法变了：加拿大人说 on the weekend 而不是 at the weekend；How are you keeping? 听起来老派，日常用 How are you all doing?。先问对方孩子、再讲自己的消息，到哪里都是好礼貌。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentence 3第 3 句 (begins开头 “Hello. Were you …”)

  • The question uses were with you, but the answer changes to was with I.Were you …? 的简答是 Yes, I was. / No, I wasn't.：问句用 you were，答句换成 I was。
  • the butcher's means the butcher's shop, with shop left out.the butcher's：肉铺。's 后面省去了 shop。

Sentence 7第 7 句 (begins开头 “I was at …”)

  • too at the end means also.too：也，放在句末。

Sentence 10第 10 句 (begins开头 “Was he absent …”)

  • be absent from means not present at; no preposition goes before last week.be absent from：缺席。last week 前不加介词。

Sentence 12第 12 句 (begins开头 “He was absent …”)

  • Days of the week take on, and and joins the last two in a list.星期前用 on；列举时最后两项之间用 and。

Sentence 13第 13 句 (begins开头 “How are you …”)

  • A British way to ask about health; all means the whole family.How are you keeping?：英式寒暄「近来身体好吗」。all 指你们全家。

Sentence 16第 16 句 (begins开头 “We're going to …”)

  • spend plus a length of time; in the country means away from towns.spend + 时间：度过。in the country：在乡下。
  • my mother's means my mother's house, the same pattern as the butcher's.my mother's：我母亲家，同 the butcher's 一样省了名词（house）。

Sentence 18第 18 句 (begins开头 “Aren't you lucky! …”)

  • A negative question used as an exclamation, not a real question.否定疑问句形式的感叹：你们真幸运！不是真的提问。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

The past of be: was and werebe 的过去式：was / were
Use was with I, he, she and it, and were with you, we and they. Questions put was or were first; negatives are wasn't and weren't.
I / he / she / it 用 was，you / we / they 用 were。问句把 was / were 提前；否定 wasn't / weren't；简答 Yes, I was. / No, he wasn't.

Sentences 3 to 8 ask which shop each was at, and 11 to 13 ask about Jimmy's absence, all with was and were.

本课：第 3–7 句两位太太问在哪家店，第 10–12 句问吉米缺课，全是 was / were 的问答。

  • Were they at home last night? — No, they weren't.
  • It was very cold on Saturday.
Shops and homes with 's店铺所有格：the butcher's、my mother's
When shop, house or office is left out after 's, the phrase means that place.
名词所有格后面省去 shop / house / office 等，就指那个地方：the butcher's（肉铺）、the dentist's（牙医诊所）、my aunt's（姑妈家）。

Sentences 3 and 8 name two shops, and sentence 16 the mother's home.

本课：第 4、8 句是两家店，第 16 句是母亲家。

  • I'm going to the baker's for some bread.
  • We had dinner at my uncle's.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • Were you at the …? — Yes, I was. / No, I wasn't. 你刚才在……吗？——是的。／不是。
    Were you at the bank? — No, I wasn't.
  • X was absent from … on … X 在……那天没去……
    Lily was absent from work on Friday.
  • We're going to spend … in … 我们要在……度过……
    We're going to spend a week in Banff.
  • How are you all doing? — Very well, thanks. 你们全家都好吗？——很好，谢谢。
    How are you all doing? — Very well, thanks.
  • Aren't you lucky! 你真幸运！
    Two weeks off? Aren't you lucky!
