Lesson 97–98 · A Small Blue Case 一个蓝色小箱子

📘 New Concept English · Book 1 · Lesson 97–98 “A Small Blue Case”新概念英语 第一册 Lesson 97–98《A Small Blue Case》

B1🧠 16 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
Mr. Hall left a suitcase on a London train a few days ago and has come to the lost property office. He is asked to describe it: small, blue, with a zip, and a tag on the handle showing his name and address. The first case is not his. The second has a tag that seems to match, so he pays a fee of three pounds fifty. Then he looks again and finds it is somebody else's after all. This lesson teaches possessive pronouns such as mine, yours and hers, together with belong to, describe, the other day, and What's the matter?
霍尔先生前几天把一个手提箱落在了去伦敦的火车上，来失物招领处认领。工作人员请他描述：一个蓝色小箱子，带拉链，把手上挂着写有姓名地址的标签。第一个箱子不是他的；第二个有标签，名字地址都对得上。他付了三英镑五十便士的手续费——打开一看，却发现箱子不是自己的。本课学名词性物主代词（mine、yours、his、hers）、Is this case yours?、belong to、describe、the other day 与 What's the matter?

  2. How is the name on the label different from his?标签上写的名字和他的名字有什么不同？
  3. What goes wrong in the end?最后出了什么问题？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander; New Concept English, Book 1, Longman, 1967.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为教材自撰，见《新概念英语》第一册（朗文，1967）。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

British Rail ran lost property offices at its large London stations, where things left on trains were sent, logged and kept for a time. An owner had to describe the item before it was handed over, and a small fee was charged for the service, which is why Mr. Hall pays before he goes. Labels on luggage, with the owner's name and address written by hand, were the usual way of marking a case, long before printed tags or tracking devices. Three pounds fifty pence also shows that the lesson's prices come from the decimal currency, which Britain adopted in 1971, after the first edition; the pound had earlier been divided into shillings.

英国铁路在伦敦各大车站设有失物招领处，乘客落在车上的东西会送到那里登记、保管一段时间。失主必须先描述物品才能领回，还要交一笔小额手续费，所以霍尔先生得先付钱。行李上挂手写姓名地址的标签，是当时标记箱子的常规做法，远在印刷标签和定位器出现之前。三英镑五十便士也说明课文里的价钱来自十进制货币，英国 1971 年才改用，晚于初版；此前一英镑分成若干先令。

✨ How it is written赏析

The lesson plays a quiet trick on the reader. The two names are printed one after the other, and they look almost the same, so the reader, like the clerk, accepts the match. The middle initial is the only clue, and it passes in a flash. Then the business of paying and thanking closes the scene as if it were over, and the shout that follows reopens it. The real lesson in grammar hides inside the joke: the whole story turns on the difference between mine, yours and someone else's.

课文跟读者玩了一个不动声色的把戏。两个名字一前一后印出来，看上去几乎一样，于是读者也像工作人员一样认了。中间名的缩写是唯一的线索，一闪而过。接着付钱、道谢，好像这一幕已经结束，紧跟着一声喊又把它掀开。真正的语法课就藏在笑话里：整个故事的关键，正是「我的」「你的」和「别人的」之间的区别。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The scene works just as well today, with one change: the clerk would check photo ID, not just a name and address. In Metro Vancouver, items left on buses and SkyTrain go to TransLink's lost property office, and airports and ferries have their own; you describe the item, show identification, and collect it. For learners, the key point is leave with a place: Chinese speakers often say I forgot my phone in the taxi, which sounds odd in English, where forget is used without a place. Also note that mine, yours and hers never take an apostrophe, a very common written mistake even among native speakers.

这一幕今天照样成立，只有一处不同：工作人员会核对带照片的证件，而不只是姓名和地址。在大温哥华，落在公交和架空列车上的东西送到 TransLink 的失物招领处，机场和渡轮各有自己的招领处；你描述物品、出示证件、领回。对学习者来说，关键是「leave + 地点」：中国人常说 I forgot my phone in the taxi，英语里听着别扭，forget 后面一般不跟地点。还要注意 mine、yours、hers 永远不加撇号，这是连母语者也常犯的书写错误。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 3第 3 句 (begins开头 “I left a …”)

  • leave something in a place means you went away without it. With a place, English uses leave, not forget.leave sth + 地点：把东西落在某处。说地点时用 leave 不用 forget。
  • the other day means a few days ago, without saying exactly when.the other day：前几天（不确指）。

Sentence 4第 4 句 (begins开头 “Can you describe …”)

  • describe takes a direct object, with no about.describe sth：描述某物，后面直接跟宾语。
  • it's got is British spoken English for it has.it's got = it has got：有（英式口语）。

Sentence 6第 6 句 (begins开头 “There's a label …”)

  • The with phrase tells you what is written on the label.with … on it：上面写着……，补充说明标签。

Sentence 7第 7 句 (begins开头 “Is this case …”)

  • yours and mine stand alone and mean your case and my case.yours / mine：名词性物主代词，单独使用，= your case / my case。

Sentence 9第 9 句 (begins开头 “What about this …”)

  • one stands for case so the noun is not repeated.this one：one 代替前面提到的 case，避免重复。
  • What about ...? offers another choice.What about …?：……怎么样？

Sentence 21第 21 句 (begins开头 “This case doesn't …”)

  • The thing is the subject, and belong is not used in the continuous.belong to sb：属于某人，物作主语，不用进行时。
  • the wrong one is not the one you wanted.the wrong case：给错的那个箱子。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

Possessive adjectives and possessive pronouns形容词性与名词性物主代词
Possessive adjectives come before a noun: my, your, his, her, its, our, their. Possessive pronouns stand alone: mine, yours, his, hers, ours, theirs. his is the same in both, and none of them has an apostrophe.
形容词性（后面跟名词）：my、your、his、her、its、our、their。名词性（单独用）：mine、yours、his、hers、ours、theirs。his 两种都一样；its 一般不单独用。写法上没有撇号：yours，不是 your's。

Sentence 7 asks with yours, and sentence 8 answers with mine.

本课：第 7 句问 yours，第 8 句答 mine。

  • This is my seat. That one is yours.
  • Their car is red, and ours is white.
belong to and Whose ...?belong to 与 Whose …?
To ask who owns something, say Whose ... is this? or Who does it belong to? To answer, use a possessive pronoun, belongs to, or a name with 's. belong is a state verb and is not used in the continuous.
问物主：Whose case is this? / Who does this case belong to?。答：It's mine. / It belongs to me. / It's David's.。belong 是状态动词，不说 is belonging。

In sentence 21 he says the case is not his.

本课：第 21 句说箱子不属于他。

  • Whose gloves are these? — They're Tom's.
  • This house belongs to my uncle.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • I left my … on the … a few days ago. 我前几天把……落在了……上。
    I left my laptop on the SkyTrain a few days ago.
  • It's a … with a … on the side. 那是一个侧面有……的……
    It's a black backpack with a pocket on the side.
  • Is this … yours? — No, it isn't mine. 这……是你的吗？——不是我的。
    Is this jacket yours? — No, it isn't mine.
  • What about that one? 那个呢？
    What about that one, the green one?
  • This … isn't mine! You've given me the wrong one! 这……不是我的！你给错了！
    This key isn't mine! You've given me the wrong one!
