Lesson 119–120 · A True Story 一个真实的故事

📘 New Concept English · Book 1 · Lesson 119–120 “A True Story”新概念英语 第一册 Lesson 119–120《A True Story》

B1🧠 20 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
The speaker promises a true story about a friend, George, from a year back. While George was lying upstairs with a book, two burglars got into his kitchen. Once inside, they moved to the dining room and, because it was pitch black, switched on a torch. All at once a voice behind them asked what was going on. The burglars dropped the torch and fled as fast as possible. George heard the crash, hurried down, and put the light on, but the room was empty; the men were long gone. Only his parrot, Henry, remained, asking the same question. George smiled and told him to go back to sleep. This is a complete short story that teaches the past continuous with while, the past perfect after after and with already, as ... as they could, a friend of mine, What's up?, and so for a result.
讲述人说要讲一个真实的故事，发生在他朋友乔治身上，一年前的事。乔治在床上看书时，两个小偷爬进了他家厨房。进屋后，他们走进餐厅，屋里很黑，就打开了手电筒。突然身后有个声音喊：「怎么了？怎么了？」小偷扔下手电筒，拼命逃走了。乔治听到响声，赶紧下楼，开了灯，却一个人也没看见——小偷早跑了。只有乔治的鹦鹉亨利还在那儿：「怎么了，乔治？」乔治笑着说：「没事，亨利，接着睡吧。」本课是一段完整的故事叙述，学过去进行时 + while、过去完成时 after they had entered / had already gone、as … as they could、a friend of mine、What's up? 与 so 表结果。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. When and where did the thieves get in?小偷是什么时候、从哪里进屋的？
  2. Why did the thieves run away?小偷为什么逃跑了？
  3. Who called out in the dark?喊「怎么了？」的是谁？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander; New Concept English, Book 1, Longman, 1967. Stories of a talking parrot frightening burglars are a familiar type of comic tale, and we have not traced this one to a real event.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为教材自撰，见《新概念英语》第一册（朗文，1967）。会说话的鹦鹉吓跑窃贼，是一类常见的滑稽故事，这一个我们没有查到对应的真实事件。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

Pet parrots, especially African greys and budgerigars, were popular in British homes, and a bird that could say a few words was a common source of family pride. Parrots copy phrases they hear often, so a bird that says What's up, George? has probably heard its owner say What's up? many times. Burglary in a house at night while the owner was upstairs was a common worry, and houses often had a back kitchen door that was easier to force than the front. The title, A true story, is part of the game: it promises fact, but the shape of the tale is a joke.

宠物鹦鹉，尤其是非洲灰鹦鹉和虎皮鹦鹉，在英国家庭很受欢迎，一只会说几个词的鸟常常是全家的骄傲。鹦鹉会模仿常听到的话，所以一只说 What's up, George? 的鸟，多半是常听主人说 What's up?。主人在楼上、夜里有人入室行窃，是当时常见的担心；很多房子的后厨房门比前门更容易撬开。标题「一个真实的故事」本身就是游戏的一部分：它承诺讲事实，故事的形态却是一个笑话。

✨ How it is written赏析

This is the most complete story in the second half of Book One, and it shows the whole toolkit of narrative tense in a small space. The background runs in the past continuous, the main events in the simple past, and the past perfect marks the two moments when one action is already finished before the next. The writer hides the speaker's identity until almost the end, so the reader first assumes a person, then a ghost, and the final reveal makes the title funny. George's gentle reply, with a smile, turns a frightening night into a domestic scene.

这是第一册后半部分最完整的故事，在小小的篇幅里展示了叙事时态的全套工具。背景用过去进行时，主要事件用一般过去时，过去完成时标出两个时刻：一个动作在下一个之前已经完成。作者把说话者的身份一直藏到快结尾，读者先以为是人，再以为是鬼，最后揭晓才让标题显得好笑。乔治带着微笑的温和回答，把一个吓人的夜晚变成了一幕家常小景。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The story works as a model for telling any anecdote in English, and it is worth retelling aloud until the tenses come without thinking. Chinese learners often tell stories only in the simple past, because Chinese marks time with words rather than verb forms; the past continuous and past perfect let English listeners follow what was going on and what came first. What's up? is now everyday North American slang for how are you or what's happening, so the parrot's line sounds more modern today than it did in 1967. For safety in Canada, the advice is the same as George's luck suggests: if you hear a break-in, do not go and confront anyone; call 911 from a safe place.

这个故事可以作为用英语讲任何趣事的范本，值得反复大声复述，直到时态不假思索就能出来。中国学习者讲故事常常只用一般过去时，因为中文靠时间词而不是动词形态表示时间；过去进行时和过去完成时让英语听众能跟上「当时正在发生什么」「哪件事先发生」。What's up? 如今是北美日常俚语，意思是「你好吗」「怎么了」，所以鹦鹉那句台词在今天听来比 1967 年更时髦。在加拿大说到安全，建议和乔治的运气提示的一样：听到有人闯入，不要去当面对质，到安全的地方打 911。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 5第 5 句 (begins开头 “It happened to …”)

  • happen to someone.happen to sb：发生在某人身上。
  • ago takes the past simple.a year ago：配一般过去时。

Sentence 6第 6 句 (begins开头 “While my friend, …”)

  • while with the past continuous for the background, the past simple for the event.while + 过去进行时（背景），主句一般过去时（发生的事）。

Sentence 8第 8 句 (begins开头 “After they had …”)

  • after with the past perfect for the earlier action.after + 过去完成时：先发生的动作。
  • enter takes a direct object.enter 直接跟宾语，不加 into。

Sentence 10第 10 句 (begins开头 “It was very …”)

  • so introduces the result; turn on means switch on.so：所以，引出结果。turn on：打开（电器）。

Sentence 13第 13 句 (begins开头 “The thieves dropped …”)

  • as quickly as they could means as fast as possible.as + 副词 + as sb could：尽可能……。
  • run away means escape; ran is the past of run.run away：逃跑，ran 是 run 的过去式。

Sentence 16第 16 句 (begins开头 “The thieves had …”)

  • The past perfect shows they left before George came down.过去完成时：乔治下楼之前，小偷已经跑了。
  • anyone in a negative sentence.couldn't see anyone：否定句用 anyone。

Sentence 19第 19 句 (begins开头 “'Nothing, Henry,' George …”)

  • Nothing is the natural answer to What's up?What's up? 的回答可以是 Nothing：没什么。
  • go back to sleep means start sleeping again.go back to sleep：接着睡。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

The past perfect: had done过去完成时：had done
had plus the past participle describes something completed before another past time or action. It is common with after, before, when, already and by the time. When after or before already makes the order clear, the past simple is also possible in speech.
had + 过去分词：在过去某个时间或动作之前已经完成的事，叫「过去的过去」。常和 after、before、when、already、by the time 连用。如果 after / before 已经把先后说清楚，口语里也可用一般过去时。

Sentence 8 has after with the past perfect, and sentence 16 has had already gone.

本课：第 8 句用 after + 过去完成时，第 16 句用 had already gone。

  • After she had finished her homework, she watched TV.
  • When we got to the cinema, the film had already started.
as ... as somebody can or could
as plus an adjective or adverb plus as somebody can or could means to the greatest degree possible. Use can for now and could for the past. as ... as possible means the same.
as + 形容词/副词原级 + as + sb + can / could：尽可能……。现在用 can，过去用 could。也可说 as … as possible。

Sentence 13 uses the past form could.

本课：第 13 句用过去式 could。

  • Please come as soon as you can.
  • She ran as fast as she could.

Our model summary本站范文

A year ago, while George was reading upstairs, two thieves got into his kitchen. They moved to the dining room, and because it was dark, they switched on a torch. Suddenly a voice behind them asked what was wrong. They dropped the torch and ran off. George hurried down and put the light on, but the men had gone. Only his parrot, Henry, was there. He had frightened them away.（70 词）
