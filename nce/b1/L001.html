Lesson 1–2 · Excuse Me 对不起

📘 New Concept English · Book 1 · Lesson 1–2 “Excuse Me”新概念英语 第一册 Lesson 1–2《Excuse Me》

B1🧠 17 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
A man stops a woman in the street. He is holding a handbag and asks whether it belongs to her. She does not catch the question and says Pardon, so he asks again. Once she has said it is hers, she thanks him. The whole lesson uses fewer than twenty words, yet it walks through one complete exchange with a stranger: getting attention, asking, asking for a repeat when you have not heard, confirming, and saying thanks. The grammar is how Excuse me and Pardon divide the work, the yes-no question with Is this your, and the short answer Yes, it is.
一位男士在路上叫住一位女士：「打扰一下！」他手里拿着一只手提包，问这是不是她的。女士没听清，说了一声「Pardon?」，他只好再问一遍。确认是她的之后，她道了谢。全课不到二十个词，却把和陌生人打交道的完整一轮都走了一遍：引起注意、提问、没听清时请对方重复、确认、道谢。学 Excuse me 与 Pardon 的分工、Is this your …? 一般疑问句和 Yes, it is 简略回答。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. Why does the man begin with Excuse me?男士为什么要说 Excuse me？
  2. What does the woman mean by Pardon, and why does she say it?女士说 Pardon? 是什么意思？她为什么这样说？
  3. In the end, who owns the bag, and which line tells you?包最后是谁的？你从哪一句知道的？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander; New Concept English was first published by Longman in 1967, and Book 1 is built round pictures, with a short story lesson followed by a drill lesson.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为教材自撰；《新概念英语》1967 年由朗文初版。第一册以图画为主线，每一课故事后面跟一课句型操练。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

Book 1 is designed for learners who start from nothing. The odd lessons are small scenes with pictures; the even lessons that follow drill the same pattern with new nouns, which is why the second lesson is a list of everyday things such as pens, watches and coats. The first scene is chosen with care. Returning something a stranger has dropped is a moment every learner can picture, it needs only a handful of words, and it ends well.

第一册是给零起点读者的。单数课是带图的小场景，紧跟着的双数课用同一个句型换名词反复操练，所以第二课是一串日常物品：笔、手表、大衣。第一个场景选得很用心：把陌生人掉的东西还给她，每个人都能想象；只要几个词；结局还是好的。

✨ How it is written赏析

The dialogue has exactly the shape of a real exchange with a stranger. First an opener that asks for attention, then a reply that grants it, then the question, then a failure to hear, then the repeat, then the answer and the thanks. Nothing is wasted, and the misunderstanding in the middle is not a joke but the most useful line in the lesson: it teaches the learner that asking again is normal. The same word, said twice in different tunes, also shows from the first page that English meaning lives in the voice as well as in the words.

这段对话的结构和现实中跟陌生人打交道一模一样：先引起注意，对方回应，然后提问，没听清，再问一遍，最后回答和道谢。一句不多，中间那一下没听清也不是为了好笑，而是全课最有用的一句：它告诉读者，再问一遍是正常的。同一个词用不同的语调说出来，也让读者从第一页就知道，英语的意思不只在词里，也在声音里。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

For many Chinese learners the hard part is not the words but the habit. In Chinese we often open with a plain hello or simply start talking, and asking someone to repeat can feel like losing face. In English-speaking countries a quick Excuse me before you speak to a stranger is close to compulsory, and Sorry? or Pardon? after a missed word is completely ordinary; nodding without understanding causes far more trouble later. Pardon itself now sounds a little formal in Britain, but it is never wrong, and it is a far better habit than a bare What?

对很多中国读者来说，难的不是词，而是习惯。中文里我们常常直接说「你好」甚至直接开口，请别人重复又容易觉得丢面子。在英语国家，跟陌生人开口前先说一句 Excuse me 几乎是必须的；没听清说 Sorry? 或 Pardon? 再平常不过。没听懂却点头，后面的麻烦大得多。Pardon 现在在英国听起来略显正式，但从来不会错，比一个光秃秃的 What? 好得多。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 5第 5 句 (begins开头 “Yes? …”)

Yes?

  • Said with a rising voice, Yes? does not mean I agree. It means What is it? and is the natural answer when someone stops you.读升调的 Yes? 不是「是的」，而是「什么事？」——被人叫住时的回应。
  • The same word with a falling voice agrees, and with a rising voice asks. In English the tune of a word can change what it means.同一个词，降调是肯定，升调是询问。英语里语调能改变意思。

Sentence 7第 7 句 (begins开头 “Pardon? …”)

Pardon?

  • Pardon? is short for I beg your pardon? and asks the speaker to say it again. It must rise at the end.Pardon? 是 I beg your pardon? 的省略，请对方再说一遍，要读升调。
  • Asking at once is better than pretending you heard. Today many British speakers say Sorry? instead, and many Americans say Excuse me? or Come again?没听清就直接问，比装懂要好。现在英国人也常说 Sorry?，美国人常说 Excuse me? 或 Come again?。

Sentence 9第 9 句 (begins开头 “Yes it is. …”)

Yes, it is.

  • The short answer to Is this ...? is Yes, it is or No, it isn't. English does not answer with Yes, this is.简略回答：问句用 Is this …?，答句用 Yes, it is. 或 No, it isn't.，不说 Yes, this is.。
  • In a short yes answer, it is cannot be shortened to it's.肯定的简略回答 it is 不能缩成 it's。

Sentence 10第 10 句 (begins开头 “Thank you very …”)

Thank you very much.

  • very much goes with thank and makes it stronger. Thank you very on its own is not English.very much 修饰 thank，表示「非常」。不能说 Thank you very。
  • Always thank someone who has helped you. Common replies are You're welcome and Not at all.别人帮了你，一定要道谢；常见回答是 You're welcome. / Not at all.。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

Yes-no questions with be: Is this ...?be 动词的一般疑问句：Is this …?
Take the statement This is your handbag and move is to the front: Is this your handbag? The question ends with a question mark and the voice rises.
陈述句 This is your handbag. 把 is 提到句首就成了一般疑问句 Is this your handbag?，句末用问号，读升调。

Sentences 6 and 8 are the same yes-no question; the man has to ask it twice.

本课：第 6 句和第 8 句是同一个一般疑问句，男士问了两遍。

  • This is your pen. → Is this your pen?
  • It is a new watch. → Is it a new watch?
Short answers: Yes, it is. / No, it isn't.简略回答：Yes, it is. / No, it isn't.
To answer Is this ...?, change the subject to it and say only Yes, it is or No, it isn't. The yes form is never shortened, but the no form usually is.
回答这类问句时，主语一律换成 it，肯定说 Yes, it is.，否定说 No, it isn't.；肯定回答不缩写，否定回答常缩写。

In sentence 9 the woman uses the short answer to say the bag is hers.

本课：第 9 句女士用简略回答确认包是她的。

  • Is this your watch? — Yes, it is.
  • Is this your car? — No, it isn't.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • Excuse me! Is this your …? 打扰一下！这是你的……吗？
    Excuse me! Is this your umbrella?
  • Pardon? / Sorry? 请再说一遍？
    Sorry? The music is too loud.
  • Thank you very much. 非常感谢。
    Thank you very much for the lovely card.
  • Is this your …? — Yes, it is. / No, it isn't. 这是你的……吗？——是的。／不是。
    Is this your seat? — No, it isn't.
