Lesson 9–10 · How Are You Today 你今天好吗？

📘 New Concept English · Book 1 · Lesson 9–10 “How Are You Today”新概念英语 第一册 Lesson 9–10《How Are You Today》

B1🧠 15 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
Steven meets Helen in the street and they greet each other: how are you today, very well, thanks, and you? Then each asks after the other's family, Tony and Emma, and both are fine. At the end they say how good it is to see each other and part. This lesson is a greeting between people who already know each other, which is different from the first meeting in Lesson 5. The grammar is How are you? and How is ...? with their answers, very well and fine, the return question And you?, and Nice to see you.
史蒂文在路上遇到海伦，两人互相问好：「你今天好吗？」「很好，谢谢，你呢？」接着又问候对方的家人——托尼好吗，艾玛好吗——都很好。最后说「见到你真好」，道别。本课是熟人之间的问候，和第 5 课初次见面的问候不一样。学 How are you? / How is …? 问候与回答、very well 与 fine、And you? 回问和 Nice to see you。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. How is Emma, and who says so?艾玛好吗？是谁说的？
  2. Whose health do Steven and Helen ask about?史蒂文和海伦互相问候了谁？
  3. Before they part, why do they use see and not the meet of Lesson 5?两人道别前为什么用 see，而不用第 5 课的 meet？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander; New Concept English, Book 1, Longman, 1967. The lesson pairs with Lesson 5 so that the learner meets both kinds of greeting early.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为教材自撰，见《新概念英语》第一册（朗文，1967）。它和第 5 课配成一对，让读者尽早学会两种问候。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

Lesson 5 taught the greeting for strangers; this one teaches the greeting for people who already know each other, with the family brought in. Asking after a person's husband, wife or children is a mark of friendship in English as in Chinese, and the drill lesson after it adds a set of simple adjectives such as busy, tired, hot and cold, so that the learner can say more than fine.

第 5 课教的是陌生人之间的问候；这一课教熟人之间的，还把家人带了进来。在英语里和在中文里一样，问候对方的丈夫、妻子或孩子是关系好的标志。后面那课操练加了一组简单形容词：忙、累、热、冷，让读者不只会说 fine。

✨ How it is written赏析

The dialogue is shaped like a game of catch. Every question is caught and thrown back: how are you, and you, how is he, how is she, nice to see you, you too. There is no news, no story and no problem, which is the point. Small talk in English is not meant to carry information; it is meant to show that the line between two people is still open. The lesson ends with a full Goodbye, which in daily life is often softened to bye or see you.

这段对话像一场抛接球：每个问题都被接住再扔回去——你好吗、你呢、他好吗、她好吗、见到你真好、我也是。没有新闻，没有故事，也没有问题，这正是关键。英语里的闲聊不是为了传递信息，而是表示两个人之间那条线还通着。全课以完整的 Goodbye 结束，日常生活里常常换成更轻的 bye 或 see you。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The hardest thing for many Chinese learners is that How are you? is usually not a real question. In Canada a shop assistant who asks it expects Good, thanks, and you?, not a report on your health. Answering with your real troubles to a stranger feels odd to them, just as a detailed answer to a casual greeting would in Chinese. With friends it is different, and a real answer is welcome. Note also that I'm good is now the most common answer in North America, even though older grammar books mark it as wrong; I'm well and I'm fine are both safe everywhere.

对很多中国读者来说，最难的是 How are you? 通常不是真的在问。在加拿大，店员这么问，期待的是 Good, thanks, and you?，而不是你的健康报告。对陌生人倒出真实的烦恼会让对方别扭，就像中文里有人对一句客套问候认真作答一样。跟朋友就不一样了，真实的回答是受欢迎的。还要注意，I'm good 现在是北美最常见的回答，尽管老语法书把它判为错误；I'm well 和 I'm fine 在哪里都稳妥。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 7第 7 句 (begins开头 “I'm very well …”)

I'm very well, thank you.

  • The usual answers to How are you? are I'm very well or I'm fine, followed by thank you or thanks. Here well is an adjective meaning in good health.回答 How are you? 的常见说法：I'm very well / I'm fine, thank you / thanks.。这里 well 是形容词「身体好」。
  • First say how you are, then thank the speaker, with a comma before thank you.要先说「好」，再说「谢谢」；thank you 前加逗号。

Sentence 8第 8 句 (begins开头 “And you? …”)

And you?

  • And you? is short for And how are you? Not asking back can sound cold.回问对方「你呢？」，是 And how are you? 的省略。不回问会显得冷淡。
  • The stress falls on you.读的时候重音在 you 上。

Sentence 10第 10 句 (begins开头 “How is Tony? …”)

How is Tony?

  • How is plus a name asks after a third person. The next question uses the short form How's.How is + 人？问第三方的近况。下一句用了缩写 How's。
  • In the answer the name becomes he or she: He's fine, thanks.回答主语换成 he/she：He's fine, thanks.。

Sentence 16第 16 句 (begins开头 “Nice to see …”)

  • see is for someone you already know; for a first meeting use meet, as in Lesson 5.see 用于已经认识的人；初次见面用 meet（第 5 课）。
  • When the other person says it first, add too in your reply; adding their name makes it warmer.对方先说了，自己回应时加 too，再叫一下对方的名字更亲切。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

Asking how someone is: How are you? / How is ...?问候近况：How are you? / How is …?
To ask the person you are talking to, say How are you, adding today if you like. To ask about someone else, say How is with their name, often shortened to How's. Answer with I'm, he's or she's, then very well or fine, then thanks.
问对方：How are you (today)?；问第三人：How is + 人?（缩写 How's）。回答：I'm / He's / She's + very well / fine, thank you / thanks.。

Sentence 6 asks Helen, and sentences 10 and 12 ask about two other people in the family; sentences 11 and 13 answer with he's and she's.

本课：第 6 句问对方，第 10 句和第 12 句问家里的另外两人；第 11 句和第 13 句用 he's 与 she's 作答。

  • How are your parents? — They're fine, thanks.
  • How's your new job? — It's very busy!
be with an adjective: saying how things arebe + 形容词：说状态
An adjective after am, is or are says what state the subject is in: I'm tired, He's busy. Put very in front to make it stronger.
形容词放在 am/is/are 后面说明主语的状态：I'm tired. / He's busy.；前面可加 very 加强。

The words well in sentence 7 and fine in sentence 9 are both adjectives, and very can go in front of them.

本课：第 7 句的 well 和第 9 句的 fine 都是形容词，前面可以加 very。

  • The children are very tired.
  • It's cold today, but the sun is out.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • How are you today? — I'm fine, thanks. And you? 你今天好吗？——很好，谢谢。你呢？
    How are you today? — I'm well, thanks. And you?
  • How's …? — He's / She's fine. ……好吗？——他／她很好。
    How's your grandmother? — She's fine, thanks.
  • Nice to see you (again). 见到你真好。
    Nice to see you again, Mr Park!
  • Goodbye, … / See you, … 再见，……
    See you on Monday, Lucy.

Summary writing摘要写作

In no more than 40 words, say what Steven and Helen talk about when they meet.

用不超过 40 个词讲讲史蒂文和海伦见面时说了些什么。

Points a good summary covers好摘要要覆盖的要点
  • Steven and Helen meet and ask how the other is; both are well.史蒂文和海伦见面，互相问对方今天好不好，两人都很好。
  • They also ask about Tony and Emma, who are both fine.他们又问候了托尼和艾玛，两人也都很好。
  • They say they are glad to see each other and part.两人说很高兴见到对方，然后道别。
