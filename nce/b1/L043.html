Lesson 43–44 · Hurry Up 快点！

📘 New Concept English · Book 1 · Lesson 43–44 “Hurry Up”新概念英语 第一册 Lesson 43–44《Hurry Up》

B1🧠 15 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
Penny asks Sam whether he can make the tea, and he says of course. There is water in the kettle; the tea is behind the teapot. Sam can see the pot but not the tea, though it is right under his nose. The cups are in the cupboard, and he finds those at last. Then Penny tells him to be quick: the water is coming to the boil. This lesson is a man who says of course and then fumbles. It teaches the modal can and can't for ability, Is there any ...? and There are some, the two phrases for finding things, There it is! and Here they are!, and three place expressions: behind, in front of and over there.
彭妮问萨姆会不会沏茶，萨姆说当然会。壶里有水；茶叶在茶壶后面——萨姆看得见茶壶，却怎么也看不见茶叶，其实茶叶就在他眼前。杯子在碗柜里，他总算找到了。这时彭妮催他快点：水开了！本课是一个嘴上说「当然会」、手上却手忙脚乱的场景，学情态动词 can / can't 表示能力、Is there any …? 和 There are some、There it is! / Here they are! 两句「找到了」，以及 behind、in front of、over there 三个方位说法。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. Where is the tea, and why can't Sam see it?茶叶在哪里？萨姆为什么看不见？
  2. What tells us that making tea is not Sam's usual job?你怎么知道萨姆不常沏茶？
  3. Why does one finding phrase use it and the other they?There it is! 和 Here they are! 为什么一个用 it、一个用 they？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander; New Concept English, Book 1, Longman, 1967.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为教材自撰，见《新概念英语》第一册（朗文，1967）。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

Tea in a British home of the 1960s was a small ceremony: water boiled in a kettle on the stove, loose tea spooned into a china teapot, and cups taken down from the cupboard. In most households it was also the wife's job, which is the joke behind the question printed before the text: Sam's confident of course is not matched by any knowledge of where things are kept. The lesson introduces can and can't, and the drill lesson after it practises them with many verbs, so the scene is built to put both forms into one sentence.

1960 年代英国家庭里喝茶是个小小的仪式：水壶放在炉上烧开，用勺把散茶舀进瓷茶壶，再从碗柜里取下杯子。在多数家庭里这也是妻子的活，课前那个问题的笑点就在这里：萨姆那句信心满满的「当然」，配不上他对家里东西放哪儿的一无所知。本课引入 can 和 can't，后面那课用很多动词来练，所以场景特意安排成能把两种形式放进同一句话里。

✨ How it is written赏析

The comedy is in the gap between word and deed. Sam's first answer is the longest and most confident line he says, and from then on every question shows him needing help: he must be told where the tea is, then told again that it is right in front of him. The finding phrases give the scene its rhythm, two small cries of success, and the last line is a little alarm bell that leaves him still not quite done. The question before the text asks the learner to read for this gap, which is a first lesson in reading between the lines.

喜剧感在于说和做之间的落差。萨姆的第一句回答是他全场最长、最自信的一句，从那以后每个问题都显出他需要帮忙：要人告诉他茶叶在哪，再告诉一遍茶叶就在他眼前。两句「找到了」给场景定了节奏，是两声小小的胜利欢呼；最后一句则像一声小警铃，让他还没完全搞定。课前的问题要读者去读出这个落差，这是「读出言外之意」的第一课。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The language is fully current, though tea-making has changed: most Canadian homes use an electric kettle and tea bags, and in many families either partner makes the tea. Two points matter for learners. First, can see is the normal way to say you are able to see something, where Chinese often uses no extra word, so I see the tea sounds odd to a native ear in this situation. Second, of course can sound rude if it answers a real question, as if the asker should have known; Sure or Yes, I can is often kinder.

语言完全不过时，只是沏茶的方式变了：多数加拿大家庭用电水壶和茶包，在很多家里夫妻谁都可能沏茶。对读者有两点要紧。第一，表示「看得见」英语通常说 can see，而中文往往不加任何字，所以在这个场景说 I see the tea，母语者听着别扭。第二，用 of course 回答一个真诚的问题，有时会显得不客气，好像对方本该知道；Sure 或 Yes, I can 往往更友善。

    写五句话说你在厨房里会做和不会做的事，至少用一次 but。

    表演一个小场景：有人在找一样就在眼前的东西，最后用 There it is! 收尾。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 5第 5 句 (begins开头 “Can you make …”)

  • can is a modal verb: in a question it goes before the subject, and the short answer is Yes, I can or No, I can't. It takes the plain verb.can 是情态动词：问句把 can 放主语前，回答 Yes, I can. / No, I can't.；后面跟动词原形。
  • of course makes the yes stronger. Sam sounds very sure, and the rest of the scene shows otherwise.of course 加强语气「当然」；萨姆说得太满，后面就露馅了。

Sentence 7第 7 句 (begins开头 “Is there any …”)

  • In a question use any. water is uncountable, so the verb is is.疑问句里用 any：Is there any …?；water 不可数，所以用 is。
  • The short answer keeps there and is.简略回答：Yes, there is. / No, there isn't.

Sentence 10第 10 句 (begins开头 “It's over there, …”)

  • over there points roughly, and behind gives the exact place.先用 over there 指个大概方向，再用 behind 说准确位置。

Sentence 12第 12 句 (begins开头 “I can see …”)

  • English usually says can see for being able to see something.can see：看得见（能力），英语里说「看得见」习惯加 can。
  • can and can't are set side by side with but; in speech can't is stressed.can 与 can't 用 but 对照，口语里 can't 要重读。

Sentence 13第 13 句 (begins开头 “There it is! …”)

  • When one thing turns up, say There it is! For several, say Here they are! as in sentence 19.找到一样东西说 There it is!（单数）；找到几样说 Here they are! / There they are!（第 19 句）。
  • With a pronoun, it comes before is: never There is it.代词放在 be 前，不倒装：不说 There is it。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

The modal verbs can and can't for ability情态动词 can / can't：能，会
can with the plain verb is the same for every person. The negative is can't or cannot. In questions can comes first, and the short answers are Yes, I can and No, I can't.
can + 动词原形，所有人称都一样；否定 can't (cannot)；问句 Can + 主语 + 动词？；简答 Yes, I can. / No, I can't.。

Sentence 5 asks about ability, sentence 12 has both can and can't, and sentence 18 asks whether he can find them.

本课：第 5 句问会不会，第 12 句一句里同时有 can 和 can't，第 18 句问找不找得到。

  • My grandmother can speak three languages.
  • Can you help me? — Sorry, I can't.
There it is! and Here they are! for finding thingsThere it is! / Here they are!：找到了
When something turns up or is handed over: There or Here, then a pronoun, then be. Use it is for one and they are for several. With a noun the order turns round, as in Here is the tea! With a pronoun it does not.
东西找到或递给对方时：There/Here + 代词 + be！单数用 it is，复数用 they are。名词时倒装：Here is the tea!；代词时不倒装。

Sentence 13 finds the tea with it, and sentence 19 finds the cups with they.

本课：第 13 句找到茶叶（it），第 19 句找到杯子（they）。

  • Where are my keys? — Here they are, on the desk.
  • Look, there's the bus! / There it is!

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • Can you …? — Yes, of course I can. 你会……吗？——当然会。
    Can you fix my bike? — Yes, of course I can.
  • Is there any … in this …? 这个……里有……吗？
    Is there any milk in this jug?
  • It's over there, behind the …. 在那边，……后面。
    It's over there, behind the sofa.
  • I can see the …, but I can't see the …. 我看得见……，但看不见……
    I can see the house, but I can't find the gate.
  • Hurry up! The … is …ing! 快点！……要……了！
    Hurry up! The train is leaving!
