Lesson 45–46 · The Boss's Letter 老板的信

📘 New Concept English · Book 1 · Lesson 45–46 “The Boss's Letter”新概念英语 第一册 Lesson 45–46《The Boss's Letter》

B1🧠 15 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
The boss calls Bob over and asks where Pamela is: in her own office, next door. He wants to know whether she can type a letter for him and sends Bob to ask. Bob passes it on, and Pamela says she will be glad to. But before Bob is out of the room she calls him back. She cannot do it after all, because she cannot make out a word: the boss writes dreadfully. This lesson is a small office mix-up about passing on a message. It teaches Can you ...? for requests, can and can't for ability, the possessive boss's, What's the matter?, and office phrases such as next door and a minute.
老板叫鲍勃过来，问帕梅拉在哪儿——在隔壁她自己的办公室。老板问她能不能帮忙打一封信，让鲍勃去问。鲍勃把信交给帕梅拉，她说当然可以。可鲍勃刚要走，帕梅拉就叫住他：这封信她打不了——她根本认不出来，老板的字太糟了！本课是办公室里传话的小插曲，学用 Can you …? 提请求、can 与 can't 的能力义、名词所有格 boss's、What's the matter? 和 next door、a minute 这些办公室口语。

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander; New Concept English, Book 1, Longman, 1967.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为教材自撰，见《新概念英语》第一册（朗文，1967）。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

In a British office of the 1960s the boss wrote letters by hand or dictated them, and a typist turned them into clean typed copies on a typewriter. Typing was a skilled job, mostly done by women, and the boss in such an office was addressed as sir. Bad handwriting from managers was a standing joke of office life, and this lesson turns it into a lesson on can for both ability and requests. The drill lesson that follows practises can with many activities, so the dialogue sets up both uses side by side.

在 1960 年代的英国办公室，老板手写信件或口述，由打字员用打字机打成整洁的正式稿。打字是技术活，大多由女性做，在那样的办公室里人们称老板为 sir。经理们的字难认，是办公室生活里一个老笑话；这一课把它变成学 can 的两种用法——能力和请求——的一课。后面那课用许多活动来练 can，所以这段对话把两种用法并排摆了出来。

✨ How it is written赏析

The joke depends on timing. Everything goes smoothly for twelve lines: a request, a message passed on, a yes, a thank you. The reader relaxes. Then a single name is called out, Bob!, and the tone changes at once. The ending reveals that the problem was never Pamela's skill but the boss himself, and it does so in three short lines that climb from can't type to can't read to the cause. The boss never learns what happened, which leaves the reader to imagine poor Bob carrying the news back.

笑点靠的是节奏。前面十二行一切顺利：一个请求、一次传话、一声答应、一句谢谢，读者松了口气。接着突然一声「鲍勃！」，语气立刻变了。结尾揭出问题从来不在帕梅拉的本事，而在老板自己，而且是用三句短话一级一级推上去的：打不了、认不出、原因在这。老板始终不知道发生了什么，留给读者去想象可怜的鲍勃怎么把这个消息带回去。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The office has changed beyond recognition: hardly anyone types a boss's handwritten letter now, and calling a manager sir would sound stiff in most Canadian workplaces, where first names are normal. The language, though, is all still useful. Can you ...? is the everyday way to ask a colleague for help, and Could you ...? is softer still; both are much better than a bare order. Chinese speakers sometimes hear Can you as a question about ability and answer only Yes, without doing anything. In English the right reply is to do it, or to say Sure and then do it.

办公室已经面目全非：如今几乎没人再替老板打手写信了；在多数加拿大职场直呼名字是常态，叫经理 sir 会显得生硬。不过语言本身都还有用。Can you ...? 是请同事帮忙最日常的说法，Could you ...? 更柔和；两者都比光秃秃的命令好得多。中国读者有时把 Can you 理解成问能力，只回答 Yes 却不动手。在英语里，正确的回应是去做，或者说一声 Sure 然后去做。

💬 Discuss and write讨论与写作
  1. Write three requests you might make to a colleague, first with Can you ...? and then with Could you ...?.
    写三个你可能对同事提的请求，先用 Can you ...?，再用 Could you ...?。
  2. Tell a short story about a message that went wrong as it was passed from one person to another.
    讲一个小故事：一条消息在人与人之间传着传着就走了样。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 3第 3 句 (begins开头 “Can you come …”)

  • Can you ...? here is not about ability but a polite request.Can you …? 在这里不是问能力，而是客气的请求「请你……好吗」。
  • a minute means a short while, not exactly sixty seconds, and for has been dropped.a minute = 一会儿，不是严格的一分钟；省了 for。

Sentence 7第 7 句 (begins开头 “She's in her …”)

  • next door means in the next room. sir is a respectful word from an employee to a boss, formal and rather old-fashioned.next door：隔壁；sir 是下属对上司的尊称，老派而正式。

Sentence 8第 8 句 (begins开头 “Can she type …”)

  • The boss asks whether she can, then sends Bob to ask, which starts the chain of messages.老板问的是「她能不能」，再叫鲍勃去问，传话就从这里开始。
  • for me means on my behalf.for me：替我、为我。

Sentence 17第 17 句 (begins开头 “What's the matter? …”)

  • What's the matter? asks what is wrong.What's the matter? 问「怎么了、出了什么事」。
  • Both can'ts are about ability: she cannot type it because she cannot read it.两个 can't 是能力义：她打不了，因为她看不懂。

Sentence 20第 20 句 (begins开头 “The boss's handwriting …”)

The boss's handwriting is terrible!

  • A singular noun ending in s still takes 's: boss's, said with an extra syllable.以 s 结尾的单数名词所有格仍加 's：boss's，读 /ˈbɒsɪz/。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

Two uses of Can you ...?: requests and abilityCan you …? 的两种意思：请求与能力
can can mean ability, or it can make a polite request. Context tells you which: Can you swim? asks about ability, while Can you open the window? asks for help. Could you ...? is more polite still.
can 可表示能力（会不会、能不能），也可用来客气地请别人做事。看语境：Can you swim? 问能力；Can you open the window? 是请求。更客气的说法用 Could you …?。

Sentences 3 and 12 are requests; the can'ts in sentences 18 and 20 are about ability.

本课：第 3 句、第 11 句是请求；第 19、20 句的 can't 是能力。

  • Can you pass the salt, please? （请求）
  • Can you speak French? （能力）
The possessive 's名词所有格 's
Add 's to a singular noun, even one ending in s. Add only an apostrophe to a regular plural ending in s. Irregular plurals take 's.
单数名词加 's：the boss's letter、Pamela's office；以 s 结尾的复数名词只加 '：the students' books；不规则复数加 's：the children's toys。

The title and sentence 20 both use boss's.

本课：标题和第 20 句都是 boss's。

  • This is my sister's bike.
  • The teachers' room is upstairs.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • Can you come here for a minute? 你能过来一下吗？
    Lucy, can you come here for a minute?
  • Where's …? — She's next door. ……在哪儿？——在隔壁。
    Where's Tom? — He's next door, in the meeting room.
  • Can she … this … for me? Ask her, please. 她能帮我……吗？请问问她。
    Can she check this report for me? Ask her, please.
  • What's the matter? — I can't …. 怎么了？——我……不了。
    What's the matter? — I can't open this jar.
  • The …'s handwriting is terrible! ……的字太难认了！
    The doctor's handwriting is terrible!

Our model summary本站范文

The boss wanted a letter typed, so he sent Bob to ask Pamela in the office next door. She agreed, and Bob gave her the letter. But she soon called him back. She could not read a word of it, because the boss's handwriting was dreadful.（46 词）
