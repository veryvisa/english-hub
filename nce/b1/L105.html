Lesson 105–106 · Full of Mistakes 错误百出

📘 New Concept English · Book 1 · Lesson 105–106 “Full of Mistakes”新概念英语 第一册 Lesson 105–106《Full of Mistakes》

B1🧠 15 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
Sandra's boss asks Bob where she is and wants her in his office straight away. When she arrives, he asks how to spell intelligent, and she spells it out correctly letter by letter. The trouble is that in the letter she typed, it has a single L, and the whole letter has errors all through it. He wants it typed again, and she apologises and agrees. Then he gives her a small gift: a dictionary, which he hopes will be useful. This lesson teaches want and tell with an object and to-infinitive, spelling words aloud, be full of, I'm sorry about that, and I hope it will help.
鲍勃的老板要找桑德拉，让她马上到办公室来。桑德拉来了，老板问她 intelligent（聪明的）怎么拼，她一个字母一个字母拼对了。老板说：可你打字时只打了一个 L，这封信错误百出，我要你重打一遍。桑德拉道歉，说这就重打。老板又递给她一份小礼物——一本词典，希望对她有帮助。本课学want / tell + 宾语 + 不定式（I want her to come、Tell her to come）、spell 与字母拼读、be full of、I'm sorry about that、I hope it'll help you。

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander; New Concept English, Book 1, Longman, 1967.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为教材自撰，见《新概念英语》第一册（朗文，1967）。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

In the 1960s, business letters were typed on manual or electric typewriters by secretaries and typists, most of them young women, and a letter with mistakes could not simply be corrected on screen. Small errors might be fixed with an eraser or correction fluid, but a letter full of mistakes had to be typed again from the start, which could take a long time. A boss signing letters would read them carefully, since they carried the firm's name. A dictionary on the typist's desk was a standard tool of the job, so the present is both practical and a pointed comment.

1960 年代，商务信函由秘书和打字员用手动或电动打字机打出来，她们大多是年轻女性，打错的信不能像今天这样在屏幕上一改了之。小错可以用橡皮或修正液补救，但错误百出的信就得从头重打，可能要花很长时间。老板签字前会仔细读信，因为信上印着公司的名称。打字员桌上放一本词典是这份工作的标准配置，所以这份礼物既实用，又带着一点不客气的意味。

✨ How it is written赏析

The joke is built on the word itself. The boss chooses intelligent, a word that means clever, to test a typist who has just shown she is careless; she then spells it perfectly, which makes the mistake on the page look even worse. The scene moves in three steps: a summons, a test, a verdict. Sandra's replies are short and polite, and the boss is never openly rude. The present at the end is the sting: a kind gesture on the surface, a criticism underneath, and the final hope sounds friendly while saying she needs help.

笑点就建在这个词本身上。老板挑了 intelligent 这个意思是「聪明」的词，去考一个刚刚暴露了粗心的打字员；她偏偏拼得一字不差，这让纸上的错显得更糟。场景分三步推进：传唤、考问、裁决。桑德拉的回答简短而有礼，老板也从未当面失礼。最后的礼物就是那根刺：表面是好意，底下是批评，那句「希望对你有帮助」听着友善，说的其实是她需要帮助。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

Spell-checkers have made this exact mistake rarer, but they cannot catch every wrong word, and the skill of spelling a word aloud is still essential in Canada: on the phone with a bank, a government office or a pharmacy, you will be asked to spell your name and street letter by letter. Many newcomers find their Chinese names misspelled on documents, so learning to say double, as in a double N, and to use code words such as B for bravo can save a lot of trouble. The grammar point, want someone to do, is one of the most common errors for Chinese speakers, who often say I want he come or I want that he comes.

拼写检查让这种错少了，但它抓不住所有用错的词，而把一个词逐个字母拼出来的本领在加拿大仍然必不可少：给银行、政府部门或药房打电话，对方会请你把姓名和街名一个字母一个字母拼出来。很多新移民发现证件上的中文名被拼错，所以学会说 double（比如两个 N），学会用 B for bravo 这类代号，能省去很多麻烦。语法点 want someone to do 是中国人最常犯的错误之一，常说成 I want he come 或 I want that he comes。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 5第 5 句 (begins开头 “Do you want …”)

  • I want her means I am looking for her; want to do means wish to do.I want her：我要找她（找某人）。want to do：想做某事。
  • speak to someone.speak to sb：跟某人说话。

Sentence 7第 7 句 (begins开头 “I want her …”)

  • want plus a person plus to do; do not use a that clause.want sb to do：要某人做某事，不能说 want that she comes。
  • tell plus a person plus to do gives an order; at once means now.tell sb to do：叫某人做某事。at once：立即。

Sentence 11第 11 句 (begins开头 “How do you …”)

  • To answer, say the letters one by one.How do you spell …?：……怎么拼？回答时逐个说字母。

Sentence 15第 15 句 (begins开头 “You've typed it …”)

  • be full of means there is a lot of it.be full of：满是……。

Sentence 17第 17 句 (begins开头 “I want you …”)

  • The same pattern again.want sb to do：第二次出现。
  • will for a promise made now; sorry about a thing.I'll do that：当场答应（a promise）。sorry about + 事：为某事道歉。

Sentence 23第 23 句 (begins开头 “I hope it'll …”)

  • here's ... for you is said when giving something.here's … for you：给你……（递东西）。
  • I hope with a future clause.I hope + 将来时从句：希望……会……。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

want, tell and ask with an object and to dowant / tell / ask + 宾语 + to do
Use want, tell or ask, then the person, then to and the base verb. The negative is tell someone not to do. The person is in the object form: her, him, me. Do not use a that clause after want.
want sb to do（要某人做）、tell sb to do（叫某人做）、ask sb to do（请某人做）。否定：tell sb not to do。宾语用宾格（her、him、me）。中文说「我要她来」，英语不能说 I want she come 或 I want that she comes。

Sentences 7 and 18 use want with a person and to do, and sentence 8 uses tell in the same way.

本课：第 7 句与第 17 句是 want sb to do，第 8 句是 tell sb to do。

  • My mum wants me to study medicine.
  • The teacher told us not to use our phones.
Spelling words aloud拼写字母
Some letter names cause trouble: A, E, I, G, J, R, Y and Z. Two of the same letter together are often said with double, as in double L in the middle of intelligent.
英语字母读法：A /eɪ/、E /iː/、I /aɪ/、G /dʒiː/、J /dʒeɪ/、R /ɑː/、Y /waɪ/、Z /zed/（英）/ziː/（美）。两个相同字母连在一起常说 double：intelligent 中间是 double L。

Sentence 13 spells the word letter by letter.

本课：第 13 句是逐个字母拼读。

  • My name is Kerry: K-E-double R-Y.
