Lesson 63–64 · Thank You, Doctor 谢谢你，医生

📘 New Concept English · Book 1 · Lesson 63–64 “Thank You, Doctor”新概念英语 第一册 Lesson 63–64《Thank You, Doctor》

B1🧠 15 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
The doctor calls a second time. Jimmy is much improved but is not yet allowed out of bed; two more days of rest are needed, with no school and no heavy food. His mother asks about fever, and there is none. He may be up for a couple of hours daily, as long as his room is kept warm. Before going, the doctor asks after the father, who turns out to be laid up with a cold of his own. This lesson is a doctor's orders: what you may do, what you must not do, and what you have to do. It teaches must against mustn't, questions and answers with Must he ...?, another two days, Does he have ...?, and the closing joke with too.
医生第二次上门。吉米好多了，可还不能起床，得再躺两天；不许去上学，不许吃油腻的东西。妈妈问他发不发烧——不发烧。医生说每天可以起来两小时左右，但房间要保持暖和。临走前医生问威廉斯先生在哪儿，原来他也感冒躺下了。本课是医嘱：能做什么、不能做什么、必须做什么。学 must 与 mustn't 的对照、Must he …? 的问答、another two days、Does he have …? 和 too 的笑点。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. How is Jimmy today, and how many more days must he spend in bed?吉米今天怎么样？还要卧床几天？
  2. What things does the doctor say the boy must not do?医生说了哪几件「不许」的事？
  3. Why is the word too in the last line funny?最后一句的 too 为什么好笑？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander; New Concept English, Book 1, Longman, 1967.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为教材自撰，见《新概念英语》第一册（朗文，1967）。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

This lesson follows straight on from the previous one, and the doctor's second visit shows how a family GP worked in Britain at the time: he came back to check, gave orders to the mother rather than the child, and asked after the rest of the household. Advice such as keeping the room warm and avoiding rich food reflects ordinary medical common sense of the 1960s, when many houses had no central heating and a cold bedroom was a real concern. The phrase have a temperature is still the normal British way to say that someone has a fever; Canadians are more likely to say have a fever.

本课紧接上一课，医生的第二次上门显示了当时英国家庭医生的工作方式：回头复查，把医嘱交代给母亲而不是孩子，还顺便问起家里其他人。保持房间暖和、不吃油腻食物这类叮嘱，反映的是 1960 年代普通的医疗常识——那时很多房子没有集中供暖，卧室太冷是实实在在的问题。have a temperature 至今仍是英国人说「发烧」的通常说法；加拿大人更可能说 have a fever。

✨ How it is written赏析

The doctor's speech is a neat lesson in giving instructions. Each rule is short, and they alternate between what is forbidden and what is required, so the reader hears the difference between mustn't and must again and again. The mother's questions keep the scene moving and let the doctor repeat his main point in a more formal word. The final line repays the reader who remembers the last lesson: the father who laughed about his son's week in bed now needs the same treatment, and a single word at the very end carries the whole joke.

医生的话是一堂关于「怎样下指令」的好课。每一条规定都很短，而且「不许」与「必须」交替出现，于是读者一遍又一遍听到 mustn't 和 must 的区别。母亲的提问推动场景往前走，也让医生用一个更正式的词把要点再说一遍。最后一句奖励记得上一课的读者：上回拿儿子卧床开玩笑的父亲，现在自己也需要同样的治疗，整个笑话就落在句末那一个词上。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The difference between mustn't and don't have to is one of the most common mistakes Chinese learners make, because Chinese keeps 'no need' and 'not allowed' clearly apart, while in English both look like a negative of must in English. It is worth fixing early: a Canadian teacher who says you don't have to come means you may stay home, while you mustn't come means you are not allowed. The medical advice has changed less than in the last lesson. Rest, warmth and light food are still sensible, though today a doctor would also say that a child without fever can usually return to school within a day or two.

mustn't 和 don't have to 的区别，是中国学习者最常犯的错误之一：中文里「不必」和「不许」分得清清楚楚，英语里两者看起来却都像 must 的否定。这值得早点纠正：加拿大老师说 you don't have to come，意思是你可以不来；说 you mustn't come，意思是你不许来。医嘱方面的变化比上一课小。休息、保暖、清淡饮食至今仍合理，只不过今天的医生还会说，不发烧的孩子一般一两天内就能回学校。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 10第 10 句 (begins开头 “You are better …”)

  • mustn't is a ban; not ... yet says the time has not come.mustn't：不许。not … yet：还不，表示时候未到。

Sentence 11第 11 句 (begins开头 “You must stay …”)

  • another before a number and a plural noun treats the time as one more block.another two days：再两天。another 后面接「数词 + 复数名词」时把这段时间当作一个整体。

Sentence 14第 14 句 (begins开头 “Does he have …”)

  • have a temperature means to have a fever. Questions with have use does, and the short answer repeats it.have a temperature：发烧（不是「有体温」）。have 作「有」，问句借助 does，简答 No, he doesn't.

Sentence 16第 16 句 (begins开头 “Must he stay …”)

  • The short yes answer is Yes, he must; remain says the same as stay, more formally.Must he …? 的肯定简答是 Yes, he must.；remain 比 stay 正式，意思相同。

Sentence 18第 18 句 (begins开头 “He can get …”)

  • can gives permission and must the condition; keep plus object plus adjective means to hold it in that state.can 表示允许，must 表示条件；keep + 宾语 + 形容词：使……保持……。

Sentence 22第 22 句 (begins开头 “He has a …”)

  • too at the end means also. The father who joked about his son is now ill himself.too：也（放句末）。爸爸上一课说儿子不爱上学，现在自己也躺下了。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

must and mustn'tmust 与 mustn't
must means it is necessary, and mustn't means it is forbidden. A no answer to Must I ...? is usually No, you don't have to, because No, you mustn't would mean it is not allowed.
must 表示「必须」，mustn't 表示「不许」。问句 Must I …? 的否定回答常用 No, you needn't 或 No, you don't have to（不必），不用 No, you mustn't（那是「不许」）。

Sentences 10, 13 and 14 give three bans, and sentences 11 and 19 give two things that must happen.

本课：第 11、13、14 句是三个「不许」，第 12、19 句是两个「必须」。

  • You must take the medicine after meals.
  • You mustn't swim right after a big lunch.
have for illnesshave 表示患病：have a cold / have a temperature
English uses have for an illness. Questions use does, and negatives doesn't have.
英语说「生了某病」用 have：have a cold、have a headache、have a temperature（发烧）。问句 Does he have …?，否定 He doesn't have …。

Sentence 14 asks about fever and sentence 22 tells us the father is ill too.

本课：第 14 句问发烧，第 22 句说父亲也感冒了。

  • I have a headache. Can I lie down?
  • Does she have a temperature? — Yes, 38.5.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • You're much better, but you mustn't … just yet. 你好多了，但还不许……
    You're much better, but you mustn't run just yet.
  • X must stay in … for another … X 还得在……再待……
    The car must stay in the garage for another week.
  • You can … for about …, but you must … 你可以……大约……，但必须……
    You can watch TV for about an hour, but you must finish your homework first.
  • Does he have a …? — No, he doesn't. 他有……吗？——没有。
    Does he have a fever? — No, he doesn't.

The doctor found Jimmy much improved but ordered two more days in bed, with no school and no heavy food. Having no fever, he could be up a couple of hours daily if his room stayed warm. Then it turned out the father had caught a cold too.（48 词）
