Lesson 123–124 · A Trip to Australia 澳大利亚之行

📘 New Concept English · Book 1 · Lesson 123–124 “A Trip to Australia”新概念英语 第一册 Lesson 123–124《A Trip to Australia》

B1🧠 15 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
Mike shows his friend Scott some pictures from his time in Australia: friends he made on the way, the vessel that carried them, and the man who once offered him work there. In the last picture there is someone with a big beard, and Scott has to guess. It is Mike himself. He let his beard grow while away, then got rid of it at home because his wife disliked it. This is a chat over photos that teaches relative clauses with the relative word left out, a preposition at the end of the clause, the tag question in It's not you, is it?, and shave off.
迈克给朋友斯科特看他去澳大利亚旅行时拍的照片：路上认识的人、坐的那艘船、曾给他提供工作机会的那个人。最后一张里有个大胡子，斯科特猜了半天——原来就是迈克自己。他旅行时留了胡子，回家就刮掉了，因为妻子不喜欢。本课是看照片聊天的对话，学省略关系词的定语从句（a photo I took、the ship we went on）、介词放在从句末尾、反意疑问句 It's not you, is it? 和 shave off。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. Where were the pictures taken?照片是在哪里拍的？
  2. Who turns out to be the bearded man?那个大胡子到底是谁？
  3. Why did Mike get rid of his beard at home?迈克回家后为什么刮了胡子？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander; New Concept English, Book 1, Longman, 1967. The scene is invented to practise relative clauses with the relative word left out.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为教材自撰，见《新概念英语》第一册（朗文，1967），是为操练省略关系词的定语从句编的场景。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

In the 1950s and 1960s many Britons sailed to Australia; the Australian government paid most of the fare for British migrants under an assisted passage scheme, and people called them Ten Pound Poms because the ticket cost about ten pounds. A long sea voyage took several weeks, so a beard grown on board was a common souvenir. A job offer in Australia was a real choice for young British men at the time.

1950–1960 年代很多英国人乘船去澳大利亚；澳大利亚政府在资助移民计划下替英国移民付了大部分船费，船票只要约十英镑，人们叫他们「十英镑英国佬」。远洋航行要好几个星期，船上留的胡子是常见的「纪念品」。在澳大利亚得到一份工作，对当时的英国年轻男子是实实在在的选择。

✨ How it is written赏析

Every caption Mike gives is a noun with a short clause after it, and almost all of them drop the relative word, which is how people really talk over photos. The guessing game gives the lesson a small plot, and the last line is a domestic joke: the man who travelled half the world still obeys his wife's taste.

迈克给出的每一句说明都是「名词＋短从句」，而且几乎都省掉了关系词，这正是人们一起看照片时真实的说话方式。猜人游戏给这一课加了一点情节，最后一句是家常笑话：周游了半个世界的男人，还是得听妻子的。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

Learners often keep that in every clause because it feels safe, but native speakers drop it most of the time in speech, and hearing it dropped is a listening skill in itself. Practise by describing your own phone photos aloud in this pattern. For newcomers in Canada, the history of assisted migration to Australia is a reminder that moving countries for a job offer is an old story; the same questions about family approval still come up.

学习者常在每个从句里都留着 that，觉得保险，但母语者说话时多数会省掉；听懂省略本身就是听力技能。练法是用这个句型大声描述自己手机里的照片。对来加拿大的新移民来说，澳大利亚资助移民的历史提醒我们：为一份工作远走他乡是个老故事，家人同不同意的问题至今照样会出现。

💬 Discuss and write讨论与写作
  1. Show a partner three photos on your phone and describe each with a clause: This is the place we ….
    给同伴看手机里的三张照片，每张用从句介绍：This is the place we …。
  2. Have you ever changed your look on a trip? Tell the story in five sentences.
    你在旅途中改变过形象吗？用五句话讲讲。
  3. Write four short captions for holiday photos, each with a clause that ends in a preposition, such as the lake we swam in.
    给四张度假照片各写一句短说明，每句的从句以介词结尾，比如 the lake we swam in。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 4第 4 句 (begins开头 “This is a …”)

  • The relative word is the object, so it is left out.a photograph (that) I took：关系词作宾语，省略了。
  • during takes a noun.during + 名词：在……期间。

Sentence 9第 9 句 (begins开头 “That's the ship …”)

  • on stays at the end: we travelled on the ship.介词 on 留在从句末尾：we travelled on the ship。
  • The formal version moves on before which.正式说法：the ship on which we travelled。

Sentence 12第 12 句 (begins开头 “That's the man …”)

  • tell someone about someone; about stays at the end.tell sb about sb：给某人讲某人的事，about 留在句末。
  • Remember? is short for Do you remember?Remember? 是 Do you remember? 的口语短说。

Sentence 19第 19 句 (begins开头 “It's not you, …”)

  • A negative statement with a positive tag shows surprise.前否后肯的反意疑问句，说话人觉得「不会是你吧」。
  • That's right confirms the guess.回答 That's right 表示「对，就是我」，此处意思是肯定他的猜测。

Sentence 21第 21 句 (begins开头 “I grew a …”)

  • grow a beard means let it grow; with it, shave it off.grow a beard：留胡子。shave it off：代词放中间。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

Relative clauses without a relative word省略关系词的定语从句
When who, which or that is the object of the clause, everyday English usually drops it. When it is the subject, it must stay. A quick test: if the clause already has its own subject, the relative word can go.
关系词 who / which / that 在从句里作宾语时，口语里通常省略：the photo (that) I took、the people (who) I met。作主语时不能省：the man who offered you a job。判断方法：看从句里是否已经有主语。

Sentences 4, 9, 10 and 13 drop the relative word; in sentence 15 who is the subject and stays.

本课：第 5、9、10、13 句都省了关系词，第 15 句 who 作主语没省。

  • Is this the book you recommended?
  • The girl who won the prize is in my class.
A preposition at the end of the clause介词在从句末尾
When a verb has a preposition, everyday English leaves the preposition at the end of the clause. Formal English moves it to the front with which or whom. Either way, the preposition must not disappear.
动词带介词时（travel on、talk about、look for、live in），介词在口语里留在从句末尾：the house I live in。正式文体把介词提前，配 which / whom：the house in which I live。介词不能丢。

In sentences 9 and 13, on and about stay at the end.

本课：第 9 句 on 与第 12 句 about 都留在句末。

  • This is the song I was talking about.
  • That's the key you were looking for.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • This is a picture I took in … 这是我在……拍的照片。
    This is a picture I took in Jasper last summer.
  • They're friends I made at … 他们是我在……交的朋友。
    They're friends I made at university.
  • That's the bus we came on. 那就是我们坐的那辆车。
    That's the bus we came on this morning.
  • It isn't her, is it? 不会是她吧？
    It isn't her, is it? She looks so different!
  • I let my hair grow, but I cut it short later. 我留过长发，后来剪短了。
    I let my hair grow, but I cut it short later.

Mike showed his friend Scott some pictures from Australia. There were friends he made on the way, the vessel they sailed in, and the man who had offered him work. In the last one Scott saw a man with a beard. It was Mike. He removed it back home because his wife hated it.（54 词）
