Lesson 5–6 · Nice to Meet You 很高兴见到你

📘 New Concept English · Book 1 · Lesson 5–6 “Nice to Meet You”新概念英语 第一册 Lesson 5–6《Nice to Meet You》

B1🧠 17 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
One morning Mr Blake brings a new student, Sophie, into the class and introduces her to the others: Hans is from Germany, Naoko from Japan, Chang-woo from Korea, and Luming and Xiaohui from China. After each name the two people greet each other. This lesson is the standard routine for a first meeting: the person in the middle says who someone is and where they are from, and the two who are meeting exchange a greeting. The grammar is This is for introducing, he and she with is, capital letters for nationality words, and too meaning also.
早上，布莱克先生把新来的学生索菲介绍给班上的同学：汉斯是德国人，直子是日本人，昌宇是韩国人，鲁明和晓惠是中国人。每介绍一个，两人都说一句「很高兴认识你」。本课是第一次见面的标准流程：介绍人先说「这是某某」，再补一句他是哪国人，被介绍的双方互致问候。学 This is … 介绍他人、he/she + is、国籍词首字母大写和 too「也」。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. Who makes the introductions, and who is the new student?谁在给大家做介绍？被介绍的新同学是谁？
  2. How many countries are there in the class?班上一共有几个国家的学生？
  3. Why does everyone say the same thing, and would they say it at a second meeting?为什么每个人都说同一句话？换成第二次见面还这么说吗？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander; New Concept English, Book 1, Longman, 1967. The class list of Chinese, Korean and Japanese names comes from the later revised edition prepared for learners in China.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为教材自撰，见《新概念英语》第一册（朗文，1967）；班上中、韩、日学生的名字出自后来为中国读者修订的新版。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

The setting is a language school, the kind of place many of the book's readers sat in: a teacher, a new arrival and a room full of adults from different countries. Every introduction follows the same steps, so the learner hears the routine five times without one wasted word. The names are chosen so that the class looks like the world, and in the newer edition three of the five classmates come from East Asia, which makes the scene feel close to home for its main readers.

场景是一所语言学校，正是许多读者自己坐过的地方：一位老师、一位新来的学生、一屋子来自不同国家的成年人。每一次介绍都走同样几步，读者把这套流程听上五遍，一个字都不浪费。名字选得让这个班看起来像一个小世界；新版里五位同学有三位来自东亚，让主要读者觉得格外亲切。

✨ How it is written赏析

The lesson teaches by rhythm. A name, a nationality, a greeting; then the same three beats again with a new name. By the fourth round the learner can say the next line before it comes, which is exactly the point. The only change in the pattern is the last one, where too appears because two classmates share a country, so the one new word arrives just when the pattern has made room for it.

本课靠节奏教人：一个名字、一个国籍、一句问候，然后换个名字再来一遍。到第四轮，读者已经能抢在录音前说出下一句——这正是目的。整套模式只在最后变了一处：两位同学来自同一个国家，所以出现了 too。唯一的新词恰好在模式给它腾出位置时出场。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

Two things here are worth adding for a Chinese reader living in an English-speaking country. First, Nice to meet you is for a first meeting only; next time it becomes Nice to see you, which the book saves for Lesson 9. Second, nationality and background are not always the same thing. In Vancouver or Toronto a classmate with a Chinese name may be Canadian, and asking where are you really from can sound unfriendly. The safer question is where did you grow up, and the person can then say as much as they like.

对住在英语国家的中国读者，这里有两点值得补充。第一，Nice to meet you 只用于初次见面，第二次就变成 Nice to see you，书里留到第 9 课才教。第二，国籍和出身不总是一回事。在温哥华或多伦多，一位中文名字的同学可能就是加拿大人；追问 where are you really from 听起来不太友好。更稳妥的问法是 where did you grow up，对方想说多少都行。

💬 Discuss and write讨论与写作

    用英语把两位朋友互相介绍，用上 This is、he's 或 she's，再各说一件关于他们的事。
  2. Do you use the same words to greet a person the first time and the tenth time? What changes in Chinese, and what changes in English?
    第一次见面和第十次见面，你打招呼的话一样吗？中文里变什么，英语里变什么？

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 4第 4 句 (begins开头 “Good morning Mr. …”)

Good morning, Mr Blake.

  • To greet a man by title, use Mr with his family name. You cannot put Mr before a first name.打招呼时称呼人：Mr + 姓，不能用 Mr + 名（不说 Mr John）。
  • The name comes after the greeting, with a comma between. British English writes Mr with no full stop; American English writes Mr. with one.称呼放在问候语后，中间用逗号隔开。英式写法 Mr 不加点，美式写成 Mr.。

Sentence 5第 5 句 (begins开头 “This is Miss …”)

This is Miss Sophie Dupont.

  • To introduce someone, English uses This is. He is or She is is not used for the introduction itself.介绍别人用 This is …，不说 He is Sophie 或 She is Sophie Dupont 来介绍。
  • In Western names the given name comes first and the family name last; a formal introduction often uses both.西方人名：名在前（Sophie），姓在后（Dupont）；正式介绍可以用全名。

Sentence 7第 7 句 (begins开头 “She is French. …”)

She is French.

  • Nationality is shown by an adjective after is. She is France is wrong; France is the country.国籍用形容词作表语：She is French.，不说 She is France.。
  • Nationality words always start with a capital letter in English.国籍词首字母永远大写：French、German、Chinese。

Sentence 21第 21 句 (begins开头 “She is Chinese …”)

She is Chinese too.

  • too, meaning also, goes at the end of the sentence. In everyday writing it often has no comma before it.too「也」放在句末，口语里前面常不加逗号。
  • In a negative sentence use either instead: She isn't French either.否定句不用 too，要用 either：She isn't French either.。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

Introducing people: This is ... / He's ... / She's ...介绍他人：This is … / He's … / She's …
Use This is with a name to say who someone is. After that, use he for a man or she for a woman with is to add more; in speech they become he's and she's.
第一句用 This is + 名字点出是谁；接下来用 he（男）或 she（女）+ is 补充信息，口语缩写 he's、she's。

Sentence 5 gives the name and sentences 6 and 8 add facts with she; sentence 14 gives a name and sentence 15 adds a fact with he's.

本课：第 5 句点名字，第 7、8 句用 she 补充；第 14 句点名字，第 15 句用 he's 补充。

  • This is Tom. He's my brother.
  • This is Anna. She's from Vancouver.
Nationality words and too国籍词与 too「也」
The country is a noun, such as China or France; the nationality is an adjective, such as Chinese or French. Both take a capital letter. To say where someone is from, use be with the adjective. To say also, add too at the end.
国家名是名词（China、France），国籍是形容词（Chinese、French），首字母都大写；说「某人是哪国人」用 be + 国籍形容词。表示「也」在句末加 too。

In sentence 18 Luming is from China; in sentence 21 Xiaohui is from China as well, so too is added.

本课：第 18 句鲁明是中国人，第 21 句晓惠「也」是中国人，所以用了 too。

  • Kenji is Japanese. Yuki is Japanese too.
  • Paris is in France. Pierre is French.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • Good morning, Mr/Miss … ……先生／小姐，早上好。
    Good morning, Miss Green.
  • This is …, and this is … 这位是……，这位是……
    This is my aunt, and this is my uncle.
  • X is a new … X 是新来的……
    Ken is a new driver at the bus company.
  • He's / She's + nationality 他／她是……国人
    She's Canadian, but her parents are Greek.
  • Nice to meet you (too). 很高兴认识你（我也是）。
    Nice to meet you, Ali. — Nice to meet you too.
