Lesson 21–22 · Which Book 哪一本书？

📘 New Concept English · Book 1 · Lesson 21–22 “Which Book”新概念英语 第一册 Lesson 21–22《Which Book》

B1🧠 16 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
A man asks Jane to pass him a book. Jane asks which one, and picks one up: this? No, not that, the red one. Another: this? Yes. Jane hands it over and he says thank you. The whole lesson is a dozen short lines, but it is the pointing-out talk we use every day: ask for something, ask which, and point to check. The grammar is Give me, that is give with a person and then a thing; Which ...? for choosing among several; and one standing in for a noun already mentioned, as in this one, that one and the red one.
一位男士请简递给他一本书。简问：哪一本？拿起一本：这本吗？不是那本，是红色的那本。再拿一本：这本吗？对了。简递过书，他道谢。全课只有十来句，却是生活里最常用的「指认」对话：要东西、问哪个、指着确认。学 Give me …（give + 人 + 物）、Which …? 问「哪一个」，以及用 one 代替前面说过的名词：this one、that one、the red one。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. In the end, which book is handed to the man?男士最后拿到的是哪一本？
  2. Jane says this one about the first book; why does the man call it that one?简拿起第一本时问 This one?，男士回答时为什么说 that one？
  3. Why does he say the red one and not the red book?为什么不直接说 the red book，而说 the red one？

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander; New Concept English, Book 1, Longman, 1967. Lessons 21 and 23 are a matching pair, one singular and one plural, built on the same shape.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为教材自撰，见《新概念英语》第一册（朗文，1967）。第 21 课与第 23 课是一对，一个单数、一个复数，结构完全相同。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

By this point the learner can name many objects, so the book turns to the next practical need: asking for one thing out of several. The drill lesson after it lists everyday items, boxes, cups, bottles, tins, knives and forks, with pairs of adjectives such as large and small, sharp and blunt, full and empty, so that Give me the sharp one or Give me an empty one can be said about nearly anything in a kitchen or office.

到这里，读者已经能叫出很多东西的名字，于是书转向下一个实际需要：从几样里要一样。后面那课操练列了日常物品——盒子、杯子、瓶子、罐头、刀叉——再配上大与小、锋利与钝、满与空几对形容词，让 Give me the sharp one 或 Give me an empty one 几乎能用在厨房或办公室里的任何东西上。

✨ How it is written赏析

The whole lesson depends on a picture: two people, several books, a hand that moves. The words are almost empty without it, and that is the lesson's real point. In daily speech we do not name things; we point and say this one, that one, the red one, and let the shared scene do the rest. The switch between this and that is the subtle part: the same book is near for Jane and far for the man, and the dialogue gets it right without explaining anything.

整课全靠一幅画面：两个人、几本书、一只在移动的手。没有画面，这些话几乎是空的——这正是本课的要点。日常说话时我们并不说出东西的名字，而是指着说「这个」「那个」「红的那个」，剩下的交给双方都看得见的场景。this 和 that 的切换是最微妙的地方：同一本书，对简是近的，对男士是远的，对话一句不解释就用对了。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

Chinese learners often say Give me without please, because in Chinese a bare give me between family members is perfectly friendly. In English, a request with no please can sound like an order, even at home, and a Canadian child will be reminded to add the magic word from the age of two. Another common slip is to repeat the noun: the red book, the red book, where English prefers the red one. Using one well is a small sign that a speaker has moved beyond word-for-word translation.

中国读者常说 Give me 不带 please，因为中文里家人之间说「给我」完全没问题。在英语里，不带 please 的请求听起来像命令，即使在家里也一样；加拿大孩子两岁起就会被提醒加上这个「魔法词」。另一个常见毛病是重复名词：the red book、the red book，而英语更喜欢 the red one。会用 one，是一个人已经走出逐词翻译的小标志。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 4第 4 句 (begins开头 “Give me a …”)

  • give takes the person first and then the thing: Give me a book. Or put the thing first and add to: Give a book to me.give + 人 + 物：Give me a book.；也可以说 give + 物 + to + 人：Give a book to me.。
  • After a verb, a pronoun takes its object form: me, him, her, us, them.人称代词放在动词后要用宾格：me、him、her、us、them。

Sentence 7第 7 句 (begins开头 “No, not that …”)

  • The book in Jane's hand is this one to Jane but that one to the man; near and far are measured from the speaker.简手里那本，对简来说是 this one，对男士来说是 that one——近远是按说话人自己算的。
  • one stands for book, so the red one means the red book without saying book again.one 代替前面的 book，避免重复：the red one = the red book。

Sentence 9第 9 句 (begins开头 “Yes, please. …”)

Yes, please.

  • When someone offers you something, accept with Yes, please, not a bare Yes.对方要给你东西时，接受说 Yes, please.，不要只说 Yes.。
  • To refuse, say No, thank you or No, thanks.拒绝说 No, thank you. / No, thanks.。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

Give me ...: two objects and object pronounsGive me …：双宾语与宾格代词
give can take two objects: the person first, then the thing. When the person is a pronoun, use the object form: me, you, him, her, us or them.
give 后面可以跟两个宾语：give + 人 + 物。人用代词时要用宾格：me / you / him / her / us / them。

Sentence 4 uses give with me and then the thing.

本课：第 4 句就是 give + me + 物 的说法。

  • Give him a pencil, please.
  • Give us some water, please.
Which ...? and the pronoun oneWhich …? 与代词 one
To choose from a small group, ask Which ...? or Which one? Instead of repeating a countable noun already mentioned, use one: this one, that one, the red one.
在几样东西里挑一样，问 Which …? 或 Which one?。前面说过的可数名词，可以用 one 代替：this one、that one、the red one。

Sentence 5 asks which book, and sentences 6 and 7 both use one in place of book.

本课：第 5 句问哪一本，第 6 句和第 7 句都用 one 代替 book。

  • Which cup is yours? — The blue one.
  • I don't want this knife. Give me a sharp one.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • Give me a/an …, please. 请给我一个……
    Give me a fork, please.
  • Which …? This one? 哪一个？这个吗？
    Which bottle? This one?
  • Not that one — the (adjective) one. 不是那个，是……的那个。
    Not that one — the empty one.
  • Yes, please. / No, thank you. 好的，谢谢。／不用了，谢谢。
    More tea? — Yes, please.
