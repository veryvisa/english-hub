Lesson 117–118 · Tommy's Breakfast 汤米的早餐

📘 New Concept English · Book 1 · Lesson 117–118 “Tommy's Breakfast”新概念英语 第一册 Lesson 117–118《Tommy's Breakfast》

B1🧠 19 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
One morning, on his way into the dining room, the husband dropped a handful of coins. They rolled all over the floor, and the couple could not find every one. During breakfast their small son Tommy picked up two little coins and put both in his mouth. His parents rushed to stop him, but he had already swallowed them. Later, while his wife was cleaning the house, the husband rang from work to ask how the boy was. Her reply ends with a pun on change, which means both coins and something different. This is a first-person story that teaches the past continuous with when and while, the past perfect, where both goes, and a play on words.
早上丈夫往餐厅走时，把一些硬币掉在了地上，滚得到处都是，两口子找了半天也没找全。吃早饭时，小儿子汤米在地上捡到两枚小硬币，一下全塞进嘴里。夫妻俩都去抢，可已经晚了，汤米把硬币吞下去了！上午晚些时候，妻子正在做家务，丈夫从办公室打电话问：汤米怎么样？妻子答：不知道，汤米上午上了三次厕所，可我还没看到「找回的零钱」！本课是一段第一人称叙事，学过去进行时（was going、were having、was doing）与 when / while 的配合、过去完成时 had already swallowed、both 的位置，以及 change 一语双关。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. How did the coins get onto the floor?硬币是怎么掉到地上的？
  2. What did Tommy do, and why couldn't his parents stop him?汤米做了什么？父母为什么没能阻止他？
  3. What does the wife mean by change at the end?妻子最后那句话里的 change 是什么意思？

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander; New Concept English, Book 1, Longman, 1967.

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

This is one of the first real stories in Book One, told in the first person by a mother, and it ends on a pun, a kind of joke British readers love. Small children swallowing coins was, and still is, one of the most common reasons parents phone a doctor. In the 1960s the usual advice for a small smooth coin was to wait and watch, since most pass through on their own within a few days. That is why the mother is calm enough to joke: the story assumes, as the doctor would have, that the coins will come out. A husband ringing home from the office in the late morning was also normal then.

这是第一册里最早的真正故事之一，由一位母亲用第一人称讲述，结尾是一个双关语笑话，这类笑话英国读者很爱。小孩子吞硬币，过去和现在都是父母打电话找医生最常见的原因之一。1960 年代，对小而光滑的硬币，通常的建议是观察等待，因为大多数几天内会自行排出。所以母亲才能平静地开玩笑：故事默认，也正如医生会说的，硬币会出来的。上午晚些时候丈夫从办公室往家里打电话，在当时也很平常。

✨ How it is written赏析

The story is told in three short scenes, each opened by a time clause with a continuous verb, so the reader always knows what was going on when something happened. The scenes grow from a small accident, to a real worry, to a joke, and the last line has to be read twice. The writer plants the word for coins early, in a neutral sense, and saves the other meaning for the end. The past perfect appears once, at the moment of real alarm, and makes it feel final. It is a model of how to build a short anecdote: setting, problem, punch line.

故事分三个短场景讲，每个场景都以一个带进行时动词的时间从句开头，于是读者总知道事情发生时正在进行的是什么。三个场景从一次小意外，到一次真正的担心，再到一个笑话，最后一句得读两遍才明白。作者早早埋下「零钱」这个词，用的是普通意思，把另一个意思留到最后。过去完成时只出现一次，用在真正惊慌的那一刻，让它显得已成定局。这是讲短趣事的范本：背景、问题、包袱。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The pun still works, but the medical advice has changed in one important way. Small coins usually pass on their own, but button batteries and magnets can cause serious harm within hours, and any child who has trouble breathing, keeps coughing, drools or vomits after swallowing something needs emergency help at once. In British Columbia parents can call HealthLink BC at 811 for advice or 911 in an emergency, and the BC Drug and Poison Information Centre is another source. Keep small objects away from toddlers. For learners, the story is a perfect drill for the past continuous with when and while, which Chinese speakers often avoid by using only the simple past.

双关今天依然好笑，但医学建议在一个重要方面变了。小硬币通常能自行排出，但纽扣电池和磁铁可能在几小时内造成严重伤害；孩子吞了东西以后如果呼吸困难、咳个不停、流口水或呕吐，必须马上急救。在不列颠哥伦比亚省，家长可以打 811 找 HealthLink BC 咨询，紧急情况打 911，BC 药物与中毒信息中心也是一个求助渠道。小物件要放在幼儿够不着的地方。对学习者来说，这个故事是练过去进行时配 when 和 while 的绝佳材料，而中国人常常只用一般过去时，绕开这个结构。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 3第 3 句 (begins开头 “When my husband …”)

  • The past continuous gives the longer background action; the past simple gives the sudden event in the middle.过去进行时 was going：一个较长的背景动作；一般过去时 dropped：中途突然发生的事。

Sentence 6第 6 句 (begins开头 “We looked for …”)

  • look for is the search; find is the result.look for（找）是过程，find（找到）是结果。
  • all goes after the pronoun.them all：它们全部，all 放在代词后。

Sentence 7第 7 句 (begins开头 “While we were …”)

  • while with the past continuous gives the time during which something happened.while + 过去进行时：在……期间。
  • Tommy is set off by commas to name the boy.our little boy, Tommy：同位语，逗号隔开。

Sentence 9第 9 句 (begins开头 “He put them …”)

  • both comes after an object pronoun, or after the subject and before the main verb.them both / we both：both 放在代词宾语后或主语后、实义动词前。
  • too late means after the time when it could help.too late：太晚了，来不及了。

Sentence 11第 11 句 (begins开头 “Tommy had already …”)

Tommy had already swallowed them!

  • The past perfect shows it happened before they could reach him.过去完成时：在他们抢之前，汤米已经吞下去了。

Sentence 12第 12 句 (begins开头 “Late that morning, …”)

  • Again, the background is in the past continuous and the call in the past simple.又一次「背景用过去进行时，突发事用一般过去时」。
  • do the housework.do the housework：做家务。

Sentence 16第 16 句 (begins开头 “'Tommy's been to …”)

  • The present perfect, because the morning is not over.Tommy's been = Tommy has been：现在完成时，上午还没过完。
  • change means both coins and something different, which makes the joke.change 双关：零钱 / 变化。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

The past continuous with when and while过去进行时与 when / while
was or were plus the -ing form describes an action in progress at a past time. It often frames a shorter event in the past simple. while usually introduces the longer background; when can introduce either.
was / were + 现在分词：过去某时正在进行的动作。常和一般过去时配合：较长的背景动作用过去进行时，中途插进来的短动作用一般过去时。while 后常接过去进行时（背景），when 后既可接背景也可接突发事。

Sentences 3 and 5, 8 and 9, and 13 and 14 each pair a background action with a sudden event.

本课：第 4 与第 4 句、第 8 与第 8 句、第 13 与第 13 句，每对都是「正在……时，突然……」。

  • While I was cooking, the phone rang.
  • She was walking home when it started to rain.
Where to put bothboth 的位置
both goes after the subject and before a main verb, after be or a helper verb, or after an object pronoun. You can also say both of us or both of them.
主语 + both + 实义动词（We both tried）；主语 + be/助动词 + both（We are both tired / They have both left）；动词 + 代词宾语 + both（put them both）；both of + 代词（both of us）。

Sentence 9 has both after the object, and sentence 10 has both after the subject.

本课：第 9 句 them both，第 10 句 We both tried。

  • My sisters both live in Toronto.
  • We are both from Shanghai.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • When I was going out, I dropped my keys. 我出门时把钥匙掉了。
    When I was going out, I dropped my keys.
  • There were … everywhere. 到处都是……
    There were leaves everywhere.
  • While we were eating, the baby found … 我们吃饭时，宝宝找到了……
    While we were eating, the baby found a crayon.
  • We both tried, but it was too late. 我们俩都试了，可已经来不及了。
    We both tried, but it was too late.
  • … had already left when we arrived. 我们到时……已经走了。
    The bus had already left when we arrived.

Our model summary本站范文

One morning a man let some coins fall on the floor, and they could not find them all. At breakfast, little Tommy found two and swallowed them before his parents could stop him. Later, the man rang from work to ask about Tommy. His wife said the boy had used the toilet three times, but no change had come yet.（60 词）
