Lesson 65–66 · Not a Baby 不是小孩子了

📘 New Concept English · Book 1 · Lesson 65–66 “Not a Baby”新概念英语 第一册 Lesson 65–66《Not a Baby》

B1🧠 18 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
Jill has plans to see friends tonight. Her father lays down the rule: back by half past ten and no later. She protests that this is too early and asks for her own key, which he refuses. Her mother steps in: the girl is eighteen, no longer a child, and is never late anyway. The father gives way and hands over the key, though he sets a new limit of a quarter past eleven. Her mother wishes her a good time, and Jill answers that they always have one. This lesson is bargaining at home: a parent sets a rule, the child pushes back, and the other parent takes her side. It teaches be going to for plans, must, mustn't and can't side by side, telling the time with half past and a quarter past, and the reflexive pronouns yourself and ourselves.
吉尔晚上要和朋友见面。爸爸立规矩：不许晚回来，十点半必须到家。吉尔说那么早回不来，想要前门钥匙，爸爸不给。妈妈出来说话：她十八岁了，不是小孩子，而且一向回来得早。爸爸只好让步，把钥匙给了她，但最晚十一点一刻到家。妈妈说「玩得开心」，吉尔说「我们总是玩得很开心」。本课是家里讨价还价：父母立规矩、孩子争取、另一方帮腔。学 be going to 表示打算、must / mustn't / can't 的对照、时间的说法 half past、a quarter past，以及反身代词 yourself、ourselves。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. What is Jill going to do tonight, and what time does her father first say she must be home?吉尔今晚打算做什么？爸爸一开始要她几点到家？
  2. What two reasons does her mother give to change his mind?妈妈用了哪两个理由说服爸爸？
  3. What time does he finally set?最后爸爸定的时间是几点？

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

In 1960s Britain a teenager having her own key to the front door was a real sign of growing up; the phrase the key of the door was even used to mean coming of age, which at the time was twenty-one. Jill is eighteen, old enough to work and to marry with her parents' consent, but still living under her father's roof and his rules. The legal age of majority in Britain was lowered from twenty-one to eighteen in 1970, a few years after the book appeared. In British Columbia today the age of majority is nineteen, while in most other provinces it is eighteen.

在 1960 年代的英国，青少年有一把自己的前门钥匙，是长大成人的实打实的标志；「门钥匙」这个说法甚至被用来指成年，而当时的成年年龄是二十一岁。吉尔十八岁，够年龄工作，经父母同意也能结婚，但仍住在父亲的屋檐下、守他的规矩。英国的法定成年年龄在 1970 年从二十一岁降到十八岁，就在这本书出版后几年。今天在不列颠哥伦比亚省，成年年龄是十九岁，大多数其他省份是十八岁。

✨ How it is written赏析

The scene is a small negotiation with three voices. The father opens with a pair of rules that say the same thing twice, which tells us how firm he means to be. Jill answers with a complaint and then a request, and the request is cleverer than the complaint: a key means she will not need to be let in at all. The mother does not argue about the time; she changes the question to one of trust, and offers two facts in support. The father then gives way in three short steps, keeping some dignity by setting a new limit and adding a stern question at the end.

这场戏是一场三方小谈判。爸爸开口就是一对规矩，把同一件事说了两遍，可见他打算多强硬。吉尔先抱怨、再请求，而请求比抱怨聪明：有了钥匙，就根本不需要人给她开门。妈妈不去争时间，而是把问题换成「信不信得过」，并拿出两条事实撑腰。爸爸随后分三小步让了步，用一个新的期限和结尾那句严厉的反问保住了面子。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

Curfews are still a normal part of family life in Canada, and the negotiation in this lesson sounds very familiar to parents of teenagers today, though the tools have changed: a phone that shares where you are often takes the place of the front door key. Many Chinese families are stricter about evening hours than Canadian ones, and the idea that the mother can openly take the child's side against the father may surprise some readers. The lesson's point still holds, however: trust is earned by a record of keeping the rules, and the mother's strongest argument is not age but the fact that Jill always comes home early.

宵禁至今仍是加拿大家庭生活的平常部分，本课这场谈判在今天有青少年的父母听来非常熟悉，只是工具变了：手机和定位软件常常取代了前门钥匙。许多中国家庭对晚归比加拿大家庭更严，母亲当面站在孩子一边反对父亲，可能会让一些读者意外。不过课文的道理依然成立：信任是靠守规矩的记录换来的，妈妈最有力的理由不是年龄，而是吉尔一向回来得早。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentence 3第 3 句 (begins开头 “What are you …”)

  • be going to gives a plan already made.be going to do：打算做（事先定好的计划）。

Sentence 6第 6 句 (begins开头 “You must be …”)

  • A ban and a rule say the same thing in two ways. British speakers often shorten the time to half ten.一否一肯说同一件事。half past ten：十点半；英国人也常说 half ten。

Sentence 7第 7 句 (begins开头 “I can't get …”)

  • so early means as early as that; Can I have ...? is a polite request.so early：这么早。Can I have …? 请求「能给我……吗」。
  • We say the key to a door, with to.key to + 门：门的钥匙用 to，不用 of。

Sentence 11第 11 句 (begins开头 “She's not a …”)

  • not a baby means old enough to be trusted. With give, the person comes before the thing.not a baby：不是小孩子了。give sb sth：双宾语，人在前、物在后。

Sentence 16第 16 句 (begins开头 “But you mustn't …”)

  • Adding Oh makes all right a reluctant yes; Here you are is said while handing something over.all right 前加 Oh：不情愿地答应；递东西时说 Here you are。
  • The new limit is 11:15, and Do you hear? presses the order home.Do you hear?：听清楚了吗？带命令口气。a quarter past eleven：十一点一刻。

Sentence 23第 23 句 (begins开头 “We always enjoy …”)

  • enjoy in this sense needs a reflexive pronoun.enjoy 要带反身代词：enjoy yourself / ourselves。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

be going to: plans and intentionsbe going to：打算，准备
Use am, is or are going to plus the bare infinitive for something already decided. In questions, put be first.
am / is / are going to + 动词原形，表示事先打算好的事。问句把 be 提前：What is she going to do?

Sentence 3 asks about the plan and sentence 4 gives it.

本课：第 3 句爸爸问计划，第 4 句吉尔答。

  • I'm going to visit my aunt on Sunday.
  • Are you going to buy a new laptop?
Reflexive pronouns反身代词：myself、yourself、ourselves…
Use a reflexive pronoun when the doer and the receiver are the same. enjoy yourself and help yourself are fixed phrases.
动作的发出者和承受者是同一人时用反身代词：myself、yourself、himself、herself、ourselves、yourselves、themselves。enjoy oneself（玩得开心）、help yourself（自便）是固定说法。

In sentence 22 the mother wishes her fun, and in sentence 23 Jill answers for the group.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • What's he going to do …? — He's going to … 他打算……做什么？——他打算……
    What's he going to do on Saturday? — He's going to clean the house.
  • You mustn't …. You must … 你不许……，你必须……
    You mustn't use your phone at dinner. You must put it away.
  • Could I have a key to …, please? 能给我……的钥匙吗？
    Could I have a key to the garage, please?
  • X's eighteen. She's not a baby. X 都十八了，不是小孩子了。
    Sam's sixteen. He's not a baby.
  • Enjoy yourself! 玩得开心！
    Have a great trip and enjoy yourselves!

Our model summary本站范文

Jill was going out with friends, and her father wanted her back by 10:30. She said that was too early and asked for her own key, which he refused. Her mother reminded him that the girl was eighteen and always came back on time, so he gave in, but insisted she return by 11:15.（54 词）
