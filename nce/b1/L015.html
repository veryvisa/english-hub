Lesson 15–16 · Your Passports, Please 请出示你们的护照

📘 New Concept English · Book 1 · Lesson 15–16 “Your Passports, Please”新概念英语 第一册 Lesson 15–16《Your Passports, Please》

B1🧠 16 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
A customs officer asks two travellers whether they come from Sweden. No, they say, they are from Denmark, and their friends are from Norway. The officer looks at their passports and points to some cases; those are not theirs, because theirs are brown, and they point out their own. Last the officer asks whether they are on holiday, and they are, and so are their friends. Everything is in order. This lesson is the questions you meet at a border, all in the plural: we, they and these. The grammar is plural questions and answers such as Yes, we are and No, they aren't, plural nouns with -s, these and our, and Here they are.
海关官员问一对旅客是不是瑞典人，他们说不是，是丹麦人；同行的朋友是挪威人。官员查了护照，又指着几只箱子问是不是他们的，他们说不是，他们的是棕色的，并把自己的箱子指认出来。最后问他们是不是游客，是；朋友也是。官员说没问题，放行。本课是过关时的一组问答，全部是复数：我们、他们、这些。学 Are you …? 复数问答 Yes, we are / No, they aren't、名词复数 -s、these 与 our，以及 Here they are。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. Does anything go wrong with the customs officer?海关官员那里有没有遇到什么麻烦？
  2. Which country do the travellers come from, and which do their friends come from?旅客和他们的朋友分别是哪国人？
  3. How many questions does the officer ask, and how is each answered?官员一共问了几个问题？每个都是怎么回答的？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander; New Concept English, Book 1, Longman, 1967. The scene is invented to bring in the plural forms of be all at once.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为教材自撰，见《新概念英语》第一册（朗文，1967）。场景为一次引入 be 动词的全部复数形式而设。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

Before cheap flights, many Britons and Europeans travelled by car and ferry, and border checks within Europe meant a customs officer looking at passports and asking about bags. Choosing three Nordic countries is a neat touch: the nationalities sound alike to a beginner, so a wrong guess by the officer is natural, and their languages are in fact close enough that people from those countries can often follow each other. Most of these border checks inside Europe were later removed.

廉价航班普及之前，许多英国人和欧洲人开车坐渡轮出行；欧洲内部过境时，海关官员会查护照、问行李。选三个北欧国家是巧妙的一笔：对初学者来说这几个国籍听起来很像，官员猜错很自然；而这几国的语言本来就很接近，彼此常能听懂。欧洲内部的这类边检后来大多取消了。

✨ How it is written赏析

The scene is a string of questions and short answers, with no small talk at all, which is just how a border conversation should be. The officer asks; the travellers answer exactly what is asked and add only the one fact that settles it, such as the colour of their own cases. Each answer is a full short form, never a bare yes or no. The lesson thus teaches grammar and a useful habit at once: at a border, answer the question you are asked, clearly and briefly.

这一幕是一串问题和简短回答，完全没有寒暄——边检对话本该如此。官员问，旅客只答所问的，再加一条能把事情说清的事实，比如自己箱子的颜色。每个回答都是完整的简略回答，从不是光秃秃的 yes 或 no。这课同时教了语法和一个有用的习惯：在边境，问什么答什么，清楚简短。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

For readers who travel between Canada, the United States and China, this lesson is still close to real life, though the questions have changed. Officers now ask the purpose of your trip, how long you will stay and whether you carry food, cash over a set amount or gifts. Short, true answers work best; long stories or jokes slow things down. If you do not understand a question, saying Sorry, could you repeat that? is far better than guessing. The rules on what to declare change, so check the official border agency site before each trip rather than trusting old advice.

对在加拿大、美国和中国之间往来的读者，这一课仍然贴近现实，只是问题变了。现在官员会问你此行目的、停留多久、有没有带食品、超过限额的现金或礼物。简短真实的回答最好；长篇大论或开玩笑只会耽误时间。听不懂就说 Sorry, could you repeat that?，比瞎猜强得多。申报规定会变，每次出行前去官方边境机构网站查一下，别信旧经验。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 7第 7 句 (begins开头 “Are your friends …”)

Are your friends Danish too?

  • A plural subject takes are, and the answer uses they.复数主语用 are：Are your friends …?；答句主语换成 they。
  • too at the end of a question means also.too 在问句末尾表示「也」。

Sentence 12第 12 句 (begins开头 “Are these your …”)

Are these your cases?

  • these is the plural of this, so the noun after it is plural and the verb is are.these 是 this 的复数，后面名词也要复数（cases），动词用 are。
  • In the answer, use they: No, they aren't, not No, these aren't.答句用 they：No, they aren't.，不说 No, these aren't.。

Sentence 14第 14 句 (begins开头 “Our cases are …”)

Our cases are brown.

  • our means belonging to us; with the plural subject cases the verb is are.our 我们的；复数名词 cases 作主语，动词用 are。
  • By giving the colour of their own cases, they show that the others are not theirs.说明自己箱子的颜色，就是在说「那些不是我们的」。

Sentence 16第 16 句 (begins开头 “Are you tourists? …”)

Are you tourists?

  • you means one person or several; the plural tourists shows that the officer is asking both.you 既是「你」也是「你们」；后面的名词复数 tourists 说明是在问两个人。
  • The answer uses we: Yes, we are.答句用 we：Yes, we are.。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

Plural questions and answers with we, you and they复数的问与答：we / you / they + are
Every plural subject takes are: we are, you are, they are. To ask, move are to the front. In the answer, you becomes we, and a noun becomes they: Yes, we are, or No, they aren't.
复数主语一律用 are：We are / You are / They are。问句把 are 提前；答句 you → we，名词 → they：Yes, we are. / No, they aren't.。

Sentence 5 answers with we, sentences 8 and 13 with they, and sentences 17 and 19 use each once more.

本课：第 5 句用 we 作答，第 8 句与第 13 句用 they 作答，第 17 句与第 19 句再各用一次。

  • Are you students? — Yes, we are.
  • Are your parents at home? — No, they aren't.
Plural nouns and these / those名词复数与 these / those
Most nouns add -s for the plural: cases, friends, tourists. this becomes these and that becomes those, and the noun and verb after them become plural too.
多数名词加 -s 变复数：case → cases，friend → friends，tourist → tourists。this → these，that → those，后面的名词和动词都要跟着变复数。

Sentence 12 uses these with a plural noun, and in sentence 14 a plural subject takes are.

本课：第 12 句用 these 加复数名词，第 14 句的复数主语带 are。

  • This passport is mine. → These passports are ours.
  • That case is brown. → Those cases are brown.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • Your passports, please. 请出示你们的护照。
    Your tickets, please.
  • Are you …? — No, we aren't. We're … 你们是……吗？——不，我们是……
    Are you Australian? — No, we aren't. We're from New Zealand.
  • Are your friends … too? 你们的朋友也是……吗？
    Are your parents teachers too?
  • Our … are (colour). Here they are. 我们的……是……色的。在这儿。
    Our coats are black. Here they are.
