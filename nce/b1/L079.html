Lesson 79–80 · Carol's Shopping List 卡罗尔的购物单

📘 New Concept English · Book 1 · Lesson 79–80 “Carol's Shopping List”新概念英语 第一册 Lesson 79–80《Carol's Shopping List》

B1🧠 17 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
Carol is writing a shopping list, and Tom asks what they are short of. Quite a lot, it seems. She has to visit the grocer's because they are low on tea and coffee and have run out of sugar and jam. At the greengrocer's she needs tomatoes, though there are plenty of potatoes at home. And the butcher's is a must, since there is no meat in the house. Tom asks whether there is beer or wine left; there is not, and she has no plans to buy any. She says she hopes Tom has some cash, and he admits he has only a little. Neither does she. This lesson is checking what the house is short of. It teaches have got in positive, negative and question forms, much with uncountable and many with countable nouns, some and any, a lot of, not ... at all, and either at the end of a negative.
卡罗尔在列购物单，汤姆问家里缺什么。这周缺的东西很多：要去食品杂货店，因为茶和咖啡不多了，糖和果酱一点也没有；要去蔬果店，西红柿不多，土豆倒是很多；还得去肉铺，家里一点肉都没有。汤姆问啤酒和葡萄酒还有没有——没有了，而且她不打算买！卡罗尔说希望汤姆身上有钱，汤姆说他没多少；卡罗尔说：我也没多少！本课是清点家里缺什么，学 have got 的肯定、否定与问句，much 与 many（不可数与可数），some 与 any，a lot of，not … at all，以及 either 用在否定句里。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. Which shops must Carol go to, and what does she need at each?卡罗尔要去哪几家店？各买什么？
  2. What do they have plenty of, so she need not buy it?哪样东西家里有很多，不用买？
  3. What won't Carol buy?卡罗尔不打算买什么？

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander; New Concept English, Book 1, Longman, 1967.

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

A weekly shopping list that sends one person to three different shops was the normal way of buying food in Britain in the 1960s. The grocer sold dry goods such as tea, coffee, sugar and jam; the greengrocer sold fruit and vegetables; the butcher sold meat. Many households did one big shop a week, and the housewife usually managed both the list and the money. The note at the end, that neither has much money, would have been a familiar joke in a time when many families lived from one weekly pay packet to the next. In Canada today, a single supermarket trip replaces all three shops, and many people keep the list on their phone.

一张让人跑三家店的每周购物单，是 1960 年代英国买食物的常规做法。杂货店卖茶、咖啡、糖、果酱之类的干货；蔬果店卖水果蔬菜；肉铺卖肉。很多家庭每周集中采购一次，主妇通常既管单子又管钱。结尾两人都没多少钱这一笔，在那个许多家庭靠每周一封工资袋过日子的年代，是大家熟悉的笑话。今天在加拿大，一趟超市就能代替三家店，很多人把购物单记在手机上。

✨ How it is written赏析

The lesson is a list turned into a conversation, and its order is the order of a real shopping trip: one shop, then the next, then the next. Inside each shop the grammar changes in a small, careful way, from much for things you cannot count to many for things you can, and from not any to the stronger not any at all. Tom's only real question is about drink, and Carol's answer is the one moment of feeling in the text, a firm refusal. The ending reverses the roles neatly: she hopes he will pay, he has little, and her reply with either shows that they are in the same position.

这一课是把一张清单变成了一段对话，顺序就是一趟真实的购物路线：一家店，再一家，再一家。在每家店里，语法都做了细小而用心的变化：从修饰不可数东西的 much，到修饰可数东西的 many；从 not any 到语气更重的 not any at all。汤姆唯一真正的问题是关于酒的，卡罗尔的回答是全文唯一带情绪的一刻：坚决拒绝。结尾把角色整齐地翻了过来：她希望他付钱，他钱不多，而她用 either 回答，表明两人处境一样。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The grammar here causes trouble for Chinese learners, because Chinese uses one and the same word for both much and many, and no articles at all. A simple test helps: if you can put a number in front of the noun, as in three tomatoes, use many; if you cannot, as with tea, use much. Have got is mainly British, and in Canada learners will hear do you have far more often, so it is worth knowing both. The habit of planning food for a week and buying only what is missing is still good household sense, and with food prices rising in Canada, the list in this lesson is worth copying in spirit.

这里的语法让中国学习者头疼，因为中文 much 和 many 都说「很多」，而且根本没有冠词。有个简单的测法：名词前能加数字的，比如 three tomatoes，就用 many；不能加的，比如 tea，就用 much。have got 主要是英式说法，在加拿大学习者听到 do you have 的机会多得多，所以两种都要会。按一周规划饮食、只买缺的东西，至今仍是持家的好办法；在加拿大食品价格上涨的今天，这一课的购物单在精神上值得照学。

Sentence 5第 5 句 (begins开头 “What do we …”)

  • is making is happening now; a lot of goes with both kinds of noun.be making：正在做（现在进行时）。a lot of：很多，可数不可数都能用。

Sentence 8第 8 句 (begins开头 “We haven't got …”)

  • have got means have in British speech; the negative is haven't got.have got = have（英式口语）；否定 haven't got。
  • much goes with uncountable nouns; in a negative, or joins two things instead of and.much 修饰不可数名词（tea、coffee）；否定句里两样东西用 or 连，不用 and。

Sentence 11第 11 句 (begins开头 “We haven't got …”)

  • many goes with plural countable nouns; in positive sentences, a lot of is more natural than many.many 修饰可数名词复数；肯定句里说「很多」通常用 a lot of，不用 much / many。

Sentence 14第 14 句 (begins开头 “We haven't got …”)

  • some is used in positives and any in negatives; at all makes the negative stronger.肯定句用 some，否定句用 any；at all 加强否定：一点也没有。

Sentence 15第 15 句 (begins开头 “Have we got …”)

  • have got questions put have first; short answers repeat have.have got 的问句：Have we got …?；简答 No, we haven't.
  • not going to means she has decided not to; after any the nouns are left out.not going to：不打算。any 后面省了 beer and wine。

Sentence 20第 20 句 (begins开头 “Well, I haven't …”)

  • either goes at the end of a negative; too is for positives.either：也（不），放在否定句末；肯定句用 too。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

much, many and a lot of
much goes with uncountable nouns and many with plural countable nouns, and both are used mostly in negatives and questions. Positive sentences usually use a lot of instead.
much 修饰不可数名词（much tea、much money），many 修饰可数名词复数（many tomatoes、many friends），两者多用于否定句和问句；肯定句一般用 a lot of。问数量：How much …? / How many …?

Sentence 8 uses much for tea and coffee, sentence 11 uses many and a lot of, and sentence 19 uses much for money.

本课：第 8 句 tea、coffee 用 much，第 11 句 tomatoes 用 many、potatoes 用 a lot of，第 19 句钱用 much。

  • There isn't much milk in the fridge.
  • How many eggs do we need?
have got with some and anyhave got 与 some / any
have got means have. Positives use some, negatives and questions use any. In North America, have with do is more common.
have got（= have）：I've got … / I haven't got … / Have you got …?；简答 Yes, I have. / No, I haven't.。肯定句用 some，否定句和问句用 any。北美更常说 I have / I don't have / Do you have …?。

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • I'm making a … list. What do we need? 我在列……单，我们缺什么？
    I'm making a packing list. What do we need?
  • We haven't got much …, and we haven't got any … ……不多了，……一点也没有了。
    We haven't got much rice, and we haven't got any eggs.
  • We haven't got many …, but there are lots of … ……不多，……倒是很多。
    We haven't got many apples, but there are lots of bananas.
  • I hope you've got some … 希望你有点……
    I hope you've got some cash for the parking.
  • I haven't got much either! 我也没多少！
    I haven't got much time either!

Summary writing摘要写作

In no more than 60 words, say what Carol needs to buy this week, what she will not buy, and how it ends.

Our model summary本站范文

Carol was making a long shopping list. They were low on tea and coffee and out of sugar and jam, so she had to visit the grocer's. She needed tomatoes but had plenty of potatoes, and there was no meat at all. She refused to buy beer or wine. In the end, neither she nor Tom had much money.（59 词）
