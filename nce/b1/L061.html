Lesson 61–62 · A Bad Cold 重感冒

📘 New Concept English · Book 1 · Lesson 61–62 “A Bad Cold”新概念英语 第一册 Lesson 61–62《A Bad Cold》

B1🧠 16 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
Jimmy is in bed, looking and feeling unwell. His parents decide to send for the doctor, and his mother knows the number by heart. The doctor has the boy open wide and put out his tongue, then gives his verdict: a heavy cold and a week in bed. The father remarks that the boy will be pleased, since school is not his favourite place. This lesson is a visit from the doctor: asking what is wrong, describing how someone seems, and hearing the decision. It teaches What's the matter with ...?, feel and look followed by an adjective, the modal must, so introducing a result, and ways of saying someone is ill, such as have a cold.
吉米躺在床上，脸色难看，人也不舒服。爸妈决定请医生，妈妈记得诊所的电话。医生来了，让吉米张嘴、伸舌头，说他得了重感冒，要卧床一周。爸爸说这对吉米倒是好消息——因为他不爱上学。本课是看病的一来一回：问「怎么了」、答症状、医生下结论。学 What's the matter with …?、feel / look + 形容词、情态动词 must、so 引出结果，以及 have a cold 这类「生病」的说法。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. What is wrong with Jimmy, and what does the doctor tell him to do?吉米怎么了？医生让他怎么做？
  2. How are feels in sentence 6 and looks in sentence 7 different?第 6 句和第 7 句的 feels 和 looks 有什么不同？
  3. Why is a week in bed good news for the boy?为什么说「卧床一周」对吉米是好消息？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander; New Concept English, Book 1, Longman, 1967.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为教材自撰，见《新概念英语》第一册（朗文，1967）。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

In Britain in the 1960s it was normal for a family doctor to come to the house when a child was ill in bed. The National Health Service had been running since 1948, home visits were free, and a GP often knew the family well enough to be called by telephone at any hour. A five-figure phone number like the one the mother remembers was typical for a town exchange of the time. Today in Canada the picture is very different: a doctor coming to the house for a cold is almost unheard of, many families have no family doctor at all, and the usual advice for a cold is rest, fluids and a walk-in clinic only if it gets worse.

在 1960 年代的英国，孩子病倒在床上时请家庭医生上门是很平常的事。国民医疗服务体系自 1948 年开始运作，出诊免费，家庭医生常常和这家人熟到随时可以打电话叫来。妈妈记得的那种五位数电话号码，也是当时城镇交换台的典型号码。今天加拿大的情形很不一样：为了感冒请医生上门几乎闻所未闻，很多家庭根本没有家庭医生，感冒的常规建议是休息、多喝水，病情加重才去免预约诊所。

✨ How it is written赏析

The lesson is built on a contrast between two verbs that look almost the same. The boy feels unwell, which is his own report, and he also looks unwell, which is what the parents see; together they make the case for calling the doctor. The doctor then speaks only in short commands, the way doctors really talk to children, and gives a judgement joined to an order with a single so. The joke at the end works because the father turns the bad news into good news, and the reason comes last in the shortest line of the text.

这一课建立在两个几乎一样的动词的对照上。孩子「感觉」不舒服，这是他自己说的；他也「看起来」不舒服，这是父母看到的；两者合起来，才构成请医生的理由。医生接着只用短短的祈使句说话，正是医生对孩子说话的真实样子；他的判断和命令只靠一个 so 连起来。结尾的笑点在于爸爸把坏消息说成好消息，而理由放在最后、用全课最短的一句交代出来。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The grammar here is still exactly right: What's the matter? and feel or look plus an adjective are the core of any conversation about health. What has changed is the medicine. A week in bed for an ordinary cold is no longer the advice in Canada; most doctors would say rest at home for a few days and go back to school once the fever has gone. Chinese readers may also notice a difference in tone: many Chinese families treat a child's cold with home remedies and more clothing, while the lesson's family turns to the doctor at once. Both approaches share one idea, that a sick child needs an adult to decide what happens next.

本课的语法今天依然完全对路：What's the matter? 以及 feel / look 加形容词，是任何关于健康的对话的核心。变了的是医学。普通感冒卧床一周，如今已不是加拿大的建议；多数医生会说在家休息几天，退烧后就回学校。中国读者还会注意到语气上的差别：许多中国家庭给孩子治感冒用家常办法、加衣服，而课文里这家人第一时间就找医生。两种做法有一个共同的想法：生病的孩子需要大人来决定接下来怎么办。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 5第 5 句 (begins开头 “What's the matter …”)

  • This is the usual question when someone seems ill or upset.What's the matter with sb?：某人怎么了？常用于问身体或情绪。
  • feel here is a linking verb, so an adjective follows, never an adverb.feel + 形容词：feel 是系动词，后面跟 ill，不用副词 illy。

Sentence 7第 7 句 (begins开头 “He looks ill. …”)

He looks ill.

  • look as a linking verb means 'appear'. Sentence 6 gives how the boy feels inside, sentence 7 how he seems to others.look 作「看起来」也是系动词。第 6 句说他自己的感觉，第 7 句说别人看到的样子。

Sentence 15第 15 句 (begins开头 “He has a …”)

  • English uses have for having an illness.have a bad cold：得了重感冒。英语用 have 表示「患病」：have a headache / have a fever。
  • so gives the result; must shows the doctor's instruction.so：所以，引出结果。must：必须，这是医生的要求。

Sentence 18第 18 句 (begins开头 “Because he doesn't …”)

  • A why question is answered with because, and the reply is the joke.Why? 的回答用 Because …；最后一句是笑点。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

The modal must: something is necessary情态动词 must：必须
must takes the bare infinitive and never adds -s. In questions it goes first, and the negative mustn't means 'it is not allowed'.
must 后跟动词原形，没有人称变化（he must，不说 he musts）；问句把 must 提前：Must he …?；否定 mustn't 表示「不许」。

Sentence 8 is the parents' decision and sentence 15 the doctor's order, and both use must.

本课：第 8 句父母决定请医生，第 15 句医生要求卧床，都用了 must。

  • You must wear a seat belt.
  • Must I finish it today?
feel and look followed by an adjectivefeel / look + 形容词
As linking verbs, feel and look are followed by adjectives that describe a state.
feel、look 作系动词时后面接形容词，说明状态：feel ill、look tired、feel better。

Sentence 6 is about the boy's feelings and sentence 7 about his appearance.

本课：第 6 句讲感受，第 7 句讲外表。

  • The soup smells good.
  • You look very pale this morning.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • What's the matter with …? — He/She … ……怎么了？——他/她……
    What's the matter with your sister? — She has a headache.
  • X has a bad …, so X must … X 得了……，所以必须……
    Lucy has a bad cough, so she must stay at home.
  • We must call … 我们得叫……
    We must call a plumber.
  • That's good news for … 那对……倒是好消息
    The snow day is good news for the kids.
