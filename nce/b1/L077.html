Lesson 77–78 · Terrible Toothache 牙痛得厉害

📘 New Concept English · Book 1 · Lesson 77–78 “Terrible Toothache”新概念英语 第一册 Lesson 77–78《Terrible Toothache》

B1🧠 15 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
Mr. Croft comes into a dental surgery early one morning, asking to see the dentist. The nurse checks whether he has an appointment; he has not. Is it urgent? Very, he says: he feels dreadful and his tooth is killing him. She offers him ten o'clock on Monday the twenty-fourth of April, but he insists that it has to be now. The dentist is busy, she explains; would two in the afternoon suit him? He thinks that is far too long to wait. She is sorry, but there is no earlier slot. Surely he can hold on till the afternoon? He can, he replies, but the pain cannot. This lesson is trying to get seen at a front desk. It teaches Do you have an appointment?, Is it urgent?, Can you come at ...? for fixing a time, I'm afraid that ..., negative questions with Can't you ...?, and how to say a.m., p.m. and dates.
克罗夫特先生一早来到牙科诊所，说要看牙医。护士问他有没有预约——没有。急吗？急，非常急：他难受得要命，牙痛得厉害。护士说可以约在 4 月 24 日周一上午十点；他说必须现在就看。护士说牙医此刻很忙，下午两点行不行？他嫌太晚。护士说恐怕不行，问他不能等到下午吗？他说：我能等，我的牙痛可等不了！本课是在诊所前台争取一个号，学 Do you have an appointment?、Is it urgent?、Can you come at …? 的约时间说法、I'm afraid that …、Can't you …? 否定疑问句，以及 a.m. / p.m. 和日期的读法。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

In the 1960s a British dentist usually worked in a small surgery with one assistant who also managed the appointment book, and the lesson calls her a nurse. Dental care had been part of the National Health Service since 1948, but patients still needed to book ahead, and a walk-in patient had to hope for a gap. The date in the lesson, Monday the twenty-fourth of April, depends on the year and was probably changed between editions; we have not checked which year it fits. In Canada today, dental care is mostly paid privately or through work insurance, though a federal dental plan has been rolling out in stages since late 2023 for people without coverage, and most dental offices keep some time each day for emergencies.

1960 年代英国牙医通常在一间小诊所工作，只有一名助手，兼管预约簿，课文里称她为护士。牙科自 1948 年起已纳入国民医疗服务体系，但病人仍需要提前预约，没预约直接来的人只能指望有空档。课文里的日期「4 月 24 日周一」取决于年份，很可能在不同版本之间改过；我们没有核对它对应哪一年。今天在加拿大，看牙大多自费或靠单位保险，不过联邦牙科计划自 2023 年底起分阶段为没有牙科保险的人提供保障，多数牙科诊所每天也会留出一些时间看急诊。

✨ How it is written赏析

The dialogue is a polite tug of war in which each side has one weapon. The nurse has the appointment book and a set of courteous phrases, and she uses them in order: a question about appointments, a question about urgency, a first date far away, a second time closer, and finally a negative question that asks him to give in. Mr. Croft has only his pain, and he repeats it in stronger and stronger words. The ending turns the conflict into a joke without settling it: we never learn whether he gets seen. By giving the toothache its own will, he makes his case in a way the nurse cannot answer with a rule.

这段对话是一场礼貌的拉锯战，双方各有一件武器。护士手里有预约簿和一套客气话，按顺序使出来：问有没有预约，问急不急，先给一个很远的日期，再给一个近一点的时间，最后用一个否定疑问句请他让步。克罗夫特先生只有他的疼痛，他一遍比一遍说得更重。结尾把冲突变成一个笑话，却没有给出结果：我们始终不知道他最后看上没有。他给牙痛安上了自己的意志，用一种护士没法拿规定来回答的方式为自己辩护。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

Any newcomer to Canada will meet this conversation, at a dentist's, a family doctor's or a government office. The key phrases are exactly the ones in the lesson: Do you have an appointment?, Is it urgent?, and the time offers with at and on. One cultural point is worth knowing. Front-desk staff in Canada usually cannot break the rules, but they can often find a cancellation or put you on a waiting list, so the useful move is to ask Could you call me if there is a cancellation? rather than simply insisting. And for real dental emergencies, such as swelling or fever, many cities have emergency dental clinics that see patients the same day.

任何刚到加拿大的人都会遇到这段对话：在牙科诊所、家庭医生诊所或政府办事处。关键说法正是课文里的这些：Do you have an appointment?、Is it urgent?，以及带 at 和 on 的约时间。有一个文化点值得知道：加拿大的前台通常不能破例，但常常可以帮你找到别人取消的空档，或者把你放进候补名单，所以有用的做法是问一句 Could you call me if there is a cancellation?，而不是一味坚持。真正的牙科急症，比如肿胀或发烧，很多城市都有当天就诊的牙科急诊。

  • Everyone says Good morning, so it is before noon.课前问题的答案：大家都说 Good morning，所以是上午。

Sentence 8第 8 句 (begins开头 “Is it urgent? …”)

  • feel awful means feel very unwell; illnesses take have.feel awful：难受极了。have a toothache：牙痛，「患病」用 have。

Sentence 13第 13 句 (begins开头 “Can you come …”)

  • Use at for a clock time and on for a date. British speakers say April the twenty-fourth; North Americans often drop the.钟点用 at，日期用 on。April 24th 读作 April the twenty-fourth（英）或 April twenty-fourth（北美）。

Sentence 15第 15 句 (begins开头 “The dentist is …”)

  • must here is his own sense of necessity; at the moment means right now.must 表示他自己觉得非这样不可。at the moment：此刻。

Sentence 19第 19 句 (begins开头 “I'm afraid that …”)

  • A negative question gently presses the other person: surely you can?Can't you …?：否定疑问句，带一点劝说：你就不能……吗？
  • wait till means wait until.wait till：等到。

Sentence 21第 21 句 (begins开头 “I can wait, …”)

  • wait is left out after can't. Treating the toothache like a person who cannot be patient is the joke.省略：can't 后面省了 wait。把牙痛说成会「等不及」的人，是个巧妙的拟人。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

Making an appointment: Can you come at ...? and time prepositions约时间：Can you come at …? 与时间介词
Use at for clock times, on for days and dates, and in for months and years. No preposition goes before this afternoon or tomorrow.
约时间常用 Can you come at …? / How about …? / Is … OK?。钟点用 at（at 2 p.m.），星期和日期用 on（on Monday, on April 24th），月份和年份用 in（in April），this afternoon / tomorrow 前不加介词。

Sentence 13 uses both at and on, sentence 16 only at, and in sentence 20 this afternoon has no preposition.

本课：第 13 句同时用了 at 和 on，第 16 句只用 at，第 20 句的 this afternoon 不带介词。

  • Can you come at 3:30 on Friday?
  • The clinic is closed in August.
Negative questions: Can't you ...? and Aren't you ...?否定疑问句：Can't you …? / Aren't you …?
A negative question often shows what the speaker expects or hopes. Answer according to the facts: Yes, I can if you can, No, I can't if you cannot.
否定疑问句常带说话人的期待或劝说：Can't you wait?（你就不能等等吗？）；也可作感叹：Aren't you lucky!。回答看事实：能等就说 Yes, I can；不能就说 No, I can't。

In sentence 20 the nurse uses one to persuade him to wait.

本课：第 20 句护士用它劝他等。

  • Can't you stay a little longer?
  • Don't you like coffee? — No, I don't.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • I'd like to see the …. — Do you have an appointment? 我要看……——您有预约吗？
    I'd like to see the manager. — Do you have an appointment?
  • Is it urgent? — Yes, it's very urgent. 急吗？——非常急。
    Is it urgent? — Yes, the water is everywhere!
  • Can you come at … on …? 您能……日……点来吗？
    Can you come at 9:15 on Thursday, May 2nd?
  • I'm afraid he can't. Can't you wait till …? 恐怕不行。您不能等到……吗？
    I'm afraid she can't. Can't you wait till Friday?
  • I can …, but my … can't! 我能……，可我的……不能！
    I can wait for dinner, but my stomach can't!

Our model summary本站范文

Mr. Croft arrived at the dentist's one morning without an appointment and with a terrible toothache. The nurse offered him ten o'clock the next Monday, then two that afternoon, since the dentist was busy. When she asked whether he could wait until then, he said he could, but his toothache could not.（52 词）
