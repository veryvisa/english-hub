Lesson 133–134 · Sensational News 爆炸性新闻

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
At the airport a reporter asks the film star Karen Marsh whether her new film is finished and whether another is coming. She answers yes to the first and no to the second: she plans to stop working, because she is worn out and wants a long break from films. Liz and Kate buy a paper in which the reporter, Alan Jones, has turned her words into a sensational story, describing her clothes as well. Kate wonders aloud what the real reason is. This lesson tells the same talk twice, first in direct speech and then in reported speech. It teaches said and told someone with a clause, moving tenses back, and I wonder why.
机场上，记者问女演员卡伦·马什是不是刚拍完新片，还拍不拍下一部。她说片子刚拍完，以后不拍了，要退休：她觉得很累，很长时间内都不想再拍片。莉兹和凯特买了份报纸，上面记者艾伦·琼斯把这段话写成了「爆炸性新闻」，连她穿什么都写上了。凯特嘀咕：「我倒想知道为什么！」本课把同一段话先用直接引语、再用间接引语说一遍，学 said / told sb + 从句、时态后移（am going to → was going to，have made → had made）和 I wonder why。

  2. How is the newspaper story different from her own words?报纸上的报道和她的原话有什么不同？

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander; New Concept English, Book 1, Longman, 1967. The actress Karen Marsh first appears in Lesson 127; the reporter Alan Jones and the newspaper are invented.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为教材自撰，见《新概念英语》第一册（朗文，1967）。女演员卡伦·马什第一次出场在第 127 课；记者艾伦·琼斯和报纸都是虚构的。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

London's main airport, then called London Airport and renamed Heathrow in 1966, was where stars arriving from America met the press, and photographers waited at the bottom of the aircraft steps. Popular papers loved such airport interviews because they were quick and gave a photo. A mink coat was the classic sign of film-star wealth. Stars announcing that they would retire, and then making another film, was a familiar pattern that readers learned not to take too seriously.

伦敦主要的机场当时叫「伦敦机场」，1966 年才改名希思罗；从美国来的明星在那里见记者，摄影师就守在舷梯下面。大众报纸很喜欢这种机场采访，因为快，还能配一张照片。貂皮大衣是电影明星阔气的经典标志。明星宣布要退休、过后又拍新片，是读者早已熟悉、不太当真的套路。

✨ How it is written赏析

The lesson places two versions of one conversation side by side so the learner can see each change. Every sentence in the report matches a line in the interview, and the tenses move back one step at a time. The writer adds one detail the actress never said, her clothes, which shows how a reporter fills out a story, and the word sensational in the headline is a gentle joke, since nothing she said is surprising at all.

这一课把同一段对话的两个版本并排放着，学习者能看清每一处变化。报道里每一句都对应采访中的一句，时态一步一步往后推。作者还加了一个女演员从没说过的细节——她的衣着——显示记者是怎样把一个故事撑满的；标题里的 sensational 是一个温和的玩笑，因为她说的话一点也不惊人。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

Reported speech is everyday English, not only a textbook rule: at work in Canada you will often pass on what a client, a manager or a doctor said, and moving the tense back sounds natural to listeners. In speech people often keep the present when the fact is still true, as in She said she lives in Surrey, so do not panic if you hear both. The lesson is also a small lesson in media reading: compare the headline with the quotes, and ask, as Kate does, what is not being said.

间接引语是日常英语，不只是课本规则：在加拿大工作，你常要转述客户、经理或医生说的话，时态后移在听者耳里很自然。口语里如果事情到现在还成立，人们常保留现在时，比如 She said she lives in Surrey，所以两种都听到也别慌。这一课也是一堂小小的读报课：把标题和引语对照着看，像凯特那样问一问，有什么没说出来。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

Reported speech: moving tenses back间接引语：时态后移
When you report words with said or told in the past, the verb in the clause usually moves one step back: present simple to past simple, present continuous to past continuous, am going to to was going to, present perfect to past perfect. Pronouns change too, so I becomes she. that can be left out.
用过去时的 said / told 转述别人的话时，从句的时态一般往过去推一步：一般现在 → 一般过去（feel → felt），现在进行 → 过去进行，am going to → was going to，现在完成 → 过去完成（have made → had made）。人称也要跟着换：I → she。that 可省。

Sentences 17 to 20 report the words from sentences 4 to 10, with every tense moved back.

本课：第 17–20 句把第 4–10 句的原话转述一遍，时态全部后移。

  • 'I'm hungry.' → He said he was hungry.
  • 'We have finished.' → They told me they had finished.
say and tellsay 与 tell
tell needs a listener after it: tell me, tell reporters, then the clause. say goes straight to the clause; to mention the listener, use say to someone. tell also has fixed uses: tell a story, tell the truth, tell a lie.
tell 后面要接听话人：tell me / tell reporters + (that) 从句。say 后面不直接接人，直接接从句；要提听话人说 say to sb。tell 还有固定搭配：tell a story、tell the truth、tell a lie。

Sentences 17 and 20 use told with a person; sentences 18 and 19 use said.

本课：第 17、20 句用 told + 人，第 18、19 句用 said。

  • She told me she was leaving.
  • She said she was leaving.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

In no more than 70 words, use reported speech to tell what Karen Marsh said to the press at the airport.

用不超过 70 个词，用间接引语转述卡伦·马什在机场对记者说的话。

Points a good summary covers好摘要要覆盖的要点

Our model summary本站范文

Karen Marsh landed in London today in blue, with a mink fur on her shoulders. She told the press her latest film was complete. She had no plans for another, and she planned to retire. She said she felt exhausted and wanted a long rest from acting. Kate wondered if that was the real reason.（55 词）
