Mr Jackson shows a visitor, Mr Richards, round the office and introduces the staff. Two women work at keyboards, and he praises them as hard-working; two men are sales reps, and he says they have little to do and are lazy; last comes a young man, Jim, the office assistant. Each introduction is met with How do you do? This lesson is a formal introduction at work, and the first time the book talks about people in the plural: those women, they're, their jobs. The grammar is How do you do?, They're and They aren't, What are their jobs? and Who is ...?

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander; New Concept English, Book 1, Longman, 1967. The office and its staff are invented to drill plural people and the formal greeting How do you do.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为教材自撰，见《新概念英语》第一册（朗文，1967）。办公室与员工是虚构的，用来操练复数的人和正式问候 How do you do。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

How do you do? was once the standard greeting at a formal first meeting in Britain. It is not a question: the correct reply is to say the same words back, and it would be odd to answer with a report on your health. The drill lesson after it adds more jobs and the plural forms of nouns, including the irregular ones such as men and women, which every learner needs early.

How do you do? 曾经是英国正式初次见面的标准问候。它不是问题，正确的回答就是原话奉还；如果认真回答自己的身体状况，反而奇怪。后面那课操练又加了一些职业和名词复数，包括 men、women 这类不规则复数，这是每个读者都要早早掌握的。

✨ How it is written赏析

The lesson hides a small joke in its plain sentences. The visitor hears the women praised and the men criticised, and the only reason given is how busy each pair looks at that moment. The boss's words are short and blunt, and the exclamation mark after the men's line tells the reader to hear it as a remark, not a fact. Then the scene moves on to Jim without comment, so the learner has to notice the joke alone.

平铺直叙的句子里藏着一个小玩笑。客人听到女士被夸、男士被批，唯一的理由是那一刻谁看起来忙。老板的话又短又直，男士那句后面的感叹号提醒读者：这是一句吐槽，不是事实。然后场景若无其事地转到吉姆身上，玩笑得读者自己去发现。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

Two parts of this lesson have aged. How do you do? is now rare outside very formal settings; at work in Canada people say Nice to meet you or simply Hi, I'm Sam. And a manager who calls his staff lazy in front of a visitor would today be seen as unprofessional, and in many workplaces it could lead to a complaint. Sales reps, too, often look idle in the office because their real work is outside it, with clients, so judging people by how busy they look is a weak test. The lesson is a useful reminder that one glance is not a fair measure of anyone's work.

这课有两处已经过时。How do you do? 现在除了非常正式的场合已很少用；在加拿大的职场，人们说 Nice to meet you，或者干脆 Hi, I'm Sam。而经理当着客人的面说员工懒，今天会被看作不专业，在很多单位还可能招来投诉。销售代表在办公室显得清闲，往往是因为他们真正的工作在外面、在客户那里；凭「看起来忙不忙」判断人，是一把很弱的尺子。这课正好提醒我们：看一眼，不足以公平地衡量任何人的工作。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 4第 4 句 (begins开头 “Come and meet …”)

  • come and plus a verb is the spoken way to say come to meet.come and + 动词原形：「来……吧」，是 come to meet 的口语说法。
  • The name of the person spoken to goes at the end, after a comma.称呼放句末，前面加逗号。

Sentence 9第 9 句 (begins开头 “Those women are …”)

  • The plural of woman is women, an irregular form; even the first sound changes.woman 的复数是 women，不规则；读音 /ˈwɪmɪn/，第一个音节也变了。
  • those goes with a plural noun and are.those + 复数名词 + are。

Sentence 15第 15 句 (begins开头 “They aren't very …”)

They aren't very busy!

  • not very with an adjective means only a little, which is softer than not busy.not very + 形容词：「不太……」，比 not busy 委婉。
  • The exclamation mark shows he is being sarcastic: they seem to have nothing to do.感叹号说明这句话带讽刺：他们看起来没事做。

Sentence 21第 21 句 (begins开头 “He's our office …”)

He's our office assistant.

  • he's is he is, and our shows Jim belongs to the company.he's = he is；our 我们的，说明这是本公司的人。
  • The answer to Who is ...? gives the name with This is and then the job.回答 Who is …? 用 This is + 名字，再说身份。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

Talking about people in the plural: They're ... / They aren't ...复数说人：They're … / They aren't …
For two or more people use they: They are, or They're, with an adjective or a plural job. The negative is They aren't. The job word must be plural: They're keyboard operators.
说两个以上的人用 they：They are（They're）+ 形容词/职业复数；否定 They aren't。职业名词要用复数：They're keyboard operators.。

Sentences 11 and 17 give jobs, sentence 15 is a negative, and sentence 18 describes character.

本课：第 11 句和第 17 句说职业，第 15 句是否定，第 18 句说性格。

  • They're nurses. They're very busy.
  • Tom and Ann aren't at work today. They're on holiday.
Who is ...? and What are their jobs?Who is …? 与 What are their jobs?
To ask who a person is, say Who is with the person. To ask the jobs of several people, say What are their jobs?; for one person, What's his job? or What's her job?
问人是谁：Who is + 人?；问几个人的职业：What are their jobs?（单个人：What's his/her job?）。

Sentences 10 and 16 ask the jobs of two groups, and sentence 19 asks who the young man is.

本课：第 10 句和第 16 句问两组人的职业，第 19 句问那位年轻人是谁。

  • Who is that girl? — She's my cousin.
  • What are their jobs? — They're doctors.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • Come and meet … 来见见……
    Come and meet my parents.
  • How do you do? — How do you do? 您好。（正式初见）
    How do you do, Mrs Patel? — How do you do?
  • They're very … / They aren't very … 他们很……／不太……
    They're very friendly, but they aren't very quiet.
  • Who is this …? — This is … He's / She's our … 这位……是谁？——这是……，是我们的……
    Who is this lady? — This is Ms Hill. She's our accountant.
