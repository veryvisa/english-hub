Lesson 81–82 · Roast Beef and Potatoes 烤牛肉和土豆

📘 New Concept English · Book 1 · Lesson 81–82 “Roast Beef and Potatoes”新概念英语 第一册 Lesson 81–82《Roast Beef and Potatoes》

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
Sam calls at Tom's house. Carol explains that Tom is upstairs in the bath and calls up to him; he says he will not be long. When he comes down he offers Sam a cigarette, which Sam turns down, and then a whisky, which he takes. Tom asks whether dinner is ready; almost, says Carol, and they can eat at seven. Then Tom mentions that he and Sam went out for lunch together and both ordered roast beef with potatoes. Carol's face falls. That is exactly what she has cooked for tonight. This lesson is a guest at home and an awkward coincidence. It teaches the many uses of have, as in have a bath, have a cigarette, have dinner and have lunch, the past forms had and went, nearly ready, and be going to for something about to happen.
萨姆来汤姆家。卡罗尔说汤姆在楼上洗澡，喊了一声，汤姆说马上就好。汤姆下楼来请萨姆抽烟，萨姆不抽；又请他喝杯威士忌，他接受了。汤姆问晚饭好了没有，卡罗尔说快好了，七点能吃。汤姆随口说，他和萨姆中午一起在饭馆吃了烤牛肉和土豆。卡罗尔「哦」了一声——今晚她做的恰恰也是烤牛肉和土豆！本课是家里来客与一个尴尬的巧合，学 have 的多种用法（have a bath、have a cigarette、have dinner、have lunch）、一般过去时 had、went，nearly ready，以及 be going to 说「马上要发生的事」。

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander; New Concept English, Book 1, Longman, 1967.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为教材自撰，见《新概念英语》第一册（朗文，1967）。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

Roast beef with potatoes was the classic British Sunday and special-occasion meal, and a restaurant at lunch would often offer it as the main dish of the day. That is why the coincidence is believable: it was the most obvious thing for a husband to order and for a wife to cook. Offering a guest a cigarette as soon as he sat down was ordinary politeness in the 1960s, when a large share of British men smoked, and offering whisky before dinner was a common way to welcome a friend. Today in Canada, smoking indoors in public places is banned in every province, and offering a cigarette to a guest at home would seem very old-fashioned.

烤牛肉配土豆是典型的英国星期天大餐和过节大餐，饭馆中午也常把它当作当日主菜。所以这个巧合可信：对丈夫来说这是最顺手的点法，对妻子来说也是最顺手的做法。在 1960 年代，客人一坐下就递烟是平常的礼节，那时英国男性抽烟的比例很高；晚饭前请朋友喝杯威士忌，也是常见的待客方式。今天在加拿大，各省都禁止在公共室内场所吸烟，在家里给客人递烟会显得很老派。

✨ How it is written赏析

The text moves through a whole evening in short scenes: arrival, the call upstairs, the offers, the question about dinner, the story of lunch, and the reveal. The verb have carries almost all of it, used in six different ways, so the learner meets the word again and again without noticing. The joke is built on dramatic irony: the reader learns about the lunch at the same moment Carol does, and her single word Oh! tells us everything before she explains. Tom's question, What's the matter?, gives her the chance to deliver the line, and the last word, again, is where the disappointment lands.

课文用一连串短场景走完了整个晚上：来客、朝楼上喊、请烟请酒、问晚饭、讲午饭、揭底。几乎全靠 have 这一个动词撑起来，它用了六种不同的意思，读者一遍遍遇到它却不觉得重复。笑点建立在戏剧反讽上：读者和卡罗尔同时得知午饭吃了什么，她一个 Oh! 在解释之前就把一切都说了。汤姆那句 What's the matter? 给了她说出那句话的机会，而最后一个词 again，正是失望落下的地方。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The grammar point is very practical, because have is one of the words Chinese learners translate too narrowly, as a word that only means own. In English people have a shower, have a break, have a meeting and have a baby, and none of these is about owning anything. The social picture has changed more: in a Canadian home today, a host would offer coffee, tea or a soft drink, and many people would not drink alcohol before driving home. One thing is the same everywhere, though: telling the cook what you ate for lunch is best done before dinner is on the table, not after.

这个语法点非常实用，因为 have 是中国学习者理解得太窄的词之一，只当作「有」。英语里人们 have a shower、have a break、have a meeting、have a baby，这些都与「拥有」无关。社交图景变化更大：今天在加拿大的家里，主人会请客人喝咖啡、茶或饮料，很多人开车回家前也不会喝酒。不过有一点到哪里都一样：午饭吃了什么，最好在晚饭上桌之前告诉做饭的人，而不是之后。

Sentence 5第 5 句 (begins开头 “He's having a …”)

  • have here means take or do, not own, so it can be in the continuous; Canadians usually say take a bath.have a bath：洗澡。这里 have 不是「有」，是「做」，所以能用进行时。北美多说 take a bath。

Sentence 9第 9 句 (begins开头 “I'm nearly ready. …”)

  • nearly ready means almost ready.nearly ready：快好了。

Sentence 13第 13 句 (begins开头 “Have a glass …”)

  • Have a ... offers something; then at the end means in that case, after the first offer is refused.Have a …：请吃 / 喝 / 抽……（祈使句表示请客）。then：那么（放句末，接着上一句被拒）。

Sentence 18第 18 句 (begins开头 “We can have …”)

  • Meals take no article after have.have dinner：吃晚饭（不加 the / a）。

Sentence 19第 19 句 (begins开头 “Sam and I …”)

  • had and went are irregular past forms.had 是 have 的过去式，went 是 go 的过去式。
  • have here means eat.What did you have?：你们吃了什么？have 在这里是「吃」。

Sentence 25第 25 句 (begins开头 “Well, you're going …”)

  • be going to here describes something about to happen, because the meal is already cooked.be going to：根据眼前迹象说即将发生的事（饭已经做好了）。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

The many uses of havehave 的多种意思
Besides own, have joins with nouns to mean an action, and in that sense it can take the continuous. When it means own, it usually does not.
have 除了「有」，还能和名词搭配表示动作：have a bath（洗澡）、have breakfast / lunch / dinner（吃饭）、have a cup of tea（喝茶）、have a cigarette（抽烟）、have a good time（玩得开心）。表示动作时可以用进行时：He's having lunch.；表示「有」时一般不用进行时。

Sentence 5 is a bath, 12 and 14 are offers, and 19, 20, 23 and 26 are meals.

本课：第 5 句洗澡，第 12、14 句请人抽烟、喝酒，第 19、20、23、26 句吃饭。

  • We're having breakfast. Can you call back later?
  • Have a piece of cake!
Have a ...: offering thingsHave a …：请客的说法
Have a ... is a friendly way to offer something; Would you like ...? is more polite. Say No, thanks to refuse and Yes, please to accept.
Have a … 是请人吃喝的祈使句：Have a biscuit. / Have some tea.；更客气的说法 Would you like …?。拒绝：No, thanks.；接受：Yes, please. / OK, thanks.。

Sentences 11 to 16 show one refusal and one acceptance.

本课：第 11–15 句一次拒绝、一次接受。

  • Have a seat.
  • Would you like a cup of coffee? — Yes, please.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • He's upstairs. He's having a … 他在楼上，正在……
    She's in the kitchen. She's having a snack.
  • Have a …. — No, thanks. — Have a … then. 来点……——不了，谢谢。——那来点……吧。
    Have a coffee. — No, thanks. — Have some juice then.
  • Is … ready? — It's nearly ready. ……好了吗？——快好了。
    Is the car ready? — It's nearly ready.
  • X and I had … together today. 我和 X 今天一起吃了……
    Lin and I had breakfast together today.
  • You're going to have … again …! 你……又要吃……了！
    You're going to have pizza again tomorrow!

Our model summary本站范文

When Sam arrived, Tom was upstairs in the bath. Coming down, Tom offered him a cigarette, which he refused, and then a whisky, which he accepted. Carol said dinner would be ready at seven. Then Tom mentioned that he and Sam had eaten roast beef and potatoes at lunch, the very meal Carol had cooked.（55 词）
