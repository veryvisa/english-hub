Lesson 33–34 · A Fine Day 晴朗的一天

📘 New Concept English · Book 1 · Lesson 33–34 “A Fine Day”新概念英语 第一册 Lesson 33–34《A Fine Day》

B1🧠 16 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
The day is bright; there are a few clouds about, but the sun is out. Mr Jones and the rest of the family are crossing a bridge. Below them are several boats, and Mr and Mrs Jones are watching those. Sally is watching a large ship pass beneath the bridge, and Tim is watching a plane go across the sky above the water. This is not a dialogue but a short description of a picture: first the weather, then each person in turn, what they are watching and what that thing is doing. The grammar is There is and There are with some, the present continuous with plural subjects, look at, and the pair of opposite prepositions over and under.
天气晴朗，虽然天上有几朵云，太阳照着。琼斯先生和家人一起过桥；河上有几条船，琼斯夫妇在看船。萨莉在看一艘大船从桥下开过去，蒂姆在看一架飞机从河的上空飞过。本课不是对话，而是看图说话式的短描写：先交代天气，再一个人一个人说他在看什么、那东西在做什么。学 There is / There are 和 some、现在进行时的复数主语、look at，以及 over、under 这对方向相反的介词。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. Where are the Jones family, and what are they doing?琼斯一家人在哪里？在做什么？
  2. Why are sentences 4 and 6 joined with but?第 5、6 句为什么用 but 连起来？
  3. What are Sally and Tim each watching, and which way is it going?萨莉和蒂姆各自在看什么？那东西在往哪儿走？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander; New Concept English, Book 1, Longman, 1967.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为教材自撰，见《新概念英语》第一册（朗文，1967）。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

Up to this point Book 1 has been almost all dialogue. Here, for the first time in a while, the text is a plain description, the kind of thing a learner might say about a photo or write on a postcard. The Jones family are the same family met in the garden two lessons earlier, now out for the day. The scene, with a river, a bridge, boats and a passing plane, is ordinary English weekend life of the 1960s, and it gives the course a reason to teach things that move: walking, flying and sailing, each with its own preposition.

到这里为止，第一册几乎全是对话。这一课隔了一阵子又出现一段平铺的描写，就像读者对着一张照片说的话，或写在明信片上的话。琼斯一家就是两课前在花园里出现的那一家，这回出门过一天。河、桥、船和一架飞过的飞机，是 1960 年代英国普通人周末生活的样子；它也给了教材一个理由去教会动的东西：走、飞、行船，各配一个介词。

✨ How it is written赏析

The passage moves like a camera. It starts wide, with the sky and the weather, then comes down to the bridge and the family, then follows each person's eyes in turn, first the parents, then Sally, then Tim. Each child is given one object and one line about where it is going, so the picture ends with two movements crossing: one below the bridge, one above the river. The only joining word in the whole piece is a single but near the start, and that small turn, clouds yet sunshine, is enough to make the weather feel real rather than perfect.

这段文字像一架镜头在移动：先拉远，拍天空和天气；再落到桥上的一家人；然后顺着每个人的视线一个一个看过去——先是父母，再是萨莉，再是蒂姆。每个孩子只分到一样东西、一句话说它往哪儿去，于是画面收在两个交叉的运动上：一个在桥下，一个在河的上空。全篇唯一的连接词是开头那个 but，这一个小转折——有云，可是出太阳——就足以让天气显得真实，而不是完美无缺。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

Nothing here is dated except the word aeroplane, which is now rare in speech: British speakers say plane, and Canadians say plane or airplane. Learners who will live in North America should use plane in conversation and recognise the other two in print. The bigger lesson is the order of the description, from wide to close and from still to moving. Chinese learners writing in English often list everything at once; this short piece is a good model for leading a reader's eye one step at a time.

这里唯一过时的是 aeroplane 这个词，如今口语里很少说了：英国人说 plane，加拿大人说 plane 或 airplane。要在北美生活的读者，口语用 plane，另外两个在书面上认得即可。更值得学的是描写的顺序：从远到近，从静到动。中国读者用英语写作时，常常一口气把所有东西都列出来；这篇小短文是很好的范本，教你一步一步牵着读者的眼睛走。

  2. Write two sentences that use over and under for two things moving in different directions.
    写两句话，用 over 和 under 描写两个朝不同方向移动的东西。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 4第 4 句 (begins开头 “There are some …”)

  • There are with a plural noun says that something exists in a place. some in a positive sentence means a few, without a number.There are + 复数名词：表示「某处有」。some 用在肯定句，表示「一些」，不说具体数目。
  • but joins two ideas that pull against each other: clouds, and yet sunshine.but 连起两句意思相反的话：有云，可是太阳照着。

Sentence 6第 6 句 (begins开头 “Mr. Jones is …”)

  • be with someone means to be together with them. with is a preposition and takes a noun, never a verb.be with sb = 和某人在一起；with 是介词，后面不加动词。

Sentence 7第 7 句 (begins开头 “They are walking …”)

  • A plural subject takes are and the -ing form.复数主语用 are + -ing。
  • walk over here means to cross the bridge on foot; over shows movement across the top.walk over 在这里是「走过（桥）」，over 表示从上面越过。

Sentence 13第 13 句 (begins开头 “The aeroplane is …”)

  • over is the opposite of under in sentence 11: the plane is above, the ship below.over 与第 11 句的 under 方向相反：飞机在上，船在下。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

There is and There are with some
To say that something is in a place, use There is with a singular noun and There are with a plural one. In positive sentences use some for an unstated number.
说「某处有某物」用 There is + 单数名词 / There are + 复数名词；肯定句里「一些」用 some。

Sentences 4 and 9 both use There are some, for the clouds and for the boats.

本课：第 4 句说天上有些云，第 8 句说河上有些船，都用 There are some。

  • There is a cat on the wall.
  • There are some apples in the bowl.
The present continuous with plural subjects, and look at现在进行时：复数主语与 look at
With they, we, you or a plural noun, use are and the -ing form. look at takes an object, and the at cannot be left out.
they、we、you 和复数名词作主语时用 are + -ing；look at + 宾语表示「看着」，at 不能省。

In sentence 9 the parents are watching the boats, and in sentences 10 and 13 each child is watching something.

本课：第 9 句琼斯夫妇在看船，第 11、13 句萨莉和蒂姆各自在看一样东西。

  • The students are looking at the map.
  • We are waiting for the bus.
over and underover 与 under
over means above, or crossing from above; under means below, or passing beneath. With walk, fly or go they show the direction of movement.
over 表示在上方或从上方越过；under 表示在下方或从下方穿过。和 walk、fly、go 连用表示运动方向。

The family cross on top of the bridge, the ship passes beneath it, and the plane goes above the river.

本课：人在桥上走（over），大船从桥下过（under），飞机从河上飞（over）。

  • The cat jumped over the fence.
  • The train goes under the hill through a tunnel.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • It's a fine day, so …. 天气真好，所以……
    It's a fine day, so let's go out.
  • There are some … in the …, but …. ……里有些……，但是……
    There are some people in the shop, but it isn't busy.
  • … is with his/her family. ……和家人在一起。
    Anna is with her family in Spain.
  • … are looking at …. ……在看……
    The tourists are looking at the old castle.
  • The … is flying over the …. ……正从……上空飞过。
    A helicopter is flying over the town.
