📘 New Concept English · Book 1 · Lesson 89–90 “For Sale”新概念英语 第一册 Lesson 89–90《For Sale》

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
Nigel calls at a house that is up for sale and asks if he may look round. Of course, says the owner. Nigel asks how long he has lived there: twenty years, since 1976. Why sell, then? He has just stopped working and wants a little place in the country. Nigel asks the price, and it is sixty-eight thousand five hundred pounds. That seems a great deal, but the owner insists it is good value. Nigel likes the house but cannot make up his mind until his wife has seen it. In the end, says the owner, women always decide. This lesson teaches the present perfect with for and since: How long have you ...?, for twenty years, since 1976, and also I believe that ..., for sale, May I ...?, cost against be worth, and have the last word.
奈杰尔来看一栋待售的房子，问能不能进去看看——当然可以。他问房主在这儿住了多久，房主说住了二十年，从 1976 年起。那为什么要卖？因为他刚退休，想在乡下买栋小房子。奈杰尔问价钱：六万八千五百英镑。他说太贵了；房主说每一便士都值。奈杰尔说他喜欢这房子，可还不能决定，得先让妻子看看。房主说：女人总是最后拍板的。本课学现在完成时配 for 和 since：How long have you …?、for twenty years、since 1976，以及 I believe that …、for sale、May I …?、cost 与 be worth、have the last word。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. How long has the owner lived in the house, and since when?房主在这里住了多久？从哪一年开始？
  2. Why does he want to sell it?他为什么要卖房子？
  3. Why can't Nigel decide yet?奈杰尔为什么还不能决定？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander; New Concept English, Book 1, Longman, 1967. The years and the price must come from the 1990s revision: living there since 1976 for twenty years puts the dialogue in 1996.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为教材自撰，见《新概念英语》第一册（朗文，1967）。年份和房价应出自 1990 年代的修订版：从 1976 年起住了二十年，对话就落在 1996 年。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

In the mid-1990s, sixty-eight thousand five hundred pounds was a believable price for an ordinary family house in many parts of England, though well below London prices. The owner's plan, to sell the family home on retiring and buy something smaller in the country, was and still is a very common choice in Britain, where it is often called downsizing. Selling privately to a visitor at the door, as in the lesson, was less usual than going through an estate agent, but a For Sale sign in the garden would bring people to knock. In British Columbia today, the price of a detached house in Metro Vancouver is many times higher, and the same plan, moving from the city to a smaller town on retiring, is just as common.

在 1990 年代中期，六万八千五百英镑对英格兰很多地方的普通家庭住宅来说是个可信的价钱，不过远低于伦敦房价。房主的打算——退休时卖掉家里的房子、到乡下买一处小一点的——在英国过去和现在都是很常见的选择，常被称为「换小房」。像课文里这样直接卖给上门来看的人，不如通过房产中介常见，但花园里一块「待售」牌子就会引人敲门。今天在不列颠哥伦比亚省，大温哥华地区独立屋的价格要高出许多倍，而同样的打算——退休后从城市搬到小一点的镇子——也一样常见。

✨ How it is written赏析

The visit follows the shape of a real house viewing, and each step carries one piece of grammar. The polite opening uses I believe that and May I, the formal language of a stranger at the door. The question about time brings in the present perfect with for and since, and the owner says the same fact twice, first as a length and then as a starting date, which is exactly the contrast the learner needs. The money part is a small argument in three lines: price, complaint, defence. The ending hands the decision to someone who is not even there, and the owner's final remark, a general joke about wives, closes the deal for now without closing it at all.

这次看房遵循真实看房的流程，每一步都带出一个语法点。客气的开场用了 I believe that 和 May I，是陌生人站在门口时的正式说法。问时间带出现在完成时与 for 和 since，房主把同一个事实说了两遍：先说时长，再说起始年份，正是学习者需要的对照。钱的部分是三句话的小争论：报价、嫌贵、辩护。结尾把决定权交给一个根本不在场的人，而房主最后那句关于妻子的笼统玩笑，算是为这笔买卖暂时收了尾，其实什么也没定。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

How long have you been in Canada? is one of the first questions people ask newcomers in Canada, and Chinese learners very often get it wrong as How long do you live here? or How long are you here?, which ask about the future. The answer needs for or since, and getting the two right is a quick sign of good English. The final line is a joke of its time, but the idea behind it holds up: buying a home is a joint decision, and in Canada both partners usually sign. For readers thinking about buying a house in BC, the more useful lesson is the difference between cost and worth, because a high price and real value are not the same thing.

How long have you been in Canada? 是加拿大人问新移民的头几个问题之一，中国学习者常常说错成 How long do you live here? 或 How long are you here?，那问的是将来。回答需要用 for 或 since，两者用对，是英语好坏的一个快速标志。最后那句是那个年代的玩笑，但背后的道理站得住：买房是共同的决定，在加拿大通常夫妻双方都要签字。对想在 BC 买房的读者来说，更有用的是 cost 和 worth 的区别：价钱高和真正值不值，不是一回事。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

How long ...? with for and sinceHow long …? 与 for / since
For a state that began in the past and is still true, use the present perfect. Use for with a length of time and since with the point when it began.
从过去持续到现在的状态用现在完成时：How long have you lived in Surrey? — I've lived there for ten years. / since 2016.。for + 一段时间（for two weeks、for a long time）；since + 起点（since Monday、since 2010、since I was a child）。

cost and be worthcost 与 be worth
cost is the price you pay, and be worth is the value something has. A high price does not always mean high value.
cost 说价格（要付多少钱）：It costs $500. / How much does it cost?；be worth 说价值（值不值）：It's worth $500. / It's worth the money.。价格高不一定值，价值高不一定贵。

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • I believe that this … is for sale. 听说这……在出售。
    I believe that this bike is for sale.
  • May I see …, please? — Yes, of course. 我能看看……吗？——当然。
    May I see the menu, please? — Yes, of course.
  • How long have you …? — I've … for … 你……多久了？——……年了。
    How long have you lived in Richmond? — I've lived here for eight years.
  • I've been here since … 我从……年起就在这儿了。
    I've been here since 2011.
  • It's worth every … of it. 每一分钱都值。
    It's worth every dollar of it.

Our model summary本站范文

Nigel went to see a house that was for sale. The owner had lived there for twenty years, but he had just retired and wanted a small country house. He was asking 68,500 pounds. Nigel thought that was expensive, though the owner said it was good value. Nigel liked it, but his wife had to see it first.（58 词）
