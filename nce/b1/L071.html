Lesson 71–72 · He's Awful 他真讨厌！

📘 New Concept English · Book 1 · Lesson 71–72 “He's Awful”新概念英语 第一册 Lesson 71–72《He's Awful》

B1🧠 16 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
Someone asks Pauline what Ron Marston is like, and she says he is dreadful. Yesterday he rang her four times, and the day before, three. Yesterday he twice rang her at work, and both times her boss picked up and said she was busy typing and could not come to the phone. When she got home at six, he rang again, and she let it ring. At nine he tried once more, and this time she pretended to be her own mother and told him to stop calling. He has not called since. This lesson is the story of a caller who will not give up. It teaches past simple questions and answers with did, regular and irregular past forms, time words built on yesterday, how many times something happened, and reporting someone's exact words with said and quotation marks.
有人问波琳：罗恩·马斯顿这人怎么样？她说：讨厌死了！昨天他给她打了四次电话，前天打了三次。昨天上午和下午他把电话打到办公室，是老板接的，老板说波琳在打信，现在不能接。傍晚六点她到家，他又打来，她没接。晚上九点他又打，这回她说：「我是波琳的妈妈，请别再给我女儿打电话了！」从那以后，他再也没打来。本课是讲一个甩不掉的追求者，学一般过去时的问句与回答（Did he …? / What did you say?）、规则与不规则过去式、yesterday 一组时间词、次数的说法 four times，以及转述别人原话的 said, '…'。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. How many times did Ron phone over the two days?罗恩昨天和前天一共打了几次电话？
  2. What did her boss tell him on the phone?老板接电话时说了什么？
  3. How did Pauline answer at nine o'clock, and what happened after that?九点钟那次波琳是怎么接电话的？结果怎样？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander; New Concept English, Book 1, Longman, 1967.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为教材自撰，见《新概念英语》第一册（朗文，1967）。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

In the 1960s most homes in Britain had one telephone, often in the hall, and anyone could pick it up; an office had a switchboard or a shared line, and a boss or a secretary often answered calls for the staff. This is why Ron can reach Pauline only by ringing again and again, and why she can hide behind her boss in the day and behind her mother at night. Typing letters was one of the most common jobs for young women in offices at the time. There was no way to see who was calling, so the only defence against an unwanted caller was not to answer, or to answer as somebody else.

1960 年代英国大多数家庭只有一部电话，常常放在门厅，谁都能接；办公室有总机或共用线路，老板或秘书常替员工接电话。这就是为什么罗恩只能一遍又一遍地打，也是为什么波琳白天能躲在老板后面、晚上能躲在「妈妈」后面。打字是当时办公室年轻女性最常见的工作之一。那时看不到来电号码，所以对付不想接的电话，唯一的办法就是不接，或者假装是别人接。

✨ How it is written赏析

The story is told almost entirely as answers to a friend's questions, which keeps it moving and lets the grammar of past questions appear naturally. The numbers at the start make the problem clear without any complaint: seven calls in two days says more than a long description. The day is then told in order, from morning to evening to night, with each call shorter than the one before. The climax is Pauline's own line, the one moment she speaks to Ron, and she does it in someone else's voice. The last answer is just two words, which is how you know the trick worked.

故事几乎完全是用回答朋友提问的方式讲出来的，这让它推进得很快，也让过去时问句的语法自然出现。开头的数字不用抱怨就把问题说清了：两天七个电话，比一大段描写更有说服力。接着按时间顺序讲一天，从上午到傍晚再到夜里，每一次电话都比上一次讲得更短。高潮是波琳自己的那句话——她唯一一次对罗恩开口，用的却是别人的身份。最后的回答只有两个词，读者由此知道这一招管用了。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

Today Pauline would block Ron's number and he would never get through, but the social problem is the same: how to say no clearly to someone who will not take the hint. Her trick is funny, yet it also shows how hard it was for a young woman to say no in her own voice, and today most advice would be to say it directly, once, and then stop answering. If the calls continue, that becomes harassment, and in Canada repeated unwanted contact can be reported to the police. For learners, the useful lesson is the telephone language: This is ... to name yourself, and Can I speak to ...? to ask for someone.

今天波琳会直接拉黑罗恩的号码，他再也打不进来；但社交上的难题还是一样：怎样对一个不识趣的人明确说不。她的计策很好笑，却也显示出当时年轻女性用自己的身份说「不」有多难；今天大多数建议是直接、明确地说一次，然后不再回应。如果电话还不停，那就成了骚扰，在加拿大反复的不受欢迎的联系可以报警。对学习者来说，有用的是电话用语：用 This is ... 报自己的名字，用 Can I speak to ...? 找人。

    跟同伴讲讲某天有人不停给你打电话或发消息的经历，用 did 问句和简答。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 4第 4 句 (begins开头 “What's Ron Marston …”)

  • This asks about character; for looks, ask what someone looks like.What's sb like?：某人是个怎样的人（性格）？问长相要说 What does sb look like?

Sentence 6第 6 句 (begins开头 “He telephoned me …”)

  • Count with once, twice, then three times and so on; the day before yesterday is two days ago.次数：once、twice、three times、four times。the day before yesterday：前天。
  • telephoned is a regular past form with -ed.telephoned：规则动词加 -ed。

Sentence 9第 9 句 (begins开头 “What did your …”)

  • A wh-question in the past uses did plus the bare verb, so say, not said.一般过去时的特殊疑问句：疑问词 + did + 主语 + 动词原形（say，不用 said）。

Sentence 10第 10 句 (begins开头 “He said, 'Pauline …”)

  • Inside the quotation the boss's own tense stays: she was typing at the time he spoke.said, '…'：直接引语，引号里保留原话的时态（老板说话时她正在打字）。

Sentence 14第 14 句 (begins开头 “But I didn't …”)

  • No preposition before home; the negative is didn't plus the bare verb.arrive home：home 前不加 at / to。否定 didn't + 动词原形。

Sentence 15第 15 句 (begins开头 “Did he telephone …”)

  • Short answers repeat did; English says last night, not yesterday night.Did … ? 的简答 Yes, he did. / No, he didn't.。注意说 last night，不说 yesterday night。

Sentence 19第 19 句 (begins开头 “I said, 'This …”)

  • On the phone, English uses This is ... to say who is speaking.电话里自我介绍用 This is …，不用 I am …。
  • She is pretending to be her own mother.她假装是自己的母亲。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

The past simple: questions, negatives and short answers一般过去时：问句、否定与简答
Positive sentences use the past form. Questions and negatives use did, and the main verb goes back to its bare form. Short answers repeat did.
肯定句用过去式（telephoned、said）；问句和否定借助 did，动词还原：Did he telephone? / He didn't telephone. / What did he say?；简答 Yes, he did. / No, he didn't.

Sentences 15–16 and 21–22 are yes-no questions with short answers, and sentences 9 and 18 are wh-questions.

本课：第 15–16 句、第 21–22 句是一般疑问句与简答，第 9、18 句是特殊疑问句。

  • Did you see the game? — No, I didn't.
  • Where did she go last summer?
Past time words: yesterday, last, ago过去的时间词：yesterday、last、ago
We say yesterday morning, afternoon and evening, but last night. These phrases take no on or in before them.
yesterday morning / afternoon / evening；但「昨晚」是 last night；the day before yesterday（前天）；last week / month / year；three days ago。这些词前都不加 on / in。

Sentences 6, 7, 12 and 15 use them together.

本课：第 6、7、12、15 句集中出现。

  • I called you yesterday afternoon.
  • We moved here two years ago.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • What's X like? — He's/She's … X 这人怎么样？——他/她……
    What's your new neighbour like? — She's very friendly.
  • X phoned me … times yesterday. X 昨天给我打了……次电话
    My mum phoned me three times yesterday.
  • What did X say to …? X 跟……说了什么？
    What did the teacher say to you?
  • He said, '…' 他说：「……」
    She said, 'I'm busy right now.'
  • This is …'s mother. Please don't … 我是……的妈妈，请别……
    This is Leo's mother. Please don't send him any more messages.

Our model summary本站范文

Ron rang Pauline three times on one day and four times the next. Twice he called her office, and her boss said she was busy. At six she was home but ignored the phone. At nine she answered, claimed to be her own mother and told him to leave her daughter alone. He never rang again.（56 词）
