Lesson 107–108 · It's Too Small 太小了

📘 New Concept English · Book 1 · Lesson 107–108 “It's Too Small”新概念英语 第一册 Lesson 107–108《It's Too Small》

B1🧠 16 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
A woman is trying on dresses in a shop. She loves the colour of the first one, a blue dress, but it does not fit. The assistant brings a green one: very smart, and short skirts are the fashion now. She tries it, and it is tighter still, and she does not care for the colour either; it does nothing for her. She prefers the blue, and wants one like it in a bigger size. The assistant is sorry: there is nothing bigger in the shop. This lesson teaches comparatives and superlatives, too ... for me, either and as well, suit, in fashion, and my size.
女士在服装店试衣服。店员拿来一件蓝色连衣裙，她很喜欢这个颜色，可衣服太小。店员又拿来一件绿色的：很时髦，现在正流行短裙，要不要试试？试了，也太小——比蓝的那件还小，颜色她也不喜欢，一点都不合适她。她觉得蓝色那件更漂亮，想要一件同样的，但必须是她的尺码。店员说：恐怕没有更大的了，这已经是店里最大的一件。本课学形容词比较级与最高级（smaller than、prettier、larger、the largest）、too … for me、either 与 as well、suit、in fashion、my size。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. What does the lady think of the blue dress?那位女士觉得蓝裙子怎么样？
  2. What is wrong with the green dress?绿裙子有什么问题？
  3. Does she find a dress in the end? Why not?她最后买到合适的裙子了吗？为什么？

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

When the lesson was written, short skirts were the biggest fashion story in Britain: the miniskirt spread from London boutiques in the mid-1960s and became a symbol of the decade. So the shop assistant's sales line was the most up-to-date thing she could say. Dress shops then carried far less stock than a modern chain store, often only a few sizes of each style, and British women's sizes were numbered in a system that has since changed more than once. Being told that a shop simply had nothing bigger was a common experience.

课文写成时，短裙是英国最大的时尚新闻：迷你裙在 1960 年代中期从伦敦的时装小店流行开来，成了那个年代的象征。所以店员的推销词是她能说的最时髦的一句话。当时的服装店存货远比今天的连锁店少，每款往往只有几个尺码，英国女装的尺码编号也已经改过不止一次。被告知店里就是没有更大的号，是很常见的经历。

✨ How it is written赏析

The lesson is a ladder of comparisons. Every new dress is measured against the last one, so the comparative forms appear naturally, one per step, until the assistant reaches the top of the ladder with the superlative and there is nowhere left to go. The customer's lines move from praise to complaint in a polite pattern that is worth copying: she starts with what she likes before saying what is wrong. The assistant's last word is also the shop's last word, and it ends the visit without a sale.

这一课是一道比较的阶梯。每拿来一件新裙子，都跟上一件比，于是比较级一步一个，自然出现，直到店员用最高级登上阶梯顶端，再也无路可走。顾客的台词从称赞走到不满，用的是一种值得照学的客气套路：先说喜欢什么，再说哪里不对。店员的最后一句也是这家店的最后一句，这次逛店以没买成告终。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

Shopping for clothes in Canada brings its own size problem. North American sizes run differently from Chinese ones, and a size that fits in a Chinese brand may be marked much smaller here, so many newcomers need to learn to ask Do you have this in a smaller size? or Where are the fitting rooms? The polite pattern in the lesson, praise first and then the problem, also works well in Canadian shops and offices. One more trap: Chinese learners often say more pretty or more large; most short adjectives take er instead. And in Canadian English smart usually means clever, so for clothes you might hear sharp or stylish.

在加拿大买衣服有自己的尺码难题。北美尺码和中国尺码标法不同，在中国品牌里合身的号，在这里可能标得小很多，所以很多新移民需要学会问 Do you have this in a smaller size? 或 Where are the fitting rooms?。课文里那种客气的套路——先称赞、再说问题——在加拿大的商店和办公室里同样好用。还有一个陷阱：中国学习者常说 more pretty、more large，其实大多数短形容词要加 er。在加拿大英语里，smart 通常指聪明，说衣服时更常听到 sharp 或 stylish。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

Comparative and superlative adjectives形容词比较级与最高级
One-syllable adjectives add er and est. Words ending in e add r and st. Consonant plus y changes to ier and iest. A short word ending in one vowel and one consonant doubles the last letter. Use the comparative with than for two things, and the with the superlative for three or more.
单音节：small → smaller → the smallest；以 e 结尾：large → larger → the largest；辅音 + y：pretty → prettier → the prettiest；重读闭音节双写：big → bigger → the biggest。两者比较用比较级 + than；三者以上用 the + 最高级 + in/of。

Sentences 13, 17, 20 and 21 move from comparative to superlative.

本课：第 13 句用 smaller than，第 16 句 prettier，第 19 句 larger，第 20 句 the largest。

  • Vancouver is bigger than Victoria.
  • This is the cheapest phone in the store.
too and as well against eithertoo / as well 与 either
In positive sentences, also is too or as well at the end. In negative sentences it is either at the end.
肯定句里的「也」用 too 或 as well，放句末；否定句里的「也」用 either，放句末。I like it too. / I don't like it either.。

Sentence 12 is positive and takes as well; sentence 14 is negative and takes either.

本课：第 12 句是肯定句用 as well，第 14 句是否定句用 either。

  • She speaks French, and she speaks Spanish as well.
  • Tom can't come, and Lisa can't come either.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • I love the colour, but it's too … for me. 我很喜欢这颜色，可它对我来说太……
    I love the colour, but it's too long for me.
  • … are very popular now. Do you want to try one on? ……现在很流行，要试穿一件吗？
    Wide jeans are very popular now. Do you want to try one on?
  • This one is … than the other one. 这件比那件更……
    This one is cheaper than the other one.
  • X really doesn't suit me. X 真的不适合我。
    That hat really doesn't suit me.
  • I'm sorry, this is the … one we have. 抱歉，这已经是我们最……的了。
    I'm sorry, this is the smallest one we have.
