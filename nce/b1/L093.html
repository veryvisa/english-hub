Lesson 93–94 · Our New Neighbour 我们的新邻居

📘 New Concept English · Book 1 · Lesson 93–94 “Our New Neighbour”新概念英语 第一册 Lesson 93–94《Our New Neighbour》

B1🧠 16 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
Our new next-door neighbour, Nigel, flies planes for a living; he used to serve in the Royal Air Force. Next month his work takes him to New York, and a month later to Tokyo. Right now he is in Spain, having flown out seven days ago, and he gets back to London in two weeks. At forty-one he has visited almost every country on earth, so he is a lucky man. His wife is not so lucky: she mostly stays home. This is a short third-person portrait that sets past, present and future side by side: will fly, flew ... ago, has been to, and time phrases such as the month after next and next week.
新搬来的隔壁邻居奈杰尔是个飞行员，以前在英国皇家空军服役。他下个月飞纽约，下下个月飞东京；眼下人在马德里，一周前飞去的西班牙，下下周回伦敦。他才四十一岁，差不多去过世界上每一个国家，真是个幸运的人——可他太太没那么幸运，她通常待在家里。本课是一段第三人称介绍，把过去、现在、将来三种时间排在一起：will fly、flew … ago、has been to，以及 the month after next、next month、the week after next。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. What is Nigel's job, and where did he work before?奈杰尔是做什么的？以前在哪里工作？
  2. Where is he now, and when will he be back in London?他现在在哪里？什么时候回伦敦？
  3. Why is he lucky, and why is his wife not?为什么说他幸运，而他太太不幸运？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander; New Concept English, Book 1, Longman, 1967.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为教材自撰，见《新概念英语》第一册（朗文，1967）。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

In the 1960s many British airline pilots had learned to fly in the Royal Air Force, and moving from military to civil flying was a common career path. Long-haul air travel was still new and expensive: jet airliners had only been in regular service for about a decade, and an ordinary family might never fly at all. So a pilot who had seen almost every country was a figure of real glamour. The routes in the lesson, London to New York, Tokyo and Madrid, were the kind flown by the national airline of the time. At home, it was still usual for a married woman with children to give up paid work.

1960 年代，很多英国民航飞行员是在皇家空军学会飞行的，从军用飞行转到民航是一条常见的职业路线。远程航空旅行那时还新鲜而昂贵：喷气客机正式投入常规航线才十来年，普通家庭可能一辈子都没坐过飞机。所以一个几乎去遍各国的飞行员，是真正令人羡慕的人物。课文里伦敦到纽约、东京、马德里这几条航线，正是当时国家航空公司飞的那类。在家庭里，有孩子的已婚女性辞去工作，那时仍是常态。

✨ How it is written赏析

The lesson is a timetable turned into a portrait. Each sentence adds one stop on Nigel's calendar, and the stops are not in order: future, future, present, past, future. That jumping about is the exercise, because the reader has to sort the tenses by the time words. Then the text steps back from the dates to a single summing-up line about his age and his travels, and calls him lucky. The final pair of sentences flips the view from him to his wife, and the exclamation mark carries the irony that the text never states.

课文是把一张行程表变成了一幅人物素描。每句话在奈杰尔的日程上添一站，而这些站并不按顺序排：将来、将来、现在、过去、将来。这种跳来跳去本身就是练习，读者得靠时间词把时态理清。然后课文从日期中抽身，用一句话总结他的年龄和见识，说他幸运。最后两句把视角从他转到他太太，句末的感叹号带出了课文从未明说的反讽。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The joke at the end still works, but the situation behind it has changed a great deal. Today many pilots are women, and in a two-career family the partner left at home might just as well be the husband. Air travel is also no longer rare; the real luxury now may be staying home with family. For learners, the most useful point is has been to against has gone to, which Chinese speakers often mix up because Chinese uses the same word for both. If a colleague asks where your manager is, he has gone to Toronto means he is there now; he has been to Toronto only says he knows the city.

结尾的笑点现在还成立，但背后的处境已经大变。如今很多飞行员是女性，在双职工家庭里，留在家里的那一方也完全可能是丈夫。坐飞机也早已不稀罕，今天真正的奢侈也许是能待在家里陪家人。对学习者来说，最有用的是 has been to 和 has gone to 的区别，中国人常把两者混用，因为中文里都可以说「去了」。同事问你经理在哪，he has gone to Toronto 是说他现在人在那儿；he has been to Toronto 只说明他去过那座城市。

Sentence 6第 6 句 (begins开头 “He will fly …”)

  • will plus the base verb for a future event; the R.A.F. is the Royal Air Force.will + 动词原形：将来的事。the R.A.F. = the Royal Air Force，英国皇家空军。
  • No preposition before next month.next month 前不加介词。

Sentence 7第 7 句 (begins开头 “The month after …”)

  • Two months from now.the month after next：下下个月。
  • at the moment means now.at the moment：此刻，配一般现在时 be 或现在进行时。

Sentence 9第 9 句 (begins开头 “He flew to …”)

  • flew is the past of fly; ago goes only with the past simple.flew 是 fly 的过去式；ago 只能配一般过去时。
  • Two weeks from now.the week after next：下下周。

Sentence 12第 12 句 (begins开头 “and he has …”)

  • has been to is about experience: he went and came back. has gone to means he is still there.has been to：去过（并已回来），说经历；has gone to 是去了还没回来。
  • only and already together stress how young he is for so much travel.only … already：才四十一岁就已经……，两个词一起表达「早」。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

Time words for the past, present and future过去、现在、将来的时间词
Past markers such as a week ago take the past simple. Present markers such as at the moment take the present. Future markers such as next month and the week after next take will. Pick the time word first and the tense follows.
过去：a week ago、last month、yesterday → 一般过去时。现在：at the moment、now → 一般现在时（be）或现在进行时。将来：the month after next、next month、tomorrow、the week after next → will do。时间词选对了，时态就跟着对。

Sentences 6, 8 and 11 look ahead, sentence 8 is about now, and sentence 9 looks back.

本课：第 7、8、11 句说将来，第 8 句说现在，第 9 句说过去。

  • She moved to Kelowna two years ago.
  • At the moment they're in Montreal.
  • We'll start the new course the week after next.
has been to and has gone tohas been to 与 has gone to
has been to a place means the person went and came back, so it talks about experience. has gone to a place means the person is there now or on the way.
has been to + 地点：去过某地（人已回来），常配 already、ever、never、nearly every。has gone to + 地点：去了某地（人现在还在那里或在路上）。

Sentence 12 is about his experience. Since he is in Madrid now, we could also say he has gone there.

本课：第 12 句说经历；第 8 句他人在马德里，也可以说 He has gone to Madrid。

  • I've been to Japan twice.
  • Mum isn't here. She's gone to the shops.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • X has just moved in next door. X 刚搬到隔壁。
    A young couple has just moved in next door.
  • At the moment, X is in … 此刻，X 在……
    At the moment, my parents are in Shanghai.
  • X went to … a week ago. X 一周前去了……
    Amy went to Seattle a week ago.
  • X will come back the week after next. X 下下周回来。
    My manager will come back the week after next.
  • X has already visited nearly every … X 已经去过几乎每一个……
    She has already visited nearly every museum in the city.
