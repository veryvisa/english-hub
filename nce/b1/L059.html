Lesson 59–60 · Is That All 就这些吗？

📘 New Concept English · Book 1 · Lesson 59–60 “Is That All”新概念英语 第一册 Lesson 59–60《Is That All》

B1🧠 16 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
A woman is buying things at a stationer's. She asks for envelopes, and when asked which size, chooses large. She asks about writing paper; there is some. The shop has no small pads, only large ones, and she takes one. She also wants a bottle of glue and a large box of chalk, but the chalk comes only in small boxes, so she says no to it. The shopkeeper checks whether that is everything, and she says yes. Then he asks what else she wants, and she says: my change. This lesson is the back and forth of buying in a shop. It teaches have as a main verb in Do you have any ...? and I don't have any ..., where only goes, ones as a pronoun, What else ...? and Is that all?, and change meaning money given back.
一位女士在文具店买东西。她要信封——店员问要大号还是小号，她要大号。她问有没有信纸，有；店员说小本的便笺没有，只有大本的，问她要不要，她要了。她还要一瓶胶水，再要一大盒粉笔——可店里只有小盒的，她就不要了。店员问「就这些吗？」，她说就这些；店员又问「您还要什么？」，她说：要我的找零。本课是在店里买东西的一来一回，学 have 作实义动词时的 Do you have any …? / I don't have any …、only 的位置、large ones 这类代词 one、What else …? 和 Is that all?，以及 change 的「找零」义。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. How many things does the woman buy, and which does she not buy, and why?女士一共买到了几样东西？哪样没买成，为什么？
  2. In sentences 9 and 10, what do any and ones refer to?第 9 句和第 10 句里 any 和 ones 各代替什么？
  3. What makes the last line funny?最后一句 I want my change 好笑在哪里？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander; New Concept English, Book 1, Longman, 1967.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为教材自撰，见《新概念英语》第一册（朗文，1967）。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

A stationer's was a normal High Street shop in 1960s Britain, selling envelopes, writing paper and pads to a public that still wrote most of its messages by hand. Chalk was bought for children's games and for schools, where blackboards were universal. Shopping at a counter meant asking for each item and waiting while the assistant fetched it, so the conversation is a real one: sizes, what is in stock, and a final check. The lesson brings in have as a main verb with do, which British English of the time often expressed as have got instead; Alexander chose the form that later became standard everywhere.

在 1960 年代的英国，文具店是商业街上很普通的店铺，卖信封、信纸和便笺本给一个大部分消息还靠手写的公众。粉笔是买给孩子玩游戏、也买给学校用的，那时黑板到处都是。在柜台买东西意味着每样都要开口要、等店员去取，所以这段对话是真实的：问尺寸、问有没有货、最后再核对一遍。本课引入带 do 的实义动词 have；当时的英式英语常常说 have got，亚历山大选了后来在各地都成为标准的那种说法。

✨ How it is written赏析

The dialogue has the plain rhythm of real shopping until the last two lines, which turn it into a joke. The assistant asks Is that all? and gets a clear yes, then asks What else do you want? as if he had not heard, and the customer's reply shows what she has been waiting for all along. The order of the items also does quiet work: the first ones go smoothly, the pads need a small compromise, and the chalk is a failure, so the learner meets yes, a partial yes and no in turn before the final twist.

对话一直是真实购物那种平实的节奏，直到最后两句才变成一个笑话。店员问「就这些吗？」，得到明确的肯定，接着却又问「您还要什么？」，好像没听见；顾客的回答揭出她一直在等的是什么。几样东西的顺序也在不声不响地干活：前几样顺顺当当，便笺要稍作妥协，粉笔则没买成，于是读者依次遇到「行」「勉强行」「不行」，最后才是那个转折。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

Do you have ...? is now the normal form in Canada and in most of the English-speaking world, and it is the one learners should use. Is that all? and Anything else? are what every cashier says, and both deserve a ready answer: That's all, thanks, or Yes, could I also get .... Two things have changed. Most payments in Canada are now by card, so a forgotten change is rarer, though asking politely for a receipt or for the right change is still a useful skill. And I want ... sounds blunt to a shop assistant today; Could I get ... or I'd like ... is far more natural.

Do you have ...? 如今是加拿大乃至大部分英语世界的通行说法，读者就该用这个。Is that all? 和 Anything else? 是每个收银员都会说的话，两句都要备好回答：That's all, thanks，或 Yes, could I also get ...。有两点变了：在加拿大现在大多刷卡付款，忘了找零的事少了，不过礼貌地要收据、要回正确的零钱，仍然是有用的本事。另外，今天对店员说 I want ... 显得生硬；Could I get ... 或 I'd like ... 自然得多。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentence 5第 5 句 (begins开头 “Do you want …”)

  • A question with A or B cannot be answered with yes or no.A or B 选择疑问句，不能用 Yes/No 回答。

Sentence 7第 7 句 (begins开头 “Do you have …”)

  • When have means own, questions and negatives use do, and the short answer is Yes, we do.have 作实义动词「有」时，问句和否定借助 do：Do you have …? / I don't have …；简答 Yes, we do.。

Sentence 9第 9 句 (begins开头 “I don't have …”)

  • not ... any means none at all.not … any：一个也没有。
  • only goes before the main verb; ones stands for pads.only 放在实义动词前；ones 代替 pads，避免重复。

Sentence 15第 15 句 (begins开头 “And I want …”)

  • This line and the next form an exchange: she wants a large box, the shop has only small, so the sale fails.这句和下一句一来一回：想要大盒，只有小盒，所以没买成。

Sentence 21第 21 句 (begins开头 “What else do …”)

  • What else ...? asks what more someone wants.What else …? 问「还要别的什么」。
  • change here is a noun meaning the money due back: she has paid, and he has forgotten to give it.change 在这里是名词「找零」：她已经付了钱，店员却忘了找钱。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

have meaning own: Do you have ...? and I don't have ...have 作「有」：Do you have …? / I don't have …
When have is the main verb, questions use Do you have ...? or Does he have ...?, and negatives use don't or doesn't have, often with any. Positive sentences use some.
have 作实义动词「有」时，问句用 Do you have …?（第三人称 Does he have …?），否定用 don't / doesn't have，后面接 any 表示「一点也没有」；肯定句用 some。

Sentence 7 asks whether there is writing paper, sentence 8 answers briefly, and sentences 9 and 10 give a negative and a positive.

本课：第 7 句问有没有信纸，第 8 句简答，第 9 句与第 10 句一否一肯。

  • Do you have any stamps? — Sorry, we don't have any.
  • She doesn't have a car, but she has a bike.
Where only goes, and the pronoun onesonly 的位置与代词 ones
only usually goes before the main verb or after be. ones stands for a plural countable noun already mentioned, usually with an adjective in front.
only 一般放在实义动词前、be 动词后：I only have …；She is only five.。ones 代替前面说过的复数可数名词，前面常带形容词：large ones、the red ones。

Sentences 10 and 16 both put only before have, and sentence 10 uses ones for pads.

本课：第 10 句与第 16 句都是 only + have；第 10 句用 ones 代替 pads。

  • I only have two hands!
  • These shoes are too small. Do you have bigger ones?

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • I want some …, please. 我要一些……。
    I want some batteries, please.
  • Do you want the … size or the … size? 您要……号还是……号？
    Do you want the medium size or the large size?
  • Do you have any …? — Yes, we do. 有……吗？——有。
    Do you have any coat hangers? — Yes, we do.
  • I don't have any …. I only have …. 没有……，只有……
    I don't have any red ones. I only have blue.
  • Is that all? — Yes, that's everything. 就这些吗？——对，就这些。
    Anything else? — No, that's everything, thanks.

At the stationer's the woman bought large envelopes, some writing paper, a large pad and a bottle of glue. She wanted a big box of chalk, but the shop had only small ones, so she did without. When the shopkeeper asked what else she needed, she reminded him that he still owed her change.（54 词）
