Lesson 129–130 · Seventy Miles an Hour 时速七十英里

📘 New Concept English · Book 1 · Lesson 129–130 “Seventy Miles an Hour”新概念英语 第一册 Lesson 129–130《Seventy Miles an Hour》

B1🧠 22 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
Ann warns her husband Gary that a police officer is waving at him to pull over. The officer says Gary was going at least seventy; Gary says that is impossible. The officer replies that he passed Gary at eighty. Gary admits he missed the speed sign and blames a wandering mind; Ann explains that she was talking at him about slowing down, so he never noticed it. The officer checks his licence and lets him off with a warning. Afterwards Ann reminds him of her advice; Gary says she gives that advice every time; Ann says next time he should listen. This is a roadside talk with a police officer that teaches must have been doing and can't have been for guesses about the past, want or tell someone to do, had better, overtake and charge.
安提醒丈夫加里：警察在向他挥手，让他停车。警察说他刚才时速起码七十英里，加里说不可能——警察回答，自己超他车的时候都开到八十了。加里说没看见限速牌，大概是在走神；安替他解释，是她一直在叫他开慢点，所以他没看见标志。警察看了驾照，说这次不罚，下不为例。警察走后，安说：「我早叫你开慢点。」加里说：「你总叫我开慢点。」安说：「那下次你最好听我的！」本课是一次被警察拦车的对话，学must have been doing / can't have been 对过去的推测、want / tell sb to do、had better 和 overtake、charge。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. Why does the officer pull Gary over?警察为什么拦下加里？
  2. Why did Gary miss the speed sign?加里为什么没看见限速牌？
  3. How does the officer deal with him in the end?警察最后怎么处理的？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander; New Concept English, Book 1, Longman, 1967. Gary and Ann are invented characters; the scene was built to practise must have and can't have.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为教材自撰，见《新概念英语》第一册（朗文，1967）。加里和安是虚构人物，这一场景是为操练 must have 和 can't have 编的。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

Britain brought in a national speed limit of seventy miles an hour on open roads in 1965, as a trial after a run of bad crashes in fog, and made it permanent in 1967, the year this book came out. So seventy was a fresh and topical number for the first readers. Police often used their own car to follow a driver and read the speed off their own meter, which is why the officer can say he was doing eighty to catch up. A warning instead of a charge was, and still is, a common outcome for a first offence.

英国 1965 年在开放道路上试行每小时七十英里的全国限速，起因是浓雾里接连发生惨重车祸；1967 年——也就是本书出版那年——改为永久规定。所以「七十」对第一批读者来说是个很新、很应景的数字。当时警察常开车跟在司机后面，看自己车上的速度表判断车速，所以警察能说他追上来时开到了八十。初犯只警告不起诉，过去和现在都很常见。

✨ How it is written赏析

The lesson is a three-way talk with a sharp comic edge. Gary tries two excuses, impossibility and dreaming, both expressed with past modals. Ann then helps him in a way that is really a complaint, and the officer's answer uses had better as a warning. The last exchange turns from the law back to the marriage: Ann's advice, and Gary's tired reply, are the real argument of the lesson.

这一课是一段三方对话，带着鲜明的喜剧味。加里试了两个借口——「不可能」和「走神了」——都用过去式情态动词说出来。接着安「帮」他解释，实际上是在抱怨；警察的回答用 had better 作警告。最后几句从法律转回到夫妻：安的劝告和加里有气无力的回嘴，才是这一课真正的争执。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

In Canada speed is measured in kilometres an hour, and in British Columbia a driver caught going more than forty over the limit can have the car impounded on the spot, so the friendly ending of this lesson is not a safe bet. If police stop you, pull over to the right, stay in the car, keep your hands visible and have your licence, registration and insurance ready. Learners should also note the polite form officer used by Gary, and that Canadian police prefer the gender-free word officer. Had better is useful in daily life, but it sounds like a warning, so with a boss or a client should is the safer choice.

加拿大按每小时公里计速，在卑诗省超速四十公里以上的司机可能被当场扣车，所以这一课友好的结局不能当真。被警察拦下时，靠右停车、待在车里、双手放在看得见的地方，准备好驾照、车辆登记和保险。学习者还可以注意加里用的礼貌称呼 officer，加拿大警方也更喜欢这个不分性别的词。had better 日常很有用，但听起来像警告，对上司或客户用 should 更稳妥。

Sentence 8第 8 句 (begins开头 “You must have …”)

  • must have been doing is a confident guess about something going on in the past.must have been doing：对过去正在进行的事有把握的推测，「你刚才一定在……」。
  • an hour here means per hour.seventy miles an hour：每小时七十英里，an hour 表「每」。

Sentence 9第 9 句 (begins开头 “I can't have …”)

  • can't have been says it was impossible in the past.can't have been：过去不可能，是 must have been 的否定。
  • doing eighty means going at eighty.do eighty：口语，开到八十（英里/时）。

Sentence 13第 13 句 (begins开头 “I must have …”)

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

Guesses about the past: must have and can't have对过去的推测：must have / can't have
For a confident guess about the past, use must have plus the past participle, or must have been doing for an action in progress. For a negative guess, use can't have plus the past participle.
对已经发生的事做有把握的推测：must have + 过去分词（一定已经）或 must have been doing（当时一定在做）。否定推测用 can't have + 过去分词（不可能）。

Sentences 8 and 14 use must have been; sentence 9 uses can't have been.

本课：第 9、14 句 must have been，第 9 句 can't have been。

  • The ground is wet. It must have rained last night.
  • She can't have seen us — she didn't wave.
had better for strong advicehad better（最好）
had better plus the base verb, shortened to 'd better, gives strong advice with a warning. The negative is had better not plus the base verb. It is stronger than should, and despite had it is about now or the future.
had better + 动词原形，口语缩成 'd better：最好……（不然会有麻烦）。否定是 had better not + 动词原形。比 should 语气重，带警告意味。虽然有 had，说的是现在或将来。

Sentence 19 has the negative form, and sentence 24 the positive.

本课：第 19 句是否定 'd better not，第 24 句是肯定。

  • It's late. We'd better go home.
  • You'd better not forget your passport.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • He wants you to … 他要你……
    The doctor wants you to rest for a week.
  • You must have been … 你刚才一定在……
    You must have been sleeping when I called.
  • I can't have … 我不可能……
    I can't have left my keys at work.
  • I keep telling you to … 我一直叫你……
    I keep telling you to wear a helmet.
  • You'd better not … again. 你最好别再……
    You'd better not be late again.

Our model summary本站范文

An officer waved Gary down and said he had been going at seventy at least. Gary denied it, but the officer had passed him at eighty. Gary said he had missed the speed sign and must have been daydreaming. Ann said she had been warning him to slow down. The officer checked his licence, let him go, and warned him not to repeat it. Ann told Gary to listen to her next time.（73 词）
