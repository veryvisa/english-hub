Lesson 53–54 · An Interesting Climate 有趣的气候

📘 New Concept English · Book 1 · Lesson 53–54 “An Interesting Climate”新概念英语 第一册 Lesson 53–54《An Interesting Climate》

B1🧠 18 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
This time the person asked is English. He says the climate is mild but not always pleasant: cold in the North, windy in the East, wet in the West and now and then warm in the South. He enjoys spring and summer for their long days and short nights, when sunrise comes early and sunset late; he dislikes autumn and winter, when it is the other way round. He ends by saying the climate is not very good, yet never dull, and it is what the English most love to talk about. Paired with the lesson before, it teaches Which ... do you like best?, compass phrases such as in the North, rise and set in the present simple, the opposites long and short and early and late, and using but to turn a complaint into praise.
这回被问的是一位英国人。他说英国气候温和，但并不总是宜人：北部常冷，东部常刮风，西部常下雨，南部有时暖和。他喜欢春夏，因为昼长夜短、太阳早升晚落；不喜欢秋冬，因为昼短夜长、太阳晚升早落。最后他说：气候不怎么好，但肯定有意思——这是英国人最爱聊的话题。本课和上一课成对，学 Which … do you like best?、in the North 这类方位说法、rise / set 的一般现在时、反义词 long / short、early / late，以及用 but 先抑后扬。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. What is the weather like in each of the four parts of England?英国四个方向的天气各是什么样？
  2. Why does he enjoy spring and summer?他为什么喜欢春天和夏天？
  3. How can a climate be not very good and still interesting?气候「不太好」却「有意思」，你怎么理解？

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

The picture of England in the lesson is a fair summary of its weather. Westerly winds from the Atlantic bring most of the rain to the west, the east is drier but more exposed to cold winds, and the south is the warmest part. Day length changes a great deal because England is far north: London lies further north than Calgary and far north of Vancouver or Toronto, and in midsummer it has about sixteen and a half hours of daylight, in midwinter fewer than eight. The anthropologist Kate Fox, in Watching the English (2004), argues that weather talk works less as a way of sharing facts than as a social code for starting a conversation and showing friendliness.

课文里的英格兰是对它天气的公允概括。来自大西洋的西风把大部分雨水带到西部，东部较干、但更容易受冷风吹，南部最暖。由于英格兰纬度很高，昼长变化很大：伦敦比卡尔加里更靠北，比温哥华、多伦多更是靠北得多，仲夏白昼约十六个半小时，隆冬不到八小时。人类学家凯特·福克斯在《英格兰人的社会规则》（Watching the English，2004）中认为，谈天气与其说是交换信息，不如说是一套社交暗号，用来开启对话、表示友好。

✨ How it is written赏析

The lesson is built as a mirror. Two sentences about the light seasons are answered by two sentences about the dark ones, with every pair of words reversed, so the learner gets the opposites for free. The opening and closing lines are mirrors too: both begin with a complaint and turn on but to something kinder. That closing turn is the real point of the lesson. After a whole paragraph of grumbling, the speaker finds the one thing to like about bad weather, that it is never boring, and ends on a gentle joke about his own people.

这一课是按镜像搭的。讲明亮季节的两句，由讲黑暗季节的两句来回应，每一对词都对调，读者白得一组反义词。开头和结尾两句也互为镜像：都以一句抱怨起头，靠 but 转向更宽厚的说法。结尾这个转折才是全课的要点。抱怨了一整段之后，说话人找到了坏天气唯一可爱的地方——永远不无聊——并以一句拿自己国人开的温和玩笑收尾。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

Weather talk is just as useful in Canada, where it opens conversations with neighbours, cashiers and colleagues every day. Newcomers often treat a remark about the cold as a question that needs a full answer, when the right move is simply to agree and add a little: Yes, it's freezing, isn't it? One word in the lesson needs updating for Canada: autumn is understood, but fall is the everyday word. And the days in Canada change as much as in England, so the lesson's sentences about long and short days describe Vancouver or Toronto almost as well as London.

谈天气在加拿大同样有用，每天都靠它跟邻居、收银员、同事搭话。新移民常把一句「真冷啊」当成一个需要认真回答的问题，其实正确的做法是附和一句、再添一点：Yes, it's freezing, isn't it? 课文里有一个词在加拿大要更新：autumn 听得懂，但日常说 fall。加拿大的昼长变化和英国一样大，所以课文里讲昼长夜短的句子，用来描述温哥华或多伦多几乎和伦敦一样贴切。

💬 Discuss and write讨论与写作
  1. Practise three short weather openers you could use with a neighbour, and the natural reply to each.
    练三句可以跟邻居搭话的天气开场白，并给每句配一个自然的回应。
  2. Write a paragraph that starts with a complaint about where you live and turns on but to something you like about it.
    写一段话：先抱怨你住的地方，再用 but 转到你喜欢它的一点。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentence 6第 6 句 (begins开头 “It's mild, but …”)

  • not always means sometimes not, which is much weaker than never.not always：不总是，只否定一部分，不等于 never。

  • The present simple for a law of nature; with a singular subject both verbs take -s.一般现在时说自然规律，主语单数，两个动词都加 -s。
  • Sentence 15 swaps early and late, and the meaning turns round.第 15 句把 early 与 late 对调，意思正好相反。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

Which ... do you like best?
To ask someone to pick a favourite: Which, a noun, then do you like best? Answer with like ... best, or just name it.
请对方在几样中挑最喜欢的：Which + 名词 + do you like best?；回答用 like … best，或直接说出选择。

Sentence 9 asks about the seasons and sentence 10 answers.

本课：第 9 句问哪个季节，第 10 句回答。

  • Which fruit do you like best? — Mangoes, easily.
  • Which subject does she like best? — Science.
Opposites, and but for turning a point round反义词与先抑后扬的 but
To draw a contrast, use opposites in pairs. Join two opposite halves with but, often putting the weakness first and the strength second.
描述对照时把反义词成对用：long / short、early / late、rise / set；用 but 连接相反的两半，常常先说缺点再说优点。

Sentences 11 and 13 mirror sentences 14 and 16 word for word in reverse, and sentences 6 and 17 both turn on but.

本课：第 12、13 句与第 15、16 句一一对调；第 6 句与第 16 句都用 but 转折。

  • In summer the days are long; in winter they are short.
  • The flat is small, but it's very bright.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • It's …, but it's not always …. 它……，但并不总是……
    It's cheap, but it's not always good.
  • The weather's often … in the North. 北部的天气常常……
    The weather's often foggy in the North.
  • Which … do you like best? — …, I think. 你最喜欢哪个……？——大概是……吧。
    Which sport do you like best? — Tennis, I think.
  • The days are … and the nights are …. 白天……，夜晚……
    In Iceland in June the days are endless and the nights are bright.
  • Our … is not very …, but it's certainly …. 我们的……不太……，但肯定很……
    Our flat is not very big, but it's certainly comfortable.

Summary writing摘要写作

In no more than 60 words, say how the Englishman describes his country's climate.

用不超过 60 个词说说这位英国人怎么评价英国的气候。

Points a good summary covers好摘要要覆盖的要点
  • The climate is mild but not always pleasant: cold in the North, windy in the East, wet in the West, sometimes warm in the South.英国气候温和，但不总是宜人：北冷、东风、西湿、南方有时暖。

Our model summary本站范文

The Englishman says the climate is mild but not always pleasant. The North is often cold and the East windy; the West is wet, and the South is warm at times. He enjoys spring and summer, when days are long, but not autumn and winter. The climate is poor but never boring, so the English love to talk about it.（60 词）
