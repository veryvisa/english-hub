Lesson 55–56 · The Sawyer Family 索耶一家

📘 New Concept English · Book 1 · Lesson 55–56 “The Sawyer Family”新概念英语 第一册 Lesson 55–56《The Sawyer Family》

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
The Sawyers' address is 87 King Street. Each morning Mr Sawyer leaves for work and the children for school, and he drives them there. Mrs Sawyer is at home all day doing the housework. She has lunch at midday without fail, and in the afternoon she usually meets friends, often over a pot of tea. The children are back early in the evening; their father is back late. After dark the children never skip homework before bed, and Mr Sawyer usually reads the paper, though now and then he and his wife watch TV. This lesson is a family's ordinary day. It teaches the present simple with he and she (goes, takes, does, sees), time phrases such as in the morning, at noon and at night, the frequency words usually and often, and phrases without an article such as go to work and come home.
索耶一家住在国王街 87 号。早上，索耶先生上班，孩子们上学，每天由爸爸开车送去。索耶太太天天在家做家务，中午总是十二点吃午饭，下午常和朋友见面、一起喝茶。傍晚孩子们放学早早到家，索耶先生下班晚到家。晚上孩子们总是做作业，然后睡觉；索耶先生通常看报，有时和妻子一起看电视。本课是一家人一天的日常，学一般现在时第三人称单数（goes、takes、does、sees）、表示时段的 in the morning / at noon / at night、频度副词 usually 和 often，以及 go to work、come home 这类不带冠词的说法。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. At what time of day is the homework done, and what comes after it?孩子们什么时候做作业？做完做什么？
  2. Why is one arrival early and the other late, in sentences 12 and 15?第 12 句和第 14 句为什么一个 early、一个 late？
  3. In sentence 4, why is there no the in the phrases for work and school?第 4 句的 go to work、go to school 为什么不加 the？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander; New Concept English, Book 1, Longman, 1967.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为教材自撰，见《新概念英语》第一册（朗文，1967）。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

The Sawyer family is a portrait of a comfortable British household of the 1960s: a father who works in town and drives the children to school, a mother at home who does the housework and has friends round for tea in the afternoon, and children in bed early after homework. In 1960s Britain only a minority of married women worked outside the home, though the number was rising fast. The lesson is the first long passage in the present simple, and the next lesson, An unusual day, takes the same family and the same times and changes everything, so the two must be read as a pair.

索耶一家是 1960 年代英国一个殷实家庭的画像：父亲在城里上班、开车送孩子上学；母亲在家做家务，下午请朋友来喝茶；孩子做完作业早早上床。1960 年代的英国，已婚女性在外工作的只是少数，不过人数增长很快。本课是全书第一段一般现在时的长篇，而下一课《不平常的一天》用同一家人、同样的钟点，把一切都改了，所以两课必须成对来读。

✨ How it is written赏析

The passage is built on the clock. Each paragraph opens with a time of day, in the morning, in the afternoon, in the evening, at night, so the reader always knows where in the day they are, and the family is described by the rhythm of who leaves and who comes back. The writer keeps the grammar visible by putting singular and plural subjects side by side, the father with -s and the children without, again and again, and by placing a frequency word in almost every line. The early and late arrivals in the evening paragraph are a small, exact contrast that also says something about the father's working hours.

这段文字是按钟表搭起来的。每段都以一天里的一个时段开头——早上、下午、傍晚、夜里——读者始终知道现在是一天中的什么时候，而这家人是靠谁出门、谁回来的节奏被描写出来的。作者让语法一直摆在明处：一遍遍把单数和复数主语并排放——父亲的动词加 -s，孩子的不加——并且几乎每行都放一个频度副词。傍晚那段一个早到、一个晚到，是一个小而精确的对照，也顺便说出了父亲的工作时长。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The grammar is the core of everyday English, and describing a routine is one of the first speaking tasks on many English tests. The family itself looks very different today. In Canada most mothers of school-age children work, and in many families both parents share the school run and the housework. Children are more likely to take a school bus or walk than to be driven by their father every day. Learners describing their own family can keep every grammar pattern here and change the facts, which is exactly what the lesson is designed for.

这里的语法是日常英语的核心，描述日常作息也是很多英语考试最早出现的口语题之一。这一家本身在今天看起来很不一样。在加拿大，有学龄孩子的母亲大多在工作，很多家庭里父母分担接送孩子和做家务。孩子更可能坐校车或走路上学，而不是每天由父亲开车送。读者描述自己家时，可以保留这里的每一个语法句型、只换事实，这正是本课的设计用意。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 3第 3 句 (begins开头 “The Sawyers live …”)

  • The with a family name in the plural means the whole family, so the verb is plural.The + 姓的复数 = 一家人：the Sawyers 索耶一家，动词用复数 live。
  • Use at for a house number, in or on for a street, and in for a city.具体门牌号前用 at，街名前用 in/on，城市前用 in。

Sentence 4第 4 句 (begins开头 “In the morning, …”)

  • go to work and go to school have no article when they mean going there for the usual purpose.go to work / go to school 不加 the，指去上班、上学这件事。

Sentence 7第 7 句 (begins开头 “She does the …”)

  • do becomes does with she. do the housework means cleaning and cooking, not homework.do 第三人称单数是 does；do the housework 做家务，别与 homework 混。

Sentence 8第 8 句 (begins开头 “She always eats …”)

  • always goes before an ordinary verb; noon takes at.频度副词 always 放在普通动词前；at noon 用 at。

Sentence 11第 11 句 (begins开头 “In the evening, …”)

  • In come home and arrive home, home is an adverb, so there is no to.come home、arrive home：home 是副词，前面不加 to。

Sentence 15第 15 句 (begins开头 “At night, the …”)

  • in the morning, the afternoon and the evening, but at noon and at night.in the morning / in the afternoon / in the evening，但 at noon、at night。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

Spelling the he and she form of the present simple一般现在时第三人称单数的拼写
Most verbs add -s. Verbs ending in o, s, sh, ch or x add -es. A consonant plus y becomes -ies. have becomes has.
一般加 -s：stays、eats、reads；以 o、s、sh、ch、x 结尾加 -es：goes、does、watches；辅音 + y 结尾变 y 为 -ies：study → studies；have → has。

Sentences 4, 6, 8, 10 and 15 give goes, takes, does, sees and arrives.

本课：第 4 句 goes，第 5 句 takes，第 7 句 does，第 9 句 sees，第 14 句 arrives。

  • My mother watches the news and goes to bed at ten.
  • He studies French and has a lesson every Monday.
Times of the day一天里的时间：in the morning / at noon / at night
Use in with the morning, the afternoon and the evening; use at with noon, night and clock times. every day goes at the end.
in the morning、in the afternoon、in the evening 用 in；at noon、at night、at + 钟点 用 at；every day 放句末。

Sentences 4, 10, 12 and 16 move through the day with in and at, and sentence 8 has at noon.

本课：第 5、10、12、16 句依次用 in the morning、in the afternoon、in the evening、at night，第 8 句是 at noon。

  • I get up at seven in the morning.
  • Owls hunt at night.
Fixed phrases without an article不加冠词的固定说法
go to work, school or bed, come home, stay at home and at home take no article, because they mean the activity rather than the building.
go to work / school / bed、come home、stay at home、at home 都不加 the；指的是「做这件事」而不是那栋楼。

Sentences 4, 7, 12 and 17 all have one.

本课：第 5、7、12、17 句里都有。

  • I go to bed at eleven.
  • She comes home at six.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • The …s live at … Street. ……一家住在……街。
    The Wongs live at 23 Elm Street.
  • In the morning, … goes to work and … go to school. 早上，……去上班，……去上学。
    In the morning, my mother goes to work and my brothers go to school.
  • … always eats … at noon. ……总在中午吃……
    My grandfather always eats noodles at noon.
  • After lunch, she usually visits …. 午饭后她通常去看……
    After lunch, she usually visits her sister.
  • … usually reads …, but sometimes …. ……通常读……，但有时……
    Dad usually reads a book, but sometimes he plays chess.
