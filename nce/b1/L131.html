Lesson 131–132 · Don't be So Sure 别那么肯定

📘 New Concept English · Book 1 · Lesson 131–132 “Don't be So Sure”新概念英语 第一册 Lesson 131–132《Don't be So Sure》

B1🧠 18 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
A friend asks Gary about his holiday plans this year. Gary says they could go overseas, but nothing is fixed: his wife fancies Egypt and so does he, yet they cannot reach a decision. Ship or plane? Possibly the ship; it costs less, but the journey is slow. The friend says they will have a lovely time, and Gary warns him not to count on it. They might end up going nowhere, because his wife frets every year about who will care for the dog, the house and the garden, and in the end the couple stay put and do it all themselves. This is a chat about holiday plans that teaches may and might for possibility, make up one's mind, look after, enjoy yourselves and in the end.
朋友问加里今年去哪儿度假。加里说可能出国，还没定：妻子想去埃及，他也想去，可就是拿不定主意。坐船还是坐飞机？可能坐船，便宜是便宜，可路上太久。朋友说你们一定会玩得很开心，加里说别那么肯定——说不定哪儿都去不成：妻子总是操心狗谁管、房子谁管、花园谁管，年年如此，最后两人只好待在家里，什么都自己管。本课是一段关于度假计划的闲聊，学 may / might 表可能、make up one's mind、look after、enjoy yourselves 与 in the end。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. Where would Gary and his wife like to go?加里和妻子想去哪儿？
  2. What is the drawback of going by sea?他们为什么犹豫坐船？
  3. What usually happens to their plans?他们最后往往怎么样？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander; New Concept English, Book 1, Longman, 1967. Gary and his wife are invented characters, built to practise may and might.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为教材自撰，见《新概念英语》第一册（朗文，1967）。加里和他妻子是虚构人物，是为操练 may 和 might 编的。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

In the 1960s foreign holidays were becoming possible for ordinary British families. Package tours by air were growing fast, but sea travel was still common for long trips, and a voyage to Egypt through the Mediterranean could take a week or more. Pet boarding kennels and house sitters were less common than today, so leaving a dog, a house and a garden for weeks really was a problem. Egypt was a classic destination for its ancient sites along the Nile.

1960 年代，出国度假开始进入英国普通家庭。乘飞机的跟团游增长很快，但长途旅行坐船仍很常见，经地中海去埃及可能要一个星期甚至更久。寄养宠物的狗舍和看房人不像今天这么普遍，所以把狗、房子和花园丢下好几个星期，确实是件难事。埃及因尼罗河沿岸的古迹，一直是经典的旅游目的地。

✨ How it is written赏析

The whole text is built on uncertainty, so may and might appear wherever a plan is mentioned, and the title answers the friend's confident will. The three questions about looking after the dog, the house and the garden are a classic list of three that grows more comic each time. The last line reverses the holiday: the couple stay home and do the very jobs they wanted to escape.

全篇建立在「不确定」之上，所以每提到一个计划就出现 may 或 might，而标题正是对朋友那句笃定的 will 的回应。狗、房子、花园谁来照看，是经典的「三连问」，一个比一个好笑。最后一句把假期倒了过来：两口子待在家里，干的正是他们本想逃开的活。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

May and might are the polite way to talk about plans that are not fixed, and native speakers use them far more than learners do; saying I will go to Egypt too early can sound like a promise. For Canadians the same worries are real: many families book a pet sitter or a neighbour to collect mail and shovel snow, and home insurance policies often ask that a house left empty for a long time be checked regularly. So the joke still works, but there are now simple answers to the wife's questions.

may 和 might 是谈「还没定」的计划的得体说法，母语者用得比学习者多得多；太早说 I will go to Egypt，听上去像是许了诺。对加拿大人来说，同样的担心也很真实：很多家庭会请宠物看护，或托邻居收信、铲雪；家庭保险条款常要求长期空置的房子定期有人查看。所以这个笑话今天还成立，只是妻子的问题如今都有简单的答案了。

    用 may、might、might not 各至少一次，跟同伴说说你明年夏天的打算。
  2. List three things you would worry about if you left home for a month, and say who might look after each one.
    列出三件你离家一个月会担心的事，说说可能由谁来照看。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

may and might for possibilitymay / might 表示可能
may or might plus the base verb says something is possible. might is a little less sure, though speakers often use them in the same way. The negatives are may not and might not. To ask about possibility, people usually say Do you think or Are you going to rather than starting with may.
may / might + 动词原形：可能（会）。might 比 may 把握略小，口语里常可互换。否定 may not / might not（可能不），不缩写成 mayn't。问「会不会」时一般不用 may 开头问，而说 Do you think …? / Are you going to …?

Sentences 5 and 11 use may, sentence 13 may be ... but, and sentence 16 might not.

本课：第 5、11 句 may，第 13 句 may be … but，第 16 句 might not。

  • Take a jacket. It may get cold later.
  • I might not come to the party — I'm very tired.
Ways of travelling with by交通方式：by sea / by air / by train
by plus a means of transport takes no article: by car, by train, by sea, by air. Walking is on foot. With an article or possessive, change the preposition: in my car, on the train.
by + 交通工具或途径，中间不加冠词：by car、by bus、by train、by plane、by sea、by air。步行说 on foot。如果有冠词或物主代词，就换介词：in my car、on the train。

Sentence 10 asks by sea or by air; sentence 11 answers by sea.

本课：第 10 句问 by sea or by air，第 11 句答 by sea。

  • We went to Victoria by ferry.
  • She comes to work on foot.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • We may … 我们可能……
    We may move to Calgary next year.
  • She still hasn't made up her mind. 她还没拿定主意。
    She still hasn't made up her mind about the house.
  • It may be …, but … 也许……，可是……
    It may be fast, but it's very expensive.
  • Who will look after the …? 谁来照顾……？
    Who will look after the baby on Friday?
  • In the end, we … 最后我们……
    In the end, we ordered pizza.
