Lesson 13–14 · A New Dress 一件新连衣裙

📘 New Concept English · Book 1 · Lesson 13–14 “A New Dress”新概念英语 第一册 Lesson 13–14《A New Dress》

B1🧠 17 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
A friend asks Anna what colour her new dress is. Anna says it is green and invites the friend upstairs to see it. The friend looks and praises it: nice, and very smart. Anna adds that her hat is new as well, in the same colour, green, and the friend praises the hat too. This lesson is looking at a friend's new things and saying something kind: ask the colour, go and look, praise, and hear about one more thing. The grammar is the question What colour is ...?, the two places an adjective can go, before a noun or after be, the same, too, and Here it is! for showing something.
朋友问安娜新买的连衣裙是什么颜色，安娜说是绿色的，请朋友上楼去看。朋友看了连连夸：真漂亮、真时髦。安娜又说帽子也是新的，颜色一样，也是绿的；朋友又夸了一句帽子。本课是看朋友的新东西、夸一夸：问颜色、去看、称赞、对方再补一件。学 What colour is …? 问颜色、形容词放在名词前和 be 后两种位置、the same、too，以及 Here it is! 「在这儿呢」。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. What colour is Anna's hat?安娜的帽子是什么颜色的？
  2. How many times does the friend praise something, and with which adjectives?朋友一共夸了几次？用了哪些形容词？
  3. What does smart mean here, and how is it related to clever?smart 在这里是什么意思？和「聪明」有什么关系？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander; New Concept English, Book 1, Longman, 1967. The scene is invented to drill colour questions and adjectives.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为教材自撰，见《新概念英语》第一册（朗文，1967）。场景为操练问颜色和形容词而设。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

Colour is one of the first things a learner can talk about with no grammar at all, and the book uses a pleasant everyday excuse: a friend's new clothes. The drill lesson after it runs through a long list of colours and objects, from carpets to dogs, so that What colour is it? can be asked about almost anything in a room. A matching green dress and hat was a fashion of the 1960s, and the picture in the book shows exactly that.

颜色是读者几乎不用语法就能谈的第一批话题之一，书里借的是一个愉快的日常由头：朋友的新衣服。后面那课操练列了一长串颜色和物品，从地毯到狗，让 What colour is it? 几乎能问房间里的任何东西。裙子和帽子同色相配是 1960 年代的时尚，书里的插图画的正是这个。

✨ How it is written赏析

The lesson is built so that the praise grows. The friend first hears the colour, then sees the dress and gives two compliments, nice and smart. Then Anna, pleased, brings out one more thing, and the final line is the warmest word in the lesson, lovely, with an exclamation mark. The short Here it is! in the middle is the turning point: everything before it is talk about the dress, and everything after it is a reaction to seeing it.

这课的设计让称赞逐步升温。朋友先听到颜色，再看到裙子，说了两句好话：nice 和 smart。然后安娜高兴了，又拿出一样东西；最后一句是全课最暖的词 lovely，还带感叹号。中间那句短短的 Here it is! 是转折点：之前都在谈裙子，之后都是看到裙子后的反应。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

Compliments are where Chinese and English habits differ most. In English a compliment on clothes is normal, even between people who do not know each other well, and the expected reply is a simple Thank you!, perhaps with a little detail such as I got it last week. The Chinese habit of denying it, saying it is nothing or old, can confuse an English speaker, who may think you did not like the compliment. Note also that smart means well dressed in Britain but clever in North America, so a Canadian hearing that dress is smart may smile.

称赞是中英习惯差别最大的地方之一。在英语里夸别人的衣服很平常，即使不太熟也可以，标准回答就是一句 Thank you!，最多再加一点细节，比如 I got it last week。中文那种「哪里哪里，旧的」的推辞，会让英语母语者困惑，以为你不喜欢他的夸奖。还要注意 smart 在英国指衣着整洁时髦，在北美指聪明，所以加拿大人听到 that dress is smart 可能会笑。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 3第 3 句 (begins开头 “What colour's your …”)

What colour's your new dress?

  • What colour plus is plus a noun asks about colour; here 's is short for is.What colour + is + 名词？问颜色，'s 是 is 的缩写。
  • The answer is It's with the colour. No a goes before the colour word.回答用 It's + 颜色：It's green.，颜色前不加 a。

Sentence 5第 5 句 (begins开头 “Come upstairs and …”)

Come upstairs and see it.

  • An imperative starts with the plain verb and is used to invite or ask. come and see means come and have a look.祈使句：动词原形开头，用来邀请或请求。come and see = 来看看。
  • Anna is inviting her friend, not giving an order; tone makes the difference.主人邀请朋友上楼，是友好的邀请，不是命令。

Sentence 8第 8 句 (begins开头 “Here it is! …”)

Here it is!

  • Say this when you show or find something. With a pronoun, it comes before is: Here is it is wrong.拿出或找到东西时说「在这儿呢！」。代词 it 放在 is 前面：不说 Here is it.。
  • Compare: a noun goes after is, as in Here is the dress, but a pronoun goes before it.对比：名词放 is 后（Here is the dress.），代词放 is 前（Here it is.）。

Sentence 13第 13 句 (begins开头 “It's the same …”)

It's the same colour.

  • same needs the in front of it: the same colour.the same + 名词：same 前要有 the。
  • To add what it matches, use the same as: the same colour as my dress.「和……一样」：the same as …：My hat is the same colour as my dress.。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

Asking about colour: What colour is ...?What colour is …? 问颜色
Ask What colour, then is or are, then the noun. Answer with It's or They're and the colour.
What colour + is/are + 名词？；回答：It's / They're + 颜色。

Sentences 3 and 13 are the same kind of question, and sentences 4 and 15 answer them.

本课：第 3 句与第 12 句是同一种问句，第 4 句与第 14 句作答。

  • What colour is your car? — It's grey.
  • What colour are her shoes? — They're red.
Two places for an adjective形容词的两种位置
An adjective can go before a noun, as in a nice dress, or after be, as in It's very smart. Before a singular countable noun it still needs a or an.
形容词可以放在名词前（a nice dress），也可以放在 be 后（It's very smart.）。放名词前时，单数可数名词还要带 a/an。

In sentences 9 and 16 the adjective comes before the noun; in sentence 10 it comes after is.

本课：第 9 句和第 15 句形容词在名词前，第 10 句在 is 后。

  • That's a lovely garden. / The garden is lovely.
  • She has a new car. / Her car is new.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • What colour's your …? — It's … 你的……是什么颜色？——是……色。
    What colour's your umbrella? — It's yellow.
  • Come upstairs/in and see it. 上楼／进来看看吧。
    Come in and see the new kitchen.
  • That's a nice … It's very … 这……真不错，很……
    That's a nice jacket. It's very warm.
  • My … is new too. It's the same colour. 我的……也是新的，颜色一样。
    My bag is new too. It's the same colour.
  • That is a lovely …! 这……真可爱！
    That is a lovely photo!
