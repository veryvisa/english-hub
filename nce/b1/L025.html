Lesson 25–26 · Mrs. Smith's Kitchen 史密斯太太的厨房

📘 New Concept English · Book 1 · Lesson 25–26 “Mrs. Smith's Kitchen”新概念英语 第一册 Lesson 25–26《Mrs. Smith's Kitchen》

B1🧠 16 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
This is the first descriptive passage in Book 1. There is no dialogue; line by line it tells you what is in Mrs Smith's kitchen, where each thing is and what it is like. The kitchen is small. On the right stands a white fridge, on the left a blue electric cooker, and in the middle a table with an empty bottle and a clean cup on it. The grammar is There is a or There is an for bringing in something new, followed by The ... is ... for its colour and place; the phrases on the right, on the left and in the middle of; and how a and the divide the work.
这是第一册第一篇描写文：没有对话，只是一句一句地告诉你史密斯太太的厨房里有什么、在哪儿、什么样。厨房不大；右边是白色的冰箱，左边是蓝色的电灶，屋子中间一张桌子，桌上一只空瓶、一只干净的杯子。学 There is a/an … 引出新东西，接着用 The … is … 说它的颜色和位置；方位短语 on the right / on the left / in the middle of；以及 a 和 the 的分工。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. What colour is the cooker, and which side is it on?电灶是什么颜色的？在哪一边？
  2. Which two things are on the table, and what state is each in?桌子上有哪两样东西？各是什么状态？
  3. Why does the fridge take a the first time and the the second time?为什么第一次提到冰箱时用 a，第二次用 the？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander; New Concept English, Book 1, Longman, 1967. It is the first passage in the book written as description rather than dialogue.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为教材自撰，见《新概念英语》第一册（朗文，1967）。这是全书第一篇写成描写而不是对话的课文。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

After two dozen lessons of talk, the book changes form. A short passage describes a room, and the learner is expected to read it, understand it and then describe a room of their own. The kitchen of a 1960s British home was usually small, and an electric cooker and a fridge were still signs of a modern household; many homes then had no fridge at all. The drill lesson after it asks Where is it? about the same things, so that the learner moves from reading a description to asking about one.

二十多课对话之后，书换了形式：一小段文字描写一个房间，读者要读懂，然后描述自己的房间。1960 年代英国住宅的厨房通常很小，电灶和冰箱还是现代家庭的标志，那时很多家庭根本没有冰箱。后面那课操练用 Where is it? 问同样的东西，让读者从读描写过渡到问描写。

✨ How it is written赏析

The passage is a lesson in order. It starts with the whole room and its size, then moves round it like a camera: right, left, centre, then down onto the table and the two things on it. Every object gets the same two steps, first There is a, then The thing is, so the reader always knows whether something is new or already seen. The order also follows the eye: big things first, small things last. Anyone who writes a description, from a flat advert to a police report, uses the same method.

这段文字教的是顺序。先说整个房间和它的大小，然后像镜头一样绕一圈：右、左、中间，再落到桌上和桌上的两样东西。每样东西都走同样两步——先 There is a，再 The thing is——读者始终知道哪样是新的、哪样已经见过。顺序还跟着眼睛走：先大后小。从租房广告到警方报告，任何描写都用这个方法。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The distinction between a and the in this lesson is one of the hardest points for Chinese speakers, because Chinese has no articles and marks new and old information in other ways. The passage gives a clear rule of thumb that works most of the time: the first time, a; after that, the. A second point is local. In Canada the cooker is called a stove, and the word refrigerator is rarely said aloud, while fridge is heard every day. Learners who move here will want both the British words of the book and the North American words of daily life.

本课 a 和 the 的区分是中国读者最难掌握的点之一，因为中文没有冠词，新旧信息靠别的办法表示。这段文字给了一条大多数时候都管用的经验法则：第一次用 a，之后用 the。第二点是本地用语：在加拿大，cooker 叫 stove，refrigerator 这个词很少有人说出口，fridge 则天天能听到。搬到这里的读者，既要会书里的英式词，也要会日常生活里的北美词。

💬 Discuss and write讨论与写作
  1. Describe your own kitchen in six sentences, using There is a ... and then The ... is ... for each thing.
    用六句话描述你自己的厨房，每样东西都先用 There is a ...，再用 The ... is ...。
  2. Find three things in your home with different names in British and North American English.
    找出你家里三样在英式和北美英语里叫法不同的东西。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 4第 4 句 (begins开头 “There is a …”)

  • There is with a or an, a noun and a place tells the reader for the first time that something is there.There is + a/an + 名词 + 地点：第一次告诉读者某处有某物。
  • Because this is new to the reader, it takes a; the next sentence calls it the.这是新信息，所以用 a；下一句再提它就用 the。

Sentence 6第 6 句 (begins开头 “It is on …”)

It is on the right.

  • on the right and on the left both use on and the.on the right / on the left：在右边／左边，介词用 on，带 the。
  • it stands for the fridge in the sentence before.it 代替前一句的 the refrigerator。

Sentence 10第 10 句 (begins开头 “There is a …”)

  • in the middle of with a place means in the centre of it. None of the small words, in, the and of, can be dropped.in the middle of + 地点：在……的中间。注意三个小词 in、the、of 一个都不能少。

Sentence 13第 13 句 (begins开头 “There is a …”)

  • too at the end means also: a cup as well as the bottle.too 放在句末，表示「也」：桌上除了瓶子，还有杯子。
  • on the table means resting on its top; in the table is wrong.on the table：在桌面上（接触表面），不说 in the table。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

There is a ... and The ... is ...: introduce it, then describe itThere is a … 与 The … is …：先引出，再描述
When something is mentioned for the first time, use There is with a or an and the noun, because it is new. After that, talk about it with The plus the noun, then is, then an adjective or a place, or use It is.
第一次说到某物，用 There is + a/an + 名词（新信息）；接着再说它，用 The + 名词 + is + 形容词/地点（已知信息），或用 It is。

Each thing in the kitchen is handled in two steps. Sentence 4 brings in the fridge and sentences 5 and 7 describe it; sentence 7 brings in the cooker and sentences 8 and 10 describe it.

本课：冰箱、电灶、瓶子、杯子都是这样两步走：第 4 句引出，第 6、7 句描述；第 7 句引出，第 9、10 句描述。

  • There is a lamp in my room. The lamp is yellow.
  • There is an apple in the bag. It is red.
Phrases of place: on the right, on the left, in the middle of方位短语：on the right / on the left / in the middle of
On the right side is on the right; on the left side is on the left; in the centre is in the middle of; on top of is on; inside is in.
在右边 on the right；在左边 on the left；在……中间 in the middle of …；在……上面 on …；在……里面 in …。

Sentence 6 is on the right, sentence 9 on the left, sentence 10 in the middle, and sentences 11 and 14 are on the table.

本课：第 6 句在右，第 9 句在左，第 10 句在中间，第 11 句与第 13 句在桌上。

  • The door is on the left, and the window is on the right.
  • There is a fountain in the middle of the square.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • X's kitchen/room is small/large. X 的厨房／房间很小／很大。
    Tom's bedroom is very small.
  • There is a/an … in the … ……里有一个……
    There is an old piano in the hall.
  • The … is (colour). It is on the right/left. 那……是……色的，在右边／左边。
    The sofa is grey. It stands on the left.
  • There is a … on the …, too. ……上也有一个……
    There is a lamp on the desk, too.
