🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
Andy slips and falls down the stairs. Lucy asks if he is hurt; he is, and he thinks it is his back. She tells him to try to get to his feet and offers a hand, but he cannot manage it. She decides the doctor should see him and rings Dr. Carter, who promises to come straight away. She is sure he will need an X-ray. This lesson teaches object clauses after I think, I'm afraid and I'm sure, the reflexive in hurt yourself, try and do, had better, and at once.
安迪滑了一跤，从楼梯上摔了下来。露西问他伤着没有——伤着了，他觉得是伤了背。她让他试着站起来，要扶他，可他说起不来。露西说最好还是让医生来看看，她给卡特医生打电话，医生说马上就来。露西说：安迪，你肯定得拍个 X 光。本课学宾语从句 I think that … / I'm afraid that … / I'm sure that …、反身代词 hurt yourself、try and do、had better、at once。

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander; New Concept English, Book 1, Longman, 1967.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为教材自撰，见《新概念英语》第一册（朗文，1967）。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

In 1960s Britain the family doctor, or GP, still made house calls as a normal part of the job, and a patient who could not get up from the floor would expect the doctor to come to the house rather than to be taken to hospital at once. X-rays had to be done at a hospital, so the doctor's visit would come first, then a trip to the hospital if needed. Falls on the stairs were, and still are, one of the most common causes of injury at home. The National Health Service, started in 1948, meant that none of this cost the family money at the point of care.

1960 年代的英国，家庭医生出诊仍是工作的常规部分。病人从地上起不来，会等医生上门，而不是马上被送去医院。拍 X 光得去医院，所以先是医生上门，必要时再去医院。在楼梯上摔倒，过去和现在都是家中受伤最常见的原因之一。1948 年设立的国民医疗服务体系意味着，这一切看病时都不用家里掏钱。

✨ How it is written赏析

The lesson is a small emergency told almost entirely in short questions and replies, and the grammar does the calming. Lucy asks, offers, decides and reports, and nearly every one of her sentences begins with a frame such as I think, I'm sure or the doctor says. Those frames turn panic into steps: a guess, an offer, a decision, a promise. Andy's lines are the opposite, short and apologetic, with one polite frame of his own to give the bad news. The ending gives no relief; it simply points forward to the hospital.

课文讲的是一场小小的急事，几乎全用短问短答，而让人镇定下来的是语法。露西提问、提出帮忙、做决定、转告消息，她几乎每句话都以一个框架开头：I think、I'm sure、the doctor says。这些框架把慌乱变成了一步步的行动：一个判断、一次帮忙、一个决定、一个承诺。安迪的台词正相反，简短、带着歉意，只用了一个客气的框架来说坏消息。结尾没有给人松一口气，只是指向接下来要去的医院。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The advice in the lesson matches what first-aid courses still teach: do not force someone with a possible back injury to stand, and call for help. What has changed is who you call. In British Columbia family doctors rarely make house calls, so for a fall like this people would call 911 for an ambulance, or HealthLink BC at 811 for nurse advice if it seems less serious. Many newcomers to Canada also do not yet have a family doctor and use walk-in clinics. For learners, I'm afraid that is worth practising: Chinese speakers often say I'm sorry for everything, but I'm afraid is the natural way to give bad news without apologising.

课文里的做法与今天急救课教的一致：疑似背部受伤的人不要硬让他站起来，要叫人帮忙。变的是打给谁。在不列颠哥伦比亚省，家庭医生很少出诊，像这样摔一跤，人们会打 911 叫救护车；看起来不太严重的，可以打 811 找 HealthLink BC 的护士咨询。很多新移民还没有家庭医生，会去免预约诊所。对学习者来说，I'm afraid that 值得多练：中国人常常什么都说 I'm sorry，而 I'm afraid 是不必道歉也能说坏消息的自然说法。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 6第 6 句 (begins开头 “I slipped and …”)

  • Two past forms joined by and; fell is the past of fall.slip and fall：滑了一跤摔倒，两个过去式并列。fell 是 fall 的过去式。
  • downstairs is an adverb, so no to before it.downstairs 是副词，前面不加 to。

Sentence 7第 7 句 (begins开头 “Have you hurt …”)

  • Use the reflexive when the subject and object are the same person.hurt oneself：伤着自己，主语和宾语是同一个人时用反身代词。
  • The that clause is the object of think, and that can be left out.I think that …：宾语从句，that 可省。

Sentence 10第 10 句 (begins开头 “Try and stand …”)

  • In spoken British English, try and do means try to do.try and do：口语，= try to do。
  • A friendly offer of help.Let me help you：我来扶你，主动提出帮忙。

Sentence 15第 15 句 (begins开头 “I'm afraid that …”)

  • I'm afraid that softens bad news; it does not mean he is scared.I'm afraid that …：恐怕……，用来委婉说坏消息，不是「害怕」。

Sentence 16第 16 句 (begins开头 “I think that …”)

  • had better plus the base verb gives strong advice.had better + 动词原形：最好……。
  • will for a decision made while speaking.I'll phone：说话时当场决定，用 will。

Sentence 18第 18 句 (begins开头 “The doctor says …”)

  • says that reports what the doctor said; at once means immediately.says that …：转述别人的话。at once：马上。
  • I'm sure that gives a strong belief.I'm sure that …：我确信……。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

Object clauses after think, afraid and sure宾语从句：I think / I'm afraid / I'm sure + that …
A main clause is followed by that and a full sentence with its own subject and verb. Common openings are I think that, I'm afraid that for bad news, I'm sure that, and he says that. In speech that is often dropped, and the clause keeps normal word order.
主句 + that + 完整的句子（主语 + 谓语）。常见开头：I think that（我认为）、I'm afraid that（恐怕，坏消息）、I'm sure that（我确信）、He says that（他说）。口语里 that 常省略。从句用陈述语序，不倒装。

Sentences 9, 15, 16, 18 and 19 all use this structure.

本课：第 9、15、16、18、19 句都用了这个结构。

  • I think (that) the shop is closed.
  • I'm afraid (that) your flight is delayed.
  • She says (that) she'll be late.
Reflexive pronouns反身代词
Use a reflexive when the subject and the object are the same person: hurt yourself, cut himself, enjoy ourselves. Help yourself is a set phrase that invites someone to take food or drink.
myself、yourself、himself、herself、itself、ourselves、yourselves、themselves。主语和宾语是同一人时用：hurt yourself、cut himself、enjoy ourselves、help yourself（请自便）。

In sentence 7 Lucy asks whether he has hurt himself.

本课：第 7 句问他伤着自己没有。

  • Be careful with that knife. Don't cut yourself.
  • We really enjoyed ourselves at the party.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • What's wrong, …? — I slipped on the ice. 怎么了？——我在冰上滑了一跤。
    What's wrong, Mia? — I slipped on the ice.
  • I think I've hurt my … 我想我伤着……了。
    I think I've hurt my knee.
  • Try and … Let me help you. 试着……我来帮你。
    Try and lift it. Let me help you.
  • I'm afraid I can't … 恐怕我不能……
    I'm afraid I can't come to the party.
  • I'm sure you need … 我肯定你需要……
    I'm sure you need a rest.
