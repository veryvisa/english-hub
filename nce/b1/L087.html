Lesson 87–88 · A Car Crash 车祸

📘 New Concept English · Book 1 · Lesson 87–88 “A Car Crash”新概念英语 第一册 Lesson 87–88《A Car Crash》

B1🧠 17 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
Mr. Wood comes to a garage to collect his car and asks whether it is ready. The attendant does not know and needs the registration: LFZ 312 G. When did it come in? Three days back. Now he remembers. Have the mechanics finished? Not yet; they are still at work on it. The two of them walk into the workshop to take a look. Isn't that your car? asks the attendant. Mr. Wood says it used to be. It turns out he drove into a lamp-post. Can it be mended? They are doing their best, says the attendant, but honestly, he would be better off buying a new one. This lesson goes on with the present perfect. It teaches yet in questions and negatives, still for something going on, three days ago, Let's, negative questions such as Isn't that ...? and Didn't you ...?, and to tell you the truth.
伍德先生来修车厂取车，问车修好了没有。服务员不知道，问他车牌号——LFZ 312 G；什么时候送来的？三天前。服务员想起来了。伍德先生问技工修完了吗？还没有，还在修。两人走进车库去看。服务员问：那不是您的车吗？伍德先生说：那「曾经」是我的车。原来他出了车祸，把车撞到了路灯柱上。能修好吗？服务员说他们正在努力修，可说实话，您得买辆新车了！本课继续现在完成时，学 yet 用在问句和否定句、still 表示「仍在进行」、three days ago、Let's、否定疑问句 Isn't that …? / Didn't you …?，以及 to tell you the truth。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. When did Mr. Wood bring his car to the garage?伍德先生什么时候把车送来的？
  2. What happened to his car?他的车出了什么事？
  3. Can the mechanics repair it?技工能修好这辆车吗？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander; New Concept English, Book 1, Longman, 1967.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为教材自撰，见《新概念英语》第一册（朗文，1967）。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

The number plate LFZ 312 G follows the British system of the late 1960s, in which a letter at the end showed the year a car was first registered; the letter G covered roughly 1968 to 1969. So the plate is a small clue to when the lesson was written or revised. Car ownership was rising fast in Britain in that decade, and with it the number of road accidents; seat belts were not yet compulsory to wear, and drink-driving had only recently become a specific offence with a legal limit. A garage then usually meant a local workshop where the owner knew his customers by face, which is why the attendant can say he remembers.

车牌 LFZ 312 G 遵循 1960 年代后期英国的规则：末尾一个字母表示车辆首次登记的年份，G 大约对应 1968 至 1969 年。所以这块车牌是判断课文写作或修订年代的一个小线索。那十年英国私家车数量增长很快，交通事故也随之增多；当时系安全带还不是强制的，酒驾也是不久前才有了法定限值、成为专门罪名。那时的修车厂通常是本地小作坊，老板认得熟客的脸，所以服务员才会说他想起来了。

✨ How it is written赏析

The lesson keeps the bad news back as long as it can. The customer asks a simple question, and the answers come slowly: first the attendant does not know, then he needs a number, then a date, then he remembers, then the work is not finished. Only when they walk in to look does the truth appear, and even then it comes in a joke: the owner uses the past tense to describe his own car. The attendant's last line is the second piece of honest bad news, softened by a polite phrase, and the exclamation mark tells the reader how much damage there really is.

课文尽可能把坏消息往后拖。顾客问了一个简单的问题，回答却来得很慢：先是服务员不知道，接着要车牌号，再要日期，然后想起来了，然后活还没干完。直到两人走进去看，真相才露出来，而且是以玩笑的方式：车主用过去时形容自己的车。服务员最后那句是第二条实话坏消息，用一句客气话缓冲了一下，句末的感叹号告诉读者车到底坏成了什么样。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

Every car owner in Canada will need this language at some point. At a repair shop, the key questions are still Is it ready yet? and Have they finished yet?, and the honest answer may still begin with to tell you the truth. One practical difference is insurance: in British Columbia most basic car insurance comes through the public insurer ICBC, and after a crash the insurer, not the owner, often decides whether a car is repaired or written off as a total loss. The word for that, written off, is worth learning. Chinese learners also often forget that ago goes only with the past simple: say I brought it three days ago, never I have brought it three days ago.

在加拿大，每个车主迟早都用得上这些说法。在修车行，关键问题依然是 Is it ready yet? 和 Have they finished yet?，而老实的回答也可能仍以 to tell you the truth 开头。一个实际的差别是保险：在不列颠哥伦比亚省，大多数基本车险由公营保险公司 ICBC 提供，出车祸后，往往是保险公司而不是车主决定这辆车是修还是按全损报废。表示报废的说法 written off 值得学。中国学习者还常忘记 ago 只能配一般过去时：要说 I brought it three days ago，不能说 I have brought it three days ago。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 3第 3 句 (begins开头 “Is my car …”)

  • yet in a question means already, and it goes at the end.yet 用在问句里：已经……了吗？放句末。

Sentence 8第 8 句 (begins开头 “I brought it …”)

  • When asks for a past time, so the past simple is used; ago always goes with the past simple.When 问具体时间，所以用一般过去时；ago 也只能配一般过去时。
  • brought is the past of bring.brought 是 bring 的过去式。

Sentence 10第 10 句 (begins开头 “Have your mechanics …”)

  • The present perfect asks whether the job is done, with yet at the end.现在完成时问「修完了没有」，yet 放句末。
  • still with the continuous means not stopped; work on means do repairs to.still + 进行时：仍在做。work on sth：在修理、加工某物。

Sentence 12第 12 句 (begins开头 “Let's go into …”)

  • Let's makes a suggestion; have a look at means look at.Let's：我们……吧。have a look at：看一看。

Sentence 14第 14 句 (begins开头 “Well, it was …”)

  • Negative questions show the speaker already thinks he knows the answer.Isn't that …? / Didn't you …?：否定疑问句，说话人心里已有答案。
  • The past tense hints that it is hardly a car any more.it was my car：用过去时暗示车已经不算车了。

Sentence 17第 17 句 (begins开头 “I drove it …”)

  • drive into means hit while driving; drove is the past of drive.drive … into：开车撞上……；drove 是 drive 的过去式。

Sentence 20第 20 句 (begins开头 “But to tell …”)

  • This phrase prepares the listener for honest, unwelcome news.to tell you the truth：说实话，放在坏消息前缓和语气。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

yet, already and still
already goes in positives, between have and the participle; yet goes at the end of questions and negatives; still shows that an action has not stopped.
already 用于肯定句，放在 have 与过去分词之间（They've already finished.）；yet 用于问句和否定句，放句末（Have they finished yet? / They haven't finished yet.）；still 表示动作仍在继续，常和进行时连用（They're still working.）。

Sentences 3 and 11 use yet, and sentence 11 uses still.

本课：第 3 句和第 10 句用 yet，第 11 句用 still。

  • Has the bus come yet? — No, not yet.
  • She's still sleeping. Don't wake her.
Negative questions: Isn't that ...? and Didn't you ...?否定疑问句：Isn't that …? / Didn't you …?
When the speaker expects yes, a negative question checks it. Answer by the facts: yes if it is true, no if it is not.
说话人以为答案是「是」时，常用否定疑问句来确认：Isn't that your car?（那不是你的车吗？）。回答按事实：是就 Yes，不是就 No。

Sentences 13 and 16.

本课：第 14、16 句。

  • Isn't she your sister? — Yes, she is.
  • Didn't you get my message? — No, I didn't.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • Is my … ready yet? — I'm not sure, sir. 我的……好了吗？——我不清楚，先生。
    Is my laptop ready yet? — I'm not sure, sir.
  • I brought it here … ago. 我……前送来的。
    I brought it here a week ago.
  • No, we're still working on it. 还没，我们还在修。
    No, she's still working on the report.
  • Isn't that your …? — Well, it was my … 那不是你的……吗？——嗯，那「曾经」是。
    Isn't that your bike? — Well, it was my bike.
  • To tell you the truth, you'd better buy a new … 说实话，你得换个新的……了。
    To tell you the truth, you'd better buy a new phone.

Mr. Wood had left his car at a garage three days earlier after crashing it into a lamp-post. When he came back, the mechanics were still busy with it. Looking at it, he admitted it had once been a car. The attendant said they were trying, but frankly he needed a new one.（53 词）
