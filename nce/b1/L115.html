🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
Jim and Helen have been asked to lunch by Carol and Tom, but nobody answers the door. The house is silent, and Helen is certain they are out, though that makes no sense after an invitation. Peering through the window shows nothing. They go round to the back and find the whole party outside, eating in the warm garden. Carol offers drinks, and Jim asks for a beer. Sorry, it has all gone, she says; how about lemonade? Tom tells Jim not to believe her: she is teasing, and there is beer after all. This lesson teaches compound pronouns such as anyone, no one, everything, nothing, everybody and something, with none left, nice and warm, let's try, and only joking.
吉姆和海伦应邀到卡罗尔和汤姆家吃午饭，敲了门却没人应。家里静悄悄的，海伦断定没人在——可这不可能，明明是人家请的。从窗户往里看，什么也看不见。绕到后门一看：大家都在花园里，都想在花园里吃午饭，外面又暖和又舒服。卡罗尔招呼他们喝点东西，吉姆要一杯啤酒。啤酒？一点也没剩了，喝柠檬水吧。柠檬水！汤姆说：别信她，吉姆，她在开玩笑，来杯啤酒吧！本课学复合不定代词：anyone、no one、everything、anything、nothing、everyone、everybody、something，以及 none left、nice and warm、Let's try …、only joking。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

Many British houses have a small front garden facing the street and a longer private garden behind, reached through a back door from the kitchen. On a warm day, friends who were invited for lunch would often find the hosts outside in the back garden, out of earshot of the front door, which explains the whole first half of the lesson. Lunch on a weekend could be a long, relaxed meal with drinks, and beer was the most common drink for a man to ask for. In Britain lemonade usually means a clear fizzy soft drink, not the pressed lemon juice of North America.

很多英国房子临街有个小小的前花园，屋后有更长的私家花园，从厨房的后门出去。天气暖和时，应邀来吃午饭的朋友常会发现主人在后花园里，听不见前门的动静，这就解释了课文的前半段。周末的午饭可以吃得很长、很悠闲，还配着饮料，男人最常要的就是啤酒。在英国，lemonade 通常指一种无色的汽水，而不是北美那种鲜榨柠檬水。

✨ How it is written赏析

The lesson is a little mystery with a cheerful answer. The first half builds a puzzle out of silence and negative words, with no one, nothing and impossible, so the reader shares the guests' confusion. The turn comes with a single Look!, and the language flips at once to everyone and everybody. The second half then sets up a smaller puzzle of its own, the missing beer, and solves it the same way, with the truth arriving from a third person. Each half moves from no to yes, and the pronouns carry that movement.

这一课是一个结局轻松的小悬案。前半段用寂静和一串否定词——no one、nothing、impossible——搭出一个谜，让读者和客人一样摸不着头脑。转折只靠一声 Look!，用词立刻翻转成 everyone 和 everybody。后半段又摆出一个更小的谜——啤酒没了——用同样的方式解开，真相由第三个人说出。两半都从「没有」走到「有」，承载这一转变的正是那些代词。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The scene feels very familiar in a Canadian summer, where barbecues and lunches in the backyard are a big part of social life, and a guest at the front door may well go unheard. Many hosts now send a text saying come round the back, which solves the problem the lesson turns into a story. For learners, the double negative is the key trap: Chinese speakers sometimes say I don't see nothing, a form that exists in some kinds of spoken English but is marked as wrong in standard English. Also notice the gentle teasing at the end; joking with guests is common among friends in Canada, and I'm only joking is the phrase that keeps it friendly.

这个场景在加拿大的夏天很熟悉：后院烧烤、后院吃午饭是社交生活的重要部分，站在前门的客人很可能没人听见。如今很多主人会发一条短信说 come round the back，课文当成故事来讲的难题就这样解决了。对学习者来说，双重否定是关键陷阱：中国人有时会说 I don't see nothing，这种说法在某些口语里存在，但在标准英语里算错。还要注意结尾那种温和的逗趣：在加拿大，朋友之间跟客人开玩笑很常见，I'm only joking 这句话让玩笑保持友好。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

Compound pronouns复合不定代词
The some words go in positive sentences and offers; the any words in questions and negatives; the no words are already negative, so the verb stays positive; the every words mean all. As subjects they all take a singular verb.
some-（something、someone、somebody）用于肯定句和提议；any-（anything、anyone）用于疑问句和否定句；no-（nothing、no one、nobody）本身就是否定，动词用肯定形式；every-（everything、everyone、everybody）表示全部。这些词作主语时动词都用单数：Everyone is、Nothing is。

The lesson goes through almost the whole set, one per line, from sentence 4 to sentence 17.

本课：第 4 句 anyone，第 6 句 everything，第 7 句 no one，第 11 句 anything，第 12 句 nothing，第 14 句 everyone，第 15 句 everybody，第 17 句 something。

  • Did you buy anything at the market?
  • Nobody was at the bus stop.
  • Everyone is waiting for you.
Two ways to say no否定的两种写法
not with an any word means the same as a no word. Since the no words are already negative, do not add another not.
not + any- = no-：I can't see anything = I can see nothing；There isn't anyone = There's no one。no- 已经是否定，不能再加 not：不说 I can't see nothing。

Sentence 7 uses no one and sentence 12 uses nothing.

本课：第 7 句用 no one，第 12 句用 nothing。

  • I didn't say anything. = I said nothing.
  • There isn't anybody here. = There's nobody here.
