📘 New Concept English · Book 1 · Lesson 135–136 “The Latest Report”新概念英语 第一册 Lesson 135–136《The Latest Report》

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
At her London hotel a reporter asks Karen Marsh again whether she will really stop acting. She says perhaps; she has not decided and must check with the man she is going to marry, who will not allow her to make more films. She then presents him, Carlos, and says the wedding is next week. Liz and Kate read the latest story in the paper, where the reporter has put all of it into reported speech. Kate points out that Carlos will be husband number six. This lesson goes on with reported speech, now with modal verbs moving back: may to might, can't to couldn't, will to would, and will have to to would have to. It also teaches let someone do and introduce one person to another.
在伦敦的酒店里，记者又问卡伦·马什是不是真要退休。她说也许吧，还拿不定主意，得问问未婚夫——他不会让她再拍电影了。她当场介绍了未婚夫卡洛斯，说两人下周结婚。莉兹和凯特在报上读到这条「最新消息」，记者把她的话全都用间接引语转述了一遍。凯特说：「他会是她第六任丈夫！」本课继续练间接引语，重点是情态动词的后移：may → might，can't → couldn't，will → would，will have to → would have to，以及 let sb do、introduce sb to sb。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. Is the actress still sure about retiring?卡伦·马什还会退休吗？
  2. Why must she check with the man she will marry?她为什么要先问未婚夫？
  3. Why does Kate find the story so remarkable?凯特最后为什么觉得这是「爆炸性新闻」？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander; New Concept English, Book 1, Longman, 1967. This lesson continues the Karen Marsh story from Lessons 127 and 133; Carlos is an invented character.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为教材自撰，见《新概念英语》第一册（朗文，1967）。本课接着第 127、133 课卡伦·马什的故事；卡洛斯是虚构人物。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

Gossip columns of the period followed the love lives of film stars week by week, and a star's new engagement often came with a promise to give up acting for marriage. The idea that a husband could forbid his wife to work was still common in the 1960s; in many countries married women needed a husband's agreement for things such as a bank loan. Readers who remembered her first interview would see at once that her plan had changed overnight.

当时的八卦专栏一周一周地追踪明星的感情生活，明星宣布订婚，常常伴着一句「为婚姻息影」的承诺。丈夫可以不许妻子工作，这种观念在 1960 年代仍很普遍；在不少国家，已婚女性办银行贷款这类事还需要丈夫同意。还记得她上一次采访的读者，一眼就能看出她的计划一夜之间变了。

✨ How it is written赏析

Lesson 133 gave the reason for retiring as tiredness; this lesson quietly gives another one, a new husband, and the reader is left to connect them, which is the answer to Kate's earlier I wonder why. Again the interview and the report sit side by side, so every modal change can be checked line by line. The final joke counts the husbands, which ties the three Karen Marsh lessons together.

第 133 课说退休的理由是累；这一课悄悄给出另一个理由——新丈夫——让读者自己把两者连起来，这正是对凯特上一课那句 I wonder why 的回答。采访和报道又一次并排，每一处情态动词的变化都可以逐行核对。最后的笑话数的是丈夫的人数，把卡伦·马什的三课串到了一起。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

When learners report what someone said, they often forget the modal changes and say She said she may come or He said he will call. Both can be heard when the event is still in the future, but the shifted forms might and would are the safe default, especially in writing and at work. The story is also dated in a useful way: in Canada today no partner can legally stop someone from working, and readers may enjoy discussing how much has changed since 1967.

学习者转述别人的话时，常忘了情态动词要变，说成 She said she may come 或 He said he will call。如果事情还在将来，这两种说法也能听到，但 might 和 would 是更稳妥的默认，尤其在书面和工作中。这个故事也「过时」得有价值：在今天的加拿大，任何伴侣在法律上都不能阻止对方工作，读者不妨讨论一下 1967 年以来变了多少。

💬 Discuss and write讨论与写作

    用 can、will、may 问同伴三个问题，再用 could、would、might 转述答案。
  2. Introduce two classmates to each other in English, giving one fact about each.
    用英语把两位同学互相介绍，各说一件关于对方的事。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

Modal verbs in reported speech间接引语中的情态动词
In reported speech modals also move back: may to might, can to could, can't to couldn't, will to would, won't to wouldn't, will have to to would have to. must can stay or become had to. could, would, might and should do not change.
转述时情态动词也往过去推：may → might，can → could，can't → couldn't，will → would，won't → wouldn't，will have to → would have to。must 可以不变或改成 had to。could、would、might、should 本身不再变。

Sentences 16 to 20 report sentences 4 to 8 in turn.

本课：第 16–19 句分别是第 4–7 句的转述。

  • 'I can swim.' → She said she could swim.
  • 'I'll call you.' → He said he would call me.
let someone do and introduce A to Blet sb do 与 introduce A to B
let plus a person plus the base verb means allow, with no to; allow needs to. introduce A to B presents A to B; for yourself, say Let me introduce myself.
let + 人 + 动词原形：让、允许某人做，不加 to；allow 则要加 to（allow sb to do）。introduce A to B：把 A 介绍给 B；介绍自己说 Let me introduce myself。

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • She said she might … 她说她可能……
    She said she might change jobs.
  • He told me he couldn't … 他跟我说他没法……
    He told me he couldn't find the address.
  • He said he would need to … 他说他得……
    He said he would need to work on Sunday.
  • Let me introduce you to … 我来把你介绍给……
    Let me introduce you to our new neighbour.
  • They're getting married in … 他们……结婚。
    They're getting married in May.

At her London hotel, Karen Marsh told the press she might stop acting, but she was undecided. She said she would need to ask the man she was going to marry, and that he would not allow more films.
