Lesson 51–52 · A Pleasant Climate 宜人的气候

📘 New Concept English · Book 1 · Lesson 51–52 “A Pleasant Climate”新概念英语 第一册 Lesson 51–52《A Pleasant Climate》

B1🧠 20 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
Someone asks a man from Greece what the climate is like at home. He says it is very pleasant and goes through the year: March is often windy; April and May are warm, with the odd shower; the summer months are hot, with sunshine every day; early autumn is still warm, November turns cold and wet at times; and the winter months are often cold, with snow now and then. This lesson is describing a climate by seasons and months. It teaches Where do you come from?, What's ... like? for asking how something is, where the frequency words always, often and sometimes go, in before months and seasons, and weather sentences such as It rains and It snows.
有人问一位希腊人：你们国家的气候怎么样？他说很宜人，然后一季一季地讲：三月常刮风，四五月总是暖和、偶尔下雨；夏天六到八月总是很热，天天出太阳；秋天九、十月还暖，十一月常冷、有时下雨；冬天十二月到二月常冷，有时下雪。本课是按季节和月份描述气候，学 Where do you come from?、What's … like? 问「怎么样」、频度副词 always / often / sometimes 的位置、月份与季节前用 in，以及 It rains / It snows 这类天气句。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. When is it hottest in Greece, and when might it snow?希腊一年里什么时候最热？什么时候可能下雪？
  2. In What's the climate like?, does like mean enjoy?What's the climate like? 里的 like 是「喜欢」吗？
  3. Which frequency words appear in sentences 8 to 19, and what does each mean?第 9 到 19 句里出现了哪几个频度副词？分别是什么意思？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander; New Concept English, Book 1, Longman, 1967.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为教材自撰，见《新概念英语》第一册（朗文，1967）。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

Greece was becoming a popular holiday place for the British in the 1960s, when cheap package tours to the Mediterranean began, and its sunny summers were part of the attraction. The description is broadly right for Athens and the lowlands: long, hot, dry summers and mild, rainy winters. Snow does fall in Greece, but mostly in the mountains and the north; in Athens it is rare, a few days in some winters and none in others. The lesson is paired with the next one, about England, so that the two climates can be set side by side.

1960 年代地中海廉价跟团游兴起，希腊成了英国人越来越喜欢的度假地，晴朗的夏天就是吸引力之一。课文的描述对雅典和低地大体准确：夏天漫长、炎热、干燥，冬天温和多雨。希腊确实下雪，但主要在山区和北部；在雅典很少见，有的冬天下几天，有的冬天一天也没有。这一课和下一课讲英国的成对编排，好让两种气候并排对照。

✨ How it is written赏析

The text is a calendar read aloud. It walks through the year in order, season by season, and nearly every line has the same frame, a frequency word, an adjective and a time phrase, so the learner can swap pieces and build sentences about any place. The writer avoids monotony by varying the frequency words, by adding a but now and then to show weather that changes, and by giving summer the only line that is not about temperature: the sun shining every day, which is the part a British reader would envy most.

这段课文像在大声念一本日历。它按顺序走过一年，一季接一季，几乎每行都是同一个框架：一个频度副词、一个形容词、一个时间短语；读者可以替换零件，造出描述任何地方的句子。作者避免单调的办法，是变换频度副词，时不时加一个 but 表现多变的天气，并且只给夏天一句不谈温度的话：天天出太阳——这正是英国读者最羡慕的那一点。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The language is current and very practical: almost every newcomer to Canada is asked where they come from and what the weather is like at home. Learners should be ready for both questions. The climate facts need a little care: averages for Greece have warmed since the 1960s, and recent summers have brought heatwaves and wildfires that the lesson's word pleasant would not cover. When you describe your own home town, the frame of this lesson works perfectly, but check the facts on a national weather service rather than from memory.

语言现行、非常实用：几乎每个来加拿大的新移民都会被问「你是哪里人」「家乡天气怎么样」，两个问题都要准备好。气候的事实要小心一点：自 1960 年代以来希腊的平均气温已经升高，近年的夏天还带来热浪和山火，课文里的「宜人」一词已经包不住了。描述你自己的家乡时，这一课的框架完全好用，但事实要去查国家气象部门的资料，别凭印象。

    比较你现在住的地方和你长大的地方的气候。你更喜欢哪一个？为什么？

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 3第 3 句 (begins开头 “Where do you …”)

  • come from in the present simple asks where someone is from, not where they have just travelled from.come from 用一般现在时，问的是「你是哪里人」，不是「你刚从哪儿来」。

Sentence 5第 5 句 (begins开头 “What's the climate …”)

  • What's ... like? asks for a description. like here is a preposition meaning similar to, not the verb enjoy.What's … like? 问「……怎么样」，like 是介词「像」，不是「喜欢」。

Sentence 8第 8 句 (begins开头 “It's often windy …”)

It's often windy in March.

  • A frequency word goes after be, as in It's often windy, but before an ordinary verb, as in It often rains.频度副词放在 be 动词之后：It's often windy；放在普通动词之前：It often rains。
  • Months take in.月份前用 in：in March。

Sentence 9第 9 句 (begins开头 “It's always warm …”)

  • Weather verbs take it as subject, with the third-person -s.it rains：天气动词以 it 作主语，第三人称加 -s。
  • sometimes can go at the start, in the middle or at the end.sometimes 位置较灵活，可放句首、句中或句末。

Sentence 12第 12 句 (begins开头 “The sun shines …”)

The sun shines every day.

  • The present simple is for regular facts. every day, two words, is the time phrase; everyday, one word, is an adjective meaning ordinary.一般现在时说经常、规律性的事；every day 是时间状语，两个词；everyday 一个词是形容词「日常的」。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

What's ... like? for asking how something isWhat's … like? 问「怎么样」
What is, then a noun, then like asks someone to describe it: the weather, a place or a person. Answer with an adjective, not with I like it.
What is + 名词 + like? 用来请人描述某物（天气、地方、人）。回答用形容词：It's warm. / It's very pleasant.。注意不要答 I like it。

Sentence 5 asks about the climate, and sentences 7 and 11 ask about spring and summer, all with this pattern.

本课：第 5 句问气候，第 8、11 句问春天和夏天的天气，都用这个句型。

  • What's your new teacher like? — She's very kind.
  • What's the food like in that restaurant? — It's excellent.
The frequency words always, often and sometimes频度副词 always / often / sometimes
From most to least: always, usually, often, sometimes, never. Put them after be and before an ordinary verb.
频率从高到低：always（总是）> usually（通常）> often（经常）> sometimes（有时）> never（从不）。放在 be 之后、普通动词之前。

Sentence 8 puts often before an adjective, sentence 9 has always and sometimes in one line, and sentence 18 has sometimes at the end.

本课：第 8 句 often + 形容词，第 9 句 always 与 sometimes 同在一句，第 18 句 sometimes 放在句末。

  • She is always happy.
  • We often go to the beach in summer.
in before months and seasons月份和季节前用 in
Use in before months, seasons and years; use on before a particular day or date.
月份、季节、年份前用 in：in March、in summer、in 2026；具体某一天用 on：on Monday、on 5 May。

Every sentence from 9 to 19 uses in with a month or season.

本课：第 8–18 句每一句都用 in 加月份或季节。

  • My birthday is in June.
  • We met on the first of June.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • Where are you from? — I'm from …. 你是哪里人？——我是……人。
    Where are you from? — I'm from Korea.
  • What's the … like in …? — It's very …. ……的……怎么样？——很……
    What's the food like in Spain? — It's very good.
  • It's often … in …. ……经常……
    It's often foggy in November.
  • It's always … in …, but it … sometimes. ……总是……，但有时……
    It's always sunny in July, but it gets windy sometimes.
  • Is it … or … in …? ……是……还是……？
    Is it hot or cool in the mountains?
