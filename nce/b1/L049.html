Lesson 49–50 · At the Butcher's 在肉店

📘 New Concept English · Book 1 · Lesson 49–50 “At the Butcher's”新概念英语 第一册 Lesson 49–50《At the Butcher's》

B1🧠 15 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
The customer, Mrs Bird, has come in to buy meat. The butcher asks whether she wants beef or lamb, and she says beef. He praises the lamb; she enjoys it herself, but her husband does not. He suggests steak instead, she picks out a piece, and she adds a pound of minced meat. Asked about a chicken, she says no thanks: her husband is fond of steak but not of chicken. The butcher then owns up that he is not fond of chicken either. This lesson is buying at a counter. It teaches Do you want A or B? questions, the third-person forms likes and doesn't like, either for a negative too, What about ...? for suggestions, and the spoken phrase To tell you the truth.
伯德太太来买肉。肉店老板问她要牛肉还是羊肉——牛肉；老板推荐羊肉，她说自己喜欢，可丈夫不喜欢。老板又推荐牛排，她挑了一块，再要一磅肉末。问她要不要鸡，她说不要：丈夫喜欢牛排，不喜欢鸡。老板笑着坦白：说实话，他自己也不喜欢鸡！本课是在柜台前买东西，学 Do you want A or B? 选择疑问句、第三人称单数 likes / doesn't like、either 表示「也不」、What about …? 推荐，以及 To tell you the truth 这句口语。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. Which meat does Mr Bird enjoy, and which two does he not?伯德先生喜欢哪种肉、不喜欢哪两种？
  2. How do you answer the question in sentence 5? Can you use yes or no?第 5 句的问句怎么回答？能用 Yes 或 No 吗？
  3. Why does the last line use either and not too?最后一句为什么用 either 而不用 too？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander; New Concept English, Book 1, Longman, 1967.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为教材自撰，见《新概念英语》第一册（朗文，1967）。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

Before supermarkets took over, most British families bought meat from a local butcher, who knew his customers by name and cut each order at the counter. A housewife shopping for the family's meals and choosing by her husband's taste is a picture of the 1960s, as is the price-free conversation: the butcher sells by recommending. The name the butcher's, with the 's, is short for the butcher's shop, and English still names many shops this way: the baker's, the chemist's, the dentist's. This lesson is where Book 1 introduces the third-person -s, and the absent husband exists to make it necessary.

在超市一统天下之前，多数英国家庭到附近的肉店买肉，肉店老板叫得出熟客的名字，每份肉都在柜台上现切。家庭主妇为全家的饭菜采购、按丈夫的口味挑肉，这是 1960 年代的画面；整段对话不谈价钱也是：老板靠推荐来卖。the butcher's 带着 's，是 the butcher's shop 的省略，英语至今还这样称呼很多店：the baker's、the chemist's、the dentist's。第一册在这一课引入第三人称单数加 -s，那位不在场的丈夫就是为此而设的。

✨ How it is written赏析

The husband never appears, yet he decides every purchase. Mrs Bird answers each suggestion by reporting his taste, so the third-person forms come naturally, and the learner hears likes and doesn't like again and again without noticing the drill. The butcher keeps offering and keeps losing, which builds a small pressure, and the last line releases it with a confession that turns the seller into an ally. It is a neat example of how a punchline can also teach a grammar point: the final either only works because everything before it was negative.

丈夫一次也没出场，却决定了每一样东西买不买。伯德太太每次回应推荐，都是转述丈夫的口味，于是第三人称的形式来得很自然，读者一遍遍听到 likes 和 doesn't like，却不觉得是在操练。老板不停推荐、不停落空，造出一点小小的压力；最后一句用一个坦白把它释放掉，让卖东西的人变成了同一阵线。这是个漂亮的例子，说明包袱也能教语法：最后那个 either 之所以成立，正是因为前面全是否定。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The grammar is exactly current, and the third-person -s is the mistake Chinese speakers make most often in English, even after years of study, because Chinese verbs never change. It is worth drilling until he likes sounds right and he like sounds wrong. The scene itself has changed: in Canada most meat is bought prepacked in supermarkets, and a wife choosing food by her husband's taste alone would now seem old-fashioned. Independent butchers do survive in many Canadian cities, though, and the counter language, a pound of this, that piece please, is still exactly what you would say there.

语法完全现行，而第三人称单数加 -s 是中国读者在英语里最常犯的错，学了很多年也会犯，因为中文动词从不变形。值得一直练到 he likes 听着顺、he like 听着刺耳为止。场景本身变了：在加拿大，多数肉是在超市买预先包装好的；妻子只按丈夫口味挑菜，如今会显得老派。不过很多加拿大城市里仍有独立的肉铺，柜台上的说法——这个来一磅、请给我那块——在那里照样用得上。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 5第 5 句 (begins开头 “Do you want …”)

  • A question with A or B cannot be answered with yes or no; you name your choice. The voice rises before or and falls after it.选择疑问句：A or B？不能用 Yes/No 回答，要直接说选哪个。or 前升调、or 后降调。

Sentence 8第 8 句 (begins开头 “I like lamb, …”)

  • The third-person negative is doesn't with the plain verb; here like lamb is left out after it.第三人称单数否定：doesn't + 动词原形，这里省掉了 like lamb。

Sentence 9第 9 句 (begins开头 “What about some …”)

  • What about with a noun makes a suggestion. In offers like this, some is used rather than any.What about + 名词？= 要不要试试……？推荐时常用；推荐时也用 some，不用 any。

Sentence 12第 12 句 (begins开头 “And a pound …”)

  • A short form for give me a pound of mince as well.这是省略句：(Give me) a pound of mince, too。

Sentence 16第 16 句 (begins开头 “My husband likes …”)

  • In the positive he likes takes -s; in the negative he doesn't like, and like has no -s.第三人称单数肯定加 -s：likes；否定用 doesn't like，like 不再加 -s。

Sentence 19第 19 句 (begins开头 “I don't like …”)

  • to tell you the truth introduces something a little surprising that you admit.to tell you the truth：说实话（坦白一件对方可能想不到的事）。
  • In a negative sentence also is either, at the end; in a positive one it is too.否定句里的「也」用 either，放句末；肯定句用 too。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

The present simple with he, she and it一般现在时第三人称单数
With he, she, it or a singular noun, the verb takes -s. In negatives and questions use does or doesn't, and the verb goes back to the plain form.
主语是 he/she/it 或单数名词时，动词加 -s：likes、wants；否定和疑问用 does / doesn't，后面的动词回到原形：He doesn't like …；Does he like …?。

Sentences 8, 17 and 18 all describe Mr Bird's taste, once in the positive and twice in the negative.

本课：第 8 句与第 17、18 句都在说伯德先生的口味，一句肯定、两句否定。

  • My father likes fishing, but my mother doesn't.
  • Does she want any bread? — No, she doesn't.
too and either for alsotoo 与 either：也
In a positive sentence also is too; in a negative sentence it is either. Both go at the end.
肯定句的「也」用 too；否定句的「也（不）」用 either，都放句末。

In the last line the butcher uses either: he shares Mr Bird's dislike.

本课：最后一句老板用 either，表示他和伯德先生一样「不喜欢」。

  • I like jazz. — I like it, too.
  • I don't eat meat. — I don't eat it either.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • Do you want … or …? — …, please. 你要……还是……？——请给我……
    Do you want rice or noodles? — Noodles, please.
  • I like …, but my … doesn't. 我喜欢……，但我的……不喜欢。
    I like spicy food, but my son doesn't.
  • What about some …? This is a nice …. 来点……怎么样？这……不错。
    What about some fish? This is a nice one.
  • Give me that …, please. And …, too. 请把那个……给我，还要……
    Give me that loaf, please. And six rolls, too.
  • To tell you the truth, I don't … either. 说实话，我也不……
    To tell you the truth, I don't understand it either.

Points a good summary covers好摘要要覆盖的要点
  • Mrs Bird chooses beef over lamb, because her husband dislikes lamb.伯德太太要牛肉，不要羊肉，因为她丈夫不喜欢羊肉。
  • She buys a piece of steak and a pound of minced meat.她买了一块牛排和一磅肉末。

Our model summary本站范文

Mrs Bird went to the butcher's and asked for beef. She likes lamb herself, but her husband does not, so she chose a piece of steak and some mince instead. She refused a chicken because he dislikes it, and the butcher admitted that he disliked it as well.（48 词）
