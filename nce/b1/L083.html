Lesson 83–84 · Going on Holiday 去度假

📘 New Concept English · Book 1 · Lesson 83–84 “Going on Holiday”新概念英语 第一册 Lesson 83–84《Going on Holiday》

B1🧠 17 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
Sam drops in again while Tom and Carol are at lunch, and they ask him to join them. No, thanks, he says; he has eaten already, at half past twelve. A coffee, then? He has just had one, after his meal. They move to the living room, and Carol apologises for the untidiness: they are packing, because tomorrow they set off on holiday. Sam says how lucky they are. When Carol asks about his own holiday, he says he has had it already this year. Where did he go? Nowhere. He spent it at home. This lesson is the first lesson on the present perfect. It teaches have or has plus the past participle, where already and just go, how the present perfect and the past simple share the work, as in I've already eaten against I ate at half past twelve, Excuse the mess, and Let's.
萨姆又来了，汤姆和卡罗尔正在吃午饭，请他一起吃。萨姆说不用了，他已经吃过了，十二点半吃的；请他喝杯咖啡，他也刚喝过一杯，饭后喝的。三人去起居室，卡罗尔请萨姆别介意屋里乱：他们在收拾行李，明天就去度假。萨姆说：你们真幸运！卡罗尔问他什么时候度假，萨姆说不知道——今年的假他已经度过了。去哪儿了？他说：待在家里！本课是现在完成时第一课，学 have / has + 过去分词，already 与 just 的位置，现在完成时与一般过去时的分工（I've already eaten 对 I ate at half past twelve），以及 Excuse the mess、Let's 的用法。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. Why doesn't Sam have lunch with them?萨姆为什么不和他们一起吃午饭？
  2. Why are Tom and Carol packing?汤姆和卡罗尔为什么在收拾行李？
  3. Where did Sam spend his holiday this year?萨姆今年的假期是在哪里过的？

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

In Britain in the 1960s, ordinary workers had begun to take one or two weeks of paid holiday a year, and a summer trip to the seaside or abroad was becoming a normal part of family life. Package holidays by plane to Spain and other sunny places grew quickly in that decade. Staying at home for the holiday was still very common, partly for reasons of money, which gives Sam's answer a gentle double meaning: a joke, and perhaps a small fact about his budget. The modern word for this, a staycation, did not exist yet; it became popular much later, around the financial crisis of 2008.

1960 年代的英国，普通工人开始每年享有一到两周带薪假期，夏天去海边或出国旅行正在成为家庭生活的常态。乘飞机去西班牙等阳光地带的打包旅游团在那十年里增长很快。假期待在家里仍然很常见，部分原因是钱，这让萨姆的回答带上了一层温和的双关：既是玩笑，也许也透露了他钱包的一点实情。现代说法 staycation（宅度假）那时还不存在，它在很久以后、大约 2008 年金融危机前后才流行起来。

✨ How it is written赏析

The lesson is built for one grammar point and repeats it in three neat pairs. Each time, Sam first answers with the present perfect, which says the result now, and then adds a sentence in the past simple with a time or place, which tells the story. The rhythm becomes so regular that the reader expects the third pair to work the same way, and it does, but the final answer turns the pattern into a joke. The middle of the lesson, with the mess and the suitcases, gives the conversation a reason to talk about holidays without feeling forced, and Sam's envy makes his last line all the more surprising.

本课为一个语法点而设计，用三组整齐的对子反复呈现它。每一次，萨姆先用现在完成时回答，说的是现在的结果；再补一句带时间或地点的一般过去时，讲的是经过。节奏规整得让读者预料第三组也会这样，果然如此，但最后的回答把这个模式变成了笑话。课文中段屋里乱、收拾行李，让对话自然地转到度假上，不显得生硬；萨姆的羡慕又让他最后那句更出人意料。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The difference between the present perfect and the past simple is one of the hardest points for Chinese learners, because Chinese uses the same verb for 'I have eaten' and 'I ate at twelve', with only a small particle to tell them apart. This lesson gives the cleanest rule: if you name a finished time, use the past; if you talk about the result now, use the present perfect. North American speakers sometimes use the past simple where British speakers use the present perfect, such as I already ate, so learners in Canada will hear both. Sam's choice also looks modern: many Canadians now spend part of their vacation at home, and it is no longer something to joke about.

现在完成时和一般过去时的区别，是中国学习者最难的点之一：中文「我吃过了」和「我十二点吃的」用同一个动词，只差一个小小的助词。本课给出了最干净的规则：说出了过去的具体时间，就用过去时；说的是现在的结果，就用现在完成时。北美人有时在英国人用现在完成时的地方用一般过去时，比如 I already ate，所以在加拿大的学习者两种都会听到。萨姆的选择也显得很现代：如今很多加拿大人把一部分假期留在家里过，这已经不是什么可笑的事了。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 7第 7 句 (begins开头 “Do you want …”)

  • The present continuous describes what is happening now; Do you want to ...? invites.现在进行时说眼前正在做的事；Do you want to …? 邀请。

Sentence 9第 9 句 (begins开头 “I've already had …”)

  • The present perfect stresses the result now: he is not hungry. already goes between have and the participle.I've already had：现在完成时，强调「现在已经吃过了，所以不吃」。already 放在 have 和过去分词之间。
  • Once an exact time is given, the past simple is used.说出具体时间（at half past twelve）就要换成一般过去时。

Sentence 12第 12 句 (begins开头 “I've just had …”)

  • just goes in the same place as already; one stands for a cup of coffee.just：刚刚，位置同 already。one 代替 a cup of coffee。

Sentence 14第 14 句 (begins开头 “Let's go into …”)

  • Let's plus the bare verb makes a suggestion that includes the speaker.Let's + 动词原形：我们……吧。

Sentence 18第 18 句 (begins开头 “We're packing our …”)

  • A polite apology for an untidy room.Excuse the mess：屋里乱，别见怪——客套话。
  • The packing is now; leaving tomorrow is the plan.正在收拾（进行时）→ 打算明天走（be going to）。

Sentence 24第 24 句 (begins开头 “I've already had …”)

  • this year is not over, so it can go with the present perfect.this year 还没过完，所以能和现在完成时连用。
  • The follow-up asks about a finished event, so it returns to the past simple.追问「去了哪里」是问过去的一个具体时间，所以换回一般过去时 did you go。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

The present perfect: have or has plus the past participle现在完成时：have / has + 过去分词
Form it with have or has plus the past participle, often shortened. Use it when a past action matters now. already and just go between have and the participle.
构成：I / you / we / they have + 过去分词，he / she / it has + 过去分词；缩写 I've、he's。用法：过去做了的事对现在有影响（I've had lunch，所以现在不饿）。already（已经）、just（刚刚）放在 have 和过去分词之间。

Sentences 9, 13 and 25.

本课：第 10、13、25 句。

  • I've just finished my homework.
  • She's already seen this film.
Present perfect or past simple?现在完成时 还是 一般过去时？
Without a finished time, and when the result matters now, use the present perfect. Once a finished time is named, or the question asks when or where, use the past simple.
不说具体过去时间、强调现在的结果，用现在完成时：I've already had lunch.；一说出具体时间（at twelve、yesterday、last week）或问「什么时候、在哪里」，就用一般过去时：I had lunch at twelve. / Where did you go?

Sentences 9 and 11, 13 and 14, and 25 and 26 each make a pair: present perfect first, then past simple.

本课：第 10 与 11 句、第 13 与 14 句、第 25 与 26 句都是一对：先完成时，后过去时。

  • I've lost my keys. I lost them on the bus this morning.
  • Have you ever been to Japan? — Yes, I went there in 2019.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • We're having … Do you want to … with us? 我们在……，你要不要一起……？
    We're having a barbecue. Do you want to join us?
  • I've already had …. I had … at … 我已经……过了，……点吃的。
    I've already had breakfast. I had it at seven.
  • I've just had …, thank you. 我刚……过，谢谢。
    I've just had a sandwich, thank you.
  • Excuse the mess. We're … 屋里乱，别见怪，我们正在……
    Excuse the mess. We're painting the kitchen.
  • I've already had my … this year. 我今年的……已经过了。
    I've already had my flu shot this year.
