Lesson 23–24 · Which Glasses 哪几只杯子？

📘 New Concept English · Book 1 · Lesson 23–24 “Which Glasses”新概念英语 第一册 Lesson 23–24《Which Glasses》

B1🧠 15 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
This is the plural form of Lesson 21. A man asks Jane for some glasses; Jane asks which, and points to some; not those, he says, he wants the glasses on the shelf, and the next ones are right. glasses is the word worth a second look here: it can mean several drinking glasses or a pair of spectacles. The drill lesson puts glass with cups and bottles, so here it means drinking glasses, although some Chinese translations give spectacles. The grammar is some with a plural noun, these and those, ones in place of a plural noun, and a phrase with a preposition after a noun, as in the ones on the shelf.
这是第 21 课的复数版：男士请简给他一些 glasses，简问：哪些？这几只吗？不是那些，是架子上的那些。这些吗？对了。glasses 是本课最值得多想一步的词：它既可以是几只玻璃杯，也可以是一副眼镜；配套操练课把 glass 和杯子、瓶子放在一起，所以这里按「玻璃杯」理解（有的中文译本作「眼镜」）。学 some + 复数名词、these / those、ones 代替复数名词，以及介词短语作定语 the ones on the shelf。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. Where are the glasses the man is after?男士要的杯子放在哪儿？
  2. Compared with Lesson 21, which word changes in each line?和第 21 课相比，本课每一句变了哪个词？
  3. What two meanings can glasses have, and how can you tell which one this is?glasses 有哪两种意思？你怎么判断这里是哪一种？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander; New Concept English, Book 1, Longman, 1967. The dialogue repeats Lesson 21 line for line in the plural.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为教材自撰，见《新概念英语》第一册（朗文，1967）。对话逐句重复第 21 课，换成复数。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

The drill lesson after it names furniture and the things that sit on it: shelves, desks, tables, cupboards, beds, plates, magazines and newspapers. That is why the answer here is a place, on the shelf, rather than a colour. Together with Lesson 21 it gives the learner two ways to pick something out: by what it looks like, the red one, and by where it is, the ones on the shelf. Those two questions, what kind and where, cover most of the pointing we do in daily life.

后面那课操练列的是家具和放在上面的东西：架子、书桌、桌子、碗柜、床、盘子、杂志、报纸。所以这里的答案是一个位置 on the shelf，而不是颜色。和第 21 课合起来，读者学到了两种指认方法：按样子（红的那个）和按位置（架子上的那些）。「什么样的」和「在哪儿」这两个问题，几乎涵盖了日常生活里所有的指认。

✨ How it is written赏析

Placing the lesson right after its singular twin is a teaching trick. A learner who knows Lesson 21 by heart already has the whole shape, and only has to change a few words: one to ones, this to these, that to those, a to some. The change is so small that it can be heard and felt, which helps the plural stick far better than a rule on a page. The single new idea, a phrase after the noun, is given only one line, and that line carries the answer.

把复数版紧接在单数版之后，是一个教学巧思。熟记第 21 课的读者已经掌握了整体结构，只需换几个词：one 换 ones，this 换 these，that 换 those，a 换 some。改动小到能听出来、能感觉到，比纸上一条规则更容易让复数记牢。唯一的新内容——名词后的短语——只占一行，而那一行恰好是答案。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The word glasses shows why one meaning per word is not enough. A learner who stores glasses only as spectacles will misread this lesson, as some translations do, and will be puzzled by a glass of water, glass doors and a wine glass. In English the plural form alone, without a pair of or my, is genuinely open to both readings, and only the setting decides. That is the habit worth building from the first book: when a common word seems not to fit, ask what other meaning it has before deciding the text is strange.

glasses 这个词说明了为什么一个词只记一个意思不够用。把 glasses 只记成「眼镜」的读者会读错这一课——有的译本就是这样——也会对 a glass of water、玻璃门、葡萄酒杯感到困惑。在英语里，不带 a pair of 或 my 的复数 glasses 确实两种理解都行，只有场景能决定。这正是从第一册起就该养成的习惯：一个常见词好像放不进句子时，先想想它还有什么别的意思，再决定是不是课文奇怪。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 4第 4 句 (begins开头 “Give me some …”)

  • some with a plural countable noun means a few, without saying exactly how many.some + 可数名词复数：「几个」，不说具体数目。
  • glasses here means drinking glasses. Spectacles are usually my glasses or a pair of glasses.glasses 在这里指玻璃杯；如果说的是眼镜，一般说 my glasses 或 a pair of glasses。

Sentence 7第 7 句 (begins开头 “No, not those. …”)

No, not those.

  • those is the plural of that. The ones near Jane are far from the man, so to him they are those.those 是 that 的复数；简手边的那几只，对男士来说是远处的 those。
  • Compare Lesson 21: not that one becomes not those.和第 21 课对照：not that one → not those。

Sentence 8第 8 句 (begins开头 “The ones on …”)

The ones on the shelf.

  • ones stands for the plural noun glasses. The singular is one, as in Lesson 21; the plural is ones.ones 代替前面的复数名词 glasses；单数用 one（第 21 课），复数用 ones。
  • on the shelf comes after the noun and says which ones. Chinese puts this kind of description before the noun; English puts a phrase with a preposition after it.on the shelf 放在名词后面作定语，说明「哪些」：架子上的那些。中文定语在前，英语介词短语在后。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

Pointing in the plural: these, those and ones复数指认：these / those + ones
this becomes these and that becomes those, and the noun after them becomes plural. To avoid repeating a plural noun, use ones: the ones on the shelf, the red ones.
this → these，that → those；后面的名词变复数。代替前面说过的复数名词用 ones：the ones on the shelf、the red ones。

Sentences 6 and 9 use these, sentence 7 uses those, and sentence 8 uses ones.

本课：第 6 句与第 9 句用 these，第 7 句用 those，第 8 句用 ones。

  • I don't like these shoes. Show me those ones, please.
  • Which cups? — The ones in the cupboard.
A phrase with a preposition after a noun介词短语放在名词后：the ones on the shelf
English says which thing by putting a phrase with a preposition after the noun: the book on the desk, the cups in the cupboard. Chinese puts the same idea before the noun.
英语用「名词 + 介词短语」说明是哪一个：the book on the desk（桌上的书）、the cups in the cupboard（柜子里的杯子）；中文的「桌上的」放在前面，英语的 on the desk 放在后面。

In sentence 8, on the shelf tells Jane which glasses.

本课：第 8 句的 on the shelf 告诉简是哪几只。

  • The magazines on the floor are old.
  • Give me the plate on the left, please.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • Give me some …, please. 请给我一些……
    Give me some plates, please.
  • Which …? These …? 哪些？这些吗？
    Which magazines? These ones?
  • Not those — the ones in/on the … 不是那些，是……里／上的那些。
    Not those — the ones in the cupboard.
  • Are these the ones? — Yes, those are the ones. 是这些吗？——对，就是那些。
    Are these the ones you need? — Yes, those are the ones.
