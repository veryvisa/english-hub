Lesson 27–28 · Mrs. Smith's Living Room 史密斯太太的客厅

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
This is the companion to Lesson 25, moving from the kitchen into the living room. The room is large. The television stands by the window with some magazines on top; there are newspapers on the table and armchairs beside it; a stereo stands by the door with books on it; and pictures hang on the wall. Unlike the last lesson, most of the things here are plural. The grammar is There are with some and a plural noun, The ... are ... with a plural subject, the prepositions near and on, and how some is used.
这是第 25 课的姊妹篇，从厨房走进客厅。客厅很大：窗边是电视机，电视上放着几本杂志；桌上有几份报纸，桌旁是几把扶手椅；门边有一台音响，音响上有几本书；墙上挂着几幅画。和上一课不同，这里大部分东西都是复数。学 There are + some + 复数名词、复数名词作主语的 The … are …、介词 near / on，以及 some 的用法。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. Where are the books?书在哪里？
  2. Which things are singular, with There is, and which are plural, with There are?哪些东西是单数，用 There is；哪些是复数，用 There are？
  3. Could you put magazines on top of a television today, and what does that tell you?今天的电视上还能放杂志吗？这说明了什么？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander; New Concept English, Book 1, Longman, 1967. It repeats the plan of Lesson 25 with plural things.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为教材自撰，见《新概念英语》第一册（朗文，1967）。它按第 25 课的结构写，换成复数的东西。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

The living room in the book is a picture of a comfortable middle-class home of its time: armchairs rather than a sofa, a stereo for records, newspapers and magazines lying about, framed pictures on the walls. The television was a deep box with a flat top, so it was natural to pile magazines on it. The drill lesson after it asks Where are they? about plural things, and the pair of lessons together gives the learner everything needed to describe any room in a home.

书里的客厅是那个年代舒适中产家庭的画像：扶手椅而不是沙发、放唱片的立体声音响、随手放着的报纸杂志、墙上镶框的画。那时的电视是个方方正正、顶上平平的大箱子，在上面摞杂志很自然。后面那课操练用 Where are they? 问复数的东西，两课合起来，读者就有了描述家里任何房间所需的全部句型。

✨ How it is written赏析

Like the kitchen passage, this one moves round the room, but it groups things in pairs. Each large piece of furniture is placed first, near the window, near the table, near the door, and then the smaller things are put on top of it. The reader builds the room in the mind in the same order the eye would see it. The only line that breaks the pattern is the last, with pictures on the wall, which lifts the eye from the floor upward and closes the scene.

和厨房那篇一样，这段也绕着房间走，但它把东西两两成组：先摆好每件大家具——窗边、桌旁、门边——再把小东西放到它上面。读者脑子里搭房间的顺序，和眼睛看到的顺序一样。唯一打破这个模式的是最后一句：墙上的画，把视线从地面抬起，场景就此收束。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The lesson is a quiet record of how quickly homes change. Today's flat-screen television has no top to put anything on, the stereo has become a small speaker or a phone, and paper newspapers and magazines are rare in many homes. If a learner in Canada describes a living room now, the likely words are couch, smart TV, coffee table and perhaps a gaming console. The grammar has not changed at all, which is a useful reminder: the structures in Book 1 last far longer than the things they describe, so it is worth learning them well and updating the nouns.

这一课不动声色地记录了家居变化得有多快。今天的平板电视顶上什么也放不了，立体声音响变成了小音箱或手机，很多家庭已经很少有纸质报刊。如果加拿大的读者现在描述客厅，用到的词多半是 couch、smart TV、coffee table，也许还有游戏机。语法却一点没变——这是个有用的提醒：第一册的句型比它们描述的东西长寿得多，值得学扎实，名词随时更新就好。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 6第 6 句 (begins开头 “There are some …”)

  • There are with some and a plural noun says that some things are in a place. There is some magazines is wrong.There are + some + 复数名词：某处有一些……。不说 There is some magazines。
  • some does not say how many; it only says there are several.some 不说具体几本，只说「有一些」。

Sentence 5第 5 句 (begins开头 “The television is …”)

  • near goes straight before the noun. near to is possible but less common.near + 名词：在……附近，near 后面直接接名词，不加 to（near to 也可以，但较少）。

Sentence 9第 9 句 (begins开头 “There are some …”)

  • in the room means inside its space. Compare on the table, on top of a surface.in the room：在房间里。比较 on the table（桌面上）与 in the room（空间里）。

Sentence 15第 15 句 (begins开头 “The pictures are …”)

  • A plural subject takes are. Pictures hanging on a wall are on it, because they touch its surface.复数主语用 are。画挂在墙上也用 on：接触墙面就是 on。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

There are with some and a plural nounThere are + some + 复数名词
For one thing use There is with a or an; for several use There are with some and a plural noun. When you mention them again, say The plus the plural noun and are.
单数用 There is + a/an + 名词；复数用 There are + some + 名词复数。再提到时用 The + 复数名词 + are …。

Sentences 6, 9, 10, 14 and 15 use There are, while sentences 4, 8 and 12 use There is.

本课：第 7、9、10、14、15 句都是 There are，第 5、8、12 句是 There is。

  • There are some cups on the shelf. The cups are clean.
  • There is a sofa by the fire. There are some lamps, too.
The prepositions near, on and in方位介词 near / on / in
near means close to but not touching; on means touching a surface, including a wall; in means inside.
near 在……附近（不接触）；on 在……上面（接触表面，包括墙面）；in 在……里面。

Sentences 5, 11 and 13 use near, and sentences 6, 9, 14 and 16 use on.

本课：第 6、11、13 句用 near，第 7、9、14、16 句用 on。

  • The lamp is near the bed.
  • The clock is on the wall, and the books are in the box.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • X's living room is large. X 的客厅很大。
    Mr Kim's living room is large and bright.
  • There are some … on/in the … ……上／里有一些……
    There are some plants in the hall.
  • The … is near the … ……在……附近。
    The bus stop is near the bank.
  • The …s are on the wall. ……都在墙上。
    The photos are on the wall.
