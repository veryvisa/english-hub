Lesson 111–112 · The Most Expensive Model 最贵的型号

📘 New Concept English · Book 1 · Lesson 111–112 “The Most Expensive Model”新概念英语 第一册 Lesson 111–112《The Most Expensive Model》

B1🧠 18 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
Mr. and Mrs. Frith are looking at televisions. They like one set, which turns out to be the dearest in the shop at five hundred pounds. They cannot manage that. The assistant shows a cheaper one at three hundred, though it is not as good. Mr. Frith wants the better set: it costs more, but it is good value. Can they pay bit by bit? Yes: thirty pounds down, then fourteen pounds monthly for three years. His wife likes the set but not the price; he always wants the best, she says, as if he were rich. His reply: rich people never pay in instalments! This lesson teaches comparatives and superlatives of longer adjectives, not as ... as, afford, be worth the money, on instalments, and a deposit of.
弗里斯夫妇在店里看电视机。他们很喜欢一台，店员说这是店里最贵的型号，五百英镑。太贵了，买不起。店员推荐一台便宜些的，只要三百英镑，当然没有贵的那台好。弗里斯先生不喜欢便宜的：另一台贵些，但值这个钱。能分期付款吗？当然：先付三十英镑定金，以后每月十四英镑，付三年。太太说：我当然喜欢，可不喜欢这价钱。你总想要最好的，可我们买不起，有时候你以为自己是百万富翁！先生回了一句：百万富翁可不分期付款！本课学多音节形容词的比较级与最高级：more expensive、less expensive than、the most expensive，以及 not as … as、afford、be worth the money、on instalments、a deposit of。

  2. How does the instalment plan work?分期付款怎么付？
  3. Why is Mrs. Frith unhappy, and how does her husband answer?太太为什么不高兴？先生怎么回答？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander; New Concept English, Book 1, Longman, 1967.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为教材自撰，见《新概念英语》第一册（朗文，1967）。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

In the 1960s a television was one of the most expensive things a British family owned, and many families rented their sets rather than buying them. Buying on instalments, called hire purchase in Britain, was the normal way to pay for a television, a washing machine or a car: a deposit first, then fixed monthly payments, with the shop or a finance company adding interest. The government controlled hire purchase closely, setting the smallest deposit and the longest time allowed. Colour television started in Britain in 1967, and colour sets were far dearer than black-and-white ones. The prices in this lesson come from a later revision and are larger than 1967 prices.

1960 年代，电视机是英国家庭最贵重的物件之一，很多人家是租电视而不是买电视。分期付款在英国叫「租购」，是买电视、洗衣机或汽车的常规付法：先付定金，再按月定额还款，商店或金融公司另收利息。政府对租购管得很严，规定最低定金和最长期限。英国 1967 年开始播彩色电视，彩电比黑白电视贵得多。本课的价钱出自后来的修订版，比 1967 年的价钱高。

✨ How it is written赏析

The numbers in the lesson do quiet work. Thirty pounds down and fourteen pounds monthly for three years add up to more than the price of the set, so a careful reader can see the real cost of buying on credit, though no one in the scene says it. The comparatives build a small argument: the dearest, the less dear, not as good, more dear but worth it. The wife's complaint uses the best and a millionaire as weapons, and the husband wins with a joke that uses her own word, which is the oldest trick in a married quarrel.

课文里的数字在悄悄干活。三十英镑定金加上每月十四英镑、付三年，加起来超过电视的标价，细心的读者能看出分期付款的真实代价，虽然场景里没有一个人说出来。比较级搭起一场小小的争论：最贵的、没那么贵的、没那么好的、更贵但值得的。太太的抱怨拿 the best 和 a millionaire 当武器，先生则用她自己的词开个玩笑扳回一局，这是夫妻拌嘴里最古老的招数。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The arithmetic is the most useful lesson here, and it is exactly the trap that catches many families in Canada today. Buy now, pay later offers, store credit cards and phone plans spread a price over months or years, and the total often ends up much higher than the sticker price. Before signing, work out the total: deposit plus the monthly payment times the number of months, and compare it with the cash price. In this lesson the total is five hundred and thirty-four pounds for a five-hundred-pound set. For grammar, remember that afford almost always comes with can or can't, and that prices are high or low rather than expensive or cheap.

这道算术题是本课最有用的一课，也正是今天困住很多加拿大家庭的陷阱。「先买后付」、商店信用卡、手机套餐把一个价钱摊到几个月甚至几年，总额常常比标价高出不少。签字之前算一算总数：首付加上月供乘以月数，再和现金价比一比。本课里，一台标价五百英镑的电视，总共要付五百三十四英镑。语法上要记住，afford 几乎总和 can 或 can't 连用；价格说 high 或 low，而不说 expensive 或 cheap。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

Comparing with longer adjectives多音节形容词的比较
Most adjectives of two or more syllables take more and most. To show a smaller degree, use less and least. good and bad are irregular: better and best, worse and worst.
两个及以上音节的大多数形容词用 more / most：expensive → more expensive → the most expensive；表示「较少」用 less / least：less expensive、the least expensive。不规则：good → better → the best；bad → worse → the worst。

Sentences 5, 10, 14 and 21 run through the most, less, more and the best.

本课：第 5 句 the most expensive，第 9 句 less expensive than，第 13 句 more expensive，第 20 句 the best。

  • This course is more difficult than the last one.
  • That was the worst film I've ever seen.
not as ... as and less ... thannot as … as 与 less … than
A is not as good as B means the same as A is less good than B, or B is better than A. The adjective between as and as stays in its plain form.
A is not as + 形容词原级 + as B = A is less + 形容词 + than B = B is more … than A。as … as 中间用原级。

Sentence 11 uses not as ... as.

本课：第 11 句用 not as … as。

  • The train is not as fast as the plane.
  • My new phone is less heavy than my old one.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • It's the most … in the shop. 这是店里最……的。
    It's the most popular phone in the shop.
  • That's too … for us. We can't afford it. 对我们来说太……了，我们买不起。
    That's too dear for us. We can't afford it.
  • This one is less … than that one. 这个没有那个那么……
    This one is less noisy than that one.
  • It costs more, but it's worth it. 它贵一些，但很值。
    The leather bag costs more, but it's worth it.
  • You can pay … down and … a month. 先付……，然后每月付……
    You can pay fifty dollars down and twenty a month.
