Lesson 85–86 · Paris in the Spring 《巴黎之春》

📘 New Concept English · Book 1 · Lesson 85–86 “Paris in the Spring”新概念英语 第一册 Lesson 85–86《Paris in the Spring》

B1🧠 16 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
George runs into Ken and asks whether he has just come out of the cinema. He has. What was showing? A film called Paris in the Spring. George has seen it already, on television last year; it is old but very good. Paris is a lovely city, he says, though he has never visited it. Has Ken? Yes, in April. Paris in springtime, says George. It was spring, Ken replies, but the weather was dreadful, and it rained without stopping. George's answer: much like home in London. This lesson goes on with the present perfect. It teaches have been to, ever and never, Have you just been ...?, switching between the present perfect and the past simple, What's on?, all the time, and Just like ...!
乔治碰见肯，问他是不是刚看完电影。是的。在演什么？《巴黎之春》。乔治说他已经看过了，去年在电视上看的，是部老片子，但很好看。他说巴黎是座美丽的城市，可惜他从没去过；问肯去过没有——去过，四月去的。乔治说：春天的巴黎啊！肯说：是春天，可天气糟透了，一直下雨。乔治说：跟伦敦一样！本课继续现在完成时，学 have been to（去过）、ever 与 never、Have you just been …?，现在完成时与一般过去时的切换，What's on?，以及 all the time、Just like …!

  1. When and where did George see the film?乔治什么时候、在哪里看过这部电影？
  2. Has George been to Paris? Has Ken?乔治去过巴黎吗？肯呢？
  3. When in the year did Ken go to Paris, and what was the weather like?肯是一年中什么时候去巴黎的？天气怎样？

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

In 1960s Britain, going to the cinema was still a weekly habit for many people, even as television spread into most homes and began to show older films. That is the small social change behind George's line: he has seen at home, for free, the film Ken has just paid to see. Paris in the spring was already a famous romantic idea, the subject of popular songs, and it stood for sunshine and love. London, by contrast, had a reputation for grey skies and drizzle. In fact the two cities have fairly similar climates, and London's yearly rainfall is close to that of Paris, so the joke rests on reputation more than on figures.

1960 年代的英国，看电影对许多人来说仍是每周的习惯，而电视已进入大多数家庭，并开始播放老电影。这就是乔治那句话背后的小小社会变迁：肯刚花钱看的片子，乔治在家免费看过了。「春天的巴黎」那时已是有名的浪漫意象，流行歌曲里常唱，代表阳光与爱情。伦敦则以灰天和毛毛雨出名。其实两座城市气候相当接近，伦敦的年降雨量和巴黎差不多，所以这个笑话靠的是名声，而不是数字。

✨ How it is written赏析

The conversation builds a dream and then lets the weather wash it away. George supplies all the romance: the film is good, the city is beautiful, and Paris in the spring sounds perfect. Ken supplies all the facts, in short answers, and each fact is a little flatter than the dream. The turning point is a sentence with but, which accepts George's word spring and then takes away everything it promised. George has the last word, and his joke turns the disappointment back on the speakers themselves, two Londoners who know rain very well. The grammar follows the same path, from the present perfect of experience to the past simple of what actually happened.

这段对话先建起一个梦，再让天气把它冲掉。浪漫全由乔治提供：电影好看，城市美丽，春天的巴黎听上去完美。事实全由肯提供，用的是简短的回答，每一个事实都比梦想平淡一点。转折点是一个带 but 的句子：先接受乔治说的「春天」，再把它所承诺的一切拿走。乔治说了最后一句，他的玩笑把失望转回到说话人自己身上——两个对下雨再熟悉不过的伦敦人。语法也走同一条路：从表示经历的现在完成时，走到讲实际发生了什么的一般过去时。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The pattern Have you ever been to ...? is one of the most useful in daily English, and in Canada it opens many conversations about travel, food and home towns. Chinese learners often say Have you ever gone to ...?, which sounds wrong, or use the past simple too early, such as Did you ever go to Paris?, which is possible in North America but less common in questions about life experience. The idea behind the joke is still true too: places sold to us by films and songs often look different on a wet Tuesday. Vancouver, sometimes called Raincouver, gets its own share of jokes like George's.

Have you ever been to ...? 是日常英语里最有用的句型之一，在加拿大，它开启了许多关于旅行、食物和家乡的谈话。中国学习者常说 Have you ever gone to ...?，听起来不对；或者过早地用一般过去时，比如 Did you ever go to Paris?，这在北美也能说，但问人生经历时没那么常见。笑话背后的道理也依然成立：电影和歌曲卖给我们的地方，在一个下雨的星期二往往是另一副样子。温哥华有时被戏称为「雨哥华」，也没少挨乔治这样的玩笑。

💬 Discuss and write讨论与写作
  1. Ask a partner five Have you ever ...? questions, and for each yes answer, ask one follow-up in the past simple.
    问同伴五个 Have you ever ...? 的问题；对方答「是」的，就用一般过去时追问一个细节。
  2. Write about a place that was very different from what you expected. What did you expect, and what was it really like?
    写一个和你预期大不一样的地方：你原本以为怎样，实际又怎样？

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 6第 6 句 (begins开头 “Have you just …”)

  • have been to means gone and come back; just means a moment ago.have been to：去过（人已经回来了）。just：刚刚。
  • The short answer repeats only the helping verb.简答 Yes, I have.，只重复助动词。

Sentence 8第 8 句 (begins开头 “What's on? …”)

  • What's on? asks what is showing; on here means being shown.What's on?：在演什么？on 是副词「上演」。

Sentence 11第 11 句 (begins开头 “I saw it …”)

  • The present perfect first, then the past simple once the time is given.先完成时说「看过」，再一般过去时说「何时何地」。
  • Say on television, with on.on television：在电视上，介词用 on。

Sentence 12第 12 句 (begins开头 “It's an old …”)

  • but joins a weak point and a strong one.but 连接一个缺点和一个优点。

Sentence 14第 14 句 (begins开头 “I've never been …”)

  • never means not at any time, ever is for questions, and there takes no to.never：从未；ever：曾经（问句）。been there 里 there 前不加 to。
  • With a time given, the past simple is used.I was there in April：说了时间，换一般过去时。

Sentence 20第 20 句 (begins开头 “It rained all …”)

  • all the time means without stopping.all the time：一直，总是。
  • Just like ...! means exactly as in; London is famous for rain, so George is joking.Just like …!：跟……一模一样！伦敦以多雨出名，这是乔治的玩笑。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

have been to, ever and never
have been to means you went and came back, while have gone to means the person is still there. Ask about experience with ever; never is already negative.
have been to + 地点：去过（已回来）；have gone to：去了（还没回来）。问经历用 Have you ever been to …?；答 Yes, I have. / No, I haven't. / No, never.；陈述 I've never been to …。never 本身是否定，不再加 not。

Sentences 6, 14 and 15.

本课：第 6、14、15 句。

  • Have you ever been to Montreal?
  • I've never been on a plane.
Present perfect for experience, past simple for details完成时问经历，过去时讲细节
Use the present perfect to ask or say whether something has happened at all. As soon as you give when, where or how, switch to the past simple.
先用现在完成时问或答「有没有过」，一旦谈到何时、何地、怎样，就换一般过去时：Have you been to Paris? — Yes, I have. I went there in April. It rained.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • Have you just been to …? — Yes, I have. 你刚去了……吗？——是的。
    Has she just been to a concert? — Yes, she has.
  • What's on? 在演什么？
    What's on at the movie theatre tonight?
  • I've already seen that one. I saw it … 我已经看过了，……看的。
    I've already seen that one. I saw it at a friend's house.
  • I've never been to …. Have you ever been? 我从没去过……，你去过吗？
    I've never been to Whistler. Have you ever been?
  • It was …, but the weather was terrible. 是……，可天气糟透了。
    It was summer, but the weather was terrible.

Ken had just come from the cinema, where he had seen an old film about Paris. George had already watched it on television. George had never visited Paris, but Ken had been there in April. It was spring, Ken said, but it rained every day. George laughed that it sounded just like London.（53 词）
