Lesson 41–42 · Penny's Bag 彭妮的包

📘 New Concept English · Book 1 · Lesson 41–42 “Penny's Bag”新概念英语 第一册 Lesson 41–42《Penny's Bag》

B1🧠 21 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
Sam asks whether the bag is heavy; Penny says not really and tells him to set it down on a chair. He asks what is inside, and she lists the shopping: some cheese, bread, soap and chocolate, milk, sugar, coffee and tea, each in its own amount, and finally tobacco. Sam asks if that last item is for him, and Penny says it is certainly not for her. This lesson is a shopping list. It teaches how English counts uncountable nouns with a unit word in front: a piece of, a loaf of, a bar of, a bottle of, a tin of, and for weight a pound, half a pound and a quarter of a pound. It also shows the short reply Not very.
萨姆问彭妮包沉不沉，她说不太沉，让他放到椅子上。萨姆问里面有什么，彭妮一样一样报出来：一块奶酪、一条面包、一块肥皂、一块巧克力、一瓶牛奶、一磅糖、半磅咖啡、四分之一磅茶叶，还有一听烟丝。萨姆问烟丝是不是给他的，彭妮说反正不是给她的。本课是一张购物清单，学不可数名词怎么用「量词」来数：a piece of、a loaf of、a bar of、a bottle of、a tin of，重量 a pound、half a pound、a quarter of a pound；还有 Not very. 这种省略回答。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. Who is the tobacco for, and how do you know?那听烟丝是给谁的？你怎么知道？
  2. How many things are in the bag, and which are counted by weight?包里一共有几样东西？哪几样是按重量算的？
  3. In sentence 4, what is Not very short for?第 4 句 Not very. 是什么的省略？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander; New Concept English, Book 1, Longman, 1967.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为教材自撰，见《新概念英语》第一册（朗文，1967）。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

The bag holds a typical British weekly shop of the 1960s, and its units belong to that world. Britain still weighed groceries in pounds and ounces then; one pound is about 454 grams, so a quarter-pound packet of tea is a little over one hundred grams, a normal packet. Tea and coffee were sold loose by weight, and tobacco came in small tins for men who rolled their own cigarettes or smoked a pipe. Canada went metric in the 1970s, but in practice many Canadian supermarkets still show prices per pound beside the price per kilogram, and people ask for meat or cheese by the pound at the counter.

包里装的是 1960 年代英国一个家庭典型的一周采购，量词也属于那个世界。当时英国买菜还按磅和盎司称；一磅约 454 克，所以四分之一磅茶叶是一百多克，正好是一小包。茶和咖啡按重量散卖，烟丝装在小铁盒里，卖给自己卷烟或抽烟斗的男人。加拿大在 1970 年代改用公制，但实际上很多加拿大超市在每公斤价格旁边仍然标每磅价格，人们在熟食柜台买肉和奶酪也常按磅要。

✨ How it is written赏析

The dialogue is almost all list, but the frame around it gives the list a point. It opens with a small kindness, Sam offering to take the weight, and closes with a joke that only works because of what came before. The list itself is ordered roughly from solid to liquid to weighed goods, so each new unit word arrives with a little contrast. The last item is held back and given its own line with And, which marks it as different, and Penny's dry answer tells us, without saying so, who smokes in this house.

这段对话几乎全是清单，但外面那层框让清单有了意思。开头是一个小小的体贴——萨姆要帮她拎——结尾是一句只有接着前文才成立的玩笑。清单本身大致按「块状、液体、称重」排列，所以每个新量词出现时都带着一点对比。最后一样被留到最后、用 And 单独起一行，表明它和前面不一样；彭妮那句冷冷的回答，不说破地告诉我们这个家里谁抽烟。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The unit words are still exactly right and are among the most useful things in Book 1, because Chinese uses measure words too, but not the same ones; learners need to memorise loaf, bar and slice rather than translate. The pound is worth knowing in Canada, where you will see it on shop labels and hear it at the deli. Tobacco, of course, has changed its place in life: a casual tin in the weekly shopping is now unusual, and in Canada tobacco must be sold out of sight behind the counter with large health warnings. The joke still works, but a modern version would probably buy something else.

这些量词今天仍然完全正确，而且是第一册里最有用的内容之一：中文也有量词，但不是同一套，所以 loaf、bar、slice 要记下来，不能靠翻译。磅在加拿大值得认得，你会在商品标签上看到它，在熟食柜台听到它。烟草在生活中的位置当然变了：每周采购里随手带一盒烟丝，如今已不寻常；在加拿大，烟草必须放在柜台后面、不让顾客看到，还要印大幅健康警示。玩笑依然好笑，但现代版本大概会换成别的东西。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 3第 3 句 (begins开头 “Is that bag …”)

  • Not very stands for It is not very heavy. Keeping only the key words is natural in speech.Not very. = It is not very heavy. 回答时只留关键词，口语很自然。

Sentence 8第 8 句 (begins开头 “A piece of …”)

  • cheese, bread and soap are uncountable, so a unit word goes in front instead of a.cheese、bread、soap 是不可数名词，不能说 a cheese、a bread，要用量词：a piece of / a loaf of / a bar of。
  • Each one has its usual partner: loaf for bread, bar for soap and chocolate.每种东西有习惯搭配：面包用 loaf，肥皂和巧克力用 bar。

Sentence 14第 14 句 (begins开头 “Half a pound …”)

  • half goes before a: half a pound. a half pound is heard in American speech.half 直接放在 a 前面：half a pound，不说 a half pound of（美式偶见）。
  • With quarter you need a quarter of a pound, and neither a can be left out.quarter 要说 a quarter of a pound：前后两个 a 都不能少。

Sentence 18第 18 句 (begins开头 “Well, it's certainly …”)

  • certainly not means definitely not. Penny doesn't smoke, so she is telling him it is for him.certainly not：肯定不是；彭妮不抽烟，言下之意烟丝是给萨姆的。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

Counting uncountable nouns with a unit word and of不可数名词怎么数：量词 + of
Uncountable nouns such as bread, milk, sugar and soap take neither a nor -s. To count them, use a with a unit word and of: a bottle of milk. The unit word can be plural: two loaves of bread, three bottles of milk.
不可数名词（bread、milk、sugar、soap…）不加 a、不加 -s，要数就用「a + 量词 + of + 名词」。量词可以变复数：two loaves of bread、three bottles of milk。

Sentences 8 to 17 all use this pattern, each with a different unit word.

本课：第 8–16 句九行全是这个格式，量词各不相同。

  • Can I have a glass of water?
  • She bought two bars of chocolate and a bottle of juice.
Weights and fractions重量与分数：a pound / half a pound / a quarter of a pound
Say a pound for one, half a pound for a half, with half before a, and a quarter of a pound for one fourth; each is followed by of and the noun.
一磅说 a pound；一半说 half a pound（half 在 a 前）；四分之一说 a quarter of a pound；三者后面都要先加介词、再接名词。

Sentences 13 to 16 go down from a whole pound to half and then a quarter.

本课：第 13–15 句依次是一磅糖、半磅咖啡、四分之一磅茶叶。

  • I need half a kilo of rice.
  • We waited for a quarter of an hour.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • Is that … heavy? — Not very. 那个……重吗？——不太重。
    Is the box heavy? — Not very.
  • Put it on this …. 把它放在这个……上。
    Put it on the counter.
  • What's in it? — A … of …. 里面有什么？——一……的……
    What's in it? — A jar of honey.
  • Is that … for me? 那个……是给我的吗？
    Is that coffee for me?
  • Well, it's certainly not for …! 嗯，肯定不是给……的！
    Well, it's certainly not for the cat!
