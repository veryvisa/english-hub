🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
Mrs Jones calls Amy in, asks for the door to be shut, and says the bedroom is very untidy. Amy asks what needs doing, and Mrs Jones gives the jobs one by one: open the window and let fresh air in, put the clothes away in the wardrobe, straighten the bed, wipe the dust off the dressing table, and last of all sweep the floor. This lesson is a string of instructions, and almost every line begins with a verb. The grammar is the imperative, the modal must and the question What must I do?, then for order, and the fixed phrases put ... in ..., make the bed and air the room.
琼斯太太叫艾米进来、把门关上，说这间卧室太乱了。艾米问她该做什么，琼斯太太一条一条地交代：开窗透透气，把衣服放进衣柜，铺床，掸梳妆台的灰，最后扫地。本课是一连串的指令，几乎每句都以动词开头。学祈使句、情态动词 must 和 What must I do?、表示先后的 then，以及 put … in …、make the bed、air the room 这几个固定搭配。

  2. How many jobs does Mrs Jones give, and in what order?琼斯太太一共交代了几件事？按顺序说出来。
  3. In sentence 7, is air a noun or a verb, and what does it mean?第 7 句的 air 是名词还是动词？什么意思？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander; New Concept English, Book 1, Longman, 1967. The scene is a household of its time, with Mrs Jones giving orders to Amy, who works in the house.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为教材自撰，见《新概念英语》第一册（朗文，1967）。场景是那个年代的家庭：琼斯太太给在家里干活的艾米交代任务。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

In Britain in the 1960s it was still common for a better-off household to have a daily help, a cleaner who came in to do the housework. That is almost certainly Amy's role here, which is why the instructions are given so plainly. The drill lesson after it uses the same imperatives with many objects, open, shut, put, dust and sweep, so that the learner can give or understand instructions about almost any household job.

在 1960 年代的英国，家境好一些的家庭雇一个日间帮工——上门做家务的清洁工——还很常见。艾米在这里几乎可以肯定就是这个身份，所以指令下得这么直白。后面那课操练把同样的祈使句用在很多东西上：开、关、放、掸、扫，让读者能下达或听懂几乎任何家务的指令。

✨ How it is written赏析

The passage is built like a list on a fridge door. After a short opening that sets the scene, a messy room and a question, every line is one job, and then marks each step. The order is sensible, too: fresh air first, clothes away so the bed can be made, then dusting from the top before sweeping the floor, so that the dust falls where the broom will find it. Anyone who has cleaned a room will recognise that this is the right order, which makes the lesson quietly practical.

这段文字像贴在冰箱门上的一张清单。开头几句交代场景——一间乱糟糟的屋子和一个问题——之后每行一件活，用 then 标出每一步。顺序也很讲究：先通风，再收衣服好铺床，然后从高处掸灰，最后扫地，让灰尘正好落在扫帚够得着的地方。打扫过房间的人都知道这是对的顺序，所以这一课不声不响地很实用。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The grammar is right, but the tone needs care today. A string of bare imperatives and must sounds bossy in modern English, especially to someone who is not your child. At work in Canada a manager is more likely to say Could you open the window? or Would you mind putting the clothes away? and to say thanks after each job. Must is also less common in speech than have to, which sounds less personal. Learners should know the plain forms for reading and for giving instructions in writing, such as recipes and manuals, and use the softer forms when speaking to people.

语法没问题，但今天的语气要小心。一串光秃秃的祈使句加 must，在现代英语里听起来很强势，尤其对方不是你的孩子时。在加拿大的职场，经理更可能说 Could you open the window? 或 Would you mind putting the clothes away?，每做完一件还会道谢。口语里 must 也不如 have to 常用，后者听起来没那么针对个人。读者要掌握直白的形式，用于阅读和书面指令，比如菜谱和说明书；跟人说话时则用更柔和的说法。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 4第 4 句 (begins开头 “Shut the door, …”)

Shut the door, please.

  • An imperative with please is a polite request, the usual way to speak to a helper or a child at home.祈使句加 please 是礼貌的请求；对家里的帮手或孩子都常这样说。

Sentence 5第 5 句 (begins开头 “This bedroom's very …”)

This bedroom's very untidy.

  • bedroom's is short for bedroom is.
  • un- before an adjective gives the opposite: tidy becomes untidy, happy becomes unhappy.un- 加在形容词前表示相反：tidy → untidy，happy → unhappy。

Sentence 6第 6 句 (begins开头 “What must I …”)

  • must is a modal verb and is followed by the plain verb. In a question it comes before the subject: What must I do?must 是情态动词，后接动词原形；问句把 must 提到主语前：What must I do?。
  • must never changes its form, so she must, never she musts.must 没有人称变化：I must、she must，不说 she musts。

Sentence 7第 7 句 (begins开头 “Open the window …”)

  • Two imperatives joined by and mean one action after another.两个祈使句用 and 连起来，表示一个动作接一个。
  • air is a verb here: to let fresh air into the room.air 在这里是动词：让房间通风透气。

Sentence 8第 8 句 (begins开头 “Then put these …”)

  • then at the start of a sentence gives the next step in order.then 放句首表示「然后」，说明顺序。
  • put with a thing and then in or on with a place means to move it there.put + 物 + in/on + 地点：把……放进／放到……。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

Imperatives and then: giving instructions step by step祈使句与 then：一步一步地交代
To give jobs, use imperatives: the plain verb at the start, no subject. Use then for the next step, and and to join two actions that follow each other.
交代任务用祈使句：动词原形开头、无主语；用 then 表示下一步，用 and 把两个连续动作接起来。

Sentences 7 to 12 are all imperatives, and sentences 8, 10 and 12 begin with then.

本课：第 7 句到第 11 句全是祈使句，其中第 9、10、12 句用 then 开头。

  • Wash the cups, then dry them.
  • Turn off the lights and lock the door.
The modal verb must情态动词 must
must with the plain verb means it is necessary; it is the same for every person. Questions put must before the subject; the negative mustn't means it is not allowed.
must + 动词原形，表示「必须」；所有人称形式都一样。问句：must + 主语 + 动词？；否定 mustn't 表示「不许」。

In sentence 6 Amy asks with What must I do?

本课：第 6 句艾米用 What must I do? 问自己该做什么。

  • You must be home by ten.
  • Must I wear a tie? / You mustn't smoke here.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • Come in, …. Shut the door, please. 进来吧，……，请把门关上。
    Come in, Ben. Shut the door, please.
  • This … is very untidy. 这个……真乱。
    This kitchen is very untidy.
  • What must I do? 我该做什么？
    What must I do first?
  • Open the … and air the room. 打开……，给房间通通风。
    Open the back door and air the kitchen.
  • Put these … in/on the … 把这些……放进／放到……
    Put these plates in the cupboard.
