Lesson 91–92 · Poor Ian 可怜的伊恩！

📘 New Concept English · Book 1 · Lesson 91–92 “Poor Ian”新概念英语 第一册 Lesson 91–92《Poor Ian》

B1🧠 16 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
Two neighbours are talking about Ian. He sold his house last week, but he has not left yet; the move is set for tomorrow afternoon. The woman says she will miss him, because he has always been a kind neighbour, and the new owners arrive two days from now. She asks Jenny to pass on her good wishes. The last line gives the twist: it was not Ian who wanted to go, it was his wife. This lesson teaches the future with will and be going to, together with yet, still, the day after tomorrow, give somebody my regards, and short answers such as his wife did.
两个邻居在聊伊恩：他的房子上周已经卖掉了，可人还没搬走，明天下午才搬。邻居说会想他，他一向是个好邻居；新住户后天搬进来。她请詹妮见到伊恩时代为问候。最后一句点题：伊恩其实不想走，想走的是他太太。本课学一般将来时 will / shall 与 be going to，以及 yet、still、the day after tomorrow、give sb my regards 和省略回答 his wife did。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. Has Ian's house been sold, and when is he moving?伊恩的房子卖掉了吗？他什么时候搬家？
  2. When are the new people coming?新住户什么时候搬进来？
  3. Who really wanted to sell?是谁想卖房子？

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

This lesson picks up straight after the house sale in Lesson 89, though here the seller is called Ian. In Britain a sale usually goes through in two steps: contracts are exchanged, and some weeks later comes completion, when the money moves and the keys change hands. So it was quite normal for a house to be sold while the seller was still living in it, with a fixed moving day already agreed. The new family moving in two days later fits that pattern. Neighbours in a British street of that time often knew each other for decades, which is why the speaker treats Ian's departure as a real loss.

本课紧接第 89 课的卖房故事，只是卖家在这里叫伊恩。在英国，卖房通常分两步：先交换合同，几周后才完成交割，钱到账、钥匙易手。所以房子已经卖掉、卖家却还住着，搬家日早已约定，是很平常的事。新住户两天后搬进来，也正合这个流程。那个年代英国一条街上的邻居常常一住几十年，所以说话人把伊恩搬走当作一件真正的损失。

✨ How it is written赏析

The dialogue runs almost entirely on time words, and each one is placed so the learner meets it in a natural question. Past, present and future are laid out in order: the sale is finished, the man is still here, the move is set. Then the talk turns from facts to feelings, and the present perfect with always sums up years of friendship in a single short line. The ending works like a punch line in a joke: the speaker feels sorry for Ian, and the other neighbour corrects her with four words and a short verb that stands for a whole idea. The lesson teaches that helper-verb trick by making it the joke.

整段对话几乎全靠时间词推动，每个时间词都放在一个自然的问句里让学习者碰上。过去、现在、将来按顺序摆开：房子卖了，人还在，搬家定了。然后话题从事实转到感受，一句带 always 的现在完成时就把多年的交情概括了。结尾像笑话的包袱：说话人同情伊恩，另一个邻居用几个词纠正她，其中一个短助动词代替了一整个意思。课文让这个助动词用法本身成了笑点。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The final joke depends on an old idea that the wife pushes the family's big decisions while the husband goes along, the same idea as the closing line of Lesson 89. Today it reads as a dated stereotype, and many readers will smile at it rather than with it. The grammar point behind it is still very useful: English avoids repeating a verb phrase by using do, does or did, so I don't like coffee, but my sister does is far more natural than repeating like coffee. Chinese learners tend to repeat the whole verb, because Chinese often does. For newcomers in Canada, the phrase give him my regards is a little formal; in everyday speech people more often say say hi to him for me.

结尾的笑话靠的是一个老观念：家里的大事由妻子推动、丈夫随着，和第 89 课最后一句是同一个意思。今天读来是过时的刻板印象，很多读者会觉得是在笑它，而不是跟着笑。背后的语法点却仍很有用：英语用 do、does、did 避免重复动词短语，所以 I don't like coffee, but my sister does 比重复 like coffee 自然得多。中国学习者往往会把整个动词再说一遍，因为中文常常这样说。对在加拿大的新移民来说，give him my regards 稍显正式，日常更常说 say hi to him for me。

💬 Discuss and write讨论与写作
  1. Tell a partner about a neighbour or classmate who moved away. What did you miss about them? Use has always been and will miss.
    跟同伴讲一个搬走的邻居或同学：你想念他哪一点？用上 has always been 和 will miss。
  2. Write three pairs of sentences like I didn't want to go, but my brother did.
    仿照 I didn't want to go, but my brother did 写三组句子。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 4第 4 句 (begins开头 “Has Ian sold …”)

  • In a question, yet asks whether something we expect has happened.yet 用于疑问句，问期待中的事「已经」发生没有。
  • Once a past time is named, the answer switches to the past simple.回答说具体时间（last week）就换成一般过去时。

Sentence 10第 10 句 (begins开头 “He's going to …”)

  • not yet means it has not happened so far; still means the old state goes on.not yet：还没有。still：仍然在。
  • be going to gives a plan already decided.be going to：已经安排好的打算。

Sentence 14第 14 句 (begins开头 “He has always …”)

  • The present perfect with always covers the whole time up to now.has always been：一直以来都是（从过去到现在）。
  • will gives a feeling or a prediction about later.I'll miss him：一般将来时，说话时的感受与预料。

Sentence 18第 18 句 (begins开头 “I think that …”)

  • move into needs a place after it; move in stands alone.move into + 地点；move in 后面不带宾语。
  • two days from today.the day after tomorrow：后天。

Sentence 21第 21 句 (begins开头 “Please give him …”)

  • Ask someone to pass on your good wishes.give sb my regards：代我问候某人。
  • The short answer keeps the helper verb will.Will you …? — Yes, I will.：简略回答保留助动词。

Sentence 23第 23 句 (begins开头 “No, he didn't …”)

  • did stands for the whole verb phrase already used, so it means wanted to leave.his wife did = his wife wanted to leave，用 did 代替前面的整个动词短语。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

will and be going towill 与 be going to
Use will for a prediction, a promise, or something decided at the moment of speaking. Use be going to for a plan made earlier. The negative of will is won't.
will + 动词原形：预料、说话时才决定的事、承诺（I'll miss him / I'll phone you）。be going to + 动词原形：事先打算好的事（He's going to move tomorrow）。否定 won't = will not；疑问 Will you …?

Sentence 10 is a plan already fixed; sentences 13 and 16 are feelings about what is coming.

本课：第 10 句是已定的搬家计划；第 13、16 句是对未来的感受。

  • I think it will rain this evening.
  • We're going to visit Victoria next weekend.
yet, still and already
already goes in positive sentences, yet in questions and negatives, and still shows that a state is continuing.
already 用于肯定句（已经）；yet 用于疑问句（已经……了吗）和否定句（还没）；still 表示状态还在继续（仍然）。

Sentences 4 and 7 ask with yet, sentence 8 answers with not yet, and sentence 9 uses still.

本课：第 4 句和第 7 句用 yet 提问，第 8 句用 not yet 回答，第 9 句用 still。

  • Have you paid the bill yet?
  • She has already booked the tickets.
  • Are you still working at the bank?

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • Have you … yet? — No, not yet. 你……了吗？——还没有。
    Have you found a flat yet? — No, not yet.
  • X is going to … tomorrow afternoon. X 打算明天下午……
    Lucy is going to fly home tomorrow afternoon.
  • X has always been a good … X 一直都是个好……
    Mr. Wong has always been a good teacher.
  • We'll all miss … 我们都会想念……
    We'll all miss our old school.
  • Please say hello to … for me. 请代我向……问好。
    Please say hello to your sister for me.

Our model summary本站范文

Ian sold his house a week ago, but he hasn't moved out. He is leaving tomorrow afternoon, and the new family arrives two days from now. His neighbour will be sorry to see him go, because he is a kind man. Ian himself didn't want to go; his wife did.（50 词）
