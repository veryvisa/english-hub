Lesson 73–74 · The Way to King Street 去国王街的路

📘 New Concept English · Book 1 · Lesson 73–74 “The Way to King Street”新概念英语 第一册 Lesson 73–74《The Way to King Street》

B1🧠 20 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
Last week Mrs. Mills paid a visit to London. The city was strange to her, and she got lost. Then she noticed a man standing by a bus stop and decided to ask him for directions. She asked him, very politely, how to get to King Street. He gave her a friendly smile, but it turned out that he had no English at all: he was a German visitor. He reached into his pocket, pulled out a little book of useful sentences, looked up the right one, and read it out carefully. It said that he was sorry, he could not speak English. This lesson is a story about asking the wrong person the way. It teaches the past simple for telling a story, with regular and irregular forms such as went, lost, saw and took, asking directions with Can you tell me how to get to ...?, say to oneself, know a place very well, and adverbs such as slowly and pleasantly.
上周米尔斯太太去了伦敦。她对伦敦不熟，迷了路。忽然她看见公共汽车站旁有个男人，心想：可以问问他。她很客气地问去国王街怎么走。那人愉快地笑了笑——原来他不懂英语，他说德语，是个游客。他把手伸进口袋，掏出一本常用语手册，翻到一句，慢慢念出来：「对不起，我不会说英语。」本课是问路却问错了人的小故事，学一般过去时的叙事（规则与不规则过去式 went、lost、saw、took）、问路句 Can you tell me how to get to …?、say to oneself、know … very well 和副词 slowly、pleasantly。

  2. Why couldn't the man help her?那个男人为什么帮不了她？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander; New Concept English, Book 1, Longman, 1967.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为教材自撰，见《新概念英语》第一册（朗文，1967）。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

London in the 1960s was already full of foreign visitors, and getting lost among its old, winding streets was a common experience for anyone from outside the city. There was no satellite navigation and no phone map; people asked strangers, studied a folding street map, or bought a pocket guide. A phrasebook was the tourist's main tool for a foreign language, a small book of ready-made sentences arranged by situation, such as at the hotel or in the restaurant. Many places in Britain do have a street called King Street, so the name is realistic, though the lesson does not point to a real one.

1960 年代的伦敦已经满是外国游客，老街弯弯曲曲，外地人迷路是常事。那时没有卫星导航，也没有手机地图；人们问路人，研究折叠街道图，或者买一本口袋指南。常用语手册是游客应付外语的主要工具：一本按场景编排的小书，里面是现成的句子，比如在旅馆、在餐馆。英国很多地方确实有叫 King Street 的街，所以这个名字很真实，不过课文并没有指向某条真实的街道。

✨ How it is written赏析

The story is built like a joke with a long set-up and a quick punch line. Every step makes the reader expect help: the woman is lost, she sees a man, she plans what to say, she asks politely, and he smiles. Then four short sentences in a row take the hope away. The last part is told slowly on purpose, with the hand, the pocket, the book, the page and the careful reading, so that the final line lands with full force. The humour depends on a contradiction that everyone can see: the only English sentence the man knows is the one that says he knows none.

故事的结构像一个笑话：铺垫很长，包袱很快。每一步都让读者期待有人帮忙：她迷路了，她看见一个男人，她想好要说什么，她客气地问，他笑了。然后连续四个短句把希望拿走。最后一段故意讲得很慢——手、口袋、书、那一页、仔细地念——让最后一句以全部的力量落地。幽默来自一个人人都看得见的矛盾：他会说的唯一一句英语，就是说自己不会英语的那句。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The situation is still familiar, even though a phone would now solve Mrs. Mills's problem in seconds. In Vancouver and Toronto, a visitor who asks for directions may easily meet someone who speaks only Mandarin, Punjabi or Spanish, and the polite reaction is the one the German tourist shows: a smile and an honest attempt. For Chinese learners the story carries a useful lesson about fixed phrases. A learner who memorises a few complete sentences, as a phrasebook does, can say them well, but cannot answer the next question, so it is worth learning patterns that can be changed, not only fixed lines.

这个情境至今仍然熟悉，尽管今天一部手机几秒钟就能解决米尔斯太太的问题。在温哥华和多伦多，问路的人很可能碰上只会说普通话、旁遮普语或西班牙语的人，而礼貌的反应正是那位德国游客做的：一个微笑，一次真诚的尝试。对中国学习者来说，这个故事还有一个关于固定句子的有用道理：像常用语手册那样背几句完整的话，能说得很好，却回答不了下一个问题；所以值得学能替换的句型，而不只是背死句子。

💬 Discuss and write讨论与写作

    用 turn left、turn right、go straight on 给同伴指路：从你家到最近的公交站或车站。
  2. Write about a time you could not understand someone, or they could not understand you. What did you both do?
    写一次你听不懂别人、或别人听不懂你的经历。你们各自怎么做的？

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 4第 4 句 (begins开头 “She does not …”)

  • The present is used because she still does not know the city.know … very well：很熟悉某地。现在时，因为她现在还是不熟。
  • lose your way means to get lost; lost is the past of lose.lose one's way：迷路；lost 是 lose 的过去式。

Sentence 6第 6 句 (begins开头 “'I can ask …”)

  • ask takes two objects here, the person and the thing; say to oneself means to think it.ask sb the way：向某人问路（双宾语）。say to oneself：心里想，自言自语。

Sentence 8第 8 句 (begins开头 “'Can you tell …”)

  • Excuse me comes before a request; this question is the standard way to ask directions.Excuse me：打扰别人前用。问路也可以说 Can you tell me how to get to …?

Sentence 10第 10 句 (begins开头 “He did not …”)

  • The adverb describes the verb; did not plus the bare verb is the past negative.smiled pleasantly：副词修饰动词。did not + 动词原形：过去时否定。

Sentence 13第 13 句 (begins开头 “Then he put …”)

  • put into means place inside; take out means remove, and took is its past.put … into：放进；take out：拿出（took 是过去式）。

Sentence 17第 17 句 (begins开头 “'I do not …”)

  • The past of read is spelt the same but pronounced like red.read 的过去式拼写不变，读音变成 /red/。
  • The joke: he says in English that he does not speak English.笑点：他用英语说自己不会英语。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

The past simple for stories: regular and irregular verbs一般过去时讲故事：规则与不规则动词
Regular verbs add -ed. Irregular verbs must be learnt one by one, and some, like put and read, are spelt the same in the past.
规则动词加 -ed（smiled、opened）；不规则动词要记：go → went，lose → lost，see → saw，say → said，speak → spoke，put → put，take → took，find → found，read → read。

Sentences 3 to 6 and 14 to 16 have an irregular past form in almost every line.

本课：第 3–5 句和第 13–15 句几乎每句都有一个不规则过去式。

  • We went to the beach and swam in the sea.
  • She found her keys in the car.
Asking the way问路：Can you tell me how to get to …?
Start with Excuse me, then use one of these questions. Answers often use go straight on, turn left or right, and it's on the left.
礼貌问路：Excuse me. Can you tell me how to get to …, please? / How do I get to …? / Is there a … near here?；回答常用 Go straight on、Turn left / right、It's on the left。

Sentences 7 and 9 make one complete request for directions.

本课：第 7–8 句是一句完整的问路。

  • Excuse me. How do I get to the library?
  • Is there a pharmacy near here?

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • X does not know … very well, and she … X 对……不太熟，结果……
    Ken doesn't know Toronto very well, and he took the wrong train.
  • 'I can …,' she said to herself. 她心想：「我可以……」
    'I can take a taxi,' she said to herself.
  • Excuse me. Can you tell me how to get to …? 劳驾，去……怎么走？
    Excuse me. Can you tell me how to get to Granville Island?
  • She put her hand into her …, and took out … 她把手伸进……，掏出……
    She put her hand into her bag and took out a map.
