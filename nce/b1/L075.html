Lesson 75–76 · Uncomfortable Shoes 不舒适的鞋子

📘 New Concept English · Book 1 · Lesson 75–76 “Uncomfortable Shoes”新概念英语 第一册 Lesson 75–76《Uncomfortable Shoes》

B1🧠 18 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
A woman comes into a shoe shop holding a pair of shoes and asks if they have any of the same kind. The assistant asks for her size and colour: five, in black. He is sorry, but there are none. She explains that her sister bought this pair only a month ago; not here, it turns out, but in America. The shop did stock them a month back, he says, but has sold out and cannot order more, because that style was popular two years running and has now gone out. The new style is something else. She looks at it and says it seems painful to wear. It is, he agrees; but women always choose painful shoes. This lesson is shopping for a style that has gone out. It teaches the past simple with ago, last month and the year before last, like these and like those, in fashion, What size? and What colour?, and look plus an adjective against be plus an adjective.
一位女士拿着一双鞋进了鞋店，问有没有这样的。店员问尺码、颜色——五号，黑色。店员说抱歉，没有了。女士说她姐姐上个月就买了这双；店员问是在这儿买的吗？不是，是在美国买的。店员说一个月前还有，现在一双也没有了，也进不到货：这种鞋前年、去年流行，今年不流行了，现在流行的是另一种。女士一看：看上去很不舒服。店员说：确实很不舒服——可女人总是穿不舒服的鞋！本课是在鞋店买过时的款式，学一般过去时与 ago、last month、the year before last，like these / those，in fashion，What size / colour?，以及 look + 形容词 和 be + 形容词 的对照。

  1. What size and colour does the woman want?女士要什么尺码、什么颜色的鞋？
  2. Why does the shop have none, and why can't it get any?为什么店里没有这种鞋，也进不到？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander; New Concept English, Book 1, Longman, 1967.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为教材自撰，见《新概念英语》第一册（朗文，1967）。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

The 1960s were a decade of fast-changing fashion in Britain, and shoes were part of it: pointed toes, high narrow heels and then very different shapes came and went within a few years. A small shop could not keep every style in stock, and once a line was no longer made, there was simply no way to get more. The sister buying her pair in America also says something about the time, when American fashion often reached Britain a little later. Shoe sizes are a real trap for travellers even today: a British size five is roughly a North American women's size seven, so the same number does not mean the same shoe.

1960 年代是英国时尚变化很快的十年，鞋子也在其中：尖头、细高跟，然后是完全不同的鞋型，几年之内来了又走。小鞋店不可能每种款式都有货，一旦某款停产，就根本没办法再进。姐姐在美国买鞋这一点，也透露出那个时代的情形：美国的时尚往往要晚一点才到英国。鞋码至今仍是旅行者的大陷阱：英国女鞋五号大约相当于北美女鞋七号，同一个数字不是同一双鞋。

✨ How it is written赏析

The dialogue moves from simple questions to a small lesson on fashion. It opens with short practical exchanges, size and colour, and then turns on the word But, as the customer offers proof that the shoes exist. The assistant's answers move through time in order, from a month ago back across two whole years, and then to now, which is a neat way of practising past and present side by side. The ending works through repetition: the customer says the shoes look uncomfortable, the assistant changes one verb to say they are, and then turns the whole conversation into a general comment about women. That final line is a joke typical of its time.

对话从简单的问题走到一堂关于时尚的小课。开头是简短实用的问答：尺码、颜色；然后在一个 But 上转折，顾客拿出证据说这种鞋确实存在。店员的回答按时间顺序推进：一个月前、去年和前年，然后是现在，正好把过去时和现在时放在一起练。结尾靠重复起作用：顾客说鞋看上去不舒服，店员只换一个动词说它确实不舒服，然后把整段对话变成一句对女性的笼统评论。最后那句是那个时代典型的玩笑。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The grammar is timeless, but the final joke has not aged well. Saying that women always wear uncomfortable shoes treats a choice as a fixed habit of half the population, and many readers today would find it a little rude coming from a shop assistant. It is also less true than it was: comfortable trainers and flat shoes are now high fashion in Canada, and many workplaces no longer expect high heels. Chinese readers may recognise another part of the story easily, since buying something abroad because it cannot be found at home is still very common. The practical point is the size trap, which learners should check before buying shoes in a new country.

语法是不过时的，但结尾那个玩笑不太经得起时间。说「女人总穿不舒服的鞋」，是把一种选择当成一半人口的固定习惯；今天很多读者会觉得这话出自店员之口有点失礼。而且它也没以前那么真了：在加拿大，舒服的运动鞋和平底鞋如今是潮流，很多工作场所也不再要求穿高跟鞋。中国读者可能很容易认出故事的另一部分：因为国内买不到而在国外买东西，至今仍很常见。实用的一点是鞋码陷阱，到新国家买鞋前要先查清。

  • like these points to the shoes she is holding; shop assistants often cut questions down to two words.like these：像这样的（指手里的鞋，近）。What size / colour? 店员问话常省略成两个词。

Sentence 10第 10 句 (begins开头 “But my sister …”)

  • bought is the past of buy; this pair is singular.bought 是 buy 的过去式。this pair：这双，pair 用单数。

Sentence 13第 13 句 (begins开头 “We had some …”)

  • like those refers to the shoes in the woman's hand, away from the speaker; ago follows the length of time.like those：像那样的（店员指顾客手里的鞋，离他远）。a month ago：一个月前。
  • had for the past, don't have for now.had（过去）对 don't have（现在）。

Sentence 15第 15 句 (begins开头 “I'm afraid that …”)

  • I'm afraid softens a refusal.I'm afraid (that) …：恐怕……，委婉地拒绝。

Sentence 16第 16 句 (begins开头 “They were in …”)

  • in fashion means popular now; the year before last is two years ago.in fashion：流行。the year before last：前年。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

Past time: ago, last and the ... before last过去的时间：ago、last、the … before last
Use a length of time plus ago, last plus a time word, or the year before last. None of them take in or on, and the verb is in the past simple.
一段时间 + ago：a month ago、two days ago；last + 时间：last month、last year；the year / week before last：前年 / 上上周。这些说法前面都不加 in / on，句子用一般过去时。

Sentence 10 says when the sister bought her pair, sentence 13 when the shop had them, and sentence 16 the two years they were popular.

本课：第 10 句说姐姐买鞋的时间，第 13 句说店里有货的时间，第 16 句说流行的两年。

  • I saw that film two weeks ago.
  • We bought this sofa the year before last.
look plus an adjective and be plus an adjectivelook + 形容词 与 be + 形容词
look plus an adjective gives an impression, and be plus an adjective states a fact. After look, use an adjective, not an adverb.
look + 形容词说「看上去如何」，是印象；be + 形容词说「是如何」，是事实。look 后面用形容词，不用副词：look uncomfortable，不说 look uncomfortably。

Sentence 19 is the customer's impression and sentence 20 is the assistant's confirmation.

本课：第 19 句是顾客的印象，第 20 句是店员的确认。

  • This cake looks delicious, and it is delicious!
  • He looks young, but he is forty.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • Do you have any … like these? 有这样的……吗？
    Do you have any T-shirts like this one?
  • We had a few … a … ago, but there are none left now. 一……前还有……，现在没有了。
    We had a few winter boots a week ago, but there are none left now.
  • They were in fashion last …, but … 去……还流行，可……
    Big phones were in fashion last year, but now people want small ones.
  • They look …. They are …. 看着……，确实……
    These chairs look hard. They are hard.

Summary writing摘要写作

In no more than 60 words, say why the woman cannot buy the shoes she wants, and what the assistant says.

用不超过 60 个词说说这位女士为什么买不到她要的鞋，店员怎么说。

Our model summary本站范文

A woman wanted size-five black shoes like the pair her sister had bought in America. The shop had sold that style a month earlier but had none left and could not order more, because the style had gone out. The new style looked painful, and the assistant agreed, adding that women always chose painful shoes.（55 词）
