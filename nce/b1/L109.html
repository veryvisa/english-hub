Lesson 109–110 · A Good Idea 一个好主意

📘 New Concept English · Book 1 · Lesson 109–110 “A Good Idea”新概念英语 第一册 Lesson 109–110《A Good Idea》

B1🧠 19 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
Charlotte offers to make coffee, and Jane agrees it is a good plan. Milk? A drop. Sugar, two spoons? Not so many: she settles on one and a half. Jane enjoys it and has a second cup, and then she would like to smoke. Charlotte thinks there are some left in a box, but the box turns out to be empty. Never mind, says Charlotte: have a biscuit, and cut down on smoking while you are at it. Jane thinks that is sound advice. This lesson teaches talking about amounts: a little, less, enough, a few, some and any, more and less, with Shall I ...?, It doesn't matter, What a pity, and instead.
夏洛特问简要不要煮点咖啡，简说好主意。咖啡好了：要牛奶吗？一点点。糖呢，两小勺？比那少些，一勺半就够了。喝完简说很好喝，还要一杯；她还想抽支烟，夏洛特说盒子里大概还有几支——打开一看是空的。真遗憾！没关系，夏洛特说，吃块饼干吧：多吃点，少抽点！简说这可是好建议。本课学不可数名词的量：a little、less than、enough、a few、some / any、more 与 less，以及 Shall I …?、Would you like some more?、It doesn't matter、What a pity!、instead。

  2. What else does Jane want, and why can't she have it?简想要什么？为什么没得到？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander; New Concept English, Book 1, Longman, 1967.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为教材自撰，见《新概念英语》第一册（朗文，1967）。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

In 1960s Britain smoking was everywhere: in homes, offices, buses and cinemas, and offering a guest a cigarette from a box on the table was as polite as offering a drink. Well over half of British men and a large share of women smoked. Medical reports in the early 1960s had linked smoking with lung cancer, but the habit was still socially normal, which is why the line about smoking less comes as light advice and not a warning. Coffee was gaining on tea in British homes, often made from instant powder, and taking milk and sugar was the usual way to drink it.

1960 年代的英国，吸烟无处不在：家里、办公室、公交车、电影院；从桌上的烟盒里给客人递一支烟，和递一杯饮料一样是礼数。英国男性中远超一半吸烟，女性吸烟的比例也很高。1960 年代初的医学报告已经把吸烟与肺癌联系起来，但这个习惯在社交上仍属正常，所以「少抽点」那句话只是轻松的劝告，而不是警告。咖啡在英国家庭里正追赶茶，常用速溶粉冲，加奶加糖是通常的喝法。

✨ How it is written赏析

The dialogue is a little catalogue of quantities, but it never feels like a list, because each amount is a real choice at a real coffee table. The hostess keeps offering and the guest keeps measuring, smaller and smaller, until enough closes the matter. Then the direction reverses: more coffee, and something extra. The empty box is the turning point, and the ending turns a small disappointment into a joke and a lesson at the same time, using the two words the whole scene has been teaching.

这段对话是一份小小的数量目录，却从不像在列清单，因为每个量都是咖啡桌旁一个真实的选择。主人不停地提议，客人不停地掂量，越要越少，直到一句「够了」收住。然后方向反转：再来一杯，还要点别的。空烟盒是转折点，结尾把一个小小的失望同时变成了玩笑和教训，用的正是整场一直在教的那两个词。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The advice at the end has aged well, but the social world around it has changed completely. In British Columbia smoking is banned in workplaces, restaurants, bars and on patios, and within a set distance of doors and windows, and offering a cigarette to a guest at home would now be unusual. Smoking rates in Canada have fallen to around one adult in ten. The grammar, on the other hand, is as useful as ever: a little versus a few is a classic trap for Chinese speakers, because Chinese uses one word for both. Remember also that advice is uncountable: say some advice or a piece of advice, never an advice or advices.

结尾的劝告经得起时间考验，但它周围的社会已经全变了。在不列颠哥伦比亚省，工作场所、餐厅、酒吧和露台都禁烟，门窗一定距离内也不许吸烟，在家给客人递烟如今会显得很少见。加拿大成年人的吸烟率已降到大约十分之一。语法却和从前一样有用：a little 和 a few 是中国人的经典陷阱，因为中文用一个词「一点、几个」就能概括。还要记住 advice 不可数：说 some advice 或 a piece of advice，绝不说 an advice 或 advices。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

Amounts with countable and uncountable nouns可数与不可数名词的量
With uncountable nouns such as milk, sugar and advice, use a little, much and less. With plural countable nouns such as cigarettes and biscuits, use a few, many and fewer. Both take enough, some and any. some goes in positive sentences and offers; any goes in negatives and ordinary questions.
不可数名词（milk、sugar、coffee、advice）：a little、much、less、enough、some / any。可数名词复数（cigarettes、biscuits）：a few、many、fewer、enough、some / any。some 用于肯定句和提议（Would you like some …?），any 用于否定句和一般疑问句。

Sentences 6, 8, 9, 11 and 20 show any, a little, some, less and a few in turn.

本课：第 6 句用 any 提问，第 7 句 a little，第 8 句 some，第 10 句 less，第 19 句 a few。

  • There's a little juice left, but only a few cookies.
  • Would you like some tea? — No, thanks. I don't want any.
more and less; less and fewermore 与 less，less 与 fewer
more works with both kinds of noun. less is for uncountable nouns and fewer for plural nouns. Both more and less can also follow a verb, as in eat more and smoke less. In careful writing, use fewer with plural nouns.
more：更多（可数不可数都行）；less：更少（不可数）；fewer：更少（可数复数）。也可作副词修饰动词：eat more、smoke less、sleep more。口语里常有人把 less 用于可数名词，但书面语要用 fewer。

Sentence 24 puts more and less after two verbs.

本课：第 24 句把 more 和 less 放在动词后面。

  • You should drink more water and eat less salt.
  • There are fewer students in the class this year.
