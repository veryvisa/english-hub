Lesson 127–128 · A Famous Actress 著名女演员

📘 New Concept English · Book 1 · Lesson 127–128 “A Famous Actress”新概念英语 第一册 Lesson 127–128《A Famous Actress》

B1🧠 19 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
Liz and Kate spot a film star, Karen Marsh, in the street, and decide the man next to her is the actor Conrad Reeves. Is he husband number three? No, more likely number four or five. They agree she looks old: the papers give her age as twenty-nine, but she is surely over forty, since she was already a star in Kate's school days. Liz says that was ages ago. Kate protests that it was not, because she herself is only twenty-nine. This is a gossipy talk between two women that teaches must be and can't be for guessing, negative questions, exclamations shaped like questions, tag questions, and at least and not more than.
莉兹和凯特在街上认出了女演员卡伦·马什，旁边那人应该是演员康拉德·里夫斯——是她第三任丈夫？不，大概是第四或第五任了。两人议论卡伦看上去很老：报上说她二十九岁，可她起码四十了，凯特上学时她就已经出名。莉兹说那可是很久以前的事了吧，凯特赶紧说：「也没那么久！我自己也不过二十九。」本课是两个女人的八卦对话，学must be / can't be 表推测、否定疑问句 Isn't he …?、感叹式 Doesn't she look …!、反意疑问句与 at least、not more than。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. Whom do the two women spot?她们认出了谁？
  2. Why do they doubt the actress's age?她们为什么觉得卡伦不止二十九岁？
  3. Where is the joke at the end?结尾的笑点在哪里？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander; New Concept English, Book 1, Longman, 1967. Karen Marsh and Conrad Reeves are invented names; the same actress comes back in Lessons 133 and 135.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为教材自撰，见《新概念英语》第一册（朗文，1967）。卡伦·马什和康拉德·里夫斯是虚构人物，这位女演员在第 133、135 课还会出场。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

In the 1960s film stars from Hollywood and British studios were followed closely by the popular press, and marriages and divorces were front-page news. Several famous actresses of the period married five, six or even more times, which made the number of husbands a running joke. Stars were also known for giving a younger age to reporters, so readers treated a printed age with doubt.

1960 年代，好莱坞和英国制片厂的电影明星被大众小报紧紧追踪，结婚离婚都是头版新闻。当时有几位大明星结过五次、六次甚至更多次婚，「第几任丈夫」成了一个老笑话。明星也常对记者少报年龄，所以读者看到报上印的年龄都要打个问号。

✨ How it is written赏析

The scene is built on guesses, so the modal verbs do the work: must be when the women feel sure, can't be when the idea seems impossible. The gossip moves from who to how many husbands to how old, and each step gives a new chance to guess. The final line turns the joke on Kate herself: the reader now does the same sum about her that she did about the actress.

这一幕建立在一连串推测上，所以情态动词在干活：觉得有把握就 must be，觉得不可能就 can't be。八卦从「是谁」到「第几任丈夫」再到「多大年纪」，每一步都给出新的推测机会。最后一句把笑话转回到凯特身上：读者用她算女演员年龄的方法，回过头来算她自己。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

Chinese uses one word for both must do and must be, so learners often say mustn't be when they mean can't be; in English only can't be means impossible. Negative questions are another trap: when a Canadian asks Aren't you coming?, answer by the facts, Yes, I am or No, I'm not, not the Chinese way of agreeing with the question. Age is a sensitive topic in North America; guessing a woman's age aloud, as these two do, would be thought rude, which is part of why the scene is funny.

中文的「一定」既管「必须做」也管「一定是」，所以学习者常把「不可能」说成 mustn't be；英语里只有 can't be 是「不可能」。否定疑问句是另一个坑：加拿大人问 Aren't you coming? 时，按事实答 Yes, I am 或 No, I'm not，不要按中文习惯去附和问句。在北美，年龄是敏感话题，像这两人那样当众猜一位女士的年龄会被认为很失礼，这也是这一幕好笑的原因之一。

  • the actress tells us who she is.the actress 是同位语，说明她是谁。

Sentence 10第 10 句 (begins开头 “It can't be. …”)

  • can't be is the opposite of must be.can't be：不可能是，是 must be 的反面。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

Guessing with must be and can't be推测：must be / can't be
To make a confident guess about now, use must be for yes and can't be for no. When you are less sure, use may be or might be. This must is not about rules.
对现在的情况有把握地推测：肯定用 must be（一定是），否定用 can't be（不可能是）。没那么有把握用 may be / might be（可能是）。这里的 must 不是「必须」。

Sentences 5, 9, 16 and 19 use must be; sentence 10 uses can't be.

本课：第 6、9、16、19 句用 must be，第 10 句用 can't be。

  • The lights are off. They must be out.
  • That can't be Tom — he's in Toronto this week.
Negative questions and exclamations否定疑问句与感叹
A negative question such as Isn't he or Don't you shows the speaker expects yes. Answer by the facts: Yes if true, No if not. With an exclamation mark, the same shape is not a question but a strong comment.
Isn't he …? / Don't you …? 是否定疑问句，说话人心里认为答案是「是」，想让对方确认。回答按事实：是就 Yes，不是就 No。句末用感叹号的 Doesn't she look old! 不是问题，是感叹「她看上去真老啊」。

Sentence 13 is a negative question; sentence 16 is an exclamation.

本课：第 13 句是否定疑问，第 16 句是感叹。

  • Isn't that your brother over there?
  • Isn't this view amazing!

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • It must be … 那一定是……
    It must be the new manager.
  • That can't be right. 那不可能对。
    That can't be right — I paid already.
  • Isn't she your …? 她不是你的……吗？
    Isn't she your cousin from Ottawa?
  • He must be at least … 他起码有……
    He must be at least two metres tall.
  • I'm not more than … 我不超过……
    I'm not more than five minutes away.

Liz and Kate spotted Karen Marsh, a well-known actress, and the actor Conrad Reeves beside her. They thought he was her fourth or fifth husband. The papers said she was twenty-nine, but they were sure she was over forty, because she was a star when Kate was a schoolgirl. Then Kate claimed that was recent: she was twenty-nine herself!（59 词）
