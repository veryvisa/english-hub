Lesson 37–38 · Making a Bookcase 做书架

📘 New Concept English · Book 1 · Lesson 37–38 “Making a Bookcase”新概念英语 第一册 Lesson 37–38《Making a Bookcase》

B1🧠 15 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
Dan finds George hard at work and asks what he is up to. George is building a set of shelves for books and wants the hammer; Dan offers the wrong one, and George says he needs the large one. Dan then asks what comes next. George plans to paint it, and pink at that. Dan is surprised, so George explains that the shelves are not for himself but for his daughter Susan, whose best-loved colour is pink. This lesson is talk while working. The grammar is be going to for plans, Which ...? with this one, that one and the big one, What colour ...?, and for with a person to say who something is meant for.
丹看见乔治干得正起劲，问他在做什么。乔治在做一个书架，让丹递锤子；丹拿错了一把，乔治说要那把大的。丹又问接下来打算做什么——刷漆，刷成粉红色。丹很吃惊，乔治解释说书架不是给自己的，是给女儿苏珊的，粉红色是她最喜欢的颜色。本课是边干活边聊天，学 be going to 表示打算、Which …? 与 this one / that one / the big one 的指代、What colour …?，以及 for + 人「给某人的」。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. What is George making, and what will he do with it next?乔治在做什么？做好以后打算怎么处理？
  2. In sentences 7 to 11, what does one stand for?第 8 到 11 句里的 one 指的是什么？
  3. Why is Dan surprised, and how does George explain?丹为什么吃惊？乔治怎么解释？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander; New Concept English, Book 1, Longman, 1967.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为教材自撰，见《新概念英语》第一册（朗文，1967）。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

Making furniture at home was an ordinary hobby in post-war Britain, when new furniture was dear and do-it-yourself magazines sold well; a father building shelves for his child is a very plausible weekend scene. The lesson is where Book 1 first brings in be going to, and it is taught at its most useful: answering a question about what you plan to do at the next step of a job. The pronoun one comes in the same lesson because a workshop is full of objects that are alike, so the question which one is natural there.

在战后英国，自己在家做家具是很普通的爱好——那时新家具贵，教人自己动手的杂志卖得很好；一个父亲周末给孩子做书架，是很真实的场景。第一册在这一课第一次引入 be going to，而且是从最有用的地方教起：干活时被问「接下来打算做什么」。代词 one 也在这一课出现，因为工作间里满是相似的东西，「哪一个」本来就是很自然的问题。

✨ How it is written赏析

The lesson has a small joke built into its shape. All the early lines are practical and short, a hammer, which one, the big one, and the reader expects the talk to stay about tools. Then the colour question gets a one-word answer, Dan repeats it with an exclamation mark, and for a moment it seems George has odd taste. The last three lines turn it round: the shelves are a gift, and the colour belongs to someone else. It is a tiny story with a setup, a surprise and an explanation, which is what keeps a drill lesson from feeling like one.

这一课在结构里藏了一个小玩笑。前面几句都实打实、短短的——锤子、哪一把、大的那把——读者以为话题会一直围着工具转。接着颜色的问题得到一个词的回答，丹带着感叹号重复了一遍，一时间好像乔治的品味有点怪。最后三句把它翻过来：书架是礼物，颜色属于另一个人。这是一个有铺垫、有意外、有解释的迷你故事，正是它让一节操练课读起来不像操练。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The grammar is fully current. Two points are worth adding for learners in Canada. First, spelling: Canadian English usually keeps colour and favourite with a u, as the lesson does, while American English drops it, so both spellings will appear around you. Second, the line about pink carries an old assumption, that a girl's colour is pink. Many parents today avoid it on purpose, and in a modern version George might simply say Susan chose it. The language does not change, but it is a good example of how textbooks carry the habits of their time.

语法完全没过时。对在加拿大的读者有两点值得补充。第一是拼写：加拿大英语一般像课文一样保留 colour、favourite 里的 u，美国英语则去掉，所以身边两种拼法都会见到。第二，关于粉红色的那句话带着一个旧观念：女孩的颜色就是粉红。今天很多父母刻意避开这一点，放在现代版本里，乔治也许只会说那是苏珊自己选的。语言本身不变，但这是一个好例子，说明教科书会带着它那个时代的习惯。

💬 Discuss and write讨论与写作

    跟同伴说说你这个周末打算做或做的一件东西，并回答对方 Which ...? 和 What colour ...? 的提问。

    写一段商店里的短对话：一个人总拿错东西，另一个人说 not that one。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 3第 3 句 (begins开头 “You're working hard, …”)

You're working hard, George.

  • A friendly remark in the present continuous on seeing someone busy, with a touch of praise. hard here is an adverb meaning with effort, not hardly.看到对方正忙，用进行时打招呼，带一点称赞；hard 在这里是副词「努力地」，不是 hardly。

Sentence 7第 7 句 (begins开头 “Which hammer? …”)

Which hammer? This one?

  • which asks for a choice from a few things, and one stands in for hammer so the word is not repeated.which 让对方在几样东西里挑一样；one 代替前面说过的 hammer，避免重复。

Sentence 9第 9 句 (begins开头 “No, not that …”)

  • this one is near the speaker, that one is further away or in the other person's hand, and with an adjective it is the big one.指近处的用 this one，远处或对方手里的用 that one；带形容词说 the big one。

Sentence 15第 15 句 (begins开头 “What colour are …”)

  • be going to with the plain verb gives a plan already made.be going to + 动词原形：表示已经打算好、马上要做的事。
  • paint, then the thing, then a colour: to give it that colour, as in sentence 16.paint + 物 + 颜色：把……刷成某色（第 16 句：paint it pink）。

Sentence 19第 19 句 (begins开头 “It's for my …”)

  • for with a person says who it is meant for. Pink's is short for Pink is.for + 人：给某人的。Pink's = Pink is。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

be going to for plansbe going to：打算要做
Use am, is or are with going to and the plain verb for something you have already decided to do. Ask What is she going to buy? and answer I'm going to ...
am/is/are + going to + 动词原形，表示心里已经定下、准备去做的事。问句：What is she going to buy?；回答：I'm going to …。

Sentence 13 asks about the next step, and sentences 14 and 17 answer it.

本课：第 13 句问接下来的打算，第 15 和第 16 句回答。

  • I'm going to visit my aunt this weekend.
  • Are you going to buy that coat?
Which ...? and the pronoun oneWhich …? 与代词 one
Which with a noun asks for a choice from a limited group. In the answer use this one, that one or the with an adjective and one, so the noun is not repeated; for plurals use ones.
Which + 名词？让人在有限的几样里挑；回答时用 this one / that one / the + 形容词 + one 代替已说过的名词，复数用 ones。

Sentence 7 asks which, and sentences 8 to 11 answer with one.

本课：第 7 句问哪一把，第 8–10 句用 one 回答。

  • Which cake do you want? — The chocolate one.
  • I like these shoes, but not the black ones.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • You're working hard, …. 你干得真卖力，……
    You're working hard, Mia. Take a break.
  • Give me that …, please. 请把那个……给我。
    Give me that knife, please.
  • Not this one — the … one. 不是这个，是……的那个。
    Not this one — the blue one.
  • What colour will you …? 你打算……成什么颜色？
    What colour will you dye your hair?
  • It's for my …. … is her favourite …. 这是给我……的。……是她最喜欢的……
    It's for my son. Football is his favourite sport.
