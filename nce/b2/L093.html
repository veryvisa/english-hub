Lesson 93 · A Noble Gift 崇高的礼物

📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 93 “A Noble Gift”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 93《A Noble Gift》

B1🧠 20 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
The statue in New York Harbour was a present from the people of France in the nineteenth century. Designed by the sculptor Bartholdi, it took a decade to make: a copper skin hung on an iron frame that Eiffel designed for it. Before it could cross the Atlantic, the Americans had to choose a place for it and raise a base. In 1884 it stood complete in Paris; the next year it was taken apart and shipped over, and in late 1886 it was rebuilt and handed over. Ever since, it has meant freedom to the millions who came that way to settle in America. This is a piece of explanation told in time order. The grammar is the passive in several tenses, had to be done, and by plus a time.
自由女神像是十九世纪法国人民送给美国的礼物。雕像由雕塑家巴托尔迪设计，花了十年才完成：外壳是铜，里面撑着埃菲尔专门设计的金属骨架。运往美国前，先得选好地点、建好基座，地点选在纽约港入口的一座小岛上。1884 年，151 英尺高的雕像在巴黎立了起来；第二年拆成零件运过大西洋；1886 年 10 月底在纽约重新装好，由巴托尔迪正式交给美国人民。从那以后，它一直是千百万经纽约港来美国安家的人心中的自由象征。本课是一篇按时间顺序写的说明文，学被动语态的各种时态（was presented、had been constructed、had to be built、has been）和 by + 时间。

Unsigned and written for the course as a short factual account; the main dates can be checked against the United States National Park Service, which runs the monument.

本课未署名，是为教材自撰的简短说明；主要日期可以拿管理这座纪念碑的美国国家公园管理局的资料来核。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

The full name is Liberty Enlightening the World, and it was meant to mark the friendship of the two countries and the hundredth year of American independence, though it arrived ten years late. The gift was shared: the French paid for the figure, and the Americans were to pay for the base. American money came slowly, and the base was finished only after the newspaper owner Joseph Pulitzer ran a campaign in 1885 that drew small sums from well over a hundred thousand readers. The statue crossed the sea in some three hundred and fifty pieces packed in more than two hundred crates. It was not green then; the copper was the colour of a new coin and turned green over the next thirty years or so as the air worked on it. The famous lines about the tired and the poor, from a poem by Emma Lazarus, were put on a plaque inside the base only in 1903.

雕像的全名是「照耀世界的自由」，本意是纪念两国友谊和美国独立一百周年，只是晚到了十年。礼物是两国分担的：法国出雕像，美国出基座。美国的钱来得很慢，直到 1885 年报业主约瑟夫·普利策在报上发起募捐、从十几万读者那里收来一笔笔小钱，基座才建成。雕像拆成大约三百五十块、装了两百多个箱子运过大洋。那时它不是绿色的：铜是新硬币的颜色，此后三十年左右在空气作用下慢慢变绿。那几句关于「疲惫的、贫穷的人们」的名句出自艾玛·拉撒路的诗，直到 1903 年才刻在铜牌上装进基座。

✨ How it is written赏析

The lesson is almost a list of dates, but it does not read like one, because the writer keeps the statue as the subject of nearly every sentence. That is why the passive is everywhere: the people who did things, Bartholdi, Eiffel, the builders, are pushed into by phrases, and the statue moves through its own story. Notice the order of time words: took ten years, before, by 1884, the following year, by the end of October 1886, ever since then. They carry the reader forward without any first, second, third. The last sentence changes tense to the present perfect, and that one change brings the story from the past into today.

课文几乎是一串日期，读起来却不像清单，因为作者让雕像在几乎每一句里都当主语。这就是为什么到处是被动：做事的人——巴托尔迪、埃菲尔、建造者——被挤进 by 短语里，雕像自己走完它的故事。注意时间词的顺序：用了十年、在……之前、到 1884 年、次年、到 1886 年 10 月底、从那以后。它们带着读者往前走，用不着第一、第二、第三。最后一句时态换成现在完成时，这一个变化就把故事从过去带到了今天。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The title calls it a noble gift, and it was, but gifts between countries are also messages. France at the time was building a new republic and wanted to praise the American one; the statue said as much about French hopes as about American freedom. Its meaning has also grown beyond what its makers planned: it began as a sign of friendship and became, through the poem and the ships arriving at nearby Ellis Island from 1892, a welcome to immigrants. For readers who have moved to Canada or the United States themselves, it is worth asking what a symbol promises and what it actually delivers. Canada has nothing quite like it; its welcome has been written more in laws and offices than in metal.

标题说它是「崇高的礼物」，它确实是，但国与国之间的礼物也是一种表态。当时法国正在建设新的共和国，想称颂美国这个共和国；雕像说出的法国人的期望，不比它说出的美国自由少。它的含义也超出了制作者的设想：起初是友谊的标志，后来因为那首诗，也因为从 1892 年起一船船抵达附近埃利斯岛的移民，变成了对移民的欢迎。对自己也移居到加拿大或美国的读者来说，值得问一问：一个象征许诺了什么，实际兑现了什么。加拿大没有完全对应的东西；它的欢迎更多写在法律和办事处里，而不是铸在金属上。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 1第 1 句 (begins开头 “One of the …”)

  • one of the plus a superlative takes a plural noun.one of the + 最高级 + 复数名词：最……之一。
  • The name is in apposition: it explains the noun phrase before it, set off by commas.同位语：the Statue of Liberty 补充说明前面的名词，前后用逗号。
  • The past simple passive, with to for the receiver and by for the giver.be presented to … by …：一般过去时被动，to 引出接受者，by 引出赠送者。

Sentence 3第 3 句 (begins开头 “The great statue, …”)

  • A non-defining clause sits between the subject and its verb.非限制性定语从句插在主谓之间。
  • take plus a period of time plus to do: need that long to do.take + 时间 + to do：花……时间去做。

Sentence 4第 4 句 (begins开头 “The actual figure …”)

  • be made of names the material; supported by is a participle phrase describing the copper.be made of：由……制成（看得出原料）。supported by：过去分词短语作定语。
  • The past perfect passive: the frame was ready before the figure was finished.had been constructed：过去完成时被动，骨架在雕像完成之前就已造好。

Sentence 6第 6 句 (begins开头 “a site had …”)

  • Modal passives: could be done and had to be done.情态动词被动：can be done、had to be done。

Sentence 8第 8 句 (begins开头 “By 1884, a …”)

  • by plus a past time often goes with the past perfect: it was done before that point.by + 过去时间：到……为止，主句常用过去完成时。

Sentence 9第 9 句 (begins开头 “The following year, …”)

  • take something to pieces: take it apart; was is not repeated before sent.take sth to pieces：把……拆开；sent 前省略了 was。

Sentence 12第 12 句 (begins开头 “Ever since then, …”)

  • ever since then goes with the present perfect, for a state that runs up to now.ever since then + 现在完成时：从那时起一直。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

The passive in different tenses被动语态的各种时态
The passive is be plus a past participle, and the tense is carried by be: was done, had been done, has been done, and modal forms like can be done or had to be done. Use it when the doer is unknown or unimportant; add by when you want to name the doer.
被动 = be + 过去分词；时态由 be 体现：was done（一般过去）、had been done（过去完成）、has been done（现在完成）、can/must/had to be done（情态动词）。不知道或不必说谁做时用被动，要说时用 by。

Almost every sentence of this lesson uses the passive, because the statue, not the people, is the subject of the story.

本课：几乎每句都是被动：第 2 句 was presented，第 3 句 was designed，第 4 句 had been constructed，第 6 句 had to be found / built，第 8 句 had been erected，第 11 句 had been put together。

  • The bridge was built in 1930.
  • By the time we arrived, the tickets had been sold.
  • This work must be finished by Friday.
by plus a time: not later thanby + 时间：到……为止
by plus a future time often goes with will or will have done; by plus a past time often goes with the past perfect. until means the action goes on up to that time.
by + 将来时间常配将来时或将来完成时；by + 过去时间常配过去完成时；until 表示一直持续到某时。

In sentences 8 and 11, by plus a date marks the point when each stage was already finished.

本课：第 8 句 By 1884 … had been erected；第 10–11 句 By the end of 1886 雕像已重新装好。

  • By six o'clock, she had finished her work.
  • I'll be home by ten.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • One of the most famous … in the world, X, was given to … 世界上最著名的……之一 X，是送给……的
    One of the most famous gardens in the world, Butchart Gardens, was given its fame by a single family.
  • X took … years to finish X 花了……年才完工
    The cathedral took over a century to finish.
  • Before X could be …, a … had to be found X 能被……之前，得先找到……
    Before the play could be staged, a new hall had to be found.
  • By …, X had been put up in … 到……时，X 已在……建起
    By 1885, a railway had been put up across Canada.
  • Ever since, X has stood for … 从那以后，X 一直象征着……
    Ever since, the old bridge has stood for the town's spirit.

Our model summary本站范文

The Statue of Liberty was a gift from the French people to the United States. Designed by Bartholdi, it took ten years to make: a copper figure on an iron frame built by Eiffel. Meanwhile a base was built on an island in New York Harbour. It was first put up in Paris in 1884, then shipped over in pieces. In October 1886 it was rebuilt and handed to the Americans, and it has stood for freedom ever since.（79 词）
