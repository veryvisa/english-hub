🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
At a village fair the writer chose to visit a fortune-teller who went by the name of Madam Bellinsky. Inside her tent she waved him to a seat, took his money and gazed into a glass ball. Her forecast was detailed: a female relative was on her way, would turn up that evening and planned a short stay; on leaving the tent he would be startled when a woman he knew rushed up, spoke to him and took him off. He stepped out and thought no more of her, because his wife was hurrying over, cross that he had vanished. His sister, she said, was due within the hour, and they were already late to collect her from the station. He followed his wife out of the fair. The grammar is will be doing, be coming for plans, the moment and as soon as, and After I had done.
在一次乡村集市上，「我」决定去找一位叫贝林斯基夫人的算命人。进了她的帐篷，她叫「我」坐下。「我」付了钱，她望着水晶球说：「你的一位亲戚要来看你，她今晚到，打算住几天。你一走出这个帐篷，就会大吃一惊：一个你很熟的女人会朝你奔来，跟你说话，然后把你从这里领走。就这些。」「我」一出帐篷就把贝林斯基夫人忘得一干二净，因为「我」妻子急匆匆地朝「我」走来，不耐烦地问：「你躲到哪儿去了？你妹妹不到一小时就到，我们得去车站接她，已经晚了。」她转身就走，「我」跟着她出了集市。本课学将来进行时 will be doing、be coming 表计划、the moment / as soon as 和过去完成时 After I had done。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

Fortune-tellers in tents were a common sight at English village fairs and seaside resorts, often with grand foreign-sounding names to add mystery. Psychologists have studied why their readings can feel accurate. One well-known effect, named after a nineteenth-century showman, describes how people accept vague statements as true of themselves. Another technique is cold reading, in which a skilled reader guesses from clues such as age, clothes and a wedding ring. Chinese readers will know the street fortune-tellers who read faces or ask for a birth date; the methods are different, but the effect on the listener is often very similar.

帐篷里的算命人曾是英国乡村集市和海滨度假地的常见风景，常取一个听起来洋气神秘的名字。心理学家研究过为什么算命会让人觉得「很准」。一个著名的效应以十九世纪一位马戏团老板命名，说的是人们会把笼统的描述当作是在说自己；另一种手法叫「冷读」，高明的算命人从年龄、衣着、婚戒等线索中猜测。中国读者熟悉街头看相或问生辰八字的算命先生，方法不同，但对听者的作用往往十分相似。

✨ How it is written赏析

The pleasure of the lesson is that the reader notices what the narrator does not. Every detail of the forecast comes true in the next few lines, a relative, this evening, a woman he knows, rushing, leading him away, yet he has already forgotten the fortune-teller. The writer never points it out; he simply sets the two passages side by side and lets the reader make the match. The prophecy is told in future forms, the outcome in past forms, so the grammar itself marks the line between promise and event. The title is a small joke too, because the future here is less than an hour long.

这一课的乐趣在于读者察觉到了叙述者没察觉的东西。预言的每个细节都在接下来几行里应验：一位亲戚、今晚、一个熟悉的女人、奔过来、把他领走——可他已经把算命人忘了。作者从不点破，只是把两段并排放着，让读者自己去对上。预言用将来时，结果用过去时，语法本身就划出了「承诺」和「事件」的界线。标题也是一个小玩笑：这里的「未来」还不到一个小时长。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

Was Madam Bellinsky gifted, or just lucky and observant? A sceptical reader might note that she could easily have seen the wife searching the fair, or heard gossip about a visiting sister. The story leaves room for both readings, which is part of its charm. A wider lesson is about attention: the narrator paid to hear the future and then ignored it the moment real life called. Many people treat advice the same way. The healthier attitude may be to enjoy fortune-telling as entertainment while making real decisions on evidence.

贝林斯基夫人是真有本事，还是运气好、眼尖？持怀疑态度的读者会指出：她很可能看到了那位妻子在集市上找人，或听说了妹妹要来的闲话。故事给两种读法都留了余地，这正是它的魅力之一。更大的教训关乎注意力：叙述者花钱听了未来，现实生活一召唤，他就把它抛在脑后。很多人对待建议也是这样。更健康的态度或许是：把算命当娱乐，而真正的决定要靠证据。

💬 Discuss and write讨论与写作
  1. How could Madam Bellinsky have known these things without magic? Suggest two explanations.
    贝林斯基夫人不靠法术，怎么可能知道这些？提出两种解释。
  2. Write a short prediction for a friend's next week, then check at the end of the week what came true.
    给朋友的下一周写一段简短的预言，到周末核对哪些应验了。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 1第 1 句 (begins开头 “At a village …”)

  • called: a past participle after the noun, meaning named.called …：过去分词作后置定语，「名叫……的」。

Sentence 3第 3 句 (begins开头 “After I had …”)

  • after with the past perfect: the payment came first.after + 过去完成时：先付钱，后看水晶球，先后关系清楚。

Sentence 4第 4 句 (begins开头 “'A relation of …”)

  • a relation of yours: one of your relatives, a double possessive.a relation of yours：双重所有格，「你的一位亲戚」。
  • is coming: the present continuous for a planned future.is coming：现在进行时表示已安排好的将来。

Sentence 5第 5 句 (begins开头 “She will be …”)

  • will be arriving: the future continuous, for something that will happen as a matter of course.will be arriving：将来进行时，说将来某时会自然发生的事。
  • intend to do: plan to do.intend to do：打算做……。

Sentence 6第 6 句 (begins开头 “The moment you …”)

  • the moment: as soon as, with the present for the future.the moment …：一……就……，= as soon as；从句用一般现在时表将来。

Sentence 9第 9 句 (begins开头 “As soon as …”)

  • as soon as: immediately when.as soon as：一……就……。
  • forget all about: forget completely.forget all about：完全忘了。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

The future continuous: will be doing将来进行时：will be doing
will be plus -ing describes an action in progress at a future time, or something that will happen in the normal course of events; it sounds more neutral and polite than will do.
will be + -ing：将来某时正在进行的动作，或「照常会发生」的事（不强调意愿）；语气比 will do 更客观、更委婉。

In sentence 5 the fortune-teller announces the visitor's arrival as if it were already arranged.

本课：第 5 句——算命人说亲戚「今晚会到」，像在报一件已定的事。

  • This time tomorrow I will be flying to Vancouver.
  • Will you be using the car tonight?
the moment and as soon asthe moment / as soon as：一……就……
the moment, as soon as and immediately can all start a time clause meaning straight after; for the future, the clause uses the present, not will.
the moment、as soon as、immediately 都可引导时间从句，表示「一……就……」；说将来时从句用一般现在时，不用 will。

Sentence 6 uses the moment for the prediction, and sentence 9 uses as soon as for what really happened, one future and one past.

本课：第 6 句用 the moment 预言，第 9 句用 as soon as 叙述真实发生的事——一个说将来，一个说过去。

  • Call me the moment you arrive.
  • As soon as she saw me, she smiled.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • At a …, I decided to visit a … called … 在……上，我决定去拜访一位名叫……的……
    At the street market, I decided to try a stall called Grandma's Kitchen.
  • After I had …, X … and said: … 我……之后，X……并说：……
    After I had paid, the waiter smiled and said: 'Enjoy your meal.'
  • A … of yours is coming to … 你的一位……要来……
    A friend of yours is coming to dinner tonight.
  • The moment you …, you will … 你一……就会……
    The moment you open the door, you will smell the bread.
  • As X hurried off, I went after X … X 匆匆走开时，我跟着 X……
    As he ran off, I chased him down the street.

Our model summary本站范文

At a village fair I paid a fortune-teller, Madam Bellinsky, who looked into her crystal ball. She said that a woman relative would visit me that evening and stay a few days, and that when I left the tent a woman I knew would rush up and lead me away. Outside, my wife hurried over, saying my sister would arrive within the hour and we were late. I left the fair with her.（73 词）
