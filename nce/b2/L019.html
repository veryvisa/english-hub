Lesson 19 · Sold Out 售票处满员了

📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 19 “Sold Out”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 19《Sold Out》

B1🧠 15 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
The writer and Susan hurry to a play, worried that it is about to start or has started already. At the box office every seat has gone. Just as they give up, a man rushes in to hand back two tickets, and the writer asks for them at once. The girl at the counter warns him that they are for next Wednesday, and he sadly decides he may as well take them. The grammar is may, might, can and could for possibility and requests, and the phrase might as well.
作者和苏珊赶去看戏，担心戏随时会开演，甚至已经开演了。到了售票处，票已经卖完。正失望时，一个男人跑来退了两张票，作者马上过去要下。售票员却说：这两张是下周三的。作者只好无奈地说：「那我还是要了吧。」本课学 may / might / can / could 表示可能与请求，以及 might as well。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. When will the writer finally see the play?作者最后什么时候才能看到这出戏？
  2. How does he feel when he decides to take the tickets?他说「那我还是要了吧」时是什么心情？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander for New Concept English (Longman, 1967); Susan and the theatre are invented.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为《新概念英语》（朗文，1967）自撰；苏珊和剧院都是虚构的。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

London theatres in the sixties sold tickets mainly at the box office or through agencies, often days or weeks ahead for a popular show, and a full house was common. Returns, tickets handed back by people who could not go, were a recognised way of getting into a sold-out play, and some theatregoers still queue for them on the day. What a reader of the time would have known, and what the joke depends on, is that a return could be for any date; there was no screen to check, only the girl behind the window and her book of seats.

六十年代伦敦的剧院主要在售票窗口或通过代售处卖票，热门剧往往要提前几天甚至几周买，满座是常事。「退票」——去不了的人交回的票——是进入满座剧的一条公认门路，有些戏迷至今还会当天排队等退票。当时的读者知道、而这个笑话也正依赖的一点是：退回来的票可能是任何一天的；没有屏幕可查，只有窗口后面的姑娘和她手里的座位簿。

✨ How it is written赏析

Short as it is, the lesson is a sequence of hopes raised and dropped, and each step is carried by a modal verb. It opens with may and may have, two guesses that build pressure. Then three requests in a row, May, Can and Could, move from the writer to a stranger and back to the writer, each one a little more polite than the last, as if politeness might help. The answer to the last request is the same Certainly as before, but this time it is followed by but, and that single word ends the hope. The closing might as well is the right tone for a man who has won and lost in the same sentence.

课文虽短，却是一连串希望被抬起又落下，每一步都靠一个情态动词推动。开头 may 和 may have 两个推测，制造紧迫感。接着连续三个请求——May、Can、Could——从作者到陌生人再回到作者，一次比一次客气，好像客气能帮上忙似的。最后一个请求得到的回答和前面一样是 Certainly，但这次后面跟了 but，就这一个词把希望掐断了。结尾的 might as well，正是一个在同一句话里既赢又输的人该有的语气。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

Today the same scene would happen on a phone, and the joke would be harder to set up, because a booking page shows the date on every ticket before you pay. The underlying lesson has not changed: when something you want suddenly becomes available, check the details before you say yes. Learners can also take a practical point from the three requests. In English, how you ask matters a great deal, and moving from Can I to Could I is an easy way to sound more polite to a stranger. Chinese has its own layers of politeness, such as adding please or a softening word, and comparing the two systems helps you choose the right level.

今天同样的场景会发生在手机上，这个笑话就更难搭起来了，因为付钱之前，网页会把每张票的日期都显示出来。背后的教训却没变：你想要的东西突然出现时，先看清细节，再说「要」。学习者也能从那三个请求里学到实用的一点：英语里怎么开口问非常要紧，把 Can I 换成 Could I，是对陌生人显得更礼貌的简单办法。中文也有自己的礼貌层次，比如加「请」或加一个缓和语气的词，把两套系统对照着看，有助于选对分寸。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentence 2第 2 句 (begins开头 “'It may have …”)

  • may do: something possible now or soon. The play could start any minute.may do：现在或将来可能（随时可能开演）。
  • may have done: a guess about the past, meaning perhaps it has already happened.may have done：对过去的推测，「可能已经」。

Sentence 4第 4 句 (begins开头 “'May I have …”)

  • May I...? is a polite request, a little more formal than Can I...?May I …?：礼貌地请求，比 Can I …? 正式。

Sentence 8第 8 句 (begins开头 “'Can I return …”)

  • Can I...? is the everyday way to ask permission. return here means hand the tickets back.Can I …?：口语里问「可以……吗」。return 在这里是退票。

Sentence 15第 15 句 (begins开头 “'I might as …”)

  • might as well: since nothing better is possible, I will do this. It sounds resigned.might as well do：没有更好的选择，那就……吧，带点无奈。
  • The joke of the lesson is that he gets the tickets, but for the wrong week.全课笑点：他终于拿到票，却是下周三的。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

may and might for possibilitymay / might：可能性
may or might with the base verb means something is possible now or later. may or might have with a past participle is a guess about the past. might is less sure than may.
may / might + 动词原形：现在或将来可能。may / might have + 过去分词：过去可能已经发生。might 比 may 更没把握。

Sentence 1 uses may begin for the future, and sentence 2 uses may have begun for the past.

本课：第 1 句 may begin（将来可能），第 2 句 may have begun（过去可能已发生）。

  • Take a coat; it may rain later.
  • He's late. He might have missed the bus.
Ways of asking: Can I, Could I, May I请求的几种说法：Can I / Could I / May I
Can I...? is the most casual, Could I...? is more polite, and May I...? is more formal. Common answers are Certainly, Of course, or Sorry. Note that could here is not past; it is only softer.
Can I …? 最随便；Could I …? 更客气；May I …? 更正式。回答常用 Certainly. / Of course. / Sorry, … 注意 could 在这里不是过去时，只是更委婉。

Sentences 4, 8 and 11 show all three forms in one short text.

本课：第 4 句 May I、第 8 句 Can I、第 11 句 Could I，三种说法都出现了。

  • Could I borrow your pen, please?
  • May I come in?

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • X may begin at any moment. X 随时可能开始。
    The storm may start at any moment.
  • It may have … already. 可能已经……了。
    They may have left already.
  • What a pity! 真可惜！
    "The museum is closed today." "What a pity!"
  • I might as well … 那我还是……吧
    The shop is closed, so I might as well go home.

Our model summary本站范文

The writer and Susan were afraid they were late for a play, and at the box office every seat had gone. Then a man handed back two tickets. The writer asked for them at once, but they were for the following Wednesday. Rather sadly, he took them anyway.（48 词）
