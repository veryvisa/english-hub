🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
An ancient bus pulled up beside a dry riverbed, and a group of well-known screen actors climbed out. In dark glasses and shabby clothes, they had gone to great lengths not to be recognised, and their costumes turned out to work better than they wished. Gloria and Brinksley praised the spot, delighted that there were no reporters or fans. Two others, Rockwall and Merlin, carried the picnic baskets into the shade. Just as everyone had settled down, an angry stranger appeared, said he was the sheriff, pointed at a sign forbidding camping and ordered them off. When Rockwall gave their names, the sheriff mocked him, claiming to be Brinksley and Gloria himself. This is a comic sketch that pokes fun at film stars. The grammar is so that ... should, couldn't be better, in case and make oneself comfortable.
一辆老旧的公共汽车停在干涸的河床边，下来一群著名男女演员。他们戴墨镜、穿旧衣服，处处小心，不想让任何人认出来。可他们很快发现，伪装有时太完美了。格洛丽亚说这里野餐再好不过，布林克斯利附和：没有记者，没有影迷！另外两个演员把两大篮食物搬到树荫下。大家刚坐舒服，来了一个陌生人，怒气冲冲地叫他们滚：「我是这儿的治安官，看见那块牌子没有？『禁止露营』——怕你们不识字！」罗克沃尔说：「别对我们太凶，我是罗克沃尔·斯林格，这位是默林·格里夫斯。」治安官冷笑：「是吗？那我是布林克斯利·米尔斯，我的另一个名字叫格洛丽亚·格利姆。现在马上给我滚！」本课是一个讽刺明星的喜剧小品，学 so that … should、couldn't be better、in case 和 make oneself comfortable。

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Unsigned and written for the course; the actors' names are invented jokes on the names of film stars.

本课未署名、为教材自撰；演员们的名字是对影星名字的戏仿。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

The title borrows a theatre word, limelight, from the bright lamps that lit the stage in the nineteenth century, before electric lights. To be in the limelight is to be the centre of public attention, and to be out of it is to escape. The names are part of the fun: Gloria Gleam suggests shine and glory, Rockwall Slinger sounds like a tough cowboy hero, and Brinksley Meers plays on grand English surnames. A sheriff, in the American sense, is an elected officer who keeps the law in a county, so the scene is set in the American West, home of the Hollywood film industry. Today stars face cameras on every phone, and some hire bodyguards just to buy groceries.

标题借用了一个戏剧词 limelight，原指十九世纪电灯出现前照亮舞台的强光灯。in the limelight 就是处在公众注意的中心，out of the limelight 则是躲开它。名字也是笑料的一部分：Gloria Gleam 让人想到光芒和荣耀，Rockwall Slinger 听着像硬汉牛仔英雄，Brinksley Meers 是在拿派头十足的英国姓氏开玩笑。美国意义上的 sheriff 是由选举产生、负责一个县治安的官员，所以场景设在美国西部，也就是好莱坞电影业的大本营。如今明星面对的是每部手机上的摄像头，有些人连买菜都要雇保镖。

✨ How it is written赏析

The story sets up its irony in its fourth sentence and then lets it unfold. We know before the actors do that the disguise will work against them, so we watch them enjoy their freedom with a sense of what is coming. The dialogue is short and natural, and each speaker has a voice: the stars are relaxed and pleased with themselves, the sheriff is rough and sarcastic. The best joke is saved for the last line, where the sheriff throws their own names back at them. His last claim is especially funny because a rough man claims a glamorous woman's name.

故事在第 4 句就埋下了反讽，然后让它慢慢展开。演员们还没意识到，我们已经知道伪装会反过来坑他们，于是我们带着「接下来会怎样」的预感，看着他们享受自由。对话简短自然，每个人都有自己的声音：明星们放松、自得，治安官粗鲁、尖刻。最好的笑话留在最后一行：治安官把他们自己的名字甩了回去。他最后那句自称尤其好笑，因为一个粗汉自称是一位光彩照人的女明星。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The sketch makes fun of a common wish among famous people: to be treated like everyone else, but only when it suits them. The actors wanted to escape their fans, yet the moment they met trouble they tried to use their names. Most of us do something similar on a small scale, wanting privacy one moment and recognition the next. The story also shows that fame is a kind of costume: without the clothes, cars and cameras, a star looks like anyone. Chinese readers may think of celebrities who travel in masks and caps to avoid crowds, and of how quickly people online can identify them anyway.

这个小品讽刺的是名人中常见的一种心愿：想被当成普通人，但只在对自己有利的时候。演员们想躲开影迷，可一遇到麻烦，马上就想搬出自己的名字。我们大多数人在小事上也这样：这会儿想要隐私，下一刻又想被认出来。故事还说明，名气本身就是一种戏服：脱掉衣着、座驾和镜头，明星看起来和谁都一样。中国读者会想到那些戴口罩、帽子出行以躲开人群的明星，也会想到网友照样能飞快地认出他们。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 3第 3 句 (begins开头 “they had taken …”)

  • Dressed in: a past participle phrase describing they.Dressed in …：过去分词短语作状语，逻辑主语是 they。
  • take precautions: do things in advance to avoid trouble.take precautions：采取预防措施。
  • so that ... should: a purpose clause; should is more formal than could here.so that … should：以便……，should 用于目的从句，比 would/could 更正式。

Sentence 4第 4 句 (begins开头 “But as they …”)

  • as they soon discovered: a clause with as, meaning as they found out.as they soon discovered：正如他们很快发现的，as 引导非限制性从句。
  • can be: what sometimes happens.can be：表示有时会出现的情况。

Sentence 6第 6 句 (begins开头 “'It couldn't be …”)

  • couldn't be better: a negative with a comparative means the best possible.couldn't be better：否定 + 比较级 = 最高级，「好得不能再好」。

Sentence 10第 10 句 (begins开头 “When they had …”)

  • make oneself comfortable: settle into a relaxed position; had made: this came first.make oneself comfortable：舒舒服服地坐好。had made：过去完成时，先坐好，陌生人后来。

Sentence 13第 13 句 (begins开头 “'I'm sheriff here. …”)

  • in case: here used rudely, as an insult.in case：以防……；这里是讽刺，带侮辱意味。
  • I'm sheriff here: a job title after be can drop the article.I'm sheriff here：表示职位的名词作表语时可不加冠词。

Sentence 16第 16 句 (begins开头 “'Oh, is it?' …”)

  • Oh, is it?: a short question that shows disbelief.Oh, is it?：简略问句表示不相信。
  • with a sneer: with an unkind smile.with a sneer：带着冷笑。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

Purpose clauses with so that目的从句：so that + can / could / should / would
so that is usually followed by a modal: can or will for now, could, would or should for the past. should is formal and often used in negative purpose clauses. in case is different: it means as a precaution, and the clause uses the present, not will.
so that 后面常用情态动词：现在用 can / will，过去用 could / would / should。should 显得正式，常用于否定的目的（so that no one should …）。in case 不同：表示「以防万一」，从句用一般现在时，不用 will。

Sentence 3 gives the purpose of the disguise with so that ... should; sentence 13 has the sheriff using in case as an insult.

本课：第 3 句用 so that … should 说他们为什么伪装；第 13 句治安官用 in case 挖苦人。

  • She spoke slowly so that everyone could understand.
  • Take a coat in case it gets cold.
A negative with a comparative for the highest degree否定 + 比较级表示最高级
couldn't be better, nothing is more ... than, I couldn't agree more: a negative with a comparative means the very best or the most. The meaning is positive, not negative.
couldn't be better / nothing is more … than / I couldn't agree more：否定加比较级，意思是「再好不过」「最……」。注意它是肯定的意思，不是否定。

In sentence 6 Brinksley agrees with Gloria using couldn't be better.

本课：第 6 句布林克斯利用 couldn't be better 附和格洛丽亚。

  • The weather couldn't be better for a walk.
  • 'He's right.' — 'I couldn't agree more.'

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • Dressed in …, X had taken special care so that … X 穿着……，特别小心，以免……
    Dressed in a raincoat, Tom had taken special care so that his suit stayed dry.
  • But as X soon discovered, … can sometimes be too … 但 X 很快发现，……有时会太……
    But as we soon discovered, a cheap hotel can sometimes be too cheap.
  • 'What a wonderful place for …!' said X. 「这里是……的好地方啊！」X 说
    'What a wonderful place for a swim!' said Anna.
  • Once X had made themselves comfortable, … X 都坐舒服了之后，……
    Once the guests had made themselves comfortable, the music began.
  • Don't be too hard on …, I'm … 别对……太凶，我是……
    Don't be too hard on the boy, he's only seven.

Our model summary本站范文

A group of famous actors went for a picnic by a dry riverbed. Wearing sunglasses and shabby clothes, they hoped nobody would recognize them, and they were delighted that there were no reporters or fans. But soon an angry sheriff arrived and told them to leave, pointing to a no-camping sign. When one actor gave their names, the sheriff laughed at him and claimed to be the stars himself. Their disguise had worked too well.（75 词）
