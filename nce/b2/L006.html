Lesson 6 · Percy Buttons 珀西·巴顿斯

📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 6 “Percy Buttons”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 6《Percy Buttons》

B1🧠 16 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
The writer has only just moved into a house on Bridge Street when a beggar comes to the door asking for food and a beer, and pays for them on the spot by standing on his head and singing. The writer feeds him; he eats and drinks, then slips some cheese into his pocket and leaves. A neighbour explains later that the man, Percy Buttons, is known to the whole street and does the round of every house each month with the same request. The grammar is the present simple for habits and the past simple for the story, together with ways of counting such as a meal, a glass of and a piece of.
作者刚搬到桥街，就有个乞丐来敲门，要一顿饭和一杯啤酒，作为回报，他当场倒立唱歌。作者给了他饭，他吃完喝完，又顺手把一块奶酪揣进口袋才走。后来邻居告诉作者：这人叫珀西·巴顿斯，街上人人认识，每月挨家挨户来一趟，每次都要一顿饭一杯啤酒。本课学一般现在时讲习惯、一般过去时讲经过，以及 a meal、a glass of、a piece of 这类表数量的说法。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. Who is Percy Buttons?珀西·巴顿斯是谁？
  2. What did the beggar do in return for the meal?乞丐用什么「报答」作者的一顿饭？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander for New Concept English (Longman, 1967); Percy Buttons and Bridge Street are invented.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为《新概念英语》（朗文，1967）自撰；珀西·巴顿斯和桥街都是虚构的。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

The beggar who performs for his food belongs to an old British tradition of street entertainers and travelling figures who went from door to door. Many towns had a well-known local character of this kind, and residents often treated him as part of the street rather than as a stranger. The detail of a glass of beer fits the period too: beer was an ordinary drink with a meal for working people, and asking for it was not seen as strange. The name is part of the comedy. Percy is a rather grand first name, and buttons are small, cheap and ordinary, so the full name sounds both proud and humble at once.

靠表演换吃食的乞丐，属于英国一个古老的传统：街头艺人和走村串户的流浪人物。很多镇子都有一个这样的「街坊名人」，居民往往把他当成这条街的一部分，而不是陌生人。一杯啤酒这个细节也合乎那个年代：对干体力活的人来说，啤酒是佐餐的寻常饮料，开口要它不算奇怪。名字本身也是喜剧的一部分：珀西是个颇为体面的名字，纽扣却小、便宜、普通，于是全名听起来又骄傲又卑微。

✨ How it is written赏析

The story is told from the point of view of a newcomer, and that choice drives the whole joke. Because the writer has only just arrived, the visit seems strange and special to him; the reader shares his surprise. Then a neighbour supplies the missing knowledge in four short sentences, and the tense changes from the past to the present. That shift is the twist. What looked like a single odd event turns out to be a monthly routine that everybody else already knows about. The piece of cheese is the best detail in the lesson: it tells us, without a word of comment, that the beggar is relaxed, practical and quite at home.

故事从一个新来者的视角讲，这个选择推动了整个笑点。因为作者刚搬来，这次来访在他看来又怪又特别，读者也跟着他吃惊。然后邻居用四个短句补上了他不知道的事，时态随之从过去时换成一般现在时——这一换就是翻转：看似一次怪事，原来是每月一次的例行公事，别人早就知道了。那块奶酪是全课最好的细节：一字不评，就告诉我们这个乞丐从容、务实、熟门熟路。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

Every neighbourhood has its regular visitors, and newcomers are always the last to learn the rules. The lesson is a reminder that a single encounter can mislead: before judging, ask someone who has lived there longer. Chinese readers may think of the travelling performers who once went from village to village, doing tricks or singing in exchange for food, and of how villagers knew each one by name. Modern cities have fewer of these familiar faces, and many people do not know who lives next door. Percy Buttons is, in a way, a sign of a closer kind of street, where even the beggar has a name.

每个街区都有常来的「熟客」，新来的人总是最后一个弄懂规矩。课文提醒我们：一次相遇可能误导人，下判断之前，先问问住得更久的人。中国读者也许会想到旧时走村串乡的江湖艺人，耍把戏、唱小曲换口饭吃，村里人个个叫得出他们的名字。如今的城市里这种熟面孔少了，很多人连隔壁住的是谁都不知道。从这个意义上说，珀西·巴顿斯是一种更亲近的街坊关系的标志：在那里，连乞丐都有名有姓。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentence 4第 4 句 (begins开头 “He asked me …”)

  • ask sb for sth: to say that you want somebody to give you something.ask sb for sth：向某人要某物。
  • a glass of with an uncountable noun: one glassful.a glass of + 不可数名词：一杯……。

Sentence 6第 6 句 (begins开头 “the beggar stood …”)

  • in return for this: as payment for the meal.in return for this：作为对这顿饭的回报。

Sentence 9第 9 句 (begins开头 “Then he put …”)

Sentence 13第 13 句 (begins开头 “He calls at …”)

  • calls and asks are in the present simple for his habit, in contrast with the past simple of the story before.一般现在时 calls、asks：讲他一贯的习惯，和前面讲经过的过去时形成对照。
  • call at a place: to stop there for a short visit.call at + 地点：到某处（短暂）拜访。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

Present simple for habits, past simple for the story一般现在时讲习惯，一般过去时讲经过
The present simple is for things that happen regularly or are always true, often with always, every or once a month. The past simple is for what happened on one occasion.
一般现在时讲经常发生、一贯如此的事（常配 always、every、once a month）；一般过去时讲某次发生过的事。

Sentences 3 to 9 tell the visit in the past; sentences 11 to 13 change to the present to show that it is his regular habit.

本课：第 3–10 句用过去时讲那一次的经过；第 11–14 句换成一般现在时，说明这是他的老习惯。

  • Yesterday the bus was late. It usually comes at eight.
  • My aunt visits us every Christmas. Last year she brought a cake.
Counting uncountable nouns: a glass of, a piece of不可数名词怎么数：a glass of / a piece of
Words such as beer, cheese, bread, water and advice are uncountable: they cannot take a or a plural -s, so we count them with a measure word, as in a glass of, a piece of, a cup of and a loaf of. meal is countable, so a meal is fine.
beer、cheese、bread、water、advice 等不可数，不能直接加 a 或 -s，要用量词：a glass of beer、a piece of cheese、a cup of tea、a loaf of bread。meal 可数，可直接说 a meal。

Sentences 4 and 13 have a meal and a glass, and sentence 9 has a piece.

本课：第 3、13 句用 a glass of 数啤酒，第 9 句用 a piece of 数奶酪。

  • Could I have a cup of coffee, please?
  • She gave me a piece of good advice.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • X has just moved into a … on … X 刚搬进……的一所……
    My cousin has just moved into a flat on Kingsway.
  • X asked me for … X 向我要……
    The child asked me for a pencil.
  • In return for this, X … 作为回报，X……
    In return for this, she helps him with his English.
  • X calls at every … once a … X 每……到每一家……来一次
    The baker calls at every farm once a week.
