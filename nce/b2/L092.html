Lesson 92 · Asking for Trouble 自找麻烦

📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 92 “Asking for Trouble”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 92《Asking for Trouble》

B1🧠 17 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
The narrator gets home at about two in the morning and rings the bell, but his wife sleeps on. So he fetches a ladder from the garden shed and starts up towards the bedroom. Near the top, a dry voice from below remarks that the windows hardly need washing at that hour. It is a policeman, and the narrator almost falls. He answers with a silly joke, the policeman plays along in the same tone and then politely invites him to the station. At last he admits he has no key, has to shout it twice, and the noise finally wakes his wife just as the policeman starts up the ladder. This is a small comedy carried by dialogue. The grammar is must have been, regret doing, So do I, mind doing, and a possessive before an -ing form.
「我」凌晨两点左右才到家，按门铃想叫醒妻子，可她睡得正熟。于是「我」从花园的小棚里搬出梯子靠在墙上，朝卧室窗户爬。快到了，下面传来一句挖苦话：这个钟点，窗户恐怕不用擦吧？低头一看是个警察，吓得差点从梯子上摔下来。「我」嘴硬说喜欢晚上擦窗，警察用同样的腔调回了一句「我也是」，客客气气请「我」去趟警察局。「我」只好说忘了带钥匙，大喊了两遍，警察正要往上爬，妻子被喊声吵醒推开了窗。本课是一段靠对话推动的小喜剧，学 must have been、regret doing、So do I、mind doing 与物主代词＋动名词。

  2. What made the policeman suspicious?警察为什么怀疑他？

Unsigned and written for the course; it is a stock comic situation rather than a report of a real night.

本课未署名、为教材自撰；它是喜剧里的常见桥段，不是哪个真实夜晚的报道。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

A man climbing a ladder to an upstairs window at two in the morning is what a burglar looks like, so the policeman is doing exactly his job. In Britain of the period an officer on foot patrol, the so-called bobby on the beat, walked a fixed route through a neighbourhood at night and was expected to notice anything odd. The British police have been mostly unarmed since they were set up in London in 1829, and much of their tradition rests on polite firmness rather than force, which is the tone the policeman uses here. The joke also depends on a British habit of speech: saying something mild and indirect when you mean something quite sharp.

半夜爬梯子到楼上窗口的男人，看起来就是个窃贼，所以警察完全是在尽职。那个年代的英国，步行巡逻的警员——俗称「巡街的 bobby」——夜里沿固定路线走过街区，留意任何反常的事。英国警察自 1829 年在伦敦成立以来大多不带枪，他们的传统很大程度上靠客气而坚定，而不是靠武力，这正是本课警察说话的腔调。笑点还依赖英国人的一种说话习惯：心里想说很尖锐的话，嘴上却说得温和而委婉。

✨ How it is written赏析

The comedy is in the politeness. Neither man ever says what he means: the policeman does not say you look like a thief, he says the windows do not need washing; the narrator does not say I live here, he makes a joke. Each polite line raises the tension a little, and the policeman's long, courteous request is funnier than any threat. Then the story breaks into short shouts, one or two words each, and the plain truth arrives at the loudest possible volume. The narrator tells it against himself, which makes the reader like him.

喜剧出在客气上。两个人谁都不直说：警察不说「你像个贼」，而说窗户用不着擦；叙述者不说「我住这儿」，而是开个玩笑。每一句客气话都把紧张往上推一点，警察那句又长又客气的请求比任何威胁都好笑。然后故事断成一声声短促的喊叫，每句只有一两个词，最朴素的真相以最大的音量到来。叙述者讲的是自己出丑，这让读者对他有好感。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The title is the lesson. The narrator was not in trouble until he chose to be clever with a policeman; one plain sentence about the key would have ended it at the start. Most people know this feeling: when we are embarrassed, we joke, and the joke makes it worse. With police officers in Canada the practical advice is the same as in the story's moral: stay calm, be polite, say simply who you are and why you are there. Sarcasm in a second language is risky in any case, because tone is the hardest thing to hear correctly, and a joke that works with friends can sound rude to a stranger.

标题就是教训。叙述者本来没惹麻烦，是他偏要跟警察耍嘴皮才惹上的；一开始说一句「我忘了钥匙」就全结束了。很多人都有这种体会：一尴尬就开玩笑，玩笑反而让事情更糟。在加拿大跟警察打交道，实际的建议和故事的寓意一样：保持冷静、礼貌，简单说清你是谁、为什么在这儿。何况用第二语言挖苦人本来就冒险，因为语气是最难听准的东西，朋友之间好笑的玩笑，对陌生人可能就显得无礼。

    你有没有因为想逗乐反而把事情搞砸的时候？当时本该怎么说？
  2. Rewrite the conversation so that the writer tells the truth at once. Is the story still funny?
    改写这段对话，让作者一开始就说实话。故事还好笑吗？

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 1第 1 句 (begins开头 “It must have …”)

  • must have been is a confident guess about the past; the narrator himself is not sure of the exact time.must have been：对过去的肯定推测，「一定是」。作者自己也记不清，只能推断。

Sentence 4第 4 句 (begins开头 “so I got …”)

  • by doing: by means of; fast asleep: deeply asleep.by doing：通过……的方式。fast asleep：熟睡。
  • Three past verbs in a row tell the actions in order; begin doing means the same as begin to do.三个过去式动词并列（got, put, began），begin doing = begin to do。

Sentence 7第 7 句 (begins开头 “'I don't think …”)

  • need doing has a passive meaning: the windows need to be cleaned.need doing = need to be done：需要被……（主动形式表被动）。
  • English puts the not with think rather than with the clause that follows.I don't think …：否定前移，英语习惯否定 think 而不是从句。

Sentence 9第 9 句 (begins开头 “I immediately regretted …”)

  • regret doing: be sorry for something already done; in the way I did uses did to stand for answered.regret doing：后悔做了（已做的事）；in the way I did：用我那种方式（did 代替 answered）。

Sentence 10第 10 句 (begins开头 “'So do I,' …”)

  • So do I: me too, with the verb before the subject.So do I：我也是（肯定句的附和，倒装）。
  • in the same tone: with the same mocking manner.in the same tone：用同样的（挖苦）语气。

Sentence 11第 11 句 (begins开头 “Excuse my interrupting …”)

Excuse my interrupting you.

  • A possessive before an -ing form is the formal pattern; in speech people often say excuse me for interrupting.物主代词 + 动名词作宾语：excuse my doing，正式说法；口语也说 excuse me for interrupting。

Sentence 12第 12 句 (begins开头 “I hate to …”)

  • be busy doing: be occupied with; would you mind doing is a polite request, and here the politeness is part of the joke.be busy doing：忙于做。would you mind doing：客气的请求，这里客气得带讽刺。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

must have done: guessing about the pastmust have done：对过去的推测
A modal plus have plus a past participle is a guess about the past: must have done when you are nearly sure, might or may have done when it is possible, and can't have done when you rule it out.
情态动词 + have + 过去分词，推测过去的事：must have done 一定做了；might/may have done 也许做了；can't have done 不可能做了。

In sentence 1 the narrator cannot remember the exact time, so he works it out.

本课：第 1 句，作者记不清时间，用 must have been 推断。

  • The ground is wet. It must have rained last night.
  • She can't have left yet; her coat is still here.
The -ing form as object after regret, mind, enjoy and excuse动名词作宾语：regret / mind / enjoy / excuse + doing
enjoy, mind, finish, avoid and excuse take an -ing form. regret doing looks back at something done, while regret to do announces bad news. A possessive before the -ing form names who does it, as in my interrupting.
enjoy, mind, finish, avoid, excuse 后接动名词；regret doing 后悔做过，regret to do 遗憾地要（告知）；动名词前可加物主代词表示逻辑主语（my interrupting）。

Sentences 9, 11 and 12 between them use four of these patterns.

本课：第 9 句 regretted answering、enjoy cleaning；第 11 句 my interrupting；第 12 句 mind coming。

  • I regret telling him the secret.
  • We regret to inform you that the flight is cancelled.
So do I and Neither do I
To agree with a positive statement, use so plus an auxiliary plus the subject; to agree with a negative one, use neither or nor. The auxiliary matches the first speaker's verb.
附和别人的肯定句用 So + 助动词 + 主语；附和否定句用 Neither/Nor + 助动词 + 主语。助动词要和原句一致。

In sentence 10 the policeman picks up the joke with this pattern.

本课：第 10 句，警察用 So do I 接住作者的玩笑。

  • 'I can swim.' 'So can I.'
  • 'I didn't see it.' 'Neither did I.'

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • It must have been about … when I … 我……的时候大概是……了
    It must have been nearly midnight when we got off the train.
  • I tried to … by …, but … 我想通过……来……，可是……
    I tried to open the jar by hitting it, but it would not move.
  • I regretted saying it at once 我马上就后悔说了那句话
    I regretted saying it at once, but it was too late.
  • Sorry to interrupt, but would you mind …? 抱歉打扰，不过您介意……吗？
    Sorry to interrupt, but would you mind moving your car?
  • I'd rather stay here, you see … 我宁愿待在这儿，你看……
    I'd rather stay here, you see, my bus is due.

In no more than 80 words, say what trouble the writer got into that night and how it ended.

用不超过 80 个词讲讲作者那天夜里遇到了什么麻烦，以及麻烦是怎么解决的。

Our model summary本站范文

Reaching home at about two in the morning, the writer could not wake his wife, so he climbed a ladder towards their bedroom. A policeman below asked, mockingly, why he was washing windows at that hour. The writer joked that he enjoyed it, and the policeman, playing along, invited him to the station. He shouted that he had forgotten his key. The noise woke his wife, and she put her head out of the window as the policeman started climbing.（80 词）
