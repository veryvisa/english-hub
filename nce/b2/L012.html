Lesson 12 · Goodbye and Good Luck 再见，一路顺风

📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 12 “Goodbye and Good Luck”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 12《Goodbye and Good Luck》

B1🧠 15 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
The writer's neighbour, Captain Charles Alison, leaves Portsmouth by sea tomorrow. He will be in his own little boat, Topsail, a well-known craft that has made the ocean crossing to America again and again. He sets off at eight to take part in a major race across the ocean and will be gone for two months. The writer's family are going down to the harbour early to see the boat and wave him off, and they are all very proud of him. The grammar is the simple future with will and the present perfect for experience.
作者的邻居查尔斯·艾利森船长明天要从朴次茅斯出海。他会驾着自己的小船「上桅帆号」——一条横渡过大西洋许多次的名船——早上八点启程，去参加一场重要的横渡大西洋比赛，要离开两个月。作者一家明早要去码头看船、送行，大家都为他骄傲。本课学一般将来时 will，以及现在完成时表示经历（has sailed … many times）。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. Where is Captain Alison heading, and by what means?艾利森船长要去哪里？怎么去？
  2. Why is the writer's family proud of him?为什么作者一家为他骄傲？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander for New Concept English (Longman, 1967); Captain Alison and Topsail are invented, though ocean races of this kind were real and much in the news.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为《新概念英语》（朗文，1967）自撰；艾利森船长和「上桅帆号」是虚构的，不过这类远洋比赛确有其事，当时常上新闻。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

The lesson was written at a time when long-distance sailing in small boats was making headlines in Britain. A race for single sailors across the Atlantic began in 1960, starting from the south coast of England and finishing in America, and it soon became a famous event. In the same decade, a sailor in his sixties sailed alone round the world with only one stop and was knighted on his return, which made such voyages a matter of national pride. Portsmouth, the setting of the lesson, is a historic naval port on the south coast. A neighbour with a small, well-known boat taking part in a big ocean race would have felt both exciting and believable to readers then.

这课写成的年代，小船远航在英国常上头条。1960 年开始了一项单人横渡大西洋的比赛，从英格兰南岸出发、到美洲终点，很快名声大噪。同一个十年里，一位六十多岁的航海家独自驾船环游世界、途中只停靠一次，回国后被封为爵士，让这类航行成了全国的骄傲。课文的场景朴次茅斯是英格兰南岸一座历史悠久的军港。一个邻居驾着小有名气的小船去参加远洋大赛，在当时的读者看来既激动人心又真实可信。

✨ How it is written赏析

The text looks forward, not back, and nearly every verb points to tomorrow. This gives it a feeling of quiet excitement, like the evening before a journey. The writer keeps the facts simple and practical: the time of departure, the place to meet, how long he will be away. The important fact, the race, is held back until the very end, so the last sentence gives a reason for all the plans before it and for the family's pride. The only past in the text belongs to the boat, whose many crossings give it a history. The title is the message the family will say at the harbour, so the lesson ends just before the moment it names.

课文向前看，不向后看，几乎每个动词都指向明天。这给它一种安静的兴奋感，像出远门的前一晚。作者把事实写得简单实用：几点出发、在哪儿见面、要离开多久。最重要的事实——那场比赛——一直扣到最后，于是最后一句为前面所有的安排、也为一家人的骄傲给出了理由。课文里唯一的过去属于那条船：它多次横渡，有了自己的历史。标题就是一家人到码头要说的话，所以课文停在它所命名的那一刻之前。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

Sending someone off on a long journey is a ritual in every culture, and the lesson captures its pleasant tension: pride mixed with a little worry. Ocean racing is still dangerous, and modern sailors carry tracking devices and satellite phones that a sailor of the 1960s did not have. For learners, the text is a useful model for talking about plans, because it shows how much can be said with will alone. Chinese readers may think of seeing a relative off at a station or an airport, and of the custom of saying a safe journey wish. The phrase in the title does the same job in English.

给远行的人送别，在每种文化里都是一种仪式，课文抓住了其中那种愉快的紧张：骄傲里掺着一点担心。远洋比赛至今仍有危险，现代水手带着定位设备和卫星电话，这是六十年代的航海者没有的。对学习者来说，这篇是谈论计划的好样板，它展示了光用 will 就能说出多少事。中国读者或许会想到到车站、机场送亲人，和那句「一路平安」的祝福。标题里的那句英语干的是同样的活。

💬 Discuss and write讨论与写作
  1. Write about a time you saw someone off on a long journey. Use will for their plans and the past simple for the day itself.
    写一次你为远行的人送别的经历：用 will 讲对方的计划，用一般过去时讲那一天。
  2. Would you like to sail alone across an ocean? Give two reasons.
    你愿意独自驾船横渡大洋吗？给出两个理由。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 1第 1 句 (begins开头 “Our neighbour, Captain …”)

  • will with the bare verb is the simple future, here with tomorrow.will + 动词原形：一般将来时，配 tomorrow。
  • The name between commas is apposition, telling us who the neighbour is.Captain Charles Alison 是同位语，说明邻居是谁。

Sentence 5第 5 句 (begins开头 “It has sailed …”)

  • has sailed many times: the present perfect for experience, counting up to now.has sailed … many times：现在完成时表经历（到现在为止多次）。
  • across: from one side to the other.across：横越（从一边到另一边）。

Sentence 7第 7 句 (begins开头 “so we'll have …”)

  • set out: start a journey. we'll is short for we will.set out：出发。we'll = we will。

Sentence 11第 11 句 (begins开头 “He will take …”)

  • take part in: be one of those who do something.take part in：参加。
  • Only the last sentence says why he is going, which explains the good luck of the title.最后一句才说出航的目的，回答了标题里的「一路顺风」。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

The simple future: will with the bare verb一般将来时：will + 动词原形
will with the bare verb shows something that is going to happen, often with tomorrow, next week or in two months. In speech it shortens to 'll, and the negative is won't.
will + 动词原形表示将来要发生的事，常配 tomorrow、next week、in two months 等；口语缩写 'll，否定 won't。

Almost every sentence uses will or 'll, because the whole text is about tomorrow.

本课：第 1、2、3、6、7、8、9、11 句都用 will / 'll，整篇讲「明天」的计划。

  • I will call you when I arrive.
  • It won't rain this afternoon.
The present perfect for experience: have done ... times现在完成时表经历：have done … times
have or has with the past participle and many times, three times, never or ever tells experience up to now, without saying when. Once a particular time is named, use the past simple instead.
have/has + 过去分词 + many times / three times / never / ever，表示到现在为止的经历，不说具体何时。说了具体时间就改用一般过去时。

Sentence 5 tells how often the boat has crossed, without saying in which years.

本课：第 5 句讲这条船到现在为止多次横渡大西洋，没说是哪几年。

  • I have read this book three times.
  • Have you ever eaten durian?

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • X will sail / leave from … tomorrow. X 明天从……出发。
    Our class will leave from the school gate tomorrow.
  • X has … across … many times. X 已经多次横越……
    She has flown across the Pacific many times.
  • X will set out at …, so we'll have lots of time. X……点出发，所以我们时间很充裕。
    The film will start at nine, so we'll have lots of time.
  • We are very proud of … 我们为……感到骄傲。
    We are very proud of our team.

In no more than 50 words, describe Captain Alison's plans and the family's plans for tomorrow.

Our model summary本站范文

Tomorrow our neighbour, Captain Alison, sets off from Portsmouth in Topsail, a little boat famous for crossing the Atlantic. He leaves at eight and will race across the ocean for two months. We will go to the harbour early to see him off. We are proud of him.（48 词）
