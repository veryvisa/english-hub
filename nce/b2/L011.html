Lesson 11 · One Good Turn Deserves Another 礼尚往来

📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 11 “One Good Turn Deserves Another”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 11《One Good Turn Deserves Another》

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
The writer is eating out when Tony Steele walks in. Tony once worked for a firm of lawyers and is now at a bank on a decent salary, yet he is forever getting money off friends and never repaying it, though the writer has never been one of his lenders. Tony joins him at his table, and while they eat the writer asks him for a twenty-pound loan. To his amazement Tony hands it over at once and then says that, as he has never had a penny from the writer, the writer can now settle his bill. The grammar is borrow against lend, and the time markers that go with the past simple, the present perfect and the present continuous.
作者在饭馆吃饭，托尼·斯蒂尔走了进来。托尼以前在律师事务所工作，现在在银行上班，薪水不错，却总向朋友借钱、从来不还；不过他从没向作者借过钱。他坐到作者桌边，作者趁他吃饭时开口向他借二十英镑，没想到他马上就给了——然后说：我从来没向你借过钱，所以现在你来付我这顿饭吧！本课学 borrow 和 lend 的区别，以及一般过去时、现在完成时、现在进行时的时间标志。

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander for New Concept English (Longman, 1967); the title is an old English proverb, and Tony Steele is invented.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为《新概念英语》（朗文，1967）自撰；标题是一句古老的英语谚语，托尼·斯蒂尔是虚构人物。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

The proverb in the title is centuries old and means that if someone helps you, you should help them in return. The lesson uses it with a twist, because Tony's good turn is hardly generous: he lends money only to get his dinner paid for. Twenty pounds was a real sum in the late 1960s, something like a week's pay for many workers, so the writer's request is not small and Tony's speed in handing it over is part of the surprise. The setting fits Tony's character. A man who has moved from a lawyer's office to a bank and earns well, yet still lives on loans, is the kind of charming freeloader that English comedy loves.

标题里的谚语已有几百年历史，意思是别人帮了你，你也该帮回去。课文用它时翻了个面：托尼的「善举」一点也不大方，他借钱只是为了让别人请他吃饭。二十世纪六十年代末，二十英镑不是小数目，大约相当于很多工人一周的工资，所以作者的请求不小，托尼爽快地掏钱也是意外的一部分。场景合乎托尼的性格：从律师事务所跳到银行、收入不错，却还靠借钱过日子，这种讨人喜欢的「蹭饭客」正是英国喜剧的最爱。

✨ How it is written赏析

The first half of the text is a character sketch, and each fact about Tony sets a trap for the reader. We learn that he borrows and never repays, so we expect him to refuse or to make excuses. We learn that he has never borrowed from the writer, which seems a small detail. Then the writer tests him and he passes at once, which surprises both the writer and us. Only in the last line do we see why that small detail mattered: Tony has been keeping score. The joke turns the proverb on its head. A good turn is supposed to be a free act of kindness, but Tony treats it as a trade.

课文前半是一幅人物素描，关于托尼的每个事实都给读者设了一个圈套。我们得知他借钱从来不还，于是以为他会拒绝或找借口；我们得知他从没向作者借过钱，这看起来是个小细节。接着作者试探他，他一口答应，作者和读者都吃了一惊。直到最后一行我们才明白那个小细节为什么要紧：托尼一直在记账。这个笑话把谚语倒过来用：「善举」本应是不求回报的好意，托尼却把它当成一笔交易。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The story asks a real question about friends and money: should we keep score? Most people feel uneasy when friends borrow and forget to repay, and most also feel uneasy when a favour is returned with an exact bill. Chinese readers will know the idea behind the title from the phrase about giving and returning courtesy, and from the custom of taking turns to treat friends to a meal. In many Chinese circles, arguing over who pays is a form of politeness, and being the one who pays is a matter of face. Tony's habit would look rude in almost any culture, which is why the ending still works for readers today.

这个故事提出一个关于朋友和金钱的真问题：该不该记账？朋友借钱忘了还，大多数人会不舒服；帮了个忙，对方却拿着一张精确的账单来「回报」，大多数人也会不舒服。中国读者对标题背后的意思不会陌生，「礼尚往来」讲的就是有来有往；朋友之间轮流请客也是常见的习惯。在很多中国人的圈子里，抢着买单是一种礼貌，能请客关乎面子。托尼的做法在几乎任何文化里都显得失礼，所以这个结尾对今天的读者依然奏效。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 1第 1 句 (begins开头 “I was having …”)

  • The long action is in the past continuous, and when brings in the short one in the past simple.过去进行时（长动作）+ when + 一般过去时（短动作）。

Sentence 5第 5 句 (begins开头 “but he always …”)

  • borrow sth from sb: take and use something of theirs for a time.borrow sth from sb：向某人借某物。
  • pay it back: with a pronoun, it goes between pay and back.pay it back：代词 it 要放在 pay 和 back 中间。

Sentence 8第 8 句 (begins开头 “While he was …”)

  • ask sb to do sth: request that someone does something.ask sb to do sth：请某人做某事。
  • lend sb sth: lend takes two objects.lend sb sth：把某物借给某人（双宾语）。

Sentence 11第 11 句 (begins开头 “Tony said, 'so …”)

  • have never borrowed: not once up to now, in the present perfect.have never borrowed：到现在为止从未借过（现在完成时）。
  • pay for sth: give money in return for it. Tony's reasoning is that, having never taken anything from the writer, he is owed a treat in return for the loan.pay for sth：为某物付钱。托尼的逻辑：他「从没欠过你」，所以借你钱就该换你请客。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

borrow and lendborrow 与 lend
borrow means to take for a time, and its subject is the user: borrow sth from sb. lend means to give for a time, and its subject is the owner: lend sb sth or lend sth to sb.
borrow 是「借入」，主语是用东西的人：borrow sth from sb；lend 是「借出」，主语是东西的主人：lend sb sth / lend sth to sb。

Sentences 5, 7 and 10 use borrow for Tony taking money; sentence 8 uses lend for Tony giving it.

本课：第 5、7、10 句是 borrow（托尼借入）；第 8 句是 lend（作者请托尼借出）。

  • Can I borrow your pen? — Sure, I'll lend it to you.
  • She lent me her notes, so I borrowed them for a day.
Time markers: years ago, now and never时态的时间标志：years ago、now、never
years ago, last year and yesterday go with the past simple. now and at the moment go with the present continuous. never, ever, already and just often go with the present perfect for up to now. always and every day go with the present simple for habits.
years ago、last year、yesterday 配一般过去时；now、at the moment 配现在进行时；never、ever、already、just 常配现在完成时（到现在为止）；always、every day 配一般现在时（习惯）。

Each of sentences 2, 3, 5 and 7 shows one marker with its tense.

本课：第 2 句 years ago → worked；第 3 句 now → is working；第 5 句 always → borrows；第 7 句 never → has borrowed。

  • I met her two years ago. She is living in Ottawa now.
  • I have never been to Japan, but my sister goes there every year.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • I was having … when X came in. 我正在……的时候，X 走了进来。
    I was having a shower when the phone rang.
  • X always borrows … and never pays it back. X 总是借……从来不还。
    My cousin always borrows my bike and never brings it back.
  • X asked Y to lend him/her … X 请 Y 借给自己……
    She asked her brother to lend her his camera.
  • To my surprise, X … immediately. 出乎我的意料，X 立刻就……
    To my surprise, the manager agreed immediately.
