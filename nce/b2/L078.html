Lesson 78 · The Last One 最后一支烟

📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 78 “The Last One”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 78《The Last One》

B1🧠 20 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
After an article on what smoking does to your body, the writer lights up to steady himself, certain it is his final one. He then lasts a week without, and his wife has a hard time: he is cross and hungry all day, the classic signs of quitting. Friends keep pushing cigarettes at him and laugh openly at his sweets. On day seven, at a party full of smokers, an old friend presses him once too often, and he gives in, ashamed and happy at once. His wife is relieved that life is back to normal, and the friend's comment supplies the final joke. This is a self-mocking story about quitting. The grammar is keep on doing, make no effort to, more than one can bear and two uses of as.
「我」读了一篇讲吸烟危害健康的文章，为了压压惊，先点了一支烟，心想这一定是最后一支。整整一周没抽，妻子跟着受罪：「我」脾气坏、胃口奇大，正是戒烟的人常见的样子。朋友们不停地递烟递雪茄，看「我」掏出一包糖来就毫不掩饰地笑。第七天去参加聚会，周围人人都在抽，老朋友布赖恩一劝，「我」再也受不了，心虚地接过一支，抽得心满意足。妻子倒很高兴，一切又恢复了正常。最后一句点题：照布赖恩的说法，戒烟是世界上最容易的事——他自己就戒过好多次。本课是一个自嘲的戒烟故事，学 keep on doing、make no effort to、more than one can bear 和 as 的两种用法。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. How long did the writer manage to go without smoking?作者戒烟坚持了多久？
  2. Why was his wife pleased when he started again?妻子为什么在作者重新抽烟后反而高兴？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Unsigned and written for the course. The closing joke is an old one, often credited to Mark Twain in the form that quitting is easy because he had done it a thousand times, though no reliable source for those words has been found.

本课未署名、为教材自撰。结尾的笑话很老，常被说成是马克·吐温的话（戒烟很容易，我戒过一千次），但这句话一直没有找到可靠出处。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

The article in the first sentence sounds like the reports of the early sixties, when doctors in Britain and the United States published official findings linking cigarettes to lung cancer and heart disease. The symptoms the writer lists are real: in the first days after stopping, many people feel irritable and restless and eat more, because nicotine dulls the appetite. Studies of people trying to stop usually find that most attempts fail and that it often takes several tries before one sticks. Social pressure is one of the strongest reasons people start again, which is exactly what happens at the party.

第一句提到的文章，听起来像六十年代初的那些报告：当时英国和美国的医学界发表了官方结论，把吸烟与肺癌、心脏病联系起来。作者列出的症状是真实的：停烟最初几天，许多人烦躁、坐立不安、吃得更多，因为尼古丁会抑制食欲。对戒烟者的研究通常发现，大多数尝试都会失败，往往要试好几次才能成功一次。社交压力是人们重新抽起来的最主要原因之一，这正是聚会上发生的事。

✨ How it is written赏析

The story is a joke told against oneself, and its humour comes from honesty about weakness. The writer begins with an irony: he smokes in order to recover from reading about the harm of smoking. He then reports the week in plain past tenses, with comic detail, the sweets, the friends' open laughter, his wife's suffering, so we feel the pressure building. The surrender takes one sentence, and the adverbs guiltily and with satisfaction pull in opposite directions, which is how relapse really feels. The ending passes the joke to Brian, so the last laugh is gentle rather than bitter.

这个故事是拿自己开玩笑，幽默来自对弱点的坦白。作者一开头就是反讽：读了吸烟有害的文章，为了平复心情去抽烟。接着他用朴素的过去时讲这一周，配上滑稽的细节——那包糖、朋友毫不掩饰的笑、妻子受的罪——让我们感到压力一点点积累。投降只用了一句话，guiltily 和 with satisfaction 两个状语往相反方向拉，复吸的真实感受就是这样。结尾把笑话交给布赖恩，所以最后的笑是温和的，而不是苦涩的。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The lesson treats failure lightly, and that is both its charm and its weak point. Today we know far more about how addictive nicotine is, and few doctors would call a single week a real attempt. The useful lesson is about friends and settings: the writer did not fail alone but at a party where everyone was smoking and an old friend kept offering. In China, offering cigarettes has long been a way to show respect and build friendship, and refusing one can feel rude, so a person who wants to quit may need a polite, ready answer and a change of setting more than strong will. Laughing at a failed attempt, as this writer does, can also help you try again.

这一课把失败写得很轻松，这既是它的魅力，也是它的弱点。今天我们对尼古丁的成瘾性了解得多得多，很少有医生会把短短一周当成真正的戒烟尝试。真正有用的启示在于朋友和环境：作者不是独自失败的，而是在一个人人抽烟、老朋友不停递烟的聚会上失败的。在中国，敬烟长期以来是表示尊重、拉近关系的方式，拒绝一支烟会显得失礼，所以想戒烟的人也许更需要一句现成的、礼貌的推辞，以及换一个环境，而不只是意志力。像作者这样笑看一次失败，也能帮你再试一次。

💬 Discuss and write讨论与写作
  1. How can you say no politely when someone offers you something you are trying to give up? Write two answers.
    有人递给你一样你正在戒的东西时，怎样礼貌地拒绝？写两种说法。
  2. Have you ever tried to break a habit? Tell the story with keep on doing and more than I could bear.
    你有没有试过改掉一个习惯？用 keep on doing 和 more than I could bear 讲讲这段经历。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 1第 1 句 (begins开头 “After reading an …”)

  • After reading: a preposition plus an -ing form, with the same subject as the main clause; entitled: a past participle meaning 'with the title'.After reading …：介词 after + 动名词，主语与主句一致。entitled：过去分词作定语，「题为……的」。
  • The infinitive gives the purpose, and the joke is that he smokes to recover from reading about smoking.to calm my nerves：不定式表目的。笑点：为了压住看完文章的惊慌，偏偏去抽烟。

Sentence 4第 4 句 (begins开头 “I had all …”)

  • The -ing phrase describes someone: a person who is in the middle of quitting.someone giving up smoking：现在分词短语修饰 someone，「一个正在戒烟的人」。
  • give up doing: stop a habit; the colon introduces the list of signs.give up doing：戒掉、放弃做。冒号后列出症状。

Sentence 6第 6 句 (begins开头 “They made no …”)

  • make no effort to do: not even try to do.make no effort to do：一点也不想去做。
  • whenever: every time that.whenever：每当。

Sentence 9第 9 句 (begins开头 “When my old …”)

  • more than one can bear: beyond what one is able to put up with.more than one can bear：超出所能忍受的限度。
  • urge someone to do: press them hard to do it.urge sb to do：力劝某人做。

Sentence 13第 13 句 (begins开头 “He himself has …”)

  • himself stresses the person; the irony is that quitting many times means never succeeding.himself：强调「他本人」。反讽：戒过好多次，等于一次也没成功。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

Verbs followed by -ing: give up, keep on, stop动词 + doing：give up / keep on / stop
give up doing means stop a habit; keep or keep on doing means go on doing again and again; stop doing ends an action, while stop to do means stop in order to do. After a preposition, too, use the -ing form.
give up doing 戒掉；keep (on) doing 不停地做；stop doing 停止做（stop to do 是停下来去做）；介词后也用 doing。

Sentence 1 has an -ing form after after, sentence 4 has giving up, and in sentence 5 the friends keep on offering.

本课：第 1 句介词 after 后接 reading；第 4 句 giving up smoking；第 5 句朋友们 kept on offering。

  • He kept on asking the same question.
  • She gave up eating meat last year.
Two meanings of as: because, and in the way thatas 的两种意思：因为 / 正如
as before a reason means because, though more lightly than because; as with a verb of saying or thinking means 'in the way that'.
as + 原因从句：因为（语气比 because 轻）；as + 说话/认为类动词：正如……所说/所想。

In sentence 2 as gives the reason; in sentence 12 it brings in Brian's remark and means 'just as'.

本课：第 2 句的 as 说明原因；第 12 句的 as 引出布赖恩的话，是「正如」。

  • As it was late, we took a taxi.
  • As my teacher always says, practice makes perfect.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • After reading …, I … to calm my nerves. 读完……之后，我……来压压惊
    After hearing the news, I made some tea to calm my nerves.
  • I had all the usual signs of someone …ing. 我有……的人的一切常见迹象
    I had all the usual signs of someone catching a cold.
  • X did not even try to hide … whenever … 每当……，X 毫不掩饰……
    The children did not even try to hide their boredom whenever Uncle Joe told his stories.
  • … that was more than she could stand. ……她实在受不了了
    When the baby started crying again, that was more than she could stand.
  • As X said, nothing in the world is easier than … 正如 X 所说，世上没有比……更容易的事
    As my sister said, nothing in the world is easier than starting a diet.

In no more than 80 words, tell how the writer tried to give up smoking and how it ended.

Points a good summary covers好摘要要覆盖的要点

Our model summary本站范文

After reading about the dangers of smoking, the writer had what he thought was his final cigarette. For a week he did not smoke, but he became bad-tempered and hungry, and his wife suffered. His friends kept offering him cigarettes and laughed at his sweets. At a party a week later, everyone was smoking, and when Brian pressed him he gave in. His wife was relieved. Brian joked that quitting was easy: he had done it many times.（78 词）
