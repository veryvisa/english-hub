Lesson 58 · A Blessing in Disguise 因祸得福

📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 58 “A Blessing in Disguise”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 58《A Blessing in Disguise》

B1🧠 17 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
Frinley is a very small village that, people claim, owns a cursed tree. Since a newspaper wrote about it, many more visitors have been arriving. The tree went in beside the church half a century ago, but its dark name is quite new. Local belief says that touching it brings misfortune and picking one of its leaves brings death, and many villagers think it has already taken several lives. People have begged the vicar to get rid of it, but he keeps saying no, because people now travel from all over the country to look at it, and the visitors bring money. Despite every warning, tourists strip off leaves and carve their names in the bark, and so far nobody has dropped dead. The grammar is be said to do, It is only ... that, have sth done, and the present perfect continuous.
弗林利小村据说有一棵「受了诅咒的树」。自从报纸提到这棵树，来弗林利的游客多了起来。树是五十年前种在教堂旁边的，可直到近几年才落下坏名声：传说谁碰了它就会倒霉，谁摘一片叶子就会死。很多村民相信这棵树已经害了好几个人。有人请牧师把树砍掉，他至今没答应，理由是这棵树是一笔有用的收入来源——全国各地的游客都赶来看它。尽管有这么多说法，游客们照样摘叶子、在树干上刻名字，到目前为止，还没有一个人突然死掉！本课学 be said to do / It is said that、强调句 it is only … that、have sth done 和现在完成（进行）时。

  1. How did the tree become famous?这棵树是怎么出名的？
  2. Why will the vicar not cut the tree down?牧师为什么不肯砍树？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander for New Concept English (Longman, 1967); Frinley and its tree are invented.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为《新概念英语》（朗文，1967）自撰；弗林利村和那棵树都是虚构的。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

English folklore is full of trees and stones said to bring bad luck, and many real villages make the most of such legends. Churchyards often hold the oldest trees in a parish, especially yews, and some are linked to strange local stories. The title is an English idiom: a blessing in disguise is something that looks bad at first but turns out to be good. The curse, which ought to harm the village, brings it fame and money instead. Today the same thing happens on a larger scale through dark tourism, in which visitors travel to places linked with death or disaster.

英国民间传说里满是据说会带来厄运的树和石头，很多真实的村庄也善于利用这类传说。教堂墓地里常有教区最古老的树，尤其是紫杉，有些就附着离奇的地方故事。标题是一句英语习语：a blessing in disguise 指起初看来是坏事、结果却是好事。本该祸害村子的诅咒，反倒给它带来了名气和收入。今天同样的事以更大的规模发生，叫作「黑色旅游」：游客专门前往与死亡或灾难相关的地方。

✨ How it is written赏析

The lesson is told almost entirely through hearsay. Nearly every claim comes wrapped in it is said, people believe or villagers think, and the writer never says whether he agrees. That cool distance lets the last sentence land as a joke: after all the warnings, not a single tourist has been struck dead. The vicar is the most interesting figure. He is a churchman who might be expected to oppose superstition or to fear it, yet he defends the tree on practical grounds, as a source of income. The story hints, gently, that he understands the village better than the villagers.

这一课几乎全靠传闻讲述。几乎每一个说法都裹在「据说」「人们相信」「村民认为」之中，作者从不表态自己信不信。这种冷静的距离让最后一句像笑话一样落地：警告了半天，还没有一个游客暴毙。牧师是最耐人寻味的人物：按说神职人员要么反对迷信，要么畏惧它，他却从实际出发为这棵树辩护——它是收入来源。故事委婉地暗示：他比村民更懂这个村子。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The story shows how a belief can survive without evidence when it is useful or exciting. Tourists clearly do not believe the curse very strongly, because they break every rule, yet they still travel to see the tree. Many places in China and elsewhere earn money from legends in the same way, and that is not always harmful, as long as nobody is frightened or cheated. The lesson also invites a sharper question: when many people in a village believe a tree has killed someone, are real deaths being explained away by a story instead of by their true causes?

故事说明了一种信念在没有证据的情况下，只要有用或有趣，就能流传下去。游客显然并不怎么信这个诅咒，因为他们把禁忌全犯了一遍，却仍然大老远赶来看树。中国和别处许多地方也同样靠传说挣钱，只要没人被吓到或被骗，这未必有害。这一课还引出一个更尖锐的问题：当一个村子里许多人相信一棵树害死过人，真实的死亡是不是被一个故事搪塞过去了，而没有去追查真正的原因？

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 1第 1 句 (begins开头 “The tiny village …”)

  • be said to do: people say, without a clear source.be said to do：据说……，消息来源不确定。
  • the village of Frinley: of joins the name to the place.the village of Frinley：of 表示同位，「弗林利村」。

Sentence 4第 4 句 (begins开头 “The tree was …”)

  • it is ... that: a cleft sentence stressing only in recent years.it is … that：强调句，强调「只是近几年」。
  • has gained: the present perfect, built up until now.has gained：现在完成时，名声积累到现在。

Sentence 5第 5 句 (begins开头 “It is said …”)

  • It is said that: people say that.It is said that …：据说……。
  • if plus present, will: a real condition.if + 一般现在时, will …：真实条件句。

Sentence 7第 7 句 (begins开头 “The vicar has …”)

  • have sth done: arrange for someone else to do it.have sth done：请别人把……做了，这里是请人砍树。
  • has been asked: the present perfect passive.has been asked：现在完成时被动。

Sentence 9第 9 句 (begins开头 “as tourists have …”)

  • as: because.as：因为。
  • have been coming: the present perfect continuous, visitors keep arriving.have been coming：现在完成进行时，游客一直不断地来。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

be said to do and It is said that
To report what people say, use It is said that plus a clause, or subject plus is said plus to do; for the past use to have done. Believe, think, report and know work the same way.
转述传闻用两种结构：It is said that + 从句；或 主语 + is said + to do（说过去的事用 to have done）。同类动词：believe、think、report、know。

Sentence 1 uses is said to and sentence 5 uses It is said that; the whole legend rests on hearsay.

本课：第 1 句用 is said to，第 5 句用 It is said that，一个村子的传说全靠「据说」撑着。

  • It is said that the castle is haunted.
  • He is said to have been a great singer.
have sth done: get someone else to do ithave sth done：请人做某事
have plus object plus past participle means you arrange for someone else to do it; get sth done means the same.
have + 宾语 + 过去分词：让/请别人把某事做好（自己不动手）；也可用 get sth done。

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • X is said to … 据说 X……
    This little shop is said to sell the best bread in town.
  • It is only lately that … 只是最近几年才……
    It is only lately that the lake has become popular.
  • X has been asked to …, but so far X has refused. 有人要求 X……，但 X 至今没答应
    The mayor has been asked to close the market, but up to now she has said no.
  • X points out that … is a useful source of … X 指出……是有用的……来源
    My aunt points out that the garden is a good source of vegetables.
  • Up to now, not a single X has … 到目前为止，没有一个 X……
    Up to now, not a single student has failed.

Our model summary本站范文

Frinley is said to have a cursed tree, and since a newspaper wrote about it, more people have come to see it. It was planted fifty years ago but has only lately gained a bad name: touching it brings bad luck and picking a leaf brings death. The vicar refuses to cut it down, as visitors bring in money. Tourists pick leaves and carve their names, yet nobody has died.（70 词）
