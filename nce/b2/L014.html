Lesson 14 · Do You Speak English 你会讲英语吗？

📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 14 “Do You Speak English”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 14《Do You Speak English》

B1🧠 15 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
Last year the writer had a funny experience. Driving away from a little village in southern France towards the next town, he sees a young man by the road who waves for a lift. Once the stranger is in the car, the writer greets him in French and gets a French greeting back. Since he knows hardly any French beyond a handful of words, the two men sit in silence the whole way. As they near the town, the passenger slowly asks whether the driver speaks English, and the writer soon finds out that the young man is English too. The grammar is the past perfect with after, as soon as and when, together with neither and apart from.
去年作者有过一次好笑的经历。他离开法国南部一个小村子，开车去下一个镇，路上一个年轻人向他招手搭车。年轻人一上车，作者就用法语跟他说早上好，对方也用法语回答。作者除了几个单词，根本不会法语，于是一路上两人谁也没说话。快到镇上时，年轻人突然一字一顿地问：你会讲英语吗？原来他自己就是英国人！本课学过去完成时配 after、as soon as、when，以及 neither、apart from 的用法。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. Could the young man speak English?那个年轻人会讲英语吗？
  2. Why did neither of them say anything during the drive?为什么两个人一路上都没说话？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander for New Concept English (Longman, 1967); an invented travel anecdote.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为《新概念英语》（朗文，1967）自撰，是一则虚构的旅途趣事。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

Hitchhiking was a common way for young people to travel in Europe in the 1950s and 1960s. Students crossed whole countries this way, standing by the road with a small bag and sometimes a sign, and drivers who stopped for them were thought of as friendly rather than foolish. The south of France was a favourite area for British holiday-makers, many of whom drove there in their own cars. In those years most British adults had learnt some French at school but few could hold a conversation in it, so the writer's position, a handful of polite words and nothing more, was a very ordinary one.

二十世纪五六十年代，搭便车是欧洲年轻人常见的旅行方式。学生们这样走遍整个国家：背个小包站在路边，有时举块牌子；停车载他们的司机被看作友善而不是犯傻。法国南部是英国度假者最喜欢的去处之一，很多人开着自己的车去。那些年，大多数英国成年人在学校学过一点法语，能用它聊天的却很少，所以作者的处境——几句客气话，此外一窍不通——非常普通。

✨ How it is written赏析

The joke depends on what each man does not know about the other. The writer gives us the key fact early: he speaks only a few words of French. The young man's reply in French means the writer cannot tell where he is from, and the reader is kept in the same ignorance. The long silence in the car is told in a single plain sentence, but it is easy to imagine: two men side by side, each too polite or too shy to try. The final line, slow and careful, shows that the passenger has also been struggling with a foreign language, and the reversal arrives in one short clause. The past perfect keeps the steps of the journey in clear order without slowing the story.

笑点在于两个人各自不知道对方的什么。作者一早就交代了关键事实：他只会几个法语单词。年轻人用法语回答，作者便无从判断他从哪儿来，读者也被蒙在同样的无知里。车里漫长的沉默只用一句平平的话写出，却很容易想象：两个人并排坐着，各自都太客气或太害羞，不敢开口。最后那句慢吞吞、小心翼翼的话，说明乘客一直也在跟一门外语较劲，而翻转只用一个短分句就完成了。过去完成时让旅途的每一步井然有序，又不拖慢故事。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The story is still true to life for anyone who has used a second language abroad. Many people start in the local language to be polite and then get stuck, while the other person does the same, and both lose a chance to talk. The lesson suggests a simple lesson: if a conversation stops, try another language early. Chinese learners of English may recognise the opposite case, when two Chinese speakers meet abroad and keep talking in English out of habit or shyness. The fear of making a mistake can keep people silent longer than the lack of words does.

对任何在国外用过第二语言的人来说，这个故事至今很真实。很多人出于礼貌先用当地语言开口，然后卡住，对方也一样，于是双方都错过了交谈的机会。课文给出一个简单的道理：谈话一停，早点换一种语言试试。学英语的中国人也许见过相反的情况：两个中国人在国外碰面，出于习惯或害羞，一直用英语聊。怕说错的心理，往往比词汇不够更能让人沉默。

💬 Discuss and write讨论与写作

    你有没有因为语言问题闹过笑话？用过去时讲出来。
  2. Retell the drive from the young man's point of view: what was he thinking during the silence?
    从那个年轻人的角度复述这段车程：沉默时他在想什么？

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentence 2第 2 句 (begins开头 “After I had …”)

  • after with had done: leaving came first and driving on came second, so the earlier event takes the past perfect.after + had done：先离开，后开车，先发生的用过去完成时。
  • drive on: keep driving.drive on：继续往前开。

Sentence 6第 6 句 (begins开头 “As soon as …”)

  • as soon as with had done: the moment that was finished.as soon as + had done：一……就……。
  • in the same language: in French as well.in the same language：用同一种语言。

Sentence 10第 10 句 (begins开头 “Neither of us …”)

  • apart from: not counting. not at all: not even a little.apart from：除了。not … at all：一点也不。
  • neither of with a plural pronoun: not one and not the other.neither of + 复数代词：两人都不。

Sentence 13第 13 句 (begins开头 “As I soon …”)

  • as I soon learnt: a phrase dropped in to say how he found out.as I soon learnt：我很快就知道了。as 引导插入语。
  • himself adds weight: he too was English.himself：强调「他本人」也是英国人。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

The past perfect with after, as soon as and when过去完成时配 after / as soon as / when
With two past actions, the earlier one takes had done and the later one the past simple. After after and as soon as the order is already clear, so the past simple is also possible, but the past perfect stresses that the first action was complete.
过去两个动作，先发生的用 had done，后发生的用一般过去时。after、as soon as 已经说明了先后，用一般过去时也可以，但用过去完成时更清楚地强调「先完成」。

Sentences 2, 6 and 11 each put the earlier event in the past perfect.

本课：第 2 句用 after，第 6 句用 as soon as，第 11 句用 when，先发生的都是 had done。

  • After she had finished her homework, she went out.
  • As soon as the guests had left, we started cleaning.
neither, either and apart from
neither of with a plural noun or pronoun means not one and not the other, and usually takes a singular verb. either means one or the other. apart from means not counting, like except.
neither of + 复数名词或代词 = 两者都不（动词常用单数）；either = 两者中任一个；apart from = 除了（= except）。

Sentence 10 uses neither of and sentence 8 apart from.

本课：第 10 句 Neither of us，第 8 句 Apart from a few words。

  • Neither of the answers is correct.
  • Apart from the rain, it was a perfect day.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • I had an amusing experience … 我有过一次有趣的经历……
    I had an amusing experience at the airport last winter.
  • After I had …, I … 我……之后，就……
    After I had locked the door, I realised the key was inside.
  • As soon as X had …, … X 一……就……
    As soon as the bell had rung, the students ran out.
  • Neither of us … during … ……期间我们俩谁也没……
    Neither of us slept during the flight.

Our model summary本站范文

Last year, while driving in southern France, I gave a young man a lift. I greeted him in French and he answered in French. As I know almost no French, we sat in silence. Near the town he slowly asked if I spoke English. He was English too!（48 词）
