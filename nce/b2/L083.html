Lesson 83 · After the Elections 选举之后

📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 83 “After the Elections”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 83《After the Elections》

B1🧠 16 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
A former prime minister has lost the election, left politics and moved abroad. The writer's friend Patrick, who has always hated that man's party, calls at the house and asks the policeman at the door whether the old leader lives there. He is told that he has gone overseas since his defeat. Patrick comes back the next day with the very same question, and gets the same reply from a slightly wary officer. On the third day he asks once more, and the policeman explodes, repeating the whole story. Patrick's reply reveals his motive: he simply enjoys hearing it. This is a small political joke built on repetition. The grammar is reported speech and tense changes, hear someone do, the day before yesterday and shortened clauses after though.
前首相温特沃思·莱恩在最近的大选中落败，现在退出政坛，出国去了。「我」的朋友帕特里克一向狂热地反对莱恩的激进进步党。选举结束后，帕特里克到前首相家门口，问莱恩先生是不是住在这里，值勤的警察告诉他：自从落选，前首相已经出国了。第二天他又去，同一个警察正慢慢走过门口，他又问了同样的问题；警察有点起疑，还是照样回答。第三天他再去，一字不差地再问一遍，警察终于发火，大声说昨天、前天都告诉过你了。帕特里克答：「我知道，可我就是爱听你这么说！」本课是一个靠重复制造笑点的政治小笑话，学 间接引语的时态后移、hear sb do、the day before yesterday 和 though 引导的省略从句。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. What was Patrick's real reason for asking three times?帕特里克为什么一遍又一遍地问同一个问题？
  2. How did the policeman's attitude change from day to day?警察的态度是怎样一步步变化的？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Unsigned and written for the course. The names and the party are invented; the joke itself is an old political anecdote that circulates in many versions.

本课未署名、为教材自撰。人名和党名都是虚构的；这个笑话本身是一个流传很广、有许多版本的政治笑话。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

The best-known version is told about the Soviet Union: a man goes to a government office every day and asks for a famous leader who has died, and when the guard finally shouts that the leader is dead, the man says he knows, he just loves to hear it. Similar stories have been told about kings, dictators and ordinary politicians in many countries, and folklorists treat it as one of the travelling jokes that change names but keep the same shape. The textbook moves it to a democracy, where the leader has not died but simply lost an election and left the country, which makes it much milder. Its invented party name, joining radical and progressive, sounds like the kind of party people love to argue about without pointing to any real one.

最有名的版本讲的是苏联：一个人每天到一个政府机关去找一位已经去世的著名领导人；看门人终于大喊「他死了」，那人说他知道，他只是爱听这句话。类似的故事在许多国家被套在国王、独裁者和普通政客身上，民俗学者把它看作那种换了人名、结构不变、到处流传的笑话。教材把它搬到一个民主国家：领导人没有死，只是选举落败、出了国，这就温和了许多。那个把「激进」和「进步」拼在一起的虚构党名，听上去正像人们爱争论的那类党派，却不指向任何真实的政党。

✨ How it is written赏析

The joke depends on a rule of three. The first visit sets the pattern, the second repeats it with a small change, the policeman is suspicious, and the third pushes it until it breaks. The writer reports the first two visits in indirect speech, which keeps them flat and quick, and then switches to direct speech for the policeman's outburst, so his voice suddenly fills the scene. That change of style is the climb before the drop. The punch line is short and turns the meaning of everything before it: we thought Patrick was confused, and in fact he understood perfectly. A good joke, like a good story, hides its key fact, here Patrick's motive, until the last line.

这个笑话靠的是「三次法则」。第一次拜访立下模式，第二次带着一点变化重复——警察起了疑心——第三次把模式推到断裂。作者用间接引语写前两次拜访，让它们平淡而快速，然后换成直接引语写警察的爆发，于是他的声音一下子充满了场景。这种文体变化就是落下前的那一段爬升。包袱很短，却把前面的一切意思都翻过来：我们以为帕特里克糊涂了，原来他再明白不过。好笑话和好故事一样，把关键事实——这里是帕特里克的动机——藏到最后一行。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The joke is funny because many people know the feeling: taking pleasure in an opponent's defeat. That pleasure is ordinary, but it is worth noticing how it can become a habit, where politics is less about ideas than about enjoying the other side's losses. In countries with elections, a defeated leader who quietly leaves office is in fact a sign of a healthy system, and the policeman protecting the empty house is doing his job, not taking sides. For learners, the story is also a neat model of reported speech: try retelling it entirely in direct speech, and you will see how much longer and slower it becomes.

这个笑话之所以好笑，是因为许多人都熟悉那种感觉：从对手的失败中得到乐趣。这种乐趣很平常，但值得留意它如何变成一种习惯——政治不再关乎理念，而在于欣赏对方的失败。在有选举的国家，一个落败后安静离任的领导人，其实正是制度健康的标志；守着空房子的警察是在尽职，而不是站队。对学英语的人来说，这个故事也是间接引语的一个好样板：试着全部用直接引语复述一遍，你会发现它变得又长又慢。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 1第 1 句 (begins开头 “The former Prime …”)

  • A name in apposition says who the former leader is.同位语说明 former Prime Minister 是谁。
  • was defeated: the past simple passive, here 'lost'.was defeated：一般过去时被动，「落选了」。

Sentence 5第 5 句 (begins开头 “When he asked …”)

  • asked if: a reported yes-no question, with statement order.asked if：间接一般疑问句，用 if/whether，陈述语序。
  • had gone: has gone shifts back to the past perfect in reported speech.had gone：直接引语 has gone 变成间接引语时后移为过去完成时。

Sentence 8第 8 句 (begins开头 “Though a little …”)

  • though plus an adjective: he was has been left out, which is possible when both parts share a subject.though + 形容词：省略了 he was，主从句主语一致时可省。

Sentence 11第 11 句 (begins开头 “'I told you …”)

  • the day before yesterday: two days ago.the day before yesterday：前天。

Sentence 14第 14 句 (begins开头 “'I know,' answered …”)

  • hear someone do: hear the whole action; the punch line is that he already knew and only wanted to hear it said.hear sb do：听见某人做（整个过程）。笑点：他早就知道，问只是为了听人说莱恩输了。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

Reported speech: shifting tenses back间接引语：时态后移
When the reporting verb is past, the tense in the reported words moves back: present to past, present perfect to past perfect, will to would. Yes-no questions use if or whether with statement order.
主句是过去时（said、told、asked），引语里的时态往后退一步：现在 → 过去，现在完成 → 过去完成，will → would；一般疑问句用 if/whether，陈述语序。

In sentence 5 both question and answer are reported and shifted back; in sentences 11 to 13 the policeman's own words are quoted directly and keep their tenses.

本课：第 5 句的问句和回答都后移了；第 11–13 句警察说的是直接引语，保留原时态。

  • "I am tired." → She said she was tired.
  • "Have you seen my bag?" → He asked if I had seen his bag.
hear or see plus an object plus do or doinghear / see + 宾语 + do / doing
hear or see someone do means you heard or saw the whole action; hear or see someone doing means you caught it in progress.
hear/see sb do：听见/看见某人做了某事（全过程）；hear/see sb doing：听见/看见某人正在做。

In the last sentence Patrick wants to hear the whole sentence said, so the bare infinitive is used.

本课：最后一句帕特里克说爱听警察说那句话——听的是完整的一句。

  • I heard her sing a song.
  • I saw him crossing the road.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • X has always been a … opponent of … X 一向是……的……反对者
    My uncle has always been a fierce opponent of the new road.
  • When X asked if …, the … told him that … X 问……是否……时，……告诉他……
    When I asked if the shop was open, the man told me that it had closed.
  • Though a little … this time, X … 这一次虽然有点……，X 还是……
    Though a little tired this time, the runner finished the race.
  • On the third day, X went back once more and … 第三天，X 又一次回去，……
    On the third day, the boy went back once more and found his kite.
  • Yes, I know, but I enjoy hearing you … 我知道，可我就爱听你……
    Yes, I know, but I enjoy hearing you sing it again.

Our model summary本站范文

Mr Lane, the former Prime Minister, lost the election and went to live abroad. Patrick, who had always hated his party, went to Mr Lane's house and asked the policeman there whether Mr Lane lived there. He was told that he had gone abroad. Patrick returned the next day and the day after that with the same question. At last the policeman lost his temper. Patrick explained that he knew the answer but loved to hear it.（77 词）
