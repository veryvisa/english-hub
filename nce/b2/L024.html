Lesson 24 · It Could be Worse 还算走运

📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 24 “It Could be Worse”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 24《It Could be Worse》

B1🧠 15 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
The writer goes into the hotel manager's office and sits down, very upset: he has just lost fifty pounds that he left in his room. The manager is kind but helpless, observes that everyone loses money nowadays, and begins to grumble about how bad the world has become, until a knock at the door stops him. A girl comes in and lays an envelope on the desk. Inside is fifty pounds, which she picked up outside the writer's door. The writer points out to the manager that honest people still exist. The grammar is the past perfect, the passive was interrupted, and start to do and complain about.
作者走进旅馆经理的办公室坐下。他刚丢了 50 英镑，心里很难过：钱放在房间里，现在不见了。经理很同情，却也无能为力，说如今人人都在丢钱，接着就抱怨起这个邪恶的世界来——话没说完，就被敲门声打断了。一个姑娘进来，把一个信封放在桌上，里面正是 50 英镑，她说是在这位先生房门外捡到的。作者对经理说：「看来这世上还是有诚实的！」本课学过去完成时、被动语态 was interrupted 和 start to do / complain about。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. Had someone really stolen the writer's money?作者的钱是被偷了吗？
  2. Why does the knock at the door come at just the right moment?经理的抱怨为什么被打断得正是时候？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander for New Concept English (Longman, 1967); the hotel, the manager and the girl are invented.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为《新概念英语》（朗文，1967）自撰；旅馆、经理和那个姑娘都是虚构的。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

Fifty pounds was a serious amount of money in the mid-sixties, around the price of a good week's holiday or a few weeks' pay for many workers, so the writer's distress is real. Hotels then rarely had safes in rooms, and guests were told to leave money at the front desk; leaving cash in a bedroom was a known risk. Studies of lost wallets in recent years have tested the lesson's ending directly. One large experiment, published in the journal Science in 2019, left thousands of wallets in public places in forty countries and found that people returned them more often when they held more money, which surprised many economists.

在六十年代中期，五十英镑是一笔不小的钱，相当于一次不错的一周度假，或很多工人几周的工资，所以作者的难过是真实的。那时旅馆房间里很少有保险箱，客人会被告知把钱寄存在前台；把现金留在卧室里是公认的风险。近年关于丢钱包的研究直接检验过本课的结尾：2019 年发表在《科学》杂志上的一项大型实验，在四十个国家的公共场所放了数千个钱包，发现钱包里钱越多，人们归还得越多，这让很多经济学家感到意外。

✨ How it is written赏析

The joke works because the manager is given a speech and then not allowed to finish it. He has just generalised from one loss to the whole world, and the grammar shows him being cut off mid-flow: he started to complain, but was interrupted. The answer to his gloomy theory arrives as an object, an envelope laid on the desk, and the girl speaks only one plain sentence. The writer then has the last word, and he aims it at the manager, not at the girl. The title, It could be worse, sets a modest tone; the story does not claim that the world is good, only that it is a little better than the manager thought.

笑点在于：经理得到一段演说，却没被允许说完。他刚从一次丢钱推及整个世界，语法就让他话说到一半被截断：他开始抱怨，却被打断了。对他那套悲观理论的回答，是一件实物——一个放在桌上的信封；那个姑娘只说了一句朴素的话。最后一句归作者，而且是对着经理说的，不是对着姑娘。标题「本来可能更糟」定下了一个低调的基调：故事并不断言世界是好的，只是说它比经理以为的好一点。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The manager's habit of turning one bad event into a judgement on everyone is very human, and news reports often encourage it, because a theft is a story and a returned wallet usually is not. The wallet experiment mentioned above suggests that honesty is more common than people predict; when researchers asked people to guess the results, most expected fewer wallets to come back. Readers in China may know similar stories of lost phones or bags returned by taxi drivers or cleaners, and also stories that go the other way. The useful habit the lesson teaches is to wait for the facts before deciding what kind of world you live in.

经理把一件坏事变成对所有人的评判，这种习惯非常人性化，新闻也常常助长它，因为失窃是新闻，而还回来的钱包通常不是。上面提到的钱包实验表明，诚实比人们预计的更普遍；研究者请人们猜测结果时，大多数人以为能找回的钱包会更少。中国读者也许知道出租车司机或保洁员归还丢失的手机、包的故事，也知道反过来的故事。课文教的有用习惯是：先等事实，再判断自己生活在怎样的世界里。

  2. Why do people often expect others to be less honest than they really are? Give two reasons.

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 2第 2 句 (begins开头 “I had just …”)

  • had just lost: the past perfect, for a loss shortly before he went into the office.had just lost：过去完成时，丢钱在进办公室之前不久。
  • feel upset: be unhappy and troubled.feel upset：感到心烦。

Sentence 4第 4 句 (begins开头 “The manager was …”)

  • sympathetic means showing understanding of someone's trouble, not simply nice.sympathetic：同情的，不是「好感的」。
  • could do nothing means the same as could not do anything.

Sentence 6第 6 句 (begins开头 “He started to …”)

  • start to do: begin to do. complain about: say you are unhappy with.start to do：开始做。complain about：抱怨。
  • was interrupted by: the past simple passive, with by for what stopped him. The subject he is not repeated.was interrupted by：一般过去时被动，by 引出打断他的东西。主语 he 没有重复。

Sentence 9第 9 句 (begins开头 “'I found this …”)

  • outside: on the other side of the door. The money had probably fallen there.outside：在……外面。钱其实没在房间里，可能是掉在门外了。
  • Looking back at the title: things could have been worse, and the money was never stolen.回头看标题：It could be worse（还不算太糟），钱根本没被偷。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

The past simple passive: was or were with the past participle一般过去时被动：was / were + 过去分词
Use the passive when the person or thing affected is the subject: was or were with the past participle, and by for the doer. Leave out by when the doer does not matter.
动作的承受者作主语时用被动：was / were + 过去分词，动作的发出者用 by 引出。不必说出谁做的时候可以省 by。

Sentence 6 has two verbs sharing the subject he, the second one passive.

本课：第 6 句 was interrupted by a knock，两个谓语共用一个主语 he。

  • The window was broken by a football.
  • The letters were sent yesterday.
The past perfect with just过去完成时：had just done
had just done: something finished only a moment before a point in the past. It is often used at the start of a story to give the background.
had just done：在过去某个时刻之前「刚刚」完成。常放在故事开头，交代背景。

Sentence 2 uses had just lost to explain why he has come to the manager.

本课：第 2 句 had just lost，交代他为什么来找经理。

  • We had just sat down when the phone rang.
  • She had just left when you called.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • X had just lost … and felt very upset. X 刚丢了……，心里很难过。
    Sam had just lost his phone and felt very upset.
  • X was sympathetic, but X could do nothing. X 很同情，却也无能为力。
    The teacher was sympathetic, but she could do nothing.
  • X started to complain about … but was interrupted by … X 开始抱怨……却被……打断了
    She started to complain about the food but was stopped by a phone call.
  • There is still some … in this world! 这世上还是有……的！
    There is still some kindness in this world!
