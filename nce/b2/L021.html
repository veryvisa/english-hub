Lesson 21 · Mad or Not 是不是疯了

📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 21 “Mad or Not”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 21《Mad or Not》

B1🧠 15 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
The writer lives beside an airport, and the planes that pass over day and night are pushing him towards madness. It was finished long ago but stood unused for years; last year it finally opened. More than a hundred people have been forced out by the noise, and he is one of the few who remain, though he sometimes fears a plane will flatten his house. He has turned down a big offer to leave. Everyone calls him mad, and he admits they may be right. The grammar is the passive with modal verbs.
作者住在机场附近，飞机日夜从头顶过，快把他逼疯了。机场多年前就建好了，却一直没用，去年才启用。一百多人被噪音赶走了，他是少数留下的人之一，有时觉得房子都会被路过的飞机撞倒。有人出一大笔钱请他搬走，他偏不走。大家都说他准是疯了，他承认大家可能说得对。本课学带情态动词的被动语态（can be heard、must have been driven、will be knocked down）。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. Why does everyone call the writer mad?人们为什么认为作者疯了？
  2. Why might he stay in spite of the noise? The text does not say; what do you think?他为什么宁可忍受噪音也不搬走？课文没说，你怎么看？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander for New Concept English (Longman, 1967); the airport and the house are not named and the story is invented.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为《新概念英语》（朗文，1967）自撰；机场和房子都没有点名，故事是虚构的。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

The sixties were the decade when jet airliners became common, and noise from airports turned into a public issue in Britain. Jets were far louder than the propeller planes before them, and the growth of Heathrow and the search for a third London airport set off long arguments about who should bear the cost of the noise. Compensation schemes and offers to buy homes near runways have been used since then in many countries, and people who refuse to move often become news. Current guidance from the World Health Organization, published in 2018, links long exposure to aircraft noise with sleep disturbance and other harm, and recommends much lower levels than many airport neighbours live with.

六十年代是喷气式客机普及的年代，机场噪音在英国成了公共议题。喷气机比之前的螺旋桨飞机响得多，希思罗机场的扩张和伦敦第三机场的选址，引发了关于「噪音的代价该谁来承担」的长期争论。此后很多国家都用过补偿方案、或出钱收购跑道附近的房屋，而拒绝搬走的人常常上新闻。世界卫生组织 2018 年发布的现行指南，把长期暴露于飞机噪音与睡眠受扰等危害联系起来，建议的噪音水平比很多机场周边居民实际承受的低得多。

✨ How it is written赏析

The writer tells his story almost entirely through things that are done to him and to others. Planes can be heard, the airport could not be used, neighbours must have been forced out, the house will be knocked down, he has been offered money. This chain of passives makes him a man surrounded by forces he does not control, and then he chooses one active, stubborn verb of his own: he is determined to stay. The lesson ends not with a defence but with a shrug, agreeing that he is probably mad. That self-mockery keeps the reader on his side; a man who argued for his rights would be less likeable.

作者讲自己的故事，几乎全靠「被做的事」：飞机被听到，机场不能被使用，人们一定是被赶走了，房子会被撞倒，有人给他钱。这一串被动语态让他成了一个被不受他控制的力量包围的人；然后他自己选了一个主动的、倔强的动词：他决心留下。课文不是以辩护结尾，而是耸耸肩，承认自己大概是疯了。这种自嘲让读者站在他这边；一个为自己权利据理力争的人，反而不那么讨人喜欢。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The story touches a conflict that has not gone away anywhere in the world: a project that serves millions and a neighbour who pays the price. Readers in China will think of the households who refuse to leave when land is cleared for development, often reported in the press with a photograph of a single house standing in a dug-out site. The lesson never says why the writer stays, and that gap is worth discussing. He may love the house, he may want a better offer, or he may simply refuse to be pushed. Each reason would change how we judge him, and the lesson is clever to leave the choice to us.

这个故事触及一个在世界各地都没有消失的冲突：一个服务于数百万人的工程，和一个为此付出代价的邻居。中国读者会想到土地开发时不肯搬走的住户，媒体报道时常配一张照片：一栋房子孤零零立在挖开的工地中间。课文始终没说作者为什么留下，这个空白值得讨论：他可能爱这栋房子，可能想要更高的出价，也可能只是不肯被人推着走。每一种理由都会改变我们对他的评价，课文聪明的地方就在于把选择留给了我们。

    给出作者留下的三个可能理由，说说你觉得哪个最可信。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 2第 2 句 (begins开头 “I live near …”)

  • can be heard: a modal with be and a past participle, the passive. The planes are what is heard.can be heard：情态动词 + be + 过去分词，被动。主语 planes 是被听到的对象。
  • passing before a noun means going by.passing 作前置定语：路过的。

Sentence 3第 3 句 (begins开头 “The airport was …”)

  • was built is the past simple passive; could not be used is the past form of a modal passive.was built：一般过去时被动。could not be used：情态动词被动的过去形式。
  • for some reason: for a reason the writer does not know or does not give.for some reason：不知什么原因，作者不打算解释。

Sentence 5第 5 句 (begins开头 “Over a hundred …”)

  • must have been done: a confident guess about the past, in the passive. He has not counted; he is reasoning.must have been done：对过去的有把握推测，被动。作者没数过，只是推断。
  • by introduces the agent, which here is the noise, not a person.by the noise：by 引出动作的施动者，这里是噪音而不是人。

Sentence 8第 8 句 (begins开头 “I have been …”)

  • have been offered: the present perfect passive. offer takes two objects, and the passive with the person as subject is the commonest.have been offered：现在完成时被动。offer 有两个宾语，人作主语的被动最常见。
  • be determined to do: be fixed in your decision to do it.be determined to do：决心做。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

The passive with modals: can, must or will with be done情态动词的被动：can / must / will + be done
A modal with be and a past participle gives the passive for now or the future, as in can be heard or will be knocked down. A modal with have been and a past participle is a passive guess about the past, as in must have been driven.
情态动词 + be + 过去分词：现在或将来的被动（can be heard、will be knocked down）；情态动词 + have been + 过去分词：对过去的推测，被动（must have been driven）。

Sentences 2, 3, 5 and 7 each use a modal in the passive, a different one each time.

本课：第 2 句 can be heard、第 3 句 could not be used、第 5 句 must have been driven、第 7 句 will be knocked down。

  • This door must be locked at night.
  • The letter may have been sent to the wrong address.
  • The bridge will be opened next spring.
The passive of verbs with two objects: I have been offered ...双宾语动词的被动：I have been offered …
Verbs such as offer, give, show and tell take two objects, a person and a thing. In the passive the person usually becomes the subject: I was offered money. The thing can also be the subject, with to before the person.
offer、give、show、tell 等动词有两个宾语（人和物），变被动时通常让人作主语：They offered me money → I was offered money. 也可以让物作主语：Money was offered to me。

In sentence 8 the passive puts the writer himself in subject position.

本课：第 8 句的被动就是让作者本人作主语。

  • She was given a prize for her painting.
  • We were shown round the new school.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • X is slowly driving me mad. X 正慢慢把我逼疯。
    The dripping tap is slowly driving me mad.
  • For some reason X could not be … then. 不知什么原因，X 当时不能被……
    For some reason the road could not be repaired then.
  • X is one of the very few who still … X 是极少数还在……的人之一。
    Grandpa is one of the very few who still write letters.
  • I have been offered …, but I am determined to … 有人给我……，可我决心……
    I have been offered a ride, but I am determined to walk.

用不超过 50 个词讲述作者住的地方出了什么事，以及他的决定。
