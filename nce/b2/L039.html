Lesson 39 · Am I All Right 我没事吧？

📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 39 “Am I All Right”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 39《Am I All Right》

B1🧠 16 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
During his stay in hospital John Gilbert wanted his doctor to tell him if the operation had worked, and the doctor would not say. Next day Gilbert got a phone put by his bed. As soon as he had the room to himself, he rang the hospital switchboard and asked to be put through to Dr Millington. When the doctor came on the line, Gilbert said he was calling about a patient called John Gilbert. Had that patient's operation gone well? Yes, said the doctor. When could he go home? Not for another fortnight. Then the doctor asked whether the caller was a relative. 'No,' came the answer, 'I am the patient.' The grammar is reported speech: ask someone to do, ask whether or if, tell someone that, and moving tenses back.
约翰·吉尔伯特住院期间，请医生告诉他手术是否成功，医生不肯说。第二天，他要了一部床边电话。等屋里只剩他一个人，他打给医院总机，要找米林顿医生。医生接了电话，吉尔伯特先生说他想打听一位病人——约翰·吉尔伯特先生的情况。他问吉尔伯特先生的手术成不成功，医生说成功了；又问什么时候能出院，医生说还得再住两个星期。接着米林顿医生问来电的人是不是病人的亲属。「不是，」病人回答，「我就是约翰·吉尔伯特。」本课的核心是间接引语：ask sb to do、ask whether/if、told him that …，以及时态后移。

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander for New Concept English (Longman, 1967); John Gilbert and Dr Millington are invented.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为《新概念英语》（朗文，1967）自撰；约翰·吉尔伯特和米林顿医生都是虚构的。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

The doctor's silence was normal in the nineteen sixties. Studies from that time found that many doctors in Britain and America did not tell patients bad or uncertain news, and decisions were often explained to families rather than to the patient. Since then the rule has turned round: in Britain, Canada and most Western countries, informed consent and the right to know your own medical facts are now part of law and professional codes, and doctors are expected to share results with the patient first. Hospital phones also worked differently: calls went through a switchboard operator, and a bedside phone was something you had to request.

医生的沉默在六十年代很正常。那个时期的研究发现，英美许多医生不会把坏消息或没把握的消息告诉病人，决定常常是向家属解释，而不是向病人本人。此后规则倒了过来：在英国、加拿大和大多数西方国家，知情同意和了解自身病情的权利如今写进了法律和职业守则，医生应当先把结果告诉病人本人。医院电话的用法也不同：来电要经接线员转接，床边电话是需要专门申请的。

✨ How it is written赏析

The story is told almost entirely in reported speech, and that is what makes the trick work. Because we hear only what was asked and answered, not the voices, the doctor's side sounds natural, and we forget for a moment who is calling. The phrase a Mr John Gilbert, with the small word a, sounds like a stranger speaking about a stranger. Then the final line switches to direct speech for the first time, and the shift itself is the punchline: the patient steps out of the reported account and speaks as himself.

故事几乎完全用间接引语讲述，正是这一点让把戏成立。因为我们只听到问了什么、答了什么，听不到声音，医生那一方显得很自然，我们也一时忘了打电话的是谁。「a Mr John Gilbert」里那个小小的 a，听起来像一个陌生人在谈论另一个陌生人。最后一行第一次换成直接引语，这个转换本身就是笑点：病人从转述里走出来，以自己的身份开口。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

Today the doctor's refusal would be seen as a breach of the patient's rights, and Gilbert's trick as a fair response to being treated like a child. In mainland China and other places, families are still often told serious news before the patient, out of a wish to protect them, so this question is far from dead. The story also shows a weak point in phone privacy: the doctor gave out results to an unknown caller before checking who he was. Modern hospitals ask callers to prove who they are for exactly this reason.

放在今天，医生的拒绝会被视为侵犯病人权利，吉尔伯特的把戏则是对「被当成小孩」的合理回应。在中国内地等地方，出于保护的心意，严重的病情仍常常先告诉家属、后告诉病人，所以这个问题远没有过时。故事还暴露了电话隐私的一个漏洞：医生在确认来电者身份之前就把结果说了出去。现代医院要求来电者先证明身份，正是这个原因。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 2第 2 句 (begins开头 “he asked his …”)

  • ask someone to do: a request in reported speech.ask sb to do：请某人做某事（间接引语中的请求）。
  • whether: a yes or no question reported with whether or if, in statement order, with the tense moved back.whether … had been successful：间接一般疑问句用 whether/if，陈述语序，时态后移。
  • do so stands for the earlier action, telling him.refuse to do so：拒绝这么做，do so 代替前面的 tell him。

Sentence 6第 6 句 (begins开头 “the patient asked …”)

  • ask for something: to request it.ask for sth：要求得到某物。

Sentence 11第 11 句 (begins开头 “Mr. Gilbert said …”)

  • said he was inquiring: in reported speech the present continuous moves back to the past continuous.said he was inquiring：间接引语，现在进行时后移为过去进行时。
  • a certain means a particular one not named; a before a name means somebody called that.a certain：某个（不点名）；a + 人名＝「一位叫……的」。

Sentence 14第 14 句 (begins开头 “and the doctor …”)

  • it had been: in this reported answer successful is left out after had been.it had been：间接引语，had been 后省略了 successful。

Sentence 15第 15 句 (begins开头 “He then asked …”)

  • asked when he would: a wh- question reported in statement order, with will becoming would.asked when … would：间接特殊疑问句，陈述语序，will 后移为 would。
  • would have to: will have to moved back in reported speech.would have to：将来必须，在间接引语中后移。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

Reported speech: statements and questions间接引语：陈述句与疑问句
To report a statement, use said or told someone that. To report a yes or no question, use asked if or whether in statement order. To report a wh- question, use asked plus the question word in statement order. After a past reporting verb, move the tenses back: is to was, will to would, has been or was to had been.
转述陈述句：said/told sb (that) + 从句；转述一般疑问句：asked if/whether + 陈述语序；转述特殊疑问句：asked + 疑问词 + 陈述语序。主句是过去时，从句时态后移：is→was，will→would，has been/was→had been。

Sentences 13 and 14 report a yes or no question with if, and sentences 15 to 18 report a wh- question with when and would.

本课：第 13–14 句转述的是一般疑问句，用 if；第 15–18 句转述的是特殊疑问句，用 when 加 would。

  • 'Are you tired?' → She asked me if I was tired.
  • 'Where will you go?' → He asked me where I would go.
ask and tell someone to do: reporting requests and ordersask / tell sb to do：转述请求和命令
For a request use ask someone to do or not to do; for an order use tell someone to do or not to do. No that clause is needed.
请求：ask sb (not) to do；命令：tell sb (not) to do。不用 that 从句。

In sentence 2 Gilbert's request to the doctor is reported with ask plus a to-infinitive.

本课：第 2 句用 ask sb to do 转述请医生说出结果。

  • 'Please sit down.' → She asked me to sit down.
  • 'Don't touch it!' → He told us not to touch it.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • X asked … to tell him whether … X 请……告诉他是否……
    She asked the teacher to tell her whether she had passed.
  • X said he was inquiring about … X 说他想打听……的情况
    The woman said she was calling about a lost dog.
  • X asked if … and was told that … X 问……，得到的回答是……
    He asked if the bus had gone and was told that it had.
  • X would have to stay … for another … X 还得再待……
    We would have to wait at the airport for another three hours.
  • Then X asked the caller if … 然后 X 问来电者是不是……
    Then the clerk asked the caller if she had a booking number.

Our model summary本站范文

In hospital, John Gilbert asked his doctor if his operation had worked, but the doctor would not tell him. The next day Gilbert got a phone by his bed and called Dr Millington, pretending to ask about a patient called Gilbert. The doctor said the operation had gone well and the patient must stay two more weeks. When the doctor asked if the caller was a relative, Gilbert said he was the patient himself.（74 词）
