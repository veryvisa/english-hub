Lesson 50 · Taken for a Ride 乘车兜风

📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 50 “Taken for a Ride”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 50《Taken for a Ride》

B1🧠 16 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
The writer enjoys trips into the countryside but hates getting lost. On a recent outing the journey took far longer than planned. Boarding a bus, he told the conductor where he was heading and admitted he had no idea where it was; the conductor promised to tell him when to get off. He took a front seat for the view. Some time later the bus came to a halt, and he saw with alarm that every other passenger had gone. The conductor said this was the end of the line, and only when asked about Woodford Green did he remember his promise. The writer said he would simply get off there, until he heard that the bus was about to turn round. Then he chose to stay on. The grammar is love doing, longer than, tell sb where to do, an -ing phrase, and prefer to do.
「我」喜欢去乡间旅行，却讨厌迷路。最近一次出游，路上花的时间比预想的长。上车时「我」对售票员说要去伍德福德草地，但不知道在哪儿；他答应到站告诉「我」。「我」坐在车前头看风景。过了一阵车停了，「我」回头一看，吓了一跳：车上只剩「我」一个乘客。售票员说终点到了，「我」问这是不是伍德福德草地，他这才叫起来：「哎呀，我忘了让你下车了。」「我」说没关系，就在这儿下吧。售票员说车马上要往回开，「我」便说：那我还是留在车上吧。本课学 like / love doing、比较级 longer than、tell sb where to do、分词短语 Looking round 和 prefer to do。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. Why did the writer miss his stop?「我」为什么没在伍德福德草地下车？
  2. Why did he change his mind about getting off?最后「我」为什么又决定不下车了？

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander for New Concept English (Longman, 1967); Woodford Green is a real district in north-east London, but the trip is invented.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为《新概念英语》（朗文，1967）自撰；伍德福德草地是伦敦东北部真实的地区，但这次出游是虚构的。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

Until the late twentieth century most British buses carried two staff: a driver and a conductor who walked up and down selling tickets and calling out the stops. Passengers who did not know a route relied on the conductor to tell them where to get off, so the promise in the story was an ordinary part of the job. London's last regular conductors disappeared in the two thousands, replaced by machines, cards and electronic displays that name each stop. The English idiom in the title, to be taken for a ride, usually means to be cheated, which adds a joke the story never states.

直到二十世纪后期，英国大多数公交车上有两名工作人员：司机和售票员。售票员在车厢里来回走，卖票、报站。不认路的乘客全靠售票员提醒在哪下车，所以故事里那句承诺本来就是他的分内事。伦敦最后一批常规售票员在两千年代消失，取而代之的是售票机、交通卡和逐站显示站名的电子屏。标题里的英语习语 taken for a ride 通常是「被人骗了」的意思，这又添了一层故事没有明说的笑话。

✨ How it is written赏析

The joke is in the last exchange, and it depends on a quiet change of plan. Earlier the writer had a clear goal, a place on the map. By the end that goal has vanished, and he cares only about not being stranded, so riding back the way he came makes perfect sense. The conversation is short and polite on both sides, which makes the mistake funnier, because nobody is angry. The title works twice: he was given a long ride he did not ask for, and in the idiomatic sense he was let down by the man who promised to help.

笑点在最后几句对答，靠的是一个不声不响的改主意。开头作者有明确的目的地，地图上的一个点。到了结尾，这个目的地不见了，他只在乎别被困在终点站，所以原路坐回去完全合理。双方的对话简短而客气，这让失误更好笑，因为谁都没生气。标题一语双关：他白坐了一趟没要求的长途车；按习语的意思，他也被答应帮忙的人给「坑」了。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The story is about trust in small promises. The writer did the sensible thing and asked, but he then handed all responsibility to someone else and stopped paying attention. Today we hand the same job to a phone map, and the same thing can happen when the battery dies or the signal drops. The lesson is not to stop trusting, but to keep a little of your own attention switched on. It is also a gentle picture of making the best of a mistake: instead of arguing, the writer simply adjusts and enjoys the ride.

这个故事讲的是对小小承诺的信任。作者做了明智的事——开口问了，但随后把全部责任交给别人，自己就不再留神。今天我们把同样的活交给手机地图，电量耗尽或信号中断时同样会出这种事。教训不是别再信任别人，而是自己也留一分注意力。它也温和地描绘了怎样把一个错误过得不那么糟：作者不争吵，只是调整计划，享受这段车程。

💬 Discuss and write讨论与写作
  1. Tell a story of a time you missed your stop or got lost. What did you do next?
    讲一次你坐过站或迷路的经历。你接下来怎么做的？
  2. Is it the conductor's fault or the writer's? Argue one side in about 80 words.
    这是售票员的错还是作者的错？选一方用大约 80 个词论证。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 4第 4 句 (begins开头 “but my trip …”)

  • take sb plus time: the trip is the subject that uses up time.take sb + 时间：花了某人……时间，主语是事情。
  • longer than I expected: a comparative with a than-clause.longer than I expected：比较级 + than 从句。

Sentence 8第 8 句 (begins开头 “'I'll tell you …”)

  • tell sb where to do: a question word plus to-infinitive, meaning where you should do it.tell sb where to do：疑问词 + 不定式作宾语，相当于 where you should get off。
  • answered the conductor: after a quotation the verb can come before a noun subject.answered the conductor：引语后主谓倒装，说话人是名词时常见。

Sentence 9第 9 句 (begins开头 “I sat in …”)

  • to get: an infinitive of purpose.to get …：不定式表目的。
  • in the front of: inside, at the front part; in front of means outside, before it.in the front of：在（车）内的前部；in front of 是在……外面的前方。

Sentence 11第 11 句 (begins开头 “Looking round, I …”)

  • Looking round: an -ing phrase sharing the subject I.Looking round：现在分词短语作状语，主语是 I。
  • left: a past participle after the noun, meaning remaining.left：过去分词作后置定语，「剩下的」。

Sentence 20第 20 句 (begins开头 “I prefer to …”)

  • in that case: if that is so.in that case：既然那样。
  • prefer to do: would rather do; stay on means remain on board.prefer to do：宁愿做……；stay on：继续留在车上。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

A question word plus to-infinitive疑问词 + 不定式
After tell, know, show or ask, use where, how, what or when plus to-infinitive to mean where, how, what or when someone should do something. It is a short form of a clause.
tell / know / show / ask + 宾语 + where / how / what / when + to do，表示「该在哪里/怎样/什么时候做」，相当于一个从句的简写。

In sentence 8 the conductor promises to say where the writer should get off.

本课：第 8 句——售票员答应告诉「我」该在哪里下车。

  • Can you show me how to use this machine?
  • I don't know what to say.
prefer to do and prefer A to B
prefer to do means would rather do; prefer A to B means like A more, using to, not than; prefer doing A to doing B compares two actions.
prefer to do：宁愿做；prefer A to B：喜欢 A 胜过 B（用 to，不用 than）；prefer doing A to doing B 比较两件事。

In sentence 20 the bus is going back, so the writer would rather stay on board.

本课：第 20 句——车要往回开，「我」宁愿留在车上。

  • I prefer to walk when the weather is nice.
  • She prefers tea to coffee.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • I love …, but I don't like … 我喜欢……，但不喜欢……

  • The … took far longer than I'd planned. ……比我计划的花了长得多的时间

Summary writing摘要写作

In no more than 80 words, tell the story of the writer's bus trip.

用不超过 80 个词讲述「我」这次出游的经过。

Points a good summary covers好摘要要覆盖的要点

  • He sat at the front for the view, and when the bus stopped he found he was alone.「我」坐在前面看风景，车停时发现只剩自己一个乘客。
  • The conductor then remembered he had forgotten him; this was the last stop.售票员这才想起忘了让他下车，车已到终点。

Our model summary本站范文

The writer was going to Woodford Green, so he asked the conductor to tell him where to get off. He sat at the front to enjoy the scenery. When the bus finally stopped, he saw that he was its only passenger. It was the end of the route, and the conductor admitted he had forgotten to put him off. As the bus was about to return, the writer decided to stay on it.（73 词）
