Lesson 54 · Sticky Fingers 黏糊糊的手指

📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 54 “Sticky Fingers”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 54《Sticky Fingers》

B1🧠 24 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
After breakfast the writer took the children to school and went shopping. Back home, with the children at school and her husband at work, the house was peaceful, so she decided to bake some meat pies. Soon she was mixing fat and flour, and her hands were coated in pastry. That was the moment the phone chose to ring. She lifted it with two sticky fingers and her heart sank: it was Helen Bates. Persuading Helen to call again later took ten minutes. When she finally put the phone down, there was pastry everywhere, on her hands, on the phone and on every door handle. She was barely back in the kitchen when the doorbell gave a deafening ring. The postman needed her signature for a registered letter. The grammar is be busy doing, Nothing could have been more ..., It took me ... to do, and no sooner ... than.
早饭后，「我」送孩子们上学，然后去了商店。回家时还早，孩子们在学校，丈夫在上班，家里很安静，「我」便决定做几个肉馅饼。不一会儿，「我」就忙着把黄油和面粉和在一起，两手很快沾满了黏糊糊的面团。偏偏这时电话响了，真是烦透了。「我」用两根黏手指拿起听筒，一听是海伦·贝茨的声音，心里一沉。花了十分钟才劝她过会儿再打，终于挂上电话。手指上、电话上、门把手上全是面团，一团糟！刚回到厨房，门铃又响得震天动地——这回是邮递员，要「我」签收一封挂号信！本课学 be busy doing、Nothing could have been more …、It took me … to do 和 no sooner … than。

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander for New Concept English (Longman, 1967); the narrator and Helen Bates are invented.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为《新概念英语》（朗文，1967）自撰；叙述者和海伦·贝茨都是虚构的。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

The story shows a typical British middle-class morning of the time: children at school, a husband at work and a wife running the house. Homes had one fixed telephone, often in the hall, so answering it meant leaving whatever you were doing and walking across the house, which is why the pastry ends up on the doorknobs. Registered post needed a signature in person, so the postman could not simply leave the letter. Meat pies made with homemade pastry are a classic British dish; mixing fat into flour by hand, called rubbing in, really does leave your fingers thick with dough.

故事展现的是当时英国中产家庭典型的早晨：孩子上学，丈夫上班，妻子操持家务。家里只有一部固定电话，常放在门厅，接电话就得放下手头的事穿过整个屋子，所以面团最后才会沾到门把手上。挂号信必须本人签收，邮递员不能放下就走。用自制油酥皮做的肉馅饼是经典英国菜；用手把油脂搓进面粉里（叫 rubbing in），手指确实会糊满面团。

✨ How it is written赏析

The story is told by the woman herself, in a voice that is calm at first and then slightly breathless. The opening sets up a perfect peace, every family member listed as out of the way, so that the interruptions feel like an attack on it. Short sentences mark the turning points: one says how annoying it was, one cries out at the mess. The writer uses a list of three places to show the pastry spreading. The ending is a second interruption arriving at once, and the exclamation mark tells us her patience has run out.

故事由女主人自己讲，语气起初平静，后来有点上气不接下气。开头营造了一份完美的宁静，每个家人都被一一「请」出门，于是后来的打扰就像是对这份宁静的攻击。短句标出转折点：一句说有多烦，一句惊呼一团糟。作者用三个地点的排列来表现面团的扩散。结尾第二次打扰紧跟着到来，感叹号告诉我们她的耐心用完了。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

Every reader knows this kind of morning, and the modern version is a phone buzzing with messages while your hands are busy. The lesson is light, but it touches a real point about attention: being pulled away from a task costs more than the time of the interruption, because you also lose your place and make a mess along the way. People today protect focus by silencing phones or setting quiet hours. The story also hints at manners. Helen talks for ten minutes without noticing she has called at a bad time, which is a reminder to ask whether now is a good moment.

每个读者都熟悉这样的早晨，现代版本是手忙的时候手机消息嗡嗡响个不停。这一课很轻松，却点到了关于注意力的一个真问题：从手头的事被拉走，损失的不只是打扰本身那点时间，你还会丢掉进度，顺带弄得一团糟。今天的人用静音或设定免打扰时段来保护专注。故事也暗含礼貌问题：海伦聊了十分钟都没察觉自己打得不是时候，这提醒我们开口前先问一句「现在方便吗」。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 5第 5 句 (begins开头 “In a short …”)

  • be busy doing: be occupied with an action.be busy doing：忙于做……。
  • be covered with: have something all over.be covered with：沾满了，被……覆盖。

Sentence 8第 8 句 (begins开头 “Nothing could have …”)

  • Nothing could have been more: a negative with a comparative means the most of all.Nothing could have been more …：否定词 + 比较级 = 最高级意义，「再烦人不过了」。
  • could have been: about a past situation imagined differently.could have been：对过去情况的假设。

Sentence 9第 9 句 (begins开头 “I picked up …”)

  • be dismayed: upset, because she did not want a long chat.be dismayed：感到沮丧，说话人不想和海伦聊天。

Sentence 10第 10 句 (begins开头 “It took me …”)

  • It took me plus time plus to do: how long something needed.It took me + 时间 + to do：花了我……时间做……。
  • persuade sb to do: get someone to agree to do something.persuade sb to do：说服某人做……。

Sentence 12第 12 句 (begins开头 “I had no …”)

  • no sooner ... than: hardly had one thing happened when the next did; the first part is past perfect, the second past simple.no sooner … than：刚……就……，主句用过去完成时，than 后用一般过去时。
  • the enough-to phrase at the end: an exaggeration meaning extremely loud.句末 enough to 短语：响得能把死人吵醒，夸张。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

no sooner ... than: one thing straight after anotherno sooner … than：刚……就……
Use subject plus had no sooner plus past participle, then than plus the past simple. If no sooner starts the sentence, invert: No sooner had I done it than ... A similar pattern is hardly ... when.
主语 + had no sooner + 过去分词 + than + 一般过去时；no sooner 放句首时要倒装：No sooner had I … than …。同义结构 hardly … when。

In sentence 12 the doorbell rings the moment she gets back to the kitchen.

本课：第 12 句——刚回到厨房，门铃就响了。

  • I had no sooner sat down than the phone rang.
  • No sooner had we arrived than it started to rain.
A negative with a comparative means the most否定词 + 比较级 = 最高级
Nothing could be, I couldn't be and nobody is followed by a comparative all mean the very most, often more strongly than a superlative.
Nothing could be/have been + 比较级、I couldn't be + 比较级、Nobody is + 比较级 than …，都表示「再……不过」，语气比最高级更强。

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • After …, I … and then I … ……之后，我……，然后……
    After lunch, I washed the dishes and then I took the dog out.
  • Soon I was busy … 不一会儿我就忙着……
    In no time I was busy painting the fence.
  • At exactly that moment, … 偏偏在那一刻，……
    At exactly that moment, the lights went out.
  • It took me … minutes to persuade X to … 我花了……分钟才说服 X……
    It took me twenty minutes to talk my son into eating his peas.
  • I had no sooner … than … 我刚……就……
    I had no sooner closed my eyes than the baby woke.

Our model summary本站范文

After taking the children to school and doing the shopping, I came home to a quiet house and began making meat pies. My hands were soon thick with pastry when the phone rang. It was Helen Bates, and it took ten minutes to make her agree to call later. By then there was pastry everywhere. I had only just returned to the kitchen when the postman rang, wanting me to sign for a letter.（74 词）
