Lesson 85 · Never Too Old To Learn 活到老学到老

📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 85 “Never Too Old To Learn”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 85《Never Too Old To Learn》

B1🧠 19 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
A letter from the writer's old school brings news: the headmaster, Mr Page, is about to retire. Past and present pupils are sending him a gift, and everyone who has chipped in will sign a big album that will go to his home. His former pupils remember his patience, his understanding and the warm encouragement he gave children who hated going to school. Many will be at a dinner for him next week. By an odd chance, the day before he leaves he will have taught for exactly forty years. In retirement he means to take up gardening, which is new to him, but, as he likes to say, nobody is past learning. This is a warm, plain letter of memory. The grammar is the future continuous, the future perfect continuous, devote oneself to and for giving a reason.
「我」刚收到母校的来信，说老校长斯图尔特·佩奇先生下周就要退休了。新老学生都要送他一份礼物作纪念，凡是凑了份子的人都会在一本大纪念册上签名，纪念册将寄到他家。大家都会记得他的耐心与体谅，记得当年我们极不情愿去上学时他给的亲切鼓励。下周四许多老学生会参加为他举办的告别宴会。巧的是，退休前一天，他恰好教满 40 年。退休后他打算专心侍弄花园——这对他是个全新的爱好。不过这没关系，正像他常说的：活到老，学到老。本课是一封平实温暖的回忆信，学 将来进行时、将来完成进行时、devote oneself to 和 for 表原因。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. For how many years has Mr Page been a teacher?佩奇先生教书教了多久？
  2. What will he do after he retires, and why does it not matter that it is new to him?他退休后打算做什么？为什么说这没关系？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Unsigned and written for the course; the headmaster and school are invented. The closing saying is an old English proverb, recorded in various forms since at least the sixteenth century.

本课未署名、为教材自撰；校长和学校都是虚构的。结尾那句话是一句古老的英语谚语，至少从十六世纪起就以多种形式见于记载。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

In British schools a headmaster or headmistress was the head teacher of the whole school, and in many smaller schools they still taught classes as well. Collecting money for a retirement gift and signing a book or card are common customs in Britain and Canada, and a farewell dinner where former pupils speak is a typical way to honour a long career. Gardening is one of the most popular hobbies of retired people in Britain, and research on ageing suggests that learning new skills, whether a language, an instrument or a garden, helps keep the mind active and is linked to better well-being in later life.

在英国的学校里，男女校长是全校的首席教师，在许多小学校里他们同时也上课。为退休礼物凑钱、在纪念册或卡片上签名，是英国和加拿大常见的习俗；办一场告别宴会、请老学生发言，是表彰漫长职业生涯的典型方式。园艺是英国退休人士最受欢迎的爱好之一。关于衰老的研究表明，学习新技能——无论是一门语言、一种乐器，还是一座花园——有助于保持头脑活跃，也与晚年更好的身心状态有关。

✨ How it is written赏析

The text is almost a list of arrangements, yet it feels warm, because each future fact is joined to a memory. The writer uses the future continuous throughout, which sounds calm and settled, as if these events will simply happen in the natural course of time. The single sentence about the past, children who went to school without wanting to, gives the letter its heart, and it is honest rather than sweet. The curious coincidence of forty years adds a small surprise, and the ending turns what could be a sad goodbye into a beginning. The final proverb is placed in the headmaster's own mouth, so he has the last word, as a teacher should.

这篇文字几乎是一张安排清单，读来却很温暖，因为每一件将来的事都和一段回忆连在一起。作者通篇用将来进行时，听起来平静而笃定，仿佛这些事会顺着时间自然发生。唯一写过去的那一句——孩子们极不情愿去上学——是这封信的心，而且写得诚实，不甜腻。四十年的「巧合」添了一点小惊喜，结尾又把一场可能伤感的告别变成了一个开端。最后的谚语放在校长自己嘴里，于是他说了最后一句话，正像一位老师应该做的那样。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The proverb is the point: retirement here is not an end but a change of subject. That idea fits modern research and modern life well, since many people now live twenty or thirty years after they stop work. Chinese readers will know a matching saying of their own, that one should keep learning until old age, and in China universities for the elderly have grown very popular, with long waiting lists for classes in painting, music and computers. The lesson also says something about good teaching: what pupils remember is not the lessons but the patience and encouragement, especially the kindness shown to those who did not want to be there.

谚语才是重点：这里的退休不是结束，而是换一门功课。这个想法很符合现代研究和现代生活，因为许多人停止工作后还要再活二三十年。中国读者熟悉对应的说法「活到老学到老」；在中国，老年大学非常受欢迎，书画、音乐、电脑等课程往往要排很长的队。这一课也说出了好的教育是什么样：学生记住的不是课本，而是老师的耐心和鼓励，尤其是对那些并不想来上学的孩子的善意。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 1第 1 句 (begins开头 “I have just …”)

  • informing me that: an -ing phrase saying what the letter says.informing me that …：现在分词短语修饰 letter，说明信的内容。
  • will be retiring: the future continuous for something already arranged.will be retiring：将来进行时，表按计划将要发生的事。

Sentence 3第 3 句 (begins开头 “All those who …”)

  • all those who: everyone who; contribute towards: give money for.all those who：所有……的人。contribute towards：为……出钱。
  • The future simple passive.which will be sent：一般将来时被动。

Sentence 4第 4 句 (begins开头 “We shall all …”)

  • remember someone for something: keep them in mind because of it.remember sb for sth：因……而记住某人。
  • kindly here is an adjective meaning kind, not an adverb.kindly：形容词，「和蔼的」，不是副词。

Sentence 6第 6 句 (begins开头 “It is a …”)

  • it stands for the that clause.It is a … coincidence that …：形式主语 it。
  • will have been teaching: the future perfect continuous, counting up to a future moment.will have been teaching：将来完成进行时，到将来某时为止已持续多久。

Sentence 8第 8 句 (begins开头 “For him, this …”)

  • devote oneself to: give all one's time to, followed by a noun or -ing.devote oneself to + 名词/动名词：专心于。
  • In the time clause the present perfect stands for a future completed action.after he has retired：时间从句里用现在完成时表将来已完成。

Sentence 9第 9 句 (begins开头 “But this does …”)

  • for: because; one: anybody at all.for：因为。one：泛指任何人。
  • too ... to: so much that one cannot; never too old to learn: one can learn at any age.too … to …：太……而不能；never too old to learn：活到老学到老。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

The future continuous: will be doing将来进行时：will be doing
It describes an action in progress at a future time, or something arranged that will happen in the normal course of things; it sounds more neutral and polite than will do.
表示将来某时正在进行的动作，或已经安排好、自然会发生的事，语气比 will do 更客观、委婉。

Sentences 1, 2 and 5 all describe arranged events: the retirement, the gift and the dinner.

本课：第 1 句写校长下周退休，第 2 句写学生们送礼，第 5 句写老学生们赴宴，都是已安排好的事。

  • This time tomorrow I will be flying to Beijing.
  • Will you be using the car tonight?
The future perfect and future perfect continuous将来完成（进行）时：will have (been) done/doing
For something complete by a future time, use will have done; for how long something will have lasted by then, use will have been doing, often with for and a period or by and a time.
到将来某时为止已经完成：will have done；到将来某时为止已持续多久：will have been doing，常带 for + 时段、by + 时间。

In sentence 6, by the day before he retires, his teaching will have lasted forty years.

本课：第 6 句：到退休前一天，他教书就满 40 年了。

  • By next June, I will have finished the book.
  • By Christmas, we will have been living here for ten years.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • I have just had a letter from …, telling me that … 我刚收到……的来信，告诉我……
    I have just had a letter from my bank, telling me that my card will expire.
  • All those who have given towards … will … 所有为……出过钱的人将……
    All those who have given towards the trip will get a photo.
  • We shall always remember X for his … 我们会永远因为……而记住 X
    We shall always remember Grandpa for his jokes.
  • It is a strange coincidence that … 说来也巧，……
    It is a strange coincidence that both my sons were born on a Tuesday.
  • Once X has retired, X will devote … to … X 退休后将专心于……
    Once she has stopped work, she will devote her days to painting.

Our model summary本站范文

The writer has heard from his old school that Mr Page, the headmaster, is retiring next week. Former and present pupils are sending him a present, and those who have given money will sign an album for him. Many will also go to a farewell dinner. Everyone remembers his patience and kindness. By coincidence, he will have taught for exactly forty years. He plans to take up gardening, a new hobby, because he believes learning never ends.（77 词）
