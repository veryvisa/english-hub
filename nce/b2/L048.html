Lesson 48 · Did You Want to Tell Me Something 你想告诉我什么吗？

📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 48 “Did You Want to Tell Me Something”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 48《Did You Want to Tell Me Something》

B1🧠 18 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
Dentists have a habit of asking you things exactly when you cannot reply. The writer's dentist had just taken out a tooth and told him to lie still for a bit. He wanted to speak, but cotton wool filled his mouth. The dentist knew about his hobby of collecting match boxes and asked about it, then asked after his brother and his new job in London. All the writer could do was nod or make odd sounds. Meanwhile his tongue explored the gap, and he suddenly grew very anxious. When the cotton wool finally came out, he could tell the dentist the bad news: the wrong tooth was gone. The grammar is reported questions with whether and how, either ... or, the past perfect, and be able to.
牙医总在你没法回答的时候问问题。「我」的牙医刚拔掉「我」一颗牙，叫「我」休息一会儿。「我」想说话，可嘴里塞满了药棉。他知道「我」集火柴盒，就问收藏是不是又多了；又问「我」哥哥好不好、伦敦的新工作喜不喜欢。「我」只能点头或者发出怪声。这时，「我」的舌头在嘴里找拔牙留下的那个洞，忽然急得不得了，却一个字也说不出。等牙医终于取出药棉，「我」才告诉他：他拔错牙了。本课学间接疑问句 whether / how、either … or、过去完成时和 be able to。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. Why couldn't the writer answer the dentist?「我」为什么没法回答牙医的问题？
  2. What did the writer find out that made him so worried?「我」突然那么着急，是因为发现了什么？

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

The dentist's chair is one of the most shared small sufferings of modern life, and the chatty dentist who asks questions while your mouth is full is a standing joke in Britain and beyond. In the nineteen-sixties, pulling a tooth was far more common than it is now; fluoride toothpaste and regular check-ups have since saved many teeth that would once have come out. Pulling the wrong tooth sounds like a joke, but it is a real kind of error, and dental and medical bodies now use checklists and marked charts before treatment, exactly so that the patient's side and the right tooth are confirmed aloud.

牙医的椅子是现代生活里最普遍的小折磨之一，嘴里塞满东西时偏偏找你聊天的牙医，在英国内外都是老笑话。二十世纪六十年代拔牙比现在常见得多；后来含氟牙膏和定期检查保住了许多从前会被拔掉的牙。拔错牙听上去像笑话，却是真实存在的一类差错；如今牙科和医疗机构在治疗前用核对清单和标记好的牙位图，就是为了把哪一边、哪颗牙大声确认一遍。

✨ How it is written赏析

The first sentence is a general complaint that every reader has felt, which wins us over before the story begins. Then the dentist's questions pile up, each one friendly and pointless, while the narrator can only grunt. The tension comes from the gap between what the dentist is talking about and what the narrator is thinking. The tongue quietly does the detective work, the worry grows, and the title, a polite question, only makes sense in the last line. The news that matters most is kept silent until the very end, just as it was in the chair.

第一句是一句人人都有同感的抱怨，故事还没开始就把读者拉到了作者一边。接着牙医的问题一个接一个，每个都友善又无关痛痒，叙述者只能哼哼。张力来自牙医说的和叙述者想的之间的落差。舌头悄悄做了侦探的活，担心越来越重；标题那句客气的问话，到最后一行才明白是什么意思。最要紧的消息一直被堵在嘴里，直到最后才说出来，正像在诊椅上一样。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

Beneath the comedy is a lesson about communication in any service where one side has the power and the other cannot easily speak. The patient knew something important but had no way to say it, and the professional was too busy being pleasant to notice. Good practice today is to agree on a signal before treatment, such as raising a hand, and to confirm the plan before starting. The story also reminds us that small talk is kind only if the other person can join in. Readers who have struggled to speak up to a doctor in a second language will recognise the feeling exactly.

喜剧之下是一个沟通的道理：在任何一方掌握主动、另一方难以开口的服务场合都适用。病人知道要紧的事却没法说，专业人员忙着表现友好而没注意到。今天的好做法是治疗前先约好一个信号，比如举手，并在动手前把方案确认一遍。故事也提醒我们：寒暄只有在对方能接话时才是体贴。用第二语言跟医生说话、想开口却说不出来的读者，对这种感觉一定再熟悉不过。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 1第 1 句 (begins开头 “Dentists always ask …”)

  • it is impossible for sb to do: it stands in for the infinitive, and for you shows who.it is impossible for sb to do：it 作形式主语，for you 引出逻辑主语。
  • always with the present simple: a habit, said with a touch of complaint.always + 一般现在时：说经常发生、带点抱怨的事。

Sentence 2第 2 句 (begins开头 “My dentist had …”)

  • had just done: the past perfect for something that had only just happened.had just done：过去完成时，故事开始前刚发生的事。
  • tell sb to do: give someone an instruction.tell sb to do：叫某人做……。

Sentence 5第 5 句 (begins开头 “He knew I …”)

  • ask sb whether: a yes-no question reported with whether and normal word order.ask sb whether …：间接疑问句，一般疑问句变成 whether + 陈述语序。

Sentence 7第 7 句 (begins开头 “In answer to …”)

  • in answer to: as a reply to.in answer to：作为对……的回答。
  • either ... or: a choice between two verbs.either … or：二者选一，连接两个并列的谓语动词。

Sentence 8第 8 句 (begins开头 “Meanwhile, my tongue …”)

  • be busy doing: be occupied with an action.be busy doing：忙于做……。
  • the where-clause at the end: it describes the hole, with the past perfect for the place it used to be.句末的 where 从句：修饰 hole，过去完成时说「原来长牙的地方」。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

Reported questions: ask whether or how间接疑问句：ask sb whether / how …
To report a yes-no question use whether or if; to report a wh-question keep the question word. Both use statement word order, without do or did, and the tense moves back after a past verb.
转述一般疑问句用 whether（或 if）；转述特殊疑问句保留疑问词。两种都用陈述语序，不加 do/did，时态随主句后移。

Sentence 5 uses whether and sentence 6 uses how and whether; the dentist's actual words would be three direct questions.

本课：第 5 句用 whether，第 6 句用 how 和 whether——原话是 Is your collection growing? How is your brother? Do you like your new job?

  • She asked me whether I was hungry.
  • He wanted to know how old the house was.
either ... or and neither ... nor
either A or B means one of the two; neither A nor B means not one and not the other. Join matching parts, and when they are the subject the verb agrees with the nearer one.
either A or B：要么 A 要么 B；neither A nor B：既不 A 也不 B。连接成分要对等（动词对动词、名词对名词）；作主语时谓语与靠近的那个一致。

In sentence 7 the writer has only two possible replies, a nod or a strange sound.

本课：第 7 句——「我」能做的只有点头或发出怪声两种。

  • We can either eat now or wait for Tom.
  • Neither my sister nor I am good at maths.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • X always … just when you can't … X 总在你没法……的时候……
    Waiters always ask how the meal is just when you can't speak.
  • X asked me whether … X 问我是不是……
    The nurse asked me whether I felt dizzy.
  • X asked me how … was X 问我……怎么样
    My aunt asked me how my exams were.
  • I could only either … or … 我只能要么……要么……
    With the baby asleep in my arms, I could only either whisper or wave.
  • When at last X …, I was able to … 等 X 终于……，我才能……
    When at last the music stopped, I was able to hear her.

Our model summary本站范文

My dentist had just taken out a tooth of mine and told me to rest. My mouth was stuffed with cotton wool, so I could not speak. He began asking about my match box collection, my brother and my new job, and I could only nod or grunt. Meanwhile my tongue found the gap, and I became very worried. When the cotton wool came out at last, I told him he had taken out the wrong tooth.（77 词）
