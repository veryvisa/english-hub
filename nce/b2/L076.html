Lesson 76 · April Fools' Day 愚人节

📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 76 “April Fools' Day”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 76《April Fools' Day》

B1🧠 18 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
A television news special closes with a visit to the pasta fields of southern Italy, where, we are told, the plant has been farmed for six centuries. The growers expect a fine crop and have started cutting early; two men have filled three carts with golden stalks, the village is working round the clock to finish before the autumn rains, and a grower's wife chats with a factory manager. A famous eating contest will follow. Only the last line gives the game away: the date is the first of April. This is a straight-faced hoax news report. The grammar is the present perfect continuous, the present perfect passive, between them and since and for.
电视新闻特别节目的结尾，播音员说要带观众去卡拉布里亚的通心粉田：那里种通心粉已有六百多年，今年预计大丰收，收割比往年早；画面上两个工人刚割完三车金黄的通心粉秆，全村日夜不停地赶在九月雨季前脱粒，种植户的妻子正跟本地加工厂的经理说话，丰收后还要举行有名的吃通心粉大赛。最后一句才揭底：今天是 4 月 1 日，星期四。本课是一条一本正经的愚人节假新闻，学 现在完成进行时、现在完成时被动、between them 和 since/for。

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Unsigned and written for the course, but closely modelled on a real broadcast: the spaghetti harvest report shown by the BBC programme Panorama on the first of April 1957.

本课未署名、为教材自撰，但明显仿照一次真实的播出：1957 年 4 月 1 日英国广播公司 Panorama 节目播出的「意大利面丰收」报道。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

The original film was set in southern Switzerland, not Italy, and showed a family pulling strands of spaghetti off trees. It ran for about three minutes and was read by Richard Dimbleby, one of the most trusted voices on British television, which is a large part of why it worked. In the late fifties pasta was still an unusual food in Britain, so many viewers had no idea how it was made. Hundreds of people rang the BBC afterwards; some were angry that a serious current affairs programme had played a trick, and others wanted to know how to grow their own spaghetti. The textbook moves the story to Calabria, changes spaghetti to macaroni and updates the champion's year, but the method is the same: a calm voice, real place names, numbers and ordinary working people.

原片的场景在瑞士南部而不是意大利，画面里一家人从树上摘下一根根意大利面。片长大约三分钟，由理查德·丁布尔比旁白，他是当时英国电视上最受信任的声音之一，这正是骗局成功的一大原因。五十年代后期，意大利面在英国还是少见的食物，许多观众根本不知道它是怎么做的。播出后有好几百人打电话给 BBC：有人生气一个严肃的时事节目竟然开玩笑，也有人问怎样自己种意大利面。课文把故事搬到卡拉布里亚，把意大利面改成通心粉，又更新了冠军的年份，但手法完全一样：平静的语调、真实的地名、具体的数字和普通的劳动者。

✨ How it is written赏析

The whole text is written in the language of real news, and the joke lives in that contrast. Tenses do the convincing: the present perfect passive gives the crop a long history, the present perfect continuous makes the work feel as if it is happening while you watch, and stage directions such as on the right point at pictures that seem to exist. Names and figures add weight, as all good reporting does. The writer never winks at the reader until the last few lines, where the date slips in among the closing words as if by accident. A careful reader can spot the trick from the second sentence, and that is part of the fun.

全文用的都是真新闻的语言，笑点就藏在这种反差里。时态负责让人相信：现在完成时被动给庄稼一段悠久的历史，现在完成进行时让劳动仿佛正在你眼前发生，「右边你可以看到」这类镜头提示指向好像真的存在的画面。人名和数字增加分量，正像所有好的报道那样。作者直到最后几行才对读者眨眼：日期像是不经意地夹在结束语里。细心的读者第二句就能识破，这本身也是乐趣的一部分。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The joke still works because it shows how we judge news: we trust a confident voice, a familiar format and precise details more than we check the claim itself. In 1957 the trick depended on viewers not knowing where pasta came from; today anyone can search in seconds, yet fake reports spread faster than ever, because they look like the real thing and arrive from people we trust. Chinese readers will recognise the pattern from rumours forwarded in family chat groups, where an official-looking layout and a few numbers make a story feel true. The habit worth taking from this lesson is simple: before you pass on a surprising report, ask what the claim actually is and whether it could be true, not only who seems to be saying it.

这个玩笑今天依然有效，因为它揭示了我们判断新闻的方式：我们相信自信的声音、熟悉的格式和精确的细节，胜过去核对说法本身。1957 年这个骗局靠的是观众不知道意大利面从哪里来；今天谁都能几秒钟查到，可假新闻传得比以往任何时候都快，因为它们看上去像真的，又是从我们信任的人那里转来的。中国读者在家族群里转发的谣言中会认出同样的套路：一个像官方的排版、几个数字，就让故事显得真实。从这一课值得带走的习惯很简单：转发一条令人吃惊的消息之前，先问它到底说了什么、有没有可能是真的，而不只看是谁在说。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 3第 3 句 (begins开头 “said the voice …”)

  • go over to: switch to a report from another place; the present continuous is for something about to happen.go over to：（广播电视里）切换到某地的现场。现在进行时表马上要做的事。
  • The phrase pasta fields is itself the first joke, since pasta is made from flour and does not grow.macaroni fields：「通心粉田」本身就是第一个笑点——通心粉是面粉做的，不长在地里。

Sentence 4第 4 句 (begins开头 “Macaroni has been …”)

  • The present perfect passive: it started long ago and is still going on.has been grown：现在完成时被动，从过去一直到现在都在种。
  • for with a length of time says how long.for + 一段时间：表持续多久。

Sentence 7第 7 句 (begins开头 “Here you can …”)

  • between them: together, counting what both did.between them：两人合起来（一共）。
  • have just finished doing: completed a moment ago.have just finished doing：刚刚做完。

Sentence 11第 11 句 (begins开头 “She has been …”)

  • The present perfect continuous: an action that began in the past and is still going on; now stresses 'up to today'.现在完成进行时：动作从过去持续到现在、还在继续。now 加强「到现在为止」。

Sentence 14第 14 句 (begins开头 “the famous Calabrian …”)

  • The colon introduces an explanation of what will happen.冒号后解释前面的 what will happen。
  • A noun plus an -ing form joined by a hyphen makes a compound adjective.macaroni-eating：名词 + 动名词构成的复合形容词。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

The present perfect continuous: have or has been doing现在完成进行时：have/has been doing
Use it for an action that started in the past, has gone on up to now, and may continue. It often goes with for, since, all day or now.
动作从过去某时开始，一直持续到现在，而且可能还要继续；常和 for、since、all day、now 连用。

Sentence 6 has the growers' long hope, sentence 9 the village's work round the clock, and sentence 11 the wife's thirty years of help.

本课：第 6 句写种植户一直在盼丰收，第 9 句写全村日夜在干活，第 11 句写妻子帮丈夫三十年。

  • I have been waiting for you since six o'clock.
  • It has been raining all morning.
for and sincefor 和 since
for goes with a length of time, and since with the moment it began. Both usually go with a perfect tense.
for + 一段时间（for ten years），since + 起点（since 1991、since he left）。两者都常配完成时。

Sentence 4 uses for with centuries, and sentence 15 uses since with the year the champion began winning.

本课：第 4 句用 for 说种了几百年，第 15 句用 since 说冠军从哪年起年年得胜。

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • To end our …, we're going over to … 作为本节目的结尾，我们现在切换到……
    To close tonight's show, we're going over to our reporter in Paris.
  • X has been grown here for over … years. X 在这里已经种植了……多年
    Tea has been grown on these hills for over three hundred years.
  • Two workers, between them, have … 两个工人合起来已经……
    My two sisters, between them, have read every book in the house.
  • X has been helping Y for … now. X 帮 Y 已经有……了
    Grandma has been helping at the school library for ten years now.
  • X has taken the prize each year since … 从……以来 X 年年夺冠
    Our team has taken the cup each year since 2018.

Our model summary本站范文

A television news programme ended with a report from southern Italy, where pasta had supposedly been farmed for six hundred years. The growers expected an excellent crop, workers were shown cutting golden stalks, and the whole village was busy before the rains. There was even going to be a pasta-eating contest. Only at the end did the announcer mention the date, the first of April. The report was a joke, because pasta is made from flour, not grown.（78 词）
