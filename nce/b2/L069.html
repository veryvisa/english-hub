Lesson 69 · But not Murder 是真撞还是假撞

📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 69 “But not Murder”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 69《But not Murder》

B1🧠 16 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
The writer, Mr Eames, was trying for his licence for the third time. He had handled busy roads well, and once told to head out of town he grew more confident, so sure of passing that he almost liked the test. The examiner seemed pleased and set one last task: imagine a child running out ahead; at the sound of a knock on the glass, the car must halt within five feet. Some time later came the knock, loud and clear, but the writer was slow to respond, then braked so hard that both men lurched forward. The examiner gave him a sad look and announced that the child was dead. This is a first-person story where confidence comes before a fall. The grammar is passives in several tenses, having been done, must have done and Let us suppose that.
「我」第三次考驾照。考官先让「我」在车流密集的路上开，「我」开得很顺；接着又让「我」把车开出城，「我」开始有了信心，几乎以为自己已经过了，甚至开始享受这次考试。考官大概很满意，笑着说：「埃姆斯先生，还有最后一项。假设有个孩子突然在你车前横穿马路，我一敲车窗，你必须在五英尺之内停下。」「我」继续开，过了一会儿考官重重一敲，声音很清楚，「我」却过了好久才反应过来，猛踩刹车，两人都往前一冲。考官难过地看着「我」，用沉痛的声音说：「埃姆斯先生，你刚刚撞死了那个孩子！」本课是一个乐极生悲的第一人称故事，学被动语态的各种时态、having been done、must have done 和 Let us suppose that。

  2. What did the examiner mean by his last words?考官说「你刚刚撞死了那个孩子」是什么意思？

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

The emergency stop was a standard part of the British driving test for most of the twentieth century. The examiner would warn the learner, then at some moment give a signal, often by raising a hand or tapping the dashboard, and the car had to stop quickly and under control. Since the late nineteen-nineties it has been tested only on some tests rather than all, and the modern test also includes a section of independent driving and following directions. Reaction time is the hidden subject: a typical driver needs well over half a second to react, and at thirty miles an hour a car travels many metres in that time, so five feet was a strict target even for a good driver.

紧急停车在二十世纪大部分时间里都是英国驾照考试的固定项目。考官先提醒学员，然后在某个时刻发出信号，常常是举手或敲仪表台，车必须迅速、平稳地停下。九十年代末以来，这一项只在部分考试中抽考，现代考试还加了独立驾驶、按指示行驶等环节。课文暗藏的主题是反应时间：一般司机的反应要超过半秒，时速三十英里时，车在这段时间里能开出好几米，所以五英尺即使对好司机也是很严的要求。

✨ How it is written赏析

The story is shaped like a rising line that falls at the very end. The first five sentences climb steadily from doubt to confidence, and the writer even lets himself enjoy the test. The passive forms early on show him being led, and the moment he feels in control is exactly when the trap is set. The examiner's words are the only direct speech, and they are calm, which makes the last line harder. The title, a play on a phrase meaning by chance, asks whether he hit or missed, and the answer comes in one mournful sentence.

故事的形状是一条上升、最后猛跌的线。前五句一路从怀疑爬到自信，作者甚至开始享受考试。前面的被动语态表明他一直被人领着走，而他觉得一切尽在掌握的那一刻，恰恰是陷阱设下的时候。考官的话是全篇唯一的直接引语，语气平静，这让最后一句更扎心。标题玩了一个意为「碰运气」的短语，问他是撞了还是没撞，答案在一句沉痛的话里揭晓。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The examiner's sad joke carries a serious lesson that every driver should remember. Confidence is useful, but the moment we feel sure of ourselves is often when attention slips. Driving instructors say the same about the weeks after passing the test, when new drivers relax and accidents rise. Chinese learners preparing for the subject tests in China will recognise the pressure: many people fail not on hard skills but on a single lapse at the end. The real aim of a test is not a piece of paper but the habit of staying alert, even when the road looks easy.

考官这句伤感的玩笑里有一个每个司机都该记住的严肃教训。自信有用，可我们自以为稳的那一刻，往往正是注意力溜走的时候。驾校教练对刚拿到驾照的那几周也这么说：新司机一放松，事故就增多。在中国准备「科目考试」的学员会认出这种压力：很多人不是败在难的技术上，而是败在最后一下走神。考试的真正目的不是一张证，而是养成即使路看着好走也保持警觉的习惯。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 1第 1 句 (begins开头 “I was being …”)

  • was being tested: past continuous passive, in the middle of being tested.was being tested：过去进行时被动，「正在接受测试」。
  • for the third time: this hints that the first two attempts failed.for the third time：第三次，暗示前两次没过。

Sentence 2第 2 句 (begins开头 “I had been …”)

  • had been asked: past perfect passive.had been asked：过去完成时被动。
  • do so: replaces the earlier verb phrase so it is not repeated.do so：代替前面的 drive in heavy traffic，避免重复。

Sentence 3第 3 句 (begins开头 “After having been …”)

  • after having been done: a perfect passive -ing form after a preposition.after having been done：完成式被动的动名词，在介词后。
  • acquire confidence: gradually become confident.acquire confidence：逐渐有了信心。

Sentence 4第 4 句 (begins开头 “Sure that I …”)

  • Sure that: an adjective phrase at the front, meaning because I was sure.Sure that …：形容词短语作状语，= Because I was sure that …。

Sentence 5第 5 句 (begins开头 “The examiner must …”)

  • must have done: a confident guess about the past.must have done：对过去的肯定推测。
  • for: gives the evidence for the guess.for：因为，补充推测的依据。

Sentence 9第 9 句 (begins开头 “Though the sound …”)

  • it took someone ages to do: they needed a lot of time for it.it took sb a long time to do：某人花了很长时间才……。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

The passive in several tenses被动语态的几种时态
The passive is the right form of be plus the past participle: was being done, had been done, could be done, having been done. The tense goes on be; the participle stays the same.
被动＝be 的相应形式 + 过去分词：was being done（过去进行）、had been done（过去完成）、could be done（情态）、having been done（完成式动名词）。时态由 be 体现，过去分词不变。

Sentences 1, 2, 3 and 9 each use a different passive, so the writer stays in the position of the one being tested and told.

本课：第 1、2、3、9 句各用一种被动形式，整个故事里「我」一直处在被考、被指示的位置。

  • The road was being repaired when we drove past.
  • Having been told the news, she sat down.
must have done and Let us suppose thatmust have done 与 Let us suppose that
must have done: a confident guess about the past based on evidence. Let us suppose that or Suppose: asks the listener to imagine a case; use the present for a likely case and the past for an unlikely one.
must have done：根据证据推测过去「一定已经」；Let us suppose that … / Suppose (that) …：让对方设想一种情况，后面用一般现在时（真实设想）或过去式（不太可能的设想）。

In sentence 5 the writer guesses from the smile, and in sentence 6 the examiner sets the last task with Let us suppose.

本课：第 5 句「我」凭考官的笑推测他很满意；第 6 句考官用 Let us suppose 设下最后一道题。

  • She must have left — her coat has gone.
  • Suppose it rains tomorrow — what shall we do?

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • I was being tested for … for the third time. 我第三次接受……的考核
    I was being interviewed for the job for the third time.
  • After having been told to …, I began to … 在被告知……之后，我开始……
    After having been told to relax, I began to enjoy the interview.
  • Sure that I had …, I was almost beginning to … 我确信自己已经……，几乎开始……
    Sure that I had won, I was almost beginning to celebrate.
  • Let us suppose that … 我们假设……
    Let us suppose that the power goes off during the exam.
  • Though the … was perfectly clear, it took X … to … 尽管……很清楚，X 却花了……才……
    Though the alarm was perfectly clear, it took Sam ages to wake up.

Our model summary本站范文

Mr Eames was taking his driving test for the third time. He drove well in heavy traffic and on the open road, and felt sure he had passed. Smiling, the examiner set one last task: when he knocked on the window, Mr Eames had to stop within five feet, as if a child had run out. The knock was loud, but he was slow to react. When at last he braked, the examiner sadly said the child was dead.（79 词）
