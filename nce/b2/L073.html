📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 73 “The Record-Holder”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 73《The Record-Holder》

B1🧠 21 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
Most children who skip school, the writer says, have little imagination: a day by a river with a rod, or a whole day in the dark watching one film again and again, is about their limit. One boy has shamed them all by covering 1,600 miles. He thumbed lifts to Dover, climbed aboard a boat at dusk for a place to sleep, and woke to find himself in Calais. He slipped ashore unseen, got a lorry ride towards Paris and was given a snack before being dropped on the edge of town. His next lift went right past the capital and all the way south to the Spanish frontier, where a policeman stopped him and officials shipped him home. This is a news story told with a teasing tone. The grammar is play truant, put someone to shame, while doing and the future in the past, as he hoped it would.
逃学的孩子一般没什么想象力：安安静静钓一天鱼，或者在电影院里坐八个小时，把同一部片子看了又看，这差不多就是他们的极限了。可有一个男孩让他们全都相形见绌：他逃学时走了 1600 英里。他搭便车到多佛，傍晚钻进一条船找地方睡觉，第二天醒来发现船已经开到了加来。没人注意到他溜下了船。他又搭一辆卡车去巴黎，司机给了他几块饼干和一杯咖啡，把他放在城外。下一辆车没像他希望的那样把他带进巴黎市中心，而是一路开到了法国和西班牙交界的佩皮尼昂。在那里他被一个警察拦下，当地政府把他送回了英国。本课是一篇带调侃语气的社会新闻，学 play truant、put sb to shame、while doing 和过去将来时 as he hoped it would。

  2. Why does the writer call other truants unimaginative?作者为什么说别的逃学孩子「没有想象力」？

本课未署名、为教材自撰；男孩没有姓名，它可能取材的那则报纸新闻我们没有查到。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

Dover and Calais face each other across the narrowest part of the Channel, about twenty miles apart, and ferries have crossed between them for centuries. In the years when the lesson was written it was quite possible to walk off a boat without a passport check being noticed, and hitchhiking was a common way for young people to travel across Europe. Perpignan lies in the far south of France, a few miles from the Spanish border, so the boy's last lift took him the whole length of the country. Today such a journey would be much harder: passports are checked, children travelling alone are noticed, and schools in Britain must report long absences, with parents liable to fines.

多佛和加来隔着英吉利海峡最窄的地方相望，相距约二十英里，几百年来渡船一直往返其间。课文写成的年代，从船上走下来而没人注意查护照是很可能的，搭便车也是年轻人游欧洲的常见方式。佩皮尼昂在法国最南端，离西班牙边境几英里，所以男孩最后搭的那趟车几乎带他纵贯了整个法国。今天这样的旅程要难得多：护照要查，单独出行的孩子会引人注意，英国学校对长期缺勤必须上报，家长可能被罚款。

✨ How it is written赏析

The lesson opens with an opinion rather than a fact, a mock-serious complaint that truants lack imagination. That tone runs through the piece: the writer seems to admire the boy, as if he had set a sporting record, and the last sentence says so with surely and set up a record. The journey itself is told in a quick chain of short events, each one a small surprise: sleep, wake in France, creep off, a lorry, the wrong car. The phrase as he hoped it would is the only glimpse into the boy's mind, and it is a sign that he did have a plan, just not the one he got.

课文开头是一个观点而不是事实，一句假装严肃的抱怨：逃学的孩子缺乏想象力。这种语气贯穿全篇：作者似乎很欣赏这个男孩，好像他创下了一项体育纪录，最后一句用 surely 和 set up a record 明说了出来。旅程本身是一串快节奏的短事件，每件都是个小意外：睡着、在法国醒来、溜下船、一辆卡车、一辆开错方向的车。as he hoped it would 是唯一一处让我们瞥见男孩心思的地方，说明他确实有个计划，只是结果不是他计划的那样。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The tone is playful, but most readers today would worry first about the boy's safety. A child alone on boats and in strangers' cars across another country was in real danger, however funny the story sounds. The teasing also hides a real question: why do children run away from school? Boredom, bullying and trouble at home are common reasons, and a good school tries to find out which. Chinese readers may recall stories of students who left school to chase a dream; such stories are admired in hindsight when they end well, but the many that end badly are not reported. The joke is fine, as long as we remember the risk.

语气是戏谑的，但今天大多数读者首先会担心男孩的安全。一个孩子独自坐船、坐陌生人的车穿过另一个国家，不管故事听起来多好笑，他都处在真实的危险中。调侃背后还藏着一个真问题：孩子为什么要逃离学校？无聊、受欺负、家里出了事，都是常见原因，好学校会去弄清是哪一种。中国读者也许会想起学生辍学追梦的故事；结局好的事后被人称道，结局不好的大多没人报道。笑话可以笑，只要记住其中的风险。

  2. Write about a journey that did not go where you planned. Use as I hoped it would once.

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 3第 3 句 (begins开头 “or eight hours …”)

  • a quiet day's fishing: a possessive of time, a day spent fishing.a quiet day's fishing：名词所有格表时间，「安静的一天垂钓」。
  • Two subjects joined by or; the verb agrees with the nearer one, so it is singular.两个主语用 or 连接，谓语跟最近的主语，用单数 is。
  • as far as they get: the furthest they go, here meaning the limit of their ideas.as far as they get：他们最多也就到这个地步。

Sentence 4第 4 句 (begins开头 “They have all …”)

  • The passive of put someone to shame: be made to look poor in comparison.put sb to shame 的被动：使相形见绌。
  • while playing truant: while plus -ing, with he was left out.while playing truant：while + 现在分词，省略了 he was。

Sentence 6第 6 句 (begins开头 “When he woke …”)

  • had travelled: past perfect, before he woke.had travelled：过去完成时，在他醒来之前。
  • in the meantime sits between had and the participle.in the meantime 插在助动词和过去分词之间。

Sentence 7第 7 句 (begins开头 “No one noticed …”)

  • creep, crept, crept: move quietly; as: while.creep-crept-crept：悄悄溜走。as：当……时。

Sentence 10第 10 句 (begins开头 “The next car …”)

  • A relative clause with that left out.「他拦下的下一辆车」：省略 that 的定语从句。
  • as he hoped it would: would is a future seen from the past, with the rest left out.as he hoped it would：过去将来时，would 后省略了 take him there。
  • not ... but: not this, rather that.not … but …：不是……而是……。

Sentence 12第 12 句 (begins开头 “There he was …”)

  • Two passives sharing one was.两个并列的被动，共用 was。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

while and when with an -ing formwhile / when + 现在分词
When the subject of a time clause is the same as the main subject, you can drop the subject and be, leaving while doing or when doing. Check that the missing subject really is the main subject.
时间从句的主语和主句相同时，可以省去主语和 be，写成 while doing / when doing。要确认省掉的主语确实就是主句主语。

Sentence 4 uses while playing truant for how far the boy went during his time away from school.

本课：第 4 句用 while playing truant 说男孩在逃学期间走了多远。

  • While waiting for the bus, I read the paper.
  • Be careful when crossing the road.
The future in the past: would过去将来时：would 与 as … hoped it would
To speak of the future from a past point, use would plus the base form. In comparisons such as as he hoped it would, the verb after would is usually dropped to avoid repeating it.
站在过去看将来用 would + 动词原形；在 as I hoped / expected / thought it would 这类比较里，would 后面的动词常省去，以免重复。

Sentence 10 says the car did not go where the boy hoped.

本课：第 10 句说那辆车没像男孩希望的那样开进巴黎。

  • She said she would call me.
  • The film was not as good as I thought it would be.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • Children who … are usually … ……的孩子通常……
    Children who read a lot are usually good at writing.
  • … is usually as far as X get. ……通常就是 X 的极限了
    A short walk round the park is usually as far as my cats get.
  • Y, who …, has put X to shame. Y ……，让 X 相形见绌
    A girl who swam the whole lake has put the adults to shame.
  • When he woke up, X discovered that … had, in the meantime, … X 醒来后发现……在此期间已经……
    When she woke up, Ann discovered that the snow had, in the meantime, covered the car.
  • The next … did not take me … as I hoped it would; instead … 下一辆……没像我希望的那样……，而是……
    The next train did not take me home as I hoped it would; instead it went to the airport.

Our model summary本站范文

While playing truant, a boy covered 1,600 miles. He got lifts to Dover and climbed onto a boat to sleep, but by morning it had crossed to Calais. Nobody saw him leave. A lorry driver took him to the edge of Paris, and the next car carried him not into the city but far south to Perpignan, near Spain. There a policeman stopped him, and he was sent back to England.（71 词）
