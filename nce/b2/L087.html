Lesson 87 · A Perfect Alibi 完美的不在场证明

📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 87 “A Perfect Alibi”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 87《A Perfect Alibi》

B1🧠 17 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
A man tells a police inspector that at the time of the killing he was on his usual early train to London. Asked whether he always takes so early a train, he says yes: he must be at work by ten, and his boss can confirm he was on time. Would a later one get him there too? Probably, he admits, but he never takes it. He says he reached the station before eight, bought a paper and noticed nothing odd. The inspector then springs his trap: the man must have taken the later train, because that morning the early one never ran; it had broken down and been taken off the line. This is a police interview where one detail breaks a lie. The grammar is two meanings of suggest, the present simple for habits, would for likely results and I suppose so.
一个男人对探长说：凶案发生时，他正坐在开往伦敦的 8 点钟那趟火车上。探长问他是不是总赶这么早的车，他说当然，他 10 点必须上班，老板可以证明他准时到了。探长又问：晚一点的车能不能让他准时上班？他说大概能，可他从不坐晚一点的车。他说自己 7 点 50 分到站，买了份报纸等车，也没注意到任何异常。探长这才亮出底牌：你没有坐 8 点那趟，坐的是 8 点 25 那趟，照样赶得上上班——因为案发那天早上，8 点的车根本没开，它在弗恩格林站出了故障，撤下了线路。本课是一段侦探问话，靠一个细节拆穿谎言，学 suggest 的两种意思、一般现在时表习惯、would 表推测 和 I suppose so。

本课未署名、为教材自撰；弗恩格林这个站名看来是虚构的，故事属于侦探小说里经典的短谜题。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

The broken alibi is one of the oldest tools of detective fiction, and railway timetables became a favourite source of such puzzles in Britain from the nineteen twenties onward. Freeman Wills Crofts, an Irish railway engineer turned novelist, built whole books around timetables, and Agatha Christie used train schedules and travel times in several of her stories. Real police work still relies on the same idea, checking whether a person's account matches facts that can be proved, though today the evidence is more often a ticket record, a bank card payment or a security camera than a broken-down train.

拆穿不在场证明是侦探小说最古老的手法之一；从二十世纪二十年代起，铁路时刻表在英国成了这类谜题最受欢迎的素材。爱尔兰的铁路工程师出身的小说家弗里曼·威尔斯·克罗夫茨整本整本地围绕时刻表构思，阿加莎·克里斯蒂也在好几篇故事里用到列车时刻和行程时间。真实的警察工作至今依靠同一个思路：核对一个人的说法与可以证明的事实是否吻合，只是今天的证据更多是车票记录、银行卡付款或监控录像，而不是一列抛锚的火车。

✨ How it is written赏析

The whole lesson is dialogue, and the reader becomes the second detective. The inspector's questions seem polite and almost pointless, about habits and later trains, but each one closes a door: the man commits himself to the early train, admits a later train would work and claims he saw nothing unusual. The last answer is the key, because a cancelled train is exactly the kind of unusual thing anyone on the platform would notice. The writer gives the solution in a single speech, calm and exact, with the phrase I suggest repeated like steps closing in. A careful reader can spot the trap before the inspector springs it.

整课都是对话，读者成了第二个侦探。探长的问题看似客气、几乎没什么用——关于习惯、关于晚一点的车——但每一个问题都关上一扇门：这个人一口咬定自己坐的是早车，承认晚一点的车也行，又声称没注意到任何异常。最后这个回答是关键，因为一趟被取消的火车，正是站台上任何人都会注意到的那种异常。作者把答案放在一段讲话里，平静而精确，I suggest 反复出现，像一步步逼近的脚步。细心的读者能在探长收网之前看出这个圈套。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The story teaches a useful way of checking any account: not by attacking it head-on, but by asking small questions that the speaker cannot fake, then comparing the answers with known facts. It also hints at a weakness in this kind of reasoning: proving the man lied about the train does not prove he committed the murder, only that he had time to. Chinese readers who grew up with Detective Conan or translated Sherlock Holmes stories will know the pleasure of the broken alibi; the lesson's simple version is a good one to retell in English, because every step depends on a tense or a modal verb.

这个故事教给我们一种核对任何说法的好方法：不是正面攻击，而是问一些对方无法编造的小问题，再把答案和已知事实对照。它也暗示了这种推理的一个弱点：证明这个人在火车的事上说了谎，并不能证明他杀了人，只能证明他有时间去做。看《名侦探柯南》或中文译本福尔摩斯长大的中国读者，熟悉拆穿不在场证明的乐趣；这一课的简单版本很适合用英语复述，因为每一步都取决于一个时态或一个情态动词。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 1第 1 句 (begins开头 “'At the time …”)

  • at the time: when; was committed is passive.at the time (that) …：在……的时候；was committed：被动。
  • was travelling: the past continuous for what he says he was doing at that moment, which is what an alibi claims.was travelling：过去进行时，说案发时正在做什么——这正是不在场证明的说法。

Sentence 2第 2 句 (begins开头 “'Do you always …”)

  • The present with always asks about a habit; such goes before a plus an adjective plus a noun.一般现在时 + always：问习惯。such + a + 形容词 + 名词。

Sentence 6第 6 句 (begins开头 “'Would a later …”)

  • would: asks about a likely result in a supposed case; get someone to: take them to.would：推测假设情况下的结果。get sb to：把某人送到。

Sentence 7第 7 句 (begins开头 “'I suppose it …”)

  • I suppose it would: the rest of the question is left out.I suppose it would：我想会的。省略了 get me to work on time。

Sentence 13第 13 句 (begins开头 “I suggest that …”)

  • suggest here means put forward a view, so an ordinary statement follows, not should.suggest 在这里是「提出（看法）、暗示」，后接陈述句，不用 should。
  • Two that clauses are joined with not ... but.two that 从句并列：not … but …。

Sentence 16第 16 句 (begins开头 “It broke down …”)

  • break down: stop working; take off the line: remove from service.break down：出故障；take off the line：撤出运营线路。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

Two meanings of suggestsuggest 的两种意思
When it gives advice, suggest takes doing or that someone (should) do. When it puts forward a view or hints at a fact, it takes that plus an ordinary statement in the normal tense.
① 建议：suggest doing 或 suggest that sb (should) do；② 提出看法、暗示：suggest that + 陈述句（时态照常）。

In sentences 12 to 14 the inspector uses suggest three times, always to state his view of the facts.

本课：第 12–14 句探长三次用 suggest，都是第②种：提出他对事实的看法。

  • I suggest going by bus. (advice)
  • The evidence suggests that he was lying. (view)
The present simple for habits, with always and never一般现在时表习惯 + always / never
The present simple describes regular habits; frequency adverbs such as always, usually and never go before the main verb but after be.
一般现在时说经常、习惯性的动作；always、usually、never 等频度副词放在实义动词前、be 动词后。

Sentences 2, 3 and 7 are about habit: he always takes the early train and never a later one.

本课：第 2、3、7 句问答都是习惯：总是坐早车、从不坐晚车。

  • She always gets up at six.
  • He is never late.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • At the time … happened, I was …ing … ……发生的时候，我正在……
    At the time the fire started, I was sleeping upstairs.
  • Do you always … such a … ? 你总是……这么……的吗？
    Do you always wear such a heavy coat?
  • My boss will confirm that I was … 我老板可以证明我……
    My teacher will confirm that I was in class.
  • I suppose so, but I never … 我想是吧，可我从不……
    I suppose so, but I never drive at night.
  • I suggest that you are not … 我看你并没有……
    I suggest that you are not being honest with me.

Our model summary本站范文

A man told an inspector that at the time of the crime he was on his usual early train to London, which he always caught because he had to be at work by ten. He agreed that a later train would also be on time, but said he never took it. He said he had noticed nothing unusual at the station. The inspector pointed out that the early train had broken down that morning and had not run at all.（80 词）
