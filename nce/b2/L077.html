Lesson 77 · A Successful Operation 一次成功的手术

📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 77 “A Successful Operation”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 77《A Successful Operation》

B1🧠 21 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
The preserved body of a woman from ancient Egypt, nearly three thousand years old, has just been operated on. She was once a temple singer at Thebes. Odd shadows on her X-rays made doctors wonder whether some rare illness had killed her, and the only way to know was to cut. The work took more than four hours, because hard resin coated her body. They took out a piece for testing and found a small wax image of a god that the X-rays had missed. They still cannot say how she died, but the body, which they feared would crumble, came through in one piece. This is a science news item with a playful headline. The grammar is the present perfect with just and yet, non-defining relative clauses, die of and whether.
一具公元前 800 年去世的埃及女歌手的木乃伊刚刚「做了手术」。她叫谢普恩姆特，生前是底比斯神庙的歌手。X 光片上有些奇怪的斑点，医生想弄清她是不是死于一种罕见的病，唯一的办法就是动刀。手术做了四个多小时，皮肤上那层坚硬的树脂让工作很难做。医生切下一块送去化验，还发现了 X 光片上没显示出来的东西：一尊小小的蜡制神像。她怎么死的，至今还没定论；医生原先担心一切开木乃伊就会散架，幸好没有。本课是一则带幽默标题的科学新闻，学 现在完成时 just/yet、非限定性定语从句、die of 和 whether。

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Unsigned and written for the course as a news item. A mummy of a temple singer named Shepenmut does exist in a museum collection, but we have not traced the operation the lesson reports, so its details are unverified.

本课未署名，作为一条新闻为教材自撰。确有一具名叫谢普恩姆特的神庙女歌手木乃伊收藏在博物馆里，但课文报道的这次手术我们没有查到出处，细节未核实。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

Duamutef was one of the Four Sons of Horus, gods who guarded the inner organs of the dead. In art he is almost always shown with the head of a jackal, not a cow, so the lesson has his picture wrong. From around three thousand years ago, Egyptian embalmers often wrapped the organs and put them back inside the body with small wax figures of these four gods, instead of storing them in separate jars, which fits a woman who died about eight centuries before our era. Hard resin was poured over many bodies of that age as part of the process. Today researchers rarely cut a mummy open: modern scanning machines can show bones, objects and signs of illness in three dimensions without touching the wrappings.

杜阿木特夫是「荷鲁斯四子」之一，这四位神守护死者的内脏。在艺术作品里他几乎总是长着胡狼头，而不是牛头，所以课文把他的样子写错了。大约从三千年前起，埃及的防腐师常把内脏包好放回体内，旁边放上这四位神的小蜡像，不再存进单独的罐子里——这与一位约公元前八世纪去世的女子正好吻合。那个时期的许多尸体在处理时都会浇上一层坚硬的树脂。今天研究人员很少再切开木乃伊：现代扫描设备不碰裹布就能以三维方式显示骨骼、随葬物和疾病的痕迹。

✨ How it is written赏析

The fun of the piece is in its headline and its vocabulary. It borrows the words a hospital uses for a living patient, an operation, its length, the fear that the patient will not survive, and applies them to someone who died almost three thousand years ago. The last line completes the joke: the patient came through the surgery well. In between, the writer follows the order of a real news report: the event first, then who she was, why the work was done, what happened, what was found and what is still unknown. Short tense changes carry this order, from the recent past to a past story and back to the present, with not yet leaving the question open.

这篇的乐趣在标题和用词上。它借用医院形容活着的病人的那套词——手术、手术时长、担心病人挺不过来——用在一个去世将近三千年的人身上。最后一句把玩笑说圆：病人顺利挺过了手术。中间部分，作者照真实新闻的顺序写：先写事件，再写她是谁、为什么动手术、过程如何、发现了什么、还有什么未知。时态的小变化撑起这个顺序：从刚刚发生的事，到过去的经过，再回到现在，最后用 not yet 把问题留着。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The lesson is worth reading with a critical eye. A cow-headed Duamutef is a small slip, but it shows how easily a confident report can carry an error, a theme that links back to the joke news in the previous lesson. The bigger change is ethical. Many museums now ask whether cutting into human remains is right at all, and some no longer show mummies unwrapped, out of respect for the dead and for the countries they came from. Chinese readers may think of the long debates over excavating imperial tombs: scholars have often argued for waiting until methods are better rather than opening a site and losing what cannot be restored. Patience, it turns out, is also a scientific tool.

这一课值得带着批判的眼光读。牛头的杜阿木特夫只是个小错误，但它说明一篇自信的报道多么容易夹带错误——这和上一课的假新闻主题正好相连。更大的变化在伦理上。许多博物馆现在会问：切开人类遗体到底对不对；有些已经不再展出解开裹布的木乃伊，以示对逝者和其来源国家的尊重。中国读者也许会想到关于发掘帝王陵墓的长期争论：学者们常常主张等技术更好再说，而不是打开遗址、失去无法复原的东西。原来，耐心也是一种科学工具。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 1第 1 句 (begins开头 “The mummy of …”)

  • has just had: the present perfect with just, for something very recent; saying a mummy had an operation is a deliberate joke.has just had an operation：现在完成时 + just，刚刚做了手术。「木乃伊做手术」是有意的幽默说法。
  • The who clause describes the woman, not the mummy.who died in 800 B.C.：定语从句修饰 woman，不是修饰 mummy。

Sentence 2第 2 句 (begins开头 “The mummy is …”)

  • that of stands for 'the mummy of', so the noun is not repeated.that of：代替前面的 the mummy，避免重复。

Sentence 4第 4 句 (begins开头 “doctors have been …”)

  • have been trying: have kept trying; find out whether: learn if something is true.have been trying：一直在设法。find out whether：弄清是否。
  • die of: be killed by an illness.die of：死于（疾病等）。

Sentence 6第 6 句 (begins开头 “The operation, which …”)

  • The which clause between commas only adds information; the main sentence is complete without it.逗号隔开的 which 从句只是补充信息，去掉主句仍完整。
  • prove to be: turn out to be.prove to be：结果证明是。

Sentence 12第 12 句 (begins开头 “They feared that …”)

  • fear that: be afraid that; would looks forward from that past moment.fear that：担心。would：过去将来，从「当时」看以后。
  • fall to pieces: break apart.fall to pieces：散成碎块。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

The present perfect with just, already and yet现在完成时：just / already / yet
just means a moment ago and already means sooner than expected; both go in positive sentences. yet goes in negatives and questions, at the end or straight after not.
just 刚刚、already 已经（多用于肯定句），yet 还（没）、已经（用于否定句和疑问句，放句末或 not 后）。

Sentence 1 uses just to give the news, and sentence 11 uses not yet for the cause of death, which is still open.

本课：第 1 句用 just 报告新闻，第 11 句用 not yet 说死因还没定。

  • The train has just left.
  • Have you finished your homework yet? — No, not yet.
Non-defining relative clauses非限定性定语从句
Set off by commas, it only adds information. It cannot begin with that; use which or who. Without it the main sentence still makes full sense.
用逗号与主句隔开，只给补充信息，不能用 that，可用 which/who。去掉它，主句意思照样完整。

In sentence 6 the which clause adds how long the operation took; in sentence 10 the clause about the god has no commas, so it defines.

本课：第 6 句的 which 从句给手术补充时长；第 10 句说神像的 which 从句没有逗号，是限定性的。

  • My brother, who lives in Toronto, is a doctor.
  • The bridge, which was built in 1890, is still in use.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • X has just had … X 刚刚做了……
    Our old car has just had a new engine.
  • Since …, experts have been trying to learn whether … 由于……，专家一直在设法弄清是否……
    Since there was smoke in the hall, the staff have been trying to learn whether a heater was faulty.
  • The only way to … was to … ……的唯一办法就是……
    The only way to reach the island was to swim.
  • They also found something which … did not show. 他们还发现了……没有显示出来的东西
    The divers also found a wreck which the old charts did not show.
  • They feared that … would …, but fortunately … 他们曾担心……会……，幸好……
    We feared that the bridge would collapse, but fortunately it held.

Our model summary本站范文

Doctors have operated on the mummy of an Egyptian temple singer who died nearly three thousand years ago. Strange marks on X-rays suggested that she might have had a rare illness, so they had to open the body. The work lasted more than four hours, as hard resin covered her. A piece was sent for testing, and a tiny wax god was found inside. They do not yet know how she died, but the mummy did not break up.（79 词）
