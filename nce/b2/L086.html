Lesson 86 · Out of Control 失控

📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 86 “Out of Control”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 86《Out of Control》

B1🧠 20 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
A man is turning a speedboat when the wheel comes off in his hands. He signals wildly to his friend, who is water-skiing behind, and before either understands, both are flung into the sea. The boat, after hitting a buoy, races on, and as they start swimming for land they see to their horror that it is circling. It charges at them and roars by a few feet away. They swim hard, knowing it will be back, and are only just clear when it comes round again, this time much slower, because its fuel is almost gone. Soon the engine dies and the boat drifts quietly. This is a tense scene of danger told at speed. The grammar is hardly ... when, had just done ... when, the past perfect continuous and as quickly as one could.
一个人正想让快艇调头，方向盘竟脱落在他手里。他拼命向同伴挥手——那人已经滑了一刻钟的水。两人还没反应过来，就被狠狠地甩进海里。快艇撞上了一个浮标，却还在水面上飞驰。两人刚开始往岸边游，就惊恐地发现快艇在兜圈子，正以极快的速度朝他们冲过来，不到一分钟就呼啸着从几英尺外擦过。他们拼命往前游，知道它很快还会回来。刚游出危险区，快艇又转完一圈，不过这次慢多了——汽油差不多用光了。不久马达声完全停了，快艇在水上轻轻地漂着。本课是一段节奏紧张的险情描写，学 hardly … when、had just done … when、过去完成进行时 和 as quickly as one could。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

A boat that keeps turning in a circle after its driver falls out is a known danger on the water, sometimes called the circle of death, and it can hit the people in the water again and again. Many motorboats now carry a kill switch, a cord clipped to the driver's body that cuts the engine at once if he is thrown off, and in several countries the law requires drivers of small powerboats to wear it. Water-skiing also has safety rules: a second person on the boat should watch the skier, and the boat should keep a safe distance from buoys and swimmers.

驾驶员落水后，船还在不停地兜圈子，这是水上的一种已知危险，有时被称为「死亡圆圈」，它会一次又一次撞向水里的人。现在许多汽艇都装有熄火开关：一根绳子扣在驾驶员身上，他一旦被甩出去，发动机立刻熄火；在好几个国家，法律要求小型动力艇的驾驶员必须戴上它。滑水也有安全规则：艇上要有第二个人盯着滑水者，船要与浮标和游泳的人保持安全距离。

✨ How it is written赏析

The writer builds suspense out of timing. Three times the same pattern appears, one action has hardly begun when another arrives, so the reader feels that events are moving faster than the men can think. Each danger is told in a short sentence with a strong verb: thrown, struck, roared. Then, just as the reader expects the worst, the tension is released in small steps, slowed down considerably, the petrol nearly gone, the noise dropping, the boat drifting gently. The calm last word is a deliberate contrast with the violence at the start.

作者用时间的掐点来制造悬念。同一个句式出现了三次——一件事刚开头，另一件事就来了——让读者觉得事情发生得比人反应得还快。每一次危险都用一个短句和一个有力的动词写出：被甩出去、撞上、呼啸而过。接着，就在读者以为最坏的事要发生时，紧张感一步一步松开：速度明显放慢、汽油几乎用光、声音渐渐消失、船轻轻漂着。最后那个平静的词是故意和开头的猛烈形成对照。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The story ends happily by luck: the fuel ran out. A modern reader should notice what would have prevented the danger in the first place, a properly checked boat and an engine cut-off cord. Accident reports from water sports often show the same chain of small failures, poor maintenance, no safety device and no plan for what to do if the driver falls. Chinese families who holiday by lakes and beaches, at home or abroad, increasingly hire speedboats and jet skis; asking whether the boat has a kill switch, a life jacket for everyone and a trained driver is a simple habit that can save lives.

这个故事因为运气才有好结局：油用完了。现代读者应该注意，本来有什么能从一开始就避免这场危险：一条检修过的船和一根发动机熄火绳。水上运动的事故报告常常显示同一串小疏忽：保养不当、没有安全装置、没有想过驾驶员落水该怎么办。在国内外湖边、海边度假的中国家庭越来越多地租快艇和水上摩托；问一句船上有没有熄火开关、是不是人人都有救生衣、驾驶员有没有受过训练，是一个能救命的简单习惯。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

hardly ... when and had just ... when
Put the main clause in the past perfect with hardly or just, and the when clause in the past simple: the second event came right after the first. If hardly opens the sentence, invert: Hardly had ...
主句用过去完成时（had hardly / had just done），when 从句用一般过去时，表示前一件事刚发生，后一件事就突然来了。hardly 放句首要倒装（Hardly had …）。

Sentences 4, 5 and 10 use this structure three times to pile one surprise on another.

本课：第 3、5、10 句三次用这个结构，造出一个接一个的意外。

  • I had hardly sat down when the phone rang.
  • Hardly had we left when it began to rain.
The past perfect continuous: had been doing过去完成进行时：had been doing
It describes an action that had been going on up to a past moment, often with for and a period, and stresses how long it lasted.
到过去某时为止一直在进行的动作，常带 for + 时段，强调持续。

In sentence 3, the friend's water-skiing had lasted a quarter of an hour by the time of the wave.

本课：第 3 句：挥手之前，同伴已经滑了一刻钟。

  • She was tired because she had been working all day.
  • We had been waiting for an hour when the bus came.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • As X tried to …, the … came away in his hands. X 正想……，……竟脱落在他手里
    As Dad tried to open the door, the handle came away in his hands.
  • X had hardly had time to … when … X 还没来得及……，就……
    We had hardly had time to sit down when the bell rang.
  • X had only just started to …, when X saw with dismay that … X 刚开始……，就惊恐地发现……
    The hikers had only just started to climb, when they saw with dismay that a storm was coming.
  • After it had passed, X went on as fast as they could. 它过去之后，X 尽快地继续……
    After the truck had passed, the children walked on as fast as they could.
  • Before long, the … stopped completely and … 不久，……完全停了，……
    Before long, the rain stopped completely and the sun came out.

Our model summary本站范文

While a man was turning his speedboat, the steering wheel came off. Before he and his water-skiing friend understood what was happening, the boat hit a buoy and threw them into the sea. As they swam towards land, the boat began to circle and rushed past them very close. They swam on as fast as possible. When it came round again, it was much slower, because the petrol was nearly finished. Soon it stopped and drifted.（76 词）
