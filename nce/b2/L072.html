📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 72 “A Car Called Bluebird”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 72《A Car Called Bluebird》

B1🧠 16 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
Malcolm Campbell became the first driver ever to pass three hundred miles an hour. In September 1935 he took the world record on the salt flats of Utah, in a car called Bluebird that had been made just for him: more than thirty feet long, with an engine of 2,500 horsepower. He touched just over 304, but a tyre blew out on his first run and the car was hard to hold. Afterwards he heard, to his dismay, that his average had come out just under the mark. A few days later the officials admitted a timing error: he had in fact averaged a little over three hundred. Drivers have since gone beyond six hundred. Years later his son Donald, also in a Bluebird, took a world record too. This is a sports record story. The grammar is the first man to do, have difficulty in doing, be disappointed to learn and follow in someone's footsteps.
著名赛车手马尔科姆·坎贝尔爵士是第一个时速超过 300 英里的人。1935 年 9 月，他在美国犹他州的博纳维尔盐滩创下世界纪录。他开的「蓝鸟」是专门为他造的，长三十多英尺，发动机 2500 马力。他的最高时速虽然超过了 304 英里，但第一趟就爆了胎，车很难控制。跑完后他失望地得知平均时速只有 299 英里；几天后却有人告诉他，计时出了错，平均时速其实是 301 英里。此后赛车手的速度已超过每小时 600 英里。多年后他的儿子唐纳德步父亲后尘，也开着一辆叫「蓝鸟」的车创下了世界纪录。本课是一篇体育人物纪事，学the first man to do、have difficulty (in) doing、be disappointed to learn 和 follow in sb's footsteps。

  2. Why was the car hard to control on the first run?坎贝尔第一趟为什么很难控制车子？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Unsigned and written for the course about a real driver and a real record.

本课未署名、为教材自撰，写的是一位真实的车手和一项真实的纪录。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

The record run took place on the third of September 1935 at Bonneville in Utah, and the official figure is usually given as just over 301 miles an hour, the first time anyone had passed three hundred on land. Campbell set land speed records several times in the twenties and thirties, and his cars and boats were all called Blue Bird or Bluebird. His son Donald pushed the land record past four hundred miles an hour in 1964 on Lake Eyre in Australia, and in the same year also set a water speed record. Donald died in 1967 when his jet boat crashed at high speed on Coniston Water in England. Today the land speed record belongs to the jet car Thrust SSC, which went faster than the speed of sound in 1997.

这次创纪录发生在 1935 年 9 月 3 日，地点是犹他州博纳维尔，官方成绩一般记作每小时 301 英里多一点，这是陆地上第一次有人突破 300 英里。坎贝尔在二三十年代多次刷新陆地速度纪录，他的赛车和赛艇都叫「蓝鸟」。他的儿子唐纳德 1964 年在澳大利亚艾尔湖把陆地纪录推过每小时 400 英里，同一年还创下了水上速度纪录。唐纳德 1967 年在英格兰的科尼斯顿湖驾驶喷气快艇高速翻覆身亡。如今陆地速度纪录属于喷气式赛车「推进号 SSC」，它在 1997 年跑出了超音速。

✨ How it is written赏析

The writer builds a small drama out of numbers. The top speed, 304, sounds safe; then the average, 299, falls just short of the magic figure; then 301 rescues it. Two numbers on either side of three hundred carry all the suspense, and the reader feels Campbell's disappointment and relief without being told much about his feelings. The passive structures in sentence 9 keep the officials faceless, so the focus stays on the number. The last two sentences widen the frame across time, to faster drivers and to a son, which turns one record into a family story.

作者用数字造出一场小戏。最高时速 304 听着稳当；接着平均时速 299，刚好差那个神奇的整数；然后 301 把它救了回来。三百两边的两个数字承担了全部悬念，读者不用被告知太多，就能感到坎贝尔的失望和宽慰。第 9 句的被动结构让官员没有面孔，焦点始终在数字上。最后两句把镜头拉宽到时间里，写到更快的车手和他的儿子，把一项纪录变成了一个家族故事。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The story is a good reminder that a record is only as reliable as the people and machines that measure it. Two miles an hour on the stopwatch decided whether a famous man had made history. Modern sport uses electronic timing to a thousandth of a second, and still arguments happen. The family side is worth a thought too: Donald followed his father's path and paid for it with his life, which raises the question of how much children should be pushed, or should push themselves, to match a parent. Chinese readers will know families where a child is expected to carry on a parent's trade, with both pride and pressure.

这个故事提醒我们：一项纪录的可靠程度，取决于测量它的人和机器。秒表上每小时两英里的差别，决定了一位名人是否创造了历史。现代体育用电子计时，精确到千分之一秒，可争议照样发生。家族这一面也值得一想：唐纳德沿着父亲的路走下去，最终付出了生命，这引出一个问题：孩子应该被推着、或推着自己，去追赶父母到什么程度？中国读者也熟悉那种期望子承父业的家庭，其中既有骄傲，也有压力。

    孩子应该子承父业吗？给出一个好处和一个风险。
  2. Write about a time a small mistake in numbers changed a result for you.
    写一次数字上的小错改变了你的结果的经历。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 2第 2 句 (begins开头 “The great racing …”)

  • the first man to do: an infinitive after the noun phrase.the first man to do：第一个做……的人，不定式作后置定语。
  • at over ... miles per hour: at a speed of more than.at over 300 miles per hour：以超过每小时 300 英里的速度。

Sentence 4第 4 句 (begins开头 “Bluebird, the car …”)

  • The phrase after the comma is in apposition, explaining the car's name.「他开的那辆车」：同位语，解释前面的车名。
  • had been built: past perfect passive, finished before the record.had been built：过去完成时被动，比赛之前就造好了。

Sentence 7第 7 句 (begins开头 “he had great …”)

  • although: a concession clause, so no but.although 引导让步状语从句，主句不再用 but。
  • have difficulty (in) doing: find it hard to do; in can be dropped.have difficulty (in) doing：做……有困难，in 可省。

Sentence 8第 8 句 (begins开头 “After his attempt, …”)

  • be disappointed to learn: the infinitive gives the cause of the feeling.be disappointed to learn：得知……很失望，不定式表原因。
  • had been: the speed belonged to the earlier run.had been：过去完成时，速度在他得知之前就已经测出。

Sentence 9第 9 句 (begins开头 “However, a few …”)

  • was told: passive; had been made: past perfect passive.was told：被告知；had been made：过去完成时被动。

Sentence 12第 12 句 (begins开头 “Following in his …”)

  • An -ing phrase whose subject is Donald.现在分词短语作状语，逻辑主语是 Donald。
  • follow in someone's footsteps: do what a parent or teacher did before.follow in sb's footsteps：步某人后尘，走同一条路。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

the first, the last and the only with to dothe first / last / only + 名词 + to do
When a noun has the first, the last, the only or a superlative before it, an infinitive often follows as a short relative clause: the first man to drive means the first man who drove.
名词前有 the first、the second、the last、the only 或最高级时，后面常用不定式作定语，相当于一个定语从句：the first man to drive = the first man who drove。

Sentence 2 uses it to give Campbell's title.

本课：第 2 句用这个结构说坎贝尔的头衔。

  • She was the first woman to climb the mountain.
  • He is the only person to know the answer.
Patterns for difficulty and feelings表示困难与情绪的结构
have difficulty or trouble (in) doing: find something hard. be plus a feeling adjective plus to do, as in disappointed or glad to learn or hear: the infinitive gives the cause.
have difficulty / trouble (in) doing：做……有困难；be + 情绪形容词 + to do（disappointed / surprised / glad / sorry to learn / hear）：得知……而……。

Sentence 7 shows his struggle with the car; sentence 8 shows his feeling on hearing the result.

本课：第 7 句他控制车子很费劲；第 8 句他得知成绩后很失望。

  • I had trouble finding your house.
  • We were glad to hear that you are well.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • X was the first … to … at over … X 是第一个以超过……的速度……的人
    She was the first pilot to fly at over twice the speed of sound.
  • X, the … she was sailing, had been specially … for her. X，她驾驶的那艘……，是专门为她……的
    Silver Star, the boat she was sailing, had been specially designed for her.
  • Although X reached …, he had great difficulty in … 虽然 X 达到了……，但他……很困难
    Although the climber reached the top, he had great difficulty in breathing.
  • X was disappointed to learn that … X 失望地得知……
    The team was disappointed to learn that the match had been cancelled.
  • Following in her mother's footsteps, X also … X 步母亲后尘，也……
    Following in her mother's footsteps, Lily also became a doctor.

Our model summary本站范文

In 1935 Sir Malcolm Campbell drove Bluebird, a car built specially for him, on the salt flats of Utah. Although he went faster than 304 miles an hour, a tyre burst on his first run and the car was hard to control. He was told at first that his average was just under 300, but days later the officials found an error: it was 301. Years later his son Donald also set a world record.（75 词）
