Lesson 65 · Jumbo Versus the Police 是大象不是猪

📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 65 “Jumbo Versus the Police”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 65《Jumbo Versus the Police》

B1🧠 18 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
One Christmas Jimmy Gates, who runs a circus, set out to bring gifts to sick children. Dressed as Santa and escorted by six young women, he rode through the city centre on a young elephant named Jumbo. The police, as he might have guessed, were not pleased. An officer told him the animal was blocking cars and he ought to have used a back road. Jimmy agreed to leave, but Jumbo would not budge, and fifteen officers had to shove him clear. Afterwards one joked that the beast was heavy enough to be glad they need not lift him, and that, given his clean record, they would not charge him. This is a light-hearted news story. The grammar is should have done and ought to have done, must for guessing, past participle phrases, and let someone off.
去年圣诞节，马戏团老板吉米·盖茨决定给一家儿童医院送礼物。他扮成圣诞老人，六个漂亮姑娘作「仪仗队」，骑着一头叫江伯的小象沿着市中心大街出发。他早该知道警察绝不会允许这种事。一个警察走过来说，大象挡住了交通，他本该走小路。吉米马上同意走，江伯却一动不动，十五个警察使劲推才把它推出大街。警察事后笑着说：大象准有好几吨重，幸好不用抬；按理该逮捕它，不过它一向表现良好，这次就放过它。本课是一则幽默社会新闻，学情态动词完成式 should have done / ought to have done、must 表推测、过去分词短语作状语和 let sb off。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. What forced the officers to shift Jumbo?警察为什么要把江伯推离大街？
  2. Was the policeman serious when he talked about arresting Jumbo?警察说要逮捕江伯，是认真的吗？

本课未署名、为教材自撰；吉米·盖茨和江伯是虚构的，但大象的名字借自一头真实的名象。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

The original Jumbo was an African elephant at London Zoo in the eighteen-seventies, so loved by children that there was a public outcry when he was sold to the American showman P. T. Barnum in 1882. He toured with Barnum's circus until he was killed by a train in Canada in 1885, in the Ontario town of St Thomas, which still has a statue of him. His name gave English the word jumbo for anything very large, as in a jumbo jet. Circuses with elephants were common in Britain when the lesson was written; today many countries ban or limit wild animals in travelling shows, so a modern Jimmy would face more than a traffic warning.

最早的江伯是十九世纪七十年代伦敦动物园的一头非洲象，深受孩子们喜爱。1882 年它被卖给美国演艺商 P. T. 巴纳姆时，引起了公众抗议。它随巴纳姆的马戏团巡演，1885 年在加拿大安大略省的圣托马斯镇被火车撞死，那里至今立着它的雕像。它的名字给英语带来了 jumbo 一词，指任何特别大的东西，比如 jumbo jet（大型喷气式客机）。课文写成时，英国的马戏团常有大象；如今许多国家禁止或限制巡回演出使用野生动物，今天的吉米面对的恐怕不只是一句交通警告了。

✨ How it is written赏析

The humour comes from treating an elephant as if it were a person under the law. The policeman's last speech is written in the language of the courtroom: should arrest, a good record, let him off this time. That serious tone applied to a baby elephant is the joke. The writer also uses the modal perfect twice early on to tell us what should have happened, so the reader expects trouble; then the trouble turns out to be comic rather than serious. Notice the long opening sentence too, with its participle phrases piling up the costume, the girls and the elephant before we even reach the street.

幽默来自把一头大象当成法律面前的人来对待。警察最后那段话用的是法庭腔：should arrest、a good record、let him off this time。这种正经腔调用在一头小象身上，就是笑点。作者开头两次用情态动词完成式，告诉我们「本该」怎样，于是读者预感要出麻烦；结果麻烦是喜剧而不是正剧。还要注意那个长长的开头句：几个分词短语把服装、姑娘和大象一一堆上来，我们还没走到大街上。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The story shows a kind side of authority: the police enforce the rule, but with a sense of humour, and nobody is punished for a good deed done badly. That is an attractive picture, and it is worth asking how often it happens in real life. It also raises a question that the lesson did not consider in its time: is it right to use wild animals for shows at all? Many people today would say the elephant, not the traffic, was the real problem. Readers can enjoy the joke and still notice how our ideas about animals have changed.

故事展现了执法者温和的一面：警察执行规定，却带着幽默感，没人因为「好心办了坏事」而受罚。这是一幅讨人喜欢的画面，也值得问一问现实中有多常见。它还引出一个当年课文没考虑的问题：用野生动物来表演本身对不对？今天很多人会说，真正的问题是大象，而不是交通。读者可以享受这个笑话，同时留意我们对动物的看法已经变了多少。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 2第 2 句 (begins开头 “Dressed up as …”)

  • Two past participle phrases at the front describe he, the subject of the main clause.Dressed up … and accompanied by …：两个过去分词短语作状语，逻辑主语是 he。
  • riding: an -ing phrase for what he was doing at the same time; called is a past participle after the noun.riding … called Jumbo：现在分词短语作伴随状语，called 是过去分词作后置定语。

Sentence 4第 4 句 (begins开头 “He should have …”)

  • should have done: it was right to do, but he did not.should have done：本该……（其实没有）。
  • would never: a future seen from the past.would never：过去看将来。

Sentence 5第 5 句 (begins开头 “A policeman approached …”)

  • ought to have done means the same as should have done, with a note of blame.ought to have done = should have done，带批评意味。
  • as: because. hold up: block or delay.as：因为。hold up：阻塞。

Sentence 6第 6 句 (begins开头 “Though Jimmy agreed …”)

  • though: a concession clause, so no but in the main clause.though 引导让步状语从句，主句前不再加 but。
  • agree to do and refuse to do: an opposite pair.agree to do / refuse to do：一对反义的动词搭配。

Sentence 9第 9 句 (begins开头 “'Jumbo must weigh …”)

  • must: a strong guess about the present.must：对现在的肯定推测，「一定」。
  • said a policeman: after a quotation the verb often comes before the subject.said a policeman：直接引语后主谓倒装，常见于叙述。

Sentence 11第 11 句 (begins开头 “Of course, we …”)

  • should: ought to by the rules; shall: will, used with we in British English.should：按理应该。shall：第一人称表将来（英式）。
  • let someone off: not punish them. The officer is joking, treating the elephant as a criminal.let sb off：放过某人。这是警察的玩笑，把大象当成了「犯人」。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

Modal perfect: should have done and ought to have done情态动词完成式：should have done / ought to have done
should or ought to plus have plus the past participle says that something was the right thing to do in the past but did not happen, often with blame or regret. The negative shouldn't have done means it happened but was wrong.
should / ought to + have + 过去分词：过去本该做却没做（含批评或遗憾）；否定 shouldn't have done 表示「本不该做却做了」。

Sentences 4 and 5 each use one: the writer says Jimmy should have foreseen it, and the policeman says which way he ought to have taken.

本课：第 4 句和第 5 句各一次：作者说吉米本该料到，警察说他本该走小路。

  • You should have told me you were coming.
  • I shouldn't have eaten so much cake.
must for strong guessesmust 表示推测
must plus the base form is a confident guess about now; must have done is a confident guess about the past. For a negative guess use can't or can't have done, not mustn't.
must + 动词原形：对现在的肯定推测；must have done：对过去的肯定推测；否定推测用 can't / can't have done，不用 mustn't。

In sentence 9 the policeman guesses the animal's weight with must.

本课：第 9 句警察用 must weigh 猜大象的体重。

  • She's been working all day. She must be tired.
  • The ground is wet. It must have rained last night.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • Dressed up as … and accompanied by …, X set off … X 打扮成……，在……陪同下出发……
    Dressed up as a pirate and accompanied by his sister, Tom set off to the party.
  • X should have known that … would never … X 本该知道……绝不会……
    She should have known that her boss would never agree.
  • X told Y he ought to have … as … X 告诉 Y，他本该……，因为……
    The teacher told me I ought to have asked for help as the task was hard.
  • Though X agreed to …, Y refused to … 尽管 X 同意……，Y 却拒绝……
    Though my father agreed to stop, the car refused to start.
  • Of course, we should …, but as …, we shall … 当然，我们本该……，不过由于……，我们就……
    Of course, we should charge you, but as it is your birthday, we shall forget it.

Our model summary本站范文

Last Christmas a circus owner, Jimmy Gates, dressed as Santa and rode a young elephant called Jumbo down the city's main road, taking gifts to a children's hospital. A policeman told him he was blocking the cars and should have used a side road. Jimmy agreed at once, but Jumbo would not move, so fifteen officers had to push him. Amused, one joked that Jumbo deserved arrest but would be let off this time.（74 词）
