Lesson 63 · She Was Not Amused 她就是这么不喜欢

📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 63 “She Was Not Amused”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 63《She Was Not Amused》

B1🧠 17 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
Jeremy Hampden has plenty of friends and is a favourite guest at any party, where people love his jokes, with one exception: Jenny, his daughter, who is six. A close friend asked him lately to speak at a wedding, which is just the kind of job he enjoys. He wrote it with care, packed it with funny stories, and it went down very well. The moment it ended, though, Jenny wanted to leave. Driving back, he asked whether she had liked it. She had not, she said, because she hated seeing a crowd laugh at her father. This is a short story with a twist at the end. The grammar is admire someone for something, the aside that is, reported questions with ask if, and the sort of thing that.
杰里米·汉普登朋友很多，在聚会上很受欢迎，人人都佩服他的幽默感——只有他六岁的女儿珍妮例外。最近一位好友请他在婚宴上致辞，这正是他最喜欢做的事。他认真准备，讲稿里放了很多笑话，致辞大获成功。可他一讲完，珍妮就说要回家。路上他问女儿喜不喜欢那篇致辞，没想到她说不喜欢，因为她不愿意看到那么多人笑话她爸爸。本课是一个带反转的小故事，学 admire sb for sth、that is 插入语、ask sb if 的间接引语和 the sort of thing 句型。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. What made Jenny ask to go home early?珍妮为什么想离开婚宴？
  2. What did Jenny think the guests were doing?在珍妮看来，客人们在做什么？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Unsigned and written for the course; Jeremy and Jenny are invented.

本课未署名、为教材自撰；杰里米和珍妮都是虚构人物。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

The wedding speech is a serious social duty in Britain. At a traditional reception the bride's father, the groom and the best man each speak, and the best man in particular is expected to be funny, often by telling embarrassing stories about the groom. Whole books and websites exist to help nervous speakers find jokes. Children, however, often do not share the adult rules of humour. Researchers who study how children understand laughter have noted that young children can find it hard to tell laughing with someone from laughing at them, especially when the person is a parent they want to protect.

婚礼致辞在英国是一项正经的社交任务。传统婚宴上，新娘的父亲、新郎和伴郎各讲一段，伴郎尤其要讲得好笑，常常抖出新郎的糗事。有整本整本的书和网站专门帮紧张的发言人找笑话。可孩子往往并不懂大人的幽默规矩。研究儿童如何理解笑的学者指出，小孩子很难分清「和某人一起笑」与「笑话某人」，尤其当那个人是他们想保护的爸爸妈妈时。

✨ How it is written赏析

The joke depends on the reader expecting one answer and getting another. Everything in the first half praises Jeremy, and the little phrase that is quietly sets up the exception. The writer then holds back the reason until the very last line, so we wonder, as Jeremy does, whether the speech was too long or too dull. The answer turns the meaning of laughing at upside down. Notice too that Jenny never speaks directly; her words are all reported, which keeps the story short and lets the last clause land as a single surprise, with the exclamation mark doing the work of the punch line.

这个笑话靠的是读者等着一个答案、得到的却是另一个。前半段全在夸杰里米，那个小小的 that is 悄悄埋下了例外。作者把理由压到最后一行才说，所以我们和杰里米一样纳闷：是致辞太长，还是太无聊？答案把 laughing at 的意思翻了个面。还要注意珍妮从头到尾没有直接说话，她的话全是转述的，这让故事很紧凑，最后一个从句作为唯一的惊喜落下，感叹号就是那句包袱。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

Jenny's view is not simply childish. She notices something adults sometimes forget: a crowd's laughter can feel like a judgement, and a person who is always the funny one may also be the one people never take seriously. Many good speakers use humour about themselves on purpose, because it makes listeners relax; but there is a line between that and becoming a clown. Chinese readers may think of the pressure at wedding banquets to entertain the guests, and of how children watch their parents' public face closely. The story invites a kind response from Jeremy, and from us: to take a child's worry seriously instead of laughing at it.

珍妮的看法并不只是孩子气。她注意到了大人有时会忘记的一点：一群人的笑声可能像一种评判，总是扮演「搞笑的那个人」，也可能是大家从不认真对待的那个人。很多会讲话的人故意拿自己开玩笑，因为这能让听众放松；但这和沦为小丑之间有一条线。中国读者会想到婚宴上要让宾客尽兴的压力，也会想到孩子是多么留心父母在人前的样子。故事邀请杰里米、也邀请我们，用善意回应：认真对待孩子的担心，而不是一笑了之。

💬 Discuss and write讨论与写作

    别人「跟你一起笑」和「笑你」有区别吗？你怎么分辨？

    为朋友的生日写一段约 80 词的致辞，里面放一个善意而不伤人的笑话。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 2第 2 句 (begins开头 “Everybody admires him …”)

  • admire someone for something: respect them because of it.admire sb for sth：因……佩服某人。
  • that is: here it adds an exception, correcting a statement that was too complete.that is：也就是说，这里用来补一个例外，修正前面说得太满的话。
  • six-year-old: a hyphenated adjective before the noun, with no s on year.six-year-old：连字符形容词作定语，year 不加 s。

Sentence 4第 4 句 (begins开头 “This is the …”)

  • the sort of thing that: a relative clause describing the kind of job.the sort of thing that …：……正是那种事；that 引导定语从句。

Sentence 6第 6 句 (begins开头 “He had included …”)

  • had included: past perfect, done before the speech was given.had included：过去完成时，致辞之前就放进去了。

Sentence 9第 9 句 (begins开头 “On the way …”)

  • ask someone if: a yes-no question in reported speech, with normal word order and the tense moved back.ask sb if …：间接引语里的一般疑问句，用 if/whether，语序不倒装；时态后退为过去完成时。

Sentence 11第 11 句 (begins开头 “Jeremy asked her …”)

  • see someone doing: watch them in the middle of an action.see sb doing：看到某人正在做。
  • laugh at has two senses here: the adults were enjoying the jokes, but the child heard it as mocking.laugh at：在这里有两层意思，大人觉得是「被逗笑」，孩子理解成「嘲笑」。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

Reported questions: ask if and ask why间接引语里的疑问句：ask if / ask why
To report a yes-no question use ask if or whether; to report a wh-question keep the question word. Both use statement order with no do or did, and after a past reporting verb the tense moves back one step.
一般疑问句转述用 ask (sb) if/whether，特殊疑问句保留疑问词（why、what、where）；两种都用陈述语序，不用 do/did；主句是过去时，从句时态后退一步。

Sentence 9 reports a yes-no question with if, and sentence 11 reports a why question.

本课：第 9 句用 if 转述「你喜欢吗」，第 11 句用 why 转述「为什么」。

  • She asked me if I was tired.
  • He asked where I had put the keys.
that is and except: correcting and exceptingthat is 与 except：修正和例外
that is, short for that is to say, explains or corrects what came before. except means leaving something out, and often follows words like everybody, everything or all.
that is（= that is to say）用来解释或修正前面的话；except 表示「除……之外」，被除去的不包括在内。everybody / everything / all 后面常跟 except。

Sentence 2 first says everyone admires him, then uses that is and except to take one person back out.

本课：第 2 句先说人人都佩服他，再用 that is 和 except 收回一个人。

  • The shop is open daily, that is, from Monday to Sunday.
  • Everyone came except Tom.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • X has a large circle of … and is very … X 有一大群……，很……
    My aunt has a large circle of neighbours and is very well known.
  • Everybody admires X for …, everybody, that is, except … 人人都因……佩服 X，只有……例外
    Everybody admires her for her cooking, everybody, that is, except her son.
  • This is the kind of thing that X loves. 这正是 X 喜欢的那种事
    This is the kind of thing that my grandfather loves.
  • To his surprise, she said … 让他吃惊的是，她说……
    To my surprise, the shop said the price had gone up.
  • X asked Y why …, and Y told X that … X 问 Y 为什么……，Y 告诉 X……
    I asked the waiter why it was late, and he told me the oven had broken.

Our model summary本站范文

Jeremy Hampden is well known for his humour, and everyone enjoys his company except Jenny, his six-year-old daughter. When a close friend asked him to speak at a wedding, he wrote a speech full of jokes, and the guests loved it. But as soon as it was over, Jenny wanted to go home. On the way he asked whether she had liked it. She said no: she hated seeing so many people laugh at her dad.（76 词）
