Lesson 81 · Escape 逃跑

📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 81 “Escape”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 81《Escape》

B1🧠 18 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
Having killed a sentry, a prisoner of war hides the body in the bushes and puts on the man's uniform in the dark. Now, rifle on shoulder, he paces boldly before the gate while the camp behind him fills with lights, shouts and running men: his absence has just been found out. A big black car arrives with four officers; he stiffens and salutes as they go in. Their driver, an old man with white hair and kind blue eyes, strolls over for a chat. The prisoner pities him, yet sees no other way out. One hard punch, and he is in the car and away. This is a tense escape scene. The grammar is participle phrases, the past perfect, there was nothing else one could do and as quickly as one could.
一名战俘杀死看守后，迅速把尸体拖进灌木丛，摸黑换上死者的衣服。现在他穿着蓝制服、肩扛步枪，大摇大摆地在营地门前来回踱步。营里人声鼎沸、灯火通明，人们正四处奔跑——他们刚发现有个战俘逃了。就在这时，一辆坐着四名军官的黑色大轿车停在营门口。军官下车时，他立正敬礼。等他们走了，司机朝他走过来，显然想聊天。那是个上了年纪的人，头发花白、眼睛清亮。战俘心里过意不去，可他别无选择：等司机走近，一拳把他打倒，跳上车飞快地开走了。本课是一段紧张的逃亡场景，学 分词作状语、过去完成时、there was nothing else one could do 和 as quickly as one could。

  2. What did the prisoner feel about the driver, and how do we know?战俘对司机有什么感觉？从哪里看得出来？

Unsigned and written for the course; no war, country or camp is named, and we have not found it in any published memoir.

本课未署名、为教材自撰；没有点明是哪场战争、哪个国家、哪座营地，我们也没有在已出版的回忆录里找到它。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

Escape stories were a popular kind of writing in Britain after the Second World War, when many former prisoners published memoirs of breaking out of camps. In that tradition escape was seen as a duty for captured officers, partly because every escape forced the enemy to spend soldiers and time on searching. International law draws a line here: the Geneva Conventions say an escaped prisoner who is recaptured may only be given light punishment for the escape itself, but acts of violence during it can be tried as crimes. The colour of the uniform and the kind of car are the only clues to the setting, and the writer seems to keep it vague on purpose.

二战之后，逃亡故事在英国是一类很受欢迎的读物，许多前战俘出版了自己越狱的回忆录。在那种传统里，被俘军官把逃跑视为一种职责，部分原因是每一次逃跑都迫使敌方花费兵力和时间去搜捕。国际法在这里划了一条线：《日内瓦公约》规定，被重新抓获的逃跑战俘只能因逃跑本身受轻微的惩处，但逃跑过程中的暴力行为可以作为罪行审理。制服的颜色和轿车的样子是关于背景仅有的线索，作者似乎有意让它模糊。

✨ How it is written赏析

The scene is built for speed. The first sentence begins after the killing, so the violence is already over before we arrive, and participle phrases pack several actions into each sentence without slowing the pace. The middle slows down for one dangerous moment, the salute, which shows how well the disguise works. Then the writer does something surprising: he gives the driver a face, his white head and kind eyes, and gives the prisoner a feeling, pity. That one human detail turns an adventure into a moral moment. The blow and the escape are told in two quick sentences, so we have no time to think, just as the prisoner had none.

这个场景是为速度而造的。第一句从杀人之后写起，所以我们到场时暴力已经结束；分词短语把几个动作塞进一句话里，节奏丝毫不慢。中段为一个危险时刻慢下来——敬礼——显出伪装有多成功。接着作者做了件出人意料的事：他给司机一张脸——花白的头发、清亮的眼睛——又给战俘一种感情：同情。这一个人性化的细节，把一段冒险变成了一次道德时刻。那一拳和逃跑用两句短句写完，我们来不及思考，正像那个战俘也来不及思考一样。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The lesson asks the reader to share the prisoner's side without saying whether he is right. War stories often divide people into friends and enemies, but the old driver is simply a man who wants to talk, and the reader feels the cost of the escape through him. That is worth discussing: courage in war often means harming people who have done you no wrong. Chinese readers know many wartime escape stories from films and novels about the War of Resistance, usually told from one side only. Reading this lesson, try asking how the same night would look from the driver's family's point of view, or from the guard's.

这一课让读者站在战俘一边，却不说他对不对。战争故事常把人分成自己人和敌人，可那个老司机只是一个想聊天的人，读者正是通过他感受到这次逃跑的代价。这一点值得讨论：战争中的勇敢，常常意味着伤害那些并没有对你做过什么坏事的人。中国读者从抗战题材的电影和小说里知道许多战时逃亡的故事，通常只从一方讲述。读这一课时，不妨问一问：同一个夜晚，从司机家人的角度、或者从那个看守的角度看，会是什么样子？

💬 Discuss and write讨论与写作

    战俘的行为是勇敢、残忍，还是两者都有？用三句话说出你的理由。
  2. Retell the scene from the driver's point of view, using two participle phrases.
    从司机的角度复述这个场景，用上两个分词短语。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 1第 1 句 (begins开头 “When he had …”)

  • had killed: the past perfect, for what came before dragged.had killed：过去完成时，在 dragged 之前。

Sentence 2第 2 句 (begins开头 “Working rapidly in …”)

  • The -ing phrase describes what he was doing at the same time; its subject is he.现在分词短语作伴随状语，主语就是 he。
  • change into: put on different clothes.change into：换上（衣服）。

Sentence 3第 3 句 (begins开头 “Now, dressed in …”)

  • dressed in: a past participle phrase; with plus a noun plus a phrase describes how he looked.dressed in：过去分词短语作状语；with + 名词 + 介词短语：独立结构，描写样子。
  • march up and down: walk to and fro.march up and down：来回走动。

Sentence 6第 6 句 (begins开头 “Lights were blazing …”)

  • Two past perfects: the discovery came first, and the escape came even earlier.两个过去完成时：发现在先、逃跑更在先。

  • feel sorry for: pity; the second half means he had no other choice.feel sorry for：同情。nothing else 一句：他别无选择。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

Participle phrases used as adverbials分词短语作状语
An -ing phrase shows an active action happening at the same time or just before; a past participle phrase shows a passive action or a state. Its subject must be the subject of the main clause.
现在分词短语（doing）表主动、同时或紧接着的动作；过去分词短语（done）表被动或状态。分词的逻辑主语必须是主句主语。

The phrases in sentences 2, 3 and 14 all have the prisoner as their subject.

本课：第 2 句的 working、第 3 句的 dressed、第 14 句的 jumping，主语都是那个战俘。

  • Walking home, I met an old friend.
  • Tired after the long day, she went to bed early.
The past perfect: had done过去完成时：had done
When telling a past story, use had done for what happened before another past action. After when or after, where the order is already clear, the past simple is also possible.
讲过去的故事时，比另一个过去动作更早发生的事用 had done。when/after 引导时，时间先后已清楚，也可以用一般过去时。

In sentence 1 the killing comes before the dragging, in sentence 6 the escape comes before its discovery, and in sentence 9 the officers have gone before the driver moves.

本课：第 1 句杀死看守在拖走之前；第 6 句逃跑在被发现之前；第 9 句军官走了之后，司机才过来。

  • When I arrived, the film had already started.
  • She told me she had lost her key.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • When X had …, Y quickly … X ……之后，Y 迅速地……
    When the boy had broken the window, he quickly ran round the corner.
  • …ing quickly in the dark, X soon … X 摸黑迅速地……，很快就……
    Searching quickly in the dark, she soon found the light switch.
  • Dressed in … and with … over his shoulder, X … 穿着……、肩上扛着……，X ……
    Dressed in a raincoat and with a bag over his shoulder, Dad left for the station.
  • X felt sorry for Y, but there was no other choice. X 同情 Y，可是别无选择
    The teacher felt sorry for the boy, but there was no other choice.
  • Then, …ing into …, X drove off as fast as possible. 然后 X 跳进……，尽快开走了
    Then, climbing into the van, the farmer drove off as fast as possible.

Points a good summary covers好摘要要覆盖的要点

Our model summary本站范文

A prisoner of war killed a guard, pulled the body into the bushes and put on the man's uniform. Carrying a rifle, he walked up and down outside the camp, where men were shouting because his escape had been found out. A car with four officers stopped at the gate, and he saluted them. When they had gone, their elderly driver came over to talk. Though the prisoner pitied him, he knocked him down and drove off in the car.（80 词）
