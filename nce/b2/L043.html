Lesson 43 · Over the South Pole 飞越南极

📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 43 “Over the South Pole”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 43《Over the South Pole》

B1🧠 16 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
In 1929, three years on from his North Pole flight, the American explorer Richard Byrd became the first to fly over the South Pole. At first his crew were busy photographing the peaks below, but before long they were in real danger. For a while a crash looked unavoidable, because the plane had to climb to ten thousand feet to clear the range. Byrd ordered two heavy sacks of food to be thrown out. Lighter now, the plane climbed and passed the peaks with four hundred feet to spare. With no more mountains ahead, Byrd knew the Pole, three hundred miles off, was within reach, and the aircraft crossed the endless white plain with ease. The grammar is though and for clauses, it seemed certain that, only ... if, and be able to.
1929 年，在飞越北极三年之后，美国探险家伯德第一次成功地飞越了南极。起初，伯德和同伴们还能拍下很多下方山脉的照片，可很快就遇到了大麻烦。有一阵子，飞机看来非坠毁不可：只有升到一万英尺，它才能越过那些山。伯德立即下令把两个沉重的食品袋扔出去。飞机这才升了上去，以四百英尺的余量飞过了山。伯德知道，他能飞到三百英里外的南极了，因为眼前再也看不到山。飞机毫不费力地飞过了无边无际的白色平原。本课学 though / for 引导的从句、it seemed certain that、only … if 和 be able to。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. How did the plane manage to clear the mountains?飞机是怎样越过那些山的？
  2. Why was Byrd sure he could reach the Pole?伯德为什么确信自己能飞到南极？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander for New Concept English (Longman, 1967), based on Richard E. Byrd's real flight of November 1929.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为《新概念英语》（朗文，1967）自撰，依据理查德·E. 伯德 1929 年 11 月的真实飞行写成。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

Byrd's South Pole flight took place on the twenty-eighth and twenty-ninth of November 1929, from his base Little America on the Ross Ice Shelf, in a Ford Trimotor named after his friend Floyd Bennett; the Norwegian-American Bernt Balchen was the pilot. To climb the high glacier pass to the polar plateau the crew did dump food bags, and most accounts give the weight as about two hundred and fifty pounds of supplies. Byrd's earlier North Pole flight of 1926 is much less certain: doubts were raised at the time, and later study of his diary and the plane's speed has led many historians to think he may have turned back before reaching the Pole. The South Pole flight, by contrast, is generally accepted.

伯德飞越南极是在 1929 年 11 月 28 日至 29 日，从罗斯冰架上的基地「小美国」起飞，乘坐的福特三引擎飞机以他的朋友弗洛伊德·贝内特命名，驾驶员是挪威裔美国人伯恩特·巴尔肯。为了爬升越过通往极地高原的冰川山口，机组确实扔掉了食品袋，多数记载说重约二百五十磅。伯德 1926 年那次北极飞行就不那么确定了：当时就有人质疑，后来对他的日记和飞机速度的研究，让许多历史学家认为他可能在到达北极之前就折返了。相比之下，南极飞行普遍被认可。

✨ How it is written赏析

The lesson is built like a short adventure film: a confident opening, a moment of danger, a quick decision, and relief. The numbers carry the tension: ten thousand feet needed, a margin of only four hundred, three hundred miles still to go. Sentences become short at the crisis, it seemed certain, it could only, Byrd at once ordered, and then grow long and easy again once the danger is past. The final image of endless white plains is calm and wide, a picture of open space after the tight squeeze through the mountains.

这一课像一部动作短片那样搭起来：自信的开场、危险的一刻、果断的决定、松一口气。数字承载紧张感：需要一万英尺、余量只有四百英尺、还剩三百英里。危急时刻句子变短——看来必然、只有、立即下令——危险一过，句子又变得长而舒缓。结尾那片无边无际的白色平原平静而开阔，是挤过群山之后豁然开朗的画面。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The textbook tells the story as a clean triumph, and the South Pole flight deserves that. But the opening phrase about the North Pole flight is now one of the famous open questions of exploration, and it is a useful reminder that a confident sentence in a textbook is not proof. Heroic stories also tend to leave out the team: Byrd planned and led, but Balchen flew the plane through the pass. When we retell achievements, in any country, it is worth asking who did the work and what the record really shows.

课本把这个故事讲成一场干净利落的胜利，南极飞行配得上这样讲。但开头那句提到的北极飞行，如今是探险史上著名的悬案之一，这提醒我们：课本里一句自信的话并不是证据。英雄故事也往往漏掉团队：伯德负责策划和指挥，但驾机穿过山口的是巴尔肯。在任何国家讲述成就时，都值得问：活是谁干的，记录真正显示了什么？

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 1第 1 句 (begins开头 “In 1929, three …”)

  • three years after: a time phrase measured from the earlier flight.three years after …：在……三年之后，作时间状语。
  • for the first time: never done before.for the first time：第一次。

Sentence 6第 6 句 (begins开头 “they soon ran …”)

  • though means although, and the main clause does not also take but.though：虽然，引导让步状语从句，主句不再用 but。
  • lay is the past of lie, to be placed.lay：lie 的过去式，「位于」。
  • run into trouble: to meet difficulty without expecting it.run into trouble：遇到麻烦。

Sentence 7第 7 句 (begins开头 “At one point, …”)

  • would crash: the future seen from the past.would crash：过去将来时。

Sentence 8第 8 句 (begins开头 “It could only …”)

  • only ... if: on that one condition and no other.only … if：只有……才……。

Sentence 10第 10 句 (begins开头 “The plane was …”)

  • be able to: managed to, in a real past event.be able to：能够（指实际做到了）。
  • by gives the margin.clear … by 400 feet：以 400 英尺之差越过，by 表示差距。

Sentence 12第 12 句 (begins开头 “for there were …”)

  • for gives the reason, in a formal style.for：因为（补充说明理由，较正式）。
  • in sight: able to be seen.in sight：看得见的。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

though and for: contrast and reasonthough / for：让步与原因
though, the same as although, begins a clause of contrast, and the main clause does not also take but. for is a joining word that adds a reason after the main clause; it cannot start a sentence and is more formal than because.
though（＝although）引导让步从句：虽然……，主句不再加 but。for 是并列连词，引导补充的理由，只能放在主句后面，不能放句首，比 because 正式。

Sentences 4 to 6 use though for the change from a good start to trouble, and sentence 12 uses for to add the reason that no peaks were left ahead.

本课：第 4–6 句用 though 说虽然起先顺利、后来却出事；第 12 句用 for 补充理由——前面再没有山了。

  • Though it was late, he kept working.
  • She stayed at home, for she was feeling ill.
could, be able to, and only ... if
To say someone managed something on one occasion in the past, use was or were able to, or managed to, not could. could is mainly for general ability in the past. only ... if means on this condition and no other.
说过去某一次实际做成了，用 was/were able to（或 managed to），不用 could；could 多说过去的一般能力。only … if＝只有在……条件下才……。

In sentence 10 was able to rise means it actually happened that time, and sentence 8 uses only with if for the single condition for getting over.

本课：第 10 句 was able to rise 是那一次真的升上去了；第 8 句用 only 加 if 说出越过山的唯一条件。

  • After hours of work, we were able to fix the car.
  • You can only enter if you have a ticket.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • In …, three years after …, X … for the first time. ……年，在……三年之后，X 第一次……
    In 2019, three years after her first race, she won for the first time.
  • Though at first X …, they soon ran into … 虽然起初 X……，他们很快就遇到了……
    Though the trip began well, we soon hit heavy traffic.
  • For a while it looked certain that … 有一阵子看来……是肯定的
    For a while it looked certain that the team would lose.
  • X could only … if it … 只有……，X 才能……
    The boat could only reach the island if the wind dropped.
  • X knew he would be able to …, for … X 知道自己能……，因为……
    She knew she would pass, for she had studied hard.

Our model summary本站范文

In 1929 the American explorer Richard Byrd became the first to fly over the South Pole. At first his men photographed the mountains below, but soon they were in danger. The plane had to reach ten thousand feet to get over the peaks, and a crash seemed certain. Byrd had two heavy sacks of food thrown out. The plane rose and cleared the mountains by four hundred feet, then flew easily over the white plains to the Pole.（78 词）
