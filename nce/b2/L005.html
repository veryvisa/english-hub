Lesson 5 · No Wrong Numbers 无错号之虞

📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 5 “No Wrong Numbers”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 5《No Wrong Numbers》

B1🧠 15 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
Mr James Scott owns a garage in Silbury and has now bought a second one five miles off in Pinhurst, but he cannot get a phone line put in there. His answer is a dozen pigeons: the first one does the trip in three minutes. Since then, orders for parts and urgent notes all go by bird, so he has in effect set up his own private line, one where nobody can ever dial the wrong number. The grammar is the present perfect against the past simple and the linking words so and but.
詹姆斯·斯科特先生在锡尔伯里有一家汽车修理厂，又在五英里外的平赫斯特买了一家，可新厂装不上电话。他的办法是买了十二只鸽子：第一只三分钟就飞完全程。从那以后，订零件、传急事全靠鸽子，等于自己开了一条私人「电话线」——而且永远不会拨错号。本课学现在完成时与一般过去时的分工（has just bought / yesterday … carried）和 so、but 连接的因果与转折。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. Why did Mr Scott buy pigeons?斯科特先生为什么要买鸽子？
  2. What is the point of the title?标题「没有错号」是什么意思？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander for New Concept English (Longman, 1967); Silbury and Pinhurst are made-up towns.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为《新概念英语》（朗文，1967）自撰；锡尔伯里和平赫斯特是虚构的镇名。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

Pigeon post is far older than the telephone. Homing pigeons will fly back to their own loft from a long way off, so people carried them away in baskets and let them go with a note tied to a leg. They were used in war, by news agencies and by stock dealers well into the age of the telegraph. In Britain in the 1960s, getting a phone line put in could mean a long wait, especially for a new business in a small place, so the problem in the lesson was a real one for its readers. Note one thing the story leaves out: a pigeon only flies home. To send messages both ways, Mr Scott must keep birds from each garage at the other and carry them back by road.

鸽子传信比电话古老得多。信鸽能从很远的地方飞回自己的鸽舍，所以人们把它们装在篮子里带走，放飞时在腿上绑一张纸条。直到电报时代，军队、通讯社和做股票的人还在用。二十世纪六十年代的英国，装一条电话线可能要等很久，小地方的新店尤其如此，所以课文里的难题对当时的读者是真实的。注意故事略去了一点：鸽子只会往家飞。要两头互通消息，斯科特先生得把每个厂的鸽子养在另一个厂，再用车把它们运回去。

✨ How it is written赏析

The lesson is a small puzzle with a neat answer. It sets up the problem in four plain facts, two garages, a short distance, no phone, and then gives the solution in one surprising word, pigeons. The tenses mark the stages clearly: the present perfect for the new situation, a single past simple for the first flight with its date, and the present perfect again for everything since. The title is the last joke. A wrong number was a common nuisance of the time, and the writer hints that this old-fashioned system beats the modern one on at least one point. The quotation marks round telephone in the final sentence show the writer smiling at his own comparison.

这课是一道小谜题，答案很巧。它用四个平平的事实摆出难题——两个修理厂、距离很近、没有电话——然后用一个出人意料的词给出答案：鸽子。时态把各阶段标得很清楚：现在完成时讲新局面，唯一一个带日期的一般过去时讲第一次飞行，此后的一切又回到现在完成时。标题是最后一个笑点：拨错号是那个年代常见的烦恼，作者暗示这套老办法至少在一点上胜过新科技。最后一句里给「电话」加的引号，表明作者在对自己的比喻微笑。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

Five miles in three minutes works out at about a hundred miles an hour. That is very fast even for a trained bird over a short flight, and the lesson does not claim it is typical, so treat the figure as part of the fun rather than as a fact about pigeons. The deeper idea still holds: when the official service is slow, people find their own way round it. Chinese readers may remember when a home telephone was costly and hard to get, and how neighbours shared one phone or left messages at a corner shop. The urge to build a private workaround is the same, and so is the pride in it.

三分钟五英里，折合时速约一百英里。即使对受过训练、只飞短途的信鸽来说也非常快，课文并没说这是常态，所以这个数字当作故事的趣味看，不要当作关于鸽子的事实。更深的意思至今成立：官方服务太慢，人们就自己想办法绕过去。中国读者也许还记得家里装电话又贵又难的年代，邻居共用一部电话、在街口小卖部留口信。想出私人变通办法的冲动是一样的，从中得到的自豪也一样。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 5第 5 句 (begins开头 “so he has …”)

  • but shows contrast and so shows result: no phone, so pigeons.but 表转折，so 表结果：装不上电话，所以买鸽子。
  • has just bought: done a moment ago, with the result right here.has just bought：刚买，结果在眼前。

Sentence 7第 7 句 (begins开头 “The bird covered …”)

  • cover the distance: travel the whole of it.cover the distance：飞完这段路程。
  • in with a length of time tells how long it took.in three minutes：用了三分钟（in + 时间 = 用多长时间）。

Sentence 9第 9 句 (begins开头 “Mr.Scott has sent …”)

  • up to now: until this moment, usually with the present perfect.up to now：到目前为止，常配现在完成时。

Sentence 11第 11 句 (begins开头 “In this way, …”)

  • The quotation marks show that this is not a real telephone; the word is used as a joke.telephone 加引号：并不是真的电话，是打趣的说法。
  • his own: belonging to him and nobody else.his own：他自己的。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

The present perfect and the past simple现在完成时与一般过去时
When a finished past time is named, such as yesterday or last week, use the past simple. When the focus is on the result up to now and no time is given, use the present perfect.
说了具体的过去时间（yesterday、last week、in 1990）用一般过去时；只关心「到现在为止」的结果、没说具体时间，用现在完成时。

Sentences 2 and 5 use the present perfect with just; sentence 6 has Yesterday, so the past simple; sentence 9 has Up to now, so the present perfect again.

本课：第 2、5 句用 has just bought；第 6 句有 Yesterday，所以用 carried；第 9 句有 Up to now，所以用 has sent。

  • I have lost my key. (I can't open the door now.)
  • I lost my key last night.
so and but: result and contrastso 与 but：结果与转折
but joins two ideas that pull against each other; so joins a cause to its result. A comma usually comes before so.
but 连接相反的意思（虽然近，但是装不上电话）；so 连接结果（所以买了鸽子）。so 前面通常有逗号。

Sentences 3 and 4 are joined by but for contrast, and 4 and 5 by so for result.

本课：第 3、4 句用 but 表转折，第 4、5 句之间用 so 表结果。

  • It was raining, so we stayed in.
  • The hotel was cheap, but it was very clean.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • X has just bought another … in … X 刚在……又买了一个……
    Mrs Chen has just bought another shop in Richmond.
  • X cannot get …, so X has … X 弄不到……，所以就……
    We cannot get a taxi, so we have decided to walk.
  • cover the distance in + 时间 用……时间走完这段路
    The fast train covers the distance in two hours.
  • In this way, X has begun his own … 用这种办法，X 办起了自己的……
    In this way, she has begun her own small baking business.

Our model summary本站范文

Mr Scott owns garages in two towns five miles apart, but he cannot get a phone for the new one. So he has bought twelve pigeons. The first took only three minutes to fly between them, and now his birds carry all his urgent orders.（45 词）
