Lesson 41 · Do You Call That a Hat 你把那叫帽子吗？

📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 41 “Do You Call That a Hat”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 41《Do You Call That a Hat》

B1🧠 15 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
'You call that a hat?' the writer asked his wife. 'There's no need to be so rude,' she replied, studying herself in the mirror. He took a seat on a trendy chair full of holes and waited. Thirty minutes had gone by and she had still not left the glass. 'We shouldn't buy what we don't need,' he said all of a sudden, and was sorry the moment it was out. 'You didn't have to say that,' she said. 'And there's no need for me to mention the awful tie you bought yesterday.' He said he liked the tie and that a man could not own too many. She said the same was true of women and hats. Ten minutes on they left together, and she had on a hat shaped like a lighthouse. The grammar is needn't, mustn't and needn't have done, regret doing, and can never have too many.
「你把那叫帽子？」作者对妻子说。「你用不着这么没礼貌，」妻子一边照镜子一边回答。作者在一把带窟窿的现代椅子上坐下来等着。他们在帽子店里已经待了半个小时，妻子还站在镜子前。「我们可不能买不需要的东西。」作者突然冒出一句，话一出口就后悔了。「你本来不必说那句话，」妻子答道，「我也用不着提醒你昨天买的那条难看的领带。」「我觉得它挺漂亮，」他说，「男人的领带永远不嫌多。」「女人的帽子也永远不嫌多。」十分钟后两人一起走出商店，妻子头上戴着一顶像灯塔一样的帽子！本课学 needn't / mustn't / needn't have done、regret doing 和 can never have too many。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

In the Britain of the nineteen sixties hats were still part of a well-dressed woman's outfit for weddings, church and smart occasions, and hat shops with mirrors and waiting chairs for husbands were common in town centres. Modern furniture made of moulded plastic or metal with holes or cut-outs was the new fashion of the decade, which is why the writer notices his uncomfortable seat. The joke of a husband waiting while his wife shops is an old one in British comedy, and the reply about ties is just as old: each partner's habit looks silly to the other.

六十年代的英国，帽子仍是讲究的女士参加婚礼、上教堂和出席正式场合时的标准装束，城中心常有配着镜子、给丈夫们设了等候椅的帽子店。模压塑料或带孔洞、镂空的金属现代家具是那个十年的新潮流，所以作者才会注意到自己那张不舒服的座椅。丈夫等妻子购物，是英国喜剧里的老笑话；关于领带的那句反击同样古老：伴侣的嗜好在对方眼里总显得可笑。

✨ How it is written赏析

Almost the whole lesson is dialogue, and the needn't, mustn't, needn't have forms are the weapons in a small domestic fight. The husband starts with a joke and ends up losing every exchange: one careless line, an instant regret, and then his own tie is brought in as evidence against him. The wife's lines echo his: he claims there is no such thing as too many ties, and she simply repeats the sentence with women and hats. The final picture of the lighthouse hat is the one image in the lesson, and it tells us who won without saying so.

这一课几乎全是对话，needn't、mustn't、needn't have 这几种说法成了一场小小家庭战争里的武器。丈夫以一句玩笑开场，结果每个回合都输：一句不小心的话，马上后悔，然后他自己的领带被拿来当证据。妻子的话是他的回声：他说男人的领带永远不嫌多，她只是把这句话换成女人和帽子重说一遍。结尾那顶灯塔帽子是全课唯一的画面，不说一个字就告诉了我们谁赢了。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The story is about fairness more than hats. Each partner has a weakness for buying the same kind of thing, and each sees the other's habit clearly while excusing his or her own. Couples in any culture will recognise the move of answering one complaint with another. A modern reader might also notice that the husband's rule, do not buy what you do not need, is actually sound; it simply cannot be applied to one person only. The lesson's laugh is at the double standard, not at the hat.

这个故事讲的更多是公平，而不是帽子。夫妻俩各有一个买同类东西的癖好，每个人都把对方的习惯看得清清楚楚，却原谅自己的。任何文化里的夫妻都会认出这招：用一个抱怨回击另一个抱怨。现代读者也许还会注意到，丈夫那条规则——不买不需要的东西——其实是对的，只是不能只用在一个人身上。这一课笑的是双重标准，不是帽子。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

needn't, mustn't and needn't have done
needn't do means it is not necessary, so the choice is yours. mustn't do means it is not allowed or is a bad idea. needn't have done means you did it, though there was no need, often said with mild blame or comfort.
needn't do＝没必要做（做不做随你）；mustn't do＝不许做、千万别做；needn't have done＝本来不必做，可你已经做了（略带责怪或宽慰）。

Sentence 2 uses needn't, sentence 6 mustn't, sentence 8 needn't have with a past participle, and sentence 9 needn't again: all three forms in one small quarrel.

本课：第 2 句用 needn't，第 6 句用 mustn't，第 8 句用 needn't have 加过去分词，第 9 句又是 needn't——三种说法在一段口角里全出现了。

  • You needn't bring food. There will be plenty.
  • You mustn't tell anyone. It's a secret.
  • You needn't have bought flowers, but thank you!
regret doing and regret to do
regret doing means to be sorry about something you did. regret to do, mostly with tell or inform, is a formal way to announce bad news.
regret doing＝后悔做过某事（回看过去）；regret to do＝很遗憾要（告诉、通知）……（多用于正式地宣布坏消息）。

In sentence 7 regret is followed by an -ing form: the words were already out, and he was sorry.

本课：第 7 句 regret 后面接的是动名词——话已说出口，后悔了。

  • I regret telling him my secret.
  • We regret to inform you that the flight is cancelled.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • 'Do you call that a …?' I said to … 「你管那叫……？」我对……说
    'Do you call that a cake?' I asked my brother.
  • X needn't be so … about it. X 用不着这么……
    You needn't be so angry about it.
  • We had been in … for … and X was still … 我们在……待了……，X 还在……
    We had been at the station for an hour and the train was still late.
  • X was sorry the moment the words were out. X 话一出口就后悔了
    Tom was sorry the moment the joke was out.
  • You can never have too many … ……永远不嫌多
    You can never have too many good friends.

Our model summary本站范文

In a hat shop, the writer laughed at his wife's new hat, then waited for half an hour. Suddenly he said they should not buy things they did not need, and he was sorry at once. His wife reminded him of the ugly tie he had bought the day before. When he said a man could never own too many ties, she said the same about hats. Soon she left the shop in a hat like a lighthouse.（78 词）
