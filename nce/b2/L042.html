Lesson 42 · Not Very Musical 不是很懂音乐

📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 42 “Not Very Musical”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 42《Not Very Musical》

B1🧠 18 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
After a long walk through a market in old Delhi, the writer's group sat down in a square for a break. Soon they spotted a snake charmer with two big baskets across the square and went over to watch. The moment he noticed them, he took up a long pipe decorated with coins and lifted the lid of a basket. As the music started, they caught their first sight of the snake, which rose up and swayed after the pipe. Then, to their surprise, he switched to jazz and pop. The snake went on swaying at the same slow pace, quite unable to hear any change. The grammar is the past perfect had had, as soon as, have a look and its relatives, and tell the difference between.
作者一行人在德里老城的一个市场里走了很久，于是在一个广场上停下来歇脚。过了一会儿，他们注意到广场另一头有个耍蛇人，身边放着两个大篮子，就走过去看。耍蛇人一看见他们，就拿起一支缀满硬币的长笛子，打开了一个篮子。他一吹起曲子，大家第一次瞥见了那条蛇：它从篮子里立起来，随着笛子的移动摆动。让大家大吃一惊的是，耍蛇人突然吹起了爵士乐和现代流行歌曲，可那条蛇照样慢悠悠地「跳舞」——它显然分不清印度音乐和爵士乐！本课学过去完成时 had had、as soon as、have a look / have a rest / have a glimpse 和 tell the difference between。

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander for New Concept English (Longman, 1967); the scene is set in a real place, the old city of Delhi, but the charmer and the visitors are invented.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为《新概念英语》（朗文，1967）自撰；场景设在真实的德里老城，但耍蛇人和游客都是虚构的。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

The joke in the lesson turns out to be scientific fact. Snakes have no outer ears and are almost deaf to sounds carried through the air; they sense vibrations through the ground and their jaw bones. What the snake follows is the movement of the pipe, the pungi or been, which it treats as a possible threat, so the swaying is a defensive response, not a dance. Snake charming was a common street show in India for centuries, but wildlife protection laws passed in 1972 made catching and keeping snakes for display illegal, and the practice has declined sharply, though it has not disappeared.

这课的笑话其实是科学事实。蛇没有外耳，对空气传来的声音几乎是聋的；它们通过地面和颌骨感受震动。蛇追随的是笛子——印度叫 pungi 或 been——的移动，把它当作可能的威胁，所以那种摇摆是防御反应，不是舞蹈。耍蛇曾是印度几百年来常见的街头表演，但 1972 年通过的野生动物保护法把捕蛇、养蛇来表演定为违法，这一行急剧衰落，不过并未绝迹。

✨ How it is written赏析

The lesson is set up like a travel postcard: a long walk, a square, a rest, then something to see. The writer moves the camera closer step by step, from the far side of the square to the baskets, the pipe with its coins, and finally the snake itself rising up. The surprise comes in two stages: first the charmer changes to modern music, which is unexpected, and then the snake does not change at all, which is funnier still. The last line plays innocent, treating the snake as a poor music critic, and lets readers who know a little biology enjoy a second joke.

这一课像一张旅游明信片那样开场：走了很久、一个广场、歇一歇，然后有东西可看。作者一步一步把镜头推近：从广场另一边到篮子，到缀满硬币的笛子，最后是蛇本身立起来。惊喜分两步：先是耍蛇人改吹现代音乐，出人意料；接着蛇完全没变，更加好笑。最后一句装作天真，把蛇当成一个不懂行的乐评人，让懂一点生物学的读者享受第二个笑话。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The lesson is light, but it raises a fair question about how we look at other cultures. Visitors often admire a show without asking how it works or what it costs the animal, and the snakes used in street shows were often kept in poor conditions, sometimes with fangs removed. Today, many Indian charmers' families have moved into other work, and some now perform the music without live snakes. Readers in China may think of street acts with monkeys or bears that have been limited for similar reasons. Enjoy the joke, then ask the second question.

这一课很轻松，却提出一个关于如何看待其他文化的合理问题。游客常常欣赏一场表演，却不问它是怎么做到的、动物付出了什么代价；街头表演用的蛇往往饲养条件很差，有时还被拔了毒牙。如今许多印度耍蛇人家庭转了行，有些人现在只演奏音乐、不带活蛇。中国读者也许会想到耍猴、耍熊之类的街头表演，出于类似的理由已受到限制。先享受这个笑话，再问第二个问题。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

have plus a noun: have a rest, a look, a glimpsehave + 名词：have a rest / a look / a glimpse
English often uses have, or take, plus a plus an action noun to mean do something once: have a rest, have a look, have a walk, have a glimpse, have a swim. It sounds more natural in speech than the plain verb.
英语常用 have（或 take）+ a + 动作名词来表示「做一次……」：have a rest、have a look、have a walk、have a glimpse、have a swim。这种说法比单用动词更口语、更自然。

The lesson uses four of them: a walk in sentence 1, a rest in 2, a look in 5 and a glimpse in 9.

本课：第 1 句是 had had a long walk，第 2 句 have a rest，第 5 句 have a look，第 9 句 had our first glimpse——全篇一连四次。

  • Let's have a swim before lunch.
  • Can I have a look at your photos?
as soon as, and as meaning becauseas soon as / as（因为）
as soon as means at the moment that and starts a time clause. as on its own can mean because, as in sentence 1, or while, so read the context.
as soon as＝一……就……，引导时间从句。as 单独用可以表示「因为」（第 1 句），也可以表示「当……时」「一边……一边……」，要看上下文。

In sentence 1 as gives the reason, and in sentence 6 as soon as shows seeing the visitors and picking up the pipe happening one straight after the other.

本课：第 1 句的 as 表原因；第 6 句用 as soon as，看见游客与拿起笛子紧接着发生。

  • As soon as the bell rang, the children ran out.
  • As it was raining, we stayed at home.

Our model summary本站范文

After a long walk through a market in old Delhi, we sat down in a square and saw a snake charmer with two baskets. When he saw us, he took his pipe, opened a basket and began to play. The snake rose and followed the pipe. Then he switched to jazz and pop songs, but the snake went on swaying slowly. It could not hear the difference at all.（69 词）
