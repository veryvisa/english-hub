Lesson 28 · No Parking 禁止停车

📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 28 “No Parking”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 28《No Parking》

B1🧠 15 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
Jasper White is one of the few people who still trust the old myths. He has recently got himself a city home, and since moving in he has been plagued by drivers: every night someone has left a car across his gate, so he has never once managed to put his own car in the garage. Signs telling people not to park there have done nothing. So now an ugly stone head looks down from above the gate, one of the ugliest faces the writer has ever seen. It is Medusa, the Gorgon, and Jasper hopes she will turn the drivers and their cars to stone. So far, none of them has changed. The grammar is the present perfect and one of the with a superlative and a plural noun.
贾斯珀·怀特是那种少见的相信古代神话的人。他刚在城里买了新房子，可从搬进去那天起，就一直被汽车和车主折腾：每晚回家，总有人把车停在他的大门外，害得他一次也没能把自己的车开进车库。他竖过「禁止停车」的牌子，没用。现在他在大门上方装了一个丑陋的石头头像——作者见过最丑的脸之一。贾斯珀说那是蛇发女妖美杜莎，希望她把汽车和车主都变成石头。可到现在一个也没变！本课学现在完成时（has just bought、has had、has not been able、have not had）和one of the + 最高级 + 复数名词。

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander for New Concept English (Longman, 1967); Jasper White is invented, while Medusa comes from Greek myth.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为《新概念英语》（朗文，1967）自撰；贾斯珀·怀特是虚构的，美杜莎出自希腊神话。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

In Greek myth Medusa was one of three Gorgon sisters, the only mortal one, and anyone who looked at her face was turned to stone. The hero Perseus cut off her head by watching her reflection in a polished shield, and the head kept its power afterwards. For this reason Gorgon faces were carved on shields, temples and doorways in the ancient world to frighten away enemies and evil, which is exactly the use Jasper puts one to. Parking itself became a fierce city problem in Britain in the sixties, as car ownership grew far faster than streets and garages; the first parking meters had appeared in London in 1958.

在希腊神话里，美杜莎是戈耳工三姐妹之一，也是唯一会死的那个；谁看她的脸，谁就会变成石头。英雄珀耳修斯看着磨亮的盾牌里的倒影砍下了她的头，而那颗头之后依然保有魔力。正因如此，古代世界常把戈耳工的脸刻在盾牌、神庙和门口，用来吓退敌人和邪祟——这正是贾斯珀派给它的用场。停车本身在六十年代成了英国城市的尖锐问题，因为汽车数量增长得远比街道和车库快；伦敦第一批停车计时器出现在 1958 年。

✨ How it is written赏析

The comedy lies in the gap between a modern nuisance and an ancient cure. Everything in Jasper's problem belongs to the present, and the tense of the lesson reflects that: has bought, has had, has not been able, have not had. The problem is still going on, so the present perfect is everywhere. Against this run of ordinary frustration the writer sets a figure from a world of gods and monsters, and treats it quite seriously, giving her full name and title. The punch line uses the same tense one last time, in the passive, and the word yet is the final small joke: it suggests that Jasper, and perhaps the writer, have not quite given up hope.

喜剧在于：一个现代的烦恼，配上一个古代的解药。贾斯珀的问题全都属于当下，课文的时态也反映了这一点：has bought、has had、has not been able、have not had。问题还在继续，所以现在完成时无处不在。作者在这一连串日常的恼火对面，摆上一个来自众神与怪物世界的形象，而且一本正经地对待她，连全名带称号都写上。笑点句最后一次用了同一个时态，而且是被动；yet 这个词是最后一个小玩笑：它暗示贾斯珀——也许还有作者——还没完全死心。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

Jasper's method is funny because it cannot work, but the idea behind it is older and more widespread than it looks. Many cultures place frightening faces at their doors to keep away harm, from Gorgons in Greece to the door gods and stone lions found at Chinese gateways, and Chinese readers will see the likeness at once. Modern research on parking points to duller tools that do work, such as clear markings, enforcement and charging for road space. There is also a gentler lesson about neighbours. Jasper never seems to speak to the drivers; a note, a conversation, or a word with the council might help more than any statue.

贾斯珀的办法好笑，是因为它不可能管用；可它背后的想法比看上去更古老、更普遍。很多文化都在门口放吓人的面孔来辟邪，从希腊的戈耳工，到中国门口的门神和石狮子，中国读者一眼就能看出其中的相似。关于停车的现代研究指向的是一些乏味但有效的手段：清楚的标线、执法、按占用路面收费。还有一个温和一些的教训，关于邻里：贾斯珀似乎从来没有跟那些司机说过话；一张便条、一次交谈，或者向市政部门反映一下，可能比任何雕像都更有用。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 1第 1 句 (begins开头 “Jasper White is …”)

  • believe in: be sure that something exists or is true.believe in：相信……的存在。

Sentence 2第 2 句 (begins开头 “He has just …”)

  • has just bought: the present perfect for something very recent.has just bought：现在完成时，刚刚买了。
  • ever since takes the past simple, and the main clause takes the present perfect: from then until now.ever since + 过去时，主句用现在完成时：从那以后一直。

Sentence 4第 4 句 (begins开头 “Because of this, …”)

  • has not been able to: the present perfect of can, which has no perfect form of its own.has not been able to：现在完成时的「不能」，can 没有完成时。
  • even once: not a single time, which makes the negative stronger.even once：一次都（没有），加强否定。

Sentence 7第 7 句 (begins开头 “It is one …”)

  • one of the with a superlative and a plural noun: among the most.one of the + 最高级 + 复数名词：最……的之一。
  • I have ever seen: the present perfect with ever, often after a superlative.I have ever seen：现在完成时 + ever，「我见过的」，常跟在最高级后面。

Sentence 10第 10 句 (begins开头 “But none of …”)

  • has been turned: the present perfect passive. yet: so far, in a negative sentence.has been turned：现在完成时被动。yet：至今还（没）。
  • turn something to stone: change it into stone.turn … to stone：把……变成石头。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

The present perfect: from the past up to now现在完成时：从过去到现在
have or has with a past participle links the past to now. It often goes with just, ever, never, yet, since and for. A fixed past time such as yesterday needs the past simple instead.
have / has + 过去分词，说过去发生、和现在有关的事。常和 just、ever、never、yet、since、for 连用。具体的过去时间（yesterday、last year）要用一般过去时。

Almost every sentence from 2 to 10 uses the present perfect, because the trouble is still going on.

本课：第 2 句 has just bought / has had、第 4 句 has not been able、第 5 句 has put up / have not had、第 6 句 has put、第 7 句 have ever seen、第 10 句 has been turned。

  • I have just finished my homework.
  • She has lived here since 2010.
  • Have you ever been to Japan?
one of the with a superlative and a plural nounone of the + 最高级 + 复数名词
one of the with a superlative and a plural noun is often followed by I have ever seen, met or read. The subject is one, so the main verb is singular.
one of the + 形容词最高级 + 复数名词，常接 (that) I have ever seen / met / read。主语是 one，所以谓语动词用单数。

Sentence 7 uses this pattern with ugliest.

本课：第 7 句用了这个句型，形容词是 ugly 的最高级。

  • She is one of the kindest people I have ever met.
  • One of the best things about this town is the park.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • X is one of the few people who really … X 是少有的真心……的人
    My uncle is one of the few people who really enjoy the dentist.
  • Ever since X moved in, X has had trouble with … 自从 X 搬进来，就一直被……困扰
    Ever since we moved here, we have had trouble with the heating.
  • X has not been able to … even once. X 一次也没能……
    I have not been able to beat him even once.
  • It is one of the …est … I have ever seen. 这是我见过最……的……之一。
    It is one of the tallest trees I have ever seen.

Our model summary本站范文

Jasper White has just moved into a city house, but strangers always park in front of his gate, so he has never once put his car in the garage. Signs have not helped. Because he believes in old myths, he has now hung a stone Medusa over the gate. So far, no driver has turned to stone.（57 词）
