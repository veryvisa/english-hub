Lesson 15 · Good News 好消息

📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 15 “Good News”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 15《Good News》

B1🧠 15 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
The secretary lets the writer know that Mr Harmsworth wants to see him. He walks in very anxious, and his boss does not even raise his eyes from the desk. Once the writer is seated, the boss explains that trade is poor and the company can no longer pay such high wages; twenty staff have gone already. The writer is sure he is next. He starts to speak in a feeble voice, but is told not to interrupt, and then, with a smile, the boss gives him a pay rise of a thousand pounds a year. The grammar is reported speech and the difference between say and tell.
秘书通知作者，哈姆斯沃斯先生要见他。作者走进办公室时非常紧张，老板头也没抬。等他坐下，老板说生意很不好，公司付不起这么高的薪水，已经走了二十个人。作者心想：这回轮到我了。他刚有气无力地开口，老板就说「别打断我」——然后笑着告诉他，他每年会多拿一千英镑！本课学间接引语（told me that … would …）和 say / tell 的区别。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. What was the good news?好消息是什么？
  2. Why did the writer think he was about to lose his job?作者为什么以为自己要被解雇？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander for New Concept English (Longman, 1967); Mr Harmsworth and his firm are invented.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为《新概念英语》（朗文，1967）自撰；哈姆斯沃斯先生和他的公司都是虚构的。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

In British offices of the 1960s, the boss was often a distant figure who called staff in only for serious matters, and being summoned to his room usually meant news, good or bad. Pay was private, rises were decided at the top, and an employee rarely knew how the company was doing until he was told. A thousand pounds a year was a very large rise at the time, more than many people earned in a whole year, which makes the ending even more surprising. The name Harmsworth would also have sounded familiar to readers, as it belonged to a well-known family of newspaper owners, and it carries a hint of wealth and power.

二十世纪六十年代的英国办公室里，老板往往是个遥远的人物，只有大事才把员工叫进去；被叫进他的房间，通常意味着有消息，好坏难说。薪水是私密的，加薪由上头决定，员工很少知道公司经营得怎样，除非有人告诉他。在当时，一年一千英镑是非常大的涨幅，比很多人一整年的收入还多，这让结尾更加出人意料。哈姆斯沃斯这个姓当时的读者也会觉得耳熟——它属于一个有名的报业家族——带着一丝财富与权势的意味。

✨ How it is written赏析

The lesson is a model of suspense in a very small space. Every detail before the end points the same way: the formal summons, the boss who does not look up, the talk of bad business and high wages, the twenty who have gone. Reported speech keeps the boss's words at a distance, so they sound cold and official. When the writer finally speaks, his weak voice is cut off at once, which seems to confirm the worst. Then the last sentence turns everything round in a single breath. Looking back, we see that the boss was not giving bad news at all; he was explaining why the rise was a sign of how much he valued the writer.

这课是在极小篇幅里制造悬念的样板。结尾之前的每个细节都指向同一个方向：正式的召见、头也不抬的老板、关于生意差和薪水高的话、已经走掉的二十个人。间接引语让老板的话隔着一层距离，听起来冷冰冰、公事公办。作者终于开口，有气无力的声音立刻被打断，似乎坐实了最坏的结果。然后最后一句一口气把一切翻转过来。回头再看，我们才明白老板根本不是在宣布坏消息，他是在说明：这次加薪表明他多么看重作者。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

Modern managers are often told not to deliver news this way. Advice on workplace communication usually says to give good news first and clearly, because long, dark openings make people anxious and can damage trust. The boss in the lesson enjoys his little drama, and the writer pays for it with ten minutes of fear. Chinese readers may know the same feeling from being called to a teacher's office or a manager's room without warning, and the habit of fearing the worst. The story is a reminder to those in charge that the way news is given can matter as much as the news itself.

如今的管理者常被告诫不要这样传达消息。关于职场沟通的建议通常是：好消息要先说、说清楚，因为又长又阴沉的开场会让人焦虑，还可能损害信任。课文里的老板享受他的小戏码，作者却为此付出了十分钟的恐惧。中国读者对这种感觉也许并不陌生：毫无预告地被叫到老师办公室或经理房间，于是习惯性地往最坏处想。这个故事提醒管事的人：消息怎么传达，可能和消息本身一样重要。

💬 Discuss and write讨论与写作
  1. Write about a time you expected bad news but got good news. Use reported speech for what people said.
    写一次你以为是坏消息、结果是好消息的经历，用间接引语转述别人说的话。
  2. If you were Mr Harmsworth, how would you tell the writer the news? Write the conversation in direct speech.
    如果你是哈姆斯沃斯先生，你会怎样告诉作者这个消息？用直接引语写出这段对话。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 1第 1 句 (begins开头 “The secretary told …”)

  • told me that: reported speech; tell takes the person straight after it.told me that …：间接引语，tell 后面直接跟人。
  • would see: the secretary said will, and in reporting it moves back to would.would see：秘书原话是 will see，转述时退一步变 would。

Sentence 3第 3 句 (begins开头 “He did not …”)

  • enter takes a direct object, which is left out here.enter 直接带宾语，这里省略了 the office。

Sentence 6第 6 句 (begins开头 “He told me …”)

  • said that and told me that: say does not take the person directly, but tell must.said that / told me that：say 后不能直接跟人，tell 后必须跟人。
  • could not afford to do: did not have enough money to do it. such large salaries: such with an adjective and a plural noun.could not afford to do：负担不起做某事。such large salaries：这么高的薪水（such + 形容词 + 复数名词）。

Sentence 11第 11 句 (begins开头 “Then he smiled …”)

  • told me, with or without that.told me (that) …：that 可省略。
  • a year: every year. The reversal comes in the last sentence.a year：每年。全课的反转在最后一句。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

Reported speech: tenses move back间接引语的时态后移
When the reporting verb is in the past, such as said or told, the reported words usually move one step back: is becomes was, can becomes could, will becomes would, and have done becomes had done.
主句是过去时（said、told）时，转述的内容一般往过去退一步：is → was，can → could，will → would，have done → had done。

Sentences 1, 5, 6 and 11 all show reported words moved one tense back.

本课：第 1 句 would see，第 5 句 was，第 6 句 could not afford，第 11 句 would receive，都是转述后退一步的形式。

  • She said, "I am tired." → She said that she was tired.
  • He said, "I will help you." → He told me that he would help me.
say and tellsay 与 tell
say takes a that clause and does not take the person directly; you say something to somebody. tell must have the person straight after it. Set phrases: tell a story, a lie or the truth; say hello, goodbye or sorry.
say + (that) 从句，后面不直接跟人（要说 say to sb）；tell + 人 + (that) 从句，tell 后面必须有人。固定搭配：tell a story / a lie / the truth；say hello / goodbye / sorry。

Sentence 5 uses said that; sentences 1, 6 and 11 use told me.

本课：第 5 句 he said that，第 1、6、11 句 told me that / told me。

  • She said that she was busy.
  • She told me that she was busy.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • X told me that Y would … X 告诉我 Y 会……
    The nurse told me that the doctor would come soon.
  • X felt very nervous as X … X……的时候非常紧张
    She felt very nervous as she stood up to speak.
  • X could not afford to … X 负担不起……
    The family could not afford to buy a house.
  • X knew X's turn had come. X 知道轮到自己了。
    When they called the next name, she knew her moment had come.

Summary writing摘要写作

In no more than 50 words, tell what happened to the writer in his boss's office.

用不超过 50 个词讲述作者在老板办公室里的经历。

Points a good summary covers好摘要要覆盖的要点

Our model summary本站范文

When I went into Mr Harmsworth's office, I was very nervous. He said that business was bad and the firm could not pay high wages; twenty people had already gone. I thought I would lose my job. Instead he smiled and gave me a thousand pounds more a year.（49 词）
