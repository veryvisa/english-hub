Lesson 88 · Trapped in a Mine 困在矿井里

📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 88 “Trapped in a Mine”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 88《Trapped in a Mine》

B1🧠 21 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
Six miners have been underground for seventeen hours, and unless they are brought up soon they may die. Explosives cannot be used, since the shock would bring the roof down, so the rescuers are boring a shaft on the north side and plan to raise the men one by one in a special capsule. A bed of hard rock under the earth has slowed them badly: without it the job would have taken hours, but they have been at it for sixteen and are far from done. A microphone sent down two hours ago lets the men talk to their families. Food and water are running short, yet they are in good spirits, because they have been told all is going well. If they knew the truth, they would give up hope. This is a news report on a rescue still in progress. The grammar is the three conditional patterns, as it is, enable someone to do and run out of.
六名矿工被困在井下已经 17 个小时，如果不尽快把他们救上地面，他们可能会丧命。可是救援很难：一用炸药，震动就会让矿顶塌下来。所以救援人员正在矿井北侧钻一个洞，打算用一个特制的救生舱把人吊上来。要是土层下面没有一层坚硬的岩石，几个小时就能完工；实际上他们已经钻了 16 个小时，还差得很远。与此同时，两小时前放下去的一只话筒让矿工们能和最亲的家人保持联系。他们的食物和水快用完了，却情绪乐观，相信很快就能出去。他们听到的消息是救援进展顺利——要是知道钻透硬岩有多难，他们会泄气的。本课是一条正在进行中的救援新闻，学 三种条件句、as it is、enable sb to do 和 run out of。

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Unsigned and written for the course as a news report; no mine, country or date is given. The rescue method, a narrow shaft and a capsule, matches several real rescues of the mid-twentieth century.

本课未署名，作为新闻为教材自撰；没有给出矿名、国家和日期。救援方法——一个窄井加一个救生舱——与二十世纪中叶的几次真实救援相符。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

A narrow steel capsule lowered down a drilled shaft was used to bring trapped miners to the surface in Germany in the nineteen fifties and sixties; the rescue at Lengede in 1963, where men were reached after two weeks, became famous as a miracle. The same idea returned in Chile in 2010, when thirty-three miners trapped deep underground for sixty-nine days were lifted out one by one in a capsule after rescuers first made contact through a small borehole that carried notes, food and a camera. In China in 2021, eleven miners were rescued after about two weeks underground at a gold mine in Shandong, after drilling teams reached them through hard rock and sent down supplies and a phone line. In all these cases, contact with the surface was treated as vital for the men's spirits.

二十世纪五六十年代，德国曾用沿钻孔放下去的狭长钢制救生舱把被困矿工救上地面；1963 年莱格德矿难中，人们在两周后才救出被困者，被称为一个奇迹。2010 年在智利，同样的方法再次出现：三十三名矿工被困在深深的地下六十九天，救援人员先通过一个小钻孔与他们取得联系，送下字条、食物和摄像头，最后用救生舱把他们一个一个吊了上来。2021 年在中国，山东一座金矿有十一名矿工在井下被困约两周后获救，钻探队穿过坚硬的岩层打到他们所在的位置，送下补给和电话线。在所有这些事例中，与地面保持联系都被看作维持被困者士气的关键。

✨ How it is written赏析

The report packs a whole rescue into fifteen short sentences and uses conditionals as its main tool. Each kind does a different job: the real condition states the danger, the past unreal one explains the delay, and the present unreal one at the very end reveals a hidden fact. That last sentence changes how we read the whole text: the cheerful men are cheerful because they are not being told the truth. The writer never says whether this is right; the structure simply lets the reader see two pictures at once, the hopeful scene below and the worried scene above.

这篇报道用十五个短句装下了一整场救援，条件句是它的主要工具。每一种各司其职：真实条件句说明危险，与过去相反的条件句解释延误，最后那个与现在相反的条件句揭出一个隐藏的事实。最后一句改变了我们对全文的理解：矿工们之所以乐观，是因为没人告诉他们真相。作者从不评判这样做对不对；结构本身让读者同时看见两幅画面：井下充满希望的场景和地面上忧心忡忡的场景。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The ending raises a hard question that rescuers and doctors still face: should people in danger be told the whole truth? Here the choice is to protect their hope, and there are good reasons for it, since panic uses up air, water and strength. Modern rescue teams usually take a more careful middle path: they are honest about the plan and the time it may take, but give it in small steps, and they let families and trained counsellors speak to the trapped people. The lesson is also a reminder of why mining rules exist; most of these disasters began with known risks that were not dealt with in time.

结尾提出一个救援人员和医生至今仍面对的难题：身处危险中的人，该不该被告知全部真相？这里的选择是保护他们的希望，这有充分的理由，因为惊慌会消耗空气、水和体力。现代救援队通常走一条更谨慎的中间路线：对救援计划和可能需要的时间说实话，但分小步告诉他们，并让家人和受过训练的心理辅导人员与被困者交谈。这一课也提醒我们矿山安全规则为什么存在：这类灾难大多源于已知却没有及时处理的风险。

  • A real condition: if plus the present (here passive), with may or will in the main clause.第一类条件句（真实条件）：if + 一般现在时（被动），主句用 may/will。

Sentence 7第 7 句 (begins开头 “If there had …”)

  • as it is: in fact, as things really are, contrasting with the supposition before it.as it is：实际上（跟前面的假设对照）。
  • have been drilling: the present perfect continuous; the last phrase means be far from finished.have been drilling：现在完成进行时。「a long way」一句：还差得远。

  • enable someone to do: make it possible for them; keep in touch with: stay in contact with.enable sb to do：使某人能够。keep in touch with：保持联系。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

The three conditional patterns三种条件句
First, a real condition: if plus the present, then will or may do. Second, contrary to the present: if plus a past form, then would do. Third, contrary to the past: if plus had done, then would have done.
① 真实条件：If + 一般现在时，will/may do；② 与现在相反：If + 过去式，would do；③ 与过去相反：If + had done，would have done。

This lesson has all three: the real ones in sentences 2 and 4, the present unreal one in sentence 15, and the past unreal one across sentences 7 and 8.

本课：第 2、4 句是第①种；第 15 句是第②种；第 7–8 句是第③种——一课里三种都有。

  • If you heat ice, it melts.
  • If I had known your number, I would have called you.
run out of, enable someone to do, cause something to do
run out of means use up, with a person as subject; something is running out means it is nearly gone; enable someone to do means make it possible; cause something to do means make it happen.
run out of：用完（主语是人），be running out：快用完了（主语是东西）；enable sb to do：使能够；cause sth to do：导致。

Sentence 4 has cause with to, sentence 11 has enable with to, and sentence 12 has run out of in the continuous.

本课：第 4 句 cause … to collapse；第 11 句 enabled the men to keep；第 12 句 running out of。

  • We ran out of petrol on the way home.
  • The new road enables people to get to the city faster.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • X have been trapped in … for … hours. X 已经被困在……里……个小时了
    Ten climbers have been trapped in a cave for twelve hours.
  • If … are not …ed soon, they may … 如果不尽快……，他们可能会……
    If the plants are not watered soon, they may die.
  • If there had not been …, they would have … 要不是有……，他们早就……
    If there had not been so much traffic, we would have arrived on time.
  • In fact, X has been …ing for … and still … 实际上，X 已经……了……，却还……
    In fact, we have been waiting for an hour and still have no news.
  • If they knew how …, they would … 要是他们知道有多……，他们就会……
    If the children knew how cold it was outside, they would stay in bed.

Our model summary本站范文

Six miners have been trapped underground for seventeen hours and may die if they are not rescued soon. Explosives cannot be used because the roof might fall in, so rescuers are drilling a hole to lift them out in a capsule. Hard rock has slowed the work badly. The men can talk to their families through a microphone. Though their food and water are nearly gone, they are hopeful, because nobody has told them how hard the drilling is.（79 词）
