Lesson 13 · The Greenwood Boys 绿林少年

📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 13 “The Greenwood Boys”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 13《The Greenwood Boys》

B1🧠 17 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
The Greenwood Boys are a pop group now on a tour of the whole country, and tomorrow they reach this town. They are travelling by rail, and most of the town's teenagers plan to be at the station when they get in. Tomorrow night they perform at the Workers' Club; they stay five days and do five shows. As ever, the police are in for a hard time keeping the crowds under control, as happens every time. The grammar is the future continuous, will be doing, set against the simple future and the present continuous for things going on at present.
「绿林少年」是一支流行歌手组合，眼下正在全国巡演，明天就要到本镇。他们坐火车来，镇上大多数年轻人会去车站接他们；明晚他们在工人俱乐部演出，一共待五天、演五场。和往常一样，警察又要辛苦了：他们得努力维持秩序——每逢这种场合总是如此。本课学将来进行时 will be doing，并和一般将来时 will do、表示「眼下」的现在进行时作对比。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. What will make life hard for the police?为什么警察要辛苦了？
  2. How long will the group stay, and how many shows will they give?绿林少年会在镇上待多久、演几场？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander for New Concept English (Longman, 1967); the Greenwood Boys are an invented group, written in the years when British pop fans first became front-page news.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为《新概念英语》（朗文，1967）自撰；「绿林少年」是虚构的组合，课文写于英国流行乐迷首次登上头版的那几年。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

In the early and middle 1960s, British pop groups drew crowds of a kind that had rarely been seen before. Thousands of teenagers, many of them girls, waited at stations and airports, screamed through concerts and chased cars, and the newspapers reported each visit as if it were a public emergency. Police leave was cancelled and extra officers were brought in from nearby towns. Touring was mostly by train and road, playing in cinemas, dance halls and clubs, often one town each night. Readers in 1967 would have recognised every detail of the lesson, including the tired police, as something they had seen on the news or in their own town.

二十世纪六十年代前中期，英国的流行乐队吸引来的人潮前所未见。成千上万的青少年——其中很多是女孩——守在车站和机场，在演唱会上尖叫，追着汽车跑，报纸报道每一次到访都像在报道一场公共紧急事件。警察取消休假，还从附近城镇调来增援。巡演主要靠火车和公路，在电影院、舞厅和俱乐部演出，常常一晚一个镇。1967 年的读者会认出课文的每个细节，连那些疲惫的警察在内，都是他们在新闻里或自己镇上见过的。

✨ How it is written赏析

The whole text is a forecast, and the future continuous gives it the feel of a scene being described before it happens. The writer sounds calm and a little amused, as if reporting a weather pattern rather than an event. The facts arrive in a neat order: who, where, when, how long, how many. Then the focus moves from the singers to the police, and the tone becomes dry. The last sentence, short and general, says that this always happens, which turns a piece of local news into a comment on the times. No adjective describes the fans; the reader fills in the noise.

整篇是一则预告，将来进行时让它像在事情发生前就描绘出一幅场景。作者的口气平静，还带点好笑，仿佛在报一种天气规律而不是一个事件。事实按整齐的顺序到来：谁、在哪、何时、待多久、演几场。然后焦点从歌手移到警察，语气变得冷淡。最后一句短而概括，说「每次都这样」，把一条本地新闻变成了对时代的评论。没有一个形容词描写歌迷，噪音留给读者自己去想。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

Fan crowds have not gone away; they have moved online and grown larger. Today tours are planned around ticket sites and social media, and the scenes at airports are filmed and shared within minutes. Chinese readers will know the same energy from fan clubs that follow idols from city to city and gather at airports with banners. Cities still face the problem that the lesson's police face: how to keep large, excited crowds safe without spoiling the fun. The text also invites a quieter thought. The town talks about the visit for days, while the group stays for five and moves on.

追星的人潮并没有消失，只是转到了网上，而且规模更大。如今巡演围绕售票网站和社交媒体来策划，机场的场面几分钟内就被拍下来传开。中国读者对这种能量并不陌生：粉丝后援会跟着偶像一城一城地跑，举着灯牌守在机场。城市至今面对课文里警察面对的难题：怎样让大批激动的人群保持安全，又不扫大家的兴。课文还引出一个更安静的念头：小镇为这次到访热议好几天，而乐队只待五天就去了下一站。

💬 Discuss and write讨论与写作
  1. Have you ever waited in a crowd to see someone famous? Describe it in the past, then describe a future event using will be doing.
    你有没有在人群中等着看名人？先用过去时描述，再用 will be doing 描述一个将来的场面。
  2. If you were in charge of the police for the group's visit, what three things would you plan?
    如果你负责这次到访的警力，你会安排哪三件事？

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 2第 2 句 (begins开头 “At present, they …”)

  • at present with the present continuous: something going on for a period around now.at present + 现在进行时：眼下这段时间正在做（巡演中）。

Sentence 3第 3 句 (begins开头 “They will be …”)

  • will be doing is the future continuous, which paints a picture of something in progress at a future time.will be doing：将来进行时，说明明天某个时候「正在到达」，像描绘一幅将来的画面。

Sentence 4第 4 句 (begins开头 “They will be …”)

  • by train: by with a means of transport takes no article.by train：坐火车，by + 交通工具不加冠词。
  • most of the: the greater number of a known group.most of the …：……中的大多数。

Sentence 8第 8 句 (begins开头 “As usual, the …”)

  • as usual: in the same way as always.as usual：像往常一样。
  • have a difficult time: go through trouble. keep order: make people behave and stay safe.have a difficult time：日子不好过，很辛苦。keep order：维持秩序。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

The future continuous: will be doing将来进行时：will be doing
will be with the -ing form shows an action in progress at a future time, or something that will happen as a matter of course, and it sounds more neutral than will do.
will be + 现在分词，表示将来某个时间正在进行的动作，或按计划自然会发生的事，语气比 will do 更客观、平和。

Sentences 3, 4, 5, 6 and 9 use the future continuous to picture the scene; sentences 7 and 8 switch to the simple future for totals and results.

本课：第 3、4、5、6、9 句都用将来进行时描绘明天的场面；第 7、8 句换成 will give / will have，讲结果和总数。

  • This time tomorrow I will be flying to Beijing.
  • Don't call at eight — we will be having dinner.
The present continuous for a period around now现在进行时表示「眼下这段时间」
The present continuous is not only for this very moment; it is also for something going on over a period around now, even if not at this second, often with at present, this week or these days.
现在进行时不只讲「此刻」，也讲眼下一段时间里正在做的事（不一定此刻在做），常配 at present、this week、these days。

In sentence 2 the group may not be singing this second, but they are in the middle of a tour.

本课：第 2 句 at present they are visiting——他们此刻未必在唱歌，但正处在巡演期间。

  • I am reading a very long novel these days.
  • My brother is studying in Montreal this year.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • At present, X are visiting … 眼下，X 正在走访……
    At present, the doctors are visiting all the schools in the city.
  • X will be arriving / coming … tomorrow. X 明天将到达……
    Our guests will be arriving tomorrow afternoon.
  • most of the … in the town 镇上大多数的……
    Most of the shops in the town close at six.
  • As usual, X will have a difficult time. 和往常一样，X 又要辛苦了。
    As usual, the teachers will have a busy week.
