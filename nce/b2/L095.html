Lesson 95 · A Fantasy 幻想

📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 95 “A Fantasy”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 95《A Fantasy》

B1🧠 20 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
The ambassador of an imaginary country comes home for lunch, pale and in a terrible state, and his wife is shocked. Students set fire to the embassy that morning, he says. When the fire started downstairs he hurried down, and a member of his staff, thinking he was burning, covered him with foam. He means to have the man sent elsewhere. Then his wife sees a large hole in his hat. Someone shot through his window, he explains calmly, and a good shot too; luckily his head was not in the hat at the time, or he would have missed lunch for good. This is a dialogue in deadpan humour. The grammar is the unreal past conditional, get something done, go on doing, and set something on fire.
埃斯卡洛皮亚国大使回家吃午饭，妻子吓了一跳：他脸色苍白，衣服一塌糊涂。原来上午大学生放火烧了大使馆，火从地下室烧起来，大使照例在办公室，立刻下楼，结果那个蠢货霍斯特以为他着火了，拿灭火器对着他一通喷。大使说一定要把这家伙调走。妻子还在追问，忽然发现丈夫帽子上有个大洞。大使轻描淡写：有人朝他办公室窗户开了一枪，打得挺准吧？幸好当时没戴着，要是戴着，就回不了家吃午饭了。本课是一段冷面幽默的对话，学与过去事实相反的虚拟语气（If + had done, would not have done）、get sth done、go on doing 和 set … on fire。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. How had the ambassador's clothes been ruined?大使的衣服为什么弄得那么脏？
  2. What made the hole in his hat?帽子上的洞是怎么来的？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Unsigned and written for the course, as the title says, as a fantasy; the country is invented and the events are not a report.

本课未署名、为教材自撰，正如标题所说是一篇幻想故事；国家是虚构的，情节也不是报道。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

Embassies are protected by an international agreement, the Vienna Convention on Diplomatic Relations of 1961, which says the host country must take every step to protect embassy buildings from attack. Even so, embassies have been burned or stormed many times when crowds were angry with a foreign government, and students have often been part of such crowds. Ambassadors in the story's world would also have known about being posted: diplomats move from country to country every few years, and sending someone to a less pleasant post was a quiet way to punish him, which is exactly what this ambassador plans for Horst. The country's name is a joke: an escalope is a thin slice of meat fried in breadcrumbs.

大使馆受一项国际协定保护——1961 年的《维也纳外交关系公约》，它规定驻在国必须采取一切适当步骤保护使馆馆舍不受侵犯。尽管如此，民众对某国政府愤怒时，使馆被烧被冲的事发生过很多次，学生也常在人群里。故事里那个世界的大使也很熟悉「调任」：外交官每隔几年换一个国家，把人派到条件差的地方，是一种不声张的惩罚，这正是这位大使给霍斯特安排的。国名也是个玩笑：escalope 是一种裹面包屑炸的薄肉片。

✨ How it is written赏析

The humour is in the gap between what happens and how it is told. A fire and a bullet are terrible events, but the ambassador reports them as if they were small annoyances at the office. He is angrier with Horst than with the people who tried to burn him or shoot him, and his biggest worry seems to be lunch. The wife asks the questions the reader wants to ask, which keeps the story moving through dialogue alone. Watch the last line: it is a perfectly correct conditional, calm and grammatical, and it describes his own death. That is what dry humour means.

幽默出在发生了什么与怎么讲之间的落差上。火灾和子弹都是可怕的事，大使却讲得像办公室里的小麻烦。他对霍斯特的气比对想烧死他、打死他的人还大，最操心的似乎是午饭。妻子问的正是读者想问的，故事光靠对话就往前推。注意最后一句：它是一个完全正确的虚拟条件句，平静、合乎语法，说的却是他自己的死。这就是冷面幽默。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

Behind the joke is a real type: the official who stays calm because panic would be worse. Diplomats, pilots and doctors are trained to speak in a flat, steady voice when things go wrong, because calm spreads just as fear does. The story also hints at how embassies look from outside: angry students, a fire, a shot through a window. Readers living abroad sometimes see their own country's embassy only as a place for visas and passports, but it is also a small piece of politics that crowds can choose as a target. If you ever need help abroad, it is worth knowing where your embassy or consulate is before trouble starts, not after.

笑话背后是一种真实存在的人：因为慌乱只会更糟，所以保持冷静的官员。外交官、飞行员和医生都受过训练，出了事要用平稳的声音说话，因为镇定和恐惧一样会传染。故事也暗示了使馆在外人眼里的样子：愤怒的学生、一场火、一颗射进窗户的子弹。住在海外的读者有时只把本国使馆当成办签证、换护照的地方，但它也是一小块政治，会被人群选作目标。万一在海外需要帮助，最好在出事之前就知道本国使馆或领事馆在哪里，而不是出事之后。

💬 Discuss and write讨论与写作
  1. Is it better to stay calm or to show your feelings when something bad happens? Give an example.
    遇到坏事时，保持冷静好，还是表露情绪好？举个例子。
  2. Tell a frightening story of your own in a dry, calm way, as the ambassador does, and see how it changes.
    像大使那样，用平静冷淡的口吻讲一件你自己经历的吓人的事，看看效果有什么不同。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 1第 1 句 (begins开头 “When the Ambassador …”)

  • get a shock: be suddenly surprised and upset. Escalopia is a made-up country named after a dish of thin fried meat, a hint that the story is a fantasy.get a shock：吓了一跳。Escalopia 是虚构国名（escalope 是一种炸肉片），提示读者这是幻想故事。

Sentence 2第 2 句 (begins开头 “He looked pale …”)

  • look plus an adjective: appear; be in a bad state: be in poor condition.look + 形容词：看起来；be in a … state：处于……的状态。

Sentence 4第 4 句 (begins开头 “'How did your …”)

  • get into a mess: become dirty or untidy; such goes before a plus a noun.get into a mess：弄得一团糟；such a + 名词。

Sentence 6第 6 句 (begins开头 “'University students set …”)

  • set something on fire means the same as set fire to it.set sth on fire = set fire to sth：放火烧。

Sentence 14第 14 句 (begins开头 “I must definitely …”)

  • get someone done here means arrange for him to be sent to another post.get sb/sth done：让（别人把）某人/某物……；这里是「让人把他调走」。

Sentence 15第 15 句 (begins开头 “The Ambassador's wife …”)

  • go on doing: continue the same action.go on doing：继续做同一件事。
  • when here means and just then, bringing in something sudden.when 在这里表示「就在这时」，引出突然发生的事。

Sentence 22第 22 句 (begins开头 “If I had …”)

  • The unreal past conditional: if plus had done, would have done. After had been, wearing it is left out.与过去事实相反的虚拟：If + had done, would have done。had been 后面省略了 wearing it。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

The unreal past conditional与过去事实相反的虚拟语气
If plus had done, then would, could or might have done: you imagine a past event that did not happen and say what would have followed.
If + 主语 + had done, 主语 + would / could / might have done：假设过去没发生的事，并说出想象中的结果。

In sentences 21 and 22 the fact is that he was not wearing it, and the unreal clause imagines the opposite.

本课：第 21–22 句：事实是他没戴帽子；假如戴着，就回不来了——用 If … had been 配 would not have 。

  • If you had told me, I would have helped you.
  • If it hadn't rained, we could have gone to the beach.
get or have something doneget / have sth done：让别人做
get or have plus an object plus a past participle means you arrange for someone else to do it; get is more informal.
get/have + 宾语 + 过去分词：安排别人做某事，或某事被做；get 更口语。

In sentence 14 he means to arrange through others for the man to be moved.

本课：第 14 句：大使要 get that fellow posted——让人把他调走，不是自己动手。

  • I must get my car repaired.
  • She had her photo taken for her passport.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • X came home looking pale and in a frightful state X 回到家，脸色苍白，狼狈不堪
    He came home looking pale and in a frightful state after the storm.
  • How did your … get into such a mess? 你的……怎么弄成这副样子？
    How did your shoes get into such a mess?
  • Someone set … on fire last night 昨晚有人放火烧了……
    Someone set the old barn on fire last night.
  • I really must get that … moved 我真得让人把那个……调走
    I really must get that noisy fan moved.
  • If I had …, I wouldn't have been able to … 要是我当时……，就没法……了
    If I had missed the bus, I wouldn't have been able to sit the exam.

Our model summary本站范文

The ambassador came home for lunch looking pale, with his clothes ruined. Students had set the embassy on fire that morning. When the fire started in the basement he rushed down, and a foolish assistant, thinking he was burning, sprayed him with an extinguisher. Then his wife saw a hole in his hat. Someone had shot through his office window, he said calmly. Luckily he had not been wearing the hat, or he would never have come home.（78 词）
