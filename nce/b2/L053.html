Lesson 53 · Hot Snake 触电的蛇

📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 53 “Hot Snake”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 53《Hot Snake》

B1🧠 17 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
After a long fight, fire crews have finally beaten a huge blaze in the forests of California, and since then they have been looking for its cause. Such fires usually start with bits of glass or with cigarettes people drop without thinking. Yesterday the crews searched the ground closely, but there was no glass, and they were certain no cigarette was to blame. This morning one man came upon the answer by chance: the remains of a snake coiled round the wires of a 16,000-volt line. A bird had carried the snake into the air and let it fall on the wires. As it wrapped itself round them, sparks fell to the dry ground and set it alight. The grammar is since then with the present perfect, be caused by, be able to, and the past perfect.
消防队员终于扑灭了加利福尼亚的一场森林大火，此后他们一直在查起火原因。森林火灾常由碎玻璃或随手乱扔的烟头引起。昨天消防队员仔细检查了地面，没找到碎玻璃，也很肯定不是烟头。今天早上，一名消防队员无意中发现了原因：一条蛇的残骸缠在一根一万六千伏高压线上。解释简单，却很不寻常：一只鸟从地上叼起这条蛇，又把它扔到电线上，蛇缠住电线时，火花溅落地面，立刻引燃了大火。本课学现在完成时与 since then、被动语态 be caused by、be able to 和过去完成时。

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为《新概念英语》（朗文，1967）撰写；读来像一则新闻，但没有找到原始报道，来源未核。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

California has a long dry season, and wildfires there are a regular danger. Fire investigators work like detectives, tracing burn patterns back to the point where the fire began and checking known causes one by one. Power lines are a real and serious cause: official investigations in California have found that electrical equipment started some of the state's most destructive fires in recent decades, and utilities now cut power on windy days to prevent sparks. Animals touching lines also cause outages and occasional fires; birds of prey are known to drop snakes and other prey, so the story is unusual but not impossible.

加利福尼亚旱季漫长，野火是那里常年的威胁。火灾调查员像侦探一样工作，顺着燃烧痕迹追到起火点，再把已知的起火原因逐一排查。电线是真实而严重的起因：加州官方调查发现，近几十年里该州最具破坏力的几场大火有的就是电力设备引起的，电力公司如今会在大风天主动断电以防火花。动物碰到电线也会造成停电甚至起火；猛禽叼着蛇等猎物飞行时失手掉落的事确有记载，所以这个故事虽然少见，却并非不可能。

✨ How it is written赏析

The text is shaped like a short detective story. First the obvious suspects are named, glass and cigarettes, and then each is cleared. The discovery comes by accident, which makes it feel real, because investigations often turn on luck. Only then does the writer give the full chain of events, and he gives it in order, bird, snake, wire, sparks, so the reader can picture each link. The last sentence is the shortest step from cause to effect. The title is a pun as well: the snake was hot in the sense of carrying a deadly current.

课文的结构像一篇小小的侦探故事。先点出最明显的嫌疑——玻璃和烟头，再逐一排除。真相是偶然发现的，这让它显得真实，因为调查往往靠运气转折。直到这时作者才给出完整的因果链，而且按顺序给：鸟、蛇、电线、火花，让读者能想象每一环。最后一句是从原因到结果最短的一步。标题也是双关：蛇之所以「热」，是因为带上了致命的电流。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The story is a good model of careful reasoning: list the usual causes, test them, and stay open to an unusual answer when the evidence points that way. It also has a wider lesson about large systems. A single small event far from anyone's control, a bird dropping its meal, can set off a disaster when the conditions are dry and the equipment is exposed. Modern fire safety has learned this, which is why it focuses on clearing vegetation and protecting lines rather than only blaming careless people. Readers can ask which small risks in their own lives sit near something dangerous.

这个故事是缜密推理的好样本：列出常见原因、逐一检验，当证据指向别处时，对不寻常的答案保持开放。它还有一个关于大型系统的更大道理：一件谁也控制不了的小事——一只鸟丢了它的猎物——在天干物燥、设备裸露的条件下，就能引发一场灾难。现代防火已经吸取了这个教训，所以重点放在清除植被、保护线路上，而不只是怪罪粗心的人。读者可以想一想：自己生活里有哪些小风险正挨着危险的东西？

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 2第 2 句 (begins开头 “At last fireman …”)

  • fireman: the book prints it this way, but the sense is plural, firemen.fireman：此处课本原文如此，按意思应是复数 firemen。

Sentence 3第 3 句 (begins开头 “Since then, they …”)

  • since then: from that time, with the present perfect continuous.since then：从那时起，常与现在完成（进行）时连用。
  • find out how: learn how, with a clause after it.find out how …：查明……，how 引出宾语从句。

Sentence 4第 4 句 (begins开头 “Forest fires are …”)

  • be caused by: the passive, meaning something makes it happen.be caused by：被动语态，由……引起。
  • which people throw away: a relative clause about the cigarette ends.which people … throw away：定语从句修饰 cigarette ends。

Sentence 9第 9 句 (begins开头 “He noticed the …”)

  • wound: the past participle of wind, said waʊnd, not like the wound meaning injury.wound：wind（缠绕）的过去分词，读作 /waʊnd/，与「伤口」wound 不同音。
  • 16,000-volt: a compound adjective, number plus singular noun with a hyphen.16,000-volt：复合形容词，数词 + 单数名词，中间加连字符。

Sentence 12第 12 句 (begins开头 “A bird had …”)

  • had snatched: the past perfect, this came before the fire.had snatched：过去完成时，蛇被叼起发生在起火之前。
  • on to: moving to a position on top of.on to：到……上面，表示移动方向。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

The passive: be caused by被动语态：be caused by / be done by
The passive is be plus the past participle, with by for the doer. It is common in explanations because the focus is on the result.
被动语态 = be + 过去分词；要说动作执行者，用 by。说明原因、科学解释时常用被动，因为重点在结果。

Sentence 4 uses the passive to say how such fires usually start, and in sentence 9 was wound describes the state of the snake on the wire.

本课：第 4 句用被动说森林火灾通常怎么引起；第 9 句的 was wound 是被动结构，描述蛇缠在线上的状态。

  • The bridge was damaged by the flood.
  • Most colds are caused by viruses.
since then with the present perfectsince then + 现在完成（进行）时
With since then, since plus a point in time or since plus a clause, the main clause usually takes the present perfect or present perfect continuous for something lasting until now.
since then（从那以后）、since + 时间点、since + 从句，主句一般用现在完成时或现在完成进行时，说从那时持续到现在。

In sentence 3 the search has gone on from the end of the fire until now.

本课：第 3 句——火灭了之后，他们一直在查原因。

  • He left school in 2020. Since then he has worked in a bank.
  • I have been feeling better since I started running.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • At last X have put out … 终于，X 扑灭了……
    At last the workers have cleared the snow from the road.
  • Ever since, X have been working out … 从那以后，X 一直在设法弄清……
    Ever since, the police have been working out who sent the letter.
  • … are often caused by … ……常常是由……引起的
    Headaches are often caused by stress.
  • X accidentally discovered the cause. X 无意中发现了原因
    A cleaner accidentally discovered the cause of the leak.
  • The explanation was simple but … 解释很简单，但……
    The answer was simple but surprising.

Our model summary本站范文

Firemen have finally beaten a huge Californian forest fire and have been trying to learn how it began. Such fires are often started by glass or cigarettes, but they found neither. This morning, by chance, a fireman saw a dead snake round the wires of a power line. A bird had picked it up and dropped it there. As the snake coiled round the wires, sparks fell and started the fire.（71 词）
