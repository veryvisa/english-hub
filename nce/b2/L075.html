Lesson 75 · SOS 呼救信号

📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 75 “SOS”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 75《SOS》

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
Some time ago a small passenger plane strayed from its route and crashed among mountains, killing the pilot. The only people on board, a young mother and her two baby girls, came through unharmed. It was deep winter, with heavy snow everywhere, and she knew help was miles off. At nightfall she made a cot of a suitcase, laid the girls in it and piled every garment she had over them. The cold was bitter; she pressed close to them and even tried to squeeze in herself, without success. At dawn she heard aircraft above and wondered how to attract them. Then it came to her: she trod three huge letters into the snow. A pilot spotted them and radioed the nearest town, and a helicopter soon came to lift them out. This is a survival story. The grammar is turn A into B, as ... as one could, wonder how to do and It was not long before.
不久前，一架小型客机偏离航线，坠毁在山里，飞行员遇难。机上仅有的乘客，一位年轻妇女和她的两个小女儿，却毫发无伤。当时正是隆冬，地上积雪很厚，她知道最近的村子也在好几英里外。天黑以后，她把一只手提箱改成床，让孩子们躺进去，把能找到的衣服都盖在她们身上。夜里冷得要命，她尽量贴近孩子，甚至想自己也钻进箱子，可箱子太小了。第二天一早，她听见飞机从头顶飞过，不知道怎样发信号。后来她想出一个办法：在雪地上踩出 SOS 三个字母。幸好一位飞行员看见了，用无线电通知最近的城镇，没过多久，一架直升机就赶到现场，救出了这几个幸存者。本课是一个绝境求生的故事，学 turn A into B、as … as one could、wonder how to do 和 It was not long before。

  2. How did she keep the children warm at night?夜里她怎样让孩子们保暖？

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

SOS is the international distress signal first adopted for radio at a conference in Berlin in 1906 and in general use after 1908. Contrary to a common belief, the letters do not stand for save our souls or save our ship; they were chosen because in Morse code the pattern of three short, three long and three short is easy to send and hard to mistake. Survival guides still recommend making large ground signals in snow or sand, with letters several metres high and lines that cast shadows, because they can be seen from the air. The woman's other choice, keeping the children close and covered, matches modern advice too: young children lose heat quickly, and shared body warmth and dry layers are among the best protections.

SOS 是国际求救信号，1906 年在柏林的一次会议上首先被定为无线电求救信号，1908 年后普遍使用。与常见的说法相反，这三个字母并不代表「救救我们的灵魂」或「救救我们的船」；选它是因为在莫尔斯电码里，三短、三长、三短的组合容易发送，也不容易弄错。野外求生指南至今建议在雪地或沙地上做大的地面信号，字母要好几米高，笔画要能投下阴影，这样从空中才看得见。这位母亲的另一个做法，把孩子贴身护着、盖严实，也符合现代的建议：小孩子散热快，共享体温和干燥的衣物是最好的保护之一。

✨ How it is written赏析

The writer tells a frightening story in very plain sentences. Sentences 4 and 5 are only a few words long, and their shortness makes the cold feel real. The details are small and practical, a suitcase, the clothes, the case being too small for her, and these details show the mother's love without any words about her feelings. The turning point is a single short line, then she had an idea, followed at once by action. The word fortunately is the only comment the writer allows, and the story ends quickly once help comes, because the danger is over.

作者用非常朴素的句子讲了一个可怕的故事。第 4、5 句都只有几个词，短句让寒冷显得真实。细节小而实在：一只手提箱、那些衣服、箱子小得装不下她，这些细节不用一个形容感情的词，就写出了母爱。转折点只有短短一行，「后来她想出一个办法」，紧接着就是行动。fortunately 是作者唯一允许自己加的评语；救援一到故事就很快结束，因为危险已经过去。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The story praises calm thinking under pressure. The mother did not panic; she used what she had and turned a problem into a plan. That is the heart of most survival advice: protect yourself first, then make yourself easy to find, rather than walking off into danger. Today a phone or a small locator beacon might do the job of the letters in the snow, but batteries die in the cold and signals fail in mountains, so the old skills still matter. Chinese readers who hike or ski in places such as Changbai Mountain or the Altai can take a practical lesson: tell someone your route, carry warm layers, and stay with the wreck or the car if you are lost, because rescuers search there first.

这个故事赞美的是压力下的冷静思考。这位母亲没有惊慌，她用手头有的东西，把难题变成了办法。这正是大多数求生建议的核心：先保护好自己，再让别人容易找到你，而不是贸然走进危险。今天，一部手机或一个小型定位信标也许能代替雪地上的字母，可电池在寒冷中会没电，山里信号会中断，所以老办法依然重要。在长白山或阿尔泰山一带徒步、滑雪的中国读者可以从中得到实用的一课：把路线告诉别人，带足保暖衣物；迷路时守在飞机残骸或车子旁边，因为救援人员会先去那里找。

💬 Discuss and write讨论与写作

    如果你在雪地里迷了路，你首先会做什么？把三件事按顺序排好。
  2. Write about a time you solved a problem with whatever was to hand. Use turn A into B once.
    写一次你用手边现有的东西解决问题的经历，用一次 turn A into B。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 1第 1 句 (begins开头 “When a light …”)

  • light: small and not heavy; fly off course: leave the planned route.light：轻型的；fly off course：偏离航线。
  • was killed: the passive is usual for death in an accident.was killed：被动，常用来说事故中死亡。

Sentence 3第 3 句 (begins开头 “The only passengers, …”)

  • A phrase in apposition explains who the passengers were.同位语解释 the only passengers。
  • unhurt: an adjective after be.unhurt：没受伤，作表语。

Sentence 7第 7 句 (begins开头 “When it grew …”)

  • grow dark: become dark; turn A into B: make A serve as B.grow + 形容词：变得。turn A into B：把 A 变成 B。
  • covering: an -ing phrase for what she did at the same time; the noun phrase after with is a relative clause with that left out.covering …：现在分词作伴随状语；「她能找到的所有衣服」：省略 that 的定语从句。

Sentence 10第 10 句 (begins开头 “The woman kept …”)

  • as ... as she could: as much as possible.as … as one could：尽可能……。
  • too small: so small that she could not fit.too small：太小，暗含「装不下她」。

Sentence 12第 12 句 (begins开头 “she heard planes …”)

  • hear something doing: hear it in the middle of the action.hear sb/sth doing：听见……正在……。
  • wonder how: a reported question in statement order.wonder how …：心里想该怎么……，间接疑问句用陈述语序。

Sentence 15第 15 句 (begins开头 “It was not …”)

  • It was not long before: soon after that.It was not long before …：没过多久就……。
  • arrive on the scene: reach the place; to rescue: an infinitive of purpose.arrive on the scene：到达现场。to rescue：不定式表目的。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

as ... as possible and as ... as one can
as plus adjective or adverb plus as possible, or as ... as one can or could, means to the greatest degree possible. Use could for the past.
as + 形容词/副词 + as possible 或 as + 形容词/副词 + as one can / could：尽可能……。过去的事用 could。

Sentence 10 says she stayed as close as she was able; sentence 8 has a similar idea, using every piece of clothing she could find.

本课：第 10 句说妇女尽量贴近孩子；第 8 句说她把找得到的衣服全用上，也是同一个意思：能找到的全用上。

  • Please come as soon as possible.
  • He ran as fast as he could.
It was not long before ... and before long
It was not long before plus a past clause means it happened soon; It will not be long before plus the present means it will happen soon. before long alone also means soon.
It was not long before + 过去时从句：没过多久就……；It will not be long before + 一般现在时：不久就会……。also: before long（不久）。

The last sentence uses it to show how quickly the helicopter came.

本课：最后一句用 It was not long before 说直升机很快到了。

  • It was not long before the rain stopped.
  • It won't be long before the baby can walk.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • When X flew off course, it … and … was killed. X 偏离航线后……，……遇难
    When the small boat drifted off course, it hit a rock and its engine was ruined.
  • The only …, a … and her …, were unhurt. 唯一的……，一位……和她的……，都没有受伤
    The only guests, an old man and his dog, were unhurt.
  • X turned a … into a … and … X 把……改成了……，并……
    Grandpa turned a box into a boat and gave it to the twins.
  • X stayed as close as possible to … X 尽可能靠近……
    The girl stayed as close as possible to her mother in the crowd.
  • It wasn't long before … came to … 没过多久，……就赶来……
    It wasn't long before a tow truck came to help us.

Our model summary本站范文

A small plane crashed in the mountains in winter. The pilot died, but a young mother and her two little girls survived unhurt. That night she made a bed of a suitcase, put the babies in it and covered them with clothes, staying close to keep them warm. In the morning she heard planes and trod SOS into the snow.
