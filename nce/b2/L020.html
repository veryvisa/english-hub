Lesson 20 · One Man in a Boat 一钩不中

📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 20 “One Man in a Boat”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 20《One Man in a Boat》

B1🧠 17 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
Fishing is the writer's favourite sport, yet he can fish for hours and catch nothing. Some unlucky anglers pull out old boots and litter; he does not get even that, and after a whole morning on the river he always goes home with an empty bag. His friends tell him to stop wasting his time. What they miss is that the fish hardly matter to him; his real pleasure is idling afloat with no task whatever. The grammar is the -ing form after prepositions and be interested in.
钓鱼是作者最喜欢的运动，可他常常钓上几个小时也一无所获。有的钓鱼人运气差，钓上来的是旧靴子和垃圾，他连旧靴子都钓不到，每次在河上待一整个上午，都提着空包回家。朋友劝他别钓了，说是浪费时间。可他们不懂：他其实对钓鱼不感兴趣，他喜欢的只是坐在船上什么也不干。本课学介词后接动名词（without catching、instead of catching、after having spent）和 be interested in。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. Why does the writer say fishing is his favourite sport?钓鱼为什么是作者最喜欢的运动？
  2. What is it that his friends have not understood?朋友们没意识到的那件事是什么？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander for New Concept English (Longman, 1967); the writer and his friends are invented.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为《新概念英语》（朗文，1967）自撰；作者和他的朋友都是虚构的。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

Angling has long been one of the most popular pastimes in Britain, and the idea that it is really about something other than fish is very old. In 1653 Izaak Walton published The Compleat Angler, a book that mixes advice on catching fish with conversation, poems and praise of the quiet country life; its subtitle calls angling the contemplative man's recreation. The book has been reprinted hundreds of times and is one of the most published books in English. The writer of the lesson stands in that tradition, though he goes one step further than Walton: he has stopped pretending that the fish matter at all.

钓鱼长期以来是英国最受欢迎的消遣之一，而「钓鱼其实不是为了鱼」这个想法也很古老。1653 年，艾萨克·沃尔顿出版了《钓客清话》，书里既有钓鱼的诀窍，也有对话、诗歌和对宁静乡村生活的赞美；副标题把钓鱼称作「沉思者的消遣」。这本书重印了几百次，是英语里出版次数最多的书之一。本课的作者站在这个传统里，只是比沃尔顿又走了一步：他连「鱼很重要」都不再假装了。

✨ How it is written赏析

The lesson is built to hide its point until the last line. Everything before it seems to be a story of failure: hours with nothing, a comparison with unlucky men that makes him look worse, an empty bag, and friends who give the obvious advice. The reader is placed on the friends' side, ready to agree that he should stop. Then the writer turns the whole thing round. The reason he has failed is that he was never trying. The grammar helps: almost every verb after a preposition is an -ing form, and the final sentence ends with two of them, sitting and doing, so that the quiet activity he loves is also the last thing we read.

课文的写法是把要点藏到最后一行。之前的一切看起来都是失败的故事：几个小时一无所获，拿运气差的人作比较反而显得他更差，空包而归，朋友给出显而易见的劝告。读者被放在朋友那一边，准备同意他该放弃。然后作者把整件事翻了过来：他之所以「失败」，是因为他从来就没想钓。语法也在帮忙：介词后的动词几乎都是 -ing 形式，最后一句以两个 -ing 结尾——sitting 和 doing——于是他喜爱的那件安静的事，也是我们读到的最后一件事。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The friends judge the activity by its output, and the writer judges it by how it feels, and modern research on rest leans towards the writer. Studies of time spent in nature and of unstructured rest suggest benefits for mood and stress, though the evidence varies in quality and is easy to overstate. The idea will also feel familiar to Chinese readers: the old image of a recluse fishing by a river, more interested in the quiet than in the catch, appears in poems and paintings for centuries. The useful question the lesson leaves is about our own habits. How much of your free time has to produce something before you feel it was well spent?

朋友们用产出来评判这项活动，作者用感受来评判，现代关于休息的研究更倾向于作者。关于在大自然中度过时间、以及无安排的休息的研究表明，这些对情绪和压力有好处，不过证据质量参差，也容易被夸大。这个想法对中国读者也不陌生：隐士在江边垂钓、在意的是清静而不是收获，这个形象几百年来一直出现在诗和画里。课文留下的有用问题关乎我们自己的习惯：你的空闲时间有多少必须「产出点什么」，你才觉得没有白过？

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 2第 2 句 (begins开头 “I often fish …”)

  • for hours: for a long time, many hours.for hours：好几个小时。
  • without is a preposition, so the verb after it takes -ing. anything is used because without is negative in meaning.without catching：介词 without 后接动名词。anything 用在否定意味里。

Sentence 5第 5 句 (begins开头 “Instead of catching …”)

  • instead of with an -ing form: not this, but that.instead of + 动名词：「不是……而是……」。
  • The same verb is used for fish and for boots, and that repetition makes the joke.第一个 catching 是钓鱼，第二个 catch 钓的却是靴子，同一个词造出笑点。

Sentence 9第 9 句 (begins开头 “After having spent …”)

  • after having done: the perfect -ing form after a preposition stresses that one thing came first. after spending is also correct.after having done：介词 after 后接完成式动名词，强调先后。也可以说 after spending。
  • An empty bag on the way back means nothing was caught.带着空包回家＝空手而归。

Sentence 14第 14 句 (begins开头 “I am only …”)

  • be interested in takes a noun or an -ing form. Here two -ing forms are joined by and.be interested in + 名词 / 动名词。两个动名词 sitting 和 doing 并列。
  • nothing at all: absolutely nothing. at all makes the negative stronger.nothing at all：什么都不，at all 加强语气。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

A preposition followed by the -ing form介词 + 动名词
After a preposition such as without, instead of, after, before, in, of or at, a verb must take the -ing form. It cannot be the infinitive.
介词（without、instead of、after、before、in、of、at 等）后面的动词一律用 -ing 形式，不能用 to do 或动词原形。

Sentences 2, 5, 9, 13 and 14 all put an -ing form after a preposition.

本课：第 2 句 without、第 5 句 instead of、第 9 句 after having、第 13–14 句 interested in，全课的介词后都跟 -ing。

  • She left without saying goodbye.
  • Wash your hands before eating.
  • He is good at drawing animals.
interested and interesting; comparison with lessinterested 和 interesting；less 的比较
An -ed adjective describes how a person feels, as in I am interested; an -ing adjective describes the thing, as in it is interesting. less with an adjective means not as much, and even less goes one step further.
-ed 形容词说人的感受（I am interested），-ing 形容词说事物的特点（Fishing is interesting）。less + 形容词表示「没那么……」，even less 更进一步。

Sentence 7 uses even less lucky, worse off than the unlucky ones, and sentence 13 uses interested in.

本课：第 7 句 even less lucky，比运气差的人还差；第 13 句 interested in。

  • The film was boring, so I felt bored.
  • This road is less busy than the main one.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • X is my favourite … X 是我最喜欢的……
    Swimming is my favourite way to relax.
  • I often … for hours without …ing anything. 我常常……好几个小时，却什么也没……
    I often read for hours without noticing the time.
  • Instead of …ing X, they … Y. 他们没……X，反倒……Y。
    Instead of buying a new bike, they fixed the old one.
  • I'm only interested in …ing. 我只对……感兴趣。
    I'm only interested in finding a quiet seat.

Summary writing摘要写作

In no more than 50 words, say how the writer's fishing goes and why he will not stop.

用不超过 50 个词说明作者钓鱼的情况，以及他为什么不肯放弃。

Points a good summary covers好摘要要覆盖的要点
  • The writer loves fishing but often catches nothing for hours, not even an old boot.作者最喜欢钓鱼，却常常钓几个小时一无所获，连旧靴子都钓不到。
  • His friends tell him to stop, calling it a waste of time.朋友劝他放弃，说是浪费时间。
  • In truth he cares little about fish; he just enjoys sitting in a boat doing nothing.其实他不在乎鱼，他喜欢的是坐在船上什么也不干。
Our model summary本站范文

Fishing is the writer's favourite sport, though he almost never catches anything, not even rubbish. His friends say he should stop because it wastes his time. They do not understand that he has no real interest in fish. He simply loves sitting quietly afloat with nothing to do.（48 词）
