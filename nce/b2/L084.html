Lesson 84 · On Strike 罢工

📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 84 “On Strike”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 84《On Strike》

B1🧠 17 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
The city's bus drivers will stop work next week, starting on Tuesday, and nobody can say for how long: they say they will stay out until pay and conditions are settled, and most people expect a week at least. Car owners plan to give lifts to commuters, which should take some of the load off the railways. Meanwhile some university students have offered to drive the buses, though first they must pass a special driving test, due in two days. Getting to work will still be hard. So far the public have written to the papers to thank the students; only a couple of people complain that young drivers will go too fast. This is a news report about plans for the future. The grammar is ways of talking about the future, until, so far with the present perfect and to some extent.
公共汽车司机决定下周罢工，定在星期二开始，没人知道会持续多久。司机们表示，要等工资和工作条件达成全面协议才复工；多数人估计至少会罢一周。许多私家车主打算给上班的人提供「免费搭车」，多少能减轻火车的压力。与此同时，一批大学生自愿在罢工期间开公共汽车。他们都是驾驶好手，不过开车前还得通过一次专门的考试，两天后就考。即便如此，人们上班还是会很难。到目前为止，公众纷纷写信给报社，感谢这些学生；只有一两个人提出异议：学生们会把车开得太快！本课是一则讲未来安排的新闻，学 将来时的几种表达、until、so far + 现在完成时 和 to some extent。

Unsigned and written for the course as a news item; no city, union or date is given, so it cannot be checked.

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

Bus and transport strikes were common in Britain in the decades after the Second World War, when public transport was often run by public bodies and pay was set in long national talks. Volunteers driving buses or trains during a strike has a real history too: in the General Strike of 1926, many students and middle-class volunteers kept services running, and the strikers bitterly resented it. Today in most countries this would be far more difficult: bus drivers need a special professional licence, insurance and training, and employers who bring in outside workers to replace strikers can face legal limits, which differ from place to place.

第二次世界大战后的几十年里，英国的公交和运输罢工很常见；那时公共交通往往由公共机构经营，工资在漫长的全国谈判中确定。罢工期间由志愿者开公共汽车或火车，这也有真实的历史：在 1926 年的大罢工中，许多大学生和中产阶级志愿者维持了交通运转，罢工者对此非常愤恨。在今天的大多数国家，这样做要难得多：公交司机需要专门的职业驾照、保险和培训，雇主找外人来顶替罢工者也可能受到法律限制，各地规定不同。

✨ How it is written赏析

The report is built almost entirely from the future: what will happen, what is due, what people are going to do. That makes it a model of how news handles events that have not yet taken place, by stating plans, giving sources, they have stated, most people believe, and avoiding false certainty about the length of the dispute. The writer stays neutral on the strike itself and does not say whether the busmen are right. The only lightness comes at the end, where a small group's complaint about speed turns the students' good deed into a gentle joke.

这篇报道几乎全部用将来的说法写成：会发生什么、什么已经定好、人们打算做什么。这使它成为新闻怎样处理尚未发生之事的样板：说明计划、交代消息来源（他们表示、多数人认为），避免虚假的确定性（没人知道会持续多久）。作者对罢工本身保持中立，不说司机们对不对。唯一的轻松出现在结尾：一小撮人对车速的抱怨，把学生的好事变成了一个温和的玩笑。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The lesson presents the students as heroes, which is one side of a real argument. From the strikers' point of view, volunteers who keep the buses running weaken the strike and make an agreement less likely, which is why such help has often been called strike-breaking. From the public's side, people still need to get to work, hospital and school. Both views are fair, and good reporting, like this lesson, can describe the effects without choosing a side. Chinese readers who move to Canada or Britain will meet strikes far more often than at home, on buses, trains, at schools and at the post office, and it helps to know how they work: there is usually advance notice, a set start date, and talks that continue while the strike lasts.

这一课把学生写成英雄，这只是一场真实争论的一方。从罢工者的角度看，志愿者维持公交运转削弱了罢工，使协议更难达成，所以这种帮助常被称为「破坏罢工」。从公众的角度看，人们照样要上班、看病、上学。两种看法都有道理，而好的报道——就像这一课——可以描述影响而不选边。移居加拿大或英国的中国读者会比在国内频繁得多地遇到罢工：公交、火车、学校、邮局都有；了解它怎样运作很有帮助：通常会提前通知、定好开始日期，罢工期间谈判也一直在进行。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 2第 2 句 (begins开头 “The strike is …”)

  • be due to do: be planned to happen at a set time.be due to do：预定要做（已安排好的时间）。

Sentence 4第 4 句 (begins开头 “The busmen have …”)

  • The until clause uses the present for the future; is reached is passive.until 从句用一般现在时表将来；is reached：被动。
  • reach agreement: come to an agreement.reach agreement：达成协议。

Sentence 7第 7 句 (begins开头 “This will relieve …”)

  • relieve pressure on: reduce the load on; to some extent: partly.relieve pressure on：减轻……的压力。to some extent：在某种程度上。

Sentence 8第 8 句 (begins开头 “Meanwhile, a number …”)

  • a number of: some, with a plural noun; volunteer to do: offer to do.a number of：一些（接复数）。volunteer to do：自愿做。
  • while the strike lasts: the present for the future in a time clause.while the strike lasts：在罢工持续期间，一般现在时表将来。

Sentence 11第 11 句 (begins开头 “The students are …”)

  • be going to: something already planned; in two days' time: two days from now.be going to：已打算好的事。in … time：……之后。

Sentence 14第 14 句 (begins开头 “Only one or …”)

  • object that: protest that; the present perfect covers what has happened so far.object that：提出异议说。现在完成时说「到目前为止」的情况。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

Ways of talking about the future将来时的几种表达
will do is for predictions and decisions; be going to do is for plans or signs; be due to do is for a timetable; in time and condition clauses after until, while, before and if, use the present for the future.
will do 预测或决定；be going to do 已有打算或有迹象；be due to do 按时间表安排；时间、条件从句（until、while、before、if）里用一般现在时表将来。

The lesson uses all four: due to in sentence 2, going to in sentences 6, 11 and 12, will in sentences 3, 5 and 7, and the present after until and while in sentences 4 and 8.

本课：第 2 句 is due to；第 6、11、12 句 are going to；第 3、5、7 句 will；第 4、8 句的 until 和 while 从句用一般现在时。

  • The plane is due to land at 6.30.
  • I'll wait here until you come back.
so far with the present perfectso far 与现在完成时
so far, up to now, yet and already go with the present perfect to sum up results from the past up to now.
so far（到目前为止）、up to now、yet、already 等与现在完成时连用，说从过去到现在的结果。

Sentence 13 uses so far for the public's response up to now, and sentence 14 also counts complaints up to the present.

本课：第 13 句用 so far 说公众至今的反应；第 14 句的「只有一两个人提出异议」也是到目前为止。

  • So far we have sold two hundred tickets.
  • Nobody has complained up to now.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • X has voted to strike from next … X 已投票决定从下……起罢工
    The nurses have voted to strike from next month.
  • The … is due to begin on … ……定于……开始
    The concert is due to begin on Friday evening.
  • X will continue until … is reached. X 将持续到……达成为止
    The talks will continue until a deal is reached.
  • This should ease the load on … to some degree. 这在一定程度上应会减轻……的负担
    The new bridge should ease the load on the old one to some degree.
  • Up to now, the public has thanked X in … 到目前为止，公众在……里感谢了 X
    Up to now, the public has thanked the firemen in hundreds of cards.

Our model summary本站范文

Bus drivers will go on strike next Tuesday and will not return until they agree on pay and working conditions. The strike may last a week or more. Many car owners are going to give people free lifts, and some university students have offered to drive the buses, though they must first pass a special test. Travel will still be difficult. Most people have thanked the students, but one or two fear they will drive too fast.（77 词）
