Lesson 3 · Please Send Me a Card 请给我寄一张明信片

📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 3 “Please Send Me a Card”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 3《Please Send Me a Card》

B1🧠 15 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
The writer spends his summer holiday in Italy. He looks round museums and sits in parks, and a kind waiter teaches him some Italian and lends him a book, of which he understands nothing. Every day he means to write to his friends, and every day he does not. On his last day he decides to act, buys thirty-seven postcards first thing, and then spends the whole day in his room without writing one. The grammar is irregular past forms and the use of a few and a single.
作者去意大利度假：逛博物馆、在公园里坐坐，一位友好的服务员教了他几句意大利语，还借给他一本书，可他一个字也看不懂。整个假期他天天惦记着要给朋友寄明信片，却一张也没寄。最后一天他下了大决心，早早买了三十七张，结果在房间里待了一整天，一张也没写。本课学不规则动词过去式和 a few / a single 的用法。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. How many postcards did he send in the end?作者寄了多少张明信片？
  2. Why does he say postcards spoil his holidays?为什么作者说明信片「破坏」了他的假期？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander for New Concept English (Longman, 1967); an invented first-person story, not an account of a real trip.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为《新概念英语》（朗文，1967）自撰，是虚构的第一人称故事，不是真实游记。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

In the 1960s cheap package holidays were starting to take British people abroad in large numbers, and Italy and Spain were among the favourite places. Sending a picture card home was part of the ritual of being away: friends, neighbours and colleagues expected one, and a card with a sunny photo was a small proof that you had really gone. The joke depends on that social pressure. The writer is not short of time or money; he is short of the will to sit down and write the same few lines many times over. The large exact number he buys makes the point that he has a long list of people he feels he owes.

二十世纪六十年代，廉价的跟团游开始把大批英国人带出国，意大利和西班牙是最热门的去处之一。从外地寄一张风景明信片回家，是出门在外的一种仪式：朋友、邻居、同事都等着收，一张阳光照片的卡片就是你真的去过的小小证据。笑点就建立在这种社交压力上。作者不缺时间也不缺钱，缺的是坐下来把同样几句话写上几十遍的意志。他买的那个又大又精确的数字，说明他心里有一长串「欠着」的人。

✨ How it is written赏析

The first sentence gives the moral and the rest proves it, which is an unusual order for a story and makes the reader curious from the start. The middle part is a list of pleasant holiday things told in short past-tense sentences, so the trip itself seems easy and happy. Against that calm, one worry comes back day after day. The ending turns on a contrast in numbers: the big decision, the early start and the exact figure, and then nothing at all. The word single in the last sentence does the work that a long explanation could not. It is also worth seeing that the waiter's book and the unwritten cards make a small pair: words he cannot read, and words he cannot bring himself to write.

第一句先给出结论，其余部分来证明它——这在故事里是不常见的顺序，一开头就勾起读者好奇。中段用一串短短的过去时句子列出假期里的愉快事，于是这趟旅行显得轻松快乐。在这片平静上，一件心事天天回来。结尾靠一组数字的反差翻转：大决心、早起、精确的张数，然后是一张也没有。最后一句里那个「一张」，干了长篇解释也干不了的活。还值得留意：服务员借的书和没写的明信片凑成一对——一边是看不懂的字，一边是写不出来的字。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

Few people send paper postcards now; photos go straight to friends from the beach. Yet the feeling in the lesson has not gone away. Many people still find that sharing a trip turns into a duty, and that the pressure to report on a holiday can eat into the holiday itself. Chinese readers will know a close cousin of this, the habit of buying gifts for everyone back home, where a long list can fill the last day of a trip with shopping. The writer's failure is human and a little comforting: big plans made at the last minute often end like this, and the lesson invites us to laugh at him and at ourselves.

如今很少有人寄纸质明信片了，照片在海滩上就直接发给朋友。可课文里的那种感觉并没有消失。很多人仍然发现，分享旅行变成了一项任务，汇报假期的压力会吃掉假期本身。中国读者对它的近亲一定不陌生：给家里每个人带礼物，一张长长的名单能把旅行的最后一天全填成购物。作者的失败很人性，也有点让人宽慰——最后一刻才定下的大计划，往往就是这个结局；课文让我们笑他，也笑我们自己。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 1第 1 句 (begins开头 “Postcards always spoil …”)

Postcards always spoil my holidays.

  • The present simple for something that is always true, with always before the verb.一般现在时讲一贯如此的事，always 放在动词前。
  • spoil: to make worse. The story opens with its conclusion and then explains it.spoil：搞糟。开头一句先抛出结论，后面讲为什么。

Sentence 4第 4 句 (begins开头 “A friendly waiter …”)

  • friendly is an adjective even though it ends in -ly.friendly 是形容词（不是副词）。
  • teach sb sth: teach takes two objects, and taught is its past form.teach sb sth：教某人某事；taught 是 teach 的过去式。

Sentence 6第 6 句 (begins开头 “I read a …”)

  • The past form of read is spelt the same but sounds like red.read 的过去式拼写不变，读音变成 /red/。
  • not a word: nothing at all, more forceful than not anything.not … a word：一个字也不（懂）。比 not anything 更有力。

Sentence 13第 13 句 (begins开头 “but I did …”)

  • spend plus a length of time and a place or an -ing form. spent is its past.spend + 时间 + 地点/doing：在……度过。spent 是过去式。
  • not a single: not even one. The joke: thirty-seven cards bought, not one written.not a single：一个也没有。全课的笑点：买了三十七张，一张没写。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

Irregular past forms不规则动词的过去式
Many common verbs do not add -ed in the past and must be learnt one by one: go gives went, teach taught, lend lent, read read, think thought, buy bought, spend spent and make made.
很多常用动词的过去式不加 -ed，要一个一个记：go→went、teach→taught、lend→lent、read→read、think→thought、buy→bought、spend→spent、make→made。

Sentences 2, 4, 5, 7, 11 and 12 contain eight irregular past forms between them, while visited in sentence 3 is regular.

本课：第 2、4、5、7、11、12 句里一共有八个不规则过去式，第 3 句的 visited 是规则变化。

  • She bought a new coat and wore it the next day.
  • We thought the film was too long.
a few, few and a single: how manya few / few / a single：数量的轻重
a few with a countable noun means some, a small number, and sounds positive. few alone means hardly any and sounds negative. not a single means none at all, with emphasis. Uncountable nouns take a little or little.
a few + 可数名词 = 几个（肯定意味）；few = 很少，几乎没有（否定意味）；not a single = 一个也没有（强调）。不可数名词用 a little / little。

In sentences 4 and 6, a few means a small number; in sentence 13, not a single means not even one.

本课：第 4、6 句的 a few words / a few lines 是「几个、一些」；第 13 句 not a single card 是「一张也没」。

  • I know a few people here, so I don't feel lonely.
  • Few people came, so the hall looked empty.
  • There's a little milk left — enough for one cup.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • … always spoil(s) my … ……总是把我的……搞砸
    Exams always spoil my spring.
  • teach sb some + language 教某人一点某种语言
    My classmate taught me some Korean.
  • I made a big decision. 我下了一个大决心。
    At New Year I made a big decision: no more sugar.
  • spend the whole day + -ing / in … 一整天都在……（做）
    We spent the whole day at the beach.

Summary writing摘要写作

In no more than 50 words, tell the story of the holiday in Italy and the postcards.

用不超过 50 个词讲述作者在意大利的假期和明信片的事。

Points a good summary covers好摘要要覆盖的要点
  • Last summer he went to Italy, saw museums and sat in parks; a waiter taught him some Italian and lent him a book he could not read.去年夏天作者去意大利度假，逛博物馆、坐公园；一位服务员教他几句意大利语，还借他一本书，他看不懂。

Our model summary本站范文

Last summer I had a holiday in Italy. I saw museums and parks, and a waiter taught me some Italian. Every day I meant to send cards to my friends, but I never did. On my last day I bought thirty-seven, yet I wrote none of them.（47 词）
