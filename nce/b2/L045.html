Lesson 45 · A Clear Conscience 问心无愧

📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 45 “A Clear Conscience”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 45《A Clear Conscience》

B1🧠 16 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
Word soon went round the village that a lot of money had gone missing. The local butcher, Sam Benton, had dropped his wallet on his way to put his savings in the post office. He felt certain some villager had picked it up, yet nobody gave it back. Three months on, Sam found the wallet on his doorstep one morning, wrapped in newspaper, with half his money inside and a note admitting the finder was a thief, though only half a thief. Two months after that more cash arrived, with a note claiming the sender was now only a quarter a thief. Bit by bit the whole sum came back, and the final note said the writer was now completely honest. The grammar is the past perfect passive, must have been done, and while with an -ing form.
全村人很快都知道丢了一大笔钱。当地的屠夫萨姆·本顿把攒下的钱送去邮局存时，在路上丢了钱包。萨姆确信钱包一定是被村里哪个人捡到了，可一直没人还给他。三个月过去了，一天早上，萨姆在自家门口发现了钱包：用报纸包着，里面有他丢的一半的钱，还有一张字条：「是小偷，没错，不过只是百分之五十的小偷！」两个月后，又有一些钱寄给萨姆，附着另一张字条：「现在只是百分之二十五的小偷了！」就这样，萨姆的钱慢慢都还了回来。最后一张字条写着：「我现在百分之百诚实了！」本课学过去完成时被动 had been lost / had been wrapped、must have been done（对过去的肯定推测）和 while + 动名词。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. Why was Sam sure a villager had found his wallet?萨姆为什么确信钱包是被村里人捡到的？
  2. How did the thief's conscience change over time?这个「小偷」的良心是怎样一步步变化的？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander for New Concept English (Longman, 1967); Sam Benton and the village are invented.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为《新概念英语》（朗文，1967）自撰；萨姆·本顿和那个村子都是虚构的。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

In the Britain of the time, many working people kept their savings in the Post Office Savings Bank, which had branches in almost every village post office, so carrying cash there in person was normal. Village life also explains why the whole place soon knows about the loss and why Sam is sure the finder is local: in a small community, news travels fast and strangers are rare. Stories of money returned anonymously, years later, with an apology, appear in newspapers around the world; tax offices in several countries even keep what is sometimes called a conscience fund for people who send back money they owe.

那个年代的英国，很多劳动者把积蓄存在邮政储蓄银行，几乎每个村子的邮局都有它的网点，所以亲自带现金去存很正常。乡村生活也解释了为什么全村很快都知道丢钱的事，萨姆又为什么确信捡钱的是本地人：在小社区里，消息传得快，陌生人很少。多年后匿名还钱、附上道歉的故事在世界各地的报纸上都出现过；有几个国家的税务部门甚至设有所谓的「良心基金」，专门接收人们寄回的欠款。

✨ How it is written赏析

The story is told in a plain, almost official voice, with many passives: money had been lost, the wallet was not returned, it had been wrapped, more money was sent. The passive keeps the thief hidden, which is exactly right, because nobody in the story knows who he is. The real voice of the thief appears only in the notes, and the three percentages turn a crime into a slow, funny confession. The numbers fall and the honesty rises, so the reader can watch a conscience at work without the writer ever describing it.

故事用平实、近乎公文的口吻讲述，大量使用被动：钱被丢了，钱包没被归还，它已被包好，更多的钱被寄来。被动语态把小偷藏了起来，这恰到好处，因为故事里没人知道他是谁。小偷真正的声音只出现在字条里，三个百分比把一桩罪行变成了一场缓慢而好笑的忏悔。数字在下降，诚实在上升，读者看得见一颗良心在起作用，而作者从没描写过它。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The thief's change is believable because it is gradual. Few people go from wrong to right in one step; they move a part at a time, and each small return makes the next easier. The lesson also shows that a community where people know each other puts quiet pressure on everyone, which is the same idea behind the Chinese sense of face and village reputation. A modern reader might ask whether returning money makes up for the worry Sam suffered for months. Still, the story offers a hopeful view: people can choose to repair what they have done.

小偷的转变可信，因为它是渐进的。很少有人一步从错走到对；人们是一部分一部分地挪，每一次小小的归还都让下一次更容易。这一课也说明，在人人相识的社区里，每个人都承受着无声的压力——这和中国人讲的面子、乡里名声是同一个道理。现代读者也许会问：还了钱，能弥补萨姆几个月来的焦虑吗？不过故事给出了一个充满希望的看法：人可以选择弥补自己做过的事。

💬 Discuss and write讨论与写作
  1. Is a person who returns stolen money in parts really honest in the end? Give reasons.
    一个分几次还回偷来的钱的人，最终算真正诚实吗？说说理由。
  2. Write the thief's three notes again, adding one sentence to each that explains why he is paying back.
    重写小偷的三张字条，每张加一句话，解释他为什么还钱。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 1第 1 句 (begins开头 “The whole village …”)

  • learn that: to find out.learn that …：得知。
  • had been lost: the past perfect passive, because the loss came before people heard of it.had been lost：过去完成时被动，丢钱发生在村民得知之前。

Sentence 3第 3 句 (begins开头 “had lost his …”)

  • while plus an -ing form: the subject, Sam, is the one doing it.while + 动名词：在……的时候，主语是 Sam 本人。
  • the local butcher: a phrase in apposition telling us who Sam is.the local butcher：同位语，说明萨姆是谁。

Sentence 4第 4 句 (begins开头 “Sam was sure …”)

  • must have been done: a confident guess about the past, in the passive.must have been done：对过去的肯定推测（被动）：一定已被……。
  • was not returned: the past simple passive.was not returned：一般过去时被动。

Sentence 7第 7 句 (begins开头 “It had been …”)

  • had been wrapped up: the past perfect passive, done before Sam found it.had been wrapped up：过去完成时被动，萨姆发现之前已被包好。
  • half the money: half can come straight before the.half the money：一半的钱，half 后可直接跟 the。

Sentence 13第 13 句 (begins开头 “In time, all …”)

  • in time: after a while, in the end.in time：最终，经过一段时间之后。
  • was paid back: the past simple passive.was paid back：一般过去时被动。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

must have done and must have been done: certain guesses about the pastmust have done / must have been done：对过去的肯定推测
must have plus the past participle means, from the evidence, something surely happened. The passive is must have been plus the past participle. For a negative guess use can't have done.
must have + 过去分词：（根据证据）过去一定……了；被动 must have been + 过去分词。否定推测用 can't have done。

In sentence 4 must is followed by a passive perfect form: Sam did not see it, he only judged that someone surely had it.

本课：第 4 句 must 后接被动完成式——萨姆没亲眼看见，只是推断钱包一定被人捡走了。

  • The ground is wet. It must have rained last night.
  • The window is broken. It must have been hit by a ball.
The past perfect passive: had been done过去完成时被动：had been done
had been plus the past participle means it had already been done to something before a certain past time. Stories use it to show what was done to things earlier on.
had been + 过去分词：在过去某时之前已经被……。讲故事时用来交代「之前已经发生在某物身上的事」。

Sentence 1 says the money was gone before the village heard of it, and sentence 7 says the wallet was already wrapped in newspaper when Sam found it.

本课：第 1 句说钱在全村得知之前就丢了；第 7 句说钱包被发现时早已用报纸包好。

  • When I got home, the dinner had been cooked.
  • The road had been closed before the storm began.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • The whole … soon learnt that … 整个……很快都知道了……
    The whole school soon heard that the head teacher was leaving.
  • X had lost his … while taking … to … X 在把……送到……的路上丢了……
    She lost her keys while walking the dog to the park.
  • X was sure that … must have been … X 确信……一定被……了
    I was certain my bike must have been taken by a neighbour.
  • X months went by; then one day … 几个月过去了，有一天……
    Two years went by; then one evening the phone rang.
  • In time, all … was … in this way. 就这样，……最终全都……
    Little by little, the whole loan was repaid this way.

Our model summary本站范文

Sam Benton, the village butcher, lost his wallet with all his savings in it. He was sure a villager had found it, but it was not returned. Three months later he found it outside his door, wrapped in newspaper, with half the money and a note from a 'fifty per cent thief'. Two months after that more money arrived, and in the end it was all returned. The last note said the thief was now fully honest.（77 词）
