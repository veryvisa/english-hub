Lesson 17 · Always Young 永葆青春

📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 17 “Always Young”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 17《Always Young》

B1🧠 18 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
Jennifer, the writer's aunt, earns her living on the stage. She is thirty-five at the very least, yet she often plays young girls on stage. In her next play she will be seventeen, dressed in vivid red with black stockings; in a play last year she wore short socks and an orange dress. Ask her age and she always replies that being grown up must be dreadful. The grammar is the two meanings of must, guessing and duty, together with have to.
作者的姑妈詹妮弗是个演员，少说也有三十五岁了，却常在舞台上演小姑娘。下一出新戏里她要演十七岁的女孩，穿鲜红的裙子和黑色长袜；去年另一出戏里她穿的是短袜和橘色裙子。谁要问她多大，她总是回答：「亲爱的，长大成人一定很可怕吧！」本课学 must 的两种意思（推测「一定是」和义务「必须」）以及 have to。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. Why will Aunt Jennifer never say how old she is?詹妮弗姑妈为什么从不告诉别人自己的年龄？
  2. How is her answer both a way out and a joke?她的回答为什么既是回避、又是玩笑？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander for New Concept English (Longman, 1967); Aunt Jennifer and her plays are invented.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为《新概念英语》（朗文，1967）自撰；詹妮弗姑妈和她演的戏都是虚构的。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

Older actors playing much younger characters was ordinary in the theatre of the time and long before it. On stage, distance and lighting hide a great deal, and a skilled performer with the right voice and movement can be believed at seventeen by an audience twenty rows back. Theatre had its own vocabulary of terms of endearment as well: darling was so common among stage people that it became a joke about them, a word used warmly to everyone because it saves remembering names. The writer gives his aunt exactly this word, so a reader of the sixties would have heard the actress in her before she said anything else.

年纪大的演员演小得多的角色，在当时乃至更早的剧场里都很平常。舞台上，距离和灯光能遮掉很多东西；一个嗓音和身段都对的演员，坐在二十排外的观众会相信她就是十七岁。剧场也有自己的一套亲昵称呼：darling 在演艺圈里用得太多，以至于成了取笑演员的笑话——对谁都热情地叫一声，省得记名字。作者偏偏让姑妈用这个词，所以六十年代的读者在她说别的话之前，就已经听出她是个演员了。

✨ How it is written赏析

The whole lesson is a joke about must, and the joke is in the grammar. The writer's must in the second sentence is a guess, the kind of polite estimate a nephew makes about an aunt. The must in the play is a duty laid on her by the costume. Her own must at the end is a guess again, but she uses it to put herself on the other side of the line, among those who have not yet grown up. One small modal does three jobs, and the reader who notices that has learnt the grammar point without a rule. The detail of the clothes, red and black now, orange and short socks before, does the same work as a photograph: it shows how young she has to look.

整课是一个关于 must 的笑话，笑点就在语法里。第 2 句作者的 must 是推测，是外甥对姑妈年龄的客气估计。剧里的 must 是义务，是戏服加在她身上的要求。结尾她自己的 must 又是推测，可她用它把自己放到了界线的另一边——放进那些还没长大的人里。一个小小的情态动词干了三份活，留意到这一点的读者，不用背规则就学会了这个语法点。衣服的细节——现在是红配黑，以前是橘色加短袜——起的作用像一张照片：让你看到她得显得多年轻。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

Asking a woman her age is still widely treated as rude in English-speaking countries, and the lesson relies on that. In many Chinese settings the question is asked far more freely, often as a way to work out how to address someone, so the joke can need explaining. There is also a sharper point that the lesson does not make. Actresses have long complained that good parts for women dry up after forty while men go on playing leading roles much later; Jennifer's dodge is funny, but it is also a defence of her working life. A useful habit for learners is to notice which questions are polite in one culture and awkward in another, and to have a light answer ready, as she does.

在英语国家，问女性年龄至今普遍被认为不礼貌，本课就建立在这一点上。在很多中国场合，这个问题问得随便得多，常常是为了弄清该怎么称呼对方，所以这个笑话可能需要解释。课文还有一层没有点破的意思：女演员长期抱怨，过了四十岁好角色就越来越少，而男演员要晚得多才不演主角；詹妮弗的回避很好笑，但也是在保护自己的饭碗。对学习者有用的习惯是：留意哪些问题在一种文化里是礼貌的、在另一种文化里却尴尬，并且像她一样，备好一个轻松的回答。

    哪些问题在中国问没关系、在英语里却尴尬，或者反过来？举三个，并说明原因。

    用四句话写一个你认识的人：两次用 must 表推测，两次用 must 表义务。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 2第 2 句 (begins开头 “She must be …”)

  • must be: a confident guess, not a duty. The writer is sure, though he has not seen her birth certificate.must be：推测，「一定是」，不是「必须是」。说话人有把握但没亲眼看过出生证明。
  • at least: not less than, placed before the number.at least：至少，放在数字前。

Sentence 3第 3 句 (begins开头 “In spite of …”)

  • in spite of this: this points back to her age in the sentence before. in spite of takes a noun or pronoun.in spite of this：尽管如此，this 指上一句她的年龄。in spite of 后接名词或代词。
  • appear as: perform in the part of.appear as：以……的角色出场。

Sentence 4第 4 句 (begins开头 “Jennifer will have …”)

  • will have to: must for the future. must has no future form, so English uses will have to.will have to：将来「必须」。must 没有将来时，所以用 will have to。
  • take part in: be one of those doing something.take part in：参加。

Sentence 10第 10 句 (begins开头 “she always answers, …”)

  • it is the empty subject and the real subject is to be grown up. must is again a guess.it must be terrible to …：it 是形式主语，真正的主语是 to be grown up；must 又是推测。
  • She speaks as if she herself were still a child. It dodges the question and sounds like a line from a play.她这么说等于暗示自己还没长大，这是回避，也是演员的台词感。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

Two meanings of must: a sure guess and a dutymust 的两种意思：推测与义务
First, must be or must do can be a confident guess, meaning it is surely true; the negative of this guess is can't be. Second, must do can be a duty the speaker feels; its negative mustn't means it is not allowed. Context tells you which is meant.
① must be / must do：表示有把握的推测，「一定是」，否定用 can't be。② must do：表示说话人认为必须做的事，否定 mustn't（禁止）。看上下文分辨。

In sentences 2 and 10 must is a guess; in sentence 6 it is a duty, because the part requires the red dress.

本课：第 2 句、第 10 句的 must 是推测；第 6 句的 must 是义务（剧中必须穿红裙）。

  • The lights are off. They must be out.
  • You must wear a helmet on a building site.
  • That can't be Tom; he is in Paris this week.
have to: duty in other tenseshave to：义务的各种时态
must has only a present form. Use had to for the past, will have to for the future and have had to for the perfect. have to often shows a rule from outside.
must 只有现在时；过去用 had to，将来用 will have to，完成时用 have had to。have to 常表示外界的要求。

Sentence 4 uses will have to for the future and sentence 8 uses had to for the past.

本课：第 4 句 will have to（将来），第 8 句 had to（过去）。

  • Last winter we had to walk to school in deep snow.
  • You will have to show your passport at the border.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • X must be at least … years old. X 少说也有……岁了。
    Our teacher must be at least sixty years old.
  • In spite of this, X often … as … 尽管如此，X 常以……的身份……
    In spite of this, he often travels as a student.
  • This time, X will be … 这一次，X 要演/当……
    This time, she will be the captain of the team.
  • It must be terrible to … ……一定很可怕吧
    It must be terrible to lose your keys in the snow.

Summary writing摘要写作

In no more than 50 words, describe Aunt Jennifer and how she deals with questions about her age.

用不超过 50 个词描述詹妮弗姑妈，并写出她怎样应付「你多大了」这个问题。

Our model summary本站范文

Aunt Jennifer is an actress who is surely over thirty-five, yet she often plays young girls. Soon she will be seventeen in a new play, dressed in red. Whenever anyone asks her age, she says, with a smile, that being an adult must be dreadful.（45 词）
