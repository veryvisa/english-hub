Lesson 67 · Volcanoes 火山

📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 67 “Volcanoes”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 67《Volcanoes》

B1🧠 17 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
Haroun Tazieff spent his working life studying live volcanoes and deep caves all over the globe. In 1948 he made the journey to central Africa, near Lake Kivu, and watched a newly born volcano, to which he later gave the name Kituro. He camped right beside it as it blew, and took some fine pictures, but had to leave in a hurry when molten rock began flowing his way and nearly cut him off. Two days later, with the mountain calm, he went back and lowered himself into the crater to photograph it and take its temperature. Such risks were part of his job, and no living person knew more about volcanoes. This is a short portrait of a scientist. The grammar is spend time doing, manage to do, so that for purpose, and more ... than any.
哈龙·塔齐耶夫一辈子都在研究世界各地的活火山和深洞。1948 年他去刚果的基伍湖观察一座新火山，后来把它命名为基图罗。火山猛烈喷发时，他把营地扎得离它很近，拍了不少精彩照片，却没法久留：一条熔岩河朝他流过来，眼看要把他团团围住，他总算及时逃了出来。两天后火山安静了，他又回来，这次爬进了火山口拍照、测温度。他常常这样冒生命危险，所以他对活火山的了解比当今任何人都多。本课是一篇人物小传，学 spend time doing、manage to do、so that 目的状语从句和比较级 more … than any。

  2. Why could he not stay near the volcano the first time?第一次他为什么不能在火山旁待太久？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Unsigned and written for the course about a real scientist; the account seems to come from Tazieff's own popular books about the Kituro eruption.

本课未署名、为教材自撰，写的是一位真实的科学家；内容似乎取自塔齐耶夫自己写的关于基图罗喷发的科普书。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

Haroun Tazieff was born in Warsaw in 1914 and became a Belgian and later a French citizen, so calling him simply Polish is only part of the story. He trained as an agricultural engineer and a geologist, and his books and films did more than anything else to make volcanoes popular in France. The Kituro eruption he describes took place in 1948 in what was then the Belgian Congo, near Lake Kivu. Later in life he became a public figure and even served as a junior minister for major natural and industrial risks in the nineteen-eighties. He was also a controversial one: in 1976 he argued that a threatened eruption on the island of Guadeloupe was not dangerous, and he was right, but other experts disagreed sharply. He died in 1998.

哈龙·塔齐耶夫 1914 年生于华沙，后来先后入了比利时籍和法国籍，所以只说他是「波兰科学家」不算完整。他学过农业工程和地质学，他的书和电影比什么都更让火山在法国家喻户晓。他写的这次基图罗喷发发生在 1948 年当时的比属刚果，靠近基伍湖。晚年他成了公众人物，二十世纪八十年代还当过主管重大自然与工业风险的国务秘书。他也颇有争议：1976 年他认为瓜德罗普岛上一次可能的火山喷发并不危险，后来证明他是对的，但其他专家当时与他争执激烈。他于 1998 年去世。

✨ How it is written赏析

The text is built from a run of verbs of effort: was able to, managed to, managed to escape, was able to return, managed to climb. Together they tell us, without any adjectives of praise, that every step was hard and that he succeeded anyway. The danger is described in one plain image, a river of liquid rock, and the writer lets the reader supply the fear. The last two sentences step back from the story to the whole career, moving from the past simple to the present perfect, which turns one adventure into a pattern of a life.

这篇课文由一连串表示「费力」的动词搭成：was able to、managed to、managed to escape、was able to return、managed to climb。合在一起，不用任何夸奖的形容词就告诉我们：每一步都很难，他却都做成了。危险只用了一个朴素的画面，「一条液体岩石的河」，恐惧留给读者自己补上。最后两句从故事退到整个职业生涯，从一般过去时转到现在完成时，把一次冒险变成了一生的模式。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The lesson admires courage, and it is right to. But its question, why risk your life like this, deserves a modern answer. Field work on volcanoes is still dangerous: in 1991 three well-known volcano scientists, including the French couple Katia and Maurice Krafft, were killed on Mount Unzen in Japan. Today much of the measuring is done by drones, satellites and instruments left on the mountain, partly so that people need not stand where Tazieff stood. Courage still matters, but so does knowing when a machine can take the risk instead. That balance between bravery and good sense is worth discussing in any job.

课文赞美勇气，这没错。但它提出的问题，「为什么要这样冒生命危险」，值得一个现代的回答。火山野外工作至今依然危险：1991 年，三位知名火山学家在日本云仙岳遇难，其中包括法国的克拉夫特夫妇。如今很多测量交给无人机、卫星和留在山上的仪器，部分原因就是让人不必站在塔齐耶夫站过的地方。勇气依然重要，知道什么时候可以让机器替人冒险也同样重要。勇敢与理智之间的分寸，在任何职业里都值得讨论。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 1第 1 句 (begins开头 “Haroun Tazieff, the …”)

  • spend time doing: the -ing form follows, not to do.spend time doing：花时间做某事，doing 不用 to do。
  • has spent: present perfect, since he was still alive and working when this was written.has spent：现在完成时，写这篇时他还在世、还在研究。

Sentence 2第 2 句 (begins开头 “In 1948, he …”)

  • to observe: an infinitive of purpose.to observe：不定式表目的。
  • name something plus a name: the name follows the object directly.name sth + 名字：给……命名，名字直接跟在宾语后。

Sentence 3第 3 句 (begins开头 “Tazieff was able …”)

  • was able to: managed on one particular occasion, more exact here than could.was able to：过去具体一次「做成了」，比 could 更准确。
  • while with the past continuous gives the background action.while + 过去进行时：背景动作。

Sentence 6第 6 句 (begins开头 “It threatened to …”)

  • threaten to do: seem likely to do; the subject is the lava.threaten to do：眼看要……，主语是熔岩。
  • manage to do: succeed in something hard; just in time: with very little time to spare.manage to do：设法做成了（有难度）。just in time：刚好来得及。

Sentence 8第 8 句 (begins开头 “This time, he …”)

  • so that with could: a clause of purpose.so that + could：目的状语从句，「为了能……」。

Sentence 10第 10 句 (begins开头 “He has been …”)

  • more ... than any: a comparative used with the force of a superlative.more … than any …：比任何……都多，用比较级表达最高级的意思。
  • alive after the noun: now living.alive 放在名词后：当今在世的。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

could, was able to and managed to for the pastcould / was able to / managed to：说过去的能力和做成
could describes a general ability in the past. For one particular success, use was able to or managed to, not could. In the negative, couldn't is fine for one occasion.
could 说过去一般的能力（会做）；说过去某一次具体做成了，用 was able to 或 managed to，不用 could。否定时 couldn't 也可以说某一次没做成。

Sentences 3, 4, 6 and 8 keep using was able to and managed to, stressing how hard each success was; sentence 4 also has could not for what he failed to do.

本课：第 3、4、6、8 句连用 was able to 和 managed to，强调每一次都很难；第 4 句另有 could not 说没法久留。

  • She could swim when she was five.
  • The fire was big, but everyone managed to get out.
A comparative with the force of a superlative用比较级表达最高级：more … than any (other)
A comparative plus than any other plus a singular noun has the meaning of a superlative. Add other when the subject belongs to the group being compared.
比较级 + than any other + 单数名词 = 最高级；比较对象包括自己时要加 other，不包括时（如 than any man alive）可以不加。

The last sentence uses it to say no living person knows more.

本课：最后一句用这个结构说他是当今最了解活火山的人。

  • Tokyo is larger than any other city in Japan.
  • She works harder than anyone else in the office.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • X has spent his lifetime studying … X 一生都在研究……
    My grandfather has spent his lifetime collecting old maps.
  • X went to … to observe a … which he later named … X 去……观察一个……，后来把它命名为……
    She went to the coast to study a new bird which she later named Blue Wing.
  • Though X managed to …, X could not … for very long. 虽然 X 设法……，但没法……太久
    Though I managed to sleep, I could not rest for very long.
  • X threatened to … but Y managed to … just in time. X 眼看要……，但 Y 及时设法……
    The wave threatened to sink the boat, but the crew managed to reach land just in time.
  • X has told us more about … than any … X 让我们对……的了解比任何……都多
    This book has told us more about the Silk Road than any other.

Our model summary本站范文

Haroun Tazieff has spent his life studying volcanoes. In 1948 he went to the Congo to watch a newly formed volcano, later called Kituro. He camped very near it while it erupted and took some fine pictures, but a stream of hot molten rock nearly cut him off, and he escaped just in time. Two days later, when it was quiet, he climbed into its crater to photograph it and measure the heat.（73 词）
