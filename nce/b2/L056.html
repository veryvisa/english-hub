Lesson 56 · Faster Than Sound 比音速还快！

📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 56 “Faster Than Sound”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 56《Faster Than Sound》

B1🧠 16 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
Every year there is a race just for very old cars. Last year plenty of them took part, and the crowd was buzzing before the start. Among the finest-looking was a Silver Ghost made by Rolls-Royce, while the oddest was a three-wheeled Benz from 1885, the oldest car of all. The race began with a series of loud bangs. Many of the cars stopped along the route, and some drivers spent longer lying beneath their machines than sitting in them. A handful made it to the end. The winner reached forty miles an hour, well ahead of the rest, and as it raced down the final hill its driver struggled to bring it to a halt. Everyone loved the day: it was nothing like a modern race, yet just as thrilling. The grammar is a race is held, one of the most, Built in, more ... than, and no less ... than.
每年都举行一次老爷车赛。去年报名的车很多，开赛前场面非常热闹。最漂亮的车之一是一辆劳斯莱斯「银魅」，最特别的是一辆只有三个轮子的奔驰，造于 1885 年，是参赛车里最老的。一阵震耳的爆响之后，比赛开始了。很多车半路抛锚，有些车手钻在车底下的时间比坐在车里还长！不过也有几辆车跑完了全程。冠军车的速度达到每小时四十英里，远远快过对手；比赛结束时它冲下山坡，车手费了好大劲才把它停住。这场比赛给大家带来了极大的乐趣，和现代赛车很不一样，但同样激动人心。本课学被动语态 a race is held、最高级 one of the most …、过去分词短语 Built in …、比较级 more … than 和 no less … than。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. Why were some drivers lying beneath their cars so much of the time?为什么说有的车手「在车底下比在车里的时间还多」？
  2. Is the title meant seriously?题目「比音速还快」是认真的吗？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander for New Concept English (Longman, 1967); the race recalls real veteran car runs, but this event is invented.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为《新概念英语》（朗文，1967）撰写；比赛让人想起真实的老爷车大赛，但这一场是虚构的。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

The most famous real event of this kind is the London to Brighton Veteran Car Run, held every November since the nineteen-twenties for cars built before nineteen hundred and five. It is officially not a race, and the cars only need to finish within a set time, but it draws large crowds. Karl Benz's three-wheeled Motorwagen of eighteen eighty-five is usually called the first true petrol car, and the Rolls-Royce Silver Ghost, first built in nineteen hundred and six, earned a name for being smooth and reliable. Forty miles an hour was a high speed for cars of that age.

现实中最有名的此类活动是「伦敦—布莱顿老爷车大游行」，自二十世纪二十年代起每年十一月举行，只限一九〇五年以前造的车。官方说它不是比赛，车只要在规定时间内跑完就行，但每年都吸引大批观众。卡尔·本茨一八八五年造的三轮「奔驰一号」通常被称为第一辆真正的汽油汽车；劳斯莱斯「银魅」一九〇六年首产，以平稳可靠闻名。对那个年代的车来说，每小时四十英里已经是高速了。

✨ How it is written赏析

The title is a deliberate joke. It promises a sound-barrier record, and the text quietly delivers a top speed that a modern bicycle racer could match. In between, the writer sketches the day with a few strong pictures: a beautiful car, a strange car, a start made of bangs, drivers lying in the road. The best line is the comparison of time spent under the cars with time spent inside them, which turns a list of breakdowns into comedy. The ending is generous rather than mocking, insisting that this slow race was just as thrilling as a fast one.

标题是故意开的玩笑。它许诺的是突破音障的纪录，正文却平静地给出一个现代自行车赛手都追得上的最高时速。中间作者用几幅鲜明的画面勾勒这一天：一辆漂亮的车、一辆古怪的车、在爆响声中开始的比赛、躺在路上的车手。最妙的一句是拿车底下的时间和车里面的时间相比，把一串抛锚的事变成了喜剧。结尾是宽厚而不是嘲讽，坚持说这场慢吞吞的比赛和快速比赛一样激动人心。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The lesson asks what makes something exciting. It is not only speed or record-breaking; it is uncertainty, effort and the pleasure of seeing old things still working. People today love vintage cars, steam trains and old radios for the same reason: they show how far we have come and how clever early engineers were with simple tools. For Chinese readers, the story may recall how fast China's own roads moved from bicycles to cars within one lifetime. The race reminds us that progress is easier to feel when we can compare the old with the new side by side.

这一课在问：什么让一件事激动人心？不只是速度或破纪录，还有不确定、努力，以及看到老东西还能跑起来的乐趣。今天的人喜爱老爷车、蒸汽火车和老收音机，也是同样的原因：它们让我们看到自己走了多远，看到早期工程师用简单工具做出了多么聪明的东西。对中国读者来说，故事也许会让人想起中国的道路如何在一代人之内从自行车时代进入汽车时代。这场比赛提醒我们：把旧的和新的并排放在一起，进步才看得最清楚。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 1第 1 句 (begins开头 “Once a year, …”)

  • be held: the passive, the race is organised by someone.be held：被动语态，比赛「被举行」。
  • once a year: one time every year.once a year：每年一次。

Sentence 3第 3 句 (begins开头 “One of the …”)

  • one of the plus superlative plus plural noun: among the most.one of the + 最高级 + 复数名词：最……的之一。

Sentence 5第 5 句 (begins开头 “Built in 1885, …”)

  • Built in: a past participle phrase, the car was built.Built in …：过去分词短语作状语，车是「被造的」。
  • taking part: an -ing form after the noun, meaning which was taking part.taking part：现在分词作后置定语，= which was taking part。

Sentence 7第 7 句 (begins开头 “Many of the …”)

  • spend time with a place: be in a place for that time.spend time + 地点：在某处度过时间。
  • more ... than: a comparison, with they spent time left out after than.more … than …：比较；than in them 省去了 they spent time。

Sentence 12第 12 句 (begins开头 “It was very …”)

  • be different from: not the same as.be different from：与……不同。
  • no less exciting: just as exciting.no less exciting：一样激动人心，= just as exciting。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

one of the plus superlative plus plural nounone of the + 最高级 + 复数名词
one of the most plus adjective plus plural noun, or one of the plus -est plus plural noun, means among the most. As a subject it takes a singular verb, because the subject is one.
one of the most + 形容词 + 复数名词（或 one of the + -est + 复数名词）：最……的之一；作主语时谓语用单数，因为主语是 one。

In sentence 3 the Rolls-Royce is among the best-looking, and the verb is singular.

本课：第 3 句——那辆劳斯莱斯是最漂亮的车之一，句子谓语用 was。

  • She is one of the best players in the team.
  • One of the oldest buildings in town is the church.
no less: just as much
no less plus adjective, with or without than, means just as much; no more ... than means just as little.
no less + 形容词 + (than)：不比……差，= just as；no more … than 则是「和……一样不」。

In sentence 12 the old-car race is different from a modern race but every bit as thrilling.

本课：第 12 句——老爷车赛和现代赛车不一样，但一点也不逊色。

  • The second film was no less funny than the first.
  • Walking is no less healthy than running.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • Once a year, a … is held for … 每年为……举办一次……
    Once a year, a fair is held for local farmers.
  • The crowd grew more and more excited before … ……之前人群越来越兴奋
    The crowd grew more and more excited before the bride arrived.
  • One of the most … was … 最……的之一是……
    One of the most popular stalls was a cake shop.
  • Built in …, it is the oldest … 建于……，它是最古老的……
    Built in 1820, it is the oldest school in the town.
  • It was very different from … but no less … 它和……很不一样，但同样……
    The village wedding was very different from city weddings but no less joyful.

Points a good summary covers好摘要要覆盖的要点
  • A race for old cars is held every year; last year many cars entered and there was great excitement.每年都有一场老爷车赛，去年参赛的车很多，开赛前很热闹。
  • The most unusual was an 1885 Benz with three wheels, the oldest car there.最特别的是一辆 1885 年造的三轮奔驰，是最老的车。

Our model summary本站范文

Every year there is a race for old cars. Last year many entered, and the crowd was very excited. The oddest car was a three-wheeled Benz built in 1885, the oldest in the race. After many loud bangs the cars set off, but lots of them broke down, and drivers spent ages underneath them. Only a few finished. The winner reached forty miles an hour. Everyone enjoyed this race as much as a modern one.（75 词）
