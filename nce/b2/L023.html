Lesson 23 · A New House 新居

📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 23 “A New House”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 23《A New House》

B1🧠 15 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
Yesterday the writer heard from a sister who lives in Nigeria: she plans to visit England next year. If she does, a surprise is waiting. The family now lives in a lovely new house in the country. Building began before the sister went abroad, and the house was finished five months ago. The writer has replied that she can stay with them. It has big rooms and a fine garden, and it is so modern that some people find it odd; it is probably the only one of its kind in the area. The grammar is how several tenses work together in one text, and reported speech.
作者昨天收到住在尼日利亚的姐姐来信，说她明年要来英格兰。她要是来了，会大吃一惊：作者一家现在住在乡下一栋漂亮的新房子里。房子在姐姐出国前就开工了，五个月前完工。作者回信说姐姐可以来住。房子有很多大房间，还有一个可爱的花园；它非常现代，所以有些人看着觉得怪，大概是这一带唯一的现代房子。本课学多种时态在一篇里的配合（一般过去、过去完成、现在进行、将来）和间接引语。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. What makes the new house special?这栋新房子特别在哪里？
  2. Why will the sister be surprised when she comes?姐姐来了以后为什么会吃惊？

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander for New Concept English (Longman, 1967); the family, the house and the district are invented.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为《新概念英语》（朗文，1967）自撰；这家人、房子和那一带都是虚构的。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

The lesson's sister lives in Nigeria, which had become independent from Britain in 1960, only a few years before the book appeared. Many British people were still working there as teachers, engineers, doctors and in business, and families scattered between Britain and its former colonies kept in touch by airmail letters that could take a week or more. At home, the sixties saw a great deal of new building, much of it in a plain modern style of flat roofs, large windows and open plans, and in villages full of old brick and stone houses a single modern one could look very out of place. Arguments about whether new buildings should match the old are still common in English planning.

课文里的姐姐住在尼日利亚，那里 1960 年脱离英国独立，距本书出版只有几年。当时仍有很多英国人在那里当教师、工程师、医生或做生意，分散在英国和前殖民地之间的家庭靠航空信保持联系，一封信要走一周甚至更久。在英国本土，六十年代新建了大量房屋，很多是平屋顶、大窗户、开放式布局的朴素现代风格；在满是老砖房、石头房的村子里，一栋现代房子会显得格格不入。新建筑该不该和老建筑协调，至今仍是英国规划里常见的争论。

✨ How it is written赏析

The lesson is a letter about a letter, and it is a neat exercise in keeping time straight. Yesterday's letter, the sister's plans for next year, the building that began before she left and ended five months ago, and the house they live in now are all placed exactly by their tenses. A careful reader can draw a timeline from the grammar alone. The description of the house is short and generous, rooms and garden and modern style, and then the writer adds the one sentence that turns pride into a small joke: some people think it looks strange. The last line, a confident guess with must, shows that she is quite pleased to be the odd one out.

这课是「一封信里讲另一封信」，也是一个把时间理清楚的漂亮练习。昨天的信、姐姐明年的打算、她走之前开工、五个月前完工的房子、现在住的家——每件事都靠时态放在准确的位置上。细心的读者光凭语法就能画出一条时间线。对房子的描写简短而大方：房间、花园、现代风格；然后作者加了一句，把自豪变成一个小玩笑：有些人觉得它看着怪。最后一句用 must 作有把握的推测，看得出她挺得意自己是与众不同的那一家。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

Being away from home for years and coming back to find your family's life changed is a common experience for anyone with relatives overseas, and many Chinese families know it well. A child abroad hears about a new flat in letters or messages, and only on the visit home sees how different everything is. The lesson treats this lightly, as a happy surprise, but it hints at something real: the people who leave keep an old picture of home, while home moves on without them. The writer's choice to save the surprise rather than describe it all in her letter is also worth thinking about. Some news is better seen than told.

离家多年，回来发现家人的生活已经变了——这是任何有亲人在海外的人都熟悉的经历，很多中国家庭也很清楚。孩子在国外从信件或消息里听说家里换了新房，直到回家探亲才看到一切变得多么不同。课文把这件事写得很轻松，是个开心的惊喜，但也暗示了真实的一点：离开的人留着家的旧模样，而家在他们不在的时候继续变化。作者选择把惊喜留到见面，而不是在信里全说出来，这一点也值得想一想：有些消息，看到比听到好。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 3第 3 句 (begins开头 “In her letter, …”)

  • said that ... would: reported speech, with her will moved back to would.said that … would：间接引语，原话 will 变成 would。
  • next year stays the same because that year has not yet come.next year 没变，因为说话时「明年」还没到。

Sentence 4第 4 句 (begins开头 “If she comes, …”)

  • A real conditional: if with the present, will in the main clause.真实条件句：if + 一般现在时，主句 will。
  • get a surprise: be surprised.get a surprise：大吃一惊。

Sentence 6第 6 句 (begins开头 “Work on it …”)

  • had begun: the past perfect, because building started before she went.had begun：过去完成时，开工在姐姐离开之前。
  • work on it: the building of the house.work on it：（房子的）施工。

Sentence 11第 11 句 (begins开头 “It must be …”)

  • look strange to sb: seem odd to them.look strange to sb：在某人看来很怪。
  • must be: a confident guess.must be：有把握的推测。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

Several tenses in one text一篇里的多种时态
Choose each tense by when the thing happens: the past simple for yesterday, the past perfect for something earlier still, the present simple or continuous for now, and will for the future. The tense follows the time, not the order of the sentences.
叙述时按「事情发生在什么时候」选时态：昨天的事用一般过去时，更早的用过去完成时，现在的状态用现在进行时或一般现在时，以后的用将来时。时态跟着时间走，不跟着句子顺序走。

Sentences 1, 6, 5, 4 and 7 use five different time frames between them.

本课：第 1 句 had（昨天）；第 6 句 had begun（更早）；第 5 句 are now living（现在）；第 4 句 will get（将来）；第 7 句 was completed（五个月前）。

  • I met her last week. She had lived in Spain before that. Now she is working here, and next month she will start a course.
Reported speech: said that and told someone that间接引语：said that … / told sb that …
When the reporting verb is past, the reported words move back: will to would, can to could, is to was. tell must have a person after it; say does not take one directly.
主句是过去时时，转述的内容时态后移：will → would，can → could，is → was。tell 后要跟人，say 后不直接跟人。

Sentence 3 reports with said that and would; sentence 8 with told her that and could.

本课：第 3 句 said that she would，第 8 句 told her that she could。

  • He said, "I can help." → He said that he could help.
  • She told me, "I will call." → She told me that she would call.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • In her letter, X said that she would … X 在信里说她会……
    In her letter, Mum said that she would visit in May.
  • If X comes, X will get a surprise. X 要是来了，会大吃一惊。
    If Dad comes home early, he will get a surprise.
  • Work on X had begun before … ……之前 X 就已经开工了
    Work on the road had begun before we moved in.
  • It must be the only … in the … 它准是这一带唯一的……
    It must be the only bookshop in the village.
