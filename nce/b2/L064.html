Lesson 64 · The Channel Tunnel 英吉利海峡隧道

📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 64 “The Channel Tunnel”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 64《The Channel Tunnel》

B1🧠 18 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
In 1858 a French engineer reached England with a scheme for a tunnel of some twenty-one miles under the sea between the two countries. A platform halfway across would act as harbour and railway stop, and tall chimneys above the waves would bring in air. Two years later an Englishman offered something better: a pair of rail tunnels, in which each train would pull fresh air along after it. Work truly began forty-two years on, and the line would have been finished had Britain not been afraid of attack. People waited close to another century before it opened in 1994. This is a piece of engineering history. The grammar is the third conditional, suggest that ... should, serve as and put forward.
1858 年，一位法国工程师带着一份计划来到英国：在英吉利海峡下面挖一条 21 英里长的隧道，海峡中央建一个平台，既当港口又当火车站，再竖几根高烟囱通风。1860 年一个英国人提出更好的方案：修双轨隧道，火车开进去时会把新鲜空气带进来，通风问题就解决了。四十二年后隧道真的开工了，可要不是英国人怕被入侵，它早就修成了。世界又等了将近一百年，海峡隧道才在 1994 年正式开通。本课是一段工程史，学虚拟语气 If … had not …, would have done、suggest that … should、serve as 和 put forward。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. Why was the first tunnel never finished?第一条隧道为什么没有修成？
  2. How would a double tunnel solve the problem of fresh air?双轨隧道怎样解决通风问题？

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

The real history is longer and messier than the lesson's tidy dates. Albert Mathieu proposed a tunnel for horse coaches as early as 1802, and Thomé de Gamond spent decades on several designs, including bridges and floating tubes, before showing a tunnel plan in the eighteen-fifties and sixties. In the early eighteen-eighties a boring machine designed by Colonel Beaumont and Thomas English cut nearly two kilometres from each side before Parliament stopped it, largely because generals warned that an enemy could march through. A second start in the nineteen-seventies was dropped for lack of money. The present tunnel was begun in 1988; it is about fifty kilometres long, close to forty of them under the sea, and the official ceremony with the Queen and President Mitterrand is usually dated the sixth of May 1994, not March as the lesson says.

真实历史比课文整齐的年份长得多，也乱得多。早在 1802 年，阿尔贝·马蒂厄就提议修一条走马车的隧道；托梅·德·加蒙花了几十年设计过多种方案，包括桥和浮管，十九世纪五六十年代才拿出隧道计划。十九世纪八十年代初，博蒙上校和托马斯·英格利希设计的掘进机从两岸各挖了将近两公里，后来被议会叫停，主要是将军们警告说敌人可以从隧道里开过来。二十世纪七十年代的第二次开工又因缺钱放弃。现在的隧道 1988 年开工，全长约五十公里，其中将近四十公里在海底；有女王和密特朗总统出席的正式开通仪式一般记作 1994 年 5 月 6 日，而不是课文说的 3 月。

✨ How it is written赏析

The passage is a chain of plans, each one better than the last, and the grammar follows them: would for what a plan imagined, should for what a man proposed, and finally the third conditional for what history refused. That single conditional sentence carries the whole argument, because it explains the long gap without a separate paragraph about politics. The writer also uses numbers to build the feeling of waiting: dates, a length in miles, forty-two years, and almost a hundred more. The ending changes tone from the conditional to plain fact, finally connecting, so the reader feels the tunnel being completed.

这篇是一串计划，一个比一个好，语法也跟着走：would 说计划里的设想，should 说某人的提议，最后用第三种条件句说历史拒绝了什么。那一个条件句承担了全部论证：它解释了这么长的空档，却不用另写一段政治。作者还用数字营造等待的感觉：年份、英里数、四十二年，再加将近一百年。结尾从虚拟换成平直的事实，finally connecting，让读者感到隧道终于修成。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The lesson is a good example of how fear can matter as much as engineering. The technical problem of fresh air had been solved on paper long before the political problem of trust was solved. Read today, it also raises a modern question: Britain left the European Union in 2020, yet the tunnel still carries millions of passengers and much of its trade with Europe. Chinese readers might compare the long debates over the Hong Kong to Zhuhai to Macao bridge or the tunnel plans across the Qiongzhou Strait, where the hardest part was often deciding, not digging. Large projects are as much about agreement as about rock.

这一课很好地说明了恐惧和工程技术一样起作用。通风这个技术问题早在纸面上解决了，信任这个政治问题却晚得多才解决。今天读来，它还引出一个现代问题：英国 2020 年退出了欧盟，可隧道仍在运送数以百万计的乘客和大量对欧贸易。中国读者可以对比港珠澳大桥、琼州海峡隧道方案的长期论证：最难的往往是拍板，而不是开挖。大工程既关乎岩石，也关乎共识。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 4第 4 句 (begins开头 “This platform would …”)

  • would here describes what the plan imagined, a future seen from the past.would：过去看将来，说计划中的设想。
  • serve as: be used as.serve as：用作，起……作用。

Sentence 5第 5 句 (begins开头 “The tunnel would …”)

  • A second conditional for an imagined plan, with the passive were built.would + if 从句用过去式：对未发生之事的设想（第二种条件句），被动语态 were built。
  • well-ventilated: a compound adjective meaning with plenty of fresh air.well-ventilated：复合形容词，「通风良好的」。

Sentence 6第 6 句 (begins开头 “In 1860, a …”)

  • The passive of a phrasal verb keeps forward next to put.was put forward：短语动词的被动，介词 forward 跟着动词走。

Sentence 7第 7 句 (begins开头 “He suggested that …”)

  • suggest that ... should: should can be dropped, leaving the base form.suggest that sb/sth (should) do：建议……；should 可省略，动词用原形。

Sentence 10第 10 句 (begins开头 “If, at the …”)

  • A third conditional: the opposite of what really happened, which was that fear stopped the work.第三种条件句：与过去事实相反。事实是：英国人怕入侵，所以没修成。
  • would have been completed: the passive form.would have been completed：被动的虚拟。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

Unreal past: If ... had done, ... would have done与过去相反的虚拟：If … had done, … would have done
To talk about what did not happen in the past, use had plus the past participle in the if clause and would have plus the past participle in the main clause. Turn it back into fact as you read: the thing after if did not happen.
说过去没发生的事「要是……就会……」：if 从句用 had + 过去分词，主句用 would have + 过去分词。读的时候先把它翻成事实：实际上 if 里的事情没有发生。

Sentence 10 is the key to the lesson: it uses this pattern to give the real reason the first attempt stopped.

本课：第 10 句是全课的关键：用这个结构说出第一条隧道停工的真正原因。

  • If I had known you were ill, I would have visited you.
  • If it had not rained, we would have won the match.
Two ways to use suggestsuggest 的两种用法
Use suggest doing or suggest that someone (should) do. Never say suggest someone to do. When suggest means 'show' or 'hint', the that clause uses an ordinary tense.
suggest doing 或 suggest that sb (should) do，都表示「建议」；不能说 suggest sb to do。表示「暗示」时 that 从句用正常时态。

Sentence 7 uses suggest that ... should with a passive verb.

本课：第 7 句是 suggest that … should be built 的被动形式。

  • She suggested going to the beach.
  • The doctor suggested that he (should) rest for a week.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • In …, X arrived in … with a plan for … ……年，X 带着一项……的计划来到……
    In 2010, a young chef arrived in Vancouver with a plan for a noodle shop.
  • X would serve as … and … X 将用作……和……
    The hall would serve as a theatre and a sports centre.
  • A cheaper idea was put forward by … ……提出了一个更省钱的方案
    A cheaper idea was put forward by a student in the second year.
  • If, at the time, X had not …, Y would have … 要是当时 X 没有……，Y 就会……
    If, at the time, the bank had not lent us money, we would have closed.
  • X had to wait nearly another … for … X 又等了将近……才等到……
    The village had to wait nearly another ten years for a proper road.

Our model summary本站范文

In 1858 a French engineer brought England a scheme for a tunnel under the Channel. A platform in mid-sea would be a port and a station, and tall chimneys would supply air. In 1860 an Englishman proposed two rail tunnels instead, since each train would pull fresh air in behind it. Work began forty-two years later but stopped because Britain feared attack. The tunnel finally opened in 1994.（68 词）
