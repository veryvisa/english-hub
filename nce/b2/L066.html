Lesson 66 · Sweet as Honey 甜如蜜

📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 66 “Sweet as Honey”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 66《Sweet as Honey》

B1🧠 22 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
In 1963 a Lancaster bomber came down on Wallis Island, a lonely speck of the South Pacific. The damage was fairly light, but the crash slipped out of memory and nobody touched the wreck. In 1989 it turned up again by chance on photographs taken from the air. By then a Lancaster in decent shape was a rarity, so French officials had it boxed and shipped home in sections, and a group of fans set out to rebuild it. Three of its four engines would need work. When the crates were opened, the fourth was found to be perfect: bees had made it their home and sealed it in wax. This is a curious news story with a happy surprise at the end. The grammar is have something done, worth doing, imagine someone's surprise and turn A into B.
1963 年，一架兰开斯特轰炸机在南太平洋偏远的瓦利斯岛坠毁。飞机损坏不算太重，可年复一年，这次坠机被人遗忘，残骸一直没人动过。1989 年，也就是坠机二十六年后，一次航空测量偶然又发现了它。那时还算完好的兰开斯特已经很少见，值得抢救。法国当局把飞机拆开装箱运回法国，一群爱好者打算把它修复。它有四台劳斯莱斯梅林发动机，他们只需要重修三台：打开包装箱一看，第四台竟然完好如新——一窝蜜蜂把它当成了蜂巢，整台发动机被蜂蜡封得严严实实。本课是一则有惊喜结尾的趣闻，学使役结构 have sth done、worth doing、imagine sb's surprise 和 turn A into B。

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Unsigned and written for the course; it reads like a news item of around 1990 and is based on a real recovered aircraft, though the lesson's dates and details have not been checked against French records.

本课未署名、为教材自撰；读来像 1990 年前后的一则新闻，取材于一架真实被回收的飞机，但课文的日期和细节我们没有拿法国档案核对过。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

After the Second World War the French navy flew Avro Lancasters for sea patrol and rescue work, including from bases in the Pacific, so a Lancaster on Wallis Island is entirely believable. Wallis belongs to the French territory of Wallis and Futuna, between Fiji and Samoa. Of more than seven thousand Lancasters built, only a handful survive today, and only two are known to fly, one in Britain and one in Canada. The Merlin engine, made by Rolls-Royce, also powered the Spitfire and became one of the most famous engines of its age. The detail of the bees is the part we could not confirm; it is the kind of story that is told and retold, so treat it as charming rather than proven.

二战后，法国海军用阿弗罗兰开斯特执行海上巡逻和救援，其中包括太平洋上的基地，所以瓦利斯岛上有一架兰开斯特完全可信。瓦利斯岛属于法国的瓦利斯和富图纳群岛，位于斐济和萨摩亚之间。兰开斯特当年造了七千多架，如今幸存的寥寥无几，已知还能飞的只有两架，一架在英国，一架在加拿大。劳斯莱斯造的梅林发动机也装在喷火式战斗机上，是那个时代最著名的发动机之一。蜜蜂这个细节我们没能核实；这类故事常被一传再传，当作有趣的传闻看，而不是定论。

✨ How it is written赏析

The writer saves the best detail for the very end and hides it inside a puzzle title. Most of the lesson is quiet reporting, almost dull on purpose: dates, a survey, packing cases, numbers of engines. The causative structure appears again and again, which keeps the people at a distance, because others are always doing the work. Then the writer speaks to us directly with Imagine, the only imperative in the piece, and the tone changes at once. The final sentence explains the title with a simple, physical image, and the exclamation mark tells us the writer enjoys it as much as the enthusiasts did.

作者把最好的细节留到最后，还把它藏在一个谜语式的标题里。课文大半是平静的报道，几乎是故意写得平淡：年份、测量、包装箱、发动机台数。使役结构一再出现，让人物保持距离，因为干活的总是别人。接着作者用 Imagine 直接对我们说话，这是全篇唯一的祈使句，语气一下就变了。最后一句用一个简单、具体的画面解开标题，感叹号告诉我们，作者和那些爱好者一样乐在其中。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The story is a small reminder that nature and machines are not always enemies. The bees did not know they were saving an engine; they only found a dry, sheltered space. Readers today might also notice how much effort people put into rescuing machines of war, and ask why. For many enthusiasts it is about memory and craft rather than war itself: these planes connect families to fathers and grandfathers who flew or built them. Chinese readers might compare the care given to old steam trains or the restored aircraft in the Beijing aviation museum. Keeping old things working is a way of keeping their stories alive.

这个故事小小地提醒我们：自然和机器并不总是对头。蜜蜂并不知道自己在保护一台发动机，它们只是找到了一个干燥、遮风挡雨的地方。今天的读者也许会注意到，人们花那么大力气抢救战争机器，并想问为什么。对很多爱好者来说，这关乎记忆和工艺，而不是战争本身：这些飞机把一个个家庭和当年驾驶或制造它们的父辈、祖辈联系起来。中国读者可以对比人们对老蒸汽机车的精心维护，或北京航空博物馆里修复的飞机。让旧东西继续运转，也是让它们的故事活下去。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 4第 4 句 (begins开头 “but over the …”)

  • over the years: little by little as time passed.over the years：多年来，渐渐地。
  • remain plus an adjective: stay in a state; remain works like be.remain + 形容词：保持某种状态，remain 是系动词。

Sentence 5第 5 句 (begins开头 “Then in 1989, …”)

  • A phrase in apposition, explaining what 1989 means.「坠机后二十六年」：同位成分，解释 1989 年。
  • The adverb sits between was and the participle.accidentally 放在助动词和过去分词之间。

Sentence 6第 6 句 (begins开头 “By this time, …”)

  • worth doing: the subject is the thing to be rescued, yet the -ing form is active.worth doing：值得做；主语是被做的对象，用主动形式 -ing。

Sentence 7第 7 句 (begins开头 “The French authorities …”)

  • have something done: arrange for someone else to do it; here two participles share one have.have sth done：让别人做某事（不是自己做）；这里两个过去分词并列。

Sentence 9第 9 句 (begins开头 “It has four …”)

  • Another have something done, with only limiting three.have … rebuilt：又一个使役结构，only 修饰 three。

Sentence 12第 12 句 (begins开头 “A colony of …”)

  • turn A into B: change A to B; had turned shows it happened long before they looked.turn A into B：把 A 变成 B。had turned：发现之前早已发生。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

The causative: have something done使役结构：have sth done
have plus object plus past participle means you arrange for someone else to do it; get something done is similar and more informal. The tense goes on have: had it done, will have it done, is going to have it done.
have + 宾语 + 过去分词：请别人或安排别人做某事，主语自己不动手。get sth done 意思相近，更口语。时态变在 have 上：had sth done、will have sth done、is going to have sth done。

Sentences 7, 8 and 9 use it three times in a row, for shipping, restoring and rebuilding, all done by others.

本课：第 7、8、9 句连用三次：运回、修复、重修发动机，都是请人做的。

  • I had my hair cut yesterday.
  • We are going to have the kitchen painted.
worth doing and imagine someone's surpriseworth doing 与 imagine sb's surprise
be worth doing means it deserves to be done; the -ing form has a passive sense, so never worth to be done. Imagine plus a noun plus when is an imperative that invites the reader into the high point of a story.
be worth doing：值得做，-ing 形式有被动含义，不说 worth to be done。Imagine + 名词 + when …：用祈使句邀请读者想象，常用于讲故事的高潮。

Sentence 6 says the plane deserved saving; sentence 10 uses Imagine to bring the reader to the moment the boxes opened.

本课：第 6 句说飞机值得抢救；第 10 句用 Imagine 把读者拉进开箱的那一刻。

  • This book is worth reading.
  • Imagine my joy when I saw my old dog again.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • In …, X crashed on …, a remote place in … ……年，X 坠毁在……，那是……一个偏远的地方
    In 1972, a small plane came down on a hill, a remote place in northern Scotland.
  • Over the years X was forgotten and Y remained … 多年来 X 被人遗忘，Y 一直保持……
    Over the years the old mill was forgotten and its wheel stayed silent.
  • By then, X in decent condition was scarce and worth … 到这时，还算完好的 X 已经很少见，值得……
    By then, a typewriter in decent condition was scarce and worth keeping.
  • Imagine their surprise when they … 想象一下他们……时有多吃惊
    Imagine their surprise when they found a kitten in the box.
  • X had turned Y into a … X 已经把 Y 变成了……
    The children had turned the garage into a theatre.

Our model summary本站范文

In 1963 a Lancaster bomber came down on Wallis Island, far out in the South Pacific. Not badly damaged, it lay forgotten until 1989, when an aerial survey found it by chance. As such planes had become rare, the French had it shipped home for restoration. Only three of its four engines needed rebuilding: when the crates were opened, the fourth was perfect, because bees had made a hive inside and sealed it in wax.（75 词）
