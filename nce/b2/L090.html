Lesson 90 · What's for Supper 晚餐吃什么？

📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 90 “What's for Supper”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 90《What's for Supper》

B1🧠 24 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
Fish and chips is a British favourite, but overfishing has made fish dear. So it is odd to hear that divers on oil platforms in the North Sea are being terrified by enormous fish. The platforms need constant repair, and the divers, working in the dark far below the surface, have been badly scared by huge fish knocking into them. They now work inside protective cages built for them. The fish are not sharks but ordinary food fish such as cod and skate, grown to monstrous lengths. Three things explain it: warm water around the hot pipes, scraps thrown off the platforms and no fishing boats nearby. So the fish simply feed and grow, and the last line asks who is eating whom. This is an ironic news story. The grammar is have something done, cause something to do, not ... but ... and as a result.
炸鱼薯条一直是英国人爱吃的菜，可是海洋被过度捕捞，鱼越来越贵。所以听说北海石油钻井平台的潜水员被巨鱼吓坏了，真让人吃惊。钻井平台常要维修，潜水员往往要在一百英尺深的黑暗水下干活，干活时被巨鱼撞来撞去，吓得魂飞魄散。现在他们已经让人做了特制的笼子来保护自己。这些鱼不是鲨鱼，也不是逆戟鲸，而是鳕鱼、鳐鱼这类人们爱吃的鱼，长得大得出奇，有时长达 12 英尺。原因有三：海底热油管周围的水很暖；平台上的工人往海里扔的食物很多；平台周围根本没有渔船。结果这些鱼在舒服的温水里只管吃、只管长。到底谁吃谁呢？本课是一篇带讽刺味的趣闻报道，学 have sth done、cause sb/sth to do、not … but … 和 as a result。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. What sort of fish are these giants?这些是什么鱼？
  2. Why are these fish so big? Find the three reasons in the text.文中说这些鱼长得这么大有哪三个原因？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Unsigned and written for the course in the style of a light news item; no source for the twelve-foot fish is given, and we have not found one.

本课未署名，以轻松新闻的笔调为教材自撰；十二英尺巨鱼没有给出出处，我们也没有找到。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

Fish really do gather around oil and gas platforms, which act like artificial reefs: the legs give shelter, plants and shellfish grow on them, and fishing is banned or difficult nearby. Studies in the North Sea and elsewhere have found much higher numbers of fish around platforms than in open water. The sizes in the lesson, though, are exaggerated. Cod rarely grow beyond about one and a half to two metres, and the largest skates, which are now rare, reach a little over two metres, so twelve feet is closer to legend than to fact. The overfishing at the start is real and serious: in Canada the cod of the Grand Banks off Newfoundland, once among the richest fisheries in the world, collapsed so badly that a ban on cod fishing was announced in 1992, and the stocks have still not fully recovered.

鱼的确会聚集在油气平台周围，平台就像人工鱼礁：桩腿提供藏身之处，上面长满了海藻和贝类，附近又禁止或很难捕鱼。在北海和其他地方的研究发现，平台周围的鱼远比开阔海域多。不过课文里的尺寸是夸大的。鳕鱼很少长到超过一米半到两米，最大的鳐鱼（如今已很稀少）也就两米多一点，所以十二英尺更像传说而不是事实。开头说的过度捕捞是真实而严重的：在加拿大，纽芬兰外海大浅滩的鳕鱼曾是世界上最丰富的渔场之一，后来崩溃得非常厉害，1992 年宣布禁止捕捞鳕鱼，至今鱼群仍未完全恢复。

✨ How it is written赏析

The piece is built on irony. It begins with a problem of shortage, fish too expensive to eat, and then reveals a place where fish are so plentiful and so big that they frighten people. The writer chooses words of horror, terrifying, frightened out of their wits, monsters, and then gently lets the air out: they are not sharks but the fish we eat for supper. The three causes are laid out in a neat list of parallel phrases, which makes the explanation clear and a little comic. The final question, only three words, turns the whole report into a joke about humans and their food, and leaves the reader smiling.

这篇文字建立在反讽之上。开头讲的是短缺：鱼贵得吃不起；接着揭示一个地方，那里的鱼多得、大得能把人吓坏。作者选用恐怖的字眼——令人恐惧、吓得魂飞魄散、怪物——然后轻轻泄了气：它们不是鲨鱼，而是我们晚餐吃的鱼。三个原因用整齐的并列短语排开，让解释既清楚又有点滑稽。最后那个只有三个词的问题，把整篇报道变成一个关于人类与食物的玩笑，让读者会心一笑。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

Behind the joke is a serious idea that scientists now take seriously: when fishing stops, fish come back and grow larger. Areas where fishing is banned, called marine protected areas, often show more and bigger fish within a few years, and some of them spill over into nearby waters. There is also a current debate in the North Sea about what to do with old platforms: removing them destroys the reef that has grown around them, while leaving them raises questions about pollution. Canadian readers know the cost of overfishing from Newfoundland's cod, and Chinese readers may know the long fishing ban on the Yangtze River that began in 2021, a ten-year rest meant to let fish numbers recover.

笑话背后是一个如今被科学家认真对待的严肃道理：停止捕捞，鱼就会回来，而且长得更大。禁止捕鱼的海域叫海洋保护区，往往在几年内就出现更多、更大的鱼，其中一些还会扩散到附近的水域。北海目前还有一场关于旧平台怎么处理的争论：拆掉它们会毁掉周围已经形成的鱼礁，留下来又带来污染的问题。加拿大读者从纽芬兰鳕鱼的教训中知道过度捕捞的代价，中国读者或许知道长江从 2021 年开始的长期禁渔——一次为期十年的休养，好让鱼群恢复。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 2第 2 句 (begins开头 “but as the …”)

  • fish and chips is one dish, so the verb is singular.fish and chips 作为一道菜，谓语用单数 has。
  • as: because; have been overfished: the present perfect passive; more and more: increasingly.as：因为；have been overfished：现在完成时被动。more and more：越来越。

Sentence 3第 3 句 (begins开头 “So it comes …”)

  • The pattern means it is surprising; it stands for the infinitive.come as a surprise：令人吃惊；句首 it 是形式主语，代替后面的不定式。

Sentence 5第 5 句 (begins开头 “have been frightened …”)

  • frighten someone out of their wits: scare them terribly, here in the passive.frighten sb out of their wits：把某人吓得魂飞魄散；被动。
  • fish bumping into them: a noun plus an -ing phrase, the fish that knock against them.fish bumping into them：名词 + 现在分词，「鱼撞他们」。

Sentence 6第 6 句 (begins开头 “Now they have …”)

  • have something done: arrange for someone else to do it.have sth done：让别人做某事（不是自己做）。
  • protect someone from: keep them safe from.protect sb from：保护某人免受。

Sentence 7第 7 句 (begins开头 “The fish are …”)

  • not A but B: B rather than A; as much as: up to.not A but B：不是 A 而是 B。as much as：多达。

Sentence 10第 10 句 (begins开头 “Three factors have …”)

  • cause something to do: make it happen; after the colon, three noun phrases separated by semicolons list the reasons.cause sth to do：使……。冒号后用三个分号隔开的名词短语列出原因。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

have something done: getting someone else to do ithave sth done：让别人做
have or get plus an object plus a past participle means you arrange for someone else to do it; the tense is shown on have.
have/get + 宾语 + 过去分词，表示请别人或安排别人做某事；时态体现在 have 上。

In sentence 6 the divers had cages built for them; they did not build them themselves.

本课：第 6 句：潜水员让人做了特制的笼子——不是自己做的。

  • I had my hair cut yesterday.
  • We are having the house painted.
not ... but ... and as a result
not A but B means B rather than A, with A and B in the same form; as a result introduces the effect of what was just said.
not A but B：不是 A 而是 B，A、B 结构相同；as a result：结果，引出前面原因造成的后果。

Sentences 7 and 8 use not ... but to say what kind of fish they are, and sentence 14 uses as a result to sum up the three causes.

本课：第 7–8 句用 not … but 说明是什么鱼；第 14 句用 as a result 总结三个原因的后果。

  • He is not a teacher but a doctor.
  • It rained all week. As a result, the match was cancelled.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • X has always been a favourite … in … X 一直是……人喜爱的……
    Rice porridge has always been a favourite breakfast in southern China.
  • It is a surprise to hear that … 听说……真令人吃惊
    It is a surprise to hear that penguins can live in hot places.
  • X were scared out of their wits by … X 被……吓得魂飞魄散
    The campers were scared out of their wits by a bear.
  • Now they have had special … made to … 如今他们已让人做了特制的……来……
    Now the farmers have had special fences made to keep out the deer.
  • X factors have made … grow so … 有 X 个因素使……长得如此……
    Two factors have made the city grow so fast.

Summary writing摘要写作

In no more than 80 words, describe the giant fish near the North Sea rigs and say why they grow so big.

用不超过 80 个词讲讲北海钻井平台附近的巨鱼，以及它们长这么大的原因。

Points a good summary covers好摘要要覆盖的要点
  • Fish has become expensive through overfishing, yet divers on North Sea rigs are scared by giant fish and now use special cages.海洋被过度捕捞，鱼越来越贵，可北海石油钻井平台的潜水员却被巨鱼吓坏了，只好待在特制的笼子里干活。

Our model summary本站范文

Fish is a favourite food in Britain, but it has become expensive because the seas have been overfished. Surprisingly, divers repairing North Sea oil rigs are being frightened by giant fish, and they now work in special cages for protection. The fish are ordinary food fish such as cod and skate, which can reach twelve feet. They grow big because the water near the hot pipes is warm, the crews throw away plenty of food, and nobody fishes there.（79 词）
