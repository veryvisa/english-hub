Lesson 46 · Expensive and Uncomfortable 又贵又不舒服

📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 46 “Expensive and Uncomfortable”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 46《Expensive and Uncomfortable》

B1🧠 19 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
A plane from London landed in Sydney, and the ground staff started taking off a load of wooden crates full of clothes. One crate weighed far more than it should, and nobody knew why. A worker had the idea of opening it and got a shock: a man was stretched out on a heap of wool garments. He was so stunned to be found that he made no attempt to escape. Under arrest, he said he had climbed in before take-off and had spent more than eighteen hours shut inside. He was told to pay £3,500 for the journey, when an ordinary ticket cost £2,000. The grammar is account for, It occurred to sb to do, being done after a preposition, and admit doing.
一架从伦敦飞来的飞机降落在悉尼机场，工人们开始卸下一批装衣服的木箱。其中一只箱子重得出奇，谁也说不清为什么。有个工人忽然想到把它打开看看，结果大吃一惊：一个男人正躺在一堆羊毛制品上面。他被发现时吓呆了，连逃都没想逃。被捕后他承认，飞机离开伦敦之前他就藏进了箱子，在里面憋了十八个多小时。法院判他付 3,500 英镑的旅费，而一张正常机票只要 2,000 英镑。本课学 account for、It occurred to sb to do、be surprised at being done（动名词被动）和 admit doing。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. What made the workers decide to open that crate?工人们为什么会想到打开那只箱子？
  2. Did the stowaway save any money in the end?这个人偷偷搭飞机，最后省钱了吗？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander for New Concept English (Longman, 1967); the story reads like a press item of the time, but no single original report has been traced.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为《新概念英语》（朗文，1967）撰写；故事读起来像当时的社会新闻，但没有找到可以对应的原始报道，来源未核。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

In the nineteen-sixties a flight from London to Sydney was a long, costly affair, with several stops on the way, and a ticket cost far more than most workers earned in months. Air freight was loaded by hand, which is why the workers in the story notice the weight with their own arms. People who hide on planes, ships and lorries are called stowaways, and the danger is real: aviation authorities report that most people who hide in the wheel spaces of modern aircraft die of cold or lack of oxygen. A sealed crate in a cargo hold is hardly safer, and today scanners and sniffer dogs make such a trip almost impossible.

二十世纪六十年代，从伦敦飞悉尼要中途经停好几次，票价比大多数工人几个月的工资还高。航空货物靠人手装卸，所以故事里的工人是用自己的胳膊「掂」出箱子太重的。藏在飞机、轮船、卡车上偷渡的人叫 stowaway，这件事的危险是真实的：航空管理部门的资料显示，躲在现代客机起落架舱里的人，大多死于严寒或缺氧。密封木箱放在货舱里也安全不了多少；如今有扫描仪和缉查犬，这样偷渡几乎不可能。

✨ How it is written赏析

The whole joke is held back for the last line. The story first works like a small mystery: a box that is too heavy, a worker with a sudden idea, a discovery. Then it turns into a punishment that sounds fair, and only the final short sentence, set apart from the rest, gives the comparison with an ordinary fare. The writer never says the man was foolish; the two numbers say it for him. The title does the same job in advance, promising two things, high cost and discomfort, which the story delivers in reverse order.

整个笑点压到最后一句才抖出来。故事先像一个小谜题：箱子太重，工人灵机一动，打开一看。接着是一个听上去合理的惩罚，直到最后那句单独成句的短话，才拿它和正常票价比。作者从没说这人蠢，两个数字替他说了。标题也提前做了同样的事：它许诺了「贵」和「不舒服」两件事，故事则倒着把这两件事兑现。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

Under the comedy sits a serious point about false savings. The man risked his health and his freedom to avoid a cost, and ended up paying more than he would have paid honestly. Many readers will recognise the same pattern in smaller choices: skipping a check-up, buying a cheap item that breaks, avoiding a fee and getting a fine. For Chinese readers abroad there is a sharper echo, because people still risk their lives on dangerous routes across borders, and the human cost is no joke. The lesson works best as a starting point for asking what a shortcut really costs.

喜剧下面藏着一个关于「假节省」的严肃道理。这人冒着健康和自由的风险去省一笔钱，结果付得比老老实实买票还多。很多读者能在更小的选择里认出同样的模式：省掉体检、贪便宜买了很快坏掉的东西、逃了一笔费用却挨了罚款。对海外华人读者来说还有更沉重的回声：至今仍有人冒着生命危险走偷渡路线，那里的代价一点也不好笑。这一课最适合拿来问一个问题：一条捷径真正的代价是什么？

💬 Discuss and write讨论与写作
  1. Have you ever tried to save money and ended up paying more? Tell the story in about 100 words.
    你有没有为了省钱结果花得更多的经历？用大约 100 个词讲一讲。
  2. Should the man have been made to pay more than the normal fare? Give two reasons for and two against.
    应该让这个人付比正常票价更高的钱吗？各给两个赞成和反对的理由。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 3第 3 句 (begins开头 “No one could …”)

  • account for: give a reason for.account for：解释，说明原因。
  • the fact that: the that-clause tells us what the fact is.the fact that …：同位语从句，that 引出 fact 的具体内容。

Sentence 4第 4 句 (begins开头 “It suddenly occurred …”)

  • It occurred to sb to do: it stands in for the real subject, the to-infinitive.It occurred to sb to do：某人突然想到要做……，it 是形式主语，真正的主语是不定式。
  • open up: up adds the sense of opening fully.open up：把（箱子）打开，up 加强「完全打开」。

Sentence 7第 7 句 (begins开头 “He was so …”)

  • at being discovered: after a preposition use -ing, and because he is the one found, the passive being done.at being discovered：介词后用动名词；他是被发现的一方，所以用被动 being done。
  • so ... that: so much that.so … that：如此……以致……。

Sentence 9第 9 句 (begins开头 “the man admitted …”)

  • admit doing: admit takes an -ing form.admit doing：承认做过某事，admit 后接动名词。

Sentence 11第 11 句 (begins开头 “for he had …”)

  • had had: the first had is the helper, the second is the past participle of have.had had：过去完成时，第一个 had 是助动词，第二个是 have 的过去分词。
  • for: a formal word for because, coming in the middle of the sentence to add a reason.for：连词「因为」，放在句中，语气比 because 正式，补充理由。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

The passive -ing form after a preposition: being done介词后的动名词被动：being done
After a preposition use an -ing form; if the subject receives the action, use being plus the past participle.
介词后面要用动名词；如果主语是动作的承受者，就用 being + 过去分词。

In sentence 7 the man is not finding anyone; he is the one being found, so the form is passive.

本课：第 7 句的 at being discovered——他不是去发现别人，而是被别人发现。

  • She was upset at being left out of the team.
  • Nobody likes being laughed at.
It occurs to sb to do or that: an idea comes to someoneIt occurs to sb to do / that …：某人想到
The idea is the subject and the person comes after to: It occurred to me to do, or that something was true. Never make the person the subject.
想法作主语，人放在 to 后面：It occurred to me to … / It occurred to me that …。不能说 I occurred to … 。

In sentence 4 the idea of opening the crate suddenly comes into a worker's head.

本课：第 4 句——那个工人忽然冒出打开箱子的念头。

  • It occurred to me that I had left the oven on.
  • Did it ever occur to you to ring first?
admit, deny and avoid with -ing
Verbs such as admit, deny, avoid, enjoy and finish take an -ing form, not to do; for an earlier action you may also use having done.
admit（承认）、deny（否认）、avoid（避免）、enjoy、finish 等动词后接动名词，不接不定式；说过去的事也可用 having done。

In sentence 9 admitted is followed straight away by an -ing form.

本课：第 9 句 admitted 后面直接跟 -ing。

  • She denied taking the money.
  • He admitted having lied to his parents.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • No one could explain why … 谁也解释不了为什么……
    No one could explain why the lights kept going off.
  • It suddenly occurred to X to … X 突然想到要……
    It suddenly occurred to her to check the back door.
  • X was so … at being … that … X 被……时如此……以致……
    He was so pleased at being chosen that he rang his mother at once.
  • X admitted …ing … X 承认做了……
    The boy admitted eating all the cake.
  • X had to pay … for …, while … costs only … X 不得不为……付……，而……只要……
    She had to pay £80 for a taxi, while the train costs only £12.

Our model summary本站范文

At Sydney airport, workers were taking wooden crates of clothing off a plane from London when they noticed that one was strangely heavy. A worker opened it and found a man lying on some woollen goods. He was too shocked to escape. After his arrest he said he had climbed in before take-off and spent more than eighteen hours inside. He was told to pay £3,500, though a ticket normally cost £2,000.（72 词）
