Lesson 29 · Taxi 出租汽车

📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 29 “Taxi”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 29《Taxi》

B1🧠 20 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
Captain Ben Fawcett has bought a very unusual taxi and started a new business. The taxi is a small Swiss aircraft, the Pilatus Porter, which seats seven passengers and can come down almost anywhere: on snow, on water, or in a freshly ploughed field. His first fare was a doctor going from Birmingham to a remote village in the Welsh hills. Since then he has flown people to all sorts of odd places, once onto the top of a tall residential building and once into an empty car park. He has just said no to a businessman who wanted to go to Rockall, a lonely rock far out in the Atlantic, because it was too risky. The grammar is the present perfect against the past simple and the most with an adjective.
本·福西特机长买了一辆不寻常的「出租车」，开办了一项新业务。这辆「出租车」其实是一架叫「皮拉图斯·波特」的瑞士小飞机，能载七名乘客，最让人吃惊的是它哪儿都能降落：雪地、水面，甚至犁过的田里。第一位乘客是一位医生，从伯明翰飞到威尔士山区一个偏僻的村子。此后他把乘客送到过许多不寻常的地方：一次降落在一座公寓楼的楼顶，另一次降落在一个废弃的停车场。最近他拒绝了一个商人的奇怪要求：那人想飞到大西洋上孤零零的罗卡尔岛，福西特机长觉得太危险，没有带他去。本课学现在完成时与一般过去时的区别和the most + 形容词。

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander for New Concept English (Longman, 1967); Captain Fawcett is invented, but the Pilatus Porter and Rockall are real.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为《新概念英语》（朗文，1967）自撰；福西特机长是虚构的，但皮拉图斯·波特飞机和罗卡尔岛都是真的。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

The Pilatus Porter, the PC-6, is a real Swiss aircraft first flown in 1959 and built by Pilatus at Stans. It became famous for short take-off and landing: it needs only a short strip of rough ground, and versions fitted with skis or floats can use snow and water. It has been used for decades in mountains, jungles and polar regions, and it carries about seven to ten people depending on the layout. Rockall is a tiny bare rock in the North Atlantic, a few hundred kilometres west of Scotland, with no harbour and no flat ground; Britain formally claimed it in 1955, and its status and the seas around it have been disputed with Ireland and others. A landing there by plane would be almost impossible.

皮拉图斯·波特（PC-6）是真实存在的瑞士飞机，1959 年首飞，由皮拉图斯公司在施坦斯制造。它以短距起降出名：只需要一小段粗糙的场地，装上滑橇或浮筒的型号还能在雪地和水面起降。几十年来，它在山区、丛林和极地都有使用，按座舱布置可载七到十人左右。罗卡尔是北大西洋里一块光秃秃的小礁石，在苏格兰以西几百公里，没有港口，也没有平地；英国 1955 年正式宣布对它拥有主权，它的地位和周围海域一直与爱尔兰等国存在争议。在那里用飞机降落几乎是不可能的。

✨ How it is written赏析

The lesson is written like a short news feature, and it builds by raising the stakes. A taxi is ordinary, so the first surprise is that it is a plane. Then it can land anywhere, and the list climbs from snow to water to a ploughed field, with even before the last item. The examples of his work climb in the same way, from a village to a rooftop to a deserted car park. The reader begins to believe that nothing is beyond him, and that is the moment the writer gives us a refusal. Rockall is the one place even this aircraft cannot manage, and the ending answers the question printed before the text with a quiet no.

课文写得像一则短小的新闻特写，靠步步加码来推进。出租车很平常，所以第一个惊奇是：它是一架飞机。然后它哪儿都能降落，列举从雪地到水面再到犁过的田，最后一项前还加了 even。他的业绩也同样一步步升级：从山村到楼顶，再到废弃的停车场。读者开始相信没有什么是他做不到的——正在这时，作者给了我们一次拒绝。罗卡尔是连这架飞机也对付不了的唯一地方，结尾用一个平静的「不」回答了课文前印着的那个问题。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The captain's refusal is the most interesting thing in the story, because a man who has built his business on going anywhere still knows where to stop. Pilots and safety experts often say that good judgement shows less in what you can do than in what you decline, and much accident research points to the pressure to please a customer as a common cause of disaster. Air taxis of a new kind, small electric aircraft for city journeys, are being tested in several countries today, and their success will depend as much on rules about where they may land as on the machines. The lesson's quiet message works for any job: knowing your limits is part of being good at it.

机长的拒绝是故事里最有意思的地方：一个靠「哪儿都能去」起家的人，依然知道在哪里停下。飞行员和安全专家常说，好的判断力与其说体现在你能做什么，不如说体现在你拒绝什么；很多事故研究都指出，「想让客户满意」的压力是灾难的常见起因。如今好几个国家都在测试一种新式空中出租车——用于城市出行的小型电动飞行器，它们能否成功，取决于关于在哪里可以降落的规则，不亚于取决于机器本身。课文这个平静的信息适用于任何工作：知道自己的极限，是做好它的一部分。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 1第 1 句 (begins开头 “Captain Ben Fawcett …”)

  • The present perfect describes the situation now: he owns the plane and runs the service.has bought / has begun：现在完成时，说的是现在的状况：他有了这辆车、这项服务。

Sentence 4第 4 句 (begins开头 “The most surprising …”)

  • the most surprising thing: a superlative used as the subject.the most surprising thing：最令人吃惊的（一点），最高级作主语。
  • is that: a clause as the complement. The colon introduces a list.is that …：表语从句。冒号后面列举具体地方。
  • a ploughed field: a field that has been ploughed; the past participle describes it.a ploughed field：犁过的田，过去分词作定语。

Sentence 5第 5 句 (begins开头 “Captain Fawcett's first …”)

  • was and flew: the past simple, because the first flight happened at a definite time.was / flew：一般过去时，第一位乘客是过去某个确定的时候。
  • who flew: a relative clause describing the doctor.who flew …：定语从句修饰 a doctor。

Sentence 6第 6 句 (begins开头 “Since then, Captain …”)

  • since then with the present perfect: from that time until now.since then + 现在完成时：从那以后到现在。

Sentence 10第 10 句 (begins开头 “but Captain Fawcett …”)

  • The noun phrase after the comma is in apposition, explaining what Rockall is.Rockall 后面逗号隔开的名词短语是同位语，解释它是什么。
  • too dangerous: more dangerous than is acceptable.too dangerous：太危险，too 表示超过限度。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

The present perfect and the past simple现在完成时与一般过去时
The present perfect talks about experience or results up to now without giving a time; the past simple talks about a definite occasion. Words such as once, last year or in 1960 fix the time, so they take the past simple.
现在完成时说「到现在为止」的经历或结果，不说具体什么时候（has flown、has just refused）；一般过去时说过去确定的某一次（was、flew、landed、did not take）。once、last year、in 1960 等具体时间用一般过去时。

Sentences 1, 6 and 8 use the present perfect; sentences 5, 7, 9 and 10 describe single occasions in the past simple.

本课：第 1 句、第 6 句、第 8 句用现在完成时；第 5 句、第 7 句、第 9–10 句讲具体的某一次，用一般过去时。

  • I have visited Rome three times. I went there last in 2019.
  • She has just arrived. She arrived ten minutes ago.
the most with an adjective: the superlativethe most + 形容词：最高级
Longer adjectives form the superlative with the most, as in the most surprising; short ones add -est. It is often followed by about it, in the world or of all.
多音节形容词的最高级用 the most + 原级（the most surprising），单音节的加 -est（the oldest）。后面常接 about it / in the world / of all。

Sentence 4 uses the most surprising with about it.

本课：第 4 句用 the most surprising 作主语，后接 about it。

  • This is the most expensive hotel in town.
  • The most important thing is to stay calm.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • X has bought … and has begun a new … X 买了……，开办了一项新……
    My aunt has bought a van and has begun a new delivery business.
  • The most surprising thing about X is that … X 最令人吃惊的是……
    The most surprising thing about the hotel is that it has no doors.
  • Since then, X has … to many … 从那以后，X 已经……到过许多……
    Since then, she has taken her camera to many far-off countries.
  • Once X …, and on another occasion, X … 有一次 X……，另一次 X……
    Once she slept in a cave, and on another occasion, she slept on a boat.

Our model summary本站范文

Captain Fawcett has started a taxi service using a small Swiss plane that carries seven people and can land on snow, water or farmland. He first flew a doctor to a remote Welsh village, and has since landed on a roof and in an empty car park. But he refused to fly a businessman to Rockall, which was too dangerous.（60 词）
