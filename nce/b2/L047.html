Lesson 47 · A Thirsty Ghost 嗜酒的鬼魂

📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 47 “A Thirsty Ghost”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 47《A Thirsty Ghost》

B1🧠 17 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
Mr Ian Thompson bought a village pub not long ago and is already trying to get rid of it, because he believes a ghost lives there. One night, he says, odd sounds from the bar kept him awake. In the morning chairs were piled against the doors and the furniture had been shifted. He had switched the lights off before bed, yet they were burning at dawn, and five whisky bottles stood empty. When the writer guessed that local people had slipped in for drinks on the house, Mr Thompson disagreed: the villagers say they would refuse the place even as a gift. The grammar is must have done, hear something doing, the past perfect passive, and even if.
伊恩·汤普森先生不久前买下一家小酒馆，现在又要把它卖掉，因为那里闹鬼。他说有一天夜里，他听见酒吧间传来怪声，怎么也睡不着。第二天早上，门被椅子堵住了，家具也挪了位置；他睡前明明关了灯，早上灯却亮着。他还发现了五个空威士忌酒瓶，认定是鬼前一晚喝光的。「我」猜是村里人溜进来白喝酒，他摇了摇头：村民们说，就算他把酒馆白送，他们也不要。本课学 must have done（对过去的推测）、hear sb/sth doing、过去完成时被动和 even if。

  2. What other explanation does the writer offer for the empty bottles?「我」对空酒瓶有什么别的解释？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander for New Concept English (Longman, 1967); Mr Thompson and his pub are invented.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为《新概念英语》（朗文，1967）自撰；汤普森先生和他的酒馆都是虚构的。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

The English village pub is more than a bar. For centuries it has been a meeting place, a place to eat and, in many villages, the last shared public room after the shop and post office have closed. Many old pubs advertise a resident ghost, and some owners treat the story as good business, because visitors come to hear it. The opposite happens in this lesson: the owner takes the ghost so seriously that he wants out. A pub also depends entirely on local trade, so if the villagers refuse to drink there, the business is finished whatever the truth about the ghost.

英国乡村酒馆不只是喝酒的地方。几百年来它是聚会场所、吃饭的地方，在很多村子里，商店和邮局关门之后，它是最后一间公共屋子。不少老酒馆专门宣传自家「有鬼」，有的店主把它当生意经，因为游客会慕名来听故事。本课反过来：店主把鬼当真，只想脱手。酒馆全靠本地人光顾，村民不肯来，不管鬼是真是假，这门生意都做不下去。

✨ How it is written赏析

The story is reported, not told: almost everything reaches us through what Mr Thompson said, so the reader can doubt every detail. The writer's single suggestion, that thirsty locals may be the real visitors, is the reader's own thought, spoken aloud. Then the last sentence turns it round. If the villagers will not touch the pub even as a gift, they are not likely to be the ones sneaking in to drink. The joke works because it leaves the question open: either the ghost is real, or the whole village is in on the trick.

故事是转述的，不是亲历的：几乎每个细节都通过汤普森先生的话传到我们耳中，读者可以怀疑每一条。作者唯一的插话——也许是口渴的村民来过——正是读者心里的想法，被说了出来。最后一句又把它翻过来：村民连白送都不要这家酒馆，那半夜溜进来喝酒的大概也不是他们。笑点就在于问题没有答案：要么真有鬼，要么全村人合伙在演这出戏。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The lesson is a neat exercise in weighing evidence. Every clue fits two stories, and the narrator tests the boring explanation first, which is good thinking. Modern readers may also notice how belief spreads: once a few people say a place is haunted, others stay away, the place gets quieter and stranger, and the story feels confirmed. Chinese readers know the same pattern from houses said to be unlucky, which sell for less in many cities. Whether or not ghosts exist, a reputation can change prices and behaviour, and that part is entirely real.

这一课是一次漂亮的「掂量证据」练习。每条线索都能套进两个故事，叙述者先检验最平常的解释，这是好的思维习惯。现代读者也许还会注意到信念如何扩散：几个人说一个地方闹鬼，别人就躲着走，那里越来越冷清古怪，故事仿佛就被证实了。中国读者对「凶宅」并不陌生，在很多城市这种房子卖得便宜。不管鬼存不存在，名声能改变价格和人的行为，这一点完全是真的。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

must have done: a confident guess about the pastmust have done：对过去的肯定推测
must have plus the past participle means, from the evidence, something surely happened. For a negative guess use can't have done, never mustn't have done.
must have + 过去分词：根据证据推断过去「一定……了」；否定推测用 can't have done，不用 mustn't have done。

Sentences 10 and 11 both use it: Mr Thompson blames the ghost, while the writer blames the villagers.

本课：第 10 句和第 11 句都用了这个结构——汤普森说是鬼喝的，「我」却猜是村民来过。

  • The streets are wet. It must have rained in the night.
  • She can't have seen us — she didn't wave.
hear, see or notice someone or something doinghear / see / notice + 宾语 + doing
After verbs of the senses, object plus -ing means you caught the action in progress; object plus bare infinitive means you saw the whole action.
感官动词后接宾语 + -ing，表示听到/看到动作正在进行；接宾语 + 原形动词表示看到动作的全过程。

In sentence 4 the noise was going on when he heard it.

本课：第 4 句——他听见怪声正从酒吧间传出来。

  • I heard someone knocking at the door.
  • We saw the man cross the road and get into a car.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • X is up for sale because … X 要卖了，因为……
    Our old car is up for sale because we hardly use it.
  • X heard a … coming from … X 听见……从……传来
    She heard loud music coming from upstairs.
  • … which X must have … the night before ……一定是 X 前一晚……
    There were crumbs everywhere, which the children must have left the night before.
  • When I suggested that …, X shook his head. 我提出……时，X 摇了摇头。
    When I suggested that we leave early, my father shook his head.
  • They won't take it even if you … 就算你……，他们也不要
    She won't eat the soup even if you add sugar.

Our model summary本站范文

Mr Thompson has put his new pub up for sale because he thinks it is haunted. One night odd noises from the bar kept him awake. In the morning he found chairs against the doors, the furniture moved, and the lights on, though he had switched them off. Five whisky bottles were empty too. I said some villagers might have drunk the whisky, but he disagreed. They would not take the pub, he said, even for nothing.（77 词）
