Lesson 82 · Monster or Fish 是鱼还是妖怪？

📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 82 “Monster or Fish”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 82《Monster or Fish》

B1🧠 21 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
Sailors have long told of sea monsters, and people have long laughed at them, yet science now says many such sightings were simply odd fish. Strange creatures sometimes wash up on beaches, but they are seldom caught in open water. Not long ago, off Madagascar, a fisherman hooked one so strong that it towed his small boat far out. Sensing that it was something special, he took great care not to hurt it. Landed at last, it measured more than thirteen feet, with a horse-like head, large blue eyes, silvery skin and a red tail. It is now in a museum being studied, and it is called an oarfish. These fish live so deep that few people ever see one alive. This is a short science piece that explains a legend. The grammar is claim to have done, it is now known that, the passive in several tenses and such.
渔民和水手有时说在海上见过妖怪。人们常常笑话这类故事，可如今知道，许多偶尔被看到的「妖怪」其实只是怪鱼。奇特的生物偶尔会被冲上岸，在海上却很少被捕到。不久前，马达加斯加附近捕到一条怪鱼：它拽着渔线把小渔船拖出去好几英里。渔民意识到这不是普通的鱼，处处小心不弄伤它。拖上岸一量，体长超过 13 英尺，头像马，蓝色大眼睛，银色的皮闪闪发亮，尾巴鲜红。这条鱼后来被送到博物馆，正由一位科学家研究，它叫皇带鱼。这种鱼生活在 600 英尺深的海里，人类很少见到活的。本课是一篇解开传说的科普短文，学 claim to have done、it is now known that、被动语态的几种时态 和 such。

Unsigned and written for the course. The oarfish is real and well described, but the particular catch near Madagascar is not dated or named, so we cannot check it.

本课未署名、为教材自撰。皇带鱼是真实存在、描述详尽的鱼，但马达加斯加附近这次捕获没有日期和人名，无法核对。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

The oarfish is the longest bony fish known, commonly reaching five metres or more, with reports of much longer animals. It is flat like a ribbon and silver, with a bright red fin along its back that rises into a crest above the head, which may explain the horse's head in sailors' stories. It usually lives in the open ocean at depths of a few hundred metres, swims by rippling that fin while its body stays upright, and is seen mostly when sick or dying fish drift to the surface or wash ashore. Because of these long bodies and their sudden appearances, scientists think oarfish lie behind many sea-serpent legends. The waters near Madagascar are also famous for another living mystery: the coelacanth, a fish thought to have died out with the dinosaurs until one was caught off South Africa in 1938, with more later found near the Comoro Islands.

皇带鱼是已知最长的硬骨鱼，常见体长五米以上，也有远比这更长的记录。它扁得像一条缎带，通身银色，背上一条鲜红的鳍一直延伸到头顶，竖起来像鸡冠，这也许就是水手故事里「马头」的来历。它通常生活在大洋中几百米深处，靠波动那条背鳍游动、身体竖直，人们见到它多半是病鱼或垂死的鱼漂上海面、被冲上岸的时候。因为身体长、出现得突然，科学家认为皇带鱼是许多海蛇传说的来源。马达加斯加附近的海域还以另一个活着的谜而出名：腔棘鱼，人们原以为它和恐龙一起灭绝了，直到 1938 年在南非沿海捕到一条，后来又在科摩罗群岛附近发现了更多。

✨ How it is written赏析

The piece moves from a general claim to one example and back to a general fact, a pattern useful for any short explanation. It opens with what people say, admits that people laughed, and then sets science against the laughter with it is now known that, a phrase that sounds calm and certain. The story of the catch gives life to the idea: the boat dragged out, the careful fisherman, the measurement on shore. The description of the fish is a list of four plain features, head, eyes, skin, tail, each with one colour or shape, so the reader can picture it at once. The last sentence explains why the earlier stories were rare and strange, closing the circle.

这篇短文从一个笼统的说法写到一个例子，再回到一个笼统的事实，这种结构适用于任何简短的说明。开头写人们怎么说，承认有人笑话，然后用「现在人们知道」这个听来平静而确定的说法，让科学站到笑声的对面。捕鱼的故事让道理活了起来：渔船被拖出去、渔民小心翼翼、上岸后一量。对鱼的描写是四个朴素的特征——头、眼、皮、尾——每个只配一种颜色或形状，读者马上能想象出来。最后一句解释为什么早先的故事既少见又古怪，把圈子收拢。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The lesson is a gentle lesson in reasoning: a story can be honestly told and still wrongly explained. The sailors probably did see something strange; their mistake was in the explanation, not the sighting. Today there is a new version of the same problem. In Japan, oarfish washing ashore have long been linked in folk belief to coming earthquakes, and the idea spreads on social media after every sighting, yet a study published in 2019 that compared decades of sightings with earthquake records found no real connection. Chinese readers will know similar beliefs about animals sensing earthquakes. The useful habit is to separate what was seen from what it is said to mean, and to ask for records before accepting the link.

这一课是一堂温和的推理课：一个故事可以讲得很诚实，却仍然解释错了。水手们大概真的看到了奇怪的东西；他们错在解释上，不在看见上。今天，同样的问题有了新版本。在日本，民间长期把皇带鱼冲上岸和即将到来的地震联系在一起，每次有人看见，这种说法就在社交媒体上传开；可 2019 年发表的一项研究把几十年的目击记录与地震记录对比，并没有发现真正的关联。中国读者也熟悉动物能预感地震的类似说法。有用的习惯是：把「看见了什么」和「据说意味着什么」分开，先要记录，再接受其中的联系。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

The perfect infinitive: to have done不定式完成式：to have done
to have plus a past participle shows an action earlier than the main verb; it often follows claim, seem, appear and be said.
to have + 过去分词，表示动作发生在主句动词之前；常跟在 claim、seem、appear、be said 之后。

In sentence 1 the seeing happened before the claim.

本课：第 1 句的 claim to have seen：先「见过」，后「声称」。

  • He claims to have met the president.
  • She seems to have forgotten my name.
The passive in several tenses被动语态的几种时态
Past simple: was done; present perfect: has been done; present continuous: is being done; with a modal: can be done. The auxiliaries show the tense, and the last part is a form of be plus the past participle.
一般过去 was done；现在完成 has been done；现在进行 is being done；情态 can be done。助动词表时态，最后一个是 be 的某种形式 + 过去分词。

Sentence 6 has the past simple passive, sentence 10 has two passives, one perfect and one continuous, and sentence 11 has the perfect passive again.

本课：第 6 句 was carried；第 10 句一句里有 has been sent 和 is being examined；第 11 句 have been seen。

  • The bridge is being repaired this week.
  • The letter has already been sent.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • … sometimes claim to have seen … ……有时声称见过……
    Hikers sometimes claim to have seen wolves in these hills.
  • We now know that most of these … are just … 现在我们知道，这些……大多只不过是……
    We now know that most of these 'ghosts' are just shadows.
  • Seeing that it was no common …, X … 看出这绝不是普通的……，X……
    Seeing that it was no common stone, the farmer took it to an expert.
  • When it was weighed, it turned out to be over … 一称，结果超过……
    When the pumpkin was weighed, it turned out to be over fifty kilos.
  • Such … are seldom seen by man, as they live … 这类……人类很少见到，因为它们生活在……
    Such birds are seldom seen by man, as they live high in the mountains.

Our model summary本站范文

Sailors often tell stories about sea monsters, and many of these are now known to be unusual fish. Some time ago, near Madagascar, a powerful fish pulled a small fishing boat far out to sea. The fisherman took care not to harm it, and when it was landed, it measured more than thirteen feet, with a horse-like head and a red tail. It is called an oarfish and lives so deep that it is rarely seen alive.（77 词）
