📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 34 “Quick Work”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 34《Quick Work》

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
Dan Robinson had been anxious for a whole week. Last Tuesday a letter came from the local police inviting him to drop in at the station. He could not think what they wanted, but he went along yesterday and his worries are over. A policeman with a grin told him that his bike had turned up: it was found five days ago in a little village four hundred miles off, and it is on its way home by rail. Dan was astonished and amused, since he had given up hope long ago. The bike had been stolen twenty years before, when he was a lad of fifteen. The grammar is the passive in several tenses, and expect something to be done.
丹·罗宾逊整整一个星期都心神不宁。上星期二他收到当地警察局的一封信，请他到警察局去一趟。他不明白警察找他干什么，昨天还是去了，现在不再担心了。在警察局，一位笑眯眯的警察告诉他，他的自行车找到了：五天前在四百英里外的一个小村子里发现的，现在正用火车给他运回家。丹又惊讶又觉得好笑，因为他从没指望能找回那辆车——它是二十年前被偷的，那时丹才十五岁！本课的核心是被动语态：一般过去时、过去完成时、现在进行时被动，以及 expect sth to be done。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. Why did the police ask Dan to come in?警察为什么要丹去警察局？
  2. Why is the title a joke?「破案神速」这个标题为什么是反话？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander for New Concept English (Longman, 1967); Dan Robinson and the case are invented.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为《新概念英语》（朗文，1967）自撰；丹·罗宾逊和这起案子都是虚构的。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

In Britain bicycles have long had frame numbers stamped into the metal, and owners were encouraged to note them down so that a found bike could be traced; police stations kept found property for months. Stories of stolen goods coming back after decades still turn up in the news today, usually because of a serial number or a name scratched under the saddle. The idea of sending a bicycle home by train was also normal in the nineteen sixties, when British railways carried parcels and bikes as luggage.

在英国，自行车车架上很早就打着钢印编号，警方鼓励车主记下来，这样捡到的车才能追查到主人；警察局会把拾得物保管好几个月。失窃物品几十年后物归原主的故事至今仍不时见报，通常靠的是一个序列号，或者刻在车座底下的名字。用火车把自行车运回家，在六十年代也很平常：那时英国铁路把包裹和自行车当作行李运送。

✨ How it is written赏析

The whole lesson is built to hold one fact back. We follow Dan's week of worry and the policeman's news in careful order, and every detail sounds like quick, efficient police work, five days and hundreds of miles. Only in the last line do we learn the date of the theft, and the title flips from praise to irony. The passive voice suits the story well: Dan does almost nothing, things are done to him and to the bike, and the people who actually stole or found it stay out of sight.

整课都是为了把一个事实压到最后。我们按顺序跟着丹担心了一星期，再听警察报喜，每个细节都像是警方高效神速：五天、几百英里。直到最后一行才知道车是哪年丢的，标题一下子从夸奖变成了讽刺。被动语态很配这个故事：丹几乎什么都没做，事情都是「被」做在他和车身上的，真正偷车和找到车的人始终没露面。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The joke is gentle, but the point about records is real. A bike can only come home after twenty years if someone kept a number and someone else bothered to check it. In China, many cities now register electric bikes with plates and owners' details for the same reason. The story also shows how a letter from the police can cause a week of fear in an honest person, a reminder that official messages should say plainly what they are about.

笑话很温和，但关于登记记录的道理是真的。一辆车二十年后还能回家，前提是有人留了编号、又有人愿意去查。如今中国许多城市给电动自行车上牌、登记车主信息，道理相同。故事也说明，一封警察来信能让一个老实人担惊受怕一星期——官方通知最好开门见山写清楚是什么事。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

The passive in different tenses各种时态的被动语态
The passive is the right tense of be plus the past participle: was or were done, is or are being done, had been done, is or are done. When the doer does not matter, leave out by.
被动＝be 的相应时态 + 过去分词。一般过去 was/were done；现在进行 is/are being done；过去完成 had been done；一般现在 is/are done。不说动作者时省略 by …。

Nearly every sentence in the lesson is passive: the past simple in sentence 4, the past perfect in 11, the present continuous in 15, and the past simple again in 21.

本课全篇几乎每句都是被动：第 4 句一般过去，第 11 句过去完成，第 15 句现在进行，第 21 句又是一般过去。

  • The letter was written last week.
  • The road is being repaired at the moment.
  • When we arrived, the food had been eaten.
The passive infinitive: expect something to be done不定式的被动：expect sth to be done
Verb plus object plus to be done: the object receives the action. Common verbs are expect, want, ask and would like.
动词 + 宾语 + to be done：宾语是动作的承受者。常见动词 expect、want、ask、would like。

In sentences 19 and 20 the bicycle does not find, it is found, so the infinitive is passive.

本课：第 19–20 句 expect 后的不定式用了被动——车是被找到，不是自己去找。

  • She wants the room to be cleaned before Friday.
  • I didn't expect the parcel to be delivered so soon.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • X has been worried all week. X 一整个星期都在发愁
    Mum has been anxious since Monday.
  • X couldn't think why … wanted him. X 想不出……为什么找他
    She couldn't think why the manager wanted her.
  • X was told that his … had been found. X 被告知他的……找到了
    Lucy was told that her ring had been found.
  • X is being sent back by … X 正被用……送回来
    Your parcel is being sent back by air.
  • X never expected … to be … X 从没想到……会被……
    We never expected our letter to be answered.
