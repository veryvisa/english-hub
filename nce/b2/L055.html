Lesson 55 · Not a Gold Mine 不是金矿

📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 55 “Not a Gold Mine”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 55《Not a Gold Mine》

B1🧠 18 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
People who dream of hidden treasure nearly saw that dream come true not long ago. A new device named the Revealer was built to locate gold under the earth. It was taken to a cave by the sea where pirates were once supposed to have hidden their gold, often leaving it behind. A team went in with the device, full of hope. Near the entrance the leader was testing the soil when the machine signalled gold. Thrilled, the team dug down two feet and found one tiny gold coin worth almost nothing. A full search of the cave turned up only an empty tin box. Even so, many people still believe the Revealer will soon find something valuable. The grammar is the present perfect passive, it is said, used to and would for past habits, and in spite of.
找到失落宝藏的梦想最近差一点成真。有人发明了一台叫「探宝器」的新机器，用来探测埋在地下的金子。这台机器被拿到海边一个山洞里试用，据说从前海盗常把金子藏在那里，埋下之后又往往没有回来取。一支搜寻队带着机器进了山洞，满心希望找到财宝。队长在洞口附近查看泥土时，机器显示地下有金子。大家兴奋极了，挖了一个两英尺深的坑，最后只挖出一枚几乎一文不值的小金币。他们又把整个山洞彻底搜了一遍，除了一只空铁皮箱，什么也没找到。尽管如此，很多人仍然相信「探宝器」不久就能找出值钱的东西。本课学现在完成时被动 has been invented、it is said、used to / would 表过去习惯和 in spite of。

  1. Why did people think there might be gold in the cave?为什么人们认为那个山洞里可能有金子？
  2. What did the search party find in the end?搜寻队最后找到了什么？

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander for New Concept English (Longman, 1967); the Revealer and the search are invented.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为《新概念英语》（朗文，1967）自撰；「探宝器」和这次搜寻都是虚构的。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

Metal detectors became popular with the public in the nineteen-sixties and seventies, and treasure hunting with them is now a common hobby in Britain. Most finds are coins, buckles and bits of metal of little worth, but a few hobbyists have found great hoards; the Staffordshire Hoard of Anglo-Saxon gold, found in a field in two thousand and nine, is the most famous. British law requires such treasure to be reported, and museums may buy it. Pirate gold buried in caves belongs mostly to legend; historians note that real pirates usually spent their takings rather than burying them.

金属探测器在二十世纪六七十年代开始在民间流行，如今在英国用它寻宝是很常见的爱好。大多数发现是硬币、扣环和不值钱的金属片，但也有少数爱好者找到了巨大的窖藏：最有名的是二〇〇九年在一片田里发现的斯塔福德郡盎格鲁-撒克逊金器窖藏。英国法律规定这类宝藏必须申报，博物馆可以收购。山洞里埋着海盗金子大多属于传说；历史学家指出，真正的海盗通常把抢来的钱花掉，而不是埋起来。

✨ How it is written赏析

The title gives away the ending before the story starts, so the interest lies in how the hope is built and then let down. The first sentence uses almost, which warns the reader early. Then the language swells: a new machine, a legend of pirates, a team armed and hoping, a signal, great excitement. The result is told in the smallest words possible: one small coin, almost worthless, an empty box. The last sentence is quietly ironic, because the machine that was supposed to reveal something has mainly revealed how stubborn human hope can be.

标题在故事开始前就把结局说了，所以看点在于希望怎样被吹起来又落空。第一句用了 almost，早早给读者提了个醒。接着语言越来越膨胀：新机器、海盗传说、整装出发满怀希望的队伍、信号、群情激动。结果却用最小的字眼交代：一枚小金币，几乎不值钱，一只空箱子。最后一句是不动声色的反讽：本该「揭示」宝藏的机器，揭示出的主要是人的希望有多顽固。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The story is a small study in how people respond to weak evidence. One tiny coin should lower expectations, yet the public stays confident, partly because the dream is more attractive than the data. Modern readers will recognise the pattern in get-rich schemes, miracle cures and gadgets sold on big promises. A sensible reader asks what a test actually showed, not what it was hoped to show. Still, the story is not cruel: hope keeps people exploring, and real discoveries are sometimes made by those who refuse to give up after a poor first result.

这个故事小小地考察了人们怎样对待薄弱的证据。一枚小金币本该让人降低预期，公众却依然信心满满，部分原因是梦想比数据更诱人。现代读者能在发财计划、神奇疗法和靠大话推销的小玩意儿里认出同样的模式。明智的读者会问：一次测试实际证明了什么，而不是人们希望它证明什么。不过故事并不刻薄：希望让人继续探索，有时真正的发现正是那些第一次结果不理想仍不放弃的人做出来的。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 2第 2 句 (begins开头 “A new machine …”)

  • called: a past participle after the noun, meaning named.called …：过去分词作后置定语，「名叫……的」。
  • has been invented and has been used: the present perfect passive.has been invented / has been used：现在完成时被动。

Sentence 4第 4 句 (begins开头 “The machine was …”)

  • it is said: a phrase showing the story is only reported.it is said：据说，插入语，说明消息来源不确定。
  • used to do: a past habit that has stopped.used to do：过去常常做（现在不做了）。

Sentence 5第 5 句 (begins开头 “The pirates would …”)

  • would plus verb: a repeated action in the past.would + 动词原形：过去反复的习惯动作。
  • fail to do: not manage to do.fail to do：没能做……。

Sentence 6第 6 句 (begins开头 “Armed with the …”)

  • Armed with: a past participle phrase, meaning carrying.Armed with …：过去分词短语作状语，「带着……」。
  • hoping to: an -ing phrase saying how they felt as they went.hoping to …：现在分词短语表伴随。

Sentence 11第 11 句 (begins开头 “In spite of …”)

  • in spite of: although, followed by a noun or pronoun.in spite of：尽管，后接名词或代词。
  • something of value: something valuable.something of value：有价值的东西，= something valuable。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

used to do and would do: past habitsused to do / would do：过去的习惯
used to do means a past habit or state that has stopped; would do is only for repeated past actions, not states. Do not mix them up with be used to doing, which means be accustomed to.
used to do 表示过去常做、现在不再做，也可说过去的状态；would do 只说过去反复的动作，不说状态。不要和 be used to doing（习惯于）混淆。

Sentence 4 uses used to and sentence 5 uses would, both about the pirates' old habits.

本课：第 4 句用 used to，第 5 句用 would——都在讲海盗过去的习惯。

  • I used to live in Beijing.
  • Every summer we would go swimming in the lake.
The present perfect passive: has been done现在完成时被动：has been done
has or have been plus past participle means something has been done and the result matters now. News reports use it for new inventions and finds.
has/have been + 过去分词：某事已被做、结果与现在有关；新闻里报告新发明、新发现常用这个形式。

In sentences 2 and 3 the new machine has been made and has been put to use.

本课：第 2 句和第 3 句——新机器已经被发明出来，也已经被用来探测金子。

  • A new bridge has been built across the river.
  • The missing boy has been found.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • Dreams of … almost came true recently. ……的梦想最近差点成真
    Hopes of a quick win almost came true last night.
  • A new … called … has been invented. 一种名叫……的新……被发明了
    A new robot called Linko has been designed for schools.
  • … where, it is said, … used to … 据说……过去常常在那里……
    an old inn where, it is said, highwaymen used to sleep
  • Armed with …, X went into … hoping to … 带着……，X 进了……，希望……
    Armed with torches, the boys went into the tunnel hoping to find the lost cat.
  • Even so, plenty of people are sure that … 尽管如此，很多人仍有信心……
    Even so, many fans are sure the team will win next year.

Our model summary本站范文

A new machine called the Revealer has been invented to locate buried gold. A team took it into a seaside cave where pirates were said to have hidden their gold. Near the entrance it signalled gold, so they dug a hole and found one tiny, almost worthless coin. A full search revealed only an empty tin box. Even so, many people believe the machine will soon find something valuable.（69 词）
