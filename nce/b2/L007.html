Lesson 7 · Too Late 为时太晚

📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 7 “Too Late”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 7《Too Late》

B1🧠 16 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
The plane is late, and detectives spend the whole morning at the airport. They are there for a precious packet of diamonds coming from South Africa, because a tip-off a few hours before has warned the police that thieves will try to take it. When the plane lands, some officers are posted inside and some out on the airfield; two men bring the packet into customs, two detectives watch the door and two more open it, only to find nothing inside but stones and sand. The grammar is the past continuous with when and while, plus the past perfect and the future in the past, would do.
飞机晚点了，侦探们在机场等了一上午。他们在等一包从南非运来的贵重钻石，因为几小时前有人向警方报信，说窃贼要来偷。飞机一到，一部分侦探守在主楼里，另一部分守在停机坪上；两个人把包裹搬进海关，两名侦探把门，另两名打开包裹——里面装的全是石头和沙子。本课学过去进行时配 when / while，以及过去完成时和「过去将来」would do。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. Did the detectives manage to save the diamonds?侦探们保住钻石了吗？
  2. What does the title mean?标题「为时太晚」是什么意思？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander for New Concept English (Longman, 1967); an invented crime story, not a report of a real robbery.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为《新概念英语》（朗文，1967）自撰；这是虚构的罪案故事，不是真实盗窃的报道。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

For most of the twentieth century South Africa was one of the world's leading sources of diamonds, and rough stones were shipped to Europe for cutting and sale. Sending them by air was faster and was thought to be safer than by sea, but airports and freight sheds became a target for thieves. Britain in the 1960s also had a run of famous, well-planned robberies, and the idea of a clever gang outwitting the police was popular in newspapers and films. Readers of the lesson would have recognised the type of story at once. The setting of a customs house matters too: valuable goods coming from abroad had to be declared and checked, which explains why the packet is opened there.

二十世纪大部分时间里，南非是世界上最主要的钻石产地之一，原石运到欧洲切割、出售。空运比海运快，也被认为更安全，可机场和货运仓库就成了窃贼的目标。六十年代的英国还接连发生过几起策划周密、轰动一时的大劫案，聪明的团伙智胜警方，是报纸和电影里很受欢迎的题材。当时的读者一看就认得出这类故事。海关这个场景也有讲究：从国外运来的贵重物品要申报、查验，所以包裹是在那里打开的。

✨ How it is written赏析

The title gives away the ending before the story begins, and yet the story still works. That is because the interest lies in how, not in whether. Every sentence before the last shows the police doing things properly: waiting, splitting into groups, guarding the door, opening the packet in the right place. The tenses build this calm, organised picture, with long actions in the past continuous running side by side. Then the last line breaks it with an exclamation mark. We are never told when or how the swap was made, and that gap is the cleverest part. The reader has to work it out: the warning itself may have been a trick to keep the detectives in the wrong place.

标题在故事开始前就交了底，故事却照样成立。因为看点在「怎么回事」，不在「成不成」。最后一句之前的每一句都在写警方把事做得规规矩矩：等候、分组、守门、在该打开的地方打开包裹。时态营造出这幅冷静有序的画面，一个个长动作用过去进行时并排展开。然后最后一行用一个感叹号把它打破。我们始终不知道调包是何时、怎样完成的，这个空白正是最巧妙的地方。读者得自己琢磨：那条报信本身也许就是个圈套，好把侦探们引到错误的地方。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The lesson makes a point that security people still repeat: a guard on the last door is useless if the goods were taken earlier on the journey. Today, valuable freight is tracked at every step, sealed and checked by weight, precisely so that a swap of this kind would be noticed. The story is also a good exercise in reading what is not said. For learners, it shows how much meaning can sit in a gap between two sentences. Chinese readers will know the same pleasure from detective fiction, where the writer lets you see every move the police make and still hides the one that matters.

课文讲了一个安保行业至今还在重复的道理：如果货物在途中早已被偷，守住最后一道门毫无用处。如今贵重货物每一站都要追踪、封签、称重核对，正是为了让这种调包被发现。这个故事也是阅读「言外之意」的好练习：它向学习者展示，两句话之间的空白里可以藏着多少意思。中国读者在侦探小说里也熟悉同样的乐趣：作者让你看清警方的每一步，却偏偏藏起最关键的那一步。

💬 Discuss and write讨论与写作

    你认为钻石是什么时候、怎么被偷的？用 60 个词写出你的推理。
  2. Write a short story with a title that gives away the ending. Does it make the story less interesting?
    写一个标题就交了底的短故事。这样会让故事变得不好看吗？

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 1第 1 句 (begins开头 “The plane was …”)

  • were waiting with all morning: the past continuous stresses that the waiting went on for the whole morning.were waiting + all morning：过去进行时强调一整个上午都在等。

Sentence 4第 4 句 (begins开头 “someone had told …”)

  • had told: the past perfect, because the tip-off came before the waiting.had told：过去完成时，「报信」发生在「等待」之前。
  • would try: the future seen from a past moment.would try：过去将来时，从当时看「将要」做的事。

Sentence 9第 9 句 (begins开头 “while others were …”)

  • some and others divide a group into two parts.some … others …：一些……另一些……。
  • when goes with the short action of landing, and while with the long one of waiting.when + 短动作（arrived），while + 长动作（were waiting）。

Sentence 15第 15 句 (begins开头 “the precious parcel …”)

  • to one's surprise: introduces something that the people did not expect.to one's surprise：令某人吃惊的是。
  • be full of: filled with. The diamonds had been swapped long before, so the detectives were too late.be full of：装满。钻石早已被换掉，所以侦探们「来晚了」。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

The past continuous with when and while过去进行时配 when / while
When a short action happens in the middle of a longer one, the longer one takes the past continuous and the short one the past simple. while usually goes with the long action and when with the short one. Two long actions going on together can both be in the past continuous.
一个较长的动作正在进行时，另一个短动作发生：长的用过去进行时，短的用一般过去时。while 后面常接长动作，when 后面常接短动作。两个长动作同时进行，可都用过去进行时。

In sentence 1 the waiting fills the morning. In sentences 6 to 9, when introduces the short arrival and while joins two waitings at once. In sentences 12 and 13, a long action and a short one come together again.

本课：第 1 句讲一上午都在等；第 6–9 句，when 后是短动作 arrived，while 连接两个同时进行的等待；第 12、13 句又是一长一短。

  • When the lights went out, we were having dinner.
  • While Dad was cooking, Mum was reading.
The past perfect and the future in the past过去完成时与过去将来时
Inside a past story, something earlier than the main time takes had done, and something still to come from that point takes would do.
在过去的故事里：比「那时」更早的事用 had done；从「那时」看将来的事用 would do。

In sentences 3 to 5, the warning came before the waiting, so had told; what the warning said was still in the future then, so would try.

本课：第 3–5 句——报信发生在侦探们等之前，所以用 had told；报信的内容是「窃贼将要偷」，所以用 would try。

  • When I got to the station, the train had already left.
  • She said she would call me the next day.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • X was late and Y were waiting … all morning. X 晚了，Y 等了一上午。
    The bus was late and we were standing in the rain all evening.
  • Some of …, while others … 一些……，而另一些……
    Some of the guests were dancing, while others were chatting outside.
  • To their surprise, … was full of … 令他们吃惊的是，……里装满了……
    To their surprise, the old box was full of letters.
  • keep guard at … 在……守卫
    Two dogs keep guard at the farm gate.
