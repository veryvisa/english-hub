📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 33 “Out of the Darkness”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 33《Out of the Darkness》

B1🧠 17 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
A girl lay in hospital for almost a week before she could tell anyone what had happened. One afternoon she had left the coast in a little boat and been caught by a storm. Near nightfall the boat hit a rock, so she jumped overboard, and she spent the entire night in the sea, swimming eight miles. At dawn she saw a light ahead, high on the cliffs, and knew land was close. Once ashore she fought her way up towards it, and after that her memory goes blank. A day later she woke up in a hospital bed. The grammar is the past perfect, after or on with an -ing form, and find oneself.
一个女孩住进了医院，将近一个星期后才能讲出自己的遭遇。那天下午她驾一条小船从海边出发，遇上了暴风雨。傍晚时船撞上礁石，她跳进海里，在水里泡了整整一夜，游了八英里。第二天一早，她看见前方有一盏灯，灯高高地挂在悬崖上，她知道岸近了。上岸后，她拼命朝那盏灯爬上悬崖——之后的事她就不记得了。一天后醒来，她发现自己躺在医院里。本课学 过去完成时 had happened / had seen、after/on + 动名词 和 find oneself。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. How did the girl know she was close to land?女孩为什么知道自己离岸边不远了？
  2. How long was she in the sea, and how far did she swim?她在水里待了多久，游了多远？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander for New Concept English (Longman, 1967); the girl and the place are not named, and the story is presented as a news-style account.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为《新概念英语》（朗文，1967）自撰；女孩和地点都没有名字，故事以新闻报道的口吻写成。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

Lights high on sea cliffs usually mean a lighthouse or a coastguard station, and for centuries they were the main way sailors knew where land lay at night. Britain's coast is lined with them. Survival experts note that the greatest danger in a long swim is not tiredness but cold: in British waters, even in summer, a person in the sea loses heat many times faster than in air. A whole night in the water and a swim of eight miles would be an extraordinary feat, and real cases of that kind are rare enough to make the news. The lesson does not tell us the time of year or the sea temperature, so treat the details as a story, not a record.

海边悬崖上高处的灯，通常是灯塔或海岸警卫站；几百年来，水手在夜里靠它们判断陆地在哪儿。英国的海岸线上到处都是。求生专家指出，长时间游泳最大的危险不是累，而是冷：在英国海域，即使夏天，人在水里散失热量的速度也比在空气中快好多倍。在水里泡一整夜、游八英里是了不起的壮举，真实发生的这类事少到足以上新闻。课文没交代季节和水温，所以把细节当故事看，不当记录看。

✨ How it is written赏析

The story begins at the end, with the girl in hospital, and then goes back to tell what led there, so the reader knows she lives and reads for how. The middle is a chain of short actions with almost no feeling words; the danger is shown through facts: a storm, a rock, a whole night, eight miles. Then a single detail, a light placed high, gives her and us a reason to hope. The last lines drop away just as her memory does, with a blank and then a hospital bed, so the gap in the telling copies the gap in her mind.

故事从结局讲起——女孩在医院里——再倒回去讲怎么到了这一步，读者知道她活了下来，读的是「怎么活下来」。中段是一串短促的动作，几乎没有表达感情的词；危险靠事实呈现：暴风雨、礁石、一整夜、八英里。然后一个细节——一盏高处的灯——给了她，也给了我们希望的理由。最后几行像她的记忆一样突然断开：一片空白，然后是病床，叙述的空缺模仿了她脑中的空缺。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The girl survives because she keeps going and because she reads one small clue correctly: a light that high must be on land. Survival stories often turn on that mix of effort and judgment, not effort alone. Readers today might also ask the practical questions the lesson skips: did anyone know she had gone out, did she have a life jacket, and why was there no search? Safety advice on coasts in China and around the world now stresses exactly these points, telling someone your plan and wearing a buoyancy aid.

女孩活下来，是因为她坚持不放弃，也因为她读对了一个小线索：那么高的灯一定在陆地上。求生故事往往靠「努力加判断」，而不是光靠努力。今天的读者也许还会问课文略过的实际问题：有人知道她出海了吗？她穿救生衣了吗？为什么没有人去搜救？如今中国和世界各地的海滨安全提示强调的正是这几点：把行程告诉别人，穿上浮力背心。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentence 1第 1 句 (begins开头 “Nearly a week …”)

  • ... passed before ...: in Chinese the order is turned round, 'only after a week could she'.… passed before …：过了……才……。中文要倒过来说。
  • what had happened: the past perfect, because the events came before she told them.what had happened：过去完成时，事情发生在她讲述之前。

Sentence 6第 6 句 (begins开头 “and was caught …”)

  • set out from: start a journey from a place.set out from：从……出发。
  • be caught in a storm: the passive form means she met it without wanting to.be caught in a storm：遇上暴风雨（被动形式，表示遭遇）。

Sentence 11第 11 句 (begins开头 “after spending the …”)

  • after plus an -ing form: the subject is the same as in the main clause.after + 动名词：在……之后，主语与主句相同。

Sentence 18第 18 句 (begins开头 “On arriving at …”)

  • on plus an -ing form means as soon as.on + 动名词：一……就……。
  • she had seen: a relative clause after light, with the relative pronoun dropped, in the past perfect.she had seen：修饰 light 的定语从句，省略了关系代词，用过去完成时。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

after, before and on with an -ing formafter / before / on + 动名词
A verb after a preposition takes the -ing form. after doing means once that was done, before doing means earlier than that, and on doing means as soon as. The person doing the -ing action must be the subject of the main clause.
介词后面的动词用 -ing 形式。after doing＝做完……之后，before doing＝做……之前，on doing＝一……就……。用这种结构时，-ing 的动作者必须就是主句主语。

Sentence 11 uses after with an -ing form for the night in the water, and sentence 18 uses on with an -ing form for the moment she reached land.

本课：第 11 句用 after 加动名词说游了一夜之后，第 18 句用 on 加动名词说一上岸。

  • After finishing his homework, he went out to play.
  • On hearing the news, she burst into tears.
The past perfect: the past before the past过去完成时：过去的过去
When telling a past story, use had plus the past participle for something that came even earlier. It often appears in object clauses and relative clauses.
讲过去的故事时，如果要提到在那之前更早的事，用 had + 过去分词。常见于宾语从句（explain what had happened）和定语从句（the light she had seen）。

Sentences 3 and 20 both use the past perfect for things earlier than the moment being told.

本课：第 3 句 what had happened，第 20 句 she had seen——都早于叙述的那个时刻。

  • She told me what she had seen.
  • We found the key that we had lost.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • A whole … passed before X could … 整整过了……，X 才能……
    A whole month passed before he could walk again.
  • X set out from … and was caught in … X 从……出发，遇上了……
    They set out from the village and were caught in heavy snow.
  • After spending … in …, X … 在……度过……之后，X……
    After spending two years in Japan, she spoke the language well.
  • On reaching …, X … 一到……，X 就……
    On reaching the top, we sat down to rest.
  • X woke up and found himself in … X 醒来发现自己在……
    Tom woke up and found himself in a strange room.

Our model summary本站范文

One afternoon a girl went out to sea in a little boat, and a storm blew up. In the evening the boat hit a rock, so she jumped into the sea. She swam all night and covered eight miles. At dawn she saw a light on a cliff and knew the shore was near. After landing she climbed towards it, but then fainted. She woke in hospital, and could only tell her story a week later.（76 词）
