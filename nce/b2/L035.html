📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 35 “Stop Thief”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 35《Stop Thief》

B1🧠 16 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
Roy Trenton was once a taxi driver, but not long ago he switched to driving a bus, and he has no regrets; he finds the new job far livelier. Recently, driving down Catford Street, he saw two men dash out of a shop towards a car that stood waiting, one of them holding a bag of cash. Roy reacted at once and aimed his bus straight at them. The man with the money was so scared that he let go of it. As they tried to escape by car, Roy rammed their car from behind. As the smashed car limped off, he pulled up and rang the police, who soon caught the easily spotted car and both men. The grammar is the present perfect and present continuous, see someone do, such ... that, and while and as.
罗伊·特伦顿以前开出租车，前不久改开公共汽车，一点也不后悔，觉得新工作刺激多了。最近他开车经过卡特福德街时，看见两个贼从一家店里冲出来，朝一辆等着的汽车跑去，其中一个提着装满钱的包。罗伊反应很快，把公共汽车直冲贼开过去，拿钱的那个吓得把包都扔了。贼想开车逃跑，罗伊把车撞上了他们的车尾。那辆撞坏的车开走时，罗伊停车打电话报警。车撞得很厉害、很好认，不久警察就拦住了它，两个人都被抓了。本课学 现在完成时与现在进行时、see sb do、such … that 和 while / as 引导的时间从句。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. Why does Roy like his new job?罗伊为什么喜欢他的新工作？
  2. Why was the getaway car easy for the police to find?警察为什么很容易就找到了那辆车？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander for New Concept English (Longman, 1967); Roy Trenton is invented, and Catford is a real district of south-east London.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为《新概念英语》（朗文，1967）自撰；罗伊·特伦顿是虚构的，卡特福德是伦敦东南部的真实地名。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

In the London of the nineteen sixties, smash-and-grab raids on shops and banks were a regular news item, and getaway cars were a standard part of them. Bus drivers then worked with a conductor, and many London buses were large double-deckers weighing several tons, which helps explain why a driver might feel confident aiming one at a car. Today British police and transport companies advise drivers not to tackle criminals themselves, but to report and keep a safe distance; stories of brave drivers still appear, and so do warnings about the risks.

六十年代的伦敦，砸窗抢劫商店和银行是常见新闻，逃跑用车是其中的标配。那时的公共汽车司机有售票员搭档，伦敦许多公共汽车是重达好几吨的大双层巴士，这也能解释为什么一个司机敢开着它冲向一辆小汽车。如今英国警方和交通公司建议司机不要亲自对付罪犯，而是报警、保持安全距离；勇敢司机的故事仍会见报，风险提醒也同样会见报。

✨ How it is written赏析

The first paragraph is a quiet job update, and it seems almost dull, until we see that it sets up the word exciting and then pays it off. The action part runs on short main verbs, acted, drove, dropped, and the clauses with while and as keep two movements going at once, the thieves trying to flee and Roy chasing them. The ending is calm and official: the damaged car is easy to spot, and the arrest takes one plain sentence. The humour lies in the gap between Roy's mild remark about his new work and the chase that proves it.

第一段是一次平淡的工作近况介绍，几乎有点乏味，读到后面才发现它是为「刺激」这个词埋伏笔，然后兑现。动作部分靠短促的主要动词推进——行动、开车、扔下——while 和 as 引导的从句让两个动作同时进行：贼要逃，罗伊在追。结尾平静而公事公办：撞坏的车好认，抓人只用一句平常话。幽默在于罗伊谈新工作时那句平淡的话，与证明这句话的那场追逐之间的落差。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

Roy is a hero in the story, but modern readers may ask whether he was right. A bus full of passengers driven at people and into a car could have hurt many innocent riders. Most transport firms in Canada, Britain and China now train drivers to stay with their vehicle and call the police, because the safety of passengers comes first. The lesson's real lesson may be about quick thinking and calling for help, the phone call is what actually ends the case, rather than about the risky driving.

故事里罗伊是英雄，但现代读者可能会问他做得对不对。一辆满载乘客的公交车冲向人、撞向汽车，可能伤到很多无辜的乘客。如今加拿大、英国和中国的大多数交通公司都培训司机守在车上、报警，因为乘客安全第一。这一课真正的启发，也许在于反应快和求助——真正结束案子的是那通电话——而不是那段冒险驾驶。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 4第 4 句 (begins开头 “and he has …”)

  • has not regretted: the present perfect, from the day he changed until now.has not regretted：现在完成时，从当上公交车司机到现在一直不后悔。
  • however, set off by commas inside the sentence, marks a contrast.however：然而，插在句中，用逗号隔开。

Sentence 5第 5 句 (begins开头 “He is finding …”)

  • is finding: the present continuous for how he feels at this stage. find plus object plus adjective.is finding：现在进行时表示现阶段的感受，find + 宾语 + 形容词。
  • far before a comparative means much.far more：远比……更，far 修饰比较级。

Sentence 8第 8 句 (begins开头 “he saw two …”)

  • see someone do something: to see the whole action from start to end.see sb do：看见某人做了（全过程）。
  • a waiting car: a car standing ready.a waiting car：一辆等候着的汽车。

Sentence 13第 13 句 (begins开头 “The one with …”)

  • the one with: the person who had something.the one with …：拿着……的那个人。
  • such a plus a noun plus that: to such a degree that.such a + 名词 + that：如此……以至于。

Sentence 17第 17 句 (begins开头 “While the battered …”)

  • while plus the past continuous: during the time that.while + 过去进行时：在……的过程中。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

see someone do and see someone doing
see, watch or hear plus object plus the base form means the whole action was seen or heard. With the -ing form it means you caught the action in progress.
see / watch / hear + 宾语 + 动词原形：看到（听到）动作的全过程；+ 动词 -ing：看到（听到）动作正在进行的片段。

Sentences 8 and 9 use see with the base form: Roy saw the whole dash from shop to car.

本课：第 8–9 句用 see 加原形——罗伊看见了整个冲出、跑向车的过程。

  • I saw him cross the road.
  • I saw him crossing the road when I looked out.
such ... that and so ... that
such takes a noun, with a or an and an adjective if needed; so takes an adjective or adverb. get such a fright that means to be so scared that.
such + (a/an) + (形容词) + 名词 + that；so + 形容词/副词 + that。get such a fright that＝吓得……。

Sentences 13 and 14 use such a fright that, and the result is the dropped bag.

本课：第 13–14 句用 such a fright that，结果是包掉了。

  • It was such a hot day that we went swimming.
  • The day was so hot that we went swimming.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • X once …, but not long ago he became … X 以前……，不过前不久他当上了……
    Sara once taught, but not long ago she became a nurse.
  • X is finding his new … far more … X 觉得新的…………得多
    She is finding her new school far more friendly.
  • X saw … rush out of … X 看见……从……冲出来
    I saw a cat dash out of the garden.
  • X was given such a scare that … X 被吓得……
    The boy was given such a scare that he screamed.
  • X was easy to recognize. X 很好认
    His red coat was easy to recognize.

Our model summary本站范文

Roy Trenton was once a taxi driver, but now he drives a bus and finds it more exciting. Recently he saw two thieves run out of a shop with a bag of money. He drove straight at them, and one was so frightened that he let the bag fall. As they escaped by car, Roy crashed into it and then called the police. The damaged car was easy to find, and both men were arrested.（75 词）
