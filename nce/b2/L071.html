Lesson 71 · A Famous Clock 议会大厦的钟

📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 71 “A Famous Clock”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 71《A Famous Clock》

B1🧠 18 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
Big Ben is among the first sights a visitor to London notices, and the radio carries its sound around the world. Had fire not destroyed the old Parliament building in 1834, the clock would never have been put up. It is named after Sir Benjamin Hall, who was in charge of making it while the new building went up. Huge as it is, it keeps excellent time, and staff from Greenwich arrange for it to be tested twice daily. Radio listeners hear it live, thanks to microphones in the tower. It has hardly ever been wrong; the one famous lapse came when a painter at work up there hung his paint pot on a hand and dragged it behind. This is a short guide to a famous landmark. The grammar is the third conditional in the passive, take its name from, be responsible for and have something done.
到伦敦，你最先看到的东西之一就是大本钟，通过英国广播公司，全世界都能听到它的钟声。要不是 1834 年议会大厦被烧毁，这座大钟根本不会建起来。它的名字来自本杰明·霍尔爵士，新议会大厦修建时，他负责造钟。大本钟不仅巨大，而且极其准确，格林尼治天文台的工作人员每天请人检查两次。电台里能听到它正在敲响的声音，因为钟楼里接了话筒。大本钟很少出错，只有一次报错了时间：一个在钟楼上干活的油漆匠把一桶油漆挂在指针上，让它走慢了！本课是一篇介绍名胜的说明文，学第三种条件句的被动式、take its name from、be responsible for 和 have sth done。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

Strictly speaking, Big Ben is the name of the great bell inside the tower rather than the clock or the tower, which was renamed the Elizabeth Tower in 2012. Where the name came from is still argued about: many accounts credit Sir Benjamin Hall, who oversaw the works, while others suggest a famous boxer of the day, Ben Caunt. The clock was designed so that it could be checked against Greenwich by telegraph, and for fine adjustment the keepers add or remove old pennies on the pendulum. It has stopped or slowed now and then; one well-recorded case came in 1949, when a flock of starlings settled on the minute hand and held it back by several minutes. From 2017 to 2022 the tower was wrapped in scaffolding for a long repair, and the bells were silent for most of that time.

严格说来，大本钟是塔里那口大钟（bell）的名字，而不是时钟或钟楼；钟楼 2012 年改名为伊丽莎白塔。名字的来历至今有争论：很多说法归于主持工程的本杰明·霍尔爵士，也有人说是当时一位有名的拳击手本·考恩特。这座钟设计时就能通过电报与格林尼治对时；做细微调整时，守钟人会在钟摆上加减几枚旧便士硬币。它偶尔停过或走慢过，有记录的一次在 1949 年：一群椋鸟落在分针上，把它拖慢了好几分钟。2017 年到 2022 年，钟楼包在脚手架里大修，那段时间钟声大多沉寂。

✨ How it is written赏析

The passage moves like a guided tour. It starts with what a visitor sees, then looks back to history through a third conditional, then gives the name, then the facts of size and accuracy, and saves a story for the end. Sentence 9 is short and flat, which prepares us for the however that follows. The final detail is comic because it is so small compared with everything before it: a building fire, Parliament, an observatory, and in the end a single pot of paint. The exclamation mark invites the reader to smile.

这篇像一次导游讲解：从游客看到的东西开始，用第三种条件句回看历史，再讲名字，再讲大小和准确，把故事留到最后。第 9 句短而平，为后面的 however 做铺垫。最后的细节之所以好笑，是因为它和前面的一切相比太小了：一场大楼火灾、议会、天文台，到头来是一桶油漆。感叹号邀请读者会心一笑。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The lesson treats accuracy as something a machine simply has, but the real story is about people. Big Ben stays accurate because keepers wind it, oil it and adjust it by hand, week after week, and because it was built in a way that allowed checking. That is a useful lesson for any system we rely on, from bus timetables to computer networks: reliability is maintained, not owned. Chinese readers may compare the bell and drum towers of Beijing or Xi'an, which once kept time for whole cities; there too, the tower was only as good as the people who looked after it.

课文把「准确」当成机器天生就有的东西，但真实的故事是关于人的。大本钟之所以准，是因为守钟人一周又一周地上弦、上油、手工调整，也因为它当初就造得便于校对。这对我们依赖的任何系统都是有用的一课，从公交时刻表到计算机网络：可靠是维护出来的，不是拥有的。中国读者可以对比北京或西安的钟楼、鼓楼，它们曾为整座城市报时；在那里，钟楼也只有在有人精心照看时才靠得住。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentence 2第 2 句 (begins开头 “the famous clock …”)

  • one of the first things: one of plus a plural noun.one of the first things：最先……的东西之一，one of + 复数。
  • can be heard: a modal passive.can be heard：情态动词被动。

Sentence 3第 3 句 (begins开头 “If the Houses …”)

  • The third conditional in the passive: had not been done in the if clause, would have been done in the main clause.第三种条件句的被动式：if 从句 had not been done，主句 would have been done。
  • The fact: the building did burn, and so the clock was built.事实：议会大厦被烧了，所以才建了这座钟。

Sentence 5第 5 句 (begins开头 “Big Ben takes …”)

  • take its name from: be named after.take its name from：名字来自……。
  • be responsible for: be in charge of; the making of: an -ing form used as a noun.be responsible for：负责。the making of：动名词作名词用。
  • were being built: past continuous passive.were being built：过去进行时被动。

Sentence 6第 6 句 (begins开头 “It is not …”)

  • of plus a noun works like an adjective: of immense size means very big.of + 名词 = 形容词：of immense size = immensely large。
  • not only ... but ... as well: and also.not only … but … as well：不仅……而且……。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

The third conditional in the passive第三种条件句的被动式
If plus subject plus had (not) been done, then would (never) have been done. It is still contrary to the past; read it by turning it back into fact: the building did burn, and the clock was then built.
If + 主语 + had (not) been + 过去分词, 主语 + would (never) have been + 过去分词。仍然是与过去事实相反；先还原事实再读：实际上大楼被烧了，钟才建了起来。

Sentences 3 and 4 put both halves in the passive, since the building and the clock are both things that were acted on.

本课：第 3、4 句两半都用了被动式，因为大楼和大钟都是被动的对象。

  • If the letter had been posted on time, it would have been delivered yesterday.
  • If the bridge had not been repaired, the town would have been cut off.
Names and duties: take its name from, be named after, be responsible for命名与负责：take its name from / be named after / be responsible for
A takes its name from B means the same as A is named after B; the second is passive, the first active. be responsible for is followed by a noun or -ing.
A takes its name from B = A is named after B（以 B 命名）；be responsible for + 名词/动名词：负责。注意 be named after 是被动，take its name from 是主动。

Sentence 5 uses takes its name from for the name, and the same sentence uses was responsible for to give Hall's job.

本课：第 5 句用 takes its name from 讲名字来历，同一句里用 was responsible for 讲霍尔的职责。

  • The city takes its name from the river that runs through it.
  • She is responsible for training new staff.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • When you go to …, among the first sights you will see is … 你去……时，最先看到的东西之一是……
    When you go to Beijing, among the first sights you will see is the Forbidden City.
  • If … had not been … in …, … would never have been … 要不是……在……年被……，……就根本不会被……
    If the old school had not been closed in 1990, the library would never have been built.
  • X takes its name from …, who was responsible for … X 的名字来自……，他负责……
    The bridge takes its name from an engineer who was responsible for its design.
  • X is not only of immense …, but is extremely … as well. X 不仅极……，而且非常……
    The temple is not only of great age, but is extremely beautiful as well.
  • X has seldom let us down. Just once, … X 很少出错。不过有一次……
    My old car has seldom let us down. Just once, it stopped on the motorway.

Our model summary本站范文

Big Ben is one of London's best-known sights, and its chimes are broadcast around the world. It exists only because a fire destroyed the old Parliament building in 1834. It is named after Sir Benjamin Hall, who was in charge of making it. The clock is huge and very accurate, and Greenwich has it checked twice daily. It has almost never gone wrong, except once, when a painter hung his paint pot on a hand.（75 词）
