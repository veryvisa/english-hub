Lesson 57 · Can I Help You, Madam 夫人，我能为您做点什么吗？

📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 57 “Can I Help You, Madam”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 57《Can I Help You, Madam》

B1🧠 16 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
A woman wearing jeans paused outside the window of a smart, pricey shop, then went in and asked to look at a dress on display. The man who came to help disliked her clothes. With a scornful look, he claimed the dress had already gone. She left in anger and made up her mind to teach him a lesson. Next morning she came back in a fur coat, holding a handbag and a long umbrella. She found the same rude man and asked for the same dress. He had no idea who she was and was now keen to please. It took him a real struggle to climb into the window and fetch it. She took one look and said she did not care for it. Then she had him bring out nearly everything on display, enjoying every minute, before buying the dress she had wanted from the start. The grammar is participle phrases, who in relative clauses, with plus a noun and phrase, and make sb do.
一个穿牛仔裤的女人站在一家高级商店的橱窗前。她犹豫了一下，终于走进去，要求看看橱窗里的一条连衣裙。接待她的店员看不惯她的穿着，轻蔑地瞟了她一眼，说那条裙子已经卖掉了。女人气冲冲地走了，决定第二天惩罚这个店员。第二天早上她又来了，穿着皮大衣，一手拎手提包，一手拿长柄伞。她找到那个无礼的店员，要看同一条裙子。店员没认出她，这回殷勤得很，费了好大劲爬进橱窗把裙子取出来。她一看就说不喜欢。她让店员把橱窗里几乎所有东西都拿出来给她看，自得其乐，最后才买下她最初要的那条裙子。本课学分词短语 Glancing at … / Not realizing …、定语从句 who served her、with + 名词 + 介词短语和 make sb do。

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander for New Concept English (Longman, 1967); the woman and the shop are invented.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为《新概念英语》（朗文，1967）自撰；那个女人和那家商店都是虚构的。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

In the nineteen-sixties jeans were still mainly work and leisure clothing, and many people would not wear them to a smart shop in town, while a fur coat signalled wealth. High-end shops depended on assistants who judged customers quickly, and many people felt they were treated by how they looked. The same scene became famous decades later in a well-known Hollywood film, and it still happens today in luxury stores. Many shops now train staff to treat every visitor alike, partly because rich customers often dress casually, and a single bad review shared online can cost far more than one sale.

二十世纪六十年代，牛仔裤主要还是干活和休闲时穿的，很多人不会穿着它去城里的高级商店，而一件皮大衣则意味着富有。高档商店靠店员快速判断顾客，许多人觉得自己被人「以貌取人」。几十年后，同样的场景在一部知名好莱坞电影里成了经典桥段，今天在奢侈品店里也仍会发生。如今不少商店培训员工对每位来客一视同仁，一个原因是有钱的顾客常常穿得很随意，而网上一条差评的代价可能远超一单生意。

✨ How it is written赏析

The story is built in two matching halves, the first visit and the second, with the same dress and the same assistant, so the reader can measure exactly how things have changed. In the first half the assistant has all the power and the woman says almost nothing. In the second she is in control, and the details of the coat, the handbag and the umbrella work like armour. The climb into the shop window is a small piece of physical comedy. The ending is neat: she still buys the dress, so the shop does make its sale, but only after the assistant has paid for his rudeness.

故事由两半对称的情节组成：第一次来和第二次来，同一条裙子、同一个店员，读者可以准确掂量出情况变了多少。前一半店员握有全部主动权，女人几乎一言不发；后一半她掌控了局面，皮大衣、手提包和雨伞这些细节像一身铠甲。爬进橱窗是一小段肢体喜剧。结尾很利落：她还是买下了裙子，商店照样做成了生意，只是在店员为自己的无礼付出代价之后。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

Most readers cheer for the woman, and the story invites that. Yet it is worth asking whether her revenge was fair. She made the assistant work hard, but she also wasted his time on purpose and taught him only to judge more carefully by clothes, not to stop judging. A better lesson might be for the shop to treat everyone politely from the start. The story also touches a common worry among immigrants and travellers, who are sometimes judged by their accent or dress. The lasting point is simple: respect should not depend on appearance.

大多数读者会为那个女人叫好，故事也鼓励我们这样做。但值得一问：她的报复公平吗？她让店员累得够呛，却也故意浪费了他的时间，而且只教会了他更小心地「看衣服识人」，并没有教会他别以貌取人。更好的教训或许是商店从一开始就礼貌对待每个人。故事也触到了移民和旅行者常有的担心：有时会因为口音或穿着被人看低。经久不变的道理很简单：尊重不该取决于外表。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 2第 2 句 (begins开头 “Though she hesitated …”)

  • though: although, giving a contrast.though：虽然，引出让步状语从句。
  • hesitate for a moment: pause briefly, unsure.hesitate for a moment：犹豫了一下。

Sentence 4第 4 句 (begins开头 “The assistant who …”)

  • who served her: a relative clause about the assistant.who served her：定语从句修饰 the assistant。
  • the way she was dressed: how she was dressed, with that or in which left out.the way she was dressed：她的穿着方式；the way 后的从句省去了 that / in which。

Sentence 5第 5 句 (begins开头 “Glancing at her …”)

  • Glancing at: an -ing phrase for what he did at the same time.Glancing at …：现在分词短语表伴随。

Sentence 8第 8 句 (begins开头 “with a handbag …”)

  • with plus noun plus phrase: describing how she looked.with + 名词 + 介词短语：描写人物状态。
  • one ... the other: of two, the first and the second.one … the other：两者之中一个……另一个……。

Sentence 11第 11 句 (begins开头 “Not realizing who …”)

  • Not realizing: put not before the -ing form.Not realizing …：分词短语的否定形式，not 放在分词前。
  • be eager to do: want very much to do.be eager to do：急于做……。

Sentence 14第 14 句 (begins开头 “She enjoyed herself …”)

  • enjoy oneself doing: have fun doing.enjoy oneself doing：做……很开心。
  • make sb do: cause someone to do, with no to.make sb do：让某人做，do 不带 to。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

Participle phrases, including the negative分词短语作状语（含否定）
An -ing phrase shows an active action happening at the same time or giving a reason, and it shares the sentence's subject. For the negative put not before the -ing form, as in not knowing or not realizing.
现在分词短语表主动、同时或原因，主语与句子主语一致；否定形式把 not 放在分词前：Not knowing …、Not realizing …。

Sentence 5 shows the assistant's look as he speaks, and sentence 11 uses a negative participle to explain why he is so keen this time.

本课：第 5 句写店员一边轻蔑地瞟她一边说话；第 11 句用否定分词说明他为什么这次这么殷勤。

  • Not knowing the way, we asked a policeman.
  • Smiling warmly, she opened the door.
make, let and have with a bare infinitive
After make, let and have plus object, the verb has no to; but in the passive make takes to: he was made to wait.
make sb do（迫使）、let sb do（允许）、have sb do（让人去做）后面的动词都不带 to；但 make 用于被动时要加 to：He was made to wait。

In sentence 14 she gets the assistant to bring out almost everything.

本课：第 14 句——她让店员把橱窗里的东西几乎全拿出来。

  • The teacher made us rewrite the essay.
  • My parents let me stay up late at weekends.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • A … in … stood outside the window of … 一个穿……的……站在……的橱窗外
    A boy in a raincoat stood outside the window of the bakery.
  • Though X paused for a moment, X finally … X 虽然犹豫了一下，最后还是……
    Though he paused for a second, he finally jumped into the pool.
  • X did not like the way … X 看不惯……的样子
    My grandmother did not like the way I spoke to my sister.
  • … with a … in one hand and a … in the other 一手拿着……，另一手拿着……
    He came in with a cake in one hand and a bunch of flowers in the other.
  • X enjoyed herself making Y … X 让 Y……，自己乐在其中
    The cat enjoyed itself making the dog chase it round the garden.

Our model summary本站范文

A woman wearing jeans entered an expensive shop and wanted to look at a dress from the window. The assistant disliked her clothes and said it was sold. Angry, she decided to punish him. Next morning she came back in a fur coat. Not recognising her, he politely climbed into the window to fetch the dress. She claimed she disliked it and made him bring out nearly everything before buying that very dress.（73 词）
