Lesson 52 · A Pretty Carpet 漂亮的地毯

📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 52 “A Pretty Carpet”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 52《A Pretty Carpet》

B1🧠 15 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
The writer's family have just moved house, and he has spent the whole morning trying to sort out his new room. It is hard work, because he owns more than a thousand books. Worse still, the room is small, so for now the books are spread across the floor, and he has to tread on them to get anywhere. Not long ago his sister helped him carry an old bookcase upstairs. When she walked in and saw the floor, she was amazed and joked that it was the loveliest carpet she knew. After staring at it a while, she added that he could forget about bookcases and simply sit down and read the floor. The grammar is have been doing, to make matters worse, the superlative with ever, and help sb (to) do.
「我」们刚搬进新房子，「我」忙了一上午，想把自己的新房间收拾好。这可不容易，因为「我」有一千多本书。更糟的是房间挺小，「我」只好暂时把书堆在地板上，书铺满了每一寸地面，进出都得踩着书走。刚才妹妹帮「我」把一个旧书架抬上楼，她一进屋，看到满地的书大吃一惊，说：「这是我见过的最漂亮的地毯。」她盯着看了一会儿又说：「你根本用不着书架，闲下来坐在这儿『读地毯』就行了！」本课学现在完成进行时 have been doing、to make matters worse、形容词最高级 + I have ever seen 和 help sb (to) do。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. Why are the writer's books on the floor?「我」为什么要把书放在地板上？
  2. What does the sister mean by reading the carpet?妹妹说的「读地毯」是什么意思？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander for New Concept English (Longman, 1967); the narrator and his sister are invented.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为《新概念英语》（朗文，1967）自撰；叙述者和他的妹妹都是虚构的。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

A thousand books was a large private library in the nineteen-sixties, when paperbacks were cheap but homes in Britain were often small. Moving house with books is a famous headache, because they are heavy, take up space and are hard to part with. The joke in the story has a real echo today: designers sometimes use books as decoration, and collectors talk about the problem of having more books than shelves. In Chinese culture too, a home full of books has long been admired, and the old phrase about a house filled with books to the ceiling is a compliment to a family's learning.

二十世纪六十年代，平装书便宜，但英国住房普遍不大，一千本书已经算是可观的私人藏书。带着书搬家是出了名的头疼事：书沉、占地方，又舍不得扔。故事里的玩笑在今天也有回声：室内设计师有时把书当装饰，藏书人常说书比书架多是个老问题。在中国文化里，满屋藏书也一向受人敬重，「汗牛充栋」原是形容藏书之多的赞语。

✨ How it is written赏析

Most of the lesson is calm description in the present perfect: what has been done, what is still going on. The writer piles up small problems, too many books, too little room, a floor that has vanished, until the scene is almost absurd. Then the sister enters and turns the problem into a joke with one line, and her second line makes it better by giving advice that is both silly and kind. The story ends on her words, not his, so the reader is left smiling at the mess instead of worrying about it.

课文大部分是用现在完成时平静地描述：已经做了什么，还在做什么。作者把小麻烦一个个堆起来——书太多、地方太小、地板不见了——直到场面几乎荒唐。这时妹妹进来，一句话把麻烦变成了玩笑，第二句话又锦上添花，给了一条既傻气又体贴的建议。故事以她的话收尾，而不是以他的话，所以读者面对这一地狼藉时是笑着的，而不是发愁。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The sister's joke hides a fair question: do we own our things, or do they own us? Many people today try to own less and keep only what they use, while others feel that a wall of books is part of who they are. Both views have sense in them. A practical reader might say the books should be sorted, with some given away, which would solve the space problem at once. A book lover would reply that every one of them might be needed some day. The lesson lets us laugh at the problem without deciding it.

妹妹的玩笑里藏着一个公平的问题：是我们拥有东西，还是东西拥有我们？今天很多人试着少拥有一点，只留用得着的；也有人觉得一整面墙的书就是自己的一部分。两种看法都有道理。务实的读者会说该把书分类，送掉一些，空间问题立刻解决；爱书的人会回答，每一本说不定哪天都用得上。这一课让我们笑看这个问题，而不必替它下结论。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 1第 1 句 (begins开头 “We have just …”)

  • have just done: the present perfect for something just finished.have just done：现在完成时，刚刚完成。
  • have been doing: the present perfect continuous, going on from the morning until now.have been doing：现在完成进行时，动作从早上一直持续到现在。

Sentence 2第 2 句 (begins开头 “I have been …”)

  • get sth in order: make it tidy.get sth in order：把……整理好。

Sentence 3第 3 句 (begins开头 “This has not …”)

  • over: more than.over：超过，相当于 more than。

Sentence 8第 8 句 (begins开头 “and I actually …”)

  • every inch of: all of it, an exaggeration.every inch of：每一寸，夸张说法。
  • actually: showing that even the writer finds it surprising.actually：竟然，表示说话人自己也觉得夸张。

Sentence 11第 11 句 (begins开头 “'This is the …”)

  • the superlative plus noun plus I have ever seen: the most ... I know of.最高级 + 名词 + I have ever seen：我见过的最……的。
  • pretty becomes prettiest: change y to i and add -est.pretty → prettiest：辅音 + y 结尾，变 y 为 i 加 -est。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

The present perfect continuous: have been doing现在完成进行时：have been doing
have or has been plus -ing means an action began in the past and has gone on until now, maybe still going on. It stresses the activity and often goes with all morning, since or for.
have/has been + -ing：动作从过去开始，持续到现在（可能还在继续），强调「一直在做」；常与 all morning、since、for 连用。

Sentences 1 and 2 contrast a finished move with work that has been going on all morning.

本课：第 1 句和第 2 句——搬家这件事已经完成（have moved），收拾房间却一直在做（have been working / trying）。

  • I've been waiting for you since six o'clock.
  • She has been learning French for three years.
The superlative with I have ever done最高级 + (that) I have ever done
the plus superlative plus noun plus I have ever plus past participle means the most of all in my experience. You can also say I have never seen such a ...
the + 最高级 + 名词 + (that) + 主语 + have ever + 过去分词：……过的最……的。也可说 I have never seen such a … 。

In sentence 11 the sister jokes with an exaggerated superlative.

本课：第 11 句——妹妹用夸张的最高级开玩笑。

  • This is the best pizza I have ever eaten.
  • He is the kindest man I have ever met.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • X has just moved to … and has been … since … X 刚搬到……，从……起一直在……
    Our team has just moved to a new office and I have been unpacking boxes since nine.
  • To make matters worse, … 更糟的是，……
    To make matters worse, the last bus had already left.
  • At the moment, … covers every inch of … 眼下……铺满了……的每一寸
    At the moment, snow covers every inch of the garden.
  • This is the … I have ever … 这是我……过的最……的
    This is the funniest film I have ever watched.
  • You don't need … at all. You can just … 你根本不需要……，只要……就行
    You don't need a map at all. You can just follow the river.

Points a good summary covers好摘要要覆盖的要点

Our model summary本站范文

We have just moved house, and I have spent all morning trying to tidy my new room. It is not easy, as I have more than a thousand books and the room is small. For now the books lie on the floor, and I must step on them to move. My sister helped me carry up a bookcase. Seeing the books, she called them a beautiful carpet and said I could just sit and read it.（76 词）
