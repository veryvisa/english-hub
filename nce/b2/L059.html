Lesson 59 · In or Out 进来还是出去

📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 59 “In or Out”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 59《In or Out》

B1🧠 16 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
The writer's dog, Rex, had a habit of sitting by the front gate and barking whenever he wanted to be let in. The neighbours grumbled about the din, so her husband spent weeks teaching Rex to push down the latch with a paw. Rex soon mastered it. But last week, as she set off for the shops, she spotted him inside the garden by the gate, barking to be let out. Since then a new game has begun: he opens the gate, walks in, waits for it to swing shut and barks to get out again; as soon as he is out, he lets himself straight back in and starts again. Yesterday her husband took the gate away altogether, and Rex was so cross that he has not been seen since. The grammar is used to and would, so that for purpose, until, and let sb do.
「我」们家的狗雷克斯以前总坐在大门外叫，每次想进花园就一直叫，直到有人给它开门。邻居们抱怨太吵，「我」丈夫花了好几个星期训练它用爪子按门闩，自己开门进来。雷克斯很快成了开门专家。可上星期「我」出门买东西时，看见它在花园里靠近大门的地方——这回它叫是为了让人放它出去！从那以后它又添了个坏习惯：从外面一推开门，就进花园等门关上，然后坐着叫，直到有人放它出去；一出去又马上自己开门进来，接着再叫。昨天丈夫把大门拆了，雷克斯气坏了，从此再也没见到它。本课学 used to do / would do、so that 表目的、until 的用法和 let sb do / let oneself in。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. Why did Rex become even noisier after learning to open the gate?雷克斯学会开门后，为什么反而更吵了？
  2. Why did removing the gate not end the trouble?拆掉大门为什么没有解决问题？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander for New Concept English (Longman, 1967); Rex and his family are invented.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为《新概念英语》（朗文，1967）自撰；雷克斯和它的主人一家都是虚构的。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

Many British houses have a small front garden with a low wall and a gate fastened by a simple latch, which a clever dog can learn to work. Animal trainers explain Rex's behaviour easily: barking was rewarded with an open gate, so barking became the way to get anything. Dogs repeat actions that bring results, and they enjoy the game itself. Modern advice is to reward quiet behaviour instead and to give a bored dog more exercise and attention. The British take their dogs seriously, and complaints from neighbours about barking are among the most common local noise complaints.

很多英国房子前面有个小花园，围着矮墙，院门用简单的门闩扣着，聪明的狗学得会开。驯犬师很容易解释雷克斯的行为：叫一叫就能换来门开，于是叫成了得到一切的办法。狗会重复能带来结果的动作，而且这个游戏本身就让它乐在其中。现代的建议是改为奖励安静，给无聊的狗多些运动和关注。英国人很看重自己的狗，邻居对狗叫的投诉是当地最常见的噪音投诉之一。

✨ How it is written赏析

The title asks the question that Rex himself cannot answer. The story moves in a circle, like the dog: out, in, out, in, and the sentences in the middle copy that loop with repeated words such as until, lets and barks. The humour grows from a small reversal. The family solve the first problem by training, and the solution creates a new and worse one. The final line is cleverly mixed in tone: it sounds sad, a lost dog, but it is also the joke's end, because the dog leaves the moment there is nothing left to bark about.

标题提出的正是雷克斯自己也答不上来的问题。故事像那条狗一样绕圈：出、进、出、进，中段的句子用 until、lets、barks 这些反复出现的词模仿了这个循环。幽默来自一个小小的反转：一家人用训练解决了第一个问题，而这个解决办法又制造了一个更糟的新问题。最后一句语气调得巧妙：听上去有点伤感——狗丢了，但它也是笑话的收尾：没了可以对着叫的门，狗立刻就走了。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The story is a neat lesson about incentives. Rex is not naughty; he is doing exactly what he has been taught pays off. People act the same way, at work and at school, when a rule rewards the wrong thing. Fixing a problem by removing the gate is like removing a whole system because one part is being misused, and it often has costs nobody planned for. Readers may also feel some sympathy for Rex, who perhaps barked because he wanted company more than the garden. Most behaviour problems, in dogs and in people, make more sense once you ask what the behaviour is getting.

这个故事是关于激励的一堂好课。雷克斯并不调皮，它做的正是它学到的「有好处」的事。人也一样：在单位、在学校，当一条规定奖励了错误的东西，人们就会照着去做。拆掉大门来解决问题，就像因为某个环节被滥用就拆掉整个系统，往往带来谁都没料到的代价。读者也许会同情雷克斯：它叫，也许是想要人陪，而不是想要那个花园。不论是狗还是人，大多数「问题行为」，只要问一句「这样做换来了什么」，就容易理解了。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 1第 1 句 (begins开头 “Our dog, Rex, …”)

  • used to do: a past habit that changed.used to do：过去常常……（现在情况变了）。
  • Rex: in apposition, naming the dog.Rex：同位语，补充说明 our dog。

Sentence 2第 2 句 (begins开头 “Every time he …”)

  • every time: a conjunction meaning whenever.every time：每次……，作连词引导时间从句。
  • would plus verb: a repeated past action.would + 动词原形：过去反复的习惯。

Sentence 3第 3 句 (begins开头 “As the neighbours …”)

  • spend time doing: use time for an activity.spend time doing：花时间做……。
  • train sb to do: teach a skill.train sb to do：训练某人做……。
  • let himself in: open it and come in without help.let himself in：自己开门进来，反身代词。

Sentence 6第 6 句 (begins开头 “This time he …”)

  • so that with would: showing purpose, would because the main verb is past.so that + would：为了……，表目的，主句是过去时所以用 would。

Sentence 11第 11 句 (begins开头 “Yesterday my husband …”)

  • so annoyed with that left out: so ... that.so annoyed (that)：so … that 的 that 省略了。
  • have not seen since: not seen from then until now; since is an adverb here.have not seen … since：从那以后一直没见到，since 作副词。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

so that: showing purposeso that：表目的
so that plus a clause shows purpose, usually with can, could, will or would. Do not confuse it with so ... that, which has an adjective in the middle and shows result.
so that + 从句：为了……；从句里常用 can / could / will / would。和 so … that（如此……以致）不同：so that 中间不夹形容词。

In sentence 6 the barking has a new aim: to be let out.

本课：第 6 句——这回它叫，是为了让人放它出去。

  • Speak loudly so that everyone can hear you.
  • I left early so that I wouldn't miss the train.
until: up to the time whenuntil：直到
In a positive sentence until shows that an action goes on up to a point, as with wait, bark or stay; in a negative sentence not ... until shows it only happens then. Use the present tense in an until-clause for the future.
肯定句里 until 表示动作一直持续到某时（wait、bark、stay）；否定句 not … until 表示到那时才发生。until 从句用一般现在时表将来。

Sentences 2, 8 and 9 all use until: Rex barks or waits up to the moment the gate moves.

本课：第 2、8、9 句都用 until——雷克斯一直叫、一直等，直到门开或门关。

  • Wait here until I come back.
  • We stayed on the beach until it got dark.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • X used to sit … and … X 以前总是坐在……然后……
    My grandfather used to sit by the window and read.
  • Every time X wanted …, X would … until … 每次 X 想……，就会一直……，直到……
    Every time the baby was hungry, she would cry until someone fed her.
  • X spent weeks training Y to … X 花了几个星期训练 Y……
    My sister spent months teaching her parrot to say hello.
  • X soon became an expert at … X 很快就成了……的能手
    Tom soon became an expert at making pancakes.
  • X got so cross that … ever since. X 气坏了，从那以后一直……
    The cat got so cross that it has stayed upstairs ever since.

In no more than 80 words, tell the story of Rex and the gate.

Points a good summary covers好摘要要覆盖的要点
  • Rex used to bark outside until the gate was opened; after neighbours complained, he was trained to open it himself.雷克斯以前在门外叫，直到有人开门；邻居抱怨，丈夫训练它自己开门进来。
  • Last week he was inside, barking to be let out.上星期它却在花园里叫，要人放它出去。

Our model summary本站范文

Rex used to bark outside our gate until someone let him in. The neighbours complained, so my husband trained him to open it with his paw. Last week, however, Rex was barking inside the garden to be let out. Soon he began a new game: he let himself in, waited for the gate to shut, barked to be let out, then came straight back in. Yesterday my husband took the gate away, and Rex has disappeared.（76 词）
