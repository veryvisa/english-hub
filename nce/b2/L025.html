Lesson 25 · Do the English Speak English 英国人讲的是英语吗？

📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 25 “Do the English Speak English”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 25《Do the English Speak English》

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
The writer reaches London at last. The station is huge and gloomy, and not knowing how to find his hotel, he asks a porter. He speaks slowly and with great care, yet the porter cannot follow him; only after several tries does the man understand, and his reply is fast and unclear. The writer explains that he is from abroad, so the porter slows down, but it makes no difference. His teacher never sounded like this. They smile at each other, the porter assures him he will pick it up soon, and the writer is not so sure, since every English person seems to speak a language of his own. The grammar is not only ... but ... as well and neither ... nor.
作者终于到了伦敦。火车站又大又黑，他不认识去旅馆的路，就去问一个搬运工。他的英语说得又慢又清楚，搬运工却听不懂；问了好几遍，对方才明白，可回答得既不慢也不清楚。作者说自己是外国人，搬运工就放慢了，他还是听不懂——老师从来不是这么说英语的！两人相视一笑，搬运工说：「你很快就会学会英语的！」作者却怀疑：在英国，每个人说的都是不同的语言。本课学 not only … but … as well 和 neither … nor …。

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander for New Concept English (Longman, 1967); the writer, a foreign visitor, and the porter are invented.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为《新概念英语》（朗文，1967）自撰；作者这位外国来客和那个搬运工都是虚构的。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

Britain has an unusual number of strong regional accents for a small country, and London itself has several. A railway porter at a big London terminus in the sixties would very likely have spoken Cockney or a related working-class London accent, with dropped h sounds, glottal stops in place of t, and its own vocabulary. The English that foreign students learned from teachers and records was usually Received Pronunciation, the accent of the BBC and the public schools, which only a small minority of British people actually spoke. The gap the writer meets is therefore real, and it has not disappeared: learners today still say that films and textbooks did not prepare them for the street.

英国是个小国，地方口音却多得出奇，伦敦本身就有好几种。六十年代伦敦大火车站的搬运工，很可能说的是伦敦东区腔（Cockney）或相近的伦敦工人阶层口音：吞掉 h 音，用喉塞音代替 t，还有自己的一套词汇。外国学生从老师和唱片里学的，通常是「标准发音」（RP）——BBC 和公学的口音——而实际上只有一小部分英国人这样说话。所以作者遇到的鸿沟是真实的，而且至今没有消失：今天的学习者仍然说，电影和课本没有让他们准备好面对街头的英语。

✨ How it is written赏析

The lesson is built on two mirror sentences. First the writer describes his own speech with not only carefully but clearly as well, and then the porter's with neither slowly nor clearly, so the grammar of the lesson is also the plot. The middle section repeats the attempt three times, each failing for a different reason: first the porter cannot follow, then he speaks too fast, then even slowness does not help. The turn comes with the shared smile, a moment of goodwill without understanding. The last lines are funny because the writer, who has just failed to communicate, ends by asking the reader a question, as if we might know the answer.

课文建立在两个互为镜像的句子上。先是作者用「不但仔细、而且清楚」描述自己的说话，再用「既不慢也不清楚」描述搬运工的，所以本课的语法也就是情节。中间部分把尝试重复了三次，每次失败的原因都不同：先是搬运工听不懂，然后是他说得太快，最后连放慢都不管用。转折出现在两人相视一笑——一个没有理解、却有善意的瞬间。结尾好笑，是因为刚刚沟通失败的作者，最后反倒向读者提了一个问题，好像我们会知道答案似的。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

Linguists would answer the title firmly: yes, they all speak English, but English is a family of accents and dialects, and no single one is the correct one. The writer's mistake, which almost every learner makes, is to treat the classroom accent as the language itself. Chinese learners will recognise the feeling, and also the parallel at home: a student who learned standard Mandarin can struggle in a market in a southern city. The practical lesson is to listen to many kinds of English early, not only the textbook voice, and to treat not understanding as a normal stage rather than a failure. The porter's cheerful prediction is, in the end, correct.

语言学家会坚定地回答这个标题：是的，他们说的都是英语，只不过英语是由许多口音和方言组成的一个家族，没有哪一种是唯一正确的。作者的错误——几乎每个学习者都会犯——是把课堂口音当成了语言本身。中国学习者会认出这种感觉，也会想到国内的类似情形：学标准普通话的学生，到南方城市的菜市场也可能听不懂。实用的教训是：尽早听各种各样的英语，不只是课本里的声音；把「听不懂」看作正常阶段，而不是失败。搬运工那句乐呵呵的预言，最终是对的。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 6第 6 句 (begins开头 “I not only …”)

  • not only ... but ... as well means the same as not only ... but also, here joining two adverbs.not only … but … as well＝not only … but also …：不但……而且……，连接两个副词。
  • not only is not at the front, so there is no inversion.not only 不在句首，不倒装。

Sentence 9第 9 句 (begins开头 “he answered me, …”)

  • neither ... nor is already negative, so there is no other not.neither … nor：既不……也不，本身就是否定，不再加 not。
  • It is the mirror of sentence 6: the writer was slow and clear, the porter neither.和第 6 句正好相反：作者说得又慢又清楚，搬运工两样都没有。

Sentence 11第 11 句 (begins开头 “Then he spoke …”)

  • Slowing down did not help, because the problem was the accent, not the speed.慢了也没用：问题不在速度，而在口音。

Sentence 17第 17 句 (begins开头 “In England, each …”)

  • each takes a singular noun and verb. It is an exaggeration about the variety of accents.each + 单数名词 + 单数动词。夸张的说法，指口音五花八门。
  • It follows I wonder, meaning I am not so sure.紧接上一句 I wonder.（我可不这么肯定）。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

not only ... but also, or but ... as well
It joins two items of the same kind, such as two verbs or two adverbs, and stresses the second. also comes after but; as well goes at the end. If not only starts the sentence, the verb is inverted: Not only did he...
连接两个同类成分（两个动词、两个形容词、两个副词等），强调「还有」。also 放 but 后，as well 放句末。not only 放句首时要倒装（Not only did he …）。

Sentence 6 joins two adverbs and ends with as well.

本课：第 6 句连接两个副词 carefully 和 clearly，用 as well 收尾。

  • She not only sings but also plays the piano.
  • He is not only clever but kind as well.
neither ... nor and either ... or
neither A nor B means not A and not B; either A or B means one of them. When neither ... nor joins two subjects, the verb agrees with the nearer one.
neither A nor B：A 和 B 都不；either A or B：A 或 B 之一。neither … nor 连接主语时，动词跟离它近的那个主语一致。

Sentence 9 denies both adverbs with neither ... nor.

本课：第 9 句 neither slowly nor clearly，否定两个副词。

  • Neither my brother nor I am at home on Mondays.
  • You can have either tea or coffee.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • I arrived in X at last. 我终于到了 X。
    We arrived in Rome at last.
  • Not sure how to get to X, I asked … 不知道怎么去 X，我就问了……
    Not sure how to get to the bank, I asked a policeman.
  • X not only … but … as well. X 不但……而且……
    She not only cooked the dinner but washed up as well.
  • X neither … nor … X 既不……也不……
    The soup was neither hot nor tasty.

Our model summary本站范文

When the writer reached London, he asked a porter how to get to his hotel. He spoke slowly and clearly, but the porter understood him only after several tries, and replied quickly and unclearly. Even when the porter slowed down, the writer could not follow. He began to wonder whether the English all speak different languages.（56 词）
