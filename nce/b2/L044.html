Lesson 44 · Through the Forest 穿过森林

📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 44 “Through the Forest”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 44《Through the Forest》

B1🧠 17 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
Mrs Anne Sterling gave no thought to the danger when she chased two men into a forest. They had come at her as she sat picnicking with her children beside the trees, and had tried to snatch her handbag. As they pulled at it the strap snapped, and holding the bag they ran off among the trees. She was so furious that she went after them. She was soon gasping for breath, yet she kept going. When she reached them they were sitting on the ground looking through her things, so she charged straight at them. The pair were so scared that they let go of the bag and fled. Later she said that the strap would have to be repaired, but nothing was missing. The grammar is so ... that and such ... that, the past perfect and past continuous, phrasal verbs of chasing and searching, and need doing.
安妮·斯特林夫人追着两个男人穿过森林时，根本没想到自己在冒险。那两个人趁她带孩子在森林边野餐时冲过来，想抢她的手提包。争夺中包带断了，两人抢到包就往树林里跑。斯特林夫人气坏了，追了上去。她很快就喘不过气来，但还是接着跑。追上他们时，她看见那两人已经坐下来，正在翻包里的东西，于是她径直朝他们冲过去。两个人吓得扔下包就跑了。「包带得修一修，」斯特林夫人后来说，「不过他们什么也没偷走。」本课学 so … that / such … that、过去完成时与过去进行时、catch up with / go through / run after 和 need doing。

  2. In the end, how did she recover the bag?斯特林夫人最后是怎样把包要回来的？

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander for New Concept English (Longman, 1967); Mrs Sterling is invented.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为《新概念英语》（朗文，1967）自撰；斯特林夫人是虚构的。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

Bag snatching was and is one of the most common street crimes, usually done fast by people who then run to a quiet place to sort through what they have taken and throw away the rest. That is exactly what the two men in the lesson do, and it is why they are caught sitting down. Police advice in Britain, Canada and elsewhere today is consistent: do not chase thieves, especially alone or into isolated places, because the person who loses a bag is far more likely to be hurt in the chase than in the theft itself. Report quickly, note what the people looked like, and cancel cards.

抢包过去是、现在仍是最常见的街头犯罪之一，作案通常很快，之后跑到僻静处翻检抢到的东西、扔掉其余的。课文里那两个人正是这样做的，所以才会被撞见坐在地上。如今英国、加拿大等地警方的建议很一致：不要追贼，尤其不要独自追、不要追进偏僻的地方，因为丢包的人在追逐中受伤的可能远大于在抢劫本身中受伤。尽快报警，记下对方长相，挂失银行卡。

✨ How it is written赏析

The lesson opens with a verdict, that she never weighed the danger, and then shows us why she should have. The past perfect takes us back to the theft, and the past continuous fills in the peaceful picture it broke into: a mother and her children on a picnic. After that the action is pure chase: short main clauses, a breathless runner who keeps going, and a surprise reversal when the hunted turn out to be sitting still. The final quotation is the best line. Mrs Sterling speaks of the strap, not of her own danger, and her calm understatement makes her both brave and slightly comic.

课文开头先下了结论——她没想到风险——然后告诉我们她本该想到的理由。过去完成时把我们带回抢包那一刻，过去进行时补上被它打破的那幅平静画面：一位母亲带着孩子野餐。此后的动作全是追逐：短促的主句，一个喘不过气却不停的追赶者，然后是一个意外的反转：被追的人居然坐着不动。最后那句引语是全课最好的一句：斯特林夫人谈的是包带，而不是自己的危险，她冷静的轻描淡写让她既勇敢又带点喜感。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The story celebrates courage, and most readers enjoy it. But a modern reader should notice that she left her children alone at the picnic and ran into a forest after two strangers who had already used force. It ended well because they were frightened, not because chasing them was wise. In China and Canada alike, public safety campaigns now warn people not to fight for property, since a bag can be replaced and a person cannot. The lesson works best as a story about anger overcoming fear, with a quiet question underneath: was the bag worth it?

这个故事赞美勇气，大多数读者读得很开心。但现代读者应当注意到：她把孩子独自留在野餐地，追进森林去追两个已经动过手的陌生人。结局好，是因为对方被吓住了，而不是因为追赶是明智的。在中国和加拿大，公共安全宣传如今都提醒人们不要为财物搏斗——包可以再买，人不能替换。这一课最好当作「愤怒压倒恐惧」的故事来读，底下藏着一个安静的问题：那个包值得吗？

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 1第 1 句 (begins开头 “Mrs. Anne Sterling …”)

  • the risk she was taking: a relative clause with the pronoun left out. take a risk is the phrase.the risk she was taking：省略关系代词的定语从句，take a risk 冒险。
  • ran after: chased.ran … after：追着……跑。

Sentence 3第 3 句 (begins开头 “They had rushed …”)

  • had rushed: the past perfect, because this came before the chase.had rushed：过去完成时，抢包发生在追赶之前。
  • while she was having: the past continuous for the background.while she was having：过去进行时作背景。

Sentence 6第 6 句 (begins开头 “with the bag …”)

  • with plus a noun plus a phrase describes the state that goes with the action.with + 名词 + 介词短语：独立结构，表示伴随状态。

Sentence 8第 8 句 (begins开头 “Mrs. Sterling got …”)

  • so plus adjective plus that: to such a degree that.so + 形容词 + that：如此……以至于。

Sentence 11第 11 句 (begins开头 “When she caught …”)

  • catch up with: to reach someone ahead of you.catch up with：追上。
  • The past perfect and the past continuous together: first they sat down, then they were busy searching.had sat down / were going through：过去完成时与过去进行时连用，先坐下，再正在翻。

Sentence 15第 15 句 (begins开头 “'The strap needs …”)

  • need doing: active in form, passive in meaning, the same as need to be mended.need doing：需要被……，主动形式表被动意义，＝ need to be mended。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

so ... that and such ... thatso … that 与 such … that
so takes an adjective or adverb, and such takes a noun phrase. get so angry that and get such a fright that both mean to be angry or scared enough that something follows.
so + 形容词/副词 + that；such + (a/an) + (形容词) + 名词 + that。get so angry that、get such a fright that 都是「（某人）……得以至于……」。

Sentence 8 uses so with the adjective angry and sentence 14 uses such with the noun fright, a matching pair.

本课：第 8 句用 so 加形容词 angry，第 14 句用 such 加名词 fright——两句正好成对。

  • The film was so funny that we laughed all the way through.
  • It was such a funny film that we laughed all the way through.
need doing: active form, passive meaningneed doing：主动形式表被动
Thing plus needs plus an -ing form means the same as needs to be done. It is used with a thing as subject, usually for repairs or cleaning. With a person doing the action, use need to do.
物 + need(s) + doing＝物 + need(s) + to be done，需要被……。只用于物作主语、表示需要修理、清洁等。人作主语要做某事用 need to do。

In sentence 15, the strap needs mending means it needs to be mended.

本课：第 15 句的 strap needs mending，＝需要被修。

  • My car needs washing.
  • The windows need cleaning before the guests arrive.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • X gave no thought to the danger when … X……时，根本没想到自己在冒险
    Tom gave no thought to the danger when he jumped into the river.
  • They had … while X was … X 正在……时，他们（已经）……
    The burglars had broken in while we were sleeping.
  • X got so … that she … X……得以至于……
    The baby got so tired that she fell asleep at the table.
  • X finally caught up with … and saw that … X 终于追上……，看见……
    I finally caught up with the group and saw they were lost.
  • The … needs …ing. ……需要……
    The fence needs painting.

Our model summary本站范文

Mrs Sterling was having a picnic with her children by a forest when two men tried to take her handbag. The strap broke and they ran off with it. She was furious and ran after them, though she was soon gasping. She found them sitting on the ground searching the bag, so she charged at them. They were so scared that they dropped it and ran. Nothing had been taken.（70 词）
