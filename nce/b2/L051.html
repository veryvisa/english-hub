Lesson 51 · Reward for Virtue 对美德的奖赏

📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 51 “Reward for Virtue”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 51《Reward for Virtue》

B1🧠 19 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
The writer's friend Hugh has always been heavy, and lately he grew so big that he decided to diet. A week ago he started by making a long list of banned foods, which turned out to be nearly everything he enjoys: butter, potatoes, rice, beer, milk and sweet things. When the writer called on him yesterday, Hugh looked no thinner at all. He hurried his visitor in and quickly pushed a big package out of sight under the desk, clearly ashamed. Asked what he was up to, he gave a guilty grin and brought the package out again. His diet was so hard, he said, that now and then he had to give himself a treat. Inside were five large chocolate bars and three bags of sweets. The grammar is so ... that, has always been, It was obvious that, and reward oneself.
「我」的朋友休一直很胖，最近胖得实在不像话，他决定节食。一周前他开始了：先列出一长串禁吃的食物，黄油、土豆、米饭、啤酒、牛奶、巧克力、糖果——差不多全是他最爱的。昨天「我」去看他，按了门铃，看到他还是那么胖，一点也不意外。他把「我」领进屋，慌忙把一个大包裹藏到书桌底下，显然很难为情。「我」问他在干什么，他心虚地笑了笑，把包裹放回桌上，解释说节食太严格了，必须偶尔奖励自己一下。包裹里装着五大块巧克力和三袋糖。本课学 so … that、现在完成时 has always been、It was obvious that 和 reward oneself。

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander for New Concept English (Longman, 1967); Hugh is an invented character.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为《新概念英语》（朗文，1967）自撰；休是虚构人物。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

In Britain in the nineteen-sixties, slimming was becoming a popular subject, with diet books, calorie charts and slimming clubs starting to appear. The list of forbidden foods in the story reflects the thinking of the time: cut out fat, starch and sugar. Modern nutrition advice has moved away from long bans and towards steady, realistic habits, partly because research on dieting shows that very strict plans are often abandoned and the weight returns. Psychologists also describe a pattern in which forbidding a food makes people want it more, which is exactly what happens to Hugh.

二十世纪六十年代的英国，减肥正在成为热门话题，减肥书、热量表和减肥俱乐部纷纷出现。故事里的禁食清单反映了当时的思路：不吃油脂、淀粉和糖。现代营养建议已不再主张长串的禁令，而是提倡稳定、现实的习惯，部分原因是关于节食的研究显示，过分严格的计划常常半途而废，体重又回来了。心理学家还描述过一种现象：越是禁止某种食物，人们越想吃它——休身上发生的正是这件事。

✨ How it is written赏析

The title is ironic before we even begin, because the only virtue on show is a list. The writer gives us the forbidden foods early, so when the parcel appears we already suspect what is in it, and the fun is in watching Hugh try to hide it. His explanation is a small masterpiece of self-excuse: the diet is so hard that it must be broken. The very last sentence lists what was inside, mirroring the list near the start, and the count of five bars and three bags turns a small slip into a feast.

标题一开始就是反讽，因为唯一被展示的「美德」只是一张清单。作者早早把禁吃的食物列给我们看，所以包裹一出现，我们已经猜到里面是什么，乐趣在于看休怎么藏。他的解释是一小段自我开脱的杰作：节食太苦，所以必须破戒。最后一句列出包裹里的东西，与开头那张清单遥相呼应；五块和三袋这两个数字，把一次小小的破戒变成了一场大餐。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

Hugh is easy to laugh at, but most readers will recognise themselves in him. The story shows a common trap in self-control: we set rules that are too harsh, then reward ourselves for obeying them with the very thing we gave up. Better advice today is to build habits that can last, to change one thing at a time and to make treats small and planned rather than secret. The embarrassment matters too, because Hugh hides the parcel from a friend, not from a doctor. Shame rarely helps people change; honest support usually does more.

休很好笑，但多数读者会在他身上看到自己。故事揭示了自我控制中的一个常见陷阱：我们定下过于严苛的规矩，然后用刚刚戒掉的东西奖励自己的「守规矩」。今天更好的建议是：建立能坚持下去的习惯，一次只改一样，把犒劳安排得小而有计划，而不是偷偷摸摸。难为情这一点也值得注意：休是在朋友面前藏包裹，而不是在医生面前。羞愧很少能帮人改变，坦诚的支持往往更有用。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

so ... that and such ... that
Use so with an adjective or adverb, and such with a noun phrase, before that; the that-clause gives the result, and in speech that is often dropped.
so + 形容词/副词 + that；such + (a/an) + (形容词) + 名词 + that。that 从句说结果；口语中 that 常省略。

Sentences 2 and 13 both use so ... that: once for why he diets, once for why he treats himself.

本课：第 2 句和第 13 句都用 so … that——一次说他为什么节食，一次说他为什么要犒劳自己。

  • The soup was so hot that I couldn't eat it.
  • It was such a lovely day that we went to the beach.
Reflexive pronouns反身代词：reward oneself
When the subject and object are the same person, the object is a reflexive pronoun. Common uses are enjoy oneself, hurt oneself, help oneself to and reward oneself.
主语和宾语是同一个人时，宾语用 myself / yourself / himself / herself / ourselves / themselves。常见：enjoy oneself、hurt oneself、help oneself to、reward oneself。

In sentence 13 the person Hugh rewards is himself.

本课：第 13 句——休要奖励的是他自己。

  • Please help yourself to some cake.
  • The children enjoyed themselves at the party.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • X has always been …, but … got so … that … X 一直……，但最近……得厉害，于是……
    My uncle has always been forgetful, but it got so bad that he started writing notes.
  • The list had nearly everything X loves: … 清单里几乎全是 X 喜欢的：……
    The menu had most of the dishes my sister hates: fish, peppers and onions.
  • I wasn't surprised to find X still … 看到 X 还是……，我一点也不意外
    I wasn't surprised to find that the shop was still shut.
  • It was obvious that X was … 显然 X 很……
    It was clear that she was nervous.
  • X said that … was so … that X had to … X 说……太……了，所以不得不……
    She said her job was so boring that she had to take long walks at lunch.

Our model summary本站范文

Hugh has always been fat, and a week ago he went on a diet. He made a long list of banned foods, nearly all the ones he loves. When I visited him yesterday, he looked as heavy as before. He quickly pushed a big parcel out of sight and looked embarrassed. Then he admitted that his diet was very hard, so he needed a treat now and then. The parcel held chocolate and sweets.（74 词）
