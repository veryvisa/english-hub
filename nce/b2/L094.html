Lesson 94 · Future Champions 未来的冠军

📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 94 “Future Champions”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 94《Future Champions》

B1🧠 19 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
Tests have shown that very young children can learn to swim. At a pool in Los Angeles, children who cannot yet walk learn to stay under water without breathing, and babies only two months old seem happy to go in. Soon they are at home enough to fetch weights from the bottom. Their favourite game is a race on tricycles along the bottom, seven feet down. Some pedal, most push or pull, and a few get from one end to the other without coming up once. Nobody knows if they will win medals one day, but they should give hope to adults who run out of air after a few strokes. This is a light news item with a joke against the reader at the end. The grammar is be plus an adjective plus to do or at doing, It is not long before, so ... that, prefer to do, and whether ..., only time will tell.
实验证明孩子很小就能学游泳。洛杉矶有一家专门的游泳池，孩子们还不会走路，就已经很会在水下憋气了。两个月大的婴儿下水也不怕，没多久就熟悉到能从池底捡起重物。他们最爱玩水下三轮车比赛：三轮车排在七英尺深的池底，孩子们比谁先到对面。有的蹬着车走，多数宁愿推着或拖着走，有的一口气游完全程都不用上来换气。他们将来能不能成为奥运冠军，只有时间能回答；眼下，他们倒该给我们这些游不了五码就喘不上气的人打打气。本课是一篇带点自嘲的趣闻，学 be + 形容词 + to do / at doing、It is not long before、so … that、prefer to do 和 whether … only time will tell。

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Unsigned and written for the course as a light news item; no pool is named and the details have not been traced to a source.

本课未署名，以轻松新闻的笔调为教材自撰；没有点出是哪家游泳池，细节我们也没有追到出处。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

Very young babies do have a reflex that makes the lesson less surprising than it sounds. In roughly the first six months, when a baby's face meets water, it tends to stop breathing for a moment and its heart slows, which is often called the diving reflex; it fades as the child grows. That is why baby swimming classes became popular, but it is not the same as being safe in water. Health bodies in North America, such as the American Academy of Pediatrics, now say that swimming lessons from about the age of one can lower the risk of drowning, while warning that no lesson makes a small child safe near water without an adult watching closely. In Canada the Lifesaving Society runs a programme called Swim to Survive that teaches children three basic skills: roll into deep water, tread water for a minute, and swim fifty metres.

很小的婴儿确实有一种反射，让课文没有听上去那么神奇。大约出生后的头六个月里，婴儿的脸一碰到水，往往会暂时停止呼吸、心跳减慢，常被称为「潜水反射」；孩子长大后这种反射会消失。婴儿游泳班之所以流行，原因就在这里，但这不等于在水里就安全。北美的卫生机构，比如美国儿科学会，现在的说法是：大约一岁起上游泳课可以降低溺水风险，同时提醒，没有哪种课能让小孩在没有大人紧盯的情况下在水边安全。在加拿大，救生协会办了一个叫「游泳求生」的项目，教孩子三项基本功：翻身入深水、踩水一分钟、游五十米。

✨ How it is written赏析

The piece is built like a ladder of surprises, each one a little bigger than the last: babies can learn, they can hold their breath, they go in at two months, they pick up weights, they race tricycles under water. Then the writer steps back with a cautious sentence about the future and finishes with a joke at the reader's expense. The last line turns a report about babies into a gentle push for the grown-ups reading it, and it works because the writer quietly counts himself among us. Notice how small the vocabulary of the race is, push, drag, pedal, cover, and how much pleasure it gives.

文章像一架惊喜的梯子，一级比一级高：婴儿能学会、会憋气、两个月就下水、能捡重物、在水下骑三轮车比赛。然后作者退后一步，用一句谨慎的话说到将来，最后拿读者开个玩笑。最后一句把一篇讲婴儿的报道变成对成年读者的轻轻一推；它之所以奏效，是因为作者悄悄把自己也算进了「我们」。注意描写比赛的词有多简单——推、拖、蹬、走完——带来的乐趣却很多。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The lesson is cheerful, and the cheerfulness hides a serious point. Drowning is one of the leading causes of accidental death for young children in Canada and the United States, and it is usually quick and silent, not the shouting and waving shown in films. So the useful lesson for parents is not that babies are natural swimmers, but that skills and supervision work together: lessons help, fences round pools help, and an adult who is watching rather than looking at a phone helps most. For adults who never learned, the last line is worth taking seriously. Many community pools in Canada run adult beginner classes, and it is never too late to stop gasping after five yards.

课文很欢快，欢快底下藏着一个严肃的问题。在加拿大和美国，溺水是幼儿意外死亡的主要原因之一，而且溺水通常又快又无声，不是电影里那样大喊挥手。所以给家长真正有用的一课，不是「婴儿天生会游泳」，而是技能和看护要一起来：上课有用，泳池围栏有用，最有用的是一个真正在看着孩子、而不是在看手机的大人。对从没学过游泳的成年人来说，最后一句值得当真。加拿大很多社区泳池都开成人初学班，什么时候开始都不晚，不必游五码就喘不上气。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 1第 1 句 (begins开头 “Experiments have proved …”)

  • prove that: show to be true; can be instructed in is a modal passive of instruct someone in a skill.prove that：证明……；can be instructed in：情态动词被动，instruct sb in sth 教某人某技能。
  • at an early age: when very young.at an early age：年龄很小的时候。

Sentence 3第 3 句 (begins开头 “children become expert …”)

  • be or become expert at doing: be very good at it, with an -ing form after at.be/become expert at doing：擅长做（at 后接动名词）。
  • hold your breath: stop breathing for a while; even before stresses how early.hold one's breath：屏住呼吸。even before：甚至在……之前。

Sentence 4第 4 句 (begins开头 “Babies of two …”)

  • appear to be: seem to be; be reluctant to do: not want to do.appear to be：看来是；be reluctant to do：不愿意做。
  • Two infinitives follow each other, and the negative goes on appear.两个不定式连用，否定放在 appear 上。

Sentence 5第 5 句 (begins开头 “It is not …”)

  • It is not long before: soon, with the present tense in the before clause.It is not long before …：不久就……（before 从句用一般现在时表将来）。
  • be accustomed to doing: be used to, where to is a preposition; so ... that: to such a degree that.be accustomed to doing：习惯于（to 是介词）；so … that：如此……以至于。

Sentence 9第 9 句 (begins开头 “Many pedal their …”)

  • prefer to do: would rather do; the two verbs share one to.prefer to do：宁愿做；两个动词共用一个 to。

Sentence 10第 10 句 (begins开头 “Some children can …”)

  • cover: travel a distance; without doing: with no -ing action taking place.cover：走完（一段距离）；without doing：没有做……。

Sentence 11第 11 句 (begins开头 “Whether they will …”)

  • The whether clause is the object of tell, moved to the front for weight; only time will tell: we shall have to wait and see.whether 从句前置作 tell 的宾语，强调「能否」。only time will tell：只有时间能证明。

Sentence 12第 12 句 (begins开头 “Meanwhile, they should …”)

  • meanwhile: in the time before that; those among us who: the people in our group who.meanwhile：与此同时，在此期间。those among us who：我们当中那些……的人。
  • gasp for air: struggle to breathe.gasp for air：上气不接下气。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

be plus an adjective plus to do or at doingbe + 形容词 + to do / at doing
Some adjectives take an infinitive, such as reluctant, willing, eager or able to do; others take a preposition and an -ing form, such as good, expert or bad at doing, and accustomed or used to doing. Learn each one with its partner.
有些形容词后接不定式（reluctant / willing / eager / able to do），有些后接介词 + 动名词（good / expert / bad at doing，accustomed / used to doing）。记搭配，不要混。

本课：第 3 句 expert at holding；第 4 句 reluctant to enter；第 5 句 accustomed to swimming。

  • She is good at drawing.

  • I'm used to living alone.
It is not long before and It will be ... before
It is or was not long before means soon; It will be plus a time plus before means only after that much time. The before clause uses the present for the future.
It is/was not long before + 从句：不久就……；It will be + 时间 + before + 从句：要过……才……。before 从句里用一般现在时表将来。

Sentence 5 uses the pattern to say how quickly the babies settle in.

本课：第 5 句：没多久孩子们就习惯得能从池底捡东西。

  • It was not long before he found a job.
  • It will be two years before the bridge is ready.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • Tests have shown that … can be taught … at a very young age 测试表明……很小就能学会……
    Tests have shown that children can be taught a second language at a very young age.
  • X become good at … even before they can … X 甚至在会……之前就擅长……
    Some children become good at drawing even before they can write.
  • Soon they are so used to … that … 不久他们就习惯到能……
    Soon they are so used to the cold that they swim in winter.
  • Most of them would rather … 他们多数人宁愿……
    Most of them would rather walk than take the bus.
  • Whether X will …, only time will tell X 将来能否……，只有时间能回答
    Whether the new shop will succeed, only time will tell.

Points a good summary covers好摘要要覆盖的要点

Our model summary本站范文

At a Los Angeles pool, children learn to hold their breath under water before they can walk. Even two-month-old babies go in happily and soon bring up weights from the bottom. Their favourite game is a tricycle race seven feet down. Some pedal, but most push or pull their tricycles, and a few reach the far end without taking a breath. We cannot tell if they will win Olympic medals, but they put many adults to shame.（77 词）
