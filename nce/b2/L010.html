Lesson 10 · Not for Jazz 不是为了演奏爵士乐

📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 10 “Not for Jazz”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 10《Not for Jazz》

B1🧠 15 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
The writer's family owns an old keyboard instrument, a clavichord, built in Germany in 1681. It stands in the living room and has been in the family for many years, ever since the grandfather bought it. Not long ago a guest attempted some jazz on it, hit the keys far too hard and snapped two strings. The father was horrified, nobody may touch it now, and a friend of his is mending it. This lesson is a passive voice workout: the passive in the present simple, the past simple and the present continuous, plus be allowed to.
作者家里有一件老乐器——击弦古钢琴，1681 年在德国制造，放在客厅里，是祖父多年前买下的，在家里传了很久。最近一位来客想在它上面弹爵士乐，下手太重，弄断了两根弦。父亲大为震惊，从此谁都不许碰它；现在它正由父亲的一位朋友修理。本课是被动语态的集中课：一般现在时、一般过去时、现在进行时的被动，以及 be allowed to。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. What happened to the clavichord?那件古钢琴出了什么事？
  2. Why may nobody in the family touch it now?为什么现在家里人都不许碰它？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander for New Concept English (Longman, 1967); the family and the instrument are invented, but the clavichord is a real type of keyboard.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为《新概念英语》（朗文，1967）自撰；这家人和这件乐器是虚构的，击弦古钢琴则是真实存在的键盘乐器。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

The clavichord is one of the oldest keyboard instruments. When a key is pressed, a small metal blade at the other end touches the string and stays on it while the key is held, so the player can make the note swell or shake a little. The price of that control is volume: a clavichord is so soft that it was mainly an instrument for practice and for playing alone at home, and it was especially loved in the German-speaking lands in the seventeenth and eighteenth centuries. An instrument from the 1680s would be a museum piece today. The lesson's joke depends on this: jazz needs a strong, hard touch, and a clavichord is built for the opposite.

击弦古钢琴是最古老的键盘乐器之一。按下琴键，另一端一片小小的金属片碰到琴弦，并且在手按住时一直贴着它，于是演奏者能让音稍稍变强或微微颤动。这种控制力的代价是音量：它声音小到主要用于练习和在家独奏，十七、十八世纪在德语地区尤其受喜爱。一件十七世纪八十年代的乐器，放在今天就是博物馆藏品。课文的笑点就靠这一点：爵士乐要的是有力、硬朗的触键，而击弦古钢琴恰恰为相反的东西而造。

✨ How it is written赏析

The text is almost a catalogue of the passive voice, and that choice fits its subject. The instrument is the hero of the story, so it sits at the front of nearly every sentence, and things are done to it: it was made, kept, bought, damaged, repaired. The one person who acts on it directly, the visitor, gets an active verb and a sentence with an exclamation mark, which makes her stand out as the troublemaker. The title, short and dry, sums up the moral without explaining it. A reader who knows nothing about the instrument can still feel the clash between old and new, between something delicate and something loud.

课文几乎是一份被动语态的清单，而这个选择正合题材。乐器是故事的主角，所以它几乎站在每一句的句首，事情都是「对它做的」：被制造、被存放、被买下、被损坏、被修理。唯一一个直接对它动手的人——那位来客——得到了一个主动动词，还配了一个感叹号，让她作为「肇事者」格外显眼。标题短而干，概括了道理却不解释。一个对这种乐器一无所知的读者，照样能感到新与旧、精巧与喧闹之间的冲突。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The story raises a fair question about old things: are they for using or for keeping? Many musicians argue that old instruments should be played, because playing keeps them alive and lets people hear what music once sounded like. Others say that rare pieces should be protected and copied, with the copies used for playing. The father in the story takes the strict view, and the visitor's mistake is a reminder that care requires knowledge. Chinese readers may think of the guqin, an old and quiet stringed instrument that was also played mainly for oneself and a few friends, and of the same debate about whether antique instruments should be played.

这个故事提出一个关于老物件的合理问题：它们是拿来用的，还是拿来收藏的？许多音乐家认为老乐器应该弹，因为弹奏让它「活着」，也让人听到音乐当年的声音；另一些人则说珍稀的原件应该保护起来、照着复制，平时弹复制品。故事里的父亲持严格的立场，而那位来客的错误提醒我们：爱护需要知识。中国读者也许会想到古琴——同样古老、声音同样轻，也主要是弹给自己和三五知己听的——以及围绕古琴该不该弹的同一场争论。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 3第 3 句 (begins开头 “It was made …”)

  • was made: the past simple passive.was made：一般过去时被动，「被制造」。
  • in goes with a country and with a year.in + 国家 / 年份。

Sentence 5第 5 句 (begins开头 “It has belonged …”)

  • belong to: this verb has no continuous form and no passive.belong to：属于。belong 不用于进行时，也没有被动。
  • has belonged with for: it was true in the past and is still true now.has belonged … for：从过去一直到现在都属于。

Sentence 9第 9 句 (begins开头 “She struck the …”)

  • too hard: with more force than was right, so harm followed.too hard：太用力了（too 表示过度，结果不好）。
  • were broken: the passive puts the focus on the strings, not on who broke them.were broken：被动，强调弦的结果，而不是谁弄断的。

Sentence 12第 12 句 (begins开头 “It is being …”)

  • is being repaired: the present continuous passive, meaning that the work is going on now.is being repaired：现在进行时被动，「正在被修理」。
  • a friend of my father's: the double possessive, one of his friends.a friend of my father's：双重所有格，「父亲的一位朋友」。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

The passive: be with the past participle被动语态：be + 过去分词
The passive is be with the past participle, and the tense is carried by be: is or are done, was or were done, is or are being done, has or have been done. We use it when the doer does not matter or is unknown, and add by when we need to name the doer.
被动语态 = be + 过去分词，时态由 be 体现：is/are done（一般现在）、was/were done（一般过去）、is/are being done（现在进行）、has/have been done（现在完成）。动作的执行者不重要或不知道时用被动，需要时用 by 引出。

The lesson uses eight passives in all, in the present simple, the past simple and the present continuous, three of them with by.

本课：第 2 句 is called，第 3 句 was made，第 4 句 is kept，第 6 句 was bought by，第 7 句 was damaged by，第 9 句 were broken，第 11 句 are not allowed，第 12 句 is being repaired by。

  • English is spoken in many countries.
  • The letter was sent last Monday.
  • A new school is being built near our house.
The continuous passive and be allowed to进行时的被动与 be allowed to
For a passive action in progress use am, is or are being done, or was or were being done for the past. be allowed to do means to have permission to do; the negative means you may not.
正在进行的被动用 am/is/are being done（现在）或 was/were being done（过去）。be allowed to do = 被允许做；否定 be not allowed to do = 不许做。

Sentence 11 uses the negative of be allowed to, and sentence 12 the present continuous passive.

本课：第 11 句用 are not allowed to touch，第 12 句用 is being repaired。

  • The road is being repaired, so take the other way.
  • Children are not allowed to swim here alone.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • It is called a … 它叫做……
    This dish is called mapo tofu.
  • X was made in … in + 年份 X 于某年在某地制造
    My watch was made in Switzerland in 1975.
  • X has belonged to … for a long time. X 归……所有已经很久了。
    This farm has belonged to her family for a long time.
  • X is being repaired by … X 正由……修理
    The roof is being repaired by a local builder.

Our model summary本站范文

Our clavichord was built in Germany in 1681, and my grandfather bought it long ago. Not long ago a visitor attempted some jazz on it. She hit the keys so hard that two strings broke. Now nobody may touch it, and it is being mended.（45 词）
