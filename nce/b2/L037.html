Lesson 37 · The Olympic Games 奥林匹克运动会

📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 37 “The Olympic Games”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 37《The Olympic Games》

B1🧠 17 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
In four years' time the Olympic Games are coming to 'our country'. Because huge numbers of visitors are expected, the government plans new hotels, a vast stadium and a pool built to Olympic standard, plus new roads and a railway line just for the Games. Everything will stand on the edge of the capital, in an area to be known as 'Olympic City'. The roads should be ready before this year is out, and the stadium a year after that. The striking modern designs are the work of Kurt Gunter, and people will be keeping a close eye on them as they rise. The whole country is thrilled and eager, since it has never hosted the Games before. The grammar is the future continuous, the future perfect, and look forward to.
四年后，奥运会将在「我们国家」举行。因为会有大批人来访，政府要建新酒店、一座巨大的体育场和一个奥运标准的游泳池，还要修新路和一条专用铁路。比赛场地就在首都郊外，整个区域将叫作「奥林匹克城」。工人们到今年年底会修完新路，到明年年底会完成体育场的工程。那些新奇的现代建筑是库尔特·冈特设计的。新楼一座座拔地而起，大家都会关切地看着。全国上下都很兴奋，盼着奥运会，因为这个国家从没办过奥运。本课学将来进行时 will be doing 和将来完成时 will have done，以及 look forward to + 名词/动名词。

  1. What will the government build for the Games?为了奥运会，政府要建哪些设施？
  2. Why is everyone so excited?人们为什么这么盼望这届奥运会？

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander for New Concept English (Longman, 1967); the country, the city and the designer Kurt Gunter are invented.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为《新概念英语》（朗文，1967）自撰；那个国家、那座城和设计师库尔特·冈特都是虚构的。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

When the lesson was written, the Games had just been held in Tokyo in 1964, the first time in Asia, and Mexico City was preparing for 1968. Both governments built roads, rail links and stadiums, and Tokyo opened its first high-speed train line just before the Games. Since then the pattern of a host country building an Olympic park or Olympic village on the edge of its capital has repeated many times, from Montreal in 1976 to Beijing in 2008 and London in 2012. The cost has grown as well: Montreal took about thirty years to pay off its Olympic debt, and in recent years several cities have withdrawn their bids after public votes.

课文写成时，东京刚在 1964 年办完奥运——第一次在亚洲举办——墨西哥城正在筹备 1968 年那届。两国政府都修了公路、铁路和体育场，东京还在奥运前开通了第一条高速铁路。此后，主办国在首都边上建奥林匹克公园或奥运村的做法重复了很多次，从 1976 年的蒙特利尔到 2008 年的北京和 2012 年的伦敦。花费也越来越大：蒙特利尔用了约三十年才还清奥运债务；近些年还有好几座城市在公众投票后撤回了申办。

✨ How it is written赏析

The text reads like a government announcement, and almost every verb is in the future: will be, will be building, will have completed. That makes it a perfect showroom for future tenses, but it also gives the passage a mood of promise and confidence, nothing has gone wrong yet because nothing has happened. Lists do much of the work: hotels, stadium, pool, roads, railway, one after another, so the scale is felt rather than described. Only the final sentence turns from plans to feelings, and the reason given there, a first time, is the emotional heart of the whole plan.

课文读起来像一份政府公告，几乎每个动词都在将来时里：will be、will be building、will have completed。这让它成了将来时态的完美展厅，也让全文带着一种承诺与自信的气氛——什么都还没出错，因为什么都还没发生。列举承担了大部分工作：酒店、体育场、游泳池、公路、铁路，一个接一个，规模靠感受而不是描写传达。只有最后一句从计划转向感情，而那里给出的理由——第一次——是整个计划的情感核心。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The excitement in the lesson is real and still common, but today's readers know the other half of the story: budgets that double, stadiums that stand empty after the Games, and local people moved to make room. Beijing's 2008 Games left the Bird's Nest as a city landmark, while several venues in other host cities have fallen into disuse. A fair question for any host is the one the lesson never asks: who pays, and what will the buildings be used for when the visitors have gone home? Read the plan as hope, not as a promise.

课文里的兴奋是真实的，如今也依然常见，但今天的读者知道故事的另一半：预算翻倍、赛后空置的体育场，以及为腾地方而搬迁的本地居民。北京 2008 年奥运会留下的「鸟巢」成了城市地标，而其他主办城市有好几座场馆已经废弃。对任何主办方都值得问课文从没问过的问题：谁来买单？客人回家之后，这些建筑拿来做什么？把这份计划当作希望来读，而不是当作承诺。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 3第 3 句 (begins开头 “The Olympic Games …”)

  • in four years' time: four years from now. Note the apostrophe after years.in four years' time：四年以后，注意 years' 的撇号。

Sentence 7第 7 句 (begins开头 “the government will …”)

  • as here means because.as：由于，引导原因状语从句。
  • will be visiting and will be building: the future continuous, for actions going on over a period to come.will be visiting / will be building：将来进行时，将来一段时间里持续进行的事。

Sentence 15第 15 句 (begins开头 “by the end …”)

  • will have completed: the future perfect, done by a time in the future.will have completed：将来完成时，到将来某时已经完成。
  • by the end of: up to that time, very common with the future perfect.by the end of …：到……末为止，常与将来完成时连用。

Sentence 20第 20 句 (begins开头 “Everybody will be …”)

  • as the buildings go up: in a time clause about the future the present simple is used.as …go up：随着……建起来，从句用一般现在时表将来。

Sentence 24第 24 句 (begins开头 “they have never …”)

  • look forward to: to is a preposition, followed by a noun or an -ing form.look forward to：to 是介词，后接名词或动名词。
  • have never been held: the present perfect passive, up to now.have never been held：现在完成时被动，到现在为止从没办过。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

The future perfect: will have done将来完成时：will have done
will have plus the past participle shows that an action will be finished by a certain future time. It often goes with by plus a future time.
will have + 过去分词，表示到将来某个时间已经完成的动作。常与 by + 将来时间（by then、by June、by 2030）连用。

Sentences 14 and 14 say the roads will be done before this year is out, and sentences 16 and 16 give the stadium one more year.

本课：第 14–15 句说路到今年底会修完，第 16–17 句说体育场到明年底会完工。

  • By next June I will have finished university.
  • The film will have started by the time we get there.
The future continuous and the future perfect将来进行时 vs 将来完成时
will be doing shows the action in progress at a future time; will have done shows it finished by that time.
will be doing：将来某时正在做（过程）；will have done：到将来某时已做完（结果）。

In sentence 7 the building is under way, and in sentence 17 the work will be over by then.

本课：第 7 句的 will be building 是工程在进行中；第 17 句的 will have finished 是到时候已完工。

  • At this time next year we will be living in Toronto.
  • By this time next year we will have moved to Toronto.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • X will be held in … in … years' time. ……年后 X 将在……举行
    The music festival will be held in our town in two years' time.
  • As many people will be …, the government will be … 由于很多人将会……，政府将会……
    As many tourists will be arriving, the city will be opening more buses.
  • By the end of …, they will have finished … 到……底，他们将会完成……
    By the end of May, they will have finished the new library.
  • X have been designed by … X 是由……设计的
    The new bridges have been designed by a young engineer.
  • We are looking forward to … because … 我们盼着……，因为……
    We are looking forward to the holiday because we haven't had one for years.

Our model summary本站范文

In four years the Olympic Games will come to our country. Because so many visitors are expected, the government will build hotels, a huge stadium, a new pool, roads and a railway line just outside the capital, in an area called Olympic City. The roads will be ready this year and the stadium next year. Kurt Gunter designed the buildings. Everyone is excited, because the country has never hosted the Games before.（72 词）
