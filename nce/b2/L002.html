Lesson 2 · Breakfast or Lunch 早餐还是午餐？

📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 2 “Breakfast or Lunch”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 2《Breakfast or Lunch》

B1🧠 15 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
It is Sunday, and as usual the writer sleeps in. When he finally gets up, the sky is dark and it is raining once more. The phone rings: Aunt Lucy has just come in on the train and is on her way over. He says he is still at breakfast, and she is shocked, because it is already one in the afternoon. The grammar is where frequency adverbs go (never, sometimes, always), the present continuous for now and for plans, and just with the present perfect.
星期天，作者照例睡懒觉，起床时天阴着、又在下雨。电话响了，是刚坐火车到的露西姑妈，说马上过来。作者说自己还在吃早饭，姑妈吃了一惊：现在已经下午一点了。本课学频度副词的位置（never、sometimes、always）、现在进行时（正在做／马上要做）和 just + 现在完成时。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. What surprised the aunt?姑妈为什么吃惊？
  2. What does the title, breakfast or lunch, refer to?标题「早餐还是午餐」指的是什么？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander for New Concept English (Longman, 1967); an invented everyday scene.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为《新概念英语》（朗文，1967）自撰，是一个虚构的日常场景。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

The British Sunday of the 1960s was a quiet day. Most shops were shut by law, there was little to do in town, and for many working people it was the one morning when nobody had to be anywhere. Sleeping late was a common pleasure, and a big lunch in the middle of the day was the main family meal. So the writer's habit would have looked normal to readers of the time, and the visiting relative who arrives without much warning is a familiar figure in British comedy. The lesson relies on this background: the joke is not that he is lazy, but that he has lost track of the clock so completely that his first meal of the day falls at lunchtime.

二十世纪六十年代的英国星期天很安静：法律规定大多数商店关门，城里没什么可做，对很多上班的人来说，这是唯一一个哪儿都不用去的早上。睡懒觉是常见的享受，中午那顿丰盛的午饭才是一家人的正餐。所以作者的习惯在当时读者眼里很正常，而几乎不打招呼就上门的亲戚，也是英国喜剧里的熟面孔。课文靠的就是这层背景：笑点不在于他懒，而在于他完全忘了时间，一天的第一顿饭落到了午饭时间。

✨ How it is written赏析

The story holds back one fact until the last line. We learn the day, the habit, the weather and the phone call, but not the hour. The aunt's surprise prepares us, and then a single short sentence gives the time and explains everything at once, including the title. The tenses do quiet work as well. The opening uses the present simple for habits, the story itself is told in the past, and the dialogue switches to the present continuous and present perfect, because people on the phone talk about now. In under a hundred words the learner meets three time frames and sees why each one is there.

故事把一个事实一直扣到最后一行。我们知道了是哪天、有什么习惯、天气怎样、谁来了电话，却不知道几点。姑妈的吃惊先做了铺垫，然后一句很短的话报出时间，一下子解释了全部，连标题也一并解释了。时态也在默默干活：开头用一般现在时讲习惯，故事本身用过去时讲，对话一转成现在进行时和现在完成时——因为人在电话里说的是「现在」。不到一百个词，学习者就碰到三种时间框架，并且看到每一种为什么出现在那里。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

Weekend sleep is now a subject of research, and the usual finding is that a big gap between weekday and weekend waking times leaves people feeling worse on Monday, a little like flying across time zones. That is a fair reason to be gentle with the writer and also to doubt the habit. For a Chinese reader the scene has its own echo: the call from a relative who is already at the station, and the rush to look presentable, is a very common family story. The aunt's question is the one that every family has its version of.

周末睡懒觉如今是研究课题，常见的结论是：工作日和周末起床时间差得太多，人到星期一反而更难受，有点像坐飞机倒时差。这既是体谅作者的理由，也是怀疑这个习惯的理由。对中国读者来说，这个场景也有自己的回声：亲戚已经到了车站才打电话，自己手忙脚乱收拾，这是很常见的家庭故事。姑妈那句「你总起这么晚吗」，每个家庭都有自己的版本。

💬 Discuss and write讨论与写作
  1. What do you usually do on a Sunday morning? Write five sentences using always, usually, sometimes and never.
    你星期天早上一般做什么？用 always、usually、sometimes、never 写五句话。
  2. Write the phone call again from the aunt's side: what does she see and think when she arrives?
    从姑妈那边重写这通电话：她到了以后看到什么、心里怎么想？

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 3第 3 句 (begins开头 “I sometimes stay …”)

  • sometimes is a frequency adverb and goes before the main verb.sometimes 是频度副词，放在实义动词前。
  • stay in bed: not get up. until lunchtime: all the time up to the midday meal.stay in bed：待在床上，不起床。until lunchtime：一直到午饭时间。

Sentence 7第 7 句 (begins开头 “'What a day!' …”)

  • What a plus a noun makes an exclamation. Here it is ironic: the day is a bad one.What a + 名词！是感叹句。这里是反话：天气很糟。

Sentence 11第 11 句 (begins开头 “'I've just arrived …”)

  • have just done: something finished a moment ago whose result is still there; she is here now.have just done：刚刚做完，结果还在（人已经到了）。
  • I'm coming: the present continuous for something already decided and about to happen.I'm coming：现在进行时表示马上要做、已经定了的事。

Sentence 17第 17 句 (begins开头 “'Do you always …”)

  • In a question, always goes after the subject and before the main verb.always 在一般疑问句中放在主语后、动词前。
  • so late: as late as this. The line about one o'clock gives the answer: his breakfast is at lunchtime.so late：这么晚。一句「一点钟了」揭开谜底：他的早饭其实是午饭时间吃的。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

Where frequency adverbs go频度副词的位置
Words such as always, usually, often, sometimes and never go before a main verb but after be and after a helping verb.
always、usually、often、sometimes、never 等放在实义动词之前、be 动词和助动词之后。

Sentence 2 uses never and sentence 3 sometimes, both before the verb; in the question of sentence 17, always comes after the subject.

本课：第 2 句用 never，第 3 句用 sometimes，都在动词前；第 17 句疑问句里 always 在主语后。

  • She often walks to work.
  • He is always cheerful in the morning.
  • I have never seen snow.
The present continuous: now, and plans现在进行时：正在做，马上要做
am, is or are with the -ing form. First, for what is going on at this moment, as in the breakfast line. Second, for a plan that is fixed and close, as in the aunt's visit. The present simple, by contrast, is for habits.
am/is/are + doing。① 说话这一刻正在做：I'm having breakfast。② 已经安排好、马上要做：I'm coming to see you。一般现在时则讲习惯：I never get up early。

Sentences 13 and 15 are about now, sentence 12 is about a plan, and sentences 2 and 3 describe habits in the present simple.

本课：第 13、15 句是「正在做」，第 12 句是「马上要做」，第 2、3 句讲习惯用一般现在时。

  • Be quiet — the baby is sleeping.
  • We are flying to Rome on Friday.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • I never / sometimes … on Sundays. 我星期天从不／有时……
    My father never reads the paper on Mondays.
  • stay in bed until … 在床上一直待到……
    On holiday I stay in bed until nine.
  • What a + noun! 多么……啊！
    What a lovely garden!
  • Do you always … so …? 你总是这么……吗？
    Do you always drive so fast?

Summary writing摘要写作

Points a good summary covers好摘要要覆盖的要点

Our model summary本站范文

Last Sunday I stayed in bed till very late. It was dark and wet outside. Then my aunt Lucy phoned. She had come by train and wanted to visit me. When I told her I was eating breakfast, she was shocked: it was one in the afternoon.（47 词）
