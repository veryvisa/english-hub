Lesson 16 · A Polite Request 一个有礼貌的请求

📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 16 “A Polite Request”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 16《A Polite Request》

B1🧠 15 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
The writer begins with what everyone knows: leave your car where it should not be and a traffic officer will soon spot it, and you are lucky to escape a fine. Yet there are exceptions. On holiday in Sweden he found a note on his car. It welcomed him to the city, told him parking was not allowed there, promised he would have a good time if he watched the signs, and ended by calling itself just a reminder. Nobody, he says, could refuse a request put like that. The grammar is the real conditional (if with the present, will in the main clause) and cannot fail to do.
作者先讲一条常识：车停错地方，交警很快会发现，能不被罚款就算走运。可也有例外——在瑞典度假时，他在车上看到一张便条：先欢迎他来到这座城市，再告诉他这里禁止停车，只要留意路牌就会玩得愉快，最后说「这只是一个提醒」。作者说，收到这样的请求，你不可能不照办。本课学真实条件句（if + 现在时，主句 will）和 cannot fail to do。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. Why did the note work better than a fine would have?这张便条为什么比一张罚单更有用？
  2. What happened to the writer's car in Sweden?作者在瑞典遇到了什么事？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander for New Concept English (Longman, 1967); the Swedish note is invented, though friendly notices to foreign drivers were a real practice in some tourist towns.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为《新概念英语》（朗文，1967）自撰；瑞典的便条是虚构的，不过有些旅游城镇确实有给外地司机留客气告示的做法。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

In the nineteen sixties, more and more British families took their cars abroad on holiday by ferry, and driving on unfamiliar roads with signs in another language was a new experience for many. Sweden was a good choice for the story. It was known for calm, orderly public life, and it was then still one of the few countries in Europe that drove on the left like Britain; it changed to the right in September 1967, the same year this book appeared. A visitor who parked badly there was not a criminal but a guest who could not read the signs, and the note treats him exactly that way.

二十世纪六十年代，越来越多的英国家庭坐渡轮把车开到国外度假，在陌生的路上看另一种语言写的路牌，对很多人是新鲜事。故事选在瑞典很合适：瑞典以公共生活平和有序出名，而且当时还是欧洲少数和英国一样靠左行驶的国家之一；它在 1967 年 9 月改为靠右，正是本书出版那一年。一个外国游客在那里停错车，不是违法者，而是看不懂路牌的客人，便条正是这样对待他的。

✨ How it is written赏析

The text is built like a small experiment. The opening states the usual rule in two real conditionals: do this, and that will follow. Then a single however turns the reader towards the exception. The note itself is the heart of the lesson, and it uses the same grammar as the opening for the opposite purpose: instead of if you do wrong, you will be punished, it says if you watch our signs, you will enjoy yourself. The rule is placed in the middle, between a welcome and a reassurance, so it feels like information rather than blame. The last sentence returns to if and will, and the writer's own reaction becomes the proof that the method works.

课文像一个小实验。开头用两个真实条件句陈述常规：你这样做，就会有那样的后果。然后一个 however 把读者引向例外。便条是全课的核心，它用的语法和开头一模一样，目的却相反：不是「你做错了就会受罚」，而是「你留意我们的路牌，就会玩得开心」。规则被放在中间，前面是欢迎，后面是安抚，于是它读起来像信息，而不是指责。最后一句又回到 if 和 will，作者自己的反应成了「这个办法管用」的证据。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

Behavioural research since then has given some support to the idea. Studies of tax letters and fines have found that friendly wording and a reminder of what most people do can raise the rate at which people comply, though the effect is usually modest and depends on the setting. A note works best on a visitor who simply did not know the rule; it will do little against a local who parks there every day on purpose. Chinese readers may notice the same contrast at home: a sign that says please keep the grass green and a sign that lists a fine aim at the same behaviour in different ways. The useful question is which kind of person you are trying to reach.

后来的行为研究在一定程度上支持这种做法。关于税务信件和罚款的研究发现，友好的措辞，加上提醒「大多数人都会这样做」，能提高人们的守规率，不过效果通常不大，也取决于场合。便条对「只是不知道规矩」的游客最有效；对每天故意在那里停车的本地人，作用不大。中国读者在国内也会看到同样的对照：「请爱护草地」和「践踏草坪罚款若干元」针对的是同一种行为，方式却不同。真正有用的问题是：你想说服的是哪一种人？

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 3第 3 句 (begins开头 “You will be …”)

  • A real conditional: the if clause takes the simple present, here lets, and the main clause takes will.真实条件句：if 从句用一般现在时（lets），主句用将来时（will be）。
  • let sb go: allow them to leave. let is followed by the bare infinitive, with no to.let sb go：放某人走，let 后跟不带 to 的不定式。
  • without a ticket: with no fine.without a ticket：不开罚单。

Sentence 9第 9 句 (begins开头 “You will enjoy …”)

  • The note runs welcome, rule, benefit, then 'just a reminder'. It shows respect first and states the rule second.便条的顺序：欢迎 → 规则 → 好处 → 只是提醒。先给面子，再说规矩。
  • pay attention to: to is a preposition here and takes a noun or an -ing form.pay attention to：注意，to 是介词，后面接名词或 -ing。
  • The two clauses can change places; when the if clause comes second, no comma is needed.条件句主从可以互换：if 从句放后面时不用逗号。

Sentence 10第 10 句 (begins开头 “If you receive …”)

  • cannot fail to do: a double negative that means you are certain to do it.cannot fail to do：双重否定，等于「一定会」。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

The real conditional: if with the present, will in the main clause真实条件句：if + 一般现在时，主句 will do
For things that are likely to happen, put the if clause in the simple present, not with will, and use will plus the base verb in the main clause. The if clause can come first or second.
说将来很可能发生的事：if 从句用一般现在时（不用 will），主句用 will + 动词原形。if 从句可以在前也可以在后。

Sentences 1 and 2, 3, 9 and 10 all use this pattern, four times in a short text.

本课：第 1–2 句、第 3 句、第 9 句、第 10 句都用了这个结构，全课一共四次。

  • If it snows tonight, the school will close.
  • You will miss the train if you don't leave now.
cannot fail to do and never fail to do
fail to do means not to manage to do it. cannot fail to do means you are bound to do it, and never fail to do means you always do it.
fail to do＝没能做。cannot fail to do＝不可能不做（一定会）；never fail to do＝从不忘记做（总是做）。

Sentence 10 uses cannot fail to, saying everyone would do what such a note asks.

本课：第 10 句用 cannot fail to，表示这样的请求谁都会照办。

  • You cannot fail to notice the tower; it is the tallest building in town.
  • He never fails to call his mother on Sundays.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • If you leave X in the wrong place, Y will soon … 如果你把 X 放错地方，Y 很快就会……
    If you leave your bike in the wrong place, the guard will soon move it.
  • You'll be really lucky if … 如果……你就算很走运了
    You'll be really lucky if the shop still has any left.
  • You will enjoy … if you pay attention to … 只要留意……你就会喜欢……
    You will enjoy the walk if you pay attention to the birds.
  • Given an X like this, nobody could fail to … 收到这样的 X，谁都不可能不……
    Given an invitation like this, nobody could fail to go.

Summary writing摘要写作

In no more than 50 words, describe the note the writer found in Sweden and say why it worked.

用不超过 50 个词讲述作者在瑞典收到的便条，并说明它为什么管用。

Points a good summary covers好摘要要覆盖的要点
  • Usually a badly parked car earns its driver a fine from the traffic police.车停错地方，交警通常会开罚单。
  • On holiday in Sweden, the writer found a note that first welcomed him and then said parking was not allowed.作者在瑞典度假时，车上留了一张便条：先欢迎他，再告诉他这里禁止停车。

Our model summary本站范文

Traffic police usually give you a fine for bad parking, but not always. In Sweden the writer found a note on his car. It welcomed him, explained that he could not park there, and asked him to watch the signs. A request so polite is impossible to ignore.（48 词）
