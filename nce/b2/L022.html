Lesson 22 · A Glass Envelope 玻璃信封

📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 22 “A Glass Envelope”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 22《A Glass Envelope》

B1🧠 16 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
Jane, the writer's daughter, never imagined that a girl her own age in Holland would write to her. Crossing the Channel the year before, Jane had sealed a note with her name and address in a bottle and dropped it over the side, then forgot all about it. Ten months on, a reply came. The two girls now write often, but by post, which costs a bit more and is far quicker. The grammar is dream of and think of with -ing, phrases after a noun, and will for confident predictions.
作者的女儿简从没想到会收到荷兰一个同龄女孩的来信。去年一家人横渡英吉利海峡时，简把写着姓名地址的纸条装进瓶子扔进了海里，之后就忘了。十个月后，荷兰女孩来信了。现在两个女孩经常通信——不过改用邮局了：贵一点，但肯定快得多。本课学 dream of / think of + 动名词、介词短语作后置定语和will 表示将来的肯定判断。

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander for New Concept English (Longman, 1967); Jane and her pen friend are invented.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为《新概念英语》（朗文，1967）自撰；简和她的笔友都是虚构的。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

Messages in bottles are a real and very old practice. Sailors have used them to send news home, scientists have dropped thousands of numbered bottles to trace sea currents, and some have been found many decades after they were thrown. Guinness World Records has listed finds of bottles that spent over a hundred years at sea. A bottle dropped in the Channel could reach the Dutch coast, since the waters there generally flow north-east towards the North Sea, though wind and tide can carry a bottle almost anywhere. Pen friends were very popular in the sixties, and schools in Britain often arranged them with children in other countries to practise languages.

漂流瓶是一种真实而古老的做法。水手用它往家里报信，科学家投放过成千上万个编了号的瓶子来追踪洋流，有些瓶子在扔下几十年后才被发现。吉尼斯世界纪录收录过在海上漂了一百多年的瓶子。在英吉利海峡扔下的瓶子漂到荷兰海岸是可能的，因为那里的海水总体朝东北流向北海，不过风和潮汐几乎能把瓶子带到任何地方。笔友在六十年代非常流行，英国的学校常给学生配外国笔友来练语言。

✨ How it is written赏析

The story has a beginning that sounds like a fairy tale and an ending that sounds like a shopkeeper. A child sends her name across the sea with no hope of an answer, and a stranger answers. That is the magic. Then the last two sentences bring everything down to earth with however and a comparison of cost and speed. The title prepares the joke: a bottle is called an envelope, as though the sea were a slow postal service. The writing stays very plain, with no adjectives for the sea or the bottle, so the small surprise at the end is the only colour in the text.

这个故事开头像童话，结尾像店老板在算账。一个孩子把自己的名字送过大海，并不指望有人回信，可是一个陌生人回信了，这是魔法。然后最后两句用 however 和「价钱与速度」的比较，把一切拉回地面。标题早就为笑点做了铺垫：瓶子被叫作「信封」，好像大海是一家很慢的邮局。行文非常朴素，没有一个形容大海或瓶子的形容词，于是结尾那个小小的惊喜成了全文唯一的色彩。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

Today the girls would find each other online in seconds, and many would say the bottle has lost its point. Yet the slowness was part of the value. A letter that took ten months by sea, or a week by post, was something to wait for and keep, and many people who had pen friends as children still have the letters. It is also worth noting that putting your name and address into the sea would now be seen as unwise, and that plastic in the oceans has made throwing anything overboard a different matter. For learners, writing to a real person in English, slowly and carefully, is still one of the best ways to improve.

今天两个女孩在网上几秒钟就能找到对方，很多人会说漂流瓶已经没有意义了。可是慢本身就是价值的一部分：在海上走了十个月、或者邮寄走了一周的信，是值得等待和珍藏的东西，很多小时候有过笔友的人至今还留着那些信。也值得指出：如今把自己的姓名地址扔进海里会被认为不够谨慎，而海洋塑料污染也让「往海里扔东西」成了另一回事。对学习者来说，慢慢地、认真地用英语给一个真实的人写信，仍是提高的最好办法之一。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 1第 1 句 (begins开头 “My daughter, Jane, …”)

  • dream of with -ing, because of is a preposition. never dreamed of means it never crossed her mind.dream of + 动名词：of 是介词，后接 -ing。never dreamed of＝做梦也没想到。
  • of with an age after a noun describes the person: someone as old as she is.of + 年龄作后置定语，「和她同龄的」。

Sentence 2第 2 句 (begins开头 “Last year, we …”)

  • were travelling: the past continuous sets the background.were travelling：过去进行时，交代背景。
  • with ... on it: a phrase after the noun saying what was written on the paper.with … on it：介词短语后置，修饰 paper，「写着……的纸」。

Sentence 4第 4 句 (begins开头 “She never thought …”)

  • think of: bring to mind. never ... again: not once after that.think of：想起。never … again：再也没有。
  • ten months later: later comes after the length of time.ten months later：十个月后，later 放在时间段之后。

Sentence 7第 7 句 (begins开头 “Letters will cost …”)

  • The joke is that ten months in the sea is beaten by a stamp.笑点：海里漂了十个月的「玻璃信封」，被一张邮票打败了。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

A verb with a preposition and -ing: dream of and think of doing动词 + 介词 + 动名词：dream of / think of doing
Many verbs need a preposition before their object: dream of, think of, talk about, succeed in, look forward to. A verb after such a preposition takes the -ing form.
很多动词要先接介词再接宾语：dream of、think of、talk about、succeed in、look forward to。这些介词后面的动词用 -ing 形式。

Sentence 1 has dream of with an -ing form; sentence 4 has think of with a pronoun.

本课：第 1 句 dream of + -ing，第 4 句 think of + it。

  • I'm thinking of buying a new bike.
  • She succeeded in finding a flat near work.
  • We look forward to seeing you.
A prepositional phrase after a noun介词短语作后置定语
A phrase with a preposition can follow a noun and describe it: a boy of my age, a man in Paris. Chinese puts such describing words before the noun; English often puts them after.
介词短语可以放在名词后面修饰它：a boy of my age、a card with a name on it、a man in Paris。中文把修饰语放前面，英文常放后面。

Sentences 1, 2 and 4 each contain one, using of, with and in.

本课：第 1、2、4 句都有这种后置的介词短语，常见的介词是 of、with、in。

  • The man in the blue coat is my uncle.
  • I want a room with a view of the sea.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • X never dreamed of …ing. X 做梦也没想到会……
    Tom never dreamed of meeting a famous singer.
  • … a piece of paper with … on it 一张写着……的纸
    She handed me a card with a phone number on it.
  • X forgot all about it, but … later, … X 把这事忘得一干二净，可……之后……
    He forgot all about it, but a year later the money came back.
  • X costs a bit more, but … X 贵一点，但……
    The train costs a bit more, but it is much quicker.

Last year, as her family crossed the Channel, Jane dropped a bottle into the sea with her name and address inside. She forgot about it, but ten months on a Dutch girl wrote to her. They now exchange letters often, but by post, because it is much quicker.（48 词）
