Lesson 32 · Shopping Made Easy 购物的乐趣

📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 32 “Shopping Made Easy”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 32《Shopping Made Easy》

B1🧠 16 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
The writer says people are less honest than they used to be, and the pull towards stealing is stronger now than at any time before, above all in big stores. A detective was keeping an eye on a smartly dressed woman who came to one large store every Monday morning. One Monday the store was quieter than normal, which made her easier to follow. She bought a few cheap items first, then picked out one of the dearest dresses and gave it to a shop girl, who wrapped it in a hurry. The woman took the parcel and left without paying. Only after the arrest did the detective learn that the shop girl was her daughter, who had been giving her mother a free dress every week. The grammar is comparatives, one of the most and as ... as possible.
作者说，人们不如从前诚实了，偷东西的诱惑比以往任何时候都大，尤其在大商店里。一位侦探最近盯上了一个衣着讲究的女人，她总是在星期一上午来一家大商店。有个星期一店里人比平时少，侦探盯得更容易。那女人先买了几件小东西，过了一会儿挑了店里最贵的一条裙子交给店员，店员飞快地包好，她拿起包裹，不付钱就走了出去。她被捕后，侦探才发现那个店员是她女儿——女儿每星期「送」母亲一条免费的裙子！本课学 比较级 not so … as / greater than ever / fewer … than、最高级 one of the most … 和 as … as possible。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. Why was that Monday a good day for the detective?侦探为什么那个星期一更容易盯住那个女人？
  2. How did the woman get away with the dress for nothing?那个女人是怎么不付钱就拿到裙子的？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander for New Concept English (Longman, 1967); the store, the detective and the family are invented.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为《新概念英语》（朗文，1967）自撰；商店、侦探和那一家人都是虚构的。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

Large department stores in Britain and America employed their own store detectives from the late nineteenth century, often dressed as ordinary shoppers. The 1960s brought self-service and open displays to many shops, and shoplifting figures rose with them, which is the worry behind the opening claim. Retail studies have long noted a less visible kind of loss as well: goods that leave with the help of staff, sometimes called sweethearting when a worker lets a friend or relative go without paying in full. The lesson's twist is a textbook case of that, decades before the term was common.

英美的大百货公司从十九世纪末起就雇用自己的商店侦探，常常打扮成普通顾客。六十年代许多商店改成自选和开放式陈列，商店盗窃的数字随之上升，这正是课文开头那句担忧的背景。零售研究也早就注意到一种不太显眼的损失：在店员帮助下流出去的货——员工让朋友或亲戚少付或不付钱就走，行内叫 sweethearting（「放水」）。课文的反转正是这类情形的教科书案例，比这个说法流行早了几十年。

✨ How it is written赏析

The opening sounds like a newspaper complaint about modern times, and it lulls the reader into expecting a lesson about clever thieves. Then the story is told entirely from the detective's eye: we see what he sees, the small buy that looks innocent, the costly dress, the fast wrapping, and we notice that speed only on a second reading. The real clue is planted in plain sight. The final sentence explains everything at once, and the single word in quotation marks does the work of a whole paragraph of comment.

开头听起来像报纸上对世风日下的抱怨，让读者以为这是一课讲高明小偷的故事。接着整个故事都从侦探的眼睛里讲：我们看到他看到的——那几件看似无辜的小东西、那条贵裙子、那个飞快的包装——而「飞快」这一点，只有读第二遍才会留意。真正的线索就摆在明处。最后一句一下子解释了一切，那个加了引号的词抵得上一整段评论。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The opening claim, that people are less honest than they once were, is the kind of thing every generation says, and it is hard to prove. What the story actually shows is narrower and more convincing: rules are easiest to break when the person meant to enforce them is on your side. That is why shops today separate the person who rings up a sale from the person who checks it, and why many Chinese firms worry about the same thing in the form of favours between relatives at work. The moral is not that people have got worse, but that trust needs a second pair of eyes.

开头那句「人们不如从前诚实」是每一代人都会说的话，很难证明。故事真正展示的要窄一些，也更有说服力：当负责执行规则的人站在你这边时，规则最容易被打破。所以今天的商店把收银的人和核查的人分开；许多中国企业担心的也是同一件事，只是表现为工作中亲属之间的「照顾」。寓意不是人变坏了，而是信任需要第二双眼睛。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 2第 2 句 (begins开头 “People are not …”)

  • not so ... as means less ... than, here comparing people now with people in the past.not so … as：不如……那样，比较两个时期。
  • once means at some time in the past, as in once upon a time.once were：过去曾经是（once 作「从前」）。

Sentence 4第 4 句 (begins开头 “The temptation to …”)

  • the temptation to do: the to-infinitive tells us what the temptation is towards.the temptation to do：做某事的诱惑，不定式作定语。
  • greater than ever before: greater than at any earlier time.greater than ever before：比以往任何时候都大。

Sentence 10第 10 句 (begins开头 “there were fewer …”)

  • fewer than usual: a smaller number than normal. fewer goes with countable nouns.fewer … than usual：比平时少，fewer 修饰可数名词。
  • it was easier for him to do: it stands for the to-infinitive phrase that follows.it was easier for sb to do：it 作形式主语。

Sentence 15第 15 句 (begins开头 “she chose one …”)

  • one of the most plus an adjective plus a plural noun: one from the top group.one of the most + 形容词 + 复数名词：最……的之一。
  • as quickly as possible: as fast as could be done.as quickly as possible：尽可能快地。

Sentence 23第 23 句 (begins开头 “The girl 'gave' …”)

  • The quote marks round gave are ironic: it was really theft.gave 加了引号：反话，其实是偷。
  • once a week: one time in each week.once a week：每周一次。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

Ways of comparing比较级的几种说法
First, not so or not as with the plain adjective and then as means less than. Second, a comparative with than ever before means more than at any earlier time. Third, fewer goes with plural countable nouns and less with uncountable ones.
① not so/as + 原级 + as：不如……；② 比较级 + than ever (before)：比以往任何时候都……；③ fewer + 可数名词复数 / less + 不可数名词 + than：比……少。

Sentence 2 uses the first pattern, sentences 4 and 4 the second, and sentences 10 and 10 use fewer with than usual.

本课：第 2 句是第①种，第 4–5 句是第②种，第 10–11 句用 fewer … than usual。

  • This book is not so interesting as the last one.
  • Prices are higher than ever before.
  • There were fewer students and less noise today.
one of the most ... and as ... as possible
one of the plus a superlative takes a plural noun, but the verb agrees with one and is singular. as plus the plain form plus as possible means as much as can be done, the same as as ... as one can.
one of the + 最高级 + 复数名词：……之一，名词一定用复数，谓语跟 one 用单数。as + 原级 + as possible：尽可能……，＝ as … as one can。

Sentence 15 uses one of the most with a plural noun for the dress, and sentence 17 uses the as ... as possible pattern for the fast wrapping.

本课：第 15 句用 one of the most 挑裙子（名词用复数）；第 17 句用 as … as possible 说包得飞快。

  • She is one of the best players in the team.
  • Please reply as soon as possible.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • X are not so … as they once were. X 不如从前那样……了
    Winters here are not so cold as they once were.
  • The temptation to … is greater than ever. ……的诱惑比以往都大
    With phones everywhere, the urge to check messages is greater than ever.
  • It was easier for X to … 对 X 来说……更容易了
    With the lights on, it was easier for us to find the key.
  • X chose one of the most … in the … X 挑了……里最……的之一
    He chose one of the cheapest rooms in the hotel.
  • X walked out without paying. X 没付钱就走了
    The man finished his meal and walked out without paying.

Our model summary本站范文

A store detective had been watching a smartly dressed woman who visited a big shop each Monday morning. One Monday, when the shop was quiet, she bought a few small things and then picked out a very costly dress. An assistant wrapped it quickly, and the woman left without paying. After her arrest, the detective found that the assistant was her own daughter, who had been giving her a free dress every week.（73 词）
