Lesson 38 · Everything Except the Weather 除了天气，什么都想到了

📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 38 “Everything Except the Weather”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 38《Everything Except the Weather》

B1🧠 16 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
The writer's old friend Harrison spent many years around the Mediterranean before coming home to England, where he had long dreamed of retiring to a country house. The moment he got back he bought one and moved in. Almost at once he started grumbling about the weather: it was summer, yet the rain never stopped and the cold was often harsh. After all that sunshine it came as a shock, and he behaved like a man who had never lived in England at all. In the end he could stand no more. Before he had even got settled, he sold up and left. His long dream was over; he had planned for everything except the weather. The grammar is the past perfect, no sooner ... than and hardly ... when, as if, and more than one can bear.
作者的老朋友哈里森在地中海一带住了很多年，后来回到英国。他早就梦想回英国养老，打算在乡下安家。他一回来就买了一所房子住了进去，几乎马上就开始抱怨天气：虽然还是夏天，却雨下个不停，还常常冷得刺骨。过惯了阳光充足的日子，哈里森吃了一惊，表现得好像从没在英国住过似的。最后他实在受不了了，还没来得及安顿下来就把房子卖了，离开了英国。他做了那么多年的梦就此结束——他什么都考虑到了，唯独没考虑天气。本课学过去完成时、no sooner … than / hardly … when（一……就……）、as if 和 more than one can bear。

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander for New Concept English (Longman, 1967); Harrison is invented.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为《新概念英语》（朗文，1967）自撰；哈里森是虚构的。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

In the decades after the Second World War many Britons spent long working lives abroad, in the colonies, in the forces or in business around the Mediterranean, and a common plan was to come home and retire to an English village. The joke about English summer rain rests on real figures: much of England has rain on more than a hundred and fifty days a year, and even July can be cool and wet. The flow later went the other way: from the nineteen seventies onward, large numbers of British retirees moved south to Spain, Portugal and Cyprus for the sun, the very move Harrison makes in reverse.

二战后的几十年里，许多英国人在海外度过漫长的职业生涯——在殖民地、在军队，或在地中海一带经商——常见的打算是回国退休，住进英格兰的某个村子。关于英国夏雨的笑话有真实数据作底：英格兰大部分地区一年有一百五十天以上下雨，连七月都可能又凉又湿。后来人流的方向反了过来：从七十年代起，大批英国退休者南下西班牙、葡萄牙和塞浦路斯追逐阳光——正是哈里森那一步的反向。

✨ How it is written赏析

The lesson is a whole life plan undone in a few lines, and its speed is the joke. The writer chains the past perfect through the story so that dream, plan and return all sit behind the main events, and then uses two fast as-soon-as structures to show how little time passed between arriving and leaving. The comparison with a man who has never known an English summer adds a gentle mockery: Harrison of all people should have known. The last line is the title spoken again, which turns a small story into a saying about plans.

这一课用几行字拆掉了一整个人生规划，速度本身就是笑点。作者用过去完成时贯穿全文，把梦想、计划、回国都放在主要事件的背后，再用两个「一……就……」的快节奏结构，显示从到达到离开之间时间多么短。「好像他从没在那里住过似的」这句话带着温和的嘲讽：偏偏是他，本该最清楚。最后一行把标题又说了一遍，让一个小故事变成一句关于计划的格言。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

Harrison's mistake is a common one: he planned with memory and longing rather than with facts. People who move back to a home country after decades away, including many overseas Chinese who return to China or Hong Kong, often find that the place, or they themselves, have changed, and the hardest things to bear are the small daily ones, weather, noise, food, rather than the big ones. A short trial stay before buying a house would have cost Harrison very little. The lesson's moral holds up well: test the dream before you live in it.

哈里森犯的错很常见：他凭记忆和思念做计划，而不是凭事实。离开几十年后回到故乡的人——包括许多回到内地或香港的海外华人——常常发现那个地方变了，或者自己变了，最难忍受的往往是日常小事：天气、噪音、饮食，而不是大事。买房前先短住一段试试，对哈里森来说几乎不花什么成本。这课的寓意经得起时间：住进梦想之前，先试一试。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 1第 1 句 (begins开头 “My old friend, …”)

  • had lived ... before: the past perfect, for the years spent there before he came back.had lived … before …：过去完成时，回英国之前已经住了多年。

Sentence 6第 6 句 (begins开头 “He had no …”)

  • had no sooner done than did: as soon as. The main clause is in the past perfect and the than part in the past simple.had no sooner done than did：一……就……，主句过去完成时，than 后一般过去时。

Sentence 14第 14 句 (begins开头 “He acted as …”)

  • as if plus the past perfect: as though, for something not true in the past. In fact he had lived there.as if + 过去完成时：好像（与过去事实相反）。他其实在英国住过。

Sentence 15第 15 句 (begins开头 “In the end, …”)

  • more than one can bear: beyond what one can stand.more than one can bear：超出某人能忍受的限度，即「受不了」。

Sentence 16第 16 句 (begins开头 “He had hardly …”)

  • had hardly done when did: only just, and then. It takes when, not than.had hardly done when did：刚……就……，用 when 不用 than。
  • had had: the first had is the helping verb and the second is the past participle of have.had had：过去完成时，第一个 had 是助动词，第二个是 have 的过去分词。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

no sooner ... than and hardly ... when: as soon asno sooner … than / hardly … when：一……就……
Subject plus had no sooner plus the past participle, then than and the past simple. Subject plus had hardly plus the past participle, then when and the past simple. Both mean the second thing came straight after the first. no sooner goes with than and hardly with when; do not mix them.
主语 + had no sooner + 过去分词 + than + 一般过去时；主语 + had hardly + 过去分词 + when + 一般过去时。两者都表示前一件事刚发生，后一件就紧跟着发生。no sooner 配 than，hardly 配 when，不能混用。

Sentence 6 uses no sooner with than, and sentences 16 and 17 use hardly with when.

本课：第 6 句用 no sooner … than，第 16–17 句用 hardly … when。

  • She had no sooner left the house than it started to snow.
  • I had hardly begun my speech when the lights went out.
as if: as thoughas if：好像，仿佛
as if plus a clause means as though. For something untrue, use a past form when it is contrary to the present and the past perfect when it is contrary to the past.
as if + 从句：好像……。说不真实的事时，从句用过去时（与现在相反）或过去完成时（与过去相反）。

In sentence 14 as if is followed by the past perfect, though he had in fact lived in England.

本课：第 14 句 as if 后接过去完成时——他明明在英国住过。

  • She talks as if she knew everything.
  • He looked as if he had seen a ghost.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • X had lived in … for many years before he … X 在……住了很多年后才……
    Lily had lived in Japan for ten years before she moved to Canada.
  • X had no sooner … than he … X 一……就……
    Tom had no sooner got home than he fell asleep.
  • After so many years of …, X got a shock. 过了那么多年……的日子，X 吃了一惊
    After years of city noise, she found the quiet of the farm a shock.
  • It was more than X could bear. X 再也受不了了
    The pain was more than he could stand.
  • X had thought of everything except … X 什么都想到了，就是没想到……
    We had planned for everything except the traffic.

Our model summary本站范文

Harrison lived for many years by the Mediterranean, but he had always dreamed of retiring to the English countryside. As soon as he came back he bought a house there. Almost at once he started to grumble, because even in summer it rained all the time and was often very cold. After so much sunshine he could not bear it, so he sold up and went abroad again. He had planned everything except the weather.（75 词）
