Lesson 91 · Three Men in a Basket 篮子里的三个人

📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 91 “Three Men in a Basket”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 91《Three Men in a Basket》

B1🧠 18 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
A pilot sees a balloon drifting towards an air force base and reports it, but nobody on the ground can say what it is. The officer in the tower is furious, because balloons are a real hazard to planes and somebody could be spying, so the pilot is told to stay with it. Flying round it, he sees three people in the basket, one with field glasses and another busy with a camera over the base. When it comes down the police arrive, yet they cannot take anyone away: the passengers are two MPs and the base's own commander. This is a story with the joke saved for the last line. The grammar is might be doing, be ordered to do, keep track of, make out and see someone doing.
一名飞行员发现一只气球正朝附近的皇家空军基地飘去，赶紧报告基地，可谁也说不清是怎么回事。控制塔里的军官很恼火：气球对飞机是大威胁，说不定还有人在刺探基地，于是命令飞行员盯住它。飞行员绕着气球飞了好一阵，看清吊篮里有三个人，一个拿着望远镜，基地上空时另一个在拍照。气球降落后警察赶到，却没法抓人——篮子里是两位议员和基地司令本人。本课是一个结尾抖包袱的笑话，学 might be doing、be ordered to do、keep track of、make out 和 see sb doing。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. What did the pilot see, and why did it worry the base?飞行员看到了什么，为什么基地那么紧张？
  2. Why could the police not take anybody away?警察为什么没能抓人？

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

Balloons and airfields really do not mix, which is why the officer's anger is not just comic. A balloon cannot steer, so it goes wherever the wind takes it, and a plane taking off or landing has little room to swerve. Today in Canada and Britain, anyone who wants to fly a balloon or a drone near an airport needs permission, and the air around many bases is closed altogether. The other half of the joke is older than flight: armies have always worried about being watched from above. Balloons were used to look down on enemy lines as early as the wars of the 1790s in Europe and again in the American Civil War, so an officer seeing a camera in a basket over his base would think of spies first.

气球和机场确实不相容，所以那位军官的火气不只是好笑。气球没法掌舵，风往哪吹它就往哪飘，而正在起飞或降落的飞机几乎没有躲闪的余地。如今在加拿大和英国，谁要在机场附近放飞气球或无人机都得先获批准，许多军事基地周围的空域干脆完全关闭。笑话的另一半比飞机还古老：军队一向担心被人从上面偷看。早在十八世纪九十年代的欧洲战争里，后来在美国内战中，就有人乘气球俯瞰敌军阵地，所以军官看见吊篮里有人举着相机对准基地，第一个念头就是间谍。

✨ How it is written赏析

The story is told like a spy report, and that is the trick. Every sentence adds a little more danger: a strange object, an angry officer, the word spying, binoculars, a camera over the base. The reader is led to expect enemies, and the police are even called. Then one long sentence after for turns everything round, and the punch line is given by the commander himself, so the joke is on the people who were most worried. Notice that the writer never says the officer in the tower felt foolish; the last line lets the reader imagine it.

故事按间谍报告的口吻来讲，这正是诀窍。每一句都添一点危险：不明物体、发火的军官、「刺探」这个词、望远镜、基地上空的相机。读者被一步步引向「来了敌人」，连警察都叫来了。然后 for 后面一句话把一切翻转过来，而点破笑料的正是司令本人，所以被取笑的恰恰是最紧张的那些人。注意作者从没说控制塔里的军官觉得自己多傻；最后一句留给读者自己去想象。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The last line is a real problem in any large organisation, not only in armies: one office makes a plan and the office next door never hears about it. Hospitals, schools and city councils all have their versions, and the result can be funny, as here, or costly, as when two teams fix the same road twice in one year. The cure is usually dull: a shared calendar, one person who has to be told, a habit of asking who else should know. English has an old phrase for it, the left hand not knowing what the right hand is doing; in Chinese the usual phrase is about each office going its own way.

最后那句话在任何大机构里都是真问题，不只在军队：一个部门定了计划，隔壁部门从来没听说。医院、学校、市政府都有各自的版本，结果可能像这里一样好笑，也可能代价不小，比如两支施工队一年里把同一段路修了两遍。解决办法通常很枯燥：一份共享的日程表、一个必须被告知的人、养成问一句「还有谁该知道」的习惯。英文有一句老话，说左手不知道右手在干什么；中文常说的则是「各自为政」。

💬 Discuss and write讨论与写作
  1. Have you ever seen one part of a school or company not know what another part was doing? What happened?
    你见过学校或公司里一个部门不知道另一个部门在干什么吗？结果怎样？
  2. Pick one of the three people in the basket and retell what happened as that person.
    从吊篮里三个人之一的角度重讲这个故事。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 1第 1 句 (begins开头 “A pilot noticed …”)

  • seem to be doing: the -ing form after to be shows something in progress as it appeared at that moment.seem to be doing：看起来正在……（不定式进行式）。
  • make for: move towards.make for：朝……驶去。

Sentence 4第 4 句 (begins开头 “because balloons can …”)

  • can here describes what is generally possible, not ability.can 表示「有可能，往往会」，讲一般情况，不是能力。
  • be a danger to: be a risk for.be a danger to：对……构成危险。

Sentence 5第 5 句 (begins开头 “He said that …”)

  • might be doing is a guess about what may be going on right now, weaker than may.might be doing：对正在发生的事的推测，语气比 may 更弱。
  • be ordered to do: be told officially to do; keep track of: keep watching where it goes.be ordered to do：被命令做；keep track of：盯住、跟踪。

Sentence 7第 7 句 (begins开头 “He could make …”)

  • could make out: managed to see, with some difficulty.could make out：（费劲地）看清了。

Sentence 8第 8 句 (begins开头 “When the balloon …”)

  • see someone doing: you catch part of the action in progress; see someone do means you watch the whole of it.see sb doing：看见某人正在做（看到过程的一部分）；see sb do 则看到全过程。

Sentence 12第 12 句 (begins开头 “As the Commanding …”)

  • as: in the way that; one half ... the other half: one part ... the remaining part.as：正如。one half … the other half：一半……另一半。
  • what ... was doing is an object clause, so the word order is that of a statement.what … was doing：宾语从句，用陈述语序。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

A modal with the progressive: might be doing and its relatives情态动词 + 进行式：might/must/can't be doing
A modal plus be plus an -ing form is a guess about something going on now: must be doing for near certainty, may or might be doing for a possibility, and can't be doing for something you rule out.
情态动词 + be + -ing，推测此刻正在进行的事：must be doing 几乎肯定；may/might be doing 有可能；can't be doing 不可能。

In sentence 5 the officer guesses that a spy may be at work at that very moment.

本课：第 5 句：军官猜有人此刻 might be spying（正在刺探）。

  • Don't call now; she might be sleeping.
  • The lights are on, so they must be watching TV.
see someone doing and see someone do
After see, hear, watch or notice, an object plus -ing means you catch the action in progress; an object plus the bare infinitive means you see it from start to finish.
感官动词（see, hear, watch, notice）+ 宾语 + -ing：看到动作的进行中；+ 宾语 + 不带 to 的不定式：看到动作的全过程。

In sentence 8 the pilot catches one of them in the middle of taking pictures.

本课：第 8 句用 saw … taking：飞行员只看到拍照的那一刻。

  • I saw him crossing the road. (he was in the middle of it)
  • I saw him cross the road. (from one side to the other)

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • X seemed to be heading for … X 好像正朝……驶去
    The little boat seemed to be heading for the rocks.
  • X informed … at once, but nobody could explain … X 立刻通知了……，可谁也解释不了……
    She informed the bank at once, but nobody could explain the missing money.
  • Someone may be … on us 可能有人在……我们
    Someone may be listening in on us.
  • X was told to keep track of … X 奉命盯住……
    The nurse was told to keep track of his temperature.
  • I could just make out … 我勉强看清了……
    I could just make out a figure at the end of the road.

Our model summary本站范文

A pilot spotted a balloon drifting towards an air force base and reported it, but nobody could explain it. Worried that spies were watching, the officer ordered him to follow it. Circling it, the pilot saw three people aboard; one held field glasses and another was photographing the base. The balloon came down near an airfield, but the police arrested nobody: the passengers were two MPs and the commander himself. Clearly, the base's left hand did not know its right.（80 词）
