Lesson 79 · By Air 乘飞机

📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 79 “By Air”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 79《By Air》

B1🧠 18 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
As a boy the writer often flew alone between Europe and South America, where his parents lived, with a stewardess looking after him, and never had a bad time. Flying became normal to him, and he has been scared only once. Soon after take-off, while climbing slowly over the city, the plane swung round and headed back. Passengers were asked to stay calm and leave quietly once on the ground. Everyone was anxious to know why. It turned out that an important passenger was aboard and the police had been warned about a bomb. The aircraft was searched from end to end, nothing turned up, and they left five hours later. This is a childhood memory and a false alarm. The grammar is used to do, be used to doing and would, inversion after a negative adverb and the past perfect passive.
「我」小时候常坐飞机：父母住在南美，放假时「我」就从欧洲飞过去，一路有空乘照看，从没遇到过不愉快的事。坐飞机早已习惯，只有一次真的害怕了。那次起飞后，飞机正低低地掠过城市慢慢爬升，突然掉头飞回机场。等着降落时，空乘叫大家保持镇静，一着陆就安静地下机。人人都担心，也都想知道出了什么事。后来才知道机上有一位要人，警方接到消息说飞机上被放了炸弹。降落后飞机被彻底搜查，幸好什么也没找到，五小时后又重新起飞。本课是一段童年乘机的回忆和一次虚惊，学 used to do / be used to doing / would、否定副词开头的倒装 和 过去完成时被动。

  2. Why does the writer say flying feels normal to him?作者为什么说自己习惯了坐飞机？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Unsigned and written for the course as a first-person memory; no airline, year or city is named, so it cannot be checked.

本课未署名，以第一人称回忆的形式为教材自撰；没有给出航空公司、年份和城市，所以无法核对。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

Bomb warnings against airliners were a common worry from the late sixties onward, the same years that saw a wave of hijackings, and many turned out to be hoaxes. Calling one in remains a serious crime in most countries even when no bomb exists, because every warning forces the same response: the plane lands, passengers are taken off and the cabin and hold are searched. Systematic screening of every passenger and every bag spread through the seventies and became much stricter after the attacks of 2001. Children flying alone, as the writer did, are still common; airlines run an escort service for them, usually with a form, a badge and a member of staff who hands the child to a named adult on arrival.

从六十年代末起，针对客机的炸弹警告就是一个常见的担忧，那几年也正是劫机事件频发的时期，而很多警告事后证明是恶作剧。在大多数国家，即使根本没有炸弹，打这种电话也是严重的罪行，因为每次警告都会引起同样的处置：飞机降落、乘客下机、客舱和货舱都要搜查。对每位乘客、每件行李进行系统检查，是在七十年代逐步推开的，2001 年的袭击之后又严格了许多。像作者这样独自乘机的儿童至今仍很常见；航空公司有专门的陪护服务，通常要填一张表、戴一个牌子，由工作人员在到达时把孩子交给指定的成年人。

✨ How it is written赏析

The writer spends three sentences making us believe that flying is safe and dull for him, so the one frightening flight stands out. The grammar does the work: used to and would describe a calm routine, while the inverted only on one occasion signals that something different is coming. The scene itself is built from small, controlled actions, a slow climb, a sudden turn, a calm voice asking for order, and the facts are held back until later, just as they were held back from the passengers. The ending is quick and flat on purpose: a search, nothing found, a late departure. The fear is real, but the story refuses to turn it into drama.

作者先用三句话让我们相信，对他来说坐飞机安全又平常，于是那唯一一次害怕的航班就格外显眼。语法在干活：used to 和 would 描写平静的日常，而倒装的 only on one occasion 则预告不一样的事要来了。场景本身由一些小而受控的动作组成——慢慢爬升、突然转弯、平静的声音要求大家守秩序——真相被压到后面才说，正像当时乘客也是后来才被告知一样。结尾故意写得又快又平：搜查、什么也没发现、晚点起飞。恐惧是真实的，但故事拒绝把它写成戏剧。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The lesson captures something true about risk: most frightening moments in travel end in nothing, and the right response is calm routine, not panic. Air travel today is far safer per kilometre than road travel, and the long waits at security are the price of that safety. What has changed is how fast fear spreads: in the writer's day passengers learnt the reason later, while today someone on board might post rumours online before the plane lands, and a false story can cause more distress than the event itself. For Chinese families who send children to study abroad, the lesson is also practical: a child who travels alone needs clear instructions on what to do if a flight turns back, and a contact who can be reached at any hour.

这一课抓住了关于风险的一个真相：旅途中大多数让人害怕的时刻最后都不了了之，正确的反应是按部就班地保持冷静，而不是惊慌。按每公里计算，今天的航空旅行比公路安全得多，安检时的长时间排队就是这份安全的代价。变化的是恐惧传播的速度：作者那个年代，乘客是事后才得知原因的；今天，机上可能有人在飞机落地之前就把谣言发到网上，一条假消息造成的惊慌可能比事件本身还大。对送孩子出国留学的中国家庭来说，这一课也很实用：独自乘机的孩子需要清楚地知道航班返航时该怎么做，还要有一个随时联系得上的人。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 1第 1 句 (begins开头 “I used to …”)

  • used to do: a past habit that is no longer true.used to do：过去常常（现在不了）。
  • a great deal: very much, here meaning very often.a great deal：程度副词，「很多」。

Sentence 3第 3 句 (begins开头 “A flight attendant …”)

  • would: for repeated past actions, close to used to, but it cannot be used for past states.would：表过去反复发生的动作，和 used to 相近，但不能表过去的状态。

Sentence 4第 4 句 (begins开头 “I am used …”)

  • be used to doing: be accustomed to it; here to is a preposition, so -ing follows.be used to doing：习惯于做，to 是介词，后接动名词。
  • With only plus a phrase at the front, the auxiliary comes before the subject.only + 状语放句首，主句部分倒装（助动词在主语前）。

Sentence 5第 5 句 (begins开头 “After taking off, …”)

  • was doing ... when: one action was in progress when another suddenly happened.was doing …, when …：正在做……的时候，突然……。

Sentence 11第 11 句 (begins开头 “The police had …”)

  • Two past perfect passives: both the warning and the planting happened before the passengers learned of them.两个过去完成时被动：警察被告知 → 炸弹被放置，都在「后来得知」之前。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

used to do, be used to doing, and would
used to do is a past habit that has stopped; be or get used to doing means be accustomed to it, with to as a preposition; would do is for repeated past actions, not for states.
used to do 过去常做（今已不做）；be/get used to doing 习惯于（to 是介词）；would do 过去反复的动作（不表状态）。

Sentences 1 and 2 use used to three times, sentence 3 switches to would, and sentence 4 has be used to with -ing.

本课：第 1、2 句连用三个 used to，第 3 句换成 would，第 4 句是 be used to travelling。

  • We used to live in the country.
  • She is used to getting up early.
Inversion after a negative or limiting adverb at the front否定或限定副词开头的倒装
When only plus a phrase, never, seldom or hardly opens the sentence, the auxiliary comes before the subject.
only + 状语、never、seldom、hardly 等放句首时，主句的助动词提到主语前（如 only once have I …）。

The second half of sentence 4 is inverted to stress that it happened only once.

本课：第 4 句后半句就是这种倒装，强调「只有一次」。

  • Only then did I understand what she meant.
  • Never have I seen such a beautiful sunset.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • I used to … a lot as a … 我……的时候常常……
    I used to swim a lot as a schoolboy.
  • I am used to …ing and only once have I … 我习惯了……，只有一次我……
    I am used to working at night and only once have I fallen asleep at my desk.
  • We were …ing, when suddenly … 我们正在……，突然……
    We were eating dinner, when suddenly the lights went out.
  • Later we learnt that … 后来我们才得知……
    Later we found out that the shop had closed for good.
  • Luckily, nothing was found, and … hours later we … 幸好什么也没发现，……小时后我们……
    Luckily, nothing was broken, and two hours later we drove home.

Our model summary本站范文

As a boy the writer often flew alone from Europe to South America to see his parents, so he is used to flying. Only once has he been afraid. Shortly after take-off, the plane suddenly turned back to the airport, and the passengers were asked to keep calm and leave quietly. They later learnt that the police had been warned of a bomb, as an important person was aboard. The search found nothing, and they left five hours later.（79 词）
