Lesson 40 · Food and Talk 食物与谈话

📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 40 “Food and Talk”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 40《Food and Talk》

B1🧠 16 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
At a dinner party last week the hostess seated the writer beside Mrs Rumbold, a big, stern woman squeezed into a black dress. She did not so much as glance up when he sat down; her eyes stayed on her food, and soon she was eating hard. He tried to start a conversation. Was she going to see the new play at the Globe? No. A holiday abroad this year? No. Staying in England, then? No. At his wits' end, he asked whether she liked the meal. 'Young man,' she said, 'eat more and talk less, and we'll both enjoy it.' The grammar is questions in the future continuous, ask whether, and the unreal conditional.
上星期在一次晚宴上，女主人安排作者坐在兰伯尔德夫人旁边。兰伯尔德夫人是位身材高大、不苟言笑的女士，穿一件紧身黑裙。作者落座时她连头都没抬，眼睛盯着盘子，不一会儿就埋头吃了起来。作者想找话说：「环球剧院快要上演一出新戏，您会去看吗？」「不。」「今年您会出国度假吗？」「不。」「那您会留在英国吗？」「不。」作者没辙了，问她这顿饭吃得可好。「年轻人，」她答道，「要是你多吃少说，我们俩都能好好享用这顿饭！」本课学将来进行时的疑问句 Will you be doing …?、ask whether 和虚拟条件句 if you ate …, we would …。

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander for New Concept English (Longman, 1967); Mrs Rumbold is invented, and The Globe was a real London theatre name in the nineteen sixties.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为《新概念英语》（朗文，1967）自撰；兰伯尔德夫人是虚构的，「环球剧院」是六十年代伦敦真实存在的剧院名。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

At an English dinner party of the period, the hostess decided who sat where, usually mixing men and women and separating couples so that guests would talk to new people. Each guest was expected to make conversation with the people on either side, and safe topics were the theatre, holidays and the weather, exactly the subjects the writer tries. In the nineteen sixties The Globe was the name of a West End theatre on Shaftesbury Avenue; it was renamed the Gielgud in 1994, and the reconstructed Shakespeare's Globe on the South Bank is a different building that opened in 1997.

那个年代的英式晚宴上，座位由女主人安排，通常男女相间、把夫妻分开，好让客人和新面孔交谈。每位客人都应当和左右邻座搭话，安全的话题是戏剧、度假和天气——正是作者尝试的那几样。六十年代，「环球剧院」是伦敦西区沙夫茨伯里大道上一家剧院的名字，1994 年改名为吉尔古德剧院；南岸那座重建的莎士比亚环球剧院是另一座建筑，1997 年才开放。

✨ How it is written赏析

The lesson is a scene built on repetition. Three questions, each a little more desperate, get the same one-word reply, and the reader begins to laugh at the pattern before the end arrives. The polite future continuous makes the writer's questions sound well-mannered, which only makes the wall of no funnier. The description at the start does careful work: large, unsmiling, tight black dress, eyes on her plate, so that we know who will win before a word is said. Then the old lady gets the only long sentence of speech, and she uses it to turn the rules of politeness upside down.

这一课是一个靠重复搭起来的场景。三个问题一个比一个急，得到的都是同一个单词的回答，读者在结局到来之前就开始被这个模式逗笑。礼貌的将来进行时让作者的问题显得很有教养，反倒让那堵「不」字墙更好笑。开头的描写下了细功夫：高大、不笑、紧身黑裙、眼睛盯着盘子，一句话还没说，我们就知道谁会赢。然后那位老太太拿到了全课唯一一句长台词，用它把礼貌的规矩整个翻了过来。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

Most readers side with the young man at first, then laugh with Mrs Rumbold, and both reactions are fair. Social customs that require constant small talk can feel like hard work, and some people, in England and elsewhere, simply prefer to eat in peace. Chinese banquet culture puts the stress on toasts and serving others rather than on one-to-one chat with the person beside you, so the same evening might feel different in Beijing or Guangzhou. The lesson quietly asks what conversation at a meal is for: to fill silence, or to enjoy each other.

大多数读者起初站在年轻人一边，接着又跟着兰伯尔德夫人笑起来，这两种反应都合理。要求不停寒暄的社交习惯会让人很累，而有些人——在英国和别处都一样——就是更想安安静静地吃饭。中国的宴席文化重在敬酒、给别人夹菜，而不是和邻座一对一地聊天，所以同样一个晚上放在北京或广州，感觉可能不一样。这一课悄悄地问：饭桌上的谈话是为了什么？是为了填满沉默，还是为了彼此享受？

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

Questions in the future continuous: Will you be doing ...?将来进行时的疑问句：Will you be doing …?
Will you be doing ...? asks about plans someone already has. It sounds more polite than Will you do ...?, which can sound like a request or pressure.
Will you be doing …? 问对方已有的安排，比 Will you do …? 更客气，因为它不像在请求或施压。

The writer asks three times, in sentences 10, 12 and 14, always with Will you be and an -ing form: polite, and refused each time.

本课：作者连问三次，第 10、12、14 句都用 Will you be 加 -ing——礼貌，但都碰了钉子。

  • Will you be using the car this evening?
  • Will you be coming to the meeting tomorrow?
Unreal conditionals about the present与现在事实相反的条件句
If plus a past form, with were for be, then would plus the base form: for a situation that is not true now and is only imagined.
If + 过去式（be 用 were）, 主语 + would + 动词原形：说现在不真实、只是假设的情况。

Mrs Rumbold uses this pattern in sentence 18, because in fact he was talking a lot and eating little.

本课：第 18 句兰伯尔德夫人用这个句式——作者其实说得多吃得少。

  • If I had more time, I would learn French.
  • If she were here, she would know what to do.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • The hostess seated me beside … 女主人把我安排在……旁边
    The teacher seated me beside the new student.
  • X didn't so much as glance up when … ……时 X 连眼都没抬
    He didn't so much as glance up when his boss came in.
  • X's eyes were fixed on … X 的眼睛盯着……
    The cat's eyes were fixed on the bird.
  • Will you be … this year? 今年你会……吗？
    Will you be travelling to China this year?
  • If you … more and … less, we would … 如果你多……少……，我们就会……
    If you slept more and worried less, you would feel better.

Our model summary本站范文

At a dinner party last week I sat next to Mrs Rumbold, a large, unsmiling woman who did nothing but eat. I tried to start a conversation. I asked about a new play, about holidays abroad and about staying in England, but each time she just said no. In the end I asked if she liked her dinner. She replied that we would both enjoy it more if I ate more and talked less.（74 词）
