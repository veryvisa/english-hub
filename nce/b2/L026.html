Lesson 26 · The Best Art Critics 最好的艺术评论家

📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 26 “The Best Art Critics”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 26《The Best Art Critics》

B1🧠 16 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
The writer is an art student who paints a great deal. Plenty of people, he says, act as if they understand modern art and always explain what a picture means, when many pictures mean nothing at all and are just attractive patterns, enjoyed the way we enjoy a pretty fabric. He believes small children often appreciate such work better than anyone, because they notice more. His seven-year-old sister always lets him know if a picture is any good. Yesterday, as he was putting a new one on the wall, she studied it for a moment, said it was fine, and asked whether it was upside down. It was. The grammar is whether ... or not, in the same way that, and better than anyone else.
作者是学美术的，画了很多画。他说很多人假装懂现代艺术，总要告诉你一幅画「讲的是什么」，其实很多画什么也不讲，只是好看的图案，我们喜欢它们，就像喜欢好看的窗帘布一样。他认为小孩子往往比谁都更会欣赏现代画，因为他们观察得更细。他七岁的妹妹总能说出他的画好不好。昨天他正往墙上挂一幅新画，妹妹认真看了一会儿说：「还行，可是不是挂倒了？」他再一看——还真是！本课学 whether … or not、the same way that 和比较级 better than anyone else。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. Whose opinion of his pictures does the student trust most, and why?谁是这个学生最好的评论家？为什么？
  2. Do you agree that many pictures are not about anything?作者说很多画什么也不「讲」，你同意吗？

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

The joke of a modern picture hung upside down is not only a joke. The painter Wassily Kandinsky wrote that one evening he came home and saw a picture of extraordinary beauty glowing on his wall, and only then realised it was one of his own paintings standing on its side; the experience helped convince him that a picture did not need to show objects. In 1961 the Museum of Modern Art in New York is widely reported to have hung Matisse's paper cut-out Le Bateau upside down for well over a month before anyone pointed it out, and in 2022 a German museum said a Mondrian work may have been displayed the wrong way up for decades. Abstract art, which the lesson calls pretty patterns, was the great argument of the art world in the sixties.

「现代画挂倒了」这个笑话不只是笑话。画家康定斯基写过：有一天傍晚他回到家，看见墙上一幅美得出奇的画在发光，然后才发现那是他自己的一幅画侧放着；这次经历让他更加确信，一幅画不需要描绘物体。据广泛报道，1961 年纽约现代艺术博物馆把马蒂斯的剪纸作品《船》倒挂了一个多月才有人指出来；2022 年，一家德国博物馆说，蒙德里安的一件作品可能被倒着展出了几十年。课文称之为「好看的图案」的抽象艺术，正是六十年代艺术界最大的争论。

✨ How it is written赏析

The lesson argues a position and then proves it with a story, and the story turns the argument against the arguer. The writer begins by mocking people who pretend to understand, and he sets himself on the side of honest seeing. His evidence is his little sister, who notices more. Then she notices something about his own work that he, the art student, has missed. The reader laughs at him, but the joke also supports his point: children do look more freshly. The short sentences at the end, she was right, it was, are the writer admitting defeat with good humour, which makes him far more likeable than the people he criticised.

课文先摆出一个观点，再用一个故事证明它，而这个故事让论点反过来打到了论者自己身上。作者一开始嘲笑那些假装懂的人，把自己放在「诚实地看」那一边。他的证据是小妹妹——她观察得更多。然后她恰恰在他自己的作品上看出了他这个美术学生漏掉的东西。读者笑的是他，可这个笑话同时也支持了他的论点：孩子确实看得更新鲜。结尾的短句——她说对了，确实是——是作者带着幽默认输，这让他比他批评的那些人可爱得多。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The lesson's view that many pictures are not about anything is one side of a long debate. Many abstract painters would reject it; Kandinsky and Rothko, for example, believed their colours and forms carried deep feeling, and some viewers report strong reactions to such work. Others side with the writer and say a pattern can be enjoyed for itself. Chinese readers may find a helpful link in their own tradition, where calligraphy is admired for the life in its lines even by people who cannot read the characters. The fair conclusion is that both kinds of looking are real: you can enjoy the pattern first, and learn the story behind it later.

课文「很多画什么也不讲」的观点，只是一场长期争论的一方。很多抽象画家会反对；比如康定斯基和罗斯科就相信，他们的色彩和形式承载着深沉的情感，也有观众说自己面对这类作品时反应强烈。另一些人站在作者这边，认为图案本身就可以欣赏。中国读者也许能在自己的传统里找到一座桥：书法被人欣赏的是线条里的生气，连不认识那些字的人也能欣赏。公允的结论是，两种看法都是真实的：你可以先欣赏图案，再去了解它背后的故事。

    谁是你最诚实的评论者？你为什么信任这个人？

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

whether ... or notwhether … or not：是否
whether starts a clause that offers two possibilities, in normal word order. or not can go at the end or right after whether. if can also mean whether, but whether is more formal and is the only choice at the start of a sentence or after a preposition.
whether 引导名词性从句，表示两种可能中的一个，从句用陈述语序。or not 可放句末，也可紧跟 whether（whether or not）。if 也能表示「是否」，但 whether 更正式，放句首或介词后只能用 whether。

Sentence 10 uses whether ... or not as the object of tells.

本课：第 10 句 whether … or not，作宾语从句。

  • I don't know whether he is at home or not.
  • It depends on whether it rains.
A comparative with a superlative meaning比较级表示最高级：better than anyone else
A comparative with than anyone else, or than any other and a singular noun, means the same as a superlative. You need else or other, or the comparison includes the person himself.
比较级 + than anyone else / any other + 单数名词，意思等于最高级。注意要有 else 或 other，否则就把自己也比进去了。

Sentence 7 uses better than anyone else.

本课：第 7 句 better than anyone else。

  • She runs faster than anyone else in the class.
  • This mountain is higher than any other mountain in the country.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • Plenty of people act as if they understand … 不少人装作懂……
    Plenty of people act as if they understand wine.
  • X is enjoyed in just the way that … is 人们喜欢 X，就像喜欢……一样
    A good song is enjoyed in just the way that birdsong is.
  • X often appreciates … better than anyone else. X 往往比谁都更会欣赏……
    Old people often appreciate quiet better than anyone else.
  • X always tells me whether … or not. X 总能告诉我……是不是……
    My dog always tells me whether a visitor is friendly or not.

In no more than 60 words, give the writer's view of modern art and say what his sister noticed.

Our model summary本站范文

The writer studies art and believes that many people only pretend to understand modern pictures, which are often just pleasing patterns. Children, he thinks, judge them best because they notice more. When he hung a new picture, his seven-year-old sister looked at it carefully and asked if it was upside down. She was right.（54 词）
