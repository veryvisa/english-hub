Lesson 49 · The End of a Dream 美梦告终

📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 49 “The End of a Dream”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 49《The End of a Dream》

B1🧠 17 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
A young man in Tehran was fed up with lying on the floor at night, so he put money aside for years and at last bought a proper bed, with springs and a mattress, the first he had ever owned. Because it was so hot, he took it up to his roof. He slept beautifully for two nights; on the third, a storm came. The wind picked up the bed and hurled it from the roof down into the yard. He only woke when it hit the ground. The frame was in pieces, but he was not hurt at all and was still lying on the mattress. He looked sadly at the broken pieces, took the mattress indoors, laid it on the floor and fell asleep again. The grammar is tired of doing, participle phrases, not ... until, and the past perfect.
德黑兰有个年轻人睡腻了地板，攒了好几年钱，终于买下一张真正的床——有弹簧、有床垫，这是他头一回拥有这样的东西。天气太热，他把床搬到了屋顶上。头两夜他睡得很香，第三夜却刮起了风暴，一阵狂风把床从屋顶卷下去，摔进下面的院子。床撞到地面他才醒，床已摔得粉碎，人却奇迹般毫发无伤，还躺在床垫上。他看了看四周的碎木头和铁件，难过地抱起床垫走进屋，放在地板上，倒头又睡着了。本课学 tired of doing、分词短语作状语、not … until 和 过去完成时。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

Tehran, the capital of Iran, has long hot summers, and in older houses families often slept on flat roofs to catch the night air. Many homes were built around a closed yard, so a bed blown off the roof would land in the courtyard below, exactly as the story says. Sleeping on the floor on thick quilts or mats was normal in much of Asia, including in Iran, Japan and Korea, and in many Chinese villages people slept on a heated brick bed. A bed with springs was a symbol of modern comfort, which is why saving for years to buy one makes sense.

伊朗首都德黑兰夏季长而炎热，老式房屋里的人家常常睡在平屋顶上乘凉。很多房子围着一个封闭的院子建造，所以床从屋顶刮下来，正好落进下面的院子，和故事说的一模一样。在亚洲很多地方，铺厚被褥或垫子睡在地上都很平常，伊朗、日本、韩国都是如此，中国北方不少乡村则睡炕。带弹簧的床象征着现代的舒适，所以攒好几年钱去买一张，完全说得通。

✨ How it is written赏析

The story is built like a small rise and fall. It begins with years of saving and the pride of ownership, climbs to two perfect nights, and then drops with the bed itself. The writer is careful with time: the man stays asleep until the frame hits the ground, and the long participle phrase near the end slows us down as he looks at the pieces. The final sentence is the quiet punch line. He goes back to sleeping on the floor, only now with a mattress, and he falls asleep at once, as if nothing much has been lost.

故事像一段小小的起落。先写多年积攒和拥有的自豪，升到两夜好觉，然后随着那张床一起跌下。作者对时间很讲究：床架撞到地面之前，那人一直在睡；结尾处那个长长的分词短语让节奏慢下来，跟着他看那些碎片。最后一句是轻轻的包袱：他又回到地板上睡，只不过如今多了个床垫，而且立刻就睡着了，好像也没损失多少。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The man's calm is the most interesting part. He has lost the thing he worked years for, yet he does not rage or weep; he keeps the useful part and goes back to sleep. Some readers see this as a gentle lesson about not holding on too tightly to possessions, close to the Chinese saying that a loss may turn out to be a gain. Others notice the practical mistake: putting a heavy, prized object somewhere exposed to the weather. Both readings are fair. The story asks what we really wanted, the bed or the good sleep, and the ending suggests it was the sleep.

那人的平静是最耐人寻味的地方。他失去了多年辛苦换来的东西，却不发火也不哭，留下有用的那部分，倒头又睡。有些读者把它看作一个温和的提醒：别把身外之物攥得太紧，和「塞翁失马，焉知非福」很接近。也有人看到一个实际的失误：把贵重又笨重的东西放在风吹雨打的地方。两种读法都说得通。故事在问：我们真正想要的是那张床，还是一夜好觉？结尾似乎说，是好觉。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 1第 1 句 (begins开头 “Tired of sleeping …”)

  • save up to do: keep money in order to do something.save up to do：攒钱以便……。

  • the proud owner of: a slightly humorous way to say he owned it.the proud owner of：自豪地拥有……，带一点幽默。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

not ... until: only then and not beforenot … until：直到……才……
Make the main clause negative and use until for the moment the action finally happens. The verb is a single action such as wake up, leave or arrive.
主句用否定，until 引出时间点：在那之前动作一直没发生。主句动词是一次性的动作（wake up、leave、arrive）。

Sentences 10 and 11 together say he did not wake before the crash.

本课：第 10 句和第 11 句连起来——床落地之前他一直没醒。

  • She didn't go to bed until midnight.
  • We didn't know the answer until the teacher told us.
Participle and adjective phrases at the start of a sentence分词与形容词短语作状语
A phrase such as Tired of, Glancing at or Built in at the start must share the subject of the sentence. An -ing form is active or at the same time, a past participle is passive, and an adjective phrase often gives a reason.
句首的 Tired of …、Glancing at …、Built in … 等短语，逻辑主语必须是句子的主语；现在分词表主动或同时，过去分词表被动，形容词短语常表原因。

Sentence 1 gives a reason with an adjective phrase, and sentence 15 uses an -ing phrase for what he did at the same moment.

本课：第 1 句用形容词短语说原因，第 15 句用 -ing 短语说他一边看一边做。

  • Tired of waiting, we went home.
  • Looking out of the window, I saw a fox in the garden.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • Tired of …, X saved up for years to … 厌倦了……，X 攒了好几年钱去……
    Tired of the long bus ride, she saved up for months to buy a scooter.
  • X is now the proud owner of … X 如今自豪地拥有……
    My brother is now the proud owner of a second-hand piano.
  • A gust of wind blew … off … 一阵风把……从……吹落
    A gust of wind blew the washing off the line.
  • X did not … until … X 直到……才……
    The baby did not stop crying until her mother came back.
  • Glancing at …, X sadly … 看了一眼……，X 难过地……
    Glancing at the empty plate, the dog sadly lay down.

Our model summary本站范文

A young man in Tehran was fed up with lying on the floor, so he saved for years and bought a proper bed. It was hot, so he put it on his roof. He slept well at first, but two nights later a storm blew the bed into the yard below. The frame broke, yet he was not hurt. Sadly he took the mattress indoors, laid it on the floor and fell asleep again.（74 词）
