🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
Firemen battled a blaze in the woods for close to three weeks before they had it in hand. Not long earlier, tall trees had spread over the land in every direction; now bare hills smoked. With winter close, rain could strip the earth away and flood the villages below. Once the fire was out, the forestry officials bought tons of fast-growing grass seed, and planes scattered it widely. They kept at it for almost a month before the rain came, and by that point the grass had rooted in many spots, showing green against the black. This is a story of recovery after disaster. The grammar is had been doing, not only ... but ... as well, by then and in place of.
一场森林大火烧了将近三个星期才被消防员控制住。不久前这里还是绵延几英里的大树，如今只剩光秃秃的山头，地面还冒着烟。冬天快到了，大雨一来不仅会冲走泥土，还会引发洪水，威胁山下的村庄。火一灭，林业部门就订了几吨长得快的草籽，用飞机大量撒播。撒了将近一个月才下雨，那时很多地方的草已经扎了根，黑色的土地上冒出一片片绿色。本课是灾后叙事，学过去完成进行时 had been doing、not only … but … as well、by then 和 in place of。

  2. How did the forestry officials protect the villages?林业部门用什么办法保护村庄？

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

What the lesson describes is a real practice. After big wildfires, land managers in places such as California and Australia have often dropped grass seed from the air to hold the soil before winter rain, a method sometimes called aerial seeding. The worry is real too: when a fire burns off the plants and leaves, the ground can turn almost waterproof, so rain runs straight off the slopes and carries mud and stones down into towns. Mudslides after fires have killed people more than once. In recent decades, however, scientists have argued about how well quick seeding works. Some studies found that the grass often came too late to stop the first storms, and that fast-growing foreign grasses could crowd out the native plants that would have come back by themselves.

课文写的是真实存在的做法。大火之后，加州、澳大利亚等地的土地管理部门常用飞机撒播草籽，在冬雨来临前固住土壤，这种方法有时叫「飞播」。担心也是真的：大火烧掉植物和落叶后，地面可能变得几乎不透水，雨水直接从坡上冲下，把泥石带进城镇。火后泥石流不止一次夺去人命。不过近几十年来，科学家对速播草籽的效果有争论。一些研究发现，草往往长得太晚，挡不住头几场暴雨；而长得快的外来草种还会挤掉本来能自己长回来的本地植物。

✨ How it is written赏析

The lesson is built on a race between two clocks, the grass and the rain. The writer sets up the danger, then starts the planes, and keeps us waiting with the phrase for nearly a month before the rain arrives. The word however at the start of the next sentence is the turning point: the rain has come, but the grass has won. The final image is a contrast of colour and time, centuries of trees against a few weeks of green. Notice also how the tenses carry the drama: the past perfect continuous appears three times, each time measuring how long something went on before a change.

这一课是两只钟的赛跑：草和雨。作者先摆出危险，再让飞机出动，用「将近一个月」吊着读者，雨才来。下一句开头的 however 是转折点：雨来了，但草赢了。最后一个画面是颜色和时间的对比：几百年的大树对几星期的新绿。还要注意时态怎样带出戏剧性：过去完成进行时出现了三次，每次都在量某件事在变化发生前持续了多久。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The story has a happy ending because people acted fast and the weather was kind. Read today, it is a useful reminder that a quick fix can look like success while the full picture takes years to see. Fire scientists now tend to prefer careful local plans: native seeds where they are needed, barriers of straw or logs on steep ground, and sometimes simply leaving the land to recover. Chinese readers may think of the large tree-planting and grass-planting campaigns on the Loess Plateau, which also aim to stop soil being washed away; there, too, people have learned that the right plant in the right place matters more than speed.

故事结局圆满，是因为人们行动快、天也帮忙。今天读来，它提醒我们：一个快办法可能看上去成功了，全貌却要几年后才看得清。如今火灾科学家更倾向于因地制宜：需要的地方撒本地草种，陡坡上铺稻草或横放木头挡土，有时干脆让土地自己恢复。中国读者会想到黄土高原上大规模的植树种草，目的也是防止水土流失；在那里人们同样体会到，适地适种比速度更要紧。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 1第 1 句 (begins开头 “Firemen had been …”)

  • had been fighting: past perfect continuous, for a long effort that went on up to a past point.had been fighting：过去完成进行时，在过去某时之前一直持续的动作。
  • get something under control: stop it getting worse.get sth under control：把……控制住。

Sentence 4第 4 句 (begins开头 “Winter was coming …”)

  • threaten A with B: put A in danger of B; the subject is the hills, not a person.threaten A with B：以 B 威胁 A，这里主语是山，不是人。
  • come on: of a season or night, begin to arrive.come on：（季节、夜晚）来临。

Sentence 5第 5 句 (begins开头 “for heavy rain …”)

  • for gives the reason, in a slightly formal way.for：因为（补充理由）。
  • not only ... but ... as well: as well at the end does the work of also.not only … but … as well：不仅……而且……；as well 放句尾，等于 also。
  • would here is a future seen from the past.would：过去看将来，表示「可能会」。

Sentence 9第 9 句 (begins开头 “The planes had …”)

  • had been doing ... when: one action had been going on for some time when another came.had been planting … when …：一直在做某事，这时另一件事发生了。

Sentence 10第 10 句 (begins开头 “By then, however, …”)

  • by then: up to that point, usually with a perfect tense.by then：到那时（为止），常和完成时连用。
  • take root: grow roots and become fixed.take root：生根。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

The past perfect continuous: had been doing过去完成进行时：had been doing
had been plus -ing shows that an action had gone on for some time before a point in the past, and may still have been going on. It often goes with for, since, when or before.
had been + -ing：一个动作在过去某时之前已经持续了一段时间（可能还在继续）。常和 for、since、when、before 连用。

Sentences 1, 9 and 11 each use it, for the length of the fire-fighting, the seeding and the life of the old trees.

本课：第 1 句、第 9 句、第 11 句各用一次，分别说救火、撒种和大树生长持续了多久。

  • She had been waiting for an hour when the bus finally came.
  • His eyes were red because he had been crying.
not only ... but ... as well
not only A but also B links two things, with also after but or as well at the end. If not only starts the sentence, the verb comes before the subject: Not only did it ...
not only A but (also) B：不仅 A 而且 B。also 放在 but 后，as well 放在句尾。not only 放在句首时要倒装：Not only did it …

Sentence 5 joins the two dangers of the rain using the as well form.

本课：第 5 句说大雨会冲走泥土，还会引发洪水，用的是 as well 的形式。

  • She speaks not only French but also Spanish.
  • Not only did he forget my birthday, he forgot his own.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • X had been … for nearly … before they could … X 已经……了将近……才……
    The team had been searching for nearly two days before they could find the child.
  • A short time before, … had covered … 不久前，……还覆盖着……
    A short time before, snow had covered the whole valley.
  • X would not only … but would … as well. X 不仅会……还会……
    A strike would not only stop the buses but would close the schools as well.
  • By then, however, … had already … 然而到那时，……已经……
    By then, however, the guests had already gone home.
  • In place of …, … had begun to … ……原来的地方，已经开始……
    In place of the old shops, new flats had begun to rise.

Our model summary本站范文

Firemen took nearly three weeks to bring a huge forest fire in hand, leaving the hills bare and smoking. Winter was near, and heavy rain could wash the earth away and flood the villages below. So the forestry officials ordered tons of fast-growing grass seed, which planes spread over the hills for almost a month. When the rain finally came, the grass had already rooted in many places, and green began to show on the black ground.（77 词）
