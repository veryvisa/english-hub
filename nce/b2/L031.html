Lesson 31 · Success Story 成功者的故事

📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 31 “Success Story”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 31《Success Story》

B1🧠 19 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
Yesterday afternoon Frank Hawkins told the writer about his youth. Before retiring he ran a big company, yet as a boy he mended bicycles in a little shop for fourteen hours a day. After years of saving he bought a small workshop in 1958, and in his twenties he made aircraft parts there with just two men to help him. Within a few years that workshop had grown into a factory with more than seven hundred workers. He smiled at the memory of those hard years, and he was still smiling when his wife walked in: their grandson's bike needed fixing. The grammar is used to do, the past perfect and of one's own.
昨天下午，弗兰克·霍金斯跟作者聊起自己年轻时的经历。他退休前是一家大公司的老板，可小时候在一家小店里修自行车，一天干十四个小时。他攒了好几年钱，1958 年买下一间属于自己的小作坊，二十几岁时给飞机做零件，手下只有两个帮工。没几年，小作坊变成了雇七百多人的大工厂。他想起早年的辛苦和一路走来的成功，笑了——正笑着，妻子推门进来，要他给孙子修自行车！本课学 used to do、过去完成时 had become 和 of one's own。

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander for New Concept English (Longman, 1967); Frank Hawkins and his firm are invented.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为《新概念英语》（朗文，1967）自撰；弗兰克·霍金斯和他的公司都是虚构的。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

The story belongs to a familiar British and American type, the self-made man who rises from the shop floor to the boardroom. In the decades after the Second World War, aircraft making was a growing industry in Britain, and many small engineering firms began by supplying parts to bigger makers before growing into large works of their own. The date in the lesson, the late nineteen fifties, puts Frank's rise in exactly that period of expansion. Long hours for boys were also real: before modern labour laws were fully enforced, a teenager working a twelve or fourteen hour day in a shop was not unusual.

这个故事属于英美常见的一个类型：白手起家的人，从车间一路做到董事会。二战后的几十年里，飞机制造是英国的成长产业，许多小机械厂先给大厂供零件，后来自己发展成大厂。课文里那个年份——五十年代末——恰好把弗兰克的崛起放在那段扩张期里。少年工长时间劳动也是真的：现代劳动法完全落实之前，十几岁的孩子在店里一天干十二到十四个小时并不罕见。

✨ How it is written赏析

The lesson is a whole life squeezed into a dozen short lines, and it moves by numbers: hours, a year, two helpers, then a very exact head count for the factory. That last figure is deliberately precise, the way a proud owner would say it. The tenses carry the timeline for us: used to for the long early stages, a single past simple for the purchase, and the past perfect for how far things had come. The ending is a small turn back to the start. After all the talk of success, his wife hands him the very job he began with, and the story closes on the bicycle where it opened.

这一课把一个人的一生压进十几行短句里，靠数字推进：工时、一个年份、两个帮手，然后是工厂人数一个非常精确的数。最后这个数故意说得很精确，就像一个自豪的老板会说的那样。时态替我们排好了时间线：长长的早年用 used to，买下作坊那一下用一般过去时，「已经走到多远」用过去完成时。结尾是一个绕回开头的小转折：谈了半天成功，妻子递给他的恰恰是他最初干的那份活，故事在自行车上开始，也在自行车上结束。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The joke works because success does not change what a family needs from you. To the outside world Frank is the head of a large business; at home he is still the man who can fix a bike, and that is a kind of respect too. Today's readers may also notice what the tale leaves out: luck, loans, and the many small workshops that never grew. Stories of the self-made founder are popular in China as well, and they are worth reading with the same question: what does the story count, and what does it quietly skip?

笑点在于：成功并不改变家人需要你做的事。在外人眼里，弗兰克是大企业的老板；在家里，他仍是那个会修自行车的人，这也是一种尊重。今天的读者还会注意到故事没说的东西：运气、贷款，以及许许多多没能长大的小作坊。白手起家的创业故事在中国同样流行，读它们时值得问同一个问题：这个故事数了什么，又悄悄略过了什么？

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 5第 5 句 (begins开头 “a very large …”)

  • used to work: something he did regularly in the past and no longer does.used to work：过去常常做、现在不再做的事。
  • as a boy: when he was a boy, a common short way to give the time of life.as a boy：作为一个孩子时，＝ when he was a boy。

Sentence 6第 6 句 (begins开头 “It was his …”)

  • It is someone's job to do something: it stands in for the real subject, the to-infinitive at the end.It was one's job to do：it 作形式主语，真正的主语是后面的不定式。
  • a here means each or per, as in twice a week.fourteen hours a day：一天十四小时，a 表示「每」。

Sentence 9第 9 句 (begins开头 “in 1958 he …”)

  • of his own comes after the noun and stresses that nobody else owned it.of one's own：属于自己的，放在名词后面。

Sentence 12第 12 句 (begins开头 “In a few …”)

  • had become: the past perfect, because the change was complete by a point in the past.had become：过去完成时，到作者讲的那个过去时间点为止已经变成。
  • which employed: a relative clause telling us about the factory.which employed …：定语从句修饰 factory。

Sentence 15第 15 句 (begins开头 “He was still …”)

  • The past continuous gives the background and the past simple gives the event that broke into it.was still smiling when …：过去进行时（背景）＋一般过去时（打断）。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

used to do: past habits and statesused to do：过去常常
used to plus the base form tells of a habit or state in the past that is over now. The negative is didn't use to and the question is Did you use to. Do not mix it up with be used to doing, which means be accustomed to.
used to + 动词原形，说过去经常做、现在已经不做的事，或过去的状态。否定 didn't use to，疑问 Did you use to …?。不要和 be used to doing（习惯于）混淆。

Sentences 5, 7 and 10 all use used to for stages of his life that are long over.

本课：第 5、7 句两次用 used to work，第 10 句 used to make——都是已经过去的阶段。

  • I used to live in a small town, but now I live in the city.
  • She didn't use to like coffee.
The past perfect: had done过去完成时：had done
had plus the past participle shows that something was finished before a certain time in the past. It often goes with phrases such as by then or in a few years, counting up to that time.
had + 过去分词，表示在过去某个时间之前已经完成的事。常和 by then、by the end of、in a few years（指到某个过去时间为止）连用。

In sentence 12, counting on from 1958, the change was already complete within a few years.

本课：第 12 句 the workshop had become a factory——从 1958 年数起，没过几年就已经变成了。

  • By the time we got to the station, the train had left.
  • In ten years the village had grown into a busy town.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • Before he retired, X was the head of … 退休前，X 是……的负责人
    Before she retired, my aunt was the head of a primary school.
  • It was X's job to … ……是 X 的工作
    It was my job to feed the chickens every morning.
  • X saved money for years and bought … of his own. X 攒了多年钱，买了自己的……
    Mia saved for years and bought a flat of her own.
  • Within a few years X had grown into a big … 没几年，X 就长成了大……
    Within ten years the corner stall had grown into a big supermarket.
  • X was still … when … X 还在……的时候，……
    We were still eating when the lights went out.

Our model summary本站范文

Frank Hawkins once ran a huge company, but he started out as a boy mending bicycles in a shop, working fourteen hours a day. After saving for years, he bought his own small workshop in 1958 and made aircraft parts there with two helpers. Within a few years it had become a factory employing over seven hundred people. While he was smiling at these memories, his wife came in and asked him to fix their grandson's bike.（77 词）
