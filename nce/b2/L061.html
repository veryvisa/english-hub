Lesson 61 · Trouble with the Hubble 哈勃望远镜

📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 61 “Trouble with the Hubble”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 61《Trouble with the Hubble》

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
NASA sent the Hubble up in 1990, spending more than a billion dollars on it, and it went wrong at once: the main mirror had a flaw and the first images were a let-down. Four astronauts were to fly up on the shuttle Endeavour, a mechanical arm would hold the instrument, and they would fix it by hand. Since it orbits above the air, it would then send back sharper views of the stars than anyone had had. This is a science news piece that explains and looks ahead. The grammar is the future continuous, the future perfect, by the time and put something right.
哈勃望远镜 1990 年由美国宇航局送上太空，花了十亿多美元，可一开始就出了毛病：主镜有缺陷，传回的照片令人失望。宇航局准备派四名宇航员乘「奋进号」航天飞机上去修，机械臂抓住望远镜，宇航员动手修理。因为它在大气层之上，修好后会给我们传回有史以来最清晰的星空照片。本课是一篇说明加预告的科技新闻，学将来进行时 will be doing、将来完成时 will have done、by the time 和 put sth right。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. What went wrong with the Hubble at the start?哈勃望远镜一开始出了什么问题？
  2. Why would its pictures be clearer than those from telescopes on the ground?为什么修好之后它拍的照片会比地面望远镜清楚？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Unsigned and written for the course; it appears to have been added to the revised edition of the early nineteen-nineties, before the repair it describes had taken place.

本课未署名、为教材自撰；看内容应是二十世纪九十年代初修订版新加的课，写在它所预告的那次维修之前。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

The lesson gives the launch date as the twentieth of April, but the shuttle Discovery actually carried the telescope up on the twenty-fourth of April 1990, so treat that detail with care. The fault was real and famous: the main mirror had been ground to the wrong shape by about one fiftieth of the width of a human hair, which was enough to blur every image. In December 1993 the shuttle Endeavour did fly up, and seven astronauts, not four, took turns on long spacewalks to fit corrective optics, rather like giving the telescope a pair of glasses. After that the pictures were superb, and later missions kept it working for decades. It helped fix the age of the universe at roughly fourteen billion years and gave evidence that its expansion is speeding up.

课文说发射日期是 4 月 20 日，实际上是「发现号」航天飞机在 1990 年 4 月 24 日把它送上去的，这个细节要留意。毛病确有其事，而且很出名：主镜的形状磨错了，误差大约只有头发丝粗细的五十分之一，却足以让每张照片都模糊。1993 年 12 月，「奋进号」确实上去了，不过上去的是七名宇航员而不是四名，他们轮流出舱、长时间作业，装上校正光学部件，好比给望远镜配了一副眼镜。此后照片极其清晰，后来几次维修又让它工作了几十年。它帮助把宇宙年龄定在约一百四十亿年，并给出了宇宙膨胀正在加速的证据。

✨ How it is written赏析

The piece is a small lesson in tense planning. It opens in the past for the launch and the failure, moves into the present for what the agency means to do, and then into a chain of futures: a plan, an arranged step, a continuing step, and finally a future perfect that jumps ahead of the reader. That last move is clever, because it imagines the reader's own moment of reading as a point already past the repair. The writer also keeps the human side small and the machine large; the astronauts are numbers, while the telescope gets an eagle eye. For a learner, the useful habit to copy is how each tense change is marked by a time word such as now, soon or by the time.

这篇是一堂时态安排的小课。开头用过去时讲发射和失败，转到现在时讲宇航局的打算，再进入一串将来时：打算、安排好的步骤、持续进行的步骤，最后用一个将来完成时跳到读者前面。最后这一步很巧：它把读者「正在读」的那一刻设想成维修早已完成之后的时间点。作者还把人写得小、把机器写得大：宇航员只是一个数字，望远镜却得到了「鹰眼」的称号。对学习者来说，值得学的是每次换时态都有一个时间词作标志，比如 now、soon、by the time。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The lesson's confident forecast came true, which is not always so with science news. Its tone is a good model of how engineering projects are reported: problem, fix, promise. Reading it today, two things stand out. First, a costly failure was not the end of the story; the telescope became one of the most productive instruments ever built because people were willing to go back and repair it. Second, the newer James Webb telescope, launched in 2021, sits far beyond the reach of any shuttle, so no repair crew can visit it. That shows how much the Hubble's design, built to be serviced, mattered.

课文那句自信的预言应验了，科技新闻并不总是这样。它的语气是报道工程项目的好样板：问题、修法、承诺。今天读来有两点值得注意。第一，一次昂贵的失败并没有让故事结束，正因为人们肯回去修，它才成了史上产出最多的仪器之一。第二，2021 年发射的詹姆斯·韦布望远镜远在任何航天飞机够不着的地方，没有维修队能去。这说明哈勃「设计成可维修」这一点有多重要。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 2第 2 句 (begins开头 “space by NASA …”)

  • was launched: past simple passive, with NASA brought in by by.was launched：一般过去时被动，主语是望远镜，NASA 用 by 带出。
  • at a cost of: spending, often used for the price of a project.at a cost of：以……的代价，常用来说工程花了多少钱。

Sentence 4第 4 句 (begins开头 “The pictures it …”)

  • The pictures it sent us: a relative clause with that left out.The pictures it sent us：省略了 that/which 的定语从句。
  • disappointing describes the pictures; disappointed would describe the people.disappointing：说东西令人失望；人感到失望用 disappointed。

Sentence 6第 6 句 (begins开头 “so it will …”)

  • be going to shows a plan already made; put right means fix.be going to：已有打算。put … right：修好、纠正。
  • will be sending up: future continuous for something that is planned.will be sending up：将来进行时，说按计划会发生的事。

Sentence 8第 8 句 (begins开头 “A robot-arm from …”)

  • In the while clause the present make refers to the future.while 引导的时间从句说将来，用一般现在时 make，不用 will。
  • make repairs: carry out repairs.make repairs：进行修理。

Sentence 12第 12 句 (begins开头 “By the time …”)

  • by the time plus the present, with will have done in the main clause: finished before then.by the time + 一般现在时，主句用将来完成时：到那时已经完成。
  • eagle eye: a figure of speech for the telescope.eagle eye：鹰眼，比喻望远镜。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

The future perfect: will have done将来完成时：will have done
will have plus the past participle says that something will be finished before a point in the future. It often goes with by, by the time or by then.
will have + 过去分词：到将来某一时刻为止，某事已经完成。常和 by、by the time、by then 连用。

The last sentence uses by the time with the future perfect: by the moment you read it, the work will be done.

本课：最后一句用 by the time 引出将来完成时，作者设想读者读到文章时望远镜早已传回了大量照片。

  • By next June I will have finished my degree.
  • By the time we get there, the film will have started.
The future continuous and be going to for plans将来进行时与 be going to：说计划
be going to stresses an intention already formed; will be doing reports something arranged, in a calm, neutral tone, and is common in news.
be going to 强调已有的打算；will be doing 说按安排、照常会发生的事，语气平静客观，新闻里常见。

Sentence 5 states the intention with going to; sentences 6 and 7 switch to the future continuous for the arranged steps.

本课：第 5 句用 is going to 说宇航局的打算，第 6、7 句改用 will be sending、will be taking 说安排好的过程。

  • I'm going to learn to drive this summer.
  • The train will be leaving from platform four.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • X was launched at a cost of over … X 以超过……的代价推出
    The new metro line was opened at a cost of over two billion dollars.
  • Right from the beginning there was trouble with X. X 从一开始就有麻烦
    Right from the beginning there was trouble with the new printer.
  • X is going to put Y right, so it will shortly be … X 打算把 Y 修好，所以很快会……
    The council is going to put the road right, so it will shortly be sending workers.
  • By the time you …, X will have … 等你……的时候，X 已经……
    By the time you get home, I will have cooked dinner.
  • X will tell us a lot about … X 会告诉我们很多关于……的事
    These old letters will tell us a lot about village life.

Our model summary本站范文

In 1990 NASA put the Hubble into orbit, spending more than a billion dollars. From the start its images were poor, as its main mirror had a flaw. Four astronauts would fly up on Endeavour, and a mechanical arm would hold the telescope while they fixed it. Working high above the air, it would then send the clearest views of space yet, helping scientists learn how old and how big the universe is.（73 词）
