Lesson 4 · An Exciting Trip 激动人心的旅行

📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 4 “An Exciting Trip”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 4《An Exciting Trip》

B1🧠 15 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
The writer has had a letter from his brother Tim, an engineer with a large company, who went out to Australia half a year ago and has already seen a good deal of the country. He has bought a local car and driven to Alice Springs, a little place in the middle of the continent, and next he will go on to Darwin and then fly to Perth. Tim had never left his own country before, which is why it all thrills him. This lesson is a present perfect workout, with just, already, never and for each at work, plus the difference between have been and have gone.
作者收到弟弟蒂姆从澳大利亚寄来的信。蒂姆是工程师，在一家大公司工作，到澳大利亚半年已经跑了很多地方；他刚买了一辆澳洲车，开到了大陆正中的小镇艾丽斯斯普林斯，接下来还要去达尔文，再飞珀斯。他以前从没出过国，所以觉得这趟很刺激。本课是现在完成时的集中课：just、already、never、for 各显身手，再加上 have been / have gone 的区别。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. Why does Tim find this trip so thrilling?蒂姆为什么觉得这趟旅行很刺激？
  2. How long has Tim been in Australia, where has he been, and where is he going next?蒂姆在澳大利亚多久了？去过哪些地方，还要去哪里？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander for New Concept English (Longman, 1967); Tim is invented, but the towns are real.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为《新概念英语》（朗文，1967）自撰；蒂姆是虚构人物，地名都是真的。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

The places in the letter make a real route. Alice Springs sits close to the middle of the continent, Darwin is on the north coast, and the road between them runs for something like fifteen hundred kilometres through very dry country. Perth is on the far west coast, and from Darwin it is a very long way by any road, which is why flying makes sense. When this lesson was written, Australia was keen to bring in skilled workers from Britain, and many young engineers went out to work on roads, mines and power schemes. A letter home about a new car and a long drive inland was a common kind of news for families at that time.

信里的地名连成一条真实的路线。艾丽斯斯普林斯在大陆正中附近，达尔文在北部海岸，两地之间的公路大约一千五百公里，穿过非常干旱的地带。珀斯在遥远的西海岸，从达尔文走哪条路都极远，所以坐飞机合情合理。写这课的年代，澳大利亚很想从英国引进有技术的劳动力，许多年轻工程师远赴那里修路、开矿、建电站。一封寄回家的信，讲新买的车和深入内陆的长途驾驶，是当时很多家庭常收到的消息。

✨ How it is written赏析

The lesson is a letter reported at second hand, so the tense is set by the writer's present moment: everything that has happened to Tim so far is in the present perfect, and the plans are in the future. This makes the text a map in time as well as in space. Past events are piled up with just, already and for; future ones follow with soon and from there. The last sentence ties it together with a reason, and the reason is personal rather than geographical. We are not told what Tim builds or whom he meets. The excitement comes from the simple fact that everything is new to him, and the grammar of firsts, never before, carries it.

这课是转述的一封信，所以时态以作者「此刻」为准：到目前为止发生在蒂姆身上的事一律用现在完成时，计划用将来时。这让课文既是一张空间地图，也是一张时间地图。过去的事用 just、already、for 叠起来，将来的事用 soon 和「从那里」接上。最后一句用一个理由收拢全文，而这个理由是个人的，不是地理的。我们不知道蒂姆建的是什么、遇到了谁；兴奋就来自一件简单的事——一切对他都是新的，而「从来没有……过」这种表示「头一回」的语法承载了它。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The kind of move Tim makes is still common, though the direction has changed. Many young engineers from China, India and elsewhere now go to Australia and Canada for work, and the first months bring the same mix of big distances, a first car and a sense that everything is new. For a reader planning such a move, the lesson is a useful reminder that the country is huge: places that look close on a map can be a day's drive apart. It is also a good model for writing home. Tim's news is short, concrete and full of place names, which is exactly what people at home want to hear.

蒂姆这种远走他乡的工作迁移今天依然常见，只是方向变了。如今许多来自中国、印度等地的年轻工程师去澳大利亚、加拿大工作，头几个月同样是远得惊人的距离、人生第一辆车、一切都新鲜的感觉。对正在计划这类迁移的读者，课文是个有用的提醒：那片国土大得惊人，地图上看着挨着的地方，开车可能要一整天。这也是写家书的好样板：蒂姆的消息短、具体、满是地名，恰恰是家里人最想听的。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 1第 1 句 (begins开头 “I have just …”)

  • have just done: finished a moment ago, with the result right here: the letter is in his hands.have just done：刚刚做完。信刚到手，结果就在眼前。
  • After the comma, Tim names which brother; this is apposition.my brother, Tim：逗号后是同位语，说明是哪个弟弟。

Sentence 3第 3 句 (begins开头 “He has been …”)

  • has been with for and a length of time: it began in the past and is still true now.have been + for + 一段时间：从过去持续到现在，人还在那里。
  • Do not use has gone here: with for and a length of time English needs has been.中文说「他去了半年了」，英语不能用 has gone for six months。

Sentence 7第 7 句 (begins开头 “He has just …”)

  • has gone to: he went and is still there, not back yet.has gone to：去了（人还在那里，没回来）。
  • The phrase after the comma explains what Alice Springs is.逗号后的短语是同位语，解释 Alice Springs 是什么地方。

Sentence 11第 11 句 (begins开头 “My brother has …”)

  • has never been abroad: abroad is an adverb, so no to before it.have never been abroad：从来没出过国。abroad 是副词，前面不加 to。
  • is finding: the present continuous for his feelings during this period.is finding：现在进行时，说的是他眼下这段时间的感受。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

The present perfect with just, already, never and for现在完成时：just、already、never、for
have or has with the past participle joins a past event to the present. just means a moment ago, already means by now, never means at no time up to now, and for with a length of time means it has lasted until now.
have/has + 过去分词，把过去的事和现在连起来。just 刚刚，already 已经，never 从未，for + 一段时间 表示持续到现在。

The lesson runs through all four: just in sentences 1 and 7, for in sentence 3, already in sentence 5, and never in sentence 11.

本课：第 1 句用 just，第 3 句用 for，第 5 句用 already，第 7 句又用 just，第 11 句用 never。

  • The train has just left.
  • I have already read that book.
  • She has worked here for ten years.
have been and have gonehave been 与 have gone
have been to a place means you went and came back. have gone to a place means you went and are still there or on the way. have been there for a time means you have stayed there that long.
have been to + 地点 = 去过（已经回来）；have gone to + 地点 = 去了（还在那里或在路上）。have been + 在某地 + for … = 在那里待了多久。

Sentence 3 tells how long Tim has been in the country; sentence 7 shows he is in Alice Springs right now.

本课：第 3 句 has been there 讲待了多久；第 7 句 has gone to 说明蒂姆此刻人在那里。

  • Have you ever been to Japan? — Yes, twice.
  • Where is Mum? — She has gone to the market.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • I have just received a … from … 我刚收到……寄来的……
    I have just received a parcel from my aunt.
  • X has been … for + 时间 X 在……已经……了
    My grandmother has been in hospital for a week.
  • X has never been … before, so … X 以前从没……，所以……
    She has never flown before, so she is a little nervous.
  • X will soon … From there, X will … X 很快就要……，从那里再……
    We will soon reach Banff. From there, we will drive to Jasper.

Our model summary本站范文

My brother Tim, an engineer, has worked in Australia for six months and has seen many places. Now he has bought a car and driven to Alice Springs. Soon he will visit Darwin and fly on to Perth. It is his first trip overseas, so he loves it.（48 词）
