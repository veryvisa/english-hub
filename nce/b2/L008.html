Lesson 8 · The Best and the Worst 最好的和最差的

📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 8 “The Best and the Worst”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 8《The Best and the Worst》

B1🧠 15 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
Joe Sanders has the loveliest garden in town. Almost everyone takes part in the yearly garden contest, and Joe wins it every year. Bill Frith's plot is bigger than Joe's, Bill puts in more effort and grows more, yet Joe's is the one people find interesting, with tidy paths and a little wooden bridge over a pond. The writer likes gardens too but dislikes hard work, so he enters every year and every year takes home a small prize, for the worst garden of all. The grammar is comparatives and superlatives of adjectives and adverbs, including the irregular forms of good and bad.
乔·桑德斯的花园是镇上最美的。镇里几乎人人都参加一年一度的花园评比，可每回都是乔赢。比尔·弗里斯的园子比乔的大，他比乔更卖力，种的花和菜也更多，可乔的园子更有意思：小径修得整整齐齐，池塘上还架了一座木桥。作者也喜欢花园，可不爱干重活，每年也报名，每年都能拿个小奖——全镇最差花园奖。本课学形容词与副词的比较级、最高级，包括 good、bad 的不规则变化。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. Why is Joe's garden the finest in town?乔的花园为什么是镇上最美的？
  2. What prize does the writer win?作者拿的是什么奖？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander for New Concept English (Longman, 1967); Joe Sanders and Bill Frith are invented.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为《新概念英语》（朗文，1967）自撰；乔·桑德斯和比尔·弗里斯是虚构人物。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

Gardening is close to a national hobby in Britain, and small towns and villages often hold yearly shows where people bring their best flowers and vegetables to be judged. Prizes are usually modest, a small cup, a card or a few pounds, but the pride is real, and neighbours take the results seriously. Many towns also give prizes for the best front garden or the best street, judged by walking round. Against this background the writer's prize is a gentle joke: a real show would never reward the worst entry, so the town is either very kind or making fun of him, and he does not seem to mind which.

园艺在英国几乎算得上全民爱好，小镇和村子常办一年一度的园艺展，大家把最好的花和菜拿来评比。奖品通常不大——一只小奖杯、一张证书或几镑钱——可荣誉感是真的，邻居们把评比结果看得很认真。很多镇子还评最佳前院、最佳街道，评委挨家走一圈来打分。放在这个背景下，作者拿的奖是个温和的玩笑：真正的园艺展绝不会奖励最差的参赛者，所以要么这个镇非常宽厚，要么是在拿他开心——而他似乎并不在乎是哪一种。

✨ How it is written赏析

The lesson is built entirely on comparison, so the grammar and the meaning are the same thing. First comes the top, Joe and his superlative. Then Bill is placed against Joe with a row of comparatives that seem to favour Bill: larger, harder, more. The word but turns the row round, and we learn that effort and size are not what wins; ideas do, a path, a bridge, a pool. Then the writer adds himself at the bottom of the scale with the other superlative. The three men make a neat ladder: the clever one, the hard worker and the honest idler. The final exclamation lets the weakest man have the last laugh.

这课完全建立在比较之上，所以语法就是意思本身。先摆出顶端：乔和他的最高级。然后把比尔放在乔对面，一串比较级看似都偏向比尔——更大、更勤快、更多。一个「但是」把这一串翻过来：赢的不是力气和面积，而是心思——一条小径、一座桥、一方水池。接着作者把自己放到最底端，用的是另一个最高级。三个人排成一架整齐的梯子：聪明的、勤快的、老实偷懒的。最后的感叹号让最弱的那个笑到了最后。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The contrast between Bill and Joe makes a point that holds well beyond gardens: working harder and producing more is not the same as doing better. Judges, readers and customers often reward design and care over sheer quantity. The writer's own position is worth a thought too. He has chosen to enjoy something without trying to be good at it, and he is cheerful about coming last. Many Chinese students are taught that only first place counts; the lesson offers another view, that taking part with good humour is also a kind of success. Balcony vegetable plots in Chinese cities show the same simple pleasure in growing things.

比尔与乔的对照讲了一个远不止于花园的道理：更卖力、产量更大，不等于做得更好。评委、读者和顾客往往奖励设计与用心，而不是单纯的数量。作者自己的位置也值得想一想：他选择享受一件事而不求擅长，排在最后也乐呵呵的。很多中国学生从小被教导只有第一名才算数；课文给了另一种看法：带着好心情参与，也是一种成功。中国城市里阳台上的小菜园，体现的也是这种种东西本身的简单乐趣。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 3第 3 句 (begins开头 “Joe Sanders has …”)

  • Longer adjectives form the superlative with the most.多音节形容词的最高级：the most + 形容词。
  • The superlative is often followed by in and a group or place.in our town：最高级后常用 in + 范围。

Sentence 5第 5 句 (begins开头 “Nearly everybody enters …”)

  • enter for: put your name down for a contest. everybody takes a singular verb.enter for：报名参加（比赛、考试）。everybody 作主语，动词用单数 enters。
  • every time: on each occasion.every time：每次都。

Sentence 9第 9 句 (begins开头 “Bill works harder …”)

  • harder is the comparative of the adverb hard and describes works.harder 是副词 hard 的比较级，修饰 works。
  • more is the comparative of many; more interesting is the long-adjective comparative.more：many 的比较级；more interesting：多音节形容词的比较级。

Sentence 19第 19 句 (begins开头 “for the worst …”)

  • worst is the superlative of bad: bad, worse, worst.worst 是 bad 的最高级（bad – worse – worst）。
  • The twist: even the worst garden wins a prize.最后一句翻转：「最差」也能拿奖。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

Forming comparatives and superlatives比较级和最高级的构成
Short words add -er and -est, as with large and hard. Longer words take more and most, as with interesting. A final y after a consonant changes to i, as in happier. A comparative is followed by than, and a superlative takes the.
单音节词加 -er / -est（large → larger → largest，hard → harder）；多音节词前加 more / most（interesting → more interesting → most interesting）；以辅音 + y 结尾的改 y 为 i（happy → happier）。比较级后用 than，最高级前加 the。

Sentence 8 has larger than, sentence 9 harder than, sentence 11 more interesting, and sentence 3 the most beautiful.

本课：第 8 句 larger than，第 9 句 harder than，第 11 句 more interesting，第 3 句 the most beautiful。

  • This road is wider than that one.
  • It was the most exciting film I've ever seen.
Irregular forms: good, bad, many and much不规则变化：good、bad、many / much
good and well become better and best; bad and badly become worse and worst; many and much become more and most; little becomes less and least.

Sentence 10 has more, from many; sentence 19 has the worst, from bad; and the name of the contest uses nicest, from nice.

本课：第 10 句 more flowers（many 的比较级）；第 19 句 the worst garden（bad 的最高级）；评比名称里用了 nicest（nice 的最高级）。

  • Her English is better than mine.
  • That was the worst meal I've ever had.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • X has the most … in our town. X 有我们镇上最……的……
    Mrs Wong has the most popular bakery in our town.
  • X is larger than Y's, but Y's is more … X 比 Y 的大，但 Y 的更……
    Our flat is larger than theirs, but theirs is more comfortable.
  • X works harder than Y. X 比 Y 更努力。
    My sister studies harder than I do.
  • X wins every time. X 每次都赢。
    When we play chess, Grandpa wins every time.

Our model summary本站范文

Joe Sanders wins the garden contest in our town every year. Bill Frith works harder and his garden is bigger, but Joe's is more interesting. I enter every year too, but I hate hard work, so I always win a small prize for the worst garden.（46 词）
