Lesson 27 · A Wet Night 雨夜

📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 27 “A Wet Night”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 27《A Wet Night》

B1🧠 19 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
Late in the day the boys pitch their tent in the centre of a field and then cook over an open fire. They are all hungry, and the food smells good. After a fine meal they tell stories and sing by the fire. Later rain begins, and feeling sleepy they let the flames die and climb inside, where their warm sleeping bags give them a deep sleep. In the night two of them wake up shouting: water has filled the tent. Everyone jumps up and rushes out into heavy rain, to find that a stream has appeared in the field and is running straight beneath them. The grammar is as soon as, so for a result, and the past perfect.
傍晚，男孩们在一块地的中间搭起帐篷，接着生火做饭。大家都饿了，饭菜很香。吃完一顿美餐，他们围着篝火讲故事、唱歌。过了一阵开始下雨，他们困了，就灭了火钻进帐篷，睡袋又暖又舒服，都睡得很香。半夜两个男孩醒来大叫：帐篷里全是水！大家跳出睡袋冲到外面，雨下得很大，原来地里形成了一条小溪，弯弯曲曲穿过田野，正好从帐篷底下流过！本课学 as soon as、so 表结果和过去完成时 had formed。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. What went wrong for the boys in the night?夜里男孩们出了什么事？
  2. What mistake did they make when they chose where to camp?他们搭帐篷时犯了什么错？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander for New Concept English (Longman, 1967); the boys and the field are invented.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为《新概念英语》（朗文，1967）自撰；这群男孩和那块地都是虚构的。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

Camping was hugely popular with British young people in the mid twentieth century, helped by the Scouts, youth hostels and cheap canvas tents. Tents of the time usually had no sewn-in groundsheet, so water running over the ground could pass straight through them; modern tents have a waterproof floor joined to the walls, which would have kept the boys much drier. Every camping guide gives the same first rule: do not pitch in a hollow or at the bottom of a slope, because rain gathers there. In heavy rain a dry field can form temporary streams in a few hours, and in hilly country such flash flooding can be dangerous, not just funny.

二十世纪中叶，露营在英国年轻人中极受欢迎，童子军、青年旅舍和便宜的帆布帐篷都推波助澜。那时的帐篷通常没有缝死的地布，地面流过的水可以直接穿过帐篷；现代帐篷有和四壁连在一起的防水底，男孩们会干爽得多。每本露营指南的第一条规矩都一样：不要把帐篷扎在洼地或坡底，因为雨水会往那里汇。下大雨时，一块干地几小时内就会冒出临时的小溪；在山区，这种突发洪水可能很危险，而不只是好笑。

✨ How it is written赏析

The lesson spends more than half its length on comfort before it lets anything go wrong. A tent goes up, a meal is cooked, food smells good, there are stories and songs, and sleeping bags are warm. Each sentence adds a little more ease, and the linking words, as soon as, after, so, keep everything moving smoothly. Even the first rain causes no worry. Then the middle of the night arrives with shouting and a short exclamation, and the pace changes: they leap, they hurry, they find. The final sentence follows the stream across the field like a camera and stops on the word tent, so the reader sees the cause of the trouble at exactly the moment the boys do.

课文用了一半以上的篇幅写舒服，才让事情出错。搭帐篷，做饭，饭菜香，讲故事，唱歌，睡袋暖和。每一句都再添一点安逸，连接词——as soon as、after、so——让一切顺顺当当地往前走。连开始下雨都没让人担心。然后半夜来了，有喊叫，有一句短促的感叹，节奏变了：他们跳起来，冲出去，发现了。最后一句像镜头一样跟着那条小溪穿过田野，停在「帐篷」这个词上，于是读者恰好和男孩们同时看到了麻烦的根源。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The story is told as a comedy, but its lesson is practical and still taught: where you sleep matters more than how warm your bag is. The boys did nothing wrong except choose the spot by what was convenient, the middle of the field, rather than by where water would go. People make the same mistake on a larger scale when homes are built on flood plains because the land is flat and cheap. Readers in China, where summer rains can be very heavy, will know how quickly low ground floods. It is worth asking, of any comfortable plan, what happens to it if the weather changes.

故事是当喜剧讲的，可它的教训是实用的，而且至今仍有人在教：睡在哪里，比睡袋多暖和更重要。男孩们没做错什么，只是按方便来选地方——地的正中间——而没有按水会往哪里流来选。人们在更大的尺度上犯同样的错误：因为地势平、地价便宜，把房子建在洪泛区上。中国夏季雨水可能非常大，中国读者知道低洼地被淹得有多快。对任何一个舒舒服服的计划，都值得问一句：天气一变，它会怎样？

💬 Discuss and write讨论与写作
  1. Tell of a time when a trip went wrong because of the weather. Use as soon as and so at least once each.
    讲一次因为天气而出岔子的出行，as soon as 和 so 至少各用一次。
  2. Write five rules for choosing a safe place to camp.
    写出选择安全露营地点的五条规矩。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 2第 2 句 (begins开头 “As soon as …”)

  • as soon as: right after. The passive that follows refers to putting up the tent.as soon as：一……就……，表示紧接着发生。后面是一般过去时被动，this 指搭帐篷。
  • over an open fire: above flames that are not inside a stove.over an open fire：在明火上，over 表示在火的上方。

Sentence 6第 6 句 (begins开头 “The boys felt …”)

  • so introduces the result.so：所以，引出结果。
  • crept: the past of creep, which shows how low the tent was.crept：creep 的过去式，钻进去，形象地写出帐篷低矮。

Sentence 11第 11 句 (begins开头 “It was raining …”)

  • was raining: the past continuous describes the scene.was raining：过去进行时，描写当时的背景。
  • had formed: the past perfect, because the stream was there before they found it.had formed：过去完成时，小溪在他们发现之前就已经形成。

Sentence 12第 12 句 (begins开头 “The stream wound …”)

  • wind one's way: follow a twisting course. wound, said to rhyme with sound, is its past form.wind one's way：蜿蜒前进；wound /waʊnd/ 是 wind 的过去式。
  • right under: exactly under. right adds force, and this is where the joke lands.right under：正好在……下面，right 加强语气。笑点在这里。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

as soon as: immediately afteras soon as：一……就……
as soon as introduces a time clause for something that happens right after something else. For the future, the clause takes the present: as soon as I arrive, I will call.
as soon as 引导时间状语从句，表示一件事紧接着另一件事发生。说将来时从句用一般现在时（as soon as I arrive, I will call）。

Sentence 2 uses as soon as for the tent, followed at once by the cooking.

本课：第 2 句 as soon as 引出搭帐篷，紧接着做饭。

  • As soon as the bell rang, the children ran out.
  • I'll phone you as soon as I get home.
so for a resultso 表示结果
reason, so result. so is a joining word and often has a comma before it. Do not use it together with because in the same sentence.
原因, so 结果。so 是并列连词，前面常有逗号。不要和 because 同时用（不说 because …, so …）。

Sentences 6 and 7 both use so for a result: tired, so they slept; warm, so they slept well.

本课：第 6 句、第 7 句都用 so 引出结果：累了所以睡，暖和所以睡得香。

  • It was late, so we took a taxi.
  • The shop was closed, so I went home.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • As soon as that was finished, X … 那件事一做完，X 就……
    As soon as that was finished, we sat down to rest.
  • X felt …, so X … X 觉得……，于是……
    She felt cold, so she closed the window.
  • X woke up and began …ing. X 醒来就开始……
    The baby woke up and began crying.
  • X found that … had formed … X 发现……已经形成了
    We found that ice had formed on the pond.
