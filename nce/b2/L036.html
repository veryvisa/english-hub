Lesson 36 · Across the Channel 横渡英吉利海峡

📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 36 “Across the Channel”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 36《Across the Channel》

B1🧠 17 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
Tomorrow Debbie Hart plans to swim the English Channel, starting from France at five in the morning. She is just eleven and wants to break the world record. She swims powerfully, and plenty of people think she will certainly make it. Her father, who has coached her for years, will go with her in a little boat and will keep a nervous eye on her all the way to England. She means to stop briefly every couple of hours to drink, but will eat nothing solid. On the English side a crowd of classmates will be there to meet her, and so will her mother, who made the same crossing as a girl. The grammar is be going to, will and will be doing for the future, and be sure to do.
黛比·哈特明天要游泳横渡英吉利海峡。她将在早上五点从法国海岸出发。黛比只有十一岁，希望创造一项新的世界纪录。她游得很好，很多人觉得她一定能成功。父亲会坐一条小船陪她出发，他训练女儿已经好几年了，明天他会一路紧张地看着她游向英国。黛比打算每两小时短暂休息一次，可以喝点东西，但不吃固体食物。她的很多同学会在英国海岸等她，其中有她的妈妈——妈妈小时候也游过海峡。本课学三种将来：be going to、will、will be doing（将来进行时）和 be sure to do。

  2. Who will be waiting in England, and why is one of them special?谁会在英国海岸等她，其中有一个人为什么特别？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander for New Concept English (Longman, 1967); Debbie Hart is invented, but Channel swimming is a real and long-recorded sport.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为《新概念英语》（朗文，1967）自撰；黛比·哈特是虚构的，但横渡海峡游泳是一项真实且记录悠久的运动。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

The first recorded swim across the English Channel without a life jacket was made by Matthew Webb in 1875, and the first woman to do it was Gertrude Ederle in 1926. The usual crossing is about twenty miles at its narrowest, between Dover and Cap Gris-Nez, though tides make the real distance longer. Swims are watched and timed by official bodies, and the support boat that follows each swimmer is a real part of the rules. Very young swimmers did cross in the past, which led the organisers later to set a minimum age, and today a swimmer must be at least sixteen. Rules about feeding are also real: swimmers may take drinks and food passed from the boat, but may not touch it.

有记录的第一次不穿救生衣横渡英吉利海峡，是马修·韦伯在 1875 年完成的；第一位女性是格特鲁德·埃德尔，1926 年。通常的线路在多佛尔与格里内角之间，最窄处约二十英里，但潮汐让实际距离更长。横渡由官方机构见证计时，每位游泳者身后跟一条护航船，这是规则的一部分。过去确实有年纪很小的孩子游过，后来组织方因此设了最低年龄，如今游泳者至少要十六岁。关于补给的规定也是真的：游泳者可以接过从船上递来的饮料和食物，但不能扶船。

✨ How it is written赏析

The whole text is set in the future, and that is its point: nothing has happened yet, so every sentence is a plan, a hope or a prediction. The writer moves from plan, going to, to confident forecast, sure to, to a picture of tomorrow in progress, will be watching. The details are practical rather than emotional, start time, rests, food, which makes the event feel real and close. The last sentence adds a surprise that is not a twist but a link: the mother on the far shore has already done what the daughter is about to try.

全文都设在将来，这正是它的用意：什么都还没发生，所以每一句都是计划、希望或预测。作者从计划（going to）写到有把握的预测（sure to），再写到明天那一刻的画面（will be watching）。细节都是实际的而不是抒情的——出发时间、休息、饮食——让事件显得真实、近在眼前。最后一句添了一个惊喜，它不是反转而是连接：对岸的母亲已经做过女儿即将尝试的事。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

An eleven-year-old swimming twenty miles of cold sea now sounds worrying rather than inspiring, and the modern age rule shows that officials came to share that worry. The story is still about something real: years of family training and a parent who has walked the same road. Parents in China who push children towards hard goals, in sport, music or exams, may see themselves in Mr Hart. The fair question is where support ends and pressure begins, and who decides when a child is ready.

一个十一岁的孩子在冰冷的海里游二十英里，如今听起来更让人担心而不是激励；现代的年龄规定说明官方后来也有同样的担忧。不过故事讲的仍是真实的东西：多年的家庭训练，还有一位走过同一条路的父母。在体育、音乐或考试上督促孩子冲击难目标的中国家长，也许能在哈特先生身上看到自己。值得问的是：支持在哪里结束，压力从哪里开始，又由谁来判断孩子是否准备好了？

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

Three ways to talk about the future将来时的三种说法
be going to is for a plan already made; will is for the plain future and for predictions; will be doing, the future continuous, is for an action in progress at a future time. In time and if clauses, use the present simple for the future.
be going to：已有的打算或计划；will：一般将来、预测；will be doing（将来进行时）：将来某段时间正在进行的动作。时间、条件从句里用一般现在时表将来（as she swims）。

Sentence 1 uses be going to, sentence 9 uses will, and sentences 12 and 17 use will be with an -ing form.

本课：第 1 句是 be going to，第 9 句用 will，第 12 和第 17 句用 will be 加 -ing。

  • I'm going to visit my aunt next week.
  • This time tomorrow I will be flying to Paris.
be sure to do and be sure that
Subject plus be sure to do means the speaker is certain it will happen. Subject plus be sure that means the subject feels certain.
主语 + be sure to do：说话人认为主语一定会……。主语 + be sure (that) …：主语本人确信……。

In sentence 8 it is the many people who feel certain, not Debbie herself.

本课：第 8 句 she is sure to succeed——是「很多人」认为她一定成功，不是她自己确信。

  • It is sure to rain this afternoon.
  • I am sure (that) he will help us.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • X is going to swim across … tomorrow. X 明天要游过……
    Jack is going to cycle across Canada next summer.
  • X wants to break the … record. X 想打破……纪录
    The young runner wants to break the school record.
  • Many people feel that X is sure to … 很多人觉得 X 一定会……
    Most fans believe the team is sure to win.
  • Tomorrow X will be … as Y … 明天 Y……时，X 会一直在……
    Tomorrow we will be cheering as the runners finish.
  • Among them will be … 他们当中会有……
    Among the guests will be the mayor.

Our model summary本站范文

Tomorrow eleven-year-old Debbie Hart will try to swim the English Channel, starting from France at five in the morning. She hopes to set a world record. Her father, who has trained her for years, will follow her in a boat. She will rest every two hours and have drinks, but no food. Her classmates and her mother, who swam the Channel as a girl, will meet her on the English coast.（71 词）
