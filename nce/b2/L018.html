Lesson 18 · He Often does This 他经常干这种事！

📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 18 “He Often does This”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 18《He Often does This》

B1🧠 15 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
After lunch at a country pub, the writer finds that the bag he put on a chair near the door has gone. While he is searching, the landlord comes in and asks about the meal. The writer says it was good but he cannot pay, as his bag is missing. The landlord smiles, goes out, and a few minutes later brings the bag back with an apology: his dog carried it off to the garden, and does so regularly. The grammar is the past perfect (had done) and time clauses with as.
作者在一家乡村酒馆吃完午饭，发现放在门边椅子上的包不见了。他正找着，店主进来问饭菜怎么样。作者说饭很好，可是付不了账，因为包没了。店主笑了笑就出去，几分钟后拿着包回来道歉：是他的狗把包叼到花园里去了，「它常干这种事！」本课学过去完成时（had done）和 as 引导的时间状语。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. Where had the writer's bag really gone?作者的包到底去哪儿了？
  2. Why did the landlord smile as soon as he heard?店主为什么一听就笑了？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander for New Concept English (Longman, 1967); the pub, the landlord and the dog are invented.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为《新概念英语》（朗文，1967）自撰；酒馆、店主和狗都是虚构的。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

The village pub was, and in many places still is, the centre of English country life: a place to eat and drink, but also to meet neighbours, hear the news and leave messages. The landlord usually lived on the premises with his family, and often a dog, which is why a dog can wander between the bar and the garden without anyone thinking it strange. Pub dogs are still a familiar sight in Britain, and many pubs welcome customers' dogs too. The British pub has been in long decline since the period of the lesson; thousands have closed in recent decades, though the exact count depends on who is counting and how a pub is defined.

乡村酒馆过去是、在很多地方至今仍是英格兰乡村生活的中心：吃饭喝酒的地方，也是见邻居、听消息、留口信的地方。店主通常带着家人住在店里，往往还养一条狗，所以狗在吧台和花园之间转来转去，谁也不觉得奇怪。「酒馆狗」在英国至今很常见，很多酒馆也欢迎客人带狗来。从本课那个年代起，英国酒馆一直在长期衰落，近几十年关掉了成千上万家，不过具体数字取决于是谁在统计、怎么定义一家酒馆。

✨ How it is written赏析

The story is told in the order the writer lived it, and the past perfect is what makes that possible. He tells us about lunch and the missing bag in the plain past, and uses had done only for the things he did not see happen: where he had put the bag, and what the dog had done. So the grammar marks what the narrator knew and when he knew it. The landlord's smile in the middle is the clue a careful reader can catch: a man who smiles at news of a theft already knows it is not one. The final line switches to the present simple, and that change of tense is the joke, because it tells us this was not an accident but a habit.

故事是按作者亲历的顺序讲的，而这正是靠过去完成时才做得到的。午饭和包不见了，用一般过去时讲；只有他没亲眼看见的事——包是他什么时候放的、狗干了什么——才用 had done。所以语法标出了叙述者知道什么、什么时候知道的。中间店主那一笑，是细心的读者能抓住的线索：听说东西被偷还笑得出来的人，早就知道这不是偷。最后一句换成一般现在时，这次换时态就是笑点，因为它告诉我们，这不是意外，而是习惯。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The writer's first thought is that the bag has been stolen, and most readers think the same, which is worth noticing. When something goes missing in a public place, people tend to assume the worst, and in a small community a quick accusation can do lasting harm. The landlord handles it well: he says sorry at once, even though the fault is the dog's. Readers in China will recognise the value of a host who takes responsibility rather than arguing about blame, and the way a small apology keeps a customer coming back. The practical lesson for travellers is older than the story: in a strange place, ask before you assume.

作者第一个念头是包被偷了，大多数读者也这么想，这一点值得留意。在公共场所丢了东西，人们往往往最坏处想；在小地方，一句仓促的指控可能造成长久的伤害。店主处理得很好：虽然错在狗，他还是马上道歉。中国读者会懂得这种店家的价值——先担责任，而不是争是谁的错；一句小小的道歉，能让客人愿意再来。对旅行者来说，实用的教训比这个故事还老：在陌生的地方，先问清楚，再下结论。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 1第 1 句 (begins开头 “After I had …”)

  • had had: the past perfect. The first had is the helping verb and the second is the past participle of have, meaning eat. Lunch came before the search.had had：过去完成时，第一个 had 是助动词，第二个是 have（吃）的过去分词。吃饭在找包之前。
  • look for is the search; find is the result.look for：寻找（过程）；find 是找到（结果）。

Sentence 2第 2 句 (begins开头 “I had left …”)

  • had left: he put it there before he found it missing.had left：在发现包不见之前就已经放在那里。
  • now with the past tense means at that moment in the story.now 和过去时连用：叙事里的「这时」。

Sentence 3第 3 句 (begins开头 “As I was …”)

  • as: while. The longer action is in the past continuous, was looking, and the short one, came in, breaks into it.as：当……时。长动作用过去进行时（was looking），短动作用一般过去时（came in）打断它。

Sentence 9第 9 句 (begins开头 “'My dog had …”)

  • had taken: the dog did it before the landlord spoke.had taken：店主说话时，狗叼包早已发生。
  • often does: the present simple for a habit. This is where the title comes from.often does：一般现在时表示习惯，这就是标题的出处。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

The past perfect: had plus the past participle过去完成时：had + 过去分词
When you tell a past story and want to show that one event came before another past event, use the past perfect. It often goes with after, before, when and by the time.
讲过去的事时，如果要说某件事发生在另一件过去的事之前，用过去完成时。常和 after、before、when、by the time 连用。

Sentences 1, 2 and 9 each use had with a participle for an action earlier than the search or the discovery.

本课：第 1 句 had had、第 2 句 had left、第 9 句 had taken，都比「找包」「发现」更早。

  • When we got to the station, the train had already left.
  • She had never seen snow before she moved to Canada.
as or while with the past continuous, broken by the past simpleas / while + 过去进行时，被一般过去时打断
When a short action happens in the middle of a longer one, put the longer one in was or were doing and the short one in the simple past. as and while introduce the long action; when often introduces the short one.
一个较长的过去动作正在进行时，另一个短动作发生：长的用 was/were doing，短的用一般过去时。as 和 while 引导长动作，when 常引导短动作。

Sentence 3 opens with as and the past continuous, and came in breaks into it.

本课：第 3 句用 as 引出较长的「找」，再被 came in 打断。

  • While I was cooking, the phone rang.
  • I was walking home when it started to rain.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • After X had had …, X looked for … X 吃完……之后，找……
    After we had had breakfast, we looked for the bus stop.
  • X had left it … and now it wasn't there! X 把它放在……，可现在不见了！
    She had put it on the shelf and now it was gone!
  • As X was …ing, Y came in. X 正在……的时候，Y 进来了。
    As I was washing up, my brother came in.
  • In a few minutes X returned with … 几分钟后 X 带着……回来了
    In a few minutes the waiter came back with our drinks.

Our model summary本站范文

After lunch in a country pub, the writer could not find his bag. He told the landlord that he was unable to pay. Smiling, the landlord went out and soon returned with the bag. His dog had carried it into the garden, which it often did.（46 词）
