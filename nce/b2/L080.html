Lesson 80 · The Crystal Palace 水晶宫

📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 80 “The Crystal Palace”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 80《The Crystal Palace》

B1🧠 18 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
Perhaps no building of the eighteen hundreds was more remarkable than the great iron-and-glass hall put up in a London park for the world fair of 1851. Nothing like it existed anywhere, and it ranked among the largest structures ever built. Goods poured in from every continent, along with a mass of machines, the star being a huge steam-driven hammer. Travel was slow then, yet steamers ferried thousands of visitors over from the Continent, and trains carried them on to the hall. Six million came in all, and the surplus paid for museums and colleges. The hall was later rebuilt in the south of the city and stood until a fire in 1936. This is a short piece of historical description. The grammar is one of the plus a superlative, a great many and a great deal of, not as ... as and on doing.
十九世纪最不寻常的建筑，也许要算水晶宫：它为 1851 年的万国博览会建在伦敦海德公园，与世上一切房子都不同，因为全用铁和玻璃造成，是有史以来最大的建筑之一。世界各地送来大批展品，还有许多机器，其中最了不起的是内史密斯的蒸汽锤。那时出行远不如今天方便，汽船却把成千上万的欧洲游客运过英吉利海峡，一上岸就坐火车直奔会场。参观的人一共六百万，博览会的盈利拿去建了博物馆和学院。后来水晶宫搬到伦敦南部，一直是世界名楼，直到 1936 年毁于大火。本课是一篇简明的历史说明文，学 one of the + 最高级 + 复数、a great many / a great deal of、not as … as 和 on doing。

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Unsigned and written for the course; it summarises well-known history about the Great Exhibition held in London in 1851.

本课未署名、为教材自撰，概述的是 1851 年伦敦万国博览会的通行史实。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

The building was designed by Joseph Paxton, a gardener who had built large glasshouses, and it went up in only a few months because it was made of standard iron and glass parts that could be produced in factories and bolted together. The exhibition was championed by Prince Albert, Queen Victoria's husband, and ran from May to October 1851. The official count of visits was a little over six million. The surplus, about one hundred and eighty thousand pounds, bought land in South Kensington, where today's great museums, a concert hall and a university college now stand. The hall was taken down and rebuilt, larger, at Sydenham in south London in 1854, and was lost to fire on the last night of November 1936. James Nasmyth's steam hammer, patented a decade earlier, could forge huge pieces of iron yet was precise enough to crack an egg in a glass.

这座建筑由约瑟夫·帕克斯顿设计，他原本是园艺师，造过大型温室。它只用几个月就建成了，因为它由标准化的铁件和玻璃件组成，可以在工厂里生产、在现场用螺栓拼装。博览会由维多利亚女王的丈夫阿尔伯特亲王大力推动，1851 年 5 月到 10 月举办。官方统计的参观人次略多于六百万。盈余约十八万英镑，用来在南肯辛顿买地，今天那里矗立着几座大博物馆、一座音乐厅和一所大学学院。这座展厅后来被拆下，1854 年在伦敦南部的锡德纳姆以更大的规模重建，1936 年 11 月的最后一个夜晚毁于大火。詹姆斯·内史密斯的蒸汽锤十年前就已获得专利，能锻造巨大的铁件，又精确到能把玻璃杯里的鸡蛋敲开而不碰坏杯子。

✨ How it is written赏析

This is a model of clear informative writing. Each sentence carries one fact, and the order moves from the building, to what was inside, to the visitors, to the money, to the building's later life, so the reader always knows where they are. The writer uses comparison to make size and novelty understandable: different from all other buildings, one of the biggest of all time, less easy than now. Numbers are few but well chosen, a year, six million, another year, and they frame the whole story from opening to fire. There is almost no opinion except the careful perhaps in the first line.

这是清晰说明文的一个样板。每句只讲一个事实，顺序从建筑本身，到里面的展品，到参观者，到那笔钱，再到建筑后来的命运，所以读者总知道自己读到哪里了。作者用比较来让规模和新奇变得好懂：与世上一切建筑都不同、有史以来最大之一、不像今天这么方便。数字很少但选得好：一个年份、六百万、另一个年份，从开幕到大火框住了整个故事。除了第一行那个谨慎的 perhaps，几乎没有任何评论。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The exhibition was a celebration of industry and empire, and it showed what factory methods could do; the building itself was an early example of prefabrication, the idea behind many modern buildings. It also had a lasting public benefit, since the profits still support places that anyone can visit. A same-period comparison is useful: 1851 was the first year of the Xianfeng reign in China and the year the Taiping uprising began, so while Britain displayed its machines, China was entering one of its most destructive civil wars. Chinese accounts record that a Shanghai merchant sent silk to the exhibition and won a prize, but we have not checked the original records. The gap in industrial power that the fair made visible would shape the next century of contact between the two countries.

这次博览会是对工业和帝国的颂扬，展示了工厂化生产能做到什么；建筑本身就是预制装配的早期范例，这正是许多现代建筑背后的思路。它还留下了长久的公共利益，因为那笔盈利至今仍在支撑人人都能参观的场所。做个同期对比很有用：1851 年是中国咸丰元年，也是太平天国起事的一年；当英国在展示机器的时候，中国正走进一场破坏性极大的内战。中文资料记载有一位上海商人把生丝送去参展并获了奖，但我们没有核对原始记录。博览会让人看得见的工业实力差距，将塑造此后一百年两国之间的交往。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 1第 1 句 (begins开头 “Perhaps the most …”)

  • The superlative is limited by of plus a period; perhaps softens the claim.the most + 形容词 + of + 时段：最高级的范围。perhaps 使语气留有余地。
  • which was built: a relative clause in the past simple passive.which was built：定语从句，一般过去时被动。

Sentence 3第 3 句 (begins开头 “The Crystal Palace …”)

  • be different from: not like.be different from：与……不同。
  • for adds the reason; be made of names a material you can still see.for：因为（补充说明理由）；be made of：用……制成（看得出原材料）。

Sentence 4第 4 句 (begins开头 “It was one …”)

  • one of the plus a superlative plus a plural noun; of all time: ever.one of the + 最高级 + 复数名词。of all time：有史以来。

Sentence 8第 8 句 (begins开头 “Though in those …”)

  • though: in spite of the fact, with no but in the main clause; not as ... as: less than.though：虽然（主句不再用 but）。not as … as：不如……。

Sentence 10第 10 句 (begins开头 “On arriving in …”)

  • on doing: as soon as, or at the moment of doing.on + doing：一……就……。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

a great many and a great deal of
a great many goes with plural countable nouns and a great deal of with uncountable ones; a lot of goes with both.
a great many + 可数名词复数；a great deal of + 不可数名词。a lot of 两者都能用。

Sentence 5 uses a great many for the goods, and sentence 6 uses a great deal of for the machinery, because machinery is uncountable.

本课：第 5 句用 a great many 说物品多，第 6 句用 a great deal of 说机器多——因为 machinery 不可数。

  • A great many people came to the meeting.
  • She spends a great deal of time reading.
not as ... as, and on doingnot as … as 与 on doing
not as (or so) plus an adjective plus as means less than; on plus -ing means as soon as.
not as/so + 形容词/副词 + as：不如……；on + doing：一……就……（相当于 as soon as）。

Sentence 8 compares travel then with travel now, and sentence 10 says what happened the moment visitors landed.

本课：第 8 句比较当年与今天的出行；第 10 句写游客一上岸就被送往会场。

  • My bike is not as fast as yours.
  • On hearing the news, she burst into tears.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • Perhaps the most … of the … was … ……中最……的也许是……
    Perhaps the most famous singer of the decade was a shy girl from Ohio.
  • X was unlike any other … in the world, for … X 与世上任何……都不同，因为……
    The house was unlike any other home in town, for it was round.
  • X ranks among the biggest … of all time. X 是有史以来最大的……之一
    That storm ranks among the worst of all time.
  • Though travel was not as … then, … 虽然那时出行不如……，……
    Though roads were not as good then, carts brought farmers to market every week.
  • There were … in all, and the money was used to … 总共有……，那笔钱被用来……
    There were two hundred runners in all, and the money was used to buy a new bus for the school.

Our model summary本站范文

The Crystal Palace, made entirely of iron and glass, went up in a London park for the 1851 world fair. It was one of the largest buildings ever. Goods and machines came from all over the world, and the most admired was a steam hammer. Six million people visited, many crossing from Europe by steamer and coming on by train. The profits paid for museums and colleges. The building was later moved and was destroyed by fire in 1936.（79 词）
