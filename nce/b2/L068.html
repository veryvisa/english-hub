📘 New Concept English · Book 2 · Lesson 68 “Persistent”新概念英语 第二册 Lesson 68《Persistent》

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
The writer, Elizabeth, crossed the road to dodge Nigel Dykes, but he spotted her and ran over. Pretending was pointless, so she waved. Nigel has nothing to fill his days, and however much you have on, he will tag along. She needed a way to shake him off before he followed her all morning. After the greetings he asked if she was free, and she said yes; before she could say where she was heading, he asked to come too. She agreed through her teeth, then mentioned the dentist, hoping that would put him off. He simply said he would come, since waiting rooms always have magazines. This is a comic story told largely in dialogue. The grammar is it is no use doing, no matter how, insist on doing, prevent someone from doing and Would you mind my doing.
「我」过马路想躲开奈杰尔·戴克斯，可他看见了「我」，跑着迎上来。装没看见已经没用，「我」只好朝他招手。「我」从不喜欢碰到奈杰尔：他从来无事可做，不管你多忙，他都非要跟着你。「我」得想个办法，免得他一上午都跟着「我」。寒暄之后，他问「我」忙不忙，「我」说不忙，正要说去哪儿，他就抢着问能不能一起去。「我」违心地说「当然可以」，又补了一句：「不过我要去看牙医。」他回答：「那我陪你去，候诊室里总有很多东西可看！」本课是一段带讽刺的对话体叙事，学 it is no use doing、no matter how、insist on doing、prevent sb from doing 和 Would you mind my doing。

本课未署名、为教材自撰；伊丽莎白和奈杰尔都是虚构人物。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

The story depends on a very British habit: avoiding a direct refusal. Rather than say I would rather go alone, Elizabeth offers an excuse that she hopes will make Nigel withdraw, and he does not take the hint. Writers on English manners often point out that politeness in Britain leans on indirectness, where the listener is expected to understand what is not said. The joke works because Nigel breaks that unwritten rule. Readers from many cultures will recognise the problem, though the solutions differ; in some places a frank no is perfectly polite, while in others, as in much of China, people also prefer a soft excuse to save face for both sides.

这个故事靠的是一种很英国的习惯：避免直接拒绝。伊丽莎白不说「我想一个人去」，而是找个借口，希望奈杰尔知难而退，可他偏偏没听懂暗示。写英国礼仪的人常指出，英国人的客气很依赖委婉，听的人应该明白没说出口的话。笑点就在于奈杰尔打破了这条不成文的规矩。很多文化的读者都会认出这个难题，只是解法不同：有些地方直截了当说「不」完全算礼貌；另一些地方，比如中国的很多场合，人们也更喜欢用一个软借口，给双方都留面子。

✨ How it is written赏析

The story is told by the victim, so we share her impatience from the first line. The narration gives us her private thoughts, while the dialogue shows the polite surface, and the comedy lives in the gap between the two. Nigel interrupts her, which the writer shows by breaking her sentence off with three dots. The single word lied does a great deal of work, and the excuse is held back to the end of the same line, so that we expect it to work. Then Nigel's cheerful answer turns her weapon against her. The last line belongs to him, which is exactly how such people always seem to win.

故事由受害者讲述，所以从第一行起我们就和她一样不耐烦。叙述给我们她心里的话，对话展示礼貌的表面，喜剧就活在两者之间的缝隙里。奈杰尔打断了她，作者用三个点截断她的话来表现。lied 这一个词干了很多活；借口留到同一行末尾才说，让我们以为它会奏效。接着奈杰尔一句轻快的回答，把她的武器反过来对准了她。最后一行属于他，这类人似乎总是这样赢。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

Nigel is funny on the page and tiring in life. Most of us know someone like him, and many of us have been Elizabeth, telling a small lie to avoid a long morning. The story raises a useful question: is the white lie kinder than the truth? It did not work here, and it often does not, because a vague excuse invites a vague answer. A clear, friendly sentence such as I need to do this alone today may be both more honest and more effective. On the other hand, Nigel may simply be lonely, which gives the story a slightly sadder side if we look at it from his point of view.

奈杰尔在纸上好笑，在生活里累人。我们大多认识这样的人，很多人也当过伊丽莎白，用一个小谎躲开一个漫长的上午。故事提出一个有用的问题：善意的谎言比真话更体贴吗？这里它没奏效，往往也不奏效，因为含糊的借口只会招来含糊的回答。一句清楚、友好的话，比如「我今天得一个人去办这事」，可能既更诚实，也更有效。另一方面，奈杰尔也许只是孤单；从他的角度看，这个故事就多了一点伤感。

💬 Discuss and write讨论与写作

    有人想跟着你，你怎么礼貌地拒绝？给出两种说法。
  2. Rewrite the ending so that Elizabeth's plan works. Use no matter how once.
    改写结尾，让伊丽莎白的计划成功。用一次 no matter how。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 2第 2 句 (begins开头 “It was no …”)

  • it is no use doing: it stands in for the -ing phrase.it is no use doing：做……没有用，it 是形式主语。
  • had not seen: past perfect, for the time before.had not seen：过去完成时，「之前没看见」。

Sentence 5第 5 句 (begins开头 “No matter how …”)

  • no matter how plus adjective plus subject and verb: however much.no matter how + 形容词 + 主语 + 动词：无论多么……。
  • insist on doing: demand to do, and refuse to take no.insist on doing：坚持要做……。

Sentence 6第 6 句 (begins开头 “I had to …”)

  • a way of doing: a method of doing.a way of doing：做……的方法。
  • prevent someone from doing: stop them doing it.prevent sb from doing：阻止某人做……。

Sentence 9第 9 句 (begins开头 “You're not busy …”)

  • be busy doing: be occupied with doing.be busy doing：忙于做……。
  • A negative statement with a positive tag: Nigel is hoping for the answer no.否定陈述句 + 肯定反意疑问：说话人希望得到「不忙」的回答。

Sentence 11第 11 句 (begins开头 “'Would you mind …”)

  • Would you mind my doing: asks permission for the speaker; me can replace my. Not at all means you do not mind.Would you mind my doing：你介意我……吗？my 也可以换成 me。回答「不介意」用 Not at all。
  • before I had finished: I had not yet finished.before I had finished：过去完成时强调「我」还没说完。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

Fixed patterns with the -ing form动名词的几个固定句型
These are always followed by -ing: it is no use doing; insist on doing; prevent someone from doing; a way of doing; would you mind doing, or my doing for your own action.
it is no use / no good doing（做……没用）；insist on doing（坚持要做）；prevent / stop sb (from) doing（阻止某人做）；a way of doing（做……的方法）；Would you mind (my) doing（介意……吗）。这些后面都只能跟 -ing。

Sentences 2, 5, 6 and 11 each contain one, so the lesson is almost a list of -ing patterns.

本课：第 2、5、6、11 句连续出现，几乎每句都在学一个 -ing 句型。

  • It's no use crying about it now.
  • He insisted on driving me home.
no matter how, what and who
no matter plus a question word starts a clause meaning it makes no difference: no matter how is however, no matter what is whatever. The clause has statement order and uses the present for the future.
no matter + 疑问词引导让步状语从句，意思是「无论……」；no matter how = however，no matter what = whatever。从句用陈述语序，说将来也用一般现在时。

Sentence 5 uses no matter how busy to show how little Nigel notices others.

本课：第 5 句用 no matter how busy 说奈杰尔多么不识趣。

  • No matter how hard he tries, he can't win.
  • No matter what you say, I won't change my mind.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • I crossed the … to avoid … but … 我穿过……想避开……，可是……
    I crossed the hall to avoid my boss, but he called my name.
  • It was pointless to pretend that … 假装……已经没用了
    It was pointless to pretend that I understood the question.
  • No matter how … you are, X always insists on … 不管你多……，X 总是非要……
    No matter how full you are, my aunt always insists on giving you more food.
  • I needed a way of stopping X from … 我得想个办法不让 X……
    I needed a way of stopping the dog from eating my shoes.
  • 'Would you mind my …?' he asked. 「你介意我……吗？」他问
    'Would you mind my opening the window?' she asked.

Our model summary本站范文

Elizabeth crossed the road to avoid Nigel Dykes, but he saw her and ran over. Nigel never has anything to do and always wants to go along with people, so she needed a way to escape him. When he asked if he could join her, she had to say yes, but she added that she had a dental appointment. To her dismay, Nigel replied that he would come anyway, since there was always plenty to read there.（77 词）
