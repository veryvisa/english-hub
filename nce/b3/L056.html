Lesson 56 · Our Neighbour, the River 我们的邻居——河

📘 New Concept English · Book 3 · Lesson 56 “Our Neighbour, the River”新概念英语 第三册 Lesson 56《Our Neighbour, the River》

B2🧠 25 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
The river that marks the eastern edge of the writer's farm has always mattered to the family: the spring gives only enough water for the house, so farm water is pumped from the river. They tell the river all their secrets, as beekeepers tell their bees, sure by instinct that bad luck would follow if they did not. In summer there are birthday parties by the water, sometimes at a quiet backwater upstream, sometimes at the boathouse an earlier owner built beside the deepest pool, and in a heat wave at midnight, which is best of all. The seasons are marked there too: a crown of flowers for the youngest girl in spring, a festival on Midsummer Eve, thanks for the harvest in autumn and a holly wreath thrown into the stream in winter. After long rain the river can burst its banks, though the mild climate makes this rare; only their low fields, a small part of the farm, ever flood, while other farms are worse placed and may be ruined. One winter the water crept over the low meadows. Their cattle were already indoors, so they had little to lose, but they worried about their nearest neighbours, newcomers on low ground. The flood had cut the phone line, so they took turns at an attic window watching the spot where the two farms met. First a dead sheep floated past, then a horse swimming hard, and then a raft, rather like Noah's ark, with the whole family, hens, dogs, a cat and a caged bird. The neighbours had clearly panicked: their house, on firm foundations, would have stayed standing even if almost under water. The men waded out with boat hooks, hoping to catch the raft and pull it to their bank, and to everyone's amazement they did. This is a country essay with a rescue: the first half shows how close the family is to the river, the second shows the river turning dangerous, and the rescued family turn out to have had no real need to flee. The grammar to learn is play an important part in, make a living, just as, overtake, hard by, in that, spell disaster, stand to lose, take turns in, must have become, would have stood ... even if, and in the hope of.
作者家农场东边以一条河为界，河一直在他们生活里很重要：泉水只够家用，农场用水得从河里抽。他们把所有秘密都告诉河，像养蜂人把大事告诉蜜蜂一样，凭直觉觉得不告诉就会遭殃。夏天在河边过生日：有时去上游一处心爱的回水湾，有时在一位前任农场主建在深潭边草地上的船屋；热浪来时就开午夜生日会，那最刺激。四季也在河边迎送：春天给最小的女孩戴花冠，仲夏夜过节，秋天感恩丰收，冬天把冬青花环扔进河里。久雨之后河会漫堤，但这里气候少走极端，很少发生；他们幸运，只有占农场很小一部分的低地会被淹，别的农场位置差，洪水可能带来灾难。有一年冬天洪水漫上低地草场，他们的牛早已关进牛栏，损失不大，却担心离得最近、地势低又刚搬来的邻居。电话被洪水冲断，只能在阁楼窗口轮流盯着两家地界相接的那段河。先漂下来一只死羊，接着一匹马奋力游着；忽然出现一只木筏，像诺亚方舟，载着全家人、几只鸡、狗、猫和一只鸟笼。他们看出邻居是被涨水吓过了头——那所房子地基牢固，就算几乎被淹也不会倒。家里的男人拿着带钩的篙蹚过被淹的草场，想钩住木筏一角拉到岸边，居然成功了。本课是乡居随笔＋一次救援：前半写人和河的亲近，后半写河翻脸时的紧张，而救下的邻居其实没必要逃。学 play an important part in、make a living、just as、overtake、hard by、in that、spell disaster、stand to lose、take turns in、must have become、would have stood … even if、in the hope of。

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course in the manner of an English country memoir; the farm, the family and the flood read as invented or adapted, and we have not found an earlier source. The custom of telling the bees is a real English folk tradition.

课文为教材编写，是英国乡居回忆录的笔调；农场、这家人和那场洪水读来是虚构或改写的，我们没有找到更早的出处。「告诉蜜蜂」是真实的英国民间传统。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

The writer leans on a real English folk custom. In many country districts it was believed that bees must be told of births, marriages and deaths in the family, or they would leave the hive or stop making honey; in some places the hive was draped in black after a death. The family transfer this habit to their river, which turns the river into a kind of household member. The seasonal customs are also English in flavour: Midsummer Eve, the night before the twenty-fourth of June, was long marked with fires and gatherings, and harvest thanksgiving is still held in many village churches. The flood itself reflects the English lowlands, where rivers rise slowly over flat meadows rather than rushing through valleys, which is why the family can watch it creep and plan a rescue.

作者借用了一个真实的英国民间习俗。许多乡村地区相信，家里的生、婚、死都必须告诉蜜蜂，否则蜜蜂会离巢或不再酿蜜；有些地方在人去世后还给蜂箱罩上黑布。这家人把这个习惯转到河身上，河于是成了家里的一员。四季习俗也带着英国味：仲夏夜（6 月 24 日前夜）长期有篝火和聚会，丰收感恩礼拜至今在许多乡村教堂举行。洪水本身也反映了英格兰低地的特点：河水在平坦的草场上慢慢上涨，而不是在山谷里奔涌，所以这家人能看着它一点点爬上来，并计划救援。

✨ How it is written赏析

The essay spends more than half its length on happiness before it allows any danger, and that patience is what gives the flood its force. The early paragraphs are full of lists of pleasures, birthdays and seasons, described in a warm, slightly old-fashioned voice. When the flood comes, the sentences shorten, and the story is told through what the family see from a high window, one object at a time: a dead animal, a living one, and then people. This order is exactly the order of rising fear. The comic picture of the raft, compared to Noah's ark, breaks the tension at the moment it is highest. The writer then adds a small dry judgement, that the neighbours had panicked without need, and the final line praises the rescue while quietly admitting it was luck.

文章用一大半篇幅写幸福，才允许危险出现，而这份耐心正是洪水那一段有力量的原因。前几段满是乐事的清单——生日、四季——用温暖、略带旧式的口吻写出。洪水一来，句子变短，故事通过这家人从高窗看到的东西讲出来，一次一样：一只死去的动物，一只活着的，然后是人。这个顺序恰好就是恐惧上升的顺序。把木筏比作诺亚方舟的滑稽画面，在紧张最高的那一刻把它打破。作者接着加上一句冷淡的小判断——邻居是白白慌了——结尾称赞救援，同时悄悄承认那靠的是运气。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The lesson's quiet point about the neighbours still matters: in floods, people often face more danger from leaving than from staying, especially when they set off on water they do not know. Modern flood advice in Britain and Canada says much the same thing: if your home is sound and above the worst water, move upstairs and wait for help rather than trying to cross moving water, since a few inches of fast water can knock an adult down. The other half of the essay, the family's close bond with the river, now reads differently because of climate change; floods that the writer could call rare are more frequent in many places, and the Fraser Valley floods of November 2021 in British Columbia showed how quickly farms on low ground can be overwhelmed. Chinese readers may find an echo in old village customs of offering to a river god, and in the long Chinese experience of living with great rivers such as the Yellow River, which has been called both a mother and a sorrow.

课文对邻居那句不动声色的评语至今仍有意义：洪水中，离开往往比留下更危险，尤其是驶进自己不熟悉的水里。英国和加拿大现在的防洪建议说的基本是同一件事：如果房子结实、高过最严重的水位，就上楼等待救援，不要试图穿过流动的水——几英寸深的急流就能把成年人冲倒。文章的另一半——这家人和河的亲密——在气候变化之下读来不同了：作者能称为「少见」的洪水，如今在许多地方越来越频繁；2021 年 11 月卑诗省菲沙河谷的洪水显示，低地上的农场可以多快被淹没。中国读者也许会想起旧时乡村祭河神的习俗，以及与黄河这样的大河共处的长久经验——黄河被称为「母亲河」，也曾被称为「中国之忧患」。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 1第 1 句 (begins开头 “The river which …”)

  • play an important part in: matter greatly in something.play an important part in：在……中起重要作用（= play an important role in）。
  • has always: from the past up to now.has always：现在完成时，从过去一直到现在。

Sentence 3第 3 句 (begins开头 “There is only …”)

  • only enough ... to do: just enough for one purpose and no more.only enough … to do：只够……；supply the needs of：满足……的需要。

Sentence 6第 6 句 (begins开头 “that misfortune might …”)

  • just as introduces a comparison; know is left out after it.just as …：正如……一样；后面省略了 know。
  • might with were not related is an unreal condition. relate here means to tell.might … if … were not related：与现在情况相反的虚拟。relate：讲述（不是「与……相关」）。

Sentence 10第 10 句 (begins开头 “which a predecessor …”)

  • hard by is old-fashioned for very near.hard by：紧挨着（旧式说法，= close to）。

Sentence 16第 16 句 (begins开头 “We are lucky …”)

  • in that means because or in the sense that, explaining what came before.in that：因为，在于（解释前面的判断）。
  • make up: form a part of.make up：构成，占……。

  • less favourably sited: in a worse position.less favourably sited：位置没那么有利。

Sentence 24第 24 句 (begins开头 “and at the …”)

  • take turns in doing: do something one after another.take turns in doing / take turns to do：轮流做。

Sentence 29第 29 句 (begins开头 “We realized that …”)

  • An unreal past condition: even had it been almost under water, the house would not have fallen.would have stood … even if it had been：与过去事实相反的虚拟：即使（当时）几乎被淹，房子也不会倒。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

must have done and would have done even ifmust have done / would have done even if：对过去的推测与虚拟
must have done is a confident guess about the past. would have done with if or even if and had done describes something contrary to past fact; even if means it would not have made a difference.
must have done＝对过去有把握的推测；would have done (even) if … had done＝与过去事实相反的虚拟；even if 表示「就算……也……」。

Sentence 29 guesses that the neighbours must have panicked; sentence 30 uses an unreal past to say the house would have stood firm anyway.

本课：第 29 句推测邻居一定是吓坏了；第 30 句用虚拟说房子即使几乎被淹也会屹立不倒。

  • The streets are wet; it must have rained last night.
  • Even if he had run, he would have missed the bus.
in that, in the hope of, take turns inin that / in the hope of / take turns in：介词短语的固定搭配
in that with a clause explains why a judgement is true; in the hope of with an -ing form means hoping to; take turns in doing or take turns to do means doing something one after another.
in that + 从句＝因为，在于（解释判断的理由）；in the hope of + 动名词＝希望能……；take turns in doing / to do＝轮流做。

Sentence 16 explains the family's luck, sentence 24 has them watching in turn, and sentence 32 shows the men's hope of hooking the raft.

本课：第 16 句用 in that 解释为什么幸运；第 24 句轮流盯着；第 32 句男人们希望能钩住木筏。

  • The plan is good in that it costs little.
  • She waited outside in the hope of seeing the singer.
  • The children took turns in riding the pony.
The double possessive: a friend of mine双重所有格：a friend of mine
a, an, this, that or some, then a noun, then of, then a possessive form such as mine, ours or Tom's. It means one of several, or adds feeling.
a/an/this/that/some + 名词 + of + 名词所有格或名词性物主代词（mine/yours/ours…）；表示「……中的一个」或带感情色彩。

本课：第 10 句说船屋是以前一位农场主人建的，用 a predecessor of ours。

  • A friend of mine lives in Paris.
  • That dog of yours never stops barking.

  • To this day it seems a miracle that … 我们至今还觉得……真是奇迹
    To this day it seems a miracle that nobody was hurt.

Summary writing摘要写作

In no more than 80 words, tell how the neighbours were rescued during the winter flood.

用不超过 80 个词讲那年冬天洪水中邻居一家是怎样获救的。

Points a good summary covers好摘要要覆盖的要点
  • One winter the river rose over the low meadows; the writer's cattle were already indoors, so the family had little to lose.一年冬天河水上涨，淹了低地草场；作者家的牛已关进栏里，损失不大。
  • They worried about their neighbours, newcomers on low ground, but the phone was dead, so they watched in turn from an attic window.他们担心地势低、刚搬来的邻居，可电话断了，只好在阁楼窗口轮流看。
  • First a dead sheep and a horse came past, then a raft carrying the whole family and their animals.先漂下一只死羊和一匹马，接着出现一只载着全家和动物的木筏。
  • The men waded out with boat hooks and, almost miraculously, pulled the raft to the bank.男人们拿带钩的篙蹚水过去，竟把木筏拉到了岸边。
Our model summary本站范文

One winter the river flooded the low meadows. The writer's family had moved their cattle indoors, but they feared for their new neighbours, whose farm lay low. With the phone cut off, they took turns watching from the attic. A dead sheep drifted by, then a struggling horse, and then a raft carrying the whole family and their pets. The men waded out with hooks and, to everyone's amazement, dragged the raft safely to the bank.（76 词）
