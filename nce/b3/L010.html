📘 New Concept English · Book 3 · Lesson 10 “The Loss of the Titanic”新概念英语 第三册 Lesson 10《The Loss of the Titanic》

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
In April 1912 the liner that was said to be beyond sinking set out from Southampton for New York on her first crossing. She had sixteen watertight sections, and two of them could fill without her going under. On the fourth night a lookout in the North Atlantic saw ice ahead; the ship swung hard and slid past it, and all anyone felt was a slight shiver from below. Nobody made anything of it. The captain went down and found five sections already full. There were not enough boats, and fifteen hundred people died. This is historical narrative built on suspense: first the claim that she cannot sink, then the near escape, then the reversal. The grammar to learn is even if with unreal conditions, not only ... but, the past perfect passive, so ... that, and the three conditional patterns.
1912 年 4 月，号称「永不沉没」的泰坦尼克号从南安普敦首航纽约。它有 16 个水密舱，就算进水两个也能浮着。第四天夜里，瞭望员在北大西洋发现冰山，船急转弯擦身而过，只听到下面一声轻微的震动——没人当回事，船长下去一看，五个舱已经进水。救生艇不够，1500 人遇难。本课是有悬念的历史叙事：先铺「不可能沉」，再写「差一点躲过」，最后反转。学 even if 虚拟、not only … but、过去完成时被动、so … that 和三种条件句。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. Why did people at the time believe this ship could not go under?为什么当时的人认为泰坦尼克号不会沉？
  2. Why did nobody realise the hull had been hurt?船撞上冰山时，为什么谁都没觉得船受了损？
  3. Why was the loss of life so heavy?为什么死了那么多人？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Unsigned and written for the course. The event is one of the most heavily documented disasters of the century, so every figure in the lesson can be checked against the two official inquiries of 1912.

本课未署名、为教材自撰。它写的是二十世纪记录最详尽的灾难之一，所以课文里每个数字都能拿 1912 年那两场官方调查来核。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

The British Wreck Commissioner's inquiry gives the ship's gross tonnage as 46,328, and she left Southampton on the tenth of April 1912 with something over two thousand two hundred people aboard. Her lifeboats could take about 1,178 of them. Accounts of her subdivision are more interesting than the lesson suggests: she was designed to stay afloat with any two of her sixteen compartments open to the sea, with any three of the first five, and even with the whole of the first four gone — but not with the first five. Five were breached, and some accounts make it six once water spilled over the tops of the bulkheads. The death toll is still given differently by different sources, from about 1,496 to over 1,500 and higher, because the passenger and crew lists themselves do not agree; the lesson's round figure sits at the low end of the usual range.

英国海难调查专员的报告给出的船舶总吨位是 46,328 吨；她于 1912 年 4 月 10 日离开南安普敦，船上两千二百多人，救生艇大约能装 1,178 人。关于水密分舱的记述比课文透露的更有意思：她的设计是十六个水密舱中任意两个进水仍能浮着，前五个舱中任意三个进水仍能浮着，甚至前四个全进水也还能浮——但前五个一起进水就不行。实际被撕开的是五个，而且有记述说，等水越过舱壁顶部漫过去之后变成了六个。死亡人数至今各家不同，从约 1,496 到一千五百多乃至更高，因为乘客与船员名单本身就对不上；课文那个整数落在常见区间的低端。

✨ How it is written赏析

The suspense is built out of grammar, not out of adjectives. The first paragraph is all solid fact and settled tense, and the designers' claim is put in an unreal conditional, which is the only honest way to report a boast: if two had filled, she would still have floated. Then comes the one sentence that tells you the end before the story reaches it, so that everything after is read with the answer already in hand. The near miss gets the longest sentence in the lesson and the softest sound in it, and the reversal arrives in a subordinate clause after a comma, almost thrown away. Even the last sentence does its work by order: the reason comes first, the number second, and the number is left as the final word with nothing after it. Nothing in the lesson is described as tragic except once, early, where it costs nothing.

悬念是用语法造的，不是用形容词造的。第一段全是坚实的事实和安定的时态，而设计者的那句话被放进虚拟条件句里——这是报道一句夸口唯一诚实的办法：如果两个舱进水，她仍会浮着。接着出现那一句提前交底的话，于是后面的一切都是在已经知道结局的情况下被读的。「险险擦过」拿到了全课最长的句子和最轻的声响，而反转出现在一个逗号之后的从句里，几乎是随手抛出来的。连最后一句也靠语序干活：理由在前，数字在后，数字作为全课最后一个词，后面什么都不加。整课只有一次用了「悲惨」这个词，而且用在很早、代价最小的地方。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The lesson undersells its own subject in one place and that changes the moral. Saying she could float with two compartments flooded makes the design sound thin and the builders sound complacent; the real standard was higher, and the accident went one compartment past a margin that was, by the thinking of the day, generous. Put that way the failure is not stupidity but the ordinary limit of designing against the accidents you can imagine. The lifeboats are the sharper point, and the lesson states the arithmetic without comment: boats for roughly half the people aboard. Whether that met the regulations of 1912, and how those regulations were written, is the question worth taking to the inquiry reports rather than to a textbook, and we have not traced it here. Treat the figures in this lesson as a starting point and go to the inquiries for anything you intend to repeat.

课文在一处低估了自己的题材，而这改变了结论。说「两个舱进水还能浮」，会让设计听起来单薄、让建造者听起来自满；真实的标准更高，而事故比当时看来相当宽裕的余量多走了一个舱。这样说，失败就不是愚蠢，而是「按想象得到的事故做设计」这件事的常规极限。救生艇才是更锋利的一点，课文把算术摆出来、一字不评：艇位只够船上大约一半的人。这是否符合 1912 年的法规、那些法规又是怎么写的，这个问题值得去查调查报告，而不是查教科书；本轮我们没有追查。课文里的数字当起点看，凡是你准备转述的，去调查报告里取。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 4第 4 句 (begins开头 “At that time, …”)

  • not only ... but (also): it joins two complements here, one a noun phrase and one a passive participle. Because not only is not at the front of the sentence, there is no inversion.not only … but (also) …：连接两个表语（the largest ship / regarded as unsinkable），不在句首，不倒装。
  • The past perfect passive after a superlative means 'of all those built up to that time'.that had ever been built：过去完成时被动，「到那时为止造过的」。最高级 + that 定语从句。
  • for adds the reason, as a co-ordinating conjunction.for：因为（补充理由）。

Sentence 5第 5 句 (begins开头 “Even if two …”)

  • even if: granted that it happened, it would still make no difference.even if：即使。
  • A past form in the if clause with would in the main clause makes the supposition unreal, that is, contrary to the present state of things. This was the designers' claim at the time rather than a fact.were … would …：与现在/将来情况相反的假设（虚拟语气），这是当时设计者的说法。

Sentence 6第 6 句 (begins开头 “The tragic sinking …”)

  • The future simple passive.will be remembered：一般将来时被动。
  • go down: of a ship, to sink; with heavy loss of life means that very many died.go down：（船）沉没。with heavy loss of life：伤亡惨重。

Sentence 10第 10 句 (begins开头 “The Titanic turned …”)

  • The -ing phrase gives the result that came with the turn: she came within a very little of striking it.narrowly missing：现在分词作结果/伴随状语，「差一点就撞上」。

Sentence 13第 13 句 (begins开头 “The noise had …”)

  • so + adjective + that: to such a degree that something follows.so + 形容词 + that：如此……以至于。
  • The past perfect passive, for what had happened before they thought about it.had been damaged：过去完成时被动。

Sentence 14第 14 句 (begins开头 “Below, the captain …”)

  • to one's horror: a phrase dropped in between the verb and its object clause to say how the news struck him.to one's horror：令某人惊恐的是（插在动词和宾语从句之间）。
  • It had already happened before he came down and found out.had already been flooded：在船长发现之前就已经进水。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

The three conditional patterns, including unreal ones三种条件句（含虚拟）
First, the real condition: if plus the present, then will do. Second, contrary to the present or future: if plus a past form, with were for be, then would do. Third, contrary to the past: if plus had done, then would have done. even if only adds the sense of 'granted that', and the tense rules do not change.
① 真实条件：If + 现在时, will do。② 与现在/将来相反：If + 过去式（be 用 were）, would do。③ 与过去相反：If + had done, would have done。even if 只加「即使」的意味，时态规则不变。
  • If it rains tomorrow, we will stay at home.
  • If I were you, I would take the job.
  • If they had had more lifeboats, more people would have survived.
so ... that / such ... that: to such a degree thatso … that / such … that：如此……以至于
Use so with an adjective or adverb and then that; use such with a noun phrase, so that an article and an adjective come in between.
so + 形容词/副词 + that；such + (a/an) + 形容词 + 名词 + that。

In sentence 13 the sound was slight to such a degree that nobody drew the obvious conclusion.

本课：第 13 句：那声音 so faint that 没人认为船受了损。

  • The box was so heavy that I couldn't lift it.
  • It was such a heavy box that I couldn't lift it.
The past perfect passive: had been done过去完成时被动：had been done
When you are telling a story set in the past and something was already done to the subject before another past event, use had been plus the past participle.
叙述过去时，某个动作在另一过去动作之前已经被完成，用 had been + 过去分词。

Four of them run through this lesson: the building of the ship, the question of damage, the flooding of the compartments, and the raising of the alarm.

本课：第 4 句 the largest ship … had ever been built；第 13 句 the ship had been damaged；第 15 句 had already been flooded；第 9 句 the alarm had been given。

  • When we arrived, all the tickets had been sold.
  • He found that his car had been stolen.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • Even by modern standards, X was a colossal … 即使按现代标准，X 也是个庞然大物
    Even by modern standards, the old library is a colossal building.
  • X was not only the …, but was regarded as … X 不仅是最……的，还被认为是……
    She was not only the youngest player, but was regarded as the best.
  • Even if …, X would still be able to … 即使……，X 也仍然能……
    Even if the power failed, the hospital would still be able to run.
  • X realized to his horror that … X 惊恐地意识到……
    She realized to her horror that she had left her passport at home.

In April 1912 the Titanic, then the biggest ship in the world, left Southampton for New York. With sixteen watertight sections, she was thought impossible to sink. Four days later, in the freezing North Atlantic, an iceberg was seen ahead. The ship swung away and just avoided hitting it head-on.
