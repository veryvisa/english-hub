Lesson 47 · Too High a Price 代价太高了？

📘 New Concept English · Book 3 · Lesson 47 “Too High a Price”新概念英语 第三册 Lesson 47《Too High a Price》

B2🧠 28 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
Pollution is what we pay for a planet with too many people and too much industry. There are only four things you can do with rubbish, yet the amount the world throws away threatens to bury us. The drive for ever cheaper food brings a second kind of pollution: factory farming, pesticides and fertilizers give us low-priced meat and crops, and the bill comes as disease in cattle, poultry, eggs and dairy. Turning vegetarian means either expensive organic produce or a regular dose of chemicals, and even a glass of water is not safe. Worse still is the noise of towns: alarms on houses and cars, dogs barking all night, mowers, late parties, heavy lorries, aircraft, radios at full volume in public, and loud calls on mobile phones in restaurants and buses. The survey's most surprising old-fashioned culprit turns out to be snoring, with men as the chief offenders. The conclusion: silence seems to have become a golden memory. This is an argument that moves step by step, from rubbish to food to noise, coming closer to daily life as it goes. The grammar to learn is come to think about it, threaten to, lead to, serve only to, object to, turn out to be, Against these figures, and Whatever ..., one thing is certain.
污染是人口过多、工业过度的地球付出的代价。处理垃圾只有四种办法：倾倒、焚烧、回收再用、少产生，可全世界的垃圾量多到快把我们淹没。为了生产越来越多的廉价食品，又带来另一种污染：工厂化养殖、农药化肥换来便宜的肉和粮菜，代价是疯牛病、沙门氏菌和李斯特菌；改吃素，要么买昂贵的有机菜，要么天天吃农药，连喝杯水都不放心。比这更阴险的是城市里的噪音：防盗铃、汽车警报、整夜狂吠的狗、割草机、深夜派对、重型卡车、飞机直升机、公共场所开到最大声的收音机，还有在餐馆和公交上大声讲的手机。调查最出人意料的「老式噪音源」竟是打鼾——男人是最大的罪魁。结论：安静似乎已成了美好的回忆。本课是层层推进的议论文，从垃圾到食品再到噪音，越写越贴近日常生活。学 come to think about it、threaten to、lead to、serve only to、object to、turn out to be、Against these figures、Whatever … , one thing is certain。

  2. What price do we pay for cheap food?廉价食品的代价是什么？
  3. Why does the writer think noise is a more insidious kind of pollution than the others?为什么作者认为噪音比其他污染更「阴险」？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

In the form given here the text belongs to the revised edition of the 1990s. The references to Mad Cow Disease, mobile phones in restaurants and car alarms fix it firmly in Britain at the end of that decade.

这里的课文属于 1990 年代的修订版。文中的疯牛病、餐馆里的手机和汽车警报，把它牢牢定在那个年代末的英国。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

The list of food scares was not invented for effect. In March 1996 the British government announced that the most likely explanation for a new form of a fatal brain disease in young people was exposure to infected beef, and the European Union banned exports of British beef for several years. Salmonella in eggs had caused a political storm in Britain a few years earlier, and listeria in soft cheese and ready meals was a regular warning for pregnant women. Noise has since moved from complaint to public-health evidence. In 2018 the World Health Organization published noise guidelines for Europe, which found stronger evidence than before linking long exposure to traffic noise with heart disease and disturbed sleep, and strongly recommended keeping average road-traffic noise below 53 decibels. The snoring figures in the lesson come from a survey the writer does not name, and we have not traced it; studies of snoring do generally find that it is more common in men and rises with age, but the exact numbers vary widely with how snoring is measured.

课文列的食品恐慌并非为了效果编出来的。1996 年 3 月，英国政府宣布，年轻人中出现的一种致命脑病新变种，最可能的原因是吃了受感染的牛肉，欧盟随后禁止英国牛肉出口达数年之久。几年前，鸡蛋里的沙门氏菌就已在英国掀起过一场政治风波；软奶酪和即食食品里的李斯特菌，则是对孕妇的常规警告。此后，噪音从「抱怨」变成了公共卫生证据。2018 年世界卫生组织发布欧洲噪音指南，发现长期暴露于交通噪音与心脏病、睡眠受扰之间的关联比过去认为的更强，并强烈建议把道路交通的平均噪音控制在 53 分贝以下。课文里打鼾的数字来自一项作者没有点名的调查，我们没有查到出处；关于打鼾的研究大体都发现男性更常见、随年龄增加，但具体数字因测量方法不同差别很大。

✨ How it is written赏析

The essay is built like a funnel. It begins with the whole planet and a cold economic word, price, then narrows to food on the plate, then to noise in the street, and finally to the bed — the sound of the person lying next to you. Each step brings the problem closer to the reader's own body, which is why the ending lands as a joke rather than a lecture. The tone shifts as the subject narrows. The rubbish paragraph is plain and factual; the food paragraph turns sarcastic with an innocent glass of water and its exclamation mark; the noise paragraph is a breathless list with almost no verbs, imitating the crowding of sound it describes. The single bracket with a question mark, surprisingly?, lets the writer wink at us without breaking the frame of a survey report. And the last sentence uses it seems to soften a large claim, a small piece of honesty that is worth copying.

这篇文章像一个漏斗。它从整个星球和一个冷冰冰的经济学词「代价」开始，收窄到盘子里的食物，再到街上的噪音，最后落到床上——躺在你身边那个人的鼾声。每一步都让问题更贴近读者自己的身体，所以结尾落成了一个笑话，而不是一场训话。随着题目收窄，语气也在变。垃圾那段平实陈述；食品那段带上讽刺，一杯「无辜的水」配一个感叹号；噪音那段是一口气念下去、几乎没有动词的清单，模仿它所描写的声音挤作一团。那个带问号的括号 surprisingly? 让作者冲我们眨了眨眼，却没有打破「调查报告」的框架。最后一句用 it seems 软化一个很大的论断，这一点小小的诚实值得学。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The lesson's opening sentence blames pollution on too many people, a common view in the 1990s that most researchers would now state more carefully. The amount of waste and pollution depends less on the number of people than on how much each person consumes and how things are made; a small rich population can pollute more than a large poor one. The food paragraph also mixes very different risks. Pesticide residues on vegetables in most countries with regulators are usually far below safety limits, while the infections the writer lists are real but come mostly from handling and cooking, not from farming being cheap. Noise, by contrast, is the part the writer may have underrated: the evidence on health has grown stronger, and quiet has become something that richer people buy and poorer people live without. Read the essay as a good model of building an argument, and check each of its claims against a current source before repeating it.

课文第一句把污染归咎于人太多，这是 1990 年代常见的看法，如今多数研究者会说得更谨慎。废物和污染的多少，与其说取决于人口数量，不如说取决于每人消费多少、东西是怎么生产的；人少而富的群体，污染可能比人多而穷的更重。食品那段也把风险很不相同的事混在了一起。在有监管的多数国家，蔬菜上的农药残留通常远低于安全限值；而作者列举的那些感染是真实存在的，但主要来自处理和烹饪环节，并不是因为养殖便宜。相反，噪音可能是作者低估了的部分：关于健康的证据越来越强，安静正在变成富人能买到、穷人只能忍着没有的东西。把这篇文章当作「如何搭建论证」的好范本来读，转述其中任何一条论断之前，先拿当下的资料核一核。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 3第 3 句 (begins开头 “Pollution is the …”)

  • the price we pay for: the cost of something; we pay for is a relative clause with that left out.the price we pay for：为……付出的代价。we pay for 是省略 that 的定语从句。
  • The prefix over- means too much: too many people, too much industry.over- 前缀：过度。overpopulated 人口过多，overindustrialized 工业过度发达。

Sentence 6第 6 句 (begins开头 “We keep trying …”)

  • the sheer volume of: the very size of the amount, with sheer for emphasis.the sheer volume of：数量之大（sheer 加强语气）。
  • threaten to do: of something bad, to seem likely to happen.threaten to do：（不好的事）可能要发生，大有……之势。

Sentence 18第 18 句 (begins开头 “Burglar alarms going …”)

  • The -ing phrase describes the alarms; go off means start ringing.going off：现在分词短语作后置定语，修饰 alarms。go off 指警报响起。
  • serve only to do: to have only the effect of doing, usually the opposite of what was meant.serve only to do：结果只是……（与本意相反）。
  • The phrase is ironic: alarms that sound all the time train people to ignore them.assist burglars to burgle：帮窃贼行窃，带讽刺——警报总响，就没人当回事了。

Sentence 21第 21 句 (begins开头 “that dogs barking …”)

  • The bracket with a question mark is the writer's aside: is this really a surprise?(surprisingly?)：作者插一句带问号的评论，意思是「这出人意料吗？」。
  • rate here means to be judged as; ranging from ... to ... gives the range of the scale.rate：被评为（主动形式表被动意义）。ranging from … to …：范围从……到……。

Sentence 34第 34 句 (begins开头 “Against these figures, …”)

  • Against these figures: in contrast with these numbers.Against these figures：与这些数字相比。
  • while marks the contrast; trumpeting compares the snoring to a trumpet for comic effect.while：而（表对比）。trumpeting 像吹喇叭一样的，幽默夸张。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

Verbs of result and tendency: threaten to, serve only to, turn out to be结果与倾向：threaten to / serve only to / turn out to be
threaten to do means something bad looks likely; serve only to do means the only effect is, often the opposite of what was hoped; turn out to be means proves in the end to be. All three take an infinitive and show the speaker's view of the outcome.
threaten to do＝（坏事）大有发生之势；serve only to do＝结果只是（往往适得其反）；turn out to be＝结果证明是。三者后面都接不定式，表达说话人对结果的判断。

Sentence 6 has threatens to, sentence 18 serve only to, and sentence 31 turned out to be.

本课：第 6 句垃圾量 threatens to 淹没我们；第 18 句警报 serve only to 惹恼路人；第 31 句「老式噪音源」turned out to be 打鼾。

  • The river threatens to burst its banks after the storm.
  • His apology served only to make her angrier.
  • The stranger at the door turned out to be my cousin.
Verbs followed by to as a preposition: object to, lead to, contribute to表示「反对、导致」的介词搭配：object to / lead to / contribute to
Here to is a preposition, so it takes a noun or an -ing form, not the plain verb: object to doing, lead to something, make a contribution to.
这里的 to 是介词，后面接名词或动名词，不接动词原形：object to doing、lead to + 名词、make a contribution to。

Sentence 8 has leads to, sentence 27 contribution to, and sentence 28 object to.

本课：第 8 句用 leads to；第 27 句用 contribution to；第 28 句用 object to。

  • Many residents object to the new road being built.
  • Lack of sleep can lead to mistakes at work.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • Come to think of it, … 仔细想想，……
    Come to think of it, most arguments start over small things.
  • The price we pay for X may be already too high 我们为 X 付出的代价也许已经太高了
    The price we pay for fast fashion may be already too high.
  • X serve(s) only to … X 结果只是……
    Shouting at the children served only to make them louder.
  • Whatever …, one thing is certain: … 不管……，有一点是肯定的：……
    Whatever the weather, one thing is certain: the match will go ahead.

  • snoring men: one in five at 35, three in five at 60; women: one in twenty

Summary writing摘要写作

In no more than 80 words, sum up the three kinds of pollution the writer lists and name the most surprising source of noise in the survey.

Points a good summary covers好摘要要覆盖的要点
  • There are only four ways to deal with rubbish, but the world produces so much that it threatens to bury us.处理垃圾只有四种办法，但全世界的垃圾量多得快淹没我们。
  • Cheap food depends on factory farming and chemicals, and the price is disease in food and pesticides in vegetables and water.廉价食品靠工厂化养殖和农药化肥，代价是食品带病、蔬菜和水里有农药。
  • Noise in towns is more insidious: alarms, dogs, traffic, aircraft and mobile phones invade daily life.城市噪音更阴险：警报、狗叫、车辆、飞机、手机，都在侵扰日常生活。
  • The survey's surprise was snoring, far commoner in men than in women; silence has become a memory.调查发现最意外的噪音源是打鼾，男人比女人严重得多；安静已成回忆。
Our model summary本站范文

We pay for a crowded, industrial planet with pollution. Rubbish piles up faster than we can dump, burn or recycle it. Cheap food brings diseases in meat and eggs and chemicals in vegetables and water. Worst of all is noise: alarms, barking dogs, traffic, aircraft and loud phone calls. A survey found an unexpected offender, snoring, which affects far more men than women. Silence, it seems, is now only a memory.（71 词）
