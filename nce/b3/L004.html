🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
Manual workers often take home more than people in offices, yet plenty of people would rather earn less and be called a white-collar worker. Alf, a dustman, cannot bring himself to tell his wife what he does: he leaves home in a suit, changes into working clothes, showers at the end of the shift and changes back, and keeps this up for over two years. Now he has an office job at half the money and feels the gain in standing is worth it. It is a wry little story about face and status. Grammar focus: the future continuous (will be working / will be earning), multiples (half as much as), and the inverted Such is ..., that ... .
体力工人比坐办公室的挣得多，可很多人宁可少拿钱也要当「白领」。清洁工阿尔夫不好意思告诉妻子自己的工作，每天穿西装出门、到单位换工作服、下班洗澡再换回西装，瞒了两年多；现在他找到办公室工作，钱只有原来一半，却觉得身份的提升值得。一个关于面子与身份的幽默小故事。语法重点：将来进行时（will be working / will be earning）、倍数表达（half as much as）、such is …, that…。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. Why are so many people ready to earn less in order to work in an office?为什么很多人愿意少拿工资去当白领？
  2. What did Alf do every day to keep his wife from finding out?阿尔夫每天是怎样瞒住妻子的？
  3. How do his pay and his standing change when he takes the new job?他换了新工作后收入和地位有什么变化？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Unsigned and written for the course by L. G. Alexander; Alf Bloggs is an invented man, and Bloggs is the British equivalent of calling somebody Mr Nobody.

本课由编者 L. G. 亚历山大自撰、未署名；阿尔夫·布洛格斯是虚构人物，Bloggs 在英语里相当于管人叫「某某先生」。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

The premise was a real argument of its decade, not a joke the writer made up. In the Britain of the 1960s well-paid manual work could out-earn clerical work, and sociologists spent years asking what that meant. The best-known attempt to settle it was published in 1968 by John Goldthorpe, David Lockwood, Frank Bechhofer and Jennifer Platt, who studied men at three of the best-paying industrial firms in Luton precisely because those men were the strongest test case. The question was whether money alone was pulling manual workers into the middle class. Their answer was that it was not: the men kept their own politics and their own loyalties, and the second volume reports that most of them went on voting as they had before. A later re-reading of the same fieldnotes suggests something sharper, and rather awkward for this lesson: the workers themselves made remarkably little of the line between the collar and the overall.

这个前提是那个年代真实的争论，不是作者编的笑话。1960 年代的英国，报酬高的体力活确实可能比办公室文书挣得多，社会学家为此争了好几年。最有名的一次正面回答是 1968 年出版的，作者是戈德索普、洛克伍德、贝克霍弗和普拉特：他们专挑卢顿三家待遇最好的工业企业的工人来研究，正因为这些人是最强的试验样本。问题是：单靠钱，体力工人会不会被拉进中产阶级。他们的答案是不会——这些人守着自己的政治立场和自己的归属，第二卷报告说他们大多数照旧投原来的票。后来有人重读同一批田野笔记，得出一个更锋利、对本课也更尴尬的印象：工人自己其实相当不在意「衬衫领子」和「工作服」之间那条线。

✨ How it is written赏析

The shape of this piece is the reverse of the usual one. Most writers give you the case first and draw the moral afterwards; here the general law comes first, stated as a flat fact about people, and the man is produced as its single proof. That is a bold move, and it works because the law is stated in a sentence whose word order is itself unusual, so you cannot read past it. Then the comedy is built entirely out of routine: the suit, the change of clothes, the shower, the suit again, two years of it. Nobody is mocked, nothing is explained, and the workmates who covered for him are given one clause and no praise. The ending refuses to judge. He will earn half as much, and he counts it a gain; the writer reports the arithmetic and the feeling side by side and then stops.

这一篇的形状和常见的正好相反。多数作者先给案例、后点道理；这里先出结论，把它当成关于人性的平直事实说出来，再把一个人当作唯一的证明推上来。这是个大胆的走法，之所以成立，是因为那句结论用的语序本身就不寻常，你读不过去。接下来的喜剧完全由日常构成：西装、换衣、淋浴、再换回西装，两年如此。没有人被取笑，没有一处被解释，替他保密的同事只占一个分句，连一句夸奖都没有。结尾拒绝裁判：他的收入会减半，而他算作得了便宜；作者把这笔账和这份心情并排摆着，然后收笔。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The weakest thing in the lesson is the word nature. One invented dustman is produced to prove a claim about everybody, and the decade's own fieldwork, done on exactly these men in exactly these years, found the hunger for a collar and tie much weaker than the lesson needs it to be. What the story is really good at is something narrower and still true: it shows that pay and standing are two different currencies, that people do trade one for the other, and that the trade can be invisible to the person they live with. Read it that way and it keeps its force without the shaky generalisation. What would change my mind is a study of the same period that found manual workers routinely taking large pay cuts for office work. Until that turns up, treat the opening as the writer's hunch rather than as a finding.

本课最弱的地方是「人的本性」这几个字。一个虚构的清洁工被推出来证明一个关于所有人的论断，而那个年代真正下到田野、研究的恰是这批人、恰是这些年份的调查，发现对「衬衫领带」的渴望比课文所需要的弱得多。这个故事真正厉害的是一件范围更小、但至今成立的事：它让人看见报酬和体面是两种不同的货币，人确实会拿一种去换另一种，而这笔交易可能对同住的人完全不可见。这样读，它的力量还在，摇晃的那句概括可以不要。什么能改变我的看法？同时期的一项研究，显示体力工人普遍为了办公室的工作接受大幅减薪。在那之前，开头那句当作者的直觉看，不当结论看。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 2第 2 句 (begins开头 “People who work …”)

  • be referred to as: the passive of refer to something as, so the name is given by people in general.be referred to as：被称为。
  • for the simple reason that ...: the reason is nothing complicated; that opens an appositive clause explaining reason.for the simple reason that…：原因很简单——that 引导同位语从句，解释 reason。

Sentence 3第 3 句 (begins开头 “Such is human …”)

  • Such is ... that: an inversion for emphasis, the same meaning as saying that human nature is such that something follows.Such is … that：倒装强调，「人的本性就是如此，以致……」（= Human nature is such that…）。
  • sacrifice A for B: give up A in order to gain B.sacrifice A for B：为 B 牺牲 A。

Sentence 4第 4 句 (begins开头 “This can give …”)

  • as it did: did stands in for the whole verb phrase already used, so it need not be repeated (Book 4 Lesson 13).as it did：did 替代 gave rise to curious situations（见第四册第 13 课）。
  • in the case of: as far as this particular person or example goes.in the case of：就……而言。

Sentence 11第 11 句 (begins开头 “Alf's wife has …”)

  • The present perfect and the future stand in contrast: nothing has come out so far, and nothing will.现在完成时与将来时对照：到现在没发现、以后也不会。
  • never will: the main verb is left out and only the auxiliary stays behind.never will：省略 discover，保留助动词 will。

Sentence 13第 13 句 (begins开头 “He will be …”)

  • will be doing: the future continuous, describing what will be going on over a stretch of future time.will be doing：将来进行时，描述将来一段时间里的状态。
  • half as much as: a multiple in front of as ... as, here meaning one half.half as much as：倍数 + as … as（一半）。
  • The verb after used to is left out because it has just been said; be well worth something means it clearly repays what it costs.used to 后省略 earn；be well worth sth：很值得。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

The future continuous: will be doing将来进行时：will be doing
It describes something that will be going on over a period of future time, or that will happen as a matter of course; it sounds more detached and more settled than will do.
表示将来某段时间正在进行、或按计划自然会发生的事；语气比 will do 更客观、更「已成定局」。
  • This time tomorrow I will be flying to Paris.
  • Will you be using the car tonight?（委婉询问计划）
Multiples: half / twice / three times + as ... as倍数表达：half / twice / three times + as … as
Put the multiple in front of as + adjective or adverb + as. You can also use a multiple with a comparative and than (three times bigger than), or a multiple with the + noun + of (twice the size of).
倍数 + as + 形容词/副词（much/many）+ as；也可用 倍数 + 比较级 + than（three times bigger than），或 倍数 + the + 名词 + of（twice the size of）。
  • This room is twice as large as that one. = twice the size of that one.
  • He has three times as many books as I do.
Such is ... that / too ... to / be worth
Such is + noun + that is an emphatic inversion with the same sense as noun + is such that; too + adjective + to do means so much of something that you cannot; be worth + noun or -ing means it repays the cost.
Such is + 名词 + that…（倒装强调）＝名词 + is such that…；too + adj. + to do＝太……而不能；be worth + 名词/doing＝值得。
  • Such was his anger that he couldn't speak.
  • The film is worth seeing.（不说 worth to see）

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • Such is human nature that … 人性就是如此，以致……
    Such is human nature that we remember insults longer than praise.
  • sacrifice … for the privilege of … 为了……的荣幸而牺牲……
    Many fans sacrifice sleep for the privilege of seeing the match live.
  • … is well worth … ……很值得……
    The extra hour of travel is well worth the lower price.

Manual workers often earn more than office workers, yet many people will accept lower pay to become white-collar workers. Alf Bloggs, a dustman, was too embarrassed to tell his wife about his job. For over two years he left home in a smart suit, changed into overalls at work, and showered and changed back before going home.
