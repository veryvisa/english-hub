Lesson 55 · From the Earth Greetings 来自地球的问候

📘 New Concept English · Book 3 · Lesson 55 “From the Earth Greetings”新概念英语 第三册 Lesson 55《From the Earth Greetings》

B2🧠 22 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
New advances in astronomy mean we can now find planets in our own galaxy and in others, which is a real achievement since planets are tiny by comparison and give off no light. Finding them is hard; finding life on them will be harder still. First comes the question of whether a planet can hold life at all. In our own solar system Venus is too hot and Mars too cold; only Earth is right, and even here plants and animals took over four billion years to appear. Whether life is possible depends on the planet's star: a big, hot star means the planet must be far away, while a small one means it must be close and still offer perfect conditions. Then comes the search itself. No existing telescope can detect life, and building one will be a great project of this century. Ground-based instruments will not do, because our warm air and the telescope's own heat get in the way; even an orbiting one like Hubble is spoiled by dust in the solar system, so the telescope would need to be as far out as Jupiter, where the dust thins. Once a planet is found, its star's light must be blocked so that the planet's air can be studied. The first target would be plant life, not little green men, and the likeliest form would be bacteria, which made Earth's oxygen and were for most of its history the only life here. We hope for visitors we can talk to, but that belongs to science fiction; even finding simple life like bacteria elsewhere would change how we see ourselves. This is popular science exposition, moving step by step: can there be life, how to look, what we would find, and what it would mean. The grammar to learn is make it possible to, in relative terms, prove with an adjective, far too ... to, depend on, up to, Alternatively, in existence, the further ... the thinner, blot out, the It is ... that cleft sentence, and the unreal If we were able to ... would.
天文学的新进展让人能在银河系乃至其他星系里探测到行星——这很了不起，因为行星相对很小、又不发光。找行星已经够难，找行星上的生命还要难得多。第一个问题是行星能否维持生命：太阳系里金星太热、火星太冷，只有地球条件理想，而且即便在地球上，动植物也花了四十多亿年才进化出来。能否有生命取决于它那颗恒星的大小和亮度：恒星大而热，行星就得离得很远；恒星小，行星就得靠得很近，还要条件完美。可怎么找？现在还没有能探测生命的望远镜，造出它将是 21 世纪的大工程。地面望远镜不行，地球大气和仪器自身的热会干扰；连哈勃那样绕地球的望远镜也不行，因为太阳系内有尘埃；望远镜得远到木星那里，尘埃才够稀。找到行星后还得遮住恒星的光，才能「看清」行星、分析大气。首先要找的是植物，而不是「小绿人」；最可能出现的是细菌——地球上的氧气正是细菌造的，地球大部分历史里它们是唯一的生命。我们总盼着小绿人来访、能和他们交流，但那只是科幻；哪怕只在别的星球上找到细菌这样的低级生命，也会彻底改变我们对自己的看法。本课是科普说明文：层层推进——能不能有、怎么找、找到什么、意味着什么。学 make it possible to、in relative terms、prove + adj.、far too … to、depend on、up to、Alternatively、in existence、the further … the thinner、blot out、It is … that 强调句、If we were able to … would 虚拟。

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the 1990s revision of New Concept English; the closing quotation is attributed to Daniel Goldin, head of NASA from 1992 to 2001, who often spoke of the search for life as the agency's central question. We have not traced the exact words to a dated speech.

课文为九十年代修订版编写；结尾那句引语出自 1992–2001 年任 NASA 局长的丹尼尔·戈尔丁，他常说寻找生命是 NASA 的核心问题。我们没有把这句话追溯到某次有日期的讲话。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

The lesson was written just as the hunt began. The first planet found round an ordinary star like the sun, 51 Pegasi b, was announced in 1995 by two Swiss astronomers, who later shared the Nobel Prize for it. Since then more than five thousand planets beyond the solar system have been confirmed, most by measuring tiny dips in a star's light when a planet passes in front of it, the method used by NASA's Kepler and TESS missions. One claim in the lesson has been overtaken: planets have been found in our own galaxy in great numbers, but those reported in other galaxies are only a few uncertain candidates. Another has partly come true: the James Webb Space Telescope, launched in 2021 and parked far beyond the Moon, can already read the chemistry of some planets' atmospheres, and NASA is planning a later telescope designed to block starlight and search for signs of life.

这一课写成时，寻找行星的工作刚刚起步。第一颗绕类太阳普通恒星运行的行星飞马座 51b，由两位瑞士天文学家在 1995 年宣布，二人后来因此分享诺贝尔奖。此后已确认的太阳系外行星超过五千颗，大多是用测量恒星亮度微小下降（行星从前面经过时）的方法找到的，NASA 的开普勒和 TESS 任务都用这个办法。课文的一个说法已被事实超越：银河系内的行星找到了大量，但在其他星系里报告的只有几个不确定的候选。另一个说法部分成真：2021 年发射、停在远离月球之外的詹姆斯·韦布太空望远镜，已经能读出一些行星大气的化学成分；NASA 还在规划一台后续望远镜，专门遮挡星光、寻找生命迹象。

✨ How it is written赏析

The essay is built like a ladder, each step raising the difficulty. It begins with something already achieved, then names a harder goal, then asks a first question, then a second, then a third, so that by the end the reader understands why the search will take a century. The writer keeps the reader close with small invitations: imagine a star, and then the question of how anyone would ever spot such a world. Distances and temperatures stay vague on purpose, since the essay cares more about logic than about figures. The tone changes only at the end, where the hope for little green men is gently set aside and replaced by a humbler hope, which turns out to be the bigger one. The last word is given to someone else, a NASA chief, so that the claim of changing everything sounds like an expert's verdict rather than the writer's excitement.

文章像一架梯子，每一级都提高难度。从一件已经做到的事开始，接着提出更难的目标，再问第一个问题、第二个、第三个，到最后读者明白为什么这场寻找要花上一个世纪。作者用一些小邀请拉住读者：想象一颗恒星；可我们怎么找到这样的行星呢？距离和温度故意写得含糊，因为文章在乎的是逻辑，不是数字。语气只在结尾一变：「小绿人」的期待被轻轻放下，换上一个更谦卑的希望，结果却是更大的那一个。最后一句交给别人——一位 NASA 局长——于是「改变一切」听起来像专家的判断，而不是作者的兴奋。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The most interesting idea in the lesson is the reversal of hopes: what would matter most is not intelligent aliens but any life at all. That still holds. Scientists now speak of biosignatures, gases such as oxygen together with methane that are hard to explain without living things, and claims of finding them are treated with great caution; a 2023 report of a possible sign on a planet called K2-18 b caused excitement and then strong doubt. Within our own solar system the search has also moved: the icy moons Europa and Enceladus, which hide oceans under their crusts, are now seen as better places to look than Mars. For Chinese readers the story has a new chapter of its own: China landed the Zhurong rover on Mars in 2021 and has brought back samples from the far side of the Moon, joining a search that was once almost entirely American and European.

这一课最有意思的想法是期待的反转：真正重要的不是有智慧的外星人，而是任何生命。这一点今天依然成立。科学家现在讲「生物标志」——例如氧气与甲烷同时存在，若没有生物很难解释；任何声称找到它们的报告都被极其谨慎地对待，2023 年有关行星 K2-18 b 可能有生命迹象的报道先引起轰动，随后又遭到强烈质疑。在太阳系内，寻找方向也变了：冰壳下藏着海洋的木卫二和土卫二，如今被认为比火星更值得找。对中国读者，这个故事有了自己的新篇章：中国 2021 年让祝融号火星车登陆火星，又从月球背面带回了样本，加入了这场曾几乎只属于美国和欧洲的寻找。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

The cleft sentence: It is ... thatIt is … that 强调句
It is or was, then the part you want to stress, then that or who, then the rest. You can stress a subject, object or adverbial; if you take away It is and that, the sentence still works.
It is/was + 被强调部分 + that/who + 其余部分。被强调的可以是主语、宾语、状语；去掉 It is … that 后句子仍完整。

Sentence 28 stresses that bacteria, and nothing else, made the oxygen.

本课：第 28 句强调「是细菌」造出了氧气。

  • It was my sister who found the key.
  • It is in winter that the city looks most beautiful.
far too ... to do and too ... to dofar too … to do 与 too … to do
too plus adjective or adverb plus to do means so much that something cannot happen. You can put far, much or a bit before too to change the degree.
too + 形容词/副词 + to do＝太……而不能……；前面可加 far / much / a bit 调节程度。

Sentence 7 says one planet is much too hot and the other much too cold for life.

本课：第 7 句说金星太热、火星太冷，都没法有生命。

  • The box is far too heavy for me to lift.
  • It is a bit too early to decide.
the ... the ...: the more ..., the more ...the + 比较级, the + 比较级：越……越……
The plus a comparative with its clause, then the plus another comparative with its clause. Either part can come first, and the can be dropped from one half, as in this lesson.
The + 比较级 + 主谓, the + 比较级 + 主谓。两个部分都可以倒过来放，也可以省掉一半中的 the（如本课）。

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • New advances in X have made it possible to … X 的新进展使……成为可能
    New advances in medicine have made it possible to repair a damaged heart.
  • Doing A is turning out to be hard, and doing B will be harder still. 做 A 已经够难，做 B 还要难得多
    Finding a flat is turning out to be hard, and finding a cheap one will be harder still.
  • Whether …, depends on … 能不能……，取决于……
    Whether the plants grow depends on how much rain we get.
  • At present there is no … in existence that can … 目前还没有任何……能……
    At present there is no medicine in existence that can cure the common cold.
  • If we were able to …, it would completely change … 要是我们能……，就会彻底改变……
    If we were able to read minds, it would completely change how we live.

  • the first question: can it support life? Venus, Mars, Earth
  • the star decides: big and hot → far away; small → close
  • the telescope: not on Earth, not in Earth orbit, out near Jupiter
  • block the starlight, study the air
  • look for bacteria, not little green men — it would change everything

Summary writing摘要写作

In no more than 80 words, explain the problems in searching for life on other planets and what kind of life we would most likely find.

用不超过 80 个词说明寻找外星生命要解决哪些问题，以及最可能找到什么生命。

Points a good summary covers好摘要要覆盖的要点
  • Astronomers can now find planets in other galaxies, but finding life on them is far harder.天文学家现在能探测到其他星系的行星，但要找到上面的生命难得多。
  • A planet's chance of life depends on its distance from its star and on the star's size and brightness.行星能否有生命，取决于它离恒星的远近和恒星的大小亮度。
  • A life-detecting telescope would need to be as far out as Jupiter to avoid dust, and the star's light would have to be blocked so the planet's air could be studied.要探测生命，望远镜得远到木星那里避开尘埃，还要遮住恒星的光才能分析行星大气。
  • The likeliest life would be simple, like bacteria, yet even that would transform how we see ourselves.最可能的是细菌这样的简单生命，但即便如此，也会彻底改变人类对自己的看法。
Our model summary本站范文

Astronomers can now find planets beyond our solar system, but finding life on them is much harder. A planet can only support life if it lies at the right distance from a star of the right size. No present telescope can detect life; one would have to work out near Jupiter, clear of dust, and block the star's glare to study the planet's air. The likeliest life would be bacteria, but even that discovery would change everything.（77 词）
