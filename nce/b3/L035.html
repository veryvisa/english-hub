Lesson 35 · Justice was Done 正义得到伸张

📘 New Concept English · Book 3 · Lesson 35 “Justice was Done”新概念英语 第三册 Lesson 35《Justice was Done》

B2🧠 26 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
Justice is usually tied to the courts: when guilt or innocence has been proved, we say that justice is served. But judges, however wise, are human and can be wrong. Now and then, the writer says, justice seems to act like a living force, handing out reward and punishment with no human help, and the everyday phrase for a wrongdoer getting what he deserves admits as much. Then comes an example. A jeweller's shop stands in an old house full of unused fireplaces and tall, narrow chimneys. At midday an assistant hears a muffled cry from inside a wall; firemen tap their way to the right chimney, cut through eighteen inches of brick and pull out a blackened figure. He had tried to climb in during the night to rob the shop and had been stuck for nearly ten hours. This is an argument that first defines a word and then gives a real example: one word with two meanings. The grammar to learn is however with an adjective for concession, must have done, by doing, and the past perfect with even before.
「正义」通常和法庭连在一起：证据确凿、定了有罪或无罪，我们才说正义得到伸张。可法官再英明也是人，会出错。作者说，偶尔正义会像一股活的力量，不靠人插手就自己把赏罚分配好——我们说「活该」，就是承认了这一点。接着讲一个例子：一家珠宝店由旧房子改建，有许多废弃的壁炉和又高又窄的烟囱。中午店员听见墙里传来闷闷的呼救声，消防员敲墙找到那根烟囱，凿穿十八英寸厚的墙，拖出一个浑身漆黑的人——他夜里想从烟囱钻进店里行窃，卡在里面将近十个小时。本课是先界定概念、再举实例的议论文：同一个词两种意思。学 however + 形容词（让步）、must have done、by doing、过去完成进行时与 even before。

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander. The trapped burglar is a type of story that newspapers really do print every few years, but this one has no named place or date, and we have not traced a source for it.

课文为编者 L. G. 亚历山大为教材自撰。卡在烟囱里的窃贼是报纸每隔几年就真会登一回的那类故事，但这一则没有具体地点和日期，我们没有查到它的出处。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

The lesson's second kind of justice has a name in literary history. The English critic Thomas Rymer, in a book on the tragedies of the earlier seventeenth century published in 1678, used the phrase poetic justice for the rule that a story should reward the good and punish the wicked by the end. He thought plays ought to obey it; later critics mostly thought real life did not, and that a story which always did so was false. The first kind of justice, the courtroom kind, also has a detail the lesson simplifies. English criminal courts do not require guilt to be proved beyond all doubt, but beyond reasonable doubt, and the burden lies on the prosecution. A famous statement of this is the House of Lords judgment in Woolmington v Director of Public Prosecutions, 1935, which called the duty of the prosecution to prove guilt a golden thread running through English criminal law. Innocence, in that system, is not proved at all; it is presumed.

课文说的第二种正义，在文学史上有名字。英国批评家托马斯·赖默在 1678 年出版的一本评论十七世纪前期悲剧的书里，用 poetic justice（诗的正义、「善有善报恶有恶报」）一词指一条规矩：故事到结尾应当奖善罚恶。他认为戏剧应当守这条规矩；后来的批评家大多认为现实生活并不如此，而一个总是如此的故事是假的。第一种正义——法庭上的正义——课文也简化了一处。英国刑事法庭并不要求「毫无疑问」地证明有罪，而是要求排除合理怀疑，举证责任在控方。最有名的表述是 1935 年上议院对「伍明顿诉检察长案」的判决，称控方证明被告有罪的责任是贯穿英国刑法的「金线」。在这个体系里，无罪根本不需要证明，它是被推定的。

✨ How it is written赏析

The essay moves from the abstract to the concrete in strict steps. The first paragraph keeps justice in the courts and in general words — machinery, concept, instances — and closes with a phrase from everyday speech, which is the hinge: the moment we use that everyday phrase about a man getting what he deserved, we are already in the second meaning. The story then delays its subject on purpose. We hear a cry, then a repeated cry, then the fire brigade, then the tapping, then the thickness of the wall, and only after all that does the man appear, described not as a thief but as a sorry, blackened figure. His confession is reported in one sentence, the length of his ordeal in a second, and the last line gives the moral in the past perfect: the punishment was over before the law began. The structure lets the reader feel the irony before being told it, which is why the closing sentence lands.

这篇文章从抽象到具体，一步一步走。第一段把正义留在法庭里，用的都是概括性的词——机器、概念、事例——最后落在一句日常口语上，那就是转折的合页：我们一说「活该」，就已经站进了第二种含义。接着故事有意拖延它的主角：先是一声叫喊，然后是反复的叫喊，然后是消防队，然后是敲墙，然后是墙有多厚，等这一切都过去，那人才出现——被描写成一个可怜兮兮、浑身漆黑的身影，而不是「窃贼」。他的供认只用一句话转述，他受困多久用另一句，最后一句用过去完成时给出寓意：法律还没开始，惩罚已经结束了。这样的结构让读者先感到讽刺、再被告知，所以末句才落得稳。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

It is pleasant to read and hard to defend. Ten hours stuck in a chimney is a real danger — people have died this way, from cold, lack of air or the pressure on their chest — and saying that it served him right comes close to saying that the punishment fits whatever happens to the guilty. That is exactly the view Rymer's critics objected to. Chinese readers know the same instinct in the saying that good and evil are always repaid in the end, and in the belief that heaven's net is wide but lets nothing through. Both sayings comfort, and both can make us careless: they encourage us to read any misfortune as deserved, and to be less troubled when the courts fail, because something else will see to it. The lesson's own first paragraph gives the better answer. Because judges are human and can be wrong, we need careful courts, not luck, and the man in the chimney still needed a trial.

这个故事读来痛快，却难以站得住。在烟囱里卡十个小时是真有生命危险的——确实有人这样死于寒冷、缺氧或胸口受压——说他「活该」，就近乎在说：有罪的人遇到什么都算罚得其当。这正是赖默的批评者所反对的观点。中国读者对同样的直觉并不陌生：「善恶到头终有报」，「天网恢恢，疏而不漏」。这两句话都给人安慰，也都可能让人松懈：它们鼓励我们把任何不幸都读成应得的，在法庭失职时也不那么不安，因为自有别的东西来料理。课文自己的第一段给了更好的回答：正因为法官是人、会出错，我们需要的是审慎的法庭而不是运气——烟囱里的那个人，也仍然需要一场审判。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

however / no matter how with an adjective or adverb: conceding a pointhowever / no matter how + 形容词或副词：让步
however plus an adjective or adverb, often followed by a subject and verb, means 'no matter how'. The subject and verb are often dropped, as in however wise. Do not confuse it with however meaning 'but', which is followed by a comma.
however + 形容词/副词（+ 主语 + 谓语）= no matter how …，「无论多么……」。主谓常常省略：however wise (they are)。注意与表示转折的 however（然而）区分：转折时后面跟逗号。

Sentence 7 grants that judges may be very wise and famous, and still says they are human.

本课：第 7 句：法官无论多么英明、多么显赫，也是人。

  • However hard he tries, he can't sing in tune.
  • No matter how tired you are, finish your homework first.
  • Everyone makes mistakes, however careful they may be.
find it + adjective + to do: it as an empty objectfind it + 形容词 + to do（it 作形式宾语）
When the real object is an infinitive or a clause and there is also a complement, put it in the object place and move the real object to the end. Common verbs are find, think, make, consider and feel.
当宾语是不定式或从句、而后面又有宾补时，用 it 作形式宾语，把真正的宾语放到句末。常见动词：find、think、make、consider、feel。

by doing for method; even beforeby doing：方式；even before：甚至在……之前
by plus a gerund gives the method by which something is done. even placed before before, after or when adds emphasis.
by + 动名词表示做某事的方法或手段（= by means of doing）。even 放在 before / after / when 之前加强语气。

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • The word X is usually associated with … X 这个词通常和……联系在一起
    The word home is usually associated with comfort and safety.
  • X, however wise or …, are human and can … X 无论多么英明或……，也是人，也会……
    Doctors, however clever or careful, are human and can get things wrong.
  • At such times, X acts like a living force. 在这种时候，X 就像一股活的力量
    At such times, fear acts like a living force in a crowd.
  • They located … by …ing and …ing. 他们通过……和……找到了……
    They located the leak by turning off taps and checking the meter.
  • … was done even before … 甚至在……之前，……就已经完成了
    The harm was done even before the doctor arrived.

Points a good summary covers好摘要要覆盖的要点
  • A jeweller's shop was in an old house with many unused fireplaces and narrow chimneys.一家珠宝店由一座有许多废弃壁炉和窄烟囱的旧房子改建而成。
  • Towards noon a young assistant heard someone crying out inside a wall, and the manager called the fire brigade at once.中午一个女店员听到墙里传出闷闷的呼救声，经理立刻打电话叫来消防队。
  • The firemen found the chimney by tapping, cut through a thick wall and freed the trapped man through a large hole.消防员敲墙找到那根烟囱，凿穿厚墙，开了个大洞，把被卡住的人救了出来。
  • He admitted that he had tried to climb in during the night to rob the shop and had been stuck for nearly ten hours.那人承认夜里想爬烟囱进店行窃，结果被卡了将近十小时。
Our model summary本站范文

A jeweller's shop occupied an old house with many unused chimneys. Around midday a girl heard someone crying out behind a wall and told the manager, who called the fire brigade. The firemen tapped the walls to find the right chimney and cut through eighteen inches of brick to free a trapped man. Black with soot, he admitted that he had planned to rob the shop at night and had been stuck for nearly ten hours.（76 词）
