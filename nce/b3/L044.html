Lesson 44 · Speed and Comfort 速度与舒适

📘 New Concept English · Book 3 · Lesson 44 “Speed and Comfort”新概念英语 第三册 Lesson 44《Speed and Comfort》

B2🧠 33 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
People travelling far must choose between land, sea and air, and the writer compares them in turn. Few can enjoy more than a few hours on a train: the carriages grow cramped and stuffy, reading is soon defeated by the rhythm of the wheels, daytime sleep comes in bits, night-time sleep hardly at all, and even with a sleeper you spend the night staring at a small blue light or hunting for your ticket, and arrive worn out. Long car journeys are worse, since you cannot even read, and most roads are crowded and short of service stations. Ships offer spacious decks, games, company and good food — provided the sea is calm; if not, and you are prone to seasickness, nothing is worse, and sea journeys are slow anyway, so few people give up holiday time for them. Planes are thought dangerous and expensive, but nothing beats them for speed and comfort: you sit back high above the clouds at five hundred miles an hour, watch a film or sip champagne, see the real shape of the land or plains of sunlit cloud, and arrive fresh and uncreased. This is an argument by point-by-point comparison, whose conclusion shows in how much space each kind of travel is given. The grammar to learn is Hardly anyone, take one's mind off, in snatches, more often than not, by comparison, always assuming that, have the reputation of, nothing can match, and However you decide.
出远门要选走陆路、水路还是航空。作者逐一比较：坐火车超过几个小时谁也受不了，车厢又挤又闷，看书看不了多久就被车轮的节奏催睡，白天睡不踏实，夜里想睡又睡不着，就算有卧铺也是盯着车顶的小蓝灯、摸黑找票，到站时已累得半死；坐汽车更糟，连书都看不成，高速公路之外路况差、车多、服务站少。坐船有甲板可以散步、玩游戏、交朋友、吃好饭——前提是海面平静，否则一晕船，再没有比这更糟的交通工具了；而且海路太慢，没几个人舍得拿假期去换。飞机有危险、昂贵的名声，可论速度和舒适谁也比不上：三万英尺高空、时速五百多英里，坐在深深的扶手椅里看电影、喝香槟，看山川的形状，看阳光下望不到边的云海，到达时精神饱满、衣衫不皱。本课是逐项比较的议论文，结论藏在篇幅分配里。学 Hardly anyone、take one's mind off、in snatches、more often than not、by comparison、always assuming that、have the reputation of、nothing can match、However you decide。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. What does the writer find uncomfortable about long train journeys?作者认为坐火车长途旅行有哪些不舒服的地方？
  2. On what condition do the pleasures of sea travel depend?坐船旅行的好处要以什么为前提？
  3. Which form of transport do you think the writer prefers, and how can you tell?你认为作者最喜欢哪种交通工具？从哪里看得出来？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander. The essay is a period piece: it describes long-distance travel as it was experienced in Britain and Europe in the second half of the twentieth century, when air travel was still a luxury and a novelty for most people.

课文为编者 L. G. 亚历山大为教材自撰。这是一篇带着时代印记的文章：它描写的是二十世纪下半叶英国和欧洲的长途旅行，那时对大多数人来说，坐飞机还是一种奢侈和新鲜事。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

Several of the lesson's facts still hold. Large passenger jets do cruise at heights of roughly thirty to forty thousand feet and at speeds of around five hundred miles an hour. Air travel's reputation for danger, which the lesson mentions, has moved even further from the facts since then: the airline industry's own safety reports show that fatal accidents on commercial flights have become very rare, and per journey flying is now among the safest ways to travel. Other parts of the picture have changed in ways the writer could not foresee. In Europe, concern about the carbon cost of flying has produced a word borrowed from Swedish, flight shame, and a modest revival of sleeper trains on routes between major cities. In China, a high-speed rail network built mostly since 2008 now runs to tens of thousands of kilometres, and on many routes of a few hundred miles the train is as fast as the plane once you count the trip to the airport and the security queue. The lesson's verdict was right for its time and place; for a modern traveller the answer often depends on the distance.

课文里的几个事实至今仍成立。大型客机的巡航高度确实大约在三万到四万英尺之间，速度约为每小时五百英里。课文提到的「飞机危险」的名声，从那以后离事实更远了：航空业自己的安全报告显示，商业航班的致命事故已经非常罕见，按每次出行计算，坐飞机如今是最安全的出行方式之一。画面里的另一些部分，则以作者无法预见的方式变了。在欧洲，对飞行碳排放的担忧催生了一个从瑞典语借来的词——「飞行羞耻」，也让主要城市之间的卧铺列车有了小规模的复兴。在中国，主要在 2008 年以后建起来的高速铁路网如今已达数万公里；在许多几百英里的线路上，把去机场和排队安检的时间算进去，高铁和飞机一样快。课文的结论在它那个时代和地方是对的；对今天的旅行者来说，答案往往取决于距离。

✨ How it is written赏析

The essay pretends to be fair and is not, and the way it tips the scales is the lesson in writing. Each form of transport gets a paragraph, but the lengths are very different: trains and cars are dismissed in a run of complaints, ships get a short paragraph of praise followed by a sharp 'if it is not', and planes get the longest section of all, almost entirely positive. The weaknesses of flying — danger and expense — are admitted in two short sentences and then answered by a third that begins with 'But'. The vocabulary changes too. The train passages are full of small physical miseries — stuffy, cramped, fumbling, exhausted — while the flying passages are full of space and light: soar, effortlessly, brilliantly, unbroken. The final sentence answers the first paragraph's complaints point by point: fresh instead of exhausted, and no days of recovery. The question before the text, about which form of transport the writer prefers, is almost a joke: the essay answers it in the way it is built.

这篇文章装作公平，其实并不公平，而它让天平倾斜的方法，正是这一课要学的写作。每种交通工具各占一段，但篇幅大不相同：火车和汽车在一连串抱怨中被打发掉；轮船得到一小段称赞，紧接着是尖锐的一句「如果不是这样」；飞机得到全文最长的一段，几乎全是好话。飞行的弱点——危险和昂贵——用两个短句承认，然后由第三句一个以 But 开头的句子来回应。用词也变了：写火车的段落满是身体上的小痛苦——闷、挤、摸索、精疲力竭——而写飞行的段落满是空间与光：翱翔、毫不费力、灿烂、连绵不断。最后一句逐条回应了第一段的抱怨：精神饱满而不是筋疲力尽，也不用花好几天恢复。课前那个问题——作者更喜欢哪种交通工具——几乎是个玩笑：文章用它的结构就回答了。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The lesson is a useful model of comparison writing precisely because it shows how easily comparison becomes advertising. Count what is left out. The essay never mentions getting to the airport, waiting, security, delays or lost luggage, and it judges the train by its worst moments and the plane by its best. A fair comparison would set the same questions against each choice — door-to-door time, cost, comfort, reliability, and now environmental cost — and would admit that the answer changes with the trip. For a journey of a few hundred kilometres in China, Japan or much of Europe, a fast train usually wins; for crossing an ocean, nothing yet replaces the plane. A good exercise for a reader is to rewrite one paragraph of the essay from the other side, praising the train with the same tricks the writer uses for the plane. Seeing how easily it can be done is the best defence against persuasive writing of every kind.

这一课之所以是一个有用的比较类写作范本，恰恰因为它显示了比较是多么容易变成广告。数一数被省掉的东西：文章从没提到去机场、候机、安检、延误或行李丢失，它用火车最糟的时刻和飞机最好的时刻相比。公平的比较会对每个选项提同样的问题——门到门的时间、费用、舒适、准点，以及如今的环境代价——并承认答案随行程而变。在中国、日本或欧洲大部分地区，几百公里的行程，快速列车通常胜出；要跨越大洋，至今还没有什么能取代飞机。给读者的一个好练习是：站在另一边重写文章的一段，用作者夸飞机的同样手法去夸火车。看到这有多容易做到，就是抵御各种说服性文字最好的办法。

💬 Discuss and write讨论与写作
  1. Plan a journey of five hundred kilometres. Compare train, car and plane fairly, using the same three questions for each.
    规划一次五百公里的行程，用同样三个问题公平地比较火车、汽车和飞机。
  2. Rewrite the paragraph about trains so that it praises them, in about 100 words.
    用约 100 词重写写火车的那一段，让它变成夸火车。
  3. Should people fly less because of climate change? What would make you choose a slower way to travel?
    人们是否应该为了气候变化少坐飞机？什么会让你选择一种更慢的出行方式？

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

Hardly, scarcely or barely with anyone, anything or ever: almost noneHardly / Scarcely / Barely + anyone / anything / ever：几乎没有
hardly, scarcely and barely are negative in meaning, so they go with any- words or ever: hardly anyone, hardly anything, hardly ever. Do not add not, and do not use no- words after them.
hardly、scarcely、barely 本身带否定意义，后面接 any- 词（anyone、anything、any）或 ever，表示「几乎没有、几乎从不」。不要再加 not，也不要接 no- 词。

Sentence 2 begins with Hardly anyone: almost nobody really enjoys long hours on a train.

本课：第 2 句用 Hardly anyone 开头：几乎没人能真正享受长时间坐火车。

  • Hardly anyone came to the meeting.
  • There was hardly anything to eat.
  • We hardly ever see them now.
Gerunds as subjects and after prepositions动名词作主语与介词宾语：have the reputation of being / take one's mind off
A gerund can be the subject of a sentence, and it is also the form used after a preposition, as in of being or for travelling. A preposition cannot be followed by the plain verb or an infinitive.
动名词（-ing）可以作主语（Travelling at a height … is exhilarating），也常跟在介词后（of being、for travelling）。介词后不能直接接动词原形或不定式。

Sentence 23 has a long gerund phrase as its subject, and sentence 20 has of being after a preposition.

本课：第 23 句以一个很长的动名词短语作主语；第 20 句 of being 用在介词之后。

  • Swimming in the sea is good for you.
  • She is tired of waiting.
  • He has a reputation for being late.
However, whatever or wherever with a clause: no matter howHowever / Whatever / Wherever + 从句：让步
however with a clause and no adjective means 'in whatever way': However you decide is the same as No matter how you decide. however can also go with an adjective or adverb, as in Lesson 35.
however + 从句（不加形容词）= in whatever way，「不管怎样」；However you decide … = No matter how you decide …。也可以 however + 形容词 / 副词（见第 35 课）。

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • People travelling long distances often have to decide whether … 出远门的人常常要决定是……还是……
    People buying a car often have to decide whether to choose petrol or electric.
  • Hardly anyone can positively enjoy … 几乎没有人能真正享受……
    Hardly anyone can really enjoy queuing in the rain.
  • X is only part of the answer, for … X 只是部分的解决办法，因为……
    Buying a bigger fridge is only part of the answer, for the kitchen is still tiny.
  • By comparison, X offers … — always assuming that … 相比之下，X 提供……——当然前提是……
    By comparison, the beach offers sun and sand — always assuming that it does not rain.
  • Nothing can match X for … 论……，什么都比不上 X
    Nothing can match a bicycle for short trips in town.

Summary writing摘要写作

In no more than 80 words, explain why the writer thinks air travel is best.

用不超过 80 个词说明作者为什么认为乘飞机旅行最好。

Points a good summary covers好摘要要覆盖的要点
  • Long train trips are stuffy and tiring: reading makes you drowsy and you cannot sleep properly.长途坐火车又闷又累，看书会犯困，睡觉也睡不好。
  • Long car trips are worse: you cannot read, and the roads are busy and lack service stations.长途开车更糟，连书都看不了，路上车多、服务站少。
  • Sea travel is comfortable but can cause seasickness and is slow.坐船虽然舒适，但可能晕船，而且很慢。
  • Planes are fast and comfortable; you can enjoy the view, watch a film, read or sleep, and arrive fresh.飞机快而舒适，可以看风景、看电影、看书或睡觉，到达时精神饱满。
Our model summary本站范文

The writer thinks air travel is best. Long train journeys are stuffy and tiring, and passengers can neither read nor sleep well. Car journeys are worse, as you cannot read at all and the roads are crowded. Ships are pleasant but slow, and a rough sea brings seasickness. Planes, though expensive, are fast and comfortable. You can enjoy the view, watch a film, read or sleep, and you arrive fresh instead of exhausted.（73 词）
