📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Book 3 texts were written for the course by L. G. Alexander; this one retells a real event, the first flight across the English Channel in July 1909. We checked its dates against aviation histories; see the notes below on where the lesson simplifies.

第三册课文由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为教材自撰；本课复述的是真实事件——1909 年 7 月的首次飞越英吉利海峡。我们按航空史资料核了日期，课文简化之处见下文。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

Lord Northcliffe owned the Daily Mail, the best-selling newspaper in Britain, and used prizes to sell papers and to push Britain to take flying seriously. The Channel prize was first announced in October 1908 at £500 and doubled to £1,000 when nobody claimed it that year — the lesson gives only the final sum. In 1909 flying was barely six years old, and engines were the weak point: Latham's first attempt on 19 July ended about eleven kilometres out from Sangatte, and a French navy ship picked him up. Blériot, who had already crashed many of his own designs, took off near Calais before dawn on 25 July with an injured foot, flew without a compass, and landed on a field near Dover Castle after about 37 minutes, where a memorial now marks the spot.

诺思克利夫勋爵是英国最畅销的报纸《每日邮报》的老板，他用悬赏来卖报，也借此催促英国认真对待航空。飞越海峡奖 1908 年 10 月首次公布时是 500 英镑，当年无人领取，才翻倍到 1000 英镑——课文只写了最后的数目。1909 年，人类会飞才不过六年，发动机是最大的短板：莱瑟姆 7 月 19 日的第一次尝试在离桑加特约十一公里处结束，被一艘法国海军舰艇救起。布莱里奥此前已摔坏过不少自己设计的飞机，7 月 25 日天亮前他带着伤脚从加来附近起飞，没带罗盘，约 37 分钟后降落在多佛城堡旁的一块草地上，那里如今立着纪念标志。

✨ How it is written赏析

Alexander tells this story almost like a sports report. Every paragraph is anchored by a time: 1908, over a year, July 19th, two days later, a week before, July 25th, 4.15 a.m., half an hour later, thirty-seven minutes. The tenses do the ordering for you: the past perfect jumps back to what had already happened (had travelled, had been making, had completed), so the main line of the story can stay in the simple past. The structure is a rivalry with a twist: the expected race never happens because one man oversleeps. And the ending is gently comic — the hero lands, and history is greeted by a local policeman. When you write about a historical event, try this: a clear timeline, two people whose fortunes go in opposite directions, and one small human detail that the reader remembers.

亚历山大几乎是用体育报道的笔法讲这个故事。每一段都钉着一个时间：1908 年、一年多以后、7 月 19 日、两天后、一周前、7 月 25 日、凌晨 4:15、半小时后、37 分钟。时态替你排好了顺序：过去完成时往回跳，交代此前已经发生的事（had travelled、had been making、had completed），故事主线就能一直留在一般过去时。结构是「对手戏加反转」：人人期待的比赛没发生，因为其中一人睡过了头。结尾带点喜剧感——英雄着陆了，迎接历史的是一位当地警察。写历史事件可以照学：一条清楚的时间线，两个命运朝相反方向走的人，再加一个读者记得住的小细节。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

Check the textbook against the record and two simplifications appear. The prize was not £1,000 from the start, and Latham's second attempt came on 27 July, two days after Blériot, not a week later — when he too came down in the sea within sight of Dover. The lesson's version is easier to follow, but it is a reminder that even a coursebook can round off the facts, so dates in any story are worth checking. The larger point has held up very well: within a few years aircraft were crossing the Channel in war, and the sea that had protected Britain for centuries no longer did. For Chinese readers there is a useful parallel: in the same year, 1909, Feng Ru, a young Cantonese engineer in California, was building and testing his own aeroplane — the age of flying began for China, too, with self-taught pioneers.

拿课文和史料一对，能看出两处简化：奖金不是一开始就是 1000 英镑；莱瑟姆的第二次尝试在 7 月 27 日，比布莱里奥只晚两天，不是一周后——那次他也是在望得见多佛的地方落进了海里。课文的版本更好懂，但它提醒我们：连教材也会把事实「取整」，任何故事里的日期都值得核一核。更大的判断经受住了时间：没过几年，飞机就在战争中飞越海峡，几百年来护卫英国的那片海不再是屏障。对中国读者有个有意思的对照：同在 1909 年，旅居加州的广东青年工程师冯如正在制造和试飞自己的飞机——中国的航空时代，同样是由自学成才的先驱开启的。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 1第 1 句 (begins开头 “In 1908 Lord …”)

  • offer sth to sb: make something available to someone.offer sth to sb：把……提供给。
  • who would fly: future in the past (seen from 1908, nobody had done it yet).who would fly：过去将来（站在 1908 年看，还没人飞过）。
  • The pound sign in the source text is a full-width ￡; keep this in mind when you search for the sentence.原文英镑符号用的是全角 ￡，查原句时注意。

Sentence 2第 2 句 (begins开头 “Over a year …”)

  • before: 'not until' (the first situation lasted over a year before the next event happened).before：直到……才（前一件事持续了一年多，后一件事才发生）。
  • Passive of make an attempt: an attempt was made.make an attempt 的被动：an attempt was made。

Sentence 5第 5 句 (begins开头 “He had travelled …”)

  • had done ... when ...: 'had just done ... when suddenly ...'; when means 'and at that moment'.had done … when …：刚……就（突然）……，when 是并列连词「就在这时」。
  • be forced to do: have to do because there is no choice.be forced to do：被迫。

Sentence 8第 8 句 (begins开头 “Bleriot had been …”)

  • had been doing since: past perfect continuous — he had been building planes continuously up to 1909.had been doing since：过去完成进行时——到 1909 年为止一直在造。
  • latest: newest (not last, 'final').latest：最新的（不是 last「最后的」）。

Sentence 12第 12 句 (begins开头 “It looked as …”)

  • It looked as if ...: it seemed that ...It looked as if…：看来好像……
  • there would be: future in the past of there is.there would be：there be 的过去将来时。

Sentence 13第 13 句 (begins开头 “Both planes were …”)

  • was/were going to: a past plan (which did not happen).was/were going to：过去打算（结果没实现）。
  • fail to do: not manage to do. enough comes after the adverb early.fail to do：没能。enough 放在副词 early 后面。

Sentence 16第 16 句 (begins开头 “When he landed …”)

  • the first + noun + to do: the first person who did ... (an infinitive after the noun).the first + 名词 + to do：第一个做……的人（不定式作后置定语）。
  • The man who has just made history is met by nobody grander than a policeman — a touch of humour.迎接这位历史性人物的只是个警察——一点幽默。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

had done ... when ...: 'had just ... when suddenly ...'had done … when …：刚……就……（突然情况）
The first half uses the past perfect to say how far something had got; the half after when (= and then suddenly) uses the past simple for the event that interrupted it. Similar: hardly/scarcely had ... when ... (no sooner ... than).
前半句用过去完成时说明「已经做到某一步」，when（= and then suddenly）引出的后半句用一般过去时，表示突然打断的事件。类似的还有 hardly/scarcely had … when …（一……就……）。

In this lesson, sentence 5: Latham had travelled only seven miles ... when his engine failed.

本课：第 5 句莱瑟姆 had travelled only seven miles … when his engine failed。

  • We had just sat down to dinner when the phone rang.
  • Hardly had I left the house when it began to rain.
the first / last / only + noun + to dothe first / last / only + 名词 + to do
After a noun with an ordinal number, last or only, English often uses an infinitive: the first man to land on the moon. A relative clause is also possible: the first man who...
序数词、last、only 修饰名词时，后面常用不定式作定语：the first man to land on the moon。也可用定语从句：the first man who…。
  • She was the first woman to win the prize.
  • He is always the last to leave the office.
It looks/looked as if ... and be forced to doIt looks/looked as if … 与 be forced to do
It looks as if + clause: it seems that (a judgement based on what you can see); if it is clearly unreal, the clause can use the subjunctive (as if he were...). be forced to do: have to, because of outside pressure.
It looks as if + 从句：看起来好像（有依据的判断）；如果明显不真实，从句可以用虚拟（as if he were…）。be forced to do：被迫，强调外力。

In this lesson: sentence 12 It looked as if ... (a race seemed likely); sentence 5 he was forced to land (he had to come down on the sea).

本课：第 12 句 It looked as if …（似乎要有一场比赛）；第 5 句 he was forced to land（迫降海上）。

  • It looks as if it's going to rain.
  • He talks as if he were the boss.
  • The bad weather forced us to cancel the trip. → We were forced to cancel the trip.

Our model summary本站范文

In 1908 a £1,000 prize was offered for the first flight over the English Channel. In July 1909 Hubert Latham tried first, but after just seven miles his engine stopped and he came down in the sea. A few days later both Latham and Louis Bleriot were ready to fly. Latham overslept, while Bleriot took off at dawn and landed near Dover 37 minutes later. Latham's next attempt also ended in the sea, half a mile from England.（78 词）
