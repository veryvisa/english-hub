Lesson 46 · Do It Yourself 自己动手

📘 New Concept English · Book 3 · Lesson 46 “Do It Yourself”新概念英语 第三册 Lesson 46《Do It Yourself》

B2🧠 33 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
People grow ever keener on doing jobs themselves, and we rely less and less on trained workers. There are endless books and magazines that teach the skills; young couples decorate their own homes, and men spend their free time building fireplaces, laying out gardens, putting up garages and making furniture, while shops run advice services for beginners and sell parts to be put together at home. The trouble is that not everyone is born good with tools, yet some wives believe their husbands can fix anything, and worse, the men believe it too; in the end it is a matter of pride. The writer is one of these men. His lawn mower has been broken for a year. When his wife suggests calling in someone, he refuses flatly and does the job himself. He takes it to pieces, finds the broken link, buys a new chain, and then cannot put the machine back together, with several odd pieces left over. He gives up. Now the house stands in a jungle, and the mower lies rusting somewhere in the grass. This is a humorous essay that argues first and then gives an example, turning from a social trend to a joke against the writer himself. The grammar to learn is inversion after So great is ... that, Armed with, go so far as to, not only by ... but by, be supposed to, would not hear of, get round to, as good as new, Needless to say, and inversion after Buried ....
我们越来越热衷于自己动手，越来越不依赖专业工人：教人动手的书刊多得数不清，新婚夫妇自己装修，男人们业余时间砌壁炉、修花园、造车库、打家具，商店专门为生手开咨询服务、卖回家自己拼装的零件。麻烦在于不是人人天生会修东西，可有些妻子偏偏相信丈夫无所不能，更糟的是男人自己也这么以为——说到底是面子问题。作者就是这样一个人：割草机坏了一年，妻子劝他请人来修，他一口回绝，自己动手，拆了一地零件，找到了断掉的链节，换了新链条却装不回去，还多出几块不知道放哪儿的零件，最后放弃。如今房子四周杂草丛生，那台割草机就埋在草里生锈。本课是先议论后举例的幽默小品，前半段讲社会现象，后半段拿自己开涮。学 So great is … that 倒装、Armed with、go so far as to、not only by … but by、be supposed to、would not hear of、get round to、as good as new、Needless to say、Buried … there is 倒装。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. Did the writer get the mower working again in the end, and why or why not?作者最后修好割草机了吗？为什么？
  2. What does the writer think really lies behind the craze for doing things yourself?作者认为自己动手热背后的真正原因是什么？
  3. Why would the writer not let anyone else repair the machine?作者为什么不肯请人来修？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander. It is an essay with a personal story at its end, a pattern the third book uses often, and the story of the mower appears to be invented for the joke rather than reported.

课文为编者 L. G. 亚历山大为教材自撰，是「议论在前、个人小故事在后」的写法，第三册常用这种结构；割草机的故事看来是为了笑点编的，不是纪实。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

The craze the lesson describes was real. In post-war Britain, materials were short, tradesmen were expensive and more families owned their homes, and a whole industry grew up to teach ordinary people how to fix and build things: magazines devoted to home improvement appeared in the 1950s, and large warehouse shops selling tools and materials to the public spread from the late 1960s. Something similar happened in North America, where home-improvement chains became some of the largest retailers on the continent. There is also research on the pride the writer mentions. In 2012 three American psychologists published a study they called the IKEA effect, named after the flat-pack furniture company: people who had assembled a simple box or folded a paper figure themselves valued it more highly than identical ones made by others, and were willing to pay more for it — but only if they had actually finished the job. When people built something and then had to take it apart, or failed to complete it, the extra love disappeared. The writer of this lesson, with his pile of parts that fit nowhere, is exactly the case the study warns about.

课文写的这股热潮是真实的。战后的英国材料紧缺、工匠昂贵、有房的家庭又多了，于是长出了一整个教普通人修东西、造东西的行业：五十年代出现了专讲家居改造的杂志，六十年代末起，面向大众卖工具和建材的大型仓储店铺开。北美也有类似的情形，家居建材连锁成了这块大陆上最大的零售商之一。作者提到的「面子」也有研究。2012 年，三位美国心理学家发表了一项研究，借平板包装家具公司的名字称之为「宜家效应」：自己拼装过一个简单盒子、或亲手折过一个纸模型的人，比起别人做的一模一样的成品，会把自己做的看得更值钱，也愿意出更高的价——但前提是真的做完了。如果做好后又被拆掉，或者根本没做成，这份额外的喜爱就没了。本课作者守着一堆装不回去的零件，正是这项研究提醒的那种情形。

✨ How it is written赏析

The lesson's comedy comes from a gap that widens step by step. The first half is written in the calm voice of a social observer, full of general nouns: enthusiasts, consumers, novices. Then the writer quietly includes himself among the deluded men, and the essay turns into a confession. Each stage of the repair is told with more confidence than the last: only a small adjustment, a screw to turn, a drop of oil. The word Inevitably arrives exactly when that confidence peaks, and from then on the sentences become shorter and flatter, two of them only five or six words long, with weeks passing and grass growing. The writer never says he was foolish; the facts say it for him. Notice also how he hides a small triumph in the middle — he was extremely pleased with himself for finding the broken link — because pride is the real subject. The ending puts the mower last in the sentence, buried in the grass and in the grammar, with the same empty promise that started the trouble.

这一课的喜剧感来自一道一步步拉大的落差。前半篇用冷静的社会观察者口吻，满是泛称名词：爱好者、消费者、生手。然后作者悄悄把自己也算进那群「以为自己什么都能修」的男人里，文章变成了一份自白。修理的每一步都比上一步说得更有把握：只要稍加调整，这里拧一下螺丝，那里滴一滴油。偏偏在自信到顶点时冒出一个 Inevitably，此后句子越来越短、越来越平，有两句只有五六个词：日子一周周过去，草一天天长高。作者从不说自己傻，事实替他说了。还要留意他在中间藏了一次小小的得意——找到断掉的链节时他非常满意——因为面子才是真正的主题。结尾把割草机放在句子最后，既埋在草里也埋在语法里，后面跟着的还是当初惹出麻烦的那句空头承诺。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The lesson treats pride as the problem, but today the argument for doing things yourself has changed. Manufacturers have made many products harder to repair, with glued parts, special screws and software locks, and a right-to-repair movement has pushed back: the European Union and several North American governments have passed or proposed rules requiring makers to supply parts, tools and repair information. Seen that way, the writer's failure is not only a joke about vanity. Repairing things saves money and waste, and online video has made it far easier for a novice to learn. Yet the lesson's warning still holds where safety is involved: electrical wiring, gas appliances and structural work are areas where local rules often require a licensed tradesperson, and an amateur mistake can cost much more than the repair. A sensible rule is to try the jobs where failure costs little, and to call in the specialist when failure could hurt someone. The real lesson is less about tools than about knowing the limit of your own skill.

课文把面子当成问题，可如今「自己动手」的理由变了。厂家把很多产品做得越来越难修：零件用胶粘、螺丝是特制的、软件上了锁；于是有了「维修权」运动来反击，欧盟和北美若干政府已经通过或提出规则，要求厂家提供零件、工具和维修资料。这样看，作者的失败就不只是一个关于虚荣的笑话。自己修东西省钱、少浪费，网上的视频也让生手学起来容易得多。但凡涉及安全，课文的警告依然成立：电线、燃气设备和结构性的施工，当地规定往往要求持牌技工来做，外行一个失误的代价可能远超修理费。比较明智的做法是：失败代价小的活自己试，失败可能伤人的活请专业人员。真正的教训与其说是关于工具，不如说是关于认清自己本事的边界。

💬 Discuss and write讨论与写作
  1. What is one thing you have repaired or made yourself? Did you value it more because you did it?
    说说一件你自己修过或做过的东西。你会因为是自己做的而更珍惜它吗？
  2. Write 120 words about a time your confidence was greater than your skill. Tell it so that the reader sees the mistake before you do.
    写 120 词讲一次你的信心超过本事的经历。要写得让读者比你先看出错在哪里。
  3. Should makers be required by law to make their products easy to repair? Give one reason for and one against.
    法律该不该要求厂家把产品做得容易维修？各给一个赞成和反对的理由。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 1第 1 句 (begins开头 “So great is …”)

  • When so plus an adjective is moved to the front for emphasis, the verb comes before the subject. The plain order would be: our passion is so great that ...So + 形容词 + 系动词 + 主语 + that：so 引导的部分提到句首，主谓倒装，强调「热情之大」。正常语序是 Our passion … is so great that …。
  • increasingly less dependent on: relying on it less and less as time goes on.increasingly less dependent on：越来越不依赖。dependent on 依赖于。

Sentence 3第 3 句 (begins开头 “No one can …”)

  • not ... any longer has the same meaning as no longer; for adds the reason.not … any longer = no longer：再也不。for 补充理由。

Sentence 4第 4 句 (begins开头 “Armed with the …”)

  • The past participle phrase describes the subject, the young couples, as they set out with what they need.Armed with …：过去分词短语作状语，「备好了……」，逻辑主语是 newlyweds。
  • gaily is slightly ironic here: cheerfully, without a thought of the trouble ahead.gaily 在这里带点讽刺：兴冲冲地，没想到会有麻烦。

Sentence 7第 7 句 (begins开头 “Some really keen …”)

  • go so far as to do: to go to the length of doing something that seems extreme.go so far as to do：甚至（走到）做……的地步。

Sentence 12第 12 句 (begins开头 “Even men who …”)

  • can hardly: can almost not; driving a nail in, and keeping it straight, is the simplest job with a hammer.「把钉子钉直」（drive a nail in straight）是最简单的木工活；can hardly 几乎不能。
  • be supposed to be: to be thought to be, usually wrongly.be supposed to be：被认为是（往往与事实不符）。

Sentence 20第 20 句 (begins开头 “I would not …”)

  • would not hear of: refused even to consider.would not hear of：不肯听，坚决不同意。

Sentence 37第 37 句 (begins开头 “Buried somewhere in …”)

  • The participle phrase comes first and there is follows, so the reader's eye goes to the grass before the mower itself appears at the end.过去分词短语提到句首，后面接 there is，相当于倒装，把读者的目光先引到草丛里，最后才亮出割草机。
  • The which clause at the end echoes the promise in sentence 19, and the irony is plain.结尾的 which 从句回扣第 19 句的承诺，讽刺意味很浓。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

Inversion when so plus an adjective comes first表语提前的倒装：So great is X that …
When so plus an adjective, or such, is moved to the start for emphasis, the verb be goes before the subject: so plus adjective plus be plus subject plus that. In speech the ordinary order is fine; the inverted form belongs to emphatic writing.
把 so + 形容词（或 such）提到句首表示强调时，系动词放到主语前：So + adj. + be + 主语 + that …。口语里用正常语序即可，书面强调时才倒装。

Sentences 1 and 4 open the essay with it, pushing the topic, our passion for doing things ourselves, right to the front.

本课：第 1–2 句开篇就用这个结构，一句话把全文话题「自己动手的热情」推到最前。

  • So heavy was the snow that the school closed for three days.
  • Such was her anger that she could not speak.
Inversion after a fronted place or participle phrase地点或分词短语提前引起的倒装
When a phrase of place or a past participle phrase such as Buried ... or Hidden ... starts the sentence, it is often followed by there is or by be and then the subject, so that the subject comes last and keeps the reader waiting.
表示地点的短语或过去分词短语（Buried …, Hidden …）放到句首时，后面常接 there is / 系动词 + 主语，主语放在最后，造成悬念。

The last sentence uses it, keeping the rusting mower back until the end.

本课：最后一句以 Buried somewhere … 开头，接 there is，割草机最后才出场。

  • Hidden behind the curtain was a small door.
  • At the top of the hill stands an old church.
Set phrases for putting off, refusing and barely managing几个表达「拖延、拒绝、勉强」的习惯说法
get round to doing something means at last finding time for it; would not hear of means refusing to consider; can hardly do means can almost not do; go so far as to do means going to an extreme. They carry the speaker's attitude as well as the facts.
get round to (doing) sth＝终于抽出时间做；would not hear of sth＝坚决不同意；can hardly do＝几乎不会做；go so far as to do＝甚至到……地步。这些短语让叙述带上说话人的态度。

Sentence 19 uses got round to, sentence 20 would not hear of, and sentence 12 can hardly.

本课：第 19 句用了 got round to；第 20 句用了 would not hear of；第 12 句用了 can hardly。

  • I meant to answer her email, but I never got round to it.
  • She offered to pay, but he wouldn't hear of it.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • So great is X that … X 之大，以至于……
    So great was the crowd that the doors had to be closed.
  • Armed with …, X embark(s) on … 带上……，X 着手……
    Armed with a map and a torch, we embarked on the night walk.
  • Some … go so far as to … 有些人甚至到了……的地步
    Some fans go so far as to follow the band from city to city.
  • X is a question of … as much as anything else X 主要是……的问题
    Learning a language is a question of habit as much as anything else.
  • Needless to say, … 不用说，……
    Needless to say, the cake disappeared in five minutes.

Summary writing摘要写作

In no more than 80 words, tell how the writer tried to repair his lawn mower and what came of it.

用不超过 80 个词讲述作者修割草机的经过和结果。

Points a good summary covers好摘要要覆盖的要点

Our model summary本站范文

The writer's mower had been broken since the summer before, and when his wife suggested getting a repairman, he refused. One Saturday he took a close look and expected a small adjustment to do. The machine still would not work, so he took it apart and found a broken link. He bought a new chain but could not rebuild the mower, and several pieces would not fit. He gave up, and the grass has grown wild ever since.（78 词）
