Lesson 49 · The Ideal Servant 理想的仆人

📘 New Concept English · Book 3 · Lesson 49 “The Ideal Servant”新概念英语 第三册 Lesson 49《The Ideal Servant》

B2🧠 32 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
It is just as well that the writer's aunt Harriet died years ago, for if she were alive now she could no longer hold forth on her favourite subject: servants. She lived in an unhurried age when people employed staff to run their homes, in a huge country house called The Gables, which she would not leave even after her husband died. When she was younger she gave grand parties, and however many guests came, the house was spotless: floors like mirrors, silver gleaming behind glass, and not a speck of dust on her husband's books. An unseen army of servants scrubbed and polished, but they came and went so quickly that she called them the shifting population. Her rules were advanced for the time, since none of her staff was allowed a working day over eight hours, yet she was very hard to please and searched all her life for the perfect servant. Bessie served her for three years, won her trust, ran the whole staff, worked tirelessly and cooked beautifully. Then one day the aunt came home early with guests. Dinner was poor, Bessie could not walk straight, and bringing in a huge pudding she tripped, sending it flying past her mistress onto the table. The guests had known from the start that she was drunk and had struggled not to laugh. Bessie was dismissed at once, and stacks of empty wine bottles were later found in her wardrobe, having mysteriously travelled there from the cellar. This is an ironic portrait from memory, using exaggeration and understatement to describe a difficult old lady. The grammar to learn is the unreal present with If she were alive today, air one's views, persist in, used to, no matter how, in that meaning because, so ... that split by other words, inversion after Not only was ... but, come to the conclusion, and have a difficult time doing.
哈丽雅特姑妈幸亏早就过世了——要是活到今天，她就没法再大谈最爱的话题：家里的仆人。她生活在雇仆人做家务的悠闲年代，住在一座叫「山墙庄」的大宅里，丈夫死后仍舍不得搬走。她年轻时常大宴宾客，不管来多少客人，宅子总是一尘不染：拼花地板亮得像镜子，银器在玻璃柜里闪光，连姑父满屋的书都没有灰。一支「看不见的大军」日夜擦洗，可仆人来去太快，她管他们叫「流动人口」。她的规矩在当时算开明——每天不许干超过八小时——但人极难伺候，一辈子都在找理想的仆人。贝茜在她家干了三年，深得信任，管着全部家仆，勤快能干，还是一流厨师。直到一天姑妈出门一周后突然带客人回来，贝茜做的饭大失水准，走路东倒西歪，最后端上一大盆布丁时被地毯绊倒，布丁飞过姑妈头顶砸在餐桌上。客人们早就看出她醉了，一直憋着笑。贝茜当场被辞退，后来在她的衣柜里发现一堆空酒瓶——都是从酒窖里「神秘地」跑过去的。本课是带讽刺的人物回忆，用夸张和反话写一位挑剔的老太太。学 If she were alive today（虚拟）、air one's views、persist in、used to、no matter how、in that（因为）、so … that 拆开用、Not only was … but（倒装）、come to the conclusion、have a difficult time doing。

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Unsigned in the course and probably written for it. Aunt Harriet and Bessie read as comic types rather than real people, and the house, with its parquet, silver cabinets and wine cellar, is the standard English country house of a hundred years ago.

本课未署名，大概是为教材写的。哈丽雅特姑妈和贝茜读起来像喜剧里的典型人物，而不是真人；那座有拼花地板、银器柜和酒窖的宅子，是一百年前英国乡间大宅的标准样子。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

The world the lesson looks back on was very large. Around 1900, domestic service was the biggest single source of paid work for women in Britain, employing well over a million women and girls, most of them young and many from poor rural families. Hours were long — accounts from the period describe days starting before six and ending late at night — so an aunt who limited her staff to eight hours really was unusual. The shifting population the aunt complains of was also real: servants changed places often, looking for better pay, kinder employers or a way out altogether. After the First World War, when women had worked in factories and offices, fewer were willing to go into service, and by the middle of the century the large staffed household had almost disappeared. That is the joke inside the first sentence. By the time the lesson was written, a woman who talked endlessly about her servants already belonged to a vanished age, and the writer can laugh at her because her world is gone.

课文回望的那个世界规模很大。1900 年前后，家政服务是英国女性最大的单一有薪职业，雇用的妇女和女孩远超一百万，多数很年轻，很多来自贫穷的乡村家庭。工时很长——那个时期的记述里，一天常常六点以前开始、深夜才结束——所以一位把仆人工时限在八小时的姑妈确实不寻常。姑妈抱怨的「流动人口」也是真的：仆人频繁换东家，为了更好的工钱、更和善的主人，或干脆离开这一行。第一次世界大战期间女性进了工厂和办公室，战后愿意去当仆人的少了；到二十世纪中叶，雇着一屋子仆人的大户人家几乎消失了。这正是第一句里藏着的笑点。课文写成时，一个没完没了谈论自家仆人的女人已经属于一个逝去的时代，作者之所以能笑她，正因为她的世界已经不在了。

✨ How it is written赏析

The portrait is built from praise that keeps turning into mockery. The writer never says his aunt was snobbish or difficult in plain terms until late; instead he lets her own words betray her, as in the shifting population, a phrase that turns people into a statistic. He reports her enlightened policy fairly, then attaches the clause that cancels it: nobody found her easy to satisfy. Bessie is introduced in a crescendo of praise — confidence, charge of the staff, industriousness, efficiency, an expert cook — so that the fall is as long as possible. The famous pudding scene is told in one long sentence that tracks the pudding through the air like a slow-motion shot, ending with a crash. Then comes the second joke, quieter and better: the guests had known all along. The last sentence uses mysteriously and an exclamation mark, a mock-innocent tone that lets the reader enjoy working out the obvious.

这幅人物画是用不断转成嘲讽的赞美搭起来的。作者直到很后面才直说姑妈势利难缠；在此之前，他让她自己的话出卖她，比如「流动人口」这个说法，把一个个人变成了一个统计数字。他公允地交代了她开明的规矩，随即接上一个把它抵消掉的从句：她极难伺候。贝茜出场时伴随着层层递进的赞美——信任、管家权、勤奋、高效、一流厨师——让她的跌落拉得尽可能长。那个著名的布丁场面写成一个长句，像慢镜头一样跟着布丁飞过空中，最后一声巨响。接着是第二个笑点，更安静也更妙：客人们早就知道了。最后一句用 mysteriously 加一个感叹号，装出一副无辜的口吻，让读者自己去享受推出那个显而易见的答案。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The lesson invites us to laugh at both women, and it is worth asking whether the joke is fair to Bessie. She worked for three years at a level her demanding employer could not fault, running a large staff while apparently drinking heavily in secret, and she was thrown out instantly after one bad evening. Today we would describe her behaviour as a sign of alcohol dependence rather than a little weakness, and many employers would be expected to treat it as a health problem before dismissing someone. The lesson also never asks why the aunt's servants kept leaving. Read from the servants' side, the shifting population looks less like human fickleness and more like people voting with their feet. None of this spoils the comedy, which is skilfully done. But it shows how much a story depends on whose eyes we see it through: the same evening is a farce for the guests, an embarrassment for the aunt and a disaster for Bessie.

课文邀请我们同时笑这两个女人，值得问一句：这个笑话对贝茜公平吗？她连续三年干到一个苛刻的雇主都挑不出毛病的水平，一边管着一大班仆人，一边显然在偷偷酗酒；仅仅一个晚上出了岔子，她就被当场赶走。今天我们会把她的行为看作酒精依赖的迹象，而不是一个「小毛病」；很多雇主在辞退一个人之前，会被期望先把它当作健康问题来处理。课文也从不问：姑妈的仆人为什么总是走？站在仆人那一边读，「流动人口」看起来不像人性的反复无常，倒更像人们在用脚投票。这些都不妨碍这篇喜剧写得很巧。但它显示出，一个故事有多么依赖于我们透过谁的眼睛去看：同一个晚上，对客人是一场闹剧，对姑妈是一场难堪，对贝茜则是一场灾难。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 2第 2 句 (begins开头 “If she were …”)

  • An unreal condition about the present: she is in fact dead.If she were …, she would …：与现在事实相反的虚拟（她已经死了）。
  • air one's views on: to speak one's mind about. The colon holds back the topic for a moment.air one's views on：就……发表看法。冒号后点出话题，制造一点小悬念。
  • The opening is mock-cruel: the writer means only that she was spared a world without servants.第一句说「幸亏姑妈早死了」，是幽默的反话：作者不是盼她死，而是说「幸亏她看不到今天没有仆人的世界」。

Sentence 6第 6 句 (begins开头 “for even though …”)

  • persist in doing: to keep on doing, with a hint of stubbornness.persist in doing：坚持做（带一点固执的意味）。
  • even though is a stronger though.even though：尽管（比 though 语气强）。

Sentence 9第 9 句 (begins开头 “No matter how …”)

  • no matter how many means however many.no matter how many …：不管有多少……，= however many。

Sentence 17第 17 句 (begins开头 “in that she …”)

  • what was ... an enlightened policy is a noun clause, with in those days dropped in.what was … an enlightened policy：what 引导宾语从句，in those days 插入。
  • in that means because, or in the sense that, explaining why the policy was enlightened.in that：因为，在于（解释为什么说它开明）。
  • difficult to please: the infinitive is active in form but passive in meaning, she was hard to satisfy.difficult to please：难以讨好，不定式主动形式表被动意义。

Sentence 22第 22 句 (begins开头 “During that time …”)

  • so modifies the verb here and comes before it: she won the aunt's trust to such a degree that ...so … that：这里 so 修饰动词 gained，放在动词前，意思是「如此赢得……以至于……」。
  • be put in charge of: to be given control of.be put in charge of：被派负责。

Sentence 28第 28 句 (begins开头 “Not only was …”)

  • When not only starts the sentence, the verb comes before the subject.Not only 放句首，后面部分倒装（was the meal）。
  • well below: far below, with well for emphasis.well below：远远低于。well 加强 below。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

The unreal present: if plus a past form, then would do与现在事实相反的虚拟：If + 过去式, would do
When you suppose something contrary to present fact, the if clause takes the past simple, with were for every person of be, and the main clause takes would, could or might with the plain verb.
假设与现在事实相反时，if 从句用一般过去时（be 一律用 were），主句用 would/could/might + 动词原形。

Sentence 2 uses it, because the aunt is dead.

本课：第 2 句 If she were alive …, she would not …（姑妈早已过世）。

  • If my grandfather were here, he would love this garden.
  • If we had a car, we could visit the lake more often.
Inversion after not only at the startNot only … but … 放句首要倒装
When not only starts the sentence, the clause right after it is inverted, with the auxiliary or be before the subject; the clause after but, or but also, keeps normal order.
Not only 放在句首时，紧跟的分句主谓倒装（助动词/系动词提前）；but (also) 后面的分句不倒装。

Sentence 28 uses the inverted form; compare Lesson 10, where not only was in mid-sentence and no inversion was needed.

本课：第 28 句 Not only was the meal …, but Bessie seemed …。对比 L10 的 not only 不在句首就不倒装。

  • Not only did he forget her birthday, but he also lost her present.
  • Not only is the hotel cheap, but it is also near the beach.
Adjective plus infinitive with a passive meaning形容词 + 不定式，主动形式表被动
With easy, difficult, hard, impossible or pleasant followed by an infinitive, the subject of the sentence is really the object of the infinitive: she is difficult to please means it is difficult to please her. The infinitive stays active.
easy / difficult / hard / impossible / pleasant + to do，句子主语其实是不定式的宾语：She is difficult to please.＝It is difficult to please her. 不定式不用被动。

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • It is a good thing … 幸亏……
    It is a good thing we left early; the roads are already full.
  • No matter how many …, X was always … 不管有多少……，X 总是……
    No matter how many times I call, the line is always busy.
  • X never even got the chance to … X 甚至没机会……
    We moved so often that I never even got the chance to make friends.
  • X could not find enough words to praise … X 对……赞不绝口
    The teacher could not find enough words to praise her essay.
  • X came, reluctantly, to the conclusion that … X 不情愿地得出结论……
    He came, reluctantly, to the conclusion that the car was beyond repair.

For three years Bessie was aunt Harriet's perfect servant: hard-working, efficient, a fine cook and head of the household staff. Then the aunt came home early one afternoon with guests.
