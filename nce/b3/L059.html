Lesson 59 · Collecting 收藏

📘 New Concept English · Book 3 · Lesson 59 “Collecting”新概念英语 第三册 Lesson 59《Collecting》

B2🧠 27 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
People tend to pile up possessions, sometimes without noticing, and can be pleasantly surprised to find something useful they had forgotten they owned. Those who never move house gather what can only be called clutter, leaving unwanted things in drawers and lofts for years in case they need them one day. As they age, people keep even more, for two further reasons: they lack the physical and mental energy needed to clear things out, and they are sentimental, since old objects carry memories, often of relatives who have died, and so become worth more to them than their real value. Some things are saved on purpose to avoid waste, such as the string and brown paper that careful people keep from parcels. Collecting small things can turn into an obsession: the writer knows a woman who cuts out newspaper pictures of fashionable clothes she cannot afford, and cannot make herself stop, though her desk overflows with paper. Treated as a serious hobby, collecting is another matter and brings many benefits. It relaxes you, since just looking at your treasures is a pleasure; you need not go out for entertainment; and whatever you collect, from stamps and records to old furniture or model cars, you never run out of tasks, from finding a place for a new piece to checking facts in reference books. You learn about your subject and much around it. You meet people with the same interest, swap advice and items, and make friends; the hobby leads to travel, even abroad, in search of rare pieces. In time you may become an expert, asked to speak to small groups and then larger ones, and your confidence grows. By filling spare time so usefully, collecting makes a person content and leaves no room for boredom. This is exposition built on a contrast: first careless hoarding, then deliberate collecting, with the second half listing the benefits of the hobby. The grammar to learn is tend to, without being aware of, in the belief that, two other reasons ... both of which, acquire a value beyond, in an attempt to, to such an extent that, have some bearing on, may well, and the role of a long -ing phrase as subject.
人总爱攒东西，有时自己都没察觉；偶尔翻出一件自己都不知道有的有用东西，还会惊喜一番。从不搬家的人会不加挑选地攒下一堆只能叫「杂物」的东西：没用的东西放在抽屉、柜子、阁楼里一放多年，总想着哪天正好用得上。人老了还因为两个原因越攒越多：没精力也没心力去清理扔掉；以及感情——用久了的东西连着过去、连着已故的亲人，慢慢有了超出本身的价值。有些东西是为了不浪费而有意留的，比如节俭的人拆包裹后留下的绳子和牛皮纸。收集小东西很容易成癖：作者认识一个人，总从报纸上剪下她买不起的时装图样，明知几乎不可能买，却没毅力停下，桌子塞得一打开碎纸就四处飞。认真的收藏爱好则完全不同、好处很多：闲时放松，光看看宝贝就开心；不必外出找乐子；不管收的是邮票、唱片、初版书、瓷器、古董家具还是标本，总有事可做，从给新藏品找位置到查参考书核事实；它不但让人精通所选领域，还学到相关的知识。还能认识同好，交换心得和藏品，朋友圈子扩大；还会带人出门、甚至出国寻宝；多年后可能成为行家，被请去给小聚会、再给更多听众讲，自信心随之增长。收藏这样建设性地占用空闲时间，使人知足、没空无聊。本课是对比式说明文：先写无意识地「囤」，再写有意识地「藏」，后半列出收藏的种种好处。学 tend to、without being aware of、in the belief that、two other reasons … both of which、acquire a value beyond、in an attempt to、such an extent that、have some bearing on、may well、by doing so … that。

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander as a reflective essay in the plain English tradition; it draws on everyday observation rather than on any one source.

课文为编者 L. G. 亚历山大自撰的随笔，属英式平实文风；它取材于日常观察，而不是某一个出处。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

The essay was written decades before hoarding became a medical term. Since 2013 the American Psychiatric Association's manual has listed hoarding disorder as a condition of its own: persistent difficulty in parting with possessions, whatever their value, to the point where living spaces can no longer be used as intended. The lesson's quiet distinction between clutter and collecting is close to how clinicians now separate the two: collectors choose, arrange and display, and their collections usually bring pleasure; people with hoarding problems keep without order and feel distress at the thought of letting go. Economists have studied a milder version of the same pull. In well-known experiments, people asked to sell a mug they had just been given wanted roughly twice as much as others were willing to pay for the same mug, an effect known as the endowment effect, which helps explain why the old string and brown paper are so hard to throw out.

这篇随笔写成时，「囤积」还不是一个医学术语，比那早几十年。自 2013 年起，美国精神医学学会的诊断手册把「囤积障碍」列为独立的疾病：无论物品价值如何，长期难以舍弃，严重到生活空间无法正常使用。课文对「杂物」和「收藏」那种不动声色的区分，和今天临床上的区分很接近：收藏者有选择、有整理、会展示，收藏通常带来快乐；有囤积问题的人则毫无秩序地留着东西，一想到要丢就痛苦。经济学家研究过同一股力量的温和版本：在著名的实验里，刚拿到一只杯子的人要价大约是别人愿意出价的两倍，这叫「禀赋效应」，它能部分解释为什么那些旧绳子和牛皮纸那么难扔。

✨ How it is written赏析

The essay is built like a slope: it starts with the most aimless kind of keeping and climbs towards the most rewarding. Each paragraph moves one step up, from accident, to habit, to thrift, to mania, and finally to a hobby that changes a life. The writer's tone shifts as he climbs. In the first half he is amused and a little teasing, with his dry label for all that useless stuff; in the second he becomes warm and almost persuasive, listing benefits in long, generous sentences. The single concrete example, the woman with her newspaper cuttings, sits at the turning point, and it is both funny and sad, since she collects pictures of a life she cannot have. The final sentence draws all the threads together, but in the voice of a gentle adviser rather than a preacher.

文章像一道斜坡：从最漫无目的的「留东西」开始，往上爬向最有回报的那一种。每一段往上走一步——从偶然、到习惯、到节俭、到癖好，最后到一种能改变生活的爱好。作者的语气也随之变化。前半段他觉得好笑，带点调侃，像「只能称为杂物的东西」这样的说法；后半段变得温暖、几乎是在劝说，用长而慷慨的句子罗列好处。唯一一个具体例子——那位剪报的女士——正好处在转折点上，既好笑又令人伤感：她收集的是一种她过不上的生活的图片。最后一句把所有线索收在一起，用的是温和的建议者而不是说教者的口吻。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The lesson's praise of collecting is attractive, but today it needs one more step. Many people in rich countries now own far more than their grandparents did, and the most popular advice of the past decade, from tidying methods to minimalism, pushes the other way: keep only what you use or love. The two ideas can be joined. Collecting works when it is chosen and limited; clutter grows when nothing is chosen at all. The social benefits the writer lists have also moved online, where collectors of stamps or model cars now meet in forums and video channels long before they meet at a club. For many Chinese families the pull of keeping things is strong for a reason the lesson does not mention: parents and grandparents who lived through hard times learned never to throw away anything that might be useful, and the habit often outlives the need.

课文对收藏的赞美很动人，但今天还需要再往前走一步。如今富裕国家的许多人拥有的东西远多于祖父母那一代，过去十年最流行的建议——从整理方法到极简主义——都在往反方向推：只留你用得上或真心喜欢的东西。两种想法可以合在一起：收藏之所以好，在于它有选择、有限度；什么都不选，杂物就会长起来。作者列出的社交好处也搬到了网上：集邮或收集模型车的人，早在去俱乐部之前，就在论坛和视频频道里认识了。对许多中国家庭来说，留东西的拉力很强，原因课文没提到：经历过困难年代的父母和祖父母学会了绝不扔掉任何可能有用的东西，而这个习惯常常比需要活得更久。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

Prepositions followed by -ing forms介词 + 动名词
After a preposition, a verb takes the -ing form: without doing, in doing, by doing, of doing, from doing to doing. Common patterns include be aware of doing, be essential in doing and by doing.
介词后的动词用 -ing 形式：without doing、in doing、by doing、of doing、from … to doing。常见搭配：be aware of doing、be essential in doing、in connection with、by doing。

Sentences 1, 8, 24 and 34 all put an -ing form after a preposition.

本课：第 1 句 without being aware；第 6 句 essential in turning out；第 22 句 from finding … to verifying；第 32 句 by occupying。

  • She left without saying goodbye.
  • He is good at fixing bikes.
  • You can save money by cooking at home.
both of which, some of whom: non-defining clauses非限制性定语从句：both of which / some of whom
A pronoun or quantity word plus of plus which or whom introduces a non-defining clause after a comma: both of which, all of whom, most of which, none of which. Use which for things and whom for people.
代词/数量词 + of + which/whom 引导非限制性定语从句，前有逗号：both of which、all of whom、most of which、none of which。指物用 which，指人用 whom。

Sentence 6 uses both of which for the two kinds of energy just named.

本课：第 6 句用 both of which 指前面提到的两种精力。

  • He has two sisters, both of whom are doctors.
  • I bought ten apples, most of which were bad.
Degree and result: to such an extent that程度与结果：to such an extent that / so … that / such … that
to such an extent that means to such a degree that, with a result; it is more formal than so ... that and can come after a verb to describe the whole action.
to such an extent that＝到……的程度以至于；比 so … that 更正式、可放在动词后修饰整个动作。

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • People tend to …, sometimes without realizing it. 人们往往……，有时自己都没察觉
    People tend to eat more when they are tired, sometimes without realizing it.
  • … gradually become worth more than … ……慢慢变得比……更有价值
    Old photographs gradually become worth more than any jewellery.
  • The chances that … are very small. ……的可能性极小
    The chances that we will find the ring are very small.
  • … is another matter and brings many rewards. ……完全不同，好处很多
    Swimming for fitness is another matter and brings many rewards.
  • In this way … grows, first from …, then from … 这样……就增长起来，先是因为……，再是因为……
    In this way trust grows, first from small promises, then from big ones.

Summary writing摘要写作

In no more than 80 words, say why people pile up possessions and what the benefits of serious collecting are.

用不超过 80 个词说明人们为什么会囤积东西，以及认真收藏有哪些好处。

Points a good summary covers好摘要要覆盖的要点
  • People often pile things up without noticing, thinking they may need them; in old age they lack the energy to clear out and cling to things with memories.人们常常不知不觉地囤东西，总觉得哪天用得着；老了缺精力清理，又舍不得有纪念意义的东西。
  • Some keep things to avoid waste, and for some, gathering small items becomes an obsession.有人为了节约而留东西，有人把收集小东西变成了癖好。
  • Serious collecting relaxes people, gives them pleasure at home and teaches them a great deal.认真的收藏爱好能让人放松、在家就有乐趣、学到知识。
  • It also brings friends and travel, and in time can make one an expert who gives talks, building confidence and keeping boredom away.它还能结交同好、带人出行，多年后可能成为行家并去讲座，增强自信、远离无聊。
Our model summary本站范文

Many people collect clutter without meaning to, keeping things in case they are needed, and older people hold on to objects for lack of energy or for sentimental reasons. Some save things to avoid waste, and some small habits grow into obsessions. Serious collecting is different. It relaxes you, keeps you busy at home and teaches you about your subject. It also brings friends, travel and, in time, expert knowledge and confidence, leaving no room for boredom.（77 词）
