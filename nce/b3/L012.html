Lesson 12 · Life on a Desert Island 荒岛生活

📘 New Concept English · Book 3 · Lesson 12 “Life on a Desert Island”新概念英语 第三册 Lesson 12《Life on a Desert Island》

B2🧠 20 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
Say 'desert island' and two pictures come to mind. One is a paradise of endless sunshine, where fruit drops ready-ripe into your hand and nobody has to work. The other is misery: you either starve, or you sit watching, Crusoe-like, for a ship that never comes. The writer allows each picture a grain of truth, then tells a true story. Two men take a badly holed boat towards Miami to get it mended; the boat goes down under them; they paddle a rubber dinghy to a speck of coral, catch rain in the dinghy, spear lobster and fish, eat (in one man's words) like kings — and are genuinely sorry to go when a tanker takes them off five days later. The shape of the lesson is a claim first, then a case. Learn imagine ... to be, either ... or, wish + had done, have something done, and prove (to be).
说到荒岛，人们脑子里有两幅画：一幅是阳光永远灿烂、熟果自己掉下来的天堂；另一幅是要么饿死、要么像鲁滨孙一样苦等永远不来的船。作者说两幅都有点道理，然后讲了个真事：两个人驾一条破船去迈阿密修，船中途下沉，他们划橡皮艇到一个珊瑚小岛，接雨水、用鱼叉枪捕龙虾和鱼，「吃得像国王」，五天后被油轮救起时竟然舍不得走。本课是先立论再举例的议论文结构。学 imagine … to be、either … or、wish + had done、have sth done 和 prove (to be)。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. What two pictures do people carry about living alone on an island?人们对荒岛生活有哪两种想象？
  2. How did the two men end up on that coral island?两个人为什么会到了那个珊瑚岛上？
  3. With no fresh water and almost no trees, how did they live so well?岛上没有水也没什么树，他们是怎么过得那么好的？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander. The second half reads like a newspaper item of the period — two men taken off a Caribbean islet after five days — but we could not trace the report, so the news source is unverified; the two pictures the writer sets up do have traceable histories, below.

课文由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为教材自撰。后半段读起来像当年的一条新闻——两个人在加勒比海小岛上待了五天后被救起——但我们没有找到那条报道，新闻出处未核；而作者摆出的那两幅画，来历是查得到的，见下文。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

Both pictures in this lesson have a history. The grim one descends from Defoe's novel of 1719, and the novel in turn borrows from Alexander Selkirk, a Scottish sailing master put ashore on an uninhabited island in the Juan Fernandez group off Chile in 1704 and taken off again in February 1709 — four years and four months on his own. Chile leaned into the legend in 1966, renaming Selkirk's island Robinson Crusoe Island and giving his name to the next island along, where he had never set foot. The cheerful picture is much younger: it belongs to the age of air travel and holiday brochures, which turned the uninhabited tropical island from a sentence into a product. The real episode sits between the two. The men were on a long ocean passage, not a coastal hop, and what saved them was ordinary seamanship: they abandoned ship with food and matches, kept the dinghy, and used it as a rain catcher — the drill that survival manuals for life rafts still teach. Reef water in the Caribbean holds spiny lobster in easy reach of a swimmer with a spear gun, so protein was the least of their problems. Water was the real constraint, and rain solved it.

本课的两幅画都有来历。阴惨的那幅出自笛福 1719 年的小说，而小说又取材于亚历山大·塞尔柯克——一位苏格兰航海长，1704 年被放到智利外海胡安·费尔南德斯群岛的一座无人岛上，1709 年 2 月才被接走，独自生活了四年四个月。1966 年智利索性把传说坐实：把塞尔柯克待过的那座岛改名「鲁滨孙·克鲁索岛」，又把他从未踏足的邻岛命名为「塞尔柯克岛」。欢快的那幅画年轻得多，属于航空旅行和度假手册的时代——无人荒岛从一种判决变成了一件商品。真实的那件事落在两者之间。两人走的是远洋航线，不是沿岸短途；救他们的是普通的航海常识：弃船时带上食物和火柴，留住橡皮艇，再把它当成接雨的容器——这正是今天救生筏手册仍在教的动作。加勒比海的礁区里，带鱼叉枪的人游几下就能摸到龙虾，蛋白质根本不成问题。真正的制约是淡水，而雨水解决了它。

✨ How it is written赏析

Alexander argues here, and the method is worth stealing. He does not begin with a thesis; he begins with two stereotypes, drawn in three or four short sentences each, deliberately overdrawn so that the reader recognises them and smiles. Then one sentence concedes something to each side and immediately admits the weakness of the whole debate: almost nobody arguing about this has ever been there. That concession is what earns him the right to the second half, where a single case does all the work of proof. Notice the tenses: the pictures are in the timeless present, the case in a plain past, and the switch tells you which part is talk and which part is evidence. Notice too how little the writer comments at the end. He could have written a paragraph on what the episode proves; instead he gives two facts — a quoted phrase from one of the men, and the men's reluctance to leave — and lets them land. When you write an argument in English, try this frame: two opposed pictures, a concession, one real case, no summing-up.

亚历山大在这里是在论证，这套手法值得偷。他不先抛论点，而是先画两幅刻板印象，各用三四个短句，故意画得过了头，让读者一眼认出来、会心一笑。接着一句话各让一步，并立刻承认整场争论的软肋：争这件事的人几乎没有谁真去过。正是这一让，换来了他写后半段的资格——那里只用一个案例，就把论证做完了。注意时态：画用的是没有时间的一般现在时，案例用的是朴素的过去时，时态本身在告诉你哪一半是空谈、哪一半是证据。也注意他在结尾几乎不作评论。他本可以写一段「这件事说明了什么」，却只给了两个事实：其中一人说的那句话，和两人不愿离开——然后让事实自己落地。你用英文写议论时可以照这个框架：两幅对立的画，一处让步，一个真实案例，不作总结。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The lesson's own conclusion is modest and has held up: both stereotypes are wrong, and comfort on a small island turns on water, food within reach, and knowing you will be found. Where the case is weaker than it looks is the length. Five days is a holiday; Selkirk had four years, and the fictional castaway twenty-eight, and what breaks people over those spans is not hunger but isolation and the loss of anything to aim at. The modern footnote is the ending. Two men adrift for five days before a passing ship happens to see them is a story of the era before cheap distress beacons; a small boat on that passage today would normally carry one, and the five days would more likely have been five hours — which is a gain in safety and a loss to the anecdote. For Chinese readers the castaway has his own history. Defoe's book reached China early: a first partial version by Shen Zufen in 1902, then the famous rendering by Lin Shu and Zeng Zonggong for the Commercial Press around 1905, with the sequel following. Lin Shu's preface did something telling — it read the castaway through the doctrine of the mean, presenting self-reliance not as adventure but as a moral middle path, which is some distance from the way British readers of the day took the same book.

课文自己的结论很克制，而且站住了：两种刻板印象都不对；在小岛上过得舒不舒服，取决于水、伸手可得的食物，以及知不知道有人会来找你。案例比看上去弱的地方在时长。五天是度假；塞尔柯克待了四年，小说里的那位待了二十八年，而在这种长度上压垮人的不是饥饿，是孤绝和无事可指望。现代的脚注在结尾。两个人漂了五天，等一条路过的船偶然看见——这是廉价遇险信标出现之前的故事；今天走同一条航线的小船通常会带一个，五天大概会变成五小时：安全上是进步，故事上是损失。对中国读者来说，这个漂流者另有一段自己的历史。笛福这本书进中国很早：1902 年沈祖芬先出了节译，1905 年前后林纾与曾宗巩为商务印书馆译出那个著名的版本，续集随后也出了。林纾的序做了一件很说明问题的事——他用「中庸」去读这个漂流者，把自立讲成一条道德中道、而不是冒险故事，这和当时英国读者读同一本书的方式隔着不小的距离。

💬 Discuss and write讨论与写作
  1. The two men were sorry to leave after five days. Would you still be sorry after five weeks? Say what would run out first for you — food, quiet, or something to do.
    两个人五天后舍不得走。如果是五周，你还会舍不得吗？说说对你来说最先耗尽的会是什么——食物、安静，还是可做的事。
  2. Write 120 words about a time when something you had dreaded turned out to be far better, or far worse, than the picture in your head. Use wish + had done once and prove to be once.
    写 120 词，讲一件你原本害怕、结果却比想象中好得多或坏得多的事。用一次 wish + had done，用一次 prove to be。
  3. Lin Shu read the castaway as a lesson in moderation; British readers of 1719 read him as an enterprising colonist. Can one story carry both readings honestly, or does each country simply take what it needs?
    林纾把这个漂流者读成一堂中庸的课，1719 年的英国读者把他读成一个有闯劲的殖民者。同一个故事能诚实地承载两种读法吗，还是每个国家只是各取所需？

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 2第 2 句 (begins开头 “We sometimes imagine …”)

  • imagine sth to be ...: picture something as being ... (imagine sth as ... also works).imagine sth to be…：把……想象成……（也可 imagine sth as…）。
  • where ...: a relative clause describing paradise, a place.where…：定语从句修饰 paradise（表地点）。

Sentence 7第 7 句 (begins开头 “You either starve …”)

  • either ... or ...: one or the other; here it joins two verb phrases.either … or …：要么……要么……，连接两个谓语。
  • starve to death: die for lack of food. waiting ...: a present participle for what is going on at the same time.starve to death：饿死。waiting…：现在分词作伴随状语。
  • Robinson Crusoe: the hero of Defoe's novel of 1719, cast away for twenty-eight years.Robinson Crusoe：《鲁滨孙漂流记》主人公，流落荒岛 28 年。

Sentence 8第 8 句 (begins开头 “Perhaps there is …”)

  • few carries a negative sense, almost none; have had the opportunity to do: have been in a position to.few（几乎没有）带否定意味；have had the opportunity to do：有过机会做。

Sentence 9第 9 句 (begins开头 “Two men who …”)

  • Backbone: Two men ... wished ...; the who clause describes the men.主干：Two men … wished…；who…island 是定语从句。
  • wish + had done: regret about the past — they really would have liked to stay longer.wish + had done：对过去的遗憾（真希望当时待得更久）。

Sentence 10第 10 句 (begins开头 “They were taking …”)

  • have sth done: get somebody else to do it; the boat is being taken away to be mended, not mended by them.have sth done：请别人做（把船送去让人修），不是自己修。
  • to have ...: an infinitive of purpose.to have…：不定式表目的。

Sentence 13第 13 句 (begins开头 “There were hardly …”)

  • hardly any = almost no.hardly any = almost no：几乎没有。
  • prove to be: turn out to be; prove is a linking verb here.prove to be：结果是（系动词用法）。

Sentence 16第 16 句 (begins开头 “They caught lobster …”)

  • as sb put it: in somebody's own words (put = express).as sb put it：正如某人所说（put = express）。
  • ate like kings: ate extremely well.ate like kings：吃得像国王一样（好极了）。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

The subjunctive after wish: about now, and about the pastwish 后的虚拟：对现在与对过去
wish + past tense (were for be) = you want things to be different now; wish + had done = you regret the past; wish + would do = you want somebody or something to change.
wish + 过去式（be 用 were）＝希望现在不是这样；wish + had done ＝遗憾过去没有那样；wish + would do ＝希望别人/情况改变。

In this lesson, sentence 9: the two men wished they had stayed longer — regret about the past.

本课：第 9 句，两个人 wished they had stayed 更久（对过去的遗憾）。

  • I wish I were taller.（现在）
  • I wish I had studied harder for the test.（过去）
  • I wish it would stop raining.（希望改变）
have sth done: get it done by somebody else, or have it happen to youhave sth done：请人做／使……被做
have + object + past participle: the work is done by somebody else (a haircut, a car repair); it can also describe something that happens to you (have one's bag stolen). get sth done means much the same and is more colloquial.
have + 宾语 + 过去分词，动作由别人完成（理发、修车）；也可表示遭遇（have one's bag stolen）。get sth done 意思相近，更口语。

In this lesson, sentence 10: they are taking the damaged boat away to have it repaired.

本课：第 10 句他们开船去迈阿密 to have it repaired。

  • I had my hair cut yesterday.
  • We are having the kitchen painted.
  • She had her phone stolen on the bus.
either … or / neither … nor / both … and
either A or B: one of the two; neither A nor B: not one and not the other; both A and B: the two together. When one of these is the subject, the verb usually agrees with the nearer noun.
either A or B 二选一；neither A nor B 两者都不；both A and B 两者都。作主语时 either/neither 结构的谓语一般跟靠近的那个主语一致。

In this lesson, sentence 7: on a desert island you either starve or keep watch like Crusoe.

本课：第 7 句：在荒岛上你 either 饿死 or 像鲁滨孙那样苦等。

  • You can either stay here or come with us.
  • Neither my brother nor I am good at maths.

  • rescued by a tanker after five days; sorry to leave

Summary writing摘要写作

In no more than 80 words, tell what happened to the two men on the coral island.

用不超过 80 个词讲述这两个人在珊瑚岛上的经历。

Points a good summary covers好摘要要覆盖的要点
  • Two men were taking a badly holed boat out of the Virgin Islands, bound for Miami and a repair yard, when it began to go down.两个人驾一条破船从维尔京群岛去迈阿密修理，途中船开始下沉。
  • They put food, matches and beer into a rubber dinghy and paddled a few miles to a tiny coral island.他们把食物、火柴和啤酒装上橡皮艇，划了几英里到了一个小珊瑚岛。
  • The island had almost no trees and no fresh water; they caught rain in the dinghy and speared lobster and fish daily.岛上几乎没树也没水，他们用橡皮艇接雨水，用鱼叉枪每天捕龙虾和鱼。
  • A passing tanker picked them up five days later, and both men were truly sorry to go.五天后一艘路过的油轮救了他们，两人都真心舍不得离开。
Our model summary本站范文

Two men were taking a broken boat to Miami for repairs when it started to sink. They filled a small rubber boat with food, matches and beer and rowed to a tiny coral island. There was almost nothing there, not even fresh water, but they caught rain in the boat and speared lobster and fish every day. When a tanker picked them up five days later, they were truly sad to leave.（72 词）
