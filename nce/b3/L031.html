Lesson 31 · A Lovable Eccentric 可爱的怪人

📘 New Concept English · Book 3 · Lesson 31 “A Lovable Eccentric”新概念英语 第三册 Lesson 31《A Lovable Eccentric》

B2🧠 24 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
A rich businessman called Dickie is known all over his small town for his odd ways: he owns a car and walks everywhere, and he will not carry an umbrella in a downpour. Soaked through one day, he walks into a smart jeweller's to buy his wife a three-hundred-pound watch, and an assistant, judging by his looks, will not serve him. Without a word he goes out, comes back with a huge sack of coins, and in front of the manager makes the young man count every one. He also spends four years painting a set of 'masterpieces' himself and invites the critics to admire them, just to show that they can be fooled. This is a character essay that states a claim and then gives examples: three opening sentences define the true eccentric, and two stories serve as the evidence. The grammar to learn is after having been done, insist on someone's doing, such ... that, and It took someone ... to do.
小城里有位富商叫迪基，人人知道他古怪：有车不坐，下大雨也不打伞。一次他浑身湿透走进高档表店要给太太买块 300 英镑的表，店员看他狼狈不肯接待；他一声不吭出门，扛回一大袋硬币，当着经理的面让那位店员一枚一枚数完。他还花四年自己画了一批「名家作品」请评论家来看，只为证明评论家未必懂行。本课是先立论、后举例的人物随笔：开头三句定义「真正的怪人」，后面两个故事作证据。学 after having been done、insist on sb's doing、such … that 和 It took sb … to do。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. According to the writer, what marks out a genuine eccentric?作者心目中「真正的怪人」有什么特点？
  2. Why did Dickie pay for the watch in coins?迪基为什么要用硬币付那块表的钱？
  3. What did the exhibition of paintings prove?那场画展证明了什么？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Book Three texts were written for the course by L. G. Alexander, so Dickie Colson and his town are invented. The two stunts in the story, though, have real cousins, and one of them is among the most famous art frauds of the twentieth century.

第三册课文由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为教材自撰，迪基·科尔森和他那座小城都是编的。但故事里的两个把戏在现实里都有亲戚，其中一个是二十世纪最出名的艺术骗局之一。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

Look first at the coins. Britain switched to decimal money on the fifteenth of February 1971; before that a pound was two hundred and forty pennies, so the same three hundred pounds in old pennies would have come to seventy-two thousand coins, not thirty thousand. The figure in the edition you are reading belongs to decimal reckoning. A modern British penny weighs about three and a half grams, so thirty thousand of them come to something over a hundred kilograms — far more than one man could carry into a shop, and the lesson's 'extremely heavy' is an understatement. The second stunt is closer to history. In the 1930s the Dutch painter Han van Meegeren produced pictures in the style of Vermeer that leading experts accepted as newly discovered works, and a Rotterdam museum bought one. He was exposed only after the Second World War, when he was charged with selling a national treasure to the Nazi leader Hermann Göring and saved himself by proving that he had painted it. Chinese readers have their own version: the painter Zhang Daqian is widely reported to have made paintings in the manner of the early Qing master Shitao that fooled collectors and connoisseurs of his day.

先看那袋硬币。英国 1971 年 2 月 15 日改行十进制货币；在那之前一英镑是 240 便士，同样的 300 英镑若换成旧便士是七万二千枚，不是三万枚。你手里这个版本的数字属于十进制的算法。现在的英国一便士约重 3.5 克，三万枚就是一百多公斤——一个人根本扛不进店里，课文那句「非常重」其实说轻了。第二个把戏更接近真实的历史。二十世纪三十年代，荷兰画家汉·范·米格伦仿维米尔的风格作画，顶尖专家把它们当作新发现的真迹，鹿特丹一家博物馆还买下了一幅。直到二战结束他才被揭穿：他被控把「国宝」卖给纳粹头目戈林，为了脱罪，他证明那幅画是自己画的。中国读者也有自己的版本：画家张大千仿清初石涛作画，据说骗过了当时不少收藏家与鉴赏家。

✨ How it is written赏析

The essay is built like an argument in miniature. Three general sentences come first and define the type; then a single named man is brought in as the proof, and the writer moves from 'we' and 'others' to one town and one face. The definition does real work later. Because we have been told that true eccentrics do not set out to be noticed, the shop story reads not as a man showing off but as a man quietly correcting a snob, and the silence in it matters: he says nothing in the shop, nothing on the way back, and lets the bag and the manager do the talking. The punch line is a number with an exclamation mark, the only one in the lesson. The second story is told much faster and ends with an explicit moral, which is the essay's way of closing the loop it opened at the start: the eccentric adds colour to dull lives, and here he adds it at the critics' expense.

这篇随笔是一场小型论证。先来三句概括、给这类人下定义；然后拉进一个有名有姓的人作证据，作者的视角从「我们」「别人」收到一座小城、一张脸上。那个定义在后面真起了作用：既然我们已经被告知真怪人不是存心惹人注意，表店的故事读起来就不是在炫耀，而是在不声不响地教训一个势利眼——其中的沉默很要紧：他在店里一言不发，回来的路上也一言不发，让那袋钱和那位经理替他说话。笑点是一个带感叹号的数字，全课只有这一个感叹号。第二个故事讲得快得多，结尾把寓意直接说出来，这是随笔在收它开头张开的那个圈：怪人给平淡的生活增色，这一回增色的代价由评论家来付。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

Two things in the story are worth questioning today. The first is the shop scene, which readers usually enjoy as justice: the snob is punished. But look at who pays. The assistant is the lowest person in the shop, the manager who scolds him is fawning to a rich man he has recognized, and the lesson the young man learns is not 'treat everyone with respect' but 'find out who is rich before you are rude'. Dickie's revenge confirms the rule it seems to mock. The second is the art joke. Van Meegeren's case shows what really happens when experts are fooled: the forger became something of a folk hero, but the deeper damage was to trust in expert judgement, and museums had to re-examine other works. Proving that critics can be wrong is easy; the harder and more useful question is what they should do differently afterwards, and the lesson stops before it.

这个故事有两处今天值得追问。第一是表店那一幕，读者通常当作「恶有恶报」来看：势利眼受了罚。可是看看谁在买单：店员是店里地位最低的人，训他的经理是在对一个刚认出来的有钱人赔笑；年轻人学到的不是「对谁都要尊重」，而是「凶人之前先打听清楚对方有没有钱」。迪基的报复恰恰坐实了它看似在嘲讽的那条规矩。第二是那个艺术玩笑。范·米格伦一案显示，专家真被骗了之后会怎样：造假者几乎成了民间英雄，但更深的损伤是人们对专家判断的信任，博物馆不得不回头重查别的藏品。证明评论家会看走眼很容易；更难也更有用的问题是：他们事后该怎么改——课文在这里停下了。

💬 Discuss and write讨论与写作
  1. Is it fair that the shop assistant was punished while the manager, who also judged by appearances, was not?
    店员受了罚，而同样以貌取人的经理没事——这公平吗？
  2. Describe someone you know who ignores a social rule without seeming to notice. Do people admire or dislike them, and why?
    写一个你认识的人：他无视某条社会规矩、自己却好像没察觉。别人是欣赏他还是讨厌他？为什么？
  3. If experts can be fooled by a clever fake, what should an ordinary buyer rely on instead?
    如果专家都会被高明的赝品骗过，普通买家又该依靠什么？

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 1第 1 句 (begins开头 “True eccentrics never …”)

  • set out to do: to begin with the clear intention of doing something.set out to do：着手、有意要做某事（强调动机）。
  • draw attention to oneself: to make others notice you. This is the essay's definition: a real eccentric is not putting on a show.draw attention to oneself：引人注意。这句是全文的定义：真怪人不是「装怪」。

Sentence 2第 2 句 (begins开头 “They disregard social …”)

  • without plus a gerund means 'and are not aware that'.without + 动名词（being conscious）：「而没有意识到」。
  • The adjective follows anything, as it does with something and nothing.anything extraordinary：形容词放在 anything 后面。

Sentence 3第 3 句 (begins开头 “This invariably wins …”)

  • win someone something: to gain it for them, with two objects.win sb sth：使某人赢得某物（双宾语）。
  • add colour to: to make something livelier. for gives the reason.add colour to：给……增添色彩。for 引出理由。

Sentence 9第 9 句 (begins开头 “Though he owned …”)

  • hardly ever: almost never.hardly ever：几乎从不。
  • The -ing phrase explains the reason behind what has just been said.preferring …：现在分词作伴随/说明，补充他为什么不用车。

Sentence 11第 11 句 (begins开头 “One day, he …”)

  • after having been done: a perfect passive gerund after a preposition, meaning 'once this had happened to him'. after being caught means the same.after having been done：介词后接完成式被动动名词，表示「在被……之后」。也可简化为 after being caught。
  • be caught in a shower: to be out when sudden rain falls.be caught in a shower：遇上阵雨。

Sentence 21第 21 句 (begins开头 “He insisted on …”)

  • insist on someone's doing: to demand that they do it. The possessive is the doer of the gerund; in speech people often drop the 's.insist on sb's doing：坚持要某人做；sb's 是动名词的逻辑主语（口语也说 insist on the assistant counting）。
  • in all: altogether.in all：总共。

Sentence 26第 26 句 (begins开头 “It took him …”)

  • It took someone a length of time to do something: the usual way to say how long a task lasted.It took sb + 时间 + to do：某人花了多少时间做某事。
  • simply to prove: the infinitive gives the purpose, and simply stresses that this was the only reason.simply to prove：「只是为了证明」，不定式表目的。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

A preposition with a perfect gerund: after having been done介词 + 动名词完成式（被动）：after having been done
The perfect gerund having done stresses that one action came before another; the passive form having been done says that something first happened to the subject. It can follow prepositions such as after, on, for and without. Because after already shows the order, the simple forms being done or doing are also fine.
动名词的完成式 having done 强调动作发生在主句动作之前；被动形式 having been done 表示主语「先被……」。after、on、for、without 等介词后都可以接。after 本身已表先后，所以也可以只用 being done 或 doing。

In sentence 11 the perfect passive gerund shows that he was soaked before he went into the shop.

本课：第 11 句用了 after having been caught，说明他是先淋了雨才进店。

  • After having been told the news, she phoned her mother at once.
  • He was praised for having saved the child.
  • She left without having been seen by anyone.
insist on / object to + someone + doing: the doer of a gerundinsist on / object to + 某人 + doing（动名词的逻辑主语）
To say who does the action of a gerund, put a possessive before it: my, his, Tom's. Formal writing uses the possessive, while speech often uses the object form or a plain noun, as in insist on him going. Common verbs are insist on, object to, mind, excuse, forgive and appreciate.
动名词前加物主代词或名词所有格（my / his / Tom's）说明「谁」做这件事。正式文体用所有格，口语常用宾格或普通名词（insist on him going）。常见动词：insist on、object to、mind、excuse、forgive、appreciate。

In sentence 21 the person who must count is the assistant, so the possessive form sits in front of the gerund.

本课：第 21 句里要数钱的是店员，所以动名词前加了店员的所有格。

  • Do you mind my opening the window?
  • She objected to his smoking in the car.
  • We appreciate your coming so far to help us.
It takes / took + someone + time + to doIt takes / took + 某人 + 时间 + to do
Use the empty subject it to say how much time something cost a person; in place of time you can put courage, patience and so on. Avoid spend with an infinitive: spend takes doing, as in spend four years painting.
用形式主语 it 说「做某事花了某人多少时间」；时间可以换成 courage、patience 等「付出的东西」。不要说 He took four years to…（虽然也可以），更不要说 He spent four years to stage…——spend 后接 (in) doing。

Sentence 26 tells us the joke took him four years.

本课：第 26 句：他花了四年策划这个玩笑。

  • It took us two hours to find the hotel.
  • It takes a lot of courage to admit you are wrong.
  • She spent the whole weekend painting the kitchen.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • True X never deliberately set out to … 真正的 X 从不刻意去……
    True experts never deliberately set out to confuse beginners.
  • X disregards … without being conscious that … X 无视……而自己意识不到……
    Children often disregard danger without being conscious of it.
  • Until his death, X was among the most … 直到去世，X 都是最……的人之一
    Until her death, she was among the best-loved teachers in the school.
  • He was in such a … condition that … 他狼狈到……以至于……
    He was in such a tired state that he fell asleep at the table.
  • It took him … to …, simply to prove that … 他花了……做……，只为证明……
    It took her a year to learn the piano, simply to prove that she could.

Summary writing摘要写作

In no more than 80 words, tell what happened when Dickie went to buy the watch.

用不超过 80 个词讲述迪基在表店里发生的事。

Points a good summary covers好摘要要覆盖的要点
  • Soaked by heavy rain, Dickie went into a smart shop to buy his wife a watch costing three hundred pounds.迪基被大雨淋得狼狈不堪，走进一家高档商店想给太太买块 300 英镑的表。
  • Because he looked so wet and untidy, an assistant would not serve him.店员见他那副样子，拒绝接待他。
  • He left in silence and came back with a heavy sack of coins, asking for the manager.他一言不发离开，又扛着一个装满硬币的沉重布袋回来，要求见经理。
  • The manager knew him, said sorry and scolded the assistant; Dickie got the watch and made the young man count all thirty thousand coins.经理认出他，连连道歉并训斥了店员；迪基买到表后，让那位店员把三万枚硬币全部数完。
Our model summary本站范文

One day Dickie, soaked by heavy rain, went into an expensive shop to buy his wife a watch. Because he looked so untidy, an assistant would not serve him. He left without speaking, came back with a heavy sack and asked for the manager. The manager knew who he was, apologized and scolded the assistant. When Dickie had the watch, he handed the sack to the young man, who had to count thirty thousand coins before Dickie left.（78 词）
