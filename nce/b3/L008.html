Lesson 8 · A Famous Monastery 著名的修道院

📘 New Concept English · Book 3 · Lesson 8 “A Famous Monastery”新概念英语 第三册 Lesson 8《A Famous Monastery》

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
The pass that joins Switzerland to Italy stands at 2,473 metres and is the highest in Europe. About a mile from the top is a monastery begun in the eleventh century, with the dogs that have pulled travellers out of the snow for hundreds of years. The lesson is exposition built on contrast: geography and history first, then summer against winter, crowds and shut-in dogs on one side, thirty degrees of frost, quiet monks and free-running dogs on the other. The grammar to learn is the non-defining relative clause, now that, prefer A to B, and a run of passive verbs.
连接瑞士和意大利的大圣伯纳德山口海拔 2473 米，是欧洲最高的山口；附近一座 11 世纪建的修道院养着圣伯纳德犬，几百年来救过无数翻山的旅人。本课是说明文的对比结构：先讲地理与历史，再把夏天（游客如潮、狗被关起来）和冬天（零下 30 度、修士清静、狗可以自由走动）对照着写。学非限制性定语从句、now that、prefer A to B，以及一组被动语态。

  2. Is there still work for the dogs after the tunnel?隧道修好以后，狗还有事做吗？
  3. How do summer and winter differ at the monastery, and why do the monks like winter better?修道院的夏天和冬天有什么不同？修士为什么更喜欢冬天？

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

The hospice on the Great St Bernard stands at 2,469 metres and was founded by Augustinian canons around 1050; the road over the pass is open only from about June to October, so for more than half the year the place is reached on skis or not at all. The road tunnel under the mountains opened in 1964, three years before Book 3 was published, which means the lesson was describing something very new when it said the pass had become less dangerous. The dogs are now bred not at the hospice but at Martigny, down in the valley, by a foundation set up in 2004 to take the kennels over from the canons; there is always a male dog there called Barry, after the animal the hospice is famous for. From about June to September the dogs go back up to the pass, where visitors can see them.

大圣伯纳德山口的修道院海拔 2469 米，约 1050 年由奥古斯丁修会的教士创建；翻越山口的公路大约只在六月到十月通行，所以一年里有一半以上的时间，这地方要靠滑雪进去，或者根本进不去。穿山公路隧道 1964 年通车，比第三册出版早三年——也就是说，课文写「山口没那么危险了」时，说的是一件非常新的事。狗如今不在修道院繁育，而在山谷下面的马蒂尼，由 2004 年成立的一个基金会从修会手里接过犬舍；那里永远有一条叫巴里的雄犬，名字取自修道院最有名的那条狗。大约六月到九月，狗会回到山口，游客可以在那里看到它们。

✨ How it is written赏析

The lesson is built as a hinge. The first half is geography and history, written almost entirely in the passive and the present perfect, which is the natural grammar for things that were done long ago and still hold: the monastery was founded, the dogs were brought, lives have been saved. Then one short sentence announces that winter is quite different, and everything after it comes in pairs — crowds against emptiness, a pen against the open mountain, thousands of visitors against a few parties of skiers, the monks' lost quiet against the quiet regained. The two halves are almost equal in length, and the writer never once tells you which season he prefers; the monks are allowed to say it instead, in a clause that gives their reason and nothing more. That restraint is the lesson in composition here: set the two pictures side by side, keep them the same size, and let a single quoted preference do the arguing.

这一课是按合页结构搭的。前半是地理与历史，几乎全用被动和现在完成时——这正是「很久以前做成、至今仍然成立」的事情最自然的语法：修道院被创建、狗被带来、人命一直被救下。然后一句短句宣布冬天大不一样，之后的一切都成对出现：人潮对空荡、围场对敞开的山野、成千游客对几队滑雪者、修士失去的清静对重新得到的清静。两半长度几乎相等，作者一次也没说自己偏爱哪个季节；他把这句话让给修士，用一个只给理由、不加评语的分句说出来。这份克制就是本课在写法上要教的：把两幅画并排放好，让它们一样大，然后让一句引语去完成论证。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

Three things in the lesson no longer stand, and the biggest one never did. The dogs were not brought from Asia and were not Roman watchdogs: the breed was built up at the hospice itself from local Valais farm dogs in the 1660s and 1670s, which makes them about three and a half centuries old there, not two thousand. The height is a few metres out; the figure in the sources is 2,469. And the seasons have swapped round. Since the move to Martigny the dogs are up at the pass in summer, exactly the season in which the lesson shuts them in a pen, and they are not there in winter at all, which is the season the lesson gives them their freedom. None of this spoils the writing, but it shows what happens to a descriptive text about a working place: the composition keeps its value and the facts quietly expire. Check the dates on any passage that tells you how something is done today.

课文里有三处已经不成立，而最大的那一处从来就不成立。狗不是从亚洲带来的，也不是罗马时代的看门狗：这个品种是在修道院当地，于 1660 至 1670 年代用瓦莱州的农家犬培育起来的，所以它们在那里的历史约三个半世纪，不是两千年。海拔差了几米，资料给的是 2469 米。而两个季节已经调了个头：自从犬舍迁到马蒂尼，狗在夏天上到山口——正是课文把它们关进围场的那个季节——而冬天根本不在那里，而课文偏偏在冬天给它们自由。这些都不妨碍文章写得好，但它显示出一篇描写「正在运转的场所」的文章会遭遇什么：章法保值，事实悄悄过期。凡是告诉你「今天某事如何进行」的段落，先看日期。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 4第 4 句 (begins开头 “which was founded …”)

  • The comma-plus-which clause is non-defining: it adds information about the monastery and could be taken out without touching the main statement.which…：非限制性定语从句，用逗号隔开，补充说明修道院（去掉也不影响主干）。
  • The backbone of the sentence is simply that the monastery lies about a mile away; lie means to be situated, and its forms are lie, lay, lain.主干：The monastery … lies about a mile away。lie 位于（lie-lay-lain）。

Sentence 5第 5 句 (begins开头 “For hundreds of …”)

  • for hundreds of years with the present perfect: it has been so for centuries and still is.for hundreds of years + 现在完成时：几百年来一直如此。
  • The -ing form after the noun does the work of a relative clause with who were.crossing…：现在分词作后置定语 = who were crossing。

Sentence 8第 8 句 (begins开头 “Now that a …”)

  • now that: seeing that something has now come about; it brings in a new situation as the reason.now that：既然，由于现在（引出新情况作为原因）。
  • One verb is the present perfect passive, for the change that has taken place; the other is the present simple passive, for what still happens every year.has been built：现在完成时被动；are sent out：一般现在时被动。
  • whenever: every time that it happens.whenever：每当。

Sentence 11第 11 句 (begins开头 “there are still …”)

  • despite + noun: the tunnel exists, and yet.despite + 名词：尽管有隧道。
  • a few means some, however small the number; few on its own would mean hardly any, which is a different tone.a few（还有几个）与 few（几乎没有）语气不同。

Sentence 14第 14 句 (begins开头 “As there are …”)

  • as here means because, and such a clause very often comes first in the sentence.as：因为（原因从句放句首很常见）。
  • about as an adverb means in the area, round and about, the same as around.about 副词：在附近、到处（= around）。
  • have to be done: a modal idea followed by the passive.have to be done：情态 + 被动。

Sentence 18第 18 句 (begins开头 “The monks prefer …”)

  • prefer A to B: like A better than B, where to is a preposition.prefer A to B：喜欢 A 胜过 B（to 是介词）。
  • for as a co-ordinating conjunction adds the reason in the middle of a sentence.for：因为（并列连词，放在句中，补充理由）。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

Non-defining relative clauses: comma + which / who非限制性定语从句：逗号 + which/who
Marked off by commas, it only adds information and does not narrow down which one you mean. You cannot use that, and the relative word cannot be left out. A defining clause has no commas and carries the information you need to know which one it is.
用逗号隔开，只补充信息，不限定范围；不能用 that，关系词不能省。限制性定语从句没有逗号，是「哪一个」所必需的信息。

Compare the two versions: without commas it tells you which brother out of several; with commas it tells you there is only one, and adds where he lives in passing.

对比：My brother who lives in Paris is a doctor.（我有不止一个兄弟）／My brother, who lives in Paris, is a doctor.（只有一个，顺带说他住巴黎）

  • Vancouver, which is on the west coast, has mild winters.
  • My grandmother, who is ninety, still walks every day.
now that: seeing that, now that something has happenednow that：既然；由于现在
It brings in a newly arrived state of affairs as the reason, and carries more of a sense of 'now it has happened' than because does; in speech the that is often dropped.
引出一个「新出现的情况」作为理由，比 because 多了「现在已经……」的意味；that 口语中可省。
  • Now that you've finished your homework, you can watch TV.
  • Now (that) the rain has stopped, let's go out.
prefer A to B / prefer doing to doing / prefer to do rather than do
In prefer A to B the word to is a preposition, so a noun or an -ing form follows it. If you want an infinitive instead, use prefer to do one thing rather than do another.
prefer A to B 里 to 是介词，后接名词或动名词；如果后面接不定式，用 prefer to do … rather than do …。

In sentence 18 the monks like one season better than the other, with to as a preposition between them.

本课：第 18 句修士 prefer winter to summer。

  • I prefer tea to coffee.
  • She prefers walking to driving.
  • He prefers to stay at home rather than go out.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • X connects A to B. X 把 A 和 B 连接起来
    The new bridge connects the island to the mainland.
  • For hundreds of years, X have … 几百年来，X 一直……
    For hundreds of years, fishermen have lived on this coast.
  • Now that …, X is less … 既然……，X 就没那么……了
    Now that the road has been widened, driving here is less stressful.
  • In winter, however, life at X is quite different. 然而到了冬天，X 的生活就大不一样了
    At night, however, life in the market is quite different.

Our model summary本站范文

The St. Bernard Pass, the highest in Europe, links Switzerland and Italy. Near it stands a monastery built in the eleventh century, whose dogs have rescued travellers for centuries. Even with the new tunnel, they still go out when someone is lost in the snow. In summer crowds of visitors arrive by car, so the dogs are shut in. In winter it is bitterly cold and quiet, the monks enjoy their privacy, and the dogs roam freely.（77 词）
