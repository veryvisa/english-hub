Lesson 5 · The Facts 事实

📘 New Concept English · Book 3 · Lesson 5 “The Facts”新概念英语 第三册 Lesson 5《The Facts》

B2🧠 20 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
A short satirical story: a magazine editor who is obsessed with 'exact figures' sends a journalist to count the steps outside a president's palace and measure the wall. The journalist does not reply, so the editor has to print the article as first written. A week later a fax arrives: the journalist was arrested while counting the 1,084 steps and is in prison. The satire is the absurd price paid for an unimportant number. Grammar focus: the past perfect (including the passive) and the inverted Not only had ... but ....
一则讽刺小故事：杂志编辑一味追求「精确数字」，要记者去数总统府外有多少级台阶、墙有多高；记者迟迟不回，编辑只好照原稿登出。一周后才收到记者的传真——他在数那 1084 级台阶时被逮捕入狱了。讽刺点在于：为了无关紧要的数字，付出了荒唐的代价。语法重点是过去完成时（含被动）和 Not only had … but …（倒装）。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. Why did the editor refuse to publish the article?编辑为什么拒绝刊登那篇文章？
  2. Why did the journalist take so long to reply?记者为什么迟迟不回复？
  3. What is the story making fun of?这个故事讽刺了什么？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Book 3 texts were written for the course by L. G. Alexander; this is an invented satirical story, not a report of a real event, and no real country or magazine is named.

第三册课文由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为教材自撰；本课是虚构的讽刺故事，不是真实事件，也没点名任何真实的国家或杂志。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

The story belongs to the 1960s. In 1960 alone seventeen African countries became independent — the "Year of Africa" — and Western magazines sent reporters to describe new presidents, new capitals and new palaces. It was also the high point of the big picture magazines, which were proud of their research departments: every name, date and figure in an article was checked before printing. Alexander turns that pride into a joke. The editor's demand is not wrong in itself; checking facts is part of good journalism. What is absurd is the choice of fact: a reader learns nothing from knowing that there are 1,084 steps instead of "hundreds". The darker joke is that in a newly formed state, a foreigner counting the steps of the presidential palace looks exactly like a spy.

这个故事属于 1960 年代。仅 1960 年一年，非洲就有 17 个国家独立——史称「非洲年」——西方杂志纷纷派记者去写新总统、新首都、新总统府。那也是大型画报杂志的鼎盛期，它们以自家的资料核查部门为荣：文章里每一个人名、日期、数字付印前都要核过。亚历山大把这份自豪变成了笑话。编辑的要求本身没错，核实事实本就是好新闻的一部分；荒唐的是他挑的事实：读者知道台阶是 1084 级而不是「数百级」，什么也没多得到。更黑色的一层是：在一个新成立的国家，外国人去数总统府的台阶，看上去完全就是间谍。

✨ How it is written赏析

The whole story is built to deliver one last line. Alexander gives us the editor's side in detail — the refusal, the first fax, two more faxes, the threat to fire, the reluctant decision — and tells us almost nothing about the journalist. We are as impatient and as ignorant as the editor. Then the final paragraph opens with a delay (a week later, at last) and drops the truth in a single long sentence built on the past perfect: everything we have been waiting for had already happened. The exact numbers at the end — 1,084 steps, a 15-foot wall — are the punch line, because they are the very facts the editor wanted. When you write a story with a twist, hide the key event, tell it from the point of view of the person who does not know, and let the final detail echo the beginning.

整个故事就是为最后一句搭的。亚历山大把编辑这一边写得很细——拒稿、第一份传真、又两份传真、威胁解雇、勉强登稿——对记者几乎只字不提。读者跟编辑一样不耐烦、一样蒙在鼓里。最后一段先拖一下（a week later、at last），再用一个以过去完成时撑起的长句把真相抖出来：我们一直在等的事，早就发生了。结尾那两个精确数字——1084 级台阶、15 英尺高的墙——正是笑点，因为这恰恰是编辑要的那两个事实。写带反转的故事可以这样做：把关键事件藏起来，从不知情的人的角度讲，让最后一个细节呼应开头。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

Sixty years later the satire has two edges. One still cuts: organisations still ask for numbers that nobody uses — the report with twenty charts, the form that asks for figures already on file. Before you ask someone for an exact number, ask what decision it will change. But the other edge has turned around. In an age of online rumours and AI tools that can invent convincing figures, editors who insist on checking are more valuable than ever; the problem in the story is not checking, but checking the wrong thing. And the journalist's fate is not only a joke: press-freedom groups count hundreds of journalists in prison around the world every year. For Chinese readers, the story is a useful mirror for any workplace where the form of the report matters more than what it says.

六十年后，这个讽刺有两面。一面依然锋利：机构里仍然到处要没人用的数字——二十张图表的汇报、索要档案里早就有的数据的表格。向别人要一个精确数字之前，先问问它会改变哪个决定。另一面却翻了过来：在网上谣言和能编出逼真数字的 AI 工具面前，坚持核实的编辑比任何时候都珍贵；故事里的问题不是核实本身，而是核错了对象。记者的遭遇也不只是笑话：新闻自由组织每年都统计到全球有数以百计的记者身陷囹圄。对中国读者来说，凡是「报告的形式比内容更重要」的工作场合，这个故事都是一面好镜子。

💬 Discuss and write讨论与写作

    你有没有被要求提供明显无关紧要的信息？用五句话讲出来，至少用两次过去完成时。
  2. Rewrite the story in 120 words from the journalist's point of view, starting on the day he arrived at the palace.
    从记者的角度把故事改写成 120 词，从他到达总统府那天写起。
  3. When does an exact number really matter in a news story, and when is "about" or "hundreds" good enough? Give one example of each.
    新闻里什么时候必须给精确数字，什么时候写「约」或「数百」就够了？各举一例。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 2第 2 句 (begins开头 “Last year a …”)

  • had been instructed: past perfect passive (the task had been given before the article arrived). With last year the simple past is more usual; the past perfect here stresses that it came before the chain of events that follows.had been instructed：过去完成时被动（在文章送到之前已经被布置）。严格说有 last year 时常用一般过去时，这里用过去完成时强调「先于后面一系列事件」。
  • Passive of instruct sb to do: sb is instructed to do.instruct sb to do 的被动：sb be instructed to do。

Sentence 5第 5 句 (begins开头 “The editor at …”)

  • send sb sth: a verb with two objects.send sb sth：双宾语。
  • instructing him ...: a present participle phrase after fax (the fax told him to ...).instructing him…：现在分词作后置定语修饰 fax（传真内容是指示他……）。

Sentence 7第 7 句 (begins开头 “Meanwhile, the editor …”)

  • was getting: past continuous for a gradual change (more and more impatient).was getting：过去进行时表渐变（越来越不耐烦）。
  • would: future in the past (seen from then: 'soon going to').would：过去将来时（站在当时看「很快要」）。

Sentence 9第 9 句 (begins开头 “He sent yet …”)

  • yet another: one more (with a note of impatience).yet another：又一个（带不耐烦语气）。
  • A condition in reported speech: the direct form If you do not reply soon, you will be fired moves back one tense.间接引语中的条件句：直接引语 If you do not reply soon, you will be fired → 时态后退。

Sentence 10第 10 句 (begins开头 “When the journalist …”)

  • as introduces a clause of manner: in the way it had first been written.as 引导方式从句：照原来写的样子。
  • had been written: past perfect passive.had been written：过去完成时被动。

Sentence 12第 12 句 (begins开头 “Not only had …”)

  • With Not only at the front, the first clause is partly inverted (had comes before the subject); the clause after but keeps normal order.Not only 放句首，前半句部分倒装（had 提到主语前）；but 后的分句不倒装。
  • as well = too。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

The past perfect (including the passive): had done / had been done过去完成时（含被动）：had done / had been done
When you tell a story in the past, use had done for 'the past before the past'; the passive is had been done.
叙述过去时，「过去的过去」用 had done；被动用 had been done（第四册第 9 课有主动用法）。

Timeline: task given → article written → editor reads it → faxes sent → article printed → fax received (revealing he had already been arrested).

时间线：布置任务 → 写稿 → 编辑读稿 → 发传真 → 登稿 → 收到传真（得知他之前已被捕）。

  • When I got home, I found that my flat had been broken into.
  • He told me he had been offered a new job.
Not only ... but (also) ... with inversionNot only … but (also)… 与句首倒装
When Not only starts a sentence, its clause is partly inverted (the auxiliary comes first); the but also clause keeps normal order; also can be replaced by as well or too at the end.
Not only 放句首时，它所在的分句要部分倒装（助动词提前）；but also 分句照常语序；also 可换成 as well 或 too 放句末。
  • Not only does he speak French, but he also speaks German.
  • He not only speaks French but also German.（not only 不在句首，不倒装）
Tense back-shift in reported speech间接引语里的时态后退
When the reporting verb is past (said, told, informed), the tense in the reported clause usually moves back one step: present → past, future → would, past → past perfect.
主句动词是过去时（said, told, informed）时，从句时态一般后退一步：现在 → 过去，将来 → 过去将来（would），过去 → 过去完成。

Exception: general truths and facts that are still true need not move back.

例外：客观真理、仍然成立的事实可不后退。

  • He said, "I will come." → He said (that) he would come.
  • She said, "I have finished." → She said she had finished.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • X often go to extremes to … X 常常为了……走极端
    Some parents go to extremes to protect their children.
  • X sent Y a … instructing him to … X 给 Y 发去……，指示他……
    The manager sent the team an email instructing them to stay late.
  • X was getting impatient, for … would soon … X 越来越不耐烦，因为……很快就要……
    We were getting impatient, for the train would soon leave.
  • Not only had …, but … as well. 不仅……，而且还……
    Not only had he missed the bus, but he had lost his wallet as well.
