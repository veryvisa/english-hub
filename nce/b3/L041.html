📘 New Concept English · Book 3 · Lesson 41 “Illusions of Pastoral Peace”新概念英语 第三册 Lesson 41《Illusions of Pastoral Peace》

B2🧠 30 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
The writer was born and raised in the city; to him the country is something seen from a train window or visited now and then at weekends. His friends grow lyrical at the very word, yet only one of them ever moved there, and he was back within six months, still praising the friendly people, the clean air, nature and the slow pace — the cock crowing, the birds at dawn, sunlight on trees and fields. That, the writer says, is only half the picture. There are the long, lonely winter evenings with nothing but television, the poor choice in the shops, and the people who travel four hours a day to work in town. In the city your friends live round the corner, exhibitions, films and plays are a short bus ride away, and shopping is a pleasure; country people who come in for the theatre spend the last act wondering whether they will catch the last train. Even the city has its beauty: the glow of advertisements on a wet winter night, and the quiet of the streets at weekends when the commuters have gone home to the country. This is an argument against the common view, written with a good deal of irony. The grammar to learn is City born and city bred, go into raptures, the mere mention of, be forever doing, fail to, is beyond me, make do with, and inversion after Nor.
作者是在城里出生、城里长大的，乡村对他来说是火车窗外的风景、周末偶尔去一趟的地方。他的朋友们一提乡村就心驰神往，可真搬去住的只有一个，半年就回城了，嘴上还念叨着乡下人友善、空气清新、亲近自然、节奏舒缓，鸡鸣鸟唱、朝阳照着树林牧场。作者说这只是画面的一部分：漫长孤寂的冬夜只能对着电视，商店货少，还有每天来回四小时进城上班的人。城里朋友就住在附近，展览、电影、戏剧坐一小段公共汽车就到，购物永远是享受；乡下人一年进城看一两回戏，散场前就担心赶不上末班火车。连城市也有它的美：冬夜里广告灯的暖光，周末通勤者都回了乡下后街道上的宁静。本课是唱反调的议论文：大家都夸乡村，作者偏夸城市，语气带着讽刺。学 City born and city bred、go into raptures、the mere mention of、be forever doing、fail to、is beyond me、make do with、Nor is …（倒装）。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. Which facts does the writer say his friend leaves out when he praises the country?作者认为他那位搬去乡下的朋友漏掉了哪些事实？
  2. What worry spoils a visit to the theatre for country people but not for city dwellers?城里人看戏时没有、乡下人却有的担心是什么？
  3. Which of the city's 'moments of beauty' does the writer describe with irony?作者说的城市之美，有哪一处是带讽刺的？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander, in the voice of a confirmed city dweller. The essay takes part in an argument as old as English writing about the countryside, in which the pastoral ideal is praised by town people and doubted by those who actually live in the country.

课文为编者 L. G. 亚历山大为教材自撰，用的是一个铁杆城里人的口吻。这篇随笔加入了一场和英语乡村写作一样古老的争论：田园理想总是被城里人赞美，而被真住在乡下的人怀疑。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

The argument has a classic modern study. In The Country and the City, published in 1973, the critic Raymond Williams showed how English writers had praised a happier, simpler country life for centuries, and how that life always seemed to belong to a generation just before the writer's own: the golden age kept moving back as you looked for it. The lesson's narrator is in effect replying to that tradition from the other side. Meanwhile the world has been voting with its feet. The United Nations estimated in 2018 that about fifty-five per cent of the world's people lived in urban areas, and projected that the share would reach about two-thirds by the middle of the century. The lesson's commuter, who travels four hours a day for the sake of a house in the country, also has a modern descendant: many people in large cities now live far out because housing near the centre is too expensive, so the 'dubious privilege' is often not a choice at all.

这场争论有一部经典的现代研究。批评家雷蒙德·威廉斯在 1973 年出版的《乡村与城市》中指出，英国作家几百年来一直在赞美一种更幸福、更简单的乡村生活，而那种生活似乎总是属于作者之前的上一代人：你越去找那个黄金时代，它就越往后退。课文的叙述者实际上是从另一边回应这一传统。与此同时，世界已经在用脚投票。联合国 2018 年估计，全球约 55% 的人口住在城市地区，并预计到本世纪中叶这一比例将达到约三分之二。课文里那位为了乡下一栋房子每天通勤四小时的人，在今天也有后代：大城市里许多人住到很远的地方，是因为市中心附近的房子太贵——那份「可疑的特权」往往根本不是选择。

✨ How it is written赏析

The essay is a debate in which only one side is allowed to speak, and the fun lies in how unfairly the writer argues while sounding reasonable. He starts by admitting his bias openly — city born and city bred — which disarms us. Then he gives his opponents their best case, and gives it generously, in a long sentence full of cockcrow, birdsong and sunlight, so that when he cuts it down with 'only part of the picture' we feel he has earned the right. From there he chooses his evidence: the worst of the country against the best of the city. The cleverest move comes at the end. Praising the city's beauty, he picks the least likely examples — the glow of advertisements, empty streets — and the second of them is really a joke at the country's expense, since the streets are peaceful only because the commuters have gone home to their villages. The final sentence turns the attack back onto his own friends, and leaves the reader suspecting that the writer protests a little too much.

这篇随笔是一场只允许一方发言的辩论，乐趣在于作者听上去很讲理，论证却很不公平。他一开头就公开承认自己的偏见——城里生、城里长——这让我们放下戒心。接着他把对手最好的论据摆出来，而且摆得很大方：一个长句里满是鸡鸣、鸟唱和阳光，于是当他用「这只是画面的一部分」一刀砍下去时，我们觉得他有资格这样做。从那以后，他挑选证据：拿乡村最差的一面对比城市最好的一面。最妙的一招在结尾。夸城市之美，他挑了最不可能的例子——广告的光、空荡的街道——而第二个其实是在拿乡村开玩笑：街道之所以安静，只是因为通勤者都回了他们的村子。最后一句把矛头转回他自己的朋友，留给读者一个疑心：作者是不是辩解得有点过了头。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

Read today, the lesson's argument has been tested by events its writer could not foresee. After 2020 many people in large cities were able to work from home, and a good number moved out to smaller towns and the country, discovering both the pleasures the friend praises and the problems the writer lists — slow internet, fewer shops, long winters. Some came back, much like the friend in the lesson; others stayed. Chinese readers have their own version of the debate: young people talk about escaping the pressure of the biggest cities, while the same cities keep drawing people in for jobs, schools and hospitals. The honest answer may be the one the essay hints at in its last line without meaning to: most people want the city's services and the country's peace, and the arguments on each side are partly ways of living with having chosen one.

今天再读，课文的论点已经被作者无法预见的事件检验过。2020 年以后，大城市里许多人可以在家办公，不少人搬到了小城镇和乡下，既发现了那位朋友赞美的乐趣，也遇到了作者列举的问题——网速慢、商店少、冬天长。有些人回来了，很像课文里那位朋友；另一些人留了下来。中国读者也有自己版本的这场辩论：年轻人说要逃离大城市的压力，而同样这些城市又因为工作、学校和医院不断把人吸进去。诚实的答案，也许正是这篇随笔在最后一句无意中暗示的那个：大多数人既想要城市的服务，又想要乡村的宁静；两边的种种论证，部分只是人们为自己已经做出的选择找到安顿的方式。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 2第 2 句 (begins开头 “City born and …”)

  • The participle phrase gives the reason: because I was born and raised in the city.City born and city bred：过去分词短语作原因状语（= As I was born and bred in the city）。
  • regard ... as ...: to think of ... as ....regard … as …：把……看作……。

Sentence 4第 4 句 (begins开头 “Most of my …”)

  • go into raptures over something: to become wildly enthusiastic.go into raptures（over sth）：欣喜若狂、赞不绝口。
  • at the mere mention of: as soon as someone just names it.at the mere mention of：一提到……就。

Sentence 7第 7 句 (begins开头 “Even he still …”)

  • live under the illusion that: to keep believing something false.live under the illusion that：一直抱着……的幻想（that 从句是同位语）。
  • superior to: better than; it takes to, not than.superior to：比……好（不用 than）。

Sentence 8第 8 句 (begins开头 “He is forever …”)

  • be forever doing: always doing it, said with irritation.be forever doing：老是做（进行时 + forever，表示厌烦）。

Sentence 9第 9 句 (begins开头 “Nothing can be …”)

  • he maintains: dropped in, 'he insists'.he maintains：插入语，「他坚持说」。
  • Nothing can be compared with: nothing is as good as.Nothing can be compared with：什么都比不上。

Sentence 15第 15 句 (begins开头 “Why people are …”)

  • The why clause is the subject, and the verb comes at the very end.Why … 主语从句作主语，谓语 is beyond me 在句末。
  • be beyond me: I cannot understand it. dubious privilege is ironic.be beyond me：我理解不了。dubious privilege：反讽，「可疑的特权」。

Sentence 26第 26 句 (begins开头 “There is so …”)

  • so much plus an uncountable noun plus that.so much + 不可数名词 + that：如此多……以至于。
  • make do with second best: to accept what is not the best.make do with second best：将就次等货。

Sentence 28第 28 句 (begins开头 “Nor is the …”)

  • Nor at the front causes inversion.Nor 放句首，后面倒装（is the city）。
  • Nor ... without: a double negative meaning 'it too has'.not without：双重否定，「也不乏」。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

be always / forever / constantly doing: the continuous with feelingbe always / forever / constantly doing：带感情色彩的进行时
The continuous tense with always, forever, constantly or continually says that someone does something again and again, usually with annoyance and sometimes with admiration. It describes a habit, not what is happening right now.
进行时加 always、forever、constantly、continually，表示某人老是做某事，常带厌烦、不满（有时是赞赏）的语气。注意它说的是习惯，不是此刻正在做。

In sentence 8 the friend keeps going on about the same things, and the writer is clearly tired of hearing them.

本课：第 8 句：那位朋友老是在说乡下人友善、空气清新……作者显然听烦了。

  • She is always losing her keys.
  • He is forever complaining about the weather.
  • My neighbours are constantly playing loud music.
Inversion after Nor, Neither and SoNor / Neither / So 放句首的倒装
Nor or Neither plus a helping verb, be or a modal plus the subject means 'and not ... either'; So plus a helping verb plus the subject means 'and ... too'. Nor can also open a sentence that adds a new negative point, still with inversion.
Nor / Neither + 助动词 / be / 情态动词 + 主语：「……也不」；So + 助动词 + 主语：「……也是」。Nor 也可以单独开头引出新的否定论点，后面照样倒装。

Sentence 28 opens with Nor and inversion to add a new point: the city has its beauty too.

本课：第 28 句以 Nor 开头倒装，带出「城市也不乏美的时刻」这一新论点。

  • I don't like coffee, and nor does my sister.
  • He can't swim. Neither can I.
  • She loves jazz, and so do I.
Clauses as subjects; be beyond someone主语从句（Why / What / Whether … is …）与 be beyond sb
A clause with a question word or whether can be the subject, with the verb after it. To avoid a heavy start, use the empty subject it instead: It is beyond me why .... be beyond someone means that they cannot understand or manage it.
疑问词或 whether 引导的从句可以作主语，谓语放在后面；为避免头重脚轻也可用形式主语 it：It is beyond me why …。be beyond sb：超出某人的理解或能力。

Sentence 15 has a long why clause as its subject, and only at the end says the writer cannot understand it.

本课：第 15 句用一个很长的 Why 从句作主语，末尾才说「我理解不了」。

  • Why he left is still a mystery.
  • Whether we go depends on the weather.
  • It is beyond me why anyone would buy that car.

Summary writing摘要写作

In no more than 80 words, sum up why the writer prefers city life to country life.

用不超过 80 个词概括作者为什么认为城市生活比乡村生活好。

Points a good summary covers好摘要要覆盖的要点
  • In the country, winter evenings are long and lonely, shops are poor, and many people spend four hours a day getting to work.乡村的冬夜漫长孤单，只能看电视，商店货少，很多人每天要花四小时进城上班。
  • In the city, friends live nearby and are always free for a chat or an evening out.在城里，朋友住得近，随时可以聊天或一起消遣。
  • Shows, films and plays are close at hand, with no worry about the last train home.展览、电影、戏剧近在咫尺，看完戏不用担心赶不上末班车。
  • Shopping offers endless choice, and the city has its moments of beauty too.购物选择多，城市也有它美的时刻。
Our model summary本站范文

The writer thinks country life is overrated. Its winter evenings are long and lonely, its shops offer little choice, and commuters waste four hours a day travelling. In the city, by contrast, friends live close by and are always ready for a chat or an evening out. Exhibitions, films and plays are a short bus ride away, with no fear of missing the last train. Shopping is a pleasure, and even the city has moments of beauty.（77 词）
