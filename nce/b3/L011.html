📘 New Concept English · Book 3 · Lesson 11 “Not Guilty”新概念英语 第三册 Lesson 11《Not Guilty》

B2🧠 28 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
You walk through the nothing-to-declare lane with nothing to declare, and still, if you are stopped, you feel shifty; the professional runs goods past the desk without a flicker. Coming home from abroad, the writer is marked down as a smuggler by one very keen young officer: the case is turned upside down, a small bottle is found at the bottom, and the officer announces with satisfaction that it is perfume and liable to duty. The writer says it is hair gel of his own making and invites him to try it, and one whiff settles the matter. The lesson is a first-person story with a dry edge. The grammar to learn is be made to do, where the passive keeps the to, should have done, Would you mind doing, the have something done pattern, and a set of adverbs describing manner.
走海关「绿色通道」，明明没东西要申报，被拦下时老实人也会心虚，职业走私犯反倒若无其事。作者回国时被一个爱管闲事的年轻海关官员当成走私犯：箱子被翻得一团糟，官员在箱底找到一个小瓶，得意地说是香水、该交税；作者说那是自己调的发胶，请他闻一闻——一股怪味让他信了。本课是带反讽的第一人称小故事，学 be made to do（被动里 to 不能省）、should have done、Would you mind doing、have sth done 结构和一组描写神态的副词。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. Why does even an honest traveller feel uneasy at the customs desk?为什么连诚实的人过海关时也会觉得心虚？
  2. What did the officer find in the case, and what did he take it to be?海关官员在箱子里发现了什么？他以为是什么？
  3. How did the writer get the officer to believe him?作者用什么办法让官员相信了他？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Unsigned and written for the course, in the first person. The customs arrangement it turns on is real; the chalk marks at the end, and the date on which the two-channel system began, we could not confirm from a primary source this round, so they are left as unverified.

本课未署名、为教材自撰，用第一人称写。它赖以成立的那套海关安排是真的；结尾的粉笔记号，以及双通道制度始于哪一年，本轮没能从一手源核实，所以标为未核。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

The system is simple and has a sting in it. Arriving travellers with goods to declare take the red channel and those with nothing to declare take the green one, and the choice of channel is itself the declaration: walking into the green lane states, in law, that you are carrying nothing that needs declaring. A traveller found in that lane with undeclared goods can therefore be dealt with as a smuggler, without ever having said a word. Once you have entered a channel you cannot go back to the baggage hall, though officers can move people between lanes through a connecting corridor. The blue lane that used to serve arrivals from other European countries has since closed, so at British airports the choice is now the plain one the lesson describes.

这套制度很简单，但带着一根刺。入境者有东西要申报就走红色通道，没有就走绿色通道，而选哪条通道本身就是申报：走进绿色通道，在法律上就等于声明你没有携带需要申报的东西。因此，被查到在绿色通道里带着未申报物品的人，可以按走私处理，哪怕他一句话也没说过。进了通道就不能再回到行李厅，不过海关人员可以通过连接的走廊把人从一条调到另一条。过去供欧洲其他国家入境者使用的蓝色通道已经取消，所以现在英国机场只剩课文写的这两条。

✨ How it is written赏析

The whole piece is one joke told twice, once as a general truth and once as a scene, and the two halves are the same length. The opening paragraph sets up the asymmetry — the honest are made to feel guilty, the professional feels nothing — and the body then proves it on the writer's own body, in the first person, with his own suitcase. Watch the adverbs, because they carry the characterisation: the officer is officious, he looks the writer in the eye, he pounces with delight, he asks sarcastically. The writer answers confidently, then not at all, then with a smile, and the one word encouragingly is the moment he takes control. Every judgement about either man is delivered by an adverb rather than by a statement. The title does the rest: two words, borrowed from a courtroom, over a story in which nobody is charged with anything.

整篇是一个笑话讲了两遍，一遍作为普遍道理，一遍作为场景，两半一样长。开头那段立起不对称——老实人被弄得心虚，职业的毫无感觉——接下来就用作者自己的身体、自己的箱子、第一人称来证明它。留意副词，人物全靠它们立起来：官员爱管闲事、直视对方的眼睛、得意地猛扑、挖苦地问。而作者先是自信地答、接着干脆不答、然后带着笑答，那个「鼓励地」正是他夺回主动的一刻。关于这两个人的每一处评判，都由副词而不是由陈述句送出。标题完成剩下的活：两个从法庭借来的词，罩在一个没有任何人被指控的故事上。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The lesson treats the guilt of the innocent as a quirk of psychology, and it is partly something harder than that. In the green lane you have already made a statement by walking, so the uneasiness is not irrational; it is the correct feeling of someone who has sworn to something they cannot check. That is a real design choice, and it buys speed at the cost of making ordinary people carry a small legal risk they do not understand. What the story also shows, without meaning to, is how thin the traveller's position is. The officer's confidence rested on a theory, and nothing the writer said could touch it; what saved him was a smell, which is to say an accident. If the bottle had been odourless he had no way at all to prove his case. The comedy depends on a lucky chemical, and that is worth noticing before you laugh.

课文把「老实人的心虚」当成心理上的小怪癖，其实它一部分是更硬的东西。在绿色通道里，你一走进去就已经作出了声明，所以那份不安并不是没道理的：它恰恰是一个人对自己核不清的事情作了保证之后应有的感觉。这是一项真实的制度设计，它用速度换来的代价，是让普通人背上一点他们并不理解的法律风险。故事还无意间显示出旅客的处境多么薄：那位官员的自信建立在一个推断上，作者说什么都碰不动它；救了他的是一股气味，也就是一次偶然。要是那瓶东西没有味道，他根本无从自证。这出喜剧靠的是一种运气好的化学物质，笑之前值得留意这一点。

💬 Discuss and write讨论与写作

    你有没有在排队、安检或考试时，明明没做错事却觉得心虚？是那个场合里的什么东西造成了这种感觉？
  2. Write 120 words of a scene in which two people misjudge each other, giving every judgement through an adverb rather than a statement.
    写 120 词的一个场景：两个人彼此误判，所有评判都通过副词、而不是陈述句给出。
  3. If you were stopped and could not prove your own account, what would you actually do? Does the answer differ depending on where you are?
    如果你被拦下、又无法自证，你实际会怎么做？这个答案会因为你在哪个国家而不同吗？

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 1第 1 句 (begins开头 “Customs Officers are …”)

  • these days: at the present time, as against the past.these days：如今。
  • An infinitive after nothing works like a relative clause: nothing that you need to declare.have nothing to declare：不定式作后置定语修饰 nothing。
  • The green lane at customs is for travellers with nothing to declare; those with goods to declare take the red one.Green Channel：海关的「无申报通道」，有东西要申报走 Red Channel。

Sentence 2第 2 句 (begins开头 “Even really honest …”)

  • make somebody do takes a bare infinitive in the active, but the passive puts the to back in.make sb do（主动不带 to）→ sb be made to do（被动必须加 to）。

Sentence 4第 4 句 (begins开头 “even if he …”)

  • on the other hand sets this man against the honest traveller of the sentence before.on the other hand：另一方面，对比前一句的老实人。
  • have + object + past participle: the object is in the state of having had something done to it, so the watches are stowed out of sight.have + 宾语 + 过去分词：宾语处于被……的状态（手表是被藏起来的）。

Sentence 7第 7 句 (begins开头 “'Have you anything …”)

  • Have you anything ...? is the British form; Americans would more often say Do you have anything ... ?Have you anything…? 是英式说法，美式多说 Do you have anything…?
  • look somebody in the eye: to meet their gaze without flinching; the -ing form shows what he did as he spoke.look sb in the eye：直视某人的眼睛；looking… 现在分词作伴随状语。

Sentence 12第 12 句 (begins开头 “All the things …”)

  • in a mess: in complete disorder.in a mess：一团糟。

Sentence 26第 26 句 (begins开头 “He was greeted …”)

  • be greeted by: what met him as he opened it, a mock-polite way of putting it.be greeted by：迎面而来的是……（幽默拟人）。
  • convince somebody that: bring them to believe it.convince sb that…：使某人相信……。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

Active make somebody do becomes passive be made to do主动 make sb do → 被动 be made to do
Verbs of causing and of the senses, such as make, see and hear, take a bare infinitive in the active, and the to must be restored in the passive. let is not normally used in the passive; use be allowed to instead.
make、see、hear、let 等感官/使役动词，主动语态后接不带 to 的不定式；变成被动时要把 to 补回来。（let 一般不用被动，改用 be allowed to。）
  • The teacher made us rewrite the essay. → We were made to rewrite the essay.
  • Someone saw him leave the house. → He was seen to leave the house.
should have done: you ought to have, and did notshould have done：本该做而没做
should have plus a past participle blames or regrets something in the past; shouldn't have done says that you did something you ought not to have done.
should have + 过去分词，表示对过去的责备或后悔；shouldn't have done 表示「不该做却做了」。

In sentence 18 the officer uses it to score a point about the bottle he believes to be perfume.

本课：第 18 句，官员挖苦作者 should have declared 那瓶「香水」。

  • You should have told me earlier.
  • I shouldn't have eaten so much.
Would you mind doing ... ? and how to answer itWould you mind doing …? 与回答
Would you mind + an -ing form is a polite request. Since mind means to object, agreement is expressed by No, Not at all or Of course not, and only a refusal takes Yes, I would or Sorry, but ... .
Would you mind + doing（礼貌请求）；mind 的意思是「介意」，所以同意要说 No / Not at all / Of course not，拒绝才说 Yes, I would 或 Sorry, but …。

In sentences 9 and 12 the officer makes his request in this polite form, and the writer's answer means that he does not object in the least, that is, he agrees.

本课：第 9–10 句，官员请作者 mind unlocking 箱子，作者答 Not at all——表示「一点也不介意」，也就是同意。

  • Would you mind closing the window? — Not at all.
  • Would you mind if I sat here? — No, go ahead.

  • He said he had nothing to declare; the officer had the case opened and went through it until nothing was where it had been.作者说没东西申报，官员让他打开箱子，把东西翻得一团糟。
  • At the bottom the officer found a small bottle and declared with satisfaction that it was perfume, on which duty was owed.官员在箱底发现一个小瓶，得意地说是香水，该交进口税。
  • The writer said it was hair gel of his own making and asked him to try it; one unpleasant whiff was enough, and he was let through.作者说那是自己调的发胶，请他试；官员闻到一股怪味，相信了，放他走。
Our model summary本站范文

Coming home from abroad, I was stopped by a keen young officer who clearly took me for a smuggler. I said I had nothing to declare, but he searched my suitcase and turned everything upside down. At the bottom he found a little bottle and happily claimed it was perfume, which carries duty. I explained it was hair gel I mixed myself and invited him to smell it. The awful smell convinced him, and soon I was free to go.（80 词）
