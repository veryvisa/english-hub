🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
The Cutty Sark, one of the best known sailing ships of the eighteen hundreds, now stands on land at Greenwich, where thousands of visitors come to her every year and are reminded how grand the old ships were. Until steamers took the work away, ships of her kind carried China tea home to London and brought wool back from Australia. On the eighteenth of June, eighteen seventy-two, she and the one rival that could keep up with her, the Thermopylae, set out from Shanghai to see which would reach England first. It was the last of the great sailing races, and it closed an age. The Cutty Sark went ahead in the Indian Ocean, but in August a storm took her rudder away. Her crew built a makeshift one out of spare timber, she had to be sailed slowly, and her lead was gone. By the time a proper rudder had been fitted, her rival was more than five hundred miles in front, and she reached England a week behind. The writer's verdict: but for the rudder she would have won with ease. This is a piece of maritime history told with regret. Learn the unreal past conditional (if ... had not ..., would have ...), a superlative plus that has ever been, the only ... to do, there was a danger that, have something done and considering that.
19 世纪最有名的帆船之一卡蒂萨克号，如今停在格林尼治的陆地上，每年接待成千上万的游客，提醒人们过去的大帆船有多辉煌。在被蒸汽船取代之前，这类船从中国运茶、从澳大利亚运羊毛。1872 年 6 月 18 日，它和唯一能与它匹敌的「塞莫皮莱」号从上海出发，比赛谁先到英国——这是最后一场帆船大赛，标志着一个时代的结束。卡蒂萨克号在印度洋领先，8 月却遇上风暴，舵被打掉；船员用备用木板做了个临时舵，只能慢慢开，领先优势没了；过了赤道进港换舵时，对手已领先 500 多英里，最后晚了一周到英国。作者说：要不是丢了舵，它本可轻松获胜。本课是带惋惜语气的航海史叙事。学与过去相反的虚拟（if … had not … would have …）、「最高级 + that has ever been」、the only … to do、there was a danger that、have sth done 和 considering that。

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Book Three texts were written for the course by L. G. Alexander, and this one retells a real event: the tea race of eighteen seventy-two between two famous clippers. We checked the ship, the race and the rudder against museum and reference accounts, and the lesson turns out to simplify the story in three places, noted below.

第三册课文由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为教材自撰；本课复述的是真实事件——1872 年两艘名船之间的运茶比赛。我们按博物馆与工具书材料核了船、比赛与那把舵，课文有三处简化，见下文。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

The ship was launched on the twenty-second of November, eighteen sixty-nine, at Dumbarton on the Clyde; the first yard went bankrupt while she was on the stocks and a second firm finished her. Her name comes from a poem of seventeen ninety-one by Robert Burns, in which a witch called Nannie wears a cutty sark, a short shift, and her carved figure stands under the bowsprit to this day. The timing was cruel: the canal at Suez opened in the same year she was launched, and a steamer going that way reached China far sooner than a sailing ship round the Cape, so her years in the tea trade were few and she spent her best seasons carrying Australian wool. She later sailed under a Portuguese flag and another name, came back into British hands in the nineteen twenties, and has stood in a dry dock at Greenwich since nineteen fifty-four, when she opened as a museum ship. A fire in May two thousand and seven damaged her badly; she reopened in April twenty twelve, and a smaller fire followed in twenty fourteen.

这条船 1869 年 11 月 22 日在克莱德河畔的邓巴顿下水；第一家船厂造到一半破产，由第二家接手完工。名字出自罗伯特·彭斯 1791 年的一首诗：诗里的女巫南妮穿着 cutty sark，即一件短衬衣，她的雕像至今立在船首斜桅下。时机很残酷：苏伊士运河与她下水同年开通，走运河的汽船到中国远快于绕好望角的帆船，所以她运茶的年头很短，最好的几个季节是在运澳洲羊毛。她后来挂葡萄牙旗、改过名字，1920 年代回到英国人手里，1954 年起停在格林尼治的干船坞，以博物馆船的身份开放。2007 年 5 月的一场火灾损毁严重，2012 年 4 月重新开放，2014 年又着了一次小火。

✨ How it is written赏析

The lesson is a model of how to tell a race. First the finish line is shown before the start: here she is on dry land, visited by crowds. That gives the reader a safe place to stand. Then the two rivals are set up in a single sentence each, and the stakes are named: there would never be another race like it, so that the reader knows the race means more than itself. From there the writing speeds up and slows down with the ship. Short clauses carry the storm; the long sentence about the makeshift rudder, with its for and its if, moves as awkwardly as the ship did. And the ending refuses to be a defeat: two sentences of consolation, then one conditional that lets the reader imagine the other outcome. Notice how much of the feeling is carried by the pronoun she, which is not decoration but the ordinary way of speaking about a ship in English, and which quietly turns a cargo vessel into somebody who deserved better.

这一课是「怎么讲一场比赛」的范本。它先把终点摆在起点之前：看，她停在陆地上，游客成群。这给读者一个安稳的落脚处。接着用一句话各自立起两个对手，再点明赌注——此后不会再有这样的比赛——让读者知道这场比赛的分量超出比赛本身。从这里起，文字随着船一起加速和减速：风暴用短句推着走；讲临时舵那个长句带着 for 与 if，走得和当时的船一样别扭。结尾则拒绝认输：两句安慰，再一句虚拟，让读者自己去想另一种结局。还要注意，有多少情绪是由 she 这个代词担着的——它不是装饰，而是英语里说起船的寻常用法，一句话就把一条货船变成了一个「本该有更好结局」的人。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

Put the lesson beside the records and three differences appear. The first is the repair. In the lesson the captain puts into harbour so that a fresh rudder can be fitted; the accounts of the voyage have the ship's carpenter building a jury rudder on board, in a gale, and the crew refusing to break off the race, though a part-owner who was sailing with them urged them to put into Cape Town. The second is the gap. The figure of five hundred miles belongs to the lead the Cutty Sark had built up before her accident, which the reference accounts put at about four hundred miles; the final margin at London was seven days by some accounts and nine by others. The third is the weather itself: sources disagree about which ocean the storm struck in. None of this spoils the lesson, but it shows how a story smooths itself as it is retold — and the smoothing always runs the same way, towards a simpler cause and a rounder number. What has lasted is the larger judgement. The age of sail really did end, and it ended for an undramatic reason: a canal and a coal fire beat the wind. For Chinese readers there is a detail worth keeping. The race started in Shanghai because the cargo was China tea, and the whole point of these ships was to get the first of the new crop to London; within a few years that trade had moved to steamers through Suez, and the fastest sailing ships ever built were put to carrying wool at the other end of the world.

把课文和史料并排，有三处差别。第一是修舵：课文让船长进港换新舵；航程记载里是船上木匠在大风里造出一把应急舵，船员拒绝中断比赛，尽管随船的一位船东亲属劝他们进开普敦。第二是差距：五百英里这个数，更像是出事之前卡蒂萨克号已经拉开的领先量，而工具书把那个领先记作约四百英里；抵达伦敦的最终差距，有的材料记七天，有的记九天。第三是风暴本身：风暴发生在哪片海，各源说法不一。这些都不损害这一课，但它显示出一个故事在转述中如何被磨平——而且磨的方向总是一样：成因更简单，数字更整。经受住时间的是更大的那个判断。帆船时代确实结束了，而结束的理由并不戏剧：一条运河加一炉煤，赢了风。对中国读者还有一处细节值得记住：比赛从上海起跑，因为货是中国茶，这类船存在的意义就是把当年新茶的头一批送到伦敦；没过几年，这桩生意就转给了走苏伊士的汽船，而人类造过的最快帆船，被派去地球另一端运羊毛了。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 1第 1 句 (begins开头 “One of the …”)

  • The noun phrase at the front is in apposition to the ship's name and is put first for emphasis.句首的名词短语是 the Cutty Sark 的同位语，提到前面起强调作用。
  • can still be seen: a modal in the passive.can still be seen：情态 + 被动。

Sentence 4第 4 句 (begins开头 “Before they were …”)

  • be used to do means to be employed for a purpose; be used to doing means to have the habit.be used to do：被用来做（≠ be used to doing 习惯于）。

Sentence 6第 6 句 (begins开头 “The Cutty Sark …”)

  • A superlative plus that has ever been done: the best of its kind up to now.最高级 + that has ever been done：有史以来……的。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

The unreal past conditional: if + had done, would have done与过去事实相反的虚拟：if + had done, would have done
To suppose the opposite of what has already happened, put had (not) done in the if-clause and would / could / might (not) have done in the main clause. The pattern is the usual way of expressing regret, or blame.
对已经发生的事作相反的假设：从句 had (not) done，主句 would/could/might (not) have done。用来表达惋惜、后悔或批评。

In this lesson, sentence twenty-eight: if she had not lost the rudder, she would have won.

本课：第 26 句——if she had not lost（舵），她 would have won（本可以赢）。

  • If I had left earlier, I would have caught the train.
  • If it hadn't rained, we could have gone to the beach.
A superlative plus that has ever been / the only ... to do最高级 + that has ever been / the only … to do
After a superlative, or after one of the plus a superlative, the relative clause usually goes into the present perfect to mean up to now, and the relative word is that. After the only, the first or the last plus a noun, an infinitive can be used instead of a clause.
最高级（或 one of the + 最高级）后的定语从句常用现在完成时，表示「迄今为止」，关系词用 that；the only / the first / the last + 名词后可用不定式作定语。
  • This is the best film that I have ever seen.
  • She was the only student to answer the question.
there is a danger that / there is no doubt that: clauses in appositionthere is a danger that / there is no doubt that：同位语从句
Abstract nouns such as danger, doubt, fact, news, idea and hope can be followed by a that-clause which says what they consist of. This that plays no part in its own clause, but it cannot be left out.
抽象名词（danger、doubt、fact、news、idea、hope）后接 that 从句说明其内容，叫同位语从句；that 不作成分，不能省。

In this lesson, sentence twenty: there was a danger that her makeshift rudder might go the same way; sentence twenty-eight: There is no doubt that she would have won.

本课：第 18 句 there was a danger that 临时舵也会被扯掉；第 26 句 There is no doubt that 她本来会赢。

  • There is no doubt that he is the best player.
  • The news that she had won surprised us all.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • The only other … to match her was … 唯一能和她匹敌的……是……
    The only other team to match them was Brazil.
  • It marked the end of … and the beginning of … 它标志着……的结束和……的开始
    The smartphone marked the end of one age and the beginning of another.
  • It seemed certain that …, but … 看来……已成定局，可是……
    It seemed certain that we would win, but we lost in the last minute.

Summary writing摘要写作

In no more than eighty words, tell the story of the race of eighteen seventy-two and say why the Cutty Sark lost it.

用不超过 80 个词讲述 1872 年那场帆船比赛，说明卡蒂萨克号为什么输了。

Points a good summary covers好摘要要覆盖的要点
  • In June eighteen seventy-two the Cutty Sark and the Thermopylae left Shanghai to see which would reach England first.1872 年 6 月，卡蒂萨克号和塞莫皮莱号从上海出发，比赛谁先到英国。
  • The Cutty Sark went ahead in the Indian Ocean, but a storm in August took her rudder away, and she had to sail slowly with a makeshift one.卡蒂萨克号在印度洋领先，但 8 月遭遇风暴，舵被扯掉，只好装上临时舵慢慢航行。
  • By the time a new rudder had been fitted, after the Equator, her rival was more than five hundred miles in front.过了赤道进港换新舵时，对手已领先 500 多英里。
  • She reached England a week behind the other ship, and the writer thinks she would have won easily but for the rudder.它比对手晚一周到达英国；要不是丢了舵，它本可以轻松获胜。
Our model summary本站范文

In June 1872 the Cutty Sark and the Thermopylae, the two fastest sailing ships of their day, left Shanghai in a race to England. The Cutty Sark went ahead in the Indian Ocean, but a storm in August destroyed her rudder. A makeshift one forced her to sail slowly, and she lost her lead. By the time a new rudder was fitted, her rival was far ahead. She reached England a week late but would surely have won otherwise.（79 词）
