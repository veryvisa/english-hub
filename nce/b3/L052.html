Lesson 52 · Mud is Mud 泥就是泥

📘 New Concept English · Book 3 · Lesson 52 “Mud is Mud”新概念英语 第三册 Lesson 52《Mud is Mud》

B2🧠 33 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
The writer's cousin Harry keeps an oddly shaped, pale green bottle in his study, and inside is a thick grey paste: scented mud. If you doubt him, he asks you to sniff it and rub a little on your skin. The bottle has a story he loves to tell, and it also cured him of a habit of many years. He used to walk into smart beauty shops and make up products on the spot, such as a perfume called Scented Shadow or bath cubes that would not dissolve. When the assistant said she had never heard of them, he acted hurt; he was happiest when told an item was out of stock for now, and he always promised to return, which he never did. One day in an expensive London shop he asked for Myrolite, gravely describing it as a hard yellow-brown stuff for removing freckles, and the assistant hunted through her shelves and offered to order it. Carried away, he then asked for scented mud, expecting a blank stare. Instead her face lit up and she brought out several bottles. Harry had to give in, picked the smallest, paid twenty pounds and hurried out. This is a character sketch with a twist: first the strange bottle, then the story of how he got it, and the joke is that the joker is caught out. The grammar to learn is on permanent display, what looks like, If you were to ..., used to and would for past habits, On doing, keep a straight face, be one's turn to, and get away with.
作者的表兄哈里书房里摆着一只形状古怪的淡绿色瓶子，里面装着灰糊糊的东西——香泥。你若不信，他就请你闻一闻、往皮肤上抹一点。这瓶泥背后有个他爱讲的故事，而且治好了他一个多年的坏毛病：他爱跑进高档化妆品店，当场编出根本不存在的商品名，比如「香影」香水、「不溶浴块」；店员说没听过，他就装作很不高兴；听说「暂时缺货」最开心，答应改天再来，当然从不再去。有一天在伦敦一家高级店，他要「麦罗莱特」，还一本正经解释是去雀斑用的琥珀状硬块，店员翻遍货架、答应替他订货。他得意忘形，又要「香泥」，满心等着看店员发呆——没想到店员眼睛一亮，搬出好几瓶。哈里只好认输，挑了最小的一瓶，付了二十镑赶紧溜走。本课是带反转的人物小品：先写怪瓶子，再倒叙来历，笑点在捉弄人的人反被将了一军。学 on permanent display、what looks like、If you were to …、used to / would 表过去习惯、On doing、keep a straight face、be one's turn to、get away with。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. What was the little game Harry used to play?哈里过去爱玩什么「小把戏」？
  2. What did he expect when he asked for scented mud, and what actually happened?他要「香泥」的时候期待发生什么？实际发生了什么？
  3. Why did he give the game up?哈里为什么从此不再玩这个把戏？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander, in the light comic manner of an English magazine sketch; Harry and the London shop are inventions, and the products Harry dreams up are meant to sound absurd.

课文为编者 L. G. 亚历山大自撰，是英国杂志小品式的轻喜剧；哈里和那家伦敦店都是虚构的，他编的那些商品就是要听起来荒唐。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

The joke depends on a real fact that has only grown more true: mud really is sold as a beauty product. Black mud from the shores of the Dead Sea, rich in salts and minerals, has been exported for skin care for decades, and spa treatments with heated volcanic or clay mud, often called fango in Italian resorts, go back much further. Clay masks are a standard item in today's chemists. Whether these products do more than ordinary clay is less clear; claims about minerals soaking into the skin are mostly marketing, though clay does absorb oil and can calm some skin conditions. The shop in the lesson is therefore not foolish at all. It is Harry who has fallen behind the market.

这个笑话靠的是一个真实的事实，而且今天比当年更真：泥确实被当作美容品出售。死海岸边富含盐分和矿物质的黑泥，作为护肤品出口已有几十年；意大利温泉地用加热的火山泥或黏土泥做理疗（常叫 fango）的历史更久。黏土面膜如今是药妆店的常规货。这些产品是否比普通黏土更有用就不那么清楚了：「矿物质渗进皮肤」的说法大多是营销，不过黏土确实能吸油，对某些皮肤问题有舒缓作用。所以课文里那家店一点也不傻，落在市场后面的是哈里。

✨ How it is written赏析

The story is told backwards so that the reader sees the trophy before the battle. We start with the bottle on its shelf, then a teasing invitation to smell it, then the history of the habit, and only at the end the scene that produced the bottle. This order turns the ending into a promise kept: we know from the first paragraph that the mud exists, so the pleasure lies in learning how. The habit is described with repeated would, which makes Harry's tricks sound like a well-practised routine, and the London scene speeds up with short actions until the single line where the trick turns round. The writer stays a polite cousin throughout, admitting he cannot understand how Harry kept from laughing, which lets us enjoy the mischief without approving of it.

故事倒着讲，读者先看见战利品，再看战斗。我们先看到架子上的瓶子，再被半开玩笑地请去闻一闻，然后是那个习惯的来历，最后才是买到瓶子的那一幕。这种顺序把结尾变成兑现承诺：第一段就知道泥是真的，乐趣在于知道怎么来的。习惯部分反复用 would，让哈里的恶作剧听起来像练熟的套路；伦敦那一场用短促的动作加快节奏，直到戏法翻转的那一句。作者全程是个客气的表弟，承认自己想不通哈里怎么忍住不笑——这让我们享受恶作剧，又不必赞成它。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

Harry's game would play differently today. A shop assistant now has a search box, and many would simply say the product does not exist, or offer something close. The more interesting change is that inventing names for beauty products has become an industry: a cream with an exotic ingredient, a serum with a scientific-sounding word, a mask made of rare mud. Harry's Myrolite would not look out of place on a real shelf. For readers in China, where beauty shopping has moved online and bought through livestreams, the lesson's moral still holds in a new form: the more confident and fanciful the name, the more carefully you should ask what is actually in the bottle. The shop assistant was not the fool; the buyer who pays for a name is.

哈里的把戏放到今天会是另一个样子。店员手边有搜索框，很多人会直接说没有这东西，或推荐一个相近的。更有意思的变化是：给美容产品编名字已经成了一门生意——含异域成分的面霜、带科学味词汇的精华、用稀有泥做的面膜。哈里的「麦罗莱特」放在真货架上也不显突兀。对中国读者来说，美妆消费已经转到网上和直播间，这一课的道理换了个样子依然成立：名字越自信、越花哨，越要仔细问瓶子里到底装着什么。傻的不是店员，而是为一个名字付钱的买家。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 1第 1 句 (begins开头 “My cousin, Harry, …”)

  • An adverb joined to a past participle makes a compound adjective before the noun.curiously-shaped：副词 + 过去分词构成复合形容词，「形状奇特的」。
  • on permanent display is museum language, used here with a smile.on permanent display：长期陈列，像博物馆展品，带点调侃。

Sentence 3第 3 句 (begins开头 “an observant visitor …”)

  • despite cannot take a clause by itself, so the fact that is added.Despite the fact that …：尽管……（despite 后不能直接接从句，要借 the fact that）。
  • would describes what any such visitor would do.would：表推测的可能情况，「（任何）细心的客人都会注意到」。
  • what looks like means something that seems to be, without saying for sure what it is.what looks like：看上去像……的东西，what 引导宾语从句作介词宾语。

Sentence 4第 4 句 (begins开头 “If you were …”)

  • If you were to do sets up an imagined future event, a little more tentative than if you asked.If you were to do：对将来的假设，语气比 if you asked 更「假如真的」。

Sentence 8第 8 句 (begins开头 “How Harry came …”)

  • The whole How clause is the subject, so the verb is singular.How … stuff：主语从句，谓语用单数 makes。
  • come into the possession of means to come to own something, a formal phrase.come into (the) possession of：得到（正式）。
  • be fond of doing means to like doing; relate here means to tell.be fond of doing：喜欢做。relate：讲述（正式）。

Sentence 10第 10 句 (begins开头 “Harry used to …”)

  • used to: a past habit that has stopped.used to：过去的习惯，现在已不再。
  • it stands in for the infinitive that follows, which is the real object.consider it + 名词 + to do：it 是形式宾语，真正的宾语是后面的不定式。

Sentence 17第 17 句 (begins开头 “How Harry managed …”)

  • keep a straight face: to stop yourself from laughing.keep a straight face：忍住不笑，绷着脸。
  • be beyond me: I cannot understand it.be beyond me：我理解不了。

Sentence 28第 28 句 (begins开头 “However, it was …”)

  • it is one's turn to do: here the joke is reversed, and now he is the one surprised.it is one's turn to do：轮到某人……；这里是反讽：本该别人吃惊，这回轮到他了。
  • light up: of eyes or a face, to become bright with pleasure.light up：（眼睛）发亮，露出喜色。

Sentence 32第 32 句 (begins开头 “He was glad …”)

  • get away with here means to escape paying more than this.get away with：只付出（较小代价）就脱身。
  • a mere stresses how small the sum was; beat a hasty retreat means to leave in a hurry; clutching tells us how he went.a mere：仅仅；beat a hasty retreat：匆忙溜走；clutching 伴随状语。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

used to and would for past habitsused to 与 would：过去的习惯
used to do describes a past habit or state that has stopped. would do describes only repeated past actions, not states, and is used once the past setting is clear.
used to do＝过去常常（现在不再），可表动作也可表状态；would do 只表过去反复的动作，不表状态，多在已经交代了过去背景之后用。

Sentence 10 sets up the habit with used to, then sentences 11 to 18 switch to would to describe each step of the routine.

本课：第 10 句先用 used to 交代习惯，第 13、15、16、18 句接着用 would，一连串描述他每次怎么做。

  • We used to live near the sea. (state: would is not possible)
  • Every summer my father would take us fishing.
If ... were to do: imagined future eventsIf + were to do：对将来的假设
If plus were to do, with would or might in the main clause, imagines a future event that is unlikely or purely supposed. It sounds more hypothetical than the plain past form.
If + 主语 + were to do, … would/might do：对将来不太可能或纯属设想的情况；比 If + 过去式更「设想」。

Sentence 4 imagines the reader asking Harry about the bottle.

本课：第 4 句用 If you were to ask 设想「如果你问他」。

  • If the river were to flood, the whole village would be under water.
  • If you were to win the lottery, what would you do?
On doing: as soon ason / upon doing：一……就
On or upon with an -ing form means as soon as, and is written style. The person doing it must be the subject of the sentence.
On/Upon + 动名词＝as soon as / when，书面语；逻辑主语必须是句子主语。

Sentence 12 tells us what he did the moment he walked in.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • X keeps … on permanent display in … X 把……长期摆在……
    My aunt keeps her wedding dress on permanent display in a glass case.
  • … holds what seems to be … ……里装着看上去像……的东西
    The jar held what seemed to be old coffee.
  • How X managed to … is quite beyond me. X 怎么能……，我实在想不通
    How she managed to finish in an hour is quite beyond me.
  • This time it was X's turn to be … 这回轮到 X ……了
    This time it was the teacher's turn to be surprised.
  • X was glad to escape with a mere … X 只花了……就脱身，很高兴
    He was glad to escape with a mere scratch.

Summary writing摘要写作

In no more than 80 words, describe Harry's game and how he came to buy the bottle of mud and stop playing it.

用不超过 80 个词讲哈里的把戏，以及他是怎么买到那瓶香泥、从此不再玩的。

Points a good summary covers好摘要要覆盖的要点
  • Harry used to ask in expensive beauty shops for goods he had invented, to watch the assistants struggle.哈里以前爱在高档化妆品店要根本不存在的商品，看店员出糗。
  • Once in London he asked for Myrolite, and after searching everywhere the assistant offered to get it in for him.有一次在伦敦，他要「麦罗莱特」，店员找遍货架后答应替他订。
  • Pleased with himself, he asked for scented mud, expecting a puzzled look, but she at once produced several bottles.他得意之下又要香泥，以为店员会愣住，没想到她立刻拿出几瓶。
  • He had to give in, paid twenty pounds for the smallest bottle, and never played the game again.他只好认输，花二十镑买下最小的一瓶，从此不再玩这个把戏。
Our model summary本站范文

Harry once loved to visit smart beauty shops and ask for products he had just made up. One day in London he asked for Myrolite, and the assistant searched in vain and offered to order it. Delighted, Harry then asked for scented mud, sure she would be lost for words. Instead she cheerfully brought out several bottles. Beaten for once, he bought the smallest for twenty pounds and left quickly. That bottle ended his game for good.（77 词）
