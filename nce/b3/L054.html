Lesson 54 · Instinct or Cleverness 本能还是机智？

📘 New Concept English · Book 3 · Lesson 54 “Instinct or Cleverness”新概念英语 第三册 Lesson 54《Instinct or Cleverness》

B2🧠 31 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
We are taught from childhood to fear insects. They spoil food, spread disease and eat crops; they sting and bite for no reason, and on summer nights they fly into our rooms or knock against lit windows. We fear not only nasty ones like spiders and wasps but harmless ones like moths. Reading increases knowledge without removing the fear: knowing how well ants are organised does not stop disgust when a crowd of them covers a picnic, and however much we like honey or admire a bee's sense of direction, we hate being stung. Yet insects also hold a strange appeal. We like to read about them, above all about creatures like the praying mantis whose lives are truly horrible, and we love to watch a spider leap on a fly or a line of ants carry home a huge dead beetle. Last summer the writer spent days watching thousands of ants climb his treasured peach tree, which grows against a warm wall, has lived through several hard winters and now and then bears juicy fruit. The leaves were drying up, the undersides were covered with aphids, and the ants came to collect a sweet liquid from them. He wrapped sticky tape round the base of the trunk; the ants would not cross it and ran about confused, and at midnight, by torchlight, they were still circling. Early next morning he found they had a new route: up the house wall and across onto the leaves. This is an essay that argues first and experiments second: the first half describes our mixture of fear and fascination, the second uses one failed experiment to show how clever insects are. The grammar to learn is be brought up to, do more harm than good, wage war on, live in dread of, No matter how, do nothing to prevent, embark on, making it impossible for, and in despair.
我们从小就被教着怕昆虫：它们污染食物、传播疾病、啃食庄稼，无缘无故叮人咬人，夏夜不请自来飞进屋、撞着亮灯的窗。不光蜘蛛、黄蜂让人害怕，连无害的飞蛾也怕。读书能增进了解，却消除不了恐惧：知道蚂蚁社会组织严密，看到一大群爬满精心准备的野餐，照样恶心；再爱吃蜂蜜、再佩服蜜蜂的方向感，也怕被蜇。可昆虫又奇妙地迷人：我们爱读它们（尤其是螳螂那种可怕的一生），爱看蜘蛛扑向苍蝇、蚁群扛着巨大的死甲虫凯旋。去年夏天，作者在花园里一连几天看成千上万只蚂蚁爬上他心爱的桃树——那棵树靠着暖墙、熬过几个严冬、偶尔结出多汁的桃子。叶子开始枯萎，背面爬满蚜虫，蚂蚁来吸蚜虫分泌的「蜜」。他在树根缠上粘胶带，蚂蚁不敢过，团团转；半夜打着手电去看，还在打转。第二天一早，他发现蚂蚁找到了新路：先爬上房子的墙，再爬到树叶上。本课是先议论、后实验的随笔：前半写人对昆虫又怕又爱，后半用一次失败的小实验证明昆虫的机灵。学 be brought up to、do more harm than good、wage war on、live in dread of、No matter how、do nothing to prevent、embark on、making it impossible for、in despair。

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander as a personal essay; the peach tree and the experiment read as the writer's own invention or experience, and we have not found an earlier source.

课文为编者 L. G. 亚历山大自撰的个人随笔；桃树和那次实验读来像是作者自己编的或亲历的，我们没有找到更早的出处。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

What the writer saw is one of the best known partnerships in nature. Aphids feed on plant sap and give off a sugary liquid called honeydew; many kinds of ant gather it, and in return they guard the aphids from ladybirds and other enemies, even carrying them to fresh shoots. Scientists call this kind of exchange mutualism, and some ants are said to farm aphids as people keep cattle. The writer's method is also a real gardening technique: sticky bands or grease rings round a tree trunk are still sold to stop crawling insects. Gardeners know the weak point he found, which is that any bridge, such as a wall, a fence, a stake or a branch touching another plant, lets the ants walk round the barrier. The usual advice today is to prune anything touching the tree before putting the band on.

作者看到的是自然界最有名的合作关系之一。蚜虫吸植物汁液，排出一种叫「蜜露」的含糖液体；许多种蚂蚁收集它，作为回报替蚜虫挡住瓢虫等天敌，甚至把蚜虫搬到新嫩枝上。科学家把这种互惠叫「互利共生」，有些蚂蚁被说成像人养牛一样「放牧」蚜虫。作者的办法也是真实的园艺技巧：至今还有人卖缠在树干上的粘虫带或油脂环，专门挡爬行的昆虫。园丁都知道他碰到的那个弱点：只要有一座「桥」——墙、篱笆、支柱，或碰到别的植物的枝条——蚂蚁就能绕过屏障。今天的通常建议是：先把碰着树的东西都剪开，再缠粘虫带。

✨ How it is written赏析

The essay sets a trap for its reader and then springs it on its writer. The first half speaks in a confident we, listing our fears and our fascinations in long balanced sentences, so that the voice sounds like an expert's. Then the writer steps into the garden alone and becomes the one who is watched. The experiment is told in small time markers, for a long time, at midnight, early next morning, which slow the story down and build our expectation of success. The midnight visit, with its note of satisfaction and the word surprise in brackets, is a lovely touch: he is already half aware that the ants are better than he thought. The final sentence calls his own methods unscientific, which turns the title question round. Whether the ants were clever or simply following instinct, they were more effective than the man.

文章给读者设了一个圈套，最后套住的却是作者自己。前半用自信的「我们」，长而匀称的句子列出我们的害怕和着迷，声音像个专家。然后作者独自走进花园，反而成了被观察的那一个。实验用一个个小时间标记来讲——很久、半夜、第二天一早——把故事放慢，让我们期待成功。半夜那次探看，带着「满意」以及括号里的「惊讶」，是很妙的一笔：他已经隐约觉得蚂蚁比他想的厉害。最后一句说自己的方法毫不科学，把标题里的问题调了个头：蚂蚁是聪明还是只凭本能都不要紧，它们比人更有效。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The title asks whether the ants acted from instinct or cleverness, and modern research suggests the line is less sharp than the question assumes. Ant colonies solve problems through many simple individual actions: scouts wander, find a path, and leave chemical trails that others follow and strengthen. No single ant needs to understand the problem for the group to find a new route overnight. Computer scientists have borrowed this idea, called ant colony optimisation, to plan delivery routes and networks. The essay's first half also rings true today: studies of insect populations in Europe report steep falls in recent decades, which makes our habit of waging war on all insects look less wise. A gardener now might see the aphids and ants as a problem for ladybirds to solve. Chinese gardeners and farmers have a similar old practice: citrus growers in southern China placed weaver ants in their orchards to eat pests, one of the oldest recorded uses of insects to control other insects.

标题问蚂蚁是凭本能还是靠机智，现代研究表明，这条界线没有问题设想的那么分明。蚁群靠许多简单的个体行为解决问题：侦察蚁四处游走，找到路，留下化学气味，其他蚂蚁顺着走并不断加强。不需要任何一只蚂蚁「懂得」问题，整个群体就能一夜之间找到新路。计算机科学家借用了这个思路，叫「蚁群算法」，用来规划配送路线和网络。文章前半在今天也站得住：欧洲对昆虫数量的研究报告说，近几十年数量大幅下降，这让我们对所有昆虫「开战」的习惯显得不那么明智。今天的园丁看到蚜虫和蚂蚁，也许会把它们交给瓢虫去解决。中国的果农也有类似的老办法：华南柑橘园在树上放黄猄蚁吃害虫，这是有记载的最早的「以虫治虫」之一。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 3第 3 句 (begins开头 “We have been …”)

  • be brought up to do: to be taught from childhood to do or feel something.be brought up to do：从小被教育要……；bring up＝养育。

Sentence 4第 4 句 (begins开头 “We regard them …”)

  • regard A as B: think of A as being B.regard A as B：把 A 看作 B。
  • do more harm than good: cause more problems than benefits.do more harm than good：弊大于利。

Sentence 5第 5 句 (begins开头 “We continually wage …”)

  • wage war on: to fight against, used here with deliberate exaggeration.wage war on：向……开战（正式说法，这里是夸张）。
  • for gives the reason after a comma.for：因为（补充理由，前有逗号）。

Sentence 7第 7 句 (begins开头 “We live in …”)

  • live in dread of: to be always afraid of.live in dread of：一直害怕……。
  • Two of phrases are parallel; also is left out after but.not only of … but of …：两个 of 短语平行；注意第二部分省了 also。

Sentence 10第 10 句 (begins开头 “does nothing to …”)

  • The -ing phrase is the subject, so the verb is singular.Knowing that …：动名词短语作主语，谓语 does 单数。
  • do nothing to prevent: be of no help at all in stopping.do nothing to prevent sb from doing：一点也阻止不了某人……。
  • find + object + -ing describes what you see happening.find hordes of them crawling：find + 宾语 + 现在分词。

Sentence 11第 11 句 (begins开头 “No matter how …”)

  • No matter how much means however much.No matter how much＝However much，无论多么……。
  • have a horror of doing: be terrified of it; the -ing form here is passive.have a horror of doing：非常害怕……；being stung 被动动名词。

Sentence 26第 26 句 (begins开头 “I immediately embarked …”)

  • The core is an experiment which kept me fascinated; the even though clause is set into the middle.主干：an experiment which kept me fascinated；中间插入让步从句 even though …。
  • embark on: begin something important; keep sb fascinated: hold someone's interest.embark on：开始（较大的事）；keep sb + 形容词：使某人保持……。

Sentence 27第 27 句 (begins开头 “I bound the …”)

  • The -ing phrase gives the result, with it standing in for the infinitive.making it impossible for sb to do：现在分词作结果状语，it 是形式宾语。
  • bound is the past of bind. aphides is an old plural; today we write aphids.bound 是 bind 的过去式。aphides 是 aphid 的旧式复数，今天多写 aphids。

Sentence 32第 32 句 (begins开头 “I got up …”)

  • hoping describes his state of mind; had given up is past perfect.hoping …：伴随状语；had given up：过去完成时，「（到那时）已经放弃」。
  • in despair: having lost all hope.in despair：绝望地。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

-ing phrases as the subject动名词短语作主语
An -ing phrase can be the subject of a sentence and takes a singular verb. It is often used for general truths and experience.
动名词短语可以作主语，谓语用单数；常用来表达一般性的事实或经验。

Sentence 8 starts with Reading about them as subject; in sentences 9 and 10 a long phrase beginning with Knowing is the subject, and its verb does not arrive until much later.

本课：第 8 句的主语是 Reading about them；第 9–10 句的主语是一整个以 Knowing 开头的长短语，谓语 does nothing to prevent 要隔很远才出现。

  • Learning a language takes time.
  • Knowing the rules does not make you a good player.
No matter how, what, who ...No matter how / what / who … 让步状语
No matter followed by a question word means however, whatever, whoever and so on. After how, you usually put an adjective, adverb or much and many.
No matter + 疑问词（how/what/who/where/when）＝however/whatever/whoever…，无论……都……。how 后常接形容词、副词或 much/many。

Sentences 11 and 12 use two how much clauses: however much we like honey or read about bees, we still fear stings.

本课：第 11–12 句并列两个 how much，说再喜欢蜂蜜、再了解蜜蜂，也怕被蜇。

  • No matter how hard he tried, he could not open the jar.
  • No matter what you say, I won't change my mind.
-ing for a result: ..., making it hard for sb to do现在分词作结果状语：…, making it + adj. + for sb to do
An -ing phrase after a comma at the end of a sentence can give the natural result of the action before it. In make it plus adjective plus for sb to do, it stands in for the infinitive.
句末逗号后的现在分词短语可以表示前面动作自然带来的结果；make it + 形容词 + for sb to do 中 it 是形式宾语。

In sentence 27 the tape was wrapped round the trunk, with the result that the ants could not get to the aphids.

本课：第 27 句：他用胶带缠住树根，结果使蚂蚁够不着蚜虫。

  • Snow fell all night, making it difficult for cars to move.
  • The river flooded, cutting off the village.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • We have been taught from childhood to … 我们从小就被教着……
    We have been taught from childhood to respect older people.
  • X does more harm than good. X 弊大于利
    Shouting at children often does more harm than good.
  • Knowing that … does nothing to stop us from … 知道……一点也挡不住我们……
    Knowing that the plane is safe does nothing to stop me from feeling nervous.
  • Who has not stood in wonder at the sight of …? 谁没有对着……的景象惊叹过？
    Who has not stood in wonder at the sight of a rainbow?
  • I saw with sorrow that X had beaten me. 我难过地意识到自己被 X 打败了
    I saw with sorrow that a ten-year-old had beaten me.

Summary writing摘要写作

In no more than 80 words, describe how the writer tried to keep the ants off his peach tree and why he failed.

Points a good summary covers好摘要要覆盖的要点
  • The leaves of the writer's peach tree were drying up; aphids covered them, and crowds of ants came to collect their honey.作者的桃树叶子开始枯萎，背面有蚜虫，一大群蚂蚁来吸蚜虫的蜜。

  • At midnight he checked with a torch and was pleased to see them still circling.半夜去看，蚂蚁还在打转，他很满意。

Our model summary本站范文

Last summer the writer noticed that his peach tree was in trouble: aphids covered the leaves and thousands of ants came to feed on their honey. He wrapped sticky tape round the trunk so the ants could not climb it. They ran about in confusion, and at midnight they were still stuck below. He went to bed pleased, but early next morning he saw that they had found another way up, along the house wall. The ants had outwitted him.（80 词）
