Lesson 58 · A Spot of Bother 小麻烦

📘 New Concept English · Book 3 · Lesson 58 “A Spot of Bother”新概念英语 第三册 Lesson 58《A Spot of Bother》

B2🧠 23 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
An old lady comes home to her block of flats. Her basket has grown heavier all the way, and in the lift she thinks only of lunch and a rest. As she steps out, both are forgotten: her front door is open. Her first thought is to scold her home help for carelessness, until she remembers that she went out after the help had left and locked both locks herself. In the hall every room door stands open, though she always shuts them before leaving, and the area round her writing desk is in a mess. Clearly, burglars have broken in while she was away. She is tempted to search the rooms for them herself, but decides that at her age it would be wiser to have company, and fetches the porter from the basement. Her legs begin to shake, so she sits down with a strong cup of tea while he calls the police. Calm again, she goes through the flat with him, touching nothing, so as not to spoil the search for fingerprints. The mess is beyond belief. She has lived there thirty years and keeps everything, and it all seems to have been pulled out and turned over, though at least this will make it easier to throw away what she should have thrown away long ago. A police inspector and a constable arrive. The locks have not been forced, so the burglars must have used skeleton keys or come in over the balcony. There are no fingerprints, but there is a dirty red bundle holding jewellery that is not hers: the thieves had already robbed someone else that day and had been disturbed here. The inspector wants a list of losses within a day and tells her not to stay alone for a few nights. She thinks him fussy, but the porter agrees, so she phones her daughter and asks for help with what she calls a small spot of bother. This is a realistic story with a light comic touch: a calm, independent old woman faces a burglary, and her understatement at the end tells us most about her. The grammar to learn is be glad to be back, grow heavier with every step, forget in, reprimand sb for, as clear as daylight, force an entry, more prudent to, the absolute phrase her composure regained, be careful to, hinder sb in, as though, should have done, thereby, and must have been.
老太太买完东西回到公寓楼，篮子一路越拎越沉，电梯里只想着午饭和好好歇一歇。一出电梯，这两样全忘了：自家前门开着。她先想明天得训训钟点工这么大意，转念想起钟点工走后她才出门，两把锁都锁过。走进门厅，各屋门都开着，可她出门前照例全关了；客厅写字台那边一片狼藉——显然有贼趁她不在破门而入。她本想挨屋去找贼，又觉得这把年纪还是带个人更稳妥，就去地下室叫来门房；腿开始发抖，她坐下喝了杯浓茶，门房打电话报警。镇定下来后，两人小心翼翼、什么也不碰地把各屋查了一遍，免得妨碍警察找指纹。乱得难以想象：她在这里住了三十年，是个十足的「囤积狂」，似乎所有东西都被翻出来抖了个遍——倒也方便她清理那些早该扔掉的东西。警督和一名警员来了：门锁没被撬，说明贼要么用了万能钥匙，要么是从阳台进来的。没有指纹，却找到一个脏兮兮的红布包，里面的首饰老太太说不是她的——看来贼那天已作过一次案，在这里被惊走了。警督请她第二天前查清丢了什么，并劝她这几夜别一个人住。她觉得警督小题大做，可门房也这么说，她只好给女儿打电话，请她来帮忙处理这件「小麻烦」。本课是带幽默的写实叙事：一位镇定、要强的老太太面对入室盗窃，结尾的轻描淡写最见性格。学 be glad to be back、grow heavier with every step、forget in、reprimand sb for、as clear as daylight、force an entry、more prudent to、composure regained（独立主格）、be careful to、hinder sb in、as though、should have done、thereby、must have been。

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course; the old lady, her flat and her daughter are invented. The title uses a typically British phrase, a spot of bother, which plays down a serious event, and the lesson is built round that habit of understatement.

课文为教材编写，老太太、她的公寓和女儿都是虚构的。标题用的是一句典型的英式说法 a spot of bother（一点小麻烦），把严重的事说轻，整篇就是围绕这种轻描淡写的习惯写成的。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

Several details in the lesson belong to a particular world: a block of flats with a resident porter in the basement, a home help who comes in daily, and a drawing room with a writing desk. This is the London of mansion flats for the comfortable middle class, and of elderly women who often lived alone in them for decades. Skeleton keys, cut to open many simple old locks, were a real risk then, which is why the constable checks the locks before anything else. Police advice today is close to what the inspector gives: do not go in alone if you think someone may still be inside, touch nothing, list what is missing as soon as you can, and arrange for company if you feel unsafe.

课文里好几个细节属于一个特定的世界：带常驻门房、门房住地下室的公寓楼，每天来做事的钟点工，带写字台的客厅。这是伦敦那种供殷实中产住的「公寓大楼」，也是许多老年妇女独自在里面住上几十年的地方。当时万能钥匙能开很多结构简单的老锁，是真实的风险，所以警员先查门锁。今天的警方建议和警督说的很接近：如果怀疑有人还在屋里，不要独自进去；什么都别碰；尽快列出丢失的物品；觉得不安全就找人陪着。

✨ How it is written赏析

The comedy lies in the gap between what happens and how the old lady sees it. The story is told almost entirely from inside her head, so that we follow her thoughts in order: tiredness, lunch, an open door, a plan to scold the home help, and then the slow, logical move to the truth. Each step is a small piece of reasoning, which shows us a sharp mind before any description of her character. The writer allows her one moment of weakness, the trembling legs and the strong tea, and then restores her in a single absolute phrase. The best joke is the line about sorting out what she should have thrown away, where she finds a benefit in disaster. The ending repeats the trick: the inspector's sensible advice is dismissed as fussy, and the burglary is shrunk to a spot of bother.

喜剧在于事情本身和老太太看待它的方式之间的落差。故事几乎全在她的脑子里讲，我们按顺序跟着她想：累、午饭、开着的门、要训钟点工的念头，然后一步步慢慢、有条理地走向真相。每一步都是一小段推理，在任何性格描写之前就让我们看到一个清醒的头脑。作者只允许她软弱一下——发抖的腿和浓茶——然后用一个独立主格就让她恢复过来。最妙的笑点是那句「倒也方便她清理早该扔掉的东西」：她在灾难里找到了好处。结尾重复了这一招：警督合情合理的建议被她看成小题大做，入室盗窃被缩成了「一点小麻烦」。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

British understatement can be charming, but the lesson also shows its cost: the old lady nearly searches for burglars on her own, and she accepts help only when two men agree. Readers who care for elderly parents will recognise this. Older people living alone are often targeted by burglars and, increasingly, by phone and online scams, and many of them hide the event from their families out of pride or a wish not to be a burden. A good son or daughter does what this daughter is asked to do: comes, sorts things out, and does not make a fuss. In Chinese, the matching habit is the two-word reply meaning it's nothing, which parents use to keep worries from their children; the practical lesson is to ask twice.

英式轻描淡写可以很可爱，但这一课也显示了它的代价：老太太差点一个人去找贼，而且要两个男人都这么说，她才肯接受帮助。照顾年迈父母的读者会认出这一点。独居老人常常是入室盗窃的目标，如今越来越多是电话和网络诈骗的目标，很多人出于自尊或不想给子女添麻烦而瞒着家人。好儿女就做这位女儿被请去做的事：来，帮着收拾，不大惊小怪。中文里对应的习惯是一句「没事」，父母用它把担心挡在子女之外；实际的启示是：多问一遍。

Sentence 2第 2 句 (begins开头 “Her shopping had …”)

  • A comparative with with every shows a change that grows step by step.grew heavier（with every step）：每走一步就重一点，比较级 + with every …。

Sentence 4第 4 句 (begins开头 “but when she …”)

  • both refers back to the two things she was thinking of.both：指前面的 lunch 和 a good rest。
  • The noun phrase does the work of a clause, and the that clause explains the discovery.in her sudden discovery that …：名词化写法，相当于 when she suddenly discovered that …；that 引导同位语从句。

Sentence 8第 8 句 (begins开头 “yet following her …”)

Sentence 20第 20 句 (begins开头 “and it seemed …”)

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

should have done, must have done, might be doingshould have done / must have done / might be doing：情态动词的时间与语气
should have done means something ought to have happened but did not, used for blame or regret. must have done or must have been done is a confident guess about the past. might be doing means something may be happening right now.
should have done＝本该做而没做（批评或后悔）；must have done/been done＝对过去有把握的推测；might be doing＝此刻可能正在做。

Sentence 15 imagines thieves who may still be hiding; sentence 21 says she ought to have thrown things out long ago; sentence 26 concludes the burglars were disturbed.

本课：第 15 句推测贼可能还潜伏着；第 21 句说那些东西早该扔；第 26 句推测贼一定是被惊扰了。

  • You should have told me earlier.
  • The door was open; someone must have left it unlocked.
  • Don't call now; she might be sleeping.
Absolute phrases: noun plus participle独立主格结构：名词 + 分词
An absolute phrase is a noun or pronoun followed by a participle, adjective or prepositional phrase. It has its own subject, separate from the sentence subject, and gives time, reason or accompanying details. A past participle shows something done or completed.
独立主格＝名词/代词 + 分词（或形容词、介词短语），有自己的「主语」，不依附句子主语，常用来交代时间、原因或伴随情况；过去分词表被动或已完成。

Sentence 15 uses her composure regained, meaning once she was calm again.

本课：第 15 句用 her composure regained，意思是「她恢复镇静以后」。

  • The work done, we went home.
  • She sat by the fire, her eyes closed.
Nominalisation: turning actions into nouns名词化：把动作变成名词
Written English often turns a verb into a noun inside a prepositional phrase, which makes the sentence tighter and more formal: when she discovered becomes in her discovery, and while she was away becomes during her absence.
书面语常把动词变成名词放进介词短语里，句子更紧凑、更正式：when she discovered → in her discovery；while she was absent → during her absence。

Sentences 4, 12 and 24 all turn an action into a noun phrase.

本课：第 4 句的 her sudden discovery；第 10 句的 during her absence；第 22 句的 her discovery（of the flat）。

  • On his arrival, the band began to play. (= When he arrived)
  • Her refusal to help surprised us. (= She refused to help)

Summary writing摘要写作

In no more than 80 words, tell what happened after the old lady found that her flat had been burgled.

用不超过 80 个词讲老太太回家发现被盗后发生的事。

Points a good summary covers好摘要要覆盖的要点
  • Back from shopping, the old lady found her front door and all the room doors open and her desk in disorder, and realised there had been a burglary.老太太购物回来，发现前门开着，各屋门也开着，写字台一片混乱，知道有贼进来过。
  • She fetched the porter, who called the police, and they looked round without touching anything; the flat had been turned upside down.她找来门房报警，两人什么都不碰地查看了屋子，里面翻得乱七八糟。
  • The police found the locks untouched and no fingerprints, but discovered a bundle of jewellery that was not hers.警察发现门锁没被撬，也没有指纹，但找到一包不是她的首饰。
  • Told by the inspector that she should not be alone, she phoned her daughter for help.警督劝她别一个人住，她就打电话请女儿来帮忙。
Our model summary本站范文

Returning from the shops, an old lady found her flat door open and realised that burglars had broken in. She fetched the porter, who phoned the police, and the two of them looked round carefully. Everything had been thrown about. The police found no sign that the locks had been forced and no fingerprints, but they did find a bundle of someone else's jewellery. Advised not to stay alone, she rang her daughter about her little spot of bother.（79 词）
