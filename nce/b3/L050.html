Lesson 50 · New Year Resolutions 新年的决心

📘 New Concept English · Book 3 · Lesson 50 “New Year Resolutions”新概念英语 第三册 Lesson 50《New Year Resolutions》

B2🧠 30 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
The New Year is the season for resolutions. Most of us could reel off a long list of things to do and not do, and they are the same each year: get up earlier, eat less, spend more time with the children, finish countless jobs around the house, be kind to people we dislike, drive with care, walk the dog daily. Experience tells us some of these are out of reach. Most of us fail because our plans are too big and we never find the time, and we make the basic mistake of telling everyone, so that we look all the more foolish when we slide back. Having learned this, the writer set himself just two small aims this year and told nobody: exercise every morning and read more in the evenings. A New Year's Eve party gave him an excuse to skip the first day. On the second he began, rising eleven minutes early to exercise in secret in the living room, but he came to breakfast so worn out that he gave himself away, and next morning the whole family came to watch. He took their teasing in good humour, yet his keenness faded, the eleven minutes shrank to nothing, and by January the tenth he was back where he started. He then told himself that less tiring exercise would leave him fresh for evening reading. For a few nights he resisted the television, but one cold, lonely evening he went downstairs, sat in front of it pretending to read, and was soon dozing again. He has not given up the reading resolution; he has just bought a book on reading a thousand words a minute, but has not had time to read it. This is an essay that argues and then laughs at itself: the first half lists the mistakes everyone makes, and the second half shows the writer making every one. The grammar to learn is year in year out, be beyond attainment, slip back into, Aware of ... as an opening phrase, of an evening, apply oneself to, the cleft sentence It was this that, fend off, and prove to be one's undoing.
新年是下决心的时候。我们心里都能列出一长串「要做」和「不做」，而且年年都是老一套：早起、少吃、多陪孩子、做完一千零一件家务、对不喜欢的人客气、小心开车、每天遛狗。经验告诉我们有些事做不到；多数人失败，是因为计划太大、没时间执行，还犯了一个根本错误——把决心告诉所有人，退回老路时就更丢脸。作者吸取教训，今年只给自己定两个小目标、而且不声张：每天早上锻炼，晚上多读书。元旦通宵派对给了他第一天不做的借口；第二天他开始了，每天提前 11 分钟起床，偷偷在客厅做操，可早饭时累得筋疲力尽，露了馅，第二天全家都来围观。他好脾气地顶住了嘲笑，可热情很快消退，11 分钟一点点减到零，到 1 月 10 日回到原点。他转而安慰自己：早上少累一点，晚上就有精神读书。几个晚上他抵住电视的诱惑，可一天晚上觉得又冷又孤单，下楼坐在电视前假装看书，结果又在屏幕前打起盹来。他至今没放弃多读书的决心，刚买了一本《如何一分钟读一千字》——只是还没时间读。本课是先议论后自嘲的小品，议论部分写人人都犯的错，叙述部分作者一样不落地全犯了。学 year in year out、be beyond attainment、slip back into、Aware of …（形容词短语作状语）、of an evening、apply oneself to、It was this that（强调句）、fend off、prove to be one's undoing。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. How did the writer's New Year resolutions come to an end?作者的新年决心最后是怎么收场的？
  2. Why, according to the writer, do most people fail to improve themselves?作者认为多数人为什么实现不了自我改进的计划？
  3. Which mistake did the writer avoid, and which did he still make?作者避开了哪个错误，又犯了哪些错误？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander. Like the lawn-mower lesson a few pages earlier, it sets out general truths and then makes the writer the main exhibit, and the book title at the end is a joke rather than a real publication.

课文为编者 L. G. 亚历山大为教材自撰。和前几课修割草机那篇一样，它先摆出普遍道理，再把作者自己当成头号展品；结尾那本书名是个玩笑，并不是真有其书。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

Two of the writer's claims have since been studied, with mixed results. First, do resolutions fail? A well-known American study published in 2002 followed people who made New Year resolutions and people who wanted to change but made none. After six months, about 46 per cent of the resolvers said they were still keeping their resolution, against only about 4 per cent of the others. So resolutions often fail, but people who make them do better than people who do not. Second, should you keep them secret? Here the writer has some support. In experiments published in 2009, psychologists found that when people announced a goal tied to who they wanted to be, such as becoming a lawyer, and others took note of it, they then worked less hard towards it, as if the announcement itself gave them some of the satisfaction of success. Finally, the book on reading a thousand words a minute: a major review of reading research in 2016 concluded that speed-reading methods cannot greatly raise reading speed without a loss in understanding. The writer's unread book would probably not have solved his problem.

作者的两个论断后来都有人研究过，结果有好有坏。第一，决心会落空吗？2002 年发表的一项知名的美国研究，追踪了立新年决心的人和想改变却没立决心的人。六个月后，立决心的人中约 46% 说自己还在坚持，另一组只有约 4%。所以决心常会落空，但立决心的人比不立的人做得好。第二，该不该保密？这一点作者有些依据。2009 年发表的一组实验发现，当人们宣布一个与「想成为什么样的人」有关的目标——比如当律师——而且别人注意到了，他们随后为之付出的努力反而更少，仿佛宣布本身就让他们提前尝到了一部分成功的满足。最后说那本一分钟读一千字的书：2016 年一篇关于阅读研究的重要综述得出结论，速读方法无法在不损失理解的前提下大幅提高阅读速度。作者那本没读的书，大概也解决不了他的问题。

✨ How it is written赏析

The essay is a trap that the writer walks into on purpose. The first paragraph speaks in the confident plural — we resolve, we fail, we make the fundamental error — so that the reader nods along. When the writer turns to himself, he presents his plan as the wise exception: two modest aims, told to nobody. Then, step by step, he commits the very errors he has just listed, and in addition is found out without saying a word, which defeats his one clever precaution. The comedy depends on precise numbers: eleven minutes, two days, January the tenth. They make the decline measurable, and the four short sentences that follow — waned, diminished, fell to zero, back where I started — read like a chart going down. The final line is a perfect circle. His solution to having no time to read is a book, which he has no time to read. The writer does not point out the irony; he leaves it as the last word and trusts us to laugh.

这篇文章是作者故意走进去的一个圈套。第一段用自信的复数口吻——我们下决心，我们失败，我们犯根本错误——让读者一路点头。转到自己身上时，作者把他的计划说成聪明的例外：两个小目标，不告诉任何人。然后他一步一步犯下刚刚列举过的每一个错误，此外还在一句话都没说的情况下被识破，连他唯一的一招聪明防备也落了空。喜剧感依赖精确的数字：十一分钟、两天、一月十日。这些数字让衰退可以度量，接下来四个短句——减退、减少、降到零、回到原点——读起来像一条往下走的曲线。最后一句是一个完美的闭环。他解决「没时间读书」的办法是一本书，而他没时间读这本书。作者没有点破这个讽刺，只把它留作最后一句，相信我们会笑出来。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The lesson offers a cheerful theory of failure: plans too big, too public, too little time. Modern research on habits suggests a different emphasis. People who succeed at changing behaviour tend to make the new action small, tie it to an existing routine, and change their surroundings rather than rely on willpower. The writer actually did some of this — eleven minutes is a small goal — but he chose a time that cost him sleep, and he left the television exactly where it was. His reading plan failed not because of weak character but because a warm room with a screen competed against a cold one with a book. Seen this way, the lesson is less about human weakness than about design. A reader who wants to keep a resolution might take three things from it: make the start tiny, remove the easy alternative, and plan for the day it goes wrong, because that day will come.

课文给了一套轻松的失败理论：计划太大、太张扬、时间太少。关于习惯的现代研究强调的重点不同。成功改变行为的人，往往把新动作做得很小，挂在已有的日常环节上，并且改变环境，而不是靠意志力硬撑。作者其实做到了一部分——十一分钟是个小目标——但他选的时间要牺牲睡眠，电视也原封不动地摆在那里。他的读书计划失败，不是因为意志薄弱，而是因为一间有屏幕的暖和房间，在和一间只有一本书的冷房间竞争。这样看，这一课与其说是关于人性的弱点，不如说是关于「设计」。想坚持一个决心的读者可以从中带走三点：开头做到极小，拿走那个更容易的替代选项，并且为「哪天搞砸了」提前做好打算，因为那一天一定会来。

    说说你试过养成或戒掉的一个习惯。什么帮了你，什么让你放弃？
  2. Should you tell people about your goals or keep them secret? Write 120 words, using an example from your own life.

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

The cleft sentence: It is or was ... that ...强调句：It is / was … that …
Put the part you want to stress between It is or It was and that, or who for people, and leave the rest unchanged. If you remove It was ... that and the sentence is still complete, it is a cleft sentence.
把要强调的部分放在 It is / was 与 that（指人可用 who）之间，其余部分不变。去掉 It was … that 句子仍然完整，这是判断强调句的办法。

Sentence 21 puts this up front with It was ... that; the plain version would be this betrayed me. Lesson 55 uses the same pattern with bacteria.

本课：第 21 句用强调句把「这一点」提到前面，等于 This betrayed me。对比 L55 的 It is bacteria that …。

  • It was my sister who broke the window, not me.
  • It is practice, not talent, that makes the difference.
Adjective phrases at the start of a sentence形容词短语作状语：Aware of … / Tired of …
An adjective phrase can open a sentence to give a reason or a state, as in Aware of the danger, meaning because I was aware of it. Its subject must be the subject of the main clause.
形容词（短语）可以放在句首作状语，说明原因或状态，相当于 Because I was aware of …；逻辑主语必须是主句主语。

wane is used of enthusiasm or the moon, diminish of size or amount; fall to zero, slip back into and be back where one started complete the picture. Stories of failed resolve often use this set.
wane（热情、月亮）减退；diminish 减少；fall to zero 降到零；slip back into 退回；be back to where one started 回到原点。叙述「坚持失败」常用这一组词。

Sentences 24 to 29 use one each, step by step down to nothing.

本课：第 24–27 句一句一个：waned → diminished → fell to zero → 回到原点，层层递减。

  • His interest in chess waned after a few months.
  • She slipped back into her old habit of staying up late.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • Most of us fail in … because … 我们大多数人在……上失败，是因为……
    Most of us fail in saving money because we never track what we spend.
  • Aware of …, X attempted to … 意识到……，X 试图……
    Aware of the risks, the climbers attempted a shorter route.
  • X gave me a handy excuse for not … X 给了我不……的好借口
    The rain gave me a handy excuse for not going to the gym.
  • It was this that … 正是这一点……
    She smiled at the wrong moment; it was this that gave her away.
  • That turned out to be my downfall, for … 那正是我失败的原因，因为……
    I checked my phone once more; that turned out to be my downfall, for I missed the bus.

Summary writing摘要写作

In no more than 80 words, tell how the writer's two resolutions this year came to nothing.

用不超过 80 个词讲述作者今年的两个新年决心是怎样落空的。

Points a good summary covers好摘要要覆盖的要点
  • This year the writer kept two modest resolutions to himself: to exercise each morning and to read more in the evenings.作者今年只定了两个小目标且不告诉别人：每天早上锻炼，晚上多读书。
  • He exercised early in secret, but his tiredness at breakfast gave him away, and the family came to watch.他偷偷早起做操，可早饭时累坏了，被家人发现，全家来围观。
  • His enthusiasm faded, the exercises shrank to nothing, and by 10 January he was back where he began.热情消退，锻炼时间逐渐减到零，到 1 月 10 日回到原点。
  • The reading failed too: one evening he sat down in front of the television and fell asleep as before.晚上读书也没坚持住：一天晚上他坐到电视前，又打起盹来。
Our model summary本站范文

This year the writer chose two small resolutions and told no one: morning exercise and more reading. He began on 2 January, exercising quietly before the family got up, but he looked so worn out at breakfast that they found out and came to watch. His keenness soon faded, and by 10 January he had stopped. He read in his room for a few evenings, then went back to dozing in front of the television.（75 词）
