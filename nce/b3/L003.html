🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
An archaeological report: an American team working on the Greek island of Kea finds, in one temple room, the broken clay pieces of fifteen female figures. One figure's body comes out of a level dated to the fifteenth century B.C., while its head lay in a level a thousand years younger, which means people in Classical times had already found that head and kept it as a treasure. Put back together, the figure looks startlingly modern. The whole chain of reasoning rests on two words: must have been, for a conclusion about the past, and for, which supplies the evidence.
考古报道：美国考古队在希腊凯阿岛的一座神庙里发现 15 尊女神陶像的碎片。其中一尊的身体出自公元前 15 世纪的地层，头却在公元前 5 世纪的地层——说明古典时代的人已经找到这颗头并当宝贝收藏。拼起来后，女神竟像个很「现代」的女人。全文的推理靠两个词：must have been（对过去的推断）和 for（给出证据）。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. On what grounds did the archaeologists conclude that this city had once been rich?考古学家凭什么断定这座城市曾经很繁荣？
  2. Why did the head and the body turn up in layers a thousand years apart?女神像的头和身体为什么出现在相隔一千年的地层里？
  3. What surprised the archaeologists once the figure had been put back together?复原后的女神像让考古学家吃惊的是什么？

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

Ayia Irini stands on a low headland on Kea, the Cycladic island nearest to Attica, and was dug through the 1960s by John Caskey of the University of Cincinnati. The temple there gave up broken terracotta female figures from almost every room, and Miriam Caskey's study of them counts, conservatively, about forty of them rather than the fifteen of the lesson. She dates them to Late Minoan IB and Late Helladic II, with one later Mycenaean piece among them. The great mass of fragments came off a floor in a single room in 1963. The building did go on being treated as sacred long after the Bronze Age settlement around it was wrecked, and the figures were handled again in that later phase, which is the solid ground under the lesson's most memorable detail: a head that somebody found, recognised as old and valuable, and kept. The figures are now the best-known objects from the island and are shown as the Korai of Kea.

阿伊亚伊里尼在凯阿岛一处低矮的岬角上，凯阿是离阿提卡最近的基克拉泽斯群岛之一；整个 1960 年代，辛辛那提大学的约翰·卡斯基在这里发掘。那座神庙几乎每个房间都出了陶制女性塑像的碎片，米里亚姆·卡斯基的专著按保守口径数出约四十尊，而不是课文说的十五尊。她把它们定在米诺斯晚期 IB 与希腊本土晚期 II，其中混有一件更晚的迈锡尼制品。大批碎片是 1963 年从一个房间的地面上起出来的。青铜时代的聚落被毁之后，这座建筑仍长期被当作圣地，塑像在那个更晚的阶段又被人动过——这正是课文里最难忘的那个细节的扎实依据：有人找到一颗头，认出它古老珍贵，把它收起来。如今这些塑像是全岛最有名的文物，以「凯阿的少女像」之名展出。

✨ How it is written赏析

This is a short course in how a report earns the right to a conclusion. Every claim about a vanished city is tied to a thing that was dug up: the town was well off, and the reason given is the standard of living it plainly enjoyed; it had drainage, and the reason given is the pipes found under the streets. The verbs do the same work in the other direction — a discovery was made, pipes were found, fragments were found, the figure had been painted — so that the finders stay out of the way and the finds stand in front. Only twice does the writer step past the evidence, and both times he marks the step with the same two words of inference. Then the last sentence refuses to finish the story. If you write up anything you did not see for yourself, this is the discipline: one fact, one ground; and where you are guessing, say so in the grammar.

这一课是「一篇报告怎样赢得下结论的资格」的速成课。每一个关于消失之城的说法都拴着一件出土物：说它富裕，给出的理由是它当年明显享有的生活水准；说它有排水，给出的理由是街下挖到的陶管。动词从另一头做同一件事——发现被作出、陶管被找到、碎片被找到、塑像曾被上色——于是发掘者退到后面，出土物站到前面。全文只有两次迈出证据之外，两次都用同一个推测结构标明。然后最后一句干脆拒绝把故事讲完。写任何你没亲眼见过的事，这就是纪律：一个事实配一个依据；凡是在猜的地方，让语法替你说出来。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

Two things in the lesson are worth holding at arm's length. The first is the count. Forty figures in the reports become fifteen in the lesson, and a find spread through a building becomes a find in one holy room; retellings tidy up, and the tidying always runs towards the neater story. The second is the title. Calling her a goddess is not an observation but a conclusion, and it is the very conclusion the last sentence claims not to have reached. Scholars have since argued over whether these figures were deities at all, or ancestors, or something closer to large dolls — the open question is not only her name but her kind. The lesson's judgement that she looks like a modern woman is also of its moment: hands on hips and a long skirt read as modern in the 1960s, and that is a statement about the 1960s. What would change my mind on the count is simple enough: a published total that matches.

课文里有两处值得先拿远一点看。第一是数目。报告里的四十尊到课文里变成十五尊，散布在整座建筑里的发现变成了圣室里的一堆；转述总会顺手整理，而整理的方向永远朝着更齐整的那个故事。第二是标题。叫她「女神」不是观察，是结论，而且恰恰是最后一句声称还没得出的那个结论。后来的学者争论过：这些塑像究竟是神，是祖先，还是更接近大型人偶——悬而未决的不只是她的名字，还有她的类别。课文说她像个现代女人，这句判断也带着年代印记：手扶腰、长裙及地，在 1960 年代读起来是「现代」，这其实是关于 1960 年代的一句话。至于数目，要改变我的看法很容易：拿出一个对得上的已发表总数就行。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 3第 3 句 (begins开头 “The city at …”)

  • must have been: a firm conclusion about the past drawn from evidence (see Lesson 1); at one time means at some point long ago.must have been：根据证据推断过去（见第 1 课）；at one time 曾经。
  • for introduces the ground on which the conclusion rests.for 引出推断的依据。

Sentence 6第 6 句 (begins开头 “The city was …”)

  • even: there was a drainage system as well as everything else, which says a good deal about how advanced the place was.even：甚至（连排水系统都有），强调文明程度。
  • The second half again uses for plus the evidence, and the evidence here is what was dug up under the streets.后半句又是 for + 证据。

Sentence 7第 7 句 (begins开头 “The temple which …”)

  • The relative pronoun is the object of the verb inside the clause, so it could be left out.which 作 explored 的宾语，可省略。
  • be used as: to serve as. Keep it apart from be used to doing, which means accustomed, and used to do, which looks back at a past habit.be used as：被用作（注意与 be used to doing 习惯于、used to do 过去常常区分）。

Sentence 9第 9 句 (begins开头 “Each of these …”)

  • each of plus a plural noun takes a singular verb. The past tense of the first verb hides this, but the auxiliary of the second is singular too.each of + 复数名词作主语，谓语用单数（represented 是过去式看不出，但 had 也是单数形式）。
  • The past perfect passive puts the painting earlier than the finding, and a time phrase has been dropped in between the auxiliary and the participle.had been painted：过去完成时被动，比发现时更早被上过色；at one time 插在 had 与 been 之间。

Sentence 12第 12 句 (begins开头 “This head must …”)

  • must have been done: a firm conclusion about something done to the subject in the past; the second verb shares the same must have been.must have been done：对过去的被动推测；第二个谓语共用 must have been。

Sentence 14第 14 句 (begins开头 “When the archaeologists …”)

  • be amazed to do: the infinitive gives the cause of the feeling.be amazed to do：不定式表情绪的原因。
  • turn out to be: to prove in the end to be.turn out to be：结果是，原来是。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

Conclusions in the passive: must have been done被动语态的推测：must have been done
To draw a conclusion about something that was done to the subject in the past, use must, may or can't followed by have been and a past participle.
对过去被动的事情推测，用 must/may/can't + have been + 过去分词。

Compare the active and the passive: with a person as subject you say somebody must have found it, and with the object as subject you say it must have been found.

对比主动：Someone must have found it.（主语是人）→ It must have been found.（主语是物）

  • The letter must have been sent to the wrong address.
  • The window can't have been broken by the wind.
Singular or plural with each, every and either as subjecteach / every / either 作主语的单复数
each of, every, either and neither take a singular verb when they are the subject or stand in front of it. When each follows a plural subject as an appositive, the verb still agrees with that plural subject.
each（of）、every、either、neither 作主语或修饰主语时，谓语用单数；each 放在复数主语后作同位语时，谓语仍按复数主语。
  • Each of the students has a book.
  • The students each have a book.（each 作同位语）
turn out (to be) and happen to do
turn out to be, or turn out that, means to prove in the end to be. happen to do means to do something by chance, and it happens that is the impersonal form.
turn out to be / turn out that…＝结果是，原来是；happen to do＝碰巧做；it happens that…＝碰巧。
  • The weather turned out to be fine.
  • I happened to meet her in the street.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • X was even equipped with … X 甚至还配备了……
    The old castle was even equipped with a secret tunnel.
  • … was used as … from … until … 从……到……，……一直被用作……
    The building was used as a school from 1900 until the war.
  • Despite …, …; but, so far, … have been unable to … 尽管……，……；但到目前为止，……还没能……
    Despite years of research, the cause is known; but, so far, scientists have been unable to find a cure.

Summary writing摘要写作

In no more than 80 words, sum up the find on Kea, with the head and the body of that one figure at the centre.

用不超过 80 个词概述凯阿岛的考古发现，重点写那尊女神像的头和身体。

Points a good summary covers好摘要要覆盖的要点
  • An American team worked on a temple in an ancient city on Kea; the tall stone houses, the painted walls and the pipes under the streets all point to a wealthy place.美国考古队在凯阿岛一座古城的神庙里考察；古城房屋高大、装饰华美、有排水系统，曾很繁荣。
  • Worship went on in the temple from the fifteenth century before Christ down to Roman times, and the innermost room held the clay pieces of fifteen figures.神庙从公元前 15 世纪用到罗马时代，圣室中发现 15 尊女神陶像碎片。
  • One body came from a fifteenth-century layer while its head lay in a fifth-century one, so people in Classical times must have found the head and kept it.一尊像的身体在公元前 15 世纪的地层，头却在公元前 5 世纪的地层，说明古典时代已有人发现并珍藏了它。
  • Put together again, the figure looks like a woman of our own day, graceful after all those centuries, and nobody yet knows who she was meant to be.复原后女神像很像现代女性，优雅，但身份至今不明。
