Lesson 48 · The Silent Village 寂静的村庄

📘 New Concept English · Book 3 · Lesson 48 “The Silent Village”新概念英语 第三册 Lesson 48《The Silent Village》

B2🧠 27 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
We tend to think that people in faraway villages are friendly and welcoming, but people who seldom see foreigners, or even visitors from their own country, may well turn hostile to outsiders. Touring the Balkans, the writer and his wife spent a few days in a small town looking at old churches with finely kept wall paintings nearby. When coachloads of tourists arrived the day before they left, the couple could not stand it and set off into the countryside. They crossed fields and a thick wood, walked for over two hours to a deep stream, woke a sleeping boatman who rowed them across, and came to a small village clinging to a steep mountainside. Grey mud-brick houses lined one rough road, and the place seemed empty except for a black goat tied to a tree. They sat on a broken fence for a picnic of tinned sardines and suddenly found themselves ringed by ragged children who stared without a word and would not take the food offered. Walking through the village, they were trailed by silent children; faces filled the windows, men stood in their doorways glaring, and old women in black shawls watched from the shadows. Worst of all, there was no sound at all. The couple needed no more warning and hurried back towards the stream, hoping the boatman was still there. This is a travel piece that states a claim and then tells a story, growing quieter and more frightening as it goes. The grammar to learn is inaccessible to, not only ... but even, more than we could bear, -ing phrases such as Taking ..., the absolute phrase with the only sign ... being, the strong negative not a sound, and There was no doubt that.
我们总以为偏远地方的村民热情好客，可那些不但与外国游客隔绝、连同胞也难得见到的人，可能对外来者充满敌意。作者和妻子游巴尔干半岛时，在一个小镇住了几天，看附近保存着精美壁画的古教堂。离开前一天大批游客涌进小镇，两人受不了，决定去乡间走走：穿过田野和密林，走了两个多小时到一条深溪边，叫醒船夫把他们渡过去，来到一个建在陡峭山坡上的小村。灰泥砖房，一条没铺的路，看上去荒无人烟，只有一只拴在树上的黑山羊。他们坐在破篱笆上吃沙丁鱼罐头野餐，忽然发现被一群衣衫褴褛的孩子默默围住；给他们食物，孩子们一动不动。走进村子，孩子们一声不响跟在后面，窗口出现一张张脸，男人站在门外怒目而视，裹着黑披肩的老妇在门口窥视——最可怕的是听不到一点声音。两人不需要更多警告，掉头快步走向溪边，盼着船夫还在等。本课是先立论、后叙事的游记，越写越静，越静越怕。学 inaccessible to、not only … but even、more than we could bear、Taking …（分词作状语）、the only sign … being（独立主格）、There was no doubt that、not a sound。

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander, and probably invented. The story names no town or country beyond the Balkans, and its shape — a fixed belief stated, then overturned by experience — is one the third book uses many times.

课文为编者 L. G. 亚历山大为教材自撰，大概是虚构的。故事除了「巴尔干」之外没有点出任何城镇或国家；它的结构——先亮出一个固有看法，再让亲身经历推翻它——是第三册反复用的写法。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

The setting is believable for the time the course was first written, the 1960s. Much of the Balkans then lay inside communist states, and while Yugoslavia and Bulgaria were opening to Western visitors, mountain villages could still be very isolated, and Albania was almost closed to foreigners. The old churches the couple visit are a real feature of the region: Orthodox churches across Bulgaria, Serbia, North Macedonia and Greece are covered inside with medieval wall paintings, and some, such as the small Boyana Church outside Sofia with its paintings from 1259, are now World Heritage Sites. There is a complication the lesson leaves out. Mountain societies in the region have some of the strongest traditions of hospitality in Europe. In northern Albania, for example, the old customary law called the Kanun treated the guest as almost sacred: a household was bound to protect anyone it took in, even an enemy. So the silence the couple met may not be simple hostility to strangers. Two foreigners arriving unannounced, eating their own food on a fence beside the fields, may have broken expectations they did not know existed.

课文的背景放在它最初写成的 1960 年代是可信的。那时巴尔干大部分地区处在社会主义国家内部，南斯拉夫和保加利亚正在向西方游客开放，但山村仍可能非常闭塞，阿尔巴尼亚几乎不对外国人开放。夫妇俩参观的古教堂是这一地区真实的特色：保加利亚、塞尔维亚、北马其顿和希腊各地的东正教堂，内壁满是中世纪壁画，有的——比如索非亚城外小小的博亚纳教堂，壁画绘于 1259 年——如今已是世界遗产。课文略去了一个复杂之处。这一地区的山地社会拥有欧洲最浓厚的好客传统之一。比如在阿尔巴尼亚北部，名为《卡农》的古老习惯法把客人视为近乎神圣：一户人家必须保护它收留的任何人，哪怕是仇人。所以夫妇俩遇到的沉默，未必只是对陌生人的敌意。两个外国人不打招呼就来了，坐在田边篱笆上吃自带的东西，可能触犯了一些他们根本不知道存在的规矩。

✨ How it is written赏析

The lesson is a study in how to make silence frightening on the page, where every word is a sound. The writer does it by withholding. The first half is busy and noisy: busloads of tourists, crossing fields, waking a boatman. Then the village appears, and the sentences describe only what can be seen — grey bricks, an unmade road, a goat on a rope. The one living animal is described as ugly-looking, a small hint of threat. The children arrive without being heard; the writer simply looks up and they are there. After that, each new group of villagers is added in a short sentence of its own: faces at windows, men glaring, old women peering. The camera moves from far to near and the crowd grows, but nobody speaks. Only then does the writer name the thing directly, and he puts it last, in a passive sentence with nobody in it. The escape is told in the plainest possible words, with no description at all, because the reader is already running with them.

这一课示范了怎样在纸面上让寂静变得可怕——而纸面上的每一个词本身都是一种声音。作者的办法是「扣着不给」。前半段热闹嘈杂：成车的游客、穿过田野、叫醒船夫。然后村子出现，句子只写眼睛看到的东西：灰砖、没铺的路、一只拴着绳的羊。唯一的活物被形容为「长得难看」，透出一丝威胁。孩子们来时没有一点声响；作者一抬头，他们就在那里了。此后每一批新出现的村民都单独占一个短句：窗口的脸、怒视的男人、窥视的老妇。镜头由远及近，人越聚越多，却没有一个人说话。直到这时作者才直接点破，而且放在最后：一点声音也听不到。逃离写得极其朴素，没有任何描写，因为读者早已和他们一起跑起来了。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The opening claim is honest, and it is also a little too easy. It warns readers not to romanticize remote people as naturally kind, which is a fair point. But the story that follows is told entirely from the outside. We never learn why the village behaved as it did: whether outsiders had brought trouble before, whether the couple had wandered onto private land, whether the silence was fear rather than anger. The children who refused food may have been told never to take anything from strangers, the same rule many parents teach today. A modern travel writer would probably feel bound to ask those questions, or at least admit not knowing the answers. The lesson also says nothing about the tourists the couple were fleeing, though they were tourists themselves. That is the most useful thing to take from it: the visitor who wants an untouched place is part of what changes it, and the people who live there are entitled not to welcome them.

开头的论点是诚实的，但也有点太轻巧。它提醒读者别把偏远地方的人浪漫化成天生善良，这一点说得有理。可接下来的故事完全是从外面讲的。我们始终不知道村子为什么那样反应：是外人以前带来过麻烦，是夫妇俩误入了私人土地，还是那份沉默其实是害怕而不是愤怒。拒绝食物的孩子们，也许只是被教导过绝不拿陌生人的东西——今天很多父母也这样教孩子。现代的旅行作家大概会觉得有责任问这些问题，至少承认自己不知道答案。课文对夫妇俩躲避的那些游客也只字未提，尽管他们自己就是游客。这正是最值得带走的一点：想找一个未被打扰之地的访客，本身就是改变那个地方的力量之一；而住在那里的人，有权不欢迎他们。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

The absolute phrase: noun plus participle独立主格结构：名词 + 分词
A noun or pronoun with an -ing or -ed participle can sit before or after the main clause to add background or detail. It has its own subject, different from the subject of the main clause.
名词（或代词）+ 现在分词/过去分词，放在主句前后作状语，补充背景或细节。它有自己的逻辑主语，与主句主语不同。

Sentence 21 has one; compare sentence 9, where Taking a path shares the subject we, so it is an ordinary participle phrase.

本课：第 21 句 the only sign … being 那只黑山羊；对比第 9 句 Taking a path（主语就是 we，不是独立主格）。

  • The rain having stopped, we went out for a walk.
  • She sat by the window, her eyes fixed on the road.
Strong negatives: not a and not even a否定加强：not a … / not even a …
not a with a singular noun means not even one, and it is stronger than no. It often stands as the subject or at the front: not a word was said. Adding single makes it stronger still.
not a + 单数名词＝一个也没有，比 no 更强调；常放在句首或作主语：Not a word was said. 加 single 更强：not a single …

Sentence 32 uses it at the climax of the silence.

本课：第 32 句用 not a sound 作被动句主语，正是全篇「寂静」的高潮。

  • Not a single car passed us in two hours.
  • There was not a cloud in the sky.
Participle phrases for time and manner分词作状语表示先后或方式
An -ing phrase at the start, such as Looking up or Turning back, shows an action happening at the same time as, or just before, the main one. Its subject must be the subject of the main clause.
现在分词短语（Taking …, Looking up …, Turning back …）放在句首，表示与主句几乎同时或紧接着发生的动作，逻辑主语必须是主句主语。

Sentences 9, 24, 26 and 37 all open this way, moving the walk along.

本课：第 9 句 Taking a path；第 22 句 Sitting down；第 24 句 Looking up；第 35 句 Turning back。

  • Opening the door, she saw a parcel on the step.
  • Hearing the bell, the children ran outside.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • In this much-travelled world, … still … 在这个四处有人游历的世界上，仍有……
    In this much-travelled world, a few islands still have no hotel at all.
  • X was more than sb could bear X 让某人无法忍受
    By noon the heat was more than any of us could bear.
  • We expected X to …, but we found that … 我们以为 X 会……，却发现……
    We expected the shop to be closed, but we found that it was open late.
  • The most … thing of all was that … 最……的是……
    The most surprising thing of all was that nobody had noticed.
  • There was no doubt that … 毫无疑问……
    There was no doubt that the dog wanted its dinner.

Summary writing摘要写作

In no more than 80 words, tell how the couple reached the mountain village and why they left it.

用不超过 80 个词讲述作者夫妇去山村的经过以及他们离开的原因。

Points a good summary covers好摘要要覆盖的要点
  • To escape crowds of tourists, the couple left the town and walked through fields and a wood to a stream.为躲开涌来的游客，作者夫妇离开小镇去乡下，穿过田野和树林来到一条溪边。
  • A boatman took them across, and the path led to a mountain village that seemed empty.船夫把他们渡过溪，他们沿小路来到一个看似无人的山村。
  • While they ate, ragged children surrounded them in silence and would not take any food.他们野餐时被一群衣衫褴褛的孩子默默围住，孩子们不接受食物。
  • The villagers watched them without a sound, and realizing they were unwelcome, the couple hurried back to the stream.村民们沉默地盯着他们，两人明白自己不受欢迎，赶紧回到溪边。
Our model summary本站范文

Tired of the tourists who filled the town, the writer and his wife walked into the countryside. After two hours they reached a stream, where a boatman rowed them across, and they climbed to a lonely village that seemed deserted. As they picnicked, silent children in rags gathered round and refused their food. In the village, men glared and old women stared, all without a sound. Feeling unwelcome, the couple hurried back towards the stream.（75 词）
