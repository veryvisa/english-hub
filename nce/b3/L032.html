Lesson 32 · A Lost Ship 沉船

📘 New Concept English · Book 3 · Lesson 32 “A Lost Ship”新概念英语 第三册 Lesson 32《A Lost Ship》

B2🧠 23 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
The salvage ship Elkor has spent weeks in the Barents Sea looking for a wreck that carried gold bars, and a message from the mainland tells her to give up. The captain will not quite let go and tries once more. Up comes a chest, to great excitement, but inside are a sailor's books, clothes, photographs and letters from his wife: this is a different ship, sunk long before. The captain works it out piece by piece: a heavy gun says she was a cruiser, an unfinished letter gives a date and a name, and a logbook fills in the rest. She was the Karen, torpedoed while sailing with a wartime convoy to Russia. This is detective-style narrative: history is worked out from objects. The grammar to learn is the past perfect continuous, must have been, non-defining clauses with which, and prove to be.
打捞船「埃尔克」号在巴伦支海找了几个星期，要找一艘装着金条的沉船，大陆发来电报叫它收工。船长不甘心，决定再撒一网——捞上来一只箱子，全船欢腾，打开一看却是另一艘多年前沉没的船：一位水手的书、衣服、照片和妻子写给他的信。船长顺着线索一件件推：一门重炮说明它是巡洋舰，一封没写完的信给出了日期和船名，一本航海日志拼出了全部经过——它是二战时开往俄国的护航船队里被潜艇击沉的「卡伦」号。本课是侦探式叙事：从实物推出历史。学过去完成进行时、must have been、which 引导的非限制定语从句和 prove to be。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. Why did the captain try once more after he had been told to come home?船长为什么在收到返航电报后还要再试一次？
  2. What did the crew think they had found at first, and how did they learn they were wrong?船员们最初以为找到了什么？他们是怎么知道自己弄错了的？
  3. Which objects let the captain work out what the lost ship was and what had happened to her?船长从哪几样东西推断出沉船的身份与经历？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander; the Elkor, the Karen and Alan Fielding are invented. But every element of the plot — a wartime convoy to Russia, a cruiser sunk by a submarine, a cargo of gold in the Barents Sea, a later salvage — happened, and in one case all of them happened to the same ship.

课文为编者 L. G. 亚历山大为教材自撰；埃尔克号、卡伦号和艾伦·菲尔丁都是编的。但情节里的每个元素——开往俄国的战时护航船队、被潜艇击沉的巡洋舰、巴伦支海里的一船黄金、后来的打捞——都真实发生过，而且有一艘船把这些全占了。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

The Arctic convoys of the Second World War carried tanks, aircraft, fuel and food from Britain and Iceland round the north of Norway to the Soviet ports of Murmansk and Archangel, from 1941 to 1945. They sailed through the Barents Sea in darkness, ice and storms, within reach of German aircraft, warships and submarines, and losses were heavy. The real ship behind the lesson is the British cruiser HMS Edinburgh. In the spring of 1942, sailing home from Murmansk, she carried several tonnes of Soviet gold bars sent as payment for war supplies. A U-boat torpedoed her, German destroyers attacked, and she was finally sunk by her own side rather than let her fall into enemy hands. The gold lay in deep, cold water until 1981, when a salvage team led by the diver Keith Jessop brought up most of the bars, and more were recovered later; the proceeds were shared between the British and Soviet governments and the salvors. The wreck is also a war grave, with sailors who died aboard still inside it.

二战时的北极护航船队从英国和冰岛出发，绕过挪威北端，把坦克、飞机、燃料和粮食运到苏联的摩尔曼斯克与阿尔汉格尔斯克，时间从 1941 年到 1945 年。它们在黑暗、浮冰与风暴中穿过巴伦支海，处在德国飞机、军舰与潜艇的攻击范围之内，损失惨重。课文背后的真船是英国巡洋舰「爱丁堡」号。1942 年春，她从摩尔曼斯克返航，舱里装着几吨苏联金条——那是苏方支付战争物资的货款。一艘德国潜艇用鱼雷击中了她，德国驱逐舰又来攻击，最后是英方自己把她击沉，免得落入敌手。那批黄金在冰冷的深水里躺到 1981 年，才由潜水员基思·杰索普带领的打捞队捞起大部分金条，之后又捞上来一些；所得由英苏两国政府与打捞方分成。这艘沉船同时也是战争墓地，殉难水兵的遗骸至今还在里面。

✨ How it is written赏析

The lesson is a little detective story told by objects. It opens on failure, not hope, and the failure is stated in the past perfect, so that the action proper begins with a choice: one more try against orders. Then the writer plays a trick on us that the crew also suffer. A chest comes up, excitement runs high, and for one sentence we believe in the gold; the very next sentence turns it round. From then on the story slows down and becomes a chain of inferences, each tied to a thing: a gun gives the type of ship, a letter gives a date and a name, a logbook gives the cause. Notice how the grammar follows the reasoning. Certain facts are in the past simple, the guess from the gun is in must have been, and the final story is in the past perfect continuous and passive, because it is history reconstructed rather than seen. The last line sends everything to a museum, turning salvage into memory.

这一课是一个由实物讲述的小侦探故事。它以失败开场而不是以希望开场，失败用过去完成时陈述，于是正式的情节从一个选择开始：违令再试一次。接着作者对读者耍了一个船员也中了的花招：一只箱子浮上来，群情激昂，有那么一句话的工夫我们也信了是黄金；紧接着下一句就把它翻了过来。从这里起故事放慢，变成一串推理，每一步都拴在一件东西上：一门炮给出船型，一封信给出日期和名字，一本日志给出死因。注意语法如何跟着推理走：确定的事实用一般过去时，从炮做出的推断用 must have been，最后那段经过用过去完成进行时与被动态——因为那是重建出来的历史，而不是亲眼所见。最后一句把一切送进博物馆，把打捞变成了纪念。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

Read today, the story raises a question the lesson does not ask: should anyone be raising things from a wreck where men died? The captain orders his crew to bring up as much as possible, and nobody hesitates over the seaman's letters from his wife. Attitudes have shifted. In Britain the Protection of Military Remains Act of 1986 allows the government to protect the wrecks of military vessels, and disturbing a protected wreck without a licence is an offence; the salvage of the Edinburgh's gold itself was carried out under government contract and was followed by debate about how war graves should be treated. The lesson's ending — everything goes to a museum and nothing of value is found — quietly avoids the hardest case. When the cargo really is gold, as it was in 1981, the pull of money and the duty to the dead pull in opposite directions, and a good reader should notice that the textbook has let its captain off easily.

今天读这个故事，会冒出一个课文没有问的问题：人死在里面的沉船，该不该去捞东西？船长下令尽量多捞，对那位水手妻子写给他的信，谁都没有犹豫。观念已经变了。在英国，1986 年的《军人遗骸保护法》允许政府保护军舰残骸，未经许可扰动受保护的残骸属违法；爱丁堡号黄金的打捞本身就是在政府合同下进行的，事后还引发了关于如何对待战争墓地的争论。课文的结尾——东西全进了博物馆、没找到任何值钱的东西——悄悄绕开了最难的那种情形。当货物真的是黄金，像 1981 年那样，钱的吸引力与对死者的责任就会朝两个方向拉扯；一个好读者应该看出，教科书让它的船长轻松过关了。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

The past perfect continuous: had been doing过去完成进行时：had been doing
In a story set in the past, use had been doing for an action that had gone on for a while before another past moment, whether or not it had just stopped. It often goes with for, since or all day. had done stresses that something was finished; had been doing stresses that it went on.
讲过去的事时，表示某个动作在另一个过去时刻之前已经持续了一段时间（可能刚停或还在进行）。常和 for、since、all day 连用。与过去完成时 had done 的区别：had done 强调「做完了」，had been doing 强调「一直在做」。

Sentence 2 uses it for the weeks of searching, and sentences 5 and 24 use it for what the lost ship was doing before she sank.

本课：第 2 句用于「找了好几个星期」，第 5 句与第 22 句用于沉船出事前那段航程。

  • She had been waiting for an hour when the bus finally came.
  • His eyes were red because he had been crying.
  • They had been living in Paris for ten years before they moved to Rome.
must have done: a confident guess about the pastmust have done：对过去的肯定推测
must have plus a past participle gives a firm conclusion about the past drawn from evidence. The negative is can't or couldn't have done; for a weaker guess use may or might have done. It does not mean 'was obliged to'; for that, use had to.
must have + 过去分词：根据证据推断过去「一定是……」；否定推测用 can't / couldn't have done（「不可能是……」）；把握小用 may / might have done。注意 must have done 不是「过去必须」——过去必须是 had to。

In sentence 16 the captain reasons from a heavy gun to the conclusion that the ship was a cruiser.

本课：第 16 句：船长从一门重炮推断沉船一定是巡洋舰。

  • The ground is wet. It must have rained in the night.
  • He can't have seen me — he walked straight past.
  • She might have missed the train; let's wait ten more minutes.
Noun + of which / of whom in non-defining clauses名词 + of which / of whom：非限制定语从句
To refer to part or number of what has just been named, use some of which, most of whom, parts of which, none of which, after a comma. which is for things and whom for people.
表示「其中的部分/数量」：some of which、most of whom、parts of which、none of which。which 指物、whom 指人；前面用逗号。也可写作 whose + 名词（物也可用 whose）。

Sentence 20 adds, with parts of which, that some of the logbook could still be read.

本课：第 20 句用 parts of which 补充说明日志「有些部分还能读」。

  • He has three brothers, two of whom live abroad.
  • She bought ten apples, most of which were bad.
  • They found a letter, the end of which was missing.

  • X were under the impression that …, but … proved them wrong. X 起初以为……，但……证明他们错了

  • From …, X realized that … must have been … 从……，X 意识到……一定是……

  • X was able to piece together … X 得以拼凑出……

Summary writing摘要写作

In no more than 80 words, say what the crew of the Elkor really found and how the captain worked out the story of that ship.

Points a good summary covers好摘要要覆盖的要点
  • The Elkor had searched the Barents Sea in vain for a ship carrying gold; she was ordered home, but the captain tried once more.埃尔克号在巴伦支海搜寻一艘载金条的沉船失败，奉命返航，但船长决定再试一次。
  • A chest brought up from the bottom held a sailor's personal things, which showed that this was a different and much older wreck.捞上来的箱子里装着一名水手的私人物品，说明这是另一艘早年沉没的船。
  • A heavy gun showed she had been a cruiser, and an unfinished letter from 1943 gave her name, the Karen.一门重炮表明它是巡洋舰；一封 1943 年的未写完的信给出了船名「卡伦」号。

Our model summary本站范文

After weeks of fruitless searching for a ship loaded with gold, the Elkor was ordered home, but her captain made one last try. The nets brought up a chest full of a sailor's belongings, so the crew knew this was another, older wreck. A heavy gun showed she had been a cruiser, a letter from 1943 named her as the Karen, and her logbook revealed that a submarine had sunk her on the way to Russia.（76 词）
