Lesson 57 · Back in the Old Country 重返故里

📘 New Concept English · Book 3 · Lesson 57 “Back in the Old Country”新概念英语 第三册 Lesson 57《Back in the Old Country》

B2🧠 25 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
The narrator pulls over to let the car cool and to check the map. He thought he would be nearly there, but nothing looks familiar. Eighteen years earlier his mother died in an accident, and his father, unable to bear a home full of reminders, emigrated with his five-year-old son. In the new country the father threw himself into building a life for them both and slowly stopped mourning. He never remarried and was mother and father in one, so the boy wanted for nothing. His plan was always to return for a visit, to see old friends and the grave, but not to stay, since both had put down roots in their new home. A few months before the trip, he fell gravely ill, and as he was dying he made his son promise to go alone. On arrival the son rented a car and bought a road atlas, which helped across the country, but he expected not to need it at the end: though he remembered nothing himself, his father had described every landmark after the last town again and again. Now he was lost. Ten miles out, he should have seen a valley with farms and the distant spire of the village church; instead there was only a lake, not shown on his map. He drove back and tried again, and ended at the same corner, as if in a bad dream. Then a man on horseback appeared. There was no village any more, he said, and pointed at the lake: the village and the whole valley lay under a man-made reservoir. This is a suspense narrative: it opens with the narrator lost, steps back to tell his family story, returns to the road, and saves the answer for the last line. The grammar to learn is let ... cool off, had expected to, become absorbed in, cease to, lack for nothing, mean to, make sb promise, It was not that, over and over again, be positive, must have done, land up, as if, there appeared, and no longer.
「我」停下车让发动机凉一凉，顺便看地图——本以为快到了，可周围一切都很陌生。十八年前，母亲在一场意外中去世，父亲走不出悲伤与孤独，处处都是她的影子，于是带着五岁的「我」移民。在新国家他一心为两人重建生活，渐渐不再悲伤；他没有再婚，又当爹又当妈，「我」什么都不缺。他一直打算哪天回去看看老乡亲、给母亲扫墓，但不留下，两人的根已经扎进新土地。计划成行前几个月他病危，临终让「我」答应一个人去。「我」下船第二天租车、买了地图册，一路很管用，只是最后一段觉得用不着：虽然什么都不记得，可父亲一遍遍讲过出城后每个里程碑能看到什么。结果迷了路：出城十英里，按父亲的说法该看到山谷、农舍、远处村里教堂的尖顶，眼前却只有一片湖，地图上还没标。退回城里重走一遍，又到了同一个拐角，像掉进了噩梦。幸好地平线上来了个骑马的人。他说已经没有那个村子了，又指了指湖：村子和整个山谷都被淹了，这湖是人工水库。本课是悬念式叙事：从「迷路」开始倒叙身世，再回到眼前，谜底到最后一句才揭开。学 let … cool off、had expected to、become absorbed in、cease to、lack for nothing、mean to、make sb promise、It was not that、over and over again、be positive、must have done、land up、as if、there appeared、no longer。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. Why did the father emigrate, and why did he want only to visit and not to stay?父亲为什么要移民？为什么他只想回去看看而不留下？
  2. Why did the narrator think he would not need the map for the last part of the trip?「我」为什么觉得最后一段路用不着看地图？
  3. What had happened to the village?村子为什么不见了？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course; the country is never named, which is part of the design. The situation recalls many real valleys flooded in Britain and elsewhere in the twentieth century to supply growing cities with water.

课文为教材编写，始终没说是哪个国家，这是有意为之。这个情景让人想起二十世纪英国及其他地方为给扩张中的城市供水而淹没的许多真实山谷。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

Drowned villages are a real part of British history. In the Peak District, the villages of Ashopton and Derwent were cleared and flooded when the Ladybower Reservoir filled in the mid nineteen-forties; in very dry summers the ruins of Derwent have reappeared above the water. The most remembered case is Capel Celyn in north Wales, flooded in 1965 to supply Liverpool despite strong local protest; the slogan Cofiwch Dryweryn, remember Tryweryn, is still painted on walls in Wales, and Liverpool's council made a formal apology in 2005. Graves raised the hardest questions: in several such schemes, families were offered the chance to move remains before the water came. The narrator's search for his mother's grave would therefore have had a real answer, if anyone had told him where to look.

被淹没的村庄是英国历史的真实一部分。在峰区，二十世纪四十年代中期拉迪鲍尔水库蓄水时，阿肖普顿和德文特两个村子被清空并淹没；在特别干旱的夏天，德文特的废墟曾重新露出水面。最常被人记起的是北威尔士的卡佩尔·塞林，1965 年不顾当地强烈抗议被淹，为利物浦供水；「记住特里韦林」（Cofiwch Dryweryn）这句标语至今还刷在威尔士的墙上，利物浦市议会在 2005 年正式道歉。坟墓提出的问题最难：在好几个这样的工程中，家属在水来之前有机会迁移遗骨。所以，叙述者寻找母亲坟墓这件事本来是有答案的——如果有人告诉他该去哪里找。

✨ How it is written赏析

The lesson keeps one secret and hides it in plain sight. Everything the reader needs is given early: a valley, a church spire, a man-made world built for water. The narrator's long memory of his father is placed between the moment of being lost and the moment of discovery, so that the suspense stretches over the most emotional part. The map does quiet work as a symbol: it is reliable everywhere except at the one place he cares about, and the new lake on the road is missing from it, which suggests that official knowledge and private memory have drifted apart. The rider arrives like a figure from a folk tale, and the answer is given in two short steps, a statement and then a gesture. The final sentence is flat and factual, which makes the loss feel larger than any description could.

这一课只守一个秘密，而且把它藏在明处。读者需要的一切都很早给出：山谷、教堂尖顶、为水而建的人造世界。叙述者对父亲的长篇回忆被放在「迷路」与「发现」之间，于是悬念横跨全文最动情的部分。地图默默起了象征作用：它处处可靠，偏偏在他最在乎的地方失灵，路边的新湖地图上也没有——暗示官方知识和私人记忆已经分道扬镳。骑马的人像民间故事里的角色一样出现，答案分两步给出：一句话，然后一个手势。最后一句平淡、只讲事实，这让失去显得比任何描写都更大。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

For emigrant families the lesson's ending is not unusual at all; the place of memory often changes faster than the memory. Many people who left China in the eighties and nineties have returned to find their old streets demolished and rebuilt, or their villages merged into new towns. Reservoirs make the loss complete: the Xin'anjiang dam in Zhejiang, finished in the late fifties, flooded two old county towns to create what is now the Thousand Island Lake, and divers have since filmed the stone gates still standing under the water. The lesson's quieter lesson is about promises to the dead. The father wanted to see the grave, but his son ends up learning a fact instead, and the essay invites us to ask whether that is a failure or simply what returning always involves: finding that home has carried on without you.

对移民家庭来说，这一课的结局一点也不罕见：记忆中的地方往往比记忆变得更快。许多八九十年代离开中国的人回去时发现，老街已经拆了重建，村子并进了新镇。水库让失去变得彻底：浙江新安江水库五十年代末建成，淹没了两座古县城，形成了今天的千岛湖；后来潜水员拍到了水下依然矗立的石牌坊和城门。这一课更安静的一层是关于对逝者的承诺。父亲想看那座坟，儿子最后得到的却是一个事实；文章请我们思考：这是失败，还是归乡本来就意味着的东西——发现家乡没有等你，自己走下去了。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 2第 2 句 (begins开头 “I had expected …”)

  • had expected to do: he thought it would happen, but it did not.had expected to do：（过去）本来以为会……，暗示没实现。
  • alien to: strange and unfamiliar to.be alien to sb：对某人来说很陌生。

Sentence 3第 3 句 (begins开头 “I was only …”)

  • The past perfect marks this as earlier than the main story, which is the drive.这里用过去完成时 had taken，是为了标明这件事在叙述主线（开车迷路）之前。

Sentence 5第 5 句 (begins开头 “Everything around him …”)

  • full of her presence: everything reminded him of her.full of her presence：处处都有她的影子。
  • The -ing phrase gives the result: the pain kept coming back.reopening the wound：现在分词作结果状语，「不断揭开伤疤」。

Sentence 7第 7 句 (begins开头 “In the new …”)

  • become absorbed in: give all your attention to.become absorbed in doing：全神贯注于……。
  • so that here gives the result; cease to do means stop doing.so that：结果，以致于；cease to do：不再……（正式）。

Sentence 8第 8 句 (begins开头 “He did not …”)

  • lack for nothing: have everything you need.lack for nothing：什么都不缺。
  • for gives the reason; the both ... and phrase means he did the work of two parents.for：因为；both A and B to me：对我来说既是 A 又是 B。

Sentence 10第 10 句 (begins开头 “His roots and …”)

  • mean to do: intend to do.mean to do：打算做。
  • roots embedded: a picture of a plant firmly set in new soil.roots … embedded：比喻，根已深深扎进新土地。

Sentence 12第 12 句 (begins开头 “He became mortally …”)

  • After make, the verb that follows has no to.make sb do：使役动词后接不带 to 的不定式。
  • on my own: alone.on my own：独自。

Sentence 15第 15 句 (begins开头 “It was not …”)

  • It was not that rules out one reason before the real one is given.It was not that …：并不是说……（后面接真正的原因）。
  • over and over again: many times.over and over again：一遍又一遍。

Sentence 24第 24 句 (begins开头 “I decided that …”)

  • must have done is a confident guess about the past; take a wrong turning means turn into the wrong road.must have done：对过去的肯定推测。take a wrong turning：拐错弯。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

as if and as though with unreal formsas if / as though + 虚拟
After as if or as though, use a past form (were for be) for something not true now, and the past perfect for something not true in the past. If it may well be true, the ordinary tense is also possible.
as if/as though 后，与现在事实相反用过去式（be 用 were），与过去事实相反用过去完成时；如果是很可能的真实情况，也可用陈述语气。

In sentence 28 the narrator feels he has wandered into a dream world, using the past perfect.

本课：第 28 句「我」觉得像误闯了噩梦中的国度，用 had stumbled。

  • He talks as if he knew everything.
  • She looked as though she had seen a ghost.
The past perfect in flashbacks过去完成时在倒叙中的作用
When the main story is in the past simple, open a flashback with the past perfect, and then you can return to the past simple, since the reader now knows it is a memory.
故事主线用一般过去时，倒叙更早的事用过去完成时开个头，之后可以转回一般过去时，读者已经知道是在回忆。

Sentences 3 and 6 open the memory with the past perfect, and from sentence 6 the past simple takes over.

本课：第 3–4 句用 had taken、had died 引入回忆，第 6 句起又用一般过去时 decided、became。

  • She sat on the train. Years earlier she had travelled this route with her father; they talked all the way.
  • When I got home, I found that someone had broken the window.
there with an intransitive verb: introducing something newthere + 不及物动词 + 主语：引出新事物
Besides be, there can be followed by verbs such as appear, come, live, stand, exist and remain, with the subject after the verb. This brings a new person or thing into a story, mostly in written English.
there 后除 be 外还可接 appear、come、live、stand、exist、remain 等，主语放在动词后面，用来引出新出现的人或物，多见于叙事和书面语。

Sentence 31 brings in the rider with there appeared.

本课：第 31 句用 there appeared 引出骑马的人。

  • Once upon a time there lived a king who had three daughters.
  • Suddenly there came a knock at the door.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • I had expected to … by now, but … 我本以为这会儿该……了，可……
    I had expected to be home by now, but the trains were late.
  • X became absorbed in …, so that … gradually … X 一心扑在……上，渐渐……
    She became absorbed in her studies, so that she gradually forgot her worries.
  • X was both A and B to me. X 对我来说既是 A 又是 B
    My aunt was both teacher and friend to me.
  • It was not that …; but … 并不是说……，而是……
    It was not that I disliked him; but we had nothing to say.
  • X no longer exists because … X 已经不存在了，因为……
    The old shop no longer exists because the street was rebuilt.

Summary writing摘要写作

In no more than 80 words, tell why the narrator went back, how he got lost, and what he found out in the end.

用不超过 80 个词讲「我」为什么回故乡、怎样迷了路，以及最后发现了什么。

Points a good summary covers好摘要要覆盖的要点
  • The narrator had emigrated with his father at five; dying, his father made him promise to go back alone and visit his mother's grave.「我」五岁随父亲移民；父亲临终让他答应独自回去给母亲扫墓。
  • He hired a car and followed the landmarks his father had often described, but ten miles from town he found only a lake.他租了车，凭父亲多次描述的路标去找老村子，可出城十英里只看到一片湖。
  • Thinking he had gone wrong, he drove back and tried again, but ended at the same spot.他以为拐错了，返回重走，结果又到了同一个地方。
  • A rider told him that the village and the valley had been flooded, and the lake was a reservoir.一个骑马的人告诉他，村子和山谷已被淹没，湖是人工水库。
Our model summary本站范文

The narrator had left his home country with his father at five, after his mother's death. Before dying, his father made him promise to return alone and visit her grave. He hired a car and followed the directions his father had repeated so often, but where the village should have been there was only a lake. He tried the route again and came back to the same place. A passing rider explained that the village now lay under a reservoir.（80 词）
