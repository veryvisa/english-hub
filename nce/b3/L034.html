Lesson 34 · A Happy Discovery 幸运的发现

📘 New Concept English · Book 3 · Lesson 34 “A Happy Discovery”新概念英语 第三册 Lesson 34《A Happy Discovery》

B2🧠 27 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
Antique shops hold a strange pull for a great many people. The grand kind, with rare things locked in glass cases, keeps visitors at a distance; the humble, untidy kind lets anyone in, with the hope of finding treasure in a heap of junk. The writer says that a lucky find is never luck alone: it takes patience and, above all, an eye for value, at least as good as the dealer's. His old friend Frank is such a man. One Saturday morning a dealer did not feel like opening a newly arrived crate; Frank talked him into it, and under a load of broken plates lay a small painting whose design reminded him of an Italian work he knew well. The dealer glanced at it and asked fifty pounds. Experts later identified it as a lost work by Correggio. This is an essay that argues first and then gives an example. The grammar to learn is the more ... the more, at least as ... as, be about to do ... when, be bent on, and a noun with of it used without a verb.
古董店对很多人有一种特别的吸引力。贵的那种把珍品锁在玻璃柜里，叫人望而却步；不起眼的那种乱七八糟，人人敢进，总盼着在一堆破烂里翻出宝贝。作者说，捡到宝不靠运气：要有耐心，更要识货，至少懂得和店主一样多。他的老朋友弗兰克就是这样的人：一个周六上午，店主懒得开一只新到的板条箱，弗兰克求他打开，里面大多是破碗碟，箱底却压着一幅小画——构图和线条让他想起一幅熟悉的意大利名画。店主瞥了一眼要价 50 英镑，后来它被证实是柯勒乔一幅不为人知的杰作。本课是先议论、后举例的随笔。学 the more … the more、比较结构 at least as … as、be about to do when、be bent on、名词 + of it 的独立结构。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. What qualities does the writer think a serious bargain hunter needs?作者认为一个真正的「淘宝者」需要具备哪些条件？
  2. Why did Frank decide to buy the little painting?弗兰克为什么决定买下那幅小画？
  3. Why did the dealer ask only fifty pounds, and what does that show?店主为什么只要 50 英镑？这说明了什么？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander; Frank Halliday and the shop are invented. Correggio is real: Antonio Allegri, called Correggio after his home town, was an Italian painter of the early sixteenth century who worked mainly in Parma.

课文为编者 L. G. 亚历山大为教材自撰；弗兰克·哈利迪和那家店都是编的。柯勒乔是真人：原名安东尼奥·阿莱格里，因故乡小城得名「柯勒乔」，是十六世纪初的意大利画家，主要在帕尔马工作。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

Correggio is best known for the dome paintings of Parma, where figures seem to rise through the ceiling into an open sky, and for soft, glowing pictures of the Madonna and of scenes from classical myth. He died young, in the early 1530s, and left a fairly small body of work, which is one reason why the discovery of an unknown painting by him would be a real event. Stories like Frank's do still happen, which is why they are told. In 2019 a small panel painting found hanging in an elderly woman's kitchen near Compiègne in northern France was identified as a work by Cimabue, a Florentine master of the thirteenth century; she had thought it an old religious icon of no special value. It sold at auction for more than twenty million euros. As in the lesson, it was recognized not by luck but by someone who knew what to look for: an auctioneer called in to clear the house saw the quality and sent it to specialists.

柯勒乔最出名的是帕尔马的穹顶壁画——画中人物仿佛穿过天花板升入敞开的天空——以及柔和发光的圣母像与古典神话题材。他死得早，卒于十六世纪三十年代初，留下的作品不多，这也是为什么发现一幅他不为人知的画会是一件大事。弗兰克这样的故事至今仍会发生，所以才有人讲。2019 年，法国北部贡比涅附近一位老太太厨房里挂着的一块小木板画，被鉴定为十三世纪佛罗伦萨大师契马布埃的作品；她原以为那只是一幅不值钱的旧宗教圣像。这幅画在拍卖会上卖出两千多万欧元。和课文一样，认出它的不是运气，而是知道该看什么的人：一位被请来清点遗物的拍卖师看出了画的品质，把它送去给专家鉴定。

✨ How it is written赏析

The essay spends half its length on the shop and the hunter before Frank appears at all, and that order is the point. By the time the crate is opened we have been told what the find will need — patience, knowledge, a scientist's hope — and the story simply ticks the boxes. Frank's patience is the long morning; his knowledge is the moment when design and line remind him of a known picture; his hope is that he asks for a crate to be opened that the dealer could not be bothered with. The description of the shop is the richest writing in the lesson, heaped with adjectives in threes, while the climax is plain and quick. Notice also the small comedy of the dealer, who glances briefly and names a price: his carelessness is the mirror image of Frank's care. The final sentence contains a slip worth noticing — the phrase meaning 'hundreds of thousands' has come out as 'hundreds and thousands', which in British English is the name for tiny coloured sugar sprinkles on cakes.

这篇随笔用了一半篇幅写古董店和淘宝者，然后弗兰克才出场，这个顺序正是要点。等箱子打开时，我们已经被告知这一发现需要什么——耐心、知识、科学家式的希望——故事只是逐项打勾：弗兰克的耐心是那个漫长的上午；他的知识是构图与线条让他想起一幅熟悉名画的那一刻；他的希望是，他求店主打开一只连店主都懒得开的箱子。全课写得最丰满的是对店铺的描写，形容词三个一组地堆上去；而高潮写得朴素而快。再注意那个店主的小小喜剧：他只瞥一眼就报了价，他的粗心正是弗兰克用心的镜像。最后一句有个值得留意的笔误：本该是「几十万」的短语写成了 hundreds and thousands，而在英式英语里这恰好是撒在蛋糕上的彩色糖粒的名字。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The lesson tells the bargain hunter's story from the winner's side, and it is worth turning it round. Frank buys a masterpiece for fifty pounds from a man who does not know what he is selling, and the essay calls this a happy discovery. Many readers today would ask whether Frank owed the dealer anything — at least the truth. In law the answer is usually no: a buyer who has not lied is generally not obliged to point out that the seller has priced something too low, and dealers accept the risk as part of the trade. In ethics the question is harder, and collectors argue about it. There is also the survivor's view: we hear about the Correggio in the crate, not about the thousands of hopeful buyers who paid too much for fakes. The Chinese collecting world has its own word for a bargain picked up from someone who missed its value, and the same warning goes with it: for every real find there are many more pieces made to look like one.

课文从赢家一方讲淘宝者的故事，值得把它反过来看。弗兰克从一个不知道自己在卖什么的人手里，花 50 英镑买走一幅杰作，随笔称之为「幸运的发现」。今天不少读者会问：弗兰克是否欠那位店主什么——至少一句实话？法律上答案通常是否定的：没有撒谎的买方一般没有义务指出卖方定价过低，古董商把这种风险当作行业的一部分。伦理上这个问题更难，收藏界自己也争论不休。还有「幸存者」的视角：我们听说的是箱子里的柯勒乔，而听不到成千上万满怀希望、却为赝品付了高价的买家。中国收藏界管这种从不识货的人手里拣到宝叫「捡漏」，与这个词一起流传的还有一句提醒：每有一件真漏，就有更多件是专门做成「漏」的样子等人来捡的。

💬 Discuss and write讨论与写作
  1. Should Frank have told the dealer what the painting was really worth? Give reasons on both sides.
    弗兰克该不该告诉店主那幅画真正的价值？正反两方面都给出理由。
  2. Describe a shop, market or website where you have hunted for bargains. What did you need to know to succeed?
    描述一个你淘过便宜货的店、市场或网站。要成功，你需要懂些什么？
  3. Why do stories of lucky finds spread so widely, while stories of expensive mistakes do not?
    为什么捡到宝的故事传得那么广，而花大钱买错的故事却很少有人讲？

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 2第 2 句 (begins开头 “The more expensive …”)

  • The subject is long: a noun phrase, a where clause and an infinitive of purpose, and the verb is comes only at the end.主语很长：The more expensive kind … + where 定语从句 + to 不定式（目的），谓语 is 到句末才出现。
  • The more expensive kind: of the two kinds, the dearer one, so a comparative is used.The more expensive kind：较贵的那一类（两类之中，比较级）。
  • keep something free from: to protect it from.keep sth free from：使……免受。

Sentence 4第 4 句 (begins开头 “But no one …”)

  • muster up courage to do: to find enough courage.muster up courage to do：鼓起勇气做。
  • a less pretentious shop: one that makes fewer claims to grandeur.a less pretentious shop：不那么讲究门面的店。

Sentence 9第 9 句 (begins开头 “To do this, …”)

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

at least as ... as / not so ... as: comparisons of equalityat least as … as / not so … as：同级比较
as plus the plain adjective plus as means 'equal to'; the negative is not as or not so ... as. You can add words in front: at least, almost or nearly, just, and twice or three times for multiples.
as + 原级 + as 表示「和……一样」；否定用 not as / not so … as。前面可加修饰：at least（至少）、almost / nearly（几乎）、just（正好）、twice / three times（倍数）。

Sentence 9 says a bargain hunter must know no less than the dealer does.

本课：第 9 句：淘宝者至少要和店主一样内行。

  • The new road is at least as dangerous as the old one.
  • This flat is twice as big as our last one.
  • The film was not so good as the book.
be about to do ... when ...: on the point of ... when suddenly ...be about to do … when …：正要……忽然……
be about to do means 'just going to'. It often goes with when, which brings in the sudden event that interrupts, much like 'and at that moment'. Similar are be on the point of doing ... when and had just done ... when. Do not use be about to with an exact future time.
be about to do 表示「即将、正要」，常和 when 连用，when 引出打断它的突发事件（when 在这里是「这时」，并列连词的意味）。同类：be on the point of doing … when；had just done … when。be about to 不和具体的将来时间连用。

In sentence 16 Frank is on his way out when he spots the crate.

本课：第 16 句：弗兰克正要走，忽然注意到地上的箱子。

  • I was about to go to bed when the phone rang.
  • She was on the point of giving up when she found the answer.
  • We had just sat down when there was a knock at the door.
A noun or pronoun with an adjective or participle, used without a verb名词/代词 + 形容词或分词：独立结构
A noun or pronoun followed by an adjective, participle or prepositional phrase can add a comment at the end of a sentence, as if being or which was had been left out. Examples are much of it broken, some of them still alive, his hands in his pockets.
句末用「名词/代词 + 形容词、分词或介词短语」补充说明，相当于省掉了 being 或 which was。常见：much of it broken、some of them still alive、his hands in his pockets。

The end of sentence 20 uses this pattern to add that a lot of the crockery was broken.

本课：第 20 句末尾用这种结构说碗碟「很多是破的」。

  • The children came home, their clothes covered in mud.
  • There were forty passengers, most of them tourists.
  • He stood at the window, hands in his pockets.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • X exert a peculiar fascination on … X 对……有一种奇特的吸引力
    Old railway stations exert a peculiar fascination on some travellers.
  • There is always hope that … will be found. 总有希望能找到……
    There is always hope that a missing cat will be found.
  • No one discovers … by chance. 没有人是偶然发现……的
    No one learns a language by chance.
  • X was about to … when he noticed … X 正要……，忽然注意到……
    She was about to close the shop when she noticed a child at the window.
  • X could hardly hide his delight, for … X 几乎掩饰不住兴奋，因为……
    The boy could hardly hide his delight, for his team had won.

Summary writing摘要写作

In no more than 80 words, tell how Frank found the masterpiece.

用不超过 80 个词讲述弗兰克是如何发现那幅名画的。

Points a good summary covers好摘要要覆盖的要点
  • One Saturday morning Frank spent a long time in an antique shop near the writer's home and was on his way out.一个周六上午，弗兰克第一次去作者家附近的一家古董店，看了很久，正要离开。
  • He saw a large unopened crate and persuaded the dealer to open it; apart from a carved dagger, it held mostly broken plates.他看见地上有只没开的大箱子，求店主打开；里面除了一把雕花匕首，大多是破碗碟。
  • At the bottom was a small painting that reminded him of an Italian work he knew, and the dealer asked only fifty pounds.箱底有一幅小画，构图和线条让他想起一幅熟悉的意大利画，店主只要 50 英镑。
  • It turned out to be a lost work by Correggio, worth a fortune.这幅画后来被证实是柯勒乔一幅不为人知的杰作，价值极高。
Our model summary本站范文

On a Saturday morning Frank spent hours in an antique shop he had never visited. As he was leaving, he saw an unopened crate and persuaded the dealer to open it. Inside were a carved dagger and a lot of broken plates. At the bottom lay a small painting which reminded Frank of an Italian picture he knew well. The dealer asked only fifty pounds. It later proved to be an unknown work by Correggio, worth a fortune.（78 词）
