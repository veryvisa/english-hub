Lesson 16 · Mary Had a Little Lamb 玛丽有一只小羊羔

📘 New Concept English · Book 3 · Lesson 16 “Mary Had a Little Lamb”新概念英语 第三册 Lesson 16《Mary Had a Little Lamb》

B2🧠 20 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
The title borrows an English nursery rhyme. In a small Greek village, Mary's most treasured white lamb is taken: the rope has been cut. Her husband Dimitri asks around, hears that his neighbour Aleko has just come by a lamb, goes over, accuses him to his face and threatens the police. Aleko denies it and walks him through to the backyard: the lamb he has bought is black. Dimitri apologises, ashamed of himself, and because it is raining he sits in the house for half an hour. He steps outside, and the black lamb is almost white. The lesson is a small detective story with a turn in the last line: the writer keeps the one clue that explains everything — that wool can be dyed — until the final sentence. Learn accuse sb of, deny doing, apologize for having done, had better, and the perfect gerund.
标题借用英语童谣《玛丽有只小羊羔》。希腊小村里，玛丽最珍爱的白羊羔被人割断绳子偷走了。丈夫季米特里打听到邻居阿列科刚「弄到」一只新羊，便上门指控他偷羊，扬言不还就报警。阿列科否认，领他看后院：他新买的羊是黑的。季米特里羞愧地道歉，因下雨在邻居家坐了半小时——出门一看，黑羊被雨淋得几乎全白了。本课是结尾反转的推理小故事：作者把关键线索（羊毛是染黑的）藏到最后一句。学 accuse sb of、deny doing、apologize for having done、had better 和完成式动名词。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. What made Dimitri suspect Aleko?季米特里凭什么怀疑阿列科？
  2. How did Aleko answer the charge?阿列科是怎样为自己辩解的？
  3. What did Dimitri notice when the rain stopped, and was his apology deserved?雨停后季米特里发现了什么？他道歉道对了吗？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander. The title is lifted from the American nursery rhyme by Sarah Josepha Hale, printed in Boston in eighteen thirty; the village, Perachora, is a real place, and the lesson's description of where it is needs a small correction.

课文由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为教材自撰。标题取自萨拉·约瑟法·黑尔的美国童谣，一八三〇年在波士顿印行；而故事里的村子佩拉霍拉是真实地名，课文对它位置的说法需要一点修正。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

Perachora exists. It is an inland village in Corinthia, up in the foothills of the Geraneia mountains at about three hundred metres, roughly seven kilometres north-west of the spa town of Loutraki, and it sits on the peninsula that closes the eastern end of the Corinthian Gulf. At the tip of that peninsula stands one of the oldest sanctuaries in Greece, the shrine of Hera, excavated in the nineteen thirties and still the main reason outsiders go there. The village has been emptying slowly for decades: a little over a thousand people at the last census, down from about twelve hundred and fifty a generation earlier. Olive oil and pine resin are what it sells. All of that matters to the story in one practical way. A place of that size is a place where everyone's animals are known by sight, which is exactly what the lesson relies on when Dimitri says that finding the thief will not be difficult in such a village — and it is why the thief needed the dye. Marking sheep with coloured pigment is normal husbandry, so the dye itself would not have been hard to come by, and in a village of a thousand people it was the only way to make a stolen animal into a different animal.

佩拉霍拉确有其地。它是科林西亚的一个内陆村子，位于杰拉尼亚山麓海拔约三百米处，在温泉镇卢特拉基西北约七公里，坐落在封住科林斯湾东端的那个半岛上。半岛尖端立着希腊最古老的圣所之一——赫拉神庙，一九三〇年代发掘，至今仍是外人去那里的主要理由。村子几十年来一直在慢慢空掉：上一次人口普查略过一千人，比上一代人时的约一千二百五十人少了。它卖的是橄榄油和松脂。这一切对故事有一个很实际的意义。这种规模的地方，人人的牲口都认得脸，而这正是课文所倚仗的——季米特里说在这么小的村子里找贼不难；也正因如此，贼才需要染料。用彩色颜料给羊做记号是正常的畜牧操作，所以染料本身不难弄到；而在一个千把人的村子里，这是把一只偷来的牲口变成另一只牲口的唯一办法。

✨ How it is written赏析

This is a lesson in how to hide a clue in plain sight. Count the facts the writer gives you before the ending: the rope was cut, so a person did it; the village is tiny, so the thief is local; the neighbour has suddenly come by a lamb; the neighbour's lamb is black. Every one of those is true, and together they point the wrong way, because the reader has silently added a fifth fact that was never stated — that the colour of an animal cannot change. Then it rains. Notice the economy of the ending: the rain is introduced for an entirely innocent reason, as the thing that keeps Dimitri in the house, and it is a full sentence away from doing any detective work. Notice too that the writer never lets Dimitri work it out; the discovery is handed to the reader at the same moment, in a sentence with an exclamation mark, the only one in the text. One more thing to steal: the apology. Making the wronged man apologise, in the perfect gerund, with proper shame, is what makes the reversal land — the further the writer pushes Dimitri into being wrong, the harder the last line hits.

这一课教的是怎样把线索藏在明处。数一数结尾之前作者给了你哪些事实：绳子是割断的，所以是人干的；村子很小，所以贼是本地人；邻居忽然弄到一只羊；邻居的羊是黑的。每一条都是真的，合起来却指向错的方向，因为读者悄悄补上了第五条从未被说出的事实——牲口的颜色不会变。然后下雨了。注意结尾的省俭：雨是以一个完全无辜的理由被引入的，它只是让季米特里留在屋里，而且距离做任何侦探工作还隔着整整一句。也注意作者从不让季米特里自己想通；真相是和读者同时被交出的，在全文唯一一个带感叹号的句子里。还有一招值得偷：那次道歉。让受害的一方用完成式动名词、带着真切的羞愧去道歉，正是反转能落地的原因——作者把季米特里推得越错，最后一句打得越重。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The reasoning in the story is better than it first looks, and the moral it is usually taught with is worse. Dimitri's inference was sound: in a village of that size, a sudden new lamb next door is strong evidence. What defeated him was not haste but an unexamined assumption about what cannot change, and the lesson is really about that, not about the danger of speaking out. The practical point has held up too. Livestock theft is still a live rural crime, and it is still fought with identification rather than with suspicion: ear tags and, in much of Europe, electronic identification, so that an animal's paperwork rather than its colour settles who owns it. A dyed fleece would now fail at the first check. Readers in China can set the episode beside the familiar village logic of a society where everybody is an acquaintance, without any translation at all: in a place where everyone's property is known on sight, theft requires disguise rather than concealment, which is why the countryside has always had marks, brands and notches. And the nursery rhyme in the title has its own curious afterlife worth knowing: its first line is what Edison recited into the tinfoil phonograph in late eighteen seventy-seven, which makes a children's verse about a lamb among the first human words ever recorded and played back.

故事里的推理比初看好，而通常配给它的那条寓意比它差。季米特里的推断是站得住的：在那种规模的村子里，隔壁忽然多出一只羊，是很强的证据。击败他的不是急躁，而是一条没被检查过的假设——什么东西是不会变的；这一课真正讲的是这个，而不是「别乱说话」。实用的那一层也站住了。盗窃牲畜至今仍是活着的农村犯罪，而对付它靠的仍是识别而不是怀疑：耳标，以及在欧洲大部分地方的电子标识，于是决定归属的是牲口的档案而不是它的颜色。一张染过的羊皮如今过不了第一道核查。中国读者可以不加翻译地把这件事摆到熟人社会的村庄逻辑旁边：在一个人人的财产都认得脸的地方，偷东西需要的是伪装而不是藏匿，这正是乡间自古就有记号、烙印和耳缺的原因。标题里那首童谣还有一段值得知道的后事：它的第一行正是爱迪生在一八七七年末对着锡箔留声机念的那句话——于是一首关于小羊的儿歌，成了人类最早被录下又放出来的话语之一。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 2第 2 句 (begins开头 “One of Mary's …”)

  • One of + plural noun as subject takes a singular verb, hence was.One of + 复数名词 作主语，谓语用单数 was。
  • had given: past perfect, because the gift came before the loss.had given：过去完成时，送羊在丢羊之前。

Sentence 3第 3 句 (begins开头 “She kept it …”)

  • keep + object + past participle: hold something in a state (here, fastened up).keep + 宾语 + 过去分词：使……保持某种状态（拴着）。
  • go to fetch: go and bring it back.go to fetch：去把……接回来。

Sentence 5第 5 句 (begins开头 “The rope had …”)

  • Two past perfect passives: the cutting and the stealing both happened before the discovery.两个过去完成时被动：绳子被割、羊被偷都发生在发现之前。
  • it was obvious that ...: it is a dummy subject standing in for the clause.it was obvious that…：it 形式主语。

Sentence 8第 8 句 (begins开头 “He knew it …”)

  • it stands for the business of finding the thief; prove + adjective: turn out to be.it 指「找到小偷」这件事；prove + 形容词：结果是……。
  • such a small village: such + a + adjective + noun.such a small village：such + a + 形容词 + 名词。

Sentence 11第 11 句 (begins开头 “Dimitri immediately went …”)

  • accuse sb of doing: say that somebody has done it.accuse sb of doing：指控某人做了……。

Sentence 12第 12 句 (begins开头 “He told him …”)

  • had better do: you would be wise to, with a threat behind it; it stays unchanged in reported speech.had better do：最好（带威胁）；间接引语里 had better 不变。
  • or: otherwise. would: will becomes would in reported speech.or：否则。would：间接引语中 will 变 would。

Sentence 15第 15 句 (begins开头 “Ashamed of having …”)

  • Ashamed of ...: an adjective phrase giving the reason = As he was ashamed of ...Ashamed of…：形容词短语作原因状语 = As he was ashamed of…
  • having done: the perfect gerund, which marks the action as earlier — first the accusation, then the shame and the apology.having done：完成式动名词，强调动作发生在前（先指控，后羞愧、道歉）。

Sentence 18第 18 句 (begins开头 “Its wool, which …”)

  • dye sth black: an object plus a complement; the passive is be dyed black.dye sth black：把……染成黑色（宾补）；被动 be dyed black。
  • wash sth clean: the same object-plus-complement pattern.wash sth clean：洗干净（同样是宾补结构）。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

Verb + preposition + doing: accuse sb of / apologize for / be ashamed of / deny doing动词 + 介词 + doing：accuse sb of / apologize for / be ashamed of / deny doing
accuse sb of doing; apologize (to sb) for doing; be ashamed of doing; deny doing — deny takes the gerund straight, with no preposition. After a preposition, always use the gerund.
accuse sb of doing（指控）；apologize (to sb) for doing（为……道歉）；be ashamed of doing（为……羞愧）；deny doing（否认做过，deny 后直接接 doing，没有介词）。介词后一律用动名词。
  • She accused me of lying.
  • He denied stealing the money.
  • I apologize for keeping you waiting.
The perfect gerund having done完成式动名词 having done
Use having done when the gerund's action came before the main verb and you want to mark that order; where the order is already plain, the simple gerund will do (He denied taking it).
动名词动作发生在主句谓语之前，并要强调这种先后时，用 having done；不强调时用一般 doing 也可以（He denied taking it）。
  • He was proud of having won the prize.
  • She regretted having said those words.
had better (not) do: you would be wise tohad better (not) do：最好（不）
had better + bare infinitive gives advice or a warning, usually with 'or there will be trouble' understood; the negative is had better not do, and the short form is 'd better. Do not use it for polite requests.
had better + 动词原形，表示劝告或警告，常隐含「不然会有麻烦」；否定是 had better not do；缩写 'd better。不用于礼貌请求。

In this lesson, sentence 12: Dimitri warns his neighbour — had better return it, with the police as the alternative.

本课：第 12 句，季米特里警告阿列科 had better return it，否则就报警。

  • You 'd better take an umbrella. It looks like rain.
  • We had better not be late again.

Our model summary本站范文

Mary's little white lamb disappeared one evening; its rope had been cut. Her husband Dimitri soon heard that his neighbour Aleko had just got a new lamb, so he rushed over and accused him of theft. Aleko showed him the animal in his yard: it was black. Feeling foolish, Dimitri said sorry. Rain kept him in Aleko's house for half an hour, and when he left, the black lamb had turned almost white. Its wool had been dyed.（78 词）
