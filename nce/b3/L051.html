Lesson 51 · Predicting the Future 预测未来

📘 New Concept English · Book 3 · Lesson 51 “Predicting the Future”新概念英语 第三册 Lesson 51《Predicting the Future》

B2🧠 19 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
Forecasting is famously hard. In the mid seventies, who would have guessed that by the year 2000 a computer would sit in almost every home, like the television? At that time computers lived only in big companies, government offices and large organisations: huge mainframes filling cooled rooms and needing full-time staff to run them. In 1975 a crude American machine, the Altair, appeared; it was the first that deserves the name home computer. An Apple followed in the late seventies, and in the early eighties IBM built the first Personal Computer, running an operating system from a small firm called Microsoft. Yet back in the sixties an Englishman, Leon Bagrit, had foreseen many of today's uses: machines that fit in the hand, warn of traffic and suggest a way round, help doctors find what is wrong, and take dull routine work off office staff. He also rejected the popular fear that computers would think for themselves and take over. What he could not foresee was the Internet. This is exposition with a judgement of one man: it begins with how hard forecasting is, then uses a short history of the computer to show how far ahead one person saw. The grammar to learn is notoriously, as common as, take over, be capable of doing, relieve sb of sth, couldn't possibly have done, inversion after Nor, and the unreal were alive.
预测未来出了名地难。七十年代中期谁能想到，到世纪末电脑会像电视机一样走进千家万户？那时电脑只在大企业、政府和大机构里，是占满整间空调房、配专职技术员的「大型机」。1975 年美国推出原始的 Altair，算得上第一台「家用电脑」；七十年代末出了 Apple；八十年代初 IBM 造出第一台个人电脑，跑的是当时还很小的微软公司写的 DOS。可早在六十年代，英国人利昂·巴格里特就预言了今天的许多用途：掌上大小、报堵车并建议绕行、帮医生诊断、替文员做枯燥的重复工作；他也不信电脑会「自己思考」「统治世界」。他想不到的是互联网。本课是说明文＋人物评价：先讲预测之难，再用一段电脑简史反衬一个人的眼光。学 notoriously、as common as、take over、be capable of doing、relieve sb of sth、couldn't possibly have done、Nor could he 倒装与虚拟 were alive。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：

  2. Which uses did Bagrit get right, and which popular belief did he reject?巴格里特预言对了哪些事？他否定了哪种流行看法？
  3. Why does the writer call his forecasts particularly remarkable?为什么作者说巴格里特的预言「格外了不起」？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course; the 1990s edition of New Concept English replaced an older lesson with this one, which is why it mentions the IBM PC and the Internet. Leon Bagrit was a real person, a British industrialist who gave the BBC Reith Lectures in 1964 under the title The Age of Automation.

课文为教材编写，九十年代修订版用它替换了旧课，所以才会讲到 IBM 个人电脑和互联网。利昂·巴格里特是真实人物：英国实业家，1964 年在 BBC 做过里斯讲座，题为《自动化时代》。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

The milestones in the lesson can be checked. The Altair 8800, sold as a kit by a small firm in New Mexico, appeared on the cover of Popular Electronics in January 1975 and is usually counted as the spark of the home-computer boom; the young founders of Microsoft wrote a version of the BASIC language for it. The Apple II arrived in 1977. IBM announced its Personal Computer in August 1981, and the decision to buy its operating system from Microsoft, and to build the machine from parts others could also buy, is the main reason it was copied so widely. Bagrit's six Reith Lectures were broadcast late in 1964. In them he argued that automation would take over routine work and serve people rather than replace their judgement, a sober position at a time when talk of thinking machines was everywhere.

课文里的几个节点都能核对。Altair 8800 由新墨西哥州一家小公司以套件出售，1975 年 1 月登上《大众电子》封面，通常被视为家用电脑热潮的起点；微软的两位年轻创始人为它写了一版 BASIC 语言。Apple II 在 1977 年问世。IBM 于 1981 年 8 月发布个人电脑；它向微软购买操作系统、用别人也能买到的零件组装整机，这正是它被大量仿制的主要原因。巴格里特的六讲里斯讲座在 1964 年底播出，他认为自动化会接手例行工作、为人服务而不是取代人的判断——在「会思考的机器」说法满天飞的年代，这是很冷静的立场。

✨ How it is written赏析

The writer makes a man look wise by first making the future look impossible. The opening question invites the reader to admit that nobody in the seventies saw the home computer coming, and the history that follows is told in short dated steps, each one a small surprise. Only then does the essay jump back a decade further, to someone who saw much of it anyway. The list of his predictions is built with four clauses that all begin the same way, so that the reader feels the forecasts piling up. The admission that he missed the Internet is placed after the list, not before it, which makes it sound like a fair limit rather than a failure. The ending turns the whole piece round: instead of praising the past, it wishes for a Bagrit of our own.

作者先把未来写得不可预测，再让一个人显得有远见。开头的问句请读者承认七十年代没人看得见家用电脑，接着的简史用一步一个年份的短句讲，每一步都是一个小意外。然后文章才再往前跳十年，落到一个照样看到了大半的人身上。他的预言列成四个开头相同的从句，读者能感到预言一层层堆起来。「他没想到互联网」放在清单之后而不是之前，于是听起来像公允的局限，而不是失误。结尾把整篇调了个头：不是夸过去，而是盼望我们这个时代也有一个巴格里特。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

Read today, the lesson is itself a forecast that has aged. It ends with the Internet as the last wonder, and says nothing of phones that are computers, of maps that reroute us as we drive, or of software that writes and talks. Bagrit's traffic prediction came true in a form he might not recognise, through satellites and phones rather than a box in an office. The question he answered most clearly, whether machines would think and rule, is open again in the age of large language models, and the honest answer now is less tidy than his. For a Chinese reader there is a second story hidden in the dates: in 1981, when the IBM PC appeared, very few Chinese homes had a television; within one generation China became the world's biggest maker of personal computers. Forecasts made in one country often miss how fast another can leap.

今天读来，这一课本身就是一则已经过时的预言。它以互联网收尾、当作最后的奇迹，却没提到手机就是电脑、导航边开车边改道、软件会写会说。巴格里特的路况预言成真了，只是形式他未必认得出：靠卫星和手机，不是办公室里的一台机器。他回答得最清楚的那个问题——机器会不会思考、会不会统治——在大语言模型时代又重新打开了，现在诚实的回答没有他那么干脆。对中国读者，年份里还藏着另一个故事：1981 年 IBM 个人电脑问世时，中国有电视的家庭还很少；一代人之内，中国成了全球最大的个人电脑制造国。在一个国家做的预言，常常看不到另一个国家能跳得多快。

    巴格里特说电脑会为人服务而不会统治人。今天这还是稳妥的回答吗？各给一个正反理由。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 1第 1 句 (begins开头 “Predicting the future …”)

  • notoriously means famous for being bad or troublesome, which famously does not imply.notoriously：出了名地，带「让人头疼」的色彩；比 famously 多一层贬义。

Sentence 3第 3 句 (begins开头 “that by the …”)

  • could have imagined in a question is rhetorical: it means nobody could.could have imagined：反问，「当时谁想得到」＝没人想得到。
  • would be is the future seen from a point in the past.would be：过去的将来，站在七十年代往后看。
  • as ... as compares two things, here with a place phrase set in the middle.as common … as：同级比较，中间插入地点状语 in people's homes。

Sentence 7第 7 句 (begins开头 “Mainframe computers were …”)

  • Two -ing phrases add details about the machines, one after the other.often occupying / employing：两个现在分词并列，补充说明大型机的特点。
  • run here is a past participle with a passive sense, so the three items are not quite parallel, a small slip in the text.run on：注意这里是过去分词（被动），「靠专门编写的软件运行」，和前两个 -ing 形式不平行，是课文的一处不工整。

Sentence 10第 10 句 (begins开头 “many of their …”)

  • take over means to take control of a job someone else used to do; here it is present perfect passive.take over：接管、取代；这里用现在完成时被动。
  • commonly known as introduces the ordinary name.commonly known as：通称为。

Sentence 22第 22 句 (begins开头 “Bagrit dismissed the …”)

  • The that clause tells us what the idea was; it is an appositive clause.the idea that …：同位语从句，说明 idea 的内容。
  • which refers back to the whole idea, not just to one noun.which：非限制性定语从句，指代前面整个想法。

Sentence 28第 28 句 (begins开头 “Of course, Leon …”)

  • couldn't possibly have done is the strongest way to say something was impossible in the past.couldn't possibly have done：对过去的否定推测，语气最强——「绝不可能预见到」。
  • enable sb to do: make it possible for someone to do something.enable sb to do：使某人能够做。

Sentence 30第 30 句 (begins开头 “Nor could he …”)

  • With Nor at the start, the auxiliary comes before the subject, carrying on the negative of the sentence before.Nor 放句首，后面部分倒装（could he），承接上一句的否定。

Sentence 34第 34 句 (begins开头 “If he, or …”)

  • were with might is an unreal present: Bagrit is no longer alive.were … might：与现在事实相反的虚拟（巴格里特已经不在了）。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

Modal plus have done: judging the past情态动词 + have done：对过去的推测
could have done means it was possible in the past; couldn't possibly have done means it was impossible; must have done means you are sure it happened; might have done means it perhaps happened. In each case you are looking back from now.
could have done＝过去本可以/有可能；couldn't (possibly) have done＝过去不可能；must have done＝过去一定；might have done＝过去也许。都是「现在回头看过去」。

Sentence 2 asks a question that expects the answer nobody; sentences 28 and 32 state flatly that he could not have known.

本课：第 2 句用反问的 could have imagined；第 28 句用 couldn't possibly have foreseen；第 30 句用 Nor could he have foreseen。

  • Nobody could have guessed that the shy boy would become a famous singer.
  • She couldn't possibly have seen me; I was behind the door.
  • The road is wet. It must have rained in the night.
Inversion after Nor and NeitherNor / Neither 开头的倒装
To carry on a negative idea, put Nor or Neither first, then the auxiliary, modal or be, then the subject.
承接前面的否定句，用 Nor/Neither + 助动词/情态动词/be + 主语。

Sentence 28 says he could not foresee the Internet; sentence 30 adds, with inversion, that he could not foresee how we use it either.

本课：第 28 句说他想不到互联网，第 30 句用 Nor could he … 接着说他也想不到我们怎么用它。

  • I don't like coffee. Nor does my sister.
  • He had never been abroad, nor had he wanted to go.
relieve sb of sth and enable sb to dorelieve sb of sth 与 enable sb to do
relieve sb of sth means to take a load or task off someone, and jokingly can mean to steal; enable sb to do means to make someone able to do something. In both, the person comes straight after the verb.
relieve sb of sth＝替某人卸下（负担、工作），也可戏说「偷走」；enable sb to do＝使某人能够做。两者都把人放在动词后面。

Sentence 26 has computers taking dull work off office staff; sentence 29 has the Internet making instant contact possible.

本课：第 26 句说电脑会替文员卸下枯燥工作；第 29 句说互联网使我们能即时联系任何人。

  • The new machine relieved the workers of the heaviest lifting.
  • A scholarship enabled her to study abroad.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • X is notoriously difficult to … ……出了名地难
    This old lock is notoriously difficult to open.
  • Who could have imagined, back in …, that … would …? 当年谁能想到……会……？
    Who could have imagined, back in 1990, that phones would replace maps?
  • Many of X's jobs have been taken over by … X 的许多工作已被……取代
    Many of the bank's jobs have been taken over by machines.
  • X foresaw a time when … X 预见到将来会有……的时候
    My grandmother foresaw a time when everyone would shop from home.
  • Considering how recent …, it is all the more surprising that … 考虑到……如此之新，……就更让人惊讶了
    Considering how young she is, it is all the more surprising that she won.

用不超过 80 个词说明巴格里特预见了电脑的哪些用途，并说明为什么作者认为这很了不起。

  • Home machines appeared from 1975, IBM's PC followed in the early eighties, and home computers then spread everywhere.1975 年起出现家用电脑，八十年代初 IBM 推出个人电脑，此后家用电脑普及。
  • As early as the sixties Bagrit predicted hand-held machines, traffic advice, help with diagnosis and the end of routine office work, and rejected the fear that computers would rule.早在六十年代，巴格里特就预见到掌上电脑、交通信息、辅助诊断和替代文书工作，并否定了电脑会统治世界的说法。
  • He could not have foreseen the Internet, but given how fast computers changed, his forecasts stand out all the more.他想不到互联网；但在电脑发展如此迅速的情况下，他的预见尤其难得。
Our model summary本站范文

In the seventies computers were giant machines kept by big organisations. Home computers arrived in 1975, and IBM's PC in the early eighties made them common. Yet in the sixties Leon Bagrit had already seen that computers would one day be small enough to carry, give traffic advice, help doctors and do dull office tasks, and he laughed at the idea that they would rule us. He missed the Internet, but given how quickly things changed, his vision was remarkable.（80 词）
