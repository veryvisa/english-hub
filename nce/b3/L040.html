Lesson 40 · Who's Who 真假难辨

📘 New Concept English · Book 3 · Lesson 40 “Who's Who”新概念英语 第三册 Lesson 40《Who's Who》

B2🧠 23 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
Nobody has ever explained why college students seem to love practical jokes more than anyone. They look down on crude tricks such as a false fire alarm and go in for the clever hoax, which is funny to everyone except its victims. One student sees two workmen with a pneumatic drill outside his college. He rings the police and says that two students dressed as workmen are digging up the road; then he goes to the workmen and warns them that a student dressed as a policeman is going round playing silly tricks, so they should ignore him. Both sides thank him for the warning. From a doorway nearby he watches: a policeman politely asks the men to leave, gets a rude answer, calls four more officers, and they try to take the drill. One workman loses his temper and threatens to call the police; the officers point out that he is already under arrest. He asks to make one call, an officer goes with him to a phone box, and only when he hears the man really ringing the police does he realize that everyone has been fooled. This is a story that follows a general claim, and the joke is that each side is sure the other is fake. The grammar to learn is never been explained why, no self-respecting, be grateful to someone for, Sure enough, remonstrate with, and inversion after Only when.
没人解释得清，为什么大学生比谁都爱搞恶作剧。他们看不上打电话谎报火警这种粗俗把戏，专搞精巧的骗局——除了受害者，人人都觉得好笑。有个学生看见两个工人在学校门口用风钻挖路，先打电话报警，说有两个学生扮成工人在挖马路；挂了电话又跑去告诉工人：等会儿有个学生扮成警察来捣乱，别理他。警察和工人都谢谢他「提前通风报信」。他躲在附近门洞里看好戏：警察客客气气请工人走，挨了一顿粗话，叫来四个帮手要抢风钻，一个工人气急了，说要报警——警察讥讽地说你们已经被捕了。工人请求打个电话，警察陪他去公用电话，听见他真的在给警察局打电话，这才明白大家都上了当。本课是先概括、后举例的叙事，笑点来自「双方都以为对方是假的」。学 never been explained why、no self-respecting、be grateful to sb for、Sure enough、remonstrate with、Only when … did … （倒装）。

  2. What did the student tell the police, and what did he tell the workmen?那个学生分别对警察和工人说了什么？
  3. How did the police finally discover the trick?警察最后是怎么发现自己上当的？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander and set in an unnamed university. The trick itself, though, is an old one, and a version of it is attached to the most famous English hoaxer of the twentieth century, Horace de Vere Cole.

课文为编者 L. G. 亚历山大为教材自撰，故事发生在一所没点名的大学。不过这个把戏本身由来已久，二十世纪英国最出名的恶作剧大王霍勒斯·德维尔·科尔的名下就挂着一个版本。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

Cole was a wealthy Cambridge-educated prankster whose best-known stunt was the Dreadnought hoax of 1910. He and a group of friends, among them the young Virginia Stephen, later the novelist Virginia Woolf, blacked their faces, put on robes and false beards, and were received on board the Royal Navy's newest battleship as a visiting party of Abyssinian royalty; the navy was badly embarrassed when the truth came out. Stories about Cole multiplied, and one of them is very like this lesson: he and his friends are said to have dressed as workmen and dug a trench across a busy London street, with police officers helping to direct the traffic round them. Versions differ on the details, and some may have grown in the retelling, so treat the street-digging tale as legend rather than record. The pattern in the lesson — telling each side that the other is the impostor — is the cleverest part, and it is the one detail that turns an ordinary prank into a hoax: nobody has to be fooled by a costume, only by a warning.

科尔是一位受过剑桥教育的富家子弟、恶作剧高手，最出名的一桩是 1910 年的「无畏舰骗局」。他和一群朋友——其中有年轻的弗吉尼亚·斯蒂芬，即后来的小说家弗吉尼亚·伍尔夫——把脸涂黑，穿上长袍、戴上假胡子，冒充一支来访的阿比西尼亚王室代表团，登上了英国皇家海军最新的战舰并受到接待；真相大白后，海军颜面扫地。关于科尔的故事越传越多，其中一个与本课十分相似：据说他和朋友们扮成工人，在伦敦一条繁忙街道上横着挖了一道沟，警察还帮着指挥车辆绕行。各个版本在细节上说法不一，有些可能是越传越神，所以挖马路的故事应当当作传说而不是记录来看。课文里的套路——分别告诉双方「对方是冒牌货」——是最巧妙的部分，也正是这一个细节把普通的恶作剧变成了骗局：没有人需要被一身行头骗过，只需要被一句提醒骗过。

✨ How it is written赏析

The lesson is built like a well-made farce. Its first paragraph sets the rule — students prefer the elegant hoax — and even tells us whose side to be on, since the joke amuses all but the people it is played on. The setup then takes only four sentences: two lies, each tailored to its listener, and the hiding place. After that the student vanishes from the action and the two sides play out the script he has written for them, each behaving exactly as a real policeman or a real workman would, which is why the misunderstanding can only grow. The writer measures the rise in steps: polite request, rude reply, threat, help called, four more officers, a struggle. The climax is a perfect reversal in speech: the workman threatens to call the police, and the police answer that he is already under arrest. The last sentence holds back the realization until its very end with an inverted only when, so that the reader reaches the truth a beat after the officer does.

这一课的结构像一出做工精良的闹剧。第一段立下规矩——学生偏爱高雅的骗局——甚至告诉我们该站在哪一边：除了受害者，人人都觉得好笑。接下来布局只用了四句话：两句谎言，各按听者量身定做，再加一个藏身之处。此后那个学生就从情节里消失了，双方按他写好的剧本表演，每个人的举动都和真警察、真工人一模一样，所以误会只会越来越大。作者一级一级地量着冲突的升高：客气的请求、粗鲁的回答、威胁、叫来帮手、又来四名警察、扭打。高潮是一次完美的语言反转：工人威胁要叫警察，警察回答他已经被捕了。最后一句用倒装的 only when 把醒悟一直留到句末，于是读者比警官慢半拍才弄明白真相。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The lesson says the hoax amuses all but the people it is played on, and today that exception carries more weight. The workmen lost a day's work and were nearly arrested; the police spent an hour on nothing, and in a real emergency that hour would have mattered. Many countries now treat hoax calls to the emergency services as an offence, because they draw officers away from people who need them. The student's trick is also one that only works when both sides trust a stranger's warning and distrust each other, which is a good description of how false information spreads now. A message that says 'the people coming to you are fakes' is cheap to send and hard to check, and it turns honest people against each other without anyone having to wear a costume. Read this way, the lesson is less a celebration of student wit than a small, accurate model of a modern problem: the quickest way to fool two groups is to give each a reason not to believe the other.

课文说这种骗局「除了受害者，人人都觉得好笑」，在今天，这个「除了」分量更重了。工人损失了一天的活，还差点被捕；警察白忙了一个小时，而在真正的紧急情况下，那一个小时是要命的。如今许多国家把对紧急服务的恶作剧报警列为违法，因为它会把警力从真正需要的人身边调走。这个学生的把戏还有一个特点：只有当双方都信任一个陌生人的提醒、彼此又不信任时才奏效——这恰好描述了今天虚假信息是怎样传播的。一条写着「来找你的那些人是冒牌货」的消息，发出去成本很低，核实起来很难，不用任何人换一身行头，就能让老实人彼此反目。这样读，这一课与其说是在赞美学生的机智，不如说是一个精确的小模型，对应一个现代问题：要骗倒两群人，最快的办法是分别给他们一个不相信对方的理由。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

Only when / then / by ... at the front: inversion in the main clauseOnly when / Only then / Only by … 放句首：主句倒装
When only goes with an adverbial — a clause, adverb or phrase — and comes first, the main clause is inverted: the helping verb, be or the modal goes before the subject. It is the main clause that is inverted, not the when clause.
only 修饰状语（从句、副词、介词短语）并放在句首时，主句要部分倒装：助动词 / be / 情态动词放到主语前。注意倒装的是主句，不是 when 从句。

Sentence 23 puts Only when first, so its main clause is inverted.

本课：第 23 句 Only when 放句首，主句用了倒装 did he realize。

  • Only when the lights went out did we notice the stars.
  • Only then did she understand what he meant.
  • Only by working hard can you pass this exam.
be (not) to do: instructions and arrangementsbe (not) to do：命令、安排
be plus an infinitive can give an order, an instruction or an arrangement, much like must or are supposed to. It is common in reported speech, as in He told us that we were not to leave, and in formal plans, as in The Queen is to visit Canada in May.
be + 不定式可表示命令、指示或安排（相当于 must / should / are supposed to）。在间接引语里常用：He told us that we were not to leave. 也用于正式安排：The Queen is to visit Canada in May.

In sentences 8 and 11 the student instructs the workmen not to treat the policeman as real.

本课：第 8–9 句：学生告诉工人，警察来了「不要把他当真」。

  • You are not to open this door.
  • The children were to stay indoors until the storm passed.
  • The new bridge is to open next year.
A preposition left at the end of a relative clause; no self-respecting ... would定语从句中介词后置；no self-respecting … would
In a relative clause the preposition of a phrasal verb can go at the end, as in which no one would indulge in; more formally, in which no one would indulge. no self-respecting plus a noun plus would means that nobody of that kind with any pride would do it.
定语从句里动词短语的介词可以放在句末：which no one would indulge in（正式写法：in which no one would indulge）。no self-respecting + 名词 + would：「凡有点自尊的……都不会」，用来表达强烈否定。

Sentence 3 uses both to say that students are above false fire alarms.

本课：第 3 句两样都用了：学生不屑于谎报火警。

  • This is the house which I grew up in.
  • She is someone whom you can rely on.
  • No self-respecting footballer would miss a penalty like that.

Summary writing摘要写作

In no more than 80 words, tell how the student fooled the police and the workmen.

用不超过 80 个词讲述那个学生是怎样捉弄警察和工人的。

Points a good summary covers好摘要要覆盖的要点
  • Seeing two workmen drilling the road outside his college, a student told the police that they were students in disguise.一个学生看见两个工人在学校外用风钻挖路，便报警说那是两个扮成工人的学生。
  • He then warned the workmen that a student dressed as a policeman was playing tricks, so they should ignore him.他又告诉工人，有个学生扮成警察在捣乱，警察来了别理他。
  • A policeman came and told the men to leave; they refused, more police came, and a struggle for the drill began.警察来叫工人离开，遭到拒绝，叫来帮手要抢风钻，双方扭打起来。
  • One workman asked to call the police; when an officer heard him really doing so, he understood they had all been tricked.一个工人要打电话报警，警察陪他去，发现他真的在给警察打电话，才明白大家都上当了。
Our model summary本站范文

A student saw two workmen drilling the road outside his college. He phoned the police to report two students disguised as workmen, then warned the workmen that a student dressed as a policeman was playing tricks on people. When a policeman asked the men to leave, they were rude, and more police arrived to take the drill. One workman asked to phone the police. Only when an officer heard him do so did the police see they had been fooled.（80 词）
