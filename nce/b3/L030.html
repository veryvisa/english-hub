Lesson 30 · The Death of a Ghost 幽灵之死

📘 New Concept English · Book 3 · Lesson 30 “The Death of a Ghost”新概念英语 第三册 Lesson 30《The Death of a Ghost》

B2🧠 25 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
For years the village was sure that Endley farm had a ghost. Hired men never stayed long, and everyone who left told the same tale: in the night somebody finished your work for you, and one man who sat up till morning said he had seen a shape cutting corn by moonlight. In the end it was taken as settled that the Cox brothers kept a hard-working ghost. Only when the ghost died, and the whole village went to the funeral, did it come out that he was Eric, the brothers' eldest. Made to join the army in the Second World War, he ran home when he heard he was to be sent overseas, his father kept him out of sight until the fighting stopped, and told everyone he had been killed. Afraid of what the authorities would do, Eric stayed out of sight after the war as well. He slept by day and worked by night for more than fifty years, never knowing what the village had made of him. The lesson is a narrative that withholds its explanation: the first half gives only what the villagers saw, and the second half tells you what was really there. Learn the past perfect passive, claim to have done, none other than, be supposed to have done, be obliged to do, think it + noun + to do, unaware of the fact that, and used to do.
村里人多年来都相信恩德利农场闹鬼：雇工干不长，走的人都说同一件事——夜里活儿总有人替你干完，有人还说月光下看见一个身影在割麦子。久而久之，大家认定考克斯兄弟雇了个「尽职的鬼」。直到「鬼」死了、全村去参加葬礼，才知道它是兄弟俩的大哥埃里克：二战时被迫参军，听说要被派往海外就开了小差，父亲把他藏到战争结束；战后他仍怕当局追究，继续躲着，父亲对外说他阵亡了。他白天睡、夜里干活，五十多年不知道自己成了「恩德利的鬼」。本课是先设悬念、后揭谜底的叙事：前半段只写村民看到的现象，后半段才交代真相。学过去完成时被动、claim to have done、none other than、be supposed to have done、be obliged to do、think it + 名词 + to do、unaware of the fact that 和 used to do。

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Book Three texts were written for the course by L. G. Alexander, so Endley farm and the Cox brothers are his invention and the story has no outside source. The situation behind it, though, was real and is documented in parliamentary records, and those records also show that the plot rests on a mistake — an interesting one.

第三册课文由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为教材自撰，恩德利农场与考克斯兄弟是他编的，故事没有外部出处。但它背后的处境是真的，而且有议会记录可查；这些记录还显示，这个情节立在一个错误上——而且是个有意思的错误。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

Men who left the forces during the Second World War and never went back were a known problem for years afterwards. In nineteen fifty the Minister of Defence told the House of Commons that the nominal total still outstanding was nineteen thousand four hundred and seventy-seven: one thousand two hundred and sixty-seven from the Navy, thirteen thousand eight hundred and forty-four from the Army and four thousand three hundred and sixty-six from the air force, of whom about five thousand had gone after the end of nineteen forty-five. Then, in nineteen fifty-three, the government announced an amnesty, tied to the coronation, for those who had deserted during the war of nineteen thirty-nine to forty-five; a later answer, given in nineteen seventy-five, confirmed that this was the last such amnesty. Hiding at home was the usual way of staying out of reach, since a man without papers could not easily take work or draw rations. For the extreme form of the same story, there is the Japanese officer on an island in the Philippines who stayed at his post until his old commander came out to relieve him formally in March nineteen seventy-four, nearly thirty years after the surrender.

二战期间离队不归的人，在战后多年都是一个公开的难题。1950 年，国防大臣在下院答复说，仍未归案的名义总数是 19,477 人：海军 1,267、陆军 13,844、空军 4,366，其中约五千人是在 1945 年底之后离队的。1953 年，政府宣布一次与加冕礼相连的特赦，适用于 1939–45 年战争期间开小差的人；1975 年的一份答复确认，那是最后一次此类特赦。躲在家里是最常见的避开办法，因为没有证件的人很难找工作、也领不到配给。同一个故事的极端形态，是菲律宾一座岛上的那位日本军官：他坚守岗位，直到老上级亲赴岛上正式解除他的任务，那是 1974 年 3 月，距投降已近三十年。

✨ How it is written赏析

The lesson is built on information, not on events, and the shape is worth studying. For sixteen sentences the writer tells you only what the village could see, and he is careful about how he reports it: the labourers said that, a man claimed to have seen, and then, as the years passed, the village took it as an accepted fact. Each of those phrases quietly refuses to vouch for the story. Meanwhile the passive voice does the real concealing — the work had been done, the hay had been cut — because a passive sentence can leave out the one thing the reader wants, which is the name of the doer. The joke at the centre is a single adjective: a conscientious ghost. The word belongs to an employer's report on a farmhand, and putting it next to the word ghost tells you how comfortably the village had settled into the story. Then the turn comes in half a sentence, in a subordinate clause: the ghost was none other than Eric. Notice what is not in the piece. No fright, no atmosphere, no dark and stormy night. The ghost story is told in the flattest possible prose, which is why the ending reads as sad rather than spooky.

这一课是用「信息」搭的，不是用「事件」搭的，它的形状值得研究。整整十六句，作者只讲村里人能看见的东西，而且他对「怎么转述」很讲究：雇工说、有人声称看见、久而久之成了公认的事实——每一个短语都在悄悄推开担保责任。同时，真正在遮掩的是被动语态：活儿已被干完、干草已被割——因为被动句可以恰好省掉读者最想要的那一样东西：干活者的名字。全篇中心的那个玩笑只是一个形容词：「尽职的」鬼。这个词本该出现在雇主对农工的评语里，把它和「鬼」摆在一起，就告诉了你村里人把这个故事住得多安稳。然后转折在半句话里到来，而且在一个从句里：那鬼不是别人，正是埃里克。请注意文中没有什么：没有惊骇，没有气氛，没有风雨交加的夜晚。这个鬼故事是用最平的文字讲的——所以结尾读起来是难过，不是阴森。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

Put the lesson next to the record and the plot falls apart in a way that makes it better. Eric hides during the war, which is understandable; he goes on hiding afterwards because he fears the authorities, which is also understandable; but in nineteen fifty-three an amnesty was announced for exactly his case, and it was reported in every newspaper in the country. A man on a farm with two brothers and a village around him would have heard of it. So the long middle of his life was not spent escaping punishment: it was spent inside a habit. That is the real subject of the story, and the lesson very nearly says so. Once the father had told the neighbours that his son was dead, the lie had to be defended, and after the father died the brothers inherited the defending, and kept it even from the women they married. A secret of that age is no longer about the thing concealed but about everyone who has helped to conceal it, which is why it could only end at a funeral. For readers in Chinese-speaking families the pattern will be recognisable without any war in it: the relative who is never mentioned, the marriage or the debt or the illness that the older generation agreed to leave unsaid, and the discovery, usually late, that the whole arrangement outlived its own reason by decades. The practical lesson is small and useful. A secret has a maintenance cost, and the cost is paid by the people who keep it, long after the danger has gone.

把课文和史料并排，这个情节会垮，而垮的方式让它变得更好。战时躲起来，可以理解；战后继续躲，因为怕当局，也可以理解；但 1953 年宣布的那次特赦，恰恰适用于他这种情形，而且全国的报纸都登了。一个住在农场、有两个弟弟、周围有一个村子的人，不可能没听说。所以他生命中漫长的中段，并不是在躲惩罚：是在一个习惯里过。这才是这个故事真正的主题，而这一课几乎说了出来。父亲一旦对邻居说了儿子阵亡，这句谎话就必须被守住；父亲死后，弟弟们继承了「守」这件事，连妻子都没告诉。一个这么老的秘密，已经不再关于被藏的那件事，而是关于所有帮着藏它的人——所以它只能在一场葬礼上结束。对华语家庭的读者，这个模式不需要战争也认得出来：那个从不被提起的亲戚；上一辈商定不说的某桩婚事、某笔债、某场病；以及通常很晚才有的发现——整套安排比它自己的理由多活了几十年。能带走的一课很小，也很有用：秘密有维护成本，而付这笔成本的是守它的人，在危险早已过去之后很久。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 1第 1 句 (begins开头 “For years, villagers …”)

  • that introduces an object clause, and haunted is an adjective used as the complement. For years at the front says how long the belief lasted and sets up the reversal to come.that 引导宾语从句；haunted 作表语形容词。For years 置于句首，交代「信了很多年」，为后文反转埋伏笔。

Sentence 5第 5 句 (begins开头 “Farm labourers said …”)

  • woke up to find ...: an infinitive expressing an unexpected result — they woke and then discovered — not a purpose.woke up to find…：不定式表出乎意料的结果（醒来却发现），不是目的。

Sentence 7第 7 句 (begins开头 “A farm worker, …”)

  • A who-clause set off by commas is non-defining; it adds information about the man, namely that he had stayed awake all night.主语后用逗号隔开的 who 从句：非限定定语从句，补充说明这个人（他熬了一整夜）。
  • claim to have done: say that one did it; the perfect infinitive puts the seeing earlier than the claiming.claim to have done：声称（之前）做过；完成式不定式表示「看见」发生在「声称」之前。
  • see somebody doing: see them in the middle of the action, which is what cutting shows.see sb doing：看见某人正在做（cutting 表进行中）。

Sentence 8第 8 句 (begins开头 “In time, it …”)

  • it is an empty subject and the real subject is the first that-clause.it 是形式主语，真正的主语是第一个 that 从句（同位语性质的主语从句）。
  • The second that is a relative pronoun introducing a clause that describes the ghost. The two uses of that are different, and it helps to read them apart.第二个 that 是关系代词，引导定语从句修饰 ghost。两个 that 功能不同，读的时候要分开。
  • In time: as the years went by, which is not the same as on time, meaning punctually.In time：久而久之（≠ on time 准时）。

Sentence 9第 9 句 (begins开头 “No one suspected …”)

  • suspect that: think it may well be so, leaning towards belief. might marks a guess.suspect that：猜想可能是（倾向于相信）；might 表推测。
  • The relative clause describes someone else and is in the past perfect passive.who had never been seen：定语从句修饰 someone else，过去完成时被动。

Sentence 12第 12 句 (begins开头 “Everyone went to …”)

  • Here for is a co-ordinating conjunction meaning because; it is bookish, and a comma comes before it.for 作并列连词＝because，书面语，前面用逗号。
  • none other than: it was that very person, said with surprise.none other than：正是（带惊讶）。
  • a third brother stands in apposition to the name. be supposed to have done: people believed he had, when in fact he had not.a third brother：Eric Cox 的同位语。be supposed to have done：（人们）以为他已经……，实际并没有。

Sentence 24第 24 句 (begins开头 “When their father …”)

  • think it + noun or adjective + to do: it is an empty object, and the real object is the infinitive phrase that follows.think it + 名词/形容词 + to do：it 是形式宾语，真正的宾语是 to keep Eric in hiding。

Sentence 27第 27 句 (begins开头 “quite unaware of …”)

  • used to do: did so regularly in the past.used to do：过去常常。
  • quite unaware of ...: an adjective phrase used as an adverbial, equal to and he was quite unaware; its subject is he.quite unaware of…：形容词短语作伴随状语（＝and he was quite unaware…），逻辑主语是 he。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

The past perfect passive: had been done过去完成时被动：had been done
had been plus a past participle means that something had already been done to the thing before a given moment in the past. Narrative uses it to say that the deed was over before anyone arrived.
had been + 过去分词＝在过去某个时间点之前「已经被做完」。叙事里用来交代「发现时事情早已发生」。

In this lesson, sentences seven and eight: by the time the men woke, the work had been done, the hay had been cut and the sheds had been cleaned. The writer never says who did it, and the passive is what lets him keep the worker out of sight, which is where the suspense comes from.

本课：第 5–6 句雇工醒来时，活儿 had been done、干草 had been cut、牛棚 had been cleaned——作者不说谁干的，被动语态正好把「干活的人」藏起来，制造悬念。

There are three usual reasons for leaving the doer out of a passive sentence: nobody knows who it was, it does not matter, or the writer is holding it back. This lesson is the third kind.

被动省掉施动者，常见的三个理由：不知道是谁、不重要、故意不说。本课是第三种。

  • When we got back, the window had been broken and the TV was gone.
  • By the time the fire engines arrived, the whole building had been evacuated.
  • The report had been finished long before the deadline.
The perfect infinitive: claim / be supposed / seem to have done完成式不定式：claim / be supposed / seem to have done
to have plus a past participle shows that the action of the infinitive happened before the main verb. It goes with claim, seem, appear, be said, be supposed and be believed.
to have + 过去分词，表示不定式的动作发生在谓语动词之前。常跟 claim、seem、appear、be said、be supposed、be believed 连用。

In this lesson, sentence nine: the worker claimed to have seen something in the moonlight — he saw it first and reported it afterwards; sentence fifteen: Eric was supposed to have died young, which is what people believed.

本课：第 7 句雇工 claimed to have seen 一个人影（先看见，后声称）；第 13 句埃里克 was supposed to have died 年轻时（人们以为他早死了）。

be supposed to has two senses: you ought to, as in You are supposed to wear a seat belt, and people say or believe, as in He is supposed to be very rich. This lesson uses the second, and the perfect infinitive puts the believing after the event.

be supposed to 有两个意思：「本应该」（You are supposed to wear a seat belt.）和「据认为」（He is supposed to be very rich.）；本课是第二个，加上完成式表示「据认为早已……」。

  • He claims to have met the Queen twice.
  • The castle is said to have been built in the 12th century.
  • She seems to have lost her keys again.
think it + noun or adjective + to do; used to dothink it + 名词/形容词 + to do；used to do
think / find / consider / feel + it + a noun or adjective + to do uses it as an empty object so that the long infinitive can go to the end. used to do describes a past habit or state that no longer holds.
think / find / consider / feel + it + 名词或形容词 + to do：it 作形式宾语，把长的不定式移到句末。used to do 表示过去的习惯或状态，现在已不再如此。

Keep three patterns apart: used to do, a past habit; be used to doing, to be accustomed to it; and be used to do, the passive of use, meaning to be employed for that purpose.

注意区分：used to do（过去常常）／be used to doing（习惯于）／be used to do（被用来做）。

  • I think it a pity to waste such good food.
  • We found it impossible to finish in time.
  • My father used to walk to work, but now he drives.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • For years, people believed that … 多年来人们一直以为……
    For years, people believed that the old well brought good luck.
  • … woke up to find that … had been … ……醒来却发现……已经被……
    I woke up to find that my car had been towed away.
  • … claimed to have seen … ……声称看见过……
    My neighbour claimed to have seen a bear in his garden.
  • … was none other than … ……正是……
    The mystery donor was none other than the school's first headmaster.
  • … thought it their duty to … ……认为……是自己的责任
    The villagers thought it their duty to look after the old church.

Points a good summary covers好摘要要覆盖的要点

Our model summary本站范文

The "ghost" was Eric, the eldest Cox son. Forced to join the army in the Second World War, he ran away home when he heard he would be sent overseas, and his father hid him and announced he had been killed. Afraid of being punished, Eric stayed hidden after the war, and his younger brothers kept the secret after their father's death. Working at night, he became the farm's ghost until he died.（73 词）
