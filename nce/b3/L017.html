📘 New Concept English · Book 3 · Lesson 17 “The Longest Suspension Bridge in the World”新概念英语 第三册 Lesson 17《The Longest Suspension Bridge in the World》

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
In 1524 an Italian of whom almost nothing is known, Verrazano, sailed into New York Harbour. He does not rank as a great explorer, yet his name may well outlive everybody else's: when the world's longest suspended span was finished in 1964, it was named after him. The bridge joins Brooklyn to Staten Island, with a main span of 4,260 feet — long enough that the designer had to allow for the curve of the earth. Its two towers stand on steel and concrete platforms sunk more than a hundred feet below the sea, and each of its four cables is made of 26,108 lengths of wire. The lesson is expository writing that moves from a person to a thing: first where the name came from, then the figures, and at the end a judgement about beauty. Learn relative clauses with a preposition before whom or which, by no means, be named after, take ... into account, It has been estimated that, and the unreal conditional.
1524 年，一个生平几乎无人知晓的意大利人韦拉扎诺驶进纽约港；他算不上伟大的探险家，名字却可能永垂不朽——1964 年建成的世界最长吊桥以他命名。韦拉扎诺大桥连接布鲁克林和斯塔滕岛，主跨 4260 英尺，长到设计者不得不考虑地球的曲率；两座塔立在海底 100 多英尺的钢筋混凝土平台上，四根主缆每根有 26108 股钢丝。本课是由人到物的说明文：先讲名字的来历，再列数据，最后落到「既简洁又优雅」的审美。学「介词 + whom」定语从句、by no means、be named after、take … into account、It has been estimated that 和虚拟语气。

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander. Everything factual in it is checkable, and we checked: the opening date, the span, the designer, the record it held, and the one thing the lesson could not have known, which is how long the record would last. The phrase quoted at the end of the text, about drawing a vast thing as faintly as one can, is Othmar Ammann's own description of what he was after.

课文由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为教材自撰。其中的事实全都可核，我们也核了：通车日期、跨度、设计者、它保持的纪录，以及课文不可能知道的那一件——这项纪录能保持多久。关于「一个尽可能画得轻淡的庞然大物」那句引语，出自设计者奥特马·阿曼自己对目标的描述。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

The bridge opened on the twenty-first of November, nineteen sixty-four, and its main span of four thousand two hundred and sixty feet made it the longest in the world. It came out of the office of Othmar Ammann, the Swiss-born engineer who had already given New York the George Washington Bridge, working with Leopold Just at the firm of Ammann and Whitney; Ammann was in his eighties when it was finished, and it was his last great work. The detail about the curve of the earth is not a flourish. Over a span that wide, two towers built truly vertical are not parallel, because each points at the centre of the planet, so their tops stand measurably further apart than their bases, and a designer who ignores that will not have a fit. The man the bridge honours deserves a word too. Giovanni da Verrazzano sailed for the French crown in fifteen twenty-four, entered the harbour, and named it Angouleme after his royal patron; so the first European name for New York was French, given by an Italian. The lesson's remark that little is known about him is fair for a sailor of that period, though not quite as fair as it sounds: his own letter describing the voyage survives.

大桥一九六四年十一月二十一日通车，四千二百六十英尺的主跨使它成为世界最长。它出自奥特马·阿曼的事务所——这位瑞士出生的工程师此前已为纽约造了乔治·华盛顿大桥——与阿曼—惠特尼公司的利奥波德·尤斯特合作；桥建成时阿曼已八十多岁，这是他最后一件大作。关于地球曲率那个细节不是修辞。在这么宽的跨度上，两座真正垂直的塔并不平行，因为每一座都指向地心，于是塔顶之间的距离比塔基之间可测地更远；设计时无视这一点，构件就装不上。桥所纪念的那个人也值得一提。乔瓦尼·达·韦拉扎诺一五二四年为法国王室出航，驶进这座港湾，并以他的王室赞助人之名把它叫作「昂古莱姆」；所以纽约最早的欧洲名字是法文的，由一个意大利人起的。课文说「关于他所知甚少」，对那个年代的航海者来说算公道，但不像听起来那么公道：他自己描述这次航行的信件留存至今。

✨ How it is written赏析

The lesson is a model of how to write about a large object without writing a list. The opening move is the clever one: it begins with a man who did almost nothing, in order to arrive at a thing that is enormous, and that gap is the whole argument of the first paragraph — a name can outlive the deeds it was earned by. Then come the numbers, and they are arranged by a rule worth copying. Each figure is attached to a consequence rather than left standing on its own: the span is so long that the earth's shape mattered; the platforms took sixteen months to build; if every lane were packed, two thirds of the capacity would still be spare. A number with a consequence is readable; a number alone is furniture. Finally the writer refuses the obvious ending. Having spent a paragraph on size, he says plainly that size is not the point, and closes on a quoted phrase about drawing faintly — an aesthetic claim, in the engineer's own words, which no amount of measurement could have produced. If you write about a bridge, a building or a machine, take the shape: one human hook, figures that each earn their place, and a last line that changes what the figures were for.

本课是「写大物件而不写清单」的样板。开场那一手很聪明：它从一个几乎什么都没做成的人写起，为了走到一件庞然大物跟前，而这道落差就是第一段的全部论点——一个名字可以活得比换来它的事迹更久。接着是数字，而它们的排布方式值得抄。每个数字都挂着一个后果，而不是孤零零立着：跨度之长，让地球的形状成了要紧事；平台单是建造就用了十六个月；即使每条车道都挤满，总承载力还空着三分之二。带后果的数字可读；孤立的数字只是家具。最后，作者拒绝了那个顺理成章的结尾。用了一整段写尺寸之后，他明说尺寸不是要点，并以一句关于「画得轻淡」的引语收束——一个审美判断，用的是工程师自己的话，而再多的测量也产生不出它。你若要写一座桥、一栋楼、一台机器，就把这个形状拿走：一个人性的钩子，每个都挣得了位置的数字，和一句改变前面数字用途的尾句。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The engineering has held; the superlative has not. The bridge lost the world record to the Humber Bridge in England in nineteen eighty-one, and after four decades of longer spans in Japan, China, Denmark and Turkey it now sits around eleventh in the world, while remaining the longest suspension span in the Americas. So the title of the lesson is simply no longer true, and that is the most useful thing in it: a text that says 'the longest in the world' is dated the moment it is printed, which is why careful writing attaches a year to any superlative. Two footnotes have arrived since. The name was misspelled from the start, with one z instead of two, because of an error in a construction contract, and New York corrected it by law in October twenty eighteen, with the signs replaced gradually; so even the name in the lesson's title is now out of date. And the social cost that the lesson does not mention has become the main thing historians discuss about the project: the approaches cut through a settled Brooklyn neighbourhood and thousands of people were moved out to make room. For Chinese readers the comparison is immediate. When this lesson was written, the longest spans were American; today several of the longest are in China, and the question the lesson quietly raises — whether a very large structure can also be beautiful — is now argued about in Chinese cities rather than in New York.

工程站住了，最高级没站住。这座桥在一九八一年把世界纪录输给了英国的亨伯大桥，此后四十年里日本、中国、丹麦、土耳其相继出现更长的跨度，它如今大约排在世界第十一位，同时仍是美洲最长的悬索桥跨。所以本课的标题已经不再成立，而这恰是课文里最有用的东西：写着「世界最长」的文本，一印出来就过期了，所以讲究的写法会给任何最高级配上年份。此后又添了两个脚注。这个名字从一开始就拼错了，只有一个 z 而不是两个，原因是施工合同上的笔误；纽约州在二〇一八年十月以立法改正，路牌逐步更换——于是连课文标题里的名字也已过时。还有课文没提的社会代价，如今成了史家讨论这项工程时的主题：引桥穿过布鲁克林一片成熟的社区，为腾地方迁走了数千人。对中国读者，对照是即时的：这一课写成时，最长的跨度在美国；今天几座最长的在中国，而课文悄悄提出的那个问题——一个极大的结构能不能同时是美的——现在争论的地点在中国的城市，不在纽约。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

Relative clauses with a preposition before whom or which介词 + whom / which 引导定语从句
When the relative pronoun is the object of a preposition, the preposition can go in front of it, which is the more formal order: about whom, from which, in which. In that order only whom or which will do — never that, never who. If the preposition stays at the end of the clause, the relative pronoun can be left out.
关系代词作介词宾语时，介词可以提到关系词前面（较正式）：about whom、from which、in which。此时只能用 whom/which，不能用 that 或 who；介词放句末时可以省略关系词。
  • The man to whom I spoke was very helpful. ＝ The man (who) I spoke to was very helpful.
  • This is the house in which I grew up.
Compound passive forms被动的几种复合形式
modal + be done (had to be taken); present perfect passive have/has been done (has been suspended); It + be + past participle + that (It has been estimated that ...); and set phrases such as be considered to be and be named after.
情态 + be done（had to be taken）；完成时被动 have/has been done（has been suspended）；It + be + 过去分词 + that（It has been estimated that…）；be considered to be / be named after 等。
  • The work must be finished by Friday.
  • It is said that the castle is haunted.
  • She is considered to be the best teacher in the school.
by no means / not at all / by all means
by no means = not in the least, used in writing to strengthen a negative; put it at the front of a sentence and the main clause inverts: By no means is it easy. by all means = certainly, go ahead, which is agreement, not negation.
by no means ＝一点也不（书面，加强否定）；放句首时主句要部分倒装：By no means is it easy。by all means ＝当然可以（表示同意）。

Summary writing摘要写作

In no more than 80 words, introduce the Verrazano Bridge: where its name comes from, how it is built, and what is special about it.

用不超过 80 个词介绍韦拉扎诺大桥：名字的来历、结构与特点。

Points a good summary covers好摘要要覆盖的要点
  • Verrazano reached New York Harbour in 1524; he is not rated a great explorer, but the world's longest suspended span, opened in 1964, carries his name.韦拉扎诺 1524 年驶入纽约港，他算不上伟大的探险家，但 1964 年建成的世界最长吊桥以他命名。
  • It joins Brooklyn to Staten Island with a span of 4,260 feet, long enough that the curve of the planet had to be allowed for.大桥连接布鲁克林和斯塔滕岛，跨度 4260 英尺，长到设计时要考虑地球形状。
  • Two towers nearly 700 feet high, standing on steel and concrete platforms driven deep below the sea, carry the four great cables.两座塔建在深入海底的钢筋混凝土平台上，高近 700 英尺，支撑四根巨大的缆索。
  • For all its size it is simple and elegant, and it realised what its designer had hoped for.它规模巨大，却简洁优雅，实现了设计者的梦想。
Our model summary本站范文

In 1524 Verrazano, an Italian sailor we know little about, entered New York Harbour. He was not a great explorer, yet in 1964 the world's longest suspension bridge was named after him. Joining Brooklyn and Staten Island, it is so long that its designer had to allow for the curve of the earth. Two towers standing on huge platforms deep under the sea hold up four giant cables. Though enormous, the bridge looks simple and graceful.（76 词）
