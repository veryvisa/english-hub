Lesson 60 · Too Early and Too Late 太早和太晚

📘 New Concept English · Book 3 · Lesson 60 “Too Early and Too Late”新概念英语 第三册 Lesson 60《Too Early and Too Late》

B2🧠 26 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
In civilised society, punctuality is a habit that all public life depends on; without it nothing could be finished and everything would fall into chaos. Only in thinly peopled country areas can it be ignored. In private life there is some room for lateness: a scholar deep in a difficult problem can be forgiven for arriving late to dinner. People are often blamed for lateness when their only fault is leaving too little time. Energetic, quick-thinking people hate wasting time, so they like to get one more task done before leaving; if nothing goes wrong on the way, such as a flat tyre, a diversion or sudden fog, they arrive on time, and they are often more hard-working and useful than people who always arrive on the dot. But people who are too punctual can be just as annoying: the guest who turns up half an hour early is the worst. Some friends of the writer's family did this, so they were simply invited half an hour later than everyone else, and then they arrived at exactly the right time. With trains, arriving comfortably early beats arriving a moment late. Waiting a little is better than missing the train and waiting an hour, and it saves the misery of arriving just as it pulls out. Worse still is reaching the platform early and watching the train go anyway. That is what happened to one girl travelling by herself for the first time. Told by her parents that missing the train would be unforgivable and would send her hosts to the station twice, she came twenty minutes ahead. The porter looked at her ticket and said she was two hours early. She handed him the note her father had written; yes, he said, a train was due then, but it halted only to load the post. Unwilling to believe her father had slipped, she asked for the printed times, and the station master arrived with the book and, with some ceremony, showed her a tiny letter o by the time: post only. At that moment the train pulled in. Crying, she pleaded to travel with the guard, but rules were rules, and she watched it carry on towards her destination without her. This is an argument with an illustrating story: it first discusses why punctuality matters and where the limits lie, then tells a tale in which being early is no help. The grammar to learn is bring to a conclusion, inversion after Only in ..., be forgiven if late, cut things fine, be tempted to, as much a trial as, it is better to ... than, less than if, the very moment, befall, impress upon sb that, To one's horror, feel in, with a flourish, the absolute phrase tears streaming, and be adamant.
在文明社会里，守时是一切公共事务的必要习惯；没有它什么事也办不成，一切都会乱套；只有在人烟稀少的乡村才能不管它。日常生活中对不守时可以宽容一些：埋头研究深奥问题的学者，晚到晚宴是可以原谅的。人们常被指责不守时，其实他们唯一的毛病是把时间掐得太紧：精力旺盛、脑子快的人舍不得浪费时间，总想把手头的活干完再去赴约，路上没碰上爆胎、改道、突然起雾，就能准时到；他们往往比从不迟到的人更勤奋、更有用。过分守时的人和不守时的人一样烦人：提前半小时到的客人最讨厌。作者家有几个朋友就这样，只好请他们比别的客人晚半小时来，结果刚好。赶火车宁可舒舒服服早到，也不要晚哪怕一点点：早到浪费一点时间，总比误车再等一个多小时少，也免得眼睁睁看着车开走。更难受的是早早到了站台，还是看着车开走。一个小姑娘第一次独自出门就碰上了：父母叮嘱她误车不可原谅，会让要去投宿的朋友白跑两趟，所以她提前二十分钟进站。把行李交给搬运工、出示车票，搬运工却说她早到了两个小时。她拿出父亲写的行程纸条，搬运工承认那个时刻确实有趟车进站停车，但只装邮件、不载客。她不信父亲会弄错，要看时刻表；站长拿来时刻表，得意地指着到站时间旁一个极小的「o」——表示只停靠装邮件。就在这时火车进站了，小姑娘泪流满面，求着让她溜进守车，站长毫不通融：规定不能破。她只能看着火车朝她的目的地驶去。本课是议论＋例证：先论守时的必要与分寸，再用一个故事说明「早到」也可能误事。学 bring to a conclusion、Only in … is it possible（倒装）、be forgiven if late、cut things fine、be tempted to、as much a trial as、it is better to … than、less than if、the very moment、befall、impress upon sb that、To one's horror、feel in、with a flourish、tears streaming（独立主格）、be adamant。

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander as an argument followed by an anecdote; the girl, the station and the friends of the family are not traced to any real source. The tiny o in the timetable reflects a real feature of British railway timetables, where small symbols marked trains that stopped only for particular purposes.

课文为编者 L. G. 亚历山大自撰，先议论、后讲逸事；小姑娘、车站和那几位朋友都找不到真实出处。时刻表上那个极小的「o」反映了英国铁路时刻表的真实特点：用小符号标出只为特定用途停靠的车次。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

British railway timetables of the mid twentieth century were dense printed books, and their footnotes were notorious. Letters and symbols beside a time could mean that a train stopped only on request, only to set down passengers, only to pick up, or, as here, only for mail and parcels; many travellers learned the code the hard way. Punctuality itself is partly a product of the railway. Before trains, each English town kept its own local time by the sun; it was the railways that pushed Britain onto a single standard time in the eighteen-forties, and the law followed in 1880. Researchers who compare countries, such as the American psychologist Robert Levine, have found large differences in how strictly people treat time, from the clock-watching cultures of Switzerland and Japan to places where an agreed hour is only a rough guide.

二十世纪中叶的英国铁路时刻表是密密麻麻的印刷册，脚注出了名地难懂。时间旁的字母和符号可能表示列车只应乘客要求停、只下客、只上客，或者像课文里那样，只装邮件和包裹；很多旅客是吃了亏才学会这套代码的。守时本身在一定程度上就是铁路造出来的。有火车之前，英格兰每个城镇按太阳用自己的地方时；是铁路在十九世纪四十年代把英国推向统一的标准时间，法律在 1880 年跟上。比较各国的研究者，比如美国心理学家罗伯特·莱文，发现人们对待时间的严格程度差别很大：从瑞士、日本那样盯着钟表的文化，到约好的钟点只是个大概的地方。

✨ How it is written赏析

The essay surprises the reader twice. First, it defends late people, at least the busy and energetic ones, which is not what one expects from an essay that opens by calling punctuality necessary. Second, it attacks early people, and it does so with a neat family joke: the over-punctual friends are simply given a later time, and the problem disappears. By the time the story begins, the reader has been taught that both too early and too late have costs, so the girl's case lands as the hardest version of the argument: she did everything right and still lost. The details are chosen to make her helpless: her father's note, the porter's polite correction, the station master's flourish, the tiny letter. The final image is quiet and cruel, the train going towards the place she should be, while she stays.

文章两次出乎读者意料。第一，它替迟到的人辩护——至少是忙碌、精力旺盛的那种——而开头才说守时是必要的，这不是读者预期的。第二，它批评早到的人，并且用了一个家里的小笑话：给过分守时的朋友报一个晚一点的时间，问题就没了。等故事开始时，读者已经知道太早和太晚都有代价，所以小姑娘的遭遇成了这个论点最难受的版本：她什么都做对了，还是输了。细节都是为了让她无能为力而挑的：父亲的纸条、搬运工客气的纠正、站长得意的动作、那个小小的字母。最后的画面安静而残忍：火车朝她该去的地方开去，她留在原地。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The lesson's argument has worn well, though trains have changed. Today a girl in her situation would check a journey planner on her phone, and the tiny o would be a line of plain text. Yet the deeper problem, trusting a source without checking it, is more common than ever, because the information we rely on is now passed along in screenshots and forwarded messages. The station master's rigidity is the part modern readers argue about. Some see a man protecting safety and order; others see a lack of the common sense that rules are meant to serve. Chinese readers will know both sides from daily life: railway travel in China is now strictly timed and ticketed by identity card, while many social occasions still run on a looser clock, where arriving exactly on time can seem almost too eager.

课文的论点至今站得住，尽管火车已经变了。今天处在小姑娘位置上的人会用手机查行程规划，那个小「o」会变成一行明白的文字。但更深的问题——不核对就相信一个来源——比以往任何时候都更常见，因为我们依赖的信息如今靠截图和转发流传。站长的死板是现代读者争论的部分：有人看到的是一个维护安全与秩序的人，有人看到的是缺少常识——而规则本来是为常识服务的。中国读者在日常生活里两面都见过：中国的铁路出行现在时刻严格、凭身份证购票，而许多社交场合仍然用比较松的钟点，准点到达有时反倒显得太急切。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 2第 2 句 (begins开头 “Without it, nothing …”)

  • Without it with could and would is a hidden unreal condition: if there were no punctuality.Without it, … could/would：含蓄虚拟，相当于 If there were no punctuality。
  • bring sth to a conclusion: finish it.bring sth to a conclusion：使……结束、办成。

Sentence 3第 3 句 (begins开头 “Only in a …”)

  • When only with an adverbial comes first, the verb comes before the subject.Only + 状语放句首，主句倒装（is it）。

Sentence 6第 6 句 (begins开头 “He is therefore …”)

  • have plus object plus past participle shows the state of things he has arranged.have everything coordinated：have + 宾语 + 过去分词，「把一切都安排妥当」。
  • the matter in hand is the job he is doing; if late leaves out he is.the matter in hand：手头的事。if late＝if he is late，省略。

Sentence 7第 7 句 (begins开头 “But people are …”)

  • be reproached for: be blamed for.be reproached for：因……受责备。
  • cut things fine: leave very little spare time.cut it/things fine：把时间掐得很紧，留余地太少。

Sentence 9第 9 句 (begins开头 “so they are …”)

  • be tempted to do: feel a strong wish to do.be tempted to do：忍不住想……。
  • keep an appointment: go to it as arranged.keep an appointment：赴约。

Sentence 12第 12 句 (begins开头 “The over-punctual can …”)

  • the plus an adjective stands for a type of person.the + 形容词：表一类人。
  • as much a ... as compares two types; a trial is a person who annoys.as much a … as：与……一样是……；trial：讨厌、折磨人的人。

Sentence 17第 17 句 (begins开头 “If you are …”)

  • it is better to ... than compares two choices.it is better to … than …：与其……不如……。
  • a fraction of a minute: the smallest possible amount of time.a fraction of a minute：哪怕一分钟的零头，极言其少。

Sentence 20第 20 句 (begins开头 “and you avoid …”)

  • avoid takes a noun or -ing form; the very moment means exactly then.avoid + 名词 / 动名词；the very moment：恰恰在那一刻。
  • draw out of: of a train, to leave the station.draw out of：（火车）驶出。

Sentence 22第 22 句 (begins开头 “Such an experience …”)

  • befall: of a bad event, to happen to someone.befall：（坏事）发生在……身上，正式。
  • the first time works like a conjunction meaning when for the first time.the first time：连词用法，「第一次……时」。

Sentence 24第 24 句 (begins开头 “since her parents …”)

  • impress upon sb that: make someone understand firmly.impress upon sb that …：反复叮嘱某人……，使牢记。
  • A preposition comes before whom in the relative clause.with whom（she would stay）：介词 + whom 的定语从句。

Sentence 27第 27 句 (begins开头 “To her horror …”)

  • To one's horror: to one's great shock.To one's horror：令某人惊恐的是。
  • An amount of time before too soon says how early.时间量 + too soon：早了多少时间。

Sentence 30第 30 句 (begins开头 “The girl asked …”)

  • could not have done: it was impossible in the past.could not have done：对过去的否定推测，「不可能……」。

Sentence 32第 32 句 (begins开头 “who produced it …”)

  • produce means bring out; with a flourish means with a showy movement.produce：拿出；with a flourish：夸张地、得意地。

Sentence 35第 35 句 (begins开头 “The girl, tears …”)

  • A noun with an -ing form makes an absolute phrase describing how she looked.tears streaming down her face：独立主格（名词 + 现在分词），表伴随。
  • beg to be allowed to do: plead for permission.beg to be allowed to do：恳求获准……。

Sentence 36第 36 句 (begins开头 “But the station …”)

  • be adamant: refuse to change your mind.be adamant：坚决不让步。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

Inversion after Only with an adverbial at the frontOnly + 状语置于句首的倒装
When only with an adverbial phrase, adverb or clause is placed first, the main clause inverts: auxiliary, modal or be, then the subject, then the rest. When only goes with the subject, there is no inversion.
Only + 状语（介词短语、副词、从句）放句首时，主句部分倒装：助动词/情态动词/be + 主语 + 其余。Only 修饰主语时不倒装。

Sentence 3 begins with Only in and inverts the verb and subject.

本课：第 3 句以 Only in … 开头，后面写 is it possible。

  • Only then did I realise my mistake.
  • Only when the rain stopped could we go out.
  • Only Tom knew the answer. (no inversion: only goes with the subject)
it is better to ... than, would rather ... than
it is better to do A than to do B means A is the wiser choice. would rather do A than do B means you choose A. prefer doing A to doing B says the same with -ing forms.
it is better to do A than (to do) B＝做 A 比做 B 好；would rather do A than do B＝宁可 A 也不 B；prefer doing A to doing B。

Sentence 17 says being comfortably early beats being even slightly late.

本课：第 17 句说宁可宽裕地早到，也不要晚到一点点。

  • It is better to ask than to guess.
  • I would rather walk than wait for the bus.
Absolute phrases with -ing and past participles独立主格：名词 + 现在分词 / 过去分词
A noun with an -ing form means the noun is doing the action, as with tears streaming. A noun with a past participle means it has been done, as with the work done. They show accompanying details, time or reason.
名词 + 现在分词：名词主动做这个动作（tears streaming）；名词 + 过去分词：名词被动或已完成（the work done）。多表伴随、时间、原因。

Sentence 35 uses an -ing absolute to describe how the girl looked.

本课：第 35 句用 tears streaming down her face 描写小姑娘的样子。

  • He sat there, his hands shaking.
  • The exams over, the students went on holiday.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • Without X, nothing could ever be … 没有 X，什么事都……
    Without trust, nothing could ever be agreed.
  • Only in … is it possible to … 只有在……才可能……
    Only in a small town is it possible to know everyone.
  • X is often blamed for … when the only fault is … X 常因……受责备，其实唯一的毛病是……
    She is often blamed for rudeness when her only fault is shyness.
  • The over-careful can be as much a nuisance as the careless. 过分小心的人和粗心的人一样烦人
    The over-helpful can be as much a nuisance as the unhelpful.
  • It is always better to … than … ……总比……好
    It is always better to ask twice than to guess once.

Summary writing摘要写作

In no more than 80 words, tell why the girl arrived early and why she could not get on the train.

Points a good summary covers好摘要要覆盖的要点
  • On her first journey alone, the girl, warned by her parents not to miss the train, reached the station twenty minutes early.小姑娘第一次独自出门，父母叮嘱不能误车，她提前二十分钟到站。

  • The station master showed her a timetable with a tiny o beside the time, meaning mail only.站长拿出时刻表，指出时间旁一个极小的「o」，表示只装邮件。

Our model summary本站范文

A girl travelling alone for the first time reached the station twenty minutes early, as her parents had insisted. But the porter told her she was two hours early, because the train her father had chosen stopped only for mail. The station master proved it by pointing to a tiny o in the timetable. When the train arrived, she begged in tears to be let on, but he would not break the rules, and she watched it leave without her.（80 词）
