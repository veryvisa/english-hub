📘 New Concept English · Book 3 · Lesson 1 “A Puma at Large”新概念英语 第三册 Lesson 1《A Puma at Large》

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
The first lesson of Book 3: a puma is reported south of London. Zoo experts do not believe it at first, and only investigate as the evidence grows; the animal moves so unpredictably that weeks of searching fail to catch it. It is a news narrative that moves from rumour to evidence, search, inference and conclusion. Language focus: the passive (be spotted / be taken seriously / be found), must have been for guessing, and for meaning 'because' — structures that come back again and again in Book 3.
第三册开篇：伦敦以南出现一只美洲狮，动物园专家起初不信，证据越来越多才去调查；美洲狮行踪不定，几周都没抓到。这是一则新闻叙事，按「传闻—证据—搜寻—推断—结论」推进。语言重点：被动语态（be spotted / be taken seriously / be found）、must have been 推测、for 表原因，都是第三册反复出现的结构。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. Why did the zoo experts not take the reports seriously at first, and why did they later feel they had to investigate?动物园专家为什么一开始不当真，后来又觉得必须调查？
  2. Why was the search for the puma difficult?为什么搜寻美洲狮很困难？
  3. Where did the experts decide the puma had come from?专家推断这只美洲狮是从哪里来的？

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

Book 3 was published in 1967, at the height of one of Britain's strangest news stories. From 1964 onwards, people around Godalming in Surrey, about 45 miles from central London, reported a large cat in the woods and heaths. The police station kept a special book of sightings and logged several hundred reports in two years (sources disagree on the exact figure: 326 or 362). As in the lesson, one early witness was picking blackberries, and a cast of a paw print was put on show at the police station. No puma was ever caught. The "Surrey Puma" became the start of a long British tradition of "big cat" sightings that still appears in local papers today.

第三册出版于 1967 年，正赶上英国最离奇的新闻之一。从 1964 年起，伦敦以南约 45 英里、萨里郡戈德尔明一带的居民不断报告在树林和荒地里看见一只大猫。当地警察局专门设了登记簿，两年里记下好几百条目击报告（具体数字各说不一：326 或 362）。和课文一样，早期的一位目击者正在摘黑莓；一只爪印的石膏模还在警察局展出过。美洲狮始终没有抓到。「萨里美洲狮」从此开了英国「野外大猫」目击传统的头，今天地方报纸上还时有出现。

✨ How it is written赏析

The lesson is a small model of how a careful report handles uncertain events. Look at the verbs of evidence: reports came in, people claimed to have seen, experts confirmed, prints were seen, fur was found, people complained of noises. The writer never says "a puma lived there" until the evidence has piled up. The passive does real work: it keeps the focus on the evidence rather than on who found it. Then the story turns on one piece of reasoning — no zoo had lost a puma, so it must have been a private pet — and ends not with an answer but with an uneasy thought. When you report something you cannot fully prove, copy this: say who claimed what, list the evidence in order of strength, and mark your inference with must have or may have.

本课是「怎样报道一件不确定的事」的小范本。看那些表示证据的动词：报告传来、有人声称看见、专家证实、发现爪印、找到皮毛、有人抱怨夜里有叫声。证据没堆起来之前，作者从不直说「那里有一只美洲狮」。被动语态在这里真有用：把焦点放在证据上，而不是谁找到的。故事随后靠一步推理转折——没有哪家动物园丢了美洲狮，所以它一定是私人养的——最后不给答案，只留一个让人不安的念头。报道你无法完全证实的事时，可以照学：写清谁声称了什么，按证据强弱排列，推断处用 must have 或 may have 标出来。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The story's logic holds, but its conclusion is open. In the real Surrey case, many experts later concluded that most sightings were of dogs, foxes or large domestic cats, and that hundreds of reports can arise from one striking newspaper story — people see what they expect to see. So the lesson's claim that the experts no longer had any doubt goes further than the real evidence ever did. The idea of an escaped private animal is not fanciful, though. In 2021 three young leopards escaped from Hangzhou Safari Park, and the park kept quiet for about two weeks, fearing lost visitors over the May Day holiday; local people spotted the animals first. Then and now, the real question is the same: who is responsible for telling the public, and how quickly?

故事的推理成立，但结论是开放的。在真实的萨里事件里，不少专家后来认为，大多数目击其实是狗、狐狸或大个子家猫；一篇抢眼的报道就能引出几百条报告——人们看见的是自己预期的东西。所以课文那句「专家们现在完全确信」，比现实中证据所能达到的程度更强。不过「私人饲养的动物逃逸」并非空想：2021 年杭州野生动物世界有三只小豹逃出，园方怕影响「五一」客流，隐瞒了约两周，最先发现的是当地居民。当年和今天，真正的问题都一样：谁有责任告诉公众，要多快？

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 2第 2 句 (begins开头 “When reports came …”)

  • that introduces an appositive clause giving the content of the reports; it is separated from reports by came into London Zoo.that 引导同位语从句，说明 reports 的内容；被 came into London Zoo 隔开（见第四册第 11、18 课）。
  • had been spotted: past perfect passive (it had been seen before the reports arrived).had been spotted：过去完成时被动（在报告到达之前已被看见）。
  • they refers to the reports; passive of take something seriously.they 指 reports；take sth seriously 的被动。

Sentence 6第 6 句 (begins开头 “for the descriptions …”)

  • for gives a reason, added as an explanation.for 表原因，补充说明。

Sentence 9第 9 句 (begins开头 “and experts confirmed …”)

  • will here describes typical behaviour, a general truth, so the clause after confirmed does not change to would.will 表习性、规律（见第四册第 23 课），所以 confirmed 后从句不随主句变成 would。
  • unless = if … not。

Sentence 12第 12 句 (begins开头 “Wherever it went, …”)

  • wherever: to any place at all.wherever：无论到哪里。
  • left behind it a trail ...: the long object is moved to the end.left behind it a trail…：长宾语后移（见第四册第 13 课）。

Sentence 18第 18 句 (begins开头 “this one must …”)

  • as gives a reason.as 表原因。
  • must have been: a confident guess about the past; managed is joined to been and shares must have.must have been：对过去的肯定推测；managed 与 been 并列，共用 must have。
  • in the possession of sb: owned by someone (note it runs the opposite way from in possession of sth).in the possession of sb：被某人拥有（注意与 in possession of sth 方向相反）。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

Passive tenses: is done / was done / had been done被动语态的时态：is done / was done / had been done
Passive = be + past participle, and the tense is carried by be: is done (present), was done (past), had been done (past perfect), has been done (present perfect), will be done (future).
被动 = be + 过去分词，时态体现在 be 上：is done（现在）、was done（过去）、had been done（过去完成）、has been done（现在完成）、will be done（将来）。

Why the passive: the doer is unknown or does not matter (who saw it or found it is not important).

用被动的理由：动作执行者不重要或不知道（谁看见的、谁发现的不重要）。

  • The window was broken last night.
  • The thief had been caught before the police arrived.
must have done / may have done: guessing about the pastmust have done / may have done：对过去的推测
Guessing about past events: must have done (certainly), may/might have done (possibly), can't have done (impossible) (Book 4 Lesson 1 compares them in detail).
对过去发生的事作推测：must have done（一定）、may/might have done（可能）、can't have done（不可能）（第四册第 1 课有详细比较）。
  • The ground is wet. It must have rained.
  • He can't have gone far; his coat is still here.
Passive of see/find + object + doing: be seen/found doing感官动词 + 宾语 + doing 的被动：be seen/found doing
When see/find/hear sb doing becomes passive, the -ing form stays: sb is seen/found doing.
see/find/hear sb doing 变被动时 doing 保留：sb is seen/found doing（见第四册第 6 课）。

Compare: see sb do → sb is seen to do.

对比：see sb do → sb is seen to do。

  • He was seen leaving the building at midnight.
  • The children were found playing in the street.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • … will not … unless … 除非……否则不会……
    A dog will not bite unless it is frightened.
  • As no …, this one must have … 既然没有……，那这一个一定是……

Summary writing摘要写作

In no more than 80 words, tell the puma story from the first reports to the experts' conclusion. Keep to time order and use the passive and must have.

用不超过 80 个词讲述美洲狮事件：从报告到推断。按时间顺序写，注意用被动语态和 must have 推测。

Points a good summary covers好摘要要覆盖的要点
  • Reports of a puma south of London were not taken seriously at first; as evidence grew, zoo experts investigated.伦敦以南发现美洲狮的报告起初没人当真，证据增多后动物园专家去调查。
  • A woman picking blackberries saw it; experts confirmed that a puma does not attack people unless it is trapped.摘黑莓的妇女看见了它，专家确认美洲狮不被逼急不会伤人。
  • It moved unpredictably, leaving dead deer, paw prints and fur; people heard it at night and one man saw it in a tree.它行踪不定，留下死鹿、爪印、皮毛，有人听到叫声，有人看到它在树上。
  • No zoo had lost a puma, so it must have belonged to a private collector and escaped.没有动物园报告丢失美洲狮，所以它一定是私人收藏者的，逃了出来。
  • Weeks of searching failed, and a dangerous animal was still loose in the countryside.搜寻几周没有结果，危险动物仍在乡间。
Our model summary本站范文

Reports of a puma south of London were ignored at first, but as evidence grew, zoo experts investigated. A woman saw it while picking blackberries, and it was seen in places twenty miles apart, leaving dead deer, paw prints and fur behind. Since no zoo had lost a puma, it must have escaped from a private collector. After weeks of searching it was still free in the countryside.（68 词）
