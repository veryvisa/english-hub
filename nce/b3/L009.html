Lesson 9 · Flying Cats 飞猫

📘 New Concept English · Book 3 · Lesson 9 “Flying Cats”新概念英语 第三册 Lesson 9《Flying Cats》

B2🧠 20 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
Is the saying about nine lives pure superstition? A New York animal hospital looked at 132 cats brought in over five months after falling from tall buildings, and only eight of them died; one, Sabrina, came down 32 floors and lost a single tooth. The reason is that the longer the drop, the more time the animal has to relax and spread its legs like a flying squirrel, which slows it down. This is popular science writing: first what people make of cats, then the figures, then the explanation. The language to learn is the + comparative ... the + comparative, never fail to, have something in common, and there is no shortage of.
「猫有九条命」是不是迷信？纽约一家动物医学中心五个月里研究了 132 只从高楼摔下的猫，只有 8 只死了；一只叫萨布丽娜的猫从 32 层掉下来只断了一颗牙。原因在于：摔得越高，猫越有时间放松、像飞鼠一样伸开四肢，增加空气阻力。本课是科普说明文：先讲人们对猫的印象，再给研究数据，最后解释原理。学 the + 比较级…the + 比较级、never fail to、have … in common 和 there is no shortage of。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. Why do people find cats so interesting, and how are cats unlike dogs and horses?人们为什么对猫着迷？猫和狗、马有什么不同？
  2. What did the New York study find, and why was that city a good place to do it?纽约的研究发现了什么？为什么纽约特别适合做这项研究？
  3. Why does a longer drop leave a cat less hurt rather than more?为什么猫摔得越高反而越不容易受伤？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Unsigned and written for the course, but the study it reports is a real one and can be looked up: W. O. Whitney and C. J. Mehlhaff, writing in the Journal of the American Veterinary Medical Association in 1987.

本课未署名、为教材自撰，但它引的研究是真的、可以查到：惠特尼与梅尔哈夫，1987 年发表于《美国兽医协会杂志》。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

The paper describes 132 cats brought to the Animal Medical Center in New York over a five-month period after falling from a height, and reports that ninety per cent of the cats that were treated survived, though a great many were badly hurt. The hospital coined a name for the pattern in the early 1980s and still uses it, and still puts out warnings every warm season, because the falls happen when windows are opened. The physics the lesson appeals to is ordinary enough: a falling body stops picking up speed once the drag of the air matches its weight, and an animal that spreads its limbs widens its own area and so raises that drag. Figures given for the speed at which a cat levels off vary between sources, which is one reason to treat the exact numbers in the lesson as illustration rather than measurement.

那篇论文记录的是五个月里送到纽约动物医学中心的 132 只高处坠落的猫，报告说接受治疗的猫有九成活了下来，但其中很多伤得很重。这家医院在 1980 年代初给这一现象起了名字，至今还在用，也至今每到暖季就发警示，因为这类坠落都发生在开窗的时候。课文倚靠的物理很平常：下落物体一旦空气阻力与自身重量相抵，就不再加速；而一只把四肢摊开的动物增大了自身面积，于是阻力也增大。至于猫稳定下落的速度，各家给的数字并不一致——这也是把课文里那些具体数字当示意、不当测量的一个理由。

✨ How it is written赏析

Watch how carefully the writer hedges, and then how he stops hedging. The claim about nine lives is introduced as a popular belief, the truth in it is granted with an apparently and with a phrase meaning no more than 'a fair amount', the pattern in long falls is offered with an it seems that, and the quoted comparison with trained parachutists is placed in a doctor's mouth rather than asserted. Only the last two sentences are flat statements with no hedge at all, and they are the ones that describe a mechanism. That is exactly the right distribution: be cautious about what the numbers mean, be firm about what the physics does. Compare it with the lesson on the puma, which also handles a popular belief, and you can see the same house style — claim, source, hedge, then one unhedged explanation at the end.

留意作者多么小心地加限定，然后又在哪里不加了。关于九条命的说法被当作「流行的看法」引入；其中有多少真，用「看来」「有相当多的」来让步；长距离坠落的规律用「似乎」托着；把猫比作受训伞兵这句，被安排在一位医生口中，而不是直接断言。全课只有最后两句是不带任何限定的平直陈述，而它们恰恰讲的是机理。这个分配完全正确：对数字意味着什么要谨慎，对物理怎么起作用要果断。拿它和讲美洲狮那一课比一比，会看到同一套文风：给说法、给来源、加限定，最后给一句不加限定的解释。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The argument has one hole, and it is in the sample, not in the physics. Everything we know here comes from cats that someone carried into a hospital. A cat killed outright, or never found, or taken nowhere at all, never enters the figures. So ninety per cent is a survival rate among patients, not among falling cats, and the lesson quietly converts the first into the second. The same flaw undermines the striking line about longer falls hurting less: cats that fall very far and die on the pavement are the ones least likely to become patients, so a patient sample will always make long falls look safer than they are. The mechanism may still be real — spreading out does increase drag — but it cannot be proved from this kind of data. What would settle it is a study that finds the cats first and the hospital second, and that is very hard to do.

这套论证有一个漏洞，而且在样本上，不在物理上。我们在这里知道的一切，都来自被人抱进医院的猫。当场死亡的、根本没找到的、或者谁也没送去的猫，从来不进这个数。所以「九成」是患者中的存活率，不是坠落猫的存活率，而课文悄悄把前者换成了后者。同一个毛病也拆掉了「摔得越高伤得越轻」这句漂亮的话：摔得极高、落地即死的猫，恰恰最不可能成为患者，所以患者样本永远会让长距离坠落看起来比实际安全。机理也许仍然成立——摊开四肢确实增加阻力——但靠这类数据证明不了。真正能定案的，是一项先找到猫、再找到医院的研究，而这非常难做。

③ How the hard sentences are built难句结构

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 2第 2 句 (begins开头 “They can be …”)

  • lead a certain kind of life: to live that way; lead here has nothing to do with leading others.lead a … life：过……样的生活（lead 不是「领导」）。
  • of their own: belonging to them and nobody else; as well at the end means too.of their own：属于它们自己的；as well 句末 = too。

Sentence 6第 6 句 (begins开头 “One of the …”)

  • The subject is One, with of the things attached to it, so the verb is singular.主语是 One（of the things）…，谓语 is 用单数。
  • The relative clause describes the things. The textbook writes the verb as though One were its subject; strict grammar would make it agree with the plural noun instead.that fascinates us：定语从句修饰 the things。原文用 fascinates 是按 one 作主语来理解；更严格的语法是 fascinate（先行词 things 是复数）。
  • An appositive clause after belief spells out what the belief actually is.the belief that…：同位语从句，说明 belief 的内容。

Sentence 10第 10 句 (begins开头 “All these cats …”)

  • have something in common: share a feature. What follows the colon spells out the shared experience.have sth in common：有共同点。冒号后面解释那个共同经历。
  • The past perfect puts the fall earlier than the study.had fallen：过去完成时（研究之前已经摔下）。
  • yet means however; die from names the outside cause of death.yet：然而；die from 死于（外因）。

Sentence 12第 12 句 (begins开头 “Of course, New …”)

  • there is no shortage of: there is plenty of, a dry and slightly humorous way of putting it.there is no shortage of：不缺少（= plenty of），一种幽默的说法。
  • such + a or an + adjective + noun.such an interesting study：such + a/an + 形容词 + 名词。

Sentence 16第 16 句 (begins开头 “It seems that …”)

  • the + comparative, the + comparative: as one goes up, so does the other.the + 比较级…, the + 比较级…：越……就越……。
  • The usual order would put likely straight after the subject and verb; here it has been left to the end.正常语序应为 the less likely they are to…，原文把 likely 留在后面。

Sentence 20第 20 句 (begins开头 “This increases their …”)

  • This points back to the whole act of stretching out described in the sentence before.This 指代上一句「伸开四肢」整件事。
  • Two opposite verbs are set side by side: one quantity goes up and the other comes down.increase / reduce 一对反义动词并列。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

the + comparative, the + comparative: the more ..., the more ...the + 比较级…, the + 比较级…：越……就越……
Two comparatives are set out symmetrically: the first clause gives the condition, the second the result, and each comparative takes the in front of it.
两个比较级结构对称放置，前一句是条件，后一句是结果；比较级前都要加 the。

In sentence 16 the distance of the drop goes up while the chance of injury comes down.

本课：第 16 句：猫摔得 the further，受伤的可能 the less。

  • The more you practise, the better you get.
  • The older he grew, the quieter he became.
  • The sooner, the better.（越快越好，省略式）
never fail to do and fail to donever fail to do 与 fail to do
fail to do means not to manage it; never fail to do is a double negative and so comes out as always, without exception.
fail to do 表示「没能做到」；never fail to do 是双重否定，等于「总是、必定」。

The opening sentence uses it of cats and human beings: there is never a case where they do not hold our attention.

本课：开篇第 1 句说猫 never fail to fascinate 人类——总能让人着迷。

  • He never fails to call his mother on Sundays.
  • The train failed to arrive on time.
have something in common: to share a featurehave something in common：有共同之处
The pattern is have + much, a lot, little, nothing, something or one thing + in common, optionally with a with phrase; the quantity word goes between the verb and in common.
have + (much / a lot / little / nothing / something / one thing) + in common (with sb)。量词放在 have 和 in common 之间。

In sentence 10 every animal in the study shared the same one experience.

本课：第 10 句，这些猫 had one experience in common。

  • We have a lot in common — we both love jazz.
  • The two brothers have nothing in common.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • X never fail(s) to fascinate … X 总能让……着迷
    Old castles never fail to fascinate tourists.
  • One of the things that … most about X is … X 最让人……的一点是……
    One of the things that surprises me most about Canada is its size.
  • A …'s ability to … is based on fact. ……的……能力是有事实依据的
    A dog's ability to find its way home is based on fact.
  • The further …, the less … ……越远，就越不……
    The further you live from work, the less time you have at home.

Summary writing摘要写作

In no more than 80 words, say what the New York study found and why cats can fall a long way and live.

用不超过 80 个词说明纽约的研究发现了什么，以及猫为什么能从高处摔下而不死。

Points a good summary covers好摘要要覆盖的要点
  • People often say a cat has nine lives, and there is some real ground for the saying.人们常说猫有九条命，这种说法有一定的事实根据。
  • A New York animal centre looked at 132 cats that had come down from tall buildings, and only eight of them died; one fell 32 floors and lost only a tooth.纽约动物医学中心研究了 132 只从高楼摔下的猫，只有 8 只死亡；一只猫从 32 层摔下只断了一颗牙。
  • The longer the drop, the smaller the chance of serious injury.猫摔得越高，受伤的可能越小。
  • At speed the animal has time to let its body go loose and spread its legs, which raises the drag and softens the landing.高速下落时猫有时间放松，伸开四肢，增加空气阻力，减轻落地的冲击。
