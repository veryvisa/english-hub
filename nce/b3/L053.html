📘 New Concept English · Book 3 · Lesson 53 “In the Public Interest”新概念英语 第三册 Lesson 53《In the Public Interest》

B2🧠 24 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
The Nordic countries are admired everywhere for their humane social policies. Sweden built a system to protect ordinary citizens from officials who are high-handed or simply bad at their jobs, and it worked so well that other countries copied it. The Swedes were first to accept that civil servants, police, health inspectors and tax collectors can get things wrong, or go too far while believing they serve the public. In 1809 the Swedish parliament set up a scheme: a committee of all parties appoints a qualified person to look into citizens' complaints against the State. The formal title is Justitieombudsman, usually shortened to Ombudsman. He is free of political pressure and handles complaints of every size. Complaints must be in writing, about twelve hundred a year arrive, he has eight lawyers to help, every letter is studied, and the correspondence is open to anyone. If a complaint is sound, he acts for the citizen, from a gentle word to an official up to a proposal that parliament change a law. A typical case: a foreigner in a Swedish village said the police had treated him badly because he was foreign. The chief of police denied it and the records showed nothing. Only when a second foreigner from the same village complained did the Ombudsman send a lawyer, who found the policeman had indeed been rough with foreigners several times; prejudice is not something written in files. The man was sharply reprimanded and warned he would be prosecuted if it happened again. This is exposition with a case study: first the system, then one case to show why it matters. The grammar to learn is protect sb from, in the belief that, be subject to, refer to sb as, on one's behalf, suggest that with the bare verb, ascertain, and a The fact that clause as subject.
北欧国家开明的社会政策举世称道。瑞典发展出一套保护普通公民、防止官员专横或失职的制度，效果好到别国也照搬。瑞典人最早认识到：文官、警察、卫生检查员、税务员都会犯错，或者自以为在为公众服务而做过了头。早在 1809 年，瑞典议会就设立了这套办法：由各党组成的委员会任命一位合格人选，专门调查公民对国家的申诉——正式名称 Justiteombudsman，俗称「监察专员」。他不受政治压力，大小投诉都管；投诉必须书面提出，每年约一千二百封，他有八位律师助手，每封都细看，往来信件公开供人查阅。投诉属实，他会代表公民出面：轻则温和地训诫官员，重则建议议会修法。典型一例：一位住在瑞典乡村的外国人投诉警察因他是外国人而虐待他；警察局长否认，档案里也没有证据。直到同村另一位外国人也来投诉，专员派律师实地调查，查实那名警察确实多次粗暴对待外国人——偏见是写不进官方档案的。那名警察受到严厉训诫，并被告知再有投诉就起诉。本课是说明文＋典型案例：先讲制度，再用一个案子说明它为什么有用。学 protect sb from、in the belief that、be subject to、refer to sb as、on one's behalf、suggest that + 动词原形（虚拟）、ascertain、The fact that … 主语从句。

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course, drawing on a real institution. The Swedish Parliamentary Ombudsman, Justitieombudsmannen or JO, was created by the Instrument of Government of 1809 and is usually described as the first parliamentary ombudsman in the world. The village case reads as an illustration rather than a reported decision; we have not traced it.

课文为教材编写，取材于真实制度。瑞典议会监察专员（Justitieombudsmannen，简称 JO）由 1809 年的《政府组织法》设立，通常被认为是世界上第一个议会监察专员。课文里的乡村案子读来是示例而不是可查的裁决，我们没有找到原案。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

The lesson's spelling of the title, with no i after Justit, is a small slip; the Swedish word is Justitieombudsman. Today Sweden has not one but four parliamentary ombudsmen sharing the work, and the office receives several thousand complaints a year rather than twelve hundred, which says more about how much the state has grown than about any decline in officials. The idea spread slowly at first and then fast: Finland followed in 1919, Denmark in 1955, Norway and New Zealand in the early sixties, and the United Kingdom created its Parliamentary Commissioner for Administration in 1967. Most Canadian provinces have an ombudsperson, British Columbia since 1979, and there is a federal ombud for taxpayers and others for banking and veterans.

课文把头衔拼成 Justiteombudsman，少了一个 i，属小笔误；瑞典文是 Justitieombudsman。今天瑞典不是一位而是四位议会监察专员分担工作，每年收到的投诉是几千件而不是一千二百件——这说明的是国家机构变大了，不是官员变坏了。这个制度起初传播很慢，后来很快：芬兰 1919 年跟进，丹麦 1955 年，挪威和新西兰六十年代初，英国 1967 年设立议会行政专员。加拿大大多数省有监察专员，卑诗省自 1979 年起设立；联邦层面有纳税人监察员，银行和退伍军人事务也各有一位。

✨ How it is written赏析

The essay persuades by staying calm. It never tells a story about a cruel official until the system has been fully described, so that by the time the case arrives the reader already trusts the process. The case itself is built on a pause: one complaint and nothing can be done, because the record is clean and the chief says no. That pause is the most honest moment in the lesson, since it shows the Ombudsman obeying evidence rather than sympathy. Only the second complaint changes the weight, and the writer then places the key idea in the plainest possible sentence: some truths never reach the files. The ending does not celebrate the punishment; it praises speed, and reminds us of the harm that would have stayed hidden.

文章靠冷静说服人。在制度完整讲清之前，它不讲任何坏官员的故事，所以案子出场时读者已经信任了程序。案子本身靠一个停顿造起来：一次投诉，什么也做不了，因为档案干净、局长否认。这个停顿是全课最诚实的时刻，它说明专员服从的是证据，不是同情。只有第二次投诉才改变了分量，接着作者用最朴素的一句说出关键：有些真相永远进不了档案。结尾不庆祝惩罚，而是称赞行动迅速，并提醒我们本来会被掩盖的伤害。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The lesson's point about records is its most lasting one. Prejudice leaves few written traces, and a complaints system that only reads files will rarely find it; it needs people who can go and look, and a rule that a pattern of complaints counts even when each one alone proves nothing. Modern ombudsman offices work in much the same way, looking for repeated complaints that reveal a system problem. For Chinese readers in Canada the practical lesson is simple: if a public body treats you unfairly, there is usually a free, independent office whose job is to listen, and complaints must be in writing, with dates and names. Keep copies of everything you send; the Swedish rule that every letter is read in full only helps a complainant whose letter says clearly what happened.

课文关于档案的那个观点最经得起时间。偏见很少留下书面痕迹，只看档案的投诉制度很难发现它；它需要能实地去看的人，也需要一条规矩：多起投诉形成的规律本身就算数，哪怕每一件单独都证明不了什么。现代监察专员机构基本也是这样工作的，专门寻找能暴露制度问题的重复投诉。对在加拿大的华人读者，实际用处很简单：公共机构对你不公，通常有一个免费、独立、职责就是听你说的机构；投诉要书面，写清日期和姓名。寄出的每样东西都留底；瑞典那条「每封信都细读」的规矩，只帮得了把事情写清楚的投诉人。

Sentences are counted in the lesson text, title not included. Find the sentence in your book, then read our notes on its structure.句子按课文正文数（不含标题）。在教材里找到这一句，再看本站的结构讲解。

Sentence 2第 2 句 (begins开头 “Sweden has evolved …”)

  • The present perfect stresses that it grew over time and still works today.has evolved：现在完成时，强调制度逐步形成、至今有效。

  • The present perfect passive.has been adopted：现在完成时被动，「已被采用」。

  • The point is that officials can do harm while sincerely meaning well.这句的重点在后半：官员犯错不一定出于恶意，往往是「好心办坏事」。

Sentence 7第 7 句 (begins开头 “A parliamentary committee …”)

  • The core is the committee appoints a person; do not let the extra phrases hide it.主干是 committee appoints a person；不要被分词和定语从句隔开。

  • It was only possible ... by doing: this was the only way.It was only possible for sb to do … by doing：只有通过……才能……。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

suggest, insist, demand that plus the bare verbsuggest / insist / demand that + 动词原形
After verbs of suggesting or demanding, such as suggest, recommend, insist, demand and propose, the that clause uses the bare verb (do, or be done for the passive). British English often puts should in front.
表建议、要求、命令的动词（suggest、recommend、insist、demand、propose）后的 that 从句用动词原形（主动 do，被动 be done），英式常加 should。

In sentence 16 the Ombudsman may propose to parliament a change in the law, and the clause uses the bare passive.

本课：第 16 句：专员可以建议议会修改法律，从句里用 be altered。

  • The doctor suggested that he take more exercise.
  • They insisted that the meeting be held in public.
The fact that ... and appositive clausesThe fact that … 与同位语从句
A that clause after nouns like fact, idea, belief, news and hope tells you what the noun contains. That has no job inside the clause and cannot be left out. The fact that ... often serves as the whole subject.
名词 fact、idea、belief、news、hope 后的 that 从句说明名词的内容（同位语从句），that 不作成分、不能省；The fact that … 常整体作主语。

  • The fact that she was late again annoyed everyone.

on one's behalf and by doing介词短语表方式与身份：on one's behalf / by doing
on sb's behalf and on behalf of sb mean acting for someone; by doing gives the means; It was only possible ... by doing stresses that there was no other way.
on sb's behalf / on behalf of sb＝代表某人、为了某人；by doing＝通过……方式；It was only possible … by doing 强调唯一途径。

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • X is admired everywhere for … X 因……而举世称道
    The city is admired everywhere for its clean streets.
  • X was the first to recognize that … X 最先认识到……
    She was the first to recognize that the plan would fail.
  • As long ago as …, X introduced … 早在……，X 就引入了……
    As long ago as 1900, the town introduced electric trams.
  • Nothing about X is kept secret, for … X 没什么可保密的，因为……
    Nothing about the vote is kept secret, for every result is posted online.
  • The action X takes varies according to … X 采取的行动因……而异
    The help we give varies according to each family's needs.

Our model summary本站范文

Since 1809 Sweden has had an Ombudsman, appointed by all parties and free from political pressure, to look into citizens' written complaints against officials and to act for them when the complaint is fair. Once a foreigner claimed a policeman had treated him badly, but the files showed nothing. When a second foreigner from that village complained, the Ombudsman sent a lawyer, who proved the policeman was biased. He was sternly warned, and the abuse ended.（76 词）
