🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
A covered shopping arcade near Piccadilly has just opened for the day. The owner of the jeweller's has finished admiring his new window and gone back inside when a car comes down the arcade with its horn going; two men with their faces covered break the glass with iron bars and take the diamonds. From the floor above, the owner and his staff throw furniture down, and a heavy statue strikes one of the raiders, who is far too busy to feel it. Three minutes later the car is gone. This is action writing with a strong pulse: quiet first, then violent, with the sentences getting shorter as the speed rises. You learn the with + noun pattern (with its headlights on), the past perfect continuous, too ... to, and a set of vivid action verbs.
皮卡迪利附近一条商场拱廊，清晨刚开门：珠宝店老板泰勒先生欣赏完新布置的橱窗回到店里，一辆车鸣着喇叭冲进来，两个蒙面人用铁棍砸窗抢钻石；泰勒在楼上和员工往下扔家具，一尊雕像砸中劫匪，劫匪却忙着抢钻石顾不上疼。三分钟后车扬长而去。这是节奏感极强的动作叙事：先静后动、句子由长变短。学 with 复合结构（with its headlights on）、had been doing、too … to 和一组动作动词。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

There was a famous raid of exactly this kind on a covered arcade off Piccadilly in 1964, three years before Book 3 appeared. Accounts of it describe a large saloon car driven up the pavement and in through the entrance, masked men who broke the window of a jewellers and took away tens of thousands of pounds' worth of stock, and people working in the floors above who threw flower pots and furniture down at them. The car was reversed out, and nobody was ever caught. Afterwards the owners of the arcade did the one thing that worked: they put posts across both ends so that no car could ever be driven in again. Sources differ on the value taken, giving either thirty-five or fifty thousand pounds, and on the make of the car. The resemblance to the lesson is close enough to be worth knowing about, but we have no word from the author that he used it, so the connection is not confirmed.

1964 年，皮卡迪利旁一条有顶拱廊里确实发生过一次完全同类的抢劫，比第三册出版早三年。各种记述都说：一辆大轿车开上人行道、从入口冲进拱廊，蒙面人砸了一家珠宝店的橱窗，带走价值数万英镑的货，而楼上办公的人往下砸花盆和家具。车倒着退出拱廊，人始终没抓到。事后拱廊的业主做了唯一管用的一件事：在两头装上石柱，让车再也开不进来。被抢金额各说不一，有三万五千英镑和五万英镑两说，车型也有分歧。它与课文像到值得一提，但我们没有作者本人说过取材于此，所以关联未核。

✨ How it is written赏析

Read this one with a watch. The first four sentences are long, flat and full of calm detail, and nothing in them moves faster than a man admiring his own window. Then one sentence breaks the quiet, and notice that it is built the wrong way round on purpose: the silence is made the subject and something is done to it, so the sentence itself is ambushed. From there the clauses shorten and the verbs change character — until that point the writing is full of was and had been, and afterwards it is roared, jumped, smashed, went flying, scrambled, moved off. The violence lasts three minutes in the story and about six lines on the page, and the ratio is the whole technique. The last movement is a man running after a car throwing ashtrays, which is both the bravest and the most useless thing in the lesson, and the writer lets it stand without comment.

读这一课要掐着表。前四句又长又平，满是从容的细节，里面最快的动作不过是一个人欣赏自己的橱窗。然后一句话打破安静——注意它是故意反着造的：把「寂静」当主语，让它被动地遭遇什么，于是这句话本身就是一次突袭。从这里起分句变短，动词换了性格：在那之前满篇是 was、had been，之后是呼啸、跳下、砸碎、横飞、慌忙爬回、绝尘而去。暴力在故事里持续三分钟，在纸上约占六行，这个比例就是全部技法。最后一个动作是一个人追着汽车扔烟灰缸——这是全课最勇敢、也最没用的举动，作者让它就那么立着，一个字不评。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

What the lesson leaves out is the only thing that ended such raids. Thrown furniture is unforgettable and achieved nothing; what stopped the next car was a row of posts set in the ground. That is the uncomfortable shape of most security: the memorable response is heroic and personal, the effective one is dull, physical and installed before anything happens. The story also quietly flatters us. We are given the owner's view throughout — his window, his staff, his statue finding its mark — and we are never told what a customer in the arcade saw, or how long it took the police to arrive, or what the insurer paid. Written the other way round, from the pavement, the same three minutes would read as terror rather than as spirit. The lesson teaches pace beautifully and is worth reading twice for that; just do not take it as a guide to what to do.

课文没写的，恰恰是真正终结这类抢劫的那件事。扔家具令人难忘，但什么也没拦住；拦住下一辆车的是地上一排石柱。多数安防都是这个别扭的形状：令人记住的那种应对是英勇的、个人的，真正有效的那种是乏味的、物理的，而且在事情发生之前就已经装好了。这篇故事还悄悄奉承了读者：全程给的是店主的视角——他的橱窗、他的员工、他那尊砸中人的雕像——却从不告诉我们拱廊里的顾客看见了什么、警察多久才到、保险赔了多少。换个方向，从人行道上写同样这三分钟，读起来会是惊恐，不是豪气。这一课把节奏教得极好，为这一点值得读两遍；只是别把它当成遇事该怎么办的指南。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

The past perfect continuous: had been doing过去完成进行时：had been doing
Something went on right up to a point in the past, and may have just stopped or may still be going; it often comes with since or for.
到过去某时为止一直在做（可能刚结束或仍在继续），常与 since / for 连用。

Compare the two: the simple past perfect reports the finished result, while the continuous form stresses the stretch of work, which is also why they were tired and had only just stopped.

对比：had worked 强调完成的结果；had been working 强调持续过程（也解释了「累」「刚完」）。

  • She was tired because she had been running.
  • When I arrived, they had been waiting for two hours.
The with pattern: with + noun + adverb / participle / prepositional phrasewith 复合结构：with + 名词 + 副词/分词/介词短语
It describes the state that goes with the main action: an adverb for things switched on, an -ing form when the noun is doing something, an -ed form when something is done to it, and a prepositional phrase for where something is.
描写伴随状态：with its headlights on（副词）、with its horn blaring（主动进行用 -ing）、with his eyes closed（被动用 -ed）、with a book in his hand（介词短语）。
  • He fell asleep with the TV on.
  • She came in with tears running down her face.
Verbs for action writing: smash / grab / roar / scramble / gaze / blare动作叙事的动词：smash / grab / roar / scramble / gaze / blare
Lively narrative leans on exact verbs instead of a weak verb plus an adverb: one verb can carry speed and noise together, another carries hurry and clumsiness, another long pleasurable looking, another violent breaking.
生动的叙事靠精确的动词代替「动词 + 副词」：roar down（= drive noisily and fast）、scramble back（= climb back hurriedly）、gaze at（= look at for a long time）、smash（= break violently）。

When you write, prefer the single exact verb to a vague verb with a long tail of adverbs.

写作时：与其 He looked at it for a long time，不如 He gazed at it。

  • The crowd poured out of the stadium.
  • She slammed the door.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • … was too busy doing … to … 忙着……而顾不上……
    She was too busy studying to notice the time.
  • The … was all over in … minutes, for … ……不到……分钟就结束了，因为……
    The match was all over in ten minutes, for the other team never turned up.
  • They had got away with … 他们带着……逃之夭夭
    The hackers had got away with millions of passwords.
