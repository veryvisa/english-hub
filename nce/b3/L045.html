Lesson 45 · The Power of the Press 新闻的力量

📘 New Concept English · Book 3 · Lesson 45 “The Power of the Press”新概念英语 第三册 Lesson 45《The Power of the Press》

B2🧠 27 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
In democratic countries, attempts to limit press freedom are rightly condemned, but that freedom is easily abused. Stories about people draw more interest than politics, and while we like reading about others, we would hardly enjoy reading about ourselves. Reporters who treat facts as sacred can cause great suffering by publishing private details, and newspapers are powerful enough to change ordinary lives and even bring down governments. The example is a poor family in Aberdeen, South Dakota, already struggling with five children, whose sixth pregnancy produced five babies at once, four girls and one boy. Next day a plane flew in sixty reporters and photographers. Television and newspapers spread the news nationwide, magazines offered large sums for exclusive rights, gifts poured in from strangers and from makers of baby food and soap, an old farmhouse was to give way to a half-million-dollar home, and lawyers had to be hired to speak to the press on the family's behalf. While the babies slept in oxygen tents, their parents were already paying for fame: they would never live normally again, and their children had become a product. This is an argument that states a case and then gives an example, and the power of the title cuts both ways. The grammar to learn is be rightly condemned, it is doubtful whether, Acting on, not only ... but even, the unreal past with If they had only had, was to be done, pay the price for, and Instead of being.
在民主国家，限制新闻自由理当受到谴责，可这种自由也很容易被滥用。关于人的故事往往比政治事件更吸引人；我们爱读别人的生活，却未必愿意读到自己。记者打着「事实是神圣的」旗号公布个人隐私，可能给人带来说不尽的痛苦；报纸的影响大到能改变普通人的命运，甚至推翻政府。课文举的例子是南达科他州阿伯丁一户穷人家：已有五个孩子，日子本就紧巴巴，第六胎竟生了五胞胎——四女一男。第二天，一架飞机载来六十名记者和摄影师，电视和报纸把消息传遍全国，杂志出巨资买独家报道权，陌生人和婴儿食品、肥皂厂商的礼物潮水般涌来，旧农舍要换成五十万美元的新房，记者天天追着要采访，只好请律师当发言人。五个孩子还在医院氧气罩里安睡，父母已在为出名付出代价——他们再也过不了正常的生活，孩子一出生就成了商品。本课是先立论、后举例的议论文，题目里的「力量」既是正面也是反面。学 be rightly condemned、it is doubtful whether、Acting on、not only … but even、If they had only had …（与过去相反的虚拟）、was to be replaced、pay the price for、Instead of being。

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander, but based on a real event. The family were the Fischers of Aberdeen, South Dakota, whose quintuplets — four girls and one boy — were born in September 1963 and were the first in the United States known to have survived infancy.

课文为编者 L. G. 亚历山大为教材自撰，但取材于真事。这家人是南达科他州阿伯丁的费舍尔一家，他们的五胞胎——四个女孩、一个男孩——生于 1963 年 9 月，是美国已知第一组活过婴儿期的五胞胎。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

The Fischer births brought exactly the attention the lesson describes: national television, magazine contracts, gifts from companies hoping to advertise, and a new house built with donations. Later accounts by the family suggest that the parents tried hard to protect the children from the press as they grew up, and that the attention faded over the years. There is an older and darker case that shows how far such stories could go, and it happened in Canada. The Dionne quintuplets, five girls born in northern Ontario in 1934, were removed from their parents' care by the provincial government, which raised them in a special nursery that became a tourist attraction: millions of visitors came to watch them play behind a screen, and the area around them was nicknamed Quintland. Decades later the surviving sisters described the harm this had done, and in 1998 the Ontario government apologized and agreed to pay them compensation. Against that background, the Fischers' story is a milder example of the same pressure, and the lesson's warning is not exaggerated.

费舍尔家的五胞胎诞生后，引来的关注正如课文所写：全国电视、杂志合同、想借机做广告的公司送来的礼物，以及一座用捐款建起来的新房。家人后来的讲述显示，孩子们成长过程中，父母竭力替他们挡住媒体，而那种关注也随着岁月淡了下来。还有一个更早、更黑暗的例子，显示这类故事能走得多远，而它就发生在加拿大。迪翁五胞胎是 1934 年生于安大略省北部的五个女孩，省政府把她们从父母身边带走，在一所专门的育婴院里抚养，那里成了旅游景点：数以百万计的游客隔着一道屏幕看她们玩耍，周边地区被戏称为「五胞胎乐园」。几十年后，在世的姐妹讲述了这一切给她们造成的伤害；1998 年，安大略省政府道歉并同意给予赔偿。以此为背景，费舍尔一家的故事是同一种压力的一个较温和的例子，课文的警告并不夸张。

✨ How it is written赏析

The essay argues against its own conclusion for a while before it reaches it. It opens by defending press freedom and only then turns, with however, to its abuse — a structure that makes the criticism sound fair rather than hostile. The example is chosen with care: no politician, no scandal, just a poor family with too many children, which makes the press's interest impossible to justify as public duty. The writer then uses one sentence of unreal past — what would have happened with just one more child — to show us the quiet life that was lost. After that the story speeds up, and its sentences fill with money and numbers: sixty reporters, huge sums, a half-million-dollar home. The turn comes in a single image of contrast: the babies sleeping quietly in oxygen tents while their parents are already paying. The final sentence names what has happened without any adjectives — they had become a commodity — and the plainness is what makes it hit.

这篇文章在得出结论之前，先替反方说了一阵话。它开头为新闻自由辩护，然后才用一个 however 转到滥用上——这样的结构让批评显得公允而不是敌对。例子是精心挑选的：没有政客、没有丑闻，只有一个孩子太多的穷人家，这让媒体的兴趣无法用「公共责任」来辩解。接着作者用一句与过去相反的虚拟——如果只多生一个孩子会怎样——让我们看到那种被夺走的平静生活。此后故事加速，句子里满是钱和数字：六十名记者、巨款、五十万美元的房子。转折落在一个对比的画面上：婴儿在氧气罩里安静地睡着，父母却已经在付出代价。最后一句不用任何形容词，直接说出发生了什么——他们成了商品——正是这份朴素让它击中人心。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The lesson was written for a world of newspapers, magazines and a few television channels, and it reads differently now that anyone can publish. The pressure it describes has not gone away; it has spread. Today the family would not need sixty reporters to become famous overnight — one viral video would do — and the people turning children into a commodity are often the parents themselves, sharing their children's lives online for followers and money. Some places have begun to respond: laws and proposals in several countries now aim to protect the earnings or the privacy of children who appear in family videos. The lesson's central question remains the best one to ask: is a story worth telling because it is true, or because the people in it have agreed to be seen? The writer's answer — that facts are not automatically sacred when they belong to someone's private life — is the one most modern codes of press ethics would also give.

这一课是为一个只有报纸、杂志和几个电视频道的世界写的，在人人都能发布内容的今天，读起来不一样了。课文描写的那种压力没有消失，而是扩散了。如今这家人不需要六十名记者就能一夜成名——一段爆红的视频就够了；而把孩子变成商品的，往往是父母自己：为了粉丝和收入在网上分享孩子的生活。一些地方已经开始回应：好几个国家的法律和提案，开始保护出现在家庭视频里的孩子的收入或隐私。课文的核心问题仍然是最值得问的那个：一个故事值得讲，是因为它真实，还是因为故事里的人同意被人看见？作者的回答——当事实属于某人的私生活时，它并不自动神圣——也是今天大多数新闻伦理守则会给出的回答。

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

The unreal past: If plus had done, would have done与过去事实相反的虚拟：If + had done, would have done
For things that did not happen in the past, use had plus a past participle in the if clause and would, could or might plus have plus a past participle in the main clause. only after if adds regret or emphasis. A mixed form, If they had ..., they would now ..., links an unreal past to an unreal present.
讲过去没有发生的事，条件句用 had + 过去分词，主句用 would / could / might + have + 过去分词。if 后可以加 only 表示惋惜或强调（If only…）。also：混合条件句 If they had…, they would now… 表示「过去如何，现在就会如何」。

Sentences 13 and 16 imagine the family having just one more child, in which case nobody would have noticed and they would have gone on struggling unseen.

本课：第 13–14 句：要是他们只多生一个孩子，这件事就不会有人注意，他们会继续默默挣扎。

  • If I had known your number, I would have phoned you.
  • If she had left earlier, she might have caught the train.
  • If we had bought that house, we would be rich now.
be to do / was to be done: plans and destinybe to do / was to be done：安排与命运
be to do describes a plan or arrangement. was or were to do means 'at that time it was planned', without saying whether it happened; was to have done means it was planned but did not happen. In stories, was to do can also look ahead to fate: He was to become famous.
be to do 表示计划、安排；过去式 was / were to do 表示「当时计划要……」（不说明后来是否实现）；was to have done 表示「本来要……（但没做成）」。叙事中 was to do 还可以表示「后来注定会……」：He was to become famous.

Sentence 22 tells us the old farmhouse was going to be replaced by a much grander house.

本课：第 22 句：那座旧农舍将被一座五十万美元的新房代替。

  • The meeting is to be held on Monday.
  • The bridge was to be finished by May.
  • She left the village at sixteen; she was never to return.
not only ... but (also / even) ...; Instead of doing
not only ... but also ... joins two parallel parts; using even in place of also makes the second part more surprising. When not only begins the sentence, inversion follows: Not only did he .... instead of plus a noun or gerund means 'rather than'.
not only … but also … 连接两个并列成分；把 also 换成 even，表示后一项更出人意料。not only 放在句首时要倒装（Not only did he…）。instead of + 名词 / 动名词：代替，而不是。

Sentence 8 uses not only ... but ... even to stress that newspapers can bring down governments, and sentence 28 uses Instead of being.

本课：第 8 句用 not only … but … even，强调报纸甚至能推翻政府；第 28 句用 Instead of being。

  • She not only sings but also writes her own songs.
  • Not only did he forget my birthday, but he even forgot my name.
  • Instead of watching TV, why not read a book?

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • Any efforts to … are rightly condemned. 任何……的企图都理当受到谴责
    Any efforts to silence teachers are rightly condemned.
  • It is extremely doubtful whether … ……非常值得怀疑
    It is extremely doubtful whether the bridge will open on time.
  • X have such … influence that they not only … but even … X 的影响之大，不仅能……甚至能……
    Celebrities have such great influence that they not only sell products but even change laws.
  • If they had only …, X would have passed unnoticed. 要是他们只……，X 就不会引人注意
    If she had only sung one song, her talent would have passed unnoticed.
  • Instead of being …, X had become … X 不是……，而成了……

⑥ Dictation and retelling听写与复述

Our model summary本站范文

A poor couple in Aberdeen, South Dakota, already had five children and could hardly make ends meet. When their next babies turned out to be quintuplets, sixty reporters flew in the following day, and the news spread nationwide. Papers and magazines paid huge sums for exclusive stories, gifts poured in, and a costly new home was planned. Yet fame had a price: the parents could never live normally again, and their babies had become a commodity overnight.（77 词）
