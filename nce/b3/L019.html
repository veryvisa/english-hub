Lesson 19 · A Very Dear Cat 一只贵重的宝贝猫

📘 New Concept English · Book 3 · Lesson 19 “A Very Dear Cat”新概念英语 第三册 Lesson 19《A Very Dear Cat》

B2🧠 19 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
Kidnappers seldom bother with animals, but they took a keen interest in a wealthy old lady's cat. Rastus kept regular hours and was always home by seven in the evening. One evening he was not, and three days later Mrs Ramsay opened an anonymous letter: pay a thousand pounds and the cat comes straight back. She thought about the police, decided she would then never see him again, and did as the letter said: the notes went into a cardboard box, and the box went outside her door. Next morning it had gone; that evening, at seven, the cat walked in, thirsty enough to empty half a milk bottle. The dear in the title is a pun: loved, and expensive. Learn be instructed to do, participles of reason such as fearing that ..., keep one's word, considering, and the present perfect for something still going on.
绑匪很少对动物感兴趣，可他们最近看上了富有的老太太拉姆齐的猫拉斯特斯。这只猫生活极有规律，每晚七点准时回家；某天没回来，三天后老太太收到匿名信：交 1000 英镑赎金，猫就会马上送回。她本想报警，又怕再也见不到猫，只好照信上说的把钱放进纸盒、搁在门外。第二天盒子不见了，当晚七点猫准时到家，只是渴得喝了半瓶牛奶。标题的 dear 是双关：「心爱的」和「昂贵的」。学 be instructed to do、分词作原因状语（fearing that…）、keep one's word、considering 和现在完成时表持续。
Questions to keep in mind as you read读的时候带着这几个问题：
  1. What was regular about the way Rastus lived, and what did that have to do with the kidnapping?拉斯特斯的生活有什么规律？这和绑架有什么关系？
  2. Why did Mrs Ramsay change her mind about going to the police?拉姆齐太太为什么改变主意，没去报警？
  3. In what sense was the cat expensive as well as beloved?为什么说这只猫在不止一个意义上 very dear？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander; Mrs Ramsay and her cat are invented, and we found no news report behind them, so the case is unverified. The law the story quietly depends on, however, is real and has since changed, which is the most interesting thing to check here.

课文由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为教材自撰；拉姆齐太太和她的猫是虚构的，我们也没找到背后的新闻报道，所以案情未核。但故事暗中依赖的那套法律是真的，而且后来变了——这是这里最值得核的一件事。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

The joke in this story rests on a legal fact that most readers never notice. For the whole of the period when this course was written and taught, a cat in Britain was in law a piece of property, and taking one was ordinary theft — the same offence as taking a bicycle, with the penalty scaled to the animal's market value rather than to what the owner felt. That is why Mrs Ramsay's thousand pounds is funny and also slightly desperate: the law could only see a cheap object, while she could see a member of the household, and the gap between those two valuations is exactly what a kidnapper sells. Britain closed that gap in twenty twenty-four. A Pet Abduction Act, which received royal assent in May of that year and came into force in the August, created separate offences of dog abduction and cat abduction, carrying up to five years in prison, and the drafting makes the change of principle plain: the wrong is defined in terms of the animal's own welfare and distress at being taken from the person who looks after it, not in terms of a lost possession. It also allows ministers to extend the offences to other animals commonly kept as pets.

这个故事的笑点建立在一个大多数读者从不留意的法律事实上。在这套教材成书与讲授的整个时期里，英国法律把猫当作一件财产，偷猫就是普通盗窃——和偷一辆自行车同罪，刑罚按动物的市价而不是按主人的感受来定。所以拉姆齐太太那一千英镑既好笑又有点绝望：法律只看得见一件便宜的物品，而她看得见一个家庭成员，绑匪出售的，正是这两种估值之间的落差。英国在二〇二四年把这道落差补上了。《宠物劫夺法》当年五月获御准、八月生效，单独设立了「劫夺犬」与「劫夺猫」两项罪名，最高可判五年；而条文的写法把原则的改变说得很清楚：不法在于动物本身的福利、在于它被带离照料者时的痛苦，而不在于一件财产的丢失。该法还授权大臣把这两项罪名扩展到其他常见的宠物。

✨ How it is written赏析

The lesson is a one-joke piece built with real care, and the care is all in the planting. Four things are set up before they are needed. The cat keeps regular hours and is home by seven, which looks like characterisation and turns out to be the clock the ending runs on. The lady is described as very wealthy, which looks like scene-setting and is in fact the kidnapper's motive. The letter has made something quite clear, tucked away between dashes, which is what defeats the idea of calling the police. And the cat comes back thirsty and empties half a milk bottle, the one detail that proves the animal was really held somewhere rather than simply out roaming. None of these is underlined. Then look at the last two sentences, which do the same trick as the title. First a plain statement in her own voice: he was very dear to her. Then the writer steps in with a comment that re-reads her words for her, and the pun lands only because the word was used innocently first. That order is the whole craft of a pun in English: use the word straight, then turn it. One more thing to notice is what the writer never says — whether paying was wise, whether the police were right to be astounded. The judgement is left entirely to you.

这是一篇单笑点的文章，却造得很用心，而用心全在埋设上。有四样东西在被需要之前就布好了。猫作息规律、七点前回家，看着像人物刻画，结果是结尾赖以运转的那只钟。老太太被写成「很有钱」，看着像背景交代，其实是绑匪的动机。那封信「已经说得很明白」，被塞在两个破折号之间，而正是它打掉了报警的念头。猫回来时很渴、喝了半瓶牛奶——这是唯一能证明它真被关在某处、而不只是出去野了的细节。这些都没有被加重语气。再看最后两句，它们玩的是和标题同样的手法。先是她自己口吻的一句平叙：这只猫对她很珍贵。然后作者插进来，替她把这句话重读一遍；而双关之所以能落地，只因为那个词先被无辜地用过一次。这个顺序就是英文双关的全部手艺：先把词用正，再把它拧过来。还有一处值得注意的是作者从不说的东西——付钱是否明智，警方的震惊是否有理。判断整个留给了你。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

As a story the ending is too kind, and that is where it has dated. Police advice on ransom demands has always pulled the other way: paying funds the next one, and the payer has no way to compel delivery. Mrs Ramsay is rewarded for ignoring that advice by a kidnapper who keeps his word, which makes the anecdote charming and makes it bad guidance. The real-world version of this crime grew sharply when demand for pets rose, and a government taskforce in Britain reported on it before the law was changed. What has not dated at all is the economics underneath. A kidnapper needed three things, and all three are still available: an animal with a predictable routine, an owner whose attachment far exceeds the market price, and an untraceable way to be paid. Only the last of those has really moved, from a cardboard box on a doorstep to a transfer that can in principle be followed — which is one reason the small-scale version of this crime is harder than it was. Readers in China will find the two valuations familiar from the other direction. Pet keeping has become ordinary in Chinese cities within about a generation, veterinary and boarding costs now run to real money, and the animal's legal standing is still that of property; so the owner's loss remains measured in money while the owner measures it in something else entirely. That gap is the whole subject of this lesson, and it is wider now than it was when the lesson was written.

作为故事，这个结尾太仁慈了，而这正是它过时的地方。警方对赎金要求的建议一直指向另一边：付钱会资助下一起，而付钱的人没有任何办法强制对方交货。拉姆齐太太因为不听这条建议，得到了一个守信绑匪的回报——这让轶事变得可爱，也让它成了糟糕的指南。这类犯罪的现实版本在宠物需求上升时急剧增加，英国政府一个专项工作组在修法之前就此作过报告。完全没有过时的是底下那套经济学。绑匪需要三样东西，而三样今天都还有：一只作息可预测的动物，一个依恋远超市价的主人，以及一条收钱而查不到的路。只有最后一样真的变了，从门口的一个纸盒变成原则上可追查的转账——这也是这类小规模犯罪比从前难做的原因之一。中国读者会从另一侧认出那两种估值。养宠物在中国城市里差不多一代人之间就变成了平常事，看病和寄养如今是实打实的花费，而动物的法律地位仍然是财产；于是主人的损失仍按钱计量，而主人自己计量的完全是另一回事。这道落差正是本课的全部主题，而它今天比课文写成时更宽。

Sentence 2第 2 句 (begins开头 “Mrs. Eleanor Ramsay, …”)

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

Words with two meanings, and puns: dear / interest / shock一词多义与双关：dear / interest / shock
English humour leans on words that mean two things. dear is both loved or precious (dear to sb) and, in British use, expensive. take interest in means pay attention to, while interest is also what money earns in a bank. When you meet in more ways than one, the writer is telling you to look for the second meaning.
英语幽默常靠一词多义。dear 既是「亲爱的、珍爱的」(dear to sb)，也是「昂贵的」(= expensive, 英式)；take interest in 既是「关注」，也可让人联想到「利息」。读到 in more ways than one（不止一层意思）就是作者在提醒你找双关。
  • Food is very dear in this town.（英式：贵）
  • She is a dear friend of mine.（亲爱的）
Passive plus infinitive: be instructed / told / asked / expected to do被动 + 不定式：be instructed / told / asked / expected to do
When instruct, tell, ask, expect or allow sb to do goes into the passive, the infinitive stays: sb be instructed / told / asked / expected / allowed to do.
instruct/tell/ask/expect/allow sb to do 变被动后，不定式保留：sb be instructed/told/asked/expected/allowed to do。

In this lesson, sentence 10: she was instructed to place the notes in a box, and then to leave that box on her doorstep.

本课：第 10 句她 was instructed to place 钱… and to leave 盒子在门外。

  • Passengers are asked to switch off their phones.
  • We were told to wait outside.
Participles of reason: fearing ... / not knowing ... / considering ...分词短语作原因状语：fearing… / not knowing… / considering…
A present participle phrase at the front or in the middle of a sentence takes the subject of the sentence as its own, and gives the reason (= because or as ...). considering has turned into a preposition, so it can take a noun or a that clause and is not bound by that rule.
现在分词短语放在句首或句中，逻辑主语就是句子的主语，表示原因（= because/as…）。considering 已经变成介词，后面可以直接接名词或 that 从句，不受逻辑主语限制。
  • Feeling tired, I went to bed early.
  • Considering the weather, the turnout was good.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • X are rarely interested in …, but … recently took considerable interest in … X 很少关心……，最近却对……格外上心
    Teenagers are rarely interested in gardening, but my son recently took considerable interest in roses.
  • X leads an orderly life. X 过着有规律的生活
    My grandfather leads an orderly life; he has lunch at noon every day.
  • X was in safe hands. X 被照顾得很好
    Don't worry — your dog is in safe hands with us.
  • Sure enough, … 果然……
    I knew he would be late, and sure enough, he arrived at ten.

  • The box was gone by morning; the cat was home at seven that evening, very thirsty, and the police were astounded when they heard.第二天早上盒子不见了；当晚七点猫准时回家，只是很渴；警方听说后大为震惊。
Our model summary本站范文

Mrs Ramsay, a rich old lady, has lived with her cat Rastus for years. He always comes home by seven, but one evening he did not appear. Three days later she got an unsigned letter asking for £1,000 in a box left outside her door. She thought of calling the police but feared losing Rastus forever, so she paid. The box vanished overnight, and that evening Rastus came home on time, thirsty but well.（74 词）
