Lesson 15 · Fifty Pence Worth of Trouble 五十便士的麻烦

📘 New Concept English · Book 3 · Lesson 15 “Fifty Pence Worth of Trouble”新概念英语 第三册 Lesson 15《Fifty Pence Worth of Trouble》

B2🧠 22 new words个新词📝 Full notes有完整讲解

🎯 Before you open the lesson开课前
Children like money handed to them. Parents pay out at regular intervals, and uncles and aunts count as extra income. Only the very thrifty ever fill a money box; for most children fifty pence turns into chocolate. The writer's nephew George owns a money box which is permanently empty. Given fifty pence and told to save it, George instead buys trouble to the value of fifty pence: on the way to the sweet shop the coin escapes, bounces along and vanishes down a drain, and when he reaches in after it he loses the coin and cannot pull his arm back out. Soap and butter fail; the fire brigade frees him with a special grease. The sweet-shop lady hears the story and sends him a large box of chocolates. The lesson is a comic narrative that generalises first and then gives the case. Learn what is more, exchange A for B, find one's way, reward sb with, and some causative and passive patterns.
孩子都喜欢零花钱：爸妈按时给，叔叔阿姨是「额外收入」；只有特别节俭的孩子才攒得满储蓄罐。作者的侄子乔治有个储蓄罐，却永远是空的。作者给了他 50 便士叫他存起来，他却「买了 50 便士的麻烦」：去糖果店路上硬币掉进下水道，他把胳膊伸进去，钱没摸到，胳膊还拔不出来；肥皂、黄油都不管用，最后消防队用特殊油脂把他弄了出来。好在糖果店老板娘听说后送了他一大盒巧克力。本课是先概括后举例的幽默记叙，学 what is more、exchange A for B、find one's way、reward sb with 和使役/被动结构。

  2. How did George lose his fifty pence, and why did he get stuck?乔治的 50 便士是怎么丢的？他为什么被卡住了？

📖 Understanding the piece读懂这篇

Written for the course by L. G. Alexander; George the nephew is a device, not a person on record. The money in the story, however, can be dated precisely, and the dating is the most interesting thing about the lesson.

课文由编者 L. G. 亚历山大为教材自撰，侄子乔治是个写作装置，不是有案可查的人。但故事里的钱是可以精确定年的，而这个定年恰恰是本课最有意思的地方。

🌍 Where it comes from出处与背景

The coin at the centre of this story was new technology when the course was being taught. Britain put a fifty-pence piece into circulation on the fourteenth of October, nineteen sixty-nine, ahead of decimal day in February nineteen seventy-one. It was the first seven-sided coin in the world, shaped that way so that nobody could mistake it for anything else by touch, and it took over from the ten-shilling note, because a note wore out in months while a coin would last for decades. Shoppers disliked it at first and called it toy money. For a child in the years that followed, fifty pence really was the sum this lesson says it is: a bar of chocolate, several times over, or a money box worth rattling. The other coin in the lesson gives away a later date. Pound coins did not exist until the twenty-first of April, nineteen eighty-three, when the round pound replaced the one-pound note; the note itself was withdrawn five years after that. So the sentence that names both of those coins together cannot come from the original nineteen-sixties course; it belongs to a revision made after nineteen eighty-three.

故事中心的那枚硬币，在这套教材开始教的年头还是新东西。英国在一九六九年十月十四日把五十便士硬币投入流通，比一九七一年二月的十进制日还早。它是世界上第一枚七边形硬币，做成这个形状是为了让人靠手感就不会认错，它取代的是十先令纸币——纸币几个月就磨坏，硬币能用几十年。顾客起初并不喜欢它，管它叫玩具钱。对此后那些年里的孩子来说，五十便士确实就是本课所写的那个数目：够买几块巧克力，或者值得往储蓄罐里一扔听响。课文里另一种硬币泄露了更晚的日期。一镑硬币到一九八三年四月二十一日才出现，那天圆形的一镑币取代了一镑纸币；纸币本身在五年后退出。所以同时提到五十便士硬币和一镑硬币的那句话，不可能来自六十年代的原版教材，它属于一九八三年之后的修订。

✨ How it is written赏析

The lesson is built in two unequal halves, and the join is the sentence that introduces George. The first half is written as a set of general laws about children and money, in the present tense, with the authority of someone who has observed many children: parents supply, relatives top up, a little money buys a surprising amount, only the thrifty fill a box. None of it is about anybody. Then one name arrives, and every law gets a counter-example in a single person. Notice the engine of the comedy: the writer keeps using the vocabulary of thrift and investment for a disaster. The coin is to be saved; what George buys is trouble, priced in pence; the coins the writer has handed over have never found their way to the box. The physical comedy is handled by understatement — nothing is described as painful or frightening, only firmly stuck — and the pay-off is a reward, the word you would use for virtue, handed to a boy for having done something foolish in public. Keep one trick from this: price an abstract noun. Fifty pence worth of trouble is funny because trouble does not come in pence, and the whole lesson is in the title.

本课由两个不等长的半边搭成，接缝就是引出乔治的那一句。前半边写成一组关于孩子与钱的普遍规律，用一般现在时，带着一个看过许多孩子的人的权威：父母按时供给，亲戚追加，小钱很经花，只有节俭的孩子才攒得满罐。这些话都不关于任何具体的人。接着一个名字出现，每条规律都在一个人身上找到了反例。注意喜剧的发动机：作者一直用节俭与理财的词汇去写一场灾难。那枚硬币是要「存起来」的；乔治买到的是「麻烦」，按便士计价；作者给过的那些硬币，从来没有「找到路」去储蓄罐。身体喜剧靠的是轻描淡写——没有一个字说疼或害怕，只说「卡得很牢」——而收尾是一个「奖赏」：这个你本来用来形容德行的词，被交给了一个在公众面前干了件傻事的男孩。从这里带走一招：给抽象名词定价。「五十便士的麻烦」之所以好笑，是因为麻烦不按便士卖，而整课的要害都在标题里。

🧭 What it argues, and does it still hold?解读

The judgement in the first half has worn well: children do value money that is handed to them more than money that merely exists, and relatives still function as an unbudgeted revenue stream. What has dated is the physical world. Both coins in the story have since been retired or reshaped — the round pound was replaced by a twelve-sided coin in March twenty seventeen and lost legal tender status that October — and a child today is as likely to be given money by transfer as in the hand, which quietly removes the whole plot: a transferred fifty pence cannot bounce along a pavement and disappear. The rescue has dated in a happier direction. A fire service turning out to free a child from a drain is not a story about fires at all, and the share of a modern brigade's work that is rescue rather than firefighting is large; this was already true then, and the lesson records it without noticing. Readers in China will recognise the economics from the other side. The equivalent of George's fifty pence was a few jiao for an ice lolly or a handful of sweets, and the equivalent money box was often a tin or a bamboo tube; what has changed there is sharper than in Britain, because the step from coins in a child's palm to a code on a phone happened within a single childhood rather than across three generations. The behaviour, though, is identical, and so is the lesson George fails to learn.

前半边的判断很耐久：孩子确实更看重递到手上的钱，而不是账面上存在的钱；亲戚也仍然扮演着没进预算的财源。过时的是物理世界。故事里的两种硬币后来都退役或改形了——圆形一镑币在二〇一七年三月被十二边形币取代，当年十月失去法定货币地位——而今天的孩子拿到钱，用转账和用手给的机会差不多，这悄悄把整个情节抽掉了：转过来的五十便士不会在人行道上弹跳，然后消失。救援这一层则朝好的方向变了。消防队出动把一个孩子从下水道里弄出来，根本不是一个关于火的故事；现代消防队的工作里，救援而非灭火的比重很大——那时候就已经如此，课文记下了却没察觉。中国读者会从另一侧认出这套经济学。乔治那五十便士的对应物是买冰棍或糖果的几角钱，储蓄罐的对应物常是一个铁盒或一截竹筒；那边的变化比英国更陡，因为从孩子手心里的硬币走到手机上的一个码，是在一个童年之内完成的，而不是跨了三代人。但行为完全一样，乔治没学会的那一课也完全一样。

💬 Discuss and write讨论与写作

    作者给了钱，也给了建议；乔治只拿了钱。关于钱的建议什么时候是无效的？对乔治这样的男孩，什么办法会更管用？
  2. Write 120 words about a small loss that turned out well, or a small gain that cost you more than it was worth. Use what is more once and a small price to pay once.
    写 120 词，讲一次结果反而不错的小损失，或一次代价超过收获的小便宜。用一次 what is more，用一次 a small price to pay。

    乔治因为在众人面前干了件傻事而得到巧克力。这是善举，还是一堂坏课？如果你开那家店，你会怎么做？

  • a source of extra income: where the extra money comes from — relatives treated, drily, as revenue.a source of extra income：额外收入的来源（幽默地把亲戚当「财源」）。

Sentence 4第 4 句 (begins开头 “If fifty pence …”)

④ Grammar in this lesson本课语法

Collocations: exchange, provide, reward, supplyexchange / provide / reward / supply 的搭配
Each of these verbs has its own preposition: exchange A for B; provide sb with sth or provide sth for sb; supply sb with sth or supply sth to sb; reward sb with sth (what they get) or reward sb for doing (what they did).
这组动词各有固定介词：exchange A for B（用 A 换 B）；provide sb with sth ／ provide sth for sb；supply sb with sth ／ supply sth to sb；reward sb with sth（用……奖励）／ reward sb for doing（因……而奖励）。

In this lesson, sentence 4: the coins are not exchanged for sweets; sentence 2: a parent provides a regular supply of money to spend; sentence 17: the shop owner rewarded him with a big box of chocolates.

本课：第 4 句硬币 are not exchanged for 糖；第 2 句爸妈 provides a regular supply of 零花钱；第 17 句老板娘 rewarded him with 一大盒巧克力。

  • Can I exchange this shirt for a bigger one?
  • The school provides students with free lunch.
  • They rewarded the boy for finding the lost dog.
what is more / besides / moreover: adding to the pointwhat is more / besides / moreover：递进
what is more is the conversational way of saying and on top of that; it usually sits between two parts of a sentence with commas round it. besides and moreover mean much the same, and moreover is more formal.
what is more 是口语化的「而且更……」，常插在两部分之间，前后用逗号隔开；besides、moreover 意思相近，moreover 更正式。

In this lesson, sentence 14: the coin cannot be found, and what is more, his arm will not come out — things have just got worse.

本课：第 14 句，硬币找不到，what is more，胳膊也拔不出来——事情变得更糟。

  • The film was long and, what is more, very boring.
  • It's too late to go out. Besides, it's raining.
a/an + adjective + price to pay, and sum of money + worth ofa/an + 形容词 + price to pay / 金额 + worth of
An infinitive can follow a noun: a small price to pay. A sum or quantity plus worth of plus a noun means to that value; written carefully it takes an apostrophe, as in fifty pence' worth of.
不定式作后置定语：a small price to pay（值得付出的小代价）；金额/数量 + worth of + 名词：价值……的……（更规范写作 fifty pence' worth of）。
  • She bought ten dollars' worth of apples.

⑤ Patterns to reuse可以套用的句型

  • Children always appreciate … 孩子们总喜欢……
    Children always appreciate a bedtime story.
  • With some children, … go a long way. 对有些孩子来说，……很经用
    With some students, a little praise goes a long way.
  • Only very … children manage to … 只有特别……的孩子才能……
    Only very patient children manage to finish a 1,000-piece puzzle.
  • X, and what is more, … X，而且更糟的是……
    I missed the bus, and what is more, it started to rain.
  • X was not too upset by …, because … X 并没有因……太难过，因为……
    She was not too upset by the lost game, because her team still made the final.

Summary writing摘要写作

In no more than 80 words, tell the story of the trouble George's fifty pence caused.

用不超过 80 个词讲述乔治的 50 便士惹出的麻烦。

Points a good summary covers好摘要要覆盖的要点
  • The writer gave his nephew George fifty pence and told him to save it; George set off to spend it on sweets.作者给了侄子乔治 50 便士，叫他存起来，他却拿去买糖。
  • On the way the coin got away from him, bounced along the pavement and went down a drain; George rolled up a sleeve and reached in.路上硬币掉了，滚过人行道，掉进下水道；乔治卷起袖子把胳膊伸进去。
  • He could not reach the coin, and could not pull his arm back either; soap and butter were no use, and firefighters freed him with a special grease.他找不到硬币，胳膊也拔不出来；肥皂和黄油都没用，最后消防员用特殊油脂把他救了出来。
  • The woman who keeps the sweet shop heard about it and sent him a large box of chocolates, so he came out of it rather well.糖果店老板娘听说后，送了他一大盒巧克力，所以他并不太难过。
Our model summary本站范文

I gave my nephew George fifty pence and told him to save it, but he set off to buy sweets. On the way he dropped the coin, and it rolled into a drain. George pushed his arm through the cover but could not find the coin, and then his arm got stuck. Soap and butter failed, so firemen freed him with special grease. Luckily, the sweet-shop owner heard the story and gave him a big box of chocolates.（78 词）
